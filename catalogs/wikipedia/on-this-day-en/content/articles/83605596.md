**Severe Tropical Storm Maysak**,[^cite_note-2] known in the Philippines as **Tropical Depression Henry** and in Vietnam as **Typhoon No. 1 of 2026** (Bão số 1 năm 2026), was a compact and moderately strong but deadly [tropical cyclone](8282374.md) that affected some portions of [Luzon](69375.md) in the [Philippines](23440.md), [Hainan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hainan,_China), [China](5405.md) and [Northern Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Vietnam) in early July 2026. It is also the tenth named storm of the [2026 Pacific typhoon season](80781992.md).

Maysak originated from an area of convection that developed east-southeast of [Manila](184334.md) on June 29. On July 1, [PAGASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PAGASA) upgraded the system into a tropical depression, assigning the local name *Henry*. The [Japan Meteorological Agency](457886.md) (JMA) followed suit on the same day while the system was moving northwestwards. The [Joint Typhoon Warning Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Typhoon_Warning_Center) (JTWC) also followed suit afterwards, designating Henry as *10W*. On July 2, JMA upgraded Henry into a tropical storm, as it was assigned the name *Maysak*. Maysak made landfall near [Lingshui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lingshui_Li_Autonomous_County), [Hainan Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hainan_Province) on July 3. On July 4, the JMA upgraded Maysak into a severe tropical storm, before it made its final landfall over [Móng Cái](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%B3ng_C%C3%A1i), [Quảng Ninh province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qu%E1%BA%A3ng_Ninh_province).

The remnants of Maysak spawned a significant tornado outbreak in southeastern and eastern China, claiming the lives of 12 people and injured 331. Overall, at least ten tornadoes were reported during this outbreak. Meanwhile, 159 people were killed from a flooding in [Guangxi](47836950.md) and caused an estimated damage amounted to ¥22.18 billion (US\$3.28 billion). In Vietnam, total reported damage was estimated to US\$1.07 million.

On June 29, an area of convection had developed 591 nm (1,095 km) of [Manila](184334.md), with satellite imagery indicating an elongated [low-level circulation center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low-level_circulation_center) (LLCC) with disorganized convection throughout the northern periphery, citing a medium potential of [tropical cyclogenesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_cyclogenesis).[^cite_note-3] At 02\:00 PHT of July 1 (18\:00 UTC of June 30), [PAGASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PAGASA) upgraded the disturbance into a tropical depression as it crossed over southern [Luzon](69375.md), with the system being assigned the local name *Henry*.[^cite_note-4] Meanwhile, at 21\:00 UTC, JTWC upgraded its chances to high, prompting to issue a TCFA warning as Henry moved through favorable conditions.[^cite_note-5] Early on July 1, the JMA classified Henry as a tropical depression, citing poor positional accuracy due to an obscure cloud system center (CSC), while inside a neutral environment under the influence of high [sea-surface temperatures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea-surface_temperatures) (SSTS), high [tropical cyclone heat potential](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_Cyclone_Heat_Potential), good [upper-level outflow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper-level_outflow), and dry air. By 18\:00 UTC, the system started moving west-northwestwards along the southwestern periphery of a mid-level [subtropical high](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subtropical_high).[^cite_note-\:0-6] At 21\:00 UTC, the JTWC followed suit, designating Henry as *10W*.[^cite_note-\:1-7]

By 06\:00 UTC, Henry entered a favorable environment for development, although its limited firmness of its structure caused the system to maintain its intensity.[^cite_note-\:0-6] [Multispectral satellite imagery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multispectral_imagery) (MSI) depicted the system with an exposed LLCC that was removed from the sheared convection to the southeast.[^cite_note-\:1-7] However, shortly after, Henry returned to a neutral environment for development, with water vapor imagery showing dry air in the system's movement, active convective cloud clusters around the CSC.[^cite_note-\:0-6] However, by 15\:00 UTC, the system started to reorganize itself around the broader-surrounding [cyclonic circulation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclonic_circulation).[^cite_note-\:1-7] At 18\:00 UTC, JMA upgraded Henry into a tropical storm, assigning the name *Maysak* to the system.[^cite_note-\:0-6] Later, Maysak exhibited multiple vortices wrapping around a cyclonic rotation that is south of [Hainan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hainan).[^cite_note-\:1-7] Early on July 3, the storm obtained good cloud characteristics of [anticyclonic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anticyclonic) [outflow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outflow_\(meteorology\)) while slowly moving north-northwestward.[^cite_note-\:0-6] By 03\:00 UTC, the JTWC upgraded Maysak into a tropical storm, with animated MSI depicting some slow consolidation with an irregular [central dense overcast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_dense_overcast) (CDO) overlaying an obscured LLCC.[^cite_note-\:1-7] At 10\:20 UTC, Maysak made its first landfall near [Lingshui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lingshui_Li_Autonomous_County), [Hainan Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hainan_Province).[^cite_note-8] [Satellite imagery](1184557.md) then depicted the storm with a compact vortex with deep convection over the LLCC in the southwestern quadrant.[^cite_note-\:1-7]

Early on July 4, MSI showed that the storm's [cumulonimbus clusters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cumulonimbus_clouds) began forming a curved band around the CSC,[^cite_note-\:0-6] before moving to a marginally favorable, [thermodynamic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermodynamic) and [kinematic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinematic) environment characterized by high [tropospheric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troposphere) moisture content, and sustained moderate upper-level divergence.[^cite_note-\:1-7] As Maysak moved through the [Gulf of Tonkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_of_Tonkin), the JMA upgraded it into a severe tropical storm[^cite_note-\:0-6] before the system made its second and final landfall in [Móng Cái](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%B3ng_C%C3%A1i), [Quảng Ninh province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qu%E1%BA%A3ng_Ninh_province) on July 4 at 12\:30 UTC.[^cite_note-9] At 15\:00 UTC, the JTWC issued their final reasoning on Maysak as the storm starts containing rich deep-layer [moisture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moisture) and land interaction.[^cite_note-\:1-7] Maysak later moved inland, causing the JMA to downgrade it to a tropical depression on July 5 at 18\:00 UTC. At the same time, JMA issued its final warning as Maysak moved further inland, tracking north-northeastwards.[^cite_note-\:0-6]

## Preparations

### Philippines

On July 1, at 05\:00 PHT (21\:00 UTC of the previous day), PAGASA issued [Tropical Cyclone Wind Signal No. 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_Cyclone_Wind_Signals#Tropical_Cyclone_Wind_Signal_No._1) in the southwestern portion of [Zambales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zambales), eastern portion of [Bataan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bataan), and [Lubang Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lubang_Islands).[^cite_note-10] All wind signals were halted six hours later.[^cite_note-11]

### Hong Kong and Macau

On July 1, the [Meteorological and Geophysical Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meteorological_and_Geophysical_Bureau) (SMG) issued Signal No. 1 at 23\:00 MST (15\:00 UTC).[^cite_note-12] On July 2, the [Hong Kong Observatory](21292238.md) (HKO) issued the [Standby Signal No. 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_tropical_cyclone_warning_signals#Meaning_of_signals) at 07\:40 HKT (23\:40 UTC).[^cite_note-13] On July 3, the SMG issued Signal No. 3 at 20\:00 MST (12\:00 UTC).[^cite_note-14] On July 4, the HKO cancelled all Tropical Cyclone Warning Signals at 03\:20 HKT (19\:20 UTC) and issued the [Strong Monsoon Signal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_Monsoon_Signal) immediately afterwards.[^cite_note-15] At 06\:00 MST (22\:00 UTC), the SMG issued Signal No. 1.[^cite_note-16] At 12\:30 MST (04\:30 UTC), the SMG canceled Signal No. 1.[^cite_note-17] At 16\:45 HKT (08\:45 UTC), the HKO canceled the Strong Monsoon Signal.[^cite_note-18]

## Impact

### Vietnam

In [Vietnam](202354.md), strong winds from Maysak uprooted trees and ripped metal roofs off buildings in [Móng Cái](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%B3ng_C%C3%A1i),[^cite_note-wric-19] and caused US\$1.07 million worth of damage in the country.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21]

### Laos

Maysak and [Typhoon Bavi](83602324.md) triggered severe flooding in Khounkham district, [Khammouane province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khammouane_province) in Laos. Total losses in Khounkham district reached [LAK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lao_kip) 39.7 billion (about USD 1.75 million).[^cite_note-22]

### China

In [Guangxi](47836950.md), [Nanning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanning) and Guigang, flooding killed 159 people, left ten missing and had affected more than 1.65 million people; 26 of the deaths were related to a dam breach in Nanning.[^cite_note-23] Severe flooding also submerged cars and trapped people in [Fangchenggang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fangchenggang) while in [Dongxing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dongxing,_Guangxi), trees were uprooted by strong winds.[^cite_note-wric-19] The storm also spawned 10 tornadoes in China, including one in [Hubei](83319.md), an EF3 tornado related to Maysak killed 12 people, injured 331, and damaged 23000 buildings.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25] Damage in [Guigang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guigang), Guangxi totaled to ¥560 million (US\$82.3 million).[^cite_note-26] Two sections of a dam in the Liulan Reservoir of [Hengzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hengzhou,_Guangxi) collapsed on July 6, which sent a massive torrent downstream.[^cite_note-27] The figure was later revised as total damage in Guangxi Province amounted to ¥22.18 billion (US\$3.28 billion).[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29]



[Sanya](1638437.md) endured a night of wind and rain, due to Maysak.

Wind gusts of 32.6 metres per second (73 mph) (level 11) were recorded in Haitang District, [Sanya](1638437.md) City, in the coastal region of Guangdong. Generally, winds were strong at levels 7–9 with gusts of 10–11, and some areas experienced gusts of 11–13. In Guangxi, strong winds of levels 10–11 were observed in the [Gulf of Tonkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_of_Tonkin) and along the coast of Fangcheng Port and Qinzhou prefectures, with localized gusts reaching level 12 or higher. Gusts in Jiangshan Town, Fangcheng District, Fangcheng Port City reached 46.9 metres per second (105 mph) (level 15). Very heavy rainfall occurred in Guangxi; Nanping Yao Township (Shangsi County, Fangcheng Port City) recorded a total accumulated rainfall of 671.9 mm (26.45 in) in 24 hours, while Shafu Town (Qinnan District, Qinzhou City) recorded a peak hourly rainfall of 186.2 mm (7.33 in).[^cite_note-30]

A deadly EF3 tornado near [Ezhou](4308457.md), China

Severe thunderstorms associated with the remnants of Severe Tropical Storm Maysak caused a small tornado outbreak in southeastern China and eastern China. Two tornadoes struck the [Baiyun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baiyun,_Guangzhou) and [Huadu districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huadu,_Guangzhou) of [Guangzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guangzhou) on July 4–5. The first tornado hit Baiyun District on July 4 and was rated EF0; the second tornado struck both Baiyun and Huadu on July 5, tearing off large areas of sheet‑metal factory roofs and uprooting or snapping numerous trees, and was rated EF1.[^cite_note-31] On July 6, the outbreak intensified. A tornado was confirmed in [Qinzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qinzhou), Guangxi.[^cite_note-\:4-32] Later in the day, an EF1 tornado struck [Qingyuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qingyuan), Guangdong. That evening, two deadly EF3 tornadoes struck populated areas in [Hubei Province](83319.md). The first EF3 tornado touched down near [Xianning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xianning) and moved through [Daye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daye), causing severe damage to a brick‑and‑concrete building, killing two people and injuring 53 before lifting near [Ezhou](4308457.md).[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34][^cite_note-\:2-35] The second, widely documented, EF3 tornado touched down in Ezhou before crossing the [Yangtze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yangtze) into [Huanggang](4088485.md).[^cite_note-\:4-32] This tornado killed 12 people and injured 331. The total damage reached ¥450 million (US\$66 million).[^cite_note-36][^cite_note-37] It collapsed walls of houses and damaged residential buildings, public facilities, windows, doors and the exterior facades of buildings and facilities on the campus of [Huanggang Normal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huanggang_Normal_University). Many trees were uprooted and a power and water outage occurred.[^cite_note-38] An EF1 tornado also struck [Yangxin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yangxin_County,_Hubei) in [Huangshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huangshi) on the same day. On July 7, an EF3 tornado passed through [Huainan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huainan), [Hefei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hefei), and [Chuzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chuzhou) in [Anhui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anhui), followed by one unrated in [Sihong County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sihong_County), Jiangsu, and another in [Hongze District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hongze,_Huai'an), Jiangsu.[^cite_note-\:4-32][^cite_note-39] Overall, at least ten tornadoes occurred during this outbreak.
