**Mittelangeln** (German pronunciation: [\[ˈmɪtl̩ˌʔaŋln̩\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Standard_German), lit. 'Middle [Angeln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angeln)'; [Danish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish_language): *Midtangel*) is a municipality in the district of [Schleswig-Flensburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schleswig-Flensburg), in [Schleswig-Holstein](27126.md), [Germany](11867.md). It was formed on 1 March 2013 by the merger of the former municipalities [Satrup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satrup), [Rüde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%BCde) and [Havetoftloit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Havetoftloit).
