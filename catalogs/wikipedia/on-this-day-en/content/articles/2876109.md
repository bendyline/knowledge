# Assisted dying

**Assisted dying**, **assisted death**, **aid in dying**, **medical aid in dying**, **medical assistance in dying** (**MAiD**) or **help to die** is the involvement of healthcare professionals in the provision of lethal drugs intended to end a patient's life, subject to eligibility criteria and safeguards.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

Assisted dying is a broad concept, which encompasses:

- [Assisted suicide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assisted_suicide), the practice of helping or assisting another person to end their own life

- [Euthanasia](9587.md), the practice of otherwise intentionally ending someone's life to relieve pain and suffering

- [Palliative sedation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palliative_sedation), which may in some cases accelerate the death of the patient, so sometimes it is also considered an assisted death
