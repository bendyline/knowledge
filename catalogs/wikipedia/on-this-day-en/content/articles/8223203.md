# List of diplomatic missions of Lithuania



Countries hosting Lithuanian diplomatic missions

This is a **list of diplomatic missions of Lithuania**, excluding honorary consulates.

Lithuania and the other [Baltic states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltic_states), together with the [Nordic countries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nordic_countries) have signed a memorandum of understanding[^cite_note-1] on the posting of diplomats at each other's missions abroad,[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] under the auspices of [Nordic-Baltic Eight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nordic-Baltic_Eight).[^cite_note-5]
