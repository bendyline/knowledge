The **Government of Kosovo** ([Albanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanian_language): *Qeveria e Kosovës*, [Serbian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_language): Влада Косова / Vlada Kosova) exercises executive authority in the [Republic of Kosovo](17391.md). It is composed of [government ministers](739237.md), and is led by the [prime minister](1260764.md). The prime minister is elected by the [Assembly of the Republic of Kosovo](1068656.md). Ministers are nominated by the prime minister and then confirmed by the assembly.

[Albin Kurti](4774301.md) is the current prime minister of Kosovo. His government, approved by the assembly and installed on March 22, 2021, consists of [Albanians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kosovo_Albanians), as well as ministers from Kosovo's ethnic minorities, which include [Bosniaks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosniaks), [Romani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romani_people_in_Kosovo), [Turks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_people) and [Serbs](59512.md). Although the government includes representatives of ethnic minorities, it is dominated by the Albanian majority, who have most influence on the decision-making.[^cite_note-epr-1]
