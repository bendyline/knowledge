**Sitaro Islands Regency** ([Indonesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_language): *Kabupaten Kepulauan Sitaro*, although its formal name is **Kabupaten Kepulauan Siau Tagulandang Biaro**) is a regency located off the northern extremity of Sulawesi Island in the southern [Sangihe Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangihe_Islands), [North Sulawesi](453688.md) Province, Indonesia. The regency was formed under *Law Number 15 Year 2007* from 2 January 2007 ([Indonesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_language): *Undang-Undang Nomor 15 Tahun 2007*), by separation from the [Sangihe Islands Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangihe_Islands_Regency) of which it had previously formed the southern part. The short form of the name is formed from one syllable from each of the names of the three principal islands — **Si**au, **Ta**gulandang and Bia**ro**.

The main island is [Siau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siau_Island), while further south (nearer Sulawesi) are [Tagulandang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagulandang) and [Biaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biaro_Island). The regency's land area is 218.22 km<sup>2</sup> and the total population was 63,801 at the 2010 census[^cite_note-Biro_Pusat_Statistik_2011-2] and 71,817 at the 2020 census;[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2021-3] the official estimate as at mid 2025 was 72,989 (comprising 36,746 males and 36,243 females).[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2026-1] Most of the population are fishermen and farmers. The religion of the majority of the population is [Christianity](5211.md), but there are also followers of [Islam](6037917.md) and [Buddhism](3267529.md). The main town and administrative centre is Ondong, in West Siau.

## Climate

Ondong, the seat of the regency has a [tropical rainforest climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_rainforest_climate) (Af) with heavy to very heavy rainfall year-round.
