**Nechayane** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Нечаяне) is a town in [Mykolaiv Oblast](494928.md), in southern Ukraine.[^cite_note-1] The town had a population of 1,874 at the time of the [2001 Ukrainian census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Ukrainian_census).[^cite_note-2]

## Geography

The town is located west of [Mykolaiv](901495.md) and north of [Ochakiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ochakiv).[^cite_note-3]
