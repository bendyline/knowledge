The **Okinawa Islands** ([Japanese](15606.md): 沖縄諸島 / 沖縄群島, [Hepburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hepburn_romanization): *Okinawa Shotō / Okinawa Guntō*) are an [island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island) group in [Okinawa Prefecture](22477.md), [Japan](15573.md), and are the principal island group of the [prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefecture).[^cite_note-n-1] The Okinawa Islands are part of the larger [Ryukyu Islands](8458939.md) group and are located between the [Amami Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amami_Islands) of [Kagoshima Prefecture](217439.md) to the northeast and the [Sakishima Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sakishima_Islands) of Okinawa Prefecture to the southwest.[^cite_note-d-2]

The Okinawa Islands, apart from the main island, contain three smaller island groups: the [Kerama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerama_Islands), [Yokatsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yokatsu_Islands) and [Iheya-Izena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iheya-Izena_Islands?action=edit\&redlink=1) island groups.[^cite_note-n-1][^cite_note-e-3][^cite_note-iheyaizena-4]

The Okinawa Islands are the political, cultural and population center of Okinawa Prefecture.[^cite_note-n-1] The prefectural capital of [Naha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naha,_Okinawa) is within the island group. 90% of the population of the prefecture reside within the Okinawa Islands, primarily on the largest island of the group, [Okinawa Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okinawa_Island).[^cite_note-n-1] Access to the various Okinawa Islands is primarily via small airports which connect to [Naha Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naha_Airport). Additionally, the islands are connected via [ferry](50771.md) service to the Port of Naha in the prefectural capital.[^cite_note-n-1]

The Okinawa Islands are within the [subtropical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subtropics) [climate zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification), which supports the production of [sugarcane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugarcane), [pineapples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pineapple) and [cut flowers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cut_flowers).[^cite_note-d-2] The [military bases of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Forces_Japan) in Okinawa Prefecture are located on the Okinawa Islands.[^cite_note-e-3]

Historically, the rule of the [Ryukyu Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryukyu_Kingdom), centered on Okinawa Island, consolidated the Okinawa Islands before spreading south to the [Miyako](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miyako_Islands) and [Yaeyama Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yaeyama_Islands) and north to [Amami Ōshima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amami_%C5%8Cshima).

## Islands

The *Okinawa Guntō*, defined by the Hydrographic and Oceanographic Department of the [Japan Coast Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Coast_Guard), cover the following islands:

- [Okinawa Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okinawa_Island) (Okinawa Hontō)

- Iheya-Izena Islands: [Iheya Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iheya_Island), [Izena Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Izena_Island), [Noho Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noho_Island)

- [Ie Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iejima)

- [Sesoko Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sesoko_Island)

- [Yokatsu Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uruma#Yokatsu_Islands): [Yabuchi Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yabuchi_Island), [Henza Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henza_Island), [Miyagi Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miyagi_Island), Ikei Island, [Hamahiga Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamahiga_Island), [Tsuken Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsuken_Island), Ukibaru Island, [Minamiukibaru Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minamiukibaru_Island), [Kudaka Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kudaka_Island)

- [Kume Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kume_Island)

- [Kerama Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerama_Islands): [Tokashiki Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokashiki_Island), [Zamami Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zamami_Island), [Aka Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aka_Island), [Geruma Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geruma_Island), [Keise Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keise_Islands) (Kamiyama Island, Kuefu Island, Nagannu Island)

- [Aguni Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aguni_Islands): [Aguni Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aguni_Island), [Tonaki Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tonaki_Island)

The [Geospatial Information Authority of Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geospatial_Information_Authority_of_Japan) (GSI) defines the *Okinawa Shotō*. They are an administratively-oriented group of islands and include the outlying [Iōtorishima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I%C5%8Dtorishima), which is otherwise seen as part of the [Amami Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amami_Islands).[^cite_note-mizutani2009-5]
