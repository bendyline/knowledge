**Samut Sakhon** ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): สมุทรสาคร, pronounced [\[samùt sǎːkʰɔːn\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Thai)) is a city in [Thailand](30128.md); it is the capital of [Samut Sakhon province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samut_Sakhon_province). It is also part of the [Bangkok Metropolitan Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangkok_Metropolitan_Region).

## Toponymy

Samut Sakhon was formerly called Tha Chin (Chinese Pier) probably because, in the old days, it had been a trading port for a vast number of Chinese junks. In 1548, a city named Sakhon Buri was established at the mouth of the [Tha Chin River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tha_Chin_River). A center for recruiting troops from various seaside towns. The name of the city was changed to Mahachai when [Khlong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khlong) (canal) Mahachai was dug in 1704 to connect the Tha Chin River to the city. Later, the city was renamed Samut Sakhon by [King Rama IV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongkut) but it is still popularly called Mahachai by its residents.[^cite_note-TAT-4]

## Geography

The general topography of the city is a coastal lowland about 1 meter above sea level. There are no islands, mountains or minerals. The Tha Chin River and Mahachai Canal flow into the [Gulf of Thailand](198107.md) at Bang Ya Phraek subdistrict. The general condition is an [urban community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_community) with houses, industrial plants and a dense population.[^cite_note-LOCATION-5]\
&#x20;Neighboring subdistricts are (from the north clockwise): Tha Sai, Khok Kham, Bang Ya Phraek and Tha Chin of [Mueang Samut Sakhon district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mueang_Samut_Sakhon_district) of [Samut Sakhon province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samut_Sakhon_province).

## Administration

### Provincial government

The combined administration of Mahachai, [Tha Chalom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tha_Chalom) and Krokkrak subdistricts (*[tambons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tambon)*) is responsible for an area that covers 10.33 square kilometres (3.99 sq mi), as of December 2024: 53,221 people and 19,235 households.[^cite_note-DOPA-2]

### Local government

As of December 2024 there is ฺSamut Sakhon city municipality (*[thesaban nakhon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesaban_nakhon)*) which covers the combined area of Mahachai, Tha Chalom and Krokkrak subdistricts. There are 32 communities (*chumchon*), although not directly chosen by the local citizens, which provides advice and recommendations to the local administrative organization.[^cite_note-COMMUNITY-6]

## Healthcare

There is no [health promoting hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hospitals_in_Thailand#Sub-district_Health_Promoting_Hospitals) in Samut Sakhon city. But there are in Mahachai subdistrict: [Samut Sakhon Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samut_Sakhon_Hospital) with 600 beds, [Ekachai Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_hospitals_in_Thailand#Private_Hospitals) with 142 beds, [Mahachai 3 Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_hospitals_in_Thailand#Private_Hospitals) with 180 beds, [Jesada Vechakarn Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_hospitals_in_Thailand#Private_Hospitals) with 10 beds and in Tha Chalom subdistricht: [Metropolitan Hospital Tha Chalom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_hospitals_in_Thailand#Community_Hospitals_\(Category_M2,_F1,_F2_and_F3\))

## Transportation

The city is the intersection of [highway 35](59841832.md) eastbound (Bangkok route), highway 35 westbound ([Pak Tho district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pak_Tho_district), [Ratchaburi province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ratchaburi_province)), Ekkachai road (no. 3242) ([Chom Thong district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chom_Thong_district,_Bangkok), Bangkok).[^cite_note-ROADS-10]\
&#x20;There is [Maeklong Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maeklong_Railway) a Wongwian Yai - Mahachai train service, starting from [Wongwian Yai station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wongwian_Yai_station), Bangkok, to [Mahachai station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahachai_railway_station) (distance 33.1 kilometres (20.6 mi)) every day from 05.30-20.00 hrs, and a Tha Chalom - Mae Klong train service, starting from [Ban Laem station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ban_Laem_railway_station), Tha Chalom subdistrict, to [Maeklong station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maeklong_railway_station), [Samut Songkhram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samut_Songkhram) (distance 33.7 kilometres (20.9 mi)).[^cite_note-TRAIN-11]
