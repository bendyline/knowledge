The **Suluhu Cabinet** was officially formed in [Tanzania](30118.md) on 31 March 2021. Following the death of former [President](516108.md) [John Magufuli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Magufuli), [Samia Suluhu Hassan](38577619.md) the [Vice President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_President_of_Tanzania) in the previous cabinet was sworn in as the new president. Within two weeks of her assignment, she appointed a new vice president and reshuffled the previous cabinet.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

## First Term

### Inaugural Cabinet

This marks the first cabinet in Tanzanian history with a female [president](516108.md). The inaugural cabinet was confirmed on 31 March 2021.[^cite_note-3]

### First Cabinet Reshuffle

Suluhu conducted her first major cabinet reshuffle on September 12, 2021. Dr [Stergomena Tax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stergomena_Tax) filled the empty seat of Kwandikwa as the Minister of Defence and National Service as the first female to hold the position in the country's history.[^cite_note-5] A new attorney general was also appointed Dr Eliezer Feleshi replaced [Adelardus Kilangi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adelardus_Kilangi).

### Second Cabinet Reshuffle

On 31 December 2021 Samia Suluhu gave her first new years address to the nation.[^cite_note-6] During the address, she hinted that she would be carrying out a cabinet reshuffle to expel ministers she suspects of siding with rival politicians in the party. On 8 January 2022 she announced her new cabinet, reshuffling ministers and creating two new cabinet positions. The first being the Prime Minister's Office Policy, Parliamentary Affairs, Labour, Employment, Youth and the Disabled was split into two: Policy and Parliament Affairs; and Labour, Youth, Employment and Persons with Disabilities. The second being the split of the Ministry of Health, Community Development, Gender, Seniors and Children into the Minister of Health Development, Seniors and Children; and the Ministry of Community Development & Gender.

All new ministers resumed their new post on 10 January 2022.[^cite_note-\:1-7]

#### Changes

- On 1 April 2022, president Suluhu made a small shift of the dockets of three ministers. Minister [George Simbachawene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Simbachawene) moves to the Office of the Prime Minister - Parliament, Policy and Coordination, replacing Dr [Pindi Chana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pindi_Chana). Dr Chana moved to the Ministry of Natural Resources and Tourism, replacing Dr [Damas Ndumabro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damas_Ndumbaro). Dr Ndumabro takes over Simbacawene's previous role as the Minister of Constitutional and Legal Affairs.

  [^cite_note-8]

- On 2 October 2022, president Suluhu dropped [Liberata Mulamula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberata_Mulamula) as the [Minister of Foreign Affairs, E.A.C., Regional and International Cooperation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Tanzania\)), and replaced her with Dr [Stergomena Tax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stergomena_Tax). Dr Tax's previous post as the Minister of Defense was given to [Innocent Bashungwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Innocent_Bashungwa). Innocent's previous post as Minister of State in the President's Office Regional Administration and Local Government was given to [Angellah Kairuki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angellah_Kairuki).

  [^cite_note-9]

- On 14 February 2023, [Mohamed Mchengerwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Mchengerwa) switched cabinet positions with [Pindi Chana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pindi_Chana) and became the new Minister of Natural Resources and Tourism and Pindi Chana became the new Minister of Culture, Artists and Sports.

  [^cite_note-10]

- On 26 February 2023, [Abdallah Ulega](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdallah_Ulega) was promoted to be the Minister for Livestock and Fisheries, replacing Mashimba Ndaki. In the re-shuffle, the Ministry for Investment was moved into the presidents office.

  [^cite_note-11]

- On 1 April 2023, president Suluhu switched the positions of [Jenista Mhagama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenista_Mhagama) and [George Simbachawene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Simbachawene), moving Simbachawene from the Prime Minister's office to the President's office.

- On 4 July 2023, president Suluhu made some changes to the cabinet mandates.

  [^cite_note-\:0-12]

  - The Ministry of Finance and Planning was re-established as the Ministry of Finance and [Mwigulu Nchemba](39528655.md) retained his position.

    [^cite_note-\:0-12]

  - The Ministry Investment, Industry and Trade was re-established as the Ministry Industry and Trade and [Ashatu Kijaji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashatu_Kijaji) retained her position.

    [^cite_note-\:0-12]

  - A new office was created in the presidents office, the Minister of State in the President's Office Planning and Investment, and [Kitila Mkumbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kitila_Mkumbo) was appointed as minister.

    [^cite_note-\:0-12]

### Third Cabinet Reshuffle

On 30 August 2023 Samia Suluhu made a third major reshuffled of the cabinet. The position of deputy prime minister was added and [Doto Biteko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doto_Biteko) was only the third person ever to be appointed in this position. Furthermore, the Ministry of Works and Transport was split into two with a new Ministry of Works and a Ministry of Transport.[^cite_note-13]

All new ministers resumed their new post on 1 September 2023.[^cite_note-\:1-7][^cite_note-14]
