The **Minister for Foreign Affairs** is a government official responsible for overseeing the foreign policy and international diplomacy of Ghana. Currently [Samuel Okudzeto Ablakwa](40018523.md), the minister is usually one of the most senior members of Cabinet. Under pre-1992 military regimes in Ghana, the title minister has been replaced by commissioner or secretary.

During the government rule of [Nana Akufo-Addo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nana_Akufo-Addo) of the [New Patriotic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Patriotic_Party) between January 2017 and January 2025, the ministry was combined with other portfolios to form the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Regional Integration under [Shirley Ayorkor Botchwey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shirley_Ayorkor_Botchwey). On 18 September 2025, the ministry awarded diplomatic passport to the following Ghanaians, [Ibrahim Mahama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_Mahama_\(artist\)), Wade Maya, [Rocky Dawuni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocky_Dawuni), Anita Erskine and Dentaa.[^cite_note-1]
