***Mosel* (A512)** is the second ship of the [*Elbe*-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbe-class_replenishment_ship) [replenishment ships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Replenishment_ship) of the [German Navy](12960.md).

## Development

Main article: [Elbe-class replenishment ship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbe-class_replenishment_ship)

The *Elbe*-class replenishment ships are also known tenders of the [German Navy](12960.md). In [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language), this type of ship is called *Versorgungsschiffe* which can be translated as "supply ship" though the official translation in English is "replenishment ship".

They are intended to support German naval units away from their home ports. The ships carry [fuel](21188370.md), [provisions](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/provision), [ammunition](18940621.md) and other [matériel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mat%C3%A9riel) and also provide medical services. The ships are named after German rivers where German parliaments were placed.

## Construction and career

*Mosel* was [launched](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceremonial_ship_launching) in April 1993 in [Bremen-Vegesack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bremen-Vegesack), Germany. She was [commissioned](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_commissioning) on 1 July 1993.[^cite_note-1]

On 31 August 2013, *Mosel* and [Rottweil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_minehunter_Rottweil?action=edit\&redlink=1) visited [Sevastopol](51587.md).[^cite_note-2]
