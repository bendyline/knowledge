**Italy–Lebanon relations** ([Italian](14708.md): *relazioni bilaterali tra Italia e Libano*, [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): العلاقات الإيطالية اللبنانية) are relations between [Italy](14532.md) and [Lebanon](17771.md). Both countries are members of the [Union for the Mediterranean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_for_the_Mediterranean). Lebanese-Italian relations go back to ancient times. [Phoenicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenicia) traded [Tyrian purple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyrian_purple) (which was extremely valuable and rare), Phoenician [wine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenicians_and_wine), olive oil and [cedar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cedrus_libani) wood with [Romans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Empire).

## History

In 64 B.C., the Roman general [Pompey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pompey) added Lebanon to the [Roman Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Republic). During and before this time, [Phoenicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenicia) and Romans exchanged knowledge, habits, and customs.

Lebanon opened a [legation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legation) in 1946, which was transformed into an embassy in 1955. Both countries signed a Treaty of Friendship, Cooperation and Navigation in 1949. The [Italian Cultural Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istituto_Italiano_di_Cultura) of [Beirut](37428.md) (*Istituto Italiano di Cultura di Beirut*) is located on the ground floor of the Embassy in the suburb of [Baabda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baabda).[^cite_note-1]

## Official visits

In April 2014, Italian Defense Minister [Roberta Pinotti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberta_Pinotti) visited Lebanese President [Michel Sleiman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_Sleiman) and Prime Minister [Tammam Salam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tammam_Salam) to discuss international support for the [Lebanese Armed Forces](17779.md).[^cite_note-2]

## Economic relations

In 2019, the volume of trade between Italy and Lebanon amounted to \$1.35 billion, with Italy exporting \$1.31 billion worth of goods to Lebanon and Lebanon exporting \$39.8 million worth of goods to Italy.[^cite_note-\:0-3]

Italy's main export to Lebanon is [refined oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petroleum_product) (41.5%), with [jewellery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewellery) (5.01%) and [eyewear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyewear) (1.98%) also among the most-exported products from Italy. Lebanon's main export to Italy are scrap [copper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copper) (24.8%) and [phosphoric acid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phosphoric_acid) (24.5%), with other notable exports being [gold](12240.md) (6.76%) and scrap [aluminium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aluminium) (5.85%).[^cite_note-\:0-3]

## Italian aid to Lebanon

The Italian Government supported the reconstruction of Lebanon after the [Taef Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taef_Agreement).

In December 2013, the Italian Government announced increased funding to Lebanon to assist Lebanon in dealing with refugees fleeing the [Syrian Civil War](30741795.md). Italy also announced a rescue mission with five to seven ships to rescue thousands of migrants, mostly Syrians, in the [Mediterranean Sea](19006.md).[^cite_note-4]

In 2023, Italy reaffirmed its commitment to ensuring stability in Lebanon and descalating conflict in Lebanon which had led to an increase in migration from Lebanon to Europe.[^cite_note-5]

## Resident diplomatic missions

- Italy has an embassy in [Beirut](37428.md).

- Lebanon has an embassy in [Rome](25458.md) and consulate-general in [Milan](36511.md).
