**Marondera**, originally known as **Marandellas**, is a capital city of [Mashonaland East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mashonaland_East), Zimbabwe, located about 72 kilometres (45 mi) east of [Harare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harare).

## History

It was first known as Marandella's Kraal, corrupted from Marondera, chief of the ruling [VaRozvi people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rozvi_Empire) who lived in the area. British colonialists as they were colonizing Zimbabwe, first used it as a rest stop on the way to Harare. Later destroyed in the Shona resistance of 1896, the town was moved 4 miles (6 km) north to the [Beira–Bulawayo railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beira%E2%80%93Bulawayo_railway) line.[^cite_note-2]

Constituted a village in 1913, it became a town in 1943. During the [South African (Boer) War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boer_War), it was used by the British as a staging point for military operations into the [Transvaal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_African_Republic). During [World War II](32927.md), over 600 [Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_people) refugees, mostly women and children, escaping from [Soviet-occupied Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupation_of_Poland_\(1939%E2%80%931945\)), were admitted in Marondera.[^cite_note-3] In 1946, the refugee camp was closed, and the Poles were relocated to [Gatooma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kadoma,_Zimbabwe), from where they were eventually repatriated to Europe.[^cite_note-4]

## Demographics

Marondera is a [multicultural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multicultural) city, with a variety of ethnic groups and a Shona majority. Within the African population is a notable proportion of people of [Malawian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malawian) origin who speak Chichewa and [Chitumbuka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tumbuka_language), and whose parents migrated and took employment on the white owned tobacco farms. White Maronderans are overwhelmingly of [British descent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo_African), with smaller groups of Dutch, Afrikaans, Greek and other European heritage. Other minorities include mixed race and Asian residents. Most residents of Marondera are traditional or syncretic Christians, predominantly [mainline Protestants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mainline_Protestants), [Roman Catholics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholics), evangelicals and *mapostori* (a sect of Old Testament bible followers who live like ancient prophets and may dress in white robes). [Islam](6037917.md) is practised by a very small minority of people in Marondera, almost all of whom are [Asians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asians) and people of Malawian descent.

The indigenous people of Marondera descend mainly from the [Shona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shona_people) royal families of [Svosve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Svosve?action=edit\&redlink=1), and the nearby royal households of [Chikwaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chikwaka?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Nyashanu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyashanu?action=edit\&redlink=1) and others royal family lives there the [Mashonganyika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mashonganyika?action=edit\&redlink=1) family etc. They are traditionally farmers.

Marondera is renowned for its high quality educational institutions, which have educated many of the country's most prominent citizens. The junior schools include Godfrey Huggins, Tapfuma School, Dombotombo School,Lendy Park School [Springvale House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springvale_House), [Digglefold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digglefold?action=edit\&redlink=1), R.G Mugabe Primary School, Nyamei Primary School, Ruware Primary School and [Ruzawi School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruzawi_School) and the senior schools are [Marondera High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marondera_High), [Peterhouse Boys' School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peterhouse_Boys'_School), [Peterhouse Girls' School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peterhouse_Girls'_School), Rakodzi High, [Watershed College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watershed_College), Nyameni High, Cherutombo High, Chitepo Secondary, [Bernard Mizeki College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernard_Mizeki_College), [Waddilove High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waddilove_High_School) and [Nagle House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagle_House?action=edit\&redlink=1). The first black mayor of Marondera was T. K. Muronda in 1980.

Marondera is situated on the [highveld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highveld) plateau, and has a [subtropical highland climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subtropical_highland_climate) (Köppen Cwb). The city enjoys a sunny climate, with the summer months (October to April) characterised by warm to hot days followed by afternoon thundershowers and cool evenings, and the winter months (May to September) by dry, sunny days followed by cold nights. Temperatures are usually fairly mild due to the city's high elevation, with an average maximum daytime temperature in January of 25.6 °C (78.1 °F), dropping to an average maximum of around 16 °C (61 °F) in June.

Winter is the sunniest time of the year, with mild days and cool nights, dropping to 5 °C (39.4 °F) in June and July. The temperature occasionally drops to below freezing at night, causing frost.

Regular cold fronts pass over the city in winter bringing cool southeasterly winds but and grey skies. The annual average rainfall is 850 millimetres, which is mostly concentrated in the summer months.[^cite_note-WMO-5] Infrequent showers can occur through the course of the year, especially during spring.
