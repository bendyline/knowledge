Following the [fall of the Assad regime](78548066.md) on 8 December 2024, [Israel](9282173.md) invaded the demilitarized [buffer zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Disengagement_Observer_Force) in southwestern [Syria](7515849.md) (adjacent to the [Israeli-occupied](60364960.md) [Golan Heights](7515865.md)) and has continued to occupy it. Israel also carried out an airstrike campaign to cripple the [Syrian Armed Forces](27479.md), and demanded that it stay out of southern Syria. The Israeli government remarked that these actions were intended to prevent any "potential threat" from post-war Syria.

Israel took advantage of the [power vacuum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_vacuum) created by the fall of former Syrian president [Bashar al-Assad](364813.md) to increase the amount of territory it controlled by several hundred square miles.[^cite_note-252] Israel declared the [1974 Agreement on Disengagement](40148472.md) with [Ba'athist Syria](57134800.md) to be void.[^cite_note-cnn20241208a-253][^cite_note-254] Israel initially said this new invasion would be "temporary", but later said it would hold onto the territory for an "unlimited time".[^cite_note-255] Israel also launched extensive aerial and naval strikes on Syrian military targets across the country, under an operation codenamed **Operation Arrow of Bashan** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): מבצע חץ הבשן, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Hebrew): *Mivtza Ḥetz HaBashan*).[^cite_note-256][^cite_note-257] Israel's campaign crippled Syria's military capabilities, including [its army](3423927.md) and [its navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_Navy), and destroyed the [Syrian chemical weapons](40505894.md) stockpiles.

Israel's campaign in Syria was internationally condemned and was accused of violating [international law](8195726.md).[^cite_note-ITWP-258] Syrian President [Ahmed al-Sharaa](44007699.md) condemned Israel's actions and demanded withdrawal, but also said his country was not in a position to be drawn into another war following the 13-year [Syrian civil war](30741795.md).[^cite_note-259][^cite_note-aljazeera20241215-260]

On 25 February 2025, Israel deepened its invasion of southern Syria while conducting a wave of airstrikes there and in [Damascus](8914.md), one day after demanding the [Syrian caretaker government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_caretaker_government) demilitarize southern Syria. Since the July 2025 [Suwayda clashes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Syria_clashes_\(July_2025%E2%80%93present\)), Israel has launched airstrikes against the Syrian military that it says are in defense of the [Syrian Druze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_Druze).

By December 2025, the [Armed Conflict Location and Event Data](43040226.md) reported that since the invasion, Israel has carried out attacks across Syria more than 600 times, averaging nearly two times a day.[^cite_note-261]

## Background

See also: [Israel–Syria relations](23533046.md)



Map of the UNDOF Zone (in purple)

Since the [Six-Day War](29328.md) in 1967, Israel has [occupied](60364960.md) most of the [Golan Heights](7515865.md) region of Syria. After the [Yom Kippur War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yom_Kippur_War) in 1973, [Israel and Syria agreed to a ceasefire](40148472.md) which created the [UN Disengagement Observer Force (UNDOF)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Disengagement_Observer_Force), which maintains a small [buffer zone](489870.md) between the two countries forces. In 1981, Israel [annexed the region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golan_Heights_Law), a move condemned by the [United Nations](31769.md) and unrecognized by any country [except the United States (recognized 2019)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_recognition_of_the_Golan_Heights_as_part_of_Israel).[^cite_note-262][^cite_note-p589-263] During its occupation, Israel has [depopulated Syrian towns and villages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_towns_and_villages_depopulated_in_the_Arab%E2%80%93Israeli_conflict) and promoted [Israeli settlement](15123.md) in the Heights.[^cite_note-264]

In November 2024, the United Nations accused Israel of violating the 1974 Agreement on Disengagement in November with engineering work and battle tanks inside the demilitarized zone. UNDOF stated it had "repeatedly engaged with the IDF to protest the construction." Israel responded that it was "working to establish a barrier on Israeli territory exclusively in order to thwart a possible terrorist invasion and protect the security of Israel's borders," and that "Israeli and IDF officials maintain close contact with UN officials who are familiar with the threats in the region."[^cite_note-e751-265]

In December 2024, the [Syrian opposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_opposition_\(2011%E2%80%932024\)) launched a [major offensive](78460726.md) against the [Bashar al-Assad](364813.md) led [Syrian regime](57134800.md). Following the [fall of the Assad regime](78548066.md), Israeli [Minister of Diaspora Affairs and Combating Antisemitism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Diaspora_Affairs_and_Combating_Antisemitism) [Amichai Chikli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amichai_Chikli) expressed apprehension at the opposition forces' political upheaval of the [Syrian government](20933383.md), claiming that: "Most of [Syria](7515849.md) is now under the control of [al-Qaeda](1921.md) and [Daesh](9087364.md)."[^cite_note-cnn20241208b-266] He implored Israel to re-fortify its defensive line at [Mount Hermon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Hermon) in Israeli-occupied [Golan Heights](7515865.md) based on 1974 borders in order to prevent potential attacks by the new regime.[^cite_note-267]

The collective of [Druze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druze) community leaders in Syria condemned the invasion.[^cite_note-268] The newly formed [Suwayda Military Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suwayda_Military_Council) declared its commitment to integrating into the Syrian Army while also supporting the demilitarization of southern Syria. The [Sheikh al-Karama Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikh_al-Karama_Forces), a Druze military organization affiliated with the FSA condemned the invasion as a violation of Syria's sovereignty and stated they would work with opposition forces.[^cite_note-269] Sheikh al-Karama Forces had issued a joint statement with the [Al-Jabal Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Jabal_Brigade) in January 2025 emphasizing that it was prepared to integrate into a united "military body" in southern Syria.[^cite_note-270]

## Israeli objectives

On 9 December 2024, Israeli Defense Minister [Israel Katz](19041378.md) issued orders for military objectives in southern Syria. The IDF received four primary strategic objectives from Katz to conduct "in the immediate term":[^cite_note-israelhayom20241209-271]

1. To secure complete control over the buffer zone and other nearby strategic positions in Syria.

2. To establish a security zone extending beyond the buffer zone, focusing on removing all heavy weaponry and terrorist infrastructure that could pose a threat to Israel, while establishing contact with local Druze communities and other regional communities.

3. To immediately prevent the reestablishment of [Iranian](14653.md) [arms smuggling routes](42210.md) to [Lebanon](17771.md) through Syrian territory and border crossings.

4. To continue destroying strategic heavy weapons systems throughout Syria, including air defense networks, missile systems, and coastal defense installations.

Israel has also stated its demands for all Syrian territory south of Damascus to be demilitarized, including the governorates of [Quneitra](7445647.md), [Daraa](5317030.md) and [Suwayda](3096933.md), and that it would not tolerate threats against the [Druze](47104446.md) in southern Syria.[^cite_note-272][^cite_note-273]

## Events

### Ground operations



Israeli soldiers patrolling the ceasefire line in the Golan Heights, December 2024

Following the [advance of the Syrian opposition in the south](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Syria_offensive_\(2024\)), Israel reinforced [Division 210](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/210th_Division_\(Israel\)) and deployed additional troops to the [Golan Heights](7515865.md).[^cite_note-274] When Syrian opposition forces first occupied the southern town of [Hader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hader,_Quneitra_Governorate), it was reported that the IDF had advanced further into the Golan Heights to repel an attack on a [United Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Disengagement_Observer_Force) post in the area.[^cite_note-275][^cite_note-276] Additionally, the IDF significantly reinforced its presence within the established buffer zone.[^cite_note-timesofisrael20241209-277][^cite_note-factjo20241208-278]

#### 2024

On 8 December 2024, Israeli armored units, including [main battle tanks](26595097.md), crossed the ceasefire line in the occupied Golan Heights fence and entered the buffer zone during early morning operations. Israeli Army Radio stated that the IDF and [Northern Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Command_\(Israel\)) initiated the operation in order to strengthen its [border with Syria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purple_Line_\(ceasefire_line\)) and prevent spillover or attacks against Israel.[^cite_note-factjo20241208-278][^cite_note-sarayanews20241208-279][^cite_note-280]



Israeli convoy advancing into Syria, December 2024

The military advance extended into the [Quneitra Governorate](7445647.md), with substantial forces entering the town of [Khan Arnabah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khan_Arnabah). Syrian media reported that Israeli forces had advanced into the city center of [al-Salam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madinat_al-Salam_\(Syria\)).[^cite_note-sarayanews20241208-279] Following the advance into the Quneitra Governorate and the Syrian-controlled side of [Mount Hermon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Hermon) by the Israeli Special Forces [Shaldag Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaldag_Unit) (Unit 5101),[^cite_note-SHLD-12] Israeli Prime Minister [Benjamin Netanyahu](56472.md) issued a statement saying that the 1974 ceasefire agreement had collapsed when Syrian soldiers abandoned their posts in the Golan Heights, and that the area was to be temporarily occupied[^cite_note-282] He also stated that "We will not allow any hostile force to establish itself on our border".[^cite_note-283] Netanyahu stated Assad's fall "offers great opportunity" to occupy more Syrian territory.[^cite_note-284] During the takeover, IDF spokesman [Avichay Adraee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avichay_Adraee) announced a curfew in five Syrian villages, including Quneitra, ordering locals of the border towns to remain inside "until further notice."[^cite_note-reuters20241208-285][^cite_note-nytimes20241208-286] On 8 December 2024, [Al Arabiya](321645.md) reported that Israel had taken control of [Tell al-Hara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tell_al-Hara).[^cite_note-287] On 10 December 2024, correspondents for [Al Jazeera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Jazeera_Arabic) and [Enab Baladi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enab_Baladi) reported Israeli tanks in several Syrian villages such as [Beer Ajam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beer_Ajam).[^cite_note-288][^cite_note-289] Although Israeli tanks were also reported as far as [Qatana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatana), 26 kilometres (16 mi) from [Damascus](8914.md)[^cite_note-thestandard20241210-290] an IDF spokesman insisted that "IDF forces are not advancing towards Damascus. This is not something we are doing or pursuing in any way" while acknowledging that, beyond the buffer zone, "a few additional points" had been seized. The Israeli Defense Minister also stated that Israel aims to establish a "sterile defence zone" in southern Syria to "prevent the establishment and organisation of terror in Syria".[^cite_note-Sterile-291][^cite_note-292]

On 11 December, orthodox [Chabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chabad) Jews printed the [Tanya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanya_\(Judaism\)), a central Chabad religious text, from a light truck on Syrian land, at the base of Mount Hermon, a short distance to the east of the purple line.[^cite_note-o764-293]

On 12 December, Syrian residents of Hader, [Hamidiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamidiya,_Quneitra_Governorate?action=edit\&redlink=1), and [Umm Batna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umm_Batna?action=edit\&redlink=1) in Quneitra Governorate were displaced from their homes after the IDF entered with military vehicles, with Israeli troops subsequently probing Umm Batna in its entirety.[^cite_note-294]

From 12 to 13 December, Syrian sources reported that Israeli forces conducted formal meetings with local community representatives in the [Yarmouk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yarmuk_\(river\)) Basin area in southwestern [Daraa Governorate](5317030.md), reportedly using loudspeakers and a low-flying drone to broadcast messages urging meetings and requirements. The Israeli military reportedly articulated specific security requirements for the local population, including the surrender of all weapons in the village, compliance with home search operations, and the prohibition of armed resistance against any military operations. Syrian sources also claimed that Israeli troops used loudspeakers to warn villagers that the Israeli forces have intentions to search and confiscate weapons in the area.[^cite_note-295][^cite_note-296]

On 15 December, Israel attempted to depopulate several Syrian villages in the newly occupied part of the Golan Heights. After the residents declined, Israel began destroying the electricity and water networks in the villages to attempt to forcibly evict them.[^cite_note-z050-297] The Israeli government also declared that it would expand Israel settlements in the Golan Heights.[^cite_note-u500-298] Netanyahu announced plans to double the Golan Heights' population in a statement on the same day, saying Israel would continue to hold onto it and that "Strengthening the Golan is strengthening the State of Israel."[^cite_note-299]

On 17 December, Netanyahu met with [Defense Minister](3226446.md) [Israel Katz](19041378.md), [Chief of the General Staff](3011266.md) [Herzi Halevi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herzi_Halevi), commander of the [Northern Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Command_\(Israel\)) [Ori Gordin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ori_Gordin), and [Shin Bet](311752.md) head [Ronen Bar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronen_Bar) on the Syrian side of Mount Hermon, where they held a security briefing and visited outposts at the summit. During a video statement filmed on the summit, Netanyahu said that the IDF would stay in Syria "until another solution ensuring Israel's security is found".[^cite_note-300][^cite_note-301]

On 18 December, it was reported that over 100 Syrian families had been forcibly expelled from the Golan Heights by the Israeli military. Witnesses describe that Israeli soldiers had opened fire on them and on their homes. The United Nations peacekeepers have been removing Israeli flags in the newly occupied area.[^cite_note-w844-302]

On 19 December, it was reported that the Israeli military is preventing Syrian farmers in [Ma'ariya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ma'ariya) from accessing their fields.[^cite_note-d217-303]

On 20 December, the Israeli military occupied two addition Syrian villages, [Jamlah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamla) and [Maaraba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ma'ariya), and then shot bullets at Syrians protesting the Israeli occupation.[^cite_note-h948-304]

On 25 December, the Israeli military shot at protesters in the Syrian villages of Suweisa and Diwaya Al-Kabira in the Quneitra Governorate.[^cite_note-y326-305]

On 30 December, Israeli forces advanced into [Madinat al-Baath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madinat_al-Baath) and searched the local administrative offices.[^cite_note-306] On the same day, Israeli military lecturer Rami Simani stated that Syria has "never had a real right to exist" and that "Israel can and must cause Syria to disappear", advocating a partition of Syria.[^cite_note-i865-307]

#### 2025

On 9 January, Israeli officials said that they are going to occupy for "the long run" a 15 km "zone of control" and 60 km "sphere of influence" deeper into Syria.[^cite_note-t283-308]

On 31 January, Israeli troops clashed with five pro-Assad Syrian rebels in [Trinjeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinjeh). *[The Jerusalem Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Jerusalem_Post)* reported that the [Islamic Resistance Front in Syria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Resistance_Front_in_Syria) was responsible for the attack, but the [Syrian Popular Resistance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_Popular_Resistance) claimed responsibility, claiming that multiple Israeli soldiers were wounded and vehicles were damaged. The IDF said that it suffered no casualties.[^cite_note-NewArab010225-28][^cite_note-309]

On 2 February, the IDF near [Jubata al-Khashab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jubata_al-Khashab) and surrounding villages established [outposts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outpost_\(military\)) and [military bases](843566.md) with calls for more [Israeli settlement](15123.md) into the Golan Heights by Israeli officials.[^cite_note-310]

On 11 February, [Israeli Army Radio](5242192.md) reported that Israel would prolong the occupation of their newly invaded territory in Syria throughout 2025.[^cite_note-permanent-281]

On 23 February, the IDF confirmed that it had built at least nine army posts in Syria, including two on Mount Hermon and seven on the buffer zone.[^cite_note-311]

On 25 February, the IDF completed raids southern [Rif Dimashq](4521946.md), in the villages of Al-Kiswa, and [Daraa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daraa), in the vicinity of the city of [Izra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Izra) and penetrating the outskirts of [Al-Bakkar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Bakkar) village, and penetrated the administrative borders between the governorates of [Daraa](5317030.md) and [Quneitra](7445647.md) in southern Syria and the Ain al-Bayda area in the countryside of Quneitra in southern Syria, also taking the villages of Sidon Al-Golan and Sidon Al-Hanout in the countryside of Quneitra.[^cite_note-312][^cite_note-313] This came after plans to install advanced military technology, border fencing, and checkpoints in the borders of Gaza, Lebanon, and Syria.[^cite_note-314]

On 27 February, the Islamic Resistance Front in Syria began forming military cells in Southern Syria, likely with Iranian backing.[^cite_note-315]

On 1 March, Netanyahu and Defense Minister Israel Katz ordered the IDF to prepare to defend the Druze community in [Jaramana](5146490.md) following [clashes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druze%E2%80%93Syria_clashes_\(2025%E2%80%93present\)) between Syrian security forces and a local group called the "[Jaramana Shield Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaramana_Shield_Brigade)" that left one Syrian soldier dead.[^cite_note-316][^cite_note-317]

On 8 March, the IDF announced that it carried out several raids in southern Syria the week prior, capturing and destroying weapons.[^cite_note-318]

On 19 March 2025, Israel launched its largest incursion yet, capturing the village of [Al-Adnaniyah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Adnaniyah) in the countryside of Quneitra Governorate.[^cite_note-319]

On 25 March, several Syrian gunmen attacked Israeli soldiers in [Kuwaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuwaya). No Israeli troops were harmed, and five people were killed by retaliatory fire and a drone strike. The Israelis have also prevented Syrians from accessing their farmland and have fired towards and threatened Syrian civilians at the Al-Mantra Dam in Quneitra.[^cite_note-m017-326]

It also evacuated 15 Syrian Druze wounded in the clashes to hospitals in Israel throughout the week.[^cite_note-328]

On 9 May, seven [BBC Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_Arabic) journalists, including [Feras Kilani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feras_Kilani), were detained at gunpoint by Israeli soldiers while they were filming in the area of an unguarded checkpoint, around 200 kilometres (120 mi) from [Quneitra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quneitra). On 3 June, a barrage of [Grad rockets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BM-21_Grad) was fired at Israel from [Tasil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tasil), setting off sirens in northern Israel and the Golan Heights. The rockets struck open areas near [Ramat Magshimim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramat_Magshimim), and the IDF responded with artillery fire.[^cite_note-329] A second barrage was fired minutes later, but it is unclear if it came from Syria.[^cite_note-330] A group called the "[Martyr Muhammad al-Deif Brigades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martyr_Muhammad_al-Deif_Brigades)" claimed responsibility for the attack.[^cite_note-331]

### Air operations

#### 2024

On 8 December 2024, the [Israeli Air Force](177619.md) conducted targeted operations against weapons storage facilities, which Israel considered strategic threats, across southern Syria to prevent them from falling into the hands of opposition forces.[^cite_note-360][^cite_note-timesofisrael20241209-277] Israeli officials claimed that the targets included small stockpiles of [chemical weapons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syria_chemical_weapons_program), mainly [mustard gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mustard_gas) and [VX gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VX_\(nerve_agent\)), radar-equipped batteries, vehicles of Russian-made air defense missiles, and stockpiles of [Scud missiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scud_missile).[^cite_note-nytimes20241208-286] The [White Helmets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Helmets_\(Syrian_civil_war\)) reported that: "there was no evidence of unusual toxic fumes during the extinguishing of the fire, and no cases of suffocation were observed among civilians"[^cite_note-f934-361] Israel also reportedly launched airstrikes on Syrian intelligence and customs headquarters, with explosions reported at their locations in Damascus.[^cite_note-reuters20241208-285][^cite_note-362] Later, Israel also heavily shelled [Mezzeh Air Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mezzeh_Air_Base).[^cite_note-363]



A Syrian warplane destroyed by Israeli airstrikes on [Mezzeh Air Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mezzeh_Air_Base)

In the early hours of 9 December 2024, Israel conducted several airstrikes across the [Daraa](5317030.md) and [Suwayda Governorates](3096933.md) in southern Syria. Six airstrikes were reported at an airbase north of [Suwayda](2590570.md), while multiple others targeted ammo depots in Nawa and the Daraa countryside.[^cite_note-364] By evening, the Israeli Air Force and Navy had struck naval assets in the [Port of Latakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Latakia),[^cite_note-365][^cite_note-Assets-251] and an alleged chemical weapons production center in [Barzeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barzeh,_Syria),[^cite_note-366] and [Qamishli Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qamishli_Airport) in northern Syria.[^cite_note-SkyNews2024-6] These roughly 200[^cite_note-thestandard20241210-290] airstrikes, including strikes on Damascus, Daraa, Latakia, and Hama,[^cite_note-367] destroyed dozens of fighter jets and helicopters in the first phase and the entire [Syrian naval fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_Navy) in the second.[^cite_note-Fleet-368] An Israeli senior official said airstrikes would continue on for a couple of days.[^cite_note-369][^cite_note-370]

In the morning of 10 December, photographs revealed sunken [Osa-class missile boats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osa-class_missile_boat) in the Port of Latakia after overnight Israeli strikes.[^cite_note-Fleet-368] The IDF announced that its air force and navy conducted over 480 strikes in Syria in the span of 48 hours, 350 of which targeted airfields, anti-aircraft batteries, missiles, drones, fighter jets, tanks, and weapon production sites,[^cite_note-cnn20241210-371] destroying between 70% and 80% of Syria's strategic weapons. It added that 15 naval vessels were destroyed in strikes on [Minet el-Beida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minet_el-Beida) and Latakia.[^cite_note-Fabian-14][^cite_note-cnn20241210-371] Overnight on 16 December, Israel struck radar and air defense systems in Tartous and Damascus, with the strikes on Tartous using notably heavy munitions. In the eight days since the fall of the Assad government, Israel struck Syria around 600 times. Al Jazeera correspondent Resul Serdar said that "Israel is pursuing a strategy of diminishing this country's air defence capability and also its air forces."[^cite_note-372][^cite_note-Sterile-291] A former rebel commander claimed that they will "need decades to rebuild a national Syrian army".[^cite_note-Decades-15] A Turkish newspaper report alleged that Assad gave information about Syrian military sites to Israel in exchange for safe passage out of the country.[^cite_note-373]

On 18 December, Netanyahu promised that he would not strike the area near the prison close to Damascus where American journalist [Austin Tice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Tice) might be held. His mother had sent him a letter two days earlier, urging him to halt Israel's airstrikes in Syria to enable the search for him to continue.[^cite_note-374]

On 29 December, 11 people, mostly civilians, were killed in what is believed to be an Israeli airstrike on a former Syrian Army weapons depot in [Adra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adra,_Syria), near Damascus.[^cite_note-AdraStrike1-86][^cite_note-AdraStrike2-375][^cite_note-376]

## Expansion of occupation

Before the invasion, the [Purple Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purple_Line_\(ceasefire_line\)) formed the mutually recognized and UN approved ceasefire line between the Israeli-occupied and Syrian controlled parts of the Golan.[^cite_note-422] The [Quneitra Crossing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quneitra_Crossing) was the official crossing point, used by UN personnel and Druze residents of the Golan.[^cite_note-423]<sup>: 2 </sup>

Following the invasion and expanded Israeli occupation, a new line demarcating Israeli positions formed to the north. One checkpoint is north-west of [Hadar, Syria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadar,_Syria),[^cite_note-424][^cite_note-425][^cite_note-al-akhbar-20250430-426] while others are at the entrances to other villages.[^cite_note-427] There are also reports of checkpoints between [Beqaasem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beqaasem), [Rima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rima,_Qatana), and [Arnah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnah)[^cite_note-428] and overlooking the [Jabal Barbar](https://ceb.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jabal%20Barbar) mountain on the heights of Mount Hermon.[^cite_note-al-akhbar-20250430-426] Beyond checkpoints, the IDF has been constructing a 6 metres (20 ft) deep trench.[^cite_note-429][^cite_note-430]

## Peace efforts

> "Israel occupied the Golan Heights in order to protect Israel, and now they are imposing conditions in the south of Syria in order to protect the Golan Heights. So after a few years, maybe they will occupy the center of Syria in order to protect the south of Syria. They will reach [Munich](19058.md) on that pathway\[...]"

— [Ahmed al-Sharaa](44007699.md) (November 11, 2025)

[^cite_note-WP-431]

Since late June 2025, Israel and Syria have been engaged in U.S.-brokered "advanced talks" aimed at ceasing hostilities and normalizing relations. The possibility of Syria joining the [Abraham Accords](64883396.md) has been raised, although Israel has insisted on retaining its control over the [Golan Heights](7515865.md).[^cite_note-432] Syrian Foreign Minister [Asaad Al-Shaibani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asaad_al-Shaibani) stated that Syria aspires to return to the terms of the [1974 Disengagement agreement](40148472.md).[^cite_note-433] Among the key obstacles is Syria's demand that Israel cease operating or striking targets within Syrian territory.[^cite_note-434] Israeli media have reported proposals involving Syria possibly regaining a third of the Golan Heights and annexing [Sunni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Sunni_Muslims) regions in [North Lebanon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Lebanon) and the [Beqaa Valley](2162385.md), including the city of [Tripoli](268796.md), in exchange for peace with Israel.[^cite_note-435]

On 20 August 2025, the official [Syrian News Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_News_Agency) issued an official statement that Syrian Foreign Minister [Asaad al-Shaibani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asaad_al-Shaibani) met in Paris with an Israeli delegation "to discuss a number of issues related to strengthening stability in the region and in southern Syria."[^cite_note-436][^cite_note-437] A few days later, Syrian President Ahmed al-Sharaa claimed that a security agreement with Israel was at an advanced stage, and emphasized that he would not hesitate to publicly reach a peace agreement with Israel if it benefited Syria and the region.[^cite_note-\:1-438] During his visit to [Washington, D.C.](108956.md) in November 2025, al-Sharaa stated that they are in direct negotiations with Israel, and reiterated that Israel should withdraw to their pre-December 8 borders before a final agreement can be reached.[^cite_note-WP-431][^cite_note-439]

On 5 January 2026, al-Shaibani and the head of the [General Intelligence Directorate](19204147.md), [Hussein al-Salama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hussein_al-Salama) along with their delegation met with Israeli officials in [Paris](22989.md), France in order to discuss of reaching a security agreement and to defuse the tensions between the two countries.[^cite_note-440] The talks were mediated and led by the [Tom Barrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Barrack), the [United States ambassador to Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ambassadors_of_the_United_States_to_Turkey).[^cite_note-441] According to the *[Associated Press](18935732.md)*, an anonymous Syrian official stated that Syria is seeking "the withdrawal of Israeli forces to the lines prior to 8 December 2024 within the framework of a reciprocal security agreement that prioritizes full Syrian sovereignty and guarantees the prevention of any form of interference in the country’s internal affairs."[^cite_note-442]

  &#x20;Syria's UN ambassador, [Qusay al-Dahhak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qusay_al-Dahhak), called on the UN Security Council to compel Israel to immediately cease its attacks and withdraw to the [Purple Line (ceasefire line)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purple_Line_\(ceasefire_line\)).

  [^cite_note-449]

  &#x20;Al-Sharaa criticized Israel's actions, saying they cannot be justified, but also emphasized that Syria is not in a position to be drawn into a new conflict.

### International

- Arab League: The Arab League condemned "attempts to expand occupation in the Golan Heights or to unilaterally nullify the 1974 disengagement agreement, actions that flagrantly violate international law."

  [^cite_note-l645-450]

- Egypt: Egypt condemned Israel for "exploiting Syria's current instability to expand its occupation and impose a new fait accompli, a clear violation of international law."

  [^cite_note-n780-451]

- France: The [Foreign ministry](250002.md) asked Israel "to withdraw from the zone and to respect Syria's sovereignty and territorial integrity", describing the military deployment as a violation of the 1974 agreement.

  [^cite_note-452]

- Iran: The [Foreign Ministry](1166971.md) spokesman said that "This aggression is a flagrant violation of the [United Nations Charter](88805.md)," and "We demand an immediate response from the UN Security Council to stop the aggression and hold the occupation regime accountable."

  [^cite_note-m221-453]

  [^cite_note-o689-454]

- Iraq: The [Foreign ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_foreign_ministry) issued a statement condemning the Israeli attacks on Syria, stating that "this action constitutes a blatant violation of international law and relevant international legitimacy resolutions" and called on the [United Nations Security Council](31956.md) to "assume its responsibilities in condemning this blatant aggression and to take necessary measures to put an end to these violations that exploit the current situation in the Syrian Arab Republic."

  [^cite_note-455]

- Jordan: Deputy Prime Minister and [Foreign Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Jordan\)) [Ayman Safadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayman_Safadi) condemned the move, stating that "it violates [international law](8195726.md)".

  [^cite_note-456]

  &#x20;On 26 February 2025, King [Abdullah II of Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdullah_II_of_Jordan) condemned Israeli attacks on Syria.

  [^cite_note-457]

- Pakistan: The spokesperson for the [Foreign office](13048437.md) asserted that the move was a serious violation of international law and a dangerous development in an already volatile region. and also expressed full support for Syria's sovereignty and territorial integrity and called for immediate international action to end Israel's violations.

  [^cite_note-458]

  [^cite_note-459]

  [^cite_note-o278-460]

- Saudi Arabia: The [Foreign ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Arabian_foreign_ministry) denounced Israel's activities, stating that it will "ruin Syria's chance of restoring its security."

  [^cite_note-461]

- Turkey: President [Recep Tayyip Erdoğan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recep_Tayyip_Erdo%C4%9Fan) said that "I say it clearly that the path the Israeli government insists on and obstinately maintains is, in fact, not a true path.   [^cite_note-462]

Turkey reiterated its support for Syria's sovereignty, political unity and territorial integrity.

## Analysis

In an interview to [Al Jazeera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Jazeera_English), Robert Geist Pinfold – lecturer in International Peace and Security at [Durham University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durham_University) – opined, "The Golan Heights is supposed to be a buffer zone to protect the rest of Israel. So now what Israel is doing is basically arguing that it needs a buffer zone, to protect its buffer zone, that's protecting the rest of Israel, and you have to wonder where does all this end."[^cite_note-471] Residents of the region expressed similar comments, saying that they're not sure how long Israel will stay, especially if they plan to keep the borders quiet "by force."[^cite_note-472] Others agreed that the future is uncertain, given that the situation has "changed completely."[^cite_note-cnn20241208a-253] Former Israeli prime minister [Ehud Olmert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ehud_Olmert) disagreed, saying that Israel has "enough problems to deal with", and questioned the idea of expanding the buffer zone further.[^cite_note-473]

According to *[Axios](52436164.md)*, the [Trump administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2nd_Trump_Administration) believes that Netanyahu bombed Syria in July 2025 because of domestic pressure from the [Israeli Druze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Druze) minority and other political considerations.[^cite_note-474]
