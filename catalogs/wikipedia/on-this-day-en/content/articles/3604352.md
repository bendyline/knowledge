**Dhale** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الضالع, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *aḍ-Ḍāliʿ*) is one of the [Yemeni](350939.md) [governorates](690277.md) that was created after the [unification of Yemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemeni_unification). The population of the province accounts for 2% of the total population of the republic.[^cite_note-CSO-3] The governorate is divided into 9 districts. The city of [Dhale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhale) is the administrative centre of the province.&#x20;

Dhale is one of the governorates that is known for [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harir_Mountain), and most of the population works in that industry. The most important agricultural crop is [coffee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffee_production). The province also contains mineral resources, most importantly [talc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talc), which is used in the manufacture of paper, paint, cosmetics and pesticides. This governorate is also home to the Damt hot-springs (*Ḥamam-Damt*), a popular tourist attraction. One of the ancient cities in the region is Juban, a city famous for its castle and the historic Mansuria school, built by the [Tahrids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tahirids_\(Yemen\)). The climate in Dhale governorate is mostly temperate throughout the year.[^cite_note-4]

## History

Before the unification of Yemen in 1990, Dhale governorate consisted of nine districts, five in [South Yemen](718749.md) and four in [North Yemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemen_Arab_Republic). The South Yemeni districts, [Al Hussein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Hussein_district), [Al Azariq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Azariq_district), [Ash Shu'ayb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ash_Shu'ayb_district), [Jahaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jahaf_district), and [Ad Dhale'e](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ad_Dhale'e_district), were all in the country's [Lahij Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahij_Governorate). The North Yemeni districts were [Juban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juban_district), part of [Al Bayda Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Bayda_Governorate), [Al Husha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Husha_district), part of [Taiz Governorate](3604497.md), and [Damt district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damt_district) and [Qa'atabah district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qa'atabah_district), both part of [Ibb Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibb_Governorate). 70% of the area of today's Dhale Governorate was in North Yemen in 1989.

## Geography

Dhale Governorate is 245 kilometers away from the Yemeni capital [Sana'a](18691665.md).

- [Al Bayda Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Bayda_Governorate) (north)

- [Ibb Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibb_Governorate) (west)

- [Lahij Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahij_Governorate) (south)

- [Taiz Governorate](3604497.md) (southwest)
