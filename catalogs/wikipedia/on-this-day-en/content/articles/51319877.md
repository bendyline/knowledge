# List of extreme temperatures in Denmark

&#x20;[Denmark](76972.md) is a [Scandinavian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scandinavia) country in [Europe](9239.md) located on the [56th parallel north](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/56th_parallel_north). Denmark is located at a zone where air masses from a diverse range of regions congregate, giving it its temperate climate. Denmark's average temperature is 7.7 °C.

In the 21st century, Denmark experienced its mildest winter, warmest spring, warmest summer and mildest autumn on record:

- Mildest winter: 2020

- Warmest spring: 2007

- Warmest summer: 2018

- Mildest autumn: 2006
