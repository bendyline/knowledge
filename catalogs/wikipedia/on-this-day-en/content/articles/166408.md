**Henan**[^cite_note-10] is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_China) in [Central China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_China). Henan is China's third-most populous province and the most populous among inland provinces, with a population of over 99 million as of 2020. The area around Henan was historically known as the Central Plains ([Zhongyuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhongyuan)) and is considered the core region of Chinese cultural history.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]Four of the [historical capitals of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historical_capitals_of_China), [Luoyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luoyang), [Anyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anyang), [Kaifeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaifeng) and [Zhengzhou](288264.md), are in Henan.[^cite_note-13] People from Henan [sometimes underwent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Henan_sentiment) [discrimination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_discrimination_in_China) within China.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15]

Henan has the 5th-largest provincial economy in China, the largest in Central China and among inland provinces, with a nominal GDP of CN¥5.88 trillion (US\$926 billion) in 2021, surpassing that of Turkey (US\$815 billion).[^cite_note-\:0-16][^cite_note-\:2-17] If it were a country, it would be the 18th-largest economy by nominal GDP as of 2021.[^cite_note-\:1-18]

## Name

"Henan" literally translates to "South of the River" in English. In this context, "He" (河) specifically refers to the [Yellow River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_River), and "Nan" (南) means south. However, due to the [changing course of the Yellow River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Changes_in_the_course_of_the_Yellow_River) and administrative redivisions, about one-quarter of Henan Province's territory is now located north of the river.[^cite_note-19]

"Yu"(豫) is the administrative abbreviation for Henan Province. Historically, during the ancient "Nine countries" (九州) system in China, this region was known as Yuzhou (豫州). The character "Yu" (豫) originally meant "comfort".[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21] In archaic China, this region was still home to [elephants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elephant), which is why it was given this name.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23]

## History



[Jiahu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiahu) [Gudi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gudi_\(instrument\)), [Wuyang County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuyang_County)

Regarded as the cradle of ancient Chinese culture, Henan is known for its historical prosperity and periodic downturns.[^cite_note-24] The economic prosperity resulted from its somewhat fertile plains and its location at the heart of the country. Its strategic location also means that it has undergone more wars compared to certain other regions in China. The floods of the [Yellow River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_River) have caused damage from time to time.[^cite_note-25]

### Ancient states and dynasties

Archaeological sites reveal that prehistoric cultures such as the [Yangshao Culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yangshao_Culture) and [Longshan Culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longshan_Culture) were active in what later is northern Henan since the [Neolithic Era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neolithic_Era). The later [Erlitou culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erlitou_culture) has been controversially identified with the [Xia dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xia_dynasty), the first and mostly legendary Chinese dynasty.[^cite_note-26]



[Hou Mu Wu Ding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houmuwu_ding), late [Shang Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shang_dynasty)

The [Shang dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shang_dynasty) (c. 1600 – c. 1046 BC) overthrew the Xia, with their capitals centered in Henan. The discovery of [oracle bone scripts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oracle_bone_script) at the [Yinxu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yinxu) site in [Anyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anyang) provided invaluable insight into their writing, rituals, and social structure.[^cite_note-27]

In the 11th century BC, the [Zhou people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhou_dynasty) arrived from the west and overthrew the [Shang dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shang_dynasty) in the [Battle of Muye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Muye).[^cite_note-28] During the [Western Zhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Zhou) period (c. 1046 – 771 BC), the capital and political and economical center was moved away from Henan for the first time. The [Spring and Autumn period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spring_and_Autumn_period), a period of warfare and rivalry, began in 721 BC. What later is Henan and all of China was several kingdoms, which are at war for control of the central plain. (逐鹿中原) While regarded formally as the ruler of China, the control that Zhou king in [Luoyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luoyang) exerted over the feudal kingdoms had virtually disappeared. [Laozi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laozi), the founder of [Taoism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taoism), was born in northern [Chu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_Chu), part of what later is Henan.[^cite_note-29]

### Imperial Era



An [Eastern Han](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Han) (25–220 AD) [Chinese tomb mural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_painting) showing scenes of a banquet, dance and music, acrobatics, and wrestling, from the [Dahuting Han tombs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dahuting_Han_tombs), on the southern bank of the [Suihe River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suihe_River?action=edit\&redlink=1) in [Xinmi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xinmi)



[Kaifeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaifeng) city gate in *[Along the River During the Qingming Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Along_the_River_During_the_Qingming_Festival)*

[Qin Shi Huang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qin_Shi_Huang) conquered six kingdoms and unified [China proper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_proper) in 221 BC, and the [Han Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Han_Dynasty) (206 BC–220 AD) made [Henan Commandery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henan_Commandery).The empire made Luoyang its political and cultural heart.

Following the Han's fall, Henan entered a period of prolonged turmoil during the [Three Kingdoms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Kingdoms), Jin, and the [Northern and Southern Dynasties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_and_Southern_Dynasties) (220–589 AD). The region changed hands repeatedly among rival regimes. Despite the destruction, Henan continued to serve as a vital agricultural base and a melting pot of diverse ethnic groups and cultures in northern China.

The [Sui dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sui_dynasty) reunified China in 589. [Emperor Yang of Sui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emperor_Yang_of_Sui) had [the Grand Canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Canal_\(China\)) extended to [Luoyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luoyang), but the massive expenditure of manpower and resources exhausted the populace, which ultimately led to the fall of the Sui empire.[^cite_note-30]

The [Tang dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tang_dynasty) lasted for three centuries before it eventually succumbed to internal strife. In the [Period of Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Period_of_Five_Dynasties_and_Ten_Kingdoms) (907–960) that followed, [Kaifeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaifeng) in eastern Henan became the capital of four dynasties. The [Song dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Song_dynasty) that reunified China in 982 also had its capital at Kaifeng. Under Song rule, Kaifeng overtook Luoyang and Chang'an as the largest city in China and in the world.[^cite_note-31]

### Conquests by Northern Peoples

In 1127, the [Jurchens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurchen_people), a people from the northeast, captured [Kaifeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaifeng) and [took the Song emperor prisoner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jingkang_incident).[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34] In 1194, the Yellow River [changed its course](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Changes_in_the_course_of_the_Yellow_River), flowing southward into the [Huai River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huai_River) through what is now northern Jiangsu, displacing millions of people.[^cite_note-35] In the early 13th century, [Mongols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongols) destroyed the [Jin dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jin_dynasty_\(1115%E2%80%931234\)) and conquered this region, establishing the Henan Jiangbei Branch Secretariat (河南江北行省).[^cite_note-36]

Due to heavy casualties and mass migration caused by warfare and flooding, the Ming court under the [Hongwu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hongwu_Emperor) reign organized large-scale, compulsory resettlement from [Shanxi](81262.md) to Henan.[^cite_note-37] In 1368, the prefectures of [Weihui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weihui) (衛輝), [Zhangde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhangdefu) (彰德), and [Huaiqing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qinyang) (懷慶)—areas north of the Yellow River—were placed under Henan's government.[^cite_note-38] In 1641, the peasant rebel leader [Li Zicheng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Zicheng) captured Luoyang. The following year, Li's forces besieged Kaifeng three times. During the third siege, troops of Ming court breached the Yellow River dikes at Zhujiazhai and Majiakou north of the city in an attempt to flood the rebels. The waters inundated Kaifeng, submerging the entire city under several zhang of water. Of the city's 378,000 residents, only 30,000 survived.[^cite_note-39]

The wars of the late Ming and early [Qing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qing_dynasty) (circa 1630–1662) once again devastated Henan. During the [Kangxi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kangxi_Emperor) reign, the province's agricultural economy recovered, and it gradually regained its status as a major population center and grain-producing region.

On September 30, 1887, a major Yellow River dike breach occurred at Xiayun Shibao in Zhengzhou (present-day Shiqiao Village, [Huayuankou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huayuankou,_Henan) Town, Huiji District).[^cite_note-40] The disaster claimed over two million lives (with estimates ranging from 930,000 to as high as 7 million). Xia Mingfang, a scholar of Qing history, called it "the most severe flood disaster in terms of population loss in modern Chinese history,"[^cite_note-清史研究-41] while Peter Hough, professor of international relations at Middlesex University, described it as "one of the deadliest natural disasters in human history."[^cite_note-Hough2014-42]

### Modern time

The Qing dynasty was overthrown by the 1911 Revolution and then the Republic of China was established in 1912,[^cite_note-43] during which a man from Henan, [Yuan Shikai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuan_Shikai), played a role and thus became the first president of Republic of China.[^cite_note-44]

In 1938, during the [Second Sino-Japanese War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Sino-Japanese_War), when the [Imperial Japanese Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Japanese_Army) captured Kaifeng, the government led by [Chiang Kai-shek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiang_Kai-shek) bombed the [Huayuankou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huayuankou,_Henan) dam in Zhengzhou in order to prevent Japanese forces from advancing further.[^cite_note-45] This caused flooding in Henan, [Anhui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anhui), and [Jiangsu](166412.md) resulting in hundreds of thousands of deaths. In 1942 Henan was hit by the [Chinese famine of 1942-43](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_famine_of_1942-43) resulting from a mix of drought, locusts and destruction caused by the war.[^cite_note-46]

In 1954, the government of the People's Republic of China moved the capital of Henan from Kaifeng to [Zhengzhou](288264.md),[^cite_note-47] as a result of its railway transportation importance. The PRC had earlier established a [Pingyuan Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pingyuan_Province) consisting of what later is northern Henan and western [Shandong](105032.md) with [Xinxiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xinxiang) as its capital. This province was abolished in 1952.[^cite_note-48]

In 1958, Yashan in [Suiping County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suiping_County), Henan, became the first [people's commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_commune) of China, heralding the beginning of the "[Great Leap Forward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Leap_Forward)".[^cite_note-49] In the subsequent famines of the 1960s, which are attributed to the Great Leap Forward, millions of people died in Henan.[^cite_note-50] Suffering under famine and economic chaos caused by the Great Leap, locals in Henan offered resistance mostly through banditry.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTERummel1991247–248-51] In 1959, [a peasant uprising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spirit_Soldier_rebellion_\(1959\)) erupted and was defeated after twenty days of fighting.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTESmith2015346-52]

A destructive flooding of the [Huai River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huai_River) in the summer of 1950 prompted construction of dams on its tributaries in central and southern Henan. Some of the dams were not able to withstand the levels of rainfall caused by [Typhoon Nina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon_Nina_\(1975\)) in August 1975. Sixty-two dams, the largest of which was the [Banqiao Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banqiao_Dam) in [Biyang County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biyang_County) collapsed; flooding, spread over counties throughout [Zhumadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhumadian) Prefecture and further downstream, killed at least 26,000 people.[^cite_note-yi-53][^cite_note-After_30_years,_secrets,_lessons_of_China's_worst_dams_burst_accident_surface-54] Unofficial human life loss estimates, including deaths from the ensuing epidemics and famine, range as high as 85,600,[^cite_note-yi-53] 171,000[^cite_note-osnos-55] or 230 000.[^cite_note-yi-53]

In July 2021, rainfall caused [flooding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Henan_floods), killing 302 and damaging amounting to 82 billion yuan.[^cite_note-56]

## Geography



[Yuntai Mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuntai_Mountain_\(Henan\)), a part of [Taihang Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taihang_Mountains)

Henan's landscape include floodplains in the east and mountains in the west. Most of the province forms part the [North China Plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_China_Plain), an area known as the "breadbasket of China". The [Taihang Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taihang_Mountains) intrude partially into Henan's northwestern borders from [Shanxi](81262.md), forming the south-eastern edge of [Loess Plateau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loess_Plateau). To the west the [Xionger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xionger_Mountains?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Funiu Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funiu_Mountains) form a network of mountain ranges and plateaus, supporting one of the remaining [temperate deciduous forests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperate_deciduous_forest) which once covered all of Henan. The [Mount Song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Song) and its [Shaolin Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaolin_Temple) is located in the far east of the region, near the capital city Zhengzhou. To the far south, the [Dabie Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dabie_Mountains) divides [Hubei](83319.md) from Henan. The [Nanyang Basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanyang_Basin), separated from North China Plain by these mountains, is another agricultural and population center, with culture and history distinct from the rest of Henan and closer to that of Hubei's. Unlike the rest of northern China, [desertification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desertification) is not a problem in Henan, while sandstorms are in cities near the Yellow River due to sand present in the river. At 2413.8 meters above sea level, the highest point in Henan province is Laoyachanao.[^cite_note-57]



Map of the Yellow River water control system in Henan Province, 1876 (Guangxu 2nd year), watercolor and ink manuscript. Oriented with north to the bottom. [Library of Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Library_of_Congress).

The Yellow River passes through central Henan. It enters from the northwest, via the [Sanmenxia Reservoir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanmenxia_Reservoir). After it passes Luoyang, the mountains gave way to plains. Sediments are formed due to the silt it picks up from the Loess Plateau, raising the river bed and causing floods which shaped the habitat of the region. Later, construction of dams and [levees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levee), and the depletion of water resources have ended the floods. The [Huai River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huai_River) in southern Henan is another river, and has been recognized as part of the boundary dividing [northern and southern Chinese climate and culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_and_southern_China).[^cite_note-58]

With a population of approximately 98.2 million, Henan is the third most populous Chinese province after Guangdong and Shandong. It is the fifth most populous sub-national division in the world. If it were a country by itself, it would be the twelfth most populous in the world, behind Mexico and ahead of the Philippines. The [hukou system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hukou_system) shows Henan as the most populous province in China with over 103 million people, as it counts the migrant Henanese laborers as residents of Henan, instead of the province they reside in. 85.14 million are considered permanent residents of their registered households.[^cite_note-\:5-78]

Henan exhibits demographic statistics indicative of a late stage in the demographic transition model. It has a birth rate of 7.06, decreasing from 9.24 in 2020 and 12.70 in 2015, while an aging population possibly explains an uptick in the death rate to 8.00 from 7.05 in 2015, and its overall population is experiencing a decreasing natural growth rate, ticking into the negatives at -.94% in 2023.[^cite_note-\:5-78] This, in combination with consistent out-migration, can explain Henan’s slower population growth. The life expectancy is 77.6 years, matching nationwide numbers. Most households in Henan have between 2-4 people. 64.47% of the population can be considered working age–between 15 and 64, with a gross dependency ratio of 54.4%.[^cite_note-\:5-78] This is similar to national levels, as China has a glut of 20s-to-middle-aged people exactly within working age.[^cite_note-79]

Henan hosts an urban population of 57.01 million, a 58% urbanization rate, below that of China’s national average of 67%. It is a jump from 2014 when the urban population was 43.45 million.[^cite_note-\:5-78] This urbanization process can be mostly attributed to internal migration from rural areas within Henan as overall population grew by less than 2 million in that same time frame. Among Chinese provinces, Henan's per-capita GDP ranks 25 out of 31.[^cite_note-80] Per-capita disposable income averages out close to the national statistic of 31.5k yuan, while regional discrimination persists against Henanese for being poor and association with criminality.[^cite_note-81][^cite_note-\:5-78]

### Population composition

98.8% of the population is [Han](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Han_Chinese). Populations of [Mongols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongols) and [Manchus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchus) exist in scattered rural communities, and urban centers. Along with [Jiangxi](173817.md), Henan has one of the most unbalanced gender ratios in China. As a result of the Chinese government's [one-child policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-child_policy) (some parents do not want the only child to be female and abort the fetus), the gender ratio was 118.46 males for 100 females in 2000. Subsequently, aborting fetuses due to their female sex was banned in Henan and fines are issued for those who violate the law. The ratio decreased to 117.8 in 2010 and down to 108.4 in 2020.[^cite_note-\:5-78] Daughter-only families receive an annual allowance from the government.[^cite_note-82] Based on a 2009 British Medical Journal study, the ratio is over 140 boys for every 100 girls in the 1–4 age group;[^cite_note-83] this might be an exaggeration, as some families with more than one child do not register their daughters to the hukou in order to escape fines. The National Bureau of Statistics of China reports that to have decreased to 100.57 in 2023, presenting a conflicting narrative from outside sources.[^cite_note-\:5-78]

### Employment

The government is an employer in Henan. Its largest sectors include manufacturing, public bureaucracy, education, and construction.[^cite_note-\:5-78] This generally mirrors larger trends across China such as the CCP’s Made in China 2025 plan to keep Chinese manufacturing central to the global supply chain.[^cite_note-84] Public administration is a sector umbrellaing public servant jobs such as social security and public management. It has employed fewer and fewer numbers year-over-year since 2020. In the public sector, reported numbers show urban employment is dominated by retail. This is reflected in gross regional product numbers by industry, with Henan’s largest value-added contributors from secondary (manufacturing) and tertiary (services) industry.[^cite_note-\:5-78]

### Religion

Religion in Henan (2012)

[^cite_note-CFPS2012-85]

1. Non religious and [traditional faiths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_folk_religion) (86.1%)

2. [Buddhism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism_in_China) (6.40%)

3. [Protestantism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestantism_in_China) (5.60%)

4. [Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholicism_in_China) (0.50%)

5. [Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_China) (1.30%)

6. Others (0.20%)

According to a 2012 survey, around 13% of the population of Henan belongs to organised religions, the largest groups being [Buddhists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism_in_China) with 6.4%, followed by [Protestants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestantism_in_China) with 5.6%, [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_China) with 1.3% and [Catholics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholicism_in_China) with 0.5%.[^cite_note-CFPS2012-85]

Henan has the largest Christian population by numbers and percentage of any province of China,[^cite_note-CFPS2012-85] 6.1% of the province's population as of 2012, corresponding to approximately 7 million Christians. A 2009 survey reported the share of Christians to be 9.33%.[^cite_note-Wang2015-86] In 2019, Communist officials demolished the [True Jesus Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_Jesus_Church_\(Caidu\)) near Zhumadian.[^cite_note-87] In 2020, Communist officials demolished the [Sunzhuang Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunzhuang_Church).[^cite_note-88]

The reports didn't give figures for other types of religion; 86% of the population may be either irreligious or involved in [worship of nature deities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_folk_religion), [Confucianism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confucianism), [Taoism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taoism) and [folk religious sects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_salvationist_religions) (for example, a sect that is endogenous to Henan is the [Tianxian miaodao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tianxian_miaodao)). According to a 2007 survey, approximately 8% of the Henanese believe in and are involved in [ancestor veneration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_ancestral_religion), the traditional Chinese religion of the lineages organised into [lineage churches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lineage_church) and [ancestral shrines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancestral_shrine).[^cite_note-Wang2015-86]

## Economy

Henan is the 5th-largest provincial economy of China, the second largest in [South Central China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Central_China) after Guangdong, the largest in [Central China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_China) and the largest among inland provinces, with a nominal GDP of 5.88 trillion RMB (US\$926 billion) as of 2021, ahead of the GDP of Turkey of 815 billion.[^cite_note-\:0-16][^cite_note-\:2-17] If it was a country, it would be the 18th-largest economy by [nominal GDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nominal_GDP) and the 17th most populous as of 2021.[^cite_note-\:1-18]

The [2021 Henan floods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Henan_floods) inflicted an estimated US\$12.7 billion of economic damage in Henan.[^cite_note-89]

### Agriculture



Farmland in [Xiping County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiping_County), [Zhumadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhumadian)

Henan is one of China's major agricultural provinces, with grain production as its primary characteristic. In 2025, the province's total grain output reached 135.097 billion [jin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jin_\(mass\)) (approximately 67.55 million metric [tons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ton)), ranking second in the country and maintaining above 130 billion jin for nine consecutive years.[^cite_note-90] The province produces roughly one-tenth of the nation's grain and over one-quarter of its wheat.[^cite_note-91]

Beyond grain production, Henan has a substantial agricultural processing sector. The province is home to 6,103 industrial-scale agricultural processing enterprises, with the food industry representing a trillion-yuan sector.[^cite_note-92] Products such as ham sausages, instant noodles, and quick-frozen tangyuan account for a significant share of the domestic market.[^cite_note-93] In 2025, Henan's food exports reached 15.05 billion yuan, an increase of 12.4 percent year-on-year, with exports shipped to over 160 countries and regions.[^cite_note-94]



[Shuanghui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shuanghui) wheat flour sausage

In the livestock sector, Henan produced 7.534 million tons of pork, beef, mutton, and poultry meat in 2025, with 64.74 million pigs slaughtered.[^cite_note-95] Vegetable and edible fungus production reached 85.03 million tons, with edible fungus output ranking first in the country for multiple consecutive years.[^cite_note-96]

### Industry



[Yutong Bus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yutong_Bus)



[Zhengzhou Futures exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_Commodity_Exchange)



[Pop Mart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_Mart) and [Labubu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labubu)

Henan is one of China's major industrial provinces and one of the few in the country that possesses all 41 industrial categories.[^cite_note-97] In 2025, the province's large-scale industrial value-added output grew by 8.4 percent year on year, exceeding the national average by 2.5 percentage points.[^cite_note-98] The five leading sectors—electronic information, equipment manufacturing, automobiles and parts, food processing, and new materials—made substantial contributions, with equipment manufacturing alone contributing 44.1 percent of the total industrial growth.[^cite_note-99]

Mining-related industries are a part of Henan's economy.[^cite_note-\:12-100]<sup>: 23 </sup> Henan has the second largest [molybdenum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molybdenum) reserves in the world. Coal, aluminum, [alkaline metals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alkaline_metals) and [tungsten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tungsten) are present in larger amounts in western Henan. Henan houses [limestone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limestone) reserves estimated over 24 billion tons.[^cite_note-101] Henan holds a dominant position in the global [superhard materials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superhard_material) industry, especially [Zhecheng County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhecheng_County). It produces approximately 80 percent of China's [synthetic diamonds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synthetic_diamond), accounting for the vast majority of the country's output and a significant share of the global market.[^cite_note-102][^cite_note-103]

Henan has planned its economy around the provincial capital of Zhengzhou, and it is hoped that the province may become an important transportation and manufacturing hub in the years to come.[^cite_note-thechinaperspective.com-104]

[Xuchang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xuchang), meanwhile, has become the world's largest [wig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wig) manufacturing and distribution hub. It is home to over 4,000 wig-related enterprises and employs roughly 300,000 people, accounting for more than 60 percent of the global wig market.[^cite_note-105][^cite_note-106] In 2025, Xuchang's wig industry recorded an import-export value of 21.91 billion yuan, with exports reaching 17.2 billion yuan.[^cite_note-107]

New industries and high-tech manufacturing have gained considerable momentum, with value-added output rising by 13.0 percent and 16.6 percent respectively.[^cite_note-108] Henan has become a significant player in new energy vehicles, hosting major manufacturers such as [Yutong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yutong) and [BYD Zhengzhou](1461760.md). Annual vehicle production climbed from 66,000 units in 2021 to 681,000 in 2024, and exceeded 750,000 in 2025.[^cite_note-109] In the first quarter of 2026, the province produced 195,200 new energy vehicles, ranking seventh nationwide.[^cite_note-110] The province also ranks second nationwide in mobile phone production, and its domestic server brand, [xFusion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XFusion?action=edit\&redlink=1), holds the largest market share in China for locally manufactured servers.[^cite_note-111] In 2025, the total operating revenue of Henan's large-scale industrial enterprises exceeded five trillion yuan.[^cite_note-112]

### Finance

[Zhengzhou Commodity Exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhou_Commodity_Exchange) (郑州期货交易所), founded in October 1990, was the first [Futures exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Futures_exchange) approved by the State Council.[^cite_note-113] In 2025, ZCE recorded a cumulative trading volume of 3.14 billion contracts and a trading value of 89.0 trillion yuan.[^cite_note-114] By the end of 2023, the total assets of financial industry corporate entities in the province reached 14.21 trillion yuan.[^cite_note-115]

### Commerce



[Mixue Bingcheng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixue_Bingcheng) headquarter

With its huge hinterland population and consumer base, Zhengzhou has become an important commercial hub in central China. [Pang Donglai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangdonglai), headquartered in Xuchang, has drawn widespread attention for its distinctive business model. The group currently carries no loans and holds 4.1 billion yuan in cash reserves, and has implemented a profit-sharing mechanism covering all employees.[^cite_note-116][^cite_note-117]

[Mixue Bingcheng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixue_Bingcheng), from the cold beverage sector, and [Pop Mart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_Mart), from the [toy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toy) industry, are Henan-born brands that have achieved global reach in recent years.

## Transportation

Henan serves as a central transportation hub in China. By the end of 2025, the province had built an integrated transport network of over 292,000 kilometers. The [Jingguang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jingguang_Railway) and [Longhai Railways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longhai_Railway) run through the province and intersect at [Zhengzhou](288264.md). Other railway hubs such as [Shangqiu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shangqiu), [Xinxiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xinxiang), and [Luohe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luohe) have also become centers of trade and manufacturing as a result.[^cite_note-ChinaDailyHenan2-120]

### Railway



[Zhengzhoudong railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengzhoudong_railway_station)

The province operates a "米"-shaped (eight-direction) [high-speed rail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-speed_rail) network centered on Zhengzhou. By 2025, Henan's total railway operations reached 6,810 kilometers, including 2,263 kilometers of high-speed rail, covering all prefecture-level cities.

A December 2022 Nanjing University report lists Henan's annual railway passenger trips for 2015–2021 in units of 10,000; the totals were 12,200 in 2015, 17,709 in 2019, 11,415 in 2020 and 13,126 in 2021.[^cite_note-NJU-rail-report-121]

### Mythology



Statue *[Kuafu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuafu) Chasing the Sun* in [Jiaozuo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiaozuo)

Henan is the heartland of [Chinese mythology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_mythology). The goddess [Nüwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N%C3%BCwa) (女娲), famous for repairing the heavens and creating humanity from clay, is celebrated in [Jiyuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiyuan), which was named a "Home of Chinese Nüwa Mythology" in 2006.[^cite_note-123] [Fuxi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuxi) (伏羲), the first of the Three Sovereigns, is said to have established his capital in [Chen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chen_\(state\)) (present-day Huaiyang) and created the Eight Trigrams.[^cite_note-124] [Suiren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suiren) (燧人), the legendary firemaker by drilling wood, is said to have lived in the area of Shangqiu, and is often listed as the first of the "[Three Augusts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Sovereigns_and_Five_Emperors)".[^cite_note-125][^cite_note-126] [Shennong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shennong) (神农), also known as the [Yan Emperor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yan_Emperor), is credited with teaching agriculture and herbal medicine and is associated with the region of [Huaiyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huaiyang_Kingdom).[^cite_note-127][^cite_note-128] According to legend, the [Yellow Emperor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_Emperor) (Huangdi) was born in [Xinzheng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xinzheng) and defeated both the Yan Emperor and [Chi You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiyou) (蚩尤) in major battles that led to the unification of the Central Plains.[^cite_note-129][^cite_note-130] [Yu the Great](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yu_the_Great) (大禹), famed for taming the great flood by dredging rivers rather than building dams, is said to have been active in [Yuzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuzhou,_Henan), which is named for him and is home to many related sites and legends.[^cite_note-131][^cite_note-132] The myth of [Kua Fu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kua_Fu) (夸父), who chased the sun until he died of thirst, is said to have taken place in the mountainous regions of western Henan, such as [Lingbao](14982335.md).[^cite_note-133] The fable of [Yu Gong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yu_Gong) (愚公), the old man who tried to move [Mount Wangwu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Wangwu) (王屋山) and [Taihang Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taihang_Mountains) (太行山), which are located in [Jiyuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiyuan).[^cite_note-134]

### Language

[Zhongyuan Mandarin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhongyuan_Mandarin) spoken across most of the province. However, the province is not linguistically uniform: in the northern-west areas along the Yellow River, dialects belonging to [Jin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jin_language) are spoken.

### Literature



[Du Fu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Du_Fu), "the Sage of Poetry"

Henan literature roots trace back to the *[Classic of Poetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classic_of_Poetry)* and texts of ancient masters. During the Han and Wei dynasties, Luoyang emerged as a literary center, giving rise to the "Jian'an" and "Zhengshi" styles that deeply influenced later literature. In the Tang and Song eras, major figures like [Du Fu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Du_Fu), [Han Yu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Han_Yu), and [Bai Juyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bai_Juyi) brought Central Plains literature to its peak. In modern times, Henan literature has maintained a focus on rural life and social reality, developing a distinctive style characterized by its weighty, unadorned tone and its direct confrontation of human struggles. Today, it continues to produce influential novels, poetry, remaining an important current within contemporary Chinese literature. Henan has also produced [Liu Cixin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liu_Cixin), one of China's most celebrated [science fiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_fiction) writers.
