The **National Athletics Centre** ([Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_language): *Nemzeti Atlétikai Központ*, pronounced [\[ˈnɛmzɛti ˈɒtleːtikɒji ˈkøspont\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Hungarian)) is a track and field stadium in [Budapest](36787.md), [Hungary](13275.md). It has held the [2023 World Athletics Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_World_Athletics_Championships). The stadium was built on the eastern bank of the [Danube River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danube_River) south of central [Budapest](36787.md), with a capacity of 36,000 for the championships, which was reduced to 15,000 for future events.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

An all-purpose stadium with a capacity of 60.000 had been planned for the site as part of [Budapest's cancelled bid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest_bid_for_the_2024_Summer_Olympics) for the [2024 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Summer_Olympics).[^cite_note-7] In 2017, when the Hungarian Athletics Federation applied to host the 2023 World Athletics Championships, a smaller stadium was planned and eventually constructed after Budapest won the bid in 2018. The stadium stood on what was the compound of Scientific Research Institute for Water Management (VITUKI); demolition of the old compound which included a tower block began in 2020, parallel with the construction start of the new stadium.[^cite_note-8]

Construction was completed in 2023, complemented with a public recreation area as well as a network of footpaths and bicycle paths which includes bridges.[^cite_note-9] After the stadium's capacity was reduced by dismantling the upper tier, another running track was installed on its footprint, which was opened for public use.

The centre is accessible by a number of public transport services, namely trams[^cite_note-10], buses, and suburban rail. The Közvágóhíd transport hub serves all three modes and is the nearest public transport access to the stadium, in addition to the stadium's own bus stop.
