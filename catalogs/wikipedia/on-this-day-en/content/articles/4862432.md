# List of airports in Cuba



Map of Cuba

This is a **list of [airports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airport) in [Cuba](5042481.md)**, grouped by type and sorted by location.

Cuba, officially the Republic of Cuba, is an island country in the [Caribbean](18956035.md). It is an [archipelago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archipelago) of islands located in the northern [Caribbean Sea](7822.md) at the confluence with the [Gulf of Mexico](21076367.md) and the [Atlantic Ocean](698.md). The United States lies to the northwest, [the Bahamas](3451.md) to the north, [Haiti](13373.md) to the east, [Jamaica](15660.md) and the [Cayman Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cayman_Islands) to the south, and [Mexico](3966054.md) to the west. The country is subdivided into 15 [provinces](239171.md) and one special municipality ([Isla de la Juventud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isla_de_la_Juventud), the country's second largest island). Cuba's [capital](181337.md) and largest city is [Havana](49719.md).
