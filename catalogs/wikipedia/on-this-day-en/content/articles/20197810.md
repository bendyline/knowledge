# Crime in Armenia



[Armenian police](11223741.md) in the capital [Yerevan](34352.md).

**Crime in Armenia** is multi-dimensional. It includes [murder](20171.md), [tax evasion](2256795.md), [corruption](66220.md), [extortion](162449.md), [money laundering](19390.md), [police brutality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_brutality), [organized crime](22625.md), and clan or gang violence.

In 2017, there were 20,284 criminal cases registered in [Armenia](10918072.md), an increase from 18,764 in 2016.[^cite_note-\:0-1] the number went up again in 2018, reaching 22,551 cases, marking an 11.2% increase from 2017.[^cite_note-2]

In 2018, Armenia had been classified as one of the safest countries in the world for travelers. The [United States Department of State](31975.md) has classified Armenia as a safe country for tourists, giving Armenia the highest safety rating in the [Caucasus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caucasus) region.[^cite_note-3]

## Crime by type

### Murder

Further information: [List of countries by intentional homicide rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_intentional_homicide_rate)

In 2012, Armenia had a murder rate of 1.8 per 100,000 population.[^cite_note-UNODC-4] There were a total of 54 murders in Armenia in 2012.[^cite_note-UNODC-4]

In 2017, there were 49 cases of murder in Armenia (about 1.6 per 100,000 population), down from 66 in 2016.[^cite_note-\:0-1] A total of 409 persons died because of various criminal cases (down from 424 in 2016), including 202 cases of death because of crime leading to road accidents.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

In 2018, with 38 cases, Armenia recorded the lowest murder rate in 38–40 years.[^cite_note-5]

### Organized crime

Organized crime permeates the Armenian economy. In [Yerevan](34352.md), there are organized, criminal clans known as "akhperutyuns" ([Armenian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_language): ախբերություն, or *brotherhoods*). They assert their power through their position and connections. The various factions sometimes battle for rights over their "turf". Members are guided by the underworld laws brought from Russian prisons.[^cite_note-MeanStreets-6]

### Corruption

Main article: [Corruption in Armenia](18292504.md)

In 2017, 634 [corruption](3608404.md) related criminal cases were registered, which led to criminal prosecutions of 376 persons.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

The [United Nations Development Programme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Development_Programme) in Armenia views corruption in Armenia as "a serious challenge to its development".[^cite_note-7]

### Domestic violence

A 2008 study by [Amnesty International](18947898.md) stated that more than a quarter of women in Armenia "have faced physical violence at the hands of husbands or other family members."[^cite_note-AmnestyIntl-Nov-13-2008-8] Since reporting domestic violence is heavily stigmatized in Armenian society, many of these women have no choice but to remain in abusive situations.[^cite_note-AmnestyIntl-Nov-13-2008-8]

In January 2018, Armenia signed the [Council of Europe Convention on Preventing and Combating Violence Against Women and Domestic Violence](31752276.md). Armenia has also signed the [Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convention_on_the_Elimination_of_All_Forms_of_Discrimination_Against_Women).

### Environmental protection

In 2017, there were 885 cases of breaches of legislation on environmental protection, leading to total 3346 million AMD compensation demands.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

In 2018, the [Government of Armenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Armenia) and the [European Union](9317.md) launched a joint action plan to develop and implement policies which will ensure a high level of environmental protection in Armenia.[^cite_note-9]

## By location

In 2017, there were 10219 criminal cases registered in Yerevan, representing about half of all 20284 criminal cases in Armenia.[^cite_note-\:0-1]
