The **Kinahan Organised Crime Group** (KOCG), also known as the **Kinahan Cartel**, is a major Irish [transnational organised crime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transnational_organised_crime) syndicate alleged to be the most powerful in Ireland and one of the largest organised crime groups in the world.[^cite_note-31][^cite_note-seven-32][^cite_note-bbc-33] It is also established in the UK, Spain, and the United Arab Emirates.[^cite_note-34] It was founded by [Christy Kinahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christy_Kinahan) in the 1990s. Estimated reports have credited them with wealth of up to [€](9472.md)1 billion.[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36]

The Kinahan Cartel has [had an ongoing feud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hutch%E2%80%93Kinahan_feud) with the [Hutch Gang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hutch_Organized_Crime_Gang) since 2015, which had resulted in 18 deaths by August 2021.[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39][^cite_note-40]

The organization was led by Christy's oldest son, [Daniel Kinahan](64256824.md), until he was arrested in Dubai on 15 April 2026 after the conclusion of a secret operation between UAE and Irish police.[^cite_note-41]

## Background

The Kinahan Organised Crime Group was founded by [Christy Kinahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christy_Kinahan), a native Dubliner, in the late 1990s and early 2000s during Ireland's "[Celtic Tiger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celtic_Tiger)" period of rapid economic growth.[^cite_note-bbc-33][^cite_note-42] Kinahan's first convictions date back to the late 1970s and involved house breaking, car theft, burglary, handling stolen goods and forgery.[^cite_note-43][^cite_note-44] The group reportedly began as a street gang of inner-city drug dealers in [Dublin](8504.md), but soon grew on a global scale to become the multi-million dollar criminal network that it is today.[^cite_note-45] Christy Kinahan served as the direct leader of the organisation up until the position was passed on to his son [Daniel Kinahan](64256824.md).[^cite_note-46] Irish courts have subsequently concluded that the group is a murderous organisation involved in the [international trafficking of drugs](218449.md) and [firearms](42210.md).[^cite_note-treasury-47][^cite_note-48]

On 12 April 2022, the [United States Department of State](31975.md) announced the offering of rewards of up to US\$5 million under the [Narcotics Rewards Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narcotics_Rewards_Program) for information leading to the arrest and/or conviction of Kinahan family members. The reward is offered jointly with the [Garda Síochána](383207.md), [National Crime Agency](28153284.md), and [Drug Enforcement Administration](146720.md).[^cite_note-49][^cite_note-50][^cite_note-51] The seven key members were named as: Daniel Kinahan; Christopher Kinahan; Christopher Kinahan Jnr; veteran Dublin-born criminal Bernard Clancy; Daniel Kinahan’s “advisor and closest confidant" Sean McGovern; former KOCG enforcer now [Marbella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marbella)-based money launderer "Johnny Cash" Morrissey; and Ian Dixon who was arrested over the 2015 Costa del Sol murder of Gary Hutch but never charged.[^cite_note-JMarrest-52]

Despite the sanctions being imposed on the group, it was reported in August 2022 that Spanish-based associates of the Kinahan cartel were continuing to operate multi-million-euro drug shipments.[^cite_note-53] "Johnny Cash" Morrissey and his wife Nicola were arrested in Marbella, Spain on Monday 13 September 2022.[^cite_note-JMarrest-52]

In February 2023, a raid on a business in the Long Mile Road area of Dublin led to the seizure of equipment, the arrest of eight people and 40 kg of cocaine.[^cite_note-tj-kinahan-gangs-supply-chain-decimated-54][^cite_note-rte-six-questioned-after-raid-55] Over 2,000 containers of [nitric oxide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nitric_oxide) were seized, as well as €78,000 in cash, a hydraulic drugs press, communications devices, mixing agent and a money counter.[^cite_note-tj-kinahan-gangs-supply-chain-decimated-54][^cite_note-rte-six-questioned-after-raid-55] Two of those arrested were later released.[^cite_note-tj-kinahan-gangs-supply-chain-decimated-54][^cite_note-rte-six-questioned-after-raid-55]

Edin Gačanin, who is involved in [organised crime in Bosnia and Herzegovina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organized_crime_in_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina), was sanctioned in 2023 by the US, which cited "close links" to Daniel Kinahan after broken encrypted chats showed how the Kinahan cartel was involved in a €350m coke plot. The US named Daniel Kinahan, his father Christy Kinahan Snr and Christy Kinahan Jnr as the three key players of the Kinahan gang.[^cite_note-56]

The Kinahan cartel had been living openly and running their operations from Dubai, enjoying the immunity that the city offers to organised crime groups. The cartel maintains ties with the authorities, along with financial links with powerful Dubai families.[^cite_note-Times-11-06-2023-57][^cite_note-58] Multiple companies were set by the group in Dubai to offer business services and to trade textiles, clothing and food. An investigation revealed that the businesses were being used to supply drugs, including cocaine, heroin, and others, and to launder dirty money.[^cite_note-59] Despite keeping a low profile, they ran the business as usual in Dubai.[^cite_note-Times-11-06-2023-57] A number of meetings are held by the Kinahan family with their criminal associates in Dubai.[^cite_note-60] In October 2024, Sean Gerard McGovern was arrested by the Interpol notice in Dubai. Fearing McGovern's extradition to Ireland, the cartel laid out a detailed plan to move out from Dubai to Russia.[^cite_note-61][^cite_note-62][^cite_note-63] On 21 October, Ireland signed an extradition treaty with the UAE.[^cite_note-64] On 29 May 2025, McGovern was extradited from the UAE to Ireland and was subsequently arrested and charged with murder.[^cite_note-65]

They laundered money via the [Smart and TGR networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_and_TGR_networks).[^cite_note-rte-money-laundering-network-used-by-kinahan-gang-dismantled-66]

Authorities believe that the Kinahan cartel coordinated with [Hezbollah](13919.md) in a failed attempt to smuggle 2.2 tons of cocaine from Venezuela to Ireland in September 2023. The seizure of the cocaine was the largest in Irish history.[^cite_note-67]
