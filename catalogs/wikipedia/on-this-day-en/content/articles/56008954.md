The **Kazakh Naval Forces** ([Kazakh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakh_language): Қазақстан Әскери-теңіз күштері, *Qazaqstan Äskeri-teñız küşterı*; [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Военно-морские силы Казахстана) is the naval force responsible for coastal defense, naval special warfare, and naval warfare branch of the [Armed Forces of the Republic of Kazakhstan](16650.md). The navy mainly operates on the [Caspian Sea](19653787.md) and is currently based in the coastal city of [Aktau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aktau).

The branch currently has a strength of 3,000 personnel and is mainly equipped with patrol crafts, minesweepers and a research vessel.

## History

Previously, the [Soviet Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Navy)'s [Caspian Flotilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caspian_Flotilla) served in the shores of the [Kazakh SSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakh_SSR). Following the [dissolution of the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissolution_of_the_Soviet_Union), the fleet shrank, leaving the Kazakh contingent to serve as a basis for the newly formed navy. Kazakhstan's Naval Forces were established in April 1993 as a naval base of the [Kazakh Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakh_Army). The base, which was stationed in [Aktau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aktau), initially became active in service on August 17, 1996, in spite of Kazakhstan being one of the largest landlocked countries on earth. In July 1999, the naval base became part of the Maritime units of the Border Guard Service of the National Security Committee of Kazakhstan. It became a separate military branch by presidential decree on 7 May 2003. In February 2010, Kazakh President [Nursultan Nazarbayev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nursultan_Nazarbayev), in his position as supreme commander in chief of the entire military, presented the Battle Flag to the Kazakh Navy. The 612th Airbase in Aktau was opened a year later, in 2011.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

On 24 September 2026, 14 sailors drowned during [combat readiness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_readiness) exercises dubbed [Khazar Korgany-2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khazar_Korgany-2026?action=edit\&redlink=1). 500 military personnel took part in the training.[^cite_note-3]

## Aktau Naval Academy

The Aktau Naval Academy of the [Ministry of Defense of Kazakhstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defense_of_Kazakhstan) was the main educational institution of the naval forces. It established in March 2001 by order of the [national government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Kazakhstan) on the basis of the Vocational Technical School No. 2. which preceded it. It was reorganized into a naval institute on July 10, 2003, effectively upgrading its status as a nationally recognized military school. Graduates of the institute have served in ships and coastal units of the Navy and maritime units of the [Border Service of the National Security Committee of the Republic of Kazakhstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_Service_of_the_National_Security_Committee_of_the_Republic_of_Kazakhstan). In 2011, the institute was deactivated and liquidated.[^cite_note-5]
