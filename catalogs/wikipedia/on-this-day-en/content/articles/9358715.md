# Karkaar

This article is about the low mountain range. For the mountain in far northern Somalia, see [Karkoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karkoor).

**Karkaar** is a [mountain range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_range) which ranges from Somalia's northwestern border with Ethiopia, until [Cape Guardafui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Guardafui).[^cite_note-1] It is also an administrative region defined by the autonomous [Puntland](233569.md) state. The territory occupies the south of what has traditionally been the [Bari](185131.md) [region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Somalia) of Somalia, and generally lies south of the Karkaar mountain range.[^cite_note-PUNTLAND-MAP-2]

Since the [Somali Civil War](3070332.md), the Karkaar mountains are often referred to as the [Golis Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golis_Mountains). According to the *CULTURAL ORIENTATION SOMALI* published by the [Defense Language Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defense_Language_Institute) in 2020, the Golis Mountains is another name for the Galgala Hills, part of the Karkaar mountain range. The [Ogo Highlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ogo_Mountains) are south of the Karkaar mountain range.[^cite_note-3]
