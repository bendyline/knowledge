The **king's representative** ([Cook Islands Māori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cook_Islands_M%C4%81ori_language): *Te Kauono o te Ariki*[^cite_note-1]), or **KR**,[^cite_note-KR-2] is the official representative of [Charles III](125248.md), as [King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monarchy_in_the_Cook_Islands) of the [realm of New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Realm_of_New_Zealand), in the [Cook Islands](7067.md).[^cite_note-3] The office of King's Representative is established by the [Constitution of the Cook Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Cook_Islands). They are appointed by the King for a term of three years, and may be reappointed.[^cite_note-CI_constitution-4] When the Cook Islands has a [queen regnant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_regnant), the viceroy is titled **queen's representative** (QR).

The king's representative fills the role normally filled by a [governor-general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor-general) in the [Westminster system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westminster_system) of a [Commonwealth realm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_realm), being both a representative of the monarch and the titular head of executive government. They appoint the [prime minister](464270.md) and [Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_the_Cook_Islands)[^cite_note-5] and chair the Cook Islands [Executive Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_council_\(Commonwealth_countries\)).[^cite_note-6] In performing their duties, they must act on the [advice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advice_\(constitutional_law\)) of their government.[^cite_note-7]

Originally these duties were performed by the [high commissioner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Commissioner_\(Commonwealth\)) of New Zealand to the Cook Islands, but in 1982 these powers were repatriated and the office of Queen's Representative was established.[^cite_note-8] The [governor-general of New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor-General_of_New_Zealand) still represents the king in matters pertaining to the entire [realm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Realm_of_New_Zealand).[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

Following the death of [Elizabeth II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_II), the office formally became known as "King's Representative".[^cite_note-KR-2] As of 2026, however, the office is still referred to throughout the constitution as the "Queen's Representative".[^cite_note-CI_constitution-4]

In August 2025, [Tom Marsters](25568551.md) was reappointed as the representative for a fifth three-year term.[^cite_note-11]
