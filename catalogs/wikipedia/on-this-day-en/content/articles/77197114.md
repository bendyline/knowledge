The **94th 24 Hours of Le Mans** (French: *94<sup>e</sup> 24 Heures du Mans*) was an [automobile endurance race](699018.md) held on 13–14 June 2026 at the [Circuit de la Sarthe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_de_la_Sarthe) in [Le Mans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Mans), [France](5843419.md). It was the 94th running of [the 24-hour race](1401596.md) organised by the [Automobile Club de l'Ouest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automobile_Club_de_l'Ouest), and the third round of the [2026 FIA World Endurance Championship](80215536.md).[^cite_note-1] Hyundai's luxury division [Genesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genesis_Motor) ran two cars in the Hypercar class, becoming the first South Korean team to compete at the 24-hour race.[^cite_note-genesis-2]

In qualifying, [pole position](630713.md) was taken by [Dries Vanthoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dries_Vanthoor) in a [BMW M Team WRT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W_Racing_Team) Hypercar. The race was closely contested, with numerous lead changes between BMW, [Cadillac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cadillac) and [Toyota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota); seven cars finished on the lead lap.[^cite_note-3] The overall classification was won by the #7 [Toyota](3796361.md) Hypercar, driven by [Mike Conway](5506888.md), [Kamui Kobayashi](9537340.md) and [Nyck de Vries](28892600.md), ending a three-year streak for [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrari). The LMP2 class saw the No. 43 [Oreca 07](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oreca_07) of [Tom Dillmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Dillmann), [Jakub Śmiechowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakub_%C5%9Amiechowski) and [Nick Yelloly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Yelloly) lead an [Inter Europol Competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inter_Europol_Competition) one-two finish, the team's third victory in four years. The LMGT3 category went to [TF Sport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TF_Sport)'s No. 33 [Chevrolet Corvette Z06 GT3.R](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevrolet_Corvette_Z06_GT3.R) of [Nicky Catsburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicky_Catsburg), [Jonny Edgar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonny_Edgar) and [Ben Keating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Keating), the tenth class win for the American brand.[^cite_note-alkamelsystems-4]

## Background

The 2026 24 Hours of Le Mans was the 94th running of the [24 Hours of Le Mans](1401596.md) endurance race, held on 13–14 June 2026 at the [Circuit de la Sarthe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_de_la_Sarthe). The race formed the third round of the [2026 FIA World Endurance Championship](80215536.md) season.[^cite_note-5] Manufacturer changes for the 2026 season included [Porsche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porsche_in_motorsport)'s withdrawal from the WEC Hypercar class and the full-season debut of [Genesis Magma Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genesis_Magma_Racing), the first South Korean manufacturer team to compete in the top class.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

## Entries

The 2026 entry list featured 62 cars across three classes; 18 Hypercar, 19 LMP2, and 25 LMGT3.[^cite_note-8] South Korean marque [Genesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genesis_Motor) made its debut in the Hypercar class, entering two [Genesis GMR-001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genesis_GMR-001) in their maiden WEC campaign.[^cite_note-genesis-2]

### Automatic entries

In addition to all full-season [WEC](80215536.md) entries, automatic invitations were given to all championship winners in the [European Le Mans Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_European_Le_Mans_Series) (ELMS), LMP2 and LMGT3 in the [Asian Le Mans Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Asian_Le_Mans_Series) (ALMS) and Bronze Cup in [GT World Challenge Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_GT_World_Challenge_Europe)'s (GTWCE) combined Endurance and Sprint championship. Second-place finishers in LMP2 in the ELMS also earned an invite. Three [IMSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_IMSA_SportsCar_Championship) teams were also granted invitations, one in Hypercar at the discretion of IMSA, and one each for the Jim Trueman and Bob Akin awards, given to the highest placed Bronze-rated drivers in IMSA's LMP2 and GT3 classes.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

## Testing

Prior to the race, all entrants were required to participate in a test day, which took place on 7 June.[^cite_note-33]

The 62 cars were given six hours of track time across two sessions, running from 10\:00 to 13\:00 and from 15\:30 to 18\:30 [CEST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_European_Summer_Time) ([UTC+02\:00](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC+02\:00)).[^cite_note-34] In the Hypercar class, [Tom Gamble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Gamble_\(racing_driver\)) in the #007 Aston Martin THOR Team [Valkyrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aston_Martin_Valkyrie_AMR-LMH) set the fastest lap of the day with an afternoon time of 3\:26.293, narrowly short of the 2025 benchmark.[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36] [Brendon Hartley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendon_Hartley) in the #8 Toyota and [Norman Nato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_Nato) in the #12 Cadillac rounded out the top three, with all eight manufacturers split by 0.915 seconds.[^cite_note-testdaytimes-37][^cite_note-38] The #15 [BMW M Team WRT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W_Racing_Team) turned the most laps of any car during the test day, with 86 laps or 1,171 kilometres (728 mi).[^cite_note-testdaystats-39] A crash during the morning session saw damage to the #8 Toyota and #25 [Algarve Pro Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algarve_Pro_Racing), as rookie [Jake Hughes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jake_Hughes) spun on cold tyres at the Dunlop Curve and hit [Ryo Hirakawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryo_Hirakawa).[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41] In LMP2, [Job van Uitert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Job_van_Uitert) in the #28 [IDEC Sport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IDEC_Sport_Racing) set the fastest time of 3\:35.344, just ahead of [Mathias Beche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathias_Beche), who was quickest in LMP2 Pro-Am aboard the #14 [TDS Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TDS_Racing). In LMGT3, silver-rated [Francesco Castellacci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesco_Castellacci) topped the times with a 3\:56.646 in his #54 [Vista AF Corse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AF_Corse) [Ferrari 296 GT3 Evo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrari_296#296_GT3_Evo).[^cite_note-testdaytimes-37][^cite_note-testdaystats-39]

A number of [reserve drivers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reserve_driver) also turned laps at the test day, including Toyota's [Esteban Masson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esteban_Masson), BMW's [Philipp Eng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philipp_Eng) and Genesis' [Jamie Chadwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamie_Chadwick), who became the first woman to drive a [Hypercar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Mans_Hypercar) at Le Mans.[^cite_note-42][^cite_note-43] Porsche and McLaren factory driver [Laurens Vanthoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurens_Vanthoor) was a late addition to [United Autosports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Autosports)' LMP2 roster and drove the #22 [Oreca 07](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oreca_07).[^cite_note-44]

## Race

### Overall race and Hypercar

The ceremonial starter was cyclist [Mark Cavendish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Cavendish). By the first corner, [Cadillac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cadillac_V-Series.R)'s [Will Stevens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Stevens) overtook pole-sitter [Kevin Magnussen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Magnussen). When the cars reached the [Mulsanne Straight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mulsanne_Straight) on the first lap, [René Rast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_Rast) in the other [BMW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BMW_M_Hybrid_V8) took the lead. Both [Ferrari AF Corse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AF_Corse) cars gained places on the opening lap, the #51 gaining one place to 7th and the #50 moving from 12th on the grid to 9th. [Mike Conway](5506888.md) in the #7 [Toyota](3796361.md) used an [undercut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undercut_\(racing\)),[^cite_note-58] pitting after only 29 minutes followed by the sister #8 car. At the one hour mark, and after the first round of stops, this strategy had put the #8 car in the lead with the #7 car in fifth position.[^cite_note-59]

After two and a half hours, [Antonio Fuoco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Fuoco) in the #50 car made contact with an LMP2 and spun, but quickly rejoined without further incident. In the fourth hour, a full-course [yellow flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_flags#Yellow_flag) forced the #20 [BMW](3772.md), #12 [Cadillac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cadillac), and #101 Cadillac into the pits for emergency services. Later, [Alessandro Pier Guidi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alessandro_Pier_Guidi) in the #51 car slid and crashed into the #9 [Proton Competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proton_Competition) LMP2 at the Forest [esses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esses), forcing them both into the bollards, although both cars had minimal damage. The #51 Ferrari AF Corse was given a drive-through penalty for the incident, dropping it from sixth to tenth. The #83 AF Corse was given a 5-second penalty for an unsafe release from the [pit lane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pit_lane).[^cite_note-\:0-60] After the #8 Toyota pitted before the fifth hour, [Jack Aitken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Aitken)'s #38 Cadillac took the lead from [Sheldon van der Linde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheldon_van_der_Linde). [Dries Vanthoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dries_Vanthoor)'s #15 BMW clipped the #3 DKR LMP2 in the Porsche Curves, damaging Vanthoor's bodywork and causing a puncture.[^cite_note-61]

The [safety car](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safety_car) was deployed after the #88 Proton Ford and #54 AF Corse Ferrari LMGT3s crashed at the Forest esses, with the #54 car becoming stranded in the gravel. As multiple cars took pit stops under the safety car, the lead rapidly changed hands several times, going to [Sébastien Buemi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A9bastien_Buemi)'s #8 Toyota, followed by René Rast's #20 BMW, Will Stevens' #12 Cadillac, [Earl Bamber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earl_Bamber)'s #38 Cadillac, and [Nicklas Nielsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicklas_Nielsen)'s #50 Ferrari.[^cite_note-\:4-62] Immediately after the end of the safety car, the #50 Ferrari was forced to pit with a fire extinguisher system issue, which took 29 minutes to fix. [Mathys Jaubert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathys_Jaubert)'s #17 Genesis then suffered a puncture.[^cite_note-\:1-63]

The #8 Toyota pitted during the tenth hour, giving Cadillac the 1–2 positions, with the #12 in the lead, after both cleared the BMW of Rast.[^cite_note-\:6-64] [Paul-Loup Chatin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul-Loup_Chatin)'s #19 Genesis briefly stopped with an issue, causing a full-course yellow flag. The #38 Cadillac suffered a [power steering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_steering) issue, forcing a half-hour pitstop that dropped them 7 laps. The #8 Toyota and #101 Cadillac were given drive-through penalties for speeding under the full-course yellow.[^cite_note-\:7-65] The #19 Genesis experienced more problems when it pulled over on the road and required a power cycle to rejoin the race several laps down. The #38 Cadillac returned to the pits with further power steering problems; they were unable to rejoin the race and retired.[^cite_note-\:2-66]



The No. 12 Hertz Team Jota Cadillac V-Series.R led through the night and early morning before being given a drive through in the 16th hour.

By the 16th hour, the #12 Cadillac held a 47-second lead over the #8 Toyota, with the #20 BMW in third.[^cite_note-67] However, the #12 of [Louis Delétraz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Del%C3%A9traz) was given a drive-through penalty for speeding in a [slow zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_flags#Full-course_yellow). This gave [Brendon Hartley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendon_Hartley)'s #8 Toyota the lead, though under pressure from Sheldon van der Linde's #20 BMW. Mathys Jaubert's #17 Genesis snapped its right-front suspension after going over a kerb, forcing retirement.[^cite_note-68] The #8 Toyota next required a [brake drum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drum_brake) mounting change, dropping to fourth. The #50 Ferrari pulled over due to an electrical issue, retiring from the race under a safety car period caused by the #91 Manthey Porsche LMGT3 crashing at Mulsanne. At the restart, the running order was [Robin Frijns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robin_Frijns)' #20 BMW leading, followed by [Norman Nato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_Nato)'s #12 Cadillac, [Kamui Kobayashi](9537340.md)'s #7 Toyota, and [Ryo Hirakawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryo_Hirakawa)'s #8 Toyota.[^cite_note-69]

With four and a half hours remaining, Robin Frijns drove through the gravel in pit entry—the time lost meaning his #20 BMW emerged fourth after the pit stops.[^cite_note-70] The #12 Cadillac driven by Norman Nato inherited the lead, but struggled at the end of its stint, allowing both Brendon Hartley in the #8 and [Nyck de Vries](28892600.md) in the #7 to pass, giving Toyota the 1–2 positions by the end of the 21st hour.[^cite_note-\:3-71] With De Vries already on new tyres, the next pit sequence allowed the #7 to cycle into the lead, with a 22-second margin to the #20 BMW.[^cite_note-72]

Just before the beginning of the final hour, the #009 [Aston Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aston_Martin) suffered a rear suspension failure; driver [Alex Riberas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Riberas) reached the pits but rejoined several laps down.[^cite_note-73] With 47 minutes left, Robin Frijns in the #20 BMW overtook Sébastien Buemi's #8 Toyota, running on older tyres, for second place. The race ended with the #7 Toyota driven by Kamui Kobayashi in first place, with Robin Frijns in the #20 BMW in second, and Sébastien Buemi third for the #8 Toyota.[^cite_note-alkamelsystems-4]

### LMP2 and LMP2 Pro-Am



The No. 43 Inter Europol Competition Oreca 07-Gibson won the LMP2 class.

Although [Esteban Masson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esteban_Masson) set the fastest time in hyperpole, a one-place grid penalty incurred by [Louis Rousset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Rousset) in qualifying meant the #29 Forestier Racing by [Panis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivier_Panis) did not start from pole.[^cite_note-74] The #28 [IDEC Sport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IDEC_Sport_Racing) led the field to green instead, and [Job van Uitert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Job_van_Uitert) kept the class lead early on followed by the #30 [Duqueine Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duqueine_Team). The #4 [CrowdStrike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CrowdStrike) Racing by [APR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algarve_Pro_Racing) started on pole for the Pro-Am class.[^cite_note-\:9-53][^cite_note-\:10-54]

The #28 IDEC underwent a driver change to [Paul Lafargue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Lafargue_\(racing_driver\)), and the #30 Duqueine of [Richard Verschoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Verschoor) took the lead before the third hour. The #29 Forestier by Panis overtook the #28 IDEC to take second position. [P. J. Hyett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P._J._Hyett) in the #99 [AO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AO_Racing) by [TF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TF_Sport) led the Pro-Am class.[^cite_note-75] As [Doriane Pin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doriane_Pin) led in the #30, the #3 [DKR Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DKR_Engineering) suffered a broken suspension and had to pit, losing half an hour.[^cite_note-\:0-60] By the fourth hour, [Oliver Gray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Gray) regained second in the #29 Forestier by Panis, but would drop to fifth after a spin by [Louis Rousset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Rousset), allowing the #343 Inter Europol to take second.[^cite_note-\:5-76]

[Julien Andlauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julien_Andlauer) in the #30 Duqueine gradually built a lead of 91 seconds going into the night, but this was erased by the first [safety car](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safety_car) intervention.[^cite_note-77] After the restart in hour 9, Andlauer pitted immediately from the lead, but after the pit cycle, retook first position in the hands of Verschoor. The #99 AO by TF led the Pro-Am class and was eleventh in LMP2.[^cite_note-\:1-63] After many different pitstop strategies among the teams, the #343 Inter Europol emerged as the leader with [Nico Müller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_M%C3%BCller) driving away from Doriane Pin in the #30 Duqueine.[^cite_note-78]

In hour 14, the #343 Inter Europol of [Reshad de Gerus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reshad_de_Gerus) and #30 Duqueine of Julien Andlauer traded places, with the latter coming out on top. The #4 CrowdStrike by APR continued to lead in Pro-Am.[^cite_note-\:2-66] The #99 AO by TF of [Dane Cameron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dane_Cameron) locked up going into Mulsanne Corner and hit the tire barrier, leading to a slow zone intervention.[^cite_note-\:8-79] With 8 hours remaining, the #14 TDS of [Kévin Estre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%A9vin_Estre) went off and got beached at Indianapolis corner while running second in the Pro-Am class.[^cite_note-80] Due to a full-course yellow for debris, Doriane Pin in the #30 Duqueine had to take an emergency service fuel stop, but still led the class. [Tom Dillmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Dillmann) in the #43 Inter Europol was shown the [black and orange flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_flags#Black_flag_with_orange_circle) due to his right-hand door not shutting, and dropped from second to third. Additionally, [Jonas Ried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonas_Ried) in the #9 Proton Competition ran through an advertising banner at the Dunlop Chicane and suffered damage.[^cite_note-81]

Shortly after Richard Verschoor lost the lead to Reshad de Gerus, the #30 Duqueine was forced to retire with a terminal brake failure in hour 21.[^cite_note-\:3-71] Going into the final hour, the #43 Inter Europol led from the #29 Forestier by VPS, who overtook the #343 Inter Europol.[^cite_note-82] The race ended with an Inter Europol 1–2, with [Tom Dillmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Dillmann), [Jakub Śmiechowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakub_%C5%9Amiechowski) and [Nick Yelloly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Yelloly) driving the #43 to victory, ahead of Nico Müller in the #343 and Esteban Masson in the #29 Forestier by VPS, which required a late splash and dash. The #4 CrowdStrike by APR of [Laurin Heinrich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurin_Heinrich), [George Kurtz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Kurtz) and [Alex Quinn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Quinn) cruised to victory in LMP2 Pro-Am, beating the #183 [AF Corse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AF_Corse) and #99 AO by TF by over a lap.[^cite_note-83]

### LMGT3



The No. 33 TF Sport Corvette Z06 won the LMGT3 class.

[Mattia Drudi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mattia_Drudi) started on pole and took the early class lead in the #27 [Aston Martin THOR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Heart_of_Racing). By the end of the first hour, however, [Jack Hawksworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Hawksworth) led in the #78 Akkodis ASP Lexus over [Ian James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_James_\(racing_driver\)) who took control of the #27 Aston Martin, with [Petru Umbrărescu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petru_Umbr%C4%83rescu) in the #87 Akkodis ASP Lexus in 3rd. Meanwhile, [Martin Berry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Berry) spun the #61 [Iron Lynx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Lynx) Mercedes-AMG, returning to the pits with damage. The #88 [Proton Competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proton_Competition) [Mustang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Mustang) was given a black-and-orange flag for bodywork.[^cite_note-84]

By the 3rd hour, the #91 [Manthey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manthey_Racing) [Porsche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porsche) took the lead on an offset strategy, ahead of the #87 Akkodis ASP Lexus and the #21 AF Corse Ferrari. The #13 [13 Autosport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Wojteczko_Autosport) [Corvette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corvette_Racing) suffered a technical issue after contact with a Mercedes-AMG and became the first retirement of the race.[^cite_note-\:0-60] The #78 Akkodis ASP took first position before the #91 Manthey Porsche regained the lead after a round of pitstops. The #61 Iron Lynx AMG retired with suspension failure.[^cite_note-\:5-76]

After the safety car period ended, the #27 Aston Martin led from the #74 [Kessel Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loris_Kessel) Ferrari and the #21 AF Corse Ferrari, with the #91 Manthey Porsche falling to 7th following a puncture.[^cite_note-\:4-62] In hour 9, the #33 [TF Sport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TF_Sport) Corvette took 2nd position.[^cite_note-\:1-63] At the 10-hour mark, the #34 Racing Team Turkey Corvette driven by [Charlie Eastwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Eastwood) rose to 2nd place ahead of the #21 Ferrari. The #54 AF Corse Ferrari collided with the #88 Proton Ford and went into the gravel, becoming the third retirement of the race.[^cite_note-\:6-64]

In the 11th hour, after fluctuating pit strategies, the #78 and #87 Akkodis ASP Lexuses held a 1–2 with the #33 TF Sport Corvette in 3rd.[^cite_note-85] Halfway through the race, the #79 Iron Lynx retired with engine damage.[^cite_note-86] In the 13th hour, Aston Martins #27 and #23 held a 1–2 with the #33 Corvette in 3rd. Meanwhile, the #2 TF Sport Corvette and #69 [Team WRT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W_Racing_Team) [BMW](3772.md) received drive-through penalties for speeding under the full-course yellow.[^cite_note-\:7-65] Then, the #33 Corvette led from two Aston Martins, although the #27 Aston Martin and #74 Ferrari were penalized for speeding in a slow zone. A problem and spin for the #62 Mercedes-AMG forced repairs.[^cite_note-\:8-79]

With just less than 6 hours remaining, [Ayhancan Güven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayhancan_G%C3%BCven) in the #91 Manthey Porsche crashed on the exit of the first Mulsanne Chicane from 4th position, causing a safety car. The #23 Aston Martin took the lead, with the #33 Corvette and #78 Lexus behind.[^cite_note-\:3-71] With 2 hours remaining, it was the #33 Corvette in the lead followed by the #23 Aston Martin. The polesitter, the #27 Aston Martin, as well as the #69 Team WRT BMW retired both due to gearbox failures.[^cite_note-87]

[Jonny Edgar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonny_Edgar) finished the race in the #33 TF Sport Corvette, taking victory alongside [Nicky Catsburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicky_Catsburg) and [Ben Keating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Keating), ahead of the #78 Akkodis ASP Lexus of [Jack Hawksworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Hawksworth). [Eduardo Barrichello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eduardo_Barrichello) brought the #23 Heart of Racing Aston Martin home to take third place, fending off the #87 Lexus of [José María López](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Mar%C3%ADa_L%C3%B3pez).[^cite_note-alkamelsystems-4]

## Post-race

Toyota Racing secured its sixth overall 24 Hours of Le Mans victory with the #7 entry of Mike Conway, Kamui Kobayashi and Nyck de Vries, who completed 381 laps in 24\:03\:01.030. It was the second overall win for Conway and Kobayashi, having already won the [2021 edition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_24_Hours_of_Le_Mans), and the first for De Vries. It was Toyota's first win since [2022](68512218.md), ending a three-year streak for the [Ferrari 499P](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrari_499P). BMW M Team WRT finished second with the #20 car driven by Robin Frijns, René Rast and Sheldon van der Linde.[^cite_note-88]

Genesis Magma Racing completed its 24 Hours of Le Mans debut with its #19 car finishing 13th overall, becoming the first South Korean manufacturer team to complete the race; the #17 sister car retired in the 17th hour following a suspension failure.[^cite_note-89][^cite_note-90] Hyundai Motor CEO Jose Munoz commented that the event "demonstrates the quality and durability of Genesis while helping the company understand future technological development directions."[^cite_note-91] [Hyundai Motor Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Motor_Group) Executive Chair [Euisun Chung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chung_Eui-sun) visited the venue to encourage the team during the race weekend.[^cite_note-92]
