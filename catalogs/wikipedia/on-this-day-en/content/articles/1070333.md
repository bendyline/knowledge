**Châtillon-sur-Seine** (French pronunciation: [\[ʃɑtijɔ̃ syʁ sɛn\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French); lit. 'Châtillon-on-[Seine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seine)') is a [commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_France) of the [Côte-d'Or](90544.md) [department](38523.md), eastern France.[^cite_note-EB1911-3] The [Musée du Pays Châtillonnais](35852701.md) is housed in the old abbey of Notre-Dame de Châtillon, within the town, known for its collection of pre-Roman and Roman relics (especially the famous [Vix Grave](8081269.md)).

## History

Some ruins on an eminence above the town mark the site of a château of the [dukes of Burgundy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duchy_of_Burgundy). Nearby stands the church of St Vorles of the 10th century, but with many additions of later date; it contains a sculptured Holy Sepulchre of the 16th century and a number of frescoes. In a fine park stands a modern château built by [Marshal Marmont, duke of Ragusa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auguste_de_Marmont), born at Châtillon in 1774. It was burnt in 1871, and subsequently rebuilt.[^cite_note-EB1911-3]

Châtillon anciently consisted of two parts, Chaumont, belonging to the duchy of Burgundy, and Bourg, ruled by the [bishop of Langres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Langres); it did not coalesce into one town until the end of the 16th century. It was taken by the English in 1360 and by [Louis XI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_XI_of_France) in 1475, during his struggle with [Charles the Bold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_the_Bold). Châtillon was one of the first cities to adhere to the League, but suffered severely from the oppression of its garrisons and governors, and in 1595 made voluntary submission to [Henry IV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_IV_of_France). It is associated with the abortive conference of 1814 between the representatives of Napoleon and the Allies.[^cite_note-EB1911-3]

During [World War II](32927.md) the German garrison were forced to withdraw after a [successful operation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Wallace-Hardy) by the [Special Air Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Air_Service) operating in the nearby forest in late August 1944.[^cite_note-4]

## Geography

### Climate

Châtillon-sur-Seine has an [oceanic climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceanic_climate) ([Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *Cfb*). The average annual temperature in Châtillon-sur-Seine is 10.8 °C (51.4 °F). The average annual rainfall is 832.8 mm (32.79 in) with October as the wettest month. The temperatures are highest on average in July, at around 19.3 °C (66.7 °F), and lowest in January, at around 3.1 °C (37.6 °F). The highest temperature ever recorded in Châtillon-sur-Seine was 42.1 °C (107.8 °F) on 25 July 2019; the coldest temperature ever recorded was −22.4 °C (−8.3 °F) on 9 January 1985.

Châtillon-sur-Seine was the birthplace of:

- [Auguste Marmont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auguste_Marmont), duke of Ragusa (1774

  [^cite_note-EB1911-3]

  –1852), [Marshal of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshal_of_France)

- [Anne Nicole Voullemier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne_Nicole_Voullemier) (1796-1886), painter and lithographer

- [Désiré Nisard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C3%A9sir%C3%A9_Nisard) (1806–1888), author and critic

- [Louis Paul Cailletet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Paul_Cailletet) (1832–1913), [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist) and inventor

- [Alice Prin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alice_Prin) (Kiki de Montparnasse) (1901–1953), nightclub singer, actress, model, partner and muse of Man Ray, and painter

- [Danielle Georgette Reddé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danielle_Georgette_Redd%C3%A9) (1911–2007), [French resistance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_resistance) member during [World War II](32927.md)
