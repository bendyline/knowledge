**Lalisa Manobal**[^cite_note-5] ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): ลลิษา มโนบาล;[^cite_note-6] born **Pranpriya Manobal**,[^cite_note-7] March 27, 1997), known [mononymously](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mononym) as **Lisa** ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): ลิซ่า; [Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 리사), is a Thai rapper, singer, dancer, and actress. She rose to prominence as a member of the South Korean girl group [Blackpink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackpink), which debuted under [YG Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YG_Entertainment) in August 2016 and became one of the [best-selling girl groups of all time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_best-selling_girl_groups).

In September 2021, Lisa released her debut single album *[Lalisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lalisa_\(single_album\))*, which made her the first female artist to sell 736,000 copies of an album in its first week in South Korea and spawned the [*Billboard* Global 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Global_200) top-ten singles "[Lalisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lalisa_\(song\))" and "[Money](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Money_\(Lisa_song\))". The music video for "Lalisa" is the most-viewed in the first 24 hours on [YouTube](3524766.md) by a solo artist, while "Money" became the first song by a [K-pop](629945.md) solo artist to reach one billion streams on [Spotify](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spotify). In 2024, Lisa established her own management company named Lloud and signed with [RCA Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RCA_Records). She reached number one on the [*Billboard* Global Excl. US](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Global_Excl._US) with "[Rockstar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockstar_\(Lisa_song\))", the lead single from her debut studio album *[Alter Ego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alter_Ego_\(Lisa_album\))* (2025). The album debuted in the top ten of the US [*Billboard* 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_200) and included the [Official Thailand Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_Thailand_Chart) number-one songs "[Born Again](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Born_Again_\(Lisa_song\))" and "[Dream](79298248.md)".

Outside of music, Lisa made her acting debut in 2025 in the third season of the [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO) television series *[The White Lotus](65625519.md)*. Her [accolades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_awards_and_nominations_received_by_Lisa) include nine [Guinness World Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinness_World_Records), a [Mnet Asian Music Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mnet_Asian_Music_Award), three [MTV Europe Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Europe_Music_Awards), and four [MTV Video Music Awards](295217.md); she became the first K-pop soloist to win at the latter two award ceremonies. Lisa was honored as a cultural ambassador leader by Thailand's [Ministry of Culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Culture_\(Thailand\)) and was acknowledged by Prime Minister [Prayut Chan-o-cha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prayut_Chan-o-cha) for her contributions to spreading Thai culture globally. She was inducted into the [Asian Hall of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Hall_of_Fame) in 2023 and has appeared on the [*Forbes* Korea Power Celebrity 40](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes_Korea_Power_Celebrity_40) (2025–2026). In addition to being the [most-followed K-pop artist on Instagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_most-followed_Instagram_accounts), Lisa has been recognized for her influence in fashion as a global ambassador for [Celine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celine_\(brand\)), [Bulgari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgari), and [Louis Vuitton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Vuitton).

## Life and career

### 1997–2010: Early life and education

Lisa was born Pranpriya Manobal on March 27, 1997,[^cite_note-li_03272019-8] in [Satuek district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satuek_district), [Buriram province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buriram_province), Thailand,[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] and later legally changed her name to Lalisa, meaning "the one being praised",[^cite_note-scmp-11] at the advice of a fortune teller for prosperity.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] An only child, she was raised by her Thai Buddhist mother, Chitthip Brüschweiler, and her Swiss stepfather, chef Marco Brüschweiler.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] Lisa completed secondary education at Praphamontree School I and II.[^cite_note-sng-17] She is [multilingual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multilingualism); along with her native Thai, she speaks fluent Korean and English, as well as basic Japanese and Chinese.[^cite_note-moon_04042019-18]

After enrolling in dance classes at the age of four, she began competing regularly in contests, including in [To Be Number One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ubol_Ratana#Charitable_work),[^cite_note-19] and joined the eleven-member dance crew We Zaa Cool alongside [BamBam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BamBam_\(singer\)) of [Got7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Got7). In September 2009, the crew entered the LG Entertainment Million Dream Sanan World competition broadcast on [Channel 9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MCOT_HD) and won the Special Team Award.[^cite_note-20] Lisa also participated as a school representative in the singing contest Top 3 Good Morals of Thailand, hosted by the Moral Promotion Center in early 2009, finishing as the runner-up.[^cite_note-sng-17]

### 2012–2015: Pre-debut activities

In 2010, at age 13, Lisa auditioned to join the South Korean record label [YG Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YG_Entertainment) in Thailand. She grew interested in K-pop as a child thanks to artists such as [BigBang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BigBang_\(South_Korean_band\)) and [2NE1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2NE1) and aspired to follow a similar path.[^cite_note-21] Among 4,000 applicants, she was the only one to qualify, which prompted then CEO [Yang Hyun-suk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yang_Hyun-suk) to offer Lisa a chance to become a YG Entertainment trainee.[^cite_note-22] At her audition, Lisa also impressed one of the judges, [Danny Im](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taebin) of [1TYM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1TYM), who later praised her on-stage confidence and off-stage attitude.[^cite_note-23]

In 2011, Lisa moved to South Korea to begin her formal training. She officially joined the label as its first non-ethnic Korean trainee on April 11.[^cite_note-D-24] In November 2013, she appeared in the music video for labelmate [Taeyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taeyang)'s single "[Ringa Linga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ringa_Linga)" as a backup dancer alongside members of labelmates and boy bands [iKon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IKon) and [Winner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winner_\(band\)).[^cite_note-25] In March 2015, Lisa undertook her first modelling job for the streetwear brand [Nona9on](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YG_Plus#Nona9on), followed by South Korean cosmetics brand [Moonshot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YG_Plus#Moonshot) in 2016.[^cite_note-26]

### 2016–2023: Debut with Blackpink and *Lalisa*

Main article: [Blackpink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackpink)



Lisa performing in May 2017

In August 2016, Lisa debuted as one of four members of South Korean girl group [Blackpink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackpink), the first non-ethnically Korean artist under the label.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-scmp-11] The group's debut single album *[Square One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_One_\(single_album\))* featured lead singles "[Whistle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whistle_\(Blackpink_song\))", which topped all South Korean charts upon debut, and "[Boombayah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boombayah)".[^cite_note-28] In 2018, Blackpink signed with [Interscope Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interscope_Records) in a global partnership with YG Entertainment.[^cite_note-29]

Lisa became a permanent cast member on the [Korea Army Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korea_Army_Academy_at_Yeongcheon) edition of the [Munhwa Broadcasting Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munhwa_Broadcasting_Corporation) military variety program *[Real Man 300](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Man_300)* from September 21, 2018, onward. Her first role on a television program since her debut,[^cite_note-\:0-30] it earned her the unofficial Character of the Year award at the [2018 MBC Entertainment Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_MBC_Entertainment_Awards).[^cite_note-31] On November 5, 2018, Lisa unveiled her [YouTube](3524766.md) channel, Lilifilm Official, focusing on travelling, lifestyle, and dance.[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33] As of July 2019, she has over 1.3 million subscribers and received a YouTube [Gold Play Button](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube_Play_Buttons).[^cite_note-34] One of her dance performance videos went viral in 2020 as a result of a meme derived from the video; celebrities such as [Dolly Parton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolly_Parton), [Stephen Colbert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Colbert), [James Corden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Corden), [Luke Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Evans), and [Lil Nas X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lil_Nas_X) participated in the trend.[^cite_note-35] In March 2020, Lisa served as the dance mentor on [iQIYI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IQIYI)'s Chinese girl group survival program *[Youth With You Season 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youth_With_You_\(season_2\))*.[^cite_note-ywys2-36] In February 2021, she returned as a dance mentor in the show's [third season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youth_With_You_\(season_3\)) for boy groups.[^cite_note-ywys3-37]

On April 19, 2021, YG Entertainment revealed to *[The Korea Herald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Korea_Herald)* that Lisa would be the third member of Blackpink to debut as a soloist, with the official schedule to be announced later.[^cite_note-38] On July 12, through *Star News*, the label revealed that filming for her music video was underway.[^cite_note-39] Lisa's debut single album *[Lalisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lalisa_\(single_album\))* and [its lead single](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lalisa_\(song\)) of the same name were released on September 10, 2021.[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41] The music video for "Lalisa" became the most-viewed video by a soloist in 24 hours with 73.6 million views, breaking the record held by [Taylor Swift](5422144.md)'s "[Me!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Me!)" featuring [Brendon Urie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendon_Urie), for which she earned two [Guinness World Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinness_World_Records).[^cite_note-42][^cite_note-43] The song debuted at number 84 on the US [*Billboard* Hot 100](423161.md)[^cite_note-US-44] and at number two on the [*Billboard* Global 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_Global_200), becoming Lisa's first top-ten song on the latter.[^cite_note-45] The album sold 736,221 copies in South Korea in its first week, setting the record for the highest first-week sales among female artists domestically and making Lisa the first female soloist to surpass the 500,000-copy mark in one week.[^cite_note-46]

The album's B-side track "[Money](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Money_\(Lisa_song\))" received an exclusive dance performance video that was released to YouTube on September 24.[^cite_note-47] The song witnessed a surge in popularity after it became viral on social media, and began to rise in the international charts.[^cite_note-48] As a result, [Interscope Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interscope_Records) sent the song to US [contemporary hit radio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contemporary_hit_radio) on November 9, 2021, as the album's second single.[^cite_note-49] The single peaked at number ten on the *Billboard* Global 200, becoming Lisa's second top-ten hit after "Lalisa".[^cite_note-Money_Global_200-50] In the US, it became Lisa's second career solo entry on the *Billboard* Hot 100 and the first song by a female K-pop soloist to chart for multiple weeks on the chart at the time.[^cite_note-51] "Money" peaked at number 46 on the [UK Singles Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Singles_Chart) and charted for eight weeks, becoming the longest-charting female K-pop soloist song in the country at the time as well.[^cite_note-52]



Lisa at [Incheon Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incheon_International_Airport) in June 2019

Lisa collaborated with French record producer [DJ Snake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DJ_Snake), Puerto Rican singer [Ozuna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ozuna), and American rapper [Megan Thee Stallion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megan_Thee_Stallion) on the song "[SG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SG_\(song\))",[^cite_note-53] which was released on October 22, 2021, and became Lisa's first number-one song on the [Latin Airplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_Airplay) chart.[^cite_note-54] On August 28, 2022, Lisa won the [MTV Video Music Award for Best K-Pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Video_Music_Award_for_Best_K-Pop) for "Lalisa" and became the first K-pop solo artist to win an MTV Video Music Award.[^cite_note-55] On November 13, she won the [MTV Europe Music Award for Best K-Pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Europe_Music_Award_for_Best_K-Pop) and became the first solo K-pop artist in history to win an MTV Europe Music Award as well.[^cite_note-56] Lisa was awarded two Guinness World Records in 2023 for the achievements, in addition to a third for becoming the most followed K-pop artist on Instagram.[^cite_note-57]

Lisa featured on [BigBang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BigBang_\(South_Korean_band\)) member [Taeyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taeyang)'s song "[Shoong!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shoong!)", an album track from his second extended play *[Down to Earth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Down_to_Earth_\(EP\))*, which was released on April 25, 2023, alongside a performance video.[^cite_note-58] Lisa was awarded two further Guinness World Records in May for the fastest solo female K-pop artist to reach 1 billion streams and the first album by a solo K-pop artist to reach 1 billion streams on [Spotify](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spotify) with *Lalisa*. With this, she became the K-pop soloist with the most Guinness World Records in history, winning seven as compared to former record-holder [Psy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psy)'s five.[^cite_note-59]

In September 2023, Lisa headlined five performances for the Parisian cabaret [Crazy Horse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crazy_Horse_\(cabaret\)). She performed several solo scenes and appeared with the troupe for original numbers from the cabaret's repertoire, including "But I Am a Good Girl" and "Crisis? What Crisis?"[^cite_note-60] The same month, she earned her eighth Guinness World Record for the first K-pop track by a solo artist to reach 1 billion streams on Spotify with "Money".[^cite_note-61] To celebrate the achievement, Lisa appeared on the fourth episode of Spotify's *Billions Club* series in November, in which she received a plate-shaped plaque in [Paris](22989.md) and cooked a Thai dish for the plate.[^cite_note-62]

On November 22, 2023, Lisa was invested by [King Charles III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Charles_III) as an [Honorary Member of the Order of the British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_Member_of_the_Order_of_the_British_Empire) (MBE) alongside her bandmates during a special investiture at [Buckingham Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckingham_Palace) which was also attended by South Korean president [Yoon Suk Yeol](62877952.md).[^cite_note-63] On December 5, YG Entertainment announced that Lisa along with the other members of Blackpink had renewed their contracts for group activities and that the members' individual contracts were still under discussion.[^cite_note-64] YG Entertainment subsequently confirmed on December 29 that Lisa and the other Blackpink members agreed not to proceed with a contract with the label for individual activities.[^cite_note-65]

### 2024–present: Self-management, *Alter Ego* and acting debut



Lisa at a [Bulgari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgari) exhibition in June 2023

Lisa headlined the Le Gala des Pièces Jaunes charity event organized by [Brigitte Macron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigitte_Macron), the first lady of France, in Paris on January 26, 2024, where she performed a medley of her solo singles "Lalisa" and "Money". At the event, she also made a surprise appearance during DJ Snake's set, in which they performed "SG" live for the first time together.[^cite_note-66] On February 8, Lisa announced that she launched her own artist management company, called Lloud.[^cite_note-67] On April 10, it was announced that under a partnership with Lloud, she had joined [RCA Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RCA_Records) to release solo music; she will have full ownership of all of her recordings as part of the deal.[^cite_note-68]

Lisa began teasing new music on June 6 by creating a new [TikTok](56822861.md) account and posting a video paired with a teaser snippet, which led her to set a Guinness World Record by gaining a million followers in just two hours and 18 minutes.[^cite_note-69] She released "[Rockstar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockstar_\(Lisa_song\))" through Lloud and RCA on June 27 as the lead single from her debut studio album *[Alter Ego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alter_Ego_\(Lisa_album\))*.[^cite_note-70] It was accompanied by a music video which was filmed in [Bangkok](56615.md) and paid homage to her Thai culture.[^cite_note-71] "Rockstar" debuted at number four on the *Billboard* Global 200 and at number one on the *Billboard* Global Excl. US, becoming Lisa's third top-ten hit on both charts and her first number-one hit on the latter chart.[^cite_note-72] It also debuted at number 70 on the US *Billboard* Hot 100, marking Lisa's highest-charting song and her third entry on the chart.[^cite_note-73]

On August 15, Lisa released the album's second single "[New Woman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Woman_\(song\))" featuring Spanish singer [Rosalía](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosal%C3%ADa).[^cite_note-74] The song debuted at number six on the *Billboard* Global Excl. US and became Lisa's fourth entry on the US *Billboard* Hot 100.[^cite_note-75][^cite_note-US-44] She performed at the [2024 MTV Video Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_MTV_Video_Music_Awards) on September 11, which marked her debut major performance as a solo artist and made her the first K-pop soloist in history to perform on the show.[^cite_note-76] "Rockstar" received four nominations at the ceremony and won the award for Best K-Pop, making Lisa the first solo artist to win the award twice.[^cite_note-77]

Lisa headlined the [Global Citizen Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Citizen_Festival) on September 28, her first solo performance at a festival.[^cite_note-GlobalCitizenFestival-78] During the performance, she surprise debuted a new song titled "[Moonlit Floor (Kiss Me)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moonlit_Floor_\(Kiss_Me\))",[^cite_note-79] which was released afterwards on October 3 as the third single from *Alter Ego*.[^cite_note-80] She also performed at the [Victoria's Secret Fashion Show 2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria's_Secret_Fashion_Show_2024) on October 15.[^cite_note-81] Lisa subsequently embarked on her 2024 'Fan Meetup' tour in Asia, which comprised five shows starting November 11.[^cite_note-Fan_Meetup-82] On New Year's Eve 2024, she headlined the Amazing Thailand Countdown 2025 event in Bangkok.[^cite_note-83]

On February 6, 2025, Lisa released "[Born Again](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Born_Again_\(Lisa_song\))" featuring American rapper [Doja Cat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doja_Cat) and British singer [Raye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raye) as the fourth single from *Alter Ego*.[^cite_note-84] The song became her first number-one single on the newly launched [Official Thailand Chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_Thailand_Chart),[^cite_note-ThailandChart-85] and her fifth and highest-charting entry on the *Billboard* Hot 100 at number 68.[^cite_note-US-44] At the [2025 MTV Video Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_MTV_Video_Music_Awards), it became Lisa's third song to win Best K-Pop. As a result, she tied BTS as the most-awarded artist in the category.[^cite_note-86] She made her acting debut under her given name Lalisa Manobal as Mook in the [third season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_White_Lotus_season_3) of the [HBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBO) television series *[The White Lotus](65625519.md)*, which premiered on February 16.[^cite_note-whitelotus-87]

Lisa released her debut album *Alter Ego* on February 28, 2025, to mixed reviews from music critics.[^cite_note-88] It debuted at number seven on the US [*Billboard* 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_200) and number one on the [Top Album Sales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Top_Album_Sales) chart.[^cite_note-89] "[Fxck Up the World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fxck_Up_the_World)" featuring American rapper [Future](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Future_\(rapper\)) was released as the fifth single alongside the album release.[^cite_note-90] The singer also announced the launch of her comic company Lalisa Comics in partnership with Zero Zero Entertainment on February 18.[^cite_note-BenjaminVariety-91] The company's first release on March 24 was a graphic novel titled *Alter-Ego: The Official Comic*, a companion to the album authored by Lisa and illustrated by Minomiyabi.[^cite_note-92] On March 2, Lisa performed at the [97th Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/97th_Academy_Awards), making her the first K-pop singer to perform at the ceremony.[^cite_note-93] On April 11 and 18, she performed songs from *Alter Ego* at [Coachella 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coachella_2025).[^cite_note-94]

Lisa featured on American band [Maroon 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maroon_5)'s song "[Priceless](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priceless_\(song\))", released on May 2 as the lead single from their eighth studio album *[Love Is Like](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_Is_Like)* (2025),[^cite_note-95] which became her sixth entry on the *Billboard* Hot 100.[^cite_note-US-44] On May 16, she released a music video for the album track "[When I'm with You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/When_I'm_with_You_\(Lisa_song\))" featuring South African singer [Tyla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyla).[^cite_note-96] A documentary film titled *[Always Lalisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Always_Lalisa)* was announced to be in production from [Sony Music Vision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Music) on May 29. Directed by Sue Kim, the film chronicles Lisa's past year embarking on her solo career.[^cite_note-97] Lisa later premiered a short film for the album track "[Dream](79298248.md)" on August 13, starring herself with Japanese actor [Kentaro Sakaguchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentaro_Sakaguchi).[^cite_note-98] Following its release, the song peaked at number one on the Official Thailand Chart, earning her second chart-topper.[^cite_note-ThailandChart-85] "Dream" received four nominations and won [Best Pop](477262.md) at the [2026 MTV Video Music Awards](82993690.md), making Lisa the first K-pop soloist to win the category.[^cite_note-99]

On September 29, Lisa signed with the talent agency [WME](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endeavor_\(company\)) for representation in her acting endeavors.[^cite_note-100] She additionally signed with the booking agency [Wasserman Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wasserman_\(company\)) for touring representation globally on October 22.[^cite_note-101] [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix) announced on December 4 that Lisa would make her film debut starring alongside [Don Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ma_Dong-seok) and [Lee Jin-wook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Jin-wook) in *[Tygo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tygo)*, a spin-off in the *[Extraction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extraction_\(franchise\))* franchise.[^cite_note-102] On January 11, 2026, Lisa appeared in person at the [83rd Golden Globe Awards](81659305.md) as a presenter for [Best Actor – Television Series Drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actor_%E2%80%93_Television_Series_Drama) alongside [Priyanka Chopra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priyanka_Chopra).[^cite_note-103] On February 5, it was announced that Lisa would be starring in a "[*Notting Hill*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notting_Hill_\(film\))-inspired" romantic comedy film for Netflix, produced by David Bernad, an executive producer on *The White Lotus.*[^cite_note-104]

On March 30, Lisa announced her concert residency [Viva La Lisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viva_La_Lisa) at [The Colosseum at Caesars Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Colosseum_at_Caesars_Palace) from November 13 to 28, making her the first K-pop artist in history to stage a Las Vegas residency.[^cite_note-105] She collaborated with Italian-American electronic music producer [Anyma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anyma) on the single "[Bad Angel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Angel)", which was released on April 8.[^cite_note-106] She made a surprise appearance during Anyma's set at [Coachella 2026](81092105.md) on April 18 to perform the song live.[^cite_note-107] On May 21, Lisa released the single "[Goals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goals_\(song\))" with Brazilian singer [Anitta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anitta_\(singer\)) and Nigerian singer [Rema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rema_\(musician\)) as an [official soundtrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_FIFA_World_Cup_2026_Album) of the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md).[^cite_note-108] They performed "Goals" as headliners for the [FIFA World Cup opening ceremony](83423432.md) in [Los Angeles](18110.md) on June 12, making Lisa the first female K-pop act and the first Thai artist to headline a FIFA opening ceremony in history.[^cite_note-109]

## Fashion and endorsements

Throughout her career, Lisa's fashion image has been widely covered by fashion journalists and international media. She has been named the global ambassador and endorser of several fashion brands, including Louis Vuitton, Bulgari, and Nike, among others. Internationally, she has been selected to be the face of numerous fashion campaigns and collections, in addition to her features on fashion magazine covers and articles.

In January 2019, Lisa became the muse of [Hedi Slimane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hedi_Slimane), the artistic, creative and image director of the French luxury fashion brand [Celine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celine_\(brand\)).[^cite_note-111] Following her appearance in the brand's Essentials campaign, photographed by Slimane in June 2020, she was named global ambassador in September 2020.[^cite_note-112] On February 16, 2021, Lisa joined the guest jury for the French fashion award [ANDAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ANDAM).[^cite_note-\:3-113] In May 2026, Lisa became the host committee member of 2026 [Met Gala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Met_Gala).

### Bulgari

In July 2020, Lisa was officially appointed as a brand ambassador for [Bulgari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgari), the Italian luxury fashion house, and appeared in digital campaigns for its Serpenti and Bzero One collections.[^cite_note-114] She has been associated with the brand for several years and is often seen representing them at high-profile events and in promotional campaigns. Her role as global ambassador includes showcasing Bvlgari's jewelry, watches, and other products. In June 2022, Lisa and Anne Hathaway attended Bvlgari Eden The Garden of Wonders.[^cite_note-115] In May 2023, she attended the jeweler's Mediterranean high jewelry event in Venice. In May 2025, she attended Bulgari's High Jewelry Show.[^cite_note-116]

Lisa collaborated with Bulgari twice for its limited-edition watch in 2023 and 2025, respectively.[^cite_note-117]

### Louis Vuitton

In July 2024, she became a house ambassador for [Louis Vuitton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Vuitton).[^cite_note-Vuitton-118] Lisa had also attended Louis Vuitton's Autumn/Winter 2024 show in March 2024. After Lisa's official ambassadorship with Louis Vuitton was announced in July 2024, Lisa was present at the Louis Vuitton Spring/Summer 2025 show during Paris Fashion Week and had been featured in Louis Vuitton campaigns, including the Spring/Summer 2025 collection alongside Saoirse Ronan.[^cite_note-119]

In September 2024, she attended 2024 MTV Video Music Awards wearing Louis Vuitton. In October 2024, Lisa attended the Louis Vuitton Spring/Summer 2025 Womenswear show. Lisa also made her Met Gala debut in Louis Vuitton.[^cite_note-120]

### Nike

In January 2026, she became a house ambassador for [Nike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nike,_Inc.) and became its NikeSKIMS spokesperson.[^cite_note-121]

## Other ventures

### Endorsements



Lisa for a [Shopee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shopee) event in 2018

Through her work as a model for the South Korean cosmetics brand [Moonshot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YG_Plus#Moonshot), she became their brand ambassador in China on March 21, 2018.[^cite_note-122] On July 25, 2019, Lisa became the brand ambassador and presenter for the new collection launched in Thailand, in which six of the products include her autograph as part of the packaging.[^cite_note-123] On March 28, 2019, Lisa signed her first solo endorsement deal for [Advanced Info Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advanced_Info_Service) (AIS), the largest GSM mobile phone operator in Thailand. She became its highest-paid brand presenter, and her advertising campaign was the highest-rated commercial in Thailand.[^cite_note-124] On May 11, 2019, Lisa began endorsing the [Samsung Galaxy S10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samsung_Galaxy_S10) in Thailand. Her first promotional material for the brand was released on May 14.[^cite_note-125] Lisa also featured as an endorsement model for the mobile game *Ragnarok M: Eternal Love*, appearing as a character on their Midnight Party server beginning on July 24, 2019.[^cite_note-126]

In January 2020, Lisa joined labelmate [Mino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mino_\(rapper\)) of South Korean boy band [Winner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winner_\(band\)) as an endorsement model for sportswear brand [Adidas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adidas)' SS20 season My Shelter clothing range.[^cite_note-127] She was revealed as a brand spokesperson in China for the [fabric softener](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fabric_softener) company [D\&G Downy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downy) on May 13,[^cite_note-a-128] soon also becoming a brand spokesperson for Zhengouli, the yoghurt brand of [Mengniu Dairy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mengniu_Dairy), one of China's largest dairy companies.[^cite_note-a-128] On June 27, [Tencent Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tencent_Games) announced Lisa as [Supercell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supercell_\(video_game_company\))'s newest spokesperson in China for their mobile game *[Brawl Stars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brawl_Stars)*.[^cite_note-129] In October 2020, Lauder-owned [MAC Cosmetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MAC_Cosmetics) named Lisa their newest global brand ambassador; she served as muse and face of many of their collections and key campaigns. Of Lisa, Senior Vice President and Global Creative Director [Drew Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drew_Elliott) stated, "Always confident and never one to shy away from risks, she embodies our commitment to celebrating individuality and self-expression above all else."[^cite_note-130][^cite_note-131][^cite_note-132]

On February 22, 2021, Lisa was announced as the endorsement model for the [Vivo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vivo_\(technology_company\)) S9 smartphone,[^cite_note-133] having endorsed the Vivo S7 smartphone in 2020.[^cite_note-134] In March 2022, she became the brand ambassador to whiskey brand [Chivas Regal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chivas_Regal) and starred in the I Rise, We Rise campaign.[^cite_note-135] In September 2024, she starred in a campaign for the American earbuds brand [Bose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bose_Corporation), promoting its Ultra Open Earbuds.[^cite_note-136]

in November 2020, the clothing brand [Penshoppe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penshoppe) introduced her as their newest ambassador.[^cite_note-137]

On November 13, 2021, she announced her own makeup collection with American beauty brand [MAC Cosmetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MAC_Cosmetics), titled MAC X L. The line features powder blushes, eyeliner, an eyeshadow palette, and a face powder.[^cite_note-138]

### Photography

Through YG Entertainment, Lisa released the limited-edition book *0327*, composed entirely of self-taken photographs with a film camera. A first volume was released on her birthday in 2020,[^cite_note-139] followed by a second on her birthday the following year.[^cite_note-140] Volumes three and four were released in 2022 and 2023, respectively.[^cite_note-141][^cite_note-142]

### Philanthropy

In September 2021, Lisa expressed interest in joining a social program led by the Korean Foundation for International Cultural Exchange in partnership with YG Entertainment for Buriram Province, soon launching an online account to help collect donations. The partnership between the foundation and YG Entertainment aims to build a 160-square-meter cultural compound at Non Suwan Phitthayakhom School in Buriram; provide computers, projectors and other multimedia equipment to the school; and establish a K-pop dance academy staffed by local instructors.[^cite_note-143]

In July 2024, she was announced as a headliner for the [Global Citizen Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Citizen_Festival), an event part of a mission to end [extreme poverty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extreme_poverty), which took place on September 28.[^cite_note-GlobalCitizenFestival-78]

## Impact



Lisa at a fan sign event at the [AK Plaza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AK_Plaza) in 2018

Lisa has been cited as an influence by other performers in the K-pop industry. Gaga of girl group [Nature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nature_\(group\)) and Mayna of girl group [Hot Issue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_Issue_\(group\)) both named Lisa as their role model.[^cite_note-144][^cite_note-145] [Destiny Rogers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Destiny_Rogers)' song "Tomboy" experienced a 1939% increase in daily streams on [Spotify](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spotify) and entered the iTunes and Apple Music charts of various countries following a dance performance video uploaded by Lisa to her YouTube channel.[^cite_note-146] Rogers herself expressed gratitude toward Lisa on [Twitter](9988187.md) for revitalizing the song.[^cite_note-147] Lisa was praised by Thai prime minister [Prayut Chan-o-cha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prayut_Chan-o-cha) for promoting Thai culture in her "[Lalisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lalisa_\(song\))" music video.[^cite_note-148] Due to the cultural references in "Lalisa" music video, it was reported that there was an increase in sales of [traditional Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Thai_clothing) costumes and [accessories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fashion_accessory) by local markets in Bangkok.[^cite_note-149][^cite_note-150] In March 2021, Lisa, along with other five Thai celebrities, was awarded the "Inspirational Role Model" Award, given by the Children and Youth Development Club organization, in recognition of her work as a leader and role model for the new generations in Thailand.[^cite_note-151] In 2023, she was honored by Thailand's [Ministry of Culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Culture_\(Thailand\)) with an honorary Wattanakunathorn Award (Cultural Ambassador Leader) in honor of her role as a "leading force promoting Thailand to the world through soft power to increase the value of the economy."[^cite_note-152]

Lisa became the most-followed K-pop idol on [Instagram](31591547.md) in April 2019, with 17.4 million followers at the time,[^cite_note-153] and later became the first K-pop idol to surpass 50 million Instagram followers in April 2021.[^cite_note-154] In January 2023, she was awarded a *[Guinness World Record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinness_World_Record)* for being the most followed K-pop artist on the platform, with 87 million followers at the time.[^cite_note-155] As of September 2021, Lisa also held the record for the most-subscribed female K-pop soloist on [YouTube](3524766.md) with her channel Lilifilm Official, which had 8.24 million subscribers.[^cite_note-156] Lisa's commercial influence and marketing potential have been noted by media outlets and cited as an example of changing promotional strategies in the fashion and makeup industries.[^cite_note-157] Upon landing her first solo magazine cover shoot with *[Harper's Bazaar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harper's_Bazaar)* *Thailand* for their May 2019 issue, the magazine's distributor reported that all 120,000 printed and in-stock copies sold out. Generally, it is reported that 30k copies (per issue) are printed, with well-known celebrities possibly having an average of 60k copies printed. However, despite selling 120k copies, the public's demand for Lisa was still not met.[^cite_note-158][^cite_note-159]

Following Lisa's appearance at and social media posts from the [Celine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celine_\(brand\)) Men's Spring Summer 2020 Collection fashion show in [France](5843419.md) during [Paris Fashion Week](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Fashion_Week), it was reported that global searches for Celine's Triomphe bag leapt 66% on June 28, 2019.[^cite_note-160] Lisa's partnership with [MAC Cosmetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MAC_Cosmetics) was named one of the top beauty collaborations of 2020 by Launchmetrics according to their Media Impact Value (MIV) algorithm, with the announcement of the partnership becoming MAC Cosmetics' top-branded post of the year.[^cite_note-\:2-161] Launchmetrics further partially attributes the success of Lisa's endorsements to their authenticity as she frequently features the same brands across her social media channels.[^cite_note-\:2-161] On April 10, 2023, researchers at [Chiang Mai University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiang_Mai_University) named the Thai flower species *[Friesodielsia lalisae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friesodielsia_lalisae)* in Lisa's honor.[^cite_note-162] That October, Lisa was named one of the 2023 inductees into the Asian Hall of Fame, alongside the likes of [Freddie Mercury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freddie_Mercury) and [Ryuichi Sakamoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryuichi_Sakamoto).[^cite_note-163]
