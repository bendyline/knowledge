**Albert Manifold** (born 1962) is an Irish business executive, who was the [chief executive officer](52234.md) of [CRH plc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CRH_plc) from January 2014 to 2024, and then the [chairman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairman) of oil giant [BP](18998720.md) from October 2025 to May 2026. He was ousted from BP due to concerns about his behaviour, including bullying.

## Early and personal life

Manifold was born in August 1962, in Ireland.[^cite_note-1] His parents ran a hardware store in the Dublin suburb of [Kimmage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kimmage).[^cite_note-\:0-2]

He attended [Templeogue College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Templeogue_College),[^cite_note-\:0-2] and then went on to attend [Dublin City University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin_City_University) where he did a master's degree in Business Studies and a [Master of Business Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Business_Administration).[^cite_note-3] He is a qualified [Chartered Accountant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chartered_Accountant) and [Certified Public Accountant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Certified_Public_Accountant).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-\:0-2]

As of April 2018, he lived in South [County Wicklow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Wicklow). He is married and has three children.[^cite_note-\:0-2] He is a follower of [Leinster Rugby](985961.md), and played [prop forward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prop_forward) at Templeogue College. Manifold is also an avid [beekeeper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beekeeper). In his childhood years, he helped a priest with a beehive.[^cite_note-\:0-2]

## Career

Before joining CRH, Manifold was [chief operating officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_operating_officer) (COO) with Dublin investment firm Allen Maguire & Partners, which was backed by various US pension funds.[^cite_note-5] Manifold was then COO and a board member of Irish building materials company [CRH plc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CRH_plc) from January 2009 until his appointment as CEO in January 2014.[^cite_note-6] He exited his role at CRH in 2024, after 28 years at the company.[^cite_note-7]

In July 2025, he was appointed chairman of [BP](18998720.md), effective 1 October 2025, replacing [Helge Lund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helge_Lund).[^cite_note-8] In May 2026, BP's board unanimously decided to remove Manifold as chairman with immediate effect, citing "serious" concerns about his conduct, such as "bullying" and "overbearing" behaviour.[^cite_note-9] Manifold publicly rejected the company's reasons for dismissal, saying "I dispute entirely the characterisation of my conduct,".[^cite_note-10]
