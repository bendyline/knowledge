The American bison (*Bison bison*). Officially recognized by Congress in 2016 as the national mammal of the United States through the National Bison Legacy Act, the bison is not a mere decoration on nickel coins or the Department of the Interior seal, but a true keystone species that shaped the geography, vegetation, and biodiversity of the entire continent for millennia. Unlike any bird or apex predator, the presence of the bison was measured not in thousands, but in tens of millions, an estimated 30 to 60 million once roamed the plains forming the largest concentration of large mammals on Earth before indiscriminate hunting in the 19th century reduced its population to fewer than 1,000 individuals by 1889. Its extraordinary recovery to over 500,000 individuals today represents one of the greatest conservation victories in history. Also chosen as the state animal in places like Wyoming, Kansas, and Oklahoma, and possessing an undeniable spiritual and historical connection to Indigenous nations, the bison physically, biologically, and historically embodies the true strength and scale of the American landscape.

The **fauna of the United States of America** is all the animals living in the [contiguous United States](83759.md) and its surrounding seas and islands, the [Hawaiian Archipelago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawaiian_Archipelago), [Alaska](624.md) in the Arctic, and several [island-territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Territories_of_the_United_States) in the Pacific and in the Caribbean. The U.S. has many [endemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endemic) species found nowhere else on Earth. With most of the North American continent, the U.S. lies in the [Nearctic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nearctic), [Neotropic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neotropic), and [Oceanic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceanian_realm) faunistic realms, and shares a great deal of its flora and fauna with the rest of the [American supercontinent](29833.md).[^cite_note-1]

An estimated 432 [mammal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mammal) species comprise the fauna of the continental U.S. There are more than 800 species of [bird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bird)[^cite_note-2] and more than 100,000 known species of [insect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insect). There are 311 known [reptiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reptile), 295 [amphibians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphibian) and 1154 known [fish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fish) species in the U.S.[^cite_note-3] Known animals that exist in all of the [lower 48 states](83759.md) include [white-tailed deer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White-tailed_deer), [bobcat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobcat), [raccoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raccoon), [muskrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muskrat), [striped skunk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Striped_skunk), [barn owl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_barn_owl), [American mink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_mink), [American beaver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_beaver), [North American river otter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_American_river_otter) and [red fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_fox). The [red-tailed hawk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red-tailed_hawk) is one of the most widely distributed hawks not only in the U.S., but in the Americas.

Huge parts of the country with the most distinctive indigenous wildlife are protected as national parks. In 2013, the U.S. had more than 6770 national parks or protected areas, all together more than 1,006,619 sq. miles (2,607,131 km<sup>2</sup>).[^cite_note-4] The first national park was [Yellowstone National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_National_Park) in the state of Wyoming, established in 1872. Yellowstone National Park is widely considered to be the finest [megafauna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megafauna) wildlife habitat in the U.S. There are 67 species of mammals in the park, including the [gray wolf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gray_wolf), the threatened [lynx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynx), and the [grizzly bear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grizzly_bears).[^cite_note-5]

## Western United States



The [raccoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raccoon) is widespread throughout the [lower 48](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_48) states.



[Mountain lions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_lion) live throughout the western U.S.

The ecoregions and ecology found in the [Western United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_United_States) are extremely varied. For instance, large areas of land are made up of everything from sand dunes in the [Central Basin and Range ecoregion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Basin_and_Range_ecoregion), which makes up much of the [State of Nevada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_Nevada), to the [ecology of the North Cascades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecology_of_the_North_Cascades) in [Washington state](13015878.md), which has the largest concentration of active [alpine glaciers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpine_glacier) in the [lower 48](83759.md). The densely forested areas found in [Northern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_California), [Oregon](26811621.md), Washington, [Idaho](14607.md), and [Montana](19978.md) have mostly species adapted to living in [temperate climates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperate_climate), while [Southern California](62520.md), [Nevada](21216.md), [Arizona](21883824.md), southern [Utah](31716.md), and [New Mexico](21649.md) have a fauna resembling its position in the dry [deserts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desert) with temperature extremes.

The western continental coast of the U.S. varies from a colder climate in the north to a warmer climate in the south. While few species live throughout the entire [West Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Coast_of_the_United_States), there are some, such as the [bald eagle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bald_eagle), that inhabit both the Alaskan [Aleutian Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleutian_Islands) and the [California Channel Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Channel_Islands). In most of the contiguous Western U.S., [mule deer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mule_deer), [white-tailed antelope squirrels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White-tailed_antelope_squirrel), [cougars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cougar), [American badgers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_badger), [coyotes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coyote), [hawks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawk) and several species of snake and lizard are common.

While the [American black bear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_black_bear) lives throughout the U.S., [brown bears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_bear) and [grizzly bears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grizzly_bear) are more common in the [northwest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_United_States) and in [Alaska](624.md). Along the West Coast there are several species of whales, [sea otters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_otter), [California sea lions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_sea_lions), [eared seals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eared_seal) and [northern elephant seals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_elephant_seal). In the dry, inland desert areas of states such as California, Nevada, Arizona and New Mexico there are some of the world's most venomous lizards, snakes and scorpions. The most notorious might be the [Gila monster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gila_monster) and [Mohave rattlesnake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohave_rattlesnake), both found in deserts in the [Southwest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwest_United_States). The Sonoran Desert has eleven species of rattlesnakes - more than anywhere else in the world.[^cite_note-6]

Along the southwestern border there are [jaguars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaguars) and [ocelots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ocelots). Other mammals include the [Virginia opossum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_opossum), which occurs throughout California and coastal areas in Oregon and Washington. The [North American beaver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_American_beaver) and [mountain beaver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_beaver) live in forested areas of Washington, Oregon and Northern California. The [kit fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kit_fox) lives throughout Arizona, New Mexico and Utah, while the [gray fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gray_fox) occurs throughout the Western U.S.

The [red fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_fox) occurs mostly in Oregon and Washington, while the [island fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island_fox) is a native to six of the eight [Channel Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channel_Islands_of_California) in Southern California. These islands are also famous for their marine life and endemic species such as the [Channel Islands spotted skunk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channel_Islands_spotted_skunk), [Garibaldi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garibaldi_\(fish\)), [island fence lizard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island_fence_lizard), [island scrub jay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island_scrub_jay), [bald eagle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bald_eagle), and their non-native [Catalina Island bison herd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalina_Island_bison_herd). The [raccoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raccoon) and [spotted skunk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spotted_skunk) occur throughout the Western U.S., while the [ring-tailed cat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring-tailed_cat) occurs throughout Arizona, New Mexico, Western Texas, Utah, Colorado, and most of California. The [American black bear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_black_bear) occurs in most western states, including Washington, Oregon, California, Arizona and Colorado.

### Channel Islands

The [Channel Islands National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channel_Islands_National_Park) consists of five of the eight [California Channel Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Channel_Islands). The Channel Islands are part of one of the richest marine biospheres of the world. Many unique species of plants and animals are [endemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endemic_\(ecology\)) to the Channel Islands, including fauna such as the [island fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island_fox), [Channel Islands spotted skunk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channel_Islands_spotted_skunk), [island scrub jay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island_scrub_jay), [ashy storm-petrel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashy_storm-petrel), [island fence lizard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island_fence_lizard), [island night lizard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island_night_lizard), [Channel Islands slender salamander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channel_Islands_slender_salamander), [Santa Cruz sheep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Cruz_sheep), [San Clemente loggerhead shrike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Clemente_loggerhead_shrike) and [San Clemente sage sparrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Clemente_sage_sparrow).[^cite_note-7] Other animals in the islands include the [California sea lion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_sea_lion), [California moray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_moray), [bald eagle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bald_eagle), [Channel Islands spotted skunk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channel_Islands_spotted_skunk) and the non-native [Catalina Island bison herd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalina_Island_bison_herd).

## Southern United States

The [South](179553.md) has a large variety of habitats that range from the [Mississippi River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippi_River) basin in [Arkansas](1930.md) and [Mississippi](16949861.md) to the Southern [Appalachian Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appalachians). As far north as the hills of [Tennessee](30395.md), [Kentucky](16846.md), and [Virginia](32432.md), all the way down to the [Everglades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everglades) in the southern end of [Florida](18933066.md). From the eastern-most point on the [Outer Banks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outer_Banks) of [North Carolina](21650.md), as far west as the deserts and prairies of [West Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Texas) and [Oklahoma](22489.md). The warmer climate allows for rich biodiversity ranging from [cypress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taxodium_distichum) swamps in [Louisiana](18130.md) to the thick bays and the [longleaf pine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longleaf_pine) biome of the [South Carolina Lowcountry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Carolina_Lowcountry). It is riddled along the way with countless [salt marshes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_marshes) in every coastal state from the [Carolinas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carolinas), through [Georgia](48830.md) to [Texas](29810.md), including the Mobile Delta that lies in the borders of [Alabama](303.md).



The [American alligator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_alligator) is endemic to eight states in the Southeast, and is the [official state reptile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_state_reptiles) of [Florida](18933066.md), [Louisiana](18130.md) and [Mississippi](16949861.md).

The Southern United States is home to a multitude of reptiles and amphibians. The [American alligator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_alligator) lives in much of the South - including every coastal state from North Carolina to Texas, along with parts of the inland states of Arkansas and Tennessee- while the less widespread [American crocodile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_crocodile) is only found in southern Florida. The [Alligator snapping turtle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alligator_snapping_turtle) and more than forty other species of turtle are found in the southern U.S. including the [eastern box turtle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_box_turtle), [red-eared slider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red-eared_slider), and the [softshell turtle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Softshell_turtle). Snakes in the region include the [eastern copperhead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_copperhead), [eastern diamondback rattlesnake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_diamondback_rattlesnake), [timber rattlesnake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timber_rattlesnake), [pigmy rattlesnake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sistrurus_miliarius), [cottonmouth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cottonmouth), and [eastern coral snake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_coral_snake), all of which are venomous. Some of the other reptiles and amphibians thriving in the South include the [Carolina anole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carolina_anole), [razor-backed musk turtle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Razor-backed_musk_turtle), [broad-headed skink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broad-headed_skink), [American bullfrog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_bullfrog), [southern toad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_toad), [spring peeper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spring_peeper) and the [coal skink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coal_skink).

Mammals of the region include the [elk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elk), the largest of which that was wiped out in the 1800s, but has been reintroduced and is making promising recoveries in Virginia, North Carolina, Kentucky, Tennessee, and Arkansas. There still remain resident populations in parts of Texas and Oklahoma. The [American black bear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_black_bear) is native to much of the South, but are prevalent in Virginia, the Carolinas, Tennessee, Georgia, Florida, Arkansas, and Oklahoma. The [Florida panther](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_panther) is the largest feline in the South and is exclusive to the wetlands of South Florida. [White-tailed deer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White-tailed_deer), [bobcat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobcat), [coyote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coyote), [wild boar](52255.md), [red](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_fox) and [grey fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grey_fox) are other mammals that inhabit parts of every state in the region. Wild horses roam parts of the South in small groups, which are remnants of horses brought by settlers in the 1400s and 1500s. These are mostly in coastal habitats.

Many water-dwelling mammals inhabit the South including the [American beaver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_beaver), [muskrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muskrat), [river otter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_American_river_otter), and [nutria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nutria), which is an invasive species and has decimated plant life in the swamps of Louisiana. [Weasels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weasels) and [mink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mink) also prefer being near water. Rabbits are common in the South; the [eastern cottontail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_cottontail) is found throughout the region, while the [desert cottontail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desert_cottontail) and [black-tailed jackrabbit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black-tailed_jackrabbit) is primarily found in Texas, and Oklahoma. The [swamp rabbit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swamp_rabbit) is found in wetlands of states like Mississippi, Alabama, Louisiana and Arkansas, while the [marsh rabbit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marsh_rabbit) resides along the coastal regions of the Carolinas, Georgia, Florida, and Alabama. Squirrels are also abundant. The [eastern grey squirrel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_grey_squirrel) and [eastern fox squirrel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_fox_squirrel) can both be found in every southern state. The southern range of the [American red squirrel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_red_squirrel) dips into the higher elevations of Virginia and North Carolina. Other common mammals are the [Virginia opossum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_opossum), [raccoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raccoon), [striped](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Striped_skunk) and [spotted skunk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spotted_skunk), [groundhog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groundhog) and in parts of the South, the [Mexican long-nosed armadillo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_long-nosed_armadillo).

There are over 1,100 species of bird in the Southern U.S. ranging from upland birds, to waterfowl. The South is home to many coastal birds including [gulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulls), [rails](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rail_\(bird\)), [gallinules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rail_\(bird\)), [skimmers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skimmer_\(bird\)), [grebes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grebes), [sandpipers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandpipers), [cranes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crane_\(bird\)), and [herons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herons). Upland birds include [wild turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_turkey) and [ruffed grouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruffed_grouse). Various game bird species such as the [bobwhite quail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobwhite_quail) and the [woodcock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodcock). The [eastern whip-poor-will](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_whip-poor-will) and the [Chuck-will's-widow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chuck-will's-widow) belong to the [nighthawk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nighthawk) family and are found in every southern state. Songbirds make up the largest portion of birds found in this region.

## Central United States



The [pronghorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pronghorn) is the fastest land mammal in the Western Hemisphere and can reach speeds up to 55 mph.

[^cite_note-8]

In the [prairie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prairie) in the [Central United States](2031425.md) live mostly animals adapted for living in grasslands. Indigenous mammals include the [American bison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_bison), [eastern cottontail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_cottontail), [black-tailed jackrabbit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black-tailed_jackrabbit), [plains coyote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plains_coyote), [black-tailed prairie dog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black-tailed_prairie_dog), [muskrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muskrat), [opossum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opossum), [raccoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raccoon), [prairie chicken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prairie_chicken), [wild turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_turkey), [white-tailed deer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White-tailed_deer), [swift foxes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swift_fox), [pronghorn antelope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pronghorn_antelope), the [Franklin's ground squirrel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin's_ground_squirrel) and several other species of ground squirrels.

Reptiles include [bullsnakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bullsnake), [common collared lizard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_collared_lizard), [common snapping turtle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_snapping_turtle), [musk turtles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sternotherus), [yellow mud turtle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_mud_turtle), [painted turtle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Painted_turtle), [western diamondback rattlesnake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_diamondback_rattlesnake) and the [prairie rattlesnake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prairie_rattlesnake). Some of the typical amphibians found in the region are the [three-toed amphiuma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three-toed_amphiuma), green toad, [Oklahoma salamander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oklahoma_salamander), [lesser siren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lesser_siren) and the [plains spadefoot toad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plains_spadefoot_toad). In the [Rocky Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocky_Mountains) and other mountainous areas of the inland is where the [bald eagle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bald_eagle) is most observed, even though its habitat includes all of the [Lower 48](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_48), as well as Alaska.

Rabbits live throughout the [Great Plains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Plains) and neighboring areas; the [black-tailed jackrabbit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black-tailed_jackrabbit) is found in Texas, Oklahoma, Nebraska and Kansas, the [white-tailed jackrabbit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White-tailed_jackrabbit) in the Dakotas, Minnesota and Wisconsin, the [swamp rabbit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swamp_rabbit) in swampland in Texas, and the [eastern cottontail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_cottontail) is found in Texas, Oklahoma, Kansas, Nebraska, the Dakotas, and every state in the Eastern U.S.



The groundhog is a common species in Iowa, Missouri, and eastern portions of Kansas, Nebraska and Oklahoma.

The [groundhog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groundhog) is widespread throughout Illinois, Iowa, Missouri, and Minnesota. [Virginia opossum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_opossum) is found in states such as Missouri, Indiana, Iowa, Oklahoma, Nebraska and Kansas.

The [Mexican long-nosed armadillo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_long-nosed_armadillo) is found throughout the [South](179553.md) and states such as Missouri, Kansas and Oklahoma. The [muskrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muskrat) is found throughout the [Central](2031425.md) U.S., excluding Texas, while the [American beaver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_beaver) is found in every central state.



The [American bison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_bison) is the heaviest land animal in North America and can be as tall as 6.5 feet (2.0 m) and weigh over a ton.

[^cite_note-animals.nationalgeographic.com-9]

Maybe the most iconic animal of the American prairie, the [American buffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_bison), once roamed throughout the central plains. Bison once covered the Great Plains and were critically important to Native-American societies in the Central U.S. They became nearly extinct in the 19th century, but have made a recent resurgence in the Great Plains. Today, bison numbers have rebounded to about 200,000; these [bison live on preserves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservation_of_American_bison) and ranches.[^cite_note-animals.nationalgeographic.com-9]

Some of the species that occupy every central state include the [red fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_fox), [bobcat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobcat), [white-tailed deer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White-tailed_deer), [raccoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raccoon), [eastern spotted skunk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_spotted_skunk), [striped skunk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Striped_skunk), [long-tailed weasel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long-tailed_weasel), and the [American badger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_badger) and [beaver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_beaver). The invasive [wild boar](52255.md) is common in the South, while the [American mink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_mink) lives in every central state with the exception of Texas. The [least weasel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Least_weasel) is found around the [Great Lakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Lakes) as well as states such as Nebraska, the Dakotas, Minnesota, Iowa, Illinois, Michigan, and Wisconsin.

The [gray fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gray_fox) is found in Iowa, Missouri, Oklahoma, Texas and also around the Great Lakes region. The [ring-tailed cat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring-tailed_cat) is found in the southern region, including in Texas, Missouri, and Oklahoma. There are many species of squirrels in the central parts of the U.S., including the [fox squirrel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_squirrel), [eastern gray squirrel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_gray_squirrel), [Franklin's ground squirrel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin's_ground_squirrel), [southern flying squirrel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_flying_squirrel), and the [thirteen-lined ground squirrel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thirteen-lined_ground_squirrel). Shrews include the [cinereus shrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinereus_shrew), [southeastern shrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southeastern_shrew), [North American least shrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_American_least_shrew), and the [Elliot's short-tailed shrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliot's_short-tailed_shrew).

## Hawaiian Islands



A [green sea turtle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_sea_turtle) (*honu* in Hawaiian) swimming by coral reefs in [Kona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kona_District,_Hawaii).

Much of the fauna in [Hawaii](13270.md) has developed special adaptations to their home and evolved into new species. Today, nearly 90% percent of the fauna in [Hawaii](13270.md) are [endemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endemic), meaning that they exist nowhere else on Earth.[^cite_note-autogenerated2-15] Kauaʻi is home to the largest number of tropical birds, as it is the only island free of [mongooses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongooses). The invasive [Javan mongoose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javan_mongoose) is widespread throughout the archipelago, except on the islands of [Lanaʻi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lana%CA%BBi) and [Kauaʻi](70961.md).

Famous birds include [ʻiʻiwi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%CA%BBI%CA%BBiwi), [nukupuʻu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nukupu%CA%BBu), [Kauaʻi ʻamakihi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaua%CA%BBi_%CA%BBamakihi) and [ʻōʻū](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%CA%BB%C5%8C%CA%BB%C5%AB). Most of these birds are extinct. The [hoary bat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoary_bat) is found in the [Kōkeʻe State Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C5%8Dke%CA%BBe_State_Park) on Kauaʻi, [feral horses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feral_horse) live in the [Waipio Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waipio_Valley), [feral cattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawaiian_wild_cattle) by the [Mauna Kea](267366.md), and the Australian [brush-tailed rock-wallaby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brush-tailed_rock-wallaby) live by the [Kalihi Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalihi_Valley) on Oʻahu. The [Hawaiian monk seal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawaiian_monk_seal), [feral goats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feral_goat), feral sheep, and [feral pigs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feral_pig) live throughout most of the archipelago.

In [Hawaii](13270.md), three species of sea turtles are considered native: [honu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honu), [honu'ea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawksbill_sea_turtle) and the [leatherback sea turtle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leatherback_sea_turtle). Two other species, the [loggerhead sea turtle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loggerhead_sea_turtle) and the [olive ridley sea turtle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olive_ridley_sea_turtle), are sometimes observed in Hawaiian waters.[^cite_note-16] The [Hawaiian](13270.md) [green sea turtle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_sea_turtle) is the most common sea turtle in [Hawaiian](13270.md) waters. As well as turtles, the sea life consist of more than forty species of shark[^cite_note-autogenerated2-15] and the [Hawaiian spinner dolphin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawaiian_spinner_dolphin) is widespread. [Hawaii](13270.md)'s coral reefs are home to over 5000 species, and 25 percent of these are found nowhere else in the world.[^cite_note-17]

## Alaska

Main article: [Fauna of Alaska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fauna_of_Alaska)



[Grizzly bears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grizzly_bear) are found throughout [Alaska](624.md), parts of [Montana](19978.md) and on the Canada–US border in [Idaho](14607.md). They are also found in [Yellowstone National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowstone_National_Park).

The [wildlife of Alaska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildlife_of_Alaska) is abundant, extremely diverse and includes for instance [polar bears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polar_bears), [puffins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puffin), [moose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moose), [bald eagles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bald_eagle), [Arctic foxes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arctic_fox), [wolves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolves), [Canadian lynx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_lynx), [muskox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muskox), [snowshoe hare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snowshoe_hare), mountain goats, [walrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walrus) and [caribou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caribou). Life zones in [Alaska](624.md) range from grasslands, mountains, tundra to thick forests, which leads to a huge diversity in terrain and geology throughout the state.

[Alaska](624.md) has also over 430 species of birds and the largest population of bald eagles in the nation. From pygmy shrews that weigh less than a penny to gray whales that weigh 45 tons, Alaska is the "Last Frontier" for animals as well as people. Many species endangered elsewhere are still abundant in Alaska.

### Aleutian Islands

The [Aleutian Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleutian_Islands) are home to an abundance of large [bird colonies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bird_colony); more than 240 bird species inhabit Alaska's [Aleutian Archipelago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleutian_Archipelago).[^cite_note-18] Large seabird colonies are present on islands like [Buldir Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buldir_Island), which has 21 breeding seabird species, including the Bering Sea-endemic [red-legged kittiwake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red-legged_kittiwake).[^cite_note-19] Large seabird colonies are also present on [Kiska Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiska_Island), [Gareloi Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gareloi_Island), [Semisopochnoi Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semisopochnoi_Island), [Bogoslof Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogoslof_Island), and several others.[^cite_note-oceana.org-20]

The islands are also frequented by vagrant Asiatic birds, including the [common rosefinch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_rosefinch), [Siberian rubythroat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siberian_rubythroat), [bluethroat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bluethroat), [lanceolated warbler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanceolated_warbler), and the first North American record of the [intermediate egret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intermediate_egret). Other animals in the Aleutian Chain include the [Arctic fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arctic_fox), [American mink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_mink), [Porcupine caribou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porcupine_caribou), [northern sea otter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_sea_otter), [horned puffin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horned_puffin), [tufted puffin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tufted_puffin), [Steller sea lion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steller_sea_lion), [spotted seal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spotted_seal), [ringed seal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ringed_seal), [northern fur seal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_fur_seal) and many more.[^cite_note-oceana.org-20]

## Territories

### American Samoa



The [blue-crowned lorikeet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue-crowned_lorikeet) is a parrot found throughout the Samoan islands.

Because of its remote location, diversity among the terrestrial species is low. The archipelago has a huge variety in animals and more than 9,000 acres is a national park: [National Park of American Samoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Park_of_American_Samoa). The park stretches over three of the six islands in the archipelago: [Tutuila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutuila), [Ofu-Olosega](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ofu-Olosega) and [Ta'ū](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ta'%C5%AB). Eight mammal species have been recorded at American Samoa, of which none of them are critically endangered.[^cite_note-21]

The mammals include three species of native bats, namely the [Samoa flying fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samoa_flying_fox), the [insular flying fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insular_flying_fox) and the [Pacific sheath-tailed bat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_sheath-tailed_bat). The avifauna includes 65 species of bird[^cite_note-22] where the more unusual distinctive ones are the [blue-crowned lorikeet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue-crowned_lorikeet), the [spotless crake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spotless_crake), the [many-colored fruit dove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Many-colored_fruit_dove), the [wattled honeyeater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wattled_honeyeater), tropical pigeons, the [samoan starling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samoan_starling), [white tern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_tern), [black noddy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_noddy) and the [red-tailed tropicbird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red-tailed_tropicbird).[^cite_note-23]

There are many reptiles in the islands, including five species of geckos, eight species of skinks and two species of snakes: the [Pacific boa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_boa) and the [Australoasian blindsnake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australoasian_blindsnake?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-autogenerated1-24] The marine life is magnificent and much concentrated around the colorful coral reefs. The Samoan ocean is a home to sea turtles as [hawksbill sea turtle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawksbill_sea_turtle), [olive ridley sea turtle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olive_ridley_sea_turtle), [leatherback sea turtle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leatherback_sea_turtle) and the [green sea turtle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_sea_turtle). Five species of dolphins live in the area: [spinner dolphin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinner_dolphin), [rough-toothed dolphin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rough-toothed_dolphin), [bottlenose dolphin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bottlenose_dolphin), [pantropical spotted dolphin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pantropical_spotted_dolphin) and [striped dolphin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Striped_dolphin).[^cite_note-autogenerated1-24]

### Guam

Shortly after World War II, the [brown tree snake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_tree_snake) was introduced to the island of Guam and caused much of the endemic wildlife to become extinct. Due to an abundance of prey species and lack of predators, the brown tree snake's population exploded and reached nearly 13,000 snakes per square mile at most.[^cite_note-coris.noaa.gov-25] Ten out of twelve endemic bird species, ten lizards and two bats all became extinct as a result of the introduction of the brown tree snake. In recent years, a lot has been done by the U.S. government to decrease the number of brown tree snakes on the island. For instance in 2013, a \$1 million program by the [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Fish_and_Wildlife_Service) dropped more than 2000 mice filled with poison on the island.[^cite_note-edition.cnn.com-26] In 2013, more than two million brown tree snakes were estimated to be on the island.[^cite_note-edition.cnn.com-26] Other introduced species include the [Philippine deer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_deer), the [Asiatic water buffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asiatic_water_buffalo), the [marine toad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marine_toad) and the [giant African land snail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_African_land_snail).[^cite_note-coris.noaa.gov-25] Several native species of skinks, geckos and a monitor lizard are still found on the island.

### Northern Mariana Islands

The Commonwealth of Northern Mariana Islands is home to 40 indigenous and introduced bird species. Some endemic bird species are the [Mariana fruit dove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariana_fruit_dove), the [Mariana swiftlet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariana_swiftlet), the [Rota white-eye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rota_white-eye), the [Tinian monarch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tinian_monarch), the [bridled white-eye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridled_white-eye) and the [golden white-eye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_white-eye). Other common, but introduced species, include the [collared kingfisher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collared_kingfisher), the [rufous fantail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rufous_fantail), the [fairy tern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairy_tern) and the [uniform swiftlet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_swiftlet). The [Mariana fruit bat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariana_fruit_bat) is endemic to both Guam and the Northern Mariana Islands. The [sambar deer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sambar_deer) is the largest mammal and lives on several of the islands. The [Mariana monitor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariana_monitor), ranging up to 3 feet long, is also present on the island of Rota. The oceans are home to more than a thousand species of marine life,[^cite_note-27] including for instance the [coconut crabs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coconut_crabs), the [mahi-mahi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahi-mahi), the [barracuda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barracuda), [tridacna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tridacna), [marlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marlin) and [tuna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuna).

### Puerto Rico

Main article: [Fauna of Puerto Rico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fauna_of_Puerto_Rico)



The [Mona ground iguana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mona_ground_iguana) is the largest native terrestrial lizard in Puerto Rico and is an endangered species.

Puerto Rico has 349 bird species, 83 mammals, 25 amphibians, 61 reptiles and 677 species of fish. Birds found nowhere else on earth include for instance the [Puerto Rican owl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rican_owl), the [Puerto Rican woodpecker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rican_woodpecker), the [Puerto Rican tody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rican_tody), the [green mango](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_mango), the [Puerto Rican emerald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rican_emerald), the [Puerto Rican lizard cuckoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rican_lizard_cuckoo), the [Puerto Rican nightjar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rican_nightjar) and many more. All current endemic 13 land mammals are bats, which includes for instance the [greater bulldog bat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_bulldog_bat), the [Antillean ghost-faced bat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antillean_ghost-faced_bat) and the [Parnell's mustached bat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parnell's_mustached_bat). Extinct native mammals include the [plate-toothed giant hutia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plate-toothed_giant_hutia) and the [Puerto Rican cave rat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rican_cave_rat). Reptiles unique to Puerto Rico include the [Puerto Rican boa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rican_boa), the guanica blindsnake, the [Mona Island iguana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mona_Island_iguana), the [Puerto Rican worm lizard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rican_worm_lizard), the Puerto Rican [galliwasp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galliwasp) and the Nichols' dwarf gecko. Amphibians native to the island include the [Puerto Rican crested toad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Rican_crested_toad), the [common coqui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_coqui), the [locust coqui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locust_coqui), the wrinkled coqui, the forest coqui, the [elfin coqui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elfin_coqui) and the [bronze coqui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronze_coqui). Endemic fish include the Puerto Rican snake eel and the Puerto Rico coralbrotula.[^cite_note-28]

### Virgin Islands

Main article: [Fauna of the United States Virgin Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fauna_of_the_United_States_Virgin_Islands)

The [Virgin Islands National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virgin_Islands_National_Park) covers approximately 60% of the Island of [St. John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._John,_United_States_Virgin_Islands) and nearly all of [Hassel Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hassel_Island,_U.S._Virgin_Islands). The national park has more than 140 species of birds, 302 species of fish, 7 species of amphibians and 22 species of mammals.[^cite_note-29] The tropical Virgin Islands are home to a huge variety of wildlife, including many unique species [endemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endemic) to the [archipelago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islands_of_the_United_States_Virgin_Islands). There are three species of sea turtles in the USVI that inhabit the local waters and utilize beaches for nesting: the green sea turtle, the hawksbill sea turtle and the leatherback sea turtle.[^cite_note-30] Several species of sharks, manatees and dolphins roam the seas.
