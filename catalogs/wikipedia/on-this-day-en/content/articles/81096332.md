**Yashar** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): יָשָׁר, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'straight' or 'truthful' or 'honest'), stylized as **Yashar! with Eisenkot** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): יָשָׁר! עִם אַיְזֶנְקוֹט), is a centrist [political party in Israel](240166.md) led by [Gadi Eisenkot](5249926.md). Founded by Eisenkot in September 2025, the former [Chief of Staff](3011266.md) of the [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md) and [National Unity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Unity_\(Israel\)) [Knesset member](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_Knesset), the party will contest the [2026 Israeli legislative election](71244652.md) on 27 October. Emerging as a major challenger to Prime Minister [Benjamin Netanyahu](56472.md)'s [Likud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Likud), the party has risen in opinion polls, positioning Eisenkot as a leading contender for the premiership.

## Background and founding

In June 2025, [Gadi Eisenkot](5249926.md) announced his intention to resign from [National Unity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Unity_\(Israel\)) and the [Knesset](74656.md). He criticized the party as being dominated by leader [Benny Gantz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benny_Gantz).[^cite_note-5] Eisenkot officially established Yashar in September 2025 to "repair, heal, and give hope" while prioritizing "security and national interests above all."[^cite_note-16septhaaretz-6] He expressed openness to merging with other factions to maintain Israel as "a Jewish, democratic and strong state".[^cite_note-tjp16sept-7]

According to MK [Matan Kahana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matan_Kahana), who also joined the party, the party's name, meaning "straight", "honest", or "truthful", was inspired by an interview with former [Gaza War hostage](75025451.md) [Eli Sharabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eli_Sharabi), who remarked that securing the return of captives was "not about right or left, but straight".[^cite_note-toi16sept-8] The party's formation was welcomed by opposition leaders [Yair Lapid](2448072.md) of [Yesh Atid](35515608.md) and [Yair Golan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yair_Golan) of [The Democrats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Democrats_\(Israel\)).[^cite_note-16septhaaretz-6]

## 2026 election campaign

Main article: [2026 Israeli legislative election](71244652.md)

Eisenkot, who is viewed as the most likely candidate to replace [Benjamin Netanyahu](56472.md) as prime minister, outlined three conditions in July 2026 for building a governing coalition with other parties, namely to "recognize Israel as a [Jewish and democratic state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_and_democratic_state), endorse the values of the [Declaration of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Declaration_of_Independence) and commit to all Israelis performing [national service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_service)".[^cite_note-9] Ilan Mans, a party member, was assaulted as he was trying to remove a banner that called for the release of [Yigal Amir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yigal_Amir), the killer of Israeli prime minister [Yitzhak Rabin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yitzhak_Rabin).[^cite_note-10]

Eisenkot has made preparations to form a [minority government](271434.md) after the election.[^cite_note-11] On 14 August, [Haaretz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haaretz) published an article reporting that he was willing to form a minority government if Zionist opposition parties fail to achieve the 61 seats needed for a [Knesset](74656.md) majority.[^cite_note-12] In response, [Yisrael Beiteinu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yisrael_Beiteinu) leader [Avigdor Lieberman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avigdor_Lieberman) rejected this idea, stating that his party will not be part of a minority government.[^cite_note-13]

Eisenkot has made outreach to [Shas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shas) supporters, including asking Shas member Tzuriel Krispel to join the Yashar slate, though he declined. Shas expressed concern that Eisenkot has the potential to attract its traditional [Sephardi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sephardic_Jews) voters towards Yashar.[^cite_note-14]

## Leadership and prominent members

Yashar was launched with a core group of founding figures from civic, security, and political backgrounds, including Shir Siegel (daughter of former Gaza hostages Keith and Aviva Siegel), former Netanyahu chief of staff [Yoav Horowitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoav_Horowitz?action=edit\&redlink=1), and former MKs [Matan Kahana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matan_Kahana) (National Unity), [Tal Russo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tal_Russo), and [Manuel Trajtenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_Trajtenberg) ([Labor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Labor_Party)). Other founding members include [Inbar Harush Gity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inbar_Harush_Gity), [Nir Zohar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nir_Zohar) of [Wix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wix.com), former general [Yishai Beer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yishai_Beer), and film producer [Yariv Mozer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yariv_Mozer).[^cite_note-16septynet3-15][^cite_note-16]

Ahead of the 2026 election, the party expanded its roster with several high-profile additions, with various politicians, including former MK [Orit Farkash-Hacohen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orit_Farkash-Hacohen) ([Blue and White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_and_White_\(political_party\))),[^cite_note-toi30april-17] and Ramat Gan Deputy Mayor [Israel Zari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Zari?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-18] The list also includes government officials, such as former Ministry of Finance budget chief [Shaul Meridor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaul_Meridor)[^cite_note-19] and former [Welfare Ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Welfare_and_Social_Affairs) chief of staff [Inbar Yehezkeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inbar_Yehezkeli?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-toi30april-17] Others include those in the security sector, such as former [Shin Bet](311752.md) chief [Yoram Cohen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoram_Cohen)[^cite_note-20] and former [COGAT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinator_of_Government_Activities_in_the_Territories) chief [Kamil Abu Rokon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamil_Abu_Rokon).[^cite_note-21] Former [Yesh Atid](35515608.md) MK [Elazar Stern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elazar_Stern) and former [Kulanu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kulanu) MK [Roy Folkman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roy_Folkman) joined the political party on 24 August.[^cite_note-22]

[Adi Altschuler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adi_Altschuler) joined the party in early September and was designated the party's [education minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education_\(Israel\)) if it comes to power.[^cite_note-23]

Shirel Hogeg, the CEO of AM\:PM, was added to the slate on 10 August.[^cite_note-24] Hogeg subsequently decided against becoming a candidate on the party's list.[^cite_note-25]

Yashar's party platform has 10 steps for working towards Israel's prosperity, listed in the party website as follows:[^cite_note-yashar-26]

1. **State commission for 7 October** – an inquiry to investigate the [October 7 attacks](74998317.md) and assist in the rehabilitation of victims

2. **National security** – establish a systemic national strategy for Israeli security through strengthening the [IDF](39237.md), fortifying [borders](12999306.md), and [expanding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab-Israeli_normalization) the [circle of peace](32359767.md), to ensure a Jewish majority

3. **Military service** – Integration of [Haredi](82822441.md) and [Arab Israelis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_Israeli) into [Israeli military service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conscription_in_Israel), to ensure an equality in burden

4. **Education** – establish a core education curriculum for all Israelis in the spirit of the [Declaration of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Declaration_of_Independence), and also to decentralize powers and improve technological studies

5. **Law enforcement** – restore security on the streets on Israel by tackling [crime](15556664.md), especially in [Arab communities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_localities_in_Israel)

6. **Cost of living** – integrate all of Israeli society into the labor and service markets, provide state incentives for those who bear security and economic burden, along with [public investment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_investment) into transportation, education, health care, and the periphery

7. **Democracy and public service** – protect [minority rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minority_rights), human rights, and the [rule of law](25166191.md), maintain legislative and [judicial independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judicial_independence), enact [term limits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Term_limits) for prime minister, and keep public service professional, for the benefit of Israeli citizens

8. **Social polarization** – reduce social polarization to renew [public trust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_trust), preserve Israel as a [Jewish and democratic state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_and_democratic_state), promote inclusive and respectful Judaism, so that all citizens can maintain their faith and lifestyle, expand independence of local authorities, provide a high [quality of life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quality_of_life) through improved transportation, health, education, and cultural services

9. **Jewish immigration** – prevent [brain drain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brain_drain) by strengthening [aliyah](351513.md), with the goal of 1 million immigrants in 10 years, and another million by 2048

10. **Centennial of State of Israel** – establish a strong planning authority that will make Israel a prosperous model state based on partnership, brotherhood, and mutual responsibility by the country's [centennial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centennial) in 2048

Current Yashar Party leader Gadi Eisenkot has also committed to preserve and protect Israel's [Law of Return](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_of_Return), including its Grandchild Clause, which allows anyone with a single Jewish grandparent to [immigrate to Israel](351513.md) and to seek an Israeli safe haven from [anti-Semitism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Semitism) abroad.[^cite_note-27]
