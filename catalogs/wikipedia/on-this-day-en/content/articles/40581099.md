The **Ministry of Justice and Public Security** ([Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language): *Ministério da Justiça e Segurança Pública*), previously known as **Ministry of Justice** ([Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language): *Ministério da Justiça*) and **Ministry of Justice and Citizenship** ([Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language): *Ministério da Justiça e Cidadania*), is a [cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Brazil)-level [federal](33367309.md) [ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_federal_institutions_of_Brazil) in [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federative_Republic_of_Brazil). The current minister is [Wellington Lima e Silva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wellington_Lima_e_Silva).[^cite_note-2]

## Divisions and programs

- The [Brazilian National Archives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_National_Archives).

- The [Administrative Council for Economic Defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_Council_for_Economic_Defense) (CADE), which regulates economic power and its abuse.

- The [Brazilian advisory rating system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_advisory_rating_system) (ClassInd), which establishes the ratings for movies, TV shows, and video games within Brazil.

- The [Federal Police of Brazil](2361410.md), which provides law enforcement of federal laws, acting as a Federal Judicial Police. It has juristicion in interstate and/or international cases, acting in cooperation with a [US](3434750.md) [federal](195149.md) [law enforcement agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement_agency), for example: the [FBI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FBI) and the [DEA](146720.md).

- The [Federal Highway Police](14512223.md) (DPRF), which provides the law enforcement of the federal highways.

- The [Federal Railroad Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Railroad_Police) (PFF), which provides the law enforcement of the federal railroads.

- The National Consumer Secretariat (Secretaria Nacional do Consumidor; SENACON), with the mission of protecting consumer rights and promoting consumer relations
