**Shiroro** is a [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Areas_of_Nigeria) in [Niger State](1723431.md), [Nigeria](21383.md). Its headquarters is in the town of Kuta. It has an area of 5,015 square kilometres (1,936 sq mi) and a population of 235,404 at the 2006 census. The [postal code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_code) of the area is 921.[^cite_note-1]

## Climate condition

Shiroro, Nigeria, experiences scorching [temperatures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperature) throughout the year. In January, the average high is 37.8 °C (100.0 °F), remaining [consistent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consistency) with December. February brings similar [heat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heat), with an average high of 39.8 °C (103.6 °F). March is the hottest of all, with temperatures ranging from 24.7 to 40.8 °C (76.5 to 105.4 °F). April remains sweltering, with an average high of 39.3 °C (102.7 °F). May sees a slight drop to 36.3 °C (97.3 °F). June maintains the heat, averaging 33.4 °C (92.1 °F). July remains hot, starting at 30.5 °C (86.9 °F). August reaches the annual high at 29 °C (84 °F). September maintains the tropical heat at 30.6 °C (87.1 °F). October stays warm, starting at 33.5 °C (92.3 °F). November is very hot, with an average high of 36.4 °C (97.5 °F).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]
