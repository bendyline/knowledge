# Pump boat



A pump boat used by the [Philippine Coast Guard](1954144.md) Auxiliary in [Iloilo City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iloilo_City)



A pump boat propeller and rudder

A **pump boat** (usually [variation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Language_change) as ***pambot*** in local languages) is an [outrigger canoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outrigger_canoe) (*_bangka 'boat'_*) native to Southeast Asia powered by a small gasoline or diesel engine. Smaller pump boats might be powered by the sort of small single-cylinder engine used to drive a water pump. Larger ones are often powered by recycled automobile engines.[^cite_note-spoehr-1]

Pump boats are utility boats in the Philippines, used for nearly everything from inter-island transportation to fishing and even the [Philippine Coast Guard](1954144.md).[^cite_note-bb-2] Pump boats are also used by [Sama-Bajau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sama-Bajau) migrants and refugees in [Sabah, Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabah,_Malaysia) and eastern [Indonesia](14579.md) (where it is known as *pombot*).[^cite_note-cliff-3]
