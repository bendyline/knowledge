# 2027 Women's Ice Hockey World Championships

| Tournament details |  |
| --- | --- |
| Host country | [Canada](5042916.md) |
|  |  |

The **2027 Women's World Ice Hockey Championships** will be the 30th such event hosted by the [International Ice Hockey Federation](16760449.md). Teams participate at several levels of competition based on their [2026 results](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Women's_Ice_Hockey_World_Championships). The competition will also serve as qualifications for division placements in the [2028 edition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2028_Women's_Ice_Hockey_World_Championships?action=edit\&redlink=1). It is also marks a change in the IIHF women's calendar, with the tournaments being moved from April to November.[^cite_note-1]

As in [2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Women's_Ice_Hockey_World_Championships), Russia will not be allowed to enter, as they remain suspended by the IIHF due to the countries' [invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine).[^cite_note-r699-2] Belarus was allowed to re-enter.[^cite_note-3]

All lower divisions will play their tournament with two groups, a knockout stage and the winner of the final to be promoted. The last-placed teams of each group will play a two-game series to determine the relegated team.[^cite_note-4]
