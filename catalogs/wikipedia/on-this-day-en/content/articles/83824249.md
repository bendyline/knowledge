***Woman Unknown*** ([Danish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish_language): ***Kvinde ukendt***) is a 2026 [drama film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drama_film) directed by [May el-Toukhy](62654474.md), co-written with [Maren Louise Käehne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maren_Louise_K%C3%A4ehne). A co-production of Denmark, Sweden, and Latvia, it stars [Mathilde Arcel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathilde_Arcel) as Marie, a young nanny and housemaid harbouring a secret shortly after [Nazi occupation of Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_occupation_of_Denmark).

The film had its world premiere in the main competition of the [83rd Venice International Film Festival](83023289.md) on 6 September 2026, where it won the [Golden Lion](2386128.md) and Arcel won the [Volpi Cup for Best Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volpi_Cup_for_Best_Actress).[^cite_note-euronews-1] It will be theatrically released in Denmark on 4 February 2027.

It was also selected as the [Danish entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Danish_submissions_for_the_Academy_Award_for_Best_International_Feature_Film) for [Best International Feature Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_International_Feature_Film) at the [99th Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/99th_Academy_Awards).[^cite_note-2]

## Premise

Set in the [aftermath of World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aftermath_of_World_War_II), a young nanny prepares to marry her wealthy employer while keeping a secret.[^cite_note-3]

## Production

In May 2024, it was reported that [May el-Toukhy](62654474.md) would direct her third feature film titled *Woman, Unknown* from a screenplay she wrote with [Maren Louise Käehne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maren_Louise_K%C3%A4ehne).[^cite_note-4] In November 2024, the project received a €349,000 production grant from [Eurimages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurimages).[^cite_note-5] In January 2026, it was presented at the Works in Progress section of the Nordic Film Market, held during the [Gothenburg Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothenburg_Film_Festival).[^cite_note-6]

## Release



Marina Bouras, [Carsten Bjørnlund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carsten_Bj%C3%B8rnlund), [May el-Toukhy](62654474.md) and Mathilde Arcel Fock at the [83rd Venice International Film Festival](83023289.md)

*Woman Unknown* had its world premiere at the [83rd Venice International Film Festival](83023289.md) on 6 September 2026, where it won the [Golden Lion](2386128.md).[^cite_note-7] It was the only film directed by a woman selected to compete in the main competition.[^cite_note-8] The film will have its North American Premiere in Centrepiece at the [2026 Toronto International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Toronto_International_Film_Festival) on 15 September 2026.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] In October 2026, it will compete at the [2026 BFI London Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_BFI_London_Film_Festival) in official competition.[^cite_note-gen\:sd-11]

In August 2025, TrustNordisk acquired the film's international sales rights.[^cite_note-12] The film will have its theatrical release in Denmark on 4 February 2027.[^cite_note-13]

## Reception

### Critical response

On [review aggregator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_aggregator) [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), the film holds an approval rating of 94% based on 16 reviews, with an average rating of 8.3/10.[^cite_note-Rotten_Tomatoes-14] [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic), which uses a [weighted average](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weighted_arithmetic_mean), assigned the film a score of 84 out of 100, based on 12 critics, indicating "universal acclaim".[^cite_note-Metacritic-15]

[Peter Bradshaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Bradshaw) of *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)* rated the film 4 out of 5 stars, deeming it to be "a very watchable, expertly managed drama about the return of the repressed".[^cite_note-16] Richard Lawson of [*The Hollywood Reporter*](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hollywood_Reporter) described the film as a "a gloomy rummaging through the grand mess of a terrible war" in which the audiance could " see some relevance to today, as institutions yet again make concessions to sinister forces striking the poses of purity".[^cite_note-17]
