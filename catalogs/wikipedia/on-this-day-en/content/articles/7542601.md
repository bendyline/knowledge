**St. Mary's Catholic Church** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): كنيسة القديسة مريم الكاثوليكية) is a [parish church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parish_church) of the [Apostolic Vicariate of Southern Arabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apostolic_Vicariate_of_Southern_Arabia) located in [Dubai](211583.md), [United Arab Emirates](69328.md). As one of only two Catholic churches in Dubai, St. Mary's has grown to become the largest [Catholic parish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parish_\(Catholic_Church\)) in the world thanks to immigration from Catholic communities abroad.[^cite_note-1]

## History

Initially, Catholics in the United Arab Emirates were served by a priest based in [Bahrain](18933277.md), but eventually there was need for a resident priest. Prime Minister [Rashid bin Saeed Al Maktoum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rashid_bin_Saeed_Al_Maktoum) initially granted a plot of land but it was unusable due to its remoteness. Fr Eusebio Daveri then obtained a more accessible plot of land in 1965 and began construction of a church. On 7 April 1967, it was solemnly blessed by the Vicar Apostolic of Southern Arabia [Irzio Luigi Magliacani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irzio_Luigi_Magliacani), [OFM Cap.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Friars_Minor_Capuchin), and dedicated as the Church of the Assumption.[^cite_note-St._Mary's_Church,_Dubai-2]

However, as the number of faithful grew, there was need for a larger church. In 1988, the Church of the Assumption was demolished and replaced with St. Mary's Catholic Church. It was dedicated on 3 November 1989 by Cardinal [Jozef Tomko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jozef_Tomko), [Prefect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefect) of the [Congregation for the Evangelisation of Peoples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congregation_for_the_Evangelisation_of_Peoples).[^cite_note-St._Mary's_Church,_Dubai-2]

Today St. Mary's Catholic Church has over 350,000 parishioners, making it among the largest churches in the [Middle East](19323.md). Its members are predominately [Filipino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filipino_people), [South Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Asian_people), and [African](5334607.md), and masses are held throughout the week in [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic), [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language), [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language), [Konkani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konkani_language), [Malayalam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malayalam), [Sinhalese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinhalese_language), [Swahili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swahili_language), [Tamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_language), [Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language), [Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language), and [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu).[^cite_note-3]
