**Az-Zawayda** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الزوايدة), also spelled **Zawaida**, is a [Palestinian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_territories) town in the [Gaza Strip](12047.md), in the [Deir al-Balah Governorate](14704596.md) of the [State of Palestine](241405.md). It is located about three kilometers northeast of the city of [Deir al-Balah](1470463.md) and just west of [Maghazi refugee camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maghazi_\(camp\)). According to the [Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Central_Bureau_of_Statistics) (PCBS) 2017 census, there were 23,841 residents.[^cite_note-PrelimCensus2017-1]

## History

During the [Gaza war](74998519.md), an Israeli attack killed at least 13 people after hitting a house in az-Zawayda.[^cite_note-2] Several weeks later, three Israeli missiles struck a warehouse used as shelter by a displaced family, killing at least 16 people, including six children.[^cite_note-3] According to a neighbor, "They were asleep in their beds, kids and babies, then three missiles targeted their place".[^cite_note-4] One of the killed was a wholesale retailer who worked with the Israeli military to bring food into the Gaza Strip.[^cite_note-5]
