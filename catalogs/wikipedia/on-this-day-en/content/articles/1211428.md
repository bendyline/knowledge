The **government of New South Wales**, also known as the **NSW Government**, is the executive state government of [New South Wales](21654.md), Australia. The government comprises 11 portfolios, led by ministerial departments and supported by several agencies. There are also a number of independent statutory bodies that fall under a portfolio but remain at [arm's length](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arm's_length_principle) for political reasons, such as the [Independent Commission Against Corruption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_Commission_Against_Corruption_\(New_South_Wales\)) and the [Electoral Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_South_Wales_Electoral_Commission). The [Executive Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_Council_of_New_South_Wales), which consists of the [governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_New_South_Wales) and senior ministers, exercises executive authority through the relevant ministerial portfolios.

The current government is held by the [New South Wales Labor Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_South_Wales_Labor_Party), led by Premier [Chris Minns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Minns). Minns succeeded [Dominic Perrottet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominic_Perrottet) of the [Liberal Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_Party_of_Australia_\(New_South_Wales_Division\)) on 28 March 2023 following that year's [state election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_New_South_Wales_state_election).

## Ministries

The following individuals serve as government ministers, appointed by the Governor, on behalf of the Monarch, and at the recommendation of the Premier.[^cite_note-2] The full ministry was announced on 4 April 2023 and was sworn in the following day on 5 April.[^cite_note-MinnsFullMinistry-3][^cite_note-Gazette_2023_2-4] All ministers are members of the ruling Labor Party, while all [shadow ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shadow_cabinet) are members of the [opposition in parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opposition_\(New_South_Wales\)).
