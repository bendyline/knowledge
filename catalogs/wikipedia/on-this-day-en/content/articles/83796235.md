On July 26, 2026, a deadly [shootout](2296086.md) occurred during the [Bite of Seattle](726657.md) festival at [Seattle Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Center) in [Seattle](11388236.md), Washington, United States. Three people were killed and four others were wounded.[^cite_note-\:1-1][^cite_note-\:2-2] The shooting involved an exchange of gunfire between at least two groups. Two suspects have been identified: an unnamed 15-year-old who was arrested at the scene and 19-year-old Junior Cee Niko Semo, who was killed during the gunfight. As of August 2026, at least one additional suspect remains unidentified and at large.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

## Background

[Bite of Seattle](726657.md) is an annual [food festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_festival) held at the [Seattle Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Center). The three-day event typically attracts approximately 350,000 attendees.[^cite_note-5] The event features a variety of food vendors, musical performances, and film screenings.[^cite_note-6]

## Shooting

The shooting occurred shortly before 6 p.m. outside the [Seattle Center Armory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Center_Armory), during the final hours of the three-day festival. A food vendor's [livestream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livestream) captured the beginning of the shooting, in which multiple gunshots can be heard as a crowd of people flees the area.[^cite_note-\:8-7]

A Seattle police sergeant, who was patrolling the area, moved towards the crowd after hearing the gunfire. The sergeant witnessed a 15-year-old suspect repeatedly firing a handgun into a group of people, and arrested him as he tried to flee the scene.[^cite_note-\:6-8] The suspect was later taken to Seattle Police Department headquarters for questioning.[^cite_note-\:2-2]

Junior Cee Niko Semo, 19, was identified as a second suspect; he died from gunshot wounds at the scene. According to police, the 15-year-old allegedly called out, "Love you bro," to Semo while being escorted by officers.[^cite_note-\:6-8]

Seven people sustained gunshot wounds during the exchange of fire.[^cite_note-\:3-9]

At least one additional unknown suspect was thought to have fled the scene. As of August 2026, they are still at large.[^cite_note-10]

## Victims

The incident resulted in three fatalities and four injuries.

**Casualties**[^cite_note-\:7-11]

- Junior Cee Niko Semo, 19 (suspect; died at the scene)

- Carlos Israel Sanchez Villalba, 44 (died at the scene)

- Ashley Whitehead, 56 (died at hospital)

- 2-year-old boy (injured)

- 39-year-old woman (injured)

- 23-year-old man (injured)

- 27-year-old man (injured)

Four of the victims were taken to [Harborview Medical Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harborview_Medical_Center), including Ashley Whitehead, who died from her injuries. Whitehead, a cancer survivor, was struck by a bullet in her [pelvis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pelvis) while waiting in line at a [cotton candy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton_candy) stand. She was survived by her daughter and son-in-law, who were also in the crowd.[^cite_note-\:9-12][^cite_note-\:3-9]

Carlos Israel Sanchez Villalba, a husband and father of three, died at the scene after being shot in the [torso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torso).[^cite_note-\:9-12][^cite_note-13]

In total, four people, including a two-year-old boy, sustained non-fatal gunshot wounds and were eventually discharged from the hospital.[^cite_note-\:5-14]

A 40-year-old woman sustained minor injuries and refused transport to the hospital.[^cite_note-\:7-11]

## Investigation

Three firearms were recovered from the scene: a black [9mm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9_mm_caliber) [Polymer80](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymer80) [ghost gun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homemade_firearm) used by the 15-year-old suspect, a [Glock 45](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glock) 9mm pistol, and an [RO compact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springfield_Armory,_Inc.) 9mm. The ghost gun had an extended magazine capable of holding 33 rounds, with 19 bullets remaining and a vanity base plate reading, "1 SHOT 1 KILL". The Glock Model 45 also had an extended magazine, capable of holding 40 rounds with 21 bullets remaining. 13 fired shell casings were also recovered. The RO compact 9mm was not believed to have been fired. Based on their preliminary findings, police believed that a total of four handguns were involved.[^cite_note-\:6-8][^cite_note-15]

Police continue to search for more suspects and have not released any descriptions as of July 27. Police have stated that they believe there were "at least three shooters".[^cite_note-\:4-16][^cite_note-\:5-14] One of the suspects, a 15-year-old boy, was charged with [first-degree assault](1466.md) and second-degree [unlawful possession of a firearm](27402555.md). According to an autopsy report, the 15-year-old's weapon was not responsible for the deaths of Whitehead or Villalba. The boy was initially charged as a juvenile; however, days after the shooting, prosecutors filed a motion to transfer the case to adult court.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-\:6-8]

[Detectives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detective) from the Seattle Police Department's Homicide Unit and Gun Prevention Unit were assigned to the investigation. Investigators from the [Federal Bureau of Investigation](11127.md) and the [Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bureau_of_Alcohol,_Tobacco,_Firearms_and_Explosives) have also been assigned to the investigation to support local law enforcement.[^cite_note-\:3-9][^cite_note-\:2-2]

As of August 2026, investigators believe the shooting to be [gang](80408.md)-related, and that Junior Cee Niko Semo had exchanged gunfire with the 15-year-old suspect.[^cite_note-\:7-11]

## Response

Police and security officers assigned to the festival heard the gunfire and responded within seconds of the shooting. According to Assistant Seattle Police Chief Daniel Harris, officers witnessed the 15-year-old suspect firing his weapon.[^cite_note-\:2-2] Officers rendered emergency aid to the wounded before [Seattle Fire Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Fire_Department) personnel arrived. Police said that they did not believe there was an ongoing threat to the public after the initial incident.[^cite_note-\:5-14] Washington governor [Bob Ferguson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Ferguson_\(politician\)) deployed members of the [Washington State Patrol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_State_Patrol) [Special Weapons and Tactics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SWAT) team to assist local authorities.[^cite_note-18]

While emergency personnel treated the victims and police secured the area, parts of Seattle Center were evacuated,[^cite_note-19] including from the [Pacific Science Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Science_Center).[^cite_note-\:0-20] Harborview Medical Center was placed on partial lockdown amid heavy police presence in the area.[^cite_note-\:0-20]

Seattle mayor [Katie Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Wilson) initially reported two suspects had been taken into custody, but her office later retracted the statement.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23][^cite_note-\:0-20][^cite_note-\:1-1]

## Aftermath

The Bite of Seattle was cancelled. Vendors reported that their booths had been looted, with equipment and cash stolen, after they were allowed to return to the site.[^cite_note-24] Multiple attractions around the Seattle Center temporarily closed in the aftermath of the shooting, as well as the [Seattle Center Monorail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Center_Monorail).[^cite_note-25]

A [candlelight vigil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candlelight_vigil) was held for the victims of the shooting on the night of July 27. The vigil was held at the Seattle Center with community members gathering to mourn Whitehead and Villalba. Among the attendees were Seattle Mayor Katie Wilson and Seattle Chief of Police Shon Barnes.[^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27]

Katie Wilson, in a press briefing on July 27, described the shooting and its impact on the community and called for a cooperative gun violence strategy.[^cite_note-28] Police chief [Shon Barnes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shon_Barnes) was criticized for his department's slow release of information related to the shooting as well as his own absence from the city to attend a conference. Barnes announced his resignation on July 30 amid a dispute with Wilson.[^cite_note-29]

A memorial honoring the victims was erected at the Seattle Center's [International Fountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Fountain).[^cite_note-\:10-30]

A fundraiser for the families of Whitehead and Villalba raised over \$50,000.[^cite_note-\:10-30]

A recall charge has been filed by a North Seattle couple citing the mayor's response to the shooting on August 11. The charges are being reviewed by the King County Prosecuting Attorney's Office, who will determine if the charges meet the legal standard for [recall](129203.md#United_States).[^cite_note-31]
