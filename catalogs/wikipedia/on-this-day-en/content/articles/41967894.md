**Lubero** is a town in the [North Kivu Province](446958.md) of the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md). It is the administrative center of the [Lubero Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lubero_Territory). Following the surrender of the [Mai-Mai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mai-Mai) fighters in 2021, construction of a new market began in 2022, involving the mayor, ex-soldiers, "young people at risk and the vulnerable women". As of March 2014, the population of Lubero is not publicly known.

## Location

The town is lies at an average altitude of 1,900 metres (6,200 ft), in the [Virunga Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virunga_Mountains), at the western edge of [Virunga National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virunga_National_Park), close to the international border with [Uganda](31816.md). Lubero lies approximately 210 kilometres (130 mi), by road, north of the provincial capital of [Goma](1210483.md).[^cite_note-1] This location is approximately 98 kilometres (61 mi), by road, southwest of [Beni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beni,_North_Kivu), the nearest large town.[^cite_note-2]

## Overview

Lubero is a medium-sized town with several modern amenities including an airport, [Lubero Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lubero_Airport), with a grass runway.[^cite_note-3] In the hills around the town, a mini-hydropower station, *Lubero Hydroelectric Power Station*, is planned to complement [Mutwanga Hydroelectric Power Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutwanga_Hydroelectric_Power_Station) and [Rutshuru Hydroelectric Power Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutshuru_Hydroelectric_Power_Station), in supplying power to the communities in and around [Virunga National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virunga_National_Park).[^cite_note-4]

Like most localities in [North Kivu](446958.md), Lubero has witnessed plenty of violence as the various militias have battled each other for real estate and treasure during the last two decades.[^cite_note-5] Battles have also been fought between the militias, government forces and United Nations "peace keepers". The toll in human lives has been high. Many have been left with physical disabilities. More suffer long-term psychological war trauma.[^cite_note-6]

In 2022 [MONUSCO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MONUSCO) announced that work had begun to build a market here involving ex-[combatants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combatant) as part of their integration into the community. The market was designed to offer protection to vulnerable women.[^cite_note-7]

On August 27, 2022, at least five people were stabbed, and several houses were burned during a [community conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communal_violence), which opposes the inhabitants of the localities of Matulu and Bukununu in the Musindi [Groupement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo) south of Lubero territory in North Kivu.[^cite_note-8]

On September 7, 2022, four people of the same family were reported dead in a house collapse in Lukanga, the capital of the Bukenye Groupement, in the Lubero territory of North Kivu.

On September 19, 2022, two people were killed in the village of [Musenda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musenda?action=edit\&redlink=1), in the chiefdom of Bashaga, in the territory of Lubero, in North Kivu, during an incursion of the Mai-Mai militiamen of the Chandenga group.[^cite_note-9]

In September 2022, ten cases of kidnappings were reported in the locality of [Bunyangingi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bunyangingi?action=edit\&redlink=1), in the Itala Groupement in the Lubero territory, North Kivu.[^cite_note-10]

On September 25, 2022, a young man died after a grenade explosion in a Kipese village in the Bulengya Groupement, Lubero territory, North Kivu.[^cite_note-11]

In October 2022, the [FARDC](8029.md) (*Forces Armées de la République Démocratique du Congo*) killed six militiamen belonging to the Mai-Mai coalition of Mazembe and [Baraka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baraka,_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo). The Congolese army retrieved three of their weapons of war during the fighting in Matembe village in the Lubero territory, North Kivu.[^cite_note-12]

In December 2022, two people died as a result of clashes between the Mai-Mai group of the *Front des Patriotes pour la Paix* (FPP/AP) of Kabido to the unidentified armed group of [Kasinga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasinga?action=edit\&redlink=1) village, in the Ngulo group, Lubero territory, North Kivu.[^cite_note-13]

In January and February 2023, at least 23 civilians were killed, more were reported missing, and thousands more were displaced as a result of clashes between Mai-Mai armed groups of the *Front des Patriotes pour la Paix* (FPP/AP) of Kabido, the *Union des Patriotes congolais* (UPC) of Kilalo and the Yira-Force and Mazembe coalition.[^cite_note-14]

In March 2023, nine people were killed and several others went missing following the armed attack carried out by ADF terrorists in the [Nguli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguli?action=edit\&redlink=1) village in the Lubero territory, North Kivu, according to the provincial deputy [Jean-Paul Lumbulumbu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Paul_Lumbulumbu?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] In the same month, the rebels took civilians [hostage](146794.md) in the village of [Katiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katiri?action=edit\&redlink=1) in Isale Kasongwere Groupement, in the [Beni territory](36374742.md), North Kivu.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] Parenthetically, the insurgents also erected a stronghold in the Virunga National Park between the villages of [Kavasewa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kavasewa?action=edit\&redlink=1) and Karuruma.[^cite_note-20]

In January 2026, [ADF](1258240.md) rebels took the lives of 15 people in 3 different villages in the Lubero territory using bladed weapons.[^cite_note-21]
