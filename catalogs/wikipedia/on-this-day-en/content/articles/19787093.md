**Yamuna Expressway** is a 6-[lane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lane) (expandable to 8), 165.5 km (102.8 mi) long [access-controlled expressway in](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Controlled-access_highway) the Indian state of [Uttar Pradesh](231623.md). It is presently India's sixth longest expressway and connects [Greater Noida](4942600.md) with [Agra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agra). It was built to de-congest the older Delhi–Agra national highway [(NH-2)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_2_\(India,_old_numbering\)) or Mathura Road. The [Noida-Greater Noida Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noida%E2%80%93Greater_Noida_Expressway) feeds into the project.

Built by [Jaypee Infratech Ltd (JIL)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaypee_Infratech) on a [BOT (Build-Operate-Transfer)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Build%E2%80%93operate%E2%80%93transfer) basis, the company has the rights to collect for a period of 36 years, starting from its Commercial Operations Date (COD) on August 9, 2012 until the agreement expires, after which the expressway will be transferred to the Yamuna Expressway Industrial Development Authority (YEIDA). The Yamuna Expressway Industrial Development Authority (YEIDA) region is being developed over an area of approximately 25,000 hectares along the Yamuna Expressway in the state of Uttar Pradesh. The region includes the Noida International Airport at Jewar which is under development and is planned to be one of the largest airports in India.[^cite_note-1]

&#x20;\
&#x20;The [Noida International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noida_International_Airport) located in Jewar of the [Gautam Buddha Nagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gautam_Buddha_Nagar_district) District also lies on the side of the expressway, with it acting as the main connection to multiple cities.

The expressway saw an investment [₹](490783.md)12,839 [crore](121420.md) (US\$1.3 billion) and was inaugurated on 9 August 2012 by then Chief Minister of UP [Akhilesh Yadav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akhilesh_Yadav).[^cite_note-indiatimes1-2][^cite_note-3] A total of 13 service roads of about 168 [km](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Km) were built for local commuters to access the expressway.[^cite_note-4]

## History

After years of delays, construction was completed in May 2012,[^cite_note-5] and the expressway was formally inaugurated on 9 August 2012,[^cite_note-indiatimes1-2] about two years behind its original target completion date.[^cite_note-6]

## Yamuna Emergency Landing Field



IAF Mirage 2000 landing on the Yamuna Expressway

In a first for military aviation in India, the Indian Air Force on 21 May 2015 successfully landed a French [Dassault Mirage 2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dassault_Mirage_2000) on the Yamuna Expressway near Raya village, Mathura. The mock drill was a practice run to evaluate the utility of highways for emergency landings by military aircraft.[^cite_note-7]

## Characteristics

The Yamuna Expressway has SOS booths along the route besides a toll-free helpline. [CCTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CCTV) cameras are installed every 5 km (3.1 mi) along the expressway. There are mobile radars to monitor compliance with minimum and maximum speed limits; and one highway patrol every 25 km (16 mi). The Expressway is expected to be used by over 100,000 vehicles every day, and reduce travel times between Greater Noida and Agra from four hours to one hour 40 minutes.[^cite_note-8] There is a patrol station at Tappal, the halfway point on the expressway. Tappal 60 km (37 mi) from Noida Sec. 37 i.e. botanical garden.[^cite_note-9] In 2025, the Yamuna Expressway successfully implemented all road safety measures recommended by an expert team from [IIT Delhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IIT_Delhi).[^cite_note-10]

### Metro Rail connectivity

Metro rail service from Pari Chowk to Yamuna Expressway Sector 18 and 20 will be added in the future, according to officials.&#x20;

## Development



Yamuna Expressway within the state of Uttar Pradesh



A toll plaza on Yamuna Expressway

The Expressway was developed by Yamuna Expressway Industrial Development Authority (YEIDA) in 3 phases and the contract was awarded to [Jaypee Infratech Limited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaypee_Group):

- Phase I: Expressway Stretch between [Greater Noida](4942600.md) & proposed Taj International Aviation Hub (near village Jewar on the southern tip of Dist. Gautam Budh Nagar).

- Phase II: Expressway Stretch between proposed Taj International Aviation Hub and an intermediate destination between proposed Taj International Aviation Hub & [Agra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agra).

- Phase III: Expressway Stretch between intermediate destination & [Agra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agra).

**Speed Limit** The speed limit on the expressway is 100 km/h (62 mph) for vehicles. The right most lane on the expressway is reserved for overtaking only.[^cite_note-11] Heavy vehicles are limited to a maximum speed of 60 kilometers per hour.[^cite_note-\:0-12]

**Upcoming Residential Project** Yamuna Expressway has become preferred for builders and developers. Many builders are bringing residential projects to Yamuna Expressway. To inject new life into the real estate market, Yamuna Expressway Industrial Development Authority has come up with affordable housing projects.[^cite_note-13] Under the new subvention scheme YEIDA has launched 200 small ticket size apartments and 80 large size residential flats.

**Agra–Lucknow connectivity** A 302 km (188 mi) [Agra–Lucknow Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agra%E2%80%93Lucknow_Expressway) Project is completed with estimated budget of ₹ 15,000 crore extends YEA to [Lucknow](392095.md) enabling fast transit. Agra–Lucknow Expressway will extend to the proposed Sanjay Khan project of a theme park of seven cities. Yamuna Expressway linked to Agra-Lucknow Expressway through Agra Inner Ring Road Expressway 11.9 km (7.39 mi) which also shortens the distance and avoid the heavy traffic of Agra, tourists can directly reach Taj Mahal. In March 2025, the Indian government approved the project to connect the Yamuna Expressway with the Delhi-Agra Highway.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15]

## Goals

The goals of the expressway are as follows:

1. To establish a high-speed corridor for efficient travel time reduction.

2. To link key [built-up area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Built-up_area_\(Highway_Code\)) and commercial zones situated on the eastern side of the Yamuna River.

3. To foster development in the surrounding areas.

4. To alleviate [traffic congestion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traffic_congestion) on NH-2, which passes through densely populated cities like **[Faridabad](1180794.md)**, Ballabgarh, and [Palwal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palwal).

5. To, combined with NH-2 and NH-91, create an extensive road network, promoting comprehensive development.

6. Facilitate swift transportation of essential supplies and aid to affected regions during emergencies such as flooding along the Yamuna River.

## Features

The Yamuna Expressway is one of India's lengthiest access-controlled [concrete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete) [expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Controlled-access_highway).

Emergency medical services, including ambulances and hospital tie-ups, are available for accidents and medical emergencies.

The expressway is equipped with [closed-circuit television](87179.md) surveillance, which is used to monitor vehicle's speeds.

Authorities plan to install [fuelling stations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuelling_station), food courts, ATMs, and public restrooms along the expressway.[^cite_note-\:0-12]

## Toll rates

Tolls are charged at three points along the expressway. Cars are charged ₹2.65 /km (1.65 /mi) and mini-buses ₹3.85 /km (2.39 /mi). Buses and Trucks are charged ₹7.85 /km (4.88 /mi). Heavy vehicles are charged ₹11.94 /km (7.419 /mi).[^cite_note-17]

There are 16 [toll](63993672.md) plazas on each side of the [expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Controlled-access_highway).[^cite_note-\:0-12]
