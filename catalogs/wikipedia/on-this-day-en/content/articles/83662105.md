The **2026 [Herculis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herculis)** was the 40th edition of the annual outdoor track and field meeting in Monaco. Held on 10 July at [Stade Louis II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stade_Louis_II), it was the tenth leg of the [2026 Diamond League](81417246.md) – the highest level international track and field circuit.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]
