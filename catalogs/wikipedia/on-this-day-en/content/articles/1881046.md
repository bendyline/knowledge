This article lists the [heads of government](37570.md) of [Sudan](27421.md) since the establishment of the office of Chief Minister of the [Anglo-Egyptian Sudan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Egyptian_Sudan) in 1952.

The office of prime minister was abolished after the [1989 coup d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_Sudanese_coup_d'%C3%A9tat),[^cite_note-1] and reestablished in 2017 as deputy head of government when [Bakri Hassan Saleh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakri_Hassan_Saleh) was appointed prime minister by [President](450116.md) [Omar al-Bashir](393625.md).[^cite_note-2]

The current prime minister is [Kamil Idris](5065735.md). He was appointed by the [Transitional Sovereignty Council](61549039.md) on 31 May 2025.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

## Titles of heads of government

- **1952–1956**: Chief Minister

- **1956–1989; 2019–present**: Prime Minister
