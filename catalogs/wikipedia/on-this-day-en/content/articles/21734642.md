[Canada](5042916.md) and the [Philippines](23440.md) have maintained [bilateral relations](429154.md) since 1949.[^cite_note-\:1-1]

## Economic relations



Canadian prime minister [Mark Carney](13571049.md) and Philippine president [Bongbong Marcos](572460.md) at a [Vancouver](32706.md) branch of the Filipino-based restaurant chain [Jollibee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jollibee) in 2026

In 2012, Canadian exports to the Philippines were (CAD) \$527.9 million, while exports to Canada from the Philippines were valued at \$991.2 million.[^cite_note-2] By 2024, exports of goods to the Philippines had increased to \$1.4 billion, and exports of goods to Canada had increased to \$1.8 billion.[^cite_note-\:1-1] The [Canada–Philippines waste dispute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada%E2%80%93Philippines_waste_dispute) was a point of trade contention between the two nations from 2013 until the return of the waste in 2019.[^cite_note-2019-05-08_NYT-3][^cite_note-4] Nonetheless, negotiations for a bilateral [free trade agreement](2533562.md) are expected to conclude in 2026.[^cite_note-Firstpost-2026-5]

## Political relations



Philippine president [Benigno Aquino III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benigno_Aquino_III) during his state visit to Canada in 2015

[Canadian prime minister](24135.md) [Jean Chrétien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Chr%C3%A9tien), together with a business delegate, visited the Philippines in 1997. Canadian prime minister [Stephen Harper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Harper) also paid a [state visit](327283.md) to the Philippines on November 9–11, 2012.[^cite_note-inquirer1-6] [Philippine president](271126.md) [Benigno Aquino III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benigno_Aquino_III) paid a state visit to Canada on May 7–9, 2015.[^cite_note-7] Philippine president [Bongbong Marcos](572460.md) paid an official visit to [Vancouver](32706.md), Canada, on July 1–4, 2026.[^cite_note-Firstpost-2026-5]

Canadian prime ministers and Philippine presidents have conducted bilateral meetings at [ASEAN Summits](3350351.md), since Canada has been a dialogue partner of ASEAN since 1977.[^cite_note-8] Former Canadian prime minister [Justin Trudeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Trudeau) met with former Philippine president [Rodrigo Duterte](7407293.md) at the 2017 summit in Manila,[^cite_note-9] and with current president Bongbong Marcos at the 2022-24 summits.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] Current Canadian prime minister [Mark Carney](13571049.md) also met with Marcos at the 2025 summit.[^cite_note-13]

## Military relations

In March 2025, the Philippines and Canada announced plans to sign a [Status of Visiting Forces Agreement](10392416.md) (SOVFA) to enhance defence cooperation and interoperability.[^cite_note-14] The SOVFA was signed in November 2025, setting a legal framework for joint [military exercises](1225714.md),[^cite_note-15] and further boosted by a mutual logistics support agreement signed in June 2026.[^cite_note-16]

## Migration and cultural relations

Main articles: [Filipino Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filipino_Canadian) and [Filipino domestic helpers in Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filipino_domestic_helpers_in_Canada)

In 2016, there were more than 850,000 people of Filipino origin in Canada;[^cite_note-inquirer1-6][^cite_note-17] by 2021, this had increased to about 957,000 Filipinos.[^cite_note-\:0-18] Most of this population (71.5%) are immigrants,[^cite_note-\:0-18] and the Philippines was the second most common country of birth for immigrants to Canada, after India.[^cite_note-19] Filipinos are the fourth largest [visible minority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visible_minority) in Canada, behind the [South Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Asian_Canadians), [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Canadians), and [Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Canadians) populations.[^cite_note-20] In 2013, the Philippines had become the largest source of temporary workers.[^cite_note-21] From 2006 to 2011, [Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language) was the fastest-growing language in Canada.[^cite_note-huffingtonpost1-22][^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24] [Filipino Heritage Month](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filipino_Heritage_Month) is commemorated every June.[^cite_note-25]

In 2013, there were about 7,500 Canadians in the Philippines.[^cite_note-canadainternational1-26] By 2020, there were 861 Canadian foreign citizens and 5,299 Filipinos with Canadian [dual citizenship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiple_citizenship) in the country.[^cite_note-27]
