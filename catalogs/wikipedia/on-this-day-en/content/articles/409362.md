This article lists political parties in Nicaragua.

## Political culture

Historically, [Nicaragua](21362.md) had a [two-party system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two-party_system), with varying two dominant [political parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_parties). The [2006 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Nicaraguan_general_election) could have marked the end of the bipartite scheme, as the anti-Sandinista forces split into two major political alliances: the [Nicaraguan Liberal Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicaraguan_Liberal_Alliance) (ALN) and the [Constitutionalist Liberal Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutionalist_Liberal_Party) (PLC).[^cite_note-1]
