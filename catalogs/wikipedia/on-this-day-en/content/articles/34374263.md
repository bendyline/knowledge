The **Sayed Jamaluddin Afghani University** (**SJAU**, [Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto_language): د سید جمال الدین افغاني پوهنتون; [Dari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dari_language): پوهنتون سید جمال الدین) or **Kunar University** is a [public](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_university) [university](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_universities_in_Afghanistan) in [Asadabad](815053.md), which is the [capital](181337.md) of [Kunar Province](2159600.md) in eastern Afghanistan.[^cite_note-auto-1][^cite_note-auto1-2][^cite_note-3] It was established in 2010. The university is named after [Sayed Jamaluddin Afghani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamal_al-Din_al-Afghani), the 19th century Islamic ideologist from Asadabad. It is under Afghanistan's [Ministry of Higher Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Higher_Education_\(Afghanistan\)).[^cite_note-auto-1][^cite_note-auto1-2]

The university originally offered admission in three faculties: education, theology and agriculture. Over the years it expanded its operation and is considered a reliable institution in the region offering admissions in five faculties and 21 degree programs, adding Economics and Computer Science and concerned departments to the list.[^cite_note-4]

On 27 April 2026, amid renewed clashes in Kunar during the [2026 Afghanistan–Pakistan war](82482902.md), the university was hit in airstrikes which injured at least 30 students and faculty. The Afghanistan government said that the university was struck by Pakistan. The [UNAMA](1234277.md), [OCHA](1602284.md), Richard Bennet (the [UN Special Rapporteur on Human Rights in Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_special_rapporteur)), and Richard Lindsay ([EU Special Representative for Afghanistan](2899127.md)) condemned the attack on the university saying it had caused several civilian casualties. Pakistan's [Ministry of Information & Broadcasting](32715412.md) denied it had carried out the strikes.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]
