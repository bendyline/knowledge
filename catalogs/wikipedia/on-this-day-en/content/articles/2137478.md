**Tuba**, officially the **Municipality of Tuba**, ([Ilocano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilocano_language): *Ili ti Tuba*; [Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language): *Bayan ng Tuba*), is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality_of_the_Philippines) in the [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Province) of [Benguet](274018.md), [Philippines](23440.md). According to the 2024 census, it has a population of 49,268 people.[^cite_note-7]

Tuba is known as the "Gateway to Baguio", as the [Asin–Nangalisan–San Pascual Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asin_Road), [Ben Palispis Highway or Marcos Highway](43975298.md), [Kennon Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kennon_Road) and [Naguilian Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naguilian_Road), four access highways of the adjacent city of [Baguio](162480.md), traverse the municipality.[^cite_note-SunStar-1][^cite_note-Midland-2]

## History

Tuba was originally a *barrio*[^cite_note-LaUnionGov-8] of the township of Baguio in the early 1900s under the [American colonial period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Philippines_\(1898%E2%80%931946\)).[^cite_note-TubaGov-9] It was separated from Baguio upon the latter's conversion into a [chartered city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chartered_city) on September 1, 1909,[^cite_note-NSCB-CAR-10] and became part of the township of Twin Peaks in Benguet.[^cite_note-LaUnionGov-8]

Twin Peaks was abolished as a township on December 11, 1911, with the issuance of *Executive Order No. 77* by [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_people) Governor General [William Cameron Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Cameron_Forbes), creating the township of Tuba.[^cite_note-LaUnionGov-8][^cite_note-TubaGov-9]

On June 25, 1963, President [Diosdado Macapagal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diosdado_Macapagal) issued *Executive Order No. 42* and by operation of Section 2 of *Republic Act No. 1515*, the municipal District of Tuba was converted into a regular municipality.[^cite_note-EO42-GovPH-11] Its first mayor was Wakat Suello.[^cite_note-12]

## Geography



Camp 6 along [Kennon Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kennon_Road)

The Municipality of Tuba is at the south-western tip of both Benguet and the [Central Cordillera Mountain Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cordillera_Central_\(Luzon\)). South of the municipality lies the province of [Pangasinan](1691800.md) and to the west lies the [Ilocos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilocos) rolling hills of the province of [La Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Union). It is bordered on the north by [Sablan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sablan,_Benguet) and [La Trinidad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Trinidad,_Benguet); east by [Baguio](162480.md) and [Itogon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itogon,_Benguet); and south by [Sison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sison,_Pangasinan) and [San Manuel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Manuel,_Pangasinan).

According to the [Philippine Statistics Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Statistics_Authority), the municipality has a land area of 295.97 square kilometres (114.27 sq mi) [^cite_note-nscb-area-13] constituting 10.69% of the 2,769.08-square-kilometre- (1,069.15 sq mi) total area of Benguet. The municipality's urban area comprises the barangays of Poblacion and Camp 3, having a composite land area of 83.85 square kilometres (32.37 sq mi), or 19.31% of the total land area.

Tuba is situated 12.64 kilometres (7.85 mi) from the provincial capital [La Trinidad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Trinidad,_Benguet), and 254.20 kilometres (157.95 mi) from the country's capital city of [Manila](184334.md).

### Topography

Tuba's topography is generally characterized by irregular rugged terrain and steep slopes with several mountain peaks rising from the table land itself. [Mount Santo Tomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Santo_Tomas), the highest peak in the municipality[^cite_note-14] soars to 2,252 metres (7,388 ft) above sea level.[^cite_note-15]

Four major rivers/streams and 49 tributary/minor rivers and creeks intersect the landform and serve as the drains of the municipality.

Twin peaks of [Mount Cabuyao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Cabuyao) (right, foreground) and the higher [Mount Santo Tomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Santo_Tomas) (left, background)

[Mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mining) is a major part of the economy of Tuba.[^cite_note-MechanicsHub-34] The town's mining industry began upon the discovery of [gold](12240.md) in the province of Benguet during the early 1930s.[^cite_note-DILG-CAR-35] Since 1958, [Philex Mining Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philex_Mining_Corporation?action=edit\&redlink=1), the largest gold and [copper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copper) producer in the Philippines, has been operating the first underground [block cave mine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Block_caving) in the [Far East](11672.md) at Padcal in Barangay Camp 3.[^cite_note-36][^cite_note-37]

[Tailings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tailings) from the copper and gold mines have wreaked havoc on the local environment: the [Bued River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bued_River), which runs through Tuba, is heavily [silted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siltation) and requires frequent dredging.[^cite_note-PIA-38][^cite_note-WorldFish-39]

A few abandoned mining sites such as those of Benguet Exploration Mine and Black Mountain, Inc. have been left unattended for years, which pose a threat to the local population and the environment.[^cite_note-CongressGov-40]

The presence of [waterfalls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterfall), [hot springs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_spring) and natural [caves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cave) in the municipality boosts tourism.[^cite_note-Midland-2][^cite_note-VisitMyPhil-41] [Mount Cabuyao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Cabuyao) and [Mount Santo Tomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Santo_Tomas), the highest mountain in the town, are frequently visited by mountaineers.[^cite_note-SunStar2-42]

[DMCI Homes Leisure Residences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DMCI_Homes) is building a P6 billion "Moncello Crest", a new [condo hotel](3633011.md) [mountain resort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_resort) project with initial 522 units in its 'Blanca' offered, in Tuba in Benguet.[^cite_note-43][^cite_note-44]

## Government

### Local government

Main article: [Sangguniang Bayan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangguniang_Bayan)

Tuba, belonging to the [lone congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benguet's_at-large_congressional_district) of the province of [Benguet](274018.md), is governed by a mayor designated as its local chief executive and by a municipal council as its legislative body in accordance with the Local Government Code. The mayor, vice mayor, and the councilors are elected directly by the people through an election which is being held every three years.
