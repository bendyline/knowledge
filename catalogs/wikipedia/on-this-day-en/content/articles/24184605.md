**Sergei Maratovich Guriev** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Серге́й Мара́тович Гури́ев, [Iron Ossetic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Ossetic_language): Гуыриаты Мараты фырт Сергей, romanized: *Gwyriaty Maraty fyrt Sergej*; born 21 October 1971) is a Russian economist, and the [dean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_\(education\)) and a professor of economics at the [London Business School](520187.md) since August 2024.

Previously at [Sciences Po](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sciences_Po), he was [provost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provost_\(education\)) from 2022 to 2024 and a professor of economics from 2013 to 2024. He was chief economist of the [European Bank for Reconstruction and Development](37963.md) from 2016 to 2019. He was a Morgan Stanley professor of economics and a [rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_\(academia\)) at Moscow's [New Economic School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Economic_School) (NES) from 2004 to 2013, before he resigned and moved to France following a search of his office and the seizure of his past five years of emails by the Russian government.

He is a member of The [Trilateral Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trilateral_Commission).

## Biography

Sergei Guriev was born to an [ethnic Ossetian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ossetians)[^cite_note-Sl-1] family, on 21 October 1971 in [Vladikavkaz](229915.md), [North Ossetia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Ossetia).[^cite_note-2] He received his straight As high school diploma in 1988 from [Kyiv Physics Mathematics High School #145.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyiv_Natural_Science_Lyceum_No._145) In 1993 he graduated [summa cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summa_cum_laude) from [Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Institute_of_Physics_and_Technology) with master's degrees in economics and computer science, and in 1994 received his PhD in applied mathematics from the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^cite_note-3] In 1997–98, he visited the Department of Economics at MIT on a post-doctoral fellowship. In 2002, he received a degree of Doctor of Science in economics (habilitation degree) from the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences). In 2003–2004, he was a visiting assistant professor at the Department of Economics at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University). Guriev was an informal advisor and speechwriter to [Russian president](239646.md) [Dmitry Medvedev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitry_Medvedev), in office from May 2008 to May 2012, during which time he also sat on government advisory boards and on the boards of state enterprises.[^cite_note-4] He has also supported and advised Russian opposition leader [Alexei Navalny](29174356.md), in particular, being one of the founding donors of his [Anti-Corruption Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Corruption_Foundation)[^cite_note-5] in 2012, working[^cite_note-6] for his [2013 Moscow Mayor electoral campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexei_Navalny_2013_mayoral_campaign), and was an informal advisor[^cite_note-7] afterwards.

He joined NES in 1998, become NES’ first tenure-track faculty member in 1999, and become a tenured professor and Rector in 2004. He was also teaching graduate courses in economics of development, [microeconomic theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microeconomic_theory) and [contract theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contract_theory).

### Departure from Russia

&#x20;Guriev left Russia on 30 April 2013 after a “frightening and humiliating interrogation”[^cite_note-8] as government investigators searched his office and secured 5 years of his emails due to his activities in a panel of legal and economical experts who critically assessed Russian position in the second [Yukos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yukos) case.[^cite_note-NYT53113-9][^cite_note-nytimes.com-10][^cite_note-NYT52913-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] In 2015 [Vladimir Putin](32817.md) denied that Guriev's departure “could have been related to any activities of the authorities”.[^cite_note-14]

See also: [Yukos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yukos) and [Yukos shareholders vs. Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yukos_shareholders_vs._Russia)

In November 2023, the official Russian news agencies [TASS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TASS) and [RIA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RIA_Novosti) reported that Russia's [interior ministry](53056.md) had placed him on a wanted list with [Sergey Aleksashenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergey_Aleksashenko), another economist in exile, on unspecified charges.[^cite_note-15]

## Awards

In 2001, Guriev was announced the Best Academic Manager in Social Sciences by the [Science Support Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Support_Foundation?action=edit\&redlink=1). In 2000 and 2005, he was awarded a gold medal for Best Research in Development Economics by the [Global Development Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Development_Network). In 2006, he was selected a Young Global Leader by the [World Economic Forum](172185.md). He is a Senior Member[^cite_note-16] of the Institut Universitaire de France, Ordinary Member[^cite_note-17] of the Academia Europaea, and an Honorary Foreign Member[^cite_note-18] of the American Economic Association.

## Professional activities



Guriev, [Premier](339344.md) [Medvedev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitry_Medvedev), CEO of [Sberbank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sberbank) [Gref](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herman_Gref), and ex-[finance minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Finance_\(Russia\)) [Kudrin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexei_Kudrin) at [World Economic Forum](172185.md) in [Davos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davos) on 23 January 2013

Guriev's primary interests are in contract theory, [corporate governance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporate_governance), [labor mobility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labor_mobility), [political economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_economics), economics of [development and transition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Development_economics). His work has been published in international refereed journals, including the [American Economic Review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Economic_Review), [The Review of Economic Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Review_of_Economic_Studies), [The Quarterly Journal of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Quarterly_Journal_of_Economics), the Journal of the [European Economic Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Economic_Association), the [Journal of Economic Perspectives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Economic_Perspectives), the [Economic Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_Journal) and the [American Political Science Review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Political_Science_Review). He ran a monthly column in *Forbes Russia* (in 2006–2013) and a biweekly column for the leading Russian business daily *[Vedomosti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vedomosti)* (in 2003–2013). He has also contributed numerous columns in the *[New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Times)*, the *[Financial Times](136566.md)*, the *[Washington Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_Post)*, *[Project Syndicate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Syndicate)*, and the *[Moscow Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Times)*, among others.[^cite_note-NYT53113-9][^cite_note-nytimes.com-10]

In 2008–2012 he was a member of President of Russia's Council on Science, Education and Technology, in 2010-12 he was a member of the president of Russia's Commission on the National Projects, and in 2012–2013 he was a member of Government of Russia's Commission on Open Government (2012–2013).

Guriev is a research fellow[^cite_note-19] at the [Centre for Economic Policy Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_for_Economic_Policy_Research) (CEPR), London. He was also a co-editor of the Economics of Transition, and a panel member of the Economic Policy journal. He was a member[^cite_note-20] of the Scientific Council of Bruegel think tank, member of the international advisory council of the Peterson Institute for International Council, member of the international advisory board of the Blavatnik School of Governance at Oxford University, and a member of the strategic council of the School of Public Affairs at Sciences Po, Paris. He is currently a member of the executive committee of the International Economic Association, a global member[^cite_note-21] of the Trilateral Commission, and a member of AXA Research Fund's scientific council.

In 2016–2017, he was the president of the Society for Institutional and Organizational Economics (formerly the International Society for the New Institutional Economics).

## Board memberships

Guriev has been a board member at [Sberbank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sberbank) (2008–2014), Agency for Home Mortgage Lending (2008–2013), [Russian Agricultural Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Agricultural_Bank) (2008–2009), Alfa Strakhovanie Insurance Company (2009–2013), [Russian Venture Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Venture_Company) (2009–2013, board chair in 2012–2013), [E.ON Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E.ON_Russia) (2013–2014), and of the [Dynasty Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynasty_Foundation) (2007–2015, board chair in 2011–2013).

In 2009 and 2010 he received the Independent Director of the Year prize from Russia's National Association of Independent Directors. In 2010, he received a Certificate in Company Directorship from the UK [Institute of Directors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Directors) and was voted the Best Independent Director by the Association of Managers of Russia and the Russian Institute of Directors.

From 2016 until 2019, Guriev was the chief economist and a member of the executive committee at the [European Bank for Reconstruction and Development](37963.md).[^cite_note-22]
