The **2026 Winter Olympics closing ceremony** took place on 22 February 2026. It started at 20\:30 [CET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_European_Time) (19\:30 [UTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinated_Universal_Time)) and concluded at 23\:00. Entitled "Beauty in Action", the ceremony was held at the [Verona Arena](703367.md) in [Verona](263991.md), Italy.

As mandated by the [Olympic Charter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_Charter), the proceedings combine the protocol-related and cultural segments.[^cite_note-\:0-1] It is the first Olympics Games closing ceremony under the [IOC presidency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidents_of_the_International_Olympic_Committee) of [Kirsty Coventry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirsty_Coventry).

The city of Verona was chosen to host the ceremony, even though no events were scheduled to take place there: the intention was for this ceremony to be held in a setting reminiscent of the ancient Olympic Games. The ancient [Roman amphitheater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_amphitheater), which regularly hosts a [famous open-air opera festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arena_di_Verona_Festival) as well as other cultural events, also hosted the opening ceremony of the [2026 Winter Paralympics](41045653.md) on 6 March. The [Teatro Filarmonico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teatro_Filarmonico), an opera theatre in Verona, served as a secondary venue.

## Preparations



The [Verona Arena](703367.md) in [Verona](263991.md), Italy hosted the closing ceremony.

The closing ceremony was filmed by [Olympic Broadcasting Services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_Broadcasting_Services) (OBS) and broadcast by [International Olympic Committee](15147.md)'s (IOC) Global Media rights holders.

The creative team for the ceremony was produced by [Filmmaster Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filmmaster_Group?action=edit\&redlink=1) guided by [Alfredo Accatino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfredo_Accatino?action=edit\&redlink=1), with the team consisting of Adriano Martella (creative director), Stefania Opipari (show director), Stefano Ciammitti (costume designer), [Vittorio Cosma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vittorio_Cosma) (music director), [Michele Braga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michele_Braga?action=edit\&redlink=1) (music director) and Claudio Santucci (set designer).[^cite_note-2]

The Olympians staying in the [Milan Olympic Village](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milan_Olympic_Village) traveled by train from [Milan](36511.md) to [Verona](263991.md).[^cite_note-3]

## Proceedings



Exterior of the [Teatro Filarmonico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teatro_Filarmonico) in Verona

The closing ceremony was held at the [Verona Arena](703367.md), and started at 20\:30 ([CET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_European_Time)) in [Verona](263991.md), Italy. It began with a segment entitled "A Night at the Opera," which serves as a tribute to [Italian opera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_opera). During the segment, a *[lumière](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light_fixture)* illuminated multiple candelabras on a constructed stage in the middle of the arena, with a camera following the stage director (portrayed by Francesco Pannofino), behind the stage into underground tunnels beneath the arena, where he prepared the rehearsal for the performance during a pre-recorded segment. The segment featured characters from various Italian operas, including the eponymous protagonists of *[Rigoletto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rigoletto)* (portrayed by Italian actor Stefano Scandaletti) and of *[Aida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aida)* (portrayed by Nigerian former model Charity Dago), Alfredo (portrayed by Italian [tenor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenor) Vincenzo Costanzo) and Violetta (portrayed by Bolivian-Albanian [soprano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soprano) Carolina Lopez Moreno) from *[La traviata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_traviata)*, Figaro (portrayed by Italian musician Antonio Melissa) from *[Il barbiere di Siviglia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Barber_of_Seville)*, and Cio-Cio San (portrayed by Japanese-Italian actress Jun Ichikawa) from *[Madama Butterfly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madama_Butterfly)*, with performers wearing extravagant costumes made using recycled materials. The segment also featured cameos from famous Italians, including [Achille Lauro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achille_Lauro_\(singer\)), [Deborah Compagnoni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deborah_Compagnoni), and a brief appearance of [Juliet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juliet) on her balcony. As Rigoletto emerged from a large chandelier that appeared above the stage, Alfredo and Violetta performed "[Libiamo ne' lieti calici](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libiamo_ne'_lieti_calici)" before Aida and Cio-Cio San took on the stage. Meanwhile, the lumière escorted Italian opera's characters paraded with the athletes through [Piazza Bra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piazza_Bra) in Verona while making their way into the arena. At the same time, the choir sang from the [Teatro Filarmonico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teatro_Filarmonico), which served as a secondary venue for the ceremony with an audience, including actress [Benedetta Porcaroli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benedetta_Porcaroli).[^cite_note-4]

After an artistic program, a short segment titled "Faces of Italy" saw portraits of various Italians captured by photographer Marco Delogu. After the seating of the dignitaries, [Paolo Fresu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paolo_Fresu) and the choir of the Fondazione Arena di Verona performed "[Il Canto degli Italiani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Il_Canto_degli_Italiani)" during the raising of the [flag of Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Italy); the flag itself was carried by people from the host sites of the games and the [Carabinieri](45173.md). [Italian athletes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy_at_the_2026_Winter_Olympics) who won medals at the Games stood on stage during the flag raising.[^cite_note-5] Afterward, Italian athletes [Silvio Fauner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silvio_Fauner), [Maurilio De Zolt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurilio_De_Zolt), [Marco Albarello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marco_Albarello), and [Giorgio Vanzetta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giorgio_Vanzetta) (members of the winning [Cross-country skiing at the 1994 Winter Olympics – Men's 4 × 10 kilometre relay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross-country_skiing_at_the_1994_Winter_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_4_%C3%97_10_kilometre_relay) team) appeared with the [Olympic flame](74217.md) in an [ampoule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ampoule); this led to the illumination of the [Olympic rings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_rings) inside the arena.

After the flags of the competing nations arrived in the arena, the athletes followed. During the athletes' parade, [Margherita Vicario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margherita_Vicario), [Davide Shorty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davide_Shorty), and [Calibro 35](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calibro_35) performed on stage. This was followed by *Elevation* segment featured an acrobatic performance represeting athletes and a victory ceremony for the medalists from [Cross-country skiing – Men's 50 kilometre classical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross-country_skiing_at_the_2026_Winter_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_50_kilometre_classical) and [Cross-country skiing – Women's 50 kilometre classical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross-country_skiing_at_the_2026_Winter_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_50_kilometre_classical).[^cite_note-6] [Gabry Ponte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabry_Ponte) and [Eiffel 65](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eiffel_65) performed *[Blue (Da Ba Dee)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_\(Da_Ba_Dee\))* during a tribute to the Games' volunteers, which was followed by a moment of remembrance segment featuring the orchestra of the Fondazione Arena plays [Puccini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giacomo_Puccini)'s "Humming Chorus" while Cio-Cio San gazing at the butterflies.

With various references to the water cycle and sunlight, Italian singer [Joan Thiele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Thiele) sings "[Il mondo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Il_mondo_\(song\))" while [La Scala Theatre Ballet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Scala_Theatre_Ballet)'s [Roberto Bolle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_Bolle) performed an aerial tribute to [Earth](9228.md). Afterward, the choir and orchestra of the Fondazione Arena di Verona performed the [Olympic Hymn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_Hymn) during the lowering of the [Olympic flag](731192.md). Shortly before, "[Hymn to Liberty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hymn_to_Liberty)" (the national anthem of [Greece](12108.md)) was performed during its flag raising. The mayors of [Milan](36511.md) and [Cortina d'Ampezzo](568829.md) handed over the flag to the presidents of the regional councils of [Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provence-Alpes-C%C3%B4te_d'Azur) and [Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes](45093325.md), the host regions of the [2030 Winter Olympics](57658858.md). This was followed by the raising of the [flag of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_France) and a performance of "[La Marseillaise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Marseillaise)" by Marine Chagnon, with a musical arrangement by Thomas Roussel. During the performance, [French athletes at the Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France_at_the_2026_Winter_Olympics) appeared; this was followed by a video from the [French Alps](961317.md), with references to light and "a new dawn".[^cite_note-7] The Olympic Flag will be raised again on 14 July 2028, for the [opening ceremony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opening_ceremony) of the [2028 Summer Olympics](12695048.md) at [Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Memorial_Coliseum) and [SoFi Stadium](45525802.md) in [Los Angeles](18110.md).

Milano Cortina 2026 organising committee president [Giovanni Malagò](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giovanni_Malag%C3%B2) and [International Olympic Committee](15147.md) president [Kirsty Coventry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirsty_Coventry) delivered official remarks, with Coventry declaring the closing of the Games, praising them as "magical games", and inviting the youth of the world and the audience to assemble in the French Alps in 4 years. The flame was extinguished at the arena, Milan, and Cortina d'Ampezzo, while pianist [Gloria Campaner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Campaner) performed *Experience* by [Ludovico Einaudi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludovico_Einaudi). This was followed by the musical performances of [Diplo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplo), [Major Lazer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_Lazer), [Meduza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meduza_\(producers\)), [MØ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%98), [Nyla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyla), [DJ Snake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DJ_Snake), and [Alfa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfa_\(singer\)). To end the ceremony, Achille Lauro performed his song "[Incoscienti giovani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incoscienti_giovani)" as a light show also took place in its stead, leaving the characters from the opera segment resting under the arena.[^cite_note-8]
