# 2026 Wimbledon Championships – Women's singles

[Linda Nosková](67929086.md) defeated [Karolína Muchová](55266410.md) in the final, 6–2, 5–7, 6–3 to win the ladies' singles tennis title at the [2026 Wimbledon Championships](80434323.md).[^cite_note-1] She [saved a match point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Grand_Slam_related_tennis_records#Won_a_title_after_saving_match_points) (in the third round against [Sorana Cîrstea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorana_C%C3%AErstea)) en route to her first [major](197638.md#Tournaments) title and third career [WTA Tour](1398173.md) title overall.[^cite_note-2] Aged 21 years and 236 days old, Nosková was the youngest [Wimbledon](91195.md) champion since [Petra Kvitová](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petra_Kvitov%C3%A1) in [2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles).[^cite_note-3] The [Czech Republic](5321.md) became the sixth country in the Open Era to have [two countrywomen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Grand_Slam_women's_singles_finals#All-countrywomen_finals_\(Open_Era\)) contest a major singles final.[^cite_note-4]

[Iga Świątek](51838558.md) was the defending champion,[^cite_note-5] but lost in the third round to [Alexandra Eala](62712906.md). Eala became the first Filipina player in the [Open Era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_tennis#Open_Era) to reach the third and fourth rounds of a major.[^cite_note-6] The defeat of [Barbora Krejčíková](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbora_Krej%C4%8D%C3%ADkov%C3%A1) in the fourth round guaranteed a first-time Wimbledon champion for the ninth consecutive edition.

[Aryna Sabalenka](50222577.md) retained the [world No. 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_WTA_number_1_ranked_singles_tennis_players) singles ranking after [Elena Rybakina](55541443.md) lost in the third round.[^cite_note-7] Sabalenka's fourth-round defeat to [Naomi Osaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naomi_Osaka) marked the first time she failed to reach the quarterfinals of a major since the [2022 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), and her first straight-sets defeat at a major since the [2020 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles).[^cite_note-8]

This tournament marked the singles return of former world No. 1 and 23-time major champion [Serena Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serena_Williams), who last played at the [2022 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles).[^cite_note-9] She lost in the first round to [Maya Joint](75466424.md).[^cite_note-10] [Maja Chwalińska](53209114.md) was the third player in the Open Era to be seeded at a major while also being entered as a wildcard, following [Martina Hingis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martina_Hingis) at the [2002 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles) and [Patty Schnyder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patty_Schnyder) at the [2004 Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles).[^cite_note-11] [Lina Gjorcheska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lina_Gjorcheska) became the first player from [North Macedonia](23564616.md) to compete in a major main draw.[^cite_note-12]
