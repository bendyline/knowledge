**Lingbao** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 灵宝; [postal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_romanization): **Lingpao**) is a [county-level city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County-level_city) and the westernmost [county-level division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County-level_division) of [Henan](166408.md) province, China, bordering the provinces of [Shanxi](81262.md) to the north and [Shaanxi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaanxi) to the west, under the administration of the [prefecture-level city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefecture-level_city) of [Sanmenxia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanmenxia). In ancient times, the [Hongnong Commandery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hongnong_Commandery) was located approximately 20 km south of there. Since 2005, there is a HVDC back-to-back station built by ABB with a transmission rate of 360 MW.

Lingbao city has a population of 720,000 residents in 2006.

The legend of [Kuafu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuafu) is said to be set in Lingbao, which has therefore previously been called Taolin (peach orchard).[^cite_note-2]

## History

Lingbao City can be traced back to 114 BC as an administrative division, when it was known as Hongnong (弘农县).[^cite_note-\:0-3]

In 1993 Linbao City was established from Lingbao [County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_China).[^cite_note-\:0-3]

## Administrative divisions

As of 2012, this city is divided to 10 towns and 5 townships.[^cite_note-4]

Towns

- Chengguan (城关镇)

- Yinzhuang (尹庄镇)

- Zhuyang (朱阳镇)

- Yangping (阳平镇)

- Guxian (故县镇)

- Yuling (豫灵镇)

- Lingbao (大王镇)

- Yangdian (阳店镇)

- Hanguguan (函谷关镇)

- Jiaocun (焦村镇)

Townships

- [Chuankou Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chuankou_Township,_Henan) (川口乡)

- Sihe Township (寺河乡)

- [Sucun Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sucun_Township,_Henan) (苏村乡)

- Wumiao Township (五亩乡)

- [Xiyan Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiyan_Township,_Henan) (西阎乡)
