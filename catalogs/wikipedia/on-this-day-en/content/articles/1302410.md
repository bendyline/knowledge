**D-Erythrulose** (also known as **erythrulose**) is the [organic compound](22203.md) with the formula HOCH<sub>2</sub>C(O)CH(OH)CH<sub>2</sub>OH (alternative notation as C<sub>4</sub>H<sub>8</sub>O<sub>4</sub>). As a ketone-containing [monosaccharide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monosaccharide), it is classified as a [ketose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ketose) and [tetrose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrose).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-mw-2]

Erythrulose is a colorless, sweet-tasting, water-soluble solid.[^cite_note-pubchem-3] Among the sugars, it is relatively obscure, being described as a "novelty tetrose".[^cite_note-Huang-4]

In 2026, [broadband](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broadband) [spectral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectral_density) surveys using large Earth-based telescopes detected erythrulose on dust grains in molecular clouds near the center of the [Milky Way](2589714.md).[^cite_note-serra-5]

## Synthesis

Erythrulose can be synthesized by [enzyme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enzyme)-mediated [condensation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Condensation) of [glycolaldehyde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glycolaldehyde):[^cite_note-6]

2 HOCH<sub>2</sub>CHO → HOCH<sub>2</sub>C(O)CH(OH)CH<sub>2</sub>OH

Multiple pathways lead to erythrulose [phosphates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phosphate) from [glucose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glucose) as well as splitting of [sedoheptulose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sedoheptulose).[^cite_note-Huang-4]

Its detection in the Milky Way molecular cloud indicates that erythrulose may be synthesized [abiotically](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abiotic_component) in the extreme environment of space.[^cite_note-serra-5]

## Occurrence

Erythrulose occurs in many fruits, including [melons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melon) and [red raspberries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rubus_idaeus).[^cite_note-Huang-4][^cite_note-cnn-7][^cite_note-nyt-8]

Among some 340 [circumstellar molecules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_interstellar_and_circumstellar_molecules) discovered in dust and gases of the [interstellar medium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstellar_medium), erythrulose is the first sugar identified.[^cite_note-cnn-7][^cite_note-nyt-8] The discovery was made from [radio telescope](46656.md) surveys of *G+0.693−0.027*, a [molecular cloud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molecular_cloud) near the center of the Milky Way.[^cite_note-serra-5] Two radio telescopes in Spain were used: [the 40 metre telescope at Yebes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yebes_Observatory_RT40m) and [the 30 metre telescope in Granada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IRAM_30m_telescope).[^cite_note-serra-5]

Identification of erythrulose in interstellar dust of the Milky Way indicates, if not [abiotic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abiotic_component) synthesis, its possible involvement in forming sugar-containing [nucleic acids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nucleic_acid) during the [origin of life on Earth](19179706.md) about four billion years ago.[^cite_note-serra-5][^cite_note-cnn-7][^cite_note-nyt-8]
