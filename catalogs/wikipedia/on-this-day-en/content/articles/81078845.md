**Fátima Bosch Fernández** ([/ˈfætɪmə ˈbɒsk/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English) [*FAT-im-ə BOSK*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:Pronunciation_respelling_key); Spanish pronunciation: [\[ˈfatima ˈβoʃ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish);[^cite_note-pron-4] born 19 May 2000) is a Mexican model and beauty queen who won [Miss Universe 2025](78060439.md).[^cite_note-5] She had previously won Miss Universe Mexico 2025. Bosch is the fourth Mexican woman to win Miss Universe.

## Early life and education

Fátima Bosch Fernández was born on 19 May 2000, in [Teapa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teapa), [Tabasco](194150.md), to Bernardo Bosch Hernández and Vanessa Fernández Balboa.[^cite_note-6] Bosch completed her elementary and high school education between [Colegio Arji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colegio_Arji), Instituto Cumbres Villahermosa.[^cite_note-7]

From 2021 to 2024, Bosch attended the [Universidad Iberoamericana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_Iberoamericana) in Mexico City, where she completed bachelor's degree in fashion and apparel design.[^cite_note-8] Her surname, [Bosch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosch_\(surname\)), is of [Catalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_language) origin and means 'forest'.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-pron-4]

## Pageantry

### Flor Tabasco 2018

Bosch won Flor Tabasco, representing [Teapa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teapa),[^cite_note-10] competed against 16 contestants during the pageant finale held in the Centro de Convenciones Tabasco 2000 of [Villahermosa](194156.md) on 25 April 2018.[^cite_note-11] She was crowned by the outgoing titleholder, Bárbara Aranguren.[^cite_note-12]



Bosch as Miss Universe Mexico 2025

### Miss Universe Mexico 2025

Bosch represented Tabasco, and won [Miss Universe Mexico 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_Mexico_2025), against 31 contestants.[^cite_note-13] The contest held on 13 September 2025, at the Sala Plácido Domingo del Conjunto Santander de Artes Escénicas in [Zapopan](2107760.md), [Jalisco](16020434.md). She was crowned by the outgoing titleholder [María Fernanda Beltrán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mar%C3%ADa_Fernanda_Beltr%C3%A1n).[^cite_note-14]

### Miss Universe 2025

On 4 November 2025, during the sash ceremony event, a dispute occurred when [Miss Universe Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_Thailand) national director [Nawat Itsaragrisil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nawat_Itsaragrisil) reprimanded Bosch and referred to her as "dumb", leading to tensions and several contestants walking out.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] Bosch later said she felt disrespected, while [Miss Universe 2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_2024), [Victoria Kjær Theilvig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_Kj%C3%A6r_Theilvig) criticized the public scolding and left the venue.[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21]

During the preliminary competition on 19 November 2025, Bosch's fellow contestant Gabrielle Henry, [Miss Universe Jamaica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_Jamaica) 2025, fell through an unguarded opening in the stage floor during the evening gown round and was hospitalised in intensive care in Bangkok.[^cite_note-22] Speaking to [Good Morning America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Morning_America) following her coronation, Bosch described the stage conditions the contestants had faced during the preliminary rounds, stating that the lighting made it impossible for those walking to see the stage: "The lights that came to us make everything darker, like you can see us, but we couldn't see."[^cite_note-ABCBosch-23] When Henry later published a personal statement about her recovery, Bosch was among the fellow contestants who responded publicly, praising Henry's strength and resilience.[^cite_note-24]

On 21 November 2025, Bosch represented Mexico and won [Miss Universe 2025](78060439.md), competing against 120 other candidates at the Impact Challenger Hall in [Pak Kret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pak_Kret), [Nonthaburi](47777336.md), Thailand.[^cite_note-25] Bosch is the fourth Mexican woman to earn the Miss Universe title, after [Lupita Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lupita_Jones), [Ximena Navarrete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ximena_Navarrete) and [Andrea Meza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Meza).[^cite_note-26]
