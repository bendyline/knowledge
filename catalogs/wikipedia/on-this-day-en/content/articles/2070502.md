**Calanogas**, officially the **Municipality of Calanogas** ([Maranao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maranao_language) and [Iranun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranun_language): *Inged a Calanogas*; [Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language): *Bayan ng Calanogas*), is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality_of_the_Philippines) in the [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Province) of [Lanao del Sur](297370.md), [Philippines](23440.md). According to the 2024 census, it has a population of 17,585 people.[^cite_note-5]

## Geography

### Barangays

Calanogas is politically subdivided into 17 [barangays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barangay). Each barangay consists of [puroks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purok) while some have [sitios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sitios).

- Bubonga Ranao

- Calalaoan ([Poblacion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poblacion))

- Gas

- Inoma

- Inudaran

- Luguna

- Mimbalawag
