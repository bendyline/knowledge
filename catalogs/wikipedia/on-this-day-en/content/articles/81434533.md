Defending champion [Jannik Sinner](60061043.md)[^cite_note-1] defeated [Carlos Alcaraz](63121147.md) in the final, 7–6<sup>(7–4)</sup>, 7–5 to win the singles tennis title at the [2025 ATP Finals](78350211.md). It was his 24th [ATP Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Tour) title overall. For the [second consecutive year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_ATP_Finals_%E2%80%93_Singles), Sinner did not lose a set en route to the title, the first player to do so for multiple years in a row after [Ivan Lendl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivan_Lendl), who achieved the feat in [1985](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985_Nabisco_Masters_%E2%80%93_Singles) and [1986](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986_Nabisco_Masters_%E2%80%93_Singles). He was the third and youngest player in the [Open Era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_tennis#Open_Era), after [Roger Federer](262376.md) and [Novak Djokovic](16100029.md), to reach the final of all four [majors](197638.md#Tournaments) and the [ATP Finals](1209231.md) in a single season, in what was the first time since [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_ATP_World_Tour_Finals_%E2%80%93_Singles) that the world's top two players contested the final.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] Upon winning the title undefeated, Sinner earned the highest prize money in tournament history, at US\$5.071 million.[^cite_note-5]

After winning all three of his round-robin matches, Alcaraz claimed the [ATP year-end No. 1 singles ranking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ATP_number_1_ranked_singles_tennis_players#Year-end_No._1_players) for the second time in his career, after [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_ATP_Tour).[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] Sinner was also in contention for the top spot at the beginning of the tournament.[^cite_note-8]

It was Djokovic's 18th qualification for the year-end championships, equaling Federer's [record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Finals_appearances#ATP_Finals_appearances).[^cite_note-9] He withdrew before the start of the tournament due to a shoulder injury and was replaced by first alternate [Lorenzo Musetti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorenzo_Musetti).[^cite_note-10]

[Ben Shelton](71310148.md) and Musetti made their respective debuts in the competition, in what marked the first time two Italian men (Sinner and Musetti) qualified for the singles event.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]
