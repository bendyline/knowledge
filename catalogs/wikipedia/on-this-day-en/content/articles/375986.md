[India](14533.md) is a [federal union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federalism) comprising 28 [states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federated_state) and 8 [union territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_territory), for a total of 36 [subnational entities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subnational_entities).[^cite_note-1] The states and union territories in turn are each subdivided into [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_districts_in_India), of which a total of around 800 exist. Districts contain further levels of [administrative subdivisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_India).[^cite_note-2]

Under the Indian Constitution and laws, the states of India are self-governing administrative divisions, each having a [state government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_governments_of_India). The legal power to manage affairs in each state is shared or *divided* between the particular state government on one hand and the national [union government](553883.md) on the other. The union territories are directly governed by the union government; no state level government (and thus no division of power) exists in these jurisdictions.[^cite_note-3]

## History

### 1876–1919



The administrative divisions of the Indian Empire in 1909

The [British Raj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Raj) was a very complex political entity consisting of various imperial divisions and states and territories of varying autonomy. At the time of its legal establishment in 1876, it was made up of 584 [constituent states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princely_state) and the [directly ruled territories of the Crown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_India). The entire empire was divided into provinces and agencies.

A [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_India) consisted of territory under the direct rule of the [Emperor of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emperor_of_India) (who was also the [King of the United Kingdom and the Dominions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_of_the_United_Kingdom_and_the_Dominions)) and a few minor states, ruled by Indian princes under the [suzerainty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzerainty) of the Emperor. A [Governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor) or [Lieutenant-Governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant-Governor) acted as representative of the Emperor in that province and as [head of government](37570.md) of the directly ruled territories in the province.

The governor or lieutenant-governor also served as the Emperor's representative to the [constituent states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princely_state) of the province. The first three of the lieutenant-governorships were territories annexed to India from other powers and temporarily governed by the erstwhile [Bengal Presidency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengal_Presidency), before being made into their own separate provinces. [Agra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agra_Province) and [Bengal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengal) were still considered *[de jure](43594.md)* parts of the defunct Bengal Presidency for judicial and legal purposes. Agra was separated in 1878 and merged with [Oudh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oudh_Province).

The [Bengal Presidency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengal_Presidency) was re-established in 1912 as a governorship. All these provinces had their own legislatures established by the [Indian Councils Acts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Councils_Act_1909), and high courts established by Indian High Courts Acts. Laws passed by these legislatures needed the dual [assent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assent) of the governor or lieutenant-governor of the province and the [governor-general of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor-general_of_India), who functioned as the representative of the [Emperor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emperor_of_India).&#x20;

- There were three governor's provinces in 1912, styled "Presidencies" as a historical memento that they had been once governed by presidents. These were:&#x20;

  - [Bombay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombay_Presidency)

  - [Madras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madras_Presidency) (officially Presidency of Fort St. George)

  - [Bengal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengal_Presidency) (from 1912)

- There were six lieutenant-governor's provinces in 1905. These were:&#x20;

  - [Burma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_rule_in_Burma)

  - [Punjab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjab_Province_\(1849%E2%80%931947\))

  - [Central Provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Provinces)

  - [Bengal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengal_Presidency) (till 1912)

  - [Eastern Bengal and Assam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Bengal_and_Assam) (1905–1912)

  - [Agra and Oudh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agra_Province)&#x20;

    - [North-Western Provinces and Oudh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North-Western_Provinces_and_Oudh) (1878–1902)

    - [United Provinces of Agra and Oudh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Provinces_of_Agra_and_Oudh) (1902–1935)

There were territories ruled directly by the Government of India through nominated chief commissioners. These were former independent states annexed to India and since ruled directly by the Supreme Government.

- There were the three chief commissioner's provinces. These did not have a legislature or a high court. These were:&#x20;

  - [Ajmer-Merwara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ajmer-Merwara)

  - [Coorg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coorg_Province)

  - [Oudh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_chief_commissioners_of_Oudh) (till 1878)

  - [Delhi](37756.md) (from 1911, capital of India)

The vast majority of the Indian states in the late nineteenth century were, in terms of imperial divisions, organised within the provinces. A good number of states were organised into imperial structures called agencies, or residencies. An Agent to the Governor-General (AGG) functioned as the Emperor's representative to all the states in the agency.&#x20;

- At the time of establishment of the empire, there were only two agencies:&#x20;

  - [Rajputana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajputana_Agency)

  - [Central India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_India_Agency)

- Several kingdoms were neither part of a province nor an agency; they each had direct relations with the Emperor:&#x20;

  - [Kashmir and Jammu](61449812.md)

  - [Nizamate of Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nizamate_of_Hyderabad)

  - [Kingdom of Mysore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Mysore)

### 1919–1935

In 1919, the fourth [Government of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_India_Act_1919) was enacted by [the Crown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Crown). This saw many major changes. The legislatures of the provinces were made elected ones rather than nominated ones. Some provinces were given [bicameral legislatures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicameral_legislature). All provinces were elevated to governorships and all lieutenant governors were made governors. [Burma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burma) was given a special status and made an autonomous province.

- The ten governor's provinces during this period:&#x20;

  - [Bombay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombay_Presidency)

  - [Madras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madras_Presidency)

  - [Bengal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengal_Presidency)

  - [Burma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_rule_in_Burma)

  - [Punjab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjab_Province_\(1849%E2%80%931947\))

  - [Central Provinces and Berar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Provinces_and_Berar)

  - [United Provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Provinces_of_British_India)

  - [North-West Frontier Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North-West_Frontier_Province)

  - [Assam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assam_Province)

  - [Bihar and Orissa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bihar_and_Orissa)

- Four chief commissioner's provinces (Their status mostly remained unchanged):&#x20;

  - [Ajmer-Merwara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ajmer-Merwara)

  - [Coorg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coorg_Province)

  - [Balochistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Commissioner's_Province_of_Balochistan)

  - [Delhi](37756.md)

The [Chamber of Princes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamber_of_Princes) was established by [Emperor George V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emperor_George_V) in 1920. One of the major consequences of this was the creation of many more agencies from the states of the provinces, granting them direct relations with the Emperor instead of with the Governors.&#x20;

- There were now 8 imperial agencies and residencies. These were:&#x20;

  - [Punjab States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjab_States_Agency) (formerly part of Punjab Province)

  - [Madras States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madras_States_Agency) (formerly part of Madras Presidency)

  - [Deccan States Agency and Kolhapur Residency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deccan_States_Agency_and_Kolhapur_Residency) (formerly part of Bombay Presidency)

  - [Western India and Gujarat States Agency and Baroda Residency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_India_and_Gujarat_States_Agency_and_Baroda_Residency) (formerly part of Bombay Presidency)

  - [Eastern States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_States_Agency) (formerly part of Bengal Presidency, Bihar and Orissa Province and Central Provinces)

  - [Gwalior Residency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gwalior_Residency) (formerly part of [Central India Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_India_Agency))

  - [Rajputana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajputana_Agency)

  - [Central India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_India_Agency)

This saw the separation of all the states from the provinces and addition to before-mentioned agencies. This left all the provinces with only territories under direct Crown rule.&#x20;

- Several states continued their direct relations with the Emperor through this period, including:&#x20;

  - [Kashmir and Jammu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jammu_and_Kashmir_\(princely_state\))

  - [Nizamate of Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nizamate_of_Hyderabad)

  - [Kingdom of Mysore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Mysore)

### 1935–1947

The latter years of the [Indian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Raj) saw the enactment of the last [Government of India Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_India_Act_1935) by the Crown. This act granted full autonomy to Indian provinces. Provincial laws no longer needed the assent of the governor-general. This act created the office of a [Premier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier) in each province, who functioned as the new head of government and was responsible to the provincial legislature.

Bengal, Madras and Bombay which had been till now styled Presidencies, were now officially styled as provinces. The provinces of [Orissa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orissa_province) and [Sind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sind_province) were created from Bihar and Bombay respectively. The [Province of Burma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Burma) which had previously functioned as an autonomous province of India was now separated from the Indian Empire, and established as the [Crown Colony of Burma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_Colony_of_Burma).

- The new set of 12 governor's provinces were:&#x20;

  - [Bombay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombay_Presidency)

  - [Sind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sind_Province)

  - [Madras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madras_Presidency)

  - [Bengal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengal_Presidency)

  - [Burma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_rule_in_Burma)

  - [Punjab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjab_Province_\(1849%E2%80%931947\))

  - [Central Provinces and Berar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Provinces_and_Berar)

  - [United Provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Provinces_\(1937%E2%80%931950\))

  - [North-West Frontier Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North-West_Frontier_Province)

  - [Assam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assam_Province)

  - [Bihar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bihar_Province)

  - [Orissa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orissa_Province)

- There were 4 chief commissioner's provinces:&#x20;

  - [Ajmer-Merwara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ajmer-Merwara)

  - [Coorg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coorg_Province)

  - [Balochistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Commissioner's_Province_of_Balochistan)

  - [Delhi](37756.md)

- There were 8 imperial agencies and residencies:&#x20;

  - [Punjab States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjab_States_Agency)

  - [Madras States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madras_States_Agency)

  - [Deccan States Agency and Kolhapur Residency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deccan_States_Agency_and_Kolhapur_Residency)

  - [Western India and Gujarat States Agency and Baroda Residency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_India_and_Gujarat_States_Agency_and_Baroda_Residency)

  - [Eastern States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_States_Agency)

  - [Gwalior Residency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gwalior_Residency)

  - [Rajputana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajputana_Agency)

  - [Central India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_India_Agency)

- Three kingdoms were in direct relations with the Emperor.

In 1947, the last Act of the Crown was passed. The act dissolved the Indian Empire, the [Imperial Legislative Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Legislative_Council) and the [Chamber of Princes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamber_of_Princes). The [Union of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_of_India) was consequently established from 9 former Indian provinces ([East Punjab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Punjab), [United Provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Provinces_\(1937%E2%80%931950\)), [Central Provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Provinces), [Madras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madras_Province), [Bombay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombay_Province), [Bihar](442482.md), [Orissa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orissa), [West Bengal](34040.md) and [Assam](186162.md)) and 562 former Indian states.

### 1947–1950

Main articles: [Political integration of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_integration_of_India), [Instrument of Accession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instrument_of_Accession), [Annexation of Junagadh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annexation_of_Junagadh), and [Annexation of Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annexation_of_Hyderabad)



The administrative divisions of the Union of India in 1950.

Between 1947 and 1950, the territories of the princely states were politically integrated into the new [Indian Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_integration_of_India). Most were merged into existing provinces. Others were organised into new provinces and [states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajpramukh), such as [Rajasthan](26291.md), [Himachal Pradesh](14190.md), [Malwa Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malwa_Union), [Baghelkhand and Bundelkhand States Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vindhya_Pradesh), and [Patiala and East Punjab States Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patiala_and_East_Punjab_States_Union), made up of multiple princely states. A few, including [Mysore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mysore_State), [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad_State_\(1948%E2%80%9356\)), [Bhopal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhopal_State_\(1949%E2%80%9356\)), and [Bilaspur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilaspur_State_\(1950%E2%80%9354\)), became separate states.[^cite_note-Consti-4]

The new [Constitution of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_India), which came into force on 26 January 1950, made India a sovereign democratic republic. The new republic was also declared to be a "Union of States". The constitution of 1950 distinguished between three main types of states:[^cite_note-Consti-4]

- Part A states, which were the former governors' provinces of India, were ruled by an elected governor and state legislature. The nine Part A states were:&#x20;

  - [Assam](186162.md) (formerly [Assam Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assam_Province)),

  - [Bihar](442482.md) (formerly [Bihar Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bihar_Province)),

  - [Bombay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombay_State) (formerly [Bombay Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombay_Presidency)),

  - [East Punjab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Punjab) (formerly [Punjab Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjab_Province_\(British_India\))),

  - [Madhya Pradesh](47945.md) (formerly the [Central Provinces and Berar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Provinces_and_Berar)),

  - [Madras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madras_State) (formerly [Madras Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madras_Presidency)),

  - [Orissa](250724.md) (formerly [Orissa Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orissa_Province)),

  - [Uttar Pradesh](231623.md) (formerly the [United Provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Provinces_\(1937%E2%80%931950\))), and

  - [West Bengal](34040.md) (formerly [Bengal Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengal_Presidency)).

- The eight Part B states were former princely states or groups of princely states, governed by a [rajpramukh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajpramukh), who was usually the ruler of a constituent state, and an elected legislature. The rajpramukh was appointed by the [President of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_India). The Part B states were:&#x20;

  - [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad_State_\(1948%E2%80%9356\)) (formerly [Hyderabad Princely State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad_State)),

  - [Jammu and Kashmir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jammu_and_Kashmir_\(state\)) (formerly [Jammu and Kashmir Princely State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jammu_and_Kashmir_\(princely_state\))),

  - [Madhya Bharat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madhya_Bharat) (formerly [Central India Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_India_Agency)),

  - [Mysore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mysore_State) (formerly [Mysore Princely State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Mysore)),

  - [Patiala and East Punjab States Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patiala_and_East_Punjab_States_Union) (PEPSU),

  - [Rajasthan](26291.md) (formerly [Rajputana Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajputana_Agency)),

  - [Saurashtra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saurashtra_\(state\)) (formerly [Baroda, Western India and Gujarat States Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baroda,_Western_India_and_Gujarat_States_Agency)), and

  - [Travancore–Cochin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Travancore%E2%80%93Cochin) (formerly [Travancore Princely State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Travancore) and [Cochin Princely State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Cochin)).

- The ten Part C states included both the former chief commissioners' provinces and some princely states, and each was governed by a chief commissioner appointed by the President of India. The Part C states were:&#x20;

  - [Ajmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ajmer_State) (formerly [Ajmer-Merwara Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ajmer-Merwara)),

  - [Bhopal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhopal_State_\(1949%E2%80%9356\)) (formerly [Bhopal Princely State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhopal_State)),

  - [Bilaspur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilaspur_State_\(1950%E2%80%9354\)) (formerly [Bilaspur Princely State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilaspur_State_\(princely_state\))),

  - [Coorg State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coorg_State) (formerly [Coorg Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coorg_Province)),

  - [Delhi](37756.md),

  - [Himachal Pradesh](14190.md),

### States reorganisation (1951–1956)

Main articles: [Goa liberation movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goa_liberation_movement), [Andhra movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_movement), [Annexation of Dadra and Nagar Haveli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annexation_of_Dadra_and_Nagar_Haveli), and [States Reorganisation Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_Reorganisation_Commission)



India before the States Reorganisation Act, 1956.



India after the States Reorganisation Act

[Andhra State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_State) was created on 1 October 1953 from the [Telugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telugu_language)-speaking northern [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_India) of [Madras State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madras_State) and Bellary district bifurcated between [Mysore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mysore_State) and Andhra.[^cite_note-5]

The [French enclave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_India) of [Chandernagore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chandannagar) was transferred to [West Bengal](34040.md) in 1954. In the same year [Pondicherry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puducherry_\(city\)), comprising the former French enclaves of [Pondichéry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pondicherry_district), [Karikal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karaikal_district), [Yanaon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yanam_district) and [Mahé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mah%C3%A9_district), was transferred to India. This became a union territory in 1962.[^cite_note-6]

Also in 1954, pro-India forces liberated the [Portuguese-held enclaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_India) of [Dadrá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dadra,_Dadra_and_Nagar_Haveli_and_Daman_and_Diu) and [Nagar Aveli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagar_Haveli), declaring the short-lived *[de facto](43594.md)* state of [Free Dadra and Nagar Haveli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Dadra_and_Nagar_Haveli). In 1961, India annexed it as the Union Territory of [Dadra and Nagar Haveli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dadra_and_Nagar_Haveli).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

The [States Reorganisation Act, 1956](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_Reorganisation_Act,_1956) reorganised the states based on linguistic lines resulting in the creation of the new states.[^cite_note-Consti-4]

As a result of this act:

- [Madras State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madras_State) retained its name, with [Kanyakumari district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanyakumari_district) added to it from [Travancore–Cochin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Travancore%E2%80%93Cochin).

- [Andhra Pradesh](2377.md) was created with the merger of [Andhra State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_State) with the [Telugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telugu_language)-speaking districts of [Hyderabad State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad_State) in 1956.

- [Kerala](4349459.md) was created by merging [Malabar district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malabar_district) and the [Kasaragod taluk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasaragod_taluk) of [South Canara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Canara) districts of [Madras State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madras_State) with [Travancore–Cochin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Travancore%E2%80%93Cochin).

- [Mysore State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mysore_State) was re-organised with the addition of the district of [South Canara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Canara) (excluding [Kasaragod taluk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasaragod_taluk)) and the [Kollegal taluk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kollegal) of [Coimbatore district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coimbatore_district) from the Madras State, the districts of [Belgaum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgaum_district), [Bijapur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bijapur_district,_Karnataka), [North Canara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Canara) and [Dharwad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dharwad_district) from [Bombay State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombay_State), the [Kannada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kannada)-majority districts of [Bidar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bidar_district), [Raichur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raichur_district) and [Kalaburagi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalaburagi_district) from [Hyderabad State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad_State_\(1948%E2%80%9356\)) and the [Coorg State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coorg_State).

- The [Laccadive Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laccadive_Islands), [Aminidivi Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aminidivi) and [Minicoy Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minicoy), which had been divided between the South Canara and Malabar districts of Madras State, were united and organised into the union territory of [Lakshadweep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakshadweep).

- [Bombay State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombay_State) was enlarged by the addition of [Saurashtra State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saurashtra_\(state\)) and [Kutch State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kutch_State), the [Marathi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marathi_language)-speaking districts of [Nagpur division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagpur_division) of [Madhya Pradesh](47945.md) and the [Marathwada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marathwada) region of [Hyderabad State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad_State_\(1948%E2%80%9356\)).

- [Rajasthan](26291.md) gained territories from [Ajmer State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ajmer_State) and [Punjab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjab,_India) from [Patiala and East Punjab States Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patiala_and_East_Punjab_States_Union), and certain territories of [Bihar](442482.md) were transferred to [West Bengal](34040.md).

### Post-1956

Main articles: [Mahagujarat movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahagujarat_movement), [Punjabi Suba movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjabi_Suba_movement), [Annexation of Goa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annexation_of_Goa), [1967 Goa status referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1967_Goa_status_referendum), [1975 Sikkimese monarchy referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1975_Sikkimese_monarchy_referendum), [Uttarakhand movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uttarakhand_movement), and [Telangana movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telangana_movement)

Bombay State was split into the linguistic states of [Gujarat](53707.md) and [Maharashtra](20629.md) on 1 May 1960 by the Bombay Reorganisation Act.[^cite_note-11] The former Union Territory of [Nagaland](408677.md) achieved statehood on 1 December 1963.[^cite_note-12] The [Punjab Reorganisation Act, 1966](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjab_Reorganisation_Act,_1966) resulted in the creation of [Haryana](14189.md) on 1 November and the transfer of the northern districts of Punjab to [Himachal Pradesh](14190.md).[^cite_note-13] The act designated [Chandigarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chandigarh) as a union territory and the shared capital of Punjab and Haryana.[^cite_note-14]

Madras State was renamed [Tamil Nadu](29918.md) in 1969. The north-eastern states of [Manipur](422642.md), [Meghalaya](326019.md) and [Tripura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripura) were formed on 21 January 1972.[^cite_note-15] Mysore State was renamed [Karnataka](16880.md) in 1973. On 16 May 1975, [Sikkim](140372.md) became the 22nd state of the Indian Union and the [state's monarchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Sikkim) was abolished.[^cite_note-16] In 1987, [Arunachal Pradesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arunachal_Pradesh) and [Mizoram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mizoram) became states on 20 February, followed by [Goa](40010153.md) on 30 May, while erstwhile union territory of [Goa, Daman and Diu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goa,_Daman_and_Diu)'s northern [exclaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exclave) [Damão](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daman_district,_India) and [Diu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diu_district) became a separate union territory as [Daman and Diu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daman_and_Diu).[^cite_note-17]

In November 2000, three new states were created, namely:&#x20;

- [Chhattisgarh](47734.md), from eastern [Madhya Pradesh](47945.md), ([Madhya Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madhya_Pradesh_Reorganisation_Act,_2000))

  [^cite_note-19]

  , ([Uttar Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uttar_Pradesh_Reorganisation_Act,_2000))

  [^cite_note-20]

  , and

- [Jharkhand](197225.md), from southern districts of Bihar ([Bihar Reorganisation Act, 2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bihar_Reorganisation_Act,_2000)).

  [^cite_note-21]

Pondicherry was renamed [Puducherry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puducherry_\(union_territory\)) in 2007 and Orissa was renamed [Odisha](250724.md) in 2011. [Telangana](990267.md) was [created on 2 June 2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_Pradesh_Reorganisation_Act,_2014) from ten former districts of north-western [Andhra Pradesh](2377.md).[^cite_note-APT-22][^cite_note-23]

In August 2019, the Parliament of India passed the [Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jammu_and_Kashmir_Reorganisation_Act,_2019), which contains provisions to reorganise the state of [Jammu and Kashmir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jammu_and_Kashmir_\(state\)) into two union territories; [Jammu and Kashmir](61449812.md) and [Ladakh](303611.md), effective from 31 October 2019.[^cite_note-24] Later that year in November, the Government of India introduced legislation to merge the union territories of [Daman and Diu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daman_and_Diu) and [Dadra and Nagar Haveli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dadra_and_Nagar_Haveli) into a single union territory to be known as [Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dadra_and_Nagar_Haveli_and_Daman_and_Diu), effective from 26 January 2020.[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27]

Article 73 broadly stated, provides that the executive power of the Union shall extend to the matters with respect to which Parliament has power to make laws. The Supreme Court has reiterated this position when it ruled in the *Ramanaiah case* that the executive power of the Union or of the State broadly speaking, is [coextensive](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/coextensive) and [coterminous](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/coterminous) with its respective legislative power
