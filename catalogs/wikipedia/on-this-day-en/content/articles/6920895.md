The **PDC World Darts Championship**, officially known as the **Paddy Power World Darts Championship** for sponsorship reasons, is a world championship competition held annually in the sport of [darts](397713.md). The championship begins in December and ends in January and has been held at [Alexandra Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandra_Palace) in [London](17867.md) since 2008. Organised by the [Professional Darts Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_Darts_Corporation) (PDC), it is the most prestigious of their tournaments, with the winner receiving the Sid Waddell Trophy, named in honour of the darts commentator [Sid Waddell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sid_Waddell). Along with the [Premier League Darts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_League_Darts) and [World Matchplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Matchplay_\(darts\)), it is part of the [Triple Crown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_Darts_Corporation_Triple_Crown).

The PDC championship began in 1994 as the **WDC World Darts Championship** as one of the consequences of the [split in darts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Split_in_darts), which saw the [World Darts Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Darts_Council) break away from the BDO. As a result of the settlement between the BDO and the WDC in 1997, the WDC became the PDC, and players were then free to choose which world championship to enter (but not both in the same year), as long as they met certain eligibility criteria. Both organisations continued to organize their own world championship until the 2020 editions, after which the BDO folded.

There have been thirteen different winners since the tournament's inception. With 14 wins from 25 appearances, [Phil Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Taylor_\(darts_player\)) dominated the competition until last winning it in 2013. Other players to win more than once are [John Part](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Part) (2003 & 2008), [Adrian Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adrian_Lewis) (2011 & 2012), [Gary Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Anderson_\(darts_player\)) (2015 & 2016), [Michael van Gerwen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_van_Gerwen) (2014, 2017 & 2019), [Peter Wright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Wright_\(darts_player\)) (2020 & 2022) and [Luke Littler](70129559.md) (2025 & 2026). The one-time winners are the inaugural champion [Dennis Priestley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dennis_Priestley) (1994), [Raymond van Barneveld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raymond_van_Barneveld) (2007), [Rob Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Cross_\(darts_player\)) (2018), [Gerwyn Price](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerwyn_Price) (2021), [ Michael Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Smith_\(darts_player\)) (2023) and [Luke Humphries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Humphries) (2024).

## History



A match between [Mensur Suljović](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mensur_Suljovi%C4%87) and [Kevin Painter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Painter) at the [2010 championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_PDC_World_Darts_Championship)

In 1992, [some high-profile players](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_PDC_World_Darts_Championship#The_16_Defectors), including all previous winners of the [BDO World Darts Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BDO_World_Darts_Championship) still active in the game, formed the WDC, and in 1994, held their first World Championship. [Dennis Priestley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dennis_Priestley) won the inaugural competition.

The players who broke away were taking a significant gamble – the tournament was broadcast on satellite television rather than terrestrial, and from 1994 to 2001, the prize fund for the players in the WDC/PDC World Championship was lower than the prize fund in the BDO version, although the 1997 PDC World Champion received £45,000 compared to that year's BDO World Champion receiving £38,000. In 2002, the PDC prize fund overtook that of the BDO for the first time, and the PDC event now boasts the largest prize fund of any darts competition. In 2010, the prize fund reached £1 million for the first time, with the champion collecting £200,000.

The 2014 and 2015 PDC world champions collected £250,000 for their wins. For the next three years, the winner's share increased by £50,000 each year culminating in a 2018 prize fund of £1.8 million.[^cite_note-1] The prize fund for the tournament was £2.5 million with £500,000 to the winner from 2019. The 2025/26 World Championship will see the field expand to 128 players with the prize fund increasing to £5 million with a record £1 million to the champion.

In the [2001 PDC World Darts Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_PDC_World_Darts_Championship) [Gayl King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gayl_King) became the first woman to play in the PDC World Darts Championship. Since then [Anastasia Dobromyslova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anastasia_Dobromyslova), [Deta Hedman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deta_Hedman), [Lisa Ashton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_Ashton), [Fallon Sherrock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fallon_Sherrock), [Mikuru Suzuki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikuru_Suzuki), [Beau Greaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beau_Greaves) and [Noa-Lynn van Leuven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noa-Lynn_van_Leuven) have all played in the PDC World Darts Championship. In the [2020 PDC World Darts Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_PDC_World_Darts_Championship) Sherrock became the first woman to defeat a man in the PDC World Darts Championship, when she defeated [Ted Evetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Evetts). In the next round she also defeated [Mensur Suljovic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mensur_Suljovic) before losing to [Chris Dobey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Dobey). Sherrock remains the only woman to defeat a man in the PDC World Darts Championship.

## Venue

The tournament has been held at [Alexandra Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandra_Palace) in [London](17867.md) since 2008, having previously been held at the [Circus Tavern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circus_Tavern) in [Purfleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purfleet), [Essex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essex), from 1994 to 2007.[^cite_note-2] Currently, the play takes place inside the venue's West Hall, which has a capacity of 3,200.[^cite_note-3] From the edition of 2027, the play will take place at the Great Hall, which expands the capacity to 5,000.[^cite_note-4]

### Averages

Since the breakaway of the PDC players, there has been much debate about the relative merits of the players within each organisation. The debate often focuses on the three-dart averages of players in matches.

In [2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_PDC_World_Darts_Championship) [Phil Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Taylor_\(darts_player\)) became the first player to average over 100 in all six rounds of the tournament. He repeated this feat (though lost the final) in [2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_PDC_World_Darts_Championship) and [Michael van Gerwen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_van_Gerwen) achieved it in [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_PDC_World_Darts_Championship) and [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_PDC_World_Darts_Championship).

### Video games

The PDC have worked with various [video game developers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_developer) since 2006 to create a number of darts-themed titles based on the World Darts Championship tournament.

Their first game *[PDC World Championship Darts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PDC_World_Championship_Darts)* was developed by [Mere Mortals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mere_Mortals) for the [PlayStation 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_2) and [PC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_computer). The second game in the series was *[PDC World Championship Darts 2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PDC_World_Championship_Darts_2008)* developed by Mere Mortals for the PlayStation 2, PC, [PlayStation Portable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_Portable), [Wii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wii) and [Xbox 360](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xbox_360). A year later [Rebellion Developments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebellion_Developments) took over development of the series, releasing PDC World Championship Darts 2009 for the Wii and [Nintendo DS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nintendo_DS).

The most recent PDC World Darts Championship console game to be released was *[PDC World Championship Darts Pro Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PDC_World_Championship_Darts_Pro_Tour)* a darts video game for the [PlayStation 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_3), Wii and Xbox 360. This game is the most comprehensive of the series featuring ten professional players and five official PDC tournaments including the [PDC World Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Grand_Prix_\(darts\)), [Las Vegas Desert Classic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Vegas_Desert_Classic), [PDC UK Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Open) and the Holland Open.

In 2021 the PDC collaborated with Blueprint Gaming to develop *PDC World Darts Championship*, an officially licensed slots game available to play on mobile devices and PC, the game was released in February 2021.[^cite_note-57]
