**Sakti district** is the 33rd district formed in the state of [Chhattisgarh](47734.md), [India](14533.md) announced by then Chief Minister [Bhupesh Baghel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhupesh_Baghel) on 15 August 2021, being carved out from [Janjgir-Champa district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janjgir%E2%80%93Champa_district).[^cite_note-amarujala-1][^cite_note-ndtv-2][^cite_note-Chhattisgarh-3][^cite_note-indiatoday-4] The district was formerly the princely state of [Sakti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sakti_State), which had joined Bilaspur district after Independence.

The district is located in the [Mahanadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahanadi) river valley, with several hill ranges in the south of the district. The Mahanadi forms the southern boundary of the district.

At the time of the 2011 census, Sakti district had a population of 653,036, of which 61,508 (9.42%) live in urban areas. Sakti district has a sex ratio of 1004 females to 1000 males. Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes make up 148,386 (22.72%) and 104,296 (15.97%) of the population respectively.[^cite_note-6]

At the time of the [2011 Census of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Census_of_India), 95.98% of the population in the district spoke [Chhattisgarhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chhattisgarhi_language) and 2.72% [Hindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindi) as their first language.[^cite_note-languages-7]

## Early history

Before being a district, Sakti was previously a princely state, being ruled by royal family of Sakti. The rulers of this princely state bore the title of 'Rana'.&#x20;

His Son Raja Surendra Bahadur Singh, represented India in its hockey team and was twice a minister for the government of the State of Madhya Pradesh.

After independence of India the government of India took over ruling power and handed it to newly formed state government of Madhya Pradesh, on November 1, 2000 along many other districts Sakti got carved out of Madhya Pradesh to form a new state called Chhattisgarh.

## Formation as district

On August 15, 2021 then Chief Minister of Chhattisgarh announced 4 new districts with Sakti being one of them, raising the number of districts in state to 33 and giving the district identity of 33rd announced district. However Sakti will get its status as District a year later on September 9, 2021 after 3 more districts were announced and formed marking Sakti as 36th district of Chhattisgarh.[^cite_note-8]
