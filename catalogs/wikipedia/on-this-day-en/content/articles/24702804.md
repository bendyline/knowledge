**Luxi County** ([simplified Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 泸溪县; [traditional Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 瀘溪縣; [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *Lúxī Xiàn*) is a [county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_the_People's_Republic_of_China) of [Hunan Province](166409.md), China. It is under the administration of [Xiangxi Autonomous Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiangxi_Tujia_and_Miao_Autonomous_Prefecture).

Located on the western part of Hunan and the south eastern Xiangxi, the county is bordered to the northeast by [Yuanling County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuanling_County), to the southeast by [Chenxi County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chenxi_County), to the south by [Mayang County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayang_Miao_Autonomous_County), to the southwest by [Fenghuang County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fenghuang_County), and to the northwest by [Jishou City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jishou_City) and [Guzhang County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guzhang_County). Luxi County covers an area of 1,565.5 km<sup>2</sup> (604.4 sq mi), and as of 2015, it had a registered population of 310,800 and a resident population of 289,500.[^cite_note-population2015-4] The county has 7 [towns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Towns_of_China) and 4 [townships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Townships_of_China) under its jurisdiction, and the [county seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_seat) is [Wuxi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuxi,_Luxi) (武溪镇).[^cite_note-division2015-5]
