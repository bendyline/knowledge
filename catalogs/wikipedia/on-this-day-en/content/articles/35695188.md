The **Proclamation of Singapore**[^cite_note-1] was the official declaration that marked [Singapore](27318.md)'s separation from [Malaysia](3607937.md) and its emergence as an [independent](62450.md) and [sovereign nation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sovereign_country). The document was drafted by [E. W. Barker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E._W._Barker), [Minister for Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Law_\(Singapore\)), and signed by [Lee Kuan Yew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Kuan_Yew), the first [Prime Minister of Singapore](350647.md), on 9 August 1965.[^cite_note-\:0-2] Its announcement marked the end of Singapore's status as a constituent state within the Federation of Malaysia, established on 16 September 1963, and signified the beginning of a new chapter of full sovereignty and national responsibility in both domestic governance and international affairs.

The Proclamation was broadcast nationwide over [Radio Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediacorp) at 10\:00 [SST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_Standard_Time) on the same day, with the official reading performed by announcer Steven Lee.[^cite_note-\:1-3] It was also published in the *Government Gazette*, reinforcing its legal and constitutional weight.[^cite_note-\:0-2] The original signed copy of the document is currently preserved in the [National Archives of Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Archives_of_Singapore), and remains an enduring artefact of the republic's founding moment.[^cite_note-4]

In 2015, to commemorate [Singapore's 50th year of independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_jubilee#Singapore), a 2012 audio recording of the Proclamation read by Lee Kuan Yew was broadcast as part of the National Day celebrations.[^cite_note-\:1-3][^cite_note-5] Meanwhile, a distinct but related document, the *Proclamation on Singapore*, was issued by [Tunku Abdul Rahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunku_Abdul_Rahman), [Prime Minister of Malaysia](496731.md), to formally acknowledge Singapore's independence. This Malaysian proclamation was appended to the [Independence of Singapore Agreement 1965](36205345.md) as an annex, affirming the bilateral and international recognition of Singapore's sovereign status.
