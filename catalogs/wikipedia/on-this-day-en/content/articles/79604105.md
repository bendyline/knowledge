&#x20;The **2025–26 EFL Championship** (referred to as the **Sky Bet Championship** for sponsorship purposes) was the 10th season of the [EFL Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EFL_Championship) under its current title and the 34th season under its current league division format.

The 2025–26 season consisted of 33 weekend rounds, 9 midweek rounds and 4 bank holiday rounds.[^cite_note-3]
