**Mambasa Territory** is an administrative area in the [Ituri Province](1545860.md) of the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md). The headquarters is in the town of [Mambasa](33379564.md).[^cite_note-2] Mambasa Territory is threatened with deforestation due to illegal forestry to meet high demand for lumber by the bordering countries of Rwanda, Uganda, Burundi and Kenya, as well as to slash and burn cultivation and growing demand for fuel-wood by large numbers of immigrants from the east.[^cite_note-3]

## Politics

Mambasa Territory is represented in the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo) by two deputies:

- Jefferson Abdallah ([PPRD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Party_for_Reconstruction_and_Democracy))

- Grégoire Lusenge (RCD/K-ML)
