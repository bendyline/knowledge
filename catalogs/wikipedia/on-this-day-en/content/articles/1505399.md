# Lying in state

For the band, see [Lying in States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lying_in_States).



[Philip IV of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_IV_of_France) lying in state



Brazilian president [Afonso Pena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afonso_Pena) lying in state in the [Catete Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catete_Palace), 1909

**Lying in state** is the tradition in which the body of a deceased [official](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official), such as a [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state), is placed in a state building, either outside or inside a [coffin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffin), to allow the public to pay their respects. It traditionally takes place in a major government building of a country, state, or city. While the practice differs among countries, in the United States, a viewing in a location other than a government building, such as a church, may be referred to as [lying in repose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lying_in_repose).[^cite_note-1] These rituals are in effect a more formal and public [wake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wake_\(ceremony\)) or funeral [viewing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viewing_\(funeral\)).[^cite_note-2] Lying in state may precede a [state funeral](714370.md), or it may be the public honor preceding a private funeral.

## Canada

In Canada, official lying in state is a part of a [state funeral](714370.md), an honour generally reserved for former [governors general](38152.md) and former [prime ministers](24135.md). It is held in the [Centre Block](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_Block) of [Parliament Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_Hill), in the national capital, [Ottawa](22219.md), Ontario. Ex-governors general lie in state in the [Senate Chamber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_Canada) while former prime ministers lie in the Hall of Honour. During the period of lying in state, the caskets are flanked at each corner by a [Guard of honour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guard_of_honour), composed of four members drawn from the [Canadian Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Forces) and [Royal Canadian Mounted Police](69080.md), as well as members of the [Governor General's Foot Guards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_General's_Foot_Guards) for former governors general, and guards from the parliamentary security forces for former prime ministers. Guards stand at each corner with heads bowed and weapons inverted (resting on Arms reversed) with their backs turned towards the casket.

Provinces may also mount state funerals and have a lying in state for a distinguished former resident. For instance, [Maurice Richard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurice_Richard), nationally known hockey player, was given a state funeral by the province of [Quebec](7954867.md) when he died in 2000; his coffin lay in state at the [Molson Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Centre).[^cite_note-3] This process was repeated for fellow [Canadiens](42966.md) players [Jean Béliveau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_B%C3%A9liveau) in December 2014 and [Guy Lafleur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Lafleur) in May 2022.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

Upon his death in October 2012, 24th [lieutenant governor of Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant_Governor_of_Ontario) and former member of [parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Canada) [Lincoln Alexander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Alexander) received only the second provincial state funeral in the province.[^cite_note-6] Ontario staged its first state funeral in October 1982 for former [premier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_of_Ontario) [John Robarts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Robarts).[^cite_note-7]

Alexander lay in state in the [Ontario Legislative Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontario_Legislative_Building) in [Toronto](64646.md) then in repose in Hamilton City Hall, his hometown.[^cite_note-8] The service was held in [Hamilton Place](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FirstOntario_Concert_Hall). During the procession from city hall, the casket was escorted by mounted police officers, marching police, firefighters and military and a massed pipe band representing several police and fire services.[^cite_note-9]

On 2 July 2019, [Lieutenant Governor of Saskatchewan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant_Governor_of_Saskatchewan) [W. Thomas Molloy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W._Thomas_Molloy) died. He was installed as the 22nd lieutenant governor on 21 March 2018, and approximately a year later, diagnosed with pancreatic cancer. His state funeral service was held at [Merlis Belsher Place](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merlis_Belsher_Place) on the campus of the [University of Saskatchewan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Saskatchewan). Malloy had earlier served as chancellor of the university. Members of the public were able to sign books of condolence at the [Saskatchewan Legislative Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saskatchewan_Legislative_Building) and [Government House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_House_\(Saskatchewan\)) in Regina and city hall in [Saskatoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saskatoon).

[Premier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_of_Saskatchewan) [Scott Moe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Moe) stated that flags in the province would fly at half-mast until sunset on the day of Malloy's funeral, which was later scheduled for 13 July. During the service, a detail of [Royal Canadian Mounted Police](69080.md) guarded the casket and following, members of 15 Wing Moose Jaw from the Royal Canadian Air Force flew over Merlis Belsher Place.[^cite_note-10]

[Jocelyne Roy-Vienneau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jocelyne_Roy-Vienneau), [Lieutenant Governor of New Brunswick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant_Governor_of_New_Brunswick), died 2 August 2019, after holding office since 23 October 2014. She received a state funeral 8 August 2019 and the flag on the [Peace Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace_Tower) of the [Parliament Buildings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_Hill) flew at half-mast from 6 August through 8 August.[^cite_note-11]

Municipalities may offer civic funerals to prominent deceased current or former politicians.

## North Korea

In North Korea, the body of the late leader [Kim Jong Il](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Jong_Il) was embalmed and displayed in a glass coffin surrounded with red flowers at the [Kumsusan Palace of the Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumsusan_Palace_of_the_Sun) in [Pyongyang](47948.md) prior to his funeral, which began and ended at the palace. An honour guard armed with AK-47s was present. Kim's father [Kim Il Sung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Il_Sung), the founding president, was also embalmed, placed in a glass casket, and is on display elsewhere in the palace.[^cite_note-12]

During the time of the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) (1917–1991), the [state funerals](714370.md) of the most senior political and military leaders, such as [Vladimir Lenin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Lenin#Death_and_funeral:_1923%E2%80%931924), [Joseph Stalin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Stalin#Death,_funeral_and_aftermath), [Leonid Brezhnev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonid_Brezhnev#Last_years_and_death), [Yuri Andropov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_Andropov#Death_and_funeral), and [Konstantin Chernenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konstantin_Chernenko#Health_problems,_death_and_legacy) all followed the same basic outline. They took place in Moscow, beginning with a public lying in state of the deceased in the [House of the Unions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_the_Unions), and ending with an interment at [Red Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Square).

For the lying in state at the [House of the Unions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_the_Unions), the coffin would be placed on display in the Column Hall, which would be decorated by flowers, numerous [red flags](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_flag_\(politics\)) and other [communist symbols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_symbolism). The mourners, which usually would be brought in by the thousands, shuffled up a marble staircase beneath chandeliers draped in black gauze. On the stage at the left side of the Column Hall, a full orchestra in black tailcoats played classical music. The deceased's [embalmed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embalmed) body, dressed in a black suit, white shirt and a tie, was displayed in an open coffin on a [catafalque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catafalque) banked with carnations, red roses and tulips, facing the queue of mourners. A small [guard of honour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guard_of_honour) would be in attendance in the background. At the right side of the hall, seats were placed for guests of honour, with the front row reserved for the dead leader's family.

On the day of the funeral, a military funeral parade would take place during which the coffin would be conveyed from the House of the Unions to Red Square where burial would take place. Lenin and Stalin were placed inside the [Lenin Mausoleum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenin_Mausoleum), while Brezhnev, Andropov, and Chernenko were interred in individual graves in the [Kremlin Wall Necropolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kremlin_Wall_Necropolis).

## Singapore

At [state funerals](714370.md) in Singapore, the state flag is draped over the coffin. The vigil guard may be deployed during the public lying in state of the deceased person at [Parliament House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_House,_Singapore). The deployment of the vigil guard is the highest form of respect accorded by the nation to the deceased.

Similar to British traditions, the vigil guard is composed of groups of five [commissioned officers](36301328.md) from the [Singapore Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_Armed_Forces) (SAF) and [Singapore Police Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_Police_Force) (SPF) who stand guard around the clock in shifts of 30 minutes. Four of the five officers stand facing outward at each of the four corners of the coffin, while the fifth and most senior stands in front and faces inward. Their heads are bowed and their ceremonial swords are inverted.[^cite_note-13]

Vigil guards were stationed at the public lying in state of [Goh Keng Swee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goh_Keng_Swee#Death) in May 2010,[^cite_note-14] [Lee Kuan Yew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Kuan_Yew#Illness_and_death) in March 2015,[^cite_note-15] and [S. R. Nathan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S._R._Nathan#Death) in August 2016.[^cite_note-16]

## South Africa

Main article: [Death and state funeral of Nelson Mandela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_and_state_funeral_of_Nelson_Mandela)

[Nelson Mandela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nelson_Mandela) was the first democratically elected president to lie in state in South Africa. The event took place at the [Union Buildings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Buildings) in [Pretoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pretoria), the same site where he was inaugurated as the [president of South Africa](321700.md) on 10 May 1994. Mandela lay in state from 11 December through 13 December 2013 with thousands of South Africans filing past the coffin before it was flown to [Mthatha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mthatha) where Mandela was buried on 15 December 2013 in nearby [Qunu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qunu) in the Eastern Cape.[^cite_note-17]

From 30 December to 31 December 2021,[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] revered anti-apartheid fighter [Archbishop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archbishop) [Desmond Tutu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desmond_Tutu) lay in state at [St. George's Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._George's_Cathedral,_Cape_Town) in [Cape Town](6653.md) prior to his funeral being held at the same location on 1 January 2022.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22]

## United Kingdom

See also: [State funerals in the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_funerals_in_the_United_Kingdom) and [Vigil of the Princes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vigil_of_the_Princes)



The lying in state of King [Edward VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_VII) in Westminster Hall, May 1910



The lying in state of Queen [Elizabeth II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_II) in Westminster Hall, September 2022

In [state and ceremonial](714370.md) funerals in the United Kingdom, lying in state traditionally takes place in [Westminster Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westminster_Hall). The body is placed in a closed coffin on a [catafalque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catafalque) and is guarded, around the clock, by detachments from the following units:

- [Sovereign's Bodyguard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sovereign's_Bodyguard)&#x20;

  - [His Majesty's Bodyguard of the Honourable Corps of Gentlemen-at-Arms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honourable_Corps_of_Gentlemen_at_Arms)

  - [The King's Bodyguard of the Yeomen of the Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeomen_of_the_Guard)

  - [The Royal Company of Archers, The King's Bodyguard for Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Company_of_Archers)

- [Household Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Household_Division)&#x20;

  - [Household Cavalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Household_Cavalry)&#x20;

    - [The Life Guards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life_Guards_\(Britain\))

    - [The Blues and Royals (Royal Horse Guards and 1st Dragoons)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Blues_and_Royals_\(Royal_Horse_Guards_and_1st_Dragoons\))

  - [Foot Guards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foot_Guards)&#x20;

    - [Grenadier Guards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grenadier_Guards)

    - [Coldstream Guards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coldstream_Guards)

    - [Scots Guards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scots_Guards)

    - [Irish Guards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Guards)

    - [Welsh Guards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welsh_Guards)



Diagram showing the positions of the guards during the lying in state prior to a State Funeral - the cross is at the head of the coffin:\

There are differences between lying in state at a State Funeral, and that of a Ceremonial Funeral. At a State Funeral, the guard detachment consists of ten members, with six drawn from the Sovereign's Bodyguard and four from the Household Division. The four members of the Household Division, all of whom are officers, and who are drawn either from the two regiments of the Household Cavalry (one pair from each), or one of the Foot Guards regiments, form the 'Inner Guard'; they stand one each corner of the catafalque next to the coffin. The six members of the Sovereign's Bodyguard consist of four from the Yeomen of the Guard, and two from either the Gentlemen-at-Arms or the Royal Company of Archers, and form the 'Outer Guard'. The Yeomen of the Guard take position at the corners of the dais on which the catafalque is mounted, outside the officers from the Household Division, while the final two take position on the dais at the foot of the coffin.

Each detachment stands vigil for twenty minutes, facing out from the coffin with heads bowed and weapons inverted. The Yeomen of the Guard maintain a constant presence throughout the period of lying in state, while the other units rotate every six hours.[^cite_note-23] At a Ceremonial Funeral, such as [that of Queen Elizabeth the Queen Mother](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_and_funeral_of_Queen_Elizabeth_the_Queen_Mother), only the Inner Guard stands vigil, with detachments of four from each of the ten named units at their post for twenty minutes, and units rotating every six hours.[^cite_note-24]

On three occasions, the guard has been [mounted by four members](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vigil_of_the_Princes) of the [royal family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_royal_family). At the lying in state of [King George V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_George_V) in 1936, the guard was mounted by his four sons [King Edward VIII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Edward_VIII), the [Duke of York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_VI) (future George VI), the [Duke of Gloucester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Henry,_Duke_of_Gloucester) and the [Duke of Kent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_George,_Duke_of_Kent). For [Queen Elizabeth the Queen Mother](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Elizabeth_the_Queen_Mother)'s lying-in-state in 2002, the guard was mounted by her four grandsons, the [Prince of Wales](125248.md) (future Charles III), the [Duke of York](125267.md), the [Earl of Wessex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Edward,_Duke_of_Edinburgh), and [Viscount Linley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Armstrong-Jones,_2nd_Earl_of_Snowdon).[^cite_note-25] All Queen [Elizabeth II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_II)'s children took guard at 19\:40 BST on 12 September 2022 at the lying-in-repose at [St Giles' Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Giles'_Cathedral), [Edinburgh](9602.md). During this vigil, [Anne, Princess Royal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne,_Princess_Royal) became the first woman ever to stand guard.[^cite_note-26] As part of the late Queen's lying in state in London, at 19\:30 BST on 16 September 2022, her four children stood vigil for the second time.[^cite_note-27] The Queen's eight grandchildren held a vigil the following day on 17 September 2022.[^cite_note-28] On the same day, the UK's [service chiefs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiefs_of_Staff_Committee) – [Admiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Admiral_\(Royal_Navy\)) Sir [Tony Radakin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Radakin) ([Chief of the Defence Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_the_Defence_Staff_\(United_Kingdom\))), Admiral Sir [Ben Key](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Key) ([First Sea Lord and Chief of the Naval Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Sea_Lord_and_Chief_of_the_Naval_Staff)), [General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_\(United_Kingdom\)) Sir [Patrick Sanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Sanders_\(British_Army_officer\)) ([Chief of the General Staff](691314.md)) and [Air Chief Marshal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Chief_Marshal) Sir [Michael Wigston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Wigston) ([Chief of the Air Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_the_Air_Staff_\(United_Kingdom\))) – stood guard over the catafalque for 20 minutes in place of four officers from the foot guards.[^cite_note-29]

The tradition of lying in state in the United Kingdom dates to the [Stuart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuart_period) sovereigns in the seventeenth century.[^cite_note-metro-30] The first British monarch to lie in state in Westminster Hall was King [Edward VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_VII) in 1910, apparently following [William Gladstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Ewart_Gladstone) in 1898.[^cite_note-31] [Queen Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Victoria) requested that she should not lie in state but after her death at [Osborne House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osborne_House) on the [Isle of Wight](15102.md) was given a semi-private lying in state for family and servants to pay their respects.[^cite_note-metro-30]

## United States

See also: [State funerals in the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_funerals_in_the_United_States)



President [Jimmy Carter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Carter) lying in state in the [Capitol rotunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Capitol_rotunda) before his [state funeral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_and_state_funeral_of_Jimmy_Carter), 7 January 2025

Lying in state in the United States is the rare honour either authorized by a congressional resolution or approved by the congressional leadership, when permission is granted by survivors, to a deceased member of government (or former member) whereby their remains are placed in the [rotunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Capitol_rotunda) of the [United States Capitol](31979.md) in [Washington, D.C.](108956.md) for public viewing.[^cite_note-32] The casket is guarded by members of the six branches of the [United States armed forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_armed_forces). By regulation and custom, only presidents, military commanders, justices of the Supreme Court, and [members of Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_Congress) are granted the honour of lying in state.

It differs from lying in honour in that individuals lying in state have guards of honour representing each branch of the U.S. Armed Forces, while individuals lying in honour have the [U.S. Capitol Police](1915214.md) as civilian guards of honour. Six Americans have lain in honour, beginning in 1998.[^cite_note-WYFF-33] In 2005, [Rosa Parks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosa_Parks) became the first woman to lie in honour in the United States Capitol.[^cite_note-34]

Except for presidents and former presidents, the honour is not automatic. Not all those entitled to the honour have it accepted by their survivors. The first leader to receive this honour was [Henry Clay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Clay), former [Speaker of the House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_of_the_United_States_House_of_Representatives), when he died in 1852. Since then, the honour has been extended to 36 people, including 12 presidents and four unknown soldiers, representing U.S. service members who have died without their remains being identified. [Harry Truman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Truman), [Richard Nixon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Nixon) and their families declined services at the Capitol.[^cite_note-35] To date, most of those who have lain in state have done so upon the [catafalque constructed for the funeral of Abraham Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Catafalque). Those who have lain in honour have been borne on other [biers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bier).[^cite_note-36]

### States and cities

Some U.S. states and cities extend similar honours.

#### Alabama

Because of his role in the [Selma to Montgomery marches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selma_to_Montgomery_marches) during the [civil rights movement](49001.md), Georgia based U.S. Congress John Lewis, who was also originally a native of Alabama, would lie in state for one day in the [Alabama Capitol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alabama_State_Capitol) in Montgomery on 26 July 2020.[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-liesingeorgiaalabama-38]

#### Arizona

Upon his death in August 2018, U.S. Senator [John McCain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_McCain) lay in state at the [State Capitol](2181578.md) of his home state of [Arizona](21883824.md) before receiving the same honour in the U.S. Capitol two days later.[^cite_note-\:0-39]

#### California

After a memorial service outside the Polk Street entrance of San Francisco City Hall on 29 November 1978, Mayor [George Moscone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Moscone) and city Supervisor [Harvey Milk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_Milk) lay in state in the Rotunda until 30 November, after [their assassination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscone%E2%80%93Milk_assassinations) by former Supervisor [Dan White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_White). The chamber was also filled with more than 400 floral tributes to the two men.[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-KRON-41]

In [San Francisco](49728.md), Mayor [Ed Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Lee_\(politician\)) lay in state in the Rotunda of [San Francisco City Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_City_Hall) after his death in office on 12 December 2017.[^cite_note-Haigney-42] Senator [Dianne Feinstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dianne_Feinstein), who served as San Francisco mayor from 1978 to 1988, also lay in state in the rotunda on 4 October 2023, prior to a funeral service the following day.[^cite_note-43]

#### Georgia

On 29 July 2020, U.S. Representative [John Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Lewis_\(civil_rights_leader\)) received similar honours from his home state of Georgia and also was the first African American member of Congress to lie in state in the Capitol Rotunda at the [Georgia Capitol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_State_Capitol) in Atlanta.[^cite_note-44][^cite_note-liesingeorgiaalabama-38]

#### Hawaii

Eight people have lain in state at the [Hawaii State Capitol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawaii_State_Capitol), among which include former Governor [John A. Burns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_A._Burns) in 1975, U.S. Senator [Spark Matsunaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spark_Matsunaga) in 1990, famous Hawaiian singer [Israel Kamakawiwoʻole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Kamakawiwo%CA%BBole) upon his death in 1997, U.S. Representative [Patsy Mink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patsy_Mink) in 2002, former U.S. Senator [Hiram Fong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiram_Fong) in 2004, [Daniel Inouye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Inouye), president pro tempore of the U.S. Senate, in 2012, and former U.S. Senator [Daniel Akaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Akaka) in 2018.[^cite_note-45]

#### Illinois

On October 31–November 1, 1893, [Carter Harrison III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carter_Harrison_III), the slain [mayor of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayor_of_Chicago), lay in state at the city hall of [Chicago](6886.md) as part of [his public funeral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funeral_of_Carter_Harrison_III). Harrison [had been assassinated days earlier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassination_of_Carter_Harrison_III).[^cite_note-46]

In March 1902, [John Peter Altgeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Peter_Altgeld), who worked as an attorney in Chicago after serving as governor 1893–1897, died after suffering poor health most of his life. Altgeld lay in state in the [Chicago Public Library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Cultural_Center) after which he was buried in [Graceland Cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graceland_Cemetery) on the city's north side.[^cite_note-47]

On July 16, 1965, [Adlai Stevenson II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adlai_Stevenson_II) who served as [Illinois Governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_governors_of_Illinois) from 1949 to 1953 and [Ambassador to the United Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ambassadors_of_the_United_States_to_the_United_Nations) from January 1961 to July 1965, lay in state in the rotunda of the [Illinois Capitol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illinois_State_Capitol) prior to his burial in Bloomington. Stevenson was also the Democratic candidate for the presidency in 1952 and 1956.[^cite_note-48]

On 19 September 2025, [Jim Edgar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Edgar) became only the second Illinois governor since 1900 to lie in state in the Illinois Capitol.[^cite_note-49]

From 26 to 27 February 2026, Rev. [Jesse Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_Jackson), a renowned civil rights activist who was also a protege of [Martin Luther King Jr.](20076.md), would in state at [Rainbow/PUSH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainbow/PUSH) headquarters in [Chicago](6886.md).[^cite_note-50][^cite_note-Tareen-51][^cite_note-52]

#### Kentucky

Through 2022, [23 people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentucky_State_Capitol#People_who_have_lain_in_state_in_the_Rotunda) have lain in state at the [Kentucky Capitol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentucky_State_Capitol) building rotunda in Frankfort. This includes not only politicians, but also civilians. Kentucky-based [Kentucky Fried Chicken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentucky_Fried_Chicken) (KFC) founder [Colonel Harland Sanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonel_Harland_Sanders) would also be among those who received the honor of lying in state in the Kentucky Capitol Rotunda following his death in 1980.[^cite_note-53] After his death in November 2022, former Kentucky governor and renowned restaurant business mogul [John Y. Brown Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Y._Brown_Jr.) would also be among those who had the honor of lying in state at the Kentucky Capitol as well.[^cite_note-54] Former Kentucky Governor [Brereton C. Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brereton_C._Jones) would lie in state at the Kentucky Capitol following his death in September 2023 as well.[^cite_note-55][^cite_note-56] On 9 November 2025, former Kentucky governor [Martha Layne Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martha_Layne_Collins), the state's first female governor, would lie in state at the state capitol's [Old State Capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_State_Capitol_\(Kentucky\)) building.[^cite_note-57][^cite_note-58][^cite_note-59]

#### Michigan

In Michigan, only four [governors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_Michigan) have lain in state in the [Capitol Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_Capitol). The first was [Frank Fitzgerald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Fitzgerald) who died while in office, on 18 March 1939.[^cite_note-60] Second was [Chase Osborn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Osborn) who held office from 1911 to 1913 and died in April 1949.[^cite_note-61] The two most recent were [George W. Romney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_W._Romney) on 29 July 1995, who served as governor from 1963 to 1969.[^cite_note-62] On 26 October 2010, the casket of Michigan's first governor [Stevens T. Mason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stevens_T._Mason) (1835–1840) lay in the capitol rotunda for a day before being re-interred in a monument in [Detroit](8687.md) after the monument was renovated and relocated in [Capitol Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitol_Park_Historic_District), the site of Michigan's first capitol building.[^cite_note-63]

[Frank Murphy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Murphy), who served as judge in [Detroit Recorder's Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recorder's_Court_\(Detroit\)) (1923–1930), [Mayor of Detroit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_mayors_of_Detroit) (1930–1933), Governor of Michigan (1937–1939) and Associate Justice of the Supreme Court (1940–1949), lay in state in [Detroit City Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit_City_Hall) in July 1949 prior to a [Requiem Mass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Requiem) and burial in [Harbor Beach, Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harbor_Beach,_Michigan).[^cite_note-64]

During funeral rites of civil rights icon [Rosa Parks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosa_Parks) in Detroit in November 2005, she lay in repose at the [Charles H. Wright Museum of African American History](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_H._Wright_Museum_of_African_American_History) before services and burial.[^cite_note-65] This followed similar arrangements in December 1997 for Mayor [Coleman Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coleman_Young), the first [African-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African-American) to hold that office.[^cite_note-66][^cite_note-patricia-67]

Upon the deaths of singer-songwriter [Aretha Franklin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aretha_Franklin) in August 2018 and former Congresswoman [Barbara-Rose Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara-Rose_Collins) in November 2021, both women were also honoured at the Wright Museum.[^cite_note-68][^cite_note-69]

In November 2019, former Congressman [John Conyers Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Conyers_Jr.) became the latest person to lie in state at the Wright Museum.[^cite_note-70][^cite_note-71][^cite_note-72]

#### Minnesota

On 27 June 2025, former [speaker of the Minnesota House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_of_the_Minnesota_House_of_Representatives) [Melissa Hortman](6892027.md), lay in state with her husband Mark and [dog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Retriever) Gilbert. #### New York

In New York, few have been afforded the honor of lying in state in the [state capitol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_State_Capitol). Two of the most recent figures to lie in state in city hall are city councilman [James Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_E._Davis_\(New_York_politician\)) in 2003 and former [U.S. Rep](19468510.md) [Charles Rangel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Rangel) in 2025.[^cite_note-rangel-78]

## Vatican City



[Pope John Paul II](23805.md)'s body lying in state

Prior to 2000, when *[Ordo Exsequiarum Romani Pontificis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordo_Exsequiarum_Romani_Pontificis)* was published under the pontificate of [Pope John Paul II](23805.md), the Vatican had no prescribed funeral rites for a deceased pope. Funerals were conducted based upon past practices with alterations according to instructions the pope wrote during his lifetime. The funerals of [John Paul II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_and_funeral_of_Pope_John_Paul_II) and [Benedict XVI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_and_funeral_of_Pope_Benedict_XVI) followed these instructions. On the first day of lying in state, John Paul II laid in the chapel of the [Apostolic Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apostolic_Palace) to allow members of the Papal household and Vatican officials to pay respects. However, Benedict XVI and Francis laid in state in the chapel of [Domus Sanctae Marthae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domus_Sanctae_Marthae) as it was their residence at the time of their deaths.[^cite_note-BBC-Francis-92]



Lying in state ceremony for [Pope Francis](1687680.md)

After a Mass of Visitation the following morning, a procession moves the body to [St. Peter's Basilica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Peter's_Basilica). It was placed on a [catafalque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catafalque) so the public may pay respects for three days. The [Dean of the College of Cardinals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_of_the_College_of_Cardinals) celebrates a [Requiem Mass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Requiem_Mass) on the fourth day.[^cite_note-93][^cite_note-94]

[Pope Francis](1687680.md) published a revised *Ordo Exsequiarum Romani Pontificis* in 2024 and his 2025 funeral followed the new rites. His revisions dispensed with the large catafalque in the basilica and his body lay in an open coffin on a low platform. The coffin itself differed from those of his predecessors who were buried in a three-part coffin of cypress, lead and oak. Per his wishes, Francis was placed in a simple wooden coffin lined with zinc.[^cite_note-95]

An earlier revision that both editions incorporated has been the number of candles surrounding the catafalque or bier. Upon the death of [Pope John XXIII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_John_XXIII), the catafalque was surrounded by 24 candles as was the catafalque of many of his predecessors.[^cite_note-96][^cite_note-97] [Pope Paul VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Paul_VI), requested only a single paschal candle placed near the catafalque, a practice continued by all subsequent pontiffs to date.[^cite_note-98][^cite_note-99][^cite_note-100]

Upon their death, [cardinals](6221.md) do not lie in state. Their casket is usually placed in vigil on the floor before the altar of the church of [St Stephen of the Abyssinians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Stephen_of_the_Abyssinians), just behind [St. Peter's Basilica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Peter's_Basilica). During this period, mourners may pay respects. The Requiem Mass is usually held within four days after death and is presided by the senior cardinal available. It is held at the [Altar of the Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chair_of_Saint_Peter) behind the [high altar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Peter's_Baldachin) of St. Peter's Basilica. The pope will attend if he is not traveling, and may participate or simply observe.[^cite_note-101][^cite_note-102]

## Coptic Patriarchate

The [Coptic Orthodox Church of Alexandria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coptic_Orthodox_Church_of_Alexandria) has a tradition of mummifying the deceased pope and placing his body on his throne to enable church followers to have a farewell look at him for a short period of time. This tradition is one of the ways that ancient Egyptian rituals have survived in the Egyptian church.[^cite_note-104]
