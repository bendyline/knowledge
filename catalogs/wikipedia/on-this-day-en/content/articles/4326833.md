Tourism is a major industry in [Tokyo](30057.md). In 2006, there were 420 million visits by [Japanese people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_people) and 4.81 million visits by foreigners. The economic value of tourist visits to Tokyo totaled ¥9.4 trillion [yen](34392.md).&#x20;

After a slow down due to closed borders due to COVID from early 2020[^cite_note-1] to the later part of 2020,[^cite_note-2] in 2022 Tokyo saw a growth of 542 million visits to Tokyo by Japan residents, and 33.13 million visits from overseas.[^cite_note-3]

Many [tourists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourism_in_Japan) visit the various urban districts, stores, and entertainment districts throughout the neighborhoods of the [special wards of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_wards_of_Tokyo).

## Tourist destinations

Japanese [schoolchildren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_in_Japan) on class trips typically visit [Tokyo Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Tower). Cultural offerings in Tokyo include both omnipresent [Japanese pop culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_pop_culture) and associated districts such as [Shibuya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shibuya,_Tokyo) and [Harajuku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harajuku), subcultural attractions such as [Studio Ghibli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Studio_Ghibli) anime center. [Tokyo National Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_National_Museum) houses over a third of the [National Treasures of Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Treasures_of_Japan). No buildings in Tokyo are listed as [World Heritage Sites](44940.md). Among buildings, only the Jizo Hall of [Shofuku-ji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sh%C5%8Dfuku-ji_\(Higashimurayama\)), a suburban temple, is a [National treasure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Treasures_of_Japan). Apart from the top attractions like [Tokyo Disneyland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Disneyland), [DisneySea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_DisneySea), [Tokyo Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Tower), [Skytree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Skytree), other popular attractions include the [Imperial Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kokyo), [Meiji Shrine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meiji_Shrine), and [Sensō-ji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sens%C5%8D-ji), a popular temple. Many tourists, particularly foreigners, visit [Tsukiji Fish Market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsukiji_Fish_Market). Contrary to a common misconception, Tokyo has many green spaces in the city center and its suburbs.[^cite_note-4]

## Transportation and Hotel Districts

Access to Tokyo is provided by airports including [Narita Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narita_Airport), [Tokyo International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haneda_Airport) (Haneda, providing primarily domestic service) for both international and domestic tourists. [Shinkansen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinkansen) is the most popular mode of transport for travelers from other Japanese cities.&#x20;

The transportation from the airports to various districts of Tokyo include trains[^cite_note-6] and Airport Limousine busses.[^cite_note-7]

Major hotel districts include [Shinjuku](147734.md) and [Tokyo Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Bay), although there are hotels in all the districts, all over Tokyo.
