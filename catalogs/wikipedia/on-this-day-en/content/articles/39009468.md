# Goppenstein

**Goppenstein** (1,216 m) is a [Swiss](26748.md) hamlet in the canton of [Valais](198789.md), located in the lower [Lötschental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%B6tschental) and traversed by the Lonza river. It belongs to the municipality of [Ferden](7139986.md).



Goppenstein

Goppenstein is mainly known for [its railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goppenstein_railway_station) on the [Lötschberg railway line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%B6tschberg_railway_line),[^cite_note-1] at the southern entrance of the [Lötschberg Tunnel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%B6tschberg_Tunnel) leading to [Kandersteg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kandersteg). Road vehicles can be carried through the tunnel to Kandersteg by open sided car shuttle trains.
