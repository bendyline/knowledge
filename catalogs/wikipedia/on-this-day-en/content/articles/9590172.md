**Harsin** ([Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdish_language): هەرسین, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Kurdish): *Hersîn*, [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): هرسين, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Persian): *Harsīn*) is a city in the [Central District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_District_\(Harsin_County\)) of [Harsin County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harsin_County), [Kermanshah](448078.md) province, [Iran](14653.md), serving as capital of both the county and the district.[^cite_note-Harsin_County_Establishment-3] Harsin was founded in 550 BC.

## Demographics

### Language and ethnicity

The city is populated by different [Kurdish](17068.md) tribes including the Osmanvand and the Jalalvand who speak [Laki Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laki_language).[^cite_note-4]

The Kurds of Harsin have their own subdialect of [Laki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laki_language) known either "Harsini" or "Kermanshahi Laki", a vernacular between [Xwarini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Kurdish) and [Laki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laki_language) Proper.[^cite_note-5]

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the city's population was 51,562 in 12,001 households.[^cite_note-2006_Kermanshah_Province-6] The following census in 2011 counted 49,967 people in 13,700 households.[^cite_note-2011_Kermanshah_Province-7] The 2016 census measured the population of the city as 44,146 people in 12,814 households.[^cite_note-2016_Kermanshah_Province-2]
