The **Head of the Security Service of Ukraine** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Голова Служби безпеки України, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Ukrainian): *Holova Sluzhby bezpeky Ukrainy*) serves as the director of the [Security Service of Ukraine](709222.md) (SBU), the internal [security agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security_agency) of the [Ukrainian government](1372585.md). The head of the Security Service of Ukraine is *[ex officio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ex_officio)* a member of the [National Security and Defense Council of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security_and_Defense_Council_of_Ukraine), and is appointed or dismissed by the [Verkhovna Rada](844993.md) (Ukraine's national parliament) on proposition of the [President of Ukraine](20688479.md).
