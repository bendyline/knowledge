**Yudhvir Sethi** is an Indian politician from Jammu & Kashmir. He is a [Member of the Jammu & Kashmir Legislative Assembly](28625798.md) from [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Jammu_and_Kashmir_Legislative_Assembly_election), representing [Jammu East Assembly constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jammu_East_Assembly_constituency) as a Member of the [Bharatiya Janta Party](149330.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]
