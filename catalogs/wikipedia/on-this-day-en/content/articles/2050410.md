**Loreto**, officially the **Municipality of Loreto** ([Cebuano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cebuano_language): *Lungsod sa Loreto*; [Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language): *Bayan ng Loreto*), is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality_of_the_Philippines) in the [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Province) of [Agusan del Sur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agusan_del_Sur), [Philippines](23440.md). According to the 2024 census, it has a population of 43,761 people.[^cite_note-5] It is the largest municipality in terms of land area in Mindanao.

## History

In 1600, the Spanish conquistadors first came to the upper reaches of Agusan. In 1879, Fr. Saturnino Urios, a Spanish Jesuit missionary from Xativa, came to what was then known as Umajam and met the Manobos. Fr. Urios convinced the Manobos to relocate into one single settlement that he named as Loreto (not from his town in Spain, as sometimes it's said).

### Rain of fish

On a rainy morning on January 13, 2012, Loreto became nationally notable when it became the site of a [rain of fish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raining_animals#Fish). Seventy-two small fish were recovered and placed in an aquarium. They were about 3 in (7.6 cm) long and had small spots, but the [species](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Species) remains unknown. The [Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bureau_of_Fisheries_and_Aquatic_Resources_\(Philippines\)) attributes the phenomenon to a [waterspout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterspout).[^cite_note-W1-6][^cite_note-W2-7][^cite_note-W3-8]

## Geography

According to the [Philippine Statistics Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Statistics_Authority), the municipality has a land area of 1,462.74 square kilometres (564.77 sq mi) [^cite_note-nscb-area-9] constituting 14.64% of the 9,989.52-square-kilometre- (3,856.98 sq mi) total area of Agusan del Sur.

Loreto is a river town situated south-west of Agusan del Sur. It is bounded on the north by [La Paz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Paz,_Agusan_del_Sur), north-east by [Bunawan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bunawan,_Agusan_del_Sur), and southeast by [Veruela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veruela,_Agusan_del_Sur). The provinces of [Bukidnon](261222.md) and [Davao del Norte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davao_del_Norte) bound its Western and Southern portions.

Loreto is a strategically situated as a junction point to various destination within Agusan del Sur and nearby provinces. The accessibility of Loreto to Provincial Center, Patin-ay in Prosperidad has three main routes: the Loreto-Santa Josefa-Trento route of 118.36 kilometres (73.55 mi), the Loreto-La Paz Talacogon route of 85.53 kilometres (53.15 mi), and the Loreto-Bunawan route of 87.52 kilometres (54.38 mi) with two hours of Pump boat cruising the [Agusan River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agusan_River).
