**Lwandle** (from a [Xhosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xhosa_language) word meaning "ocean") is a township in [Strand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strand,_Western_Cape), a town in the eastern part of the [Cape Town](6653.md) Metro in the [Western Cape](313646.md) province of [South Africa](17416221.md). Administratively it is a suburb of the [City of Cape Town](4043753.md).[^cite_note-2] Under [Apartheid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apartheid), it was a [group area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_area) for black people. It is still predominantly populated by this community.
