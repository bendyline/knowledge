The **FIFA Futsal Women's World Cup** is an international [futsal](188197.md) competition set to be contested by the senior women's national teams of the member associations of [FIFA](11049.md), the sport's global governing body.

## History

The FIFA Futsal Women's World Cup was created by the [FIFA Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Council) in December 2022.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

FIFA had previously announced plans for a women's futsal competition in 2018 and received criticism from futsal players for their "public neglect" of the sport.[^cite_note-4]

For the [first edition](76913115.md), 16 teams participated in the tournament.[^cite_note-5] The inaugural edition was played in the [Philippines](23440.md) in 2025.[^cite_note-6] [Brazil](44894373.md) won the first championship.[^cite_note-7]

The second edition is scheduled to take place in 2029.[^cite_note-8]
