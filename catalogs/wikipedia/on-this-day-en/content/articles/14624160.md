Regions of Vietnam

**Central Vietnam** ([Vietnamese](32511.md): *Trung Bộ* or *miền Trung*), also known as **Middle Vietnam** or **The Middle**, formerly known as *Trung Việt* by the [State of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_Vietnam), *Trung Phần* by the [Republic of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Vietnam),[^cite_note-Mocban-State-of-Vietnam-administrative-divisions-1] *Trung Kỳ* or *[Annam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annam_\(French_protectorate\))* under French colonial rule, is one of the three geographical regions within [Vietnam](202354.md).

The name Trung Bộ was used by the emperor [Bảo Đại](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%E1%BA%A3o_%C4%90%E1%BA%A1i) when he established administrative level higher than [Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Vietnam) in 1945, instead of the Trung Kỳ which recalled the French occupation. This name was officially used by government of the [Democratic Republic of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Republic_of_Vietnam) and is popularly used today.
