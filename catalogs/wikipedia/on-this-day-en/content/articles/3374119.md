The **Minister of Sports** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Ministre des Sports*) is a [cabinet member](739237.md) in the [Government of France](3398091.md).

The position has changed names a number of times since its creation, having occasionally been discontinued or regrouped with the [Minister of National Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_National_Education_\(France\)). Since 2022 [Amélie Oudéa-Castéra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Am%C3%A9lie_Oud%C3%A9a-Cast%C3%A9ra) has served Minister for Sport and the Olympic & Paralympic Games.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTE''Gouvernement.fr''2018-1]
