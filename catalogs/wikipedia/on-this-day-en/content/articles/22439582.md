**Jilotlán de los Dolores&#x20;**&#x20;is a town and [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Jalisco), in [Jalisco](16020434.md) in central-western [Mexico](3966054.md). The municipality covers an area of 1511.78 km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 8,579.[^cite_note-1]
