The **lord mayor of Copenhagen** ([Danish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish_language): *Københavns Overborgmester*) is the [city](5166.md)'s mayor and the leader of the [Copenhagen City Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copenhagen_City_Council). Established in 1938, all [lord mayors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lord_mayor) have since belonged to the [Social Democratic Party](403131.md) until the [2025 Copenhagen City council election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Copenhagen_City_council_election).

## Current mayors

As of the [municipal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Denmark) [elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Election) of November 2021, Copenhagen has the following [mayors](101146.md):[^cite_note-6]

- Lord mayor: [Lars Weiss](https://da.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lars%20Weiss) ([Social Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialdemokraterne))

- Mayor of Culture: [Mia Nyegaard](https://da.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mia%20Nyegaard) ([Danish Social Liberal Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish_Social_Liberal_Party))

- Mayor of Children and Youths: [Jakob Næsager](https://da.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakob%20N%C3%A6sager) ([Conservative People's Party (Denmark)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservative_People's_Party_\(Denmark\)))

- Mayor of Health and Care: [Sisse Marie Welling](https://da.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sisse%20Marie%20Welling) ([Green Left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Left_\(Denmark\)))

- Mayor of Social Affairs: [Karina Vestergård Madsen](https://da.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karina%20Vesterg%C3%A5rd%20Madsen) ([Red-Green Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red%E2%80%93Green_Alliance_\(Denmark\)))

- Mayor of Technics and the Environment: [Line Barfod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_Barfod) ([Red-Green Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enhedslisten))

- Mayor of Employment and Integration: [Jens-Kristian Lütken](https://da.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jens-Kristian%20L%C3%BCtken) ([Liberal Party](403109.md))
