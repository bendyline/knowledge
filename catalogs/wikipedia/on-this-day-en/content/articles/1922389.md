The **General Confederation of the Portuguese Workers** ([Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language): *Confederação Geral dos Trabalhadores Portugueses*, **CGTP**) is the largest [trade union federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trade_union_federation) in [Portugal](23033.md). It was founded informally in 1970, emerged publicly after the [Carnation Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnation_Revolution) in 1974 and was legalised the following year by the [National Salvation Junta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Salvation_Junta).[^cite_note-1]
