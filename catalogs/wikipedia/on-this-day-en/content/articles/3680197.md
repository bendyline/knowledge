The **Government of Turkey** ([Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_language): *Türkiye Cumhuriyeti Hükûmeti*) is the [national government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_government) of [Turkey](11125639.md). It is governed as a [unitary state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unitary_state) under a [presidential](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_system) [representative democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Representative_democracy) and a [constitutional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Turkey) [republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic) within a [pluriform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diversity_\(politics\)) [multi-party system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-party_period_of_the_Republic_of_Turkey). The term government can mean either the collective set of institutions (the [executive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_\(government\)), [legislative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature), and [judicial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judiciary) branches) or specifically the [Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Turkey) (the executive).

In its [multi-party system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-party_period_of_the_Republic_of_Turkey) era, the longest-governing and still governing party is the [AK Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justice_and_Development_Party_\(Turkey\)) under former [Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Turkey) and now [President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Turkey), [Recep Tayyip Erdoğan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recep_Tayyip_Erdo%C4%9Fan). He abolished the [Parliamentary system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_system) in favour of a [Presidential system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_system) with the controversial[^cite_note-1] [2017 Turkish constitutional referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Turkish_constitutional_referendum).

However, as of 2026, there have been concerns from the [European Union](9317.md) regarding the state of the [Copenhagen criteria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copenhagen_criteria) and democracy in Turkey, which ended up indefinitely pausing [Turkey's EU membership talks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accession_of_Turkey_to_the_European_Union).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] [Freedom House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedom_House) has considered Turkey not free since at least 2017.[^cite_note-4] The end of [Turkey's EU membership talks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accession_of_Turkey_to_the_European_Union) contributed to the abolishment of the [Ministry of the EU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_European_Union_Affairs_\(Turkey\)) from the Turkish cabinet, along with cabinets that were abolished or reshuffled after the [2013 corruption scandal in Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_corruption_scandal_in_Turkey).

## Formation



Citizens casting vote in 2019 Local elections in a public school.

The cabinet of the republic is appointed by the [President of Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Turkey) under the [Turkish constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_constitution). According to the Constitution, Turkey's government system is based on a [separation of powers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Separation_of_powers). The Constitution states that the legislative power is vested in the [Grand National Assembly of Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_National_Assembly_of_Turkey) (art. 7). The president can appeal to the [Constitutional Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_Court_of_Turkey) for the annulment of all or certain provisions of laws on the grounds that they are unconstitutional in form or in content (art. 104/7). In such a case, the decision of the Constitutional Court is final (art. 153). That the executive power is carried out by the [President of Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Turkey) (art. 8) and that the judicial power is exercised by independent and impartial courts (art. 9) It also states that parliamentary elections and presidential elections shall be held every five years (art. 77).[^cite_note-5] The daily enforcement and administration of federal laws is in the hands of the various executive ministries, to deal with specific areas of national and international affairs. The [Cabinet of Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Turkey) includes the president and cabinet ministers. As part of the separation of the legislative branch from the executive branch, members of the cabinet cannot be a member of the parliament during their ministry.

In addition to ministries, a number of staff organizations are grouped into the Executive Office of the President. These include the [National Security Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security_Council_\(Turkey\)), the [State Supervisory Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Supervisory_Council), the [National Intelligence Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Intelligence_Organization_\(Turkey\)), the [Directorate of Communications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directorate_of_Communications), [Disaster and Emergency Management Presidency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disaster_and_Emergency_Management_Presidency) and the [Presidency of Strategy and Budget](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_of_Strategy_and_Budget). There are also [state-owned enterprises](204768.md) such as the [Turkish Electricity Transmission Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_Electricity_Transmission_Corporation). The government has to be formed after the elections.

- Presidential elections: A [two-round system](25932.md), with the top two candidates contesting a run-off election two weeks after the initial election should no candidate win at least 50% +1 of the popular vote.

- Parliamentary elections: The [D'Hondt method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D'Hondt_method), a [party-list proportional representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party-list_proportional_representation) system, to elect 600 Members of Parliament to the [Grand National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_National_Assembly_of_Turkey) from 87 [electoral districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_districts_of_Turkey) that elect different numbers of MPs depending on their populations.

- Local elections: Metropolitan and District Mayors, Municipal and Provincial Councillors, [neighbourhood presidents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhtar_\(title\)) and their village councils elected through a [first-past-the-post](261709.md) system, with the winning candidate in each municipality elected by a [simple majority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majority).

## Branches separated from the Cabinet

### Legislative branch.

Main article: [Parliament of Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Turkey)



Parliament of Turkey

Legislative power is vested in a single-chamber parliament (the [Grand National Assembly of Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_National_Assembly_of_Turkey)) with 600 members. The members are elected for a period of five years according to the [D'Hondt method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D'Hondt_method). Every citizen over the age of eighteen is eligible to be a deputy. Members of the Grand National Assembly can not hold office in state departments and other public corporate bodies and their subsidiaries.

Members of parliament are able to sit on behalf of a political party or as an [independent parliamentarian](2172378.md). They are also delegates for the province in which they are elected. A simple majority is required to amend a law and a three-fifths majority to amend the constitution. Bills can be introduced by any member of parliament.

The duties and powers of the Grand National Assembly of Turkey are to enact, amend, and repeal laws; to debate and adopt the budget bills and final accounts bills; to decide to declare war; to approve the ratification of international treaties, to decide with the majority of three-fifths of the Grand National Assembly of Turkey to proclaim amnesty and pardon; and to exercise the powers and carry out the duties envisaged in the other articles of the Constitution.

### Judicial branch

Main article: [Judicial system of Turkey](2963429.md)



[Court of Cassation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court_of_Cassation_of_Turkey) in Ankara, Turkey.

The [Constitutional Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_Court_of_Turkey), the [Court of Cassation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court_of_Cassation_of_Turkey), the [Council of State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_Council_of_State), and the [Court of Jurisdictional Disputes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court_of_Jurisdictional_Disputes) are the supreme courts mentioned in the judicial section of the [Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Turkey). The courts operate within the framework of [civil law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_law_\(legal_system\)).

The Constitutional Court checks whether laws are in accordance with the constitution. Since 2005, the legal principles as laid down in the various international human rights treaties have also been assessed. Government institutions, governing parties, and the opposition have direct access to the Court. Citizens can also plead the alleged unconstitutionality of a particular law in an ongoing lawsuit.[^cite_note-Nihan-9]

International treaties, on ratification by the Parliament, have hierarchically the same effect as codes and statutes. However, international treaty provisions involving basic rights and freedoms prevail against domestic codes and statutes.[^cite_note-Nihan-9]

There are also specialised courts for certain legal areas within the scope of the powers of civil courts such as cadastral courts, commercial courts, consumer courts, intellectual and industrial property courts, and labour courts. In certain disputes, some quasi-legal authorities must be used before applying to court such as the Sports Arbitration Committee and the [Turkish Football Federation](1281753.md) Arbitration Committee.[^cite_note-Nihan-9]

## Local administration

Main article: [Local government in Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_government_in_Turkey)

The Constitution enumerates local governments as municipalities, special provincial administrations and villages. The administration of the provinces is based on the principle of [devolution of powers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devolution).[^cite_note-10] The regulatory and budgetary autonomy of local governments is secured in the Constitution.[^cite_note-11]

A governor is representing the government in the province and is also the figurehead and executive organ of the special provincial administration. Governors act as the chairman of the provincial executive committee.[^cite_note-Types-12]
