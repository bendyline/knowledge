On 14 November 2025, an explosion occurred in Nowgam police station located on the outskirts of [Srinagar](294148.md), in the [Indian administered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_administered_Kashmir) union territory of [Jammu and Kashmir](61449812.md). The blast occurred while forensic and police teams were examining the explosive materials seized earlier during an investigation into a suspected [terrorist cell](14558092.md). The blast killed nine people and injured between 27 and 32 others. The Indian government ruled out any outside involvement and declared the incident as an accident.

## Background

In early November 2025, the [Jammu and Kashmir Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jammu_and_Kashmir_Police), along with the [Haryana Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haryana_Police), recovered more than 2,900 kg (6,400 lb) of explosives, [assault rifles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assault_rifles), [handguns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Handguns), [timing devices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timer), and [ammunition](18940621.md) from two houses in [Faridabad](1180794.md), a city adjacent to Delhi.[^cite_note-ND-1] These included chemicals such as [ammonium nitrate fuel oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ammonium_nitrate_bomb), [potassium nitrate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potassium_nitrate), and [sulphur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sulphur), and were subsequently transferred to Nowgam police station for forensic analysis.[^cite_note-Hindu-2] A team of forensic specialists, police personnel, and revenue officials assembled at the station on 14 November 2025 to examine the seized chemicals.[^cite_note-Reuters-3][^cite_note-AP-4]

## Explosion

At 11\:20 pm [IST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Standard_Time), a powerful explosion ripped through the police station complex. Subseuqent [CCTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CCTV) footage showed a large fireball followed by a shock wave that destroyed vehicles parked nearby and damaged adjacent buildings.[^cite_note-BBC-5] The blast triggered multiple smaller secondary explosions.[^cite_note-Reuters-3]

## Aftermath

The explosion caused severe structural damage to the police station building, apart from damage to vehicles and nearby structures.[^cite_note-BBC-5] Nine people were killed in the explosion and between 27 and 32 others were injured.[^cite_note-Reuters-3][^cite_note-AP-4] Those killed included three forensic experts, two revenue department officials, two police photographers, one investigative officer, and a civilian tailor. The injured included police personnel, government officials, and civilians residing nearby.[^cite_note-Reuters-3][^cite_note-AP-4][^cite_note-6] Debris and human remains were found scattered for over 100 m (330 ft) from the site.[^cite_note-Reuters-3]

## Investigation and response

In the aftermath, security was tightened in the area and forensic teams were deployed to determine the cause of the detonation. [Nalin Prabhat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nalin_Prabhat), the [Director General of Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Director_General_of_Police), said that the incident was likely an accidental explosion caused by the seized material.[^cite_note-Hindu-2] The Indian government later confirmed that no evidence of foul play or outside involvement was found in the initial investigation, and the explosion was an accident.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] The [Jammu and Kashmir government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Jammu_and_Kashmir) announced a compensation of ₹1 million (US\$10,000) for the families of the deceased and ₹0.1 million (US\$1,000) for the injured.[^cite_note-9]
