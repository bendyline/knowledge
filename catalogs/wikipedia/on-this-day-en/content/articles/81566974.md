The first day of meteorological **2025-2026 winter in Asia** began on December 1, 2025, and will end on February 28, 2026;[^cite_note-2] [winter storms](244878.md) may [occur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclogenesis) outside of these limits, as when a cold front produced snowfall along the Korean peninsula in late October.

## Events

### Late October

On October 20, a cold front produced snowfall on [Mount Seorak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Seorak), the first snow of the season in South Korea.[^cite_note-3] Three days later, [Mount Fuji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Fuji) recorded Japan's first snowfall of the season, which was 21 days later than average.[^cite_note-4]

### Early November

Main article: [Cyclone Montha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclone_Montha)

Heavy rain and snow from the remnants of [Cyclone Montha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclone_Montha) caused numerous [avalanches](73321.md) in [Nepal](171166.md), resulting in nine fatalities. Seven of the fatalities came on [Mount Yalung Ri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Yalung_Ri?action=edit\&redlink=1), and two occurred on [Panbari Mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panbari_Mountain?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-5]

### Mid-January

A winter storm hit the [Russian Far East](173135.md), killing two people in [Kamchatka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamchatka).[^cite_note-6] Snowfall in the region was as high as 1.8 metres (5.9 ft).[^cite_note-7]

### Late January

Multiple rounds of snow across [Japan](15573.md) resulted in 30 fatalities throughout late January. One such snowstorm resulted in the closure of [New Chitose Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Chitose_Airport). Train services were also cancelled during the outburst of snow, while several highways were closed.[^cite_note-8] Snowfall throughout those two weeks resulted in [Aomori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aomori) reached 7 feet (2.1 m).[^cite_note-9]

### Late February

A fierce winter storm swept through [Mongolia](19271.md) on February 21-24. The storm brought heavy snow, winds as fast as 31 metres per second (69 mph), and temperatures as low as −48 °C (−54 °F). The storm led to 5 fatalities and 56 injuries.[^cite_note-10]

### Early March

A major snowstorm on March 1-2 affected many areas of northern [China](5405.md). Light snow fell in [Beijing](18603746.md), triggering alerts for icy roads. In [Tianjin](50821.md), 11 flights were cancelled due to the snow.[^cite_note-11]
