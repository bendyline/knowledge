**Nabatieh al-Fawqa** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): النبطية الفوقا, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *an-Nabaṭiyya al-Fawqā*), also known as **Upper Nabatieh**, is a municipality in the [Nabatieh Governorate](4981449.md).[^cite_note-1]

## History

In the 1596 [tax records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daftar) in the early [Ottoman](22278.md) era, the village was named in the *[nahiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahiya)* (subdistrict) of [Sagif](6853436.md) under the *[liwa'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liwa_\(Arabic\))* (district) of [Safad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safed_Sanjak). It had a population of 104 households and 25 bachelors, all [Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim). The villagers paid 2,200 *[akçe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ak%C3%A7e)* in taxes on olive trees, 450 for "occasional revenues", 602 on goats and [bee hives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bee_hives), 30 for an [olive oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olive_oil) press, or a press for [grape syrup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grape_syrup), and 5000 as a fixed amount; a total of 8,276 *akçe*.[^cite_note-Hutteroth-2][^cite_note-3]

In 1875, [Victor Guérin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Gu%C3%A9rin) described the village as being located on a hill, and having 500 [Métualis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A9tualis) inhabitants. It was surrounded by gardens planted with fig trees.[^cite_note-4]

On 16 April 1996, Israeli warplanes [bombed an apartment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nabatieh_Fawka_attack) in Nabatieh al-Fawqa, killing nine people, seven of whom were children.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-UDHR-7]

## Demographics

In 2014 [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 98.60% of registered voters in Nabatieh al-Fawqa. 90.76% of the voters were [Shiite Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims).[^cite_note-8]
