**USS *Iwo Jima* (LHD-7)** is a [Wasp-class amphibious assault ship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wasp-class_amphibious_assault_ship) of the [United States Navy](20518076.md). The ship was named for the [Battle of Iwo Jima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Iwo_Jima) of World War II. The ship was commissioned in 2001 and is in service.

## Construction and career

Fabrication work for *Iwo Jima* began at [Ingalls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingalls_Shipbuilding) [shipyard](213654.md) on 3 September 1996, and the ship's [keel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keel) was laid on 12 December 1997. At the keel laying ceremony, United States Army Captain [Jacklyn H. Lucas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacklyn_H._Lucas), who was awarded the [Medal of Honor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medal_of_Honor) for his actions while serving as a Marine at the Battle of Iwo Jima, placed his Medal of Honor citation in the hull of the ship, where it remains today.[^cite_note-2] She was launched on 4 February 2000. USS *Iwo Jima* was [christened](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_naming_and_launching) by her sponsor, Zandra Krulak, wife of General [Charles C. Krulak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_C._Krulak), the former [Commandant of the Marine Corps](7708.md), in [Pascagoula, Mississippi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pascagoula,_Mississippi), on 25 March 2000. The commissioning crew moved aboard in April 2001, and made the ship's maiden voyage on 23 June 2001, accompanied by more than 2,000 World War II veterans – many of them survivors of the Battle of Iwo Jima. She was commissioned a week later in [Pensacola, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pensacola,_Florida), on 30 June 2001.

Shortly thereafter, the ship and crew began an accelerated Inter Deployment Training Cycle, which tested virtually every system on board in realistic combat conditions. *Iwo Jima* was also the first ship on the waterfront open to the public after the terrorist attacks of [11 September 2001](5058690.md). In 2002, *Iwo Jima* participated in [Fleet Week](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fleet_Week) in [New York City](645042.md).

## Ship's history

### 2003



*Iwo Jima* operating in fog in the Atlantic Ocean

*Iwo Jima* and the Marines of the [26th Marine Expeditionary Unit (26 MEU)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/26th_Marine_Expeditionary_Unit) along with two other [amphibious assault ships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphibious_assault_ship) formed the *Iwo Jima* [Amphibious Ready Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphibious_ready_group). *Iwo Jima* left port on 4 March 2003 in support of [Operation Enduring Freedom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Enduring_Freedom) and deployed Marines in April 2003 from the [Mediterranean Sea](19006.md) into Northern [Iraq](7515928.md) for the [Iraq War](5043324.md). In July 2003, *Iwo Jima* deployed to the coast of [Liberia](17791.md) as part of [JTF Liberia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Task_Force_Liberia) in response to the [Second Liberian Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Liberian_Civil_War). During this operation, the [Southern European Task Force (SETAF)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_European_Task_Force) served as the command element of JTF Liberia and *Iwo Jima* with the 26 MEU landed Marines in Liberia to perform humanitarian assessments. "At its height, JTF Liberia consisted of over 5,000 service members from the SETAF headquarters, the 26th Marine Expeditionary Unit, the three-ship *Iwo Jima* Amphibious Ready Group, 3rd Air Force's 398th Air Expeditionary Group, U.S. Army Europe's [21st Theater Support Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21st_Theater_Sustainment_Command), and Army Special Forces."[^cite_note-3]

### 2004–2005

In 2004, *Iwo Jima* participated in Fleet Week. *Iwo Jima* served as the [2nd Fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Second_Fleet) [flagship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flagship) in 2005, based out of [Norfolk, Virginia](57898.md).

### Hurricane Katrina



USS *Iwo Jima* pier side in New Orleans

On 31 August 2005, *Iwo Jima* was sortied to the [Gulf of Mexico](21076367.md) to provide disaster relief and to conduct support operations in the wake of [Hurricane Katrina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Katrina). *Iwo Jima* sailed up the [Mississippi River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippi_River) to the city of [New Orleans](53842.md) to directly support relief operations and act as the central command center for all federal, state and local disaster recovery operations.

During this critical period, *Iwo Jima* also served as the region's only fully functional air field for helicopter operations, conducting over one thousand flight deck operations; provided hot meals, showers, drinking water, and berthing to thousands of [National Guardsmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Guard_of_the_United_States) and relief workers; provided medical services, including first aid and surgical services, for disaster victims; and conducted clean-up operations in the city and suburbs of New Orleans.

*Iwo Jima* served as flagship for the commander-in-chief, [George W. Bush](3414021.md), during [Hurricane Katrina Joint Task Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Task_Force_Katrina),[^cite_note-4] and is only the second Navy ship to have been presented the [flag of the president of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_the_president_of_the_United_States).

### 2006

On 6 June 2006, *Iwo Jima* left her homeport of Norfolk, Virginia, and began a regularly scheduled six-month deployment to the U.S. European Command and U.S. Central Command area of responsibilities, as flagship for the *Iwo Jima* Expeditionary Strike Group, encompassing 6,000 sailors and Marines. The ship was also a part of the evacuation effort of American citizens from the [conflict in Lebanon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Lebanon_War).[^cite_note-5]

News reports on 15 July 2006 stated that *Iwo Jima*, flagship of the [24th Marine Expeditionary Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/24th_Marine_Expeditionary_Unit), would be used to evacuate U. S. citizens from [Lebanon](17771.md) after the [Israeli Defense Force](39237.md) made [Beirut International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beirut_International_Airport) unusable through bombing its runways and fuel storage areas.

### 2007



A close-up of the ship's motto, "Uncommon Valor", Portland, Maine, 2 July 2005

On 16 February 2007, *Iwo Jima* was awarded the 2006 [Battle "E"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_E) award.[^cite_note-6]

### 2009–2012

In 2009, 2010, and 2011, *Iwo Jima* participated at the annual Fleet Week in New York City.

On 3 November 2010, *Iwo Jima* was deployed to [Haiti](13373.md) in anticipation of providing humanitarian assistance due to an impending [Tropical Storm Tomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Tomas).[^cite_note-Military_Channel-7]

On 27 March 2012, *Iwo Jima* was deployed as part of the *Iwo Jima* Amphibious Ready Group with Marines from the [24th Marine Expeditionary Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/24th_Marine_Expeditionary_Unit) supporting maritime security operations and security cooperation efforts in the U.S. [5th](418867.md) and [6th Fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Sixth_Fleet) areas of responsibility.[^cite_note-8]

On 11 April 2012, an [MV-22](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Boeing_V-22_Osprey) from [VMM-261](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VMM-261) crashed near [Agadir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agadir), Morocco, during a joint training exercise after taking off from USS *Iwo Jima*. Two US Marine crew chiefs were killed and the two pilots were seriously injured.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

Early in May 2012, *Iwo Jima* was operating in the [Gulf of Aqaba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_of_Aqaba) and in the south of the [Red Sea](26191.md). In November 2012, *Iwo Jima* was dispatched to the eastern Mediterranean, during escalating warfare between Israel and [Hamas](13913.md), in case the evacuation of U.S. citizens from Israel was required, delaying the scheduled return of *Iwo Jima* to Norfolk.[^cite_note-12]

### 2014

In August 2014, *Iwo Jima* changed her homeport to [Mayport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Station_Mayport).[^cite_note-13]

### 2015

In January 2015, *Iwo Jima*, [USS *Fort McHenry*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Fort_McHenry) and [USS *New York*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_New_York_\(LPD-21\)) were positioned off the coast of [Yemen](350939.md) on standby to evacuate the staff of the US embassy if the situation had arisen, due to the [collapse of the Yemeni government](45326059.md).[^cite_note-14]

### 2016

In October 2016, *Iwo Jima* sailed to [Haiti](13373.md) to relieve [USS *Mesa Verde*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Mesa_Verde), assisting victims of [Hurricane Matthew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Matthew).[^cite_note-Iwo_Jima_to_Haiti-15]

### 2018

In October and November 2018, *Iwo Jima* participated in [NATO](21133.md)'s [Exercise Trident Juncture 2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exercise_Trident_Juncture_2018) in Norway.[^cite_note-16]

### 2021

In December 2021, *Iwo Jima* changed homeports from [Naval Station Mayport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Station_Mayport) to [Naval Station Norfolk](896196.md) as a part of the Navy's plan to consolidate the East Coast-based amphibious ships to the Norfolk area.[^cite_note-17]

### 2025



Marine Units aboard USS Iwo Jima during Operation Southern Spear

*Iwo Jima* departed Norfolk base on 14 August with around 4,500 Marines embarked from the 22nd Marine Expeditionary unit alongside the [USS Fort Lauderdale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Fort_Lauderdale) and [USS San Antonio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_San_Antonio) and two destroyers as a part of the Iwo Jima Amphibious Ready Group to the South Caribbean as a deployment for [Operation Southern Spear](81593523.md). A [live-firing exercise](3366570.md) was conducted by the ARG in November, off the coast of [Venezuela](32374.md).[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21]

### 2026



Nicolas Maduro on board the USS Iwo Jima

On 3 January 2026, the [United States launched strikes in Venezuela](82021123.md), resulting in the capture of President [Nicolás Maduro](7161476.md) and First Lady [Cilia Flores](9530834.md). President [Donald Trump](4848272.md) subsequently posted an image of Maduro on [Truth Social](69077351.md) with the caption "Nicolas Maduro on board the USS Iwo Jima."[^cite_note-22] According to initial reporting and later confirmed by General [Dan Caine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Caine) in a press conference, the president and first lady were taken into custody aboard the *Iwo Jima*, during [Operation Absolute Resolve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Absolute_Resolve).[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24]

## Motto

The ship's motto, "Uncommon Valor", is based on Fleet Admiral [Chester W. Nimitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chester_W._Nimitz)'s words when he spoke of Sailors and Marines who fought at [Iwo Jima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iwo_Jima): "Among the Americans who served on Iwo Jima, uncommon valor was a common virtue."[^cite_note-26]
