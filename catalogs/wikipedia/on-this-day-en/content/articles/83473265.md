# Grammy Award for Best Asian Pop Music Performance

| Grammy Award for Best Asian Pop Music Performance |  |
| --- | --- |
| Awarded for | Artistic excellence in a vocal Asian pop music performance |
| Presented by | [National Academy of Recording Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Recording_Arts_and_Sciences) |
| First award | 2027 |
| Currently held by | TBA |
| Website | [grammy.com](https://www.grammy.com/awards/categories/best-asian-pop-music-performance/) |

The **Grammy Award for Best Asian Pop Music Performance** is an award presented by the [Recording Academy](8850489.md) to honor quality [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_music) [pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_music) music performances in any given year. The award will be presented for the first time at the [69th Annual Grammy Awards](80454676.md) in 2027, and sits in the Pop & Dance/Electronic field.

The Academy announced the new category in June 2026, stating that the award "recognizes artistic excellence in Asian pop music performances. Eligible recordings include contemporary popular music originating from or widely recognized within Asian markets, including [K-Pop](629945.md), [J-Pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J-pop), and [C-Pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C-pop). Eligible recordings typically feature melody-driven composition, mainstream pop songwriting, and commercially oriented production, as recognized within their respective markets, and may incorporate elements of pop-adjacent styles such as pop-rock, pop R\&B, and other related forms. Recordings in this category are often characterized by genre-blending arrangements, layered production, and dynamic structural shifts designed to support both audio and performance-based delivery."[^cite_note-1]

The award goes to the performing artist(s).

## Background

Prior to 2026, Asian pop music was rarely acknowledged by the Recording Academy. [BTS](39862325.md) became the first Korean artists to be nominated when their single "[Dynamite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamite_\(BTS_song\))" received a nod in the [Best Pop Duo/Group Performance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Pop_Duo/Group_Performance) category at the [63rd Annual Grammy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/63rd_Annual_Grammy_Awards) in 2021, a feat they would repeat a [year later](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/64th_Annual_Grammy_Awards) with "[Butter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butter_\(song\))" and a third time for "[My Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Universe_\(song\))" the [year after](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/65th_Annual_Grammy_Awards) that.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] "[Golden](80331847.md)" from the film *[K-Pop Demon Hunters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KPop_Demon_Hunters_\(soundtrack\))* became the first K-pop song to win a Grammy when it won [Best Song Written for Visual Media](415037.md) at the [68th Annual Grammy Awards](79128292.md) in 2025.[^cite_note-4]

The category was established in response to the continued growth and expansion of Asian pop music, which the Academy described as "one of the most significant and sustained forces in the global music industry". The increasing popularity of genres including [K-pop](629945.md), [J-pop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J-pop), and [Mandopop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandopop) were cited as major inspirations for the creation of the category, and the Academy's members felt 2027 was the right time to introduce a specific award honoring Asian pop music in line with the genre's scale, artistry, and impact worldwide.[^cite_note-5]

Best Asian Pop Music Performance was announced on June 16, 2026 alongside [Best R\&B Collaboration or Duo/Group Performance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_R\&B_Collaboration_or_Duo/Group_Performance), [Best Traditional Pop Vocal Performance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Traditional_Pop_Vocal_Performance), [Best Traditional Folk Album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Traditional_Folk_Album), and [Best Latin Song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Latin_Song).[^cite_note-6] Of the announcement, Recording Academy CEO [Harvey Mason Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_Mason_Jr.) stated, "2027 is going to be an amazing year for the Grammy Awards, and one that reflects the extraordinary growth we're seeing across music. The changes advanced by our Recording Academy members speak to the breadth of today's music industry and the many genres, crafts and creators shaping it. We're excited to see these updates come to life in the year ahead as we celebrate the music people who are driving music forward".[^cite_note-7]

## Eligibility

According to the Recording Academy's rulebook for the 2027 ceremony, eligible recordings must feature meaningful use of one or more Asian languages and that the language must be identifiable within songs that are bilingual or multilingual. Recordings that are performed entirely in an Asian language are eligible, but recordings that are primarily sung in a non-Asian language and only feature minimal or incidental use of Asian languages, such as ad-libs, brief phrases, or isolated words, are not eligible. The rules are clear that "the musical style is determinative of eligibility, not the ethnicity or nationality of the performer(s)", and that recordings performed entirely in English or other languages by Asian performers are eligible in other categories, but not in Best Asian Pop Music Performance. Additionally, other recordings that meet the language criteria but do not meet the genre criteria are instead eligible in other categories, including [Best Global Music Performance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Global_Music_Performance) or other genre categories.[^cite_note-8]

## Controversy

On July 28, 2026, the members of BTS announced that they would not be submitting their album, *[Arirang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arirang_\(album\))* (2026) or its songs for consideration at the 69th Annual Grammys.[^cite_note-9] In a joint statement posted on all seven members' [Instagram](31591547.md) accounts, the group commented: "We have decided not to submit for Grammy consideration this year. We hope our music can be heard and loved for what it is, rather than being divided by region or language. We thank Army and everyone who has always stood by us."[^cite_note-10] Grammy CEO [Harvey Mason Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_Mason_Jr.) said that he was saddened by BTS' decision and that the category was not meant to divide.[^cite_note-11] Following BTS' decision, the Grammys website removed live recordings of BTS' performances at previous years' ceremonies.[^cite_note-12] On October 2, [JYP Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JYP_Entertainment) announced that [Stray Kids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stray_Kids) had chosen to not submit music.[^cite_note-13]
