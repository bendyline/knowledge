The **2025 FIFA U-20 World Cup final** was the final match and culmination of the [2025 FIFA U-20 World Cup](73421401.md), hosted by [Chile](5489.md). The match was played at the [Estadio Nacional Julio Martínez Prádanos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estadio_Nacional_Julio_Mart%C3%ADnez_Pr%C3%A1danos) in [Santiago](51572.md) on 19 October 2025, between [Argentina](12504513.md) and [Morocco](15604185.md), being the eighth final for Argentina and the first for Morocco.[^cite_note-1] In addition, Morocco become the first African country to reach the U-20 World Cup final since [Ghana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghana_national_under-20_football_team) in [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_FIFA_U-20_World_Cup), in which they won and the first ever Arab nation to do so.

Morocco won the match after defeating Argentina 2–0, winning their first FIFA tournament title.
