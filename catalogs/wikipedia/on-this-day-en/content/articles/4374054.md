**Negombo** ([Sinhala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinhala_language): මීගමුව, romanized: *Mîgamuwa*, IPA: [\[miːɡəmuʋə\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA), [Tamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_language): நீர்கொழும்பு, romanized: *Nīrkoḻumpu*, IPA: [\[n̪iːɾxoɻumbɯ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Tamil)), also known as Punchi Romaya (Little Rome) and Mîpura (city of bees), is a major city in [Sri Lanka](26750.md), situated on the west coast and at the mouth of the [Negombo Lagoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negombo_Lagoon), in the [Western Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Province,_Sri_Lanka), 38 km (24 mi) from Colombo via the [Colombo–Katunayake Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E03_expressway_\(Sri_Lanka\)) and the nearest major city from the [Bandaranaike International Airport](2870845.md) (about 9 km distance).

Negombo is a major commercial centre in the country and the largest city in Gampaha District, and is also the administrative centre of Negombo Division. Negombo has a population of about 142,136 within its [divisional secretariat division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negombo_Divisional_Secretariat).[^cite_note-1] Negombo municipal boundary is fully extended throughout its Divisional Secretariat area.

Negombo is known for its long sandy beaches and centuries old [fishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fishing) industry. Negombo has a large bilingual (Sinhala/Tamil) population with a large [Christian](606848.md) majority.

## Etymology

The name "Negombo" is the [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language) corruption of its [Tamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_language) name *Neerkolombu*.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

The Sinhala name *Migamuva* comes from a legend mentioned in [Rajaveliya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajaveliya).[^cite_note-4] The army of King Kavantissa found bee honey in a canoe near the seashore, for [Viharamahadevi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viharamahadevi) who was pregnant with the prince [Dutugamunu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutugamunu). Because of this, the place was named "Mee-Gomuwa".[^cite_note-5]

## History

The shallow waters of the [Negombo Lagoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negombo_Lagoon) provided safe shelter for seafaring vessels and became one of the key ports along with [Kalpitiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalpitiya), [Puttalam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puttalam), [Colombo](56636.md), [Kalutara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalutara) and [Galle](426566.md), from which the Sri Lankan kingdoms conducted external trade.[^cite_note-6]

### Pre-colonialism

The region was under the rule of the Sinhala monarchy based on Sri Jayawardanapura Kotte. The language used in the area was a regional dialect of Sinhala when the [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Ceylon) arrived in the 16th century.[^cite_note-7]

Negombo also served as a shelter for Arab vessels, whose descendants are the [Sri Lankan Moors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lankan_Moors).[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] Negombo was a major port known for its trading activity and was well known for its [cinnamon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinnamon) cultivation.[^cite_note-10] The cinnamon trade was controlled by the Sri Lankan kings and later by the [Sri Lankan Moors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lankan_Moors).[^cite_note-11]

### Portuguese Ceylon

Landing in the early 1500s, the Portuguese overthrew the Sinhala monarchy who were forced to relocate to Seethawaka. The Portuguese constructed a fort in Negombo and took over the trade of cinnamon to the west.[^cite_note-12] During the Portuguese occupation, the [Karava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karava) (the dominant seafaring clan of Negombo), who were previously [Buddhist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhist) and [Hindu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu) were converted into [Catholicism](606848.md).[^cite_note-13] Due to the sheer amount of conversions to Catholicism, present-day Negombo is sometimes known as 'Little Rome' due to nearly two thirds of its population being Catholic.[^cite_note-14]

The Portuguese restructured the traditional production and management of cinnamon and maintained their control over the trade for more than a century. The decline of their power began in the 1630s when warfare between the Portuguese and the [Kingdom of Kandy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Kandy) reached a stalemate. The King of Kandy turned to the Dutch for help.[^cite_note-15]

### Dutch Ceylon



Painting by [Johannes Vingboons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Vingboons) of the [Dutch fort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negombo_fort) in Negombo, c. 1665

The Dutch captured Negombo from the Portuguese in 1646 and negotiated an armistice with Portugal for ten years. During this period, the King of Kandy sought to provoke conflict between the nations by passing through the territories of the one to attack the other. On one occasion, he captured the fort of Negombo and sent the head of the Dutch commander, Adrian Vander Stell, to his countrymen in [Galle](426566.md).[^cite_note-16] Although the Dutch managed to regain control of Negombo from the King by diplomatic means, hostilities continued. In particular, the disruption of the cinnamon trade was a favourite method of the King to harass the Dutch.[^cite_note-17]

Throughout the eighteenth century, the demand for cinnamon from Ceylon outstripped the supply, and its quality appeared to have suffered. Other factors, including the continued hostility from the Kandyan Kingdom and a rival cinnamon exporter in the form of China, led to a 40% decline in the volume of cinnamon exported between 1785 and 1791, despite attempts to clear land around Negombo and create cinnamon plantations.

The legacy of the Dutch colonial era can be seen in the [Negombo fort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negombo_fort), constructed in 1672, and other Dutch buildings, including churches and the extensive canal system that runs 120 km (75 mi) from Colombo in the south, through Negombo to Puttalam in the north.

### British Ceylon

By the time the British commander [Colonel Stuart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Stuart_\(British_Army_officer,_born_1741\)) took over the cinnamon trade in 1796, it was clear that the industry was in decline. Poor policies put in place by [Frederick North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_North,_5th_Earl_of_Guilford) the first [Governor of British Ceylon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_British_Ceylon) exacerbated the problem. By the 1830s, commercial interest had moved elsewhere.

Following the British takeover of the Kingdom of Kandy in 1815, Negombo lost its strategic value as an outpost of Colombo. However, it continued to develop in commercial influence. The Negombo fishery was at the heart of the seafood trade in Ceylon, and many migrant fisherman arrived annually with the profits of their ventures going into the small, prosperous town. In 1907 Negombo was connected to the massive railway project that was linking the island together under British control and encouraging the growth of plantations in coconuts, tea and coffee.

## Geography



A traditional fishing boat

Negombo is about 2 metres (6 ft 7 in) above sea level, and Negombo's geography is a mix of land and water. The [Hamilton Canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton_Canal) flows in the heart of the city. The [Negombo Lagoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negombo_Lagoon) is one of the most scenic landmarks of Negombo. There are over 190 species of wildlife and plenty of birds in its mangroves. The northern border of the city is formed by the [Maha Oya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maha_Oya) river which meets the [Indian Ocean](14580.md).

### Climate

Negombo features a [tropical rainforest climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_rainforest_climate) under the [Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification). The city receives rainfall mainly from the Southwestern monsoons from May to August and October to January. During the remaining months there is a little precipitation due to [Convective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convective) rains. The average annual precipitation is about 2,400 millimetres (94 in). The average temperature varies 24 °C (75 °F) to 30 °C (86 °F), and there are high humidity levels from February to April.

### Negombo Lagoon

Main article: [Negombo Lagoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negombo_Lagoon)



Built by the Dutch to transport spices, now used by the local fisherman to get to the sea, [Dutch canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton_Canal) in Negombo

The Negombo lagoon is large semi-enclosed coastal water body with plenty of natural resources. The lagoon is fed by number of small rivers and the [Hamilton Canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton_Canal) (also known as the Dutch canal). It is linked to the Indian Ocean by a narrow channel to the north, near Negombo. The lagoon and the marsh land area also support local agriculture and forestry. It has extensive mangrove swamps and attracts a wide variety of water birds. The lagoon supports so many distinct species of flora, fauna and as well as another species of birds and variety of animals. [Negombo Lagoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negombo_Lagoon) is a major local and tourist attraction primarily for sightseeing and boating tours.

The fishermen who are based at the Negombo lagoon live in shanty [thatch palm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thatch_palm) villages along the water's edge. They rely mainly on their traditional knowledge of the seasons for their livelihood, using [outrigger canoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outrigger_canoe) carved out of tree trunks and nylon nets to bring in modest catches from September through April. Their boats are made in two forms – oruvas (a type of [sailing canoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sailing_canoe)) and paruvas (a large, man-powered [catamaran](195952.md) fitted with kurlon dividers). In recent years, the villagers have supplemented the income earned from fishing by collecting 'toddy', or palm sap, which is used to brew [arrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrack_\(drink\)).[^cite_note-gv-39]

## Demographics

According by the statistics of 2011, 6.3% of the population of Gampaha district live in Negombo city limits and 11.6% of the population of the district live in Negombo Metropolis. It is a multi-ethnic and multi-cultural city. Most of Negombo's residents belong to the [Sinhalese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinhala_people) majority. There are [Tamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lankan_Tamil_people) and [Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim) people also living in the city as well as a long established [Chinese](29408939.md) community.[^cite_note-40]

### Religion

Negombo is a multi-religious city. Since the beginning of European colonisation, the township of Negombo has had a majority of [Roman Catholics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Church_in_Sri_Lanka) along with [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lanka_Muslims), [Buddhist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lankan_Buddhism) and [Hindus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinduism_in_Sri_Lanka). Before European invasions it was a Buddhist majority region.

#### Catholic and Christian churches



Interior of St. Mary's Church in Negombo

Negombo has been given the name "Little Rome" owing to the highly ornate Portuguese-era Roman Catholic churches such as [St. Mary's Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Mary's_Church,_Negombo) found in the township and because of the majority of inhabitants being Roman Catholics. [St. Sebastian's Church, Katuwapitiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Sebastian's_Church,_Katuwapitiya); [Saint Stephen's Church, Negombo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Stephen's_Church,_Negombo), Grand Street; [St. Mary's Church, Negombo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Mary's_Church,_Negombo); St. Anne's Churches at Kurana and Palangatura; St. Anthony's Church Dalupothal; and [Our Lady of Sorrows Church, Kandawala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Lady_of_Sorrows_Church,_Kandawala), are the biggest parishes in Negombo. There are over 25 [Roman Catholic](606848.md) churches in the city.

There is a branch of [the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Church_of_Jesus_Christ_of_Latter-day_Saints) in Negombo. The church building is just west of the intersection of Ave Maria Street and Old Chilaw Street. There are also Methodist churches, Baptist churches, and the other Anglican churches in Negombo.

#### Buddhist Pansal (Buddhist temples)

Agurukaramulla Raja Maha Viharaya (Bodhirajaramaya) is a famous Buddhist temple bringing Buddhists from all over [Sri Lanka](26750.md) to Negombo every year. Abhayasekararamaya Temple (Podipansala), Sri Sudarshanaramaya, Dutugamunu viharaya and Sri Buddhagaya maha viharaya are famous Buddhist temples in the city.

#### Hindu Kovil (Hindu temples)

There are many Hindu temples (Kovil) in Negombo: Kali Amman temple, Ganapathi (Pillaiar) Temple, Kamachchi Amman Temple, [Muththumari Amman Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muththumari_Amman_Temple?action=edit\&redlink=1), Murugan (Kandaswami) Temple, Karumari Amman Temple etc.

## Local government

Negombo City Local Board began in 1878. After 44 years, it became the Urban District Council on 1 January 1922. Negombo celebrated its silver jubilee of its Urban council status in grand style in February 1948. Their Royal Highnesses the Duke and Duchess of Gloucester were the main patrons on the opening day of the celebrations. The Negombo Urban Council was offered [Municipal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal) status on 1 January 1950 under the municipal ordinance of 1865.

The [Negombo Municipal Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negombo_Municipal_Council) has governed the city with a [mayor](101146.md) from the government, since 1950. Negombo's mayor and the council members are elected through the local government election held every five years. There are 29 wards in the Negombo municipal boundary. Each is represented by an elected member, but  there were only 26 members before the Local Government election held in 2018. The number of municipal councillors was increased to 48 according to new local government election system introduced in 2018. where 29 members are elected form wards and the rest form a preferential list.

In the Local Government Election held in 2018,[^cite_note-43] United National Party Won the Negombo Municipal council led by former Western Provincial councillor Royce Fernando by securing 19 seats and [Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lanka_Podujana_Peramuna) led By Dayan Lanza, brother of [Nimal Lanza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nimal_Lanza) (MP) secured 16 Seats. The rest of seats were distributed among other political Parties and independent groups including Sri Lanka Freedom Party.[^cite_note-44] Dayan Lanza became the Mayor of Negombo with the support of minor political parties and Sri Lanka Freedom Party. Royce Fernando Became the Opposition Leader in Negombo Municipal council.

## Economy



Negombo Beach

Negombo is considered one of the largest economic centres of the country. Negombo is about 4–5 km (2.5–3.1 mi) from the [Bandaranaike International Airport](2870845.md), and the free trade zone. Negombo has a moderate [port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port) (used during the periods of [Portuguese](23033.md) and [Dutch](21148.md) colonisation)

The [economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economy) of Negombo is mainly based on [tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourism) and its centuries-old [fishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fishing) industry, though it also produces [cinnamon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinnamon),[^cite_note-gv-39] [ceramics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pottery), and [brass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brass) ware.

The [Colombo Stock Exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombo_Stock_Exchange)-Negombo branch and many major financial corporations have their key branches in Negombo. There are department stores, large supermarkets, and boutiques in the bustling streets of Downtown Negombo and international food outlets are being opened.

## Transport



Negombo bus terminal

The [E03](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E03_expressway_\(Sri_Lanka\)) - [Airport Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E03_expressway_\(Sri_Lanka\)) opened in 2013 links the capital [Colombo](56636.md) through the Katunayake Interchange with Negombo minimising travelling time to approximately 20 to 30 minutes. The Katunayake Interchange from Negombo takes about 5 to 10 minutes (approximately 4 km (2.5 mi)).

There are highway bus services running between from Negombo to [Pettah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pettah,_Colombo), [Maharagama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maharagama) via [Airport Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E03_expressway_\(Sri_Lanka\)). Negombo to [Galle](426566.md), [Kataragama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kataragama) and [Matara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matara,_Sri_Lanka) (the southern tip of the country) using the [Southern Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E01_expressway_\(Sri_Lanka\)). And also bus services provided Negombo to [Kadawatha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kadawatha), [Kottawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kottawa), [Panadura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panadura) and [Moratuwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moratuwa) using the [Outer Circular Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outer_Circular_Expressway)

The [A3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A3_road_\(Sri_Lanka\)) Colombo–Negombo highway road from [Colombo](56636.md), goes through Negombo, extends to [Jaffna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaffna), and [Trincomalee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trincomalee) via [Anuradhapura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anuradhapura). Negombo is connected with some of the B grade roads from [Ja-Ela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ja-Ela), [Kurunegala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurunegala), [Mirigama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirigama), [Nittambuwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nittambuwa) and Giriulla, and there is a good road network in and around Negombo.

The Bus Terminal complex of Negombo has multiple facilities for passengers and public. It is served by many bus routes, connecting with some major destinations in the country, provided Negombo to [Colombo](56636.md), [Kandy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kandy), [Kegalle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kegalle), [Kataragama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kataragama), [Hatton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hatton,_Sri_Lanka), [Kalpitiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalpitiya), [Chilaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chilaw), [Kurunegala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurunegala), [Puttalam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puttalam), [Avissawella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avissawella), and [Kaluthara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaluthara).

Due to Negombo being situated along A3 Highway Road, it is served by many bus routes from Colombo to Northern and North western points of Sri Lanka including [Jaffna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaffna), [Anuradhapura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anuradhapura), [Vavuniya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vavuniya), [Kilinochchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilinochchi), [Mullaitivu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mullaitivu), [Mannar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mannar,_Sri_Lanka), [Point Pedro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point_Pedro), [Nikaweratiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikaweratiya), [Panduwasnuwara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panduwasnuwara), [Anamaduwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anamaduwa), Kankasanthurai, Pulmudei, Silawathurai, Velvetithurai and Padaviya.



Negombo Downtown Railway Station

Four railway stations serve Negombo, they are: [Kurana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurana,_Sri_Lanka), Negombo Downtown, Kattuwa and Kochchikade. Negombo Downtown Station is the main railway station on the [Puttalam railway line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puttalam_line). It serves [Galle](426566.md), via [Colombo](56636.md) from south and to [Puttalam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puttalam), via [Chilaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chilaw) from north. The Sri Lanka Railway Department has introduced an intercity express train between Chilaw and Colombo with stops at Negombo Downtown and Kochchikade Stations.

The Negombo Downtown Station is close to the central Bus Terminal Complex. Negombo is the closest major city to the [Bandaranaike International Airport](2870845.md) and Katunayake Interchange of the [E03](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E03_expressway_\(Sri_Lanka\)) - [Airport Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E03_expressway_\(Sri_Lanka\)).
