The **prime minister of Solomon Islands** is [Solomon Islands](265083.md)' [head of government](37570.md), as elected by the [National Parliament](4503022.md). [Matthew Wale](20098103.md) has served as the Prime Minister of the Solomon Islands since May 2026.

Solomon Islands is a [Commonwealth realm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_realm); the functions of the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) are performed on behalf of the [monarch of Solomon Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monarchy_of_Solomon_Islands) by the [governor-general of Solomon Islands](1017894.md), who is nominated by Parliament.

The prime minister's official residence is [Red House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_House_\(Solomon_Islands\)) in [Honiara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honiara).

## Constitutional basis

Section 33 of the [constitution of Solomon Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Solomon_Islands) provides for the prime minister to be elected by the members of the [national parliament](4503022.md). The other government ministers are appointed by the governor-general on the advice of the prime minister.[^cite_note-2]

The prime minister may be removed from office by the governor-general following a [no-confidence motion](208101.md) passed by an absolute majority of the national parliament. The office is also vacated if the prime minister resigns, ceases to be a member of parliament, or is elected as [speaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_of_the_National_Parliament_of_Solomon_Islands) or deputy speaker. In the event of a vacancy, the governor-general, in consultation with cabinet, appoints one of the existing government ministers to act as prime minister until the national parliament can meet to fill the vacancy.[^cite_note-3]

Schedule 2 to the constitution outlines the process for election of the prime minister. All members of parliament are eligible for nomination, but must be nominated by at least four other members. The election is then held by [secret ballot](479210.md) with the governor-general presiding. If a candidate fails to secure a majority of votes on the first ballot, an [exhaustive ballot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exhaustive_ballot) takes place with the candidate with the lowest number of votes eliminated on each ballot.[^cite_note-4]
