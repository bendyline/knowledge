**Moa** is a municipality and an industrial city in the [Holguín Province](460435.md) of [Cuba](5042481.md). Its name is believed to mean "water here".

## History

Moa, one of the youngest cities in Cuba, was founded in 1939.[^cite_note-3]

## Geography

Located in the easternmost area of its province, at the borders with [Guantánamo Province](18952312.md), Moa is bordered by the municipalities of [Sagua de Tánamo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sagua_de_T%C3%A1namo), [Frank País](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Pa%C3%ADs,_Cuba), [Baracoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baracoa) and [Yateras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yateras).[^cite_note-4]

The large municipal territory includes the [Nipe-Sagua-Baracoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nipe-Sagua-Baracoa) mountain range and the [Alejandro de Humboldt National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alejandro_de_Humboldt_National_Park). The villages belonging to Moa municipality are Arroyo Blanco, Brinquín, Cañete, Cayo Grande, Centeno, Cocalito, Cupey, Farallones, Punta Gorda, Yaguaneque and [Yamanigüey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamanig%C3%BCey). The city includes the quarters (*repartos*) of Centro, Atlántico, Caribe, José Martí, La Playa, Las Coloradas, Los Checos, Los Mangos, Miraflores and Rolo Monterrey.

## Demographics

In 2022, the municipality of Moa had a population of 71,660.[^cite_note-citypopulation-2] With a total area of 730 km<sup>2</sup> (280 sq mi),[^cite_note-statoids-1] it has a population density of 98/km<sup>2</sup> (250/sq mi). This city has the youngest citizens in all of Cuba, and is [the second largest city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_Cuba) (and the [fourth municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Cuba)) by population in the [Holguín Province](460435.md).

## Environment

Large [nickel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nickel) and [cobalt](24580536.md) bearing [laterite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laterite) located in the Moa area are exploited in part by a [joint venture](538782.md) with the [Canadian](5042916.md) company [Sherritt International](4516089.md).

The extensive mining and nickel processing has large impact on the local environment. The coastal waters and nearby land is contaminated by the pollution from mines and processing plants.[^cite_note-cathalac-5] The soil has a reddish tint due to the laterite soil, which has a high iron content.

## Economy

### Nickel production

The nickel production is concentrated on the factories "[Pedro Soto Alba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Soto_Alba?action=edit\&redlink=1)" and ["Ernesto Che Guevara"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernesto_Che_Guevara_\(factory\)?action=edit\&redlink=1). While the [Nickel Processor Plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nickel_Processor_Plant?action=edit\&redlink=1) "Pedro Soto Alba" is a limited liability company between Cuba and [Sherritt International](4516089.md) Canadian Company, the "Ernesto Che Guevara" belongs to the government enterprise [Cubaníquel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuban%C3%ADquel?action=edit\&redlink=1). As average per year, The "Soto Alba" and "Che Guevara" produce more than 30,000 tons of nickel each.[^cite_note-6] This industry is what makes Moa a leading nickel producer, and what brings the town most of its [GDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GDP).

## Transport

The city is served by the [Orestes Acosta Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orestes_Acosta_Airport) ([IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IATA_airport_code): **MOA**, [ICAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICAO_airport_code): **MUMO**), a regional airport with daily flights to [Havana](49719.md). It is crossed in the middle by the state highway "[Circuito Norte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuito_Norte)" (CN), the second longest one after the "[Carretera Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carretera_Central_\(Cuba\))".
