**Tambov Oblast**[^cite_note-8] is a [federal subject of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_subject_of_Russia) (an [oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oblast)). Its [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) is the [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) of [Tambov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tambov). As of the [2010 Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Census_\(2010\)), its population was 1,091,994.[^cite_note-2010Census-9]

## Geography

Tambov Oblast is situated in a [forest steppe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest_steppe). It borders the [Ryazan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryazan_Oblast), [Penza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penza_Oblast), [Saratov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saratov_Oblast), [Voronezh](473401.md) and [Lipetsk](480918.md) oblasts.

## History

The oldest known population of the Tambov region, the [Mordovians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mordovians)-[Moksha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mokshas), formed as a nation of local ethnic groups from the 6th century BC. The first Russian settlers arrived in the [pre-Mongol period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kievan_Rus'), but the final settlement occurred in the 17th century. To protect the southern borders of Russia from the raids of the [Tatars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatars), and to further develop the [Black Soil region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Black_Earth_Region), the Russian government built the walled cities of [Kozlov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michurinsk) (1635) and [Tambov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tambov) (1636). The cities protected the main path of nomad raids on Russian land and paved the way for a quick settlement of the region.

[Kozlovsky Uyezd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kozlovsky_Uyezd) originally existed in the Tambov area. In the course of the administrative reforms of Peter the Great in 1708 and 1719, it became part of [Azov Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azov_Governorate). New administrative divisions established the [Tambov Viceroyalty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tambov_Viceroyalty?action=edit\&redlink=1) in 1779 and from 1796 [Tambov Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tambov_Governorate), with an area of 66.5 thousand km<sup>2</sup> divided into 12 [uyezds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uyezd). With almost no change to its boundaries, the Governorate remained in existence until 1928.

An attempt to establish Soviet control over the Tambov area led to the defeat and execution of "Red Sonya" (Sofia Nukhimovna Gel'berg) in the spring of 1918.[^cite_note-10]

During the [Russian Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Civil_War), an anti-Bolshevik uprising, the [Tambov Rebellion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tambov_Rebellion), broke out in Tambov Governorate in 1920–1921.

Tambov Oblast was finally created from the [Voronezh](473401.md) and [Samara Oblasts](481962.md) on September 27, 1937. The oblast attained its present form after the separation of [Penza Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penza_Oblast) (formerly part of Kuybyshev before joining Tambov) on February 4, 1939.

## Politics

The acting head of the administration of the Tambov Oblast since 4 October 2021 and [Head of the Tambov Oblast](68990558.md) since 20 September 2022 is Maxim Yegorov.

Elections to the Regional Duma were held from 17 to 19 September 2021. 25 seats were distributed by party lists and 25 by single-member constituencies. The seats at the end of the elections were distributed as follows:

- [United Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Russia) — 42

- [Rodina](3556828.md) — 3

- [Communist Party of the Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Party_of_the_Russian_Federation) — 3

- [Liberal Democratic Party of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_Democratic_Party_of_Russia) — 1

- [A Just Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Just_Russia) — 1

According to a 2012 survey,[^cite_note-2012ArenaAtlas-19] 78.4% of the population of Tambov Oblast adheres to the [Russian Orthodox Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Orthodox_Church), making it the federal subject with the highest percentage of this religion in the whole country. In addition, 1% are [unaffiliated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nondenominational_Christianity) generic [Christians](18337522.md), 7% of the population declares to be "[spiritual but not religious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spiritual_but_not_religious)", 10% is [atheist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atheism), and 3.6% follows other religions or did not give an answer to the question.[^cite_note-2012ArenaAtlas-19]

## Economy

[Southeastern Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southeastern_Railway_\(Russia\)) passes through [Michurinsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michurinsk) and connects the central regions with the southern regions. Breeding cattle, sheep, pig, and chicken is a product of [animal husbandry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_husbandry).
