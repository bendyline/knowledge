The **2026 All-Ireland Senior Football Championship** (SFC) was the 140th edition of the [Gaelic Athletic Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaelic_Athletic_Association)'s main inter-county [Gaelic football](37688.md) tournament. Thirty-one of the thirty-two Irish counties took part, with [Kilkenny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilkenny_county_football_team) being the exception. [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_county_football_team) and [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_county_football_team) completed the lineup by entering the [Connacht SFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connacht_Senior_Football_Championship).

[Mayo](63970057.md) beat [Kerry](63970030.md) by 1-20 to 1-17 in [the final](83772902.md) to become All-Ireland football champions for the first time since 1951, bridging a 75-year gap. Kerry had been the defending champions.[^cite_note-1] The bookmakers' favourites had been Kerry and [Donegal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donegal_county_football_team), with [Tyrone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyrone_county_football_team), [Galway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway_county_football_team), [Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin_county_football_team) and [Armagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armagh_county_football_team) also considered contenders.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

## Format

At the GAA's Annual Congress in [Donegal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donegal_\(town\)) in 2025, delegates approved Motion 19 to revise the Championship structure from 2026 onwards.[^cite_note-4] The championship features 16 teams:

- The four provincial champions

- The four beaten provincial finalists

- The [2025 Tailteann Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Tailteann_Cup) winner ([Kildare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kildare_county_football_team))

- The seven next-ranked teams, based on final position in the [2026 National Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_National_Football_League_\(Ireland\))&#x20;

  - Position is based on standings after [promotion and relegation](648049.md) are applied and after finals are played; therefore, the top two teams in Division 2 outrank the bottom two teams in Division 1; if the 2nd-placed team in Division 2 wins the final, they are ranked above the first-placed finisher who lost the final.

The round-robin stage is scrapped under the new format, moving to a modified [double-elimination tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double-elimination_tournament).[^cite_note-5]

Provincial champions and runners-up are seeded in the draw for Round 1 of the knockout stage and given home-field advantage. The sixteen games are played over two weekends: the Munster and Connacht finalists on the first weekend, while the Ulster and Leinster finalists' ties are on the second weekend.

In Round 1 the eight winners move into Round 2A, while the eight losers enter Round 2B. The winners of the four Round 2A ties advance directly to the quarter-finals. The four Round 2A losers meet the four Round 2B winners in Round 3 to decide the remaining quarter-finalists. Round 2B and Round 3 losers are eliminated.

In Round 2 and Round 3, the team picked first plays at home; a re-draw or fixture swap occurs if a selected tie is a rematch of a provincial final or Round 1 match-up.[^cite_note-6]

The draw for the early stages of the competition took place on 27 November 2025.

All throw-in times are [UTC+1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC+1) ([Irish Standard Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Standard_Time) or [British Summer Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Summer_Time)), except where indicated.

## Teams

Thirty-three counties compete in the [All-Ireland Championship](885732.md): every [Irish county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_county) except [Kilkenny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilkenny_county_football_team). This number is augmented by [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_county_football_team) and [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_county_football_team), both of whom compete in the [Connacht Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connacht_Senior_Football_Championship), bringing the total there to seven teams. Eleven teams compete in the [Leinster Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leinster_Senior_Football_Championship), six in [Munster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munster_Senior_Football_Championship) and nine in [Ulster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulster_Senior_Football_Championship).

## All Ireland series

### Team allocation and draw

- A map showing the results of each county in the 2026 All-Ireland Senior Football Championship.

  A map showing the results of each county in the 2026 All-Ireland Senior Football Championship.

  Sixteen teams qualify for the All-Ireland Championship, as follows:&#x20;

  - &#x20;  8 provincial finalists; these are the seeded teams in the draw

  - &#x20;  1 winner of the [2025 Tailteann Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Tailteann_Cup)

  - &#x20;  7 highest ranking teams (not otherwise qualified) in the [2026 National Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_National_Football_League_\(Ireland\)) (NFL). Promoted teams are ranked above teams relegated from the next highest division, with league final champions ranked higher regardless of round-robin table position. If the 2025 Tailteann Cup winner were a provincial finalist or qualified via league position, their automatic berth would transfer to the 8th-highest ranking NFL team.

- &#x20;  The remaining 17 teams play in the [2026 Tailteann Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Tailteann_Cup)

## Notable events and records

- The Ulster quarter final between [Donegal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donegal_county_football_team) and [Down](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Down_county_football_team) was the first Ulster SFC game at [O'Donnell Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O'Donnell_Park), [Letterkenny](620333.md) since [1951](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1951_All-Ireland_Senior_Football_Championship).

  [^cite_note-21]

- [Galway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway_county_football_team) reached an eleventh Connacht final in a row, equalling their records of 1933–43 and 1956–66.

  [^cite_note-22]

- [Westmeath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westmeath_county_football_team) reached their first Leinster SFC final since [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_All-Ireland_Senior_Football_Championship) and won their first Leinster title since [2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_All-Ireland_Senior_Football_Championship).

  [^cite_note-23]

- [Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin_county_football_team) lost a Leinster final for the first time since [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_All-Ireland_Senior_Football_Championship).

- It was the first Ulster final between [Armagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armagh_county_football_team) and [Monaghan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monaghan_county_football_team) since [1938](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1938_All-Ireland_Senior_Football_Championship).

- [Armagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armagh_county_football_team) won their first Ulster title since [2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_All-Ireland_Senior_Football_Championship).

  [^cite_note-24]

- In Round 1 of the All-Ireland SFC, Westmeath won five championship games in the same season for the first time ever, this was accomplished with an extra-time victory over [Cavan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cavan_county_football_team) on Saturday 30 May.

  [^cite_note-25]

  [^cite_note-26]

- In Round 1 of the All-Ireland SFC, [Louth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louth_county_football_team) defeated [Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin_county_football_team) in the Championship for the first time since [1973](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1973_All-Ireland_Senior_Football_Championship#Leinster_Senior_Football_Championship).

  [^cite_note-27]

- In Round 1 of the All-Ireland SFC, [Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin_county_football_team) concede four goals in a single championship match since [2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_All-Ireland_Senior_Football_Championship).

- In Round 1 of the All-Ireland SFC, [Monaghan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monaghan_county_football_team) had a valid point scored by [Bobby McCaul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobby_McCaul) waved wide by an umpire in a one-point defeat by eventual All-Ireland winner [Mayo](63970057.md).

  [^cite_note-28]

  [^cite_note-29]

  [^cite_note-30]

- Round 2A saw the first championship meeting of [Louth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louth_county_football_team) and [Armagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armagh_county_football_team).

- [Roscommon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roscommon_county_football_team) were [Connacht champions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connacht_SFC) but were knocked out in Round 2B, equivalent to 13th–16th place; this is the worst finish by a provincial champion since the 16-team All-Ireland series was introduced in [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_All-Ireland_Senior_Football_Championship) (Prior to 2023, provincial champions would automatically reach the quarter-finals or semi-finals.) In 2023, Connacht champions [Galway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway_county_football_team) were eliminated in the preliminary quarter-finals (9th–12th place); the same result was achieved by Leinster champions [Louth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louth_county_football_team) in [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_All-Ireland_Senior_Football_Championship).

- In 2026, Ulster champions [Armagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armagh_county_football_team) and Leinster champions [Westmeath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westmeath_county_football_team) were knocked out in Round 3 (9th–12th place), meaning only 1 provincial champion ([Kerry](63970030.md)) played in the last eight.

  [^cite_note-31]

- [Cork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cork_county_football_team) failed to reach the All-Ireland semi-finals for the 14th year in a row, equalling the worst streak in their history. (Their previous worst was 1929–1942, fourteen championships.)

  [^cite_note-32]

- [Louth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louth_county_football_team) reached the All-Ireland semi-finals for the first time since [1957](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1957_All-Ireland_Senior_Football_Championship).

  [^cite_note-33]

- Mayo's victory in the All-Ireland marked the first time a [Connacht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connacht_GAA) team had won the championship since [Galway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway_county_football_team) in [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_All-Ireland_Senior_Football_Championship).

- [Mayo](63970057.md) won the All-Ireland for the first time since [1951](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1951_All-Ireland_Senior_Football_Championship), ending a 75-year drought and an eleven-final losing streak. This is the longest gap between consecutive titles by a county, with the second-longest being [Louth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louth_county_football_team)'s 45-year gap between [1912](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1912_All-Ireland_Senior_Football_Championship) and [1957](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1957_All-Ireland_Senior_Football_Championship).
