The **Union of Democrats "For Lithuania"** ([Lithuanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_language): *Demokratų sąjunga „Vardan Lietuvos“*; DSVL) is a [centre-left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre-left) and [green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_politics)[^cite_note-ideo-2][^cite_note-a414-5] [Lithuanian](17675.md) political party founded on 29 January 2022 by [Saulius Skvernelis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saulius_Skvernelis), former [Prime Minister of Lithuania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Lithuania).[^cite_note-Viluckas-6] The party is moderately [socially conservative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_conservatism).[^cite_note-ideo-2]

Most of the party's members are former members of the [Lithuanian Farmers and Greens Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_Farmers_and_Greens_Union). The party's name "For Lithuania" (Lithuanian: *Vardan Lietuvos*, literally *For the sake of Lithuania*) is a reference to the final line of the national anthem of Lithuania, [Tautiška giesmė](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tauti%C5%A1ka_giesm%C4%97). The Union of Democrats became a member of the [European Green Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Green_Party) and ran in the [2024 European Parliament election in Lithuania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_European_Parliament_election_in_Lithuania), winning one seat to the European Parliament.[^cite_note-lrt1-7] The party campaigns on environmental issues as well as redistributive socioeconomic policies.[^cite_note-greblikas-8]

## History

### Foundation

Saulius Skvernelis was appointed [Prime Minister of Lithuania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Lithuania) on 22 November 2016, supported by the [Lithuanian Farmers and Greens Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_Farmers_and_Greens_Union) and [Social Democratic Party of Lithuania](425771.md), and formed the [Skvernelis Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skvernelis_Cabinet). Though elected to the [Seimas](571739.md) in the [2016 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Lithuanian_parliamentary_election) on the Farmer-Green list, he was an [independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_\(politician\)), and his conflict with the party's chairman [Ramūnas Karbauskis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ram%C5%ABnas_Karbauskis) led to speculation that he intended to form his own political party. In 2019, the [Centre of Registers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_of_Registers?action=edit\&redlink=1) registered the name "Political Party 'For the Fatherland'" ([Lithuanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_language): *Politinė partija „Tėvynės labui“*), which was Skvernelis' election slogan during the [2019 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Lithuanian_presidential_election), further fueling speculation.[^cite_note-9]

The Farmer-Greens lost the [2020 parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Lithuanian_parliamentary_election). Skvernelis and Karbauskis disagreed over the party's role as leader of the opposition, as well as the composition of their [shadow cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shadow_cabinet), which Karbauskis sought to staff with party members loyal to him, leading to renewed speculation of an impending party split.[^cite_note-10] On 7 September 2021, Skvernelis and ten other members left the Farmer-Green parliamentary group and proclaimed the formation of the Democrats – For Lithuania parliamentary group (Lithuanian: *Demokratai – vardan Lietuvos*). They cited their opposition to the party's aggressive opposition stance and stated their interest to work as a constructive opposition.[^cite_note-11] Independent, previously Social Democratic parliamentarians [Algirdas Butkevičius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algirdas_Butkevi%C4%8Dius) and [Domas Griškevičius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domas_Gri%C5%A1kevi%C4%8Dius) also joined the group.[^cite_note-12]

The group announced its intention to reorganize into a [political party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_party), which caused numerous branches of the Farmer-Greens, as well as the mayors of [Lazdijai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lazdijai), [Šilutė](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%A0ilut%C4%97) and [Neringa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neringa_Municipality) to leave the party and declare their intention to join.[^cite_note-13] It was also joined by former [Minister of Environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Environment_\(Lithuania\)) [Kęstutis Mažeika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C4%99stutis_Ma%C5%BEeika) and [European Commissioner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Commissioner) [Virginijus Sinkevičius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginijus_Sinkevi%C4%8Dius).[^cite_note-Viluckas-6] The party was founded on 29 January 2022, in a founding conference in [Palanga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palanga).[^cite_note-14]

### Activity

Immediately after foundation, DSVL became one of the leading parties in popularity polls, while the Farmer-Greens and the [Labour Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labour_Party_\(Lithuania\)) both declined.[^cite_note-15] Over time, its support plateaued and slightly shrank due to competition with the Social Democrats. It is one of three leading opposition parties in the [Thirteenth Seimas of Lithuania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thirteenth_Seimas_of_Lithuania).

In the [2023 municipal elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Lithuanian_municipal_elections), the party won 6.68 percent of the vote, somewhat underperforming in comparison to the polls and ending up below the Farmers-Greens and the Social Democrats. Its strongest showings were in municipalities whose mayors and branches had defected to the party in 2021 and 2022.[^cite_note-16] It fielded a candidate in a by-election in the Raseniai-Kėdainiai constituency on 3 September 2023, but finished the worst of all the candidates, at 2.48 percent of the vote.[^cite_note-vrk.lt-17]

On 17 June 2023, it announced its decision to seek to join the [European Green Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Green_Party).[^cite_note-18] It joined officially in February 2024.[^cite_note-19] The party participated in the [2024 European Parliament election in Lithuania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_European_Parliament_election_in_Lithuania), earning 5.95% of the popular vote and winning a single seat in the [European Parliament](9581.md) (out of 11 Lithuanian ones). This was considered a success for the party, given how the party was only two years old at the time of the 2024 European Parliament election.[^cite_note-lrt1-7]

In the [2024 parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Lithuanian_parliamentary_election), DSVL finished in fourth place, securing 14 out of 141 total seats in the [Fourteenth Seimas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourteenth_Seimas).[^cite_note-20] [Lithuanian Social Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_Social_Democratic_Party) formed a [ruling coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paluckas_Cabinet) with DSVL and populist party [Dawn of Nemunas](75998749.md).[^cite_note-21] As being agreed on in the coalition treaty, Skvernelis was elected the [Speaker of the Seimas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speaker_of_the_Seimas) on 14 November 2024, with 107 votes in favour and 19 against.[^cite_note-22]

## Ideology

The party describes itself as being [centre-left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre-left) on economic policy and [socially conservative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_conservatism) on socio-cultural issues. It supports [guaranteed minimum income](457460.md), strengthening the traditional family, achieving [climate neutrality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Net_zero_emissions) by 2045 and [decentralization](49139.md).[^cite_note-ideo-2] In its program, it defines itself as centre-left, but maintains its focus on family values.[^cite_note-23] It is a member of the [European Green Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Green_Party) and focuses on environmentalism and green politics, as well as centre-left socioeconomic issues.[^cite_note-greblikas-8]

The Union of Democrats is considered to be ideologically heterodox, and its founding members include former members of almost every previously existing Lithuanian political party. Many of its vice-chairpeople, including Ausma Miškinienė, [Lukas Savickas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lukas_Savickas), and Vytautas Bakas were close allies of Skvernelis in the Skvernelis Cabinet.[^cite_note-24] This has led to claims that the party was founded out of [political opportunism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_opportunism) and does not have an ideology beyond being elected to parliament.[^cite_note-25]
