**Matthew Cooper Wale** [CBE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander_of_the_Order_of_the_British_Empire) (born 13 June 1968) is a [Solomon Islands](265083.md) [politician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politician) serving as [prime minister of Solomon Islands](4282815.md) since 2026. A member of the [Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solomon_Islands_Democratic_Party), he has been a member of the [National Parliament of Solomon Islands](4503022.md) since 2008, representing the [Aoke/Langalanga constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aoke/Langalanga_constituency),[^cite_note-1] and served as the official [Leader of the Opposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leader_of_the_Opposition_\(Solomon_Islands\)) from 2019 to 2026.

## Political career

Wale was elected in [a special election held on 27 March 2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Aoke/Langalanga_by-election) following the death of sitting MP [Bartholomew Ulufa'alu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartholomew_Ulufa'alu).[^cite_note-2]

Following the [2019 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Solomon_Islands_general_election), Wale was appointed as [Leader of the Opposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leader_of_the_Opposition_\(Solomon_Islands\)) on 17 May 2019.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

In 2021, amid [mass unrest in the country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Solomon_Islands_unrest), Wale called for Prime Minister [Manasseh Sogavare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manasseh_Sogavare) to step down.[^cite_note-\:2-5] On 28 November 2021, Wale filed a no-confidence motion against the Sogavare government, with debate scheduled for 6 December.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-flashpoint-7] The motion was ultimately defeated.[^cite_note-8]

Wale opposed the security agreement signed by Sogavare and the Chinese government in 2022.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

Following the [2024 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Solomon_Islands_general_election), Wale was a nominee for the position of prime minister. Wale, who received 18 votes, lost to [Jeremiah Manele](10375180.md), who received 31 votes.[^cite_note-11]

He was appointed [Commander of the Order of the British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander_of_the_Order_of_the_British_Empire) (CBE) in the [2025 New Year Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_New_Year_Honours) for political and public service.[^cite_note-12]

On 15 May 2026, Wale was elected prime minister by the [National Parliament of Solomon Islands](4503022.md) after Manele was removed in a no-confidence vote on 7 May.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] In June 2026, Wale announced that he would review a security agreement with China signed by then-prime minister Sogavare in 2022.[^cite_note-15]
