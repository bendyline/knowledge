**Palwal district** is the 21st district of [Haryana](14189.md) [state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_and_territories_of_India) in Delhi NCR region of northern [India](14533.md). It was created on 15 August 2008. [Palwal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palwal) City is the headquarters of the district. It is part of the [Braj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braj) region.

The city is situated 60 km (37 mi) from [Delhi](37756.md), 29 km (18 mi) from [Faridabad](1180794.md) and 50 km (31 mi) from [Gurugram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gurugram) on the Delhi-Agra national highway. The area of the city is 40 km<sup>2</sup> (15 sq mi).[^cite_note-1]

## Etymology

According to legend, the city of Palwal is named for the demon Palwalasur who was said to have ruled during the reign of [Pandavas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pandavas).

### Vedic period

Palwal is supposed to figure in the earliest [Aryan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aryan) traditions under the name of Apelava, part of the [Pandava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pandava) kingdom of [Indraprastha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indraprastha), which was later restored by [Vikramaditya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vikramaditya).[^cite_note-2]

#### Mound

**Kaserua Khera mound** in Manpur village, 20 km south of Palwal and 7 km west of Aurangabad on NH-44, is a 3 acres mound with an extensive layer of 3,000 years old [mahabharta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahabharta) era [Indo-Aryan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Aryan_peoples) [vedic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vedic_era) [painted grey ware culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Painted_grey_ware_culture) (PGW) of western Gangetic plain and the [Ghaggar-Hakra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghaggar-Hakra_River)-[Saraswati River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saraswati_River) Valley, of which first ever ecavation commenced in 2023.[^cite_note-3] The excavations of 2-m deposit of PGW revealed 20 m x 1 m cremation section, PGW sherds, [Red Ware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_and_red_ware) sherds, charred and uncharred animal bones, two burnt human jaws and a portion on human skull suggesting these relate to death rituals and cremation of human body. Other finds include an motif of four-legged animal with a horn and tail on a PGW pot sherd (first ever such animal motif in PGW, which is also filled with dots suggesting it is a deer). The site has a rich variety of PGW in terms of painting, designs, fabric and colours.[^cite_note-exct1-4] In 2025, Haryana government announced a INR 95 crore restoration plan for upgrade of 20 monuments across the state including the Kaserua Khera mound.[^cite_note-bhai1-5]

### Mughal period

During the reign of [Mughal Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mughal_Empire), the freedom fighter [Kanha Rawat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahin#Kanha) carried forward the legacy of Jat leader [Gokula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gokula) to fight against [Aurangzeb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aurangzeb). After Kanha refused to convert his religion to Islam, Aurangzeb had him buried alive at Rawatpara.[^cite_note-IslamicInvaders1-6][^cite_note-8] A statue of Kanha was unveiled inside the park in February 2014 by Vishvendra Singh - the scion of [Bharatpur State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bharatpur_State).[^cite_note-Web_India-9][^cite_note-10] *Kanha Rawat Memorial Park* was constructed around his [samadhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samadhi_\(shrine\)) at his native [Bahin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahin) village in [Hathin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hathin) tehsil.

### British Raj

During the [British Raj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Raj), Palwal was a part of the [Punjab Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjab_Province_\(British_India\)) and [Gurgaon district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gurgaon_district).[^cite_note-11]

[Mahatma Gandhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahatma_Gandhi) was first arrested at [Palwal railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palwal_railway_station). The historical building "Gandhi Ashrama" was erected in memory of the incident.[^cite_note-12] [Uttawar forced sterilisations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uttawar_forced_sterilisations), occurred when on November 6, 1976, when [mass vasectomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compulsory_sterilization#India) of nearly 800 men of Uttawar village was done, in Palwal district, Haryana during India’s [Emergency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Emergency_\(India\)) imposed by [Indira Gandhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indira_Gandhi).[^cite_note-times-13]

### Independent India

On 15 August 1979, [Gurgaon district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gurgaon_district) was divided, with Palwal becoming part of the new [Faridabad district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faridabad_district).[^cite_note-14] Later, Palwal became the 21st district of Haryana on 15 August 2008.

## Geography

### Topography

Palwal is located at [28°09′N 77°20′E  /  28.15°N 77.33°E  / 28.15; 77.33](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Palwal_district\&params=28.15_N_77.33_E_),[^cite_note-15] between the eastern bank of [Yamuna](354286.md) river and the western flank of [Aravalli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aravalli_Range) mountain range. It has an average elevation of 195 metres (639 ft).

### Forests

The Forest Department aims to increase forestation for soil conservation in the district, in line with the National Forests Policy of the [Ministry of Environment and Forests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Environment,_Forest_and_Climate_Change). According to the plan, about one-third of the geographical area should be under tree cover. In order to achieve this objective, large-scale plantings have been undertaken by the community, *panchayat*, government and private land over the last 20 years. Saplings of eucalyptus, shisham, neem, and other fruit plants were distributed free of costs to farmers to plant in their fields.

## Demographics

According to the [2011 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_census_of_India), Palwal district had a [population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_of_India) of 1,042,708.[^cite_note-districtcensus-16] Its [population growth rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_planning_in_India) over the decade 2001–2011 was 25.49%.[^cite_note-districtcensus-16] Palwal had a [sex ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_ratio) of 879 [females](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women_in_India) for every 1,000 males,[^cite_note-districtcensus-16] and a [literacy rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literacy_in_India) of 70.3%. Scheduled Castes make up 19.48% of the population.[^cite_note-districtcensus-16]

### Culture

Palwal has an annual festival known as "Baldev Chhat Ka Mela" which celebrates the legend of [Balarama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balarama) killing the demon Palwasur during the reign of [Pandavas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pandavas). There is also a temple dedicated to Balaram near the *chowk* (marketplace) of the municipal office.

## Administration

### District setup

Palwal district comprises 282 villages, 237 *[gram panchayats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gram_panchayat)*, one municipal council, two municipal committees, three sub-divisions, four development blocks and three *[tehsils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehsil)*. The sub-divisions are under the control of sub-divisional magistrate while each development block is under the control of a block development and panchayat officer. All the blocks are covered under Swarn Jayanti Gram Swarozgar Yojna and other developmental programmes. Developmental programmes are overseen by the additional deputy commissioner-cum chief executive officer, DRDA Palwal. The district is under the overall charge of the deputy commissioner who is also the chairman of the District Rural Development Agency.

## Economy

Palwal consists of agricultural and commercial areas, and has many temples, schools, colleges and banks. It also contains developed areas like Housing board colony, Kalra colony, New colony, main market, shivapuri, Krishna colony, kailash nagar, shiv colony, camp colony and Huda sector 2. There is a main *chowk* (English: market) called the Heart of Palwal. The government plans to develop the economy of Palwal in line with nearby cities like Delhi, Gurgaon, [Noida](986046.md), Faridabad, [Hodal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hodal), [Aligarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aligarh), and [Mathura](283812.md).
