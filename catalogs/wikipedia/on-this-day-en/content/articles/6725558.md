***Nature Medicine*** is a monthly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [medical journal](29500858.md) published by [Nature Portfolio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nature_Portfolio) covering all aspects of [medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medicine). It was established in 1995. The journal seeks to publish research papers that "demonstrate novel insight into disease processes, with direct evidence of the [physiological relevance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physiological_relevance) of the results".[^cite_note-1] *Nature Medicine* also publishes reviews, perspectives and other content commissioned from leading scientists in their fields[^cite_note-2]. As with other *Nature* journals, there is no external [editorial board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editorial_board), with editorial decisions being made by an in-house team, although [peer review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) by external expert referees forms a part of the review process. The [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is [João Monteiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jo%C3%A3o_Monteiro_\(editor\)).[^cite_note-\:0-3]

According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2025 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 52.5, ranking it 1st out of 259 journals in the category "Biochemistry & Molecular Biology".[^cite_note-WoS-4] The table below provides a detailed overview of recent bibliometric indicators for *Nature Medicine*:
