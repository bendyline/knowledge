The **2025–26 NHL season** was the [109th season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_NHL_seasons) of operation (108th season of play) of the [National Hockey League](21809.md) (NHL). The regular season began on October 7, 2025,[^cite_note-startdate-1] and was the final regular season played under the 82-game schedule before the schedule expands to 84 games in 2026–27.[^cite_note-2026CBA-2] A mid-season break took place in February due to the NHL's participation in the [2026 Winter Olympics](38416091.md) in Italy.[^cite_note-startdate-1] The [Stanley Cup playoffs](10490910.md) began in April 2026 and ended on June 14, with the [Carolina Hurricanes](70523.md) defeating the [Vegas Golden Knights](47712000.md) in six games to capture their second Stanley Cup in franchise history and first since 2006.&#x20;

After playing the previous season under its temporary identity, the Utah Hockey Club was renamed the [Utah Mammoth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_Mammoth) in the off-season.[^cite_note-3]

## League business

### Collective bargaining agreement

The 2020 extension of the 2013 [collective bargaining agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NHL_Collective_Bargaining_Agreement) (CBA) expires at the end of the 2025–26 NHL season.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] On June 27, 2025, the league and the [NHL Players' Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NHL_Players'_Association) agreed on a new four-year extension that will take effect from the 2026–27 to 2029–30 seasons. Among the changes under the agreement, the 2026–27 regular season expands from 82 to 84 games, featuring two more divisional games.[^cite_note-2026CBA-2]

### Entry draft

The [2025 NHL entry draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_NHL_entry_draft) took place on June 27–28, 2025, at the [Peacock Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peacock_Theater) in [Los Angeles](18110.md).[^cite_note-6]

### Utah rebranding

After opting to play the [2024–25 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_NHL_season) with a temporary identity, the [Utah Hockey Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_Hockey_Club) announced on January 29, 2025, that fans attending their following four home games in late January and early February could vote for a permanent identity for the club, with the final three options being the existing Utah Hockey Club, Utah Mammoth, and Utah Wasatch. While the Wasatch name was not one of the six finalists, it was intended to honor the idea of a mythical snow creature similar to the yeti, with a "Utah-centric" approach inspired by the [Wasatch Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wasatch_Mountains);[^cite_note-7] though originally considered a likely name, "[Yeti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeti)" or "Yetis" had been ruled out as a choice following trademark disputes with [Yeti Holdings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeti_Holdings).[^cite_note-8] One day later, on January 30, the team announced that "Wasatch" was being removed from the survey, being replaced by previously announced option "Utah Outlaws".[^cite_note-9]

On April 30, 2025, speculation arose that "Mammoth" was the selected name following leaks online that showed the team changing its [YouTube](3524766.md) handle to "UtahMammoth."[^cite_note-10] This speculation was confirmed on May 7, 2025, when the team officially revealed their permanent name as the "[Utah Mammoth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_Mammoth)." A new logo and uniforms were revealed as well, retaining the same color and striping scheme.[^cite_note-\:0-11]

### Canceled All–Star Game and Olympics break

The [2026 All-Star Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_National_Hockey_League_All-Star_Game) was planned to be held in early February 2026, at [UBS Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UBS_Arena) in [Elmont, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elmont,_New_York), the home of the [New York Islanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Islanders). The league intended to use the game as a "send-off" for the league's players participation at the [2026 Games](75053831.md) in [Milan](36511.md) and [Cortina d'Ampezzo](568829.md), Italy, with players who would have competed in both leaving directly from New York to Italy. This was scheduled to be the first time that the league participated in the [Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_hockey_at_the_Olympic_Games) since 2014, and it would have been the first time the All-Star Game was held in the same year as Olympic participation since [2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_National_Hockey_League_All-Star_Game).[^cite_note-58] However, the league announced on April 30, 2025, that the game had been canceled, with UBS Arena instead hosting an unspecified Olympic kickoff event.[^cite_note-59] By October 2025, the league decided to cancel the event altogether and postpone UBS Arena's All-Star Game to 2027.[^cite_note-60]

### Postponed games

- The [Columbus Blue Jackets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Columbus_Blue_Jackets_season) home game against the [Los Angeles Kings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Los_Angeles_Kings_season) scheduled for January 26, 2026, was postponed to March 9, due to a [severe winter storm](82168826.md).

## Playoffs

Main article: [2026 Stanley Cup playoffs](80157146.md)

### Bracket

In each round, teams compete in a best-of-seven series following a 2–2–1–1–1 format (scores in the bracket indicate the number of games won in each best-of-seven series). The team with [home ice advantage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_advantage) plays at home for games one and two (and games five and seven, if necessary), and the other team is at home for games three and four (and game six, if necessary). The top three teams in each division make the playoffs, along with two [wild cards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_card_\(sports\)) in each conference, for a total of eight teams from each conference.

In the first round, the lower [seeded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seed_\(sports\)) wild card in each conference was played against the division winner with the best record while the other wild card was played against the other division winner, and both wild cards were [de facto](43594.md) #4 seeds. The other series matched the second and third-place teams from the divisions. In the first two rounds, home ice advantage was awarded to the team with the better seed. In the conference finals and Stanley Cup Final, home ice advantage was awarded to the team with the better regular season record.

## Uniforms

### Wholesale team changes

- The [Boston Bruins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_Bruins) unveiled a new logo, permanently adopting the re-designed "spoked B" logo previously used in their [2023–24 centennial season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_Boston_Bruins_season) and 2024 centennial game. The team also introduced new uniforms, resembling jerseys worn by the Bruins in the 1980s and 1990s as well as the prior season's centennial game.

  [^cite_note-86]

- The [Carolina Hurricanes](70523.md) revealed a new road jersey, based on the jersey the team wore during the [2023 Stadium Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_NHL_Stadium_Series). The logo is recolored in black and red, with the [flag of North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_North_Carolina) on the left shoulder, and a "strutting Stormy" mascot logo, based on the vintage logos of the four "[Tobacco Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tobacco_Road_\(rivalry\))" college teams in the area, on the right shoulder.

  [^cite_note-87]

- The [Chicago Blackhawks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Blackhawks) debuted a centennial edition uniform, which temporarily replaced their regular red home jerseys for the entire season. This uniform mostly resembled their current home jerseys, but added a lace-up collar and gold trim around the crest and back numbers, as well as the 100th anniversary patch on the right shoulder.

  [^cite_note-88]

  &#x20;The Blackhawks also reintoduced their alternate black jerseys, to be worn for select home games.

  [^cite_note-89]

- The [Colorado Avalanche](70948.md) unveiled blue [Quebec Nordiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quebec_Nordiques) throwback uniforms as their 30th anniversary heritage jersey, paying tribute to the franchise's time in [Quebec City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quebec_City).

  [^cite_note-90]

- The [Dallas Stars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dallas_Stars) unveiled a new "'99" third jersey based on the "big star" design worn from [1997](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997%E2%80%9398_NHL_season) to [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005%E2%80%9306_NHL_season). The jersey features the design in the team's current colors, with a black base and victory green waist and sleeve sections, as well as "3OT 1999" written in the collar, referencing the Stars' [1999 Stanley Cup Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1999_Stanley_Cup_Final) game 6 victory in the original jerseys.

  [^cite_note-91]

  [^cite_note-92]

- The [Detroit Red Wings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit_Red_Wings) unveiled a centennial edition uniform, which they wore for select home games. They feature inspirations from the previous incarnations of the team, the Detroit Cougars and the Detroit Falcons, as well as a chain-stitched crest featuring the original version of the team's "winged wheel" primary logo.

  [^cite_note-93]

- The [Edmonton Oilers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmonton_Oilers) revealed a new third jersey worn seven times during the season. The light-tan uniform features an all-new "Oilers" wordmark as the crest and a new "Oil Country" shoulder patch, with a blue shoulder yoke and blue and orange striping.

  [^cite_note-94]

- The [Los Angeles Kings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Kings) debuted a new third jersey worn 16 times during the season. The jersey resembled the Kings' original 1967–68 uniforms, featuring the team's classic crown logo as the crest, but with a black base and silver striping replacing the original forum blue and gold.

  [^cite_note-95]

  [^cite_note-96]

- The [Minnesota Wild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnesota_Wild) re-introduced their former white jerseys, worn from their [2000–01 inaugural season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000%E2%80%9301_NHL_season) through [2012–13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012%E2%80%9313_NHL_season), as a 25th anniversary uniform, but with the formerly-tan striping and sleeve numbers now gold.

  [^cite_note-97]

- The [New York Rangers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Rangers) debuted a centennial edition uniform, which they wore for select home games. They feature a lighter shade of blue compared to their regular jerseys and the diagonal "RANGERS" wordmark in white lettering, paying tribute to the uniforms worn by the team during their [inaugural 1926–27 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1926%E2%80%9327_New_York_Rangers_season).

  [^cite_note-98]

- The [Ottawa Senators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottawa_Senators) released a new third jersey, their first since the [2019–20 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%9320_NHL_season). The jersey is red, trimmed in black and gold on the hem and sleeves, with the [Peace Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace_Tower) and the [flag of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Canada) on the back of the jersey.

  [^cite_note-99]

- The [Pittsburgh Penguins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pittsburgh_Penguins) unveiled a new third jersey featuring a yellow base with black striping and their skating-penguin crest without its usual triangle. The jersey features multiple design choices referencing the city of Pittsburgh itself, with the gold base evoking previous Penguins third jerseys.

  [^cite_note-100]

- The [San Jose Sharks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Jose_Sharks) re-introduced their former teal uniforms, previously worn from [1998](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998%E2%80%9399_NHL_season) to [2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006%E2%80%9307_NHL_season), as their "Heritage 2.0" 35th anniversary jersey.

  [^cite_note-101]

- The [Seattle Kraken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Kraken) unveiled a new third jersey. The jersey is black, with sonar-inspired stripes on the sleeves and socks. The crest, stripes and [Muckleshoot Tribe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muckleshoot_Tribe) sponsor patch glow in the dark, referencing [bioluminescence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioluminescence) commonly found in deep sea creatures.

  [^cite_note-102]

- The [St. Louis Blues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Louis_Blues) revealed a new logo and colors based on the "heritage" blue note used in the [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_NHL_Winter_Classic) and [2022 Winter Classic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_NHL_Winter_Classic) games. The home uniforms resemble those worn in the 2017 game and utilized as an alternate jersey from 2018 to 2025, while the road uniforms resemble those worn in the 2022 game. The previous navy-accented blue home jersey were retained as alternates.

  [^cite_note-103]

- The [Utah Mammoth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_Mammoth) debuted their first jersey set since rebranding from the Utah Hockey Club. The home jerseys have replaced the diagonal "UTAH" wordmark with the Mammoth primary logo crest and Utah state outline shoulder patches, while the road uniforms retain the wordmark in an overhauled font and feature the Mammoth logo on the shoulders.

## Media rights

### National

#### Canada

This is the 12th and final season of the Canadian national broadcast rights deal with [Sportsnet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NHL_on_Sportsnet), before a new 12-year contract begins in 2026–27.[^cite_note-SN_2026_deal-162] The original deal expiring this 2025–26 season included Sportsnet's sub-licensing agreements to air Saturday *[Hockey Night in Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hockey_Night_in_Canada)* games on [CBC Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBC_Television), French-language broadcasts on [TVA Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TVA_Sports),[^cite_note-SN_2014_deal-163] and the final year of [Amazon Prime Video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NHL_on_Prime_Video)'s two-year streaming deal.[^cite_note-Amazon_MNH-164]

This ended up being the 74th and final year of CBC's relationship with the league. After the season, CBC announced that it would no longer sub-license NHL coverage. CBC also maintained ownership of the *Hockey Night in Canada* brand, stating that it would use the brand in "different ways".[^cite_note-165]

Linear television

- Saturday *Hockey Night in Canada* games aired across CBC, one or more of the [four Sportsnet feeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sportsnet#Regional_feeds), [Sportsnet One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sportsnet_One), [Sportsnet 360](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sportsnet_360), and [Citytv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citytv). Decisions on network assignments were made on a week-by-week basis, and select games simulcast on multiple networks.

  [^cite_note-166]

- Sportsnet also exclusively aired *[Wednesday Night Hockey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotiabank_Wednesday_Night_Hockey)*, the Winter Classic, Stadium Series, and All-Star Game.

  [^cite_note-SN_2014_deal-163]

- TVA Sports' schedule included Saturday *[La super soirée LNH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TVA_Sports#NHL_coverage)* (lit. 'NHL Super Evening') games, as well as French-language broadcasts of the Winter Classic, Stadium Series, All-Star Game, among others.

If the deal goes through, DAZN and Main Street would integrate their live streaming platform in the United States and collaborate on programming.[^cite_note-183] Afterwards, *[Sports Business Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_Business_Journal)* reported that Main Street had missed a payment to the [St. Louis Cardinals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Louis_Cardinals) baseball team and that the company would be forced to dissolve operations at the end of the 2025–26 season if the deal with DAZN did not go through by the end of January 2026.[^cite_note-184] In January, *Sports Business Journal* reported that Main Street had missed payments to "several, if not all 13" of its [NBA](22093.md) teams. As a result, Main Street and the teams with missed payments entered a 15-day grace period.[^cite_note-185] Two days later, *Sports Business Journal* reported that talks with DAZN had stopped progressing and all [MLB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLB) teams except the [Tampa Bay Rays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tampa_Bay_Rays) had not received payments on-time.[^cite_note-186]

On January 8, 2026, all nine MLB teams with agreements with FanDuel Sports Network terminated their contracts with the company due to missed payments.[^cite_note-187] In February 2026 it was reported, and later confirmed, that most of the impacted teams would move their production and distribution to [MLB Local Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLB_Local_Media), but that the [Los Angeles Angels](1360083.md) and [Atlanta Braves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta_Braves) were considering in-house operations that could include potentially include team-owned RSNs. [Puck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puck_\(media_company\)) reported that Main Street intends to wind down FanDuel Sports Network in mid-April after the close of the 2025–26 NBA and NHL seasons.[^cite_note-188][^cite_note-189][^cite_note-190][^cite_note-191][^cite_note-192]
