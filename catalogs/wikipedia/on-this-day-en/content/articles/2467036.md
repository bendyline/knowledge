**Katihar district** is one of the thirty-eight [districts of Bihar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Bihar) [state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_and_territories_of_India) in India, and [Katihar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katihar) city is the administrative headquarters of this district. The district is a part of [Purnia Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purnia_Division). It is prominently known for its [Katihar Junction Railway Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katihar_Junction_railway_station), which is a Category A1 station on the [Barauni–Guwahati line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barauni%E2%80%93Guwahati_line). It has been included in the Aspirational Districts Program of the Government of India since 2018, to improve its socio-economic indicators.[^cite_note-\:0-1] Katihar was the largest maize producing district of Bihar in 2022.[^cite_note-2]

## Geography

Katihar district occupies an area of 3,057 square kilometres (1,180 sq mi),[^cite_note-Reference_Annual-3] comparatively equivalent to [Canada](5042916.md)'s [Akimiski Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akimiski_Island).[^cite_note-Islands-4]

**Rivers**: Mahananda, Ganges, Koshi, Righa

Katihar district is situated in the plains of North Eastern part of Bihar State, surrounded by [Purnia district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purnia_district) (Bihar) in the north and the west, [Bhagalpur district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhagalpur_district) (Bihar) and [Sahebganj district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sahebganj_district) (Jharkhand) in the south and [Malda district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malda_district) and [Uttar Dinajpur district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uttar_Dinajpur_district) (Paschim Bengal) in the east.

## Economy

In 2006, the [Ministry of Panchayati Raj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Panchayati_Raj) named Katihar one of the country's 250 [most backward districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_in_India) (out of a total of [640](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_India)).[^cite_note-brgf-5] It is one of the 36 districts in Bihar currently receiving funds from the [Backward Regions Grant Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backward_Regions_Grant_Fund) Programme (BRGF).[^cite_note-brgf-5] It has also been included in the Aspirational Districts Program of the Government of India since 2018, to improve its socio-economic indicators.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

The main source of economy in Katihar is agriculture, and most of its industries are agro-based. Rice is the main crop, while banana, jute and maize are the chief cash crops of the district. Makhana Phodi, an agro-industry where edible makhana ([foxnuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foxnuts)) is produced from raw makhana, is a rapidly-rising industry in Katihar.[^cite_note-6]

Katihar was once known as the "jute capital" of Bihar and boasted of two major jute mills, Sunbio Manufacturing Private Limited (also known as Old Jute Mill) and Rai Bahadur Hardutroy Motilal Chamria (RBHM) Jute Mill (also known as New Jute Mill).[^cite_note-\:1-7]

- Old Jute Mill is spread over 35 acres of land, and is run by Govind Sharda backed Sunbio Manufacturing Private Limited. It was earlier run by the Bihar State Industrial Development Corporation (BSIDC), but was leased out in 2001. The present worker capacity (as of 2020) of the mill is around 200, with a daily production of around 10 tonnes, down from its peak of approximately 3000 and hundred tons.

  [^cite_note-\:1-7]

- New Jute Mill is spread over 53.39 acres of land. It was started as a private mill in 1935 and first shut down in 1977. It was acquired by National Jute Manufacturers Corporation (NJMC) in 1980 and ran as a public sector undertaking till 2004, when it was closed again. It was restarted for a third time in the [Public-Private Partnership (PPP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public%E2%80%93private_partnerships_in_India) model in 2014. The mill closed its shutter in 2016, shortly after NJMC was shut following [NITI Ayog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NITI_Aayog) recommendation in 2016, and has been shut since.

  [^cite_note-8]

## Political history

The district has seen immense activity during freedom struggle. There has been renowned freedom fighters like [Alhaj Azhar Ali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alhaj_Azhar_Ali?action=edit\&redlink=1) after whose name there is a village called Ajhrail. The busiest market square, Shaheed Chowk, has been named so in the memory of martyrs of [1942 Quit India Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quit_India_Movement).[^cite_note-9] Among notable freedom fighters from Katihar are, Dhrub Kundu, his father Dr Kishori Lal Kundu, Nakshtra Malakar (known as Robinhood of the Kosi belt).

The [Katihar Lok Sabha constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katihar_Lok_Sabha_constituency) has been represented by politicians like [Sitaram Kesri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sitaram_Kesri) former [Teasurer and President of AICC](149333.md), [Tariq Anwar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tariq_Anwar_\(politician\)), [Nikhil Choudhary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikhil_Kumar_Choudhary), [Mohammad Yunus Saleem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Yunus_Saleem). Kashmiri veteran politician [Mufti Mohammad Sayeed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mufti_Mohammad_Sayeed) also unsuccessfully tried his luck in 1996 Lok Sabha elections.

## Sub-divisions

Katihar District comprises 3 Sub-divisions:

- Katihar

- Barsoi

- Manihari

Katihar sub-division is further divided into 10 blocks: Katihar, Korha, Falka, Sameli, Barari, Kursela, Pranpur, Hasanganj, Dandkhora and Mansahi. Barsoi sub-division consists of 4 blocks: Barsoi, Kadwa, Azamnagar and Balrampur. Manihari sub-division has 2 blocks: Manihari and Amdabad.

Hasanganj being the largest block which was under Zamindari rule with acres of land under the possession of then Late Shri Jogendranarayan Roy Choudhury. The entire landmass with school, temple and market has been donated to the people from the predecessors who remain in Katihar now with little of the possession under the Paul Choudhury fame. Mansahi was also very active Zamindari estate which was at par with Kursela and Falka.

According to the [2011 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_census_of_India), Katihar district has a [population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_of_India) of 3,071,029,[^cite_note-districtcensus-12] roughly equal to the nation of [Oman](22316.md)[^cite_note-cia-13] or the US state of [Iowa](26810748.md).[^cite_note-14] This gives it a ranking of 117th in India (out of a total of [640](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_India)).[^cite_note-districtcensus-12] The district has a population density of 1,004 inhabitants per square kilometre (2,600/sq mi).[^cite_note-districtcensus-12] Its [population growth rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_planning_in_India) over the decade 2001–2011 was 28.23%.[^cite_note-districtcensus-12] Katihar has a [sex ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_ratio) of 916 [females](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women_in_India) for every 1000 males,[^cite_note-districtcensus-12] and a [literacy rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literacy_in_India) of 53.56%. 8.92% of the population lives in urban areas. Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes make up 8.57% and 5.86% of the population respectively.[^cite_note-districtcensus-12]

## Tourist places

Katihar is a district in the state of Bihar, India, and while it may not be as well-known as some other tourist destinations, it does have a few places of interest for visitors. Here are some tourist places in and around Katihar:

1\. [Kosi River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kosi_River) Katihar is located near the Kosi River, which is known as the "Sorrow of Bihar" due to its history of flooding.&#x20;

2\. Manihari Ghat Manihari is a town near Katihar and is known for its historical significance as a trade center during the British colonial period. Manihari Ghat is a common spot for taking a boat ride on the [Ganges River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ganges).

3\. [Kalki Mandir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalki_Mandir): This is a well-known temple dedicated to Goddess Kali. It's a place of religious importance and is often visited by locals and tourists alike.

&#x20;\
&#x20;4\. [Kastaharni Ghat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kastaharni_Ghat): This ghat on the Ganges River is believed to have the power to relieve people of their sufferings and sins. It's a place of religious importance and tranquility.

5\. [Durga Mandir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durga_Mandir,_Katihar?action=edit\&redlink=1): Katihar is known for its Durga Puja celebration. During the Puja, the whole of [Katihar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katihar) is decorated as best can be seen different types of pandals at each other step. Each year, pandals are created using different types of materials and with different themes.

&#x20;\
&#x20;6\. Recently Gogabeel Jheel added to list of ramsar sites and is also 1st community reserve of Bihar
