The **third cabinet of Andrej Babiš** is the current government of the [Czech Republic](5321.md). Following [elections in October 2025](65703300.md), [Andrej Babiš](40378134.md) announced his intention to form a coalition government consisting of his party [ANO](40808475.md), with [Freedom and Direct Democracy](46867556.md) (SPD) and [Motorists for Themselves](76614519.md) (AUTO).[^cite_note-1]

On 6 November 2025, [Tomio Okamura](37423659.md) from [Freedom and Direct Democracy](46867556.md) (SPD) was [elected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_President_of_the_Chamber_of_Deputies_of_the_Parliament_of_the_Czech_Republic_election) President of the Chamber of Deputies for the new parliament with 107 votes.[^cite_note-2]

The list of ministerial candidates was presented to President [Petr Pavel](42272533.md) on 26 November 2025,[^cite_note-candidates-3] and the cabinet was sworn in on 15 December 2025.[^cite_note-4]

A vote of no confidence in the government was called at the request of the opposition. The motion was in response to the revelation of a conversation between Foreign Minister Macinka and the president's advisor Petr Kolář, which was interpreted by the president as blackmail and reported to the authorities. In the messages, Macinka sought to pressure the president into appointing the AUTO MP [Filip Turek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filip_Turek) as the Minister of the Environment.[^cite_note-10] The vote took place on 4 February 2026.

## Actions

On 7 November 2025, [Tomio Okamura](37423659.md) President of the Chamber of Deputies, orderded the removal of the Ukrainian flag from the parliament building, which had been raised there since the start of [Russia's invasion in 2022](70149799.md). In reaction, the opposition [Civic Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civic_Democratic_Party) (ODS) and [Pirates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Pirate_Party) hung Ukrainian flags from their club windows.[^cite_note-12] On 17 January 2026, Okamura ordered the removal of the [EU flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EU_flag) from his office, which was replaced by another [Czech flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_flag).[^cite_note-13]

On 7 January 2026, Babiš confirmed that the ammunition initiative for Ukraine would continue, having previously criticised the scheme as opaque and called for it to be audited or scrapped.[^cite_note-14]
