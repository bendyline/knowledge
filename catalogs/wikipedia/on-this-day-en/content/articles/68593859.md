General elections were held in the [Falkland Islands](7550182.md) on 11 December 2025 to elect the eight constituency members of the [Legislative Assembly](4504398.md) (five from the [Stanley constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_\(constituency\)) and three from the [Camp constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_\(constituency\)))[^cite_note-Election_announcement-2] through [universal suffrage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_suffrage) using [block voting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plurality-at-large_voting), with the [Chief Executive of the Falkland Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Executive_of_the_Falkland_Islands) acting as [returning officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Returning_officer). It was the fifth election since the new [constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Falkland_Islands) came into force, replacing the [Legislative Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Council_of_the_Falkland_Islands) (which had existed since 1845) with the Legislative Assembly.

## Timing and procedure

Under the [Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Falkland_Islands) of the [Falkland Islands](7550182.md), the Legislative Assembly must be [dissolved](652293.md) by the [Governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_the_Falkland_Islands) four years after the first meeting of the Legislative Assembly following the last election (unless the [Executive Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_Council_of_the_Falkland_Islands) advises the Governor to dissolve the Legislative Assembly sooner). An election must then take place within 70 days of the [dissolution](652293.md).[^cite_note-3]

With the first meeting of the previous Legislative Assembly taking place on 8 November 2021,[^cite_note-4] the Legislative Assembly had to be dissolved by midnight on 7 November 2025 and an election take place before 17 January 2026. However, on 7 August 2025, the Executive Council announced an early dissolution of the Legislative Assembly and an election to take place on Thursday 11 December 2025.[^cite_note-Election_announcement-2]
