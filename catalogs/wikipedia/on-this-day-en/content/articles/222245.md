**Nayarit**,[^cite_note-8] officially the **Free and Sovereign State of Nayarit**,[^cite_note-9] is one of the 31 states that, along with [Mexico City](18987.md), comprise the [Federal Entities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_divisions_of_Mexico) of [Mexico](3966054.md). It is divided in [20 municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Nayarit) and its capital city is [Tepic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tepic).

It is bordered by the states of [Sinaloa](222251.md) to the northwest, [Durango](222234.md) to the north, [Zacatecas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zacatecas) to the northeast and [Jalisco](16020434.md) to the south. To the west, Nayarit has a significant share of coastline on the [Pacific Ocean](23070.md), including the islands of [Marías](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islas_Mar%C3%ADas) and [Marietas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islas_Tres_Marietas). The beaches of [San Blas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Blas,_Nayarit) and the so-called "[Riviera Nayarit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riviera_Nayarit)" are popular with tourists and [snowbirds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snowbird_\(person\)). Besides tourism, the economy of the state is based mainly on agriculture and fishing. It is also one of two states where the [tarantula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarantula) species *[Brachypelma klaasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brachypelma_klaasi)* is found, the other being [Jalisco](16020434.md).

Home to [Uto-Aztecan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uto-Aztecan_languages) indigenous peoples such as the [Huichol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huichol) and [Cora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cora_people), the region was exposed to the *[conquistadores](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conquistador)* [Hernán Cortés](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hern%C3%A1n_Cort%C3%A9s) and [Nuño de Guzmán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nu%C3%B1o_de_Guzm%C3%A1n) in the 16th century. Spanish governance was made difficult by indigenous rebellions and by the inhospitable terrain of the Sierra del Nayar. The last independent Cora communities were subjugated in 1722. The state is named after the Cora people's self-designation: *Náayerite*, commemorating Nayar, a resistance leader.[^cite_note-10]

## History



Map of Nayarit before the [Spanish Conquest of the Aztec Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Conquest_of_the_Aztec_Empire)



The colonial *contaduría* (accounting offices) in the old port town of [San Blas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Blas,_Nayarit)

[Radiocarbon dating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiocarbon_dating) estimate [Aztatlán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aztatlan) colonization of the western Mexican coast – including parts of Sinaloa, Nayarit and Jalisco – as occurring as early as 900 AD, with some evidence suggesting it might have been as early as 520 AD. Encountered on the western coast by the Spanish invaders in 1500, the cultures were descended from these original Aztatlán settlements and other [Classic-stage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classic_stage) cultures who had merged with them.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

[Hernán Cortés](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hern%C3%A1n_Cort%C3%A9s) was the first known European to enter into the area now known as Nayarit, which he claimed for Spain as part of [Nueva Galicia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nueva_Galicia). Under [Nuño de Guzmán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nu%C3%B1o_de_Guzm%C3%A1n), Spaniards took the region with considerable brutality, causing the indigenous inhabitants to revolt, in what was later referred to as the [Mixtón War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixt%C3%B3n_War).[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] After almost two centuries of resistance, the last independent Cora communities were incorporated into Spanish administration by force in 1722. Then followed intense missionary efforts by [Jesuits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesuit) to convert the indigenous.[^cite_note-15]

In the colonial period, the port of [San Blas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Blas,_Nayarit) was one of the most important trade ports on the American Pacific coast. [Galleons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galleon) transporting goods from Manila, the Philippines arrived here before the rise of the port of [Acapulco](1446.md). Today, the town still boasts [colonial architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Architecture_of_Mexico#Colonial_Period) from its heyday, such as the *aduana* (customs office), the *contaduría* (accounting offices) and the fortress that protected the port against pirates.

In Nayarit, the struggle for independence from Spain was initiated by the priest José María Mercado, who conquered Tepic and San Blas before being defeated and executed by Spanish royalists. In 1824, in the first constitution of the Mexican Republic, Nayarit was a part of [Jalisco](16020434.md).  In the mid-1800s Comanche Indians, from Texas and Oklahoma, [attacked Tepic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comanche_history#Raiding_Mexico:_1779%E2%80%931870) causing widespread destruction. During the second half of the 19th century, Nayarit was one of the most turbulent territories in Mexico. The population was in open revolt, demanding access to land.[^cite_note-16]

Nayarit was one of the last territories admitted as a state of the Mexican federation, which occurred on 1 May 1917.[^cite_note-Congreso-17]

## Geography

See also: [Sierra Madre Occidental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_Madre_Occidental)



[Sayulita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sayulita) on Nayarit's Pacific coast, a former fishing village now mostly given over to tourism, part of the area now marketed as "La [Riviera Nayarit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riviera_Nayarit)"

Nayarit covers 27,815 square kilometers (10,739 sq mi), making it one of the smaller states in Mexico.[^cite_note-18] Nayarit is located between latitude lines 23°05' north and 20°36' south and longitude lines 103°43' east and 105°46' west.[^cite_note-19] Its terrain is broken up by the western ends of the [Sierra Madre Occidental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_Madre_Occidental) mountains. Its highest mountains are: [San Juan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_de_San_Juan), [Sanguangüey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanguang%C3%BCey?action=edit\&redlink=1), [El Ceboruco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Ceboruco?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Cumbre de Pajaritos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cumbre_de_Pajaritos?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Picachos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Picachos?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-infoplease-20] Nayarit has two volcanoes, [Ceboruco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceboruco) and [Sangangüey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangang%C3%BCey). In the northeast are broad, tropical plains watered by the [Río Grande de Santiago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%ADo_Grande_de_Santiago), a continuation of the [Lerma River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lerma_River). The main state rivers are the [Río Grande de Santiago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%ADo_Grande_de_Santiago), [San Pedro Mezquital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Pedro_Mezquital_River), [Acaponeta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acaponeta), [Ameca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%ADo_Ameca), and [Las Cañas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%ADo_Las_Ca%C3%B1as?action=edit\&redlink=1). The Río Grande de Santiago is the largest river in Nayarit. The Santiago and its tributaries are of major importance for agricultural irrigation. The Ameca and the Las Cañas lie on the border between Nayarit and the states of [Jalisco](16020434.md) and [Sinaloa](222251.md), respectively. Notable [lagoons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagoons) in Nayarit include [Santa María del Oro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Mar%C3%ADa_del_Oro,_Nayarit), [San Pedro Lagunillas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Pedro_Lagunillas) and [Agua Brava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agua_Brava?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22]

### Environment

Nayarit's natural vegetation varies with altitude; coastal lowlands and river valleys were, historically, covered with tropical dry forest, containing many native deciduous trees that lost their leaves during the dry seasons. The [Sinaloan dry forests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinaloan_dry_forests) now cover the northern coastal lowlands, and extend up the valleys of the [San Pedro Mezquital River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Pedro_Mezquital_River) and the [Río Grande de Santiago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%ADo_Grande_de_Santiago) and its tributaries.[^cite_note-23] The [Jalisco dry forests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalisco_dry_forests) ecoregion covers coastal Nayarit south of [San Blas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Blas,_Nayarit) and the Islas Marías.[^cite_note-24]

The [Marismas Nacionales–San Blas mangroves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marismas_Nacionales%E2%80%93San_Blas_mangroves), a network of coastal Pacific lagoons and tidal mangrove forests, extend along the state's northern coast and into adjacent Sinaloa state. The mangroves are home to abundant wildlife, including migratory and resident waterbirds.[^cite_note-25]

The mountains are home to [pine–oak forests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_Madre_Occidental_pine%E2%80%93oak_forests), which vary in density with elevation. Oak forests and woodlands tend to grow at lower elevations, interspersed with smaller areas of humid cloud forest in areas of higher rainfall. The highest elevations contain forests of conifers, pines and oak.[^cite_note-26]

Nayarit also contains hundreds of miles of rainforest in the Sierra. Its wildlife includes hundreds of [bird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bird) species, both resident and breeding/migratory populations, including parrots, like the [lilac-crowned amazon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilac-crowned_amazon) (*Amazona finschi*) and numerous hummingbirds, such as the [Mexican woodnymph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_woodnymph) (*Thalurania ridgwayi*). There are also 119 registered species of mammals, including [white-tailed deer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White-tailed_deer) (*Odocoileus virginianus*), [collared peccary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collared_peccary) (*Dicotyles tajacu*), [brocket deer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brocket_deer) (*Mazama americana*), several types of [armadillo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armadillo), [coyote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coyote), wild felines such as [puma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felis_concolor) (*Felis concolor*), [jaguarundi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaguarundi) (*Puma yagouarundi*), [bobcat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobcat) or *lince rojo*, [ocelot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ocelot) (*Felis pardalis*) and many more species. Historically, the [Mexican wolf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_wolf), [Mexican grizzly bear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_grizzly_bear) and [jaguar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaguar) would have been present in the region, as well, though the bears are now extinct; the Mexican wolf and jaguar survive today only in a few fragmented locations, due to decades of hunting and human encroachment on their habitats.[^cite_note-Pulido-27] Most of the rain forest has been exploited, especially around the region of [Santa María del Oro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Mar%C3%ADa_del_Oro,_Nayarit). The conservation and protection of the rain forest and wildlife of Nayarit is an issue of crucial importance.[^cite_note-Fodor-28]

The [Islas Marías](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islas_Mar%C3%ADas) were designated as the [Islas Marías Biosphere Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islas_Mar%C3%ADas_Biosphere_Reserve) by [UNESCO](21786641.md) in 2010.[^cite_note-29]

Nayarit is Mexico's twenty-ninth most populous state. According to the census of 2020, the state had a population of 1,235,456 and its population density was 39/km<sup>2</sup>.

### Indigenous groups

Nayarit is the home to four indigenous groups: the [Wixaritari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huichol_people) (Huichol), the [Naayeri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cora_people) (Cora), the [Odam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tepehuan_people) (Tepehuan) and the [Nahuatl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahuatl)-speaking [Mexicaneros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexicanero). The indigenous groups mostly inhabit the Nayar highlands, but are also frequently encountered in Tepic and on the Pacific coast, where they have also established colonies. They are known for their crafts and artwork which they sell. About five percent of the state population speaks an indigenous language.[^cite_note-31]

## Economy



[Punta Mita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punta_Mita) is a major tourist destination on the [Riviera Nayarit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riviera_Nayarit)



Shrimp fisherman on the coast of Nayarit

Nayarit is predominantly an agricultural state, and produces a large variety of crops such as [beans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bean), [sorghum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorghum), [sugar cane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugar_cane), [maize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize), [tobacco](30942.md), rice, chiles, [peanuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peanut), [melons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melon), [tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomato), [coffee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffee), mangoes, bananas, and avocados. In addition to these crops, livestock and fishing are also central to the local economy. Approximately six percent of the land in Nayarit is pasture land, with the most common livestock being cattle, horses, pigs, [goats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goats), and [sheep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheep). Nayarit has 289 kilometers of coastline, which provides an abundance of fish and shellfish, including [bass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bass_\(fish\)), [snapper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lutjanidae), [sharks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharks), and [oysters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oysters). There are over 75 [cooperatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooperatives) related to the fishing industry alone in Nayarit. Much of the food produced in Nayarit is exported to the larger urban areas surrounding Mexico City and Guadalajara, and much of the agricultural labor is performed by [migrant laborers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Migrant_laborers). Although mining exists in Nayarit, it is mostly of non-[metallic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metal) substances such as [limestone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limestone) or [kaolin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaolin).[^cite_note-Standish-32]

Beginning in the late 90's, Nayarit has become known as a producer of [specialty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specialty_coffee) [Arabica coffee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffea_arabica), regarded for its fine taste and high density beans grown in the volcanic soils of the [Sierra Madre Occidental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_Madre_Occidental). Nayarit coffee is exported all over the world, including to the UK and Australia via the Grupo Terruño Nayarita farmers cooperative.[^cite_note-33]

In recent years, Nayarit has worked to build its tourism sector, marketing the "[Riviera Nayarit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riviera_Nayarit)" as a safe, beautiful destination served by [Puerto Vallarta International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Vallarta_International_Airport). Popular resort towns include [Bucerías](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bucer%C3%ADas,_Nayarit), [Punta de Mita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punta_de_Mita), [La Cruz de Huanacaxtle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Cruz_de_Huanacaxtle), [San Blas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Blas,_Nayarit), [Santiago Ixcuintla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago_Ixcuintla), [Sayulita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sayulita) and [Tecuala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tecuala). However, some residents in these and other towns are concerned that the growth in the tourism industry might have harmful impacts on the community.[^cite_note-Standish-32] Timeshare scams with links to the local [Nayarit Mafia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mafia) are quite common.[^cite_note-34]
