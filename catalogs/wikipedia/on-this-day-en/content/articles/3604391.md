**Marib** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): مَأْرِب, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Maʾrib*) is a [governorate](690277.md) of [Yemen](350939.md). It is located 173 kilometers to the northeast of Yemen's capital, [Sanaa](18691665.md). The population of Marib Governorate comprises 1.2% of the country's total population. The city of [Marib](3604484.md) is the capital of the governorate, and was established after the discovery of [oil](23195.md) deposits in 1984.[^cite_note-2] The total number of residents living in the governorate was 238,522, according to the 2004 census, and the rate of growth was 2.72%.[^cite_note-National_information_center-3]

As of 28 April 2020, it is the only governorate of the former [North Yemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemen_Arab_Republic) controlled by the [internationally recognized Government of Yemen](31167279.md).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

## Geography

Marib Governorate's area is approximately 17,405 square kilometres (6,720 square miles).

### Climate

The governorate's climate varies based on elevation. The mountainous and elevated regions, which make up the western half of the governorate, are dominated by a moderate climate with hot summers and relatively cold winters. In the low-lying and plain regions, the climate is hot in the summers and moderate in the winters. The desert regions are characterized by a dry climate: hot in the summers, and cold and dry in the winters.

Summer rains fall on most parts of the governorate, but the amount of rainfall is usually small, especially in the eastern parts. Most parts of Marib Governorate suffer from drought, given the sparse rainfall.

## Agriculture and economy



[Marib Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marib_Dam)

In the 1980s, after the region was hit by [floods](50482.md), a [dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marib_Dam#Current_dam) was constructed in Maʼrib, which would later be deemed to be important for the country's agriculture and economy.[^cite_note-KhaleejTimes2013-6][^cite_note-National2015Ma’ribDam-7][^cite_note-GulfNews2015Yemen-8] Its construction was financed by [Sheikh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikh) [Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zayed_bin_Sultan_Al_Nahyan) of the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md), who himself was reportedly a descendant of people from this area.[^cite_note-Burrows2010-9]

The area is home to the largest [gas-fired power plant](12438990.md) in Yemen, the Maʼrib Gas Plant, whose electricity towers have been sabotaged continuously since the beginning of 2011. These sabotage operations did not stop until 2014, due to the inability of the [Basindawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basindawa) government to deter the saboteurs.

Agriculture is the main livelihood for the governorate's residents. Maʼrib Governorate ranks third in Yemen behind [Al Hudaydah Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Hudaydah_Governorate) and [Sanaa Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanaa_Governorate) in terms of agricultural production, making up 7.6% of the country's total agricultural production. Its most important agricultural products are [fruits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fruit), [grains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grain), and [vegetables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vegetable). Many types of [livestock](25160767.md) are found in the governorate, including [cattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cattle), [camels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dromedary), [sheep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheep), [goats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goat), [donkeys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donkey), and [poultry](23197.md). There are also quite a number of beehives. According to statistics for 2007-11, the governorate was home to approximately 1,943,564 sheep, 1,669,370 goats, 42,000 cattle, and 112,782 camels, making up 3.8% of Yemen's total livestock.

## Flora and fauna

See also: [Wildlife of Yemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildlife_of_Yemen)

Vegetation in the governorate is diverse, despite its sparseness and lack of density in terms of quantity and quality from place to place, depending on the nature of the surface and prevailing climate. The most important [trees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tree) are the [Christ's thorn jujube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christ's_thorn_jujube), qard tree, [buckthorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckthorn), [acacia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acacia) (particularly or including the [umbrella thorn acacia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umbrella_thorn_acacia), as well as other [thorny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thorn_\(botany\)) trees. There are also many types of [grasses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grass) and small [plants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plant) that grow in the [rainy seasons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainy_season).

Many types of [wild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildlife) [animals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal) are present in the governorate, and are plentiful in the western areas, including [hyenas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyena), [snakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snake), wild [rabbits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabbit), [hedgehogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hedgehog), and [hyraxes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyrax). [Birds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bird) include [falcons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falcon), [pigeons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pigeon), [eagles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eagle), and [owls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owls). The birds are numerous in areas dense with [trees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tree), as well as near [wadis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wadi), especially those with [water](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water). The presence of the *nimr* ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): نِـمْـر, [leopard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabian_leopard)) was reported in the [Sarawat Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarawat_Mountains).[^cite_note-Judas_et_al.,_2006-10][^cite_note-Spalton_et_al.,_2006-11]
