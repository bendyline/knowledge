The **Mexico national under-20 football team** represents [Mexico](3966054.md) in [association football](10568.md) at the under-20 age level, and is controlled by the [Mexican Football Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Football_Federation) (FMF), the governing body of [football in Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_in_Mexico).

The team has won the [CONCACAF Under-20 Championship](18650041.md) a record thirteen times across its various formats. Mexico's best finish came at the [1977 FIFA World Youth Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1977_FIFA_World_Youth_Championship) finishing as runners-up, the first edition of a [FIFA](11049.md)-sanctioned youth tournament. They also managed a third-place finish at the [2011 edition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_FIFA_U-20_World_Cup).
