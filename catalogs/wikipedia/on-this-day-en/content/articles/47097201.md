The **Presidential Electoral College** ([Burmese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_language): သမ္မတရွေးချယ်တင်မြှောက်ရေးအဖွဲ့) is an [electoral college](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_college) made up of [MPs](98066.md) that elects the [President of Myanmar](11195083.md).

## Composition

It consists of three separate committees:

1. MPs who represent the proportions of MPs elected from each Region or State (the [Amyotha Hluttaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amyotha_Hluttaw))

2. MPs who represent the proportions of MPs elected from each township population (the [Pyithu Hluttaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyithu_Hluttaw))

3. Military-appointed MPs personally nominated by the [Tatmadaw](20394.md) Commander-in-Chief (the combined military representatives from both above houses of the [Pyidaungsu Hluttaw](29739324.md))

## Nomination and selection

Each of the three committees nominate a presidential candidate. Afterward, all the [Pyidaungsu Hluttaw](29739324.md) MPs vote for one of three candidates—the candidate with the highest number of votes is elected President, while the other two are elected as Vice Presidents. The Vice Presidents are designated as First and Second Vice President based on who received the second and third highest number of votes.
