The **Civil Aviation Authority** (**QCAA**, [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الهيئة العامة للطيران المدني في قطر) is an agency of the government of [Qatar](8238258.md). Its head office is in [Doha](26214389.md).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] It was established in 2001. [Hamad International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamad_International_Airport) in Qatar was developed under the supervision of Civil Aviation Authority (Qatar).

## History

The authority's predecessor, the Civil Aviation Department, was established in 1973.[^cite_note-4] In August 2001, Law No. 16 of 2001 was passed, decreeing the Civil Aviation Department and the Meteorology Department be merged into one entity, thereafter known as the Civil Aviation Authority.[^cite_note-5]
