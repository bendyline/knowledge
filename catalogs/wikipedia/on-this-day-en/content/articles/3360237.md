**Prakasam district** named in honour of [Tanguturi Prakasam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanguturi_Prakasam) is in the [coastal Andhra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coastal_Andhra) region of the [Indian state](375986.md) of [Andhra Pradesh](2377.md). It was reorganised on 4 April 2022 and 31 December 2025. The headquarters of the district is [Ongole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ongole). It is bounded by [Bapatla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bapatla_district), [Palnadu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palnadu_district), [Markapuram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markapuram_district), [Sri Potti Sriramulu Nellore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nellore_district) districts and [Bay of Bengal](47892.md).

## Etymology

The district was named after [Tanguturi Prakasam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanguturi_Prakasam), also known as '*Andhra Kesari*', an Indian freedom fighter who served as the first [chief minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_minister_of_Andhra_Pradesh#1953%E2%80%931956) of [Andhra State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_State), who was born in the village of [Vinodarayunipalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vinodarayunipalem). It was accordingly renamed as Prakasam District in 1972.[^cite_note-dist_overview-5]

## History

Prakasam district was originally constituted on 2 February 1970, carved out of [Guntur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guntur_district), [Nellore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nellore_district) and [Kurnool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurnool_district) districts of [Andhra Pradesh](2377.md).[^cite_note-Statoids-6] It was carved out of three taluks of Guntur district, i.e. [Addanki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Addanki), [Chirala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chirala), and [Ongole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ongole), four taluks of Nellore district, i.e. [Kandukur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kandukur), [Kanigiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanigiri), [Darsi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darsi) and [Podili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Podili) and three taluks of Kurnool district i.e. [Markapur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markapur), Yarragondapalem and [Giddaluru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giddaluru). It is one of the nine districts in the [Coastal Andhra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coastal_Andhra) region of undivided [Andhra Pradesh](2377.md). It was restructured in 2022, where few mandals were merged into [Bapatla district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bapatla_district) and [SPSR Nellore district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nellore_district), some of which were restored in the restructure effected on 31 December 2025. In the second restructure Markapuram district was carved out.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-eenadu-20251230-8]

Naxalite activity in Prakasam district used to be high during 2000s.[^cite_note-9]

## Geography

Prakasam district following 2022 district restructure occupied an area of 14,322 square kilometres (5,530 sq mi).[^cite_note-aponline-10][^cite_note-FOOTNOTECPO20221-11] After 2025 district reorganisation, it is bounded by [Markapuram district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markapuram_district) in the west, [SPSR Nellore district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nellore_district) in the south west, [Palnadu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palnadu_district) in the north, [Bapatla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bapatla_district) district in the north east, and [Bay of Bengal](47892.md) in the south.

The district has 50 kilometres of coastline. [Kothapatnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kothapatnam) of Kothapatnam Mandal and [Pakala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakala,_Prakasam_district) of Singarayakonda Mandal are prominent beaches. Gundlakamma, [Musi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musi_River_\(India\)) and [Paleru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paleru) are the major rivers in the district. Smaller rivers such as the Thammileru, Sagileru and Gudisileru and streams like Ogeruvagu, Nallavagu and Vedimangala Vagu also flow in the district.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTECPO20222-12]

### Climate

Before the district restructure effective on 30th December 2025, the district has a moderate climate in the coastal areas and a hot climate in the non-coastal areas. The normal maximum and minimum temperatures recorded in the district are 40.20 C and 20.30 C respectively. The maximum temperature is usually recorded in the months of April - June. The district receives its rainfall mostly from South West and North-East monsoon. The normal rainfall is 366.2 mm and 384.7 mm respectively.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTECPO2022III-2]

### Flora and fauna

Orchids, casuarinas and cashew plantations are common in the coastal areas.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTECPO2022III-2]

### Irrigation

[Nagarjuna Sagar Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagarjuna_Sagar_Dam) and Krishna Western Delta are major irrigation projects. Mopadu Reservoir, Paleru-Bitragunta Anicut, and Cumbum Tank are major sources of medium irrigation.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTECPO2022III-2]

### Mineral resources

Chimakurthi is known for its [granite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granite) reserves.The minerals found in the district are [Baryte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baryte), [iron ore](145352.md), [quartz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quartz), and [silica sand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silica_sand).[^cite_note-mining-13]

## Economy

The Gross district domestic product (GDDP) of the undivided district for the FY 2013-14 was ₹35,962 crore (US\$3.7 billion) and it contributed 6.9% to the [Gross state domestic product (GSDP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_State_Domestic_Product_of_Indian_states). The [per capita income](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Per_capita_income) at current prices was ₹85,765 (US\$890). The [primary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primary_sector_of_the_economy), [secondary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_sector_of_the_economy) and [tertiary sectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tertiary_sector_of_the_economy) of the district contribute ₹12,875 crore (US\$1.3 billion), ₹7,897 crore (US\$820 million) and ₹15,190 crore (US\$1.6 billion), respectively.[^cite_note-apedb-17] The major products that contributed to the [GVA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_value_added) of the district from agriculture and allied services were, [tobacco](30942.md), [paddy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paddy_\(unmilled_rice\)), [chillies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chillies), batavia, [milk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milk), [meat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meat) and [fisheries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fishery). The GVA from the industrial and service sector was contributed from [construction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Construction), minor minerals, unorganised trade and ownership of [dwellings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwelling).[^cite_note-apedb-17]

### Industries

The district has many service industries such as industrial testing, electrical appliance repair, clinical laboratories, servicing of computer hardware, tourism, and hospitality. Major exports from the district include seafood, processed tobacco, granite blocks, granite monuments and yarn.[^cite_note-dist_prof-18] There are many industries of food and agriculture, mineral, chemical, leather, plastic and rubber, engineering, cotton and textiles, electronic products. All these industries deal with prawn and fish processing and canning, dairy products, the granite industry, pharmaceuticals, tanning, fishing nets, surgical cotton, etc. Forest-based industries produce Ayurvedic medicines, essential oil (Palm Rose Oil), wooden furniture, wooden toys, bamboo products, etc.[^cite_note-mining-13]

### Mining

The district leads in granite mining in the state. *Galaxy Granite* is found in the Chimakurthy area of the district.[^cite_note-mining-13]

## Administrative divisions

See also: [List of mandals in Andhra Pradesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_mandals_in_Andhra_Pradesh) and [List of revenue divisions in Andhra Pradesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_revenue_divisions_in_Andhra_Pradesh)

The district comprises three revenue divisions viz.,Addanki, Kandukur and Ongole. There are a total of 27 mandals, with 10 in Addanki division, 7 in Kandukur division, and 11 in Ongole division.[^cite_note-eenadu-20251230-8] Urban administrative divisions of Panchayatraj include Ongole (Municipal Corporation), Addanki (Municipality), Kandukur (Municipality) and 2 Nagar panchayats for Chimakurthy and Darsi.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTECPO20225-20] The rural administrative divisions of Panchayatraj include 715 Gram Panchayats. There are about 769 Inhabited Revenue villages in the district.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTECPO2022III-2]

## Transport

The total road length of [state highways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_highway) in the undivided district is 1,184 km (736 mi).[^cite_note-21] The district is well connected by [national highways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_highways_of_India), state highways and district roads as well. The [NH 16](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_16_\(India\)) passes through [Ongole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ongole) which is the major highway connecting the cities of [Howrah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howrah) and [Chennai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai), a part of [Asian Highway Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Highway_Network) [AH45](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AH45).

The district has a rail network of 406 km (252 mi). The entire rail network is under the [South Central Railway zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Central_Railway_zone). [Ongole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ongole_railway_station) is one of the main stations of this district and most of the stations are under [Vijayawada railway division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vijayawada_railway_division).

## Education

The primary and secondary school education is imparted by government, aided and private schools, under the state's School Education Department.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23] As per the school information report for the academic year 2015–16, there are a total of 4,311 schools. They include, 33 government, 2,949 mandal and zilla parishads, 1 residential, 1079 private, 10 model, 37 Kasturba Gandhi Balika Vidyalaya (KGBV), 50 municipal and 152 other types of schools.[^cite_note-24] The total number of students enrolled in primary, upper primary and high schools of the district are 562,510.[^cite_note-student-25] The total number of students enrolled in primary, upper primary and high schools of the district are 461,065.[^cite_note-student-25]

Rajeev Gandhi University of Knowledge Technologies, Ongole campus is at [Santhanuthalapadu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santhanuthalapadu).[^cite_note-26] [Andhra Kesari University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_Kesari_University) was approved in 2021.[^cite_note-27]
