# Migrant

Wiktionary logo

Look up ***[migrant](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/migrant)*** in Wiktionary, the free dictionary.

**Migrant** is a term that may refer to:

## Human migration

- [Human migration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_migration), including:&#x20;

  - [Emigration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emigration), leaving one's resident country with the intent to settle elsewhere. 1988 Webster's Definition. One who migrates, esp. from 1 region to another in search of seasonal work.,

  - [Immigration](4599312.md), movement into a country with the intent to settle

  - [Economic migrant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_migrant), someone who emigrates from one region to another to seek an improvement in living standards

  - [Internal migration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_migration), within one geopolitical entity, usually a nation-state

  - [Migrant worker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Migrant_worker), one who migrates, possibly to another country, for work

  - [Expatriate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expatriate)

## Other uses
