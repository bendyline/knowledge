# Ocean Dream Diamond

The **Ocean Dream** is a [diamond](8082.md), measuring 5.51 [carats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carat_\(unit\)) (1.102 g), and rated in [color](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_color) as Fancy Deep Blue-Green by the [Gemological Institute of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemological_Institute_of_America).[^cite_note-GIA-1] The Ocean Dream is the first and one of the only natural diamonds known to the GIA to possess a blue-green hue (besides the [Ocean Paradise Diamond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ocean_Paradise_Diamond) owned by the Nahshonov Group, found in [Brazil](3383.md) in 2012), making it one of the rarest diamonds in the world. A blue-green colour is commonly seen in artificially [enhanced diamonds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_enhancement), whose colour is imparted by various [irradiation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irradiation) methods. The Ocean Dream originated in [Central Africa](539606.md), and is currently owned by the Cora Diamond Corporation.[^cite_note-GIA-1][^cite_note-Smithsonian-2]

The Ocean Dream was displayed in 2003 as part of the [Smithsonian's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smithsonian_Institution) "The Splendor of Diamonds" exhibit,[^cite_note-Smithsonian-2] alongside the [De Beers Millennium Star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millennium_Star), [The Heart of Eternity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_of_Eternity_Diamond), the [Steinmetz Pink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pink_Star_\(diamond\)), and the [Moussaieff Red](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moussaieff_Red_Diamond).[^cite_note-3]
