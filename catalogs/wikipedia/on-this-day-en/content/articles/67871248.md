**Maguindanao del Sur**, officially the **Province of Maguindanao del Sur**, is a [landlocked](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landlocked) [province](165100.md) in the [Philippines](23440.md) located in the [Bangsamoro](37260549.md) region in [Mindanao](20546.md). Its capital is the municipality of [Buluan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buluan) near the city of [Tacurong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tacurong) in the neighboring province of [Sultan Kudarat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_Kudarat). It borders [Cotabato](297394.md) province to the east, [Maguindanao del Norte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanao_del_Norte) to the west, and [Sultan Kudarat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_Kudarat) to the south.

### Foundation

See also: [Maguindanao leadership crises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanao_leadership_crises)

Maguindanao del Sur was formed when [Maguindanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanao) province was split into two provinces; the other province being [Maguindanao del Norte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanao_del_Norte). The division occurred following [a plebiscite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Maguindanao_division_plebiscite) on September 17, 2022 which ratified Republic Act 11550 which proposed the partitioning of the province. As per law former Maguindanao Governor [Mariam Mangudadatu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariam_Mangudadatu) was expected to become the governor of the newly formed Maguindanao del Sur province.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] However an issue arose since the determination of the first set of officials of the province presumes that the plebiscite was held prior to the [2022 national and local elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Philippine_general_election). However the plebiscite was postponed to a date after the elections.[^cite_note-dilg-5] This led to the [Commission on Elections](1847922.md) to come up with a legal opinion.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] The position was issued on September 28, 2022, where the election body conclude that only the [Department of the Interior and Local Government](4718691.md) could appoint the first officials of the province.[^cite_note-dilg-5]

Mangudadatu would assume the position and take oath as governor of the new province in October 13, 2022. Nathaniel Midtimbang, a member of the [Maguindanao Provincial Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanao_Provincial_Board), became her vice governor.[^cite_note-8] A transition period would take place until January 9, 2023.[^cite_note-9]

## Geography



Map of Maguindanao del Sur

Maguindanao del Sur is composed of 24 municipalities and 2 legislative districts. It has a total area of 4,973.48 square meters (53,534.1 sq ft). Being landlocked, it is bounded by [Maguindanao del Norte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanao_del_Norte) to the west, [Cotabato](297394.md) to the north and east, and [Sultan Kudarat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_Kudarat) to the south.

## Economy

According to the [Philippine Statistics Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Statistics_Authority), registered economic growth of the province had decelerated from 10.2 percent (before its establishment) to 6.7 percent in 2022.[^cite_note-\:0-23]

Agricultural growth from forestry and fishery had a growth declined to 5.2 percent. However, economic growth from services grew by 8.9 percent. Among services, accommodation and food services accounted for 31.7 percent.[^cite_note-\:0-23]
