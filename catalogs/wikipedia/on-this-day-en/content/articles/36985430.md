**Holy See–Uruguay relations** are foreign relations between the [Holy See](13393.md) and [Uruguay](31843.md).

## History



Apostolic Nunciature in Montevideo

Uruguay established diplomatic relations with the Holy See in 1856. Uruguay has an embassy to the [Holy See](13393.md). The Holy See has an embassy in [Montevideo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montevideo). The first Apostolic Delegate was Vincenzo Massoni.[^cite_note-1]

[Pope John Paul II](23805.md) made two [pastoral visits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_pastoral_visits_of_Pope_John_Paul_II_outside_Italy). The first was in 1987 and the second in 1988.[^cite_note-2]

The current Uruguayan ambassador to the Holy See is the theologian [Daniel Ramada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Ramada).[^cite_note-3] The current Apostolic Nuncio to Uruguay is the archbishop [Anselmo Guido Pecorari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anselmo_Guido_Pecorari).[^cite_note-4]

In June 2013, the Uruguayan President [José Mujica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Mujica) made an official visit to [Pope Francis](1687680.md).[^cite_note-5] In October 2025, President [Yamandú Orsi](64719316.md) paid an official visit to [Pope Leo XIV](54520372.md).[^cite_note-6]
