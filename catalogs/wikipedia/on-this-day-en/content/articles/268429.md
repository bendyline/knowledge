# List of companies of Russia

&#x20;[Russia](25705.md) has a high-income\[^cite_note-[https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org-1](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org-1/)] [mixed economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed_economy) with [state ownership](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State-owned_enterprises_in_Russia) in strategic areas of the economy. Market reforms in the 1990s privatized much of Russian industry and [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture_in_Russia), with notable exceptions to this privatization occurring in the [energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy) and defense-related sectors.



Gazprom's headquarters in the [Lakhta Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakhta_Center) in Saint Petersburg. Gazprom is the largest company in Russia

Russia's vast geography is an important determinant of its economic activity, with some sources estimating that Russia contains over 30 percent of the world's natural resources.[^cite_note-ems.psu.edu-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] The [World Bank](36752.md) estimates the total value of Russia's natural resources at US\$75 trillion.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] Russia relies on energy revenues to drive most of its growth. Russia has an abundance of [petroleum](23195.md), [natural gas](22131.md) and [precious metals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precious_metals), which make up a major share of Russia's exports. As of 2012 the oil-and-gas sector accounted for 16% of the [GDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GDP), 52% of federal budget revenues and over 70% of total exports.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-EIA-8]
