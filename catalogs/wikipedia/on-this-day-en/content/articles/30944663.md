**Katingan Regency** ([Indonesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_language): *Kabupaten Katingan*) is one of the thirteen [regencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regency_\(Indonesia\)) which comprise the [Central Kalimantan](453643.md) Province on the island of [Kalimantan](165470.md) (Borneo), [Indonesia](14579.md). It was created on 10 April 2002 from what were previously the eastern districts of [East Kotawaringin Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Kotawaringin_Regency). The town of [Kasongan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasongan) is the capital of the Regency, which covers a land area of 20,380.50 km<sup>2</sup>. The population of Katingan Regency was 146,439 at the 2010 Census[^cite_note-Biro_Pusat_Statistik_2011-2] and 162,222 at the 2020 Census;[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2021-3] the official estimate as at mid 2025 was 174,341 (comprising 90,120 males and 84,220 females).[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2026-1]
