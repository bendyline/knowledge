A **car bomb**, **bus bomb**, **van bomb**, **lorry bomb**, or **truck bomb**, also known as a **vehicle-borne improvised explosive device** (**VBIED**),[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEKaaman20191−3-1] is an [improvised explosive device](265112.md) designed to be detonated in an [automobile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automobile) or other vehicles.

Car bombs can be roughly divided into two main categories: those used primarily to kill the occupants of the vehicle (often as an [assassination](2963.md)) and those used as a means to kill, injure or damage people and buildings outside the vehicle. The latter type may be parked (the vehicle disguising the bomb and allowing the bomber to leave the area), or the vehicle might be used to deliver the bomb (often as part of a [suicide bombing](18835454.md)).

It is commonly used as a weapon of [terrorism](30636.md) or [guerrilla warfare](12720.md) to kill people near the blast site or to damage buildings or other property.[^cite_note-2] Car bombs act as their own delivery mechanisms and can carry a relatively large amount of [explosives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Explosives) without attracting suspicion. In larger vehicles and trucks, weights of around 5,000 pounds (2,300 kg) or more have been used, for example, in the [Oklahoma City bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oklahoma_City_bombing).[^cite_note-3] Car bombs are activated in a variety of ways, including opening the vehicle's doors, starting the engine, remote detonation, depressing the accelerator or brake pedals, or simply lighting a fuse or setting a timing device.[^cite_note-technology-4] The gasoline in the vehicle's fuel tank may make the explosion of the bomb more powerful by dispersing and igniting the fuel.

## History

Further information: [Car bomb § Timeline](#Timeline)

See also: [Suicide bombing](18835454.md#Suicide_bombing)

Car bombs have been used for attacks motivated by a wide variety of grievances and ideologies, by people and groups from a wide variety of cultural and religious backgrounds.

Car bombs are preceded by the 16th century [hellburners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hellburners), explosive-laden ships which were used to deadly effect by the besieged [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_Republic) forces in [Antwerp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fall_of_Antwerp_\(1584%E2%80%931585\)) against the besieging [Spanish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Empire). Though using a less refined technology, the basic principle of the hellburner is similar to that of the car bomb.

The first car bomb may have been the one used for the assassination attempt on Ottoman Sultan [Abdul Hamid II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Hamid_II) in 1905 in [Istanbul](3391396.md) by [Armenian separatists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_national_liberation_movement) in the command of [Papken Siuni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papken_Siuni) belonging to the [Armenian Revolutionary Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_Revolutionary_Federation).

Car bombs were also preceded by [animal bombs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal-borne_bomb_attacks) using horses and cows, then eventually emerging into car use.[^cite_note-Davis_The_First_Car_Bomb-5] Prior to the 20th century, bombs planted in horse carts had been used in assassination plots, notably the unsuccessful "[*machine infernale*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plot_of_the_rue_Saint-Nicaise)" attempt to kill [Napoleon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Napoleon) on 24 December 1800. [Mario Buda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mario_Buda)'s improvised wagon used in the 1920 [Wall Street bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wall_Street_bombing) is considered a prototype of the car bomb.[^cite_note-Davis_The_First_Car_Bomb-5]

The first reported car bombing was the [Bath School bombings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bath_School_disaster) in [Michigan](18859.md), USA in 1927. Multiple separate explosions on the same day killed 45 people, including the bomber, and half of a school was destroyed. The bombings were all carried out by [Andrew Kehoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Kehoe), motivated by a [personal grievance](18835454.md#personal). His death was possibly an intentional [suicide](16831059.md), but the cause of the explosion was a gun shot that might not have been intended to set off the load. The explosion itself did not seem to form part of a [suicide attack](18835454.md) on a specific planned target other than possibly himself and his truck. The explosives in his truck detonated when he saw two men nearby had a gun, after he set off multiple other bombs.[^cite_note-Inquest_Page_326-6][^cite_note-Inquest_Page_352-7] The explosion may have been set off indirectly by him firing his own gun at the men.[^cite_note-Inquest_Winchester-8][^cite_note-Jones_PhD_Dissertation_Page_1-9] Most of the deaths were caused by the earlier bombs.[^cite_note-ellsworth_ch_2-10][^cite_note-Burcar-11]

Some groups in [Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_\(region\)) have used both cars and donkeys.[^cite_note-972_donkey-12][^cite_note-MEMO_1937-13][^cite_note-Donkey_human_shield-14] The [Irgun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irgun), a [Zionist militant group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zionist_political_violence) in [British controlled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Mandate_for_Palestine) [Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_Palestine), used donkeys in [two attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Irgun_attacks) on [Haifa](55606.md) vegetable market [in 1939](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1939_in_Palestine). The [donkeys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal-borne_bomb_attacks#Palestine) were loaded with explosives to [attack the market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_massacres_in_Mandatory_Palestine), one attack killed 78 people, the other killed 21 people and wounded 24.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] The previous year the [Irgun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irgun) attacked the market with a car bomb, killing 35 Arab civilians and wounding 70.[^cite_note-17] There are no clearly documented cases of the Irgun using car bombs in suicide attacks, but the Irgun and their extremist [Lehi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lehi_\(militant_group\)) splinter group [used suicide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_references_to_Samson#militant) in [other circumstances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suicide_mission) and are seen as the key developers of car bombs, that were later used by other groups in numerous suicide attacks.[^cite_note-Davis_The_First_Car_Bomb-5] The Irgun were extremely influential.

While not an adaptation of a people-carrying vehicle, the WW2 German [Goliath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goliath_tracked_mine) remote control mine shares many parallels with a vehicle-based [IED](265112.md). It approached a target (often a tank or another armoured vehicle) at speed before exploding, destroying itself and the target. It was armoured so that it could not be destroyed en route. However, it was not driven by a person, instead operated by remote control from a safe distance.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELepage2014164−166-18]

The first non-[suicide](18835454.md#1947) car bombing "fully conceptualized as a weapon of urban warfare" came on 12 January 1947 when the [Lehi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lehi_\(militant_group\)) (also known as [Stern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avraham_Stern) Gang), a [Zionist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zionism) paramilitary organization, [bombed the Haifa police station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lehi_bombing_of_Haifa_police_station_\(1947\)).[^cite_note-Davis_The_First_Car_Bomb-5] On 4 January 1948, a Lehi car bomb in [Jaffa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaffa) killed 70 Palestinian Arabs.[^cite_note-Haaretz_Dare_Compare-19]

Car bombing was a significant part of the [Provisional Irish Republican Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Irish_Republican_Army) (PIRA) campaign during [The Troubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Troubles) in [Northern Ireland](21265.md). [Dáithí Ó Conaill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C3%A1ith%C3%AD_%C3%93_Conaill) is credited with introducing the car bomb to [Northern Ireland](21265.md).[^cite_note-talks_with_IRA-20] Car bombs were also used by [Ulster loyalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulster_loyalist) groups (for example, by the [UVF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulster_Volunteer_Force) during the [Dublin and Monaghan bombings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin_and_Monaghan_bombings)).[^cite_note-Lewiston-21][^cite_note-CAIN1969-22][^cite_note-CAIN1974-23][^cite_note-comeheretome-24] PIRA Chief of Staff [Seán Mac Stíofáin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Se%C3%A1n_Mac_St%C3%ADof%C3%A1in) defines the car bomb as both a tactical and a strategic guerrilla warfare weapon. Strategically, it disrupts the ability of the enemy government to administer the country, and hits simultaneously at the core of its economic structure by means of massive destruction. From a tactical point of view, it ties down a large number of security forces and troops around the main urban areas of the region in conflict.[^cite_note-Revolutionary_in_Ireland-25]

A notable suicide car bombing was the [1983 Beirut barracks bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1983_Beirut_barracks_bombing), when two simultaneous attacks killed 241 U.S. and 58 French peacekeepers. The perpetrator of these attacks has never been positively confirmed. In the [Lebanese Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Civil_War), an estimated 3,641 car bombs were detonated.[^cite_note-Atlas_Group_and_Walid_Raad-26] The tactic was adopted by [Palestinian militant groups](75192468.md) such as the [Qassam Brigades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qassam_Brigades) (militant wing of [Hamas](13913.md)), [Al-Aqsa Martyrs' Brigades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Aqsa_Martyrs'_Brigades) (militant wing of [Fatah](11104.md),[^cite_note-27] and [Saraya al-Quds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saraya_al-Quds) (militant wing of [Palestinian Islamic Jihad](13093253.md)).[^cite_note-Jewish_Virtual_Library_-_Comprehensive_Listing-28][^cite_note-Jewish_Virtual_Library_-_Since_Oslo-29] especially during the [Second Intifada (2000–2005)](71717.md).[^cite_note-Brief_History-30][^cite_note-Dreams_Deferred-31][^cite_note-Toledo_Blade-32]

Mass-casualty suicide car bombings are predominantly associated with the Middle East, particularly in recent decades.

In the [autumn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autumn) of 2005, there were 140 car bombings happening per month.[^cite_note-Davis_The_First_Car_Bomb-5]

## As a delivery system



Car bomb in Iraq, made up of a number of [artillery shells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artillery_shell) concealed in the back of a [pickup truck](47877.md)

Car bombs are effective weapons as they are an easy way to transport a large number of explosives to a target. A car bomb also produces copious [shrapnel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrapnel_\(fragment\)), or flying debris, and secondary damage to bystanders and buildings. In recent years, car bombs have become widely used by suicide bombers.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35]

### Countermeasures

Defending against a car bomb involves keeping vehicles at a distance from vulnerable targets by using [roadblocks](585425.md) and [checkpoints](8116691.md), [Jersey barriers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jersey_barrier), concrete blocks or [bollards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bollard_\(traffic\)), [metal barriers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metalith), or by hardening buildings to withstand explosions. The entrance to [Downing Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downing_Street) in London has been closed since 1991 in reaction to the [Provisional Irish Republican Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Irish_Republican_Army) campaign, preventing the public from approaching [Number 10](226294.md). Where major public roads pass near buildings, road closures may be the only option (thus, for instance, in [Washington, D.C.](108956.md) the portion of [Pennsylvania Avenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_Avenue_\(Washington,_D.C.\)) immediately in front of the [White House](33057.md) is closed to traffic). Historically these tactics have encouraged potential bombers to target ["soft" or unprotected targets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soft_target), such as markets.[^cite_note-36]

### Suicide usage

See also: [suicide attack § Donkeys](18835454.md#Donkeys), and [suicide attack § Late Cold War and War on Terror](18835454.md#Late_Cold_War_and_War_on_Terror)

In the [Iraqi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_Civil_War_\(2014-17\)) and [Syrian Civil War](30741795.md), the car bomb concept was modified so that it could be driven and detonated by a driver but armoured to withstand incoming fire. The vehicle would be driven to its target area, in a similar fashion to a [kamikaze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamikaze) plane of [WW2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WW2).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEKaaman20193-37] These were known by the acronym SVBIED (from Suicide Vehicle Borne Improvised Explosive Device) or VBIEDs. Ordinary civilian cars were outfitted with armour plating intended to protect the VBIED as it approached its target.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEKaaman20193-37] Such SVBIEDs were driven into enemy troop areas or incoming enemy columns.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEKaaman20194-38] Most often, the SVBIEDs were used by [ISIL](9087364.md) against Government forces, but also used by Syrian rebels ([FSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Syrian_army) and [allied militias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_opposition_to_Bashar_al-Assad), especially the [Al-Nusra Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Nusra_Front)) against government troops.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEKaaman20201−13-39]

The vehicles have become more sophisticated, with armour plating on the vehicle, protected vision slits, armour plating over the wheels so they would withstand being shot at and occasionally additional [metal grating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slat_armor) over the front of the vehicle designed to crush or destroy incoming [shaped charges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaped_charge) such as those used on [rocket propelled grenades](26286.md).[^cite_note-40]



A mock explosion of a pickup truck converted to SVBIED, used by [U.S. Marines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Marines) for [OPFOR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OPFOR) purposes at [Camp Pendleton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_Pendleton)&#x20;

Trucks were sometimes used to start an assault, and benefitted from their greater storage space that could contain very heavy explosives.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEKaaman20195-41] Animal drawn carts, typically pulled by horse or mule, have also been used.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmistead201339−40-42][^cite_note-43] Tactically, a single vehicle may be used, or an initial "breakthrough" vehicle, then followed by another vehicle.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEKaaman20196-44]

While many car bombs are disguised as ordinary vehicles,[^cite_note-45] some that are used against military forces have [improvised vehicle armour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Improvised_vehicle_armour) attached to prevent the driver from being shot when attacking a fortified outpost.[^cite_note-46]

Car bombs and detonators function in a diverse manner of ways and there are numerous variables in the operation and placement of the bomb within the vehicle. Earlier and less advanced car bombs were often wired to the car's ignition system, but this practice is now considered more laborious and less effective than other more recent methods, as it requires a greater amount of work for a system that can often be quite easily [defused](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defuse). While it is more common nowadays for car bombs to be fixed [magnetically](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetism) to the underside of the car, underneath the passenger or driver's seat, or inside of the [mudguard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fender_\(vehicle\)), detonators triggered by the opening of the vehicle door or by pressure applied to the brakes or accelerating pedals are also used.[^cite_note-technology-4]

Bombs operating by the former method of fixation to the underside of the car more often than not make use of a device called a [tilt fuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tilt_fuse). A small tube made of glass or plastic, the tilt fuse is similar in operation to a [mercury switch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercury_switch) or medical tablet tube. One end of the fuse will be filled with [mercury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercury_\(element\)), while the other open end is wired with the ends of an open circuit to an electrical firing system. When the tilt fuse moves or is jerked, the supply of mercury will flow to the top of the tube and close the circuit. Thus, as the vehicle goes through the regular bumping and dipping that comes with driving over terrain, the circuit is completed, and the explosive is detonated.[^cite_note-technology-4]

Car bombs are effective as booby traps because they also leave very little evidence. When an explosion happens, it is difficult for forensics to find any evidence because things either denigrate or become charred.[^cite_note-Davis_The_First_Car_Bomb-5]

As a safety mechanism to protect the bomber, the placer of the bomb may rig a [timing device](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timer) incorporated with the circuit to activate the circuit only after a certain time period, therefore ensuring the bomber will not accidentally activate the bomb before they are able to get clear of the blast radius.[^cite_note-technology-4]
