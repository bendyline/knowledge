**Francesca Paola Albanese** (Italian: [\[franˈtʃeska ˈpaːola albaˈneːze, -eːse\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Italian); born 30 March 1977) is an Italian legal scholar and expert on [human rights](13831.md). She has served as the [United Nations](31769.md) (UN) [Special Rapporteur on the occupied Palestinian territories](70685870.md) since 1 May 2022; initially appointed for a three-year term, she was confirmed for another three years in April 2025. She is the first woman to hold the position.

As part of her position as a UN special rapporteur, Albanese has been critical of [Israel's occupation](2781576.md) of the [Palestinian territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_territories) and recommended in her first report that [UN member states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_member_states) develop a plan to end the occupation and [apartheid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_and_apartheid). After the [Israeli invasion of the Gaza Strip](74998519.md), Albanese [called for an immediate ceasefire](75313349.md) and warned that Palestinians in Gaza were at risk of [ethnic cleansing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_cleansing). In March 2024, Albanese reported to the [UN Human Rights Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_Human_Rights_Council) that [Israel's actions in Gaza amounted to genocide](75668427.md). Pro-Israel organizations, including the U.S. government, have accused Albanese of [antisemitism](1078.md) and [anti-Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Israel) bias, and have called for her removal. Several human rights groups and numerous scholars of antisemitism have said the accusations are [illegitimate attempts to discredit her](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weaponization_of_antisemitism). The UN published a report by Albanese in June 2025 stating that many corporate entities, including [Microsoft](19001.md), [Alphabet Inc.](47489893.md), and [Amazon](90451.md), were enabling and profiting from the occupation of Palestinian territories and the Gaza genocide. In response, the United States under the [Trump administration](78298108.md) imposed sanctions on Albanese under [Executive Order 14203](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_Order_14203) naming her a "[specially designated national](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specially_Designated_Nationals_and_Blocked_Persons_List)", forbidding all U.S. persons and companies from doing business with her.

Albanese holds a [law degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_degree) with honours from the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa) and a [Master of Laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Laws) in [human rights](13831.md) from [SOAS University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SOAS_University_of_London). She was an affiliate scholar at the [Institute for the Study of International Migration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_the_Study_of_International_Migration) at [Georgetown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgetown_University) until late 2025, when the University stated it was ended due to the U.S. sanctions. She is a senior advisor on Migration and Forced Displacement at the non-profit Arab Renaissance for Democracy and Development.

## Early life and education

Francesca Paola Albanese was born in [Ariano Irpino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariano_Irpino), a town in the [Campania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campania) region of [southern Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Italy),[^cite_note-2] on 30 March 1977.[^cite_note-OoFAC-2025-3][^cite_note-4]

Albanese holds a full Italian [law degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_degree) ([LMG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juris_Doctor_\(Italy\)) with honours from the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa)).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] In a May 2025 interview, she said that she had studied jurisprudence and had not taken the bar examination because she was more interested in human rights and did not want to practice law in Italy.[^cite_note-7]

She later earned a [Master of Laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Laws) in [human rights](13831.md) from [SOAS University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SOAS_University_of_London).[^cite_note-UoG-2022-8][^cite_note-9]

## Career

Albanese was an affiliate scholar at the [Institute for the Study of International Migration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_the_Study_of_International_Migration) at [Georgetown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgetown_University) until late 2025, when the University stated it was ended as a result of US sanctions imposed on her.[^cite_note-Wagenheim-2025-10] She is a senior advisor on Migration and Forced Displacement at the non-profit Arab Renaissance for Democracy and Development (ARDD),[^cite_note-UoG-2022-8] where she co-founded the Global Network on the Question of Palestine.[^cite_note-UoG-2022-8] In 2020, she and Lex Takkenberg wrote the [OUP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OUP)-published *Palestinian Refugees in International Law*.[^cite_note-11]

Albanese worked for a decade as a human rights expert for the UN, including the [Office of the UN High Commissioner for Human Rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_the_UN_High_Commissioner_for_Human_Rights) and the [UN Relief and Work Agency for Palestine Refugees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_Relief_and_Work_Agency_for_Palestine_Refugees).[^cite_note-12] During this period, she advised the UN, governments, and civil society in the [Middle East](19323.md), [North Africa](21714.md), and the [Asia Pacific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia_Pacific) regions on human rights and their application and norms, particularly for vulnerable groups like [refugees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refugees) and migrants. She holds lectures on [international law](8195726.md) and [forced displacement](477535.md) in both European and Arab universities, as well as conferences and public events about the [Israeli–Palestinian conflict](46216.md).[^cite_note-UoG-2022-8]

## UN Special Rapporteur on the occupied Palestinian territories

On 1 May 2022 Albanese was appointed [UN Special Rapporteur on the occupied Palestinian territories](70685870.md) for a three-year term.[^cite_note-13] Albanese is the second Italian (after [Giorgio Giacomelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giorgio_Giacomelli)) and the first woman to be appointed to the position.[^cite_note-14] Her appointment generated some controversy due to comments she made criticising the US and Europe during the [2014 Gaza War](43243199.md). Albanese said the United States was "subjugated by the [Jewish lobby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_lobby)", which she later said she regretted, and Europe by a "sense of [guilt about the Holocaust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_collective_guilt)", and that both "condemn the oppressed – the Palestinians" in the conflict.[^cite_note-Tress-2022-15] The [Israeli Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs) and [Michele Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michele_Taylor), the US ambassador to the UN Human Rights Council, suggested the comments were [antisemitic](1078.md).[^cite_note-16] Albanese said that she had never been antisemitic and that her [criticism of Israel](30074296.md) is related to its occupation of [Palestinian territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_territories).[^cite_note-Tress-2022-15][^cite_note-17][^cite_note-MEM-2022-18]

On 18 October 2022, Albanese recommended in her first report that UN member states develop "a plan to end the Israeli settler-colonial occupation and [apartheid regime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_and_apartheid)".[^cite_note-19] The report concluded: "The violations described in the present report expose the nature of the [Israeli occupation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_occupation), that of an intentionally acquisitive, segregationist and repressive regime designed to prevent the realization of the Palestinian people's right to [self-determination](29269.md)."[^cite_note-20]

During the 30th Meeting of the 53rd Regular Session of the UN Human Rights Council in July 2023, Albanese presented a report accusing Israel of turning the [West Bank](33209.md) into an "open-air prison". The report said that since 1967, more than 800,000 Palestinians, including children as young as 12, had been [arrested and detained by Israeli authorities](18158609.md).[^cite_note-21] Briefing journalists, Albanese said: "There is no other way to define the regime that Israel has imposed on the Palestinians – which is apartheid by default – other than an open-air prison."[^cite_note-Reuters_2023-22] Israel was not present for the presentation but rejected the findings.[^cite_note-Reuters_2023-22]

In August 2024, Albanese was one of many UN Special Rapporteurs who signed an open letter to the "International community" on the third anniversary of the [Taliban](30635.md) taking change in Afghanistan. They were concerned that the [regime's human-rights abuses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talibanization) particularly against women and girls may become accepted. They encouraged the [International Criminal Court](14880.md) (ICC) to take urgent action against those responsible.[^cite_note-23]

Albanese was confirmed in the position for another three years in April 2025.[^cite_note-24]

In September 2026, Albanese stated that the [Western world](21208200.md) should stop sending weapons to [Ukraine](31750.md) during the [Russo-Ukrainian War](42085878.md) and seek a political solution. She accused both [NATO](21133.md) and the [United States](3434750.md) of prolonging the conflict, and stated that [Russia's invasion of Ukraine](79400268.md) was unlawful. When asked how she believed the war should end, Albanese said she did not have a solution because she was not an expert.[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26]

Later that month, Albanese apologized in a social media post on [X](9988187.md) for statements she had made in an interview, acknowledging that she had "misread the term '[Muselmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muselmann)' (Muslim) used in [Nazi camps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_concentration_camps) for prisoners at the brink of death, wrongly inferring that the term antisemitism originally meant hatred of all Semitic peoples" rather than having been invented as a term to specifically denote hatred of Jews. A statement from [Yad Vashem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yad_Vashem) said that Albanese should "familiarize herself with the history she seeks to address, particularly the rise of Nazi antisemitism and the ideology that paved the way for the [Holocaust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Holocaust), before making assertions about it".[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29]

### Gaza war



Albanese in July 2024

In 2023, after the outbreak of the [Gaza war](74998519.md), Albanese [called for an immediate ceasefire](75313349.md), warning that "Palestinians are in grave danger of a mass [ethnic cleansing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_cleansing)."[^cite_note-Albanese-2023-30] She further stated that the international community must "prevent and protect populations from atrocity crimes", and that "accountability for international crimes committed by Israeli occupation forces and Hamas must also be immediately pursued."[^cite_note-Albanese-2023-30]

In February 2024, French President [Emmanuel Macron](43671127.md) described the [October 7 attacks](74998317.md) as "the largest antisemitic massacre of our century". Albanese responded on [X (formerly Twitter)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X_\(formerly_Twitter\)) that "the victims of the October 7 massacre were killed not because of their Judaism, but in response to Israeli oppression". The [French Foreign Ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Foreign_Ministry) condemned her remarks and the Israeli government declared Albanese *[persona non grata](521241.md)* in Israel and denied her future entry to the country. In response to the reactions, Albanese said: "I regret that some interpreted my tweet as 'justifying' Hamas's crimes, which I have condemned strongly several times. I reject all forms of racism, including antisemitism. However, labeling these crimes as 'antisemitic' obscures the real reason they occurred."[^cite_note-31][^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33]

On 26 March 2024, Albanese presented the report "Anatomy of a Genocide" before the 55th session of the UN Human Rights Council in Geneva,[^cite_note-reliefweb.int-2024-34] stating "reasonable grounds" to believe that Israel is intentionally committing at least three "genocidal acts"[^cite_note-reliefweb.int-2024-34]<sup>: paragraph 5 </sup> against the Palestinians as a group in Gaza proscribed in the [Genocide Convention](143988.md):

- "Killing members of the group";

  [^cite_note-reliefweb.int-2024-34]

  : paragraphs 22–26&#x20;

- "Causing serious bodily or mental harm to members of the group";

  [^cite_note-reliefweb.int-2024-34]

  : paragraphs 27–31&#x20;

- "Deliberately inflicting on the group conditions of life calculated to bring about its physical destruction in whole or in part".

  [^cite_note-reliefweb.int-2024-34]

  : paragraphs 34–45&#x20;

Albanese called for [sanctions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_sanctions_involving_Israel) and an [arms embargo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arms_embargoes_on_Israel_since_2023).[^cite_note-35] She was one of the many UN experts who spoke out against the sale of arms to Israel in June 2024 to be used in the [conflict in Gaza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conflict_in_Gaza). The experts cautioned arms suppliers and finance companies that they would be implicated in human rights violations. The signatories to the warning included special reporteurs [Paula Gaviria Betancur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paula_Gaviria_Betancur), [Tlaleng Mofokeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tlaleng_Mofokeng), and [Margaret Satterthwaite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Satterthwaite).[^cite_note-36] Furthermore, she points to the settler colonial context of genocidal practices resulting in "destruction and replacement of Indigenous peoples",[^cite_note-reliefweb.int-2024-34]<sup>: paragraph 9 </sup> and recommends the "reconstitution of the [United Nations Special Committee against Apartheid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Special_Committee_against_Apartheid)" to address the situation in Palestine.[^cite_note-reliefweb.int-2024-34]<sup>: paragraph 97e </sup>



Albanese, Palestinian diplomat [Riyad Mansour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riyad_Mansour), and Colombian President [Gustavo Petro](13391962.md) at the [Emergency Conference on Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergency_Conference_on_Palestine) in Bogota, Colombia, 16 July 2025

During a briefing on the international legal responsibilities for preventing genocide, Albanese said that given the way Israel was behaving, it was time to consider suspending their credential as a member state under Article 6 of the UN Charter.[^cite_note-37] In an interview with the [Middle East Eye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_East_Eye) in November 2024, Albanese described [Foreign Secretary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_Secretary_\(United_Kingdom\)) of the United Kingdom [David Lammy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Lammy) as a [genocide denier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genocide_denier), and stated that the British government had done nothing to prevent Israel's [atrocities](26684335.md) in Gaza.[^cite_note-38] In March 2025, [Betar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betar), a Jewish far-right organization, threatened Albanese with a pager attack during her visit to [London](17867.md). The group posted a statement on [Twitter](9988187.md) that said "Join us to give Francesca a \[pager emoji] in London on Tuesday."[^cite_note-Osman_2025-39] The pager emoji is a reference to the [2024 pager attacks in Lebanon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Lebanon_electronic_device_attacks).[^cite_note-Osman_2025-39] Albanese said that she and her family had received death threats.[^cite_note-Dedorson-2025-40]

Albanese criticized the Trump administration for welcoming Israeli Prime Minister [Benjamin Netanyahu](56472.md) to the United States and maintaining friendly relations with him, despite the fact that an [arrest warrant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Criminal_Court_arrest_warrants_for_Israeli_leaders) has been issued for Netanyahu as part of the [ICC investigation in Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_investigation_in_Palestine).[^cite_note-41] In June 2025, Albanese said Israel had [killed](75146557.md) more than 200 Palestinian journalists because they were documenting the [genocide of Palestinians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genocide_of_Palestinians) in Gaza.[^cite_note-Dedorson-2025-40] She also said EU officials like [Ursula von der Leyen](2991932.md) and [Kaja Kallas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaja_Kallas) were complicit in [Israeli war crimes in the Gaza war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_war_crimes_in_the_Gaza_war).[^cite_note-Dedorson-2025-40]

The UN published a report by Albanese in June 2025 stating that corporate entities were enabling and profiting from the occupation of Palestinian territories and the Gaza genocide.[^cite_note-UN-2025-42][^cite_note-43] The report listed 48 corporations, including [Microsoft](19001.md), [Alphabet Inc.](47489893.md), and [Amazon](90451.md),[^cite_note-44] which it said are helping Israel displace Palestinians in breach of international law.[^cite_note-45]

### Accusations of antisemitism and anti-Israel bias

In February 2023, 18 members of the [United States Congress](31756.md) called for Albanese to be removed from her position saying that she has demonstrated a consistent [bias against Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Israel_sentiment).[^cite_note-46] In July 2024, [pro-Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pro-Israel) [lobby group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lobby_group) [UN Watch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_Watch) director [Hillel Neuer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hillel_Neuer) requested [Volker Türk](71721726.md) (the [UN High Commissioner for Human Rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_High_Commissioner_for_Human_Rights)) to investigate Albanese's trip to Australia and whether flights during that trip were funded by pro-Palestinian groups,[^cite_note-47][^cite_note-48] including the Australian Friends of Palestine Association, Free Palestine Melbourne, and Australian Palestinian Advocacy Network.[^cite_note-49]

That same month Albanese expressed support on X for a post by former UN human rights official [Craig Mokhiber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craig_Mokhiber), that juxtaposed a photo of a crowd welcoming [Adolf Hitler](2731583.md) with Nazi salutes and a photo of Netanyahu being welcomed by the [US Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Congress). Mokhiber captioned his post with the words "History is always watching" and Albanese responded with "This is precisely what I was thinking today".[^cite_note-50] In response, US Ambassador to the UN [Linda Thomas-Greenfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linda_Thomas-Greenfield) stated, "It is clear \[Albanese] is not fit for this or any position at the UN."[^cite_note-51] Albanese said the comparison she and Mokhiber were making was between two crowds of politicians cheering on war criminals.[^cite_note-52]

Responding to two reports presented to the United nations in October 2024, the [World Jewish Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Jewish_Congress) accused Albanese of repeatedly engaging in [Holocaust inversion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holocaust_inversion) by drawing comparisons between [Israel and Nazi Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comparisons_between_Israel_and_Nazi_Germany), calling her statements "not only deeply offensive, but a gross distortion of history."[^cite_note-53] US Ambassador to the UN [Linda Thomas-Greenfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linda_Thomas-Greenfield) posted on X the US belief that Albanese is "unfit for her role. The United Nations should not tolerate antisemitism from a UN-affiliated official hired to promote human rights."[^cite_note-54] Albanese said in response to the accusation of antisemitism, "Antisemitism and discrimination against Jews as Jews is gross. But frankly I couldn't care less if Israel were run by Jews, Muslims, Christians or atheists... All I want is for Israel to conduct itself in line with international law."[^cite_note-55]

On 11 February 2025, the Dutch [House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_\(Netherlands\)) cancelled an invitation for Albanese to visit the House on 13 February, on account of her statements about the Gaza War.[^cite_note-56] On 20 June 2025, the Trump administration sent a letter to the UN Secretary-General [Antonio Guterres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Guterres), calling for her removal as rapporteur on Palestinian rights following what they alleged was "antisemitism and support for terrorism".[^cite_note-\:0-57]

#### Edited video of Al Jazeera address

See also: [Misinformation in the Gaza war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Misinformation_in_the_Gaza_war)

In February 2026, a video was produced by the pro-Israel lobby group [UN Watch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_Watch), in which portions of an address by Albanese at an [Al Jazeera](30204052.md) forum are spliced and edited together to suggest Albanese said Israel was the "common enemy" of humanity.[^cite_note-\:2-58] Albanese did not make the fabricated statement in the address, but rather criticized Western inaction during the Gaza genocide.[^cite_note-Ga26-59] On the basis of the alleged remarks, the foreign ministers of the Czech Republic,[^cite_note-\:4-60] Germany,[^cite_note-61] and France called for the removal of Albanese. Albanese responded that she was referring to "the system that has enabled the genocide in Palestine" as the "common enemy".[^cite_note-\:2-58] The French Foreign Minister [Jean-Noël Barrot](54388162.md) said France "unreservedly condemns the outrageous and reprehensible remarks" attributed to Albanese, arguing that they were directed not at the Israeli government, whose policies may be criticized, but "at Israel as a people and as a nation."[^cite_note-\:1-62] Barrot also accused Albanese of adding to "a long list of scandalous positions," alleging that she is a political activist who has previously justified the 7 October attacks and "compared Israel to the Third Reich."[^cite_note-\:1-62]

German Foreign Minister [Johann Wadephul](50782003.md) described her position as "untenable".[^cite_note-\:2-58] [Petr Macinka](79962349.md), the foreign minister of the Czech Republic, citing the "common enemy of humanity" remarks, also urged her to step down.[^cite_note-\:4-60] Italian Foreign Minister [Antonio Tajani](1767298.md) also condemned her conduct and positions, writing that they "do not reflect those of the Italian government".[^cite_note-\:5-63] Austria's Foreign Minister, [Beate Meinl-Reisinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beate_Meinl-Reisinger), posted a [tweet](68533868.md) condemning Albanese for using language that "undermines the impartiality and highest standards that the role of a UN representative requires,"[^cite_note-64] but later deleted that post without explanation.[^cite_note-65]

Several lawmakers likewise urged that Albanese be stripped of her UN mandate with immediate effect, citing the ostensible comments – that Albanese did not make – from the edited video of the conference.[^cite_note-Ga26-59][^cite_note-\:1-62] [Amnesty International](18947898.md) condemned the attacks on Albanese for being based on a deliberately truncated video, and called on the ministers who attacked her to apologize and retract the calls for Albanese's resignation.[^cite_note-\:3-66] Over 100 prominent artists, including [Mark Ruffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Ruffalo), [Javier Bardem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javier_Bardem), [Annie Ernaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annie_Ernaux) and [Annie Lennox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annie_Lennox), signed an open letter supporting Albanese.[^cite_note-\:2-58]

Albanese's comments were reviewed by a United Nations panel of human rights experts. The panel found that the "vicious attacks" on Albanese were based on a doctored video of her speech. It criticised the "Ministers of certain States \[who] rely on manufactured facts and criticise Ms Albanese for statements that she never made". It said that, instead of attacking Albanese, the Ministers "should join forces to hold accountable, including before the International Criminal Court, leaders and officials accused of committing war crimes and crimes against humanity in Gaza".[^cite_note-67]

### Support for Albanese



Albanese with members of the [Portuguese Parliament](1123482.md) on 27 July 2024

In December 2022, sixty-five scholars of antisemitism, the Holocaust, and [Jewish studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_studies) issued a statement,[^cite_note-68][^cite_note-69] which read: "It is evident that the campaign against \[Albanese] is not about combating today's antisemitism. It is essentially about [efforts to silence her](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weaponization_of_antisemitism) and to undermine her mandate as a senior UN official reporting about Israel's violations of human rights and international law."[^cite_note-MEM-2022-18] In late 2022, [Defence for Children International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defence_for_Children_International) led 116 human rights and civil society organisations and academic groups in issuing a statement in her support.[^cite_note-70]

In January 2023, a statement was issued in defence of Albanese by a number of human rights organizations, academic institutions, and other civil society organizations.[^cite_note-71] The statement concluded by stating: "We commend UNSR Francesca Albanese's tireless efforts toward the protection of human rights in the [OPT](2781576.md) \[Occupied Palestinian Territories] and in raising awareness of the alarming daily violations of Palestinian rights. We call on third States to strongly condemn this politically-motivated attack on the Special Rapporteur's mandate and to compel Israel to comply with its obligations under the [Charter of the United Nations](88805.md)."[^cite_note-72][^cite_note-73]

Amid continuing efforts to have Albanese removed from her post, [Amnesty International](18947898.md) Italy published on 26 April 2023 a letter of support signed by dozens of Italian rights groups, MPs, jurists, and academics.[^cite_note-74] On 27 April, three former holders of the position ([John Dugard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Dugard), [Richard Falk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Falk), and [Michael Lynk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Lynk)) publicly urged the UN to defend Albanese and said that she has been "the target of attacks that have been 'slanderous' and 'personal'". On 3 May, Albanese tweeted that she "saw too many deaths \[of Palestinians], too much arbitrariness, zero accountability" and faced accusations for her work in addressing these abuses.[^cite_note-75][^cite_note-76]

In 2025, there were calls to nominate Albanese for the [Nobel Peace Prize](26230922.md). A campaign on [Avaaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avaaz) to award her with the Prize reached over 400,000 signiatures.[^cite_note-77][^cite_note-78] Albanese was nominated for the Nobel Peace Prize by [Aodhán Ó Ríordáin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aodh%C3%A1n_%C3%93_R%C3%ADord%C3%A1in), an Irish member of the EU Parliament;[^cite_note-79] [Matjaž Nemec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matja%C5%BE_Nemec), a Slovenian politician;[^cite_note-80] the [Tunisian National Dialogue Quartet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunisian_National_Dialogue_Quartet);[^cite_note-81] and the Canadian [New Democratic Party](19283982.md) (NDP) has voiced their intention to do so.[^cite_note-82] At a 2025 meeting of the UN Human Rights Council, she was met with solidarity from the [Global South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_South) member states for her activities. She was also voted in to keep her position as UN Special Rapporteur on human rights in the occupied Palestinian territories until 2028.[^cite_note-83] [Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence) considered granting Albanese honorary citizenship to recognise her support for Palestine. The vote was shelved after members of the governing majority said there were insufficient votes to approve the measure, following opposition from Mayor [Sara Funaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sara_Funaro), who argued that the honour should be reserved for figures who "unite and do not divide."[^cite_note-84][^cite_note-85][^cite_note-86]

9 July 2025[^cite_note-TweetSecRubio-87]&#x20;

In July 2025, the [United States Department of the Treasury](53667.md) under the [Trump administration](78298108.md) imposed sanctions on Albanese under [Executive Order 14203](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_Order_14203), naming her a "[specially designated national](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specially_Designated_Nationals_and_Blocked_Persons_List)", thus forbidding all US persons and companies from doing business with her, except as necessary to the wind down of any transaction involving Albanese until 8 August 2025.[^cite_note-OoFAC-2025-3][^cite_note-AP-2025-88] The US Secretary of State [Marco Rubio](5502549.md) was quoted as saying: "Albanese's campaign of political and economic warfare against the United States and Israel will no longer be tolerated".[^cite_note-AP-2025-88][^cite_note-89]

Georgetown University stated in December 2025 that Albanese was no longer an affiliate scholar in compliance with the sanctions.[^cite_note-Wagenheim-2025-10]

Belgium foreign minister [Maxime Prévot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxime_Pr%C3%A9vot) criticised the sanctions.[^cite_note-90] Spain prime minister [Pedro Sánchez](43297512.md) has called on the European Union to block the sanctions.[^cite_note-\:6-91] Albanese called the sanctions "obscene" and said that she was being punished for her "pursuit of justice".[^cite_note-92] The move was criticised by [Amnesty International](18947898.md) as a "disgraceful affront to international justice".[^cite_note-93] The editorial board of *[The Wall Street Journal](173070.md)* praised the move as overdue.[^cite_note-94]

In May 2026, US district judge [Richard J. Leon](4348089.md) blocked the sanctions against Albanese, ruling that they violated Albanese's [First Amendment](31653.md) rights.[^cite_note-95] Later that month, after the [D.C. Court of Appeals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court_of_Appeals_for_the_D.C._Circuit) granted an [administrative stay](81102625.md) of Leon's order,[^cite_note-96] the sanctions were reinstated. [^cite_note-97]

## Awards

In April 2023, Albanese received the International Stefano Chiarini Award ([Italian](14708.md): *Premio Internazionale Stefano Chiarini*) in recognition of her journalistic work covering Palestine and the [Middle East](19323.md).[^cite_note-98][^cite_note-99]

She was chosen as the [Passblue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Passblue) UN Person of the Year for 2024. Passblue said Albanese and the other nominees "demonstrated strong leadership in 2024 to uphold the UN Charter, advocate for universal human rights, act to ensure international humanitarian law and promote peace and nonviolence throughout the world".[^cite_note-100]

On 12 February 2025, Albanese received the Dutch "[Dries van Agt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dries_van_Agt) Prize" from [The Rights Forum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rights_Forum?action=edit\&redlink=1), which honors individuals and organizations that make a strong commitment to human rights and international law in Palestine.[^cite_note-101] In March 2025, the [Islamic Republic News Agency](841314.md) reported that Albanese received the Iranian "Dr. Taghi Ebtekar Award" for her work on the humanitarian situation in Gaza and her outstanding documentation and reporting of the abuses taking place there.[^cite_note-102] On 2 April 2026, Albanese received a joint honorary doctorate from three Flemish universities: the [University of Antwerp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Antwerp), [Ghent University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghent_University) and [Vrije Universiteit Brussel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vrije_Universiteit_Brussel).[^cite_note-103][^cite_note-104]

On 7 May 2026, Spain prime minister Pedro Sánchez awarded Albanese the [Order of Civil Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Civil_Merit), one of the country's highest civilian honors, for her "extensive work in documenting and denouncing violations of international law in Gaza".[^cite_note-\:6-91]

Albanese married Massimiliano Calì, a [World Bank](36752.md) official,[^cite_note-105] and they have two children.[^cite_note-UoG-2022-8] As of 2025, she lives in [Tunis](57659.md), Tunisia.[^cite_note-OoFAC-2025-3][^cite_note-106]
