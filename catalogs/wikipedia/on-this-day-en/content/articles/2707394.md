The **Mehr News Agency** (**MNA**; [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): خبرگزاری مهر, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Persian): *Xabâr-gozâri Mehr*) is a semi-official news agency sponsored by the [government of Iran](50913538.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-\:0-2] It is headquartered in [Tehran](57654.md), and is owned by the Iranian government's [Islamic Development Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Development_Organization) (IIDO). Mehr publishes all content on its website under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_Commons_license#Version_4.0).[^cite_note-3]

## History and profile

Established on 22 June 2003,[^cite_note-4] MNA is the most multilingual (transmitting news and photos in six languages) news agency in the Islamic Republic of [Iran](14653.md). The first CEO and Director General was Parviz Esmaeili, and its current CEO and Director General is Mohammad Mahdi Rahmati.[^cite_note-5] The director of the agency is selected by the [Supreme Leader of Iran](36814344.md).[^cite_note-\:0-2]

MNA includes coverage in the following areas:

- Art (cinema, theater, music, visual arts)

- Culture and literature (poetry, stories, books)

- Religion and thought

- Seminary and university

- Modern Technology

- Social

- Economy

- Political

- International

- Sports

- Magazines

- Photos

- Provinces

MNA has five regional centers inside the country—northern, southern, central, eastern, and western Iran.

It has also [stringers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stringer_\(journalism\)) and correspondents in Europe, South America, Turkey, East Asia, and some Persian Gulf littoral states and [Commonwealth of Independent States](36870.md) for the time–being and is extending them across the world.

It transmits news and photos in six languages of [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language), [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language), [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic), [Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_language), [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu) and [Kurdish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurdish_languages).

Employing more than 300 reporters and photographers dispatched in 30 provinces of the country, MNA provides the widest news coverage in Iran.

## International cooperation

The news agency was accepted as the 40th member of the [Organization of Asia-Pacific News Agencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organization_of_Asia-Pacific_News_Agencies) (OANA)[^cite_note-6] at the 13th General Assembly[^cite_note-7] held in [Jakarta](16275.md), [Indonesia](14579.md), in 2007. The agency was the host agency of the OANA[^cite_note-name-8] 31st Executive Board Meeting (EBM) and the 25th Editorial-Technical Experts Group (ETEG) Meeting in 2009.[^cite_note-9] It had also actively participated in two international summits – the 2009 World Media Summit[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] in [Beijing](18603746.md), [China](5405.md), and the 2010 OANA Summit Congress[^cite_note-12] in [Seoul](19159283.md), [South Korea](27019.md).

The agency was also a special guest of the III News Agencies’ World Congress (NAWC)[^cite_note-13] in [Argentina](18951905.md) from 19 to 23 October 2010.

MNA also participated in the [Istanbul](3391396.md) OANA General Assembly [^cite_note-14] in November 2010 and the OANA 26th ETEG - 32nd EBM meetings[^cite_note-15] in [Ulaanbaatar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulaanbaatar) in June 2011.

According to the OANA[^cite_note-name-8] Secretary's report to the [Istanbul](3391396.md) Assembly, MNA, which has become OANA[^cite_note-name-8] member since 2007, ranked second among the OANA[^cite_note-name-8] member agencies in number of news and photos published on the organization's website. MNA initiated the OANA[^cite_note-name-8] flag,[^cite_note-16] and the 50-year-old OANA[^cite_note-name-8] adopted the flag.

## Criticism

In 2006, Mehr News Agency was described by the [Anti-Defamation League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Defamation_League) as a "megaphone for notorious [Holocaust deniers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holocaust_deniers)" because it had published interviews with [Arthur Butz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Butz), [Michael A. Hoffman II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_A._Hoffman_II), [Fredrick Toben](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredrick_Toben), [Paul Fromm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Fromm_\(white_supremacist\)), [Mark Weber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Weber) and [Robert Faurisson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Faurisson).[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18]
