# Torkham border crossing

**Torkham border crossing** ([Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu), [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language), and [Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto_language): تورخم *Tūrkham*) is a major [border crossing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_crossing) between the [Pakistani](23235.md) city of [Torkham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torkham) and [Afghanistan](737.md), located along the [Grand Trunk Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Trunk_Road) on the international border between the two countries. It connects [Nangarhar province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nangarhar_province) of Afghanistan with Pakistan's [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md) province. It is the busiest [port of entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_entry) between the two countries, serving as a major transporting, shipping, and receiving site.[^cite_note-ddafg20060404-1][^cite_note-2]

[Highway 8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kabul%E2%80%93Jalalabad_Road) connects Torkham to [Kabul](16826.md) through [Jalalabad](16274.md).[^cite_note-DefenseLink20070822-3] On the Pakistani side, the border crossing is at the end of the [N-5 National Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N-5_National_Highway), which connects it to [Peshawar](66230.md) in the east via the [Khyber Pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khyber_Pass) and further connects it to [Islamabad](51153.md) by other routes.

The [Afghan Border Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afghan_Border_Police) and Pakistan's [Frontier Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frontier_Corps) are the main agencies for controlling Torkham. They are backed by the [Pakistani](23369.md) and [Afghan Armed Forces](57836785.md). Torkham belongs to the Momand Dara district of Nangarhar.

On 15 August 2021, Torkham was seized by [Taliban](30635.md) fighters, becoming the second border crossing with Pakistan to be captured by Taliban as part of wider [2021 Taliban offensive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Taliban_offensive). As a response, Pakistan temporarily closed the border crossing.

## History

The location has been used throughout history by [Afghan](2235.md) and [Turkic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkic_peoples) [caravans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caravan_\(travellers\)), including marching armies of powerful empires. Most of these were on their way to [Northern India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_India), passing through Peshawar and then [Lahore](125315.md). Some of the well known regional historical figures who are believed to have passed through Torkham are [Chandragupta Maurya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chandragupta_Maurya), [Hsüan-tsang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xuanzang), [Jayapala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jayapala), [Al-Biruni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ab%C5%AB_Ray%E1%B8%A5%C4%81n_al-B%C4%ABr%C5%ABn%C4%AB), [Ibn Battuta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibn_Battuta), [Babur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babur), [Humayun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humayun), [Nader Shah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nader_Shah), [Ahmad Shah Durrani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmad_Shah_Durrani), [Zaman Shah Durrani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaman_Shah_Durrani), [Dost Mohammad Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dost_Mohammad_Khan), and [Akbar Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wazir_Akbar_Khan).

Over much of the last century, there have been proposals to extend the (defunct) [Khyber rail service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khyber_Pass_Railway) to Afghanistan and possibly beyond, passing through [Jalalabad](16274.md). In 2010, Pakistan and Afghanistan signed a [Memorandum of understanding](1018279.md) (MoU) for going ahead with the laying of [rail tracks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rail_tracks) between the two countries. Work on the proposed project was supposed to start in 2010.[^cite_note-4]

In November 2001 *[The New York Times](30680.md)* reported that Torkham is [Hajji Ali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hajji_Ali_\(Afghan_politician\)?action=edit\&redlink=1)'s hometown. Ali was an anti-Taliban militia leader who seized power after the Taliban retreated.[^cite_note-NewYorkTimes20011116-5] It was reported that Hajji Ali had joined forces with two other militia leaders, [Mawlawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mawlawi_\(Islamic_title\)) [Yunis Khalis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Yunus_Khalis), and [Ezatullah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ezatullah_\(Sorubi,_Nangarhar,_2001\)), to set up a regional provisional government.

On the Pakistani side, Torkham lies at the end of the [N-5 National Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N-5_National_Highway). It is connected to city of [Peshawar](66230.md) in the east. Transported goods arrive to Torkham from the port city of [Karachi](17123.md) in [Sindh](28972.md) province. Torkham is 5 kilometres (3.1 mi) west of the summit of the [Khyber Pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khyber_Pass). It lies on the most important supply route for [US](32212.md)-led [NATO forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Security_Assistance_Force) in Afghanistan. Pakistan's government sometimes block supplies due to the American use of [drone strikes in Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drone_strikes_in_Pakistan).[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

In April 2006 the [Afghan Border Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afghan_Border_Police) began requiring travelers crossing the border at Torkham to possess valid travel documents.[^cite_note-ddafg20060404-1][^cite_note-Afps20060410-8]

[Pakistan](23235.md) had completed the construction of a border gate, crossing terminal and other associated infrastructure on its side of Torkham border by July 2016. Border gate has been named as [Bab-i-Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bab-i-Pakistan) and the crossing terminal has been named "Shaheed Major Ali Jawad Changezi terminal" after the officer lost his life in skirmishes with the Afghan security forces at the same border and in the clashes that resulted from the construction of this very gate. The flag hosting ceremony has also started just like the one at [Wagah border crossing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attari%E2%80%93Wagah_border_ceremony) with India. Now no one will be allowed to cross border without proper documentations to check the infiltration of terrorists from Afghanistan into Pakistan. Work on the construction of the gate and associated facilities began in 2014 but kept getting delayed because of Afghan reservations and abrupt clashes. Pakistan plans to have similar border control measures at all six major crossing points between the two countries on the 2,600 km-long porous border.[^cite_note-9]

The border has seen repeated closures in the [Afghan-Pakistan standoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afghanistan-Pakistan_border_skirmishes).

## Climate

With an influence from the local steppe climate, Torkham features a [hot semi-arid climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_semi-arid_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *BSh*). The average temperature in Torkham is 20.3 °C, while the annual precipitation averages 407 mm. June is the driest month with an average rainfall of 8 mm, while the wettest month is March, with an average 82 mm of precipitation.

June is the hottest month of the year with an average temperature of 31.0 °C. The coldest month January has an average temperature of 8.4 °C.
