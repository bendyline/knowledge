This article is about the Senegal government. For recent and past political developments in Senegal, see [Politics of Senegal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics_of_Senegal).

The **Government of Senegal** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Gouvernement du Sénégal*) is the union government created by the [constitution of Senegal](26244481.md), consisting of the executive, parliament, and judiciary. The Seat of the Government is located in [Dakar](44251.md). The government is led by the [president](450099.md) (currently [Bassirou Diomaye Faye](76426907.md)).

## Executive branch

Main article: [President of Senegal](450099.md)

The president is elected by popular vote for a five-year term. The Council of Ministers is appointed by the president. The 2001 constitution introduced a two-term limit for the president; a [March 2016 referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Senegalese_constitutional_referendum) restored the presidential term to five years from seven.[^cite_note-1]

## Legislative branch

The [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_Senegal) (*Assemblée Nationale*) comprises 165 members elected for five-year terms by [parallel voting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_voting). Of these, 90 are elected by plurality vote in single and multi-seat [constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constituencies) across Senegal, with a further 15 elected from overseas voters. The remaining 60 elected through proportional representation.[^cite_note-2] The Senate, an indirectly elected upper chamber, was abolished in 2012, leaving a [unicameral](204304.md) system.

## Politics

Main article: [Politics of Senegal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics_of_Senegal)

Politics in [Senegal](27278.md) takes place within the framework of a [presidential](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_system) democratic republic. The president is the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) and [government](37570.md). [Executive power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_power) in Senegal is concentrated in the president's hands.[^cite_note-Freedom_House-3]

## Judicial branch

The nation's highest courts that deal with business issues are the constitutional council and the [Court of Cassation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court_of_Cassation_\(Senegal\)), members of which are named by the president.

## Administrative divisions

Further information: [Administrative divisions of Senegal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Senegal)

Senegal is subdivided into 13 regions (*régions*, singular – *région*):

[Dakar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dakar_Region), [Diourbel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diourbel_Region), [Fatick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatick_Region), [Kaolack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaolack_Region), [Kédougou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%A9dougou_Region), [Kolda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolda_Region), [Louga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louga_Region), [Matam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matam_Region), [Saint-Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Louis_Region), [Sédhiou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A9dhiou_Region), [Tambacounda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tambacounda_Region), [Thiès](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thi%C3%A8s_Region), [Ziguinchor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ziguinchor_Region). Local administrators are all appointed by and responsible to the President.

## International relations

Further information: [Foreign relations of Senegal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_relations_of_Senegal)

Senegal joined with [the Gambia](65656.md) to form the nominal confederation of [Senegambia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A9n%C3%A9gambia_Confederation) in 1982. However, the envisaged integration of the two countries was never carried out, and the union was dissolved in 1989. Despite peace talks, a separatist group in the southern [Casamance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casamance) region has sporadically clashed with government forces since 1982. Senegal has a long history of participating in international [peacekeeping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_peacekeeping).
