**Nepalis**[^cite_note-25] are the citizens and nationals of the [Federal Democratic Republic of Nepal](171166.md) under the [Nepali nationality law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepali_nationality_law). The term *Nepali* strictly refers to *nationality*, meaning people holding citizenship of Nepal. Conversely, people without Nepalese citizenship but with roots in [Nepal](171166.md) (such as [Nepalese Australians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepalese_Australians)), who speak [Nepali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepali_language) or any of the other 128 [Nepali languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Languages_of_Nepal) but hold foreign citizenship, are referred to as *Nepali-language speaking Foreigners* ([Nepali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepali_language): नेपाली भाषी विदेशी). The term *Nepali* is also not generally used to refer to non-citizen residents, dual citizens, or expatriates.[^cite_note-nrnact-26]

Nepal is a multicultural and multi-ethnic country. [Bagmati Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagmati_Province) is the most densely populated province, holding approximately 20.97 percent of Nepal's population.[^cite_note-2011NepalCensus-27][^cite_note-cia-28]

## Nepali diaspora

Main article: [Non-Resident Nepali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-Resident_Nepali)

The **Nepali diaspora**, or **non-resident Nepalese**, are Nepali people living overseas.[^cite_note-29] The *Non-resident Nepali Act, 2064* of Nepal defines a non-resident Nepalese as someone who is a foreign citizen of Nepali origin including a Nepali citizen residing abroad.[^cite_note-nrnact-26]
