The **International Islamic University, Islamabad** (**IIUI**)[^cite_note-1] is a [public](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_university) [research university](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_university) located in [Islamabad](51153.md), Pakistan. It is one of the world's most prestigious universities for [Islamic](6037917.md) learning and is considered as one of the three major Islamic universities in the [Muslim world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim_world) alongside the [Al-Azhar University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Azhar_University) in Egypt and the [Islamic University of Medina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_University_of_Medina) in Saudi Arabia.

The International Islamic University Islamabad has consistently maintained the largest international student body in Pakistan, enrolling thousands of students from more than 50 countries.[^cite_note-iiu.edu.pk-2] The university's core focus is on [Islamic law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_law), [Islamic jurisprudence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_jurisprudence) and [theology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theology). The university offers undergraduate and postgraduate degrees, including doctoral and [professional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional) degrees. The degrees offered in other disciplines include Engineering, Computer Science, Natural Sciences, Business Studies, Humanities, Architecture and Law. All of its engineering programmes are accredited under the prestigious [Washington Accord (credentials)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_Accord_\(credentials\))[^cite_note-3] as well as by the [Pakistan Engineering Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Engineering_Council).

[Times Higher Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Times_Higher_Education) has consistently ranked the university first in Pakistan for **International Outlook**.[^cite_note-timeshighereducation.com-4] According to the 2026 [QS World University Rankings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QS_World_University_Rankings) IIUI has reached the global 101–150 band for Theology, Divinity, and Religious Studies.[^cite_note-5] The university was ranked joint second among Pakistani institutions in the 2022 rankings of [Times Higher Education World University Rankings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Times_Higher_Education_World_University_Rankings) and has been since recognized among the country's leading universities in Engineering, Computer Science, and Business.[^cite_note-timeshighereducation.com-4]

## History

&#x20;The Islamic University was established in Islamabad on 11 November 1980, which corresponded to 1 Muharram 1401 AH, the first day of the 15h-century in the [Islamic calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_calendar).[^cite_note-\:0-6] This date was chosen to symbolize the beginning of an Islamic renaissance in the modern era.[^cite_note-\:0-6] It was reconstituted as the 'International Islamic University' with the promulgation of the International Islamic University Ordinance by President [Muhammad Zia-ul-Haq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_Zia-ul-Haq) in March 1985.[^cite_note-\:0-6]

The university has two campuses.

### Old campus

The old campus lies about the [Faisal Mosque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faisal_Mosque). The mosque was designed by Vedat Dalokay, a [Turkish](11125639.md) architect. It was donated to the university by [King Faisal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faisal_bin_Abdul_Aziz) of [Saudi Arabia](349303.md). The mosque is one of the largest in the world and is able to accommodate tens of thousands of people in its prayer hall, women's gallery and courtyard.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-archnet-9]

### New campus

The new campus occupies [Islamabad](51153.md)'s Sector H-10.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-google_maps-11] The first phase of construction of the new campus was completed in 2013. Since then a campus area for women has been established. In 2006, the construction of the Central Library and the Lincoln Corner was completed.

## Overview

The university is a center of learning for [Islam](6037917.md), [theology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theology) and the [Islamic studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_studies) in a contemporary context.[^cite_note-iiu.edu.pk-2]

The university was founded in 1980 with funding from inside Pakistan and foreign donations from Saudi Arabia. [Ismail al-Faruqi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ismail_al-Faruqi), a prominent scholar, played a significant role in its establishment. His advisory role was instrumental in laying the foundation of the university, integrating Islamic values with contemporary academic disciplines.[^cite_note-12] The main goal of university was to provide education and training in [Islamic law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_law) for the people in professions of [judicial officers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judicial_officer), public [prosecutors](440936.md), teachers of [madrassas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrassa), and [preachers of Friday sermons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khatib).

In February 2014, it awarded [King Abdullah of Saudi Arabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Abdullah_of_Saudi_Arabia) an honorary doctorate in politics and international relations.[^cite_note-13] The University now offers [undergraduate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undergraduate) and [post-graduate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-graduate) programs in [law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law), [science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science), [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering) & [technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering_technology), [humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humanities), [arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fine_arts), [religious studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religious_studies), [social](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_science) and [natural sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_sciences).

## Faculties

The university consists of the following faculties:

### Faculty of Computing

The Faculty of Computing (founded in 2020) teaches [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) and [software engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_engineering)

### Faculty of Arabic

The Faculty of Arabic teaches [Arabic Language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic), [literature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_literature), [Linguistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linguistics), [Translation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Translation_studies) and [Interpretation](899930.md).[^cite_note-14]

### Faculty of Sciences

The Faculty of Sciences (founded in 2003) teaches physics and [nanotechnology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanotechnology), Mathematics and Statistics, [environmental sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_sciences), [biotechnology](4502.md) and [bioinformatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioinformatics).[^cite_note-15]

### Faculty of Engineering and Technology

The Faculty of Engineering and Technology (founded in 2007) offers degrees in [Computer Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_Engineering), [Mechanical Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_Engineering), [Electrical Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_Engineering) and [Civil Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Engineering).[^cite_note-16] The faculty is highly qualified with high ratio of Professors and Assistant Professors. All of its engineering programmes are accredited under the prestigious [Washington Accord (credentials)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_Accord_\(credentials\)) as well as by the [Pakistan Engineering Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Engineering_Council).

### Iqra College of Technology

The Iqra College of Technology (ICT) is a constituent college of IIUI. It offers [B.Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.Tech) (Hons) and [Diploma of Associate Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diploma_of_Associate_Engineering) (DAE) programs. It has faculty duly recognized by PEC[^cite_note-17]

### Faculty of Languages and Literature

The faculty of languages and literature offers BS, MS and PhD degrees in English, [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language) and [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu).[^cite_note-18]

### Faculty of Management Sciences

The faculty of management sciences was founded in 1995. In 1996, the Department of Business Administration offered [Master of Business Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Business_Administration) program. The Department of Technology Management was founded in 1998. It is the largest faculty of the university with around 3,700 registered students.[^cite_note-22]

### Faculty of Shariah and Law

The Faculty of Shariah and Law was first established in [Quaid-i-Azam University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quaid-i-Azam_University) in 1979. In 1980, it was incorporated into International Islamic University, Islamabad. Students enrolling in doctorate programs complete one year of compulsory coursework before commencing a thesis. More than 900 students have graduated from this faculty.[^cite_note-23]

### Faculty of Social Sciences

The Faculty of Social Sciences offers degrees in [Anthropology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropology), [Politics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics) and [International Relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Relations), [Psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology), [History](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History) and [Pakistan Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Studies), [Islamic Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_art) and [Architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_architecture), [Mass Communications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_Communications) and [Sociology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociology).[^cite_note-24]

### Faculty of Islamic Studies

A Faculty of Islamic Studies (Usuluddin) was founded in 1981. It offers degrees in the [Tafseer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tafseer) and [Quranic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quran) sciences, [Hadith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadith), [comparative religion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comparative_religion), Da'wah and Islamic culture, Aqeedah and philosophy and Seerah and Islamic history. The [Da'wah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Da'wah) Academy was founded in March 1985. The Shari'ah Institute of Training was founded in 1981 and became an academy in 1985.[^cite_note-25]

Faculty of Usuluddin have different international faculty members such as [Al-Azhar University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Azhar_University) qualified professors.[^cite_note-26]

The Centre of Excellence for Modern Languages teaches English, Chinese and [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu) languages preparatory courses for students seeking to enroll in foreign and national universities (for example, HEC scholarships) or seeking employment. The university's International Institute of Islamic Economics was founded in 1983. ## Libraries

### Central library

The central library is situated on the new campus. There is also the Muhammad Hamidullah and the International Institute of Islamic Economics library. It contains rare books on Islamic law relating to economics and Islamic governance. in this library you can read every department's books

### Islamic Research Institute Library

The Islamic Research Institute library is a source of knowledge about Islam.

### Engineering and technology Library

The engineering and technology library contains 25,000 books. Fifteen periodicals are subscribed and ten newspapers are purchased. The library can seat fifty users. There are 550 research reports and theses.

### Da'wah Academy Library

Da’wah Academy Library has five types of libraries to fulfill the information needs of its research scholars, academicians, course participants, and the general public. These are Da’wah Research Library, Dr. Mehmood Ahmed Ghazi Library, Da’wah Centre for Women Library, Dawah Children Library Faisal Masjid Campus Islamabad and Regional Da’wah Centre (Sindh) Library, Karachi.

### Shari'ah Academy Library

The Shariah Academy Library has a collection of 8,976 books and journals on the subjects of Qur'anic studies, Hadith studies, Prophet's Biography, Islamic law and Anglo-Saxon law. There are also a number of departmental libraries such as the Research Journals library, the Library of Islamic Studies, the *Al-Dirassat Al-Islamyya*, the *Fikro Nazar*, the Insights library and the Mayar library. The university publishes the Journal of Business and Management Sciences (JBMS).

### Dr. Hamidullah Library

It is located in the basement of Faisal mosque and it has large volume of books on varieties of subjects.

## Hostels

The university infrastructure includes seven academic buildings. The university's student accommodation includes seven hostels for women and seven for men including Kuwait Hostel, located in the old campus adjacent to [Faisal Masjid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faisal_Masjid). Kuwait Hostel has seven sub-blocks in it.

## Mosque

Salman bin Abdulaziz Mosque is an under-construction grand mosque approved by King [Salman bin Abdulaziz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salman_bin_Abdulaziz).[^cite_note-29] It will be constructed in the H-10 sector [Islamabad](51153.md).[^cite_note-saudigazette-30][^cite_note-english.aawsat.com-31][^cite_note-arabnews-32]

The mosque will accommodate 6,000 people, 2,000 for special prayers for women. The project will also include the Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques Library, Museum, Muhammad Bin Salman Conference Hall, Office Services Zone and Parking Area.[^cite_note-roznama92news-33][^cite_note-urdu.geo.tv-34][^cite_note-urdu.arynews.tv-35]
