**Mohammad Baqer Saad Dawood al-Saadi** (born 1993) is a commander of [Kata'ib Hezbollah](23626646.md), a [Shia Iraqi](22350732.md) militia group backed by [Iran](14653.md)'s [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps](1285472.md). After being arrested in [Turkey](11125639.md) in 2026, he was charged by the US for plotting or being involved in having a role in the [2026 London antisemitic attacks](83012228.md).

## Background

Al-Saadi was born in 1993 in [Iran](14653.md), to [Iraqi Shia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_Shia) parents who left Iraq for exile in Iran during the [Iran–Iraq War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Iraq_War). His given name, *Mohammad Baqer*, is thought to be a reference to the martyr Grand Ayatollah [Muhammad Baqir al-Sadr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_Baqir_al-Sadr) who was executed by [Ba'athist Iraq](26215470.md) in 1980.[^cite_note-1]

One expert told The Guardian that al-Saadi had been recruited in his early 20s into a Shia militia created by the Iranian government after the [US-led invasion of Iraq in 2003](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US-led_invasion_of_Iraq_in_2003?action=edit\&redlink=1). Al-Saadi was deployed to [Syria](7515849.md) during the [Syrian civil war](30741795.md) when Shia militia forces were sent by Iran to bolster the regime of [Bashar al-Assad](364813.md). According to The Guardian, while in Syria he often exaggerated his combat experience.[^cite_note-y887-2]

## Arrest

On 15 May 2026, he was arrested in [Turkey](11125639.md) and charged by the [United States Justice Department](52563.md) for plotting or being involved in terrorist attacks in Europe and North America, including targeting a synagogue in [New York City](645042.md) and other [Jewish](15624.md) sites during the [2026 London antisemitic attacks](83012228.md), in retaliation to the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] He was accused of involvement in the stabbing of two Jewish men in [London](17867.md), [United Kingdom](31717.md) in April 2026.[^cite_note-5] Additionally, the [New York Post](102227.md) reported that he plotted to assassinate [U.S. president](24113.md) [Donald Trump](4848272.md)'s [daughter](52170275.md) [Ivanka Trump](1861441.md) in retaliation for the [2020 strike](62726668.md) against former [Quds Force](4028355.md) commander [Qasem Soleimani](16805217.md).[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]
