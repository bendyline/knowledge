The **Union Government** ([Burmese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_language): ပြည်ထောင်စုအစိုးရအဖွဲ့), is the [executive body](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_branch) of the [government of Myanmar](20390.md) led by the [president of Myanmar](11195083.md).

The [second cabinet of Min Aung Hlaing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Min_Aung_Hlaing_cabinet) is the current cabinet.

The [2008 Constitution of Myanmar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Constitution_of_Myanmar) stipulates that Union Ministers must be [Burmese citizens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_nationality_law) who have been living in the country for at least ten consecutive years:[^cite_note-const-1]

1. persons who have attained the age of 40 years;

2. persons who have qualifications, with the exception of the age limit, prescribed in Section 120 for [Pyithu Hluttaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyithu_Hluttaw) representatives;

3. persons whose qualification does not breach the provisions under Section 121 which disqualify a person from standing for election as Pyithu Hluttaw representatives;

4. persons loyal to the Union and its citizens

The Commander-in-Chief appoints the Ministers of Defence, Home Affairs and Border Affairs, selecting candidates from within the [Defence Services](20394.md) (Tatmadaw), while the President appoints the remainder.[^cite_note-const-1]

The President also appoints the Deputy Ministers of the respective ministries, following the same qualifications as those for Union Ministers, with the exception of age (35 years, instead of 40).
