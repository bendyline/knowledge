# EGOT

**EGOT** is an acronym for the [Emmy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmy_Awards), [Grammy](44635.md), [Oscar](324.md), and [Tony](54741.md) Awards, referring to all four of the major performing art awards in the United States.[^cite_note-People-1][^cite_note-2] These awards honor outstanding achievements in [television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television_in_the_United_States), [audio recording](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sound_recording_and_reproduction)/[music](171080.md), [film](418334.md), and [Broadway theatre](725252.md) respectively.[^cite_note-wowowow\.com-3] Achieving the EGOT has been referred to as the "[grand slam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_slam)" of American [show business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Show_business).[^cite_note-People-1][^cite_note-4] Including those with honorary or special awards, 28 people have achieved this status.[^cite_note-5] Only one person, [Robert Lopez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Lopez), has won all four awards twice.[^cite_note-6]

## Background

The EGOT acronym was coined by actor [Philip Michael Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Michael_Thomas) in late 1984. While starring in *[Miami Vice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami_Vice)*, he stated a desire to achieve the EGOT within five years.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] The acronym gained wider recognition following [a 2009 episode of *30 Rock*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dealbreakers_Talk_Show_No._0001) that introduced EGOT status as a recurring plotline.[^cite_note-9] There is some debate over whether only the [Primetime Emmy Award](6579025.md) should count towards an EGOT, as some (including Thomas himself) distinguish the [other types of Emmy competitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmy_Awards#Types) as subordinate to the Primetime honor.[^cite_note-thrillist-10]

Starting in 2016, the [Daytime Emmy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daytime_Emmy_Awards) had a category for [Outstanding Musical Performance in a Daytime Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daytime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Musical_Performance_in_a_Daytime_Program), which was removed after the 2019 ceremony because three of the four winners were Broadway ensembles, which between them included five people ([Cynthia Erivo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cynthia_Erivo), [Rachel Bay Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Bay_Jones), [Katrina Lenk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katrina_Lenk), [Ben Platt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Platt), and [Ari'el Stachel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ari'el_Stachel)) who had already won Tony and Grammy awards for the shows they were in, and with their Daytime Emmy wins only needed Oscars to complete their EGOT status.[^cite_note-Daytime_Emmys'_Rule_Change_Ends_Controversial_Shortcut_to_EGOT_Status-11]

In 2023, *TheaterMania* writer Zachary Stewart criticized the practice of "selling" producer credits for shows favored to win a Tony as a "shortcut" to EGOT status. He drew a distinction between the producers who actually do the work of organizing the production of a show and investing producers who merely help finance it, often late in the award season.[^cite_note-12]

*[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_\(magazine\))* noted in 2026 that technology company [Apple](856.md) had achieved a 'studio EGOT', with various Emmy wins, an Oscar for *[CODA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CODA_\(2021_film\))*, a Grammy for "[Bad as I Used to Be](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_as_I_Used_to_Be)" from *[F1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F1_\(film\))* (alongside the 2002 [Technical Grammy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_Grammy_Award)), and multiple Tony awards for *[Schmigadoon!](76848716.md)* at the [79th Tony Awards](82230076.md).[^cite_note-13]

## Competitive EGOT awardees

### Richard Rodgers



&#x20;In 1962, [Richard Rodgers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Rodgers) became the first person to win all four awards.

American composer [Richard Rodgers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Rodgers) (1902–1979) received his fourth distinct award in 1962. Between 1946 and 1979, Rodgers received a total of 10 competitive awards. He was the first person to win all four and was primarily a composer.

- **Academy Awards:**

1. [1946](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/18th_Academy_Awards): [Best Song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Original_Song) – "[It Might as Well Be Spring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/It_Might_as_Well_Be_Spring)" (from *[State Fair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Fair_\(1945_film\))*)

- **Primetime Emmy Awards:**

1. [1962](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/14th_Primetime_Emmy_Awards): [Outstanding Achievement in Original Music Composed for Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Music_Composition_for_a_Limited_or_Anthology_Series,_Movie_or_Special_\(Original_Dramatic_Score\)) – *[Winston Churchill: The Valiant Years](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winston_Churchill:_The_Valiant_Years)*

- **Grammy Awards:**

1. [1961](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3rd_Annual_Grammy_Awards): [Best Show Album (Original Cast)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Musical_Theater_Album) – *[The Sound of Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sound_of_Music_\(original_Broadway_cast_recording\))*

2. [1963](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5th_Annual_Grammy_Awards): [Best Original Cast Show Album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Musical_Theater_Album) – *[No Strings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Strings)*

- **Tony Awards:**

1. [1950](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4th_Tony_Awards): [Best Musical](249875.md) – *[South Pacific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Pacific_\(musical\))*

2. 1950: Producers (Musical) – *South Pacific*

3. 1950: [Best Score](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Award_for_Best_Original_Score) – *South Pacific*

4. [1952](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6th_Tony_Awards): Best Musical – *[The King and I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_King_and_I)*

5. [1960](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/14th_Tony_Awards): Best Musical – *[The Sound of Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sound_of_Music)*

6. [1962](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/16th_Tony_Awards): [Best Composer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Award_for_Best_Original_Score) – *[No Strings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Strings)*

- **Special Awards:**

1. [1962](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/16th_Tony_Awards): [Special Tony Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Tony_Award) "for all he has done for young people in the theatre and for taking the men of the orchestra out of the pit and putting them onstage in *No Strings*"

2. [1972](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/26th_Tony_Awards): Special Tony Award

3. [1979](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/33rd_Tony_Awards): Special Tony Award – [Lawrence Langner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Langner) Memorial Award for Distinguished Lifetime Achievement in the American Theatre

### Helen Hayes



In 1977, [Helen Hayes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Hayes) became the second person and first woman to win all four awards.

American actress [Helen Hayes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Hayes) (1900–1993) received her fourth distinct award in 1977. Between 1932 and 1980, Hayes received a total of six competitive awards. She was the first woman and the first performer to win all four. Hayes was also the first EGOT recipient to win the [Triple Crown of Acting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triple_Crown_of_Acting#Helen_Hayes) (with individual acting wins in each of the Emmy, Oscar, and Tony awards). Counting only the first award of each type, she also has the longest interval (45 years) between her first and fourth award of any EGOT winner.

- **Academy Awards:**

1. [1932](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5th_Academy_Awards): [Best Actress in a Leading Role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actress) – *[The Sin of Madelon Claudet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sin_of_Madelon_Claudet)*

2. [1971](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/43rd_Academy_Awards): [Best Actress in a Supporting Role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actress) – *[Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airport_\(1970_film\))*

- **Primetime Emmy Awards:**

1. [1953](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5th_Primetime_Emmy_Awards): [Best Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actress_in_a_Limited_or_Anthology_Series_or_Movie) – *[Schlitz Playhouse of Stars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schlitz_Playhouse_of_Stars)* (Episode: "Not a Chance")

- **Grammy Awards:**

1. [1977](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/19th_Annual_Grammy_Awards): [Best Spoken Word Recording](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Audio_Book,_Narration_&_Storytelling_Recording) – *Great American Documents*

- **Tony Awards:**

1. [1947](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1st_Tony_Awards): [Best Actress in a Play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Award_for_Best_Actress_in_a_Play) – *[Happy Birthday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Happy_Birthday_\(play\))*

2. [1958](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/12th_Tony_Awards): [Best Leading Actress in a Play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Award_for_Best_Actress_in_a_Play) – *[Time Remembered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%A9ocadia)*

- **Special Awards:**

1. [1980](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/34th_Tony_Awards): [Special Tony Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Tony_Award) – [Lawrence Langner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Langner) Memorial Award for Distinguished Lifetime Achievement in the American Theatre

### Rita Moreno



In 1977, [Rita Moreno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rita_Moreno) became the third person and first [Hispanic American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_and_Latino_Americans) to win all four awards.

Puerto Rican actress, dancer, and singer [Rita Moreno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rita_Moreno) (born 1931) received her fourth distinct award in 1977. Between 1961 and 1978, Moreno received a total of five awards.[^cite_note-30] She is also the first [Latina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_and_Latino_Americans) winner and the first winner to win a Grammy as their second award (both previous winners won Tonys as their second award). In addition, she became a [Kennedy Center Honoree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kennedy_Center_Honors) in 2015 and a [Peabody Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peabody_Awards) winner in 2019. Moreno is also the second EGOT recipient and the first [Hispanic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_and_Latino_Americans) actress to win the [Triple Crown of Acting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triple_Crown_of_Acting#Rita_Moreno).

- **Academy Awards:**

1. [1962](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/34th_Academy_Awards): [Best Actress in a Supporting Role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actress) – *[West Side Story](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Side_Story_\(1961_film\))*

- **Primetime Emmy Awards:**

1. [1977](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/29th_Primetime_Emmy_Awards): [Outstanding Continuing or Single Performance by a Supporting Actress in Variety or Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Individual_Performance_in_a_Variety_or_Music_Program) – *[The Muppet Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Muppet_Show)* (Episode: "Rita Moreno")

2. [1978](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/30th_Primetime_Emmy_Awards): [Outstanding Lead Actress for a Single Appearance in a Drama or Comedy Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Guest_Actress_in_a_Drama_Series) – *[The Rockford Files](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rockford_Files)* (Episode: "The Paper Palace")

- **Grammy Awards:**

1. [1973](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/15th_Annual_Grammy_Awards): [Best Recording for Children](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Children's_Music_Album) – *[The Electric Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Electric_Company)*

- **Tony Awards:**

1. [1975](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/29th_Tony_Awards): [Best Supporting or Featured Actress in a Play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Award_for_Best_Featured_Actress_in_a_Play) – *[The Ritz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ritz_\(play\))*

### John Gielgud



In 1991, [John Gielgud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Gielgud) became the fourth person, the oldest (at age 87), the first [LGBTQ](78174325.md) person and first non-American to win all four awards.

English actor and theatre director [John Gielgud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Gielgud) (1904–2000) received his fourth distinct award in 1991. Between 1948 and 1991, Gielgud received a total of five competitive awards. Gielgud was the first winner to win any award other than the Oscar as their first award (his first award was a Tony). At age 87 when he won his Emmy, he also became the oldest winner, the first male performer, the first [LGBTQ](78174325.md) winner, and the first non-American.

- **Academy Awards:**

1. [1982](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/54th_Academy_Awards): [Best Actor in a Supporting Role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor) – *[Arthur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_\(1981_film\))*

- **Primetime Emmy Awards:**

1. [1991](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/43rd_Primetime_Emmy_Awards): [Outstanding Lead Actor in a Miniseries or a Special](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actor_in_a_Limited_or_Anthology_Series_or_Movie) – *[Summer's Lease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summer's_Lease_\(TV_series\))*

- **Grammy Awards:**

1. [1980](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/22nd_Annual_Grammy_Awards): [Best Spoken Word, Documentary or Drama Recording](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Audio_Book,_Narration_&_Storytelling_Recording) – *[Ages of Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ages_of_Man_\(play\))*

- **Tony Awards:**

1. [1948](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2nd_Tony_Awards): [Outstanding Foreign Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Tony_Award) – *[The Importance of Being Earnest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Importance_of_Being_Earnest)*

2. [1961](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/15th_Tony_Awards): [Best Director of a Drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Award_for_Best_Direction_of_a_Play) – *[Big Fish, Little Fish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Fish,_Little_Fish_\(play\))*

- **Special Awards:**

1. [1959](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/13th_Tony_Awards): [Special Tony Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Tony_Award) "for contribution to theatre for his extraordinary insight into the writings of Shakespeare as demonstrated in his one-man play *[Ages of Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ages_of_Man_\(play\))*."

### Audrey Hepburn



In 1994, [Audrey Hepburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audrey_Hepburn) became the fifth person to win all four awards, and the first to do so posthumously.

British actress and humanitarian [Audrey Hepburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audrey_Hepburn) (1929–1993) received her fourth distinct award [posthumously](21021837.md) in 1994. Between 1954 and 1994, Hepburn received a total of four competitive awards. She was the fifth person to complete the feat and the first to do so posthumously. She was also the first winner to win two of their awards in consecutive awards shows (the [1994 Grammys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/36th_Annual_Grammy_Awards) were the first Grammys since her posthumous win at the [1993 Emmys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/45th_Primetime_Emmy_Awards)). She is the only EGOT winner to not win multiple awards in any of the four award fields.

- **Academy Awards:**

1. [1954](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/26th_Academy_Awards): [Best Actress in a Leading Role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actress) – *[Roman Holiday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Holiday)*

- **Primetime Emmy Awards:**

1. [1993](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/45th_Primetime_Emmy_Awards): [Outstanding Individual Achievement – Informational Programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Hosted_Nonfiction_Series_or_Special) – *[Gardens of the World with Audrey Hepburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gardens_of_the_World_with_Audrey_Hepburn)* (Episode: "Flower Gardens")

- **Grammy Awards:**

1. [1994](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/36th_Annual_Grammy_Awards): [Best Spoken Word Album for Children](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Spoken_Word_Album_for_Children) – *[Audrey Hepburn's Enchanted Tales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audrey_Hepburn's_Enchanted_Tales)*

- **Tony Awards:**

1. [1954](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8th_Tony_Awards): [Distinguished Dramatic Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Award_for_Best_Actress_in_a_Play) – *[Ondine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ondine_\(play\))*

- **Special Awards:**

### Robert Lopez



&#x20;In 2014, [Robert Lopez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Lopez) became the twelfth person, the first [Asian American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Americans) and the youngest (at age 39) to win all four awards.

American songwriter [Robert Lopez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Lopez) (born 1975) received his fourth distinct award in 2014. Between 2004 and 2022, he received a total of 12 awards. He is the first [Filipino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filipino_Americans) and [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Americans) to achieve this feat. He is the youngest winner (39 years, 8 days) to receive all four awards in competitive categories, as well as, at the time, the fastest to complete his qualifying run of EGOT wins (9 years, 8 months). His second series of wins set a new shortest interval of 7 years, 8 months (June 27, 2010 Emmy through March 4th, 2018 Academy Award) until 2024 when both [Benj Pasek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benj_Pasek) and [Justin Paul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Paul) topped this record with a qualifying run of 7 years and 7 months.

Lopez is the first person to win each EGOT award twice.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34] As of 2025, he is one Oscar away from becoming the first triple EGOT winner as well. He is currently the only winner to have two of each EGOT award in competitive categories, as Mel Brooks' second Oscar in 2023 was a special award. His first two Emmys were Daytime Emmys, followed by a Primetime Emmy in 2021 for *[WandaVision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WandaVision)*. He is the only EGOT recipient to follow a Daytime Emmy win with a subsequent Primetime Emmy win.

Lopez received his Grammy Award for *[The Book of Mormon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Book_of_Mormon_\(musical\))* in collaboration with fellow EGOT winner Scott Rudin (among others), making them the first pair of EGOT winners to co-win the same award. Lopez is also the first person to have won the Oscar last, a prize he shared then, and again in 2018, with his wife [Kristen Anderson-Lopez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristen_Anderson-Lopez).[^cite_note-35] As of 2024, Kristen Anderson-Lopez lacks only a Tony to achieve EGOT status in her own right.

## Non-competitive EGOT awardees

Six additional artists have received all four awards, though one was bestowed for an honorary or similar non-competitive distinction: Barbra Streisand does not have a competitive Tony; Liza Minnelli does not have a competitive Grammy; and Harry Belafonte, James Earl Jones, Quincy Jones, and Frank Marshall are all without a competitive Oscar.

### Barbra Streisand



&#x20;[Barbra Streisand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbra_Streisand)'s Tony is a non-competitive award.

American singer, actress, and director [Barbra Streisand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbra_Streisand) (born 1942) received her fourth distinct award in 1970. Between 1963 and 2001, Streisand received a total of 18 awards, three of which were non-competitive. Having obtained her fourth award with a special Tony at age 28, she is the youngest special EGOT winner. With just six years elapsing between her first award, a 1964 Grammy, and her 1970 Tony, Streisand held the record for completing the fastest special EGOT until 2023 when [Frank Marshall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Marshall_\(filmmaker\)) did so within four years.

Streisand is the only EGOT to win an Oscar in both a music and an acting category. She is the only winner to have three competitive awards for debut performances: first studio album, first feature film, and first television special. ### Quincy Jones



[Quincy Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quincy_Jones)'s Oscars are non-competitive awards.

American record producer, musician, songwriter, composer, arranger, and film and television producer [Quincy Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quincy_Jones) (1933–2024) received his fourth distinct award in 2016. Between 1964 and 2024, Jones received a total of 33 awards—the highest number so far of any EGOT winner. He competed for and won 28 Grammys, one Tony, and one Emmy, also receiving a special [Grammy Legend Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Legend_Award) and two special Oscars (the Jean Hersholt Humanitarian Award and the Academy Honorary Award). ### Frank Marshall



[Frank Marshall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Marshall_\(filmmaker\))'s Oscar is a non-competitive award.

American film producer and director [Frank Marshall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Marshall_\(filmmaker\)) (born 1946) received his fourth distinct award in 2023. With just four years elapsing between his first award (a 2019 honorary Oscar), a long format TV sports documentary, and competitive Grammy and Tony music awards, Marshall completed his EGOT collection in the shortest time of all persons to have reached this status.

### REGOT

Another variation is the **REGOT**, which includes being awarded a [Razzie Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Raspberry_Awards).[^cite_note-79][^cite_note-80] [Alan Menken](#Alan_Menken) has a REGOT due to his Razzie win (with [Jack Feldman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Feldman_\(songwriter\))) for [Worst Original Song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Raspberry_Award_for_Worst_Original_Song) for "High Times, Hard Times" from *[Newsies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newsies)*.[^cite_note-81] With her Razzie win for [Worst Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Raspberry_Award_for_Worst_Actress) for *[Rent-a-Cop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rent-a-Cop)* and *[Arthur 2: On the Rocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_2:_On_the_Rocks)*, [Liza Minnelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liza_Minnelli) has a REGOT if her non-competitive [Grammy Legend Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammy_Legend_Award) is considered.[^cite_note-82]

[Lady Gaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Gaga) reordered the acronym as EGORT, when she hosted *[Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live)* and poked fun at her win for [Worst Screen Combo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Raspberry_Award_for_Worst_Screen_Combo) (with [Joaquin Phoenix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joaquin_Phoenix)) from *[Joker: Folie à Deux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joker:_Folie_%C3%A0_Deux)*.[^cite_note-83]

Alternatively, publications such as *[Vulture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vulture_\(website\))* have listed the REGOT as including an induction into the [Rock and Roll Hall of Fame](58902.md).[^cite_note-84] The only person to have such a REGOT is [Elton John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elton_John) although [Harry Belafonte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Belafonte) and [Quincy Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quincy_Jones) would be included if counting non-competitive awards.

## Equivalent honors outside the United States

The Emmy, Grammy, Oscar, and Tony Awards are presided over by industry bodies based in the United States, and as of 2026, 17 out of the 22 EGOT winners were American nationals. The remaining five―[John Gielgud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Gielgud), [Audrey Hepburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audrey_Hepburn), [Elton John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elton_John), [Andrew Lloyd Webber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Lloyd_Webber), and [Tim Rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Rice)―were British. Many countries hold their own equivalent awards ceremonies honouring their own television, music, film, and theatre industries. In some cases, commentators in other countries have derived their own acronyms for individuals who have won at all four ceremonies.

### Canada

In 2018, Leah Collins of [CBC Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBC_Arts) proposed a Canadian equivalent of the EGOT: the [Canadian Screen Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Screen_Awards) (and their predecessors, the [Gemini Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemini_Awards) and the [Genie Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genie_Awards)) for film and television, the [Juno Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juno_Awards) for music, and the [Dora Mavor Moore Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dora_Mavor_Moore_Awards) for theatre.[^cite_note-85] Toronto-based game show Trivia Club referred to this combination as the "Two-Can-Ju-Do".[^cite_note-86] No individual has won in all four categories.

### Australia

In 2019, Caitlin Welsh of [Nova Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nova_Entertainment) proposed the "LAHA" as an Australian equivalent: the [Logie Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logie_Awards) for television, the [ARIA Music Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARIA_Music_Awards) for music, the [Helpmann Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helpmann_Awards) for theatre, and the [AACTA Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AACTA_Awards) for film.[^cite_note-87] She also could not identify any winners of all four awards, although [Noni Hazlehurst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noni_Hazlehurst) has received nominations in all four.
