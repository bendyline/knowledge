The **2026 Moscow Victory Day Parade** was a military parade that was held in [Red Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Square), [Moscow](19004.md), Russia, on 9 May 2026, to commemorate the 81st anniversary of [Victory Day](337670.md), which celebrates the defeat of [Nazi Germany](21212.md) and the [end of World War II in Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/End_of_World_War_II_in_Europe).

The parade was scaled down, lasting only 45 minutes and not featuring a mobile military column for the first time since 2008 due to security concerns from the [Russo-Ukrainian war](70149799.md).

## Parade summary



Russian President [Vladimir Putin](32817.md) speaking at the tribune

After public discussion and exchanges of threats about the safety of the parade, it was held after [US president](24113.md) [Donald Trump](4848272.md) secured the agreement of [Ukraine](31750.md) not to attack the event, contingent on an exchange of 1,000 prisoners of war by each side. [Ukrainian president](20688479.md) [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](59210523.md) signed a mocking decree granting Russia permission to hold the parade.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

The parade was scaled down and curtailed to only 45 minutes, and for the first time since its reintroduction in 2008, did not feature a mobile column of military technologies, due to security fears stemming from the [Russo-Ukrainian war](70149799.md).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-\:0-1]

In between [the anthem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthem_of_Russia) and the beginning of the Parade, a segment dedicated to the branches and arms of the [Russian Armed Forces](25709.md), including the recently founded [Unmanned Systems Forces](81580694.md), and their activities in the war with Ukraine was played.
