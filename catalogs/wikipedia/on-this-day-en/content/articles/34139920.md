**Kesikkavak** is a neighbourhood in the municipality and district of [Haymana](894283.md), [Ankara Province](370068.md), [Turkey](11125639.md).[^cite_note-1] Its population is 51 (2022).[^cite_note-2] [Harmony Jets Flight 185](81917460.md) crashed near the neighbourhood on 23 December 2025.[^cite_note-AJ-3]
