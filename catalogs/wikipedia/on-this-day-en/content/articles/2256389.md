**Fangshan District** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 房山区; [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *Fángshān Qū*) is a district of the city of [Beijing](18603746.md). It is situated in the southwest of Beijing, 38 km (24 mi) away from downtown Beijing. It has an area of 2,019 square kilometres (780 sq mi) and a population of 1,312,778 (2020 Census).[^cite_note-1]

The district administers 8 [subdistricts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdistrict_\(China\)), 14 [towns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Town_\(China\)), and 6 [townships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Townships_of_the_People's_Republic_of_China).

## Geography

The Subdistrict area of Fangshan (population 187,667) contains an urban area, has an area of 25 km<sup>2</sup> (9.7 sq mi) and an estimated population of 200,000. Other major urban areas are [Liangxiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liangxiang,_Beijing) (population estimate 110,000, 93,486 in township), Zhoukoudian (35,000, 39,877 in township), Doudian (30,000, 25,046 in township), and Liulihe (22,000, 37,936 in township).[^cite_note-2]

Fangshan is situated to the east of the [Taihang Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taihang_Mountains), with most of its territory being within Beijing's [Western Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Hills) region. The east and south of the district is a fertile plain, with a narrow hilly area running from northeast to southwest. The district has many rivers and lakes; the [Juma River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juma_River_\(China\)), Dashi River, [Yongding River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yongding_River), and Xiaoqing River run through the district, assuring abundant water resources.

The location, climate and environment provide good conditions for agriculture. The district produces wheat, rice and various high quality fruits as well as large quantities of animal products. At the moment large investments are made for greenhouse production.

### Climate

Fangshan has a [humid continental climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_continental_climate) ([Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *Dwa*). The average annual temperature in Fangshan is 12.5 °C (54.5 °F). The average annual rainfall is 542.6 mm (21.36 in) with July as the wettest month. The temperatures are highest on average in July, at around 26.6 °C (79.9 °F), and lowest in January, at around −3.9 °C (25.0 °F).

## Transportation and telecommunication

[Jingguang railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jingguang_railway) and [Jingyuan railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jingyuan_railway?action=edit\&redlink=1) cross the district. In addition, the district is serviced by 4 branch railways and 24 railway stations. [Jingshi Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jingshi_Expressway) also goes through the district. The four main roads are the Jing-Zhou, Jing-Bao, Jing-Yuan, Jing-Liang roads, with branch roads to [townships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Townships_of_the_People's_Republic_of_China) and [villages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village). The total length of roads is up to 1,800 km (1,100 mi). [Tanggu Port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanggu_Port?action=edit\&redlink=1) in [Tianjin](50821.md) is 180 km (110 mi) distant. The programme-controlled telephone capacity supports 128,000 phone sets.

## Economy

In 2017, the regional GDP of the district is 68.17 billion yuan,[^cite_note-7] with GDP per capita at 59.1 thousand yuan.

### Mineral resources



The rugged karst landscape at [Shidu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shidu,_Beijing).

Fangshan has 2.1 billion tons of [coal reserves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coal#World_coal_reserves), which makes it a veritable “[coal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coal) storehouse” in the west of Beijing; building materials resources are also present in a variety of large deposits. Marble deposits total 450 million cubic meters. A total of 17 kinds have been developed, including "Chinese white marble", "pink", "rosy cloud", and "black jade". In particular, “Chinese white [marble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marble)” is famous worldwide and is regarded as a national treasure. There are also large deposits of [quartzite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quartzite) (29 million tons); [silica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silica) (28.6 million tons), [granite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granite) and [buhrstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buhrstone?action=edit\&redlink=1), in addition to [argil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argil), [mineral water](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mineral_water) and [slab stones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slab_stone?action=edit\&redlink=1).

### Industry and agriculture



[Liao Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liao_Dynasty) (907−1125) furniture excavated from an underground palace in Fangshan District of [Beijing](18603746.md)

Both industry and agriculture are well developed in Fangshan. Over 2300 [enterprises](39206.md) owned by the district, townships or villages have been set up and form a complete industrial system in a variety of trades, with different grades and many kinds of products. [Yanshan Petrochemical Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yanshan_Petrochemical_Group?action=edit\&redlink=1), one of the largest state-owned enterprises in China, is located in Fangshan District. [Agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture) has developed from traditional growing and planting to integrated and full development in [forestry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forestry), [livestock](25160767.md), greenhouse horticulture and [fishery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fishery). The [building industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Building_industry) has become the backbone of the district, with annual building area reaching 6 million square meters.

The foreign-oriented economy of Fangshan has developed rapidly in recent years. By 1999, the District government had approved 345 foreign-invested enterprises. Over 20 countries and territories throughout five continents are represented. There are 100 export-oriented enterprises producing over 40 kinds of product. Liangxiang Satellite Town, approved by Beijing Municipal Government, has become a new hub of development. In 1998, the site of Fangshan People's Government moved to [Liangxiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liangxiang,_Beijing), making it the district's center of politics, economics and culture. The development Zone of Liangxiang extends favorable policies to business. Under the [Ninth Five-Year Plan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ninth_Five-Year_Plan_\(People's_Republic_of_China\)) and Ten-year Outline, the zone will be further opened to the outside and encourage foreign investment in the following industries: electricity, machinery and electronics, instruments, textiles, printing, new building materials, new materials, new energy resources, fine chemicals, food processing, flowers, greenhouse vegetables, tourist facilities, commercial facilities and the animal industry. The zone welcomes overseas Chinese and guests from [Hong Kong](13404.md), [Macau](19068.md), [Taiwan](25734.md) and other countries.

## Monuments and parks



Chinese stone inscription of a [Nestorian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_the_East_in_China) [Christian Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nestorian_Cross) from a monastery of Fangshan District in Beijing (then called Dadu, or [Khanbaliq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khanbaliq)), dated to the [Yuan Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuan_Dynasty) (1271–1368 AD) of medieval China.

Fangshan district has a long history and many ancient sites. Best known is [Zhoukoudian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhoukoudian), location of the “[Peking Man Site](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_Man_Site)”, which has gained Fangshan the name of “Home of the Dragon”. The 3,000-year-old ruins of the Liulihe Site are housed in the [Western Zhou Yan State Capital Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Zhou_Yan_State_Capital_Museum). The famous Buddhist shrine of [Yunju Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yunju_Temple) has precious Buddhist relics, including “Sakyamuni’s Bone” and 14,278 stone slabs with sculpted Buddhist sutras. There are also the remains of the Christian ([Jingjiao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jingjiao)) [Monastery of the Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_Temple,_Fangshan) from the [Yuan dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuan_dynasty). There are many ancient towers, [temples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple), town sites and other sites in Fangshan. Fangshan also has many scenic spots like [Shidu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shidu,_Beijing)’s [karst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karst) landscape, the unique Stoneflower Cave and natural ancient forests in [Shangfang Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shangfang_Mountains?action=edit\&redlink=1). Ecologically, Fangshan has 554 kinds of wild plant and 22 kinds of wild animal.

Fangshan, which is part of [Beijing](18603746.md), and neighboring [Hebei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebei), together contain Fangshan [Geopark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geopark). Certified as a National [Geopark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geopark), and then as a regional geopark ([Asia Pacific Geopark Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_UNESCO_Global_Geoparks_in_Asia)), it was admitted as a [UNESCO Global Geopark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNESCO_Global_Geopark) in 2006.[^cite_note-11]
