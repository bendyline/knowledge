The federal judiciary within the Swiss legal system.



A small [courtroom](1766292.md) in the Supreme Court of the [Canton of Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_Bern).

The **federal judiciary** of Switzerland consists of four federal courts: the [Federal Supreme Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Supreme_Court_of_Switzerland) in [Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lausanne) and [Lucerne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucerne); the [Federal Criminal Court](8140010.md) in [Bellinzona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bellinzona); the [Federal Patent Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Patent_Court_of_Switzerland) and the [Federal Administrative Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Administrative_Court_of_Switzerland) in [St. Gallen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Gallen).[^cite_note-1] These courts are charged with the application of Swiss federal law through the [judicial process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judicial_process).

The Federal Supreme Court in Lausanne is established in the [Swiss Federal Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Federal_Constitution) as the supreme judicial authority of Switzerland. It is the court of appeal for all decisions of the [cantonal](180752.md) courts of last instance, and also for most decisions of the three federal courts of first instance.

The Federal Criminal Court in [Bellinzona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bellinzona) tries the (relatively few) criminal cases subject to federal criminal jurisdiction, such as cases involving [organised crime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organised_crime), [terrorism](30636.md), and crimes against federal institutions. It also decides disputes between cantonal prosecuting authorities. The Federal Administrative Court in [St. Gallen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Gallen) reviews decisions made in application of federal administrative law that have been issued by federal and in some cases by cantonal authorities. The [Federal Patent Court of Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Patent_Court_of_Switzerland) is a specialized court, which started hearing patent cases in 2012, taking jurisdiction from the cantonal courts.
