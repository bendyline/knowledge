**Trump International Golf Club** is a private 27-hole [golf course](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golf_course) in [West Palm Beach, Florida](109634.md). It was designed by Jim Fazio, who was given a budget of over \$40 million, and was opened in 1999.[^cite_note-tigc-1] This venue was [Donald Trump](4848272.md)'s first golf course property.[^cite_note-2]

The Championship course measures 7,326 yards, and has a [par](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Par_\(score\)) 72 layout that has hosted championships for the [LPGA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LPGA).[^cite_note-tigc-1] In addition, the golf course has a nine-hole course. The clubhouse can host weddings and other social or business events. A three-tiered driving range is available.

The regular initiation fee for membership was reported to be \$150,000 in 2011, and the annual fee \$25,000.[^cite_note-3] The senior director of instruction for Trump Golf Properties, [Gary Wiren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Wiren), is based out of this resort.[^cite_note-Briggane-4]

## Assassination attempt

Main article: [Attempted assassination of Donald Trump in Florida](77879114.md)

See also: [Security incidents involving Donald Trump](69199956.md)

On September 15, 2024, an assassination attempt occurred at the Trump International Golf Club in West Palm Beach. The incident occurred before 2 p.m. [EDT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Daylight_Time), while Donald Trump was golfing at the club.[^cite_note-WaPoInfo-5] The perpetrator, later identified as 58-year-old [Ryan Wesley Routh](77880898.md), was spotted hiding in nearby shrubbery while aiming a rifle at a member of Trump's [security detail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security_detail).[^cite_note-DOJ_Detention_Memo-6] A [Secret Service](58221.md) agent fired upon Routh, who fled the scene.[^cite_note-7] The club's golf course was locked down shortly after,[^cite_note-8] and no injuries were reported.[^cite_note-\:0-9] Routh was detained while driving northbound on [Interstate 95](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_95).[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-\:1-11][^cite_note-12]

Routh was indicted on charges of attempting to assassinate a presidential candidate and, in September 2025, was convicted on all counts. In February 2026, he was sentenced to life in prison.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-CNN-14][^cite_note-FBI-15]
