**Favipiravir**, sold under the brand name **Avigan** among others,[^cite_note-3] is an [antiviral medication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiviral_medication) used to treat [influenza](19572217.md) in Japan.[^cite_note-Du2020-4] It is also being studied to treat a number of other viral infections, including [SARS-CoV-2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SARS-CoV-2).[^cite_note-Du2020-4] Like the experimental antiviral drugs T-1105 and T-1106, it is a [pyrazinecarboxamide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyrazinecarboxamide) derivative.[^cite_note-5]

It is being developed and manufactured by [Toyama Chemical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyama_Chemical) (a subsidiary of [Fujifilm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fujifilm)) and was approved for medical use in Japan in 2014.[^cite_note-Shi2020-6] In 2016, Fujifilm licensed it to Zhejiang Hisun Pharmaceutical Co.[^cite_note-7] It became a [generic drug](293885.md) in 2019.

## Medical use

Favipiravir has been approved to treat [influenza](19572217.md) in Japan.[^cite_note-Shi2020-6] It is, however, only indicated for novel influenza (strains that cause more severe disease) rather than [seasonal influenza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seasonal_influenza).[^cite_note-Shi2020-6][^cite_note-8] As of 2020, the probability of resistance developing appears low.[^cite_note-Shi2020-6]

## Side effects

There is evidence that use during pregnancy may result in [harm to the baby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teratogen).[^cite_note-Shi2020-6] Teratogenic and embryotoxic effects were shown on four animal species.[^cite_note-Shi2020-6][^cite_note-9] In one case report, a 6-month old infant developed benign bright blue discolouration of the cornea after treatment with favipiravir which resolved after treatment cessation.[^cite_note-10]

## Mechanism of action



Favipiravir ribofuranosyl triphosphate, the active form inside the body

The mechanism of its actions is thought to be related to the selective inhibition of viral [RNA-dependent RNA polymerase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RNA-dependent_RNA_polymerase).[^cite_note-bp-11] Favipiravir is a [prodrug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prodrug) that is metabolized to its active form, favipiravir-ribofuranosyl-5'-triphosphate (favipiravir-RTP), available in both oral and intravenous formulations.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] In 2014, favipiravir was approved in Japan for stockpiling against [influenza pandemics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Influenza_pandemic).[^cite_note-14] However, favipiravir has not been shown to be effective in primary human airway cells, casting doubt on its efficacy in influenza treatment.[^cite_note-15]

Favipiravir-RTP is a [nucleoside analogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nucleoside_analogue). It mimics both guanosine and adenosine [nucleobases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nucleobase) for [viral RNA dependant RNA polymerase (RdRp)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RNA-dependent_RNA_polymerase). Incorporating two favipiravir ribofuranosyl bases in a row stops extension of the newly made RNA, although it is unclear how as of 2013.[^cite_note-bp-11]

## Society and culture

### Legal status

The US Department of Defense developed favipiravir in partnership with MediVector, Inc. as a broad-spectrum antiviral and sponsored it through FDA Phase II and Phase III clinical trials, where it demonstrated safety in humans and efficacy against the influenza virus.[^cite_note-19] Favipiravir remains unapproved in the UK and the USA.[^cite_note-20] In 2014, Japan approved favipiravir for treating influenza strains unresponsive to current antivirals.[^cite_note-21] Toyama Chemical initially hoped that favipiravir would become a new influenza medication that could replace [oseltamivir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oseltamivir) (brand name Tamiflu). However, animal experiments show the potential for [teratogenic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teratology) effects, and the approval of production by The Ministry of Health, Labor and Welfare was greatly delayed and the production condition is limited only in an emergency in Japan.[^cite_note-22]

### Brand names



Favipiravir 800 mg tablets from India

Favipiravir is sold under the brand names Avigan (アビガン, *Abigan*), Avifavir,[^cite_note-Avifavir-1] Avipiravir,[^cite_note-23] Areplivir,[^cite_note-Areplivir-2] FabiFlu,[^cite_note-FabiFlu-24] Favipira,[^cite_note-Favipira-25] Reeqonus,[^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27] and Qifenda.

### Use in Russia

Coronavir is the brand name of favipiravir used in Russia, where it is approved for the treatment of [COVID-19](63030231.md). It is produced and sold by [R-Pharm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R-Pharm).[^cite_note-28] Coronavir was approved for use in Russia in hospitals in July 2020, and in September 2020 it received approval for prescription sales for outpatient use. Avifavir, another drug also based on favipiravir, was earlier approved in May.[^cite_note-Reuters-29]

## Research

### COVID-19

See also: [Coronavirus disease 2019 § Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coronavirus_disease_2019#Research), and [COVID-19 drug repurposing research § Favipiravir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_drug_repurposing_research#Favipiravir)

Favipiravir, as an antiviral drug, has been authorized for treating COVID-19 in several countries including Japan, Russia, Serbia, Turkey, India, and Thailand, under emergency provisions.[^cite_note-30][^cite_note-Reuters-29][^cite_note-31][^cite_note-32] A rapid meta-review in September 2020 (analyzing four studies) noted that the drug led to clinical and radiological improvements; however, no reduction in mortality or differences in oxygen-support requirement were observed and more rigorous studies were sought.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34] A Cochrane Systematic review published in Feb 2024, noted that there is actually no real benefit with Favipiravir in treating Covid-19 in terms of mortality benefits, or admission to mechanical ventillation, or hospitalisation, and it may not make any difference in adverse effects or serious adverse effects.[^cite_note-35]

### Ebola

Research in 2014, suggested that favipiravir may have efficacy against [Ebola](40817590.md) based on studies in [mouse models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mouse_model); efficacy in humans was unaddressed.[^cite_note-Gatherer_2014-36][^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38]

During the [2014 West Africa Ebola virus outbreak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_West_Africa_Ebola_virus_outbreak), a French nurse who contracted Ebola while volunteering for [Médecins Sans Frontières](20498.md) (MSF) in Liberia reportedly recovered after receiving a course of favipiravir.[^cite_note-39] A clinical trial investigating the use of favipiravir against Ebola virus disease began in [Guéckédou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gu%C3%A9ck%C3%A9dou), Guinea, in December 2014.[^cite_note-40] Preliminary results presented in 2016 at the [Conference on Retroviruses and Opportunistic Infections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conference_on_Retroviruses_and_Opportunistic_Infections) (CROI), later published, showed a decrease in mortality in patients with low-to-moderate levels of virus in blood, but no effect on patients with high levels (the group at a higher risk of death).[^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42][^cite_note-43] The trial design was concomitantly criticised for using only historical controls.[^cite_note-44]

### Nipah

[Nipah virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nipah_virus_\(virus\)) is a causative agent of outbreaks of encephalitis with pneumonia and has a high [case fatality rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Case_fatality_rate). The first outbreak occurred in Malaysia-Singapore, related to contact with pigs in slaughterhouses and an outbreak in Philippines related to slaughter of horses, most other outbreaks have affected India and Bangladesh. in Bangladesh outbreaks are often associated with consumption of raw date palm sap contaminated by saliva and urine of fruit bats.[^cite_note-45] In a study published in the Scientific Reports, Syrian hamster model for Nipah virus infection was used, which closely mirrors most aspects of human disease, such as widespread vasculitis, pneumonia, and encephalitis. The hamsters were infected with a lethal dose of 10<sup>4</sup> PFU NiV-M via the intraperitoneal (i.p.) route similar to previous studies and treatment was initiated immediately after infection. Favipiravir was administered twice daily via the peroral (p.o.) route for 14 days. The treated hamsters displayed 100% survival and no obvious morbidity after lethal NiV challenge, whereas all the control cases died of severe disease.[^cite_note-46]

### Hantavirus

Favipiravir shows efficacy against [hantavirus](416945.md) in animal models and has seen some limited trials in humans.[^cite_note-47][^cite_note-48][^cite_note-49][^cite_note-50][^cite_note-51][^cite_note-52]

### Other

In experiments in animals favipiravir has shown activity against [West Nile virus](50642063.md), [yellow fever virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_fever_virus), [foot-and-mouth disease virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foot-and-mouth_disease_virus) as well as other [flaviviruses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flavivirus), [arenaviruses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arenavirus), [bunyaviruses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bunyavirus) and [alphaviruses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphavirus).[^cite_note-Furuta-53] Activity against [enteroviruses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enterovirus)[^cite_note-Furuta2013-54] and [Rift Valley fever](26378.md) virus has also been demonstrated.[^cite_note-55] Favipiravir has showed limited efficacy against [Zika virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zika_virus) in animal studies, but was less effective than other antivirals such as [MK-608](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MK-608).[^cite_note-56] The agent has also shown some efficacy in preventing encephalitis from [rabies](16946852.md),[^cite_note-57] and has been used experimentally in some humans infected with the virus.[^cite_note-58]

### Tautomerism

The possible [tautomerism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tautomerism) of favipiravir has been investigated computationally[^cite_note-59] and experimentally.[^cite_note-60] It was found that the enol-like form was substantially more stable in organic solvents than the keto-like form, meaning that Favipiravir likely exists almost exclusively in the enol-like form. In aqueous solution the keto-like tautomer is substantially stabilized due to the specific interaction with the water molecules. Upon protonation the keto form is switched on.
