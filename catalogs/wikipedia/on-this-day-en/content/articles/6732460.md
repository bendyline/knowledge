[Formula One](10854.md) (F1) is the highest class of [open-wheeled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_wheel_car) [motor racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motor_racing) defined by the [Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile](248019.md) (FIA), motorsport's world governing body.[^cite_note-1] The [F1 World Championship season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Formula_One_seasons) consists of a series of races, known as [Grands Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Formula_One_Grands_Prix), held usually on purpose-built [circuits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Formula_One_circuits), and in a few cases on [closed city streets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Street_circuit).[^cite_note-2] Each winner is presented with a trophy and the results of each race are combined to determine two annual Championships, one for [drivers](3437295.md) and one for [constructors](3437393.md).[^cite_note-3] The World Championship for Drivers has been contended since [1950](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1950_Formula_One_season),[^cite_note-history-4] after the Formula One standard was agreed upon in 1946.[^cite_note-5] The Constructors' Championship was added for the [1958 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1958_Formula_One_season) and has been awarded ever since.[^cite_note-history-4]



[Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) has won a record 106 Grands Prix during his career: 21 with [McLaren](20994.md), 84 with [Mercedes](25098794.md), and 1 with [Ferrari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuderia_Ferrari).

[Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) holds the record for the most race wins in Formula One history, with 106 wins to date. [Michael Schumacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Schumacher), the previous record holder, is second with 91 wins, and [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) is third with 71 victories.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] Hamilton also holds the distinction of having the longest time between his first win and his last. He won his first Grand Prix at the [2007 Canadian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Canadian_Grand_Prix), and his last at the [2026 Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Barcelona-Catalunya_Grand_Prix), a span of 19 years and 4 days.[^cite_note-8] [Riccardo Patrese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riccardo_Patrese) holds the record for the longest period of time between two race wins – more than six-and-a-half years between the [1983 South African Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1983_South_African_Grand_Prix) and the [1990 San Marino Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_San_Marino_Grand_Prix).[^cite_note-9] [Mario Andretti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mario_Andretti) had to wait the longest time between his maiden victory at the [1971 South African Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1971_South_African_Grand_Prix) and his second win – coming five years, seven months and 18 days later at the [1976 Japanese Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1976_Japanese_Grand_Prix).[^cite_note-10] Verstappen holds the record for the most consecutive wins, having won ten Grands Prix in a row from the [2023 Miami Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Miami_Grand_Prix) to the [2023 Italian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Italian_Grand_Prix).[^cite_note-11] Verstappen is also the youngest winner of a Grand Prix; he was 18 years and 228 days old when he won the [2016 Spanish Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Spanish_Grand_Prix).[^cite_note-12] [Luigi Fagioli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luigi_Fagioli) is the oldest winner of a Formula One Grand Prix; he was 53 years and 22 days old when he won the [1951 French Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1951_French_Grand_Prix).[^cite_note-13]

As of the [2026 Azerbaijan Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Azerbaijan_Grand_Prix), out of the 782 drivers who started a Grand Prix,[^cite_note-14] there have been 116 Formula One Grand Prix winners.[^cite_note-\:0-15] The first Grand Prix winner was [Giuseppe Farina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_Farina) at the [1950 British Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1950_British_Grand_Prix), and the most recent driver to score their first Grand Prix win is [Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md) who took his first win at the [2026 Chinese Grand Prix](80284337.md).[^cite_note-\:0-15] Three Grands Prix, the [1951 French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1951_French_Grand_Prix), the [1956 Argentine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1956_Argentine_Grand_Prix) and the [1957 British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1957_British_Grand_Prix) Grands Prix, were won by two drivers sharing a car, so both drivers in each event were credited with a Grand Prix victory.[^cite_note-16] In addition to event-specific trophies, [gold medals](526488.md) have been awarded to all victors from the [2022 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Abu_Dhabi_Grand_Prix) onwards.[^cite_note-17]

This list includes the winners of the 11 [Indianapolis 500](159018.md) races between [1950](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1950_Indianapolis_500) and [1960](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1960_Indianapolis_500), as they were part of the World Championships in early years, even though they were not run by Formula One regulations, nor are they referred to as Grands Prix.[^cite_note-18]

## By driver



[Michael Schumacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Schumacher) held the record until [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Portuguese_Grand_Prix), with 91 victories.



[Max Verstappen](41758713.md) has won 71 Grands Prix, and is the youngest winner in Formula One history.



[Sebastian Vettel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Vettel) took 53 victories across 16 seasons.



[Alain Prost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alain_Prost) held the record until [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Belgian_Grand_Prix), with 51 victories.



[Kimi Antonelli](70530918.md) is the 116th and latest first-time Grand Prix winner, taking his maiden victory at the [2026 Chinese Grand Prix](80284337.md).

All figures are correct as of the *[2026 Azerbaijan Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Azerbaijan_Grand_Prix)*.
