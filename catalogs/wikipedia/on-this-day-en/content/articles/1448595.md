The **Province of Rieti** ([Italian](14708.md): *Provincia di Rieti*) is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Italy) in the region of [Lazio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lazio) in [Italy](14532.md). Its capital is the city of [Rieti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rieti). It was established in 1927 from the [Province of Perugia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Perugia).

It has a population of 149,335 in an area of 2,750.52 square kilometres (1,061.98 mi<sup>2</sup>) across its 73 municipalities.[^cite_note-population-2][^cite_note-area-1]

## Geography

A large part of its territory corresponds to the historical-geographical region of [Sabina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabina_\(region\)). Rieti is located in the north-east Lazio. It is bordered to the west, along the river [Tiber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiber) by the [province of Viterbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Viterbo) and to the south-west by the [Metropolitan City of Rome Capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_City_of_Rome_Capital). It is also bordered by the [regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Italy) of [Umbria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umbria) to the north and by [Marche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marche) to the east.

The province is largely mountainous with the [Monti della Duchessa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monti_della_Duchessa?action=edit\&redlink=1) and the [Monti del Cicolano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monti_del_Cicolano?action=edit\&redlink=1) ranges in the south with [Monte Nuria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monte_Nuria?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Monte Giano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monte_Giano), the [Monti Reatini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monti_Reatini) range with [Monte Terminillo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monte_Terminillo) (2217 m) in the north in part of the Abruzzese [Apennines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apennine_Mountains),[^cite_note-Fields2012-4] and [Monti della Laga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monti_della_Laga) (2458 m) to the east on the border with Abruzzo. Of particular importance are the two artificial lakes in the [Valle del Salto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valle_del_Salto?action=edit\&redlink=1): [Lago del Salto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lago_del_Salto) and [Lago del Turano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lago_del_Turano), both created during the Fascist period. Lago del Salto is the largest in the Lazio region and is situated at an altitude of 1,755 metres (5,758 ft).[^cite_note-Italia.it-5]

There are several protected areas in the province. To the south lies the Parco regionale naturale dei Monti Lucretili,[^cite_note-WorboysFrancis2010-6] to the southeast the Riserva regionale Montagne della Duchessa, and a small part of the Parco regionale naturale del Sirente - Velino. Between the two areas is the Riserva naturale Monte Navegna e Monte Cervia (between the lakes of Salto and Turano. In the east, in the Monti della Laga range is the Parco Nazionale del Gran Sasso e Monti della Laga. Also of note is the Riserva parziale naturale dei Laghi Lungo e Ripasottile on the [Rieti Plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rieti_Valley), and the Riserva naturale di Nazzano, Tevere-Farfa to the west. Riserva parziale naturale dei Laghi Lungo e Ripasottile contains the lakes of [Lungo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lungo) and [Ripasottile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ripasottile?action=edit\&redlink=1), which support a diversity of wildlife, particularly birds such as [herons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heron) and [little grebes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_grebe).[^cite_note-Italia.it-5] There are numerous natural oases, castles, fortresses and Franciscan sanctuaries dotted about the province of Rieti.[^cite_note-Italia.it-5]

## Demographics

As of 2026, the population is 149,335, of which 50.1% are male, and 49.9% are female. Minors make up 12.7% of the population, and seniors make up 28.0%.[^cite_note-population-2]

### Immigration

As of 2025, immigrants make up 12.0% of the population. The 5 largest foreign countries of birth are [Romania](25445.md), [Albania](738.md), [Ukraine](31750.md), [Argentina](18951905.md), and [Pakistan](23235.md).[^cite_note-foreignborn-9]
