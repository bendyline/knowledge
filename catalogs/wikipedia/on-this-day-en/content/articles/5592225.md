**Harkat-ul-Jihad-al-Islami** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): حركة الجهاد الإسلامي, [romanised](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Ḥarkat al-Jihād al-Islāmiyah*, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'Islamic Jihad Movement"', **HuJI**) is a Pakistani [Islamic fundamentalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_fundamentalism) organisation affiliated with [al-Qaeda](1921.md) and the [Taliban](30635.md).[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-4]

It has been most active in the South Asian countries of [Pakistan](23235.md), [Bangladesh](3454.md) and [India](14533.md) since the early 1990s. The organisation has been designated as a terrorist group by India, [Israel](9282173.md), [New Zealand](4913064.md), [United Kingdom](31717.md), [United States](3434750.md), and Bangladesh when its [Bangladesh branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harkat-ul-Jihad_al-Islami_Bangladesh) was banned in 2005.

The operational commander of HuJI, [Ilyas Kashmiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilyas_Kashmiri_\(militant\)), was killed in a U.S. [drone strike](38212231.md) in South Waziristan on 4 June 2011.[^cite_note-5] He was linked to the [13 February 2010 bombing of a German bakery in Pune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Pune_bombing). A statement was released soon after the attack that claimed to be from Kashmiri; it threatened other cities and major sporting events in India.[^cite_note-6] A local Taliban commander, Shah Sahib, was named as Kashmiri's successor.[^cite_note-7]

## History

HuJI or HJI was formed in 1984, during the [Soviet–Afghan War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet%E2%80%93Afghan_War), by [Fazlur Rehman Khalil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fazlur_Rehman_Khalil) and [Qari Saifullah Akhtar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qari_Saifullah_Akhtar). Khalil later broke away to form his own group, [Harkat-ul-Ansar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harkat-ul-Ansar) (HuA), which became a highly feared militant organisation in Kashmir.[^cite_note-8] This group re-formed as [Harkat-ul-Mujahideen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harkat-ul-Mujahideen) (HuM) when HuA was blacklisted by the [United States](3434750.md) in 1997.[^cite_note-9]

HuJI first mainly operated in Afghanistan to fight the Soviets, but after the Soviets retreated, the organisation also started operating in [Jammu and Kashmir](61449812.md). HuJI's influence expanded into [Bangladesh](3454.md) when the [Bangladeshi branch of the organisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harkat-ul-Jihad-al-Islami_Bangladesh) was established in 1992, with direct assistance from [Osama bin Laden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osama_bin_Laden).[^cite_note-Asia_Times_article-10]

## Ideology

The organisation along with other jihadist terrorist groups such as [Harkat-ul-Mujahideen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harkat-ul-Mujahideen), [Jaish-e-Mohammed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaish-e-Mohammed), [Al-Qaeda](1921.md) & [Lashkar-e-Taiba](649718.md) have similar motivations and goals. Harkat-ul-Jihad al-Islami and Harkat-ul-Mujahideen were both strongly backed by the [Taliban](30635.md) and Al-Qaeda and the group professed Taliban-style [fundamentalist Islamist ideology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_fundamentalism). The organisation aims to spread [radical Islamist ideology](15012.md), to take over [Kashmir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashmir), [Afghanistan](737.md), [Palestine](241405.md), and the rest of Muslim-majority lands from what it calls "enemies of [Islam](6037917.md)" and enforce its extremist interpretations of [Sharia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharia_law) in all those regions.[^cite_note-\:0-3]

## Activities in Bangladesh

In the 1990s, HuJI gave [recruitment training](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recruitment) near the hilly areas of [Chittagong](380451.md) and [Cox's Bazar](2271204.md).[^cite_note-Asia_Times_article-10][^cite_note-11] In 1999, members of the organisation attacked Bangladeshi poet [Shamsur Rahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shamsur_Rahman_\(poet\)).[^cite_note-12] HuJI claimed responsibility for the [2001 Ramna Batamul bombings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Ramna_Batamul_bombings), where 10 people were killed.[^cite_note-13] It was also the prime suspect in a plot to assassinate former Bangladeshi prime minister [Sheikh Hasina](623439.md) in 2000.[^cite_note-14] In 2005, it was officially banned by the [government of Bangladesh](18420484.md).[^cite_note-15] HuJI has been condemned by various [Islamist](15012.md) groups such as the [Hefazat-e-Islam Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hefazat-e-Islam_Bangladesh).[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17]

In 2008, the founders formed Islamic Democratic Party (IDP) and Abdus Salam was chosen as the leader.[^cite_note-18]

## Activities in India

The [government of India](553883.md) has declared HuJI a terrorist organisation and banned it.[^cite_note-MHA_BanList-19] In 2006, the Indian state police [Special Task Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Task_Force_\(India\)) uncovered a plot by six HuJI members, including the mastermind of the [2006 Varanasi bombings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Varanasi_bombings), involving the destruction of two [Hindu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu) temples in [Varanasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varanasi). Maps of their plans were recovered during their arrest. The organisation has claimed responsibility for blasts at the [Delhi High Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi_High_Court) that killed 10 people and injured around 60.[^cite_note-20] [Vikar Ahmed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vikar_Ahmed), a member of an [Islamist](15012.md) group connected to HuJI, has been accused of murdering police officers in Hyderabad. He is also a suspect in the [Mecca Masjid bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mecca_Masjid_bombing).[^cite_note-21]

HuJI claimed responsibility for the [2011 Delhi bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Delhi_bombing), but this has not been confirmed by the [National Investigation Agency](20712530.md).[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23] 14 people were killed and 94 injured in the blast. Police have released two sketches of the suspects.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26] HuJI has threatened to target other Indian cities.

On 6 August 2010, the [United Nations](31769.md) designated Harakat-ul Jihad al-Islami a foreign terror group and blacklisted its commander, [Ilyas Kashmiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilyas_Kashmiri_\(militant\)). State Department counterterrorism coordinator [Daniel Benjamin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Benjamin) said this demonstrated the global community's resolve to counter the group's threat. "The linkages between HUJI and Al-Qaeda are clear, and today's designations convey the operational relationship between these organisations", he said.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-USState-34]
