**Vanessa Pulgarín Monsalve** (born September 13, 1991 in [Medellín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medell%C3%ADn), [Antioquia](185251.md), Colombia) is a Colombian model and beauty pageant titleholder who won Miss International Colombia 2017, and represented Colombia at the [Miss International 2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_International_2017) in Japan.[^cite_note-1] She later won [Miss Universe Colombia 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_Colombia_2025) and represented her country at [Miss Universe 2025](78060439.md) in Thailand where she placed in the Top 12. In 2026, Pulgarín competed in and won the inaugural edition of [MGI All Stars](81808767.md).[^cite_note-2]

## Personal life

Vanessa Pulgarín was born in the city of [Medellín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medell%C3%ADn), [Antioquia](185251.md), Colombia on September 13, 1991. She is the daughter of Carlos Pulgarín and Dalila Monsalve Jiménez. Currently, she studies Social Communication and Journalism at the [Pontifical Bolivarian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontifical_Bolivarian_University).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] Pulgarín lived for several years in [Sydney](27862.md), [New South Wales](21654.md), Australia where as a model, she participated in catwalks and photoshoots.[^cite_note-5]

## Pageantry

### Señorita Colombia

Main article: [Miss Colombia 2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Colombia_2017)

On July 30, 2016, Pulgarín entered and won Miss Antioquia, and later represented her department at Miss Colombia 2017, where she was runner-up to [Laura González](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Gonz%C3%A1lez_\(Miss_Colombia\)) of [Cartagena](63927.md).[^cite_note-6]

### Miss International

Main article: [Miss International 2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_International_2017)

Pulgarín represented Colombia at Miss International 2017, held at the [Tokyo Dome City Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Dome_City_Hall) in [Tokyo](30057.md), Japan, on November 14, 2017, and was unplaced.[^cite_note-7]

### Miss Universe

Main articles: [Miss Universe Colombia 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_Colombia_2025) and [Miss Universe 2025](78060439.md)

In 2025, Pulgarín represented Antioquia and won [Miss Universe Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Universe_Colombia) 2025 on September 28, 2025. She was crowned by [Daniela Toloza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniela_Toloza) from [Valle del Cauca](549733.md).[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] She represented Colombia at Miss Universe 2025 in [Pak Kret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pak_Kret), Thailand, and reached the top 12.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]
