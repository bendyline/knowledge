**Ranga Reddy district** is a district in the [Indian state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_state) of [Telangana](990267.md). The district headquarters is located at [Kongara Kalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kongara_Kalan) Village, [Ibrahimpatnam Mandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahimpatnam,_Ranga_Reddy_district).[^cite_note-clc-3][^cite_note-district-4] The district was named after the former deputy chief minister of the [United Andhra Pradesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Andhra_Pradesh), [K. V. Ranga Reddy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K._V._Ranga_Reddy).[^cite_note-new-5] The district shares boundaries with [Nalgonda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nalgonda_district), [Yadadri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yadadri_Bhuvanagiri), [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad_district,_India), [Medchal–Malkajgiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medchal%E2%80%93Malkajgiri), [Nagarkurnool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagarkurnool_district), [Mahabubnagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahabubnagar_district), [Sangareddy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangareddy_district) and [Vikarabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vikarabad_district) districts.

### Formation of district

The district was formed in 1978 when it was split from [Hyderabad district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad_District,_India).[^cite_note-Statoids-7][^cite_note-8] Originally named Hyderabad Rural district, it was renamed after Konda Venkata Ranga Reddy, a freedom fighter who fought for the independence of Telangana from the Nizams, and who went on to become the deputy chief minister of Andhra Pradesh.[^cite_note-9] In 2016, it was carved out during the district's reorganisation to create the new [Vikarabad district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vikarabad_district) and [Medchal–Malkajgiri district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medchal%E2%80%93Malkajgiri_district).[^cite_note-new-5]

## Geography

Ranga Reddy District occupies an area of approximately 5,031 square kilometres (1,942 sq mi).[^cite_note-Reference_Annual-10]

A freshwater reservoir, called [Osman Sagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osman_Sagar), [Himayath Sagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Himayath_Sagar), on the river [Musi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musi_River_\(India\)) at [Gandipet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gandipet), is the prime drinking water source for the capital city of Hyderabad/Secunderabad.

According to the [2011 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_census_of_India), Ranga Reddy District has a population of 2,446,265, of which 1,254,184 were males and 1,192,081 were females.[^cite_note-12] The [Sex Ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_ratio) (Females per 1000 Males) was 950. The rural population in the district was 1,026,113 (41.95%) while the urban population was 1420152 (58.05%). The literacy rate was calculated to be 71.95%, higher than the state's [literacy rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literacy_in_India) of 66.54%. 300,511 (12.28%) were under 6 years of age.[^cite_note-13] Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes made up 337,023 (13.78%) and 138,710 (5.67%) of the population respectively.[^cite_note-14]

## Economy

- Medium-scale industries and [Cement Corporation Of India (CCI)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cement_Corporation_Of_India_\(CCI\)?action=edit\&redlink=1)'s cement factories are established at [Tandur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tandur).

- Another major company is Hyderabad Chemicals and Fertilizers, established at [Moula-Ali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moula-Ali) in 1942.

In 2006 the Indian government named Ranga Reddy one of the country's 250 [most backward districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_in_India) (out of a total of [640](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_India)).[^cite_note-brgf-16] It is one of the 33 districts in Telangana currently receiving funds from the Backward Regions Grant Fund Programme (BRGF).[^cite_note-brgf-16]

As of 2025, Ranga Reddy district has the highest per-capita GDP in India.[^cite_note-17]

## Administrative divisions

The district will have five [revenue divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_revenue_divisions_in_Telangana): Chevella, Ibrahimpatnam, Rajendranagar, Kandukur and Shadnagar. They are sub-divided into 27 [mandals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandal).[^cite_note-district-4] Amoy Kumar IAS is the present collector of the district.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-\:0-19]

### Municipalities/municipal corporations

There are 14 Municipalities and 3 municipal corporations in Ranga Reddy district. They are [Adibatla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adibatla), [Amangal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amangal), [Badangpet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badangpet), [Bandlaguda Jagir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandlaguda_Jagir), [Chevella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevella), [Ibrahimpatnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahimpatnam,_Ranga_Reddy_district), [Jalpally](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalpally), [Manikonda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manikonda), [Meerpet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meerpet), [Moinabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moinabad,_Rangareddy), [Narsingi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narsingi,_Ranga_Reddy), [Pedda Amberpet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedda_Amberpet), [Shadnagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shadnagar), [Shamshabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shamshabad,_Hyderabad), [Shankarpally](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shankarpally), [Thukkuguda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thukkuguda), [Thurkayamjal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thurkayamjal).
