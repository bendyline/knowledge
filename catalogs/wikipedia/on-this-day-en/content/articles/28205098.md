**Hopefield** is a settlement in [West Coast District Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Coast_District_Municipality) in the [Western Cape](313646.md) province of [South Africa](17416221.md) on the R45[^cite_note-2] between Malmesbury and Vredenburg. The town lies east of [Saldanha Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saldanha_Bay) and Langebaan, 40 km (25 mi) southeast of [Vredenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vredenburg) and 120 km (75 mi) north of [Cape Town](6653.md).

## History

Hopefield is the oldest town on the Cape West Coast.[^cite_note-wcw-3]

The [Dutch Reformed congregation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hopefield_Reformed_Church) (Zoute Rivier) was established in December 1851 after farmers donated money towards its construction[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-wcw-3] and the town was founded in 1852 on the farm Langekuil. It became a municipality in 1914, and was named after two people who laid it out, Major William Hope, Auditor-General, and a Mr Field.[^cite_note-dsapn-5]

## General

The [Air Force Base Langebaanweg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Force_Base_Langebaanweg),[^cite_note-SAAF-6] located 22 km (14 mi) west of the town, as well as the West Coast Fossil park 25 km (16 mi) west from town,[^cite_note-7] fall within town limits. Another important fossil locality, Elandsfontein (not to be confused with the town and railway station in [Gauteng](309661.md)), is found about 13 km (8.1 mi) southwest of Hopefield.[^cite_note-8]

In earlier years Hopefield was considered the capital of the West Coast, with banks, filling stations and multiple other shops and businesses. The only access to the towns of [Vredenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vredenburg), [Langebaan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langebaan) and [Saldanha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saldanha,_Western_Cape) was through Hopefield. With the re-routing of the [R45 road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R45_\(South_Africa\)) away from the town, and the construction of the R27 (West Coast Road), the importance of the town gradually faded.

## Schools



Hopefield High

There are two schools in the town, Hopefield Primary, offering Grade 1-9, and Hopefield High,[^cite_note-9] offering Grade R-12.
