The **British Consulate General in Jerusalem** is the British diplomatic representation in [Jerusalem](16043.md), the [West Bank](33209.md) and the [Gaza Strip](12047.md). The consulate is located in the [Sheikh Jarrah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikh_Jarrah) neighborhood of [East Jerusalem](343508.md).[^cite_note-gov-1]

## History

The British Consulate in Jerusalem was established in 1838, when [William Tanner Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Tanner_Young) was appointed as Britain’s first vice-consul in Jerusalem.[^cite_note-2] He arrived and began serving there in 1839, which is why some historical sources give 1839 as the founding date.[^cite_note-3] The National Library of Israel, for example, dates the British Consulate’s operation from 1839 to November 1914 when Britain declared war on the [Ottoman Empire](22278.md).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] After the First World War and the [British Mandate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandate_for_Palestine) period, the Consulate-General was re-established in 1948, with [Sir Hugh Dow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir_Hugh_Dow) serving as consul from 1948 to 1951.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

The British government's position is that per the 1967 [United Nations Security Council Resolution 242](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Security_Council_Resolution_242) and subsequent resolutions, East Jerusalem, where the consulate is located, is considered under [Israeli occupation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_occupation).[^cite_note-gov-1]

In February 2021, the British Consul-General in Jerusalem, [Philip Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Hall_\(diplomat\)), condemned [Israeli settlements](15123.md) in the [West Bank](33209.md) as "illegal and an obstacle to restarting peace talks".[^cite_note-8]

On 21 September 2025, the United Kingdom officially recognised the [State of Palestine](241405.md).[^cite_note-9]

On 8 September 2026, Israel ordered the closure of the consulate after the [UK imposed sanctions on products from Israeli settlements in the West Bank](20968669.md#Sanctions_on_Israeli_settlements_in_the_West_Bank), which are regarded as illegal.[^cite_note-10] Israel announced that the consulate must close within 30 days and that diplomats assigned ‌there will lose their accreditation after the deadline expires.[^cite_note-11]

However, a legal opinion from [Dominic Grieve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominic_Grieve) KC, who was [Attorney General for England and Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attorney_General_for_England_and_Wales) from 2010 to 2014, for the British Palestine Project points out that the British Consulate Generals in Jerusalem do not present commissions to Israel, so there is no accreditation from Israel to be withdrawn, and that the order is "an unlawful assertion of sovereignty over occupied Palestinian territory". Grieve also pointed out the UK government had formally accepted the [July 2024 International Court of Justice advisory opinion](72240449.md) that governments should consider Israel's presence in occupied Palestinian territory as unlawful, which implied the UK government had a duty to oppose the order to close.[^cite_note-guardian-20260928-12][^cite_note-mee-20260928-13] On 28 September, foreign secretary [Ed Miliband](1290018.md) said the government was "looking at all our options" regarding the consulate, which he said had operated for nearly 200 years.[^cite_note-lbc-20261028-14]
