**Tayr Felsay** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): طير فلسيه) is a municipality in [Tyre District](11166788.md) in Southern [Lebanon](17771.md), located just south of the [Litani river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Litani_river) and 14 kilometres northeast of [Tyre](30796.md).

## Etymology

According to [E. H. Palmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Henry_Palmer), the name means the fortress of the scales or small coins.[^cite_note-1]

## History

In 1881, the [PEF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Exploration_Fund)'s [*Survey of Western Palestine* (SWP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PEF_Survey_of_Palestine) described it: "A village built of stone; about 250 [Metawileh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metawileh); on side of hill near the top, with figs and arable land around. There are two springs, and [cisterns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cistern)."[^cite_note-2]

## Demographics

In 2014 [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 99.61% of registered voters in Tayr Felsay. 98.62% of the voters were [Shiite Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims).[^cite_note-3]
