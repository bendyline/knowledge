# Bahrain–Iraq relations

[Historic and current bilateral relationship](429154.md) exist between [Bahrain](18933277.md) and [Iraq](7515928.md). In recent years relations between the two have been mixed, with the Bahraini government criticizing what it sees as Iraq's interference in its internal affairs, especially since the [2011 uprising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Bahraini_uprising).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] Both countries are part of the [Organization for Islamic Cooperation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organization_for_Islamic_Cooperation).

In recent years, both countries have moved to establish a stronger relationship in fear of growing Iranian interference.

The current ambassador of the Kingdom of Bahrain in Iraq is [Salah Al Maliki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salah_Al_Maliki?action=edit\&redlink=1),[^cite_note-3] while the current ambassador of the Republic of Iraq in Bahrain is [Ahmed Nayef Rasheed Al-Dulaimi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmed_Nayef_Rasheed_Al-Dulaimi?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-iraqi-4] Bahrain maintains an embassy in [Baghdad](4492.md) and a consulate general in [Najaf](200512.md), while Iraq is represented through its embassy in [Manama](20481.md).[^cite_note-iraqi-4]
