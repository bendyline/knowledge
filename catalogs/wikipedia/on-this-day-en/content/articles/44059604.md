# General Political Bureau of the Korean People's Army

| 조선인민군 총정치국 |  |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Bureau of the [Ministry of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defence_\(North_Korea\)) |
| Director | [Colonel General](12534441.md) [Jong Kyong-thaek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jong_Kyong-thaek) |
| Parent organization | [Ministry of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defence_\(North_Korea\)) |

The **General Political Bureau** (**GPB**) is a department of the [Ministry of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defence_\(North_Korea\)), used by the leadership of the [North Korean government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Korean_government) to exert political control over the [Korean People's Army](21263.md) (KPA). The GPB operates under the direction of the [Central Military Commission of the Workers' Party of Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Military_Commission_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEMinnich2008242–244-1] The GPB controls units of the KPA on all levels down to company level. It primarily exerts control through propaganda, education, and cultural activities. Under directions of the [State Affairs Commission of North Korea](50991731.md), it also controls troop movements.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEMinnich2008244-2]

As of January 2023, the Director of the General Political Bureau is [Jong Kyong-thaek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jong_Kyong-thaek), who succeeded [Kwon Yong-jin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwon_Yong-jin).

## History

During [Kim Jong Il](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Jong_Il)'s *[Songun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songun)* (military first) era in particular, the GPB remained relatively independent and unchanged for decades. In the 3rd WPK Representatives Conference held in 2010, GPB was granted equal status to the [Central Committee of the WPK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Committee_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea).[^cite_note-\:6-3] During this time, it has been called as the internal [politburo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politburo) of the KPA.

However, the influence of the GPB has decreased under [Kim Jong Un](5962511.md)'s rule; after the [7th Congress of the WPK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7th_Congress_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea) held in May 2016, the party had regained enough power to exert influence over the GPB. Consequentially, in 2017 the party [Central Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Committee_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea) was ordered to carry out an inspection of the GPB, the first of its kind in 20 years. The inspection was carried out by [Choe Ryong-hae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Choe_Ryong-hae).[^cite_note-daily-4] The GPB director [Hwang Pyong-so](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hwang_Pyong-so) was punished, and removed from his position as Standing Commissioner.[^cite_note-\:6-3] The [8th WPK Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8th_Congress_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea) held in 2021 also oversaw the demotion of the GPB, no longer being equal to the Central Committee. Some of GPB's functions were also transferred to the newly established Military-Political Leadership Department of the WPK CC.[^cite_note-\:6-3] In May 2025, at a meeting of the Central Military Commission, GPB Director [Jong Kyong-thaek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jong_Kyong-thaek) was demoted to Colonel General, the lowest rank of an incumbent GPB director since the 1970s.[^cite_note-5]
