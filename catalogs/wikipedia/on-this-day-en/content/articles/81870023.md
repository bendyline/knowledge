A [State Opening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Opening_of_Parliament) of the [Parliament of the United Kingdom](13964.md) took place on 13 May 2026,[^cite_note-1] when the monarch opened the second [session](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_session) of the [Parliament elected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_MPs_elected_in_the_2024_United_Kingdom_general_election) at the [2024 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_Kingdom_general_election) to start the new session.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] The chosen date was in the week after the [2026 United Kingdom local elections](79139854.md).[^cite_note-4] The speech was made in the [House of Lords](13658.md) at just after 11.30am.[^cite_note-5]

It was the first State Opening of Parliament for [Ed Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Davis_\(Royal_Marines_officer\)) as [Black Rod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Rod).[^cite_note-6]

## Background

On 26 March 2026, the date for the speech was set for 13 May.[^cite_note-7] On 8 May 2026, following the [local elections](79139854.md), it was confirmed that there would be a State Opening of Parliament the following week. The prime minister's leadership has been reportedly damaged following the elections and the speech intends to refocus the government's agenda.[^cite_note-8] *[BBC News](1139893.md)* speculated that new laws on [EU alignment](9317.md) and [tourist tax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourist_tax) could be included in the speech.[^cite_note-9] The speech was held at a point of [leadership crisis](83157483.md).[^cite_note-10]

The King's Speech outlined the government's legislative programme for the 2026–27 parliamentary session, with 37 bills announced. Major proposals included legislation to strengthen economic growth, tackle threats from hostile state actors, reform energy security, and lower the voting age to 16.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

## Preparation



The King and Queen in the [Irish State Coach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_State_Coach) process in state through [Horse Guards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horse_Guards_\(building\)) into [Whitehall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitehall).

In the morning of 13 May, [Nic Dakin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nic_Dakin), [Vice-Chamberlain of the Household](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice-Chamberlain_of_the_Household), was ["taken hostage"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Opening_of_Parliament#Delivery_of_parliamentary_hostage), as is tradition.[^cite_note-13] [King Charles III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Charles_III) wore his Admiral of the Fleet Royal Naval Number 1 Dress with cap and sword,[^cite_note-14] while [Queen Camilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Camilla) wore the [State Diadem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_Diadem) worn by every queen consort since [Queen Adelaide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adelaide_of_Saxe-Meiningen).[^cite_note-15]

The King and Queen were accompanied by a Sovereign's Escort of the [Household Cavalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Household_Cavalry) as they travelled in the [Irish State Coach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_State_Coach) from [Buckingham Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckingham_Palace) to the House of Lords. A separate coach carried the [royal regalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regalia) – the [Imperial State Crown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_State_Crown), the cap of maintenance and sword of state – to Westminster.[^cite_note-16]

## Legislative programme

The King's Speech included 37 pieces of legislation that Labour hoped to pass during the next parliamentary session.[^cite_note-17] Policy areas include [digital ID](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_identity) and [northern rail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Powerhouse_Rail).[^cite_note-18]
