**Buzău County** (Romanian pronunciation: [\[buˈzəw\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Romanian) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/5/58/Ro-Buz%C4%83u.ogg/Ro-Buz%C4%83u.ogg.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/5/58/Ro-Buz%C4%83u.ogg/Ro-Buz%C4%83u.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>) is a county ([județ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jude%C8%9B)) of [Romania](25445.md), in the [historical region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historical_regions_of_Romania) [Muntenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muntenia), with the capital city at [Buzău](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buz%C4%83u).

## Geography

This county has a total area of 6,103 km<sup>2</sup>.

In the North Side there are the mountains from the southern end of the [Eastern Carpathians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Carpathians) group – the [Vrancea Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vrancea_Mountains) and the [Buzău Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buz%C4%83u_Mountains) with heights over 1,700 m. The heights decrease in the South and East passing through the subcarpathian hills to the [Bărăgan Plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C4%83r%C4%83gan_Plain) at about 80 m.

The main river crossing the county is the [Buzău River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buz%C4%83u_River) which collects many small rivers from the mountains and flows to the East into the [Siret River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siret_River).

## Economy

The predominant industries in the county are:

- Mechanical components; railway and automotive components

- Metallurgical parts

- Glass

- Food

- Textiles

- Wood

The hilly area is well-suited for wines and fruit orchards. Salt and oil are the main resources extracted in the county.

Historically, the county was located in the central-southern part of [Greater Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Romania), in the northeastern part of the historical region of [Muntenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muntenia). Its territory included the southern and western parts of today's Buzău County and several localities that are today in [Prahova County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prahova_County), including the town of [Mizil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mizil). It was bordered on the west by [Prahova County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prahova_County#Historical_county), to the north by the counties of [Brașov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bra%C8%99ov_County#Historical_county), [Trei Scaune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trei_Scaune_County), and [Putna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Putna_County), to the east by the counties of [Râmnicu Sărat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%A2mnicu_S%C4%83rat_County) and [Brăila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Br%C4%83ila_County#Historical_county), and to the south by [Ialomița County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ialomi%C8%9Ba_County#Historical_county).

Map of Buzău County as constituted in 1938.

The county has seen multiple subdivisions administratively.

At the end of the 19th century, the county was subdivided into six districts (*[plăși](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plas%C4%83)*):[^cite_note-MDGR-5]

1. Plasa Sărata, with the town of [Buzău](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buz%C4%83u) and 16 communes

2. Plasa Câmpului, with 14 communes

3. Plasa Tohani, with the town of [Mizil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mizil) and 17 communes

4. Plasa Buzău, with 17 communes

5. Plasa Pârscov, with 16 communes

6. Plasa Slănic, with 19 communes

In the interwar period, the territory of the county was initially divided into four districts:[^cite_note-Memoria-6]

1. Plasa Buzău, headquartered at [Pătârlagele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P%C4%83t%C3%A2rlagele)

2. Plasa Câmpul, headquartered at [Buzău](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buz%C4%83u)

3. Plasa Slănic, headquartered at [Săpoca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C4%83poca,_Buz%C4%83u)

4. Plasa Tohani, headquartered at [Mizil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mizil)

Subsequently, the territory of the county was reorganized into seven districts, by abolishing Plasa Câmpul and establishing four new districts:

1. Plasa Buzău, headquartered at [Pătârlagele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P%C4%83t%C3%A2rlagele)

2. Plasa Câlnău, headquartered at [Buzău](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buz%C4%83u)

3. Plasa Câmpeni, headquartered at [Pogoanele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pogoanele)

4. Plasa Orașul, headquartered at Buzău

5. Plasa Pârscov, headquartered at [Pârscov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P%C3%A2rscov,_Buz%C4%83u)

6. Plasa Slănic, headquartered at [Săpoca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C4%83poca,_Buz%C4%83u)

7. Plasa Tohani, headquartered at [Mizil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mizil)

### Population

According to the 1930 census data, the county population was 309,405 inhabitants, ethnically divided as follows: 97.4% Romanians, 1.5% Romanies, 0.5% Jews, as well as other minorities. From the religious point of view, the population was 98.9% Eastern Orthodox, 0.5% Jewish, as well as other minorities.

#### Urban population

In 1930, the county's urban population was 42,127 inhabitants, comprising 91.2% Romanians, 3.7% Jews, 2.3% Romanies, 0.9% Hungarians, as well as other minorities. From the religious point of view, the urban population was composed of 93.3% Eastern Orthodox, 3.9% Jewish, 1.0% Roman Catholic, as well as other minorities.
