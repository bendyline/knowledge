# Reza Amiri-Moghadam

**Reza Amiri-Moghadam** is an Iranian diplomat. He is the Ambassador of the Islamic Republic of Iran to Pakistan.[^cite_note-1] He is the former head of the operations unit of the Iranian Ministry of Intelligence and Security.[^cite_note-2]
