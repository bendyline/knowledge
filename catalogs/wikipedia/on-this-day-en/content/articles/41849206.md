**Emirates News Agency** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): وكالة أنباء الإمارات, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Wakalat Anba'a al Emarat*), also known as **WAM** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): وام), is the official [news agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/News_agency) of the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

## History and profile

The WAM was established in November 1976.[^cite_note-wam-3][^cite_note-4] It started an Arabic service on 18 June 1977, and an English service was established by [Peter Hellyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Hellyer) in December 1978.[^cite_note-wam-3]

It is headquartered in [Abu Dhabi](18950756.md) and and, since 2025, has operated under the National Media Authority (NMA), a federal body established by Federal Decree-Law No. 11 of 2025 that consolidated the UAE Media Council, the National Media Office and the Emirates News Agency.[^cite_note-nma-ab-5][^cite_note-nma-xinhua-6][^cite_note-abed-7]

The agency operates through a network of foreign correspondents around the world.[^cite_note-corr-nma-8] It is a member of the Gulf Cooperation Council news agencies, the [Federation of Arab News Agencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federation_of_Arab_News_Agencies) (FANA),[^cite_note-9] the [International Islamic News Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Islamic_News_Agency), the [Non-Aligned News Agencies Pool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-Aligned_News_Agencies_Pool)[^cite_note-abed-7] and of the [Organization of Asia-Pacific News Agencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organization_of_Asia-Pacific_News_Agencies) (OANA).[^cite_note-10]

The agency has cooperation and news exchange agreements with various news agencies, including the [Sudan News Agency](20867197.md), [Bernama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernama) (Malaysia), [Xinhua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xinhua_News_Agency) (China), the [Kuwait News Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuwait_News_Agency) (KUNA), [Petra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petra_\(agency\)) (Jordan), the Indonesian News Agency, and the [Saba News Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saba_News_Agency) (Yemen).[^cite_note-11] Since 2012, the agency has also been in cooperation with [Anadolu Agency](43356543.md) (Turkey).[^cite_note-12] WAM launched a [Hebrew language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language) service in April 2021.[^cite_note-13]

## Services and products

WAM provides multimedia news services spanning text, photographs, video, infographics and documentaries, alongside comprehensive coverage of the UAE's political, economic and development activities, and distributes its output in several languages to audiences across different continents.[^cite_note-una-wam-15]
