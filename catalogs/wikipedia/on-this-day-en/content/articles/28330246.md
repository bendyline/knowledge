# Time in the United Arab Emirates



UTC+04\:00 time zone (blue)

**United Arab Emirates Standard Time** or **UAE Standard Time** is the time zone for the [UAE](69328.md). It is given by [Gulf Standard Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_Standard_Time), being 4 hours ahead of [GMT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GMT)/[UTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC) ([UTC+04\:00](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC+04\:00)) and is co-linear with neighbouring [Oman](22316.md). The UAE does not change clocks for [daylight saving time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daylight_saving_time).[^cite_note-1]

## IANA time zone database

The [IANA time zone database](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IANA_time_zone_database) contains one zone for the UAE in the file [zone.tab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zone.tab), named "Asia/Dubai".
