The fourth **Constitution of [Senegal](27278.md)** (French: *Constitution de la République du Sénégal*) was adopted in a [2001 referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senegalese_constitutional_referendum,_2001). It was amended by constitutional law № 2016-10 after a [2016 referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Senegalese_constitutional_referendum).[^cite_note-1]

## History

Senegal has previously had three other constitutions: in 1959, 1960, and in [1963](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratization_in_Senegal).[^cite_note-2]
