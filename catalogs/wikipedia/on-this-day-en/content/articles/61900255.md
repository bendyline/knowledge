**Pak Thae-song** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 박태성, born 14 September 1955) is a North Korean politician who has served as the [premier of North Korea](2012485.md) and [vice president of the State Affairs Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_President_of_the_State_Affairs_Commission) since December 2024. He previously served as the [chairman of the Supreme People's Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairman_of_the_Supreme_People's_Assembly) from January 2021 to January 2023.

## Biography

Pak Thae-song was born on 14 September 1955.[^cite_note-1]

After serving as a member of the Central Committee of the Workers' Party of Korea, he became a candidate member of the party's politburo. In August 2012, he became [vice chairman of the Central Committee of the Workers' Party of Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairman_and_Vice_Chairman_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea).[^cite_note-2] In May 2014, he became the responsible secretary of the party committee in [South Pyongan Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Pyongan_Province). In May 2016, after serving as a member of the Central Committee of the Workers' Party of Korea, he became a candidate member of the Party's Politburo.[^cite_note-unikorea-3]

Pak became the [Chairman of the Supreme People's Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairman_of_the_Supreme_People's_Assembly) in April 2019.[^cite_note-unikorea-3] He became a member of the Party Politburo and a secretary of the Party Secretariat in January 2021.[^cite_note-unikorea-3] In January 2023, it was confirmed that he resigned as the chairman of the Supreme People's Assembly.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-1stSession14thSPA-5][^cite_note-6] He became the Chairman of the National Emergency Space Science and Technology Committee in March 2023.[^cite_note-unikorea-3]

On 29 December 2024, Pak was appointed [Premier of North Korea](2012485.md) at the conclusion of the annual year-end general meeting of the [Central Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Committee_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea) of the Workers' Party of Korea thereby succeeding [Kim Tok-hun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Tok-hun).[^cite_note-7] On 22 March 2026, he was re-elected as premier and vice president of the State Affairs Commission.[^cite_note-8]

In July 2026, Pak visited China to mark the 65th anniversary of the [Treaty on Friendship, Cooperation and Mutual Assistance between China and North Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_on_Friendship,_Cooperation_and_Mutual_Assistance_between_China_and_North_Korea). During the visit, he met with Chinese leader [Xi Jinping](2017814.md).[^cite_note-9]
