**Polavaram district**[^cite_note-1] is a district in the Indian state of [Andhra Pradesh](2377.md). The headquarters of the district is located at [Rampachodavaram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rampachodavaram).[^cite_note-2]

## Etymology

This district is named after [Polavaram Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polavaram_Project) which is an under-construction multi-purpose project on the [Godavari River](47988.md).

## History

On December 31, 2025, Rampachodavaram division was carved out of [Alluri Sitharama Raju district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alluri_Sitharama_Raju_district). Earlier the district region was part of [East Godavari district](674886.md).[^cite_note-3]

## Administrative divisions

The district is divided into two revenue divisions, namely [Rampachodavaram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rampachodavaram_revenue_division) and [Chinturu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinturu_revenue_division).[^cite_note-4] These revenue divisions are further subdivided into a total of 12 mandals, each administered by a Tahsildar.

Polavaram district has a population of 349,913. The district has a sex ratio of 1051 females per 1000 males. The only town in the district is [Rampachodavaram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rampachodavaram), which has a population of 9,952. Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes made up 19,634 (5.61%) and 240,729 (68.80%) of the population respectively.[^cite_note-demographics-6][^cite_note-demographics1-7]<sup>: 85–90 </sup>

Languages of Polavaram district (2011)

[^cite_note-language-8]

1. [Telugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telugu_language) (76.69%)

2. [Koya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koya_language) (20.70%)

3. [Odia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odia_language) (1.13%)

4. Others (1.48%)

At the time of the 2011 census, 76.69% of the population spoke [Telugu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telugu_language), 20.70% [Koya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koya_language) and 1.13% [Odia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odia_language) as their first language.[^cite_note-language-8]
