**Haymana** is a municipality and [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Turkey) of [Ankara Province](370068.md), [Turkey](11125639.md).[^cite_note-2] Its area is 2,164 km<sup>2</sup>,[^cite_note-3] and its population is 26,016 (2022).[^cite_note-tuik-1] It is 72 km south of the capital, [Ankara](802.md). Its elevation is 1200 m.

## Etymology

According to the 1074-dated dictionary [Dīwān ul-Lughat al-Turk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C4%ABw%C4%81n_ul-Lughat_al-Turk), haymana means [prairie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prairie) in [Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_language).[^cite_note-4]

## History

Archaeological excavations were launched in *Gavur Kalesi*, a castle situated in the village of Dereköy, in the years 1930 and 1998. The results showed that it was an important [Phrygian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phrygians) settlement. Besides, the tumuli located in Türkhöyük and Oyaca villages prove that the area was also inhabited during the [Hittite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hittites) period.[^cite_note-mun-5]

After the periods of the [Roman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Empire) and [Byzantine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byzantine_Empire) rule, the area was captured by the [Seljuk Turks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seljuk_Turks) in 1127. After the [Battle of Köse Dağ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_K%C3%B6se_Da%C4%9F) it came under [Ilkhanate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilkhanate) control for a while. In the mid 14th century the area was annexed by the [Ottomans](22278.md). After a brief period of [Timurid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timurid_dynasty) rule in the wake of the [Battle of Ankara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Ankara) in 1402, it was re-captured by the Ottomans.[^cite_note-mun-5]
