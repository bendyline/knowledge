The **Best Male Athlete ESPY Award**, known alternatively as the **Outstanding Male Athlete ESPY Award**, is an annual award honoring the achievements of individual men from the world of sports. It has been presented annually at the [ESPY Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPY_Award) (Excellence in Sports Performance Yearly Award) since 1993 to the male voted irrespective of nationality or sport contested, adjudged to be the best athlete in a given [calendar year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calendar_year).[^cite_note-ESPY-1] The Best Male Athlete ESPY Award trophy, designed by sculptor [Lawrence Nowlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Nowlan),[^cite_note-bmta-2] is presented to the recipient at an annual ceremony in Los Angeles.[^cite_note-ESPY-1] Since 2004, the winner has been chosen by online balloting through three to five choices selected by the ESPN Select Nominating Committee.[^cite_note-3] Before that, determination of the winners was made by an panel of experts.[^cite_note-4] Through the [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_in_sports) iteration of the ESPY Awards, ceremonies were conducted in February of each year to honor achievements over the previous calendar year; awards presented thereafter are conferred in July and reflect performance from the June previous.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-ESPNDateChange2002-5]

The inaugural winner of the Best Male Athlete ESPY Award was [basketball](3921.md) player [Michael Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Jordan) in 1993.[^cite_note-celeb-7] Four American athletes, [golfer](19568112.md) [Tiger Woods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiger_Woods), [road cyclist](1755460.md) [Lance Armstrong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lance_Armstrong), basketball player [LeBron James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LeBron_James), and [American football](18951490.md) player [Patrick Mahomes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Mahomes) have won the award multiple times. Woods was honored five times: in 1998 (jointly with baseball player [Ken Griffey Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Griffey_Jr.)), 2000, 2001, 2002 and 2008. Armstrong was honored four times from 2003 to 2006 inclusive. James received the award on three occasions in 2012, 2013 and 2016. Mahomes received the award twice in 2023 and 2024. Basketball is the most successful sport, its players having received a total of nine awards and thirty-three nominations since its inception, followed by American football players with six wins and twenty-seven nominations. The award has been won by a non-American five times – in 2011 by German basketball player [Dirk Nowitzki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirk_Nowitzki),[^cite_note-\:0-8] in 2018 by Russian hockey player [Alex Ovechkin](755749.md), in 2019 by Greek basketball player [Giannis Antetokounmpo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giannis_Antetokounmpo), in 2022 by Japanese baseball player [Shohei Ohtani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shohei_Ohtani), and in 2025 by Canadian basketball player [Gilgeous-Alexander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilgeous-Alexander). All five were playing for American teams. It was not awarded in [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_ESPY_Awards) due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](63136490.md).[^cite_note-GMA2020-9]
