The **Royal Cambodian Armed Forces** (**RCAF**; [Khmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_language): កងយោធពលខេមរភូមិន្ទ, [romanised](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Khmer): *Kang Youtheapol Khemaraphumin*) is [Cambodia](334751.md)'s national military force. The Supreme Commander-in-Chief is [King](1083018.md) [Norodom Sihamoni](1046517.md). Since 2018, General [Vong Pisen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vong_Pisen) has been the Commander-in-Chief of the RCAF as head of the [Army](13916116.md), [Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Cambodian_Navy), [Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Cambodian_Air_Force) and the [Gendarmerie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Gendarmerie_of_Cambodia).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] The armed forces operate under the jurisdiction of the [Ministry of National Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_National_Defence_\(Cambodia\)). Under the country's constitution, the RCAF is charged with protecting the sovereignty and territorial integrity of the Kingdom of Cambodia.[^cite_note-5]

It was created in 1993 by a merger of the [Cambodian People's Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambodian_People's_Armed_Forces) (CPAF) and the [National Army of Democratic Kampuchea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Army_of_Democratic_Kampuchea) (NADK) which included the [Khmer Rouge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_Rouge), the [Khmer People's National Liberation Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_People's_National_Liberation_Front) (KPNLF) and the [National United Front for an Independent, Neutral, Peaceful and Cooperative Cambodia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FUNCINPEC) (*Front uni national pour un Cambodge indépendant, neutre, pacifique, et coopératif*, FUNCINPEC). The forerunner of the CPAF was the [Kampuchean People's Revolutionary Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kampuchean_People's_Revolutionary_Armed_Forces) (KPRAF), established in 1979 with support from the [People's Army of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Army_of_Vietnam) (PAVN).[^cite_note-6]

### Royal Cambodian Armed Forces (RCAF)

The **RCAF** was established in 1993 after the democratic election of a government consisting of two prime ministers. The armed forces of all parties except the **NADK** were integrated into a national armed force.

To resolve security problems, the government began a "Win-Win policy" in mid-1995 of national reconciliation and unity efforts under the [Monarchy of Cambodia](1083018.md). Defections of **NADK** units began in early 1996 up until the death of Pol Pot in 1998.

## Branches

The RCAF has four branches: the [Royal Cambodian Army](13916116.md), the [Royal Cambodian Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Cambodian_Navy), the [Royal Cambodian Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Cambodian_Air_Force) and the [Royal Gendarmerie of Cambodia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Gendarmerie_of_Cambodia).

The Royal Cambodian Army (RCA) is the largest RCAF branch, with troops stationed in each of the country's 25 [provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Cambodia). The Royal Cambodian Navy (RCN), the second-largest branch and operates in the [Gulf of Thailand](198107.md), along the [Mekong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mekong) and [Bassac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bassac_River) rivers and in the [Tonlé Sap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tonl%C3%A9_Sap) lake. The Royal Cambodian Air Force (RCAF), the smallest service branch with 5,000 members, operates in every province which has an airport.[^cite_note-ReferenceA-7] The Royal Gendarmerie is the national [gendarmerie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gendarmerie) force of the Kingdom of Cambodia. It is a branch of the RCAF and it is responsible for the maintenance of public order and internal security. It has a strength of 30,000 members deployed in all provinces.
