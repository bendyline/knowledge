The **House of Assembly** is the [lower house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_house) of the [Parliament of Saint Lucia](4503828.md), elected by popular vote. The [upper house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_house) is the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_Saint_Lucia). It has 17 or 18 members: 17 members elected for a five-year term in single-seat [constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constituency), and a speaker elected by the 17 members, who may be one of their number or a person chosen from outside the House.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] To be elected to the house, a person must be at least 21 years old.

## History

House of Assembly was established in 1967 when associated statehood was attained. It replaced the [legislative council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_council).[^cite_note-3]

## Relationship with the government

Section 60 of the Constitution of Saint Lucia requires the [prime minister of Saint Lucia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister_of_Saint_Lucia) to be a member of the House of Assembly. It provides for the [governor-general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor-general_of_Saint_Lucia) to "appoint a member of the House who appears to him likely to command the support of the majority of the members of the House" and for the office to fall vacant if the prime minister ceases to be a member of the House of Assembly for any reason other than the dissolution of parliament.[^cite_note-4]
