**Mohammad Bagher Zolghadr**[^cite_note-2] (born 1954) is an Iranian politician and former military officer who serves as a political advisor to the [Supreme Leader of Iran](36814344.md). He previously served as [secretary of the Supreme National Security Council](82647047.md) from March to August 2026 and as secretary of the [Expediency Discernment Council](452730.md) from 2021 to 2026.

Before the [1979 Islamic revolution](347268.md), Zolghadr was active in carrying out attacks against the monarchy, and in 1978 he killed an American engineer and an oil company manager.[^cite_note-\:0-3] He served as the commander of the IRGC [Irregular Warfare Headquarters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irregular_Warfare_Headquarters), as well as the Ramazan Headquarters, which formed the basis for the [Quds Force](4028355.md), the IRGC’s foreign branch that conducts [asymmetric warfare](46863.md) outside of Iran. He was later a Brigadier General in the IRGC, with whom he served from 1979 to 2010. He was the Chief of the [Joint Staff of the IRGC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Revolutionary_Guard_Corps_Joint_Staff) from 1989 to 1997, and Second-in-Command of the IRGC from 1997 to 2006.

After he helped [Mahmoud Ahmadinejad](7907317.md) come to power with a "multilayered plan," from 2005 to 2007 he was [Deputy Interior Minister for Security Affairs and Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Interior_\(Iran\)) under Ahmadinejad.[^cite_note-Agence_France_Presse-1][^cite_note-pasd-4][^cite_note-\:1-5] From 2012 to 2021 he was the deputy for strategic, societal–security and crime prevention affairs in the [judicial system of Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judicial_system_of_Iran).[^cite_note-AA-6] In March 2026, he was appointed Secretary of the [Supreme National Security Council](6295948.md), succeeding [Ali Larijani](531848.md), who was assassinated by an Israeli airstrike during the war.[^cite_note-Reuters-7][^cite_note-Mehr_News_Agency-8]

He is known for his hard-line views, and said on 24 March 2026 that the IRGC should gain more control over the country.[^cite_note-\:1-5] *[The Economist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Economist)* described him as an "IRGC [apparatchik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apparatchik)".[^cite_note-9]

## Early life and education

Mohammad Bagher Zolghadr was born in 1954 in [Fasa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fasa), near [Shiraz](39622.md), [Iran](14653.md). He obtained a bachelor’s degree in [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics) from the Faculty of Economics at the [University of Tehran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tehran), prior to Iran's [1979 Islamic revolution](347268.md).[^cite_note-\:0-3]

## Military career

Before the 1979 Islamic revolution, he was a member of the *Mansourun* guerrilla group, an [Islamist](15012.md) militant organization active at the time. In 1978 he, along with [Mohsen Rezaei](1862765.md), killed an American engineer and an oil company manager.[^cite_note-\:0-3] He is reportedly one of the founders of [Ansar-e Hezbollah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ansar-e_Hezbollah), a [conservative](46181393.md) [paramilitary](146730.md) organization in Iran.[^cite_note-pasd-4]

During the [Iran–Iraq War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93Iraq_War), he co-headed the [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps](1285472.md)' educational division for military training, as well as guerrilla operations.[^cite_note-Agence_France_Presse-1][^cite_note-10] He later served as the commander of the IRGC [Irregular Warfare Headquarters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irregular_Warfare_Headquarters), as well as the Ramazan Headquarters, which formed the basis for the [Quds Force](4028355.md), the IRGC’s foreign branch that conducts [asymmetric warfare](46863.md) outside of Iran.[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-AA-6]

He was the Chief of the [Joint Staff of the IRGC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Revolutionary_Guard_Corps_Joint_Staff) from 1989 to 1997.[^cite_note-\:0-3] Zolghadr served as the deputy commander of the IRGC from 1997 to 2005.[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-pasd-4] In 2007, Zolghadr was appointed the deputy chief of the [General Staff of the Iranian Armed Forces](50548383.md) for [Basij](2892082.md)-related affairs.[^cite_note-11]

## Political career

Following the [Iranian Revolution](347268.md), Zolghadr joined the [Mojahedin of the Islamic Revolution Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mojahedin_of_the_Islamic_Revolution_Organization) and belonged to its right-wing faction,[^cite_note-12] having previously been a member of *Mansouroun* guerrilla organization along with later fellow IRGC servicemen [Rezaei](1862765.md), [Shamkhani](262775.md) and [Rashid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gholam_Ali_Rashid).[^cite_note-13]

After he helped [Mahmoud Ahmadinejad](7907317.md) come to power with a "multilayered plan," from 2005 to 2007 he was [Deputy Interior Minister for Security Affairs and Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Interior_\(Iran\)) under Ahmadinejad.[^cite_note-Agence_France_Presse-1][^cite_note-pasd-4][^cite_note-\:1-5] In April 2007, he warned the U.S. that if it were to attack Iran, Iran would fire “tens of thousands of missiles at American targets every day."[^cite_note-\:1-5]

Beginning in 2021, Zolghadr led Iran’s [Expediency Discernment Council](452730.md). The council advises Iran’s Supreme Leader.[^cite_note-\:1-5]

In March 2026, he was appointed Secretary of the [Supreme National Security Council](6295948.md), succeeding [Ali Larijani](531848.md), who had been killed.[^cite_note-Reuters-7][^cite_note-Mehr_News_Agency-8] In August 2026, he resigned from his post and was replaced by [Mohsen Rezaee](1862765.md), an advisor for Supreme Leader [Mojtaba Khamenei](23324096.md).[^cite_note-14]

## Judicial career

In 2010, Zolghadr began serving as an advisor to the Chief Justice [Sadiq Larijani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sadiq_Larijani), the brother of Ali Larijani.[^cite_note-\:1-5] He was appointed the deputy Judiciary chief for strategic affairs on 14 May 2012.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]

## Personal life

Zolghadr is married to Sedigheh Begum Hejazi, who has served as Director-General of the Office of Women and Family Affairs at the [Organization of Islamic Culture and Communication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Culture_and_Communication_Organization). His son-in-law, [Kazem Gharibabadi](78777113.md), is an Iranian diplomat who has represented Iran in Vienna at international organizations, including the [International Atomic Energy Agency](14984.md).[^cite_note-\:0-3]

## Published works

Zolghadr authored *Qesse-ye Ghorbat-e Gharbi* (Persian: قصه‌ی غربت غربی; English: *The Tale of Western Estrangement*), published in 1381 (2002–2003) in Iran by Dowrye Aliye Jang. The work, written in Persian, is a short volume (38 pages) that engages with themes related to Western society and culture, framed through a critical and ideological lens.[^cite_note-17]
