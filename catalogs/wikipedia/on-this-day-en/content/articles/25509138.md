The **Allegiant Travel Company** is an American [airline](1942.md) holding and hospitality company headquartered in [Las Vegas](47737.md), Nevada. The company owns [Allegiant Air](1372018.md) and [Sun Country Airlines](1241139.md).

## History

The Allegiant Travel Company was founded in 1999[^cite_note-Screener-2] as the parent company of Allegiant Air,[^cite_note-LVSun2009-3] which itself had been founded in 1997.[^cite_note-NYT2006-4] Initially based out of [Fresno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fresno), [California](5407.md), the company reorganized in 2000 with [Maurice J. Gallagher Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurice_J._Gallagher_Jr.) gaining an almost 20 percent stake in the company. He had previously been a prominent creditor of Allegiant and was one of the co-founders of [ValuJet Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ValuJet_Airlines).[^cite_note-Salt2013-5]

In May 2006, Allegiant Travel Company filed plans for an [initial public offering](63879.md) (IPO).[^cite_note-WeeklyMay2006-6] It officially began trading on the Nasdaq exchange under the stock ticker symbol, ALGT, in December of that year.[^cite_note-Reuters-7]

By 2024, Allegiant Travel's primary subsidiary, Allegiant Air, had switched from a fleet predominately composed of MD-80s to one exclusively composed of [Airbus](26220236.md) and [Boeing](18933266.md) jets.[^cite_note-ReviewTriples-8]

In January 2026, Allegiant Travel Company announced an agreement to acquire [Sun Country Airlines](1241139.md) in a cash-and-stock transaction valued at about \$1.5 billion.[^cite_note-9] The [United States Department of Transportation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Transportation) approved the merger on April 15, 2026,[^cite_note-10] and shareholders of both airlines approved the transaction on May 8, 2026.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] Following the merger, Allegiant shareholders would own approximately 67 percent of the combined company, while Sun Country shareholders would own the remaining 33 percent.[^cite_note-Velotta_2026-13] The transaction closed on May 13, 2026.[^cite_note-14] Allegiant and Sun Country will continue to operate separately while working toward a single operating certificate.[^cite_note-Velotta_2026-13]

## Subsidiaries

### Allegiant Air

Main article: [Allegiant Air](1372018.md)

Allegiant Air was founded in 1997[^cite_note-NYT2006-4] and is the [ninth-largest commercial airline in the United States](11898233.md) as of January 2020.[^cite_note-Dayton-22] Part of Allegiant Air's business model includes earning commissions by selling passengers ancillary items like rental cars, hotel rooms, tickets to events, amusement park passes, and other add-ons.[^cite_note-Salt2013-5] The airline has a fleet composed of 126 Airbus and Boeing jets[^cite_note-Skift2019-23] that serves more than 500 routes across the country.[^cite_note-Dayton-22]

### Sun Country Airlines

Main article: [Sun Country Airlines](1241139.md)

Sun Country Airlines has operated since 1983. Originally founded as a charter airline, Sun Country began scheduled passenger service out of [Minneapolis–Saint Paul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minneapolis%E2%80%93Saint_Paul_International_Airport) in 1995. After going public for the first time in 2021 under the leadership of CEO Jude Bricker, the airline expanded to a fleet of more than 65 aircraft, serving scheduled passenger routes primarily from its Minneapolis hub while also operating cargo flights for [Amazon](56149507.md). In early 2026, Allegiant Travel Company and Sun Country reached an agreement for Allegiant to acquire the airline for \$1.5 billion. The transaction closed on May 13, 2026, bringing Sun Country under the Allegiant Travel Company umbrella.[^cite_note-24]

### Other

The Allegiant Travel Company also counts the golf course management software firm, Teesnap, as one of its subsidiaries. The company was founded in 2013 and has been owned by Allegiant since its outset.[^cite_note-ReviewUnusual-25] The firm's software was being used by 590 golf courses as of November 2023, but was also looking for a buyer for the subsidiary as of the same date.[^cite_note-ReviewTee-26] Another Allegiant subsidiary, Game Plane, created an eponymous game show that was filmed on Allegiant Air flights, which ran during 2014 and 2015 on the [Discovery Family Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discovery_Family_Channel).[^cite_note-ReviewUnusual-25][^cite_note-LAT-27] Allegiant Travel also operated an information technology company called Allegiant Systems that had the goal of selling software systems to other airlines.[^cite_note-ReviewSystems-28]

Allegiant Travel formerly operated family entertainment centers in Utah and Michigan.[^cite_note-ReviewUnusual-25][^cite_note-Standard-29] Known as Allegiant Nonstop, the company closed the centers in 2020.[^cite_note-30]

The company also built and briefly operated Sunseeker Resort in [Charlotte County, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte_County,_Florida), which it intended to be the first in a chain. Plans for the inaugural Sunseeker Resort Charlotte Harbor were announced in August 2017.[^cite_note-Herald2019-31] Construction on the project was initially halted due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md), but resumed in 2021 with plans for the 500 room, 180 extended-stay suite resort.[^cite_note-32] The resort opened on December 15, 2023.[^cite_note-33] On September 4, 2025, Allegiant Travel Company sold the resort to the [Blackstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackstone_Inc.), an investment firm that strategically acquires and manages "troubled assets", for \$200 million,[^cite_note-34] which will operate the resort under Hilton's Curio Collection brand.

## Sponsorships

The Allegiant Travel Company is the official sponsor of several sports teams and venues.

### Allegiant Stadium



Allegiant Stadium under construction, June 2020

Main article: [Allegiant Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allegiant_Stadium)

In August 2019, Allegiant was awarded the naming rights for the home of the [Las Vegas Raiders](22312.md) and the [UNLV Rebels football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNLV_Rebels_football) team, [Allegiant Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allegiant_Stadium).[^cite_note-ReviewRaiders-35] It is also the official airline of the Raiders.[^cite_note-36]

### Other sport sponsorships

In July 2018, Allegiant was named the official airline of [Minor League Baseball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minor_League_Baseball) (MiLB).[^cite_note-ReviewMisc-37] In December of that year, it announced a credit card partnership with the MiLB that would allow Allegiant credit card holders to earn points in relation to their local baseball teams and communities.[^cite_note-Credit-38]

Allegiant is also the official domestic airline partner of the [Vegas Golden Knights](47712000.md).[^cite_note-ReviewMisc-37] In September 2019, the company unveiled a Golden Knights-themed plane that featured a [livery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livery_\(aircraft\)) with the team's logo.[^cite_note-ReviewLivery-39] In January 2020, Allegiant signed a deal to become the official airline of the [Indianapolis Colts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indianapolis_Colts),[^cite_note-ReviewColts-40] and the following year became the official airline of the [Pac-12 Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pac-12_Conference).[^cite_note-41]
