During the [Russo-Ukrainian war](70149799.md), the [Russian Armed Forces](25709.md) have launched several missile attacks over the city of [Dnipro](989795.md) in [Ukraine](31750.md). These have led to more than one hundred fatalities and about one thousand injuries among the civilian population.

## Strikes in 2022

### March

[Dnipro](989795.md) was first hit during the [Russian invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine) on 11 March 2022. Three missiles hit the city and killed one person, striking close to an apartment building and a kindergarten.[^cite_note-1] On 22 March the [Dnipropetrovsk House of Organ and Chamber Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dnipropetrovsk_House_of_Organ_and_Chamber_Music) was damaged in an attack.[^cite_note-DHOCM20220317-2] On 15 March, [Dnipro International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dnipro_International_Airport) was heavily damaged by Russian missiles.[^cite_note-3] This destroyed the runaway of the airport.[^cite_note-4] On 30 March, Russian forces struck an oil terminal in Dnipro, destroying it. There were no casualties.[^cite_note-5]

### April

Another attack on the Dnipro airport on 10 April completely destroyed the airport and the infrastructure nearby.[^cite_note-6] One [Ukrainian](1135769.md) [Su-25](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Su-25) was destroyed in the ground inside its hangar, one Ukrainian technician was wounded as well.[^cite_note-7]

### June

On 28 June, Russian forces fired six [3M-14E Kalibr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3M-54_Kalibr) [cruise missiles](6590.md) from the [Black Sea](3386.md) to Dnipro at around 5\:30 local time. One of them hit an Avtodiesel car repair shop, killing a man and a woman. Seven other people, including a six-year-old boy, were injured. Fragments of the Kalibr missile were found afterwards.[^cite_note-8]

### July

An attack on Dnipro proper was carried out by Russian armed forces[^cite_note-\:3-9] on 15 July 2022. As a result, four people died, 16 were injured.[^cite_note-\:2-10] The main target was the largest space plant of Ukraine located within the city.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

The city was shelled from [Tu-95](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tupolev_Tu-95) aircraft from the northern part of the [Caspian Sea](19653787.md) with [X-101](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kh-55#Kh-101) missiles.[^cite_note-13] According to preliminary data, a total of eight rockets were fired. Four missiles were shot down by the [Ukrainian Air Defence Forces](53599459.md).[^cite_note-14] Each missile costs 13 million dollars[^cite_note-15] (8 missiles cost Russia more than 100 million dollars).[^cite_note-16]

Part of the rockets hit the "[Pivdenmash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuzhmash)" enterprise.[^cite_note-\:0-17] As a result of the impact, the city's water supply was damaged, and part of the city's residents were left without water supply.[^cite_note-\:0-17] More than ten cars were damaged, doors and windows were destroyed in residential buildings.[^cite_note-18]

Four people were killed.[^cite_note-\:2-10] One of the victims was a city bus driver.[^cite_note-\:3-9] On the first day, 15 wounded were reported, and the next day their number increased to 16.[^cite_note-\:3-9]

### September

On the morning of 29 September 2022 missiles hit residential areas in Dnipro, and three people were killed.[^cite_note-19] The central bus station was also hit.[^cite_note-20]

### October

See also: [October–November 2022 nationwide missile strikes on Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/October%E2%80%93November_2022_nationwide_missile_strikes_on_Ukraine)

Dnipro [was also hit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/October%E2%80%93November_2022_nationwide_missile_strikes_on_Ukraine#10_October) during the [10 October 2022 Russian missile strikes on critical infrastructure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/October%E2%80%93November_2022_nationwide_missile_strikes_on_Ukraine).[^cite_note-21] It was hit by at least five missiles.[^cite_note-dnipro-missile-strikes-32075129-22] During the attack that took place during morning rush hour three civilians were killed.[^cite_note-dnipro-missile-strikes-32075129-22]

On 18 October 2022 Russian missile strikes targeted the energy infrastructure of Dnipro.[^cite_note-23] One man was injured and a large-scale fire broke out at an energy infrastructure facility that was severely damaged.[^cite_note-17466_man_wounded-24] Also more than three dozen residential buildings were damaged, including schools and kindergartens.[^cite_note-17466_man_wounded-24]

On 25 October 2022 two people were killed, including a pregnant woman, and four injured due to a fire at a petrol station in Dnipro after fragments of a Russian missile had hit it.[^cite_note-25]

### November

See also: [October–November 2022 nationwide missile strikes on Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/October%E2%80%93November_2022_nationwide_missile_strikes_on_Ukraine)



Houses in Dnipro after the strike on 26 November

In the early hours of 9 November 2022 Russian forces deployed [kamikaze drones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-way_attack_drone) in an attack hitting a logistics business and causing a large fire.[^cite_note-7375555Dniproattack-26] Four employees sustained injuries, three severe.[^cite_note-7375555Dniproattack-26] [Anti-aircraft defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-aircraft_defence) systems destroyed five [loitering munitions](53703349.md).[^cite_note-7375555Dniproattack-26]

Around 08\:30[^cite_note-27] in the morning of 15 November, while people where commuting to work, Dnipro was hit by a strike on (according to Prime Minister [Denys Shmyhal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denys_Shmyhal)) the [PA Pivdenmash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PA_Pivdenmash) missile plant.[^cite_note-dnipro-attack-28514253-28] Governor of [Dnipropetrovsk Oblast](494909.md) [Valentyn Reznichenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valentyn_Reznichenko) stated that an industrial company, houses, trolleybuses and a "lively street" were damaged.[^cite_note-Dnipro7376804-29] Reznichenko claimed that 23 people were injured, including a teenager.[^cite_note-Dnipro7376804-29][^cite_note-dnipro-attack-28514253-28] Dnipro mayor [Borys Filatov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borys_Filatov) claimed a city hall employee was wounded in the attack while out helping elderly women.[^cite_note-dnipro-attack-28514253-28]

On 26 November 2022, around noon, a Russian missile strike on Dnipro injured 13 people and partially destroyed seven private houses in Dnipro's [Amur-Nyzhnodniprovskyi District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amur-Nyzhnodniprovskyi_District).[^cite_note-Dnipro36224597378079-30] Dnipro mayor Borys Filatov reported that city communications and infrastructure were not damaged.[^cite_note-31] Governor Valentyn Reznichenko stated that due to the attack one woman was hospitalised in critical condition.[^cite_note-DniproAttack874784-32] The following day Reznichenko reported that a man was found dead under the rubble.[^cite_note-33]

A Russian nightly multiple missile strike destroyed an enterprise 29 November 2022.[^cite_note-Dnipromissle7378409-34] No casualties were reported.[^cite_note-Dnipromissle7378409-34]

## Strikes in 2023

### January

Main article: [2023 Dnipro residential building airstrike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Dnipro_residential_building_airstrike)

Aftermath of the 15 January attack

On 14 January a [multi-storey residential building in Dnipro was hit by a Russian attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Dnipro_residential_building_airstrike).[^cite_note-7384858Dnipromissile-35][^cite_note-36] The explosion was heard at approximately 3\:41 p.m.[^cite_note-viyska-rf-vluchili-37] A local air alert had begun at 2 p.m.[^cite_note-viyska-rf-vluchili-37] On 19 January, 46 people are known to be killed and 80 to be injured. Eleven people remain missing.[^cite_note-pravda_2023_01_19-38][^cite_note-washingtonpost_2023_01_19-39] Thirty-nine people were rescued.[^cite_note-pravda_2023_01_16-40]

### March

On 9 March Dnipro was also hit during [a nationwide Russian missile strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%932023_Russian_strikes_against_Ukrainian_infrastructure#March_2023).[^cite_note-yacht_club_Sich-41][^cite_note-42] According to the [Dnipro City Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dnipro_City_Council) dozens of buildings were damaged by blast waves and in more than 120 apartments windows were broken.[^cite_note-yacht_club_Sich-41] Fragments of a rocket were scattered almost all over the terrain of yacht club Sich.[^cite_note-yacht_club_Sich-41] No casualties were reported.[^cite_note-yacht_club_Sich-41]

During a night attack on 27–28 March a Russian drone hit a private business in Dnipro and caused a large-scale fire.[^cite_note-7395348DnipoAtrack-43] No casualties were reported.[^cite_note-7395348DnipoAtrack-43] According to [Governor of Dnipropetrovsk Oblast](45151996.md) [Serhiy Lysak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serhiy_Lysak) two other Russian drones were shot down over [Dnipropetrovsk Oblast](494909.md).[^cite_note-7395348DnipoAtrack-43]

### April



The house destroyed on 28 April

On 28 April a mother and her three-year-old daughter were killed in their home in a rural suburb of Dnipro.[^cite_note-0261-3077DniproStrike-44] According to Governor Lysak seven Russian missiles had targeted the city.[^cite_note-DniproStrike2023/apr/28-45] Local police told neighbours (of the victims) that fragments of one of them, shot down by air defences, appeared to have fallen on the house.[^cite_note-DniproStrike2023/apr/28-45]

### May

On 3 May, in the night, a drone hit an administrative building in Dnipro.[^cite_note-DniproStrike2023May3-46] According to Governor Lysak it was set on fire, although that was put out by morning.[^cite_note-DniproStrike2023May3-46] Lysak also reported that the attack had not led to casualties.[^cite_note-zbili-7-droniv-ye-47] [Ukrainian Air Defence Forces](53599459.md) reported that they had shot down seven missiles flying over Dnipropetrovsk Oblast, and that this one drone had (still) hit its target.[^cite_note-zbili-7-droniv-ye-47]

In the evening of 8 May a 32-year-old woman was wounded and hospitalised when debris from Russian missiles fell on a four-storey building in Dnipro.[^cite_note-dnipropetrovskoyu-oblastyu-8-vorozhih-48] A missile warhead was found on the third floor of the building.[^cite_note-dnipropetrovskoyu-oblastyu-8-vorozhih-48] All eight missiles launched by Russian forces on Dnipropetrovsk Oblast were downed by Ukrainian Air Defence Forces.[^cite_note-dnipropetrovskoyu-oblastyu-8-vorozhih-48]

During the night of 21–22 May the premises of a private firm were hit by a Russian missile strike; eight people were injured (three of them hospitalised) and three buildings were damaged as a result.[^cite_note-Dniproattack7403297-49] 20 pieces of equipment of the [State Emergency Service of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Emergency_Service_of_Ukraine) were destroyed.[^cite_note-Dniproattack7403297-49] Ukraine's air defence forces reported that they had shot down 15 Russian drones and four cruise missiles during the attack on Dnipropetrovsk Oblast.[^cite_note-Dniproattack7403297-49]



Clinic in Dnipro after the strike on 26 May

On 26 May a Russian missile strike hit an outpatient clinic and a veterinarian clinic, killing 4 people[^cite_note-7404029KIrsD-50] and injuring 30 (three severely wounded).[^cite_note-740400-clinic-dnipro-51] The missile attack hit a three-storey building within a medical institution that was partially destroyed and caught fire.[^cite_note-740400-clinic-dnipro-51] The fire then also engulfed a neighbouring building.[^cite_note-740400-clinic-dnipro-51] Among the injured were a baby and a child, born in 2020 and 2017 respectively.[^cite_note-Dnipro7404029-52] 27 May was declared a day of mourning for those who died as a result of the attack.[^cite_note-Dnipro7404029-52]

### June

During the night of 3–4 June a Russian missiles struck a residential area killing a two-year-old girl and injuring another 22 people, including five children.[^cite_note-jun/04Dmre-53] The attack destroyed or damaged several buildings in [Pidhorodne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pidhorodne), which lies on the outskirts of Dnipro city.[^cite_note-jun/04Dmre-53][^cite_note-55] According to the [General Staff of the Ukrainian Armed Forces](17313362.md) one [Iskander-K missile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iskander-K) had hit a two-story apartment building.[^cite_note-peredmistyu-dnipra-54] (According to local police) 10 private houses, cars, shops and gas pipelines were destroyed after a fire broke out as a result of the explosion.[^cite_note-igrashki-sered-rujin-56] The rescue operation was completed at 3 am.[^cite_note-igrashki-sered-rujin-56]

In the night of 24 June, 11 people, including three children, were injured by a missile attack that destroyed four homes in a residential area.[^cite_note-Dnipro-Kyiv7151474-57][^cite_note-nichnu-vorozhu-ataku-Дніпро-58] The blast wave damaged more than 20 buildings.[^cite_note-nichnu-vorozhu-ataku-Дніпро-58]

### July



The building, hit on 28 July

At around 20.30 hour on 28 July 2023 a Russian missiles attack (locals heard two explosions) hit a high-rise building in the centre of Dnipro.[^cite_note-Dnipro7413311-59][^cite_note-60] A new residential complex, where many apartments were still uninhabited, was damaged.[^cite_note-Dnipro7413311-59] An office of the [Security Service of Ukraine](709222.md) was also hit, but was reported to have been not in use for a while.[^cite_note-Dnipro7413311-59][^cite_note-61] Dnipro mayor [Borys Filatov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borys_Filatov) claimed that Russian Iskander missals had hit the buildings.[^cite_note-62] Nine injured were reported and no deaths have been reported.[^cite_note-Dnipro7413311-59][^cite_note-aketnogo-v-dnipri-28723-63] On 29 July 2023 [Russia](25391.md)'s [Defense Ministry](7875884.md) spokesman [Igor Konashenkov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igor_Konashenkov) claimed "On the evening of July 28, the [Russian Armed Forces](25709.md) targeted the Ukrainian armed forces' command center in the city of [Dnipro](989795.md) with high-precision weapons. The target was hit."[^cite_note-64][^cite_note-66]

### August



[Palace of water sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_Complex_Meteor) damaged on 15 August



Bomb shelter in Dnipro, August 2023

At 4\:20 on 15 August 2023 one of the industrial facilities in Dnipro was hit, 2 wounded were reported.[^cite_note-Dnipro7415593-67] At around the same time a [swimming pool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_Complex_Meteor) was also hit, with no casualties reported.[^cite_note-68] Later that day Russian military spokesman Igor Konashenkov claimed that these attacks had been a "concentrated blow to key enterprises in the military industry of the [Kyiv regime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyiv_regime)."[^cite_note-Konashenkov18514913-69] According to him, the goal of the strikes was achieved.[^cite_note-Konashenkov18514913-69]

At around 2\:30 on 24 August 2023 ([Ukrainian independence day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_Day_of_Ukraine)) multiple rockets hit [Dnipro's Central Bus Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dnipro's_Central_Bus_Station?action=edit\&redlink=1). [^cite_note-Dnipro8590bcc98-70][^cite_note-Dnipro7416862-71][^cite_note-72] Ten people were injured, six were hospitalised with minor injuries.[^cite_note-Dnipro8590bcc98-70] 15 shops near the station were destroyed or damaged.[^cite_note-Dnipro8590bcc98-70][^cite_note-Dnipro7416862-71] Besides the bus station 10 other buildings were also damaged including two residential buildings, a bank, a petrol station, a hotel, premises belonging to an agricultural company, a furniture manufacturer and an administrative building.[^cite_note-Dnipro7416862-71] Additionally three buses and several cars, a trolleybus network line, as well as water and gas pipelines, were damaged.[^cite_note-Dnipro7416862-71] Later that day Russian military spokesman Igor Konashenkov claimed that the previous night "a multiple-launch strike by seaborne and ground-based long-range precision weapons against a Ukrainian military command center" had destroyed this target.[^cite_note-Konashenkov1664943-73][^cite_note-74]

### September

On 23 September [Governor of Dnipropetrovsk Oblast](45151996.md) [Serhiy Lysak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serhiy_Lysak) reported that the previous night wreckage from a shot down Russian drone had damaged "a critical infrastructure facility" in Dnipro.[^cite_note-Dniprodrone7421074-75]

### October

On 3 October [Governor of Dnipropetrovsk Oblast](45151996.md) [Serhiy Lysak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serhiy_Lysak) reported that falling debris, of the 13 [UAVs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UAV) and a [cruise missile](6590.md) that had been shot down over [Dnipropetrovsk Oblast](494909.md), had caused a fire at a Dnipro private firm that was quickly doused.[^cite_note-Dniprodrone7422396-76] In a later update Lysak stated that a school, a kindergarten, a petrol station and three apartment buildings in Dnipro were damaged by "fragments of downed Russian junk"; in addition to a warehouse being destroyed by fire.[^cite_note-77]

### December

See also: [29 December 2023 Russian strikes on Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/29_December_2023_Russian_strikes_on_Ukraine)



Aftermath of the missile strike on a shopping mall

On 29 December 2023, [Russia](25391.md) launched at least 122 cruise and [ballistic missiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballistic_missiles) and 36 drones which struck multiple cities ([Kyiv](585629.md), [Odesa](55545.md), [Kharkiv](2788558.md) and [Lviv](49968.md) and) including Dnipro, in what was one of the largest aerial attacks on Ukraine so far.[^cite_note-DMSRG2023-12-29-78] [Governor of Dnipropetrovsk Oblast](45151996.md) [Serhiy Lysak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serhiy_Lysak) reported that six people died and 28 people were injured in the attack.[^cite_note-DMSRG2023-12-29-78][^cite_note-raketniy-udar-po-dnipru-kilkist-79] Missiles struck a shopping centre, a maternity hospital a house and a six-storey residential building.[^cite_note-DMSRG2023-12-29-78][^cite_note-80] Local [online newspaper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_newspaper) *Informator* reported that at the time of the attack in the maternity hospital there were 12 women giving birth, four newborns and medical personnel.[^cite_note-skilki-porodil-perebuvalo-81] All of them survived since they had relocated to a shelter.[^cite_note-skilki-porodil-perebuvalo-81] Two schools and a library were also damaged as a result of the rocket attacks.[^cite_note-82]

The following day was declared a day of mourning.[^cite_note-83]

Two days after the attack another man died in hospital due to injuries sustained in the 29 December 2023 attacks.[^cite_note-ataki-na-dnipro-upd311223-84] On 31 December 2023 18 people, including a 18-month-old baby, attack victims were still in hospital.[^cite_note-ataki-na-dnipro-upd311223-84] Two patients were in serious condition.[^cite_note-ataki-na-dnipro-upd311223-84]

## Strikes in 2024

### February

Video by the [National Police of Ukraine](44656231.md) of a [Shahed drone "type 136"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahed-136) landed by air defense in [Dnipropetrovsk Oblast](494909.md) on 9 February 2024.

In the evening of 12 February a thermal power plant in Dnipro was significantly damaged in a drone and missile attack (one missile and 11 drones).[^cite_note-65cb22da8f088eb0e737abb1-85] No casualties were reported.[^cite_note-65cb22da8f088eb0e737abb1-85] The plant stopped operations.[^cite_note-DniproS7441624-86]

[Ukrainian Air Defence Forces](53599459.md) reported that they had shot down 10 [Shahed drones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahed_drone) flying over [Dnipro Raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dnipro_Raion), but that this one drone had (still) hit its target.[^cite_note-65cb22da8f088eb0e737abb1-85] The [all-clear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All-clear) was given at 00\:35 on 13 February.[^cite_note-65cb22da8f088eb0e737abb1-85]

On 13 February a city hospital was evacuated and several city school were closed due to heating issues.[^cite_note-DniproS7441624-86]

In the night of 22–23 February a Russian Shahed kamikaze drone hit a high-rise residential building in Dnipro's [Amur-Nyzhniodniprovskyi District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amur-Nyzhniodniprovskyi_District)[^cite_note-7443373DniproStrikes-87][^cite_note-88] and an unnamed businessfacility.[^cite_note-UpravdaDs7443259-89] Eight people were injured.[^cite_note-UpravdaDs7443259-89] Ukrainian Air Defence Forces claimed five attack drones were destroyed in [Dnipropetrovsk Oblast](494909.md), but others could not be intercepted.[^cite_note-UpravdaDs7443259-89] In the afternoon of 23 February the lifeless bodies of a man and a woman were recovered from the rubble of the apartment building.[^cite_note-7443373DniproStrikes-87] [Dnipro Mayor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dnipro_Mayor?action=edit\&redlink=1) [Borys Filatov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borys_Filatov) declared 24 February a day of mourning.[^cite_note-90]

In the evening of 25 February, 10 private houses and several cars were damaged due to a Russian attack.[^cite_note-UpravdaDs7443687-91] The attack injured two men and two women.[^cite_note-UpravdaDs7443687-91] According to [Governor of Dnipropetrovsk Oblast](45151996.md) [Serhiy Lysak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serhiy_Lysak) air defence had downed three [cruise missiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cruise_missiles) and three [UAVs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UAVs).[^cite_note-UpravdaDs7443687-91]

### March

See also: [22 March 2024 Russian strikes on Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/22_March_2024_Russian_strikes_on_Ukraine)

In the [largest Russian attack on Ukrainian energy infrastructure since the 2022 invasion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_2024_Ukraine_missile_strike) Dnipro was damaged too.[^cite_note-92][^cite_note-93] In this 22 March nightly attack [high-rise buildings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-rise_building) and private houses were damaged by falling wreckage of shot down rockets and kamikaze drones, no injuries were reported.[^cite_note-94] [Governor of Dnipropetrovsk Oblast](45151996.md) [Serhiy Lysak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serhiy_Lysak) reported that 7 drones and 14 rockets had been shot down in Dnipropetrovsk Oblast.[^cite_note-95] The attack left 156 houses in Dnipro without heating.[^cite_note-96] Emergency electricity shutdown schedules were introduced.[^cite_note-97] Parts of the city were also left without water due to the attack leading to a lack of energy supply.[^cite_note-98][^cite_note-99]

### April

In the early hours of 2 April four houses were damaged by a Russian attack of [Shahed drones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahed_drone).[^cite_note-ponivecheni-4-budinki-100] No injuries were reported.[^cite_note-ponivecheni-4-budinki-100] Another nine drones heading to Dnipro were shot down.[^cite_note-ponivecheni-4-budinki-100] In the afternoon a Russian missile strike damaged a kindergarten, a college and a commercial enterprise.[^cite_note-ditsadok-koledzh-101][^cite_note-DniproStrike7449332-102] The attack injured 18 people including children aged 14 to 17 (five children were hospitalized).[^cite_note-DniproStrike7449377-103][^cite_note-ditsadok-koledzh-101][^cite_note-DniproStrike7449347-104] According to [Governor of Dnipropetrovsk Oblast](45151996.md) [Serhiy Lysak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serhiy_Lysak) "Fortunately, all of the children were hiding when the blow was delivered. This may have saved their lives."[^cite_note-DniproStrike7449332-102] Later in the afternoon the [Ukrainian Air Defence Forces](53599459.md) claimed to have shot down a Russian [Kh-59](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kh-59) [cruise missile](6590.md) that was destined to destroy an unknown target in Dnipro.[^cite_note-DniproStrike7449347-104]

At (as reported) 17.50 hours on 14 April the debris of a destroyed cruise missile fell down in Dnipro and [Lyubymivka hromada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyubymivka_hromada?action=edit\&redlink=1) injuring 13 people.[^cite_note-DniproStrike7451215-105]

In the morning of 19 April three people were killed in a Russian attack on a residential building and 24 people were injured.[^cite_note-dnipri-na-misci-rosiyskoji-106][^cite_note-Dnipro7451928-107] More than a [dozen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dozen) residential, public transport, commercial and educational buildings were damaged by the attack.[^cite_note-prilotu-u-dnipri-108] Operations at [Dnipro railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dnipro_railway_station) were temporarily suspended.[^cite_note-109][^cite_note-110] The [shockwave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shockwave) of the explosion (also) damaged [Dnipro's Central Bus Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dnipro's_Central_Bus_Station?action=edit\&redlink=1), its operations were also temporarily suspended.[^cite_note-111] 16 rockets and 10 drones were fired at [Dnipropetrovsk Oblast](494909.md).[^cite_note-112] According to the Ukrainian Air Defence Forces the Russian forces had fired a total of 22 missiles of various types and 14 attack drones at Ukraine, of this total number 22 had been reportedly shot down.[^cite_note-113] Dnipro Mayor [Borys Filatov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borys_Filatov) declared 20 April a day of mourning.[^cite_note-114][^cite_note-bagatopoverhivci-115] Filatov stated that the city had been struck with a [Kh-22](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kh-22) missile "designed to destroy [aircraft carriers](2219.md)."[^cite_note-116][^cite_note-bagatopoverhivci-115] In the early evening (of 19 April) [President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_President) [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](59210523.md) visited the site of the Russian attack where he thanked the city's emergency workers.[^cite_note-117]

In the evening of 6 January Dnipro was hit by a large-scale drone attack.[^cite_note-DniMStr4077621-231] Eight people were injured in the attack.[^cite_note-DniMStr4077621-231] A [vocational school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vocational_school) was damaged, two kindergarten and a music school, in ten apartment buildings almost all windows were smashed, numerous cars were burned-out, the heating networks near one of the residential buildings was damaged and two trolleybuses were damaged.[^cite_note-232][^cite_note-DniMStrinformatorvigorili7Jan2026-233] About about 25 tons of debris and glas had to be removed from the streets.[^cite_note-DniMStrinformatorvigorili7Jan2026-233] The [Shevchenkivskyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shevchenkivskyi_District,_Dnipro) and [Sobornyi Districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sobornyi_District,_Dnipro) suffered the most from the attack.[^cite_note-DniMStrinformatorvigorili7Jan2026-233] On 8 January 2026 Mayor Filatov declared a [national emergency](298608.md) in the city, power engineers were gradually restoring power to hospitals, the [left bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Left_bank) of the city was powered by backup sources and work was ongoing to restore heat and water supplies in many city districts.[^cite_note-DniMStr8015185-234] The rest of [Dnipropetrovsk Oblast](494909.md) was almost completely without electricity due to Russian attacks on [Ukraine's energy](32021039.md) infrastructure.[^cite_note-DniMStr8015127-235] Also because of the power outages caused by the attack school holidays in Dnipro were extended for another two days.[^cite_note-DniMStr8015185-234] At the evening of 8 January Mayor Filatov reported that water, electricity and heating supply had been restored while the trolleybuslines would not restarted until stable power returned to traction substations.[^cite_note-236]

In the evening of 10 January Dnipro was hit by a drone attack.[^cite_note-DniMStrataka-dronami10Jan2026-237] One man was injured.[^cite_note-DniMStrataka-dronami10Jan2026-237] Two garages were hit and burned out, 27 others were damaged.[^cite_note-DniMStrataka-dronami10Jan2026-237] More than 50,000 people were left without electricity.[^cite_note-DniMStrataka-dronami10Jan2026-237] Additionally, in nine apartment blocks nearly 400 windows were smashed.[^cite_note-DniMStrataka-dronami10Jan2026-237] In the morning of 12 January water and power supply had not been restored in [several city districts](989795.md#Subdivisions) on left bank of the city.[^cite_note-238] Late afternoon (of 12 January) 18,000 families remained without electricity.[^cite_note-239] On 13 January some parts of the left bank of the city were still without electricity.[^cite_note-Informator13126-shcho-meshkanciv-240] Generators were supplied to provide electricity to buildings.[^cite_note-Informator13126-shcho-meshkanciv-240]

In the night of 19 to 20 January Dnipro a Russian attack caused an enterprise was damaged and set on fire.[^cite_note-Informator20126-nich-z-19-na-20-241] Windows in a high-rise building were broken and five cars were damaged.[^cite_note-Informator20126-nich-z-19-na-20-241] A [boiler house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boiler_house) that provided heat to 90 homes, 10 educational institutions, and 2 hospitals was also damaged to such an extent that ten streets were left without heating.[^cite_note-Informator20126-nich-z-19-na-20-241] Two women were injured in the attack.[^cite_note-Informator20126-nich-z-19-na-20-241]

Midday on 22 January a Russian drone hit a 16-story building in Dnipro.[^cite_note-DniMSt8017386-242] 14 people were injured.[^cite_note-DniMSt8017386-242] 16 people had to be rescued from the upper floors of the building.[^cite_note-DniMSt8017386-242] The building was partially destroyed and two apartments with a total area of 200 square metro caught fire.[^cite_note-DniMSt8017386-242]

### February 2026

In the early hours of 1 February a Russian [UAV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UAV) strike killed a man and a woman in their home.[^cite_note-DniMSt8018905-243] Their house was destroyed and two others damaged.[^cite_note-DniMSt8018905-243] And also a car was damaged.[^cite_note-DniMSt8018905-243]

In the night of 2 to 3 February Dnipro was hit by ballistic missiles and drones.[^cite_note-dnipru-3-lyutogo-244] A [thermal power plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermal_power_plant) used exclusively to heat residential areas was attacked and damaged for the ninth time.[^cite_note-dnipru-3-lyutogo-244] In a dormitory for displaced persons took damage and it's 85 windows were broke, a three-story residential building and two private homes were also damaged.[^cite_note-dnipru-3-lyutogo-244] A car parked in a yard also took damage.[^cite_note-dnipru-3-lyutogo-244] No human casualties were reported.[^cite_note-dnipru-3-lyutogo-244] The strike caused problems with heat supply, about 8,600 subscribers were left without heat.[^cite_note-dnipru-3-lyutogo-244]

### April 2026



Destructions in Dnipro after Russian attack, April 2026

On 25 April 2026, Russian forces launched a large-scale overnight drone and ballistic missile attack on the city of Dnipro, as part of a wider wave of strikes across the country. The assault, carried out in several waves, targeted residential areas and civilian infrastructure, causing significant destruction. At least 10 people were killed and 49 injured.[^cite_note-245][^cite_note-246][^cite_note-247]

### August 2026

On 7 August 2026, Russian forces struck a [World Health Organization](33583.md) (WHO) humanitarian aid warehouse, destroying it. 5 people were killed and 16 injured in the attack on the Dnipro region.[^cite_note-248]
