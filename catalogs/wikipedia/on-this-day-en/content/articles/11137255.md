In the [Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Federation), [law enforcement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement) is the responsibility of a variety of different agencies. The [Police of Russia](30975423.md) (formerly the *_[militsiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Militsiya_\(Russia\))_*) are the primary law enforcement agency, with the [Investigative Committee of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investigative_Committee_of_Russia) as the main investigative agency, and the [Federal Security Service](385990.md) (FSB) as the main domestic security agency.

## Agencies

- [Ministry of Internal Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Ministry_of_Internal_Affairs):&#x20;

  - The [Police of Russia](30975423.md) (*_politsiya_*) provide public security functions. Formerly the *[militsiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Militsiya_\(Russia\))*

  - [Main Directorate for Drugs Control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Main_Directorate_for_Drugs_Control)

- The [Investigative Committee of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Investigative_Committee_of_Russia) is an investigative body, sometimes described as the "Russian [FBI](11127.md)".

- [Ministry of Justice](35320735.md):&#x20;

  - [Federal Service of Punishment Execution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Penitentiary_Service) (FSIN) is responsible for the penal correction and prison system of Russia

- The [Federal Security Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Security_Service_of_the_Russian_Federation) (FSB) is the domestic security service, and the main successor agency of the Soviet-era [Cheka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheka), [NKVD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NKVD), and [KGB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KGB). Responsible for [anti-terrorism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-terrorism) operations.&#x20;

  - The [Federal Border Guard Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_Guard_Service_of_Russia) is subordinate to the FSB and responsible for border protection, surveillance and [coast guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coast_Guard_\(Russia\)).

- [Federal Customs Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Customs_Service_of_Russia)

- The [Ministry for Civil Defense, Emergencies and Elimination of Consequences of Natural Disasters](1568895.md) (EMERCOM) is responsible for the civil defence regulation, protection from fire and has own troops.

  [^cite_note-1]

- [Ministry of Defence](7875884.md)&#x20;

  - [Russian Military Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_Police_\(Russia\)) provides the service police function for all branches of the [Russian Armed Forces](25709.md).

- The [National Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Guard_of_Russia) (formerly [Internal Troops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_Troops_of_Russia), [OMON](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OMON), and [SOBR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SOBR)) provide a [gendarmerie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Gendarmerie) function, supporting the [Politsiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politsiya) and dealing with large-scale riots and internal armed conflicts. They also provide security for highly-important facilities (such as [nuclear power plants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_power_plants_in_Russia)).

- The [Federal Protective Service of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Protective_Service_of_Russia) is responsible for the protection of Russian state property and high-ranking government personnel, including the [President of Russia](239646.md).

- [Presidential Security Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Security_Service_\(Russia\)) - concerned with the tasks related to the protection of the [President of Russia](239646.md).

## Prisons

Main article: [Prisons in Russia](37693741.md)

[Prisons in Russia](37693741.md) are administered by the [Federal Penitentiary Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Penitentiary_Service) (FSIN) and can be categorized under four types of facilities: pre-trial institutions; educative or juvenile labor colonies; corrective labor colonies; and prisons.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTERoth2006231-2]

The [corrective colony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corrective_labor_colony) ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Исправительно-трудовая колония, ИТК, *ispravitelno-trudovaya koloniya*, **ITK**) is the most common, with 760 institutions in 2004 across the many [administrative divisions of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Russia).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTERoth2006231-2] There were also eight prisons, 62 juvenile facilities, and 192 pre-trial facilities in 2004.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTERoth2006232-3]
