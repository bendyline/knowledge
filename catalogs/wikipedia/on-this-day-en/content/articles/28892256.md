**Association football in North Korea** is governed by the [DPR Korea Football Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DPR_Korea_Football_Association) (KFA), which was established in 1945. While football is the most popular sport in the country, the state's secrecy makes it difficult to accurately determine things such as attendances and popularity for club matches. Approximately one in five people in North Korea are considered association football fans.[^cite_note-1]

## Governing body

The KFA oversees the [North Korea national football team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Korea_national_football_team), the [North Korea women's national football team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Korea_women's_national_football_team) and the North Korean football league system, including the [DPR Korea Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DPR_Korea_Football_League). The body became an [AFC](715249.md) affiliate in 1954 and a [FIFA](11049.md) affiliate in 1958. Its chairman is Mun Jae Chol.

## Teams and competitions



North Korea vs. [Brazil](149286.md) at the [2010 FIFA World Cup](168079.md)

The [DPR Korea League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DPR_Korea_League) is the highest league in the Korean football league system. Below it comes the [DPR K-League 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DPR_K-League_2?action=edit\&redlink=1) and the [Amateur DPR K-League 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amateur_DPR_K-League_3?action=edit\&redlink=1). The DPR Korea League consists of three leagues which are played at different times of the year.

Prior to 2017, the DPR Korea League winners were not eligible for any [Asian Football Confederation](715249.md) club competition.

Since 2017, the top 2 clubs in the [DPR Korea League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DPR_Korea_League) compete in the [AFC Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_Cup).\
&#x20;The main cup competition is the [DPR Korean Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DPR_Korean_Cup), sometimes known as the *Open Cup*. It replaced the [Republican Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republican_Championship).

Other competitions held include the [Man'gyŏngdae Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Man'gy%C5%8Fngdae_Prize), [Osandŏk Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osand%C5%8Fk_Prize), [Paektusan Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paektusan_Prize) and the [Poch'ŏnbo Torch Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poch'%C5%8Fnbo_Torch_Prize).

## National teams

### Men

The [North Korea national football team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Korea_national_football_team) is ranked 112th in the world [by the FIFA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_World_Rankings), as of November 2022.[^cite_note-2] They consist of North Koreans and [Chongryon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chongryon)-affiliated Koreans born in Japan. In 1966, the team managed to make up for a 3–0 loss to the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union_national_football_team) in the first match of the group stages to qualify for the next round in second. In the group stage of the [1966 World Cup](61629.md), North Korea surprisingly beat [Italy](362466.md) 1–0 in the group stage to clinch the second place, thus qualifying for the quarter-finals.[^cite_note-3] Whilst there, Korea DPR lost 5–3 to [Portugal](576328.md) thanks to four goals from [Eusébio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eus%C3%A9bio), after being 3–0 up on 25 minutes.[^cite_note-4] In 2010, the North Korea team failed to get past the group stages, finishing bottom of the group and losing all three matches.[^cite_note-5] Subsequently, the team also failed to qualify for the 2014, 2018, 2022, or 2026 FIFA World Cup.

### Women

Unlike the men's team, [the women's squad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Korea_women's_national_football_team) consists entirely of [North Korea](21255.md)-born Koreans. It is much more successful than its male counterpart, reaching the quarter-finals of the [FIFA Women's World Cup](244862.md) in [2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Women's_World_Cup_2007)[^cite_note-6] and winning the [AFC Women's Asian Cup](2428648.md) in [2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_AFC_Women's_Asian_Cup).[^cite_note-7] They were also disqualified for the [2015 FIFA Women's World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_FIFA_Women's_World_Cup), due to using illegal drugs. The team is [ranked 9th in the World by the FIFA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Women's_World_Rankings), as of December 2023.[^cite_note-8]

## Youth football

North Korea has a distinguished record in youth football, in particularly Asia. North Korea has been a major football youth power in the continent, having won the [AFC U-16 Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_U-16_Championship) twice, [AFC U-19 Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_U-19_Championship) three times, and the [FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_U-17_Women's_World_Cup) three times. It is also a frequent participant in the [Asian Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_at_the_Asian_Games), winning one gold medal, and has participated in every editions of the [AFC U-23 Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_U-23_Championship). The team also participated in the [Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_at_the_Summer_Olympics).
