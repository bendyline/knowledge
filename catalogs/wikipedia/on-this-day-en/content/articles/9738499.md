**Ad-Doueir** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الدوير, *ad-Duwayr*), or **Dweir**, is a municipality of 7,500 inhabitants in [Southern Lebanon](4205968.md) near [Nabatieh](43794798.md), and north of the [Litani River](1920925.md). It is located directly next to the village of [Ash-Sharqiyah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Sharqiyah). Although Ad-Doueir is considered a big village, it is the home of many well-known [Lebanese](17771.md) such as former Labour minister [Ali Qanso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_Qanso), and the late physics scientist [Rammal Rammal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rammal_Rammal).

## History

In 1875 [Victor Guérin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Gu%C3%A9rin) found it to be a large village with 800 [Metuali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metuali) inhabitants.[^cite_note-1]

During the [2006 Lebanon War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Lebanon_War), Ad-Doueir was among the first targets of the [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md), with a family of 12 killed on July 13.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] The village has since benefited from investment by the Italian government to build a new olive oil mill.[^cite_note-5]

## Demographics

In 2014 [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 99.35% of registered voters in Ad-Doueir. 98.43% of the voters were [Shiite Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims).[^cite_note-6]
