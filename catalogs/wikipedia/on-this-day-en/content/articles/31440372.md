The **National Police of Ecuador** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Policía Nacional del Ecuador*) is the national [police force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_force) and the main civil [law enforcement agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement_agency) of [Ecuador](9334.md). It is commanded by the Commanding General (*_Comandante General_*) and subordinate to the Ministry of the Interior.

## Controversies

### Human rights

The [United States Country Reports on Human Rights Practices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_Reports_on_Human_Rights_Practices) has consistently identified major human rights abuses by Ecuadorian security forces, including: isolated unlawful killings and use of excessive force by security forces, sometimes with [impunity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impunity), poor prison conditions, [arbitrary arrest and detention](13435705.md), corruption and other abuses by security forces, a high number of pretrial detainees, and corruption and denial of due process within the judicial system.[^cite_note-2] Members of the National Police have been accused of murder, attempted murder, rape, extortion, kidnappings, and alien smuggling.[^cite_note-3]

### Corruption

In a 2009 diplomatic cable from the [United States diplomatic cables leak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_diplomatic_cables_leak) in April 2011, U.S. Ambassador [Heather Hodges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heather_M._Hodges) said that "corruption among Ecuadorian National Police officers is widespread and well-known" and that "U.S. investors are reluctant to risk their resources in Ecuador knowing that they could be targeted by corrupt law enforcement officials."[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] The leaked cable resulted in a major diplomatic spat, resulting in the expulsion of U.S. Ambassador Hodges from Ecuador and the reciprocal expulsion of Ecuadorian Ambassador [Luis Gallegos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Gallegos) from the U.S.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]
