**Blida** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): بليدا, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Blīdā*) is a municipality in [Marjayoun District](8968039.md) in southern [Lebanon](17771.md).

## Location

The municipality of Blida is located in the [Kaza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaza) of Marjayoun one of is one of the eight [mohafazats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governorate) (governorates) of [Lebanon](17771.md). Blida is 118 kilometers (73.3252 mi) away from [Beirut](37428.md), the capital of Lebanon. Its elevation is 630 meters (2067.03 ft – 688.968 yd) above sea level. Blida surface stretches for 1330 hectares (13.3 km² – 5.1338 mi²).

## Name

[E. H. Palmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Henry_Palmer) wrote that the name *Belidet* meant "The little village".[^cite_note-1]

## History

In 1881, the [PEF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Exploration_Fund)'s [*Survey of Western Palestine* (SWP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PEF_Survey_of_Palestine) described it as: "A village, built of stone, containing \[a] mosque, and having about 150 Moslem inhabitants, situated on a ridge, with figs, olives, and arable land. One [cistern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cistern) and a good spring one mile south-east of the village give the water supply."[^cite_note-2]

They further noted: "Here are several columns and remains of ruins. [Double triangles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_of_David) are cut on either side of \[the] door of \[the] mosque."[^cite_note-3]

## Demographics

In 2014 [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 99.68% of registered voters in Blida. 98.55% of the voters were [Shiite Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims).[^cite_note-4]
