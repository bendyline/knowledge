# 2025 National Book Awards

The 76th **[National Book Awards](63097.md)** (NBA), presented at a ceremony and benefit dinner by the [National Book Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Book_Foundation), took place on November 19, 2025, in [New York City](645042.md). The ceremony included a musical performance by [Corinne Bailey Rae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corinne_Bailey_Rae), with [Jeff Hiller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Hiller) giving the welcoming and closing remarks.[^cite_note-NBA-1]

- [Attica Locke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attica_Locke), author of *Guide Me Home* and *[Black Water Rising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Water_Rising)*

- [Elizabeth McCracken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_McCracken), author of *The Souvenir Museum*, *[The Giant's House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Giant's_House)*, and *[Thunderstruck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thunderstruck_\(short_story_collection\))*, which have all been honored by the NBA

- Cody Morrison, bookseller at [Square Books](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_Books)

### Nonfiction

- Heather Kathleen Moody Hall, proprietor of the Green Feather Book Company

- [Tiya Miles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiya_Miles), historian, professor at [Harvard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard), and author of NBA-winning *[All That She Carried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_That_She_Carried)*

- [Raj Patel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raj_Patel), journalist, filmmaker, and author of *[Stuffed and Starved](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuffed_and_Starved)*

- [Cristina Rivera Garza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristina_Rivera_Garza), author of Pulitzer prize-winning *[Liliana's Invincible Summer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liliana's_Invincible_Summer)* and professor at the [University of Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Houston)

- [Eli Saslow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eli_Saslow), twice Pulitzer prize-winning journalist and writer-at-large for *[The New York Times](30680.md)*

### Poetry

- Kate Daniels, author *In the Months of My Son's Recovery*

- [Terrance Hayes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrance_Hayes), author of 2010 NBA-winning *Lighthead*

- [H. Melt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._Melt), author of *There Are Trans People Here*

- [Anis Mojgani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anis_Mojgani), Oregon's tenth [poet laureate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon_Poet_Laureate)

- [Caridad Moro-Gronlier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caridad_Moro-Gronlier), author of *Tortillera*

### Translated Literature

- Stesha Brandon, literature and humanities program manager at the [Seattle Public Library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Public_Library)

- [Sergio Gutiérrez Negrón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_Guti%C3%A9rrez_Negr%C3%B3n), Hispanic studies professor at [Oberlin College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberlin_College) and author of *Los días hábiles*

- [Bill Johnston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Johnston_\(translator\)), Polish and French literature translator known for [National Translation Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Translation_Award)-winning translation of *[Pan Tadeusz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan_Tadeusz)*

- Annette K. Joseph-Gabriel, Romance studies professor at [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University)

- [Karen Tei Yamashita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karen_Tei_Yamashita), professor emerita at [University of California, Santa Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Cruz) and author of *[I Hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Hotel_\(novel\))*

### Young People's Literature

- Cathy Berner, bookseller at Blue Willow Bookshop and former school [librarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Librarian)

- [candice iloh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candice_Iloh), author of 2020 NBA-finalist *Every Body Looking*

- Jung Kim, education professor at [Lewis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_University)
