This is a list of **[presidents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_\(government_title\)) of the [Republic of the Congo](19599929.md)** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Président de la République du Congo*) since the formation of the post of president in 1960, to the present day.

A total of six people have served as President of the Republic of the Congo (not counting one acting/interim head of state and two collective presidencies). Additionally, one person, [Denis Sassou Nguesso](158608.md), has served on two non-consecutive occasions.

## Term limits

[The new constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Republic_of_the_Congo) in 2015 removed the age limit, reduced the length of presidential term from seven years to five, and extended term limits from two to three terms.[^cite_note-termlimits-3]
