**Latehar district** is one of the 24 districts of [ Jharkhand state](197225.md) in eastern [India](14533.md), and [Latehar](1673467.md) town is the administrative headquarters of this district. This district is part of [Palamu division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palamu_division).

Latehar district is situated on [longitude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longitude) 84° 31' E and 23° 44.4' N [latitude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latitude). The area of the district is 3660 km², with a [population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_\(human_biology\)) of 726,978 (2011 census).

## History

The territory covered by the present district became a part of [Palamu district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palamu_district), when it formed on 1 January 1928. Latehar district was created on 4 April 2001 by separating the erstwhile Latehar subdivision of [Palamu district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palamu_district). It is currently a part of the [Red Corridor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Corridor).[^cite_note-1]

## Geography

- Latehar district has two of the [highest waterfalls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_waterfalls_in_India_by_height) in Jharkhand – [Lodh Falls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lodh_Falls) and [Lower Ghaghri Falls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_Ghaghri_Falls), along with several others.

  [^cite_note-2]

- [Netarhat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netarhat) is hill station in the district.

- [Betla National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betla_National_Park) and [Mahuadanr Wolf Sanctuary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahuadanr_Wolf_Sanctuary) is located in this District.

According to the [2011 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_census_of_India) Latehar district has a [population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_of_India) of 726,978.[^cite_note-districtcensus-5] Roughly equal to the nation of [Bhutan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhutan)[^cite_note-cia-6] or the US state of [Alaska](624.md).[^cite_note-7] This gives it a ranking of 499th in India (out of a total of [640](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_India)).[^cite_note-districtcensus-5] The district has a population density of 200 inhabitants per square kilometre (520/sq mi).[^cite_note-districtcensus-5] Its [population growth rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_planning_in_India) over the decade 2001-2011 was 29.38%.[^cite_note-districtcensus-5] Latehar has a [sex ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_ratio) of 964 [females](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women_in_India) for every 1000 males,[^cite_note-districtcensus-5] and a [literacy rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literacy_in_India) of 59.51%. 7.13% of the population lives in urban areas. Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes make up 21.31% and 45.54% of the population respectively.[^cite_note-districtcensus-5]

## Economy

In 2006 the Indian government named Latehar one of the country's 250 [most backward districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_in_India) (out of a total of [640](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_India)).[^cite_note-brgf-9] It is one of the districts in Jharkhand currently receiving funds from the Backward Regions Grant Fund Programme (BRGF).[^cite_note-brgf-9]
