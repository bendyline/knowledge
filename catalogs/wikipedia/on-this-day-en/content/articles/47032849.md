**Pont-Sondé** (French pronunciation: [\[pɔ̃ sɔ̃de\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French), [Haitian Creole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haitian_Creole_language): *Ponsonde*) is a town in the [communal section](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communal_section) of [Bocozelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bocozelle?action=edit\&redlink=1) in the [Saint-Marc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Marc) [commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_communes_of_Haiti), in the [Artibonite](489220.md) [department](10937444.md) of [Haiti](13373.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

## Location

Located within the [Saint-Marc Arrondissement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Marc_Arrondissement), Pont-Sondé is located at an important crossroads on the road linking the capital [Port-au-Prince](57391.md) to the second largest city of [Cap-Haïtien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cap-Ha%C3%AFtien) on the northern coast.[^cite_note-Haiti_Mega-3]

## History

When the [Haitian national highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transport_in_Haiti) was constructed in 1880, a bridge spanning 90 metres (300 ft) across the [Artibonite River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artibonite_River) was built. The settlement of Pont-Sondé then developed around this bridge.[^cite_note-Haiti_Mega-3]

Pont-Sonde had about ten thousand inhabitants before the [2010 Haiti earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Haiti_earthquake). Since then, the village has hosted a large influx of refugees.

In September 2024, during the [Gang war in Haiti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gang_war_in_Haiti), personnel from the Anti-Gang Tactical Unit and the Departmental Operations and Intervention Brigade were deployed to Pont-Sondé.[^cite_note-4] Despite this, the town was [attacked](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Pont-Sond%C3%A9_attack) by the Grand Grif gang on 3 October 2024, leaving at least 115 people dead.[^cite_note-5]

## Economy

Pont-Sondé is the leading producer of rice in Haiti. There is an important market which is held along the road and on the bridge over the Artibonite.[^cite_note-Haiti_Mega-3]
