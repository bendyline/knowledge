**Valparaíso Province** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Provincia de Valparaíso*) is one of eight [provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Chile) of the central [Chilean](5489.md) [region](299550.md) of [Valparaíso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valpara%C3%ADso_Region) (V). Its capital is the coastal city of [Valparaíso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valpara%C3%ADso) (pop. 275,982).[^cite_note-INE-1]

## Administration

As a province, Valparaíso is a second-level [administrative division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Chile), it was governed by a provincial [governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor) who was appointed by the [president](11885300.md). Since 2021, however, it is directly governed by a regional presidential delegate, also appointed by the President, because one of its communes is the regional capital.

### Communes

The province comprises seven [communes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_Chile) ([Spanish](26825.md): *comunas*), each governed by a municipality consisting of an [alcalde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcalde) and [municipal council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_council):

- [Valparaíso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valpara%C3%ADso)

- [Viña del Mar](299067.md)

- [Concón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conc%C3%B3n)

- [Quintero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quintero)

- [Puchuncaví](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puchuncav%C3%AD)

- [Casablanca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casablanca,_Chile)

- [Juan Fernández](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Fern%C3%A1ndez_\(commune\))

## History

The province was created on 1 January 1976. On March 11, 2010, the communes of [Quilpué](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quilpu%C3%A9) and [Villa Alemana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villa_Alemana) were transferred to [Marga Marga Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marga_Marga_Province) under Law 20,368 (signed August 25, 2009).

## Geography and demography

The province spans a coastal area of 2,146.6 km<sup>2</sup> (829 sq mi), the fourth largest in the Valparaíso Region. According to the 2002 census, Valparaíso was the most populous province in the region with a population of 651,821. At that time, there were 639,255 people living in [urban areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_area), 12,566 people living in [rural areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rural_area), 315,785 men and 336,036 women.[^cite_note-INE-1]
