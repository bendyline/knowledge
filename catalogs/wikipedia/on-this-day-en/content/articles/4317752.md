The **General Security and Central Security Forces** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): قوات الأمن العام و الأمن المركزي, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Quwwāt al-Amn al- Amm wa Quwwāt al-Amn al-Markazī*, often shortened to [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الأمن المركزي, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Al-Amn al-Markazī*) is an Egyptian [paramilitary](146730.md) force which is responsible for assisting the [Egyptian National Police](17957135.md) (ENP) for the security of governmental fixed sites, foreign embassies & missions, [riots and crowd control](1148542.md), publicly crowded events, high risk arrests, disaster response and [SWAT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SWAT) operations. [^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] They are a vital arm of Egypt's [National Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security) apparatus.

## History

In 1969, a decision was made to create the Central Security Forces from well trained and equipped police forces on large scale covering the whole country under the command of the [Ministry of Interior (Egypt)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Interior_\(Egypt\)) to conduct special police operations in response to operational needs.[^cite_note-4] The creation of those forces followed the military model, and became close – in formation, training, equipment and operating procedures – to the Italian [Carabinieri](45173.md), Indian [CRPF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Reserve_Police_Force) and the Russian [OMON](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OMON). The initial batch of recruits were drawn from the Police and had to undergo military training under the [Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egyptian_Army). Later on, in 1970, a separate training institute was set up for the CSF and the force started conscripting its recruits, just like regular Army conscripts. Formed in 1977 to obviate the need to call upon the armed forces to deal with domestic disturbances, the CSF grew rapidly to 100,000 members when [Mubarak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hosni_Mubarak) took office. The CSF was set up to deal with the growing unrest and public disorder following the Egyptian defeat in the [Six-Day War](29328.md). The CSF was deployed by the [Sadat government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anwar_Sadat) to tackle large protests by Leftist and hardline [Nasserist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasserism) groups in January 1972 in Cairo, the protests were called to protest against Sadat's anti-Soviet and anti-Palestinian foreign policy, lack of action against Israel and right-wing economic measures such as cutting some subsidies and increasing some prices of welfare services like public transport. CSF units had to use force to disperse the rioting crowds in [Tahrir Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tahrir_Square) which led to the shooting death of three protestors.[^cite_note-lccn-5]

Following the [1973 Arab Israeli War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1973_Arab_Israeli_War) between Egypt and Israel – then settling the 1973 & 1974 [Ceasefire](592591.md) agreements; the 1979 [Egyptian–Israeli Peace Treaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egyptian%E2%80%93Israeli_Peace_Treaty) was signed and the subsequent Israeli withdrawal from the rest of [Sinai Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinai_Peninsula) occurred. The Treaty stipulated the Egyptian government refrain from deploying regular [Egyptian Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egyptian_Armed_Forces) units in the eastern part of [Sinai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinai_Peninsula) that directly border Israel (Zone "C"). The Egyptian government then tasked the Central Security Forces to take up the task. The CSF force deployed is effectively a motorized light infantry force without the heavy weapons and equipment.



Tanks in Cairo during the 1986 riots

In 1986, hundreds of third category, low ranking, CSF personnel (soldiers) staged a four-day [mutiny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutiny), in the [Egyptian conscripts riot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986_Egyptian_conscripts_riot). [^cite_note-6] The riot was a reaction to the spread of false rumours among its conscripts that their (three-year) conscription time will be extended by a year. The insurrection was suppressed by the [Egyptian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egyptian_Army). They rampaged through the [suburb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suburb) of [Giza](144514.md) burning some hotels and shops in tourist areas. They also burned dozens of cars and buses. Over 20,000 conscripts were dismissed from service with no benefits, and the agitators received [correctional punishment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Correctional_punishment) after being tried before [State Security Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Security_Court) for [arson](184695.md), [violent riots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rioting), and [insubordination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insubordination) according to [penal code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penal_code). Some reports related that mutiny to a conspiracy against the [Minister of Interior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Interior) in charge by then (Gen. Ahmed Roshdy) due to his policies. After the suppression the government promised to overhaul the force by raising its entry standards, increasing payment and bettering living conditions in their camps.

The Egyptian government subsequently came to rely on the CSF to quell any source of instability within the country and to uphold the emergency laws imposed on [Egypt](8087628.md) since the 1981 assassination of the [President of Egypt](335341.md), [Anwar Sadat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anwar_Sadat). The CSF was especially deployed to tackle armed Islamist insurgents. From the 1990s onwards, CSF has become operationally coordinated with the [State Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Security_Investigations_Service), meaning CSF units can be ordered to deploy by State Security officers, and has been called the "battering ram of State Security".&#x20;

## Deployment and operating procedures

Similar to other central SWAT organizations in other countries, CSF deployment is divided into three parts - Baton, Gas, and Armed. The basic unit formation of the CSF is a [company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Company_\(military_unit\)) which is commanded by a [Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police) [Captain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_captain) (*naqib*) or in some cases a [Major](201920.md) who is a commissioned officer and a graduate of the Police College. A senior [Lieutenant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant) (*mulazim awwal*) (or [Captain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_captain) when the commander is a Major), is usually second-in-command.

A CSF company consists of approximately 150 personnel and is composed of two [Baton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baton_\(law_enforcement\)) [platoons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platoon), a [Gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tear_Gas) [platoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platoon), and an [Armed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AK_47) [platoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platoon). Each [platoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platoon) is also commanded by a [Captain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain) or a [Lieutenant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant) with a [Sergeant](219424.md) (*raqib*) as second-in-command.

Approximately 3 to 4 companies make up a CSF [Battalion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battalion), which is commanded by an officer of full [Colonel](199023.md) rank.

The Baton platoons are equipped with batons and shields, the gas platoon is responsible for the deployment of [Teargas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teargas), and the armed platoon usually carries Assault Rifles - usually the [AK 47](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AK_47) variants, and is organized like a [Light Infantry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light_Infantry) [Rifles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rifles) platoon. However, under emergency laws and martial law, the non-armed CSF units can be converted into armed units, and they are additionally equipped with [LMGs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light_Machine_Gun), [recoilless rifles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recoilless_rifle) and [sniper rifles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sniper_rifle) for anti-insurgency operations.

Also, the three-part deployment of CSF is not applicable in regions like the [North Sinai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Sinai), where all units are armed at all times.

## Recruitment

The Central Security Forces personnel consists of three categories;

- [Commissioned Officers](36301328.md) (graduated from the [Police College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_College) of 4 Academic years), who enter the service with the rank of [Lieutenant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant). They are recruited via national-level exams for Police officers, they receive their commission from the [President](335341.md);

- [Sub-officers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sub-officers) (graduated from [Police School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_academy) of 2 Academic years), they are equivalent to the [Non-commissioned officers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-commissioned_officer) of the [Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egyptian_Army); and

- [Conscripts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conscripts) (of 6 months training) were recruited from the low educated recruits, as the more educated and college graduates usually end up in the different branches of the regular military service.

  [^cite_note-10]

  &#x20;However, in August of 2011, conscripts started being recruited from secondary schools graduates in order to improve the force.

  [^cite_note-11]

  &#x20;Conscripts can later be promoted to the ranks of sub-officers if they continue in the service beyond their compulsory terms and if they get a higher education and sit for the mandatory exams for entering sub-officer ranks. There is high competition among conscripts to extend their service and to become regularized troops, this is because of the high rate of unemployment in Egypt. A conscript enters the force as a "Private Recruit" and is promoted to "Private First Class" after 2 years of service. At the time of demobilization/release from compulsory 3 years' service, many who have passed their 10th grade in school, are at the rank of *Areef* or [Corporal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporal) and can command a [section](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Section_\(military_unit\)) of 10-12 conscripts and 1 riot vehicle. Less educated or illiterate conscripts, however, have to retire at the rank of Private First Class. Those among the Corporals who wish can sit for exams to extend their service and receive the rank of Deputy Sergeant or Junior Sergeant. Most of the conscripts/NCOs are forced to retire at 52 years of age with the rank of [Sergeant](219424.md) with no chance of advancement if they do not have a college degree. Less than 2% of conscripts advance into the commissioned officer ranks.
