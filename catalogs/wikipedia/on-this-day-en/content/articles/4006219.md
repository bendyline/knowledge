**Sitra** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): سترة or سِتْرَة,[^cite_note-1] *As-Sitra*),[^cite_note-2] also known as **Sitrah**[^cite_note-3] or **Sitra Island** (Arabic: جزيرة سترة *Jazīrat Sitrah*),[^cite_note-4] is an [island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island) in Bahrain situated approximately 5 km (3.1 mi) south of the capital, [Manama](20481.md), which is on [Bahrain Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahrain_Island).

## History

The island of Sitra has witnessed various conflicts. One notable conflict occurred in 1782 between the local populace and a group of [Bani Utbah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bani_Utbah) individuals who arrived in Sitra from [Zubarah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zubarah) seeking supplies. This confrontation resulted in casualties on both sides and led to the [1783 Bani Utbah invasion of Bahrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1783_Bani_Utbah_invasion_of_Bahrain), which in turn led to the end of Persian rule in Bahrain.[^cite_note-5]

The first oil infrastructure was built in 1934 by the [Bahrain Petroleum Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahrain_Petroleum_Company), including a draw bridge connecting Sitra to the main island.

### Arab Spring

Further information: [Arab Spring in Bahrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_Spring_in_Bahrain) and [Day of Rage (Bahrain)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Day_of_Rage_\(Bahrain\))

During the Arab Spring in Bahrain, Sitra played a prominent role as a site of significant protests. The island became a focal point for demonstrations, with numerous citizens gathering to express their grievances. The events during this period led to widespread unrest, injuries, and fatalities among the protesters, marking a critical chapter in the island's contemporary history.

## Geography

Situated to the east of [Bahrain Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahrain_Island) in the [Persian Gulf](24761.md), Sitra is positioned south of [Manama](20481.md) and [Nabih Saleh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nabih_Saleh). Its western coastline serves as the boundary for [Tubli Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tubli_Bay). Historically, the island was characterized by [date-palm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Date_palm) [groves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grove_\(nature\)) and agricultural land with several [freshwater springs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freshwater_spring). However, rapid urbanization and development have significantly transformed the island's landscape.

## Demography

Most of the inhabitants of the island live in nine historic villages:&#x20;

- [Wadyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wadyan)

- [Al Kharijiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Kharijiya)

- [Marquban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marquban)

- [Al Garrya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Garrya)

- [Mahazza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahazza)

- [Sufala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sufala)

- [Abul Aish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abul_Aish)

- [Halat Um al-Baidh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halat_Um_al-Baidh) (the location of the yacht club and Al Bandar resort)

- [Al Hamriya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Hamriya?action=edit\&redlink=1)

A massive land reclamation project off the eastern coast of the island called [East Sitra City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Sitra_City) increased the island size by 50% and became the site of a new city.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

## Economy

The island's economy used to be based on agriculture and fishing. The northern section of the island has been turned into an industrial area. [Bapco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bapco_Bahrain) oil storage reservoirs are located in the south. Sitra is also the terminus of the 42-km Dhahran-Sitra [natural gas](22131.md) [pipeline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pipeline_transport), which connects it to [Dahran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dahran) in [Saudi Arabia](349303.md).[^cite_note-8]

Several car and furniture showrooms also make up the new development on the island. The [Sitra Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sitra_Club) is a cultural and sports club for the island.

Today Sitra handles Bahrain's entire petroleum production. It is the location of [Port of Sitra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Sitra?action=edit\&redlink=1). It is also the export center for the oil fields in northeastern [Saudi Arabia](349303.md). [^cite_note-9]

## Education

Sitra is the site of many school campuses such as [Al Noor International School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Noor_International_School) and [Indian School, Bahrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_School,_Bahrain). The [Applied Science University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_Science_University_\(Bahrain\)) is also located here.

## Transportation

The [Sitra Causeway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sitra_Causeway) connects the north of the island to [Nabih Saleh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nabih_Saleh) and to [Umm al Hussam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umm_al_Hussam) ([Manama](20481.md)) on [Bahrain Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahrain_Island). Two small bridges on the south west of Sitra also join Bahrain Island, near the villages of [Ma'ameer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ma'ameer) and [Eker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eker).

## Administration

The southern part of the island belongs to [Southern Governorate](1292836.md) and the northern part to [Capital Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_Governorate,_Bahrain). Between 1990 and 2013 it was part of [Central Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Governorate_\(Bahrain\)) of [Bahrain](18933277.md), but that has since been dissolved. Between 1920 and 1990 it was part of Sitra Municipality.

### Sitra Municipality

This was a [municipality in Bahrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Bahrain) before they were re-organized as Governorates. The Sitra Municipality consisted of the island of Sitra and three villages close to it on the main island of Bahrain: [Ma'ameer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ma'ameer), [Eker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eker) and [Nuwaidrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuwaidrat).[^cite_note-10]
