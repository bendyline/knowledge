**Tehri Garhwal district** is a [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/District_\(India\)) in the hill state of [Uttarakhand](1429154.md), India. Its administrative headquarters is at [New Tehri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Tehri).[^cite_note-2] The district has a population of 618,931 (2011 census), a 2.35% increase over the previous decade. It is surrounded by [Rudraprayag District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudraprayag_District) in the east, [Dehradun District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dehradun_District) in the west, [Uttarkashi District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uttarkashi_District) in the north, and [Pauri Garhwal District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pauri_Garhwal_District) in the south. Tehri Garhwal is a part of the Himalayas.

## Etymology

The name *Tehri* has been derived from *Trihari*, signifying a place that washes away the three types of sins – sins born out of Mansa, Vacha and Karmana or thought, word and deed, respectively. *Garh* in [Hindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindi) means fort.

### Early

Prior to 734 CE, the region was divided into 52 *garh* which were ruled by independent kings. Kanakpal, on his visit to Badrinath, had met the then mightiest king Bhanu Pratap who later married his only daughter to the prince and handed over his kingdom to him. Kanakpal and his descendants gradually conquered all the *garh* and ruled the whole of [Garhwal Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garhwal_Kingdom) for the next 915 years,These *garh* were brought into one province by [maharaja Ajaypal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maharaja_Ajaypal?action=edit\&redlink=1), a great king of [garhwal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garhwal).

### Gorkha Rule

In 1803, the [Gorkhas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorkhas) invaded Garhwal[^cite_note-3] commanded by army generals [Amar Singh Thapa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amar_Singh_Thapa), [Hastidal Shah Chautariya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasti_Dal_Shah), [Bamshah Chautariya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bam_Shah) and [Ranjor Thapa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranajor_Singh_Thapa) against King [Pradyumna Shah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pradyumna_Shah) and his brothers Kunwar Parakrama Shah and Kunwar Pritam Shah. The Garhwal and Gorkha armies fought at [Khurbura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Khurbura) and the Garhwal king was killed. The Gorkhas occupied [Dehradun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dehradun), [Saharanpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saharanpur) and [Shimla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shimla) and later extended their kingdom up to [Kangra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kangra,_Himachal_Pradesh).



1903 map of [United Provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Provinces_\(India\)) showing the boundaries of [Garhwal Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garhwal_Kingdom)

From 1787 to 1812, the Gorkhas invaded and occupied nearly 200 villages which were under the control of the [East India Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honourable_East_India_Company). The British argued with the king but to no avail. The [Gurkha War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gurkha_War) began in 1814, when an army of 8,000 under Major General Marley attacked [Kathmandu](17168.md). Four thousand soldiers under Major General Wood started an operation from [Gorakhpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorakhpur) and 3,500 soldiers attempted to take over Dehradun under Major General Zileswy. The British army captured Dehradun on 30 November 1814 and moved towards Kumaon. The Gorkha commanders [Hastidal Shah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasti_Dal_Shah) and Jayrakha were killed at Vinayathal. The war ended with the [Sugauli Treaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Sugauli), signed on 2 December 1815 and ratified on 4 March 1816 by Gorkha supreme commander Bamshah and thus, British rule started in the hills. The East India Company then merged Kumaon, Dehradun and east Garhwal into the British Empire while west Garhwal was given back to [Sudarshan Shah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudarshan_Shah), then becoming known as Tehri Riyasat.

### Garhwal Kingdom

Main article: [Garhwal kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garhwal_kingdom)



Princely flag of Tehri Garhwal.

Tehri Garhwal, or the Garhwal Kingdom, was a [princely state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princely_state) ruled by the Rajput Parmar (Shah) dynasty. Later, it became a part of the [Punjab Hill States Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjab_Hill_States) of [British India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_India), which consists of the present day Tehri Garhwal District and most of the [Uttarkashi district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uttarkashi_district).[^cite_note-4] In 1901, it had an area of about 4,180 square miles (10,800 km<sup>2</sup>) and a population of 268,885. The ruler was given the title of raja, but after 1913, he was honoured with the title of Maharaja. The ruler was entitled to salutes of 11 guns and had a [privy purse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privy_purse) of rupees 300,000.

King Sudarshan Shah established his capital at Tehri town and afterwards his successors Pratap Shah, Kirti Shah and Narendra Shah established their capital at [Pratap Nagar](https://www.negiuttarkhandi.com/2021/04/tehri-garhwal-tehridam-history-in-hindi.html), [Kirtinagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirtinagar) and [Narendra Nagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narendranagar), respectively. Their dynasty ruled over this region from 1815 to 1949. During the Quit India Movement people of this region actively worked for the independence of the country. Ultimately, when the country was declared independent in 1947, the inhabitants of Tehri Riyasat (Tehri State) started their movement to free themselves from the clutches of the maharaja.

Due to this movement, the situation became out of his control and it was difficult for him to rule over the region. Consequently, the 60th king of [Panwar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panwar) Vansh, [Manabendra Shah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manabendra_Shah), accepted the sovereignty of the Indian government. Thus, in August 1949, Tehri Riyasat was merged into [Uttar Pradesh](231623.md) and was given the status of a new district, the Tehri Garhwal district.[^cite_note-5] Subsequently, on 24 February 1960, the state government separated its one [tehsil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehsil) which was given the status of a separate district named [Uttarkashi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uttarkashi). The former royal palace of the Maharaja of Tehri Garhwal at [Narendra Nagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narendranagar), now houses the [Ananda In the Himalayas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ananda_in_the_Himalayas) spa, established 2000.[^cite_note-6]

### Modern developments

In the 1960s, Tehri Garhwal extended much farther east than it currently does.[^cite_note-7] In 1997, much of the eastern portion of Tehri Garhwal was detached and merged with portions of the [Pauri Garhwal district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pauri_Garhwal_district) and the [Chamoli district](1430913.md) to form the [Rudraprayag district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudraprayag_district).

Tehri Lake\
With a surface area of 52 km<sup>2</sup>, a man-made lake in [New Tehri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Tehri), is the largest [lake in Uttarakhand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_lakes_of_India). It has good options for Adventure Sports and various water sports like Boating, Banana Boat, Bandwagon Boat, Jet Ski, Water Skiing, Para-sailing, Kayaking.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

## Economy

Tehri Garhwal economy revolves around [Public sector undertakings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_sector_undertakings_in_India), Agriculture and Tourism.&#x20;

## Crops

Dry season crops include wheat, barley, masoor, Bengal and red gram, rapeseed and mustard, and pea. Wet season crops include rice, barnyard millet, finger millet, black gram, sesame and soybean. Various fruits and spices grow in the district also.[^cite_note-11]

## Administrative setup

The district of Tehri Garhwal is divided into two subdivisions: Kirti Nagar and Tehri-Pratap Nagar. It has seven tehsils, one sub-tehsil, nine blocks, two municipalities and four town area committees. The district covers 76 Nagar Panchayats and 928 gram panchayats. It has 1,847 revenue villages and 2,508 clusters.

According to the [2011 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_census_of_India) Tehri Garhwal district has a [population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_of_India) of 618,931,[^cite_note-districtcensus-13] roughly equal to the nation of [Solomon Islands](265083.md)[^cite_note-cia-14] or the US state of [Vermont](32578.md).[^cite_note-15] This gives it a ranking of 520th in India (out of a total of [688](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_India)).[^cite_note-districtcensus-13] The district has a population density of 169 inhabitants per square kilometre (440/sq mi).[^cite_note-districtcensus-13] Its [population growth rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_planning_in_India) over the decade 2001–2011 was 1.93%.[^cite_note-districtcensus-13] Tehri Garhwal has a [sex ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_ratio) of 1078 [females](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women_in_India) for every 1000 males,[^cite_note-districtcensus-13] and a [literacy rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literacy_in_India) of 75.1%. Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes make up 16.50% and 0.14% of the population respectively.[^cite_note-districtcensus-13]

The major first language of the district is [Garhwali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garhwali_language), accounting for 90.5% of the population according to the 2011 census. [Hindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindi) is widely used as a [lingua franca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lingua_franca), but is the first language of only 6% of the people. Other languages spoken include [Jaunsari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaunsari_language) (0.98%) and [Nepali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepali_language) (0.95%).[^cite_note-census2011-C16-17]
