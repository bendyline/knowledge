The **Joint Chiefs of Staff of the Republic of Korea** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 대한민국 합동참모본부, [Hanja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanja): 大韓民國 合同參謀本部) is a group of chiefs from each major branch of the armed services in the [South Korean military](27027.md). Unlike the [United States](3434750.md)' [counterpart](364458.md) which is primarily advisory, the [Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairman_of_the_Joint_Chiefs_of_Staff_\(South_Korea\)) has actual operational control over all military personnel of South Korea's armed forces. The National Command Authority runs from the [President](98630.md) and the [Minister of National Defense](5985967.md) to the Chairman and then to the Operational Commands of the service branches, bypassing the headquarters of each service branch. Currently there are five Operational Commands in the [Army](1515574.md), two in the [Navy](2284275.md) (including the [Marine Corps](2052682.md)) and one in the [Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Korea_Air_Force).

## History

It was created in May 1954 and assumed its current name in 1963, though there had been the Supreme Command of the Armed Forces dating from 1948.[^cite_note-1]

All (regular) members of the Joint Chiefs of Staffs are [four-star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four-star_rank) generals and admirals, though the deputy chairman in the past has been a three-star [lieutenant general](201916.md) or vice admiral intermittently. Traditionally, the chairman is chosen from the Army (with one previous and one current exceptions as October 2013) while the deputy chairman is selected from either the Navy or the Air Force.[^cite_note-2]

The [Commandant of the Marine Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commandant_of_the_Republic_of_Korea_Marine_Corps), legally subordinate to the [Chief of Naval Operations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Naval_Operations_\(South_Korea\)) of the Republic of Korea Navy, may attend Joint Chiefs of Staff meetings when discussing matters related to the Marine Corps.[^cite_note-3]
