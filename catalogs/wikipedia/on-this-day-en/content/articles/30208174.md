[Airbus A320](164933.md), an aircraft widely used in domestic flights.

A **domestic flight** is a form of [commercial flight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commercial_flight) within [civil aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_aviation) where the departure and the arrival take place in the same [country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country).[^cite_note-1]

Airports serving domestic flights only are known as [domestic airports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domestic_airport).

Domestic flights are generally cheaper and shorter than most [international flights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_flight). Some international flights may be cheaper than domestic ones due to the short distance between the pair of cities in different countries, and also because domestic flights might, in smaller countries, mainly be used by high paying business travellers, while leisure travellers use road or rail domestically.

As far as security checks is concerned, the security check only verifies the traveler's identity.

Domestic flights are the only sector of aviation not exhibiting a global long term growth trend due to many smaller countries increasingly replacing short domestic routes with [high speed rail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_speed_rail); that said, most of the [busiest air routes in the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Busiest_air_routes_in_the_world) are domestic flights.[^cite_note-\:0-2]

Some smaller countries, like [Singapore](27318.md), have no scheduled domestic flights. Medium-sized countries like the [Netherlands](21148.md) have very few domestic flights; most of them are merely a leg between small regional airports such as [Groningen Airport Eelde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groningen_Airport_Eelde), [Maastricht Aachen Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maastricht_Aachen_Airport) and [Rotterdam The Hague Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotterdam_The_Hague_Airport) to pick up passengers from various parts of the country before proceeding to international destinations.[^cite_note-Tongeren-3] In June 2013, Dutch MP [Liesbeth van Tongeren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liesbeth_van_Tongeren) ([GreenLeft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GroenLinks), previously [Greenpeace](12233.md) Netherlands director) proposed to prohibit domestic flights in the Netherlands with the argument that they are needlessly inefficient, polluting and expensive, but Environment Secretary [Wilma Mansveld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilma_Mansveld) ([Labour Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labour_Party_\(Netherlands\))) said such a ban would violate EU regulations that allow airlines to fly domestically.[^cite_note-Tongeren-3] In larger countries such as France, [Air Tahiti Nui Flight 64](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World's_longest_domestic_flight), which was conducted between [Faa'a](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faa%CA%BCa_International_Airport) in [Tahiti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tahiti), [Polynesia](10737.md) and [Paris](22989.md) in [Metropolitan France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_France) in 2020, was the world's longest domestic flight, as Polynesia is an overseas collectivity of [France](5843419.md). The aircraft traveled over fifteen and a half thousand miles. Some political debaters propose [short-haul flight bans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Short-haul_flight_ban) in a number of countries.
