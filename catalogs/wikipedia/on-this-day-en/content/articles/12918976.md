**Bani Suheila** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): بني سهيلا) is a municipality in the [Gaza Strip](12047.md), in the [Khan Yunis Governorate](13900254.md) of the [State of Palestine](241405.md). The town is located 2 km (1.2 mi) east of the city of [Khan Younis](101330.md). As of 2017, Bani Suheila had a population of 41,439 people.[^cite_note-PrelimCensus2017-1]

## History

The history of these towns goes back to [Canaanite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canaan), [Philistine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philistine), and [Roman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Rome) times. Before 1948, these towns boasted numerous khans (inns) for travelers. [Khan Yunis](101330.md) owes its name to a [Mamluk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mamluk) official who built its large khan in the 14th century.

For centuries, the coastal area was a main thoroughfare between [Egypt](8087628.md) and the Mediterranean coast, used by traders and conquering armies alike. The trade route through Gaza to Egypt brought great economic advantage to the area. In previous centuries, the lack of restricting borders enabled unobstructed communication and travel and the intermixing of influences and styles, especially among the [Bedouin](61770.md) tribes. This rich agricultural area prospered by settled [Bedouin](61770.md) tribes that became active in regional trade on routes connecting [Egypt](8087628.md), the [Levant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levant), and Arabia. Many families benefited from the increase in regional trade and became large land owners during this time. During [Ottoman](22278.md) rule, the Al Qarra clan became the largest landowning family in southern Gaza due to their vast trade networks.

Bani Suheila was marked *Maatadieh Village* on [Jacotin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Jacotin)’s map surveyed during [Napoleon's 1799 invasion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_campaign_in_Egypt_and_Syria).[^cite_note-2]

In 1838, [Edward Robinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Robinson_\(scholar\)) called it Beni *Sehileh*, located in Gaza.[^cite_note-3] In 1863, the French explorer [Victor Guérin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Gu%C3%A9rin) found Bani Suheila to have about 1,300 inhabitants,[^cite_note-4] while an official Ottoman village list of about 1870 showed 209 houses and a population of 440, though the population count included men, only.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

As recorded in 1886, Bani Suheila was a large village containing 120 huts, partly built of stone, partly of [adobe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adobe), and surrounded by gardens of watermelons, figs, palms, jummez, apricots and legumes. In the north a good but deep well, worked by a camel, supplied drinking water. Near the town, in Sheikh Yusuf several ancient remains, including small twisted marble columns and building stones were found.[^cite_note-7]

### British era

In the [1922 census of Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1922_census_of_Palestine) conducted by the [British Mandate authorities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_Palestine), Bani Suheila had a population of 1,043 inhabitants, all Muslim,[^cite_note-Census1922-8] increasing in the [1931 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1931_census_of_Palestine) to 2,063, still all Muslims, in 406 houses.[^cite_note-Census1931-9]

At the end of the Mandate period, in the [1945 statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village_Statistics,_1945), Bani Suheila had a population of 3,220, all Muslims,[^cite_note-DoS1945-10] with 11,128 [dunams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunam) of land, according to an official land and population survey.[^cite_note-Hadawi45-11] Of this, 54 dunams were for plantations and irrigable land, 10,639 used for cereals,[^cite_note-12] while 97 dunams were built-up land.[^cite_note-13]

### Post 1948

During the night of August 31, 1955, Israeli armed forces attacked Bani Suheila.[^cite_note-14] On April 5, 1956, Israeli artillery shelled the town.[^cite_note-15]

According to the [Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Central_Bureau_of_Statistics) the population of Bani Suheila had grown to 32,800 people in mid-year 2006.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] During the [Gaza war](74998519.md) in December 2023, the [IDF](39237.md) took full control of the city.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19]

## Demography

Some of its residents are from the Arab Jaram clan, formerly residing in [Majdal Yaba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majdal_Yaba) and [Attil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attil), while others trace their origins to [Egypt](8087628.md).[^cite_note-20]
