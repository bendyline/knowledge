[Diplomatic relations](28165911.md) between the [Netherlands](21148.md) and [Iran](14653.md) ([Persia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Name_of_Iran)) have existed since the 17th century. However, the relationship changed significantly after the [Iranian Revolution](347268.md).



Embassy of Iran in The Hague

## Development

### Beginning the relation in 17th century

Political relations between Persia and the Netherlands [started](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch%E2%80%93Safavid_relations) under Shah [Abbas I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbas_I_of_Persia). In 1626, the first Persian ambassador to Holland, [Mousa Beig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mousa_Beig?action=edit\&redlink=1) ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): موسی بیگ) presented his credentials at the [States-General of the Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States-General_of_the_Netherlands). The Dutch ambassador Jan Smidt went to [Isfahan](223149.md) and met with Shah [Safi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safi_of_Persia) in 1629.[^cite_note-1]

### 20th century



Prince [Bernhard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Bernhard_of_Lippe-Biesterfeld) of the Netherlands together with Shah [Mohammad Reza Pahlavi](20065598.md) and Queen [Farah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farah_Pahlavi) of Iran. Tehran, 1970s

In the 1960s and 1970s, the Persian and Dutch royal families made various state visits to each other's countries.

### Relations with the Islamic Republic

The Netherlands has condemned Iran over its [nuclear program](721807.md).

In 2011, the Dutch Foreign Minister announced the suspension of official relations between the Netherlands and Iran, after the Dutch-Iranian [Zahra Bahrami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zahra_Bahrami) was executed in Iran.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

On June 7, 2018, the Netherlands expelled two Iranian diplomats accredited to the Iranian embassy.[^cite_note-4] In response, Iran summoned the Dutch ambassador in July 2018.[^cite_note-5]

In January 2019, the government of the Netherlands accused Iran of having arranged the assassinations of two Dutch nationals of Iranian origin: Mohammad-Reza Kolahi in 2015, and Ahmad Molla Nissi in 2017. The incident caused outcry both in the Netherlands and internationally; EU responded with sanctions against Iranian intelligence.[^cite_note-6] Iranian officials in turn accused the Netherlands of supporting the [People's Mujahedin of Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Mujahedin_of_Iran) with its members in western Europe,[^cite_note-7] who [Mohammad-Reza Kolahi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad-Reza_Kolahi) is believed to be a part of.[^cite_note-8]

In January 2024, the Dutch government summoned the Iranian ambassador to the Netherlands following the death of a Dutch baby in an attack by Iran on [Erbil](24534207.md).[^cite_note-9]

On January 28 2026, the Iranian authorities searched the [diplomatic baggage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplomatic_bag) of a Dutch diplomat at the [airport in Tehran](716542.md) in contravention of their obligations under the [Vienna convention on diplomatic relations](32753.md). The Iranian authorities stated that they seized [Starlink](45111627.md) ground stations and other [satellite phones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satellite_phone), which are illegal in Iran, from the baggage. On February 24 2026, after Iranian media published surveillance video of the incident, the government of The Netherlands summoned the Iranian ambassador to the Netherlands.[^cite_note-10] A day later on February 25, the Iranian government summoned the Dutch ambassador in response.[^cite_note-11]

## Trade

The Netherlands was formerly one of Iran's leading trade partners in Europe. At least 65 Dutch companies have economic ties with the Islamic Republic. In spite of economic sanctions imposed by the [United States](3434750.md) and the [European Union](9317.md), [Shell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Dutch_Shell), a UK-registered Anglo-Dutch oil company continues to buy billions in crude oil from Iran each year.[^cite_note-12]

## Political-economic conflicts

In 1974, Iranian students occupied the Iran Embassy in [Wassenaar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wassenaar), out of protest against suppression and executions in Iran.[^cite_note-13] Considering the 100 guilder fine too low a punishment for the students, the Iranian government suspended all imports from the Netherlands for a while.[^cite_note-14]

In 2008, [Alaeddin Boroujerdi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alaeddin_Boroujerdi), head of Iran's Majlis National Security and Foreign Policy Commission, warned the Netherlands not to air [Geert Wilders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geert_Wilders)' anti-Muslim film *[Fitna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fitna_\(film\))*.[^cite_note-15]

Iran has criticized the Netherlands for funding [Radio Zamaneh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Zamaneh). During the 2009 public unrest and demonstrations in Iran, [Majid Ghahremani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majid_Ghahremani?action=edit\&redlink=1), Iranian ambassador to the Netherlands, accused the Dutch government of interfering in Iran's internal affairs. A Dutch foreign ministry spokeswoman said subsidies to the radio station would be continued, with the aim of improving the situation of [human rights in Iran](16652449.md).[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17]

## Cultural relations

In 2011, Iran established a series of cultural exhibits showing pictures featuring Iran's historical monuments and tourist attractions.[^cite_note-18]

## Resident diplomatic missions

- Iran has an embassy in [The Hague](30269.md).

- the Netherlands has an embassy in [Tehran](57654.md).

## Notes

- The Dutch embassy in Iran is temporarily accredited at the embassy in [Baku](4566.md), Azerbaijan from March to June 2026.
