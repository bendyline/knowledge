**Sheikh Sharif Sheikh Ahmed** ([Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language): *Sheekh Shariif Sheekh Axmed*, [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): شيخ شريف شيخ أحمد; born 25 July 1965) is a Somali [politician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politician) who served as the 7th [President of Somalia](516101.md) from 2009 to 2012. Before his presidency, he became the [Chairman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairman) of the [Islamic Courts Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Courts_Union) (ICU) from 2004 to 2007 and the [Alliance of Re-liberation of Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alliance_for_the_Re-liberation_of_Somalia) (ARS) from 2007 to 2009.

In 2004, Sharif became the head of the ICU. By mid-2006, the organization had wrested control of [Mogadishu](60154.md) from [warlords](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warlord) and expanded territorial control across much of Somalia. This rapid rise prompted a full-scale [Ethiopian invasion in late 2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_in_Somalia_\(2006%E2%80%932009\)), leading to the ICU's governing body to collapse. In the ensuing [military occupation](816214.md) and Islamist [insurgency](589841.md), Sharif assumed leadership of the [Alliance for the Re-liberation of Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alliance_for_the_Re-liberation_of_Somalia) (ARS). Following Ethiopia's troop withdrawal in January 2009, he was elected President of the [Transitional Federal Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_Federal_Government_of_Somalia) (TFG).

Sharif's TFG administration faced fierce opposition from militant groups like [Al-Shabaab](8726463.md) and [Hizbul Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hizbul_Islam), which demanded the [withdrawal of African Union troops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Union_Mission_to_Somalia) that had been deployed following the invasion and the full implementation of [Sharia](28840.md) law. In early 2009, his government nearly fell to an [insurgent](589841.md) offensive in Mogadishu.[^cite_note-\:0-1] However, by 2011, his administration—with [African Union](10634933.md) support—managed to drive Al-Shabaab out of the capital.[^cite_note-2] Despite these gains, Somalia under Sharif's leadership continued to rank as a [failed state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Failed_state), struggling with limited popular support and widespread governance challenges.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] By the end of his term, the TFG transitioned into the [Federal Government of Somalia](36817379.md) (FGS). In the [2012 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Somali_presidential_election), Sharif was defeated by [Hassan Sheikh Mohamud](36973989.md).

After his presidency, Sharif founded the Islamic democratic [Himilo Qaran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Himilo_Qaran) political party and during the [2022 Somali Elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Somali_presidential_election) established the [Forum for National Parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forum_for_National_Parties). He also serves as chairperson of the Council of Presidential Candidates of Somalia. In 2025, Sharif co-established as the leader of the Somali Salvation Forum in Mogadishu.[^cite_note-5]

## Early life and career

### Education

Born on 25 July 1965 in [Mahaday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahaday), Somalia a town in the south of the [Middle Shabelle](185138.md) region where he studied [Islam](6037917.md), [Arabic language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language) and [memorized the Qur'an](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hafiz_\(Quran\)) as a child. He began his education at the Sheikh Sufi Institute, which was associated with [Al-Azhar University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Azhar_University) in [Egypt](8087628.md). He studied at [Libyan](17633.md) and [Sudanese](27421.md) universities in the mid-1990s, where he earned a bachelor's degree in [Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law) and Islamic [Shariah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shariah).[^cite_note-BBC_20090203-6]

### Law career

Main article: [Islamic Courts Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Courts_Union)

Sheikh Sharif departed for Somalia in 2000 at a time when Somalia was under the control of warlords and friends could not visit each other in [Mogadishu](60154.md) due to clan separation and mistrust. The legacy of the civil war was apparent everywhere in Somalia and Sharif had ambitions of saving his country and his people. He established Al Shuruuq Agency, a cultural and heritage institution and the Federation of Adolescents in Mogadishu which facilitated social interaction for young Somalis who had never before crossed the boundaries formed by the warlords. As a result, the residents of Mogadishu began to cross the lines where friends, schoolmates, and elders can come together and express themselves in a positive way. Sharif became a regional attorney of his home province, middle Shabelle, where he was elected chairman of a provincial court in Jowhar between 2001 and 2002.[^cite_note-BBC_20090203-6]

Armed groups in the Somali capital who exploited the disintegration of the central government had been responsible for countless kidnappings and killings. The court was established through a campaign which Sharif led to secure the release of an abducted child. The court was successful in securing the release of the child and other abductees as well as looted vehicles. The court went on to suppress the violence of gangs and warlords which was prevalent throughout the capital. Subsequently, all five Islamic courts united and Sharif, who had always been seen as the moderate face of the [Islamic Courts Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Courts_Union), was elected to become the chair.[^cite_note-SOMALIAS-MODERATE-ISLAMIST-LEADER-7]

Somalia was already beginning to see swift political and economic changes under his first six months of leadership. Warlords and their influence were eliminated from the city with the help of the people's support which made it possible for the first time in sixteen years to reopen [Mogadishu International Airport](5613319.md) and [Mogadishu Port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Mogadishu).[^cite_note-8] Top UN officials have referred to this period as a 'Golden era' in the history of Somali politics.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

At the time the [Transitional Federal Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_federal_government_of_Somalia) which was established in Mbagathi, [Nairobi](21482.md) in 2004, was a fragile body which was divided and weak. The Ethiopian army invaded Somalia claiming that it was trying to help the Transitional Federal Government and overthrew the [Islamic Court Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Courts_Union). Sharif met with the US Ambassador to Kenya for talks concerning cooperation with the Transitional Federal Government, after which he left for [Yemen](350939.md) to meet with other former Islamic Courts Union members.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]

### Pre-presidential political career

See also: [Alliance for the Re-liberation of Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alliance_for_the_Re-liberation_of_Somalia)

As an exiled opposition leader Sharif, the former leader of the Islamic Courts Union, was in search of a headquarters for establishing a new political party, the [Alliance for the Re-liberation of Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alliance_for_the_Re-liberation_of_Somalia). In September 2007 nearly 500 delegates gathered in [Asmara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asmara), [Eritrea](17238590.md), including [Islamists](15012.md), parliamentarians, civil society and the diaspora and adopted a constitution. Sharif's party the Alliance for the Re-liberation of Somalia signed a [peace treaty](404505.md) with the [Transitional Federal Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_Federal_Government) on 9 June 2008 in [Djibouti](17207794.md) called the [Djibouti Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djibouti_Agreement?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-13]

Many members of the Islamic Courts Union and other resistance factions such as Al-Shabaab were alienated by Sharif Sheikh Ahmed's conduct during the Djibouti negotiations, particularly after he signed the peace deal without consulting field commanders-despite the continued presence of Ethiopian troops. ICU personnel in Mogadishu during this period viewed Sharif's behavior as unprincipled.[^cite_note-14]

## President of Somalia

The Sharif administration successfully brought the [Federal Government of Somalia](36817379.md) through [transitional status](598010.md) following the collapse of the previous governing administration in 1991.

His administration is credited with developing [Somalia's constitution](37022532.md) and setting up key institutions such as the [police force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_Police_Force), the [military](27366.md) and the [Court system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judiciary_of_Somalia).[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] He established the [Somali National Army](3972951.md), opened the [main sea port of Mogadishu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Mogadishu) and relaunched the [central bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Bank_of_Somalia).[^cite_note-17]

Under Sharif's leadership, the [Transitional Federal Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_Federal_Government) succeeded in driving out [Al Shabaab](8726463.md) from the capital city and its surroundings, establishing security, peace and reconciliation through the difficult transitional period.[^cite_note-18]

### Presidential Election 2009

Main article: [2009 Somali presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Somali_presidential_election)

After winning the election in the early hours of 31 January 2009, Sharif was sworn in later in the day at the Kempinski hotel in [Djibouti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djibouti_\(city\)).[^cite_note-19] Sharif vowed to form a broad-based government and invited all armed groups in the war-ravaged Horn of Africa nation to join the UN-sponsored reconciliation effort. [Ahmedou Ould-Abdallah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmedou_Ould-Abdallah), the UN's special envoy for Somalia, praised the "transparent" presidential vote. "We are finally seeing progress from the hard work by all sides to create an inclusive parliament," Ould-Abdallah said in a statement.[^cite_note-20]

### Arab League summit

In March 2009, Sharif attended the Arab summit in Bagdad to get support for his fight against Al-Shabaab, and establishment of security, peace and reconciliation.[^cite_note-21]

### Press conference with Hillary Clinton

On 6 August 2009, Sharif and [US Secretary of State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Secretary_of_State), [Hillary Clinton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hillary_Clinton) held a joint press conference in [Nairobi](21482.md), [Kenya](188171.md) discussing the challenges facing [Somalia](27358.md), and the possibility of achieving peace, stability and reconciliation for the Somalis.[^cite_note-22]

### Center for Strategic and International Studies

On 30 September 2009, the [Center for Strategic and International Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_for_Strategic_and_International_Studies) (C.S.I.S.) in Washington hosted a statesman's forum with Sharif on Somalia promoting peace through international engagement.[^cite_note-23]

### Speech at United Nations headquarters in New York

On 10 October 2009, Sharif presented a speech at the General Debate of the 64th Session of the [General Assembly](31957.md) at the [United Nations headquarters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_headquarters) in New York on promoting peace, security, reconciliation and the delivery of humanitarian assistance.[^cite_note-24]

### 2010 Istanbul Conference on Somalia

In May 2010, at the Istanbul Conference on Somalia, U.N. Secretary-General [Ban Ki-moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ban_Ki-moon) said that the only chance to bring stability to Somalia was to support the government of Sharif. He specifically voiced his support for Sharif himself, saying that he needed to be in power and to strengthen his leadership.[^cite_note-25]

### Speech at the United Nations

On 25 September 2010, Sharif's speech at the [United Nations](31769.md) was about promoting regional stability, security and reconstruction in Somalia.[^cite_note-26]

### Appointment of Prime Minister

Sharif appointed former First Secretary of the Somali embassy in Washington, [Mohamed Abdullahi Mohamed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Abdullahi_Mohamed), as the new Prime Minister of Somalia in October 2010 after the resignation of [Omar Abdirashid Ali Sharmarke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omar_Abdirashid_Ali_Sharmarke).[^cite_note-Ajspanpm-27] United Nations Secretary-General [Ban Ki-moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ban_Ki-moon) issued a statement commending the Somali leadership for having reached a consensus on procedural arrangements that facilitated a transparent and consultative confirmation of the new Premier.[^cite_note-Uncenspmtfg-28]

### Al Shabaab withdrawal from Mogadishu



Sharif in 2011

On 6 August 2011, Sharif announced that his military had defeated Islamist rebels battling to overthrow his Western-backed government after [Al Shabaab](8726463.md) began withdrawing fighters from the capital Mogadishu.[^cite_note-29]

### Kampala Accord

Main article: [Kampala accord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kampala_accord)

The Kampala Accord was an agreement made in [Kampala](56689.md), [Uganda](31816.md) in line with the [Transitional Federal Charter of the Somali Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_Federal_Charter_of_the_Somali_Republic) to bring an end to the [transitional](598010.md) phase of the [Transitional Federal Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_Federal_Government) on 20 August 2011. It was signed on 9 June 2011 by HE Sharif Sheikh Ahmed, President of the Transitional Federal Government, Hon [Sharif Hassan Sheikh Aden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharif_Hassan_Sheikh_Aden), Speaker of the Transitional Federal Parliament, H.E. [Yoweri Kaguta Museveni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoweri_Kaguta_Museveni), President of the Republic of Uganda and [Augustine Mahiga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustine_Mahiga), Special Representative of the Secretary General of the United Nations.[^cite_note-30]

### AMISOM visit

In September 2011, Sharif visited Burundian peacekeepers serving in his country in a show of support for [African Union Mission to Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Union_Mission_to_Somalia) (AMISOM). "I came to pay a visit to the AMISOM peacekeepers and the Somali soldiers," the president told reporters before heading back to Villa Somalia after meetings ended. "The intention was to find out how the soldiers are doing and how ready they are to help the Somali people and the government. As you can see they are really prepared and have high spirits to end the problems affecting the Somali people. The two forces have a good working relationship, and we hope their partnership will result in a victory for our country and removal of the enemy."[^cite_note-31]

### Operation Linda Nchi

Main article: [Operation Linda Nchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Linda_Nchi)

Operation Linda Nchi ("Protect the country"; Swahili: Linda Nchi) is the codename for a co-ordinated military operation between the Kenyan military and the Somali military that began on 16 October 2011, when troops from Kenya crossed the border into the conflict zones of southern Somalia.

### Kampala Talks

In November 2011, Sharif met with Ugandan counterpart Yoweri Museveni to discuss security in Somalia and in the eastern Africa region. Sharif was accompanied by a number of ministers and legislators.[^cite_note-32]

### Garowe Conference

In February 2012, Sharif and other Somali government officials met in the northeastern town of [Garowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garowe) to discuss post-transition political arrangements. After extensive deliberations attended by regional actors and international observers, the conference ended in a signed agreement between the President, Prime Minister [Abdiweli Mohamed Ali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdiweli_Gaas), Speaker of Parliament [Sharif Hassan Sheikh Adan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharif_Hassan_Sheikh_Adan), Puntland President [Abdirahman Mohamed Farole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdirahman_Farole), Galmudug President [Mohamed Ahmed Alin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Ahmed_Alin) and [Ahlu Sunna Waljama'a](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahlu_Sunna_Waljama'a) representative Khalif Abdulkadir Noor stipulating that: a) a new 225 member [bicameral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicameral) parliament would be formed, with a lower house and an upper house seating 54 senators; b) 30% of the National Constituent Assembly (NCA) is earmarked for women; c) the President is to be appointed via a constitutional election; and d) the Prime Minister is selected by the President and he/she then names his/her Cabinet.[^cite_note-Sgcctac-33][^cite_note-Sgcc-34]

### London Conference on Somalia

Sharif spoke at the [London Conference on Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Somalia_Conference) on 23 February 2012. Sharif said: "To all of you who have exerted every effort so that you could put an end to the suffering of the Somali people, we would like to say that we appreciate this wonderful effort." US Secretary of State [Hillary Clinton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hillary_Clinton), UN Secretary-General [Ban Ki-moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ban_Ki-moon) and representatives from over 40 governments attended the London Conference on Somalia, to discuss the rebuilding of Somalia and the tackling of piracy, terrorism and famine. [^cite_note-35]

### 2012 Istanbul Conference on Somalia

On 1 June 2012, Sharif and [UN Secretary-General](162415.md) [Ban Ki-moon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ban_Ki-moon) attended the Istanbul Conference on Somalia to promote global reconstruction efforts and to back up on going stabilisation efforts.[^cite_note-36]

### New constitution



Sharif in [Balad, Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balad,_Somalia) in 2012

On 23 June 2012, the [Somali Federal Government](36817379.md) and regional leaders met again and approved a [draft constitution](37022532.md) after several days of deliberation. The National Constituent Assembly overwhelmingly passed the new constitution on 1 August, with 96% voting for it, 2% against it, and 2% abstaining.[^cite_note-Gsaacai-37]

### Dubai Charter

The second UAE counter piracy conference was held at the Madinat Jumeirah in Dubai on 27–28 June. It was attended by more than 400 delegates including Foreign Ministers, the United Nations' International Maritime Organisation, industry leaders, welfare organisations and academic experts. The Conference Keynote Address was given by Sharif from the Transitional Federal Government of Somalia. The event concluded with a strong call for a "comprehensive approach" against pirates that includes national governments, international organisations and the global maritime industry.

On 28 June 2012, Sharif signed a cooperation deal with [Ahmed Mahamoud Silanyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmed_Mahamoud_Silanyo), President of the separatist [Somaliland](51425.md) region in northwestern Somalia. Referred to as the Dubai Charter, the agreement calls for greater coordination between Somalia's various political units and is part of broader international reconciliation efforts among all Somali parties. The presidents of the autonomous [Puntland](233569.md) and [Galmudug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galmudug) regions as well as the UAE Minister of State for Foreign Affairs also attended the signing.[^cite_note-Amsscdr-38]

### 2012 Presidential Elections

Main article: [2012 Somali presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Somali_presidential_election)

Sharif's participation in the presidential contest and his acceptance of the results of his defeat was a demonstration of democracy coming to Somalia. He was widely praised for the peaceful handover of power. It was the first such transfer witnessed in Somalia in four decades.[^cite_note-Gsfpehsmap-39]

## Post-presidency (2012–present)

Sharif has been called 'The Father of Modern Somalia' due to his achievements during his presidential term.[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41]

### 2017 presidential elections



Sharif (right) and [Hassan Sheikh Mohamud](36973989.md) (left), flanking [Mohamed Abdullahi Mohamed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Abdullahi_Mohamed) at Mohamed's inauguration as president in 2017

Main article: [2017 Somali presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Somali_presidential_election)

In the 2017 presidential election, [Mohamed Abdullahi Mohamed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Abdullahi_Mohamed), who Sharif had earlier instated as Prime Minister, succeeded [Hassan Sheikh Mohamud](36973989.md) as [President of Somalia](516101.md).[^cite_note-theguardianformerpmanddualuscitizen-42] In the second and final round of parliamentary votes, Sharif received 45 votes to Mohamed's 185.[^cite_note-Africanews2017-43]

### Himilo Qaran

Main article: [Himilo Qaran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Himilo_Qaran)

Sharif founded the Himilo Qaran party in December 2018 ahead of the 2020 elections.[^cite_note-44] In 2019 Himilo Qaran joined the Forum for National Parties an alliance of Somali political parties.[^cite_note-45] In October 2019 Himilo Qaran joined the alliance of Somali political parties, the Forum for National Parties.

### Forum for National Parties

Main article: [Forum for National Parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forum_for_National_Parties)



Sharif Sheikh Ahmed holding a video conference with the Forum for National Parties

In September 2019 Sharif founded the [Forum for National Parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forum_for_National_Parties) an alliance of six political parties including his own party [Himilo Qaran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Himilo_Qaran) and the [UPD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_for_Peace_and_Development_Party) which is the party of his successor [Hassan Sheikh Mohamud](36973989.md). The FNP alliance has united the leadership of the country to work together toward resolving Somalia's political and security issues.[^cite_note-46]

Since it was formed, the FNP has campaigned on various issues which include [humanitarian aid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forum_for_National_Parties#Humanitarian_Aid), [dialogue with FGS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forum_for_National_Parties#Dialogue_between_FNP_and_FGS), and the [Somali election model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forum_for_National_Parties#Election_Model).

### Council of Presidential Candidates

Main article: [Council of Presidential Candidates in Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Presidential_Candidates_in_Somalia?action=edit\&redlink=1)

In November 2020 Sharif was elected as chairman of the Council of Presidential Candidates, which has been active in holding the Somali government to account on the procedures concerning of the upcoming elections.[^cite_note-auto-47] The CPC has worked on resolving issues such as security issues and the election impasse in the 2021 elections.

### 2022 presidential elections

Main article: [2022 Somali presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Somali_presidential_election)

In January 2019 Sharif announced his intention to stand for the [2021 presidential election](1390950.md) on the ticket of his political party [Himilo Qaran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Himilo_Qaran), citing the "inability of the current leadership to restore peace and security" against [al-Shabaab](8726463.md) militants who still control large rural regions of the country.[^cite_note-goobjoog-48][^cite_note-49] As a group of 14 presidential candidates met in Mogadishu for a six-day conference in November 2020 the [Council of Presidential Candidates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Presidential_Candidates_in_Somalia?action=edit\&redlink=1) was formed and Sharif was nominated as chairman.[^cite_note-auto-47]

On 15 May 2022, Sharif received 39 of 328 parliamentary votes cast in the first round of voting, placing him in fifth place and not proceeding to the second round.[^cite_note-50] Hassan Sheikh Mohamud was the winner.[^cite_note-51]

## Recognition

### Fellowship, Boston University

Sharif is beneficiary of the former-Presidents-In-Residence fellowship initiated by [Boston University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_University) in Massachusetts, USA. The scholarship is administered by the African Presidential Archives and Research Center (APARC). It hosts programs including debates on public policy to extend knowledge of the complexities and resources in Africa.[^cite_note-52]
