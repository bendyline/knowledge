**Gorton and Denton** is a [constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constituencies_of_the_Parliament_of_the_United_Kingdom) in [Greater Manchester](79819.md) represented in the [House of Commons](13828.md) of the [UK Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Parliament), which came into being for the [2024 UK general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_UK_general_election), following the [2023 review of Westminster constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_review_of_Westminster_constituencies).[^cite_note-2]

The [Member of Parliament](3776360.md) (MP) is [Hannah Spencer](82277289.md) of the [Green Party](79787.md), who won the [2026 Gorton and Denton by-election](82184590.md) on 26 February to fill the seat of [Andrew Gwynne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Gwynne), who resigned on health grounds in January 2026.[^cite_note-3]

## Constituency profile

Gorton and Denton is a mostly [urban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_area) constituency located in [Greater Manchester](79819.md). It includes the [Manchester](20206.md) neighbourhoods of [Gorton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorton), [Levenshulme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levenshulme), [Burnage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burnage) and [Longsight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longsight), and the connected town of [Denton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denton,_Greater_Manchester) in the borough of [Tameside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tameside). The neighbourhoods within Manchester have a history of manufacturing and engineering and currently experience high levels of deprivation, with most of this area falling within the top 10% most-deprived parts of [England](9316.md). Denton was traditionally a [textile manufacturing](2162735.md) and [coal mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coal_mining_in_the_United_Kingdom) area and has average levels of wealth.[^cite_note-4] House prices are lower than the rest of [North West England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_West_England) and considerably lower than the national average.[^cite_note-EC-5]

Compared to the rest of the country, residents of Gorton and Denton are young and have low levels of education. They are less likely to work in professional occupations and household income is low.[^cite_note-EC-5] [White people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_people_in_the_UK) made up 57% of the population at the [2021 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_UK_Census). [Asians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Asian) (mainly [Pakistanis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Pakistani)) formed the largest ethnic minority group at 27% and [Black people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_British) were 9%. The Asian population is concentrated in Longsight, where they make up a majority of residents, whilst Denton is over 90% White.[^cite_note-6] Almost all local council seats in the constituency are represented by the [Labour Party](19279158.md), although there is some [Workers Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Workers_Party_of_Britain) representation in Longsight. The Manchester wards are on average 57% white English with 42% of the population either a university graduate or a current university student. The Tameside wards are on average 83% white, 86% UK born, and 30% in routine or semi-routine jobs.[^cite_note-7]

An estimated 50% of voters in the constituency supported leaving the [European Union](9317.md) in the [2016 referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brexit_referendum), marginally lower than the nationwide figure of 52%.[^cite_note-EC-5]

## Boundaries

Further to the 2023 boundary review, the composition of the constituency is as follows from the 2024 general election (as they existed on 1 December 2020):

- The City of [Manchester](20206.md) wards of: [Burnage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burnage_\(ward\)); [Gorton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorton) & Abbey Hey; [Levenshulme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levenshulme); [Longsight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longsight).

* The Metropolitan Borough of [Tameside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tameside) wards of: [Denton North East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denton_North_East_\(ward\)); [Denton South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denton_South_\(ward\)); [Denton West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denton_West_\(ward\)).

  [^cite_note-8]

It comprises the following areas:[^cite_note-9]

- Gorton, Levenshulme and Longsight, previously part of the abolished constituency of [Manchester Gorton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_Gorton)

- Burnage, transferred from [Manchester Withington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_Withington)

- The three wards which make up the town of [Denton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denton,_Greater_Manchester), previously part of the abolished constituency of [Denton and Reddish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denton_and_Reddish)
