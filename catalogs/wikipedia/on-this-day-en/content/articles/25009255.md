**Galleh Dar** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): گله‌دار)[^cite_note-4] is a city in, and the capital of, [Galleh Dar District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galleh_Dar_District) of [Mohr County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohr_County), [Fars](448049.md) province, [Iran](14653.md).[^cite_note-Galleh_Dar_District_Formation-5] It also serves as the administrative center for [Galleh Dar Rural District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galleh_Dar_Rural_District).[^cite_note-Lar_County_Rural_Districts-6]

## Demographics

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the city's population was 9,982 in 1,995 households.[^cite_note-2006_Fars_Province-7] The following census in 2011 counted 11,354 people in 2,648 households.[^cite_note-2011_Fars_Province-8] The 2016 census measured the population of the city as 13,448 people in 3,589 households.[^cite_note-2016_Fars_Province-2]
