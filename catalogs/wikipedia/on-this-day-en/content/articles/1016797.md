The **Ecuador national football team** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Selección de fútbol de Ecuador*), nicknamed ***La Tricolor*** and ***La Tri*** represents [Ecuador](9334.md) in men's international [football](10568.md) and is controlled by the [Federación Ecuatoriana de Fútbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuadorian_Football_Federation) (English: Ecuadorian Football Federation). They joined [FIFA](11049.md) in 1926 and [CONMEBOL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONMEBOL) a year later.

Discarding an invitation to participate in the [inaugural FIFA World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1930_FIFA_World_Cup) held in [Uruguay](31843.md), Ecuador did not make their tournament debut until [2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_FIFA_World_Cup). After finishing above [Brazil](149286.md) and [Uruguay](679782.md) in the standings, the qualifying campaign marked the emergence of several players, such as [Agustín Delgado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agust%C3%ADn_Delgado), [Álex Aguinaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81lex_Aguinaga), [Iván Hurtado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iv%C3%A1n_Hurtado), [Ulises de la Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulises_de_la_Cruz) and [Iván Kaviedes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iv%C3%A1n_Kaviedes), who would set the stage for Ecuador's achievements in the next decade.[^cite_note-Ecuador's_first_World_Cup-2] Having reached the Round of 16 in a memorable [2006 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup) campaign,[^cite_note-3] they were expected to deliver at the [2007 Copa América](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Copa_Am%C3%A9rica) but were eliminated in the group stage.[^cite_note-4] Along with [Venezuela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venezuela_national_football_team), they have not won the [continental tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copa_Am%C3%A9rica). *La Tri*'s best performance was fourth in [1959](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1959_South_American_Championship_\(Ecuador\)) and [1993](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_Copa_Am%C3%A9rica), both times on home soil.

Ecuador plays the majority of their home matches at the [Estadio Olímpico Atahualpa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estadio_Ol%C3%ADmpico_Atahualpa) in [Quito](209982.md).

## History

Historically, Ecuador has been seen as a struggling footballing nation in South America. Despite their past failures, however, Ecuador has risen to be a serious South American competitor in recent years.

Football was introduced to Ecuador by Juan Alfredo Wright, who had recently returned from university in England. On 23 April 1899, he and his brother Roberto founded the first Ecuadorian football team, [Guayaquil Sport Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guayaquil_Sport_Club).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-Ecuador_football_history-6] As the popularity of the sport grew in the country, more teams were established. On 30 May 1925, the Federación Deportiva Nacional del Ecuador was founded.[^cite_note-Ecuador_football_history-6] In 1930, [FIFA](11049.md) sent an invitation encouraging for a men's national team to participate at the [maiden World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1930_FIFA_World_Cup). However, the then-Minister of Social Security and Sports declined the offer as they did not approve of the financial allocation.[^cite_note-7]

In 1938, the [I Bolivarian Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1938_Bolivarian_Games) were organized, with Ecuador set to take part in the [football tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_at_the_1938_Bolivarian_Games). On 8 August 1938, they played their first-ever match; a 1–1 draw with [Bolivia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolivia_national_football_team). Their following game saw the national team earn a 2–1 win against [Colombia](680175.md). Following a 9–1 crushing by [Peru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peru_national_football_team) and a 5–2 victory over [Venezuela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venezuela_national_football_team), Ecuador was tied for the silver medal with Bolivia. A playoff saw the Bolivians emerge triumphantly and the Ecuadorians finished the competition with the bronze medal.[^cite_note-8]



The Ecuador squad that participated at the [1942 South American Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1942_South_American_Championship)

After finishing fourth at the [1959 South American Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1959_South_American_Championship_\(Ecuador\)), the team entered the [World Cup qualifiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1962_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification) for the first time. They failed to qualify for [1962](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1962_FIFA_World_Cup) after inflicted defeats by [Argentina](454699.md).

The [1998 World Cup qualifiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification_\(CONMEBOL\)) saw the format for qualifying in [CONMEBOL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONMEBOL) changed to a league home-and-away system. This difference made a huge impact on Ecuador's performance as they clinched several important home wins during the campaign. In the end, they achieved a 6th-place finish, just under [Peru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peru_national_football_team) and [Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chile_national_football_team).



[Michael Arroyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Arroyo) executing a free kick against [Switzerland](681862.md) at the 2014 World Cup



[Jordi Cruijff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordi_Cruyff) became the manager of Ecuador in 2020

Following the appointment of [Hernán Darío Gómez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hern%C3%A1n_Dar%C3%ADo_G%C3%B3mez) for their [2002 World Cup qualifying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification_\(CONMEBOL\)) campaign, Ecuador recorded a historic 1–0 win against [Brazil](149286.md).[^cite_note-Ecuador_beats_Brazil-9] A 5–1 win over Bolivia saw *la Tricolor* only needing a point to qualify for the World Cup. They faced [Uruguay](679782.md), and, after managing to cling onto a 1–1 draw, obtained their spot in the [World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_FIFA_World_Cup) in East Asia.[^cite_note-Ecuador's_first_World_Cup-2]

Ecuador started the 2002 World Cup with a 2–0 loss to [Italy](362466.md). [Agustín Delgado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agust%C3%ADn_Delgado) scored his country's first World Cup goal; he opened the scoring in a 2–1 loss to [Mexico](808402.md).[^cite_note-10] Despite defeating [Croatia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croatia_national_football_team), who had achieved third place in the [previous tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_FIFA_World_Cup), and eliminating the Croats in process, Ecuador placed fourth in Group G and 24th overall.

A disappointing showing at the [2004 Copa América](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Copa_Am%C3%A9rica) led to the resignation of Gómez, who was replaced by [Luis Fernando Suárez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Fernando_Su%C3%A1rez). He led them successfully through the latter stages of the [qualification process for the 2006 FIFA World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification_\(CONMEBOL\)), finishing third to make the finals. In Germany, they were drawn into Group A with [the hosts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_national_football_team), [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland_national_football_team), and [Costa Rica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Rica_national_football_team). Wins over Poland and Costa Rica earned Ecuador qualification to the knockout stages for the first time.

After a dull [2014 FIFA World Cup](656933.md), and an unpleasant streak of failing to advance past the group stages of the [Copa América](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copa_Am%C3%A9rica), [Gustavo Quinteros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustavo_Quinteros) was hired to help rebuild the national team. Quinteros helped Ecuador reach the quarter-finals of the [Copa América Centenario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copa_Am%C3%A9rica_Centenario)[^cite_note-11] and started the [2018 World Cup qualifiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification_\(CONMEBOL\)) strong. They were setback after a loss to [Uruguay](679782.md) and finished eighth in the standings.

Gómez was reinstalled to lead Ecuador at the [2019 Copa América](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Copa_Am%C3%A9rica). His second stint was short, as he was soon fired after a disastrous tournament, having only earned a point.[^cite_note-12]

For [the qualifying for the 2026 FIFA World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification_\(CONMEBOL\)), as punishment for fielding the player [Byron Castillo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byron_Castillo) who had a passport with falsified information in the previous World Cup qualification cycle, Ecuador was deducted three points and fined [CHF](361362.md) 100,000.[^cite_note-ecuador_deduction-13]

## Home stadium

Main article: [Estadio Olímpico Atahualpa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estadio_Ol%C3%ADmpico_Atahualpa)



Aerial view of the stadium in 2017

The Ecuador national team plays their home games at the Estadio Olímpico Atahualpa in [Quito](209982.md). Having opened in 1951, it initially had a capacity of 45,000, but was later reduced to 35,724.

The stadium has a [running track](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Running_track), which has gone to be one of the most important in [South America](26769.md) for events organized by the former [International Association of Athletics Federations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Athletics).[^cite_note-14]

15 gates surround the stadium, allowing for an evacuation to be completed in about 10 minutes. The venue also features an electronic scoreboard located in the northern sector. The screen, manufactured by Hungarian-based company Elektroimpex in 1985, measures 10 meters tall and 30 meters wide.[^cite_note-15]

In this stadium, Ecuador defeated [Uruguay](679782.md) at the [1993 Copa América](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_Copa_Am%C3%A9rica) and [Brazil](149286.md) at the [2002 World Cup qualifiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification).[^cite_note-Ecuador_beats_Brazil-9] After tying with the former on 7 November 2001, Ecuador qualified for their first World Cup. Since then, Ecuador has qualified three times.

The stadium is set to be demolished in late-2020 for a newer stadium, which in first instance was for preparation for the [2024 Copa América](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Copa_Am%C3%A9rica).[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-Ecuador_stadium_demolished-17] However, Ecuador declined to host Copa America in 2022.[^cite_note-18]

For the [2022 FIFA World Cup qualifiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification), Ecuador played at the *[Casa Blanca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estadio_Rodrigo_Paz_Delgado).*

The Ecuador team posing before facing [Argentina](454699.md) in October 2017

The standard Ecuador uniform maintains the [colours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_colors_of_national_flags) of the [national flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Ecuador), typically a yellow top, blue shorts, and red socks.[^cite_note-19] The alternate [colours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_colours) of the uniform are white and blue, this being based on the flag of the [Guayas Province](215591.md). From 1965 to 2020, the crest featured the [Andean condor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andean_condor), Ecuador's national bird, above a shield with the country's colors. In January 2020, the [Ecuadorian Football Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuadorian_Football_Federation) announced a rebrand of the logo; a navy blue shield with an "FEF" monogram attempting to "abstractly build a condor".[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21]

### Retired numbers

Following the death of [Christian Benítez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Ben%C3%ADtez), the [Ecuadorian Football Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuadorian_Football_Federation) retired his jersey number 11 from the national team. According to the Federation's then-president, [Luis Chiriboga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Chiriboga), to honor Benítez the number would no longer be used by any other team player.[^cite_note-23] However, due to FIFA regulations, the number had to be reinstated for the [2014 World Cup squad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_FIFA_World_Cup_squads#Ecuador), taken by [Felipe Caicedo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felipe_Caicedo).[^cite_note-24]
