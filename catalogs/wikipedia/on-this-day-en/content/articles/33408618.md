The following is a list of [ambassadors](68651.md) of [Brazil](3383.md), or other [chiefs of mission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_mission), to the [United States of America](3434750.md).[^cite_note-1] The title given by the [Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Brazil\)) of the [Federative Republic of Brazil](3383.md) to this position is currently "Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary" ([Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language): *Embaixador Extraordinário e Plenipotenciário*). There have been fifty representatives of various ranks and titles from Brazil to the United States since diplomatic relations were inaugurated in 1824 to the present.

The United States of America was the second country[^cite_note-\:0-2] to recognize [Brazil's independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_of_Brazil), which was proclaimed in September 1822, by the regent, heir and crown prince to the throne of the former [United Kingdom of Portugal, Brazil and the Algarves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom_of_Portugal,_Brazil_and_the_Algarves), Pedro of the [House of Braganza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Braganza). He soon became Emperor [Pedro I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_I_of_Brazil) (reigned [1822–1831](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_reign_\(Empire_of_Brazil\))), of the [Empire of Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire_of_Brazil), which endured under his son [Pedro II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_II_of_Brazil) until 1889, when the present republic and federation were established.[^cite_note-3] The [South American](26769.md) nation's first [legation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legation) was thus established in [Washington](108956.md), only a quarter-century after the founding of the American national capital and federal district itself. The long [Brazilian-North American diplomatic relationship and inter-continental friendship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil-United_States_relations) was founded on 26 May 1824,[^cite_note-\:0-2] when José Silvestre Rebello presented his [diplomatic accreditation credentials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplomatic_accreditation) as Brazil's first Charge d'Affaires to President [James Monroe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Monroe), at the newly restored Executive Mansion, now acquiring its new nickname and title of the [White House](33057.md). He represented the new Emperor and Empire of Brazil for over five years to August 1829.

In 1905, the United States Legation representing the [U.S. Department of State](31975.md) and its [Secretary of State](32293.md) in the original capital city of [Rio de Janeiro](25936.md) was raised to a full [embassy](8970.md) under President [Theodore Roosevelt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodore_Roosevelt), similar to the gradual increased status of [diplomatic missions](8970.md) across the board in international relations in the 20th century.
