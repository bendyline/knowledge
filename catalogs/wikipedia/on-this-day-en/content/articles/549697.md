**Meta** **Department** (Spanish pronunciation: [\[ˈmeta\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish)) is one of the 32 [departments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Departments_of_Colombia) that compose the [Republic of Colombia](5222.md). Largely located within the country's [Orinoquía natural region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orinoqu%C3%ADa_natural_region) and covered by the [Llanos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Llanos), it is close to the geographic center of the country, east of the [Andes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andes) mountains. Its capital is the city of [Villavicencio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villavicencio).

## History

Meta was established as a department on 1 July 1960.[^cite_note-4]

Meta was a department particularly effected by the violence of the [Colombian conflict](1857628.md),[^cite_note-5] with large areas being under the control of the [FARC](26100.md) before the [2016 peace deal](51422824.md).[^cite_note-\:0-6]

In May 2021, flooding affected over 5,400 people and 1,100 homes in the department when the Meta River rose to 8.73 metres (28.6 ft).[^cite_note-7]

## Geography

Meta Department is close to the geographic center of Colombia, largely located within the country's [Orinoquía natural region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orinoqu%C3%ADa_natural_region).[^cite_note-8] It is east of the [Andes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andes) mountains. A significant portion of the department is a part of the [Llanos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Llanos), a grassland plain. The homonymous [Meta River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meta_River) forms the northeastern border.[^cite_note-9] It is bordered by [Cundinamarca](189405.md), [Casanare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casanare_Department), [Vichada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vichada_Department), [Caquetá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caquet%C3%A1_Department), [Guaviare](549674.md), and [Huila Departments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huila_Department).[^cite_note-\:1-10]

Parts of Chiribiquete Park, Tinigua National Natural Park, [La Macarena National Natural Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Macarena_National_Park),[^cite_note-11] and [Caño Cristales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ca%C3%B1o_Cristales) are located in the department.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-\:0-6]

It has an area of 85,635 square kilometres (33,064 sq mi), 7.49% of the country.[^cite_note-\:1-10]

Meta Department is composed of 29 municipalities across 4 subregions. Its capital city is [Villavicencio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villavicencio).[^cite_note-13]

## Demographics

As of the [2018 Colombian census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Colombian_census), Meta Department had a total population of 1,039,722 people.[^cite_note-Pop2018-1] In 2022, it had a [Human Development Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Development_Index) of 0.769.[^cite_note-HDI-3]

## Government

Like all departments in Colombia, Meta has a Governor and a Departmental Assembly.[^cite_note-\:5-19]

## Economy

Historically, the department's economy has been dependent on oil and gas. In the early 2020s, the national and departmental government began an effort to focus on and invest in tourism centered around its natural attractions.[^cite_note-\:0-6]

In 2022, Meta Department had a [GDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GDP) of 58,439.5 billion [Colombian pesos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombian_peso).[^cite_note-GDP-2]

## Symbols

The flag of Meta has 17 horizontal stripes (nine green, eight white), representing Meta's place as the seventeenth department created in Colombia.[^cite_note-20]

The department has a monument marking the exact geographic center of Colombia, in a place known as Alto de Menegua, a few kilometers from [Puerto López](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_L%C3%B3pez,_Meta).[^cite_note-21]
