**Larry Berg** is an American private equity executive and sports owner. He is the co-founder of [Los Angeles FC](44255607.md) and is the commissioner-elect of [Major League Soccer](71802.md). Berg is scheduled to succeed [Don Garber](1878607.md) on January 1, 2027.[^cite_note-sbj-1]

## Early life and education

Berg was raised in [Cherry Hill, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherry_Hill,_New_Jersey). He played youth soccer there and later played at [Cherry Hill High School East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherry_Hill_High_School_East), graduating in 1984. His high school team competed in the New Jersey state championship.[^cite_note-NPR-2][^cite_note-3] He spent one year at the [University of North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina) before transferring to the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania), where he earned a bachelor's degree in economics from the [Wharton School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wharton_School). He later received an [MBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Business_Administration) from [Harvard Business School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Business_School).[^cite_note-NPR-2]

## Career

Berg was a senior partner at [Apollo Global Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apollo_Global_Management), a [private equity](191280.md) firm, from 1992 to 2022.[^cite_note-4] He served as a director of [McGraw-Hill Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGraw-Hill_Education) beginning in 2013 and as chairman from 2014.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-Guardian-6] In September 2023, Berg joined [26North Partners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/26North_Partners), an alternatives platform founded by Josh Harris, as a partner in its private equity group.[^cite_note-7] He later moved to an advisory role and said he would leave 26North as he prepared to become MLS commissioner.[^cite_note-8]

Berg was a founding partner of [Los Angeles FC](44255607.md) and became the club's lead managing owner in 2016.[^cite_note-sbj-1][^cite_note-Guardian-6] He was named to [Major League Soccer](71802.md)'s sporting and competition committee and later became co-chair.[^cite_note-Guardian-6]

### Major League Soccer

On August 3, 2026, the [Major League Soccer](71802.md) Board of Governors selected Berg to succeed [Don Garber](1878607.md) as league commissioner in January 2027. He was chosen over former [Fox Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_Sports) executive David Nathanson following a succession process begun in late 2025.[^cite_note-sbj-1] Berg is expected to divest his stake in LAFC before taking office.[^cite_note-sbj-1]
