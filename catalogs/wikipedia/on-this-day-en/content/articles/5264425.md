**Construction and Building Unions Superannuation** (**Cbus**) is an Australian [industry super fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industry_superannuation_fund). It is one of Australia's largest super funds. It is open to all members of the public as a [public offer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_offer_fund) fund. Due to its [mutual fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutual_fund) structure, Cbus retains all profits to members.

## History

The fund was established in 1984 by the Building Workers' Industrial Union of Australia to provide a retirement savings vehicle for workers in construction and other affiliated industries. 30,000 members signed up in the first six months, employers contributing \$9.00 a week to their workers' retirement savings. Mavis Robertson oversaw the creation of the fund.&#x20;

In August 2024, the [Australian Prudential Regulation Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Prudential_Regulation_Authority) (APRA) mandated that Cbus commission an independent review into skill sets of directors on the fund's board, following the decision of the federal government to place the majority of the CFMEU into external administration.[^cite_note-2] The independent review found that the fund "lacked proper systems, processes and frameworks for documenting and assessing if spending was in members' best financial interests". It was subsequently reported that Cbus had hosted a 40th anniversary event in July 2024.[^cite_note-3]

In November 2024, the [Australian Securities and Investments Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Securities_and_Investments_Commission) (ASIC) took Cbus to court for failing to process more than 7,000 death and disability claims in a timely way. Cbus was fined \$23.5 million in November 2025. It also paid \$32 million in compensation to 7402 people in relation to the case. Cbus settled a dispute with its administrator MUFG to resolve past issues. [^cite_note-\:0-4]

## Features

There are over 136,000 employers that have made Cbus the [default fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Default_fund?action=edit\&redlink=1) for their employees, and the fund manages roughly \$85 billion in retirement savings.[^cite_note-5]

## Governance

United Super Pty Ltd is the [Trustee](506701.md) of Cbus Superannuation Fund. The Directors of United Super Pty Ltd are appointed in equal number from member and [employer associations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Employers'_organization) in the construction and building industries. Two independent directors sit on the board.[^cite_note-6]

The current [chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairman) of the board is Wayne Swan, former [Treasurer of Australia](579169.md).[^cite_note-\:0-4]

The prior chair to Swan was the former Victorian Premier [Steve Bracks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Bracks).
