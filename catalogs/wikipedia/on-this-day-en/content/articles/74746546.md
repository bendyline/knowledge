# European Union sanctions

&#x20;  An essential tool of the EU's [Common Foreign and Security Policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Foreign_and_Security_Policy) (CFSP) are restrictive measures, also known as "sanctions". They are utilised by the EU as a component of a coordinated and all-encompassing policy approach that includes political discussion, complementary actions, and the use of other tools at its disposal.[^cite_note-1]

In order to advance the CFSP's goals, sanctions aim to change the behaviour or policy of those who are targeted. They may be addressed to governments of non-EU nations because of their policies; to entities (companies) which provide a means to carry out the targeted policies; groups or organisations, such as [terrorist organisations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrorist_organisation); and people who support the targeted policies or who engage in terrorist activities, etc.

### Individuals

The European Union has also sanctioned entities and individuals. In relation to the [Sudanese civil war started in 2023](73556699.md) it took several actions. On 22 January 2024 it first sanctioned 6 entities with ties to the [Sudanese Armed Forces](27429.md) and the [Rapid Support Forces](43053874.md). With Council Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/1783 on 24 June 2024 it also added six persons on the list of sanctioned individuals and entities. They were Mirghani Idriss Suleiman, El Tahir Mohamed El Awad El Amin, [Ali Ahmed Karti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_Ahmed_Karti), [Abdulrahman Juma Barakallah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Rahman_Jumma), Mustafa Ibrahim Abdel Nabi Mohamed and Masar Abdurahman Aseel.[^cite_note-37] With Council Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/3156 of 16 December 2024 it added four others: [Salah Abdallah Mohamed Salah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salah_Gosh), [Tijani Karshom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Tijani_Karshoum), Mohamed Ali Ahmed Subir and [Osman Mohamed Hamid Mohamed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osman_Mohamed_Hamid_Mohamed).[^cite_note-38]

On 18 July 2025 it added two further individuals, [Abu Aqla Kikal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Aqla_Kikal) and [Hussein Barsham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hussein_Barsham) to the list of sanctioned indiduals. It also added two businesses.[^cite_note-39][^cite_note-40] With Council Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2368 of 20 November 2025 it added [Abdul Rahim Dagalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Rahim_Dagalo) for violations committed by his troops.[^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42] With Council Decision (CFSP) 2026/254 it added seven more individuals on 29 January 2026. They were: Algony Hamdan Dagalo Musa, [Elfateh Abdullah Idris Adam](81517671.md), [Edris Kafuti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edris_Kafuti), [Tijani Ibrahim Moussa Mohamed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tijani_Ibrahim_Moussa_Mohamed), [Gedo Hamdan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gedo_Hamdan_Ahmed), Abu Zaid Talha Al-Misbah and Al-Tayyib Al-Imam Joda.[^cite_note-43][^cite_note-44]
