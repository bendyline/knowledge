**European route E70** is an A-Class West-East [European route](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_route), extending from [A Coruña](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Coru%C3%B1a) in [Spain](26667.md) in the west to the [Georgian](48768.md) city of [Poti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poti) in the east.[^cite_note-1]

## Itinerary

The E 70 routes through ten European countries, and includes one (not currently operational) sea-crossing, from [Varna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varna,_Bulgaria) in [Bulgaria](3415.md) to [Samsun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samsun) in [Turkey](11125639.md).&#x20;
