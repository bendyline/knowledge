On the morning of November 24, 2025, a suicide bomber blew himself outside the [Federal Constabulary](25964521.md) headquarters in [Peshawar](66230.md), Pakistan, killing three officers and wounding two more. Two armed men then attempted to enter the structure and opened fire, wounding nine more people, before being both shot dead. [Jamaat-ul-Ahrar](44284567.md), a faction of the [Pakistani Taliban](18953699.md), claimed responsibility for the attack.[^cite_note-1]
