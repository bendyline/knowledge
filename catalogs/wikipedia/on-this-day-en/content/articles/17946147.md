**Gummi** is a town and [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Areas_of_Nigeria) in [Zamfara State](2227664.md), [Nigeria](21383.md).

It has an area of 2,610 km<sup>2</sup> and a population of 204,539 at the 2006 [census](6889.md).

The [postal code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_code) of the area is 891.[^cite_note-1]

Gummi was carved out of the former larger [Sokoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sokoto_\(city\)) state, which included the present [Zamfara](2227664.md) and [Kebbi](2227643.md) states. Gummi has a long history of relative peace and security.

The great majority of the people of the area are [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_people), [Fulani](102383.md) and [Muslims](19541.md) and they are predominantly [farmers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farmer); the principal crop is [calabash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calabash).

## Climate

The [weather](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather) changes throughout the year, with a wet season that is hot and oppressive and a dry season that is hot and partly [cloudy.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloud)[^cite_note-\:0-2]

### Temperature

Gummi is experiencing a warmer climate as a result of climate change, with a positive trend of increasing [temperatures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperature) indicating a shift toward a more sustainable and resilient climate.[^cite_note-3]

### Cloud

Gummi experiences notable seasonal fluctuations in the average cloud coverage percentage throughout the year.[^cite_note-\:0-2]

The period of clearer skies in Gummi typically commences on November 9, lasting for [approximately](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Approximation) 3.9 months until around March 6.[^cite_note-4]

January stands out as the clearest month in Gummi, where the sky is predominantly clear, mostly clear, or partly cloudy, accounting for an average of 65% of the time.[^cite_note-5]

Conversely, the cloudier phase starts around March 6, spanning approximately 8.1 months until November 9.[^cite_note-6]

May emerges as the cloudiest month in Gummi, characterized by overcast or mostly cloudy skies, constituting an average of 71% of the time.[^cite_note-7]

## Topography

For the purpose of this report, Gummi is situated at geographical coordinates of 12.145 degrees latitude, 5.118 degrees longitude, and an elevation of 233 m (764 ft).

The [landscape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landscape) within a 3.2-kilometre (2 mile) radius of Gummi features relatively small changes in [elevation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elevation), with the most significant elevation difference being 32 m (105 ft), and an average elevation of approximately 232 m (762 ft) above sea level. Extending the radius to 16 km (9.9 mi) continues to exhibit moderate modest elevation changes, amounting to 320 m (1,050 ft).

The region within a 3.2-kilometre (2 mile) radius of Gummi primarily comprises cropland (66%) and grassland (12%). Expanding the analysis to a 16-kilometre (10 mile) radius, the land cover remains dominated by cropland (69%) and grassland (12%).
