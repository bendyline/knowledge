**Édouard Jean Gabriel Geffray** (French pronunciation: [\[edwaʁ ʒefʁɛ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French); born 2 October 1978) is a French senior civil servant and politician who has served as [minister of national education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_National_Education_\(France\)) in the [second government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Lecornu_government) of [Prime Minister](218751.md) [Sébastien Lecornu](54364868.md) since 2025.[^cite_note-1] From 2019 to 2024, he served as [director general of school education](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direction%20g%C3%A9n%C3%A9rale%20de%20l'enseignement%20scolaire).[^cite_note-2]

## Career

In 2017, Geffray was appointed chief of staff to the Minister of Justice, [François Bayrou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fran%C3%A7ois_Bayrou). He was subsequently appointed Director-General of Human Resources at the Ministry of National Education and then, from 2019 onward, Director-General for School Education (DGESCO).[^cite_note-3] In this position, he worked, under the authority of the Minister of National Education [Jean-Michel Blanquer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Michel_Blanquer), on the implementation of a reform of French high school degrees and the management of the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_France) in schools and, under that of [Gabriel Attal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Attal), on the introduction of "needs-based groups" in lower secondary schools.

In 2021, Geffray was a candidate for the directorship of the [Paris Institute of Political Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sciences_Po) (Sciences Po); the post eventually went to [Mathias Vicherat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathias_Vicherat).[^cite_note-LF-4]

Having left the position of DGESCO in July 2024, Geffray returned to the [Council of State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conseil_d'%C3%89tat) in September 2024.[^cite_note-LF-4] In this capacity, he submitted to the government a report on film education in schools in September 2025.

In October 2025, he was appointed Minister of National Education.
