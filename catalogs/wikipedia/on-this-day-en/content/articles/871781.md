**Minister of Justice** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Ministre de la Justice*), formally known as **Keeper of the Seals, Minister of Justice** (*Garde des Sceaux, ministre de la Justice* [\[ɡaʁd de so ministʁ d(ə) la ʒystis\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French)), is a cabinet position in the [Government of France](3398091.md). The current minister of justice has been [Gérald Darmanin](54070416.md) since 2024. The ministry is headquartered on [Place Vendôme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Place_Vend%C3%B4me) in the [1st arrondissement of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1st_arrondissement_of_Paris).[^cite_note-1]

## Function

The roles of the minister are to:[^cite_note-2]

- oversee the building, maintenance and administration of courts;

- sit as vice president of the Judicial Council (which oversees the judicial performance and advises on prosecutiorial performance);

- supervise public prosecutions;

- direct corrections and the prison system

- propose legislation affecting civil or criminal law or procedure.

The Minister of Justice also holds the ceremonial office of [Keeper of the Seals of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keeper_of_the_Seals_of_France) and is custodian of the [Great Seal of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Seal_of_France). This symbolic role is still shown in the order of words of the minister's official designation, Minister of Justice, Keeper of the Seals (*Ministre de la Justice, garde des Sceaux*).

France's Ministry of Justice might oversee the administration of justice in [French Guiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Guiana), [French Polynesia](10737.md), [Guadeloupe](12343.md), [Martinique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martinique), [Mayotte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayotte), [New Caledonia](21342.md), [Réunion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%A9union), [Saint Barthélemy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Barth%C3%A9lemy), [Saint-Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collectivity_of_Saint_Martin), [Saint Pierre and Miquelon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Pierre_and_Miquelon) and [Wallis and Futuna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wallis_and_Futuna).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]
