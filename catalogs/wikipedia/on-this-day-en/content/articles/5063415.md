**Vogelwaarde** is a village in the [Dutch](21148.md) province of [Zeeland](52229.md). It is a part of the municipality of [Hulst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hulst), and lies about 29 km southwest of [Bergen op Zoom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergen_op_Zoom).

## History

The village was first mention in 1936 as Vogelwaarde, and is a combination of "land on water" and the stream Vogel.[^cite_note-3] The village was formed in 1970 from the former villages [Boschkapelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boschkapelle) and [Stoppeldijk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stoppeldijk). Both villages were located in the Stoppeldijkpolder. In 1645, the Boschkapelle, a chapel, was built which became a site of pilgrimage for the [Martyrs of Gorkum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martyrs_of_Gorkum) in 1761.[^cite_note-monument-4]

The St Gerulphus is a three-aisled church with needle spire which was constructed between 1860 and 1861. The church burnt down in 1869, and was rebuilt and enlarged the next year. The St Paul and Peter Church was built between 1875 and 1876, and used to be located in Boschkapelle.[^cite_note-monument-4]

In 1936, the villages of Stoppeldijk and Boschkapelle formed the municipality of Vogelwaarde. Vogelwaarde was a separate municipality from 1936 to 1970, when it was merged with [Hontenisse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hontenisse).[^cite_note-5] In 2003, it became part of the municipality of [Hulst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hulst).[^cite_note-plaats-6]

The village has one of the few [horseshoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horseshoe) factories in Europe. It was founded in 1906.[^cite_note-7]
