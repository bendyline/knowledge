The **Halloween solar storms** were a series of [solar storms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_storm) involving [solar flares](54648.md) and [coronal mass ejections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coronal_mass_ejection) that occurred from mid-October to early November 2003, peaking around October 28–29.[^cite_note-USGS2013-1][^cite_note-NASA2008-2][^cite_note-3] This series of storms generated the largest solar flare ever recorded by the [GOES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GOES) system, modeled as strong as X45 (initially estimated at X28 due to saturation of GOES' detectors).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

## Effects

### On Earth



Aurora as seen from [Jena, Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jena,_Germany) (51°N)

Satellite-based systems and communications were affected, aircraft were advised to avoid high altitudes near the [polar regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polar_regions),[^cite_note-NOAA2004-6] and a one-hour-long power outage occurred in [Sweden](5058739.md) as a result of the solar activity.[^cite_note-NASA2008-2] [Aurorae](49658.md) were observed at latitudes as far south as [Texas](29810.md)[^cite_note-NASA2008-2] and the [Mediterranean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediterranean) countries of [Europe](9239.md).[^cite_note-NOAA2004-6] Twelve transformers in [South Africa](17416221.md) were disabled and had to be replaced, despite the country's low [geomagnetic latitude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geomagnetic_latitude).[^cite_note-Lloyds-AER-2013-NA-risk-7]

### On satellites and spacecraft

The [SOHO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_and_Heliospheric_Observatory) satellite failed temporarily and the [Advanced Composition Explorer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advanced_Composition_Explorer) (ACE) was damaged by the solar activity.[^cite_note-NASA2008-2] Numerous other spacecraft were damaged or experienced downtime due to various issues. Some of them were intentionally put into safe mode in order to protect sensitive equipment.[^cite_note-NOAA2004-6] Astronauts aboard the [International Space Station](15043.md) (ISS) had to stay inside the more shielded parts of the [Russian Orbital Segment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Orbital_Segment) to protect themselves against the increased radiation levels.[^cite_note-Discover2005-8]

Emissions from the CME were later observed by the [*Mars Odyssey* spacecraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mars_Odyssey) orbiting [Mars](14640471.md), [*Ulysses* spacecraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulysses_spacecraft) near [Jupiter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jupiter), and the *[Cassini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassini%E2%80%93Huygens)* spacecraft en route to [Saturn](44474.md). In April 2004, *[Voyager 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voyager_2)* was also able to detect them as they reached the spacecraft.[^cite_note-Discover2005-8]

## Analysis



Various data recorded during the Halloween solar storms

One of the solar storms was compared by some scientists in its intensity to the [Carrington Event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrington_Event) of 1859.[^cite_note-9]

These events occurred during [solar cycle 23](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_cycle_23), approximately three years after its peak in 2000, which was marked by another occurrence of solar activity known as the [Bastille Day event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bastille_Day_event).
