**Michaelmas Island** is an [island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Island) located in [King George Sound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_George_Sound) near [Albany, Western Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albany,_Western_Australia).

The island is a [nature reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nature_reserve), declared in 1983, and managed by the [Department of Parks and Wildlife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Parks_and_Wildlife_\(Western_Australia\)); its reserve number is 30049. The island is also registered as a national estate. The island is 91.9 hectares (227 acres) in area.[^cite_note-1]

Michaelmas Island has steep rocky shores with submerged reefs all along its northern southern and eastern sides in depths up to 30 metres (100 ft) with large [granite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granite) boulders. The faces are covered in a diverse range in [corals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coral) and [sponges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sponge) and home to a wide variety of marine life. [*Cheynes III*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheynes_III?action=edit\&redlink=1), a whale chaser that was [scuttled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuttled) in 1982, is located off the western side of the island and is regarded highly as a diving site.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] Michaelmas Island is an important [habitat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habitat) for both resident and [migratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bird_migration) birds. The habitat of these species is threatened by weed invasion such as [taylorina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psoralea_pinnata) and [feral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feral) animals such as rats.[^cite_note-4]

[George Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Vancouver) named many of the landforms around Albany when he entered King George Sound in 1791, including Michaelmas Island (discovered on [Michaelmas Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michaelmas_Day)), [King George Sound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_George_Sound), [Breaksea Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breaksea_Island,_Western_Australia), Oyster Harbour and Point Possession. Vancouver described the island as being covered in rich grasses and other vegetables. Before leaving, he planted some [vine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vine) cuttings, some [watercresses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watercress), and sowed various seeds. [Matthew Flinders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Flinders) landed on the island in 1801 and reported the island to be covered in wiry grasses and stunted shrubs, and that it was covered in rat holes.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

In 2016 the island was chosen as a new habitat site for the critically endangered [Gilbert's potoroo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilbert's_potoroo). The island's lack of predators was a key factor in its selection, which will provide the Gilbert's potoroo with a similar level of long-term protection enjoyed by the [quokkas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quokka) of [Rottnest Island](216061.md), also in [Western Australia](33613.md).[^cite_note-7]
