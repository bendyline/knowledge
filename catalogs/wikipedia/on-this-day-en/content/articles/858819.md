**Bang Kruai** ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): บางกรวย, pronounced [\[bāːŋ krūa̯j\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Thai)) is a district (*[amphoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphoe)*) in the southern part of [Nonthaburi province](344757.md), central [Thailand](30128.md).

## History

The district was created in 1904 and named Bang Yai. Due to its size it was difficult to administer, and thus in 1917 the northern part was split off as minor district (*king amphoe*), Bang Mae Nang. In 1921 Bang Mae Nang became a full district.

On 19 October 1930 the district was renamed "Bang Kruai", after the geographic shape of district. *Kruai* is the Thai word for 'cone'. On the same date, Bang Mae Nang received the old name of the district, "Bang Yai".[^cite_note-1]

## Geography

Neighbouring districts are (from north clockwise) [Bang Yai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bang_Yai_district), [Mueang Nonthaburi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mueang_Nonthaburi_district), the districts [Bang Sue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bang_Sue), [Bang Phlat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bang_Phlat), [Taling Chan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taling_Chan), [Thawi Watthana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thawi_Watthana) of [Bangkok](56615.md), and finally [Phutthamonthon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phutthamonthon_district) ([Nakhon Pathom province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakhon_Pathom_province)).

## Religion

Most people in Bang Kruai district are Buddhist. There are total 50 [Theravada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theravada) Buddhist temples in the district, of which 49 Maha Nikai and 1 Dhammayut temples.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] \
Further there are 2 Christian churches in the district.[^cite_note-4]

## Health

### Government hospitals

There are two [community hospitals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_hospitals_in_Thailand#Community_Hospitals_\(Category_M2,_F1,_F2_and_F3\)) in Bang Kruai district.\


- Bang Kruai Hospital with 60 beds.

- Nonthaburi Medical Center with 30 beds.

### Private hospital

There is one [private hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_hospitals_in_Thailand#Private_Hospitals) in the district:

- Anan Phatthana 2 Hospital with 50 beds.

### Health promoting hospitals

There are total 11 [health-promoting hospitals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hospitals_in_Thailand#Sub-district_Health_Promoting_Hospitals) in the district.[^cite_note-15]

### Clinics

Around 59 clinics are in Bang Kruai district.[^cite_note-16]

## Environment

Bang Kruai as well as neighbouring Bang Yai and Mueang Nonthaburi is the last natural habitat of the [Alexandrine parakeet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandrine_parakeet) and [red-breasted parakeet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red-breasted_parakeet), a medium-sized parrot in [Bangkok Metropolitan Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangkok_Metropolitan_Region). They live in groups on large trees in some local temples, such as Wat Suan Yai, Wat Utthayan, Wat Ampawan, [Wat Chaloem Phra Kiat Worawihan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wat_Chaloem_Phra_Kiat_Worawihan).[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18]
