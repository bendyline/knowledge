The **Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Cuba** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Ministro de Asuntos Exteriores de la República de Cuba*) is a [cabinet](2865700.md) [minister](739237.md) in charge of the [Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Cuba\)) of [Cuba](5042481.md), responsible for conducting [foreign relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_relations_of_Cuba) of the country.
