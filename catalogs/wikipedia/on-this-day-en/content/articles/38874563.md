**Christian Harrison** (born May 29, 1994) is an American professional [tennis](29773.md) player who specializes in doubles. He has a career-high [ATP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_Tennis_Professionals) doubles ranking of world No. 4 achieved on September 14, 2026 and a singles ranking of No. 198, reached on July 2, 2018.

Harrison has won five [ATP Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Tour) doubles titles, the most significant being a [major](197638.md#Tournaments) trophy at the [2026 Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles) and [2026 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_US_Open_-_Men's_doubles), playing alongside [Neal Skupski](40157604.md). He also reached a semifinal at the [2025 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_French_Open), with compatriot [Evan King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evan_King).[^cite_note-1]In mixed doubles, he reached two semifinals in the [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Mixed_doubles) and [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Mixed_doubles) editions of the [US Open](82729.md), with [Christina McHale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christina_McHale) and [Danielle Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danielle_Collins), respectively.

Harrison represents the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Davis_Cup_team) at the [Davis Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davis_Cup).[^cite_note-2]

## Early life

Christian was coached by his father, Pat Harrison, and attended the [IMG Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMG_Academy) in Bradenton, Florida. He is the younger brother of [Ryan Harrison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Harrison).

## Professional career

### 2012: Grand Slam doubles debut and quarterfinal

In July, Christian made it to the quarterfinals of the Lexington, Kentucky Challenger event.[^cite_note-3] Christian was awarded a wildcard into the [2012 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_US_Open_\(tennis\)) to play doubles alongside Ryan Harrison, where they reached the quarterfinals.

### 2013: First ATP Tour win

Earlier in the year he reached the quarterfinals, semifinals, the final, and won, respectively, the four Futures events in which he participated. He failed to qualify in Indian Wells, losing in the first round of qualifying to [Ernests Gulbis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernests_Gulbis), who made a deep run to the fourth round after qualifying. However, he did take a set off Gulbis.&#x20;

Harrison won his first ATP World Tour match against [Alejandro Falla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alejandro_Falla) at the BB\&T Atlanta Open. He then lost in the next round to [John Isner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Isner) in three tight sets.

### 2014–15: Hiatus

Harrison spent 2014 and 2015 recovering from several surgeries.

### 2016–2018: Grand Slam and Masters 1000 debuts



Harrison at the [2018 Wimbledon Championships Qualifying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles_qualifying)

Harrison reached the final round of qualifying at the [2016 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles_qualifying) after beating [Luke Saville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Saville) and second seed [Konstantin Kravchuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konstantin_Kravchuk) in two three-set matches. He made it into main draw after beating [Steven Diez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Diez) also in three-set match. He was one set down at all three matches in qualifying. He lost in the first round to [Paul-Henri Mathieu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul-Henri_Mathieu) in straight sets.

He made his Masters 1000 singles debut at the [2017 Miami Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Miami_Open) as a qualifier but lost to [Dudi Sela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dudi_Sela) in the first round. He received a wildcard to the [2017 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_US_Open_\(tennis\)) where he won his first round match in doubles with partner [Christopher Eubanks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Eubanks).

### 2021: Maiden ATP doubles final

Harrison qualified for the [2021 Delray Beach Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Delray_Beach_Open), starting the week ranked No. 789 in the world.[^cite_note-4] He beat number 1 seed [Cristian Garín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristian_Gar%C3%ADn)[^cite_note-5] and advanced all the way to the semifinals,[^cite_note-6] where he was defeated by fourth seed [Hubert Hurkacz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hubert_Hurkacz),[^cite_note-7] who would later win the title. As a result, he climbed 444 positions in the rankings to a World No. 345 ranking on 18 January 2021 and he got five ATP Tour match victories thus far, with three of them in Delray Beach, where he also won two matches in qualifying. He also reached his maiden ATP final in doubles with his brother [Ryan Harrison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Harrison) where they lost to [Ariel Behar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariel_Behar) and [Gonzalo Escobar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gonzalo_Escobar_\(tennis\)).[^cite_note-8] As a result, he returned to the top 250 in the doubles rankings at World No. 229.

### 2022: First Wimbledon qualification since 2018 and win

He qualified for only the second time at the [2022 Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Wimbledon_Championships) and his third Major main draw and won his first match at any Major, defeating wildcard [Jay Clarke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Clarke_\(tennis\)).[^cite_note-9]

### 2024–25: Doubles success: New partnership, first title, Masters semifinals, top 20

Harrison won his 11th Challenger title in [Winnipeg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Winnipeg_National_Bank_Challenger) with [Cannon Kingsley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannon_Kingsley) and fourth of the season,[^cite_note-10] and reached the top 100 in the doubles rankings on 15 July 2024.[^cite_note-11]

Partnering with [Rajeev Ram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajeev_Ram), Harrison reached the doubles final at the [2025 Auckland Classic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_ASB_Classic_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles), but withdrew before the match against [Nikola Mektić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikola_Mekti%C4%87) and [Michael Venus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Venus) due to Ram suffering an arm injury.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

Alongside [Evan King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evan_King), he won his first ATP Tour doubles title at the [2025 Dallas Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Dallas_Open_%E2%80%93_Doubles), defeating [Ariel Behar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariel_Behar) and [Robert Galloway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Galloway_\(tennis\)) in the final.[^cite_note-14] Within three weeks they lifted their second title, also an ATP 500 in [Acapulco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Abierto_Mexicano_Telcel), having qualified for the main draw. They defeated fourth seeds [Sadio Doumbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sadio_Doumbia) and [Fabien Reboul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fabien_Reboul) 6–4, 6–0 in a 56-minute final.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] At the [2025 BNP Paribas Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_BNP_Paribas_Open) the pair reached their first Masters semifinal as wildcards with wins over [Matthew Ebden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Ebden) and [John Peers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Peers) and seventh-seeded Argentines [Maximo Gonzalez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximo_Gonzalez) and [Andres Molteni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andres_Molteni). As a result Harrison reached a new career-high ranking of world No. 36 on 17 March 2025.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] At the next Masters in [ Miami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Miami_Open), the pair reached back-to-back quarterfinals upsetting third seeds [Simone Bolelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simone_Bolelli) and [Andrea Vavassori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Vavassori) and as a result he reached the top 30 in the rankings.[^cite_note-19] The pair reached another semifinal at the [2025 Mutua Madrid Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Mutua_Madrid_Open) but again lost, this time to the world No. 1 pair [Marcelo Arevalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcelo_Arevalo) and [Mate Pavic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mate_Pavic). As a result they both reached new career-high rankings in the top 20 in the rankings on 5 May 2025.[^cite_note-20]

In October, Harrison and King defeated [Hugo Nys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugo_Nys) and [Édouard Roger-Vasselin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89douard_Roger-Vasselin) in the final to win the [European Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_European_Open_%E2%80%93_Doubles) in [Brussels](3708.md).[^cite_note-21]

### 2026: Australian and US Open doubles titles

Seeded sixth, and playing just their second tournament together, Harrison and new partner [Neal Skupski](40157604.md) won the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_doubles), defeating wildcard entrants [Jason Kubler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Kubler) and [Marc Polmans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Polmans) in the final.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23] In September, they defeated [Kevin Krawietz](23521626.md) and [Tim Pütz](41501290.md) in the final to win the [US Open](84129203.md).[^cite_note-24]

## World TeamTennis

Christian has played three seasons with [World TeamTennis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_TeamTennis), making his debut in 2015 with the [Boston Lobsters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_Lobsters) as a substitute. He has since served as a substitute for the [Orange County Breakers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_County_Breakers) in 2018 and the [San Diego Aviators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Diego_Aviators) during the 2020 WTT season played at [The Greenbrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Greenbrier).[^cite_note-25]
