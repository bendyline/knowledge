**Pakistanis** ([Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): پاكِستانى قوم, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Urdu): *Pākistānī Qaum*, lit. 'Pakistani Nation') are the [citizens and nationals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_nationality_law) of the [Islamic Republic of Pakistan](23235.md). Pakistan is the [fifth-most populous country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_and_dependencies_by_population), with a population of over 241.5 million, having the [second-largest Muslim population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_by_country#Countries) as of 2023.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-2023census-34][^cite_note-35] A majority of around 97% of Pakistanis are [Muslims](19541.md). As much as 85-90% of the population follows [Sunni Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Islam).[^cite_note-Oxford-36] The majority of Pakistanis natively speak languages belonging to the [Indo-Iranic family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Iranian_languages) ([Indo-Aryan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Aryan_languages) and [Iranic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_languages) subfamilies).

Located in [South Asia](21566765.md), the country is also the source of a [significantly large diaspora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_diaspora), most of whom reside in the [Arab countries of the Persian Gulf](3088555.md), with an estimated population of 4.7 million.[^cite_note-ophrd.gov.pk-5] The second-largest Pakistani diaspora resides throughout both [Northwestern Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_Europe) and [Western Europe](33800.md), where there are an estimated 2.4 million; over half of this figure resides in the [United Kingdom](31717.md) (see [British Pakistanis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Pakistanis)).[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-2011census-38]

## Ethnic subgroups

Main article: [Ethnic groups in Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_groups_in_Pakistan)

See also: [Indo-Iranic peoples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Iranic_peoples)

Ethnically, [Indo-Aryan peoples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Aryan_peoples) comprise the majority of the population in the eastern provinces of [Pakistani Punjab](24751.md), [Sindh](28972.md), and [Kashmir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashmir), while [Iranic peoples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_peoples) comprise the majority in the western provinces of [Balochistan](4687.md) and [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md). In addition to [its four provinces](446017.md), Pakistan also administers [two disputed territories](3030955.md) known as [Azad Jammu and Kashmir](2745.md) and [Gilgit–Baltistan](18807336.md); both territories also have an Indo-Aryan majority with the exception of the latter's subregion of [Baltistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltistan), which is largely inhabited by [Tibetan peoples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tibetan_people). Pakistan also hosts an insignificant population of [Dravidian peoples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dravidian_peoples), the majority of whom are [South Indians](678583.md) who trace their roots to historical [princely states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princely_state) such as [Hyderabad Deccan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad_State) and are identified with the multi-ethnic community of [Muhajirs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahajir_\(Pakistan\)) (lit. 'migrants'), who arrived in the country after the [partition of British India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partition_of_India) in 1947.[^cite_note-39][^cite_note-40]

Major ethnolinguistic groups in the country include [Punjabis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjabis), [Pashtuns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashtuns), [Sindhis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sindhis), [Saraikis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saraiki_people), and [Baloch people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baloch_people);[^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42] with significant numbers of [Kashmiris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashmiris), [Brahuis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brahui_people), [Hindkowans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindkowans), [Paharis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pahari_people_\(Kashmir\)), [Shina people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shina_people), [Burusho people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burusho_people), [Wakhis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wakhi_people), [Baltis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balti_people), [Chitralis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kho_people), and other minorities.[^cite_note-43][^cite_note-44]

## Culture



Men dressed in [shalwar kameez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shalwar_kameez) in a general store on the road to [Kalash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalasha_Valleys), Pakistan

Main article: [Culture of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culture_of_Pakistan)

The existence of Pakistan as an [Islamic state](2646438.md) since the [1956 constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Pakistan_of_1956) has led to the large-scale injection of [Islam](6037917.md) into most aspects of Pakistani culture and everyday life, which has accordingly impacted the historical values and traditions of the [Muslim-majority population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Pakistan). Marriages and other major events are significantly impacted by regional differences in culture but generally follow [Islamic jurisprudence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiqh) where required. The national dress of Pakistan is the [shalwar kameez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shalwar_kameez), a [unisex garment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unisex_clothing) widely-worn,[^cite_note-Marsden20052-45][^cite_note-Haines20132-46] and [national dress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_dress),[^cite_note-Ozyegin20162-47] of Pakistan. When women wear the shalwar-kameez in some regions, they usually wear a long scarf or shawl called a [dupatta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dupatta) around the head or neck.[^cite_note-SWIE2-48] The dupatta is also employed as a form of modesty—although it is made of delicate material, it obscures the upper body's contours by passing over the shoulders. For Muslim women, the dupatta is a less stringent alternative to the [chador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chador) or [burqa](165826.md).

## Languages

Main article: [Languages of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Languages_of_Pakistan)

[Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu), or *Lashkari* (لشکری ),[^cite_note-49] an [Indo-Aryan language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Aryan_languages), is the [lingua franca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lingua_franca) of Pakistan, and while it shares official status with [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language), it is the preferred and dominant language used for inter-communication between different ethnic groups. It is not believed to be a language affiliated with any ethnicity and its speakers come from various backgrounds.[^cite_note-Mukherjee2018-50][^cite_note-51] Although Indo-Aryan in classification, its exact origins as a language are disputed by scholars.[^cite_note-52] However, despite serving as the country's lingua franca, most Pakistanis speak their ethnic languages and the lingua franca as second. Numerous regional and provincial languages are spoken as native languages by Pakistan's various ethnolinguistic groups, with the [Punjabi language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjabi_language) having a national plurality as the first language of approximately 45 per cent of the total population. Languages with more than a million speakers each include [Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto), [Sindhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sindhi_language), [Saraiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saraiki_language), [Balochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balochi_language), [Brahui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brahui_language), and [Hindko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindko). The [Pakistani dialect of English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_English) is also widely spoken throughout the country, albeit mostly in urban centres such as [Islamabad](51153.md), [Lahore](125315.md) and [Karachi](17123.md).

## Religion

Main article: [Religion in Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religion_in_Pakistan)

Pakistan officially endorses [Islam](6037917.md) as a [state religion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_religion). The overwhelming majority of Pakistanis identify as [Muslims](19541.md), and the country has the [second-largest population of Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_by_country) in the world after [Indonesia](14579.md).[^cite_note-53][^cite_note-54] Other minority religious faiths include [Hinduism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinduism_in_Pakistan), [Christianity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christianity_in_Pakistan), [Ahmadiyya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmadiyya_in_Pakistan), [Sikhism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sikhism_in_Pakistan), [Zoroastrianism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parsis), and [Kalasha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalash_people#Religion). Pakistan's Hindu and Christian minorities comprise the second- and third-largest religious groups in the country, respectively.

The Pakistani diaspora maintains a significant presence in the [Middle East](19323.md), [Europe](9239.md), [North America](21139.md), and [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia_\(continent\)). According to the [United Nations Department of Economic and Social Affairs](13372331.md), Pakistan has the [seventh-largest diaspora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_sovereign_states_and_dependent_territories_by_immigrant_population#UN_2019_report:_emigrant_population) in the world.[^cite_note-55] According to the [Ministry of Overseas Pakistanis and Human Resource Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Overseas_Pakistanis_and_Human_Resource_Development) of the [Government of Pakistan](1897833.md), approximately 10+ million Pakistanis live abroad, with the vast majority (over 4.7 million) residing in the [Arab states of the Persian Gulf](3088555.md).[^cite_note-56]
