**Ashley County** is a rural [South Arkansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Arkansas) county with a culture, economy, and history based on timber and agriculture. Created as Arkansas's 52nd county on November 30, 1848, Ashley County has seven incorporated [municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality), including [Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamburg,_Arkansas), the [county seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_seat) and [Crossett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossett,_Arkansas), the most populous city. The county is also the site of numerous [unincorporated communities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unincorporated_communities) and [ghost towns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghost_town). The [county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_\(United_States\)) is named for [Chester Ashley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chester_Ashley), a prominent lawyer in the [Arkansas Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Territory) and U.S. senator from the state from 1844 to 1848.

The county is roughly divided into two halves by [Bayou Bartholomew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayou_Bartholomew), with the rich, fertile, alluvial soils of the Arkansas Delta in the east, and the [shortleaf pine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shortleaf_pine) forests of the [Arkansas Timberlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Timberlands) in the west. The county contains six protected areas: [Overflow National Wildlife Refuge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overflow_National_Wildlife_Refuge), [Felsenthal National Wildlife Refuge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felsenthal_National_Wildlife_Refuge), three [Wildlife Management Areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Arkansas_Wildlife_Management_Areas) and the [Crossett Experimental Forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossett_Experimental_Forest). Other historical features such as log cabins, one-room school houses, community centers, and museums describe the history and culture of Ashley County.

Ashley County occupies 939.08 square miles (243,220 ha) and contained a population of 19,062 as of the [2020 Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census).[^cite_note-1] The economy is largely based on agriculture and small manufacturing. Poverty and unemployment rates are above national averages, but steady. Household incomes are below state and national averages.

Ashley County is mostly served by two school districts, [Hamburg School District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamburg_School_District_\(Arkansas\)) and [Crossett School District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossett_School_District). Higher education is provided at [University of Arkansas at Monticello College of Technology—Crossett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Arkansas_at_Monticello_College_of_Technology%E2%80%94Crossett?action=edit\&redlink=1), a public two-year community college in Crossett. [Ashley County Medical Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashley_County_Medical_Center?action=edit\&redlink=1) in Crossett is a [community hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Community_hospital) providing [primary care](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primary_care) in the county. Although no [Interstate highways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_Highway_System) serve Ashley County, the county has access to three [United States highways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Numbered_Highways) ([U.S. Route 82](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_82) \[US 82], [US 165](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_165_in_Arkansas), and [US 425](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_425_in_Arkansas)) and eleven [Arkansas state highways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Arkansas_state_highways). Ashley County is also served by one public owned/public use [general aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_aviation) airport, [Z. M. Jack Stell Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Z._M._Jack_Stell_Field), one electric cooperative (Ashley-Chicot Electric Cooperative), and ten community water systems provide [potable water](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potable_water) to customers in the county. It is an alcohol prohibition or [dry county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_county).

## History

Ashley County, the fifth-largest county in Arkansas in terms of land area, was formed by the [7th Arkansas General Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7th_Arkansas_General_Assembly?action=edit\&redlink=1) on November 30, 1848, from portions of Drew, Chicot and Union Counties. It was named after [Chester Ashley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chester_Ashley), a US Senator and land speculator.[^cite_note-1868report-2] The final borders were laid in 1861.

The first settlement in Ashley County appears to have been made by the French at Longview. Now there is no trace of Longview that was a river town connecting the Arkansas Post and Monroe, Louisiana. The Fogle and Gulett families claimed to have located at Longview in 1768 or 1770. In 1846 Captain Phillip Derden came from Virginia and moved to Longview. He bought furs and skins from hunters and trappers and ran a general store for pioneer settlers. He built a log warehouse and a lived in a 2-story log cabin.[^cite_note-3] The home of Isaac Denson in [Fountain Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fountain_Hill,_Arkansas) served as the temporary county seat until a new centrally located town could be established in 1849. It was named "Hamburg" in honor of "fine deer hams" enjoyed by the county commissioners making the selection in the vicinity.[^cite_note-1868report-2] The 1850 courthouse was replaced in 1905 but was lost in a fire in 1921. The current courthouse was built in the 1960s.[^cite_note-1868report-2] The courtroom in the courthouse has a one-of-a-kind architecture: it is round, and the seats are arranged so that members of the audience can always see each other.

[John R. Steelman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_R._Steelman), who wrote his 1928 PhD dissertation on "mob action in the South", wrote:

> Ashley County has had five lynchings since 1900, the last of which occurred in 1927. On February 19, 1904 at Crossett - a sawmill town - a Negro was lynched for Murder and on September 5, another for "assaulting whites". In 1908 the only lynching in the state was at Parkdale, Ashley County. [Earnest Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynching_of_Earnest_Williams) was thrust into eternity by a band of men who were "outraged" at him for "using offensive language"'. On May 30, 1909 a Negro was lynched at Portland for Murder. The last lynching in Ashley County occurred on August 26, 1927, [Winston Pounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynching_of_Winston_Pounds) was "taken from a posse of deputy sheriffs" and hanged to a tree one and a half miles from Wilmot, charged with having "attacked a young married woman".[^cite_note-4]

## Geography



Typical Arkansas Delta field ready for planting with the Bayou Bartholomew riparian area in the background

See also: [Arkansas Delta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Delta) and [Arkansas Timberlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Timberlands)

Ashley County is split between two geographic regions, divided by the [Bayou Bartholomew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayou_Bartholomew). The rich, fertile, alluvial soils of the [Mississippi Alluvial Plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippi_Alluvial_Plain) extend into the eastern part of the county. In Arkansas, this region is called the Arkansas Delta (in Arkansas, usually referred to as "the Delta"), having a distinct history and culture from adjacent regions. West of the Ouachita, Ashley County is characterized by forests of [shortleaf pine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinus_echinata) and [hardwoods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperate_broadleaf_and_mixed_forest) typical of the [Gulf Coastal Plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_Coastal_Plain). In Arkansas, this region is referred to as the [Arkansas Timberlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Timberlands), a subdivision of the [Piney Woods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piney_Woods).

According to the [U.S. Census Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Census_Bureau), the county has a total area of 941 square miles (2,440 km<sup>2</sup>), of which 925 square miles (2,400 km<sup>2</sup>) is land and 15 square miles (39 km<sup>2</sup>) (1.6%) is water.[^cite_note-GR1-5] The [lowest point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_U.S._states_by_elevation) in the state of Arkansas is located on the [Ouachita River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouachita_River) in Ashley County and [Union County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_County,_Arkansas), where it flows out of Arkansas and into [Louisiana](18130.md).

The county is located approximately 58 miles (93 km) north of [Monroe, Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monroe,_Louisiana), 118 miles (190 km) south of [Little Rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Rock,_Arkansas), and 158 miles (254 km) northwest of [Jackson, Mississippi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackson,_Mississippi).[^cite_note-7]

### Hydrology



Looking upstream along Bayou Bartholomew on the edge of Little Bayou WMA

See also: [List of lakes in Ashley County, Arkansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_lakes_in_Ashley_County,_Arkansas)

Water is an extremely important part of Ashley County's geography, history, economy, and culture. The many rivers, streams, and ditches crossing the county have featured prominently since prehistoric times, and many archaeological sites, including the Lake Enterprise Mound from the [Archaic period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archaic_period_\(North_America\)),[^cite_note-8] are along waterways. Many early white settlements, including [Berea, Arkansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berea,_Arkansas?action=edit\&redlink=1), were founded along waterways, but many faded as railroads eclipsed waterways as the favored mode of transportation.[^cite_note-eoc-9]

Ashley County is split among four watersheds: the lower [Saline River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saline_River_\(Ouachita_River_tributary\)) in the northwest, lower [Ouachita River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouachita_River) in the southwest, [Bayou Bartholomew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayou_Bartholomew) across the majority of the county, and the [Boeuf River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeuf_River) along the eastern edge. The Saline River serves as the northwestern boundary of the county before emptying into the [Ouachita River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouachita_River), which serves as the southwestern boundary. The [Port of Crossett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Crossett?action=edit\&redlink=1) is positioned just south of the Saline River's mouth. Within the county, Bearhouse Creek, Chemin-a-haut Bayou, Fountain Creek, Flat Creek, Mill Creek, and Overflow Creek are important watercourses.[^cite_note-10]

### Protected areas

Ashley County contains [Overflow National Wildlife Refuge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overflow_National_Wildlife_Refuge), part of the [Felsenthal National Wildlife Refuge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felsenthal_National_Wildlife_Refuge), and four [Wildlife Management Areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Arkansas_Wildlife_Management_Areas) (WMAs) under [Arkansas Game and Fish Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Game_and_Fish_Commission) jurisdiction.

Beryl Anthony Lower Ouachita WMA preserves seasonally flooded [bottomland hardwood forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bottomland_hardwood_forest) habitat along the Ouachita River between the Felsenthal NWR and the Louisiana state line for preservation, recreation, and hunting opportunities. Duck hunting, especially when the Ouachita River floods, is the primary hunting opportunity due to the site's position along the [Mississippi Flyway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippi_Flyway), with deer and squirrel hunting also available during the year.[^cite_note-11] Most of the 7,020 acres (2,840 ha) is on the Union County side of the Ouachita River, leaving the only public access to the Ashley County area via boat.[^cite_note-12] A small segment in Ashley County contains the Coffee Prairie Natural Area, owned by the [Arkansas Natural Heritage Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Natural_Heritage_Commission?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-ash-13]

The Casey Jones Leased Lands WMA is a common name for five fragmented parcels in Ashley and Drew counties established in 1988. The two largest segments in Ashley County are south of Crossett and in the northwest corner of the county, with a small area northwest of the Overflow WMA also included in Casey Jones WMA.[^cite_note-14] This lease includes segments of working forest actively managed for forest products, primarily by single tree selection. Habitats range from upland pine to bottomland hardwood depending upon location. Deer and turkey hunting are most common in the WMA.[^cite_note-15]

Little Bayou WMA is located in northeastern Ashley County along the western banks of Bayou Bartholomew at the mouth of Little Bayou. The area contains a boat ramp on Bayou Bartholomew, as well as [birding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birding), camping, fishing, and hunting. The former agricultural area was replanted with bottomland and upland hardwoods, short-leaf pines, and native warm season grasses in the 1990s.[^cite_note-16]



Residence at the Experimental Forest built by the [CCC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civilian_Conservation_Corps), now listed on the [NRHP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NRHP)

The [Crossett Experimental Forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossett_Experimental_Forest) is a 1,765 acres (714 ha) property donated by [Georgia-Pacific Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia-Pacific_Corporation) in 1934 to research forest management in second-growth [loblolly pine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loblolly_pine) and shortleaf pine forests. Within the Forest is the Reynolds Natural Area, a stand that has been largely untouched since the area's creation in 1934. The property is managed by the [Southern Research Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Research_Station) of the [United States Forest Service](42652.md).

### 2020 Census

As of the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census), the county had a population of 19,062. The median age was 43.7 years. 22.7% of residents were under the age of 18 and 21.2% of residents were 65 years of age or older. For every 100 females there were 92.5 males, and for every 100 females age 18 and over there were 89.0 males age 18 and over.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-23][^cite_note-Census2020PL-24]

The racial makeup of the county was 68.0% [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Americans), 24.5% [Black or African American](2154.md), 0.3% [American Indian and Alaska Native](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Americans_in_the_United_States), 0.2% [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Americans), <0.1% [Native Hawaiian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Hawaiians) and [Pacific Islander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Islander), 3.3% from some other race, and 3.7% from [two or more races](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiracial_Americans). [Hispanic or Latino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_and_Latino_Americans) residents of any race comprised 5.8% of the population.[^cite_note-Census2020PL-24]

37.7% of residents lived in urban areas, while 62.3% lived in rural areas.[^cite_note-Census2020DHC-25]

There were 8,019 households in the county, of which 29.0% had children under the age of 18 living in them. Of all households, 45.9% were married-couple households, 19.0% were households with a male householder and no spouse or partner present, and 30.4% were households with a female householder and no spouse or partner present. About 30.4% of all households were made up of individuals and 14.7% had someone living alone who was 65 years of age or older.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-23]

There were 9,371 housing units, of which 14.4% were vacant. Among occupied housing units, 72.8% were owner-occupied and 27.2% were renter-occupied. The homeowner vacancy rate was 1.9% and the rental vacancy rate was 11.0%.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-23]

The average household size was 2.55 and the average family size was 3.02.

In the county, the population was spread out, with 26.80% under the age of 18, 8.30% from 18 to 24, 27.20% from 25 to 44, 23.90% from 45 to 64, and 13.80% who were 65 years of age or older. The median age was 36 years. For every 100 females there were 93.30 males. For every 100 females age 18 and over, there were 90.60 males.

The per capita income for the county was \$15,702. About 13.90% of families and 17.50% of the population were below the poverty line, including 25.70% of those under age 18 and 17.20% of those age 65 or over.

## Human resources

### Education

See also: [Education in Arkansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_in_Arkansas)

Educational attainment in Ashley County is typical for a rural Arkansas county, with a 2012 study finding 84.9% of Ashley County residents over age 25 held a high school degree or higher and 13.2% holding a bachelor's degree or higher, compared to Arkansas statewide averages of 84.8% and 21.1%. Ashley County is significantly below national averages of 86.7% and 29.8%, respectively.[^cite_note-31]

#### Primary and secondary education



Public school district boundaries in Ashley County as of July 2016

Two public school districts are based in Ashley County; [Hamburg School District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamburg_School_District_\(Arkansas\)) is the largest school district in Ashley County, with the [Crossett School District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossett_School_District) serving the west side of the county. Successful completion of the curriculum of these schools leads to graduation from [Hamburg High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamburg_High_School_\(Arkansas\)) or [Crossett High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossett_High_School), respectively. Both high schools are accredited by the [Arkansas Department of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Department_of_Education) (ADE). A small area in eastern Ashley County is served by the [Lakeside School District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakeside_School_District_\(Lake_Village,_Arkansas\)), which is based in [Lake Village](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Village,_Arkansas).

#### Higher education

There are no institutions of [higher education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_education) in Ashley County. The nearest public [community college](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Community_college) is [Louisiana Delta Community College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_Delta_Community_College) in [Bastrop, Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bastrop,_Louisiana), followed by [South Arkansas Community College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Arkansas_Community_College) in [El Dorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Dorado,_Arkansas). Nearby public four-year universities include [University of Arkansas at Monticello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Arkansas_at_Monticello), [University of Louisiana at Monroe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Louisiana_at_Monroe), and [Louisiana Tech University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_Tech_University) in [Ruston, Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruston,_Louisiana). The University of Arkansas at Monticello's [College of Technology—Crossett](http://www.uamont.edu/pages/uam-college-of-technology-crossett/degree-programs/), formerly the Forest Echoes Technology Institute, is located between North Crossett and Hamburg and offers associate degrees in general and industrial technology and technical certifications in a number of medical and technical programs.

#### Libraries

Ashley County has two libraries: the Ashley County Library in Hamburg and Crossett Public Library in Crossett. Both institutions offers books, [e-books](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E-book), media, reference, youth, business and [genealogy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genealogy) services.

### Public health

Ashley County's above-average poverty rate also indicates a high [Medicaid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medicaid) eligibility rate. As of 2012, 35.3% of Ashley County was eligible for Medicaid, with 72.5% of children under 19 eligible for [ARKids First](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARKids_First?action=edit\&redlink=1), a program by the [Arkansas Department of Human Services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Department_of_Human_Services) that combines children's Medicaid (ARKids A) and other programs for families with higher incomes (ARKids B).[^cite_note-32] The county's population is significantly above healthy weight, with 71.9% of adults and 39.8% of children/adolescents ranking as overweight or obese, above the state averages of 67.1% and 39.3%, themselves significantly above national averages of 62.9% and 30.3%, respectively.[^cite_note-33]

The [Ashley County Medical Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashley_County_Medical_Center?action=edit\&redlink=1) in Crossett is the nearest medical center offering acute inpatient care, [emergency care](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergency_care), [diagnostics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diagnosis), general surgery, orthopedic surgery, OB/GYN, rehabilitation, therapy, and senior care services. The facility is rated as a [Level 4 Trauma Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Level_4_Trauma_Center) by the [Arkansas Department of Health](51446191.md).

The nearest [Level 1 Trauma Centers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Level_1_Trauma_Center) are [Arkansas Children's Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Children's_Hospital) and [University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Arkansas_for_Medical_Sciences), both in Little Rock.[^cite_note-34]

### Public safety

The [Ashley County Sheriff's Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashley_County_Sheriff's_Office?action=edit\&redlink=1) is the primary law enforcement agency in the county. The agency is led by the Ashley County Sheriff, an official elected by countywide vote every four years. Police departments in Crossett, Hamburg, Portland and Wilmot provide law enforcement in their respective jurisdictions, with remaining municipalities contracting with the Ashley County Sheriff's Office for law enforcement services.

The county is under the jurisdiction of the Ashley County District Court, a [local district court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_District_Court?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-harvp|Directory|2017|p=_30-35] Local district courts in Arkansas are courts of [original jurisdiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Original_jurisdiction) for criminal, civil, small claims, and traffic matters.[^cite_note-jd-36] Local district courts are presided over by an elected part-time judge who may privately practice law. The district court has two departments, Crossett and Hamburg.[^cite_note-harvp|Directory|2017|p=_30-35]

Superseding district court jurisdiction is the [10th Judicial Circuit Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Circuit_Courts), which covers Ashley, Bradley, Chicot, Desha, Drew counties. The 10th Circuit contains three circuit judges, elected to six-year terms circuitwide.[^cite_note-37]

[Fire protection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_protection) is provided by twelve agencies in Ashley County, together covering the entire county except approximately 2 square miles (5.2 km<sup>2</sup>) in the Felsenthal NWR. The seven incorporated municipalities and two census-designated places each provide fire protection, in some cases extending beyond corporate limits. Rural areas are served by the Berlin Volunteer Fire Department, Milo Fire Department, or Unity Frost Prairie Fire Department.[^cite_note-38] All fire departments in Ashley County are volunteer-based, except the Crossett Fire Department.[^cite_note-39]

### Annual cultural events

Hamburg hosts the World Famous Armadillo Festival annually during the first weekend in May.[^cite_note-49] The Ashley County Fair is hosted during the first weekend in September at the Ashley County Fairgrounds in Hamburg, and includes a rodeo and the Miss Ashley County pageant in the Maxwell Hill Auditorium.[^cite_note-50] Crossett hosts the Crossett [Professional Rodeo Cowboys Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_Rodeo_Cowboys_Association) Rodeo in August and the Wiggins Cabin Arts & Crafts Festival each October.[^cite_note-51]

The two most famous people from Ashley County are likely former NBA world champion [Scottie Pippen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottie_Pippen), and talent manager [Joe Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Jackson_\(talent_manager\)), who was the patriarch of the [Jackson family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackson_family) that includes [Michael Jackson](14995351.md) and [Janet Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janet_Jackson).

## Government and politics

See also: [Government of Arkansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Arkansas), [County judge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_judge), and [Quorum Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quorum_Court)

The county government is a constitutional body granted specific powers by the [Constitution of Arkansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Arkansas) and the [Arkansas Code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Code?action=edit\&redlink=1). The quorum court is the legislative branch of the county government and controls all spending and revenue collection. Representatives are called *justices of the peace* and are elected from county districts every even-numbered year. The number of districts in a county vary from nine to fifteen based on population, and district boundaries are drawn by the Ashley County Election Commission. The Ashley County Quorum Court has nine members.[^cite_note-55] Presiding over quorum court meetings is the *county judge*, who serves as the [chief operating officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_operating_officer) of the county. The county judge is elected at-large and does not vote in quorum court business, although capable of vetoing quorum court decisions.[^cite_note-56][^cite_note-57]

### Presidential Elections

Since the mid-20th century, Ashley County has transitioned from reliably [Democratic](5043544.md) to steady [Republican](32070.md) in national, state and local elections. Ashley County was part of the [Solid South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solid_South), a period of Democratic hegemony which began after [Reconstruction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reconstruction_Era) following the [Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_War). This resulted essentially in a [one-party system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-party_system), in which a candidate's victory in Democratic primary elections was [tantamount to election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tantamount_to_election) to the office itself. The Solid South [started to dissolve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Democrats#1933%E2%80%931981) following the [Civil Rights Act in 1964](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Rights_Act_of_1964), which is when Ashley County's streak of supporting Democratic candidates ended. Republican presidential candidate support continued to grow after 1964, although Ashley County strongly supported fellow Southerners in Presidential elections: [Alabama](303.md) [Governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_governors_of_Alabama) [George Wallace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Wallace) in [1968](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1968_United_States_presidential_election_in_Arkansas), [Georgia](48830.md) [Governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_governors_of_Georgia) [Jimmy Carter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Carter) in [1976](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1976_United_States_presidential_election_in_Arkansas), fellow Arkansan [Bill Clinton](3356.md) in [1992](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_United_States_presidential_election_in_Arkansas) and [1996](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1996_United_States_presidential_election_in_Arkansas), and [U.S. vice president](32759.md) [Al Gore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Gore) in [2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_United_States_presidential_election_in_Arkansas). By the [2004 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_United_States_presidential_election_in_Arkansas), the county narrowly supported [George W. Bush](3414021.md), but subsequently supported [Barack Obama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barack_Obama)'s opponents and [Donald Trump](4848272.md) by large margins.[^cite_note-pol-63]

### Taxation

See also: [Taxation in the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taxation_in_the_United_States)

[Property tax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Property_tax) is assessed by the Ashley County Assessor annually based upon the fair market value of the property and determining which tax rate, commonly called a *millage* in Arkansas, will apply. The rate depends upon the property's location with respect to city limits, school district, and special [tax increment financing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tax_increment_financing) (TIF) districts. This tax is collected by the Ashley County Collector between the first business day of March of each year through October 15 without penalty. The Ashley County Treasurer disburses tax revenues to various government agencies, such as cities, county road departments, fire departments, libraries, and police departments in accordance with the budget set by the quorum court.

Sales and use taxes in Arkansas are voter approved and collected by the [Arkansas Department of Finance and Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Department_of_Finance_and_Administration). Arkansas's statewide [sales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sales_tax) and [use tax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Use_tax) has been 6.5% since July 1, 2013.[^cite_note-66] Ashley County has an additional sales and use tax of 1.50% since July 1, 2010. Within Ashley County, the City of Crossett has an additional 2.25% sales and use tax since January 1, 2014, and Fountain Hill, Hamburg, Portland, and Wilmot have a 1.00% sales and use tax rate.[^cite_note-67] The [Arkansas State Treasurer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_State_Treasurer) disburses tax revenue to counties/cities in accordance with tax rules.

## Communities



The historic [Crossett Municipal Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossett_Municipal_Building) is home to municipal government in Crossett

Six incorporated cities and one town are located within the county. The largest city is [Crossett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossett,_Arkansas), located in the southwest part of the county near the [Ouachita River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouachita_River). Crossett's population in 2010 was 5,507, but including the two adjacent [unincorporated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unincorporated_area) [census designated places](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Census_designated_place) of [North Crossett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Crossett,_Arkansas) and [West Crossett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Crossett,_Arkansas), the total population was 10,752 in 2010. The county seat, [Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamburg,_Arkansas) is centrally located in the county and had a population of 2,857 at the 2010 Census. The four remaining cities are all located along US 165 in the eastern part of the county, and have populations below 1,000 people: [Montrose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montrose,_Arkansas), [Parkdale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parkdale,_Arkansas), [Portland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland,_Arkansas), and [Wilmot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilmot,_Arkansas). The lone incorporated town, [Fountain Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fountain_Hill,_Arkansas), is located north of Hamburg and had a population of 175 in 2010.[^cite_note-68]

Ashley County has dozens of unincorporated communities and ghost towns within its borders. This is due to early settlers in Arkansas tending to settle in small clusters rather than incorporated towns, especially along bayous or river landings in Ashley County. For example, communities like [Longview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longview_Crossing,_Arkansas?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Milo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milo,_Arkansas?action=edit\&redlink=1) had a post office or other buildings at some point in their history. Other communities are simply a few dwellings at a crossroads that have adopted a common place name over time. Some are officially listed as populated places by the United States Geological Survey, and others are listed as historic settlements.

### Townships



Townships in Ashley County, Arkansas, as of 2010

[Townships in Arkansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Arkansas_townships) are the divisions of a county. Each township includes unincorporated areas; some may have incorporated cities or towns within part of their boundaries. Arkansas townships have limited purposes in modern times. However, the [United States census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_census) does list Arkansas population based on townships (sometimes referred to as "county subdivisions" or "minor civil divisions"). Townships are also of value for historical purposes in terms of genealogical research. Each town or city is within one or more townships in an Arkansas county based on census maps and publications. ## Infrastructure

### Aviation

The county contains one public owned/public use airport: [Z. M. Jack Stell Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Z._M._Jack_Stell_Field), a small, rural airport east of Crossett with over 16,000 annual operations, almost entirely agricultural spraying operations.[^cite_note-72] Ashley County also contains five private airfields in the eastern part of the county.[^cite_note-ash-13]

### Major highways



US 82/US 425/AR 8 overlap as Main Street in Hamburg

Ashley County is not served by any Interstate highways; the nearest access to the Interstate system is [Interstate 20](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_20) (I-20) in [Monroe, Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monroe,_Louisiana). The county serves as a junction point for several US highways: [US Highway 82](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_82_in_Arkansas) (US 82) crosses the county from west to east. This highway runs across South Arkansas, providing access to population centers including [El Dorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Dorado,_Arkansas), [Magnolia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnolia,_Arkansas), and [Texarkana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texarkana,_Arkansas). The highway also has two [special routes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_route) in Ashley County: a [spur route to Felsenthal National Wildlife Refuge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_routes_of_U.S._Route_82#Felsenthal_National_Wildlife_Refuge_spur) and [business route in Montrose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_routes_of_U.S._Route_82#Montrose_business_route). [US 165](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_165_in_Arkansas) and [US 425](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_425_in_Arkansas) cross the county from south to north, both run south into Louisiana, with the former running north through the Arkansas Delta to [North Little Rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Little_Rock,_Arkansas) and the latter providing northbound connections to [Monticello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monticello,_Arkansas) and [Pine Bluff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pine_Bluff,_Arkansas).[^cite_note-ahtd-73]

Eleven [state highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_state_highways_in_Arkansas) designations serve the traveling public in the county, ranging from short connector routes to long highways traversing the entire county.[^cite_note-75] [Highway 8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Highway_8) east-west across the southern part of the state, entering Ashley County from [Warren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warren,_Arkansas), passing through from northwest to southeast through Hamburg, and exiting Ashley County running east to [Eudora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eudora,_Arkansas). [Highway 133](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Highway_133) serves north–south traffic around Crossett. [Highway 144](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Highway_144), two segments of [Highway 160](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Highway_160), and three segments of [Highway 52](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Highway_52) provide east–west connectivity. [Highway 169](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Highway_169) and [Highway 189](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Highway_189) connect rural areas to Crossett and Hamburg, respectively. [Highway 173](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Highway_173) connects Overflow NWR to the state highway system. [Highway 209](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas_Highway_209) is a minor route in Parkdale.[^cite_note-ash-13]

### Utilities

See also: [List of public utilities in Arkansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_public_utilities_in_Arkansas)

The Ashley-Chicot Electric Cooperative, based in Hamburg, is a non-profit electric [utility cooperative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utility_cooperative) serving the rural areas of Ashley County with electric service, as well as parts of adjacent counties. [Entergy Arkansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entergy) provides electricity for the cities of Hamburg and Crossett and nearby populated outlying areas, and to the small communities along US 165.[^cite_note-76]

The [Arkansas Department of Health](51446191.md) (ADH) is responsible for the regulation and oversight of public water systems throughout the state. Ten community water systems are based in Ashley County: the Crossett Water Commission, Fountain Hill Waterworks, Hamburg Waterworks, Montrose/Boydell Water System, North Crossett Utilities, Northeast Crossett Water Association, Parkdale Waterworks, Portland Waterworks, Wilmot Waterworks, and the West Ashley County Water Association. All use [groundwater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groundwater) as the source.[^cite_note-79] The Crossett Water Commission has the largest direct retail population served (8,038, all in Ashley County), followed by Hamburg (5,481), and North Crossett Utilities (2,924). The remaining water systems have under 1,000 retail population served.[^cite_note-80]
