**County Galway** ([/ˈɡɔːlweɪ/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English) [*GAWL-way*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:Pronunciation_respelling_key); [Irish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_language): *Contae na Gaillimhe*) is a [county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_Ireland) in [Ireland](14560.md). It is in the [Northern and Western Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_and_Western_Region), taking up the south of the [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Ireland) of [Connacht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connacht). The county population was 276,451 at the 2022 census.[^cite_note-cso2022-5]

There are several [Irish-speaking areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaeltacht) in the west of the county. The traditional county includes the city of [Galway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway), but the city and county are separate local government areas, administered by the [local authorities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_government_in_the_Republic_of_Ireland) of [Galway City Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway_City_Council) in the urban area and [Galway County Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway_County_Council) in the rest of the county.

## History



[Dunguaire Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunguaire_Castle), [Kinvara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinvara), built c. 1520

The first inhabitants in the Galway area arrived around the 5th millennium BC. Shell [middens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midden) indicate the existence of people as early as 5000 BC.

The county originally comprised several kingdoms and territories which predate the formation of the county. These kingdoms included [Aidhne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aidhne), [Uí Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U%C3%AD_Maine), [Maigh Seóla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maigh_Se%C3%B3la), [Conmhaicne Mara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conmhaicne_Mara), [Soghain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soghain) and [Máenmag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A1enmag). County Galway became an official entity around 1569 AD.[^cite_note-6] The region known as [Connemara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connemara) retains a distinct identity within the county, though its boundaries are unclear, and it may account for as much as one third, or as little as 20%, of the county.

The county includes a number of inhabited islands, such as the [Aran Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aran_Islands) ([Irish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_language): *Oileáin Árann*) and [Inishbofin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inishbofin,_County_Galway) ([Irish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_language): *Inis Bó Fine*).

With the arrival of [Christianity](5211.md) many [monasteries](45856.md) were built in the county. Monasteries kept written records of events in the area and of its people. ## Irish language

Nearly 20% of the population of County Galway live in areas classed as Gaeltachts (Irish-speaking districts). County Galway is home to the largest [Gaeltacht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaeltacht) Irish-speaking region in Ireland. There are over 48,000 people living within this region, which extends from Galway city westwards through [Connemara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connemara). The region consists of the following Irish-speaking areas: [Galway City Gaeltacht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway_City_Gaeltacht) (parts of the city), [Gaeltacht Cois Fharraige](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaeltacht_Cois_Fharraige), [Conamara Theas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conamara_Theas), [Aran Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aran_Islands), and [Duiche Sheoigheach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joyce_Country) (a part of the northern Galway region known as "Joyce Country" and [Maam Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maam_Valley)).

All schools within the Gaeltacht use the Irish language for classroom instruction. There is also a third-level constituent college of [NUIG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NUIG) called [Acadamh na hOllscolaíochta Gaeilge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acadamh_na_hOllscola%C3%ADochta_Gaeilge) in [Carraroe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carraroe) and [Carna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carna,_County_Galway). [Clifden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clifden) is the largest town in the region. Galway City is also home to Ireland's only Irish-language theatre, [Taibhdhearc na Gaillimhe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taibhdhearc_na_Gaillimhe). There is a strong Irish-language media presence in this area too, which boasts the radio station [Raidió na Gaeltachta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raidi%C3%B3_na_Gaeltachta) and *[Foinse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foinse)* newspaper in [Carraroe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carraroe) and national TV station [TG4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TG4) in [Baile na hAbhann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baile_na_hAbhann). The [Aran Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aran_Islands) are also part of the Galway Gaeltacht.

According to Census 2016, 84,249 people in County Galway claimed they could speak Irish.[^cite_note-\:0-15] According to Census 2011, the Galway city and county Gaeltacht has a population of 48,907, of which 30,978 said they could speak Irish; 23,788 classed themselves as native Irish speakers, while 7,190 speak Irish daily only within the classroom. There are 3,006 attending the ten [Gaelscoil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaelscoil) (Irish language primary schools) and three [Gaelcholáiste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaelchol%C3%A1iste) (Irish language secondary schools) outside the Galway Gaeltacht.[^cite_note-16] According to the Irish Census 2016, there are 9,445 people in the county who identify themselves as being daily Irish speakers outside the education system.[^cite_note-\:0-15]

## Local government and politics



The island of Ireland, showing location of County Galway.



[County Hall, Galway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Hall,_Galway)

[Galway City Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway_City_Council) and [Galway County Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway_County_Council) are the local authorities for the respective local government areas. The local government area of County Galway includes some suburbs of the city not within the city area. Each local authority is responsible for certain local [services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_services) such as [sanitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanitation), [planning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planning) and [development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_estate_development), [libraries](17727.md), the collection of [motor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automobile) [taxation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tax), local [roads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road) and [social housing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_housing).

Under the [Local Government (Ireland) Act 1898](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_\(Ireland\)_Act_1898), County Galway was divided into the [urban districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_and_rural_districts_\(Ireland\)) of [Ballinasloe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballinasloe) and [Galway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway), and the [rural districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rural_district_\(Ireland\)) of Ballinasloe No. 1, Clifden, Galway, Glennamaddy, Gort, Loughrea, Mount Bellew, Oughterard, Portumna, and Tuam.[^cite_note-17] [Loughrea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loughrea), within the rural district of Loughrea, and [Tuam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuam), within the rural district of Tuam, had [town commissioners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Town_commissioners).[^cite_note-18] The rural districts were abolished in 1925.[^cite_note-19]

In 1937, the urban district of [Galway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway) became the borough of Galway, remaining part of County Galway.[^cite_note-20] In 1986, the borough of Galway became the [county borough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_borough) of Galway and ceased to part of County Galway.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22] In 2002, all county boroughs were redefined as cities.[^cite_note-23]

In 2002, the urban district of Ballinasloe and the town commissioners of Loughrea and Tuam became [town councils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Town_council_\(Ireland\)).[^cite_note-24] All town councils in Ireland were abolished in 2014.[^cite_note-25]

As part of the [Northern and Western Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_and_Western_Region), Galway County Council has three representatives and Galway City Council has two representatives on the Northern and Western Regional Assembly.[^cite_note-26]

The county is part of three [Dáil constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C3%A1il_constituencies): [Galway East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway_East) (4 seats), [Galway West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway_West) (5 seats) and [Roscommon–Galway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roscommon%E2%80%93Galway) (3 seats).[^cite_note-27] It is part of the [Midlands–North-West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midlands%E2%80%93North-West) constituency for [European elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections_in_the_European_Union).[^cite_note-28]

## Geography



The prehistoric hill fort of Dún Aonghasa, Inishmore Island

County Galway is home to Na Beanna Beola ([Twelve Bens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve_Bens)) mountain range, Na Sléibhte Mhám Toirc (the Maum Turk mountains), and the low mountains of Sliabh Echtghe (Slieve Aughty). The highest point in the county is one of the Twelve Bens, Benbaun, at 729m.

### Lakes

County Galway is partly home to a number of [Ireland's](14560.md) largest lakes including [Lough Corrib](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lough_Corrib) (the largest lake in the Republic of Ireland), [Lough Derg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lough_Derg_\(Shannon\)) and [Lough Mask](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lough_Mask). The county is also home to a large number of smaller lakes, many of which are in the [Connemara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connemara) region. These include [Lough Anaserd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lough_Anaserd), [Ardderry Lough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ardderry_Lough), [Aughrusbeg Lough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aughrusbeg_Lough), [Ballycuirke Lough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballycuirke_Lough), [Ballynahinch Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballynahinch_Lake), [Lough Bofin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lough_Bofin_\(Galway\)), [Lough Cutra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lough_Cutra), [Derryclare Lough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derryclare_Lough), [Lough Fee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lough_Fee), [Glendollagh Lough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glendollagh_Lough), [Lough Glenicmurrin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lough_Glenicmurrin), [Lough Inagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lough_Inagh), [Kylemore Lough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylemore_Lough), [Lettercraffroe Lough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lettercraffroe_Lough), [Maumeen Lough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maumeen_Lough), [Lough Nafooey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lough_Nafooey), [Lough Rea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lough_Rea), [Ross Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_Lake_\(Ireland\)) and [Lough Shindilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lough_Shindilla).

### Climate

The location of County Galway, situated on the west coast of Ireland, allows it to be directly influenced by the [Gulf Stream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_Stream). Temperature extremes are rare and short lived, though inland areas, particularly east of the Corrib, can boast some of the highest recorded temperatures of the summer in the island of Ireland (sometimes exceeding 30 °C); though these temperatures only occur when land warmed east winds sweep the area; the opposite effect can occur in the winter. Overall, however, Galway is influenced mainly by Atlantic airstreams which bring ample rainfall in between the fleeting sunshine. Rainfall occurs in every month of the year, though the late autumn and winter months can be particularly wet as Atlantic cyclonic activity increases and passes over and around the area, and which is why Galway tends to bear the brunt of severe windstorms that can occur between August and March. The county on average receives about 1300mm of rainfall annually, though some areas along the west coast of the county can receive up to 1900mm and beyond. [Extreme weather](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extreme_weather) such as blizzards, thunderstorms, flash flooding and hail, though rare, can and do occur, particularly when [air masses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_mass) of continental origin are undercut by more humid and unstable Atlantic flows.

## Economy

According to numbers published by Galway Chamber of Commerce in early 2019, there were then 196 [information and communications technology (ICT)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_and_communications_technology) organisations in Galway, including IBM, SAP, Oracle and Cisco.[^cite_note-chambereconomy-29] There is a number of medical device companies in the area, including Medtronic (with approximately 1,800 employees) and Boston Scientific (2,800 employees).[^cite_note-chambereconomy-29]

## Sports

See also: [Galway § Sport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway#Sport)

Gaelic games are the most popular sport in the county. Galway has had traditional regions in which [Gaelic football](37688.md) or hurling is played. For example, in south and eastern County Galway, in places such as [Portumna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portumna), [Gort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gort), [Clarinbridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarinbridge) and [Athenry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athenry), hurling is the dominant sport with successful teams at county and national level. [Gaelic football](37688.md) is more prominent throughout much of the rest of the county, with most of the county players being from the [Tuam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuam) area, [Oughterard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oughterard), [Moycullen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moycullen) or parts of [Galway city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway). [Corofin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corofin,_County_Galway), a village and parish in County Galway, their local Gaelic football club, [Corofin GAA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corofin_GAA_\(Galway\)), have won five [All-Ireland Senior Club Football Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All-Ireland_Senior_Club_Football_Championship). [Caltra GAA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caltra_GAA) and [Salthill–Knocknacarra GAA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salthill%E2%80%93Knocknacarra_GAA) each won a championship, in 2004 and 2006 respectively. The former is based in the village of [Caltra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caltra), while the latter represents the parishes of [Salthill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salthill) and [Knocknacarra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knocknacarra) in [Galway city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway_city).

[Galway United FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway_United_FC) competes in the [League of Ireland Premier Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/League_of_Ireland_Premier_Division) and plays home games in [Eamonn Deacy Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eamonn_Deacy_Park).

[Connacht Rugby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connacht_Rugby), which competes in the [United Rugby Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Rugby_Championship), is based in [the Sportsgrounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway_Sportsgrounds) in [Galway city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway_city). The two main amateur rugby clubs in the county are [Galway Corinthians RFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galway_Corinthians_RFC) and [Galwegians RFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galwegians_RFC) which compete in the [All-Ireland League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All-Ireland_League_\(rugby_union\)).

County Galway is home to several basketball clubs, including [Super League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_League_\(Ireland\)) teams [University of Galway Maree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Galway_Maree?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Moycullen Basketball Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moycullen_Basketball_Club).

[Athletics](43719.md) is also a popular sport in Galway. Athletics clubs in the county include: Galway City Harriers, [Craughwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craughwell) Athletic Club, [Athenry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athenry) A.C, [Tuam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuam) A.C, and [Loughrea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loughrea) A.C.
