**Goolwa South** (previously known as South Goolwa) is a locality in the [Australian state](4689264.md) of [South Australia](26716.md) located about 67 kilometres (42 miles) south of the state capital of [Adelaide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adelaide_city_centre) on both on the southern continental coastline and the south-western coast of [Lake Alexandrina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Alexandrina_\(South_Australia\)). It occupies the full extent of the [Sir Richard Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir_Richard_Peninsula), land to the immediate west and part of the Goolwa Channel in the lake system to its north.[^cite_note-PLB-2][^cite_note-postcode-5]

The name was first used in respect to a [private sub-division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivision_\(land\)) of land within the [cadastral unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cadastral_divisions_of_South_Australia) of the [Hundred of Goolwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hundred_of_Goolwa) in 1925. Boundaries were created for the “long established name” in 1993 and were again adjusted in August 2000 to include the Sir Richard Peninsula in the adjoining [Hundred of Nangkita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hundred_of_Nangkita).[^cite_note-PLB-2]

The western end of the locality is occupied by an urban area which extends into the adjoining localities of [Goolwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goolwa,_South_Australia) and [Goolwa Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goolwa_Beach,_South_Australia) while the remainder of the locality is zoned for [conservation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservation_\(ethic\)) and includes part of the [Coorong National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coorong_National_Park).[^cite_note-DP-8][^cite_note-PLB-2]

Goolwa South is located within the federal [division of Mayo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_of_Mayo), the state [electoral district of Finniss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_district_of_Finniss) and the local government area of the [Alexandrina Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandrina_Council).[^cite_note-AEC-3][^cite_note-PLB-2]
