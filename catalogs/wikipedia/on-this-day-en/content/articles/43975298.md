The **Aspiras–Palispis Highway** (formerly known and still referred to as **Marcos Highway** or **Agoo–Baguio Road**) is a major Philippine [highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highways_in_the_Philippines) in northern [Luzon](69375.md) that runs from the city of [Baguio](162480.md) landlocked by the province of [Benguet](274018.md) to the [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_the_Philippines) of [Agoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agoo,_La_Union) in the province of [La Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Union).

The 47.17-kilometer (29.31 mi)[^cite_note-DPWH-1] highway traverses the municipality of [Tuba](2137478.md) in [Benguet](274018.md) and the autonomous city of [Baguio](162480.md), and the municipalities of [Pugo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pugo,_La_Union), [Tubao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tubao,_La_Union), and [Agoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agoo,_La_Union) in [La Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Union).

It is one of the four main roads used by motorists and travelers to access Baguio from the northwestern lowlands of Luzon.[^cite_note-LCP-2] The highway's several rehabilitation and development efforts[^cite_note-Midland-3] led to the road's categorization as an "[all-weather road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All-weather_road)",[^cite_note-GoBaguio-4] and is the preferred highway by motorists over the older [Kennon Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kennon_Road).[^cite_note-Libotero-5]

The entire highway is designated as **National Route 208** (**N208**) of the [Philippine highway network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_highway_network).

## History

Marcos Highway was renamed Aspiras–Palispis Highway on October 31, 2000, with the issuance of [Republic Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_Act) 8971.[^cite_note-ChanRobles-6] The highway section covering the [province](165100.md) of [Benguet](274018.md) was designated as the Ben Palispis Highway in honor of former Benguet Governor Ben Palispis. The La Union section of Marcos Highway was named Jose D. Aspiras Highway after former La Union assemblyman and Tourism Minister [Jose Aspiras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jose_Aspiras).[^cite_note-Libotero-5][^cite_note-ChanRobles-6] However, the former name is considered by people to be more familiar and is still preferred by most.[^cite_note-SunStar-7]

The Palina Bridge, situated along the Benguet–La Union boundary, serves as the boundary between the two highways.[^cite_note-Mapcentral-8]

## Notable landmarks

The 519-meter (1,703 ft) long *Badiwan [Viaduct](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viaduct)*, constructed in 2001, with help from the [Japan International Cooperation Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_International_Cooperation_Agency) (JICA), serves as a major bridge along Badiwan, Barangay Poblacion in Tuba, Benguet.[^cite_note-PhilStar-9][^cite_note-TubaGov-10] Several meters from it is a concrete [rock shed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_shed) to protect motorists from [landslides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landslides).[^cite_note-Libotero-5]

The blasted remains of the [bust of Ferdinand Marcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bust_of_Ferdinand_Marcos),[^cite_note-CityofPines-11] a giant concrete [head sculpture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bust_\(sculpture\)) of the former president, can be seen along the highway at Barangay Palina, in Pugo, La Union.[^cite_note-PhilStar2-12] [^cite_note-TripodLU-13]
