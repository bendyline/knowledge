**Sumy Raion** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Сумський район, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Ukrainian): *Sumskyi raion*) is a [raion](6719452.md) (district) in [Sumy Oblast](1567618.md) in central [Ukraine](31750.md). The [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) of the raion is the city of [Sumy](561837.md), which was formerly administratively incorporated as a [city of oblast significance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_regional_significance_\(Ukraine\)) until 2020. Population: 434,316 (2022 estimate).[^cite_note-ua2022estimate-1]

On 18 July 2020, as part of the administrative reform of Ukraine, the number of raions of Sumy Oblast was reduced to five, and the area of Sumy Raion was significantly expanded.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-2020_reform_maps-3] The January 2020 estimate of the raion population was 61,836 (2020 est.).[^cite_note-ua2020estimate-4]

## Geographic characteristics

The area of the district is 6503.7 km<sup>2</sup>.[^cite_note-\:0-5] Sumy Raion is located on the slopes of the [Central Russian Upland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Russian_Upland).[^cite_note-\:1-6]

The climate is moderately continental. Winter is cool, summer is not hot. The average temperature in July is +19 °C, in January -7.5 °C. The maximum precipitation falls in the summer in the form of rain. The average annual amount is from 650 to 700 mm, changing from west to east.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-\:2-8]

[Psel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psel_\(river\)), left tributary of the [Dnipro](989795.md) flow through the Sumy Raion. The river in the floodplain has many [oxbow lakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxbow_lake) and [artificial lakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_lakes).[^cite_note-\:1-6][^cite_note-9]

Sumy Raion is located in the forest-steppe natural zone. Among the trees in the forests, oaks, lindens, and maples dominate. Typical large mammals are elk, roe deer, wild boar, squirrels, beavers, hares and wolves.[^cite_note-\:2-8] The most common soils in the area are typical black earths, gray, meadow, and meadow-bog soils.[^cite_note-\:2-8]

Sumy Raion has reserves of [clay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clay), [sapropel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapropel), [phosphorite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phosphorite).[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

### Environmental protection activities

In the Sumy Raion, the [Michael's Virgin Land Nature Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael's_Virgin_Land_Nature_Reserve), the [Kyianytskyi Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyianytskyi_Park?action=edit\&redlink=1) is a monument of landscape architecture of national importance, the Banny Yar and Srednoseymsky nature reserves of national importance are located.[^cite_note-12] Animals and plants listed in the [Red Book of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Data_Book_of_Ukraine) that are found in the territory of the Sumy Raion: *[Adonis vernalis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adonis_vernalis), [Pulsatilla patens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulsatilla_patens), [Stipa capillata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stipa_capillata), [Greater blind mole-rat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_blind_mole-rat), [Great jerboa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_jerboa).*[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15] Among the rare bird species, there are the [gray magpie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_grey_shrike), [Montagu's harrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montagu's_harrier), [Common kestrel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_kestrel), and [woodcock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodcock).[^cite_note-\:2-8]

## Hromadas of the raion

There are 450 [hromadas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hromada) in Sumy Oblast. The number of cities is 4. Sumy Raion includes 16 hromadas, namely: [Sumy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sumy_urban_hromada), [Bilopillia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilopillia_urban_hromada), [Vorozhba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vorozhba_urban_hromada?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Lebedyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebedyn_urban_hromada?action=edit\&redlink=1) urban hromadas; [Krasnopillia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krasnopillia_settlement_hromada), [Mykolaivka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mykolaivka_settlement_hromada,_Sumy_Oblast?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Stepanivka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stepanivka_settlement_hromada?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Khotin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khotin_settlement_hromada) settlement hromadas; and [Bezdryk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bezdryk_rural_hromada?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Verkhnia Syrovatka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verkhnia_Syrovatka_rural_hromada?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Mykolaivka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mykolaivka_rural_hromada,_Sumy_Oblast?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Myropillia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myropillia_rural_hromada), [Nyzhnia Syrovatka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyzhnia_Syrovatka_rural_hromada?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Richky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richky_rural_hromada?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Sad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sad_rural_hromada?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Yunakivka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yunakivka_rural_hromada) rural hromadas.[^cite_note-\:0-5]
