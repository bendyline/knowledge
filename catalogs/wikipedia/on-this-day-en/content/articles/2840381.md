# Pureland Industrial Complex

&#x20;**Pureland Industrial Complex** is a 3,000-acre (12 km<sup>2</sup>) [industrial park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_park) located in [Logan Township](125205.md) in [Gloucester County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloucester_County,_New_Jersey), [New Jersey](21648.md), United States. It borders the [Delaware River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delaware_River) and [Raccoon Creek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raccoon_Creek_\(New_Jersey\)), and is located 12 miles (19 km) south of the [ports of Camden and Philadelphia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delaware_River_Port_Authority). [Interstate 295](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_295_\(Delaware-New_Jersey-Pennsylvania\)), [U.S. Route 322](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_322_in_New_Jersey) and [U.S. Route 130](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_130) run through it and connect it to major [truck routes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truck_routes). [SMS Rail Lines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SMS_Rail_Lines) connect to [Penns Grove Secondary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penns_Grove_Secondary) and the national rail network. It was opened in 1975 and is the largest industrial park in the state of New Jersey, and one of the largest in the world.&#x20;

## Incidents

On March 4, 2026, an explosion occurred at the complex, leaving 4 injured and causing major disruptions in the region.
