The **NORCECA Women's Volleyball Championship** is the official competition for senior women's national [volleyball](32558.md) teams of North America, Central America and the Caribbean, organized by the North, Central America and Caribbean Volleyball Confederation ([NORCECA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North,_Central_America_and_Caribbean_Volleyball_Confederation)). Since its introduction in [1969](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1969_Women's_NORCECA_Volleyball_Championship?action=edit\&redlink=1) the tournaments have been awarded every two years. The competition has been dominated by [Cuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuba_women's_national_volleyball_team) and [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_women's_national_volleyball_team), which won together 21 of the 27 editions of the tournament.
