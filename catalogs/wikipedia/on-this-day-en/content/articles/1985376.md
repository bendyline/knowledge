**Methyl methacrylate** (**MMA**) is an [organic compound](22203.md) with the [formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_formula) CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)COOCH<sub>3</sub>. This colorless liquid, the methyl [ester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ester) of [methacrylic acid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methacrylic_acid) (MAA), is a [monomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monomer) produced on a large scale for the production of [poly(methyl methacrylate)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poly\(methyl_methacrylate\)) (PMMA).[^cite_note-Ull-3]

## History

MMA was discovered by [Bernhard Tollens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernhard_Tollens) and his student W. A. Caspary in 1873,[^cite_note-4] who noticed and described its tendency to change into a clear, hard, transparent substance especially in sunlight.[^cite_note-5] Studies on acrylic esters slowly developed until [Staudinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermann_Staudinger)'s theory of [macromolecules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macromolecules) and his research into the nature of polyacrylates allowed control over polymerization. The company [Rohm and Haas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rohm_and_Haas), founded by German chemist [Otto Röhm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_R%C3%B6hm), who investigated the topic for three decades, was finally able to start its industrial production in 1931.[^cite_note-6]

## Production and properties

Given the scale of production, many methods have been developed starting from diverse two- to four-carbon precursors.[^cite_note-7] Two principal routes appear to be commonly practiced.

### Cyanohydrin route

The principal route begins with [the condensation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyanohydrin_reaction) of [acetone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acetone) and [hydrogen cyanide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrogen_cyanide):[^cite_note-Ull-3]

(CH<sub>3</sub>)<sub>2</sub>CO + HCN → (CH<sub>3</sub>)<sub>2</sub>C(OH)CN

[Sulfuric acid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sulfuric_acid) then hydrolyzes [acetone cyanohydrin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acetone_cyanohydrin) (ACH) to a [sulfate ester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sulfate_ester)-adduct, which is [cracked](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cracking_\(chemistry\)) to the ester:

(CH<sub>3</sub>)<sub>2</sub>C(OH)CN + 2H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> → ((CH<sub>3</sub>)<sub>2</sub>C(OSO<sub>3</sub>H)C(O)NH<sub>2</sub>·H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub>\


&#x20;→ (CH<sub>3</sub>)<sub>2</sub>C(OSO<sub>3</sub>H)C(O)NH<sub>2</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub>

[Methanolysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methanolysis) gives [ammonium bisulfate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ammonium_bisulfate) and MMA:

(CH<sub>3</sub>)<sub>2</sub>C(OSO<sub>3</sub>H)C(O)NH<sub>2</sub> + CH<sub>3</sub>OH → CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)C(O)OCH<sub>3</sub> + NH<sub>4</sub>HSO<sub>4</sub>

Laboratory scale procedures are available for some of these steps.[^cite_note-8]

This technology affords more than 3 billion kilograms per year, and the economics have been optimized.[^cite_note-Koichi-9][^cite_note-10] Nevertheless, the ACH route coproduces substantial amounts of [ammonium bisulfate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ammonium_bisulfate): roughly 1.1 kg/(kg MMA). The ammonium bisulfate can be converted to [ammonium sulfate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ammonium_sulfate), which is a common [fruit tree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fruit_tree) [fertilizer](37401.md).

### Methyl propionate routes

The first stage involves [carboalkoxylation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carboalkoxylation) of ethylene to produce [methyl propionate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methyl_propionate) (MeP):[^cite_note-Barn-11]

C<sub>2</sub>H<sub>4</sub> + CO + CH<sub>3</sub>OH → CH<sub>3</sub>CH<sub>2</sub>CO<sub>2</sub>CH<sub>3</sub>

The MeP synthesis is conducted in a continuous-stirred tank reactor at moderate temperature and pressure using proprietary agitation and gas-liquid mixing arrangement.

In a second set of reactions, MeP is condensed with [formaldehyde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formaldehyde) in a single heterogeneous reaction step to form MMA:[^cite_note-12]

CH<sub>3</sub>CH<sub>2</sub>CO<sub>2</sub>CH<sub>3</sub> + CH<sub>2</sub>O → CH<sub>3</sub>(CH<sub>2</sub>)CCO<sub>2</sub>CH<sub>3</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>O

The reaction of MeP and formaldehyde takes place over a fixed bed of catalyst. This catalyst, [caesium oxide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caesium_oxide) on silica, achieves good selectivity to MMA from MeP. The formation of a small amount of heavy, relatively involatile compounds poisons the catalyst. The coke is easily removed and catalyst activity and selectivity restored by controlled, in-situ regeneration. The reactor product stream is separated in a primary distillation so that a crude MMA product stream, free from water, MeP and formaldehyde, is produced. Unreacted MeP and water are recycled via the formaldehyde dehydration process. MMA (>99.9%) is purified by vacuum distillations. The separated streams are returned to the process; there being only a small heavy ester purge stream, which is disposed of in a thermal oxidizer with heat recovered for use in the process.

### Other routes to MMA

#### Via propionaldehyde

Ethylene is first [hydroformylated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydroformylation) to give propanal, which is then condensed with formaldehyde to produce [methacrolein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methacrolein). The condensation is catalyzed by a secondary amine. Air oxidation of methacrolein to methacrylic acid completes the synthesis of the acid:[^cite_note-Koichi-9]

CH<sub>3</sub>CH<sub>2</sub>CHO + HCHO → CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)CHO + H<sub>2</sub>O

CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)CHO + ½ O<sub>2</sub> → CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)CO<sub>2</sub>H

#### From isobutyric acid

As developed by Atochem and Röhm, [isobutyric acid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isobutyric_acid) is produced by [hydrocarboxylation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrocarboxylation) of propene, using HF as a catalyst:

CH<sub>2</sub>=CHCH<sub>3</sub> + CO + H<sub>2</sub>O → (CH<sub>3</sub>)<sub>2</sub>CHCO<sub>2</sub>H

Oxidative dehydrogenation of the isobutyric acid yields methacrylic acid. Metal oxides catalyse this process:[^cite_note-Koichi-9]

(CH<sub>3</sub>)<sub>2</sub>CHCO<sub>2</sub>H + O → CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)CO<sub>2</sub>H + H<sub>2</sub>O

#### Methyl acetylene (propyne) process

Using [Reppe chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reppe_chemistry), methyl acetylene is converted to MMA. As developed by Shell, this process produces MMA in one step reaction with 99% yield with a catalyst system derived from [palladium acetate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palladium_acetate), an [organophosphine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organophosphine), and Bronsted acids:[^cite_note-Koichi-9]

CH≡CCH<sub>3</sub> + CO + CH<sub>3</sub>OH → CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)CO<sub>2</sub>CH<sub>3</sub>

#### Isobutylene routes

The reactions by the direct oxidation method consist of two-step oxidation of isobutylene or TBA with air to produce methacrylic acid and esterification by methanol to produce MMA.[^cite_note-Koichi-9]

CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)<sub>2</sub>

or (CH<sub>3</sub>)<sub>3</sub>C−OH + O<sub>2</sub> → CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)−CHO + H<sub>2</sub>O

CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)CHO + ½ O<sub>2</sub> → CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)CO<sub>2</sub>H

CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)CO<sub>2</sub>H + CH<sub>3</sub>OH → CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)CO<sub>2</sub>CH<sub>3</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>O

A process using isobutylene as a raw material has been commercialized by Escambia Co. Isobutylene is oxidized to provide α-hydroxy isobutyric acid. The conversion uses N<sub>2</sub>O<sub>4</sub> and nitric acid at 5–10 °C in the liquid phase. After esterification and dehydration MMA is obtained. Challenges with this route, aside from yield, involve the handling of large amounts of nitric acid and NO<sub>*x*</sub>. This method was discontinued in 1965 after an explosion at an operation plant.[^cite_note-Koichi-9]

#### Methacrylonitrile (MAN) process

MAN can be produced by [ammoxidation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ammoxidation) from isobutylene:

(CH<sub>3</sub>)<sub>2</sub>C=CH<sub>2</sub> + NH<sub>3</sub> + <sup>3</sup>/<sub>2</sub> O<sub>2</sub> → CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)CN + 3 H<sub>2</sub>O

This step is analogous to the industrial route to [acrylonitrile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acrylonitrile), a related commodity chemical. MAN can be hydrated by sulfuric acid to methacrylamide:

CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)CN + H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>O → CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)−CONH<sub>2</sub>·H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub>

CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)−CONH<sub>2</sub>·H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub> + CH<sub>3</sub>OH → CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)COOCH<sub>3</sub> + NH<sub>4</sub>HSO<sub>4</sub>

Mitsubishi Gas Chemicals proposed that MAN can be hydrated to methacrylamide without using sulfuric acid and is then esterified to obtain MMA by methylformate.[^cite_note-Koichi-9]

CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)CN + H<sub>2</sub>O → CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)−CONH<sub>2</sub>

CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)−CONH<sub>2</sub> + HCOOCH<sub>3</sub> → CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)COOCH<sub>3</sub> + HCONH<sub>2</sub>

HCONH<sub>2</sub> → NH<sub>3</sub> + CO

#### Esterification of methacrolein

Asahi Chemical developed a process based on direct oxidative esterification of methacrolein, which does not produce by-products such as ammonium bisulfate. The raw material is [*tert*-butanol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tert-butanol), as in the direct oxidation method. In the first step, methacrolein is produced in the same way as in the direct oxidation process by gas phase catalytic oxidation, is simultaneously oxidized and is esterified in liquid methanol to get MMA directly.[^cite_note-Koichi-9]

CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)−CHO + CH<sub>3</sub>OH + ½ O<sub>2</sub> → CH<sub>2</sub>=C(CH<sub>3</sub>)−COOCH<sub>3</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>O

## Uses

Main article: [Acrylate polymers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acrylate_polymers)

The principal application, consuming approximately 75% of the MMA, is the manufacture of [polymethyl methacrylate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymethyl_methacrylate) acrylic [plastics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plastic) ([PMMA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poly\(methyl_methacrylate\))) via an [exothermic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exothermic) [polymerization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymerization) reaction.[^cite_note-CBS-13] Methyl methacrylate is also used for the production of the co-polymer methyl methacrylate-butadiene-styrene (MBS), used as a modifier for [PVC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PVC). Another application is as cement used in [total hip replacements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Total_hip_replacement) as well as [total knee replacements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Total_Knee_Replacement). Used as the "grout" by orthopedic surgeons to make the bone inserts fix into bone, it greatly reduces post-operative pain from the insertions but has a finite lifespan. Typically, the lifespan of methylmethacrylate as bone cement is 20 years before revision surgery is required. Cemented implants are usually limited to elderly populations that require more immediate short term replacements. In younger populations, cementless implants are used because their lifespan is considerably longer.[^cite_note-14] Methyl methacrylate is also used in fracture repair in small exotic animal species using [internal fixation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internal_fixation).

MMA is a raw material for the manufacture of other methacrylates. These derivatives include ethyl methacrylate (EMA), butyl methacrylate (BMA) and 2-ethyl hexyl methacrylate (2-EHMA). [Methacrylic acid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methacrylic_acid) (MAA) is used as a chemical intermediate as well as in the manufacture of coating polymers, construction chemicals and textile applications.[^cite_note-mpa-15]

Wood can be impregnated with MMA and polymerized [in situ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_situ) to produce *[stabilized wood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stabilized_wood)*. It is also used for street and highway pavement markings.[^cite_note-16]

MMA is the raw material in many adhesives. They have advantages over other adhesives in that they show a good degree of surface tolerance, rapid drying and flexibility.[^cite_note-17]

## Environmental issues and health hazards

Methyl methacrylate (MMA) is a volatile, flammable liquid that can pose health risks through inhalation, skin contact, or eye exposure. Acute exposure may cause irritation of the eyes, skin, and respiratory tract, with symptoms including redness, coughing, headache, dizziness, and nausea at elevated concentrations.[^cite_note-niosh-18][^cite_note-icsc-19]

In experimental animals, the oral [LD<sub>50</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LD50) is approximately 7–10 g/kg (rat), indicating relatively low acute toxicity. Repeated occupational exposure may cause allergic skin sensitization, and methyl methacrylate is recognized as a potent contact allergen, particularly among workers in the dental, orthopedic, and plastics industries.[^cite_note-who-20]

Current evidence indicates that methyl methacrylate is not classifiable as to its carcinogenicity in humans (Group 3) by the [International Agency for Research on Cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Agency_for_Research_on_Cancer) (IARC), and long-term animal studies have not demonstrated carcinogenic effects.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22]

Because of its high volatility and flammability, large releases of methyl methacrylate can present significant industrial hazards. In May 2026, an overheating storage tank containing methyl methacrylate at an aerospace manufacturing facility in [Garden Grove, California](107814.md) prompted the evacuation of tens of thousands of residents while emergency crews worked to prevent a possible tank rupture or explosion. The evacuation order was lifted after the tank was stabilized and monitoring indicated no continuing threat to public health.[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24]
