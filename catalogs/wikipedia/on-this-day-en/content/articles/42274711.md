**Lance Jacob Strulovitch** (born 29 October 1998), commonly known as **Lance Stroll** ([/ˌstroʊl/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English)), is a Canadian [racing driver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_driver) who competes in [Formula One](10854.md) for [Aston Martin.](63473867.md)

Born and raised in [Montreal](7954681.md), Stroll is the son of billionaire businessman [Lawrence Stroll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Stroll), owner of the Aston Martin F1 Team, and his Belgian ex-wife. Stroll began competitive [kart racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kart_racing) aged 10, winning several regional and national titles. A member of the [Ferrari Driver Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrari_Driver_Academy) since 2010, Stroll graduated to [junior formulae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junior_formulae) in 2014, winning his first title at the [Italian F4 Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Italian_F4_Championship) that year with [Prema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prema_Racing). He then won the [Toyota Racing Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota_Racing_Series) in [2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Toyota_Racing_Series) with [M2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M2_Competition), and dominated the [FIA Formula 3 European Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIA_Formula_3_European_Championship) in [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_FIA_Formula_3_European_Championship) with Prema.

Following his record-breaking successes in [Formula Three](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_Three),[^cite_note-1] Stroll signed for [Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_Racing) in [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Formula_One_World_Championship) to partner [Felipe Massa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felipe_Massa), making his Formula One debut at the [Australian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Australian_Grand_Prix), aged 18. He scored his maiden podium finish in [Azerbaijan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Azerbaijan_Grand_Prix) that year, becoming the [second-youngest driver to score a podium](13599687.md#Youngest_drivers_to_score_a_podium_finish). Stroll finished a season-best eighth at the [same Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Azerbaijan_Grand_Prix) in [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Formula_One_World_Championship), driving the [FW41](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_FW41). After two seasons at Williams, Stroll signed for [Racing Point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_Point)—which had recently been [acquired by his father](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pay_driver)—in [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Formula_One_World_Championship) to partner [Sergio Pérez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_P%C3%A9rez), finishing fourth at the rain-affected [German Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_German_Grand_Prix). In [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Formula_One_World_Championship), Stroll achieved his maiden [pole position](24385119.md) at the [Turkish Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Turkish_Grand_Prix), with podiums at the [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Italian_Grand_Prix) and [Sakhir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Sakhir_Grand_Prix) Grands Prix. Stroll remained at the re-branded [Aston Martin](63473867.md) in [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Formula_One_World_Championship), partnered by [Sebastian Vettel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Vettel) for two seasons. Stroll has been teammates with [Fernando Alonso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Alonso) since [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Formula_One_World_Championship), finishing a career-best tenth in the [World Drivers' Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Drivers'_Championship) that year.

As of the [2026 Bahrain Grand Prix](80201648.md), Stroll has achieved one pole position and three podiums in Formula One. Stroll is contracted to remain at Aston Martin until at least the end of the 2027 season[^cite_note-2]. Outside of Formula One, Stroll competed in two editions of the [24 Hours of Daytona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/24_Hours_of_Daytona) in [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_24_Hours_of_Daytona) and [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_24_Hours_of_Daytona) with [CGR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chip_Ganassi_Racing) and [Jackie Chan DC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackie_Chan_DC_Racing), respectively.

## Early and personal life

Lance Jacob Strulovitch was born on 29 October 1998 in [Montreal](7954681.md).[^cite_note-3] Stroll is the son of Canadian billionaire businessman [Lawrence Stroll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Stroll)—part-owner and executive chairman of [Aston Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aston_Martin), as well as owner of [its Formula One team](63473867.md)—and Belgian fashion designer Claire-Anne Stroll ([née](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birth_name#Maiden_and_married_names) Callens), and has an older sister named [Chloe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chloe_Stroll).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] His parents are divorced and his father has since remarried to Raquel Diniz.[^cite_note-7] He is of [Russian Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Jewish) descent from his father's side.[^cite_note-8] Stroll races under the Canadian flag and holds both Canadian and Belgian citizenship.[^cite_note-motorsport-total.com-belgian-9][^cite_note-10] He is multilingual, speaking [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language), [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language), [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flemish_dialects), and [Italian](14708.md).[^cite_note-Aston_Martin_F1-11]

## Junior racing career

Like many racing drivers, Stroll began his motorsport career in [karting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kart_racing) at the age of ten.[^cite_note-\:0-12] He recorded numerous race and championship wins in his native Canada and North America and in 2008, his first year of karting, he won the Federation de Sport Automobile du Quebec rookie of the year award and driver of the year in 2009.[^cite_note-\:0-12][^cite_note-13] He was mentored by six-time World Champion [Mike Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Wilson_\(racing_driver\)?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-14] In 2010, Stroll became a member of the [Ferrari Driver Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrari_Driver_Academy).[^cite_note-15]

### Formula 4

Stroll's car racing debut came in the [2014 Florida Winter Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Florida_Winter_Series), a non-championship series organized by the Ferrari Driver Academy. He raced against future Formula One competitors [Nicholas Latifi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Latifi) and [Max Verstappen](41758713.md), and took two podium finishes as well as [pole position](630713.md) at [Homestead–Miami Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homestead%E2%80%93Miami_Speedway).

Stroll made his competitive car racing debut in the [2014 Italian F4 Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_Italian_F4_Championship), driving for [Prema Powerteam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prema_Powerteam). Despite missing the final round due to injury, Stroll emerged as series champion, taking seven race wins, thirteen podium finishes, and five pole positions.

### Formula Three



Stroll competing in Formula Three, 2015

#### 2015

At the beginning of 2015, Stroll won the [New Zealand](4913064.md)-based [Toyota Racing series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Toyota_Racing_Series), recording ten podiums – including four wins – from sixteen race starts. In the same year, he also contested the [FIA Formula 3 European Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_FIA_Formula_3_European_Championship) with Prema Powerteam, in which his father by this time had taken a stake.[^cite_note-\:1-16] He competed against future Formula One competitors [Antonio Giovinazzi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Giovinazzi), [Charles Leclerc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Leclerc), [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) and [Alexander Albon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Albon), winning one race at the [Hockenheimring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hockenheimring) and achieving six total podium finishes in the 33-race series. He ended the season fifth in the championship. 2015 also marked Stroll's first and only appearance at the non-championship Formula Three [Macau Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Macau_Grand_Prix), in which he finished in eighth place.

On 11 November 2015, it was announced that Stroll would leave the Ferrari Driver Academy to serve as a test driver for [Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_Grand_Prix_Engineering).

#### 2016

Stroll began 2016 by finishing 5th at the [2016 24 Hours of Daytona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_24_Hours_of_Daytona), driving for [Ford Chip Ganassi Racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Chip_Ganassi_Racing) in a Ford EcoBoost Prototype. He remained with Prema Powerteam for a third consecutive year to compete in his second season of the Formula 3 European Championship. Prior to the season, his father Lawrence hired Ferrari chief track engineer [Luca Baldisserri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luca_Baldisserri) to be Stroll's race engineer.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] He won the first race at [Circuit Paul Ricard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_Paul_Ricard) before taking a record thirteen more race victories over the season, including five consecutive victories in the final five races. He claimed the title with four races to go and ended the season a record 187 points clear of nearest competitor [Maximilian Günther](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximilian_G%C3%BCnther).[^cite_note-19]

Throughout the season, Stroll and his team were accused of manufacturing his title via team orders to his teammates. This was highlighted by [George Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Russell_\(racing_driver\)) at Paul Ricard, who claimed that "it's not a fair fight" in the wake of Stroll inheriting the lead from a slowing [Nick Cassidy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Cassidy).[^cite_note-20]

## Formula One career

### Williams (2017–2018)

#### 2017



Stroll driving the [Williams FW40](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_FW40) at the [2017 Malaysian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Malaysian_Grand_Prix)

Stroll drove for the [Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_Grand_Prix_Engineering) team for the [2017 Formula One season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Formula_One_season), partnering [Felipe Massa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felipe_Massa). He became the first [Canadian Formula One driver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Formula_One_driver) since the [1997](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997_Formula_One_World_Championship) World Drivers' Champion [Jacques Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Villeneuve).[^cite_note-Williams-21] Reportedly, Stroll's father [Lawrence Stroll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Stroll) paid \$80 million to Williams prior to his Formula One debut.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-\:1-16]

Stroll crashed in practice for his debut race, the [Australian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Australian_Grand_Prix), and received a grid penalty as this necessitated an unscheduled gearbox change.[^cite_note-23] He retired from the race with a brake failure. Two more retirements followed after collisions with [Sergio Pérez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_P%C3%A9rez) at the [Chinese Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Chinese_Grand_Prix) and [Carlos Sainz Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Sainz_Jr.) at the [Bahrain Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Bahrain_Grand_Prix). Stroll's first race finish came at the [Russian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Russian_Grand_Prix), where he finished in eleventh despite spinning on the first lap.[^cite_note-24] He retired with a brake failure at the [Monaco Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Monaco_Grand_Prix), but was still classified fifteenth. Stroll scored his first Formula One points at his [home Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Canadian_Grand_Prix) in Montreal, finishing in ninth place.

At the [Azerbaijan Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Azerbaijan_Grand_Prix), Stroll had run in second place in the closing laps, but was passed by [Valtteri Bottas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valtteri_Bottas) just metres from the finish line. He finished third to become the youngest rookie and the second-youngest driver after [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) to finish on the podium in Formula One, at the age of 18 years and 239 days.[^cite_note-25] becoming the [second-youngest driver to score a podium finish](13599687.md#Youngest_drivers_to_score_a_podium_finish) and the youngest to do so during their rookie season.[^cite_note-26]

Stroll registered the fourth fastest time during a wet qualifying session for the [Italian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Italian_Grand_Prix). Due to Red Bull drivers [Daniel Ricciardo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Ricciardo) and [Max Verstappen](41758713.md) (respectively third and second) taking grid penalties, Stroll was promoted to second place on the starting grid, making him the youngest Formula One driver to start on the front row at the age of 18 years and 310 days.[^cite_note-27] Stroll finished seventh in the race. He recorded two eighth-place finishes at the [Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Singapore_Grand_Prix) (where he started eighteenth) and [Malaysian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Malaysian_Grand_Prix) Grands Prix. At the latter, Stroll and [Sebastian Vettel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Vettel) collided on the cool-down lap after the end of the race. Both drivers blamed the other for the incident, however no action was taken by the stewards.[^cite_note-28]

At the [Mexican Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Mexican_Grand_Prix), Stroll worked his way up to sixth place in the race from eleventh on the grid.[^cite_note-29] Stroll ended the season twelfth in the standings, one place below teammate Massa who had both outqualified and outraced Stroll 17–2 and 9–4 (in races both drivers finished) respectively.[^cite_note-30] Furthermore, Stroll gained more positions on the opening lap than any other driver that year.[^cite_note-31]

#### 2018



Stroll at the [2018 Austrian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Austrian_Grand_Prix)

For the [2018 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Formula_One_Season), Stroll remained with Williams, partnered by [Sergey Sirotkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergey_Sirotkin_\(racing_driver\)) after Massa retired from Formula One. The [Williams FW41](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_FW41) was the slowest car of the field and the team finished last in the constructors' championship that season. Stroll scored the team's first points of the year at the [Azerbaijan Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Azerbaijan_Grand_Prix) by finishing eighth. Stroll's home race, the [Canadian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Canadian_Grand_Prix), ended on the first lap after a heavy collision with [Brendon Hartley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendon_Hartley). He then retired from the following [French Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_French_Grand_Prix) with a tire puncture. His second and final retirement of the year came at the [German Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_German_Grand_Prix) with a brake failure. Stroll made his only appearance in the third qualifying session (Q3) at the [Italian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Italian_Grand_Prix), qualifying tenth. He went on to finish ninth in the race with teammate Sirotkin in tenth, marking Williams' only double points-finish that season.

Stroll finished eighteenth in the Drivers' Championship, scoring six of the team's seven points. Sirotkin out-qualified Stroll at twelve of the twenty-one races.[^cite_note-32]

### Racing Point (2019–2020)

#### 2019



Stroll at the [2019 Hungarian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Hungarian_Grand_Prix)

Stroll switched to driving for the newly renamed [Racing Point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_Point) team for the [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Formula_One_World_Championship) season after the team was bought by a consortium of investors led by his father Lawrence. He replaced [Esteban Ocon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esteban_Ocon) at the team and raced alongside Sergio Pérez.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34] Stroll scored his first points for his new team at the season-opening [Australian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Australian_Grand_Prix), finishing ninth. At the [Chinese Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Chinese_Grand_Prix), Stroll criticized team strategy after finishing outside of the point-scoring positions for the second consecutive race.[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36] At the [Spanish Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Spanish_Grand_Prix) he was involved in a collision with [Lando Norris](48978813.md) that ended both drivers' races. He finished ninth at the [Canadian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Canadian_Grand_Prix) having started seventeenth.

In qualifying at the [German Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_German_Grand_Prix), Stroll progressed past the first qualifying session (Q1), ending a streak of fourteen Grands Prix in which he had been eliminated in Q1. He switched to slick tires late in the race as the track was drying, elevating him to second place. He missed out on a podium after being overtaken by [Daniil Kvyat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniil_Kvyat) and Sebastian Vettel. Stroll's next points finish came at the [Belgian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Belgian_Grand_Prix), where he started sixteenth and finished tenth. He scored his final points of the season after finishing ninth at the [Japanese Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Japanese_Grand_Prix). Stroll retired from the final two races of the season, suffering a suspension failure at the [Brazilian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Brazilian_Grand_Prix) and brake issues at the [Abu Dhabi Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Abu_Dhabi_Grand_Prix).

Stroll ended his first season at Racing Point fifteenth in the Drivers' Championship with 21 points, below teammate Pérez's tally of 52 points. He was out-qualified by Pérez at eighteen of the season's twenty-one races.[^cite_note-37]

#### 2020



Stroll during [pre-season testing in 2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Formula_One_pre-season_testing)

Stroll and Pérez were retained by Racing Point for [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Formula_One_World_Championship).*[^cite_note-racing_point_2020-38]* As a result of Canada's national sporting authority resigning its mandate from the [FIA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIA), Stroll raced under an American licence but was still recognised as Canadian on race weekends.[^cite_note-39] During the [Austrian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Austrian_Grand_Prix), he qualified in ninth place but later retired from the race following engine problems. His first points finish of the season came at the [Styrian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Styrian_Grand_Prix). He finished seventh having overtaken [Daniel Ricciardo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Ricciardo) in a move Ricciardo described as "desperate" and deserving of a penalty.[^cite_note-40] Stroll qualified third and finished fourth at the [Hungarian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Hungarian_Grand_Prix) and followed this with four more points finishes including fourth place at the [Spanish Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Spanish_Grand_Prix). Stroll claimed his second Formula One podium at the [Italian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Italian_Grand_Prix).[^cite_note-italian_grand_prix-41] He was in second place when the race restarted after a [red flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racing_flags#Red_flag) period before being passed by multiple cars including [Pierre Gasly](40843850.md). Stroll later suggested that his poor restart cost him the chance to win the race after Gasly claimed victory.[^cite_note-42] After eight races, Stroll was in fourth place in the Drivers' Championship.

A string of retirements followed beginning with the [Tuscan Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Tuscan_Grand_Prix), where a mechanical failure caused Stroll to crash heavily, having been running in fourth place.[^cite_note-43] He was eliminated from the [Russian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Russian_Grand_Prix) on the first lap after contact with [Charles Leclerc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Leclerc) caused him to hit a wall. He then withdrew from the [Eifel Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Eifel_Grand_Prix) prior to qualifying due to illness, having sat out the third practice session.[^cite_note-44] His team confirmed he did not have [COVID-19](63030231.md), saying he had passed all necessary FIA tests in this regard.[^cite_note-45] He was replaced at the event by [Nico Hülkenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_H%C3%BClkenberg).[^cite_note-46] Shortly after the race, Stroll tested positive for COVID-19. He completed a 10-day isolation period and returned to racing at the [Portuguese Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Portuguese_Grand_Prix),[^cite_note-47] in which he collided with Lando Norris. This caused Stroll's eventual retirement from the race, his fourth consecutive non-finish.

Stroll took his first pole position at the [Turkish Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Turkish_Grand_Prix) after a wet qualifying session. In doing so, he became the first [Canadian F1 driver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formula_One_drivers_from_Canada) to take pole position since Jacques Villeneuve at the [1997 European Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997_European_Grand_Prix).[^cite_note-48] Stroll led the race for 32 of the 58 laps, but reported severe [tire graining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glossary_of_motorsport_terms#G) and eventually fell to ninth place by the end of the race. After the race, Racing Point found damage on Stroll's front wing which they named as the cause of his tire issues.[^cite_note-49] Shortly after the [Bahrain Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Bahrain_Grand_Prix) was restarted following [Romain Grosjean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romain_Grosjean)'s accident, Stroll's car was flipped over after making contact with [Daniil Kvyat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniil_Kvyat) at turn eight. Stroll was uninjured and was able to extract himself from the car.[^cite_note-50] At the [Sakhir Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Sakhir_Grand_Prix), he benefited from tire issues for the leading [Mercedes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes_in_F1) cars to finish third, earning his third podium in Formula One.[^cite_note-51]

At the end of the season, Stroll stood eleventh in the Drivers' Championship, scoring 75 of the team's 210 points.[^cite_note-52]

### Aston Martin (2021–present)

#### 2021



Stroll at the [2021 Austrian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Austrian_Grand_Prix)

Stroll continued to drive for the Racing Point team in [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Formula_One_World_Championship) as the team rebranded to [Aston Martin](63473867.md).[^cite_note-53] He was partnered by Sebastian Vettel in place of Pérez.[^cite_note-54] Stroll qualified and finished tenth in the season-opening [Bahrain Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Bahrain_Grand_Prix). A video of him driving over a [curb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curb) at the [Monaco Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Monaco_Grand_Prix) became a popular [internet meme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_meme), used as a bait-and-switch similar to [rickrolling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rickrolling), after the official broadcast interrupted a battle between Vettel, Pierre Gasly and [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton) to show a replay of Stroll driving over the curbs.[^cite_note-55] A high-speed tire failure at the [Azerbaijan Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Azerbaijan_Grand_Prix) ended Stroll's race. A similar incident then happened to Max Verstappen later in the race, prompting tire manufacturer [Pirelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pirelli) to conduct an investigation into the failures.[^cite_note-56] Stroll scored a point with tenth place at the [French Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_French_Grand_Prix), having started nineteenth after being unable to set a representative lap time in qualifying.

Stroll caused a collision on the first lap of the [Hungarian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Hungarian_Grand_Prix) that eliminated himself and Charles Leclerc from the race and caused Daniel Ricciardo significant damage. As a result, Stroll was issued a five-place grid penalty for the next race, the [Belgian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Belgian_Grand_Prix). He finished seventh at the [Italian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Italian_Grand_Prix), his best finish of the season thus far, but collided with both Vettel and Pierre Gasly at the [Russian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Russian_Grand_Prix), for which he received a ten-second penalty.[^cite_note-57] He crashed in qualifying at the [Mexico City Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Mexico_City_Grand_Prix) and finished fourteenth. His best result of the season came at the [Qatar Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Qatar_Grand_Prix) with a sixth-place finish. Stroll ended the season thirteenth in the Drivers' Championship, scoring 34 points to Vettel's 43.

#### 2022



Stroll at the [2022 British Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_British_Grand_Prix)

Aston Martin retained Stroll and Vettel for the [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Formula_One_World_Championship) season. Stroll was in eleventh place in the closing laps of the [Saudi Arabian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Saudi_Arabian_Grand_Prix), but lost places after a collision with [Alex Albon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Albon). He failed to set a qualifying time at the [Australian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Australian_Grand_Prix) due to a crash with [Nicholas Latifi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Latifi) for which Stroll was penalised.[^cite_note-58] He made three pit stops during the race and later received a penalty for weaving on the straight when defending against Valtteri Bottas, finishing twelfth.[^cite_note-59] Stroll took his first points of the year with tenth at the [Emilia Romagna Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Emilia_Romagna_Grand_Prix),[^cite_note-60] and followed it up with another tenth-place finish in [Miami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Miami_Grand_Prix),[^cite_note-61] despite a collision with [Kevin Magnussen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Magnussen).[^cite_note-62] He crashed in qualifying at the [Azerbaijan Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Azerbaijan_Grand_Prix) and retired from the race with mechanical issues.

More tenth-place finishes came at the [Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Canadian_Grand_Prix), [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_French_Grand_Prix) and [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Dutch_Grand_Prix) Grands Prix.[^cite_note-63] Stroll came close to points at the [British Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_British_Grand_Prix), where he started twentieth and finished eleventh, and at the [Hungarian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Hungarian_Grand_Prix), where he was running eighth before being spun around by Daniel Ricciardo.[^cite_note-64] Stroll's best result of the season came with sixth place at the [Singapore Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Singapore_Grand_Prix), and he achieved his best grid position since his [2020 Turkish Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Turkish_Grand_Prix) pole position at the [United States Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_Grand_Prix), starting fifth. He ran as high as third in the opening laps but was eliminated in a high-speed collision with [Fernando Alonso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Alonso). A penalty for dangerous defending against teammate Vettel in the [São Paulo Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_S%C3%A3o_Paulo_Grand_Prix) sprint dropped Stroll to fifteenth on the grid for the race, in which he recovered to score a point in tenth place. He scored more points at the [Abu Dhabi Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Abu_Dhabi_Grand_Prix), starting fourteenth and finishing eighth. Stroll ended the season fifteenth in the Drivers' Championship with 18 points to Vettel's 37.[^cite_note-65]

#### 2023



Stroll at the [2023 Austrian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Austrian_Grand_Prix)

Stroll remained with Aston Martin for [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Formula_One_World_Championship), partnered with Fernando Alonso who replaced the retiring Vettel.[^cite_note-66] Stroll missed all three days of pre-season testing at [Bahrain International Circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahrain_International_Circuit) after suffering a cycling accident while training.[^cite_note-67][^cite_note-68] He sustained multiple injuries in the accident including fractures in both wrists and a broken toe.[^cite_note-69] Minor surgery was performed on his right wrist with metal screws being inserted.[^cite_note-70] He was replaced by reserve driver [Felipe Drugovich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felipe_Drugovich) for the test, but returned for the season-opening [Bahrain Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Bahrain_Grand_Prix).[^cite_note-71][^cite_note-72] In the second free practice session, he reported lingering pain from his wrists that forced him to adopt a relaxed style of driving; he said that while he felt "rather stiff", he "felt alright" in the car.[^cite_note-73][^cite_note-74] Stroll qualified eighth for the race[^cite_note-Bahrain2023quali-75][^cite_note-Bahrain2023grid-76] and finished sixth despite a first-lap collision with teammate Alonso.[^cite_note-BAH23RESULT-77][^cite_note-78] He started fifth at the [Saudi Arabian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Saudi_Arabian_Grand_Prix) but retired from the race with engine issues.[^cite_note-79] He finished fourth at the [Australian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Australian_Grand_Prix), his best result of the year, despite first-lap contact with Charles Leclerc and later running into the gravel at the second restart.[^cite_note-Sky_Sports_-_wild_finish-80]

Stroll scored points in the sprint and main race at the [Azerbaijan Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Azerbaijan_Grand_Prix), but failed to score at the [Miami Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Miami_Grand_Prix) after being eliminated in Q1. Damage from an accident caused his retirement from the following [Monaco Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Monaco_Grand_Prix). He scored points at the next three races, including finishing ninth from sixteenth at the start in the [Canadian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Canadian_Grand_Prix), and fourth place at the [Austrian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Austrian_Grand_Prix) sprint. He finished eleventh at the [British Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_British_Grand_Prix) but was demoted by a penalty for colliding with Pierre Gasly. He scored points at the [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Hungarian_Grand_Prix) and [Belgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Belgian_Grand_Prix) Grands Prix, despite crashing in the sprint shootout at the latter. He finished outside the top ten at the [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Dutch_Grand_Prix) and [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Italian_Grand_Prix) Grands Prix; his practice running at [Monza](952083.md) was curtailed by a fuel system failure and he then qualified in last place. A heavy crash in qualifying at the [Singapore Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Singapore_Grand_Prix) caused his withdrawal from the weekend, with Aston Martin team principal [Mike Krack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Krack) stating that Stroll was "still feeling the after-effects" of the accident.[^cite_note-81]

After qualifying seventeenth at the [Qatar Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Qatar_Grand_Prix), 1.1 seconds behind teammate Alonso's time, Stroll appeared to push his personal trainer in frustration as he exited the vehicle.[^cite_note-82] He finished ninth in the race but was demoted outside the points positions with a track limits penalty.[^cite_note-83] He started from the pit lane at the [United States Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_United_States_Grand_Prix) but recovered to finish the race ninth; he was later promoted to seventh after the disqualifications of Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc and scored his first points in six races.[^cite_note-84] Stroll started from the pit lane again at the [Mexico City Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Mexico_City_Grand_Prix) and retired with damage from a collision with Valtteri Bottas. He achieved his best qualifying result since 2020 at the [São Paulo Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_S%C3%A3o_Paulo_Grand_Prix) with third place, ahead of Alonso,[^cite_note-85] and finished fifth. He gained nine places from his starting position at the [Las Vegas Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Las_Vegas_Grand_Prix) to again finish fifth. Stroll ended the season tenth in the Drivers' Championship, the highest ranking in his Formula One career. He scored 74 points to Alonso's 206 and was outqualified by Alonso at nineteen of the season's twenty-two races.[^cite_note-86] Team principal Krack defended Stroll's performance relative to Alonso, stating that despite the points difference, "there \[was] not a marked gap in performance" between the two and confirmed that Stroll would remain with the team alongside Alonso for [2024](72504121.md).[^cite_note-87]

#### 2024



Stroll at the [2024 Austrian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Austrian_Grand_Prix)

Stroll started the 2024 season with a tenth-place finish at the [Bahrain Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Bahrain_Grand_Prix). He then qualified tenth at the [Saudi Arabian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Saudi_Arabian_Grand_Prix), but crashed out on lap 7 after hitting the wall at turn 23. He bounced back in [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Australian_Grand_Prix), qualifying ninth and finishing sixth. Following the weekend, it was announced that Stroll's race engineer Ben Michell would be replaced by Andrew Vizard.[^cite_note-88]

For the first sprint weekend of the season in [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Chinese_Grand_Prix), Stroll qualified 15th for the sprint and finished the shorter race 14th. He then qualified 11th for the main race, but the main race was a disaster as Stroll finished 15th after being given a 10-second penalty for crashing into the back of [Daniel Ricciardo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Ricciardo) on lap 26 following a safety car restart. After the race Ricciardo was furious with Stroll saying "I watched his onboard and he's not even looking at me, he's look at the apex, I don't know why he's not looking at the car in front."[^cite_note-89]

Stroll then qualified 7th for the sprint in [Miami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Miami_Grand_Prix) but retired on lap 1 due to teammate Alonso sliding into him after a collision between his teammate and [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton). He qualified 11th for the main race but finished 17th after a penalty for leaving the track and gaining an advantage. Stroll then qualified 13th at the [Emilia Romagna Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Emilia_Romagna_Grand_Prix) but finished in 9th scoring his first points since Australia.[^cite_note-90]

Stroll then qualified 13th for the [Monaco Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Monaco_Grand_Prix) but finished 14th after throwing his chance for points away when he got a puncture after hitting the wall in the middle of the race.[^cite_note-91] Stroll bounced back with a seventh-place finish at his home race in [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Canadian_Grand_Prix),[^cite_note-92] and achieved further points in [Britain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_British_Grand_Prix)[^cite_note-93] and [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Hungarian_Grand_Prix). However, this would end up being Stroll's last points result of the season, one which he finished 13th overall.[^cite_note-94] Of note was an error-strewn weekend in [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_S%C3%A3o_Paulo_Grand_Prix), where Stroll crashed during the qualifying session, lost his car under braking on the formation lap, and beached his car in the gravel while trying to recover back to the track.[^cite_note-95][^cite_note-96]

#### 2025

Stroll driving the Aston Martin AMR25 at the 2025 Japanese Grand Prix

Stroll (pictured at the [Japanese Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Japanese_Grand_Prix)) retained his seat for [2025](72768531.md).

Stroll retained his seat in [2025](72768531.md), finishing sixth at the rain-affected [Australian Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Australian_Grand_Prix).[^cite_note-97] He then finished ninth in [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Chinese_Grand_Prix) after disqualifications for [Charles Leclerc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Leclerc), [Lewis Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Hamilton), and [Pierre Gasly](40843850.md).[^cite_note-98] He qualified and finished last at the [Japanese Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Japanese_Grand_Prix),[^cite_note-99] before finishing seventeenth in [Bahrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Bahrain_Grand_Prix) and sixteenth in [Saudi Arabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Saudi_Arabian_Grand_Prix).[^cite_note-100][^cite_note-101]

After qualifying 14th in [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Spanish_Grand_Prix), Stroll withdrew from the Grand Prix prior to the race due to a hand injury. In an official team statement, it was announced that he had been experiencing pain in his hand and wrist for the prior six weeks, "which his medical consultant believes is in relation to the procedure he underwent in 2023".[^cite_note-102][^cite_note-103]

Stroll pictured at the [2026 Chinese Grand Prix](80284337.md) during Free Practice

Stroll retained his seat with Aston Martin in 2026, continuing his teammate partnership with [Fernando Alonso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Alonso) for the 4th consecutive season running.[^cite_note-104]
