**Prince Group**, officially **Prince Holding Group** is a [company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Company) based in [Cambodia](334751.md). Described as "one of Cambodia's largest [conglomerates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conglomerate_\(company\))",[^cite_note-1] the company has interests in various sectors such as real estate (Prince Real Estate Group), financial services (Prince Bank) and airlines ([Cambodia Airways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambodia_Airways)).[^cite_note-cna-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] In 2025, it and its chairman [Chen Zhi](81436620.md) were sanctioned by the United States and United Kingdom for their alleged role in operating [scam centers](80461265.md) and online fraud.

## History

The group was founded by [Chen Zhi](81436620.md) in 2015.[^cite_note-bbcbio-5] The Prince Group invested heavily in [Sihanoukville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sihanoukville), which became a "Chinese casino boomtown", offering physical and online casinos targeted at the mainland Chinese market, where gambling is illegal.[^cite_note-rfa-pt1-6]

In 2017, Chen and Prince Group co-founded the Jinbei Casino[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] with [Sar Sokha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sar_Sokha), a Cambodian politician who later became Deputy Prime Minister.[^cite_note-\:0-9][^cite_note-\:3-10]

The businesses flourished until 2019, when online gambling was criminalized in Cambodia, followed shortly by the travel restrictions of the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_Cambodia) and the resulting collapse in inbound travel to Cambodia.[^cite_note-rfa-pt1-6]

In 2021, Prince Group was given the Corporate Social Responsibility Model Award by China Finance Summit (CFS).[^cite_note-11] In 2026, the United States and European Union sanctioned the Prince Group.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

## Criminal charges

Main article: [Prince Group transnational criminal organization case](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Group_transnational_criminal_organization_case)

In May 2020, [Beijing Municipal Public Security Bureau](44458059.md) began investigating the Prince Group's connections to online gambling and [money laundering](19390.md). In July 2022, a court in [Wangcang County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wangcang_County), [Sichuan](65185.md) alleged that the Prince Group had profited at least 2 billion [Chinese yuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_yuan) (US\$700 million) from illegal gambling since 2016. A Prince Group spokesman denied the allegations, saying the Jinbei Group was unrelated and other entities were fraudulently impersonating the Prince Group.[^cite_note-rfa-pt1-6]

Assets belonging to the group were frozen in October 2025 as part of an investigation by the [Department of Justice](52563.md) into [wire fraud](905072.md) and money laundering by Chen. These assets included 127,271 [bitcoin](28249265.md) (worth over \$14 billion, making this the largest [cryptocurrency](36662188.md) seizure to date) and 19 properties in London.[^cite_note-bbc-14][^cite_note-15] [Hong Kong](13404.md) subsequently froze [HK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_dollar)\$2.75 billion (US\$354 million) of assets tied to Prince Group,[^cite_note-16] with [Singapore](27318.md) seizing [S\$](476174.md)150 million (US\$115 million) of Prince Group assets including six properties, 11 cars and a yacht.[^cite_note-17]

In January 2026, Cambodia stated that Chen had been arrested, stripped of his Cambodian citizenship, and extradited to China with two others to face charges.[^cite_note-arrest-18] The central bank of Cambodia also ordered the [liquidation](565034.md) of Prince Bank.[^cite_note-arrest-18]

In April 2026, [Huione Group](79531940.md)'s former chairman Li Xiong was extradited from Cambodia to China to face money laundering charges. Chinese authorities described Huione as a subsidiary of Prince Group and Li as a key member of Chen Zhi's "criminal gang".[^cite_note-19]

In June 2026, the Singapore Police Force said it was investigating Hu Xiaowei and Qiu Wei Ren, both Chinese nationals, for alleged [money-laundering](19390.md) offences connected to ongoing investigations into Prince Holding Group, its founder Chen Zhi, and related associates and companies. Police said Hu's associated company, Future Oasis Pte Ltd, was also under investigation, and that assets held across Hu's bank and securities accounts in Singapore had previously been seized in January 2026. The police added that Hu and Qiu had left Singapore before police operations began in October 2025 and were not in Singapore at the time of the announcement.[^cite_note-20]
