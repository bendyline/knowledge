**Mpumalanga** ([/əmˌpuːməˈlɑːŋɡə/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English); [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language): *Mepumalanga*) is one of the nine [provinces of South Africa](53215.md). The name means "**East**", or literally "**The Place Where the Sun Rises**" in the [Nguni languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguni_languages). Mpumalanga lies in eastern South Africa, bordering [Eswatini](27451.md) and [Mozambique](19301.md) and accounts for 6.3% of South Africa's total land area. It shares borders with the South African provinces of [Limpopo](365944.md) to the north, [Gauteng](309661.md) to the west, the [Free State](447006.md) to the southwest, and [KwaZulu-Natal](193596.md) to the south. The capital is [Mbombela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mbombela).[^cite_note-5]

Mpumalanga was formed in 1994 at the end of [apartheid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apartheid), when the area that was the Eastern [Transvaal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transvaal_\(province\)) was merged with the former [bantustans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bantustan) [KaNgwane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KaNgwane), [KwaNdebele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KwaNdebele) and parts of [Lebowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebowa) and [Gazankulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gazankulu). Much of the history and current significance of the region are as an area of trade.[^cite_note-\:3-6]

## History

### Precolonial era

Lydenburg Head

One of the [Lydenburg Heads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lydenburg_heads) from around A.D. 500 found in Mpumalanga&#x20;

Archeological sites in the Mpumalanga region indicate settlement by humans and their [ancestors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_ancestors) dating back 1.7 million years.[^cite_note-\:3-6] Rock paintings, engravings and other archeological evidence throughout the province indicate that Mpumalanga has a long history of human habitation by groups of hunter gatherers. Excavations not far from the Mpumalanga border in the [Origstad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohrigstad) district, show evidence of [Middle Stone Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Stone_Age) habitation dating back to 40,000 years ago.[^cite_note-\:2-7] The Lion Cavern, in [Ngwenya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ngwenya) on the [Eswatini](27451.md) border, shows evidence of people mining iron ore in the Mpumalanga region and surrounding areas from at least 28,000 years ago.[^cite_note-8]

Evidence from mine shafts and trade goods shows that there was notable industry in tin, copper, gold, iron, ochre, and bronze. The [Lydenburg Heads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lydenburg_heads), from around 500 CE, are Africa's oldest Iron Age artworks south of the equator and were found in Mpumalanga.[^cite_note-9] Evidence from a site near [Mbombela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mbombela) shows evidence of agricultural societies from between the 6th to 17th century.[^cite_note-10]

[Mbayi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mbayi?action=edit\&redlink=1) stone-walled sites on the Mpumalanga highveld are indicative of a large precolonial agropastoral society between \~1500 and 1820 CE.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] These societies became centers of trade, with increasingly large and centralised populations This pattern increased as they connected to the Portuguese trading post in [Maputo Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maputo_Bay).[^cite_note-\:2-7]

In this era, groups that would become the [Swazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swazi_people), [Pedi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedi_people), [Ndebele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Ndebele_people), [Tsonga people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsonga_people), [Mapulana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MaPulana) and others established themselves in the area that is now Mpumalanga.

### Colonial era

In 1845, as part of the [Great Trek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Trek), the first Dutch-speaking colonial settlers arrived in what they called the eastern Transvaal.[^cite_note-\:3-6] The rest of the 19th century in the region was characterised by colonial encroachment and conflict over land and political control. In 1852, the Boers established the [South African Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_African_Republic).[^cite_note-13] The well established Pedi, Swazi and Zulu kingdoms put up significant resistance against the settlers. The [Pedi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedi_people) fought wars with the [Boers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boers) in 1876 (see [Sekhukhune Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_African_Wars_\(1879%E2%80%931915\))) and the [Zulu](14754726.md) fought with [British settlers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1820_Settlers) in 1879 (see [Anglo-Zulu War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Zulu_War)). These interconnected conflicts played a role in shifting the balance of power in southern Africa to colonial control.[^cite_note-\:3-6] After 1860, missionaries, especially German missionaries, like [Alexander Merensky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Merensky), set up mission stations in the region, spreading Christianity and European values.[^cite_note-\:3-6]

Just as the region had been a center for trade in the precolonial era, in the colonial era, the Eastern Transvaal region became an important thoroughfare for trade between [Johannesburg](58893.md) and Delagoa Bay (now [Maputo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maputo)). Gold mining around areas like [Pilgrims Rest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilgrim's_Rest,_South_Africa) and [Barberton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barberton,_Mpumalanga) and farming were the main forms of economic activity. On farms in the Transvaal, under a system known as [Inboekstelsel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inboekstelsel), Boers carried out raids to capture children and forced them into indentured labour.[^cite_note-14] During the [South African War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Boer_War), the region was the site of notable battles.

### Apartheid

When the [National Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Party_\(South_Africa\)) came to power in 1948, their policy of [apartheid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apartheid) intensified the segregation that had defined communities in the [Transvaal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transvaal_\(province\)). Under a policy of forced removals, Black South Africans were evicted from areas reserved for white people and moved into homelands, including [KaNgwane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KaNgwane), [KwaNdebele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KwaNdebele), [Lebowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebowa) and [Gazankulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gazankulu).[^cite_note-15] As the apartheid regime implemented oppressive [policies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apartheid_legislation) that defined almost every aspect of life, activists responded with resistance.

In 1959, the small town of [Bethal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethal) on what is now the Mpumalanga highveld was the center of the [anti-apartheid consumer potato boycott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_African_potato_boycott) in response to the working conditions Black South African labourers faced on farms in the area.[^cite_note-16] The region was influenced by dynamics in exile and elsewhere in the country, for instance [Black Consciousness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Consciousness_Movement), the [Sharpeville Massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharpeville_massacre) and the [1976 Soweto Uprisings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soweto_uprising). In the 1980s, under intense repression, youth and labour organizations moblized against the government and townships and Bantustans erupted in political unrest and violence.[^cite_note-\:3-6]

In 1986, an avoidable disaster  killed 177 mine workers in [Kinross Mine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinross,_Mpumalanga) in the region's highveld. The response to the disaster by the [National Union of Mineworkers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Union_of_Mineworkers_\(South_Africa\)) was a key point in the South African labour struggle. By the late 1980s, the intensity of resistance across South Africa (including areas like Mpumalanga, which hadn't previously been seen as key areas of resistance) combined with economic factors and international pressure was strong enough to end apartheid. On 27 April 1994, when South Africa held its [first democratic elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/End_of_Apartheid), Mpumalanga province was formed. It was originally called the "Eastern Transvaal" province, but the name was later changed to Mpumalanga on 24 August 1995.

### Post-apartheid

The liberation movement turned leading party, the [African National Congress](2503.md) (ANC) came into power in Mpumalanga after white minority rule ended. A program of [land redistribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land_reform_in_South_Africa), aimed at granting land back to the black communities who were forcibly moved during apartheid, had redistributed tens of thousands of hectares to these communities and their descendants. However, the process has been marked by corruption, controversy and has been seen as inadequate to address the extent of apartheid spatial planning.[^cite_note-17]

In 2001, 1,270 hectares of the Boomplaats farm near [Mashishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lydenburg) was the first farm in South Africa to be expropriated.[^cite_note-18]

The [Drakensberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drakensberg) escarpment divides Mpumalanga into a westerly half consisting mainly of high-altitude [grassland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grassland) called the [Highveld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highveld) and an eastern half situated in low-altitude subtropical [Lowveld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lowveld)/[Bushveld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bushveld), mostly [savanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savanna) habitat. The southern half of the [Kruger National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kruger_National_Park) is in the latter region. The Drakensberg exceeds heights of 2000 m in most places, with this central region of Mpumalanga being very mountainous. These regions have alpine grasslands and small pockets of [Afromontane forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knysna-Amatole_montane_forests). The Lowveld is relatively flat with interspersed rocky outcrops. The [Lebombo Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebombo_Mountains) form a low range in the far east, on the border with [Mozambique](19301.md).

Some of the oldest rocks on earth have been found in the [Barberton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barberton,_Mpumalanga) area; these ancient [greenstones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenstone_belt) and [metamorphosed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metamorphism) [granites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granite) form the Crocodile River Mountains in the southeast of the province. The Lowveld is underlaid by [African Cratonic Basement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalahari_craton) rocks of ages in excess of 2 billion years. The Highveld is mostly [Karoo Sequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karoo_Sequence) [sedimentary rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sedimentary_rock) of a younger, [Carboniferous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carboniferous) to [Permian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permian) age.

Mpumalanga is the only South African province to border two [provinces of Mozambique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Mozambique) ([Gaza Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_Province) to the northeast and [Maputo Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maputo_Province) to the east), as well as all four [regions of Eswatini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Eswatini) ([Lubombo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lubombo_District), [Hhohho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hhohho), [Manzini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manzini_District), and [Shiselweni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiselweni) districts).

### Climate

The Lowveld is [subtropical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subtropical), due to its latitude and proximity to the warm [Indian Ocean](14580.md). The Highveld is comparatively much cooler and drier, due to its elevation of 1700 m to 2300 m above sea level. The Drakensberg escarpment receives the most [precipitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precipitation_\(meteorology\)), with all other areas being moderately well-watered by mostly-summer [thunderstorms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thunderstorms). The Highveld often experiences considerable freezing, while the Lowveld is mostly frost-free. Winter rainfall is rare, except for some drizzle on the [escarpment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escarpment). The differences in climate are demonstrated by comparing the capital, [Mbombela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mbombela), which is in the Lowveld, with [Belfast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belfast,_Mpumalanga), which is an hour away on the Highveld:

- Mbombela averages: January maximum: 29 °C (min: 19 °C), July maximum: 23 °C (min: 6 °C), annual precipitation: 767 mm

- Belfast averages: January maximum: 23 °C (min: 12 °C), June maximum: 15 °C (min: 1 °C), annual precipitation: 878 mm

&#x20;With [climate change](5042951.md) in the province, rainfall is becoming more variable, temperatures and evaporation rates are rising and extreme weather events are becoming more frequent. It is predicted that these changes will shift biomes so that most of the province will be savanna.[^cite_note-20]&#x20;

## Ecology

Some of the [earliest signs of life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earliest_known_life_forms) on Earth have been found in Mpumalanga. In the [Makhonjwa mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makhonjwa_Mountains) range's greenstone belt near [Barberton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barberton,_Mpumalanga), a fossil layer shows microbial activity that is 3.22 billion years old. This discovery indicates that life emerged on Earth 300 million years earlier than previously thought.[^cite_note-sciencedaily_2016-11-19]

Mpumalanga has a high plant and animal diversity. Many species are [endemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endemism) to the province.

## Law and government

Main article: [Politics of Mpumalanga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics_of_Mpumalanga)

Mpumalanga is governed by the [Mpumalanga Provincial Legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mpumalanga_Provincial_Legislature), a [unicameral](204304.md) system of 30 legislators democratically elected though a [closed list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_list) system. Under the constitution of South Africa, the legislature is intended to create laws, promote public participation and provide oversight.[^cite_note-\:0-33] From 1994 to the present, the African National Congress has been the leading party in the Mpumalanga legislature. As of the 2019 elections, the [Economic Freedom Fighters](40005536.md), [Democratic Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Alliance_\(South_Africa\)) and [Freedom Front Plus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedom_Front_Plus) are in opposition in the legislature.[^cite_note-\:0-33] Elected every five years, the [premier of Mpumalanga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_of_Mpumalanga) is the head of government. The [Executive Council of Mpumalanga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_Council_of_Mpumalanga) is appointed by the premier from among the members of the provincial legislature. Since 2024 [Mandla Ndlovu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandla_Ndlovu) has held the office of premier.[^cite_note-34]

## Economy

### Farming

Recently planted fields in Mpumalanga

Farm in Mpumalanga highveld

68% of land area in the province is used by agriculture. The climatic contrasts between the drier [Highveld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highveld) region, with its cold winters, and the hot, humid [Lowveld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veld) allow for a variety of agricultural activities. Mpumalanga has been an important supplier of fruit and vegetables for local and international markets for decades.[^cite_note-Mather_426–436-35] A history of dispossession mean that farming land is largely white owned, although policies of land redistribution era aim to address this.

Crops include maize, wheat, [sorghum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorghum), [barley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barley), [sunflower seed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunflower_seed), [soybeans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soybean), [macadamias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macadamia), groundnuts, [sugar](27712.md) cane, vegetables, coffee, tea, cotton, tobacco, [citrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citrus), subtropical and deciduous fruit.[^cite_note-36] Natural grazing covers approximately 14% of Mpumalanga. The main products are beef, [mutton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutton), [wool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wool), poultry and dairy.

Farming in the province is driven by both a combination of [seasonal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seasonal_labour), permanent and temporary labourers. Conditions on farms vary widely but seasonal and temporary workers, many of whom are migrant workers from nearby [Mozambique](19301.md), face intense insecurity.[^cite_note-Mather_426–436-35]

[Forestry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forestry) is extensive around [Sabie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabie) and [Graskop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graskop). Located near the forests, [Ngodwana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ngodwana) is the site of one of South Africa's largest [paper mills](417020.md) ([Sappi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sappi)).[^cite_note-37]

### Mining

Extensive mining is done and the minerals found include gold, [platinum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platinum) group metals, [silica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silica), [chromite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromite), vanadiferous [magnetite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetite), argentiferous [zinc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zinc), [antimony](898.md), [cobalt](24580536.md), copper, iron, [manganese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manganese), [tin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tin), coal, [andalusite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andalusite), chrysotile [asbestos](21492663.md), [kieselguhr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kieselguhr), [limestone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limestone), [magnesite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnesite), [talc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talc) and [shale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shale).

Gold was first discovered in Mpumalanga province in 1883 by Auguste Roberts in the mountains surrounding what is now Barberton. Gold is still mined in the Barberton area today.[^cite_note-38]

Mpumalanga accounts for 83% of South Africa's coal production. 90% of South Africa's coal consumption is used for electricity generation and the synthetic fuel industry. Coal [power stations](212141.md) are in proximity to the coal deposits. A [coal liquefaction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coal_liquefaction) plant in [Secunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secunda,_Mpumalanga) ([Secunda CTL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secunda_CTL)) is one of the country's two petroleum-from-coal extraction plants, which is operated by the [synthetic fuel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synthetic_fuel) company [Sasol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sasol).[^cite_note-39]

The high density of [coal power stations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coal-fired_power_station) on the Mpumalanga [highveld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highveld) means that the region has the highest levels of [nitrogen dioxide pollution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nitrogen_dioxide) in the world.[^cite_note-40]

### Attractions

Mpumalanga is popular with tourists. [Kruger National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kruger_National_Park), established in 1898 for the protection of Lowveld wildlife, covering 20,000 square kilometres (7,700 sq mi), is a popular destination.[^cite_note-41] The other major tourist attractions include the [Sudwala Caves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudwala_Caves) and the [Blyde River Canyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blyde_River_Canyon).

Many activities including the big jump, mountain and quad biking, horse trails, river rafting and big game viewing are endemic to the region. This is "[Big Five](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_five_game)" territory. Towns in the [Lowveld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lowveld) are [Barberton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barberton,_Mpumalanga), [Mbombela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mbombela), [White River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_River,_Mpumalanga), [Sabie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabie), [Graskop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graskop), [Hazyview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazyview), [Malelane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malelane), [Pilgrim's Rest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilgrim's_Rest,_South_Africa), [Lydenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lydenburg) and [Nkomazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nkomazi?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-42]

In 2008, an [haute cuisine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haute_cuisine) route was formed, trickling from [Mbombela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mbombela) down to Hazyview. The Lowveld gourmet route covers the four top fine dining restaurants the area has to offer.[^cite_note-43]

Brightly coloured and boldly patterned Ndebele beadwork and clothing

[Ndebele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Ndebele_people) traditional dress&#x20;

Mpumalanga is home to a diverse range of cultures, including Swazi, Ndebele, Afrikaans, Tsonga, Zulu, Mapulana, Portuguese and Pedi communities.

The Ndebele Cultural Village at [Botshabelo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botshabelo,_Mpumalanga) is a renowned center of cultural heritage, with displays of [Ndebele house painting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ndebele_house_painting). Ndebele artist [Esther Mahalangu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esther_Mahlangu) gained international acclaim for her artwork rooted in Ndebele traditions and geometric patterns. Prominent South African artist [Gerard Sekoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerard_Sekoto) was born in [Botshabelo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botshabelo,_Mpumalanga).

Many celebrated South African musicians, such as [Ray Phiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Phiri), [Rebecca Malope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebecca_Malope), [Oncemore Six](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oncemore_Six?action=edit\&redlink=1), and [Pretty Yende](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pretty_Yende), are from Mpumalanga.

Since 2003, Mpumalanga's capital city, Mbombela, has hosted the [Afrikaans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afrikaans) national arts festival Innibos. The festival features music, comedy, arts, crafts and theatre.[^cite_note-44]

Mpumalanga's population is 4,039,939 people (according to the 2011 census).[^cite_note-\:1-45] The average population density is 52.81 people per km<sup>2</sup>.[^cite_note-\:1-45]

Some 30% of the people speak [siSwati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SiSwati), the language of neighbouring [Eswatini](27451.md), with 26% speaking [isiZulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IsiZulu), 10.3% [isiNdebele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Ndebele_language), 10.2% [Sepedi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Sotho_language) and 11.6% [Xitsonga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xitsonga). As of the 2011 census, approximately 90.65% of Mpumalanga's population are Black, 7.51% are White, and 0.91% are [Coloured](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coloureds) and 0.69% are Indian or Asian.[^cite_note-\:1-45]
