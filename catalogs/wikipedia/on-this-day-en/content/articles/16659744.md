The **N-25** or **Pakistan Expressway** ([Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): قومی شاہراہ ۲۵) is a 813 km [national highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highways_of_Pakistan) in [Pakistan](23235.md) is a single-lane highway which extends along from [Karachi](17123.md) in [Sindh](28972.md) province to Pakistan's [Chaman](2288065.md) border with Afghanistan via [Quetta](93764.md) in [Balochistan province](4687.md) of Pakistan.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] The highway is also known as the [Regional Cooperation for Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_Cooperation_for_Development) Highway (RCD Highway), or notoriously as the *Killer Highway*.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] In April 2025, [Government of Pakistan](1897833.md) announced that money saved from oil prices are being spent on making this a *motorway standard* highway.[^cite_note-5] In the same month, government reiterated its commitment to improve this highway by investing Rs.300 billion.

On 5 December 2025, the [National Highway Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highways_Authority_of_Pakistan) (NHA) began reconstruction of the N-25 highway in order to improve safety on the route and reduce travel times.[^cite_note-\:0-6]

## Safety and Security

The highway is infamously known as the *killer highway* due to high number of driving accidents.[^cite_note-7] According to Pakistan [Pakistan Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Bureau_of_Statistics), more than 4000 people have died due to road accidents on this highway from 2012-2022.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

In 2022, the highway was closed for traffic due to damage caused by flash floods.[^cite_note-10] A major bridge connecting [Karachi](17123.md) to Balochistan at [Hub Chowki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hub,_Balochistan) collapsed due to the heavy [monsoon](57630.md) rains. The government announced that the rebuilding of bridge could take at least 18 months.[^cite_note-11] In February 2025, the reconstruction of the bridge was completed and it was reopened for traffic.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

Armed militants belonging to the [Baloch Liberation Army](6010135.md) (BLA) and other separatist groups have routinely blocked the major highways in Balochistan, including the N-25 National Highway.[^cite_note-14] In March 2025, [Government of Balochistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Balochistan,_Pakistan) took an unprecedented decision to ban night travel on the highway on various locations from 6PM to 6AM citing security concerns.[^cite_note-15]

On 5 December 2025, the [National Highways Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highways_Authority_of_Pakistan) (NHA) began reconstruction of the N-25 highway in order to improve safety on the route and reduce travel times.[^cite_note-\:0-6]
