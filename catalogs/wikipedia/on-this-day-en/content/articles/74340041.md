# 2026 FIFA World Cup qualification – CAF Group C

The **2026 FIFA World Cup qualification – CAF Group C** was a [CAF](715265.md) [qualifying group](60000294.md) for the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md). The group contained [Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigeria_national_football_team), [South Africa](1018598.md), [Benin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benin_national_football_team), [Zimbabwe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zimbabwe_national_football_team), [Rwanda](1145307.md) and [Lesotho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lesotho_national_football_team).

The group winners, South Africa, directly qualified for the World Cup, and the runners-up, Nigeria, qualified for the [second round](60000294.md#Second_round) to compete for a place in the [inter-confederation play-offs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification_\(inter-confederation_play-offs\)).[^cite_note-ExCon-1][^cite_note-format-2]

## Discipline

A player was automatically suspended for the next match for the following infractions:[^cite_note-regulations-7]

- Receiving a red card (red card suspensions could be extended for serious infractions)

- Receiving two yellow cards in two different matches (yellow card suspensions were carried forward to further qualification rounds, but not the finals or any other future international matches)
