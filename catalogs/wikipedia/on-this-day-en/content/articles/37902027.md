**Kargan** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): كرگان)[^cite_note-4] is a city in [Band-e Zarak District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Band-e_Zarak_District) of [Minab County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minab_County), [Hormozgan](448064.md) province, [Iran](14653.md). As a village, it was the capital of [Kargan Rural District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kargan_Rural_District)[^cite_note-Band-e_Zarak_District_Formation-5] until its capital was transferred to the village of [Sarmast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarmast,_Hormozgan).[^cite_note-Kargan_City-6]

## Demographics

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, Kargan's population was 4,380 in 789 households, when it was a village in [Band-e Zarak Rural District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Band-e_Zarak_Rural_District) of the [Central District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_District_\(Minab_County\)).[^cite_note-2006_Hormozgan_Province-7] The following census in 2011 counted 4,848 people in 1,138 households,[^cite_note-2011_Hormozgan_Province-8] by which time the rural district had been separated from the district in the formation of Band-e Zarak District. Kargan was transferred to Kargan Rural District created in the new district.[^cite_note-Band-e_Zarak_District_Formation-5] The 2016 census measured the population as 5,282 people in 1,393 households. It was the most populous village in its rural district.[^cite_note-2016_Hormozgan_Province-2]

Kargan was elevated to the status of a city after the census.[^cite_note-Kargan_City-6]
