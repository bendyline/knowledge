# Electric discharge

In [electromagnetism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electromagnetism), an&#x20;**&#x20;electric discharge** is the release and transmission of [electricity](9550.md) in an applied [electric field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_field) through a medium such as a [gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gas) (i.e., an outgoing flow of [electric current](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_current) through a non-[metal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metal) medium).[^cite_note-1]

## Applications

The properties and effects of electric discharges are useful over a wide range of magnitudes. Tiny pulses of current are used to detect [ionizing radiation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ionizing_radiation) in a [Geiger–Müller tube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geiger%E2%80%93M%C3%BCller_tube). A low steady current can illustrate the gas spectrum in a [gas-filled tube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gas-filled_tube). A [neon lamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neon_lamp) is an example of a [gas-discharge lamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gas-discharge_lamp), useful both for illumination and as a [voltage regulator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voltage_regulator). A [flashtube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flashtube) generates a short pulse of intense light useful for [photography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photography) by sending a heavy current through a gas arc discharge. [Corona discharges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corona_discharge) are used in [photocopiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photocopier).

Electric discharges can convey substantial energy to the electrodes at the ends of the discharge. A [spark gap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spark_gap) is used in internal combustion engines to ignite the fuel/air mixture on every power stroke. Spark gaps are also used to switch heavy currents in a [Marx generator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marx_generator) and to protect electrical apparatus. In [electric discharge machining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_discharge_machining), multiple tiny electric arcs erode a conductive workpiece to a finished shape. [Arc welding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arc_welding) is used to assemble heavy steel structures, where the base metal is heated to melting by the arc's heat. An [electric arc furnace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_arc_furnace) sustains arc currents of tens of thousands of amperes and is used for steelmaking and the production of alloys and other products.
