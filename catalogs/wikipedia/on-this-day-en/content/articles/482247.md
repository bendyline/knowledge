**Voskresensk** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Воскресе́нск) is a [town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) and the [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) of [Voskresensky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voskresensky_District,_Moscow_Oblast) in [Moscow Oblast](372315.md), Russia, located upon the banks of the [Moskva River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moskva_River) 88 kilometers (55 mi) southeast of [Moscow](19004.md). Population: 95,495 ([2021 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Russian_census));[^cite_note-2021Census-7] 91,464 ([2010 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Russian_census));[^cite_note-2010Census-3] 77,871 ([2002 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Russian_census));[^cite_note-PopCensus-8] 80,393 ([1989 Soviet census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_Soviet_census)).[^cite_note-Census1989-9]

## History

It was founded in 1862.[^cite_note-gr-2] Town status was granted to it in 1938.[^cite_note-10]

## Administrative and municipal status

Within the [framework of administrative divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Administrative_divisions), Voskresensk serves as the [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) of [Voskresensky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voskresensky_District,_Moscow_Oblast).[^cite_note-MoscowO_admlist-1] As an administrative division, it is, together with four [rural localities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia), incorporated within Voskresensky District as the **[Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Town_of_district_significance) of Voskresensk**.[^cite_note-MoscowO_admlist-1] As a [municipal division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Municipal_divisions), the Town of Voskresensk is incorporated within Voskresensky Municipal District as **Voskresensk Urban Settlement**.[^cite_note-MoscowOVoskresenskyD_mun-4]

## Transportation

There are station Voskresensk and stop platform 88 km at the Moscow-[Ryazan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryazan) line.

## Notable residents

See also: [Category\:People from Voskresensk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category\:People_from_Voskresensk)

The town is home to several prominent ice hockey players, including [Igor Larionov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igor_Larionov) and other Soviet national team players, such as [Vyacheslav Kozlov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vyacheslav_Kozlov), [Valeri Kamensky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valeri_Kamensky), [Andrei Lomakin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrei_Lomakin), [Aleksandr Smirnov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandr_Smirnov_\(ice_hockey\)), [Vladimir Golikov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Golikov), [Aleksandr Golikov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandr_Golikov), [Alexander Ragulin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Ragulin), [German Titov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Titov_\(ice_hockey\)), [Igor Ulanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igor_Ulanov), [Sergei Berezin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergei_Berezin), [Dmitri Kvartalnov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitri_Kvartalnov), [Roman Oksiuta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Oksiuta), [Andrei Markov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrei_Markov_\(ice_hockey\)), [Andrei Loktionov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrei_Loktionov), and [Valeri Zelepukin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valeri_Zelepukin). Current Russian NHL players from Voskresensk include [Vladislav Namestnikov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladislav_Namestnikov).
