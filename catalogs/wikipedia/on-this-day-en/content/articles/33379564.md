**Mambasa** is a town in the [Ituri Province](1545860.md) of the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md), the headquarters of [Mambasa Territory](33379399.md).[^cite_note-1]

## Geography

Mambasa is a locality located on national road number 4 (RN4) 174 km west of the provincial capital [Bunia](1925095.md).

## Administration

The territorial capital had 17,706 voters registered in 2018. The locality has the status of a rural commune of less than 80,000 voters, it has 7 municipal councilors in 2019.[^cite_note-2]
