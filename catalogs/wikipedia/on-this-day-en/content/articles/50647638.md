**Rustam Emomali**[^cite_note-1] (born **Rustam Emomalievich Rahmonov**;[^cite_note-2] 19 December 1987) is a Tajik politician who is the and the eldest son of [Emomali Rahmon](393634.md), the long-standing [president of Tajikistan](163136.md).[^cite_note-3] He has been the current Chairman of the [National Assembly of Tajikistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_Tajikistan) since 2020 and [Mayor of Dushanbe](55241266.md) since 2017.[^cite_note-speech-4]

Rustam Emomali is believed to be prepared by his father to succeed him as the leader of Tajikistan; he holds the title of the constitutionally designated successor to the presidency.[^cite_note-\:2-5] He heads the anti-corruption agency in Tajikistan and the state's financial oversight agency.[^cite_note-\:5-6] Despite not having served in the armed forces, he holds the rank of major general.[^cite_note-\:1-7][^cite_note-8] He was appointed as the mayor of Dushanbe by his father in 2017 at the age of 29.[^cite_note-\:5-6]

## Early life and football career

Rustam Emomali was born Rustam Emomalievich Rahmonov in the district of [Danghara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danghara), [Kulob oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kulob_Oblast) (present-day [Khatlon province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khatlon_Region)) in Tajikistan, to parents Emomali Rahmon and Azizmo Asadullayeva. He graduated in 2008 from the [Tajik State National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tajik_National_University) with a [specialist's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specialist_degree) in International Economic Relations, studied a specialty in State and Municipal Administration at the [Russian Presidential Academy of National Economy and Public Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Presidential_Academy_of_National_Economy_and_Public_Administration) in 2011, and took courses with the [Diplomatic Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplomatic_Academy_of_the_Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_of_the_Russian_Federation) of the Russian Ministry of Foreign Affairs.[^cite_note-\:3-9][^cite_note-10] In 2007, following his father's suit, he had dropped the Russian-style [patronymic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patronymic) and last name, adopting his father's first name, Emomali, as his new surname.

In 2007, Rustam Emomali co-founded the Dushanbe-based [football club Istiklol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Istiklol) and, during the next several years, he served as the club's [captain](633462.md) and played for it as a [striker](428681.md).[^cite_note-11] The club has won five national championships consecutively since 2011, owing at least partially to very favorable refereeing and other preferences.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] In 2011, Rustam Emomali was appointed deputy president of [Tajikistan's Football Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tajikistan_Football_Federation) (TFT) and joined the International Relations Committee of the [Olympic Council of Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_Council_of_Asia).[^cite_note-14]

In January 2012, a designated TFT pannel voted unanimously to appoint Emomali its new president for a four-year term.[^cite_note-15] Following the appointment, he stopped playing for FC Istiklol and promised to cut all ties to the club. Starting in 2012, he served as a member of the [FIFA Development Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Development_Committee) for two years.[^cite_note-16] In 2016, the TFT reelected Rustam Emomali as its president for another four years.[^cite_note-17]

Emomali is known for his two expensive hobbies: car racing and collecting sports cars.[^cite_note-18]

He is the first recipient of "Argali Conservation Achievement Award".[^cite_note-19]

## Political career

After graduating from the university, Rustam Emomali enjoyed rapid career growth thanks to his status as a son of the country's president. In 2006, he was appointed a leading specialist at Tajikistan's Organization for Cooperation with the [World Trade Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Trade_Organization).[^cite_note-\:0-20] In 2009, he got a job as a leading specialist in the State Committee on Investments and State Property (SCISP) and was soon promoted to the position of a head of a department in the committee. During his work in the SCISP, he also served as an adviser to the committee.[^cite_note-\:4-21] Also in 2009, Rustam Emomali was appointed a deputy head of the Youth Union, the Tajik successor to the Soviet-era [Komsomol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Komsomol) organization.[^cite_note-22] Starting in 2009, Rustam Emomali began attending major international summits and meetings with foreign dignitaries in Tajikistan.[^cite_note-23] In 2010, he became a member of the central executive committee of the [People's Democratic Party of Tajikistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Democratic_Party_of_Tajikistan) and was elected a member of the [Dushanbe](56658.md) municipal parliament.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25]

In February 2011, [Emomali Rahmon](393634.md) appointed Rustam Emomali head of the anti-smuggling department in the Customs Service, the first in a number of senior law-enforcement positions that the Tajik president's son has held.[^cite_note-\:4-21][^cite_note-26] Soon after the appointment, he was given the rank of [major](201920.md).[^cite_note-27] In November 2013, Rustam Emomali was appointed head of the Customs Service.[^cite_note-28] The appointment came with a new military rank, [major general](36301471.md).[^cite_note-\:1-7] In March 2015, president Emomali Rahmon appointed his son to head Tajikistan's principal anti-corruption agency, the State Agency For Financial Control and Measures Against Corruption.[^cite_note-29]

In January 2017, Rustam Emomali was appointed [Mayor of Dushanbe](55241266.md) by his father, a key position, which is seen by some analysts as the next step to the top of the government.[^cite_note-30]

### Political succession speculations



Emomali with [Nurlan Nigmatulin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nurlan_Nigmatulin), 24 September 2021

Rustam Emomali's rapid career growth and his appointments to a number of different senior government positions has fueled speculations that he was being prepared to succeed his father as the leader of Tajikistan.[^cite_note-\:2-5] Rustam Emomali attends all key international summits in the country and accompanies his father during his frequent tours around the country.

On 22 May 2016, a nationwide [referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Tajik_constitutional_referendum) approved a number of changes to the country's constitution.[^cite_note-31] One of the key amendments reduced the minimum eligibility age for presidential candidates from 35 to 30, effectively enabling Rustam Emomali to succeed his father in office after 2017.[^cite_note-32]

## Personal life

Rustam Emomali is the oldest son of Tajikistan's president, [Emomali Rahmon](393634.md). He has eight siblings, including a younger brother, Somon.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34] He got married in 2009 to a daughter of a well-connected entrepreneur who owns a number of food-processing enterprises.[^cite_note-35] The couple has three children, two sons and a daughter.[^cite_note-36]

Rustam's elder sister, [Ozoda Rahmon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ozoda_Rahmon), is the Presidential chief of staff[^cite_note-37] and a former senator in the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_Tajikistan).[^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39] One of his other sisters, Zarina Rahmon, was a deputy head of [Orienbank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orienbank), appointed in January 2017.[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41]
