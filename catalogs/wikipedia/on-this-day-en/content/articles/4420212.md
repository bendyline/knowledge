The **National Assembly of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam** (**N.A.**; [Vietnamese](32511.md): *Quốc hội nước Cộng hoà xã hội chủ nghĩa Việt Nam*)[^cite_note-1] is the [unicameral](204304.md) [supreme state organ of power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_state_organ_of_power) of [Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Republic_of_Vietnam). The National Assembly is the only branch of government in Vietnam and, in accordance with the principle of [unified power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unified_power), all state organs are subservient to it.

The National Assembly is a 500-delegate [unicameral](204304.md) body elected to a five-year term. It normally sits twice a year, or more frequently if called for by the [National Assembly Standing Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standing_Committee_of_the_National_Assembly). The assembly appoints the [president](466686.md) ([head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state)), the [prime minister](439887.md) ([head of government](37570.md)), the [chief justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_justice) of the [Supreme People's Court of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_People's_Court_of_Vietnam), the head of the [Supreme People's Procuracy of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_People's_Procuracy_of_Vietnam), and the 21-member cabinet.

Constitutionally, the National Assembly is the highest government organization and the highest-level representative body of the people. It has the power to draw up, adopt, and amend the [constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution) and to make and amend laws. It also has the responsibility to legislate and implement state plans and budgets. Through its constitution-making powers it defines its own role and the roles of the [Vietnamese State President](466686.md), the [Vietnamese Government](78640.md), the local people's councils and people's committees, the Supreme People's Court, and the Supreme People's Procuracy. The assembly can replace and remove government ministers, the chief justice of the Supreme People's Court, and the procurator general of the [Supreme People's Procuracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_People's_Procuracy_of_Vietnam). Finally, it has the *[de jure](43594.md)* power to initiate or conclude wars and to assume other duties and powers it deems necessary.&#x20;

In practice, like its counterparts in other [communist states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_state), the National Assembly has been characterized as a [rubber stamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rubber_stamp_\(politics\)) body of the [Communist Party of Vietnam](400546.md) (CPV) or only being able to affect issues of low sensitivity to the regime. The CPV controls nomination and election processes at every level. The CPV has great influence over the executive and exercises control through the 150-member [Central Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Committee_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Vietnam), which elects the 19-member [Politburo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politburo_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Vietnam) at national party congresses held every five years. All senior government positions are held by members of the party.[^cite_note-2] While vested with great lawmaking and oversight powers on paper, in practice the National Assembly does little more than ratify decisions already made by the CPV and its Politburo and only opposes the party on rare occasions, such as a rejection of a high speed rail between Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City in 2010.[^cite_note-3]

## History of the National Assembly of Vietnam

### Origins

The precursor of the current National Assembly of Vietnam was the National Representatives' Congress (*Đại hội đại biểu quốc dân*), convened on August 16, 1945, in the northern province of [Tuyên Quang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuy%C3%AAn_Quang_Province). This Congress supported [Viet Minh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viet_Minh)'s nationwide general uprising policy against [Japanese](15573.md) and [French](5843419.md) forces in Vietnam. It also appointed the National Liberation Committee (*Uỷ ban dân tộc giải phóng*) as a provisional government.

- **The First National Assembly (1946–1960)**

After a series of events which was later called collectively the "[August Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_Revolution)", [Viet Minh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viet_Minh) seized the power all over the country, and the [Democratic Republic of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Republic_of_Vietnam) (*Việt Nam dân chủ cộng hoà*) was declared by [Hồ Chí Minh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ho_Chi_Minh) in [Hanoi](56667.md) on September 2, 1945. On January 6, 1946, the first [general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1946_North_Vietnamese_legislative_election) ever in Vietnam was held all over the country in which all people 18 years old or older were eligible to vote.

The first session of the First National Assembly (*Quốc hội khoá I*) took place on March 2, 1946 with nearly 300 deputies in the [Hanoi Opera House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanoi_Opera_House). [Nguyễn Văn Tố](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_V%C4%83n_T%E1%BB%91) was appointed as the Chairman of the National Assembly's Standing Committee. The First National Assembly approved [Hồ Chí Minh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ho_Chi_Minh) as the head of government and his cabinet, and the former Emperor [Bảo Đại](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%E1%BA%A3o_%C4%90%E1%BA%A1i) as "the Supreme Advisor". The second session, [Bùi Bằng Đoàn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%B9i_B%E1%BA%B1ng_%C4%90o%C3%A0n) was appointed to be the Chairman of the National Assembly's Standing Committee. [Tôn Đức Thắng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%B4n_%C4%90%E1%BB%A9c_Th%E1%BA%AFng) was Acting Chairman from 1948, and from 1955, when Đoàn died, he was Chairman of the Standing Committee.

The first and second Constitutions of the [Democratic Republic of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Republic_of_Vietnam) were passed by this National Assembly in 1946 and 1959, respectively. The term of the First National Assembly was prolonged (14 years) due to the war situation in Vietnam, particularly the [partition of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partition_of_Vietnam) according to the [Geneva Accords of 1954](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva_Accords_of_1954). From 1954 to 1976, the National Assembly's activities were only effective in [North Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Vietnam).

### Divided Vietnam (1954–1976)

#### North Vietnam

- **The Second National Assembly (1960–1964)**

Because the [State of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_Vietnam) claimed that it did not sign the Geneva Accords and doubted about the possibility of free elections in the North in July 1955, a nationwide general election for reunification could not be organized. The Second National Assembly was formed by 362 elected deputies of the North and 91 deputies of the South from the First National Assembly who continued their term. [Trường Chinh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tr%C6%B0%E1%BB%9Dng_Chinh) was appointed as the Chairman of the National Assembly's Standing Committee and held this position until 1981.

- **The Third National Assembly (1964–1971)**

The Third National Assembly consisted of 366 elected deputies from the North and 87 deputies continuing their term. The Third National Assembly's term was prolonged due to the war situation. [President](466686.md) [Hồ Chí Minh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%E1%BB%93_Ch%C3%AD_Minh) died during the Third National Assembly's term, and was succeeded by [Tôn Đức Thắng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%B4n_%C4%90%E1%BB%A9c_Th%E1%BA%AFng).

- **The Fourth National Assembly (1971–1975)**

Four hundred and twenty deputies were elected for the Fourth National Assembly. During the fourth term, North Vietnam signed the [Paris Peace Accords](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Peace_Accords), recognizing the [Republic of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Vietnam) in South Vietnam.

- **The Fifth National Assembly (1975–1976)**

The Fifth National Assembly consisted of 424 elected deputies. The fifth term was the shortest National Assembly's term as it was shortened to organize the nationwide general election after the [re-unification of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fall_of_Saigon).

The predecessor of the [Republic of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Vietnam) was the [State of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_Vietnam) (1949–55), which had no parliament but only a National Advisory Council of 45 people appointed by the Chief of State. Due to [war conditions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Indochina_War), the state could not hold parliamentary elections but could only hold municipal elections in January 1953.[^cite_note-4] After the Republic of Vietnam was formed in 1955, it organized [parliamentary elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1956_South_Vietnamese_Constitutional_Assembly_election) on 4 March 1956, leading to the establishment of its own [parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_the_Republic_of_Vietnam) and constitution on 26 October that year. It initially had a unicameral parliament. After the [new constitution](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Constitution%20of%20South%20Vietnam%20\(1967\)) was passed in 1967, it had two parliamentary chambers: the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_\(South_Vietnam\)?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*Thượng-nghị-viện*, literally *Upper Parliament*) and the [House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_\(South_Vietnam\)?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*Hạ-nghị-viện*, literally *Lower Parliament*).

After the [Fall of Saigon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fall_of_Saigon), the [Republic of South Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Revolutionary_Government_of_the_Republic_of_South_Vietnam) also held the People's Assembly (*Đại hội đại biểu Nhân dân*) as its sole legislature.

### Since 1976

- **The Sixth National Assembly (1976–1981)**

Following a consultative conference between North and South Vietnam for their reunification in November 1975, a general parliament election took place on April 25 of the following year. The voters selected 492 members, of which 243 represented the South and 249 the North. In this term, the National Assembly adopted the name "the Socialist Republic of Vietnam" (*Cộng hoà xã hội chủ nghĩa Việt Nam*) for the re-unified country on 2 July 1976, merged corresponding organizations between the Government of [North Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Vietnam) and [South Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Revolutionary_Government_of_the_Republic_of_South_Vietnam), and renamed Saigon as [Ho Chi Minh City](66956.md). It also approved the new Constitution in 1980.

The "sixth" numbering indicates that it is the constitutional continuation of the National Assembly of the [Democratic Republic of Vietnam in the North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Vietnam).

- **The Seventh National Assembly (1981–1987)**

The Seventh National Assembly and its 496 members witnessed the end of the Vietnamese centralized and heavily planned economy and the [CPV](400546.md)'s launch of the Renewal Policy (*Đổi mới*) to adopt market economy. [Trường Chinh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tr%C6%B0%E1%BB%9Dng_Chinh) was elected as [Chairman of the State Council](466686.md) and [Nguyễn Hữu Thọ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_H%E1%BB%AFu_Th%E1%BB%8D) was elected as Chairman of the National Assembly.

- **The Eighth National Assembly (1987–1992)**

In previous elections, because successful candidates were chosen in advance, the electoral process was not genuine. No one could run for office unless approved by the Communist Party, and in many cases the local body of the party simply appointed the candidates. Nevertheless, every citizen had a duty to vote, and, although the balloting was secret, the electorate, through electoral study sessions, received directives from the party concerning who should be elected. The elections in 1987, however, were comparatively open by Vietnamese standards. It was evident that the party was tolerating a wider choice in candidates and more debate. The 1987 election chose 496 deputies for the Eighth National Assembly. In this term, the National Assembly approved the 1992 Constitution, in which the citizen's personal ownership of properties in business was guaranteed. [Lê Quang Đạo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%AA_Quang_%C4%90%E1%BA%A1o) was appointed to be the National Assembly's Chairman.

- **The Ninth National Assembly (1992–1997)**

The 1992 election established the first National Assembly after the 1992 Constitution came into effect. From this term, the National Assembly's agenda has been filled with law-making procedures to serve the *Đổi mới* Policy. National Assembly consisted of 395 elected deputies. In this term, [Nông Đức Mạnh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N%C3%B4ng_%C4%90%E1%BB%A9c_M%E1%BA%A1nh) was elected as the 7th Chairman of the National Assembly.

- **The Tenth National Assembly (1997–2002)**

National Assembly consisted of 450 elected deputies. In this term, Chairman [Nông Đức Mạnh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N%C3%B4ng_%C4%90%E1%BB%A9c_M%E1%BA%A1nh) was re-elected as the chairman of the National Assembly and become the second person to be re-elected after [Trường Chinh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tr%C6%B0%E1%BB%9Dng_Chinh). In the middle of 2001, he was chosen to be the CPV's secretary-general in its Ninth National Congress and ceased his role as the chairman of the National Assembly. [Nguyễn Văn An](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_V%C4%83n_An) was appointed to replace Mạnh and he became the 8th chairman of the National Assembly.

- **The Eleventh National Assembly (2002–2007)**

National Assembly consisted of 498 elected deputies. In this term, Nguyễn Văn An was elected as the chairman of the National Assembly. NA Vice Chairpersons: [Trương Quang Được](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tr%C6%B0%C6%A1ng_Quang_%C4%90%C6%B0%E1%BB%A3c), Nguyễn Phúc Thanh, Nguyễn Văn Yểu. After the Tenth National Congress of CPV, he retired. In the middle of 2006, [Nguyễn Phú Trọng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_Ph%C3%BA_Tr%E1%BB%8Dng) was appointed as the 9th chairman of the National Assembly.

- **The Twelfth National Assembly (2007–2011)**

The National Assembly consisted of 493 elected deputies. In this term, [Nguyễn Phú Trọng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_Ph%C3%BA_Tr%E1%BB%8Dng) was elected as the 10th Chairman of the National Assembly.[^cite_note-5] NA Vice Chairpersons: [Tòng Thị Phóng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%B2ng_Th%E1%BB%8B_Ph%C3%B3ng) (first), [Nguyễn Đức Kiên](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_%C4%90%E1%BB%A9c_Ki%C3%AAn), Huỳnh Ngọc Sơn and Uông Chu Lưu

- **The Thirteenth National Assembly (2011–2016)**

National Assembly consisted of 500 elected deputies. In this term, [Nguyễn Sinh Hùng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_Sinh_H%C3%B9ng) was elected as the 11th Chairman of the National Assembly. NA Vice Chairpersons: Tòng Thị Phóng (first), Nguyễn Thị Kim Ngân, Uông Chu Lưu and Huỳnh Ngọc Sơn.

- **The Fourteenth National Assembly (2016–2021)**

National Assembly consisted of 496 elected deputies. In this term, [Nguyễn Thị Kim Ngân](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_Th%E1%BB%8B_Kim_Ng%C3%A2n) was elected as the 12th Chairperson of the National Assembly and first woman ever to hold this position. NA Vice Chairpersons: Tòng Thị Phóng (first), Uông Chu Lưu, Phùng Quốc Hiển and [Đỗ Bá Tỵ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%90%E1%BB%97_B%C3%A1_T%E1%BB%B5).

- **[The Fifteenth National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/15th_National_Assembly_of_Vietnam) (2021–2026)**

National Assembly consisted of 499 elected deputies. In this term, [Vương Đình Huệ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C6%B0%C6%A1ng_%C4%90%C3%ACnh_Hu%E1%BB%87) was elected as the Chairman of the National Assembly. NA Vice Chairpersons: [Trần Thanh Mẫn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tr%E1%BA%A7n_Thanh_M%E1%BA%ABn) (first), [Nguyễn Khắc Định](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_Kh%E1%BA%AFc_%C4%90%E1%BB%8Bnh?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Nguyễn Đức Hải](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_%C4%90%E1%BB%A9c_H%E1%BA%A3i?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Trần Quang Phương](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tr%E1%BA%A7n_Quang_Ph%C6%B0%C6%A1ng?action=edit\&redlink=1).

- **[The Sixteenth National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/16th_National_Assembly_of_Vietnam) (2026–2031)**

National Assembly consisted of 500 elected deputies. In this term, [Trần Thanh Mẫn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tr%E1%BA%A7n_Thanh_M%E1%BA%ABn) was elected as the Chairman of the National Assembly. NA Vice Chairpersons: [Đỗ Văn Chiến](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%90%E1%BB%97_V%C4%83n_Chi%E1%BA%BFn) (first), [Nguyễn Khắc Định](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_Kh%E1%BA%AFc_%C4%90%E1%BB%8Bnh?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Nguyễn Thị Thanh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_Th%E1%BB%8B_Thanh_\(politician,_born_1967\)?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Nguyễn Doãn Anh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_Do%C3%A3n_Anh?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Nguyễn Hồng Diên](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_H%E1%BB%93ng_Di%C3%AAn) and [Nguyễn Thị Hồng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_Th%E1%BB%8B_H%E1%BB%93ng_\(economist\)); Secretary-General [Lê Quang Mạnh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%AA_Quang_M%E1%BA%A1nh?action=edit\&redlink=1).

## Structure and organization

- [Standing Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standing_Committee_of_the_National_Assembly) (*Ủy ban Thường vụ*): This is the standing and executive body of the National Assembly, including the Chairman/Chairwoman, Deputy Chairmen/Chairwomen, and other standing members. The number of the Standing Committee's members is decided by the National Assembly, these members must not concurrently hold a position in the cabinet. The Standing Committee of previous term shall continue their duties until the newly elected National Assembly establishes its new Standing Committee.&#x20;

  - The Standing Committee's constitutional duties include:&#x20;

    - (1) preparing, convening, and chairing the National Assembly's sessions;

    - (2) explaining/interpreting the Constitution, laws, acts, and ordinances;

    - (3) promulgating ordinances at the National Assembly's request;

    - (4) supervising the implementation of the Constitution and laws, and the activities of the Government, the Supreme People's Court, and the Supreme People's Procuracy;

    - (5) supervising and guiding the activities of provincial people's councils (local legislative bodies);

    - (6) directing and co-ordinating the activities of the National Assembly's Ethnic Council and other committees, providing guidance and guaranteeing the deputies' working conditions;

    - (7) approving the cabinet's personnel affairs in between the National Assembly's sessions and making reports to the National Assembly in the next session;

    - (8) declaring wars if necessary in between the National Assembly's sessions and making reports to the National Assembly in the next session;

    - (9) launching general mobilization or partial mobilization, and declaring national or local state of emergency if necessary;

    - (10) performing external relation activities of the National Assembly; and

    - (11) organizing referendum at the National Assembly's request. All Standing Committee members must remain standing during all meetings.

  - [Commission on People's Aspiration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commission_on_People's_Aspiration_of_the_National_Assembly?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*Ban Dân nguyện*): This is a subordinate of the Standing Committee. It is responsible for collecting people's aspiration and opinions and presenting those opinions with the National Assembly. It is headed by a Chief

  - [Commission on Delegate Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commission_on_Delegate_Affairs_of_the_National_Assembly?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*Ban Công tác Đại biểu*): This is a subordinate of the Standing Committee. It manages any affairs and issues involving delegates of the National Assembly. It is headed by a Chief

  - [Legislation Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislation_Research_Institute_\(Vietnam\)?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*Viện Nghiên cứu Lập pháp*): This is a subordinate of the Standing Committee

- [National Assembly Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_Office_\(Vietnam\)?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*Văn phòng Quốc hội*): headed by a Secretary-General

- [Council on Ethnic Minorities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_on_Ethnic_Minorities_of_the_National_Assembly?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*Hội đồng Dân tộc*): The main duties include contributing, investigating and supervising the legal documents (laws, acts, ordinances) and legal procedures on ethnic issues. It is headed by a Chairperson

- [Committee on Laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_on_Laws_of_the_National_Assembly?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*Ủy ban Pháp luật*), headed by a Chairperson

- [Committee on Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_on_Justice_of_the_National_Assembly?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*Ủy ban Tư pháp*), headed by a Chairperson

- [Committee on Economy](https://vi.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E1%BB%A6y%20ban%20Kinh%20t%E1%BA%BF%20c%E1%BB%A7a%20Qu%E1%BB%91c%20h%E1%BB%99i%20\(Vi%E1%BB%87t%20Nam\)) (*Ủy ban Kinh tế*), headed by a Chairperson

- [Committee on Finance – Budget](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_on_Finance_-_Budget_of_the_National_Assembly?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*Ủy ban Tài chính – Ngân sách*), headed by a Chairperson

- [Committee on Defense and Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_on_Defense_and_Security_of_the_National_Assembly?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*Ủy ban Quốc phòng và An ninh*), headed by a Chairperson

- [Committee on Culture, Education, Youths, Teenagers and Children](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_on_Culture,_Education,_Youths,_Teenagers_and_Children_of_the_National_Assembly?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*Ủy ban Văn hóa, Giáo dục, Thanh niên, Thiếu niên và Nhi đồng*), headed by a Chairperson

- [Committee on Social Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_on_Social_Affairs_of_the_National_Assembly?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*Ủy ban Các Vấn đề Xã hội*), headed by a Chairperson

- [Committee on Science, Technology and Environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_on_Science,_Technology_and_Environment_of_the_National_Assembly?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*Ủy ban Khoa học, Công nghệ và Môi trường*), headed by a Chairperson

- [Committee on External Relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_on_External_Relations_of_the_National_Assembly?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*Ủy ban Đối ngoại*), headed by a Chairperson

[^cite_note-6]

Other agencies that are elected by the National Assembly, but function independently, are:

- [National Election Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Election_Council?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*Hội đồng Bầu cử Quốc gia*), headed by a Chairperson

- [State Audit Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Audit_Office_\(Vietnam\)?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*Kiểm toán Nhà nước*), headed by a Chief State Auditor
