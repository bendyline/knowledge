The **Washington Hilton**[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] is a [Hilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilton_Worldwide) [hotel](14276.md) in [Washington, D.C.](108956.md) It is located at 1919 Connecticut Avenue, N.W., roughly at the boundaries of the [Kalorama Heights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalorama_Heights), [Dupont Circle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dupont_Circle), and [Adams Morgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adams_Morgan) neighborhoods. Since its opening in 1965, the hotel has hosted numerous major large events including the annual dinners of the [White House Correspondents' Association](4790115.md).

The hotel was the site of the [assassination attempt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Ronald_Reagan) on President [Ronald Reagan](25433.md) in 1981, as well as a [foiled assassination attempt](83038072.md) on the administration of President [Donald Trump](4848272.md) in 2026.

## History

The Washington Hilton, located on the former site of the [Oak Lawn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oak_Lawn_\(Washington,_D.C.\)) estate, was designed by architect [William B. Tabler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_B._Tabler)[^cite_note-4] and developed by [Uris Buildings Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uris_Buildings_Corporation).[^cite_note-5] A groundbreaking ceremony was held on June 25, 1962[^cite_note-6] and the hotel officially opened three years later, on March 25, 1965.[^cite_note-auto-1] The hotel structure features a distinctive double-arched design. It long sported the largest pillarless hotel [ballroom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballroom) in the city.

Opened less than two years after the assassination of President [John F. Kennedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_F._Kennedy), the ballroom and conference area was built with security for events featuring the [United States president](24113.md) in mind, with the cooperation of the [Secret Service](58221.md). That includes a secure corridor for the president and an entrance on T Street that was originally labeled "Presidential Entrance."[^cite_note-7]

Numerous large events have been regularly hosted at the Hilton Washington, including the annual dinners of the [White House Correspondents' Association](4790115.md) and the [Radio and Television Correspondents' Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_and_Television_Correspondents'_Association), Washington Mardi Gras, as well as the [National Prayer Breakfast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Prayer_Breakfast).

During the 1960s and 1970s, the hotel hosted a number of big musical acts for concerts in their large ballroom, including [The Doors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Doors) and [Jimi Hendrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimi_Hendrix).[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] In 1972 it was home to the first [International Conference on Computer Communicationsm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Conference_on_Computer_Communications), which demonstrated new [ARPANET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARPANET) technology.

The hotel was renamed the **Hilton Washington** in 1998.[^cite_note-10] It was purchased in June 2007 by an investment firm involving former professional [basketball](3921.md) star [Magic Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magic_Johnson) and [Lowe Enterprises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lowe_Enterprises). From 2009 to 2010 it underwent a \$150 million renovation. When that was completed, the hotel returned to its original name.[^cite_note-11]

### Assassination attempts

The hotel was the site of the [assassination attempt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Ronald_Reagan) on [President](24113.md) [Ronald Reagan](25433.md) by [John Hinckley Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hinckley_Jr.) on March 30, 1981. Reagan was seriously wounded but survived. The attempt occurred at the hotel's T Street NW exit. As a result, the hotel is sometimes colloquially called the **Hinckley Hilton** by locals.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]

On April 25, 2026, Cole Tomas Allen [attempted to assassinate](83038072.md) officials in [the administration](78298108.md) of President [Donald Trump](4848272.md) during the [White House Correspondents' Dinner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_House_Correspondents'_Dinner). Allen failed to reach the dinner before he was arrested, and no officials were injured.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-Reuters-manifesto-16][^cite_note-AP-writings-17]
