**Qeshm** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): قشم)[^cite_note-4] is a coastal city in the [Central District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_District_\(Qeshm_County\)) of [Qeshm County](7349780.md), [Hormozgan province, Iran](448064.md), serving as capital of both the county and the district.[^cite_note-Hormozgan_Province_Structure-5]

## Demographics

### Population

In the 2006 census, the city's population was 24,461, in 5,882 households.[^cite_note-2006_Hormozgan_Province-6] The 2011 census counted 28,602 people, in 7,524 households.[^cite_note-2011_Hormozgan_Province-7] The 2016 census counted 40,678 people, in 11,945 households.[^cite_note-2016_Hormozgan_Province-2]
