A **cabinet crisis**, **government crisis** or **political crisis** refers to a situation where an incumbent [government](12229.md) is unable to form or function, is toppled through an [uprising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uprising), or collapses.[^cite_note-Chang_2007-1] Political crises may correspond with, cause or be caused by an [economic crisis](2878852.md), and may spread among neighbouring countries.[^cite_note-Chang_2007-1][^cite_note-2]
