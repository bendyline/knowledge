The **Constitution of Guinea-Bissau** is the basic law governing [Guinea-Bissau](12186.md). It was adopted in 1984 as a [communist state constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_state_constitution), came into force on 6 May 1984, and revised in 1991, 1993 and 1996 following democratization. After the [coup d'état on 26 November 2025](81695397.md), the constitution was suspended and replaced with a transitional charter.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] A [constitutional referendum on 30 August 2026](83685925.md) was held, where the people of Guinea-Bissau could voted for a new constitution.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] The majority voted for the new constitution.[^cite_note-7]
