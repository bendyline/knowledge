# Krasne, Mykolaiv Raion, Mykolaiv Oblast

**Krasne** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Красне) is a town in [Mykolaiv Oblast](494928.md), Ukraine.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]



Krasne

## Geography

Krasne is located west of [Mykolaiv](901495.md) and north of [Ochakiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ochakiv).[^cite_note-3]
