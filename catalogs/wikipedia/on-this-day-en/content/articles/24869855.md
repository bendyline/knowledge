**Gezawa**[Listen](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/9/93/LL-Q56475_%28hau%29-Eunice_Ameh-Gezawa.wav/LL-Q56475_%28hau%29-Eunice_Ameh-Gezawa.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup> is a town and [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Areas_of_Nigeria) in [Kano State](2123717.md), [Nigeria](21383.md).[^cite_note-1] Gezawa Local Government Area's council is made up of Jogana, Gezawa, Mesar-Tudu, Gawo, Zango, Danzaki, Tumbau, Wangara, Sararin-Gezawa, Babawa, Ketawa and Tsamiya-Babba. It has an area of 340 km<sup>2</sup> and a population of 282,069 at the 2006 [census](6889.md). [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_people) and [Fulani ethnic](102383.md) groups make up the majority of the local population. The majority [religion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religion) practiced in the [region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Region) is [Islam](6037917.md), and the languages spoken there are [Fulfulde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulfulde) and [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hausa_people).[^cite_note-\:0-2]

The Gezawa government councils heads the public administrations in Gezawa government area which is made up of eleven wards represented by eleven [councilors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Councillor) inclusive of the chairman who heads the executive arm of the local government.[^cite_note-3]

The [postal code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_code) of the area is 702.[^cite_note-4]

## Climate

In Gezawa, the year-round heat and partly [foggy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fog) dry season contrast with the unpleasant wet season. The average annual [temperature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperature) fluctuates between 56 °F and 102[°F](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fahrenheit); it is rarely lower or higher than 51 °F or 107 °F. The hot season, which runs from March 18 to May 30, lasts for 2.4 months and has an average daily high temperature of more than 99 °F.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-\:1-6] May is the hottest month of the year in Gezawa, with typical highs and lows of 100 °F and 77 °F, respectively.[^cite_note-7] With an average daily maximum temperature below 88 °F, the chilly season spans 1.7 months, from December 7 to January 29.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] January is the coldest month of the year in Gezawa, with an average high temperature of 86 °F and low of 57 °F.[^cite_note-\:1-6] The annual temperature of the area is 30.57 °C (87.03 °F), which is 1.11% higher than the average for Nigeria. Every year, Gezawa experiences 62.61 wet days (17.15% of the total) and 49.5 [millimetres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millimetre) (1.95 inches) of [precipitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precipitation).[^cite_note-10] Gezawa LGA has an average temperature of 34 degrees [Celsius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celsius) or 93 degrees [Fahrenheit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fahrenheit) and a total area of 340 [square kilometres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_kilometre) or 130 [square miles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_miles). The Local Government Area has an average humidity level of 22% and an average wind speed of 9 [km/h](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilometres_per_hour) or 5.5 [mph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miles_per_hour).[^cite_note-\:0-2]

## Religion

The main religions practice in Gezawa are Islam and Christianity.

## Economy

Gezawa LGA is well known for growing a variety of crops, including [rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice), [millet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millet), [cotton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton), and [sorghum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorghum). [Rams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goat), [horses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horse), and [cows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cattle) are just a few of the species that are raised in the area. Several markets are held in Gezawa Local Government Area, giving the locals a place to exchange a range of goods and services. The Dausayi and Kasuwar duniya markets are two of these.[^cite_note-\:0-2]
