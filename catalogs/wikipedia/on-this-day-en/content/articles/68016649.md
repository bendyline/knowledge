**Valentin Vacherot** (French: [\[valɑ̃tɛ̃ vaʃʁo\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French); born 16 November 1998) is a Monégasque professional [tennis](29773.md) player. He has a career-high [ATP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_Tennis_Professionals) singles ranking of world No. 16 achieved on 4 May 2026, making him the [highest-ranked Monégasque singles player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_male_singles_tennis_players) in history.[^cite_note-VV2025-3] He is the current No. 1 Monaco singles player.[^cite_note-4] He also has a best doubles ranking of No. 104 achieved on 4 May 2026.[^cite_note-VV_Rankings-5]

Vacherot played college tennis at [Texas A\&M](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A\&M_Aggies).[^cite_note-Texas_overview-6]

In 2025, Vacherot became the first player from his nation to win an ATP Tour singles title and the lowest ranked champion in Masters 1000 history (since 1990) in [Shanghai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Rolex_Shanghai_Masters).[^cite_note-Masters1000-7][^cite_note-8] He also became the first Monégasque player to reach the top 50, and later the top 20 in singles, and to record a Top 10 win.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

Vacherot represents [Monaco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monaco_Davis_Cup_team) at the [Davis Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davis_Cup), where he has a win–loss record of 10–3.

## Early life

Vacherot was born in [Roquebrune-Cap-Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roquebrune-Cap-Martin) in the Alpes-Maritimes department of France[^cite_note-VV_Bio-2][^cite_note-Texas_overview-6] to parents Josse and Nadine Vacherot. He has three siblings.[^cite_note-ATP_Profile-1] He was raised in a border-town by [Monaco](19261.md) and holds dual French and Monegasque citizenship, and went to school in and frequently visited the country; he therefore opted to represent them internationally in 2022.[^cite_note-12]

## Professional career

### 2022: First Challenger title

In 2022, Vacherot won his first [Challenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Challenger_Tour) title at the [Nonthaburi Challenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonthaburi_Challenger), Thailand, beating [Lý Hoàng Nam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%BD_Ho%C3%A0ng_Nam) in the final.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-Nonthaburi-14][^cite_note-VV-15] Vacherot became Monaco’s second Challenger champion and the first since [Jean-René Lisnard](10204380.md) in 2004.[^cite_note-16]

### 2023: Masters debut

Vacherot received a wildcard for the [2023 Monte-Carlo Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Monte-Carlo_Masters) main draw, making his Masters debut.[^cite_note-Challenger-17][^cite_note-18] He lost in the first round to [Luca Nardi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luca_Nardi).[^cite_note-19]

### 2024: Grand Slam and top 110 debuts

Vacherot received a wildcard for the [2024 Monte-Carlo Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Monte-Carlo_Masters),[^cite_note-VV-15][^cite_note-20] losing to ninth seed [Grigor Dimitrov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grigor_Dimitrov).[^cite_note-21] Ranked No. 136, he qualified for the main draw at the [2024 Țiriac Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_%C8%9Airiac_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles) but lost his opening match to eventual champion [Márton Fucsovics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A1rton_Fucsovics).[^cite_note-22] Vacherot reached the top 120 on 6 May 2024, following a semifinal appearance as a qualifier, at the [2024 Open Aix Provence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Open_Aix_Provence).[^cite_note-VV_Rankings-5], losing to eventual champion [Alejandro Tabilo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alejandro_Tabilo).[^cite_note-23]

Vacherot made his Grand Slam debut at the [2024 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_French_Open) after qualifying, becoming the first player from the [Monégasque Tennis Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mon%C3%A9gasque_Tennis_Federation) to qualify for the main draw.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25] He lost in the first round to [Alejandro Davidovich Fokina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alejandro_Davidovich_Fokina).[^cite_note-26]

### 2025: Historic Masters title and top 30

Vacherot also received a wildcard for the [2025 Monte-Carlo Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Monte-Carlo_Masters)[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28] and recorded his first Masters win over [Jan-Lennard Struff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan-Lennard_Struff) in straight sets.[^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30] He became the first Monégasque player to win a main draw match at the tournament since Jean-René Lisnard in [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Monte-Carlo_Rolex_Masters_%E2%80%93_Singles).[^cite_note-31]

In October, ranked No. 204, Vacherot won his first title at the [2025 Rolex Shanghai Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Rolex_Shanghai_Masters) as a qualifier, becoming the lowest ranked player to win a [Masters 1000](30872553.md) tournament. After qualifying, he recorded his second and third Masters wins[^cite_note-32] by defeating [Laslo Djere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laslo_Djere)[^cite_note-33] in straight sets and then [Alexander Bublik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Bublik) in the second round.[^cite_note-34] Vacherot reached his first Masters 1000 fourth round following the retirement of 20th seed [Tomáš Macháč](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom%C3%A1%C5%A1_Mach%C3%A1%C4%8D). He then upset [Tallon Griekspoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tallon_Griekspoor) to reach his first Masters quarterfinal and thus became the lowest-ranked quarterfinalist in the event history in Shanghai, and the first player from Monaco to reach an [ATP Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_Tour) and Masters-level quarterfinal at the same time.[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36][^cite_note-37] Next he defeated [Holger Rune](60992715.md) in the quarterfinals. Vacherot defeated [Novak Djokovic](16100029.md) in the semifinals, recording the first top 10 (and top 5) win for a Monégasque player in history, and becoming the lowest ranked player to reach a [Masters 1000](30872553.md) final.[^cite_note-38] In Vacherot’s 27th tour-level match, he defeated his cousin [Arthur Rinderknech](57494697.md) to win his first ATP Tour and Masters 1000 title.[^cite_note-Masters1000-7] Going into the Shanghai final, Vacherot and Rinderknech had each won 3 matches after dropping the first set. The cousins also collectively defeated 10 of the 32 seeds (5 each) in the draw.



Valentin Vacherot in the round of 32 against Taylor Fritz at ATP 500 Basel 2025

Vacherot received a wildcard into the [Basel Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Swiss_Indoors_%E2%80%93_Singles), but lost to [Taylor Fritz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Fritz) in the first round.[^cite_note-39] Vacherot received a wildcard into the [Paris Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Rolex_Paris_Masters_%E2%80%93_Singles) and then reached the quarterfinals.[^cite_note-40] Vacherot reached a new career-high ranking in the top 30 on 3 November 2025.[^cite_note-41][^cite_note-VV2025-3]

### 2026: Major third round and top 20

Vacherot made his debut at the [2026 Australian Open](81711108.md) where he was seeded for the first time and recorded his first major wins to reach the third round, also for a first time.[^cite_note-VV2025-3]

## Personal life

Vacherot is coached professionally by his elder half-brother [Benjamin Balleret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Balleret), who previously played professional tennis as well.[^cite_note-ATP_Profile-1][^cite_note-42] Other professional tennis players in Vacherot's family include cousins [Arthur Rinderknech](57494697.md) and [Chloé Paquet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chlo%C3%A9_Paquet),[^cite_note-43] and aunt [Virginie Paquet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginie_Paquet).[^cite_note-44][^cite_note-Challenger-17]
