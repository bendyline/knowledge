# Fisheries Agency (Japan)



The Fisheries Agency is in the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries building in Kasumigaseki

The **Fisheries Agency** (水産庁, *Suisan-chō*) is an agency under the [Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries](1327960.md) of [Japan](15573.md).

Its headquarters are in [Kasumigaseki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasumigaseki), [Chiyoda](47836792.md), [Tokyo](30057.md).[^cite_note-1]

The agency ensures that fish caught in Japanese territory are done so under Japanese law.[^cite_note-2] It also sets fines for fish that are not caught under the law.[^cite_note-3]
