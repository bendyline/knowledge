The **United States women's national flag football team** represents the [United States](3434750.md) in women's international [flag football](224471.md) matches. The sport is governed by [USA Football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USA_Football). In 2023, the [International Federation of American Football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Federation_of_American_Football) (IFAF) ranked the United States women's team 1st worldwide.[^cite_note-1] In 2025 they ranked 2nd.[^cite_note-2]

The head coach is Saaid Mortazavi with Mary Kate Bula and Matthew Hernandez as assistant coaches.[^cite_note-2025_coaches-3]

In 2008, the women competed in the [IFAF Flag Football World Championship](43253761.md) where they finished fourth. They won silver in 2010,[^cite_note-Canada-4] 2012,[^cite_note-2014_Italy-5] and 2014.[^cite_note-2021_roster-6] They lost to [Canada](78747614.md) twice[^cite_note-2014_Italy-5] and [Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico_women's_national_flag_football_team) once. In the 2018 finals they defeated Panama 27–12 to win gold.[^cite_note-2018_Panama-7] The team defeated Mexico in the finals of [2021 championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_IFAF_Women's_Flag_Football_World_Championship). At the 2023 [IFAF Americas Continental Flag Football Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IFAF_Americas_Continental_Flag_Football_Championship) both the women's and [men's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_men's_national_flag_football_team) teams defeated Mexico to win gold.[^cite_note-2023_IFAF-8] In 2024 the women's team won a third gold after beating Mexico in the [World Championship](77713747.md). The women's team also gained three silvers in 2010, 2012, and 2014.

During the [2022 World Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_football_at_the_2022_World_Games), the team again went to the finals with Mexico.[^cite_note-NFL-9] This time they won silver with a score of 39–6. Quarterback [Vanita Krouch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanita_Krouch) was invited to an NFL game to honor the country's success at the games and to promote flag football.[^cite_note-NFL-9] The team repeated the success in [2025 World Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_football_at_the_2025_World_Games) held in Chengdu.
