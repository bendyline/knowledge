The **cabinet of Thailand** (formally, the **Council of Ministers of Thailand**; [Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): คณะรัฐมนตรี; [RTGS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Thai_General_System_of_Transcription): **Khana Ratthamontri*)* is a body composed of thirty-five (35) of the most senior members of the government of the [Kingdom of Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Thailand). The [cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_\(government\)) is the primary organ of the [executive branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_branch) of the [Thai government](19068660.md). Members of the cabinet are nominated by the [prime minister](273644.md) and formally appointed by the [King of Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_of_Thailand). Most members are governmental department heads with the title of "minister of state" ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): รัฐมนตรี; [RTGS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Thai_General_System_of_Transcription): **Ratthamontri*)*. The cabinet is chaired by the [Prime Minister of Thailand](273644.md). The cabinet is often collectively called "the government" or "the Royal Thai Government".

## History

See also: [Chatusadom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatusadom)

Prior to the [Revolution of 1932](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siamese_Revolution_of_1932), the absolutist [Chakri kings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Chakri) ruled [Siam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siam) through a series of "krom" ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): กรม) and "senabodi" ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): เสนาบดี).[^cite_note-1] On 15 April 1874 King [Chulalongkorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chulalongkorn) founded the [King's Privy Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privy_Council_\(Thailand\)) ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): สภาที่ปรึกษาในพระองค์) (which still exists today) made up of 49 senior [princes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince) and officials. For the first time the kings of Siam exercised his powers through a council.

On 14 July 1925 King [Prajadhipok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prajadhipok) formed the [Supreme Council of State of Siam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Council_of_State_of_Siam) ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): อภิรัฐมนตรีสภา; [RTGS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Thai_General_System_of_Transcription): *_Aphiratthamontrisapha_*) made up of 5 senior princes (all of them his brothers and all of them ministers of state) to help govern the country. However, after the revolution in 1932, the [Khana Ratsadon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khana_Ratsadon) decided to dissolve this council. Instead the [new constitution](6840408.md#1932_Temporary_Charter) created a direct precursor of the cabinet called: the **[People's Committee of Siam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Committee_of_Siam)** ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): คณะกรรมการราษฎร; [RTGS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Thai_General_System_of_Transcription): **Khana Kammakan Ratsadon*)* led by a president. The [privy council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privy_council) from then on became a royal advisory council.

With the promulgation of ["permanent" constitution](6840408.md#1932_constitution) at the end of that year, the name of the committee was changed to the "council of ministers" and the name of the chair to [prime minister](273644.md), after King Prajadhipok deemed the old names too [communistic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communism). The first cabinet of Thailand was led by [Phraya Manopakorn Nititada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phraya_Manopakorn_Nititada). All [government departments and agencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Cabinet_Ministries_of_Thailand) were then immediately transferred to its control. To date there have been 58 cabinets of Thailand.

## Ministers of State

### Qualifications



[Government House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_House_of_Thailand) in 2009, the meeting place and office of the Cabinet

According to the [2007 Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_Constitution_of_Thailand) the cabinet is restricted to no more than 35 members. Members of the cabinet, unlike the prime minister, do not need to be a member of the [House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_of_Thailand), however most of them are. To be eligible to be a minister an individual must meet the following qualifications:

- Be a [Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_people) national by birth.

- Be older than 35 years of age.

- Have graduated with no less than a [bachelor's degree](188874.md) or its equivalent.

- Not be a member of the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_Thailand) (former senators must wait two years after their term of office to be eligible)

The individual must also: not be addicted to drugs, not have been [bankrupt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bankrupt), not be a monk or a member of the clergy, not be disenfranchised, not be mentally infirm, not be under indictment or conviction, not have been expelled by a state agency for [corruption](66220.md) or incompetence, not be a paid [civil servant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_servant) or member of the [judiciary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judiciary), and not ever been removed from any office by the [Senate of Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_of_Thailand).

**Ministers of State** are in theory appointed by the [king](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King), however in truth they are appointed at the advice of the prime minister to the king. Before taking office, a minister must make a solemn declaration before the king in the following words:&#x20;

> "I, (name of the declarer), do solemnly declare that I will be loyal to the King and will faithfully perform my duties in the interests of the country and of the people. I will also uphold and observe the Constitution of the Kingdom of Thailand in every respect."

### Roles

The 2007 [Constitution of Thailand](6840408.md) calls the cabinet the "Council of Ministers". The entire IXth chapter is dedicated to it. There are only 20 cabinet ministries, which means that 15 ministers are without portfolio. They can, however, be appointed [deputy prime ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Prime_Minister_of_Thailand) or [deputy ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_minister). According to the constitution, the cabinet must, within fifteen days of being sworn in, state its policies to the [National Assembly of Thailand](14846736.md). Ministers have the right to go in person to the national assembly to state and explain policies or opinions.

Each minister is responsible for his actions and the actions of his department and is therefore accountable to the national assembly. Therefore, the assembly can compel the minister to appear before it and explain his actions. The House of Representatives and the Senate can forcefully remove a sitting minister by a [vote of no confidence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vote_of_no_confidence). A one-sixth of the chamber vote is needed to call a debate and a simple majority is required for removal. A minister can also be [removed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Prerogative) by the king on the advice of the prime minister.

## The Cabinet

### Function

As the primary government institution in the [executive branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_branch), the cabinet is ultimately responsible for the administration and management of various government agencies and departments. It is also the primary institution for the formulation of policies with regards to all areas of politics and governing. Legislatively the cabinet is one of the institutions allowed to submit [bills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_\(proposed_law\)) to the National Assembly for consideration. The cabinet is also allowed to call a joint sitting of the National Assembly to consider important bills or even join a joint sitting of the Assembly. The cabinet is also allowed to call a national [referendum](51582.md).

The cabinet is governed by the rule of [collective responsibility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_collective_responsibility), in which the members of the cabinet must support all policies despite personal or private disagreement. As a result, if the government fails or if the policies of the government fails then the entire cabinet must take responsibility; and [resign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resign) in its entirety. The cabinet's term is wedded to that of the Prime Minister. The [Leader of the Opposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leader_of_the_Opposition_\(Thailand\)) is allowed to create his own cabinet or the [Shadow Cabinet of Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shadow_Cabinet_of_Thailand).
