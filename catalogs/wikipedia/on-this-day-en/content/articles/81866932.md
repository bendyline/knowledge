This is a **list of earthquakes in 2026**. Only [earthquakes](10106.md) of magnitude 6 or above are included, unless they result in significant damage and/or casualties. All dates are listed according to [UTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC) time. The maximum intensities are based on the [Modified Mercalli intensity scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modified_Mercalli_intensity_scale). Earthquake magnitudes are based on data from the [United States Geological Survey](23814944.md) (USGS).
