**From left to right, top to bottom:**&#x20;

- [ChatGPT](72417803.md), developed by [OpenAI](48795986.md), comes into widespread use;

- Supporters of former [Brazilian president](273638.md) [Jair Bolsonaro](46603496.md) [storm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Brazilian_Congress_attack) the [National Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Congress_of_Brazil) following his defeat in the [2022 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Brazilian_general_election);

- A makeshift memorial to leaders of the [Wagner Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wagner_Group), who [died in a plane crash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Wagner_Group_plane_crash) two months after [rebelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wagner_Group_rebellion) against the [Russian government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia_under_Vladimir_Putin);

- An alleged [high-altitude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-altitude_balloon) [observation balloon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Observation_balloon) originating from [China](5405.md) is [detected in U.S. airspace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Chinese_balloon_incident), increasing [tensions between the countries](277880.md#Biden_administration_\(2021%E2%80%932025\));

- Remains of [*Titan*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titan_\(submersible\)), a [submersible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Submersible) that [imploded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titan_submersible_implosion) during an expedition to view the [wreck of the *Titanic*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wreck_of_the_Titanic);

- [Azerbaijani soldiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijani_Armed_Forces) locked in combat in [Nagorno-Karabakh](65220.md) amidst an [Azerbaijani offensive](74857564.md);

- [Israel invades the Gaza Strip](74998519.md) in retaliation for the [October 7 attacks](74998317.md) by militant group [Hamas](13913.md) and [spills over into major parts of the Middle East](75895273.md);

- [7.8 and 7.5 magnitude earthquakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Turkey-Syria_earthquake) strike [Syria](7515849.md) and [Turkey](11125639.md), leaving up to 62,000 dead and 121,000 injured.

**2023** ([MMXXIII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_numerals)) was a [common year starting on Sunday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_year_starting_on_Sunday) of the [Gregorian calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregorian_calendar), the 2023rd year of the [Common Era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Era) (CE) and *[Anno Domini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anno_Domini)* (AD) designations, the 23rd year of the [3rd millennium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3rd_millennium) and the [21st century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21st_century), and the 4th year of the [2020s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020s) decade.&#x20;

Catastrophic natural disasters in 2023 included the [5th-deadliest earthquake of the 21st century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lists_of_21st-century_earthquakes) [striking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Turkey%E2%80%93Syria_earthquakes) [Turkey](11125639.md) and [Syria](7515849.md), leaving up to 62,000 people dead; [Cyclone Freddy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclone_Freddy), the longest-lasting recorded tropical cyclone in [history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meteorological_history_of_Cyclone_Freddy) in the [Indian Ocean](14580.md), which led to over 1,400 deaths in [Malawi](19088.md) and [Mozambique](19301.md); [Storm Daniel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storm_Daniel), which became the deadliest tropical cyclone worldwide since [Typhoon Haiyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon_Haiyan) after killing at least 5,300 people in [Libya](17633.md); a major [6.8 magnitude earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Marrakesh%E2%80%93Safi_earthquake) striking western [Morocco](19291.md), killing 2,960 people; and a [6.3 magnitude quadruple earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Herat_earthquakes) striking western [Afghanistan](737.md), killing over 1,400 people. The year also saw a decline in the severity of the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md), with the [WHO](33583.md) (World Health Organization) ending its [global health emergency](42694003.md) status in May.

The [Russo-Ukrainian war](70149799.md) and [Myanmar civil war](69807451.md) continued in 2023, and a [series of](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coup_Belt) [coups](25914154.md), several armed conflicts, and [political crises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Nigerien_crisis) broke out in numerous African nations, most notably a [Sudanese civil war](73556699.md). In [Armenia](10918072.md) and [Azerbaijan](746.md), the [Nagorno-Karabakh conflict](4695860.md) ended after over 100,000 [Armenians](387816.md) [fled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_of_Nagorno-Karabakh_Armenians) the region after an [Azeri military invasion](74857564.md). In [India](14533.md), the year marked the beginning of the [ethnic violence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023%E2%80%932025_Manipur_violence) in the state of [Manipur](422642.md) between the [Kuki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuki_people) and the [Meitei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meitei_people) people. A major escalation of the [Israeli–Palestinian conflict](46216.md) occurred in October when [Hamas](13913.md), the governing body of the [Gaza Strip](12047.md), led [a major attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Hamas-led_attack_on_Israel) on [Israel](9282173.md), leading the latter to both [declare war on Hamas](74998519.md) and [invade the Gaza Strip](74998519.md); the Israeli [aerial bombardment campaign](76139149.md) killed 20,000 Palestinians within two months and caused a [humanitarian crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_humanitarian_crisis_\(2023%E2%80%93present\)), leading to [a genocide](75668427.md) that formed the basis of an [ICJ case](75736183.md) brought by [South Africa](17416221.md) that December.

A [banking crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_United_States_banking_crisis) resulted in the collapse of numerous [American](3434750.md) regional banks as well as the [buyout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acquisition_of_Credit_Suisse_by_UBS) of [Credit Suisse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Credit_Suisse) by [UBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UBS) in [Switzerland](26748.md). The two largest American banks to collapse were [Silicon Valley Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silicon_Valley_Bank) and [First Republic Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Republic_Bank), two of the three largest [banking collapses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_largest_bank_failures_in_the_United_States) in US history. The most notable of numerous acquisitions in various industries included October's energy acquisitions with [ExxonMobil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ExxonMobil) and [Chevron](284749.md) buying [Pioneer Natural Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pioneer_Natural_Resources) and [Hess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hess_Corporation) respectively, the luxury fashion holding company [Tapestry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tapestry,_Inc.) ([Coach New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coach_New_York) and [Kate Spade New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Spade_New_York)) announcing its purchase of [Capri Holdings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capri_Holdings) (Michael Kors and [Versace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Versace)), and the closure of [Microsoft](19001.md)'s [acquisition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acquisition_of_Activision_Blizzard_by_Microsoft) of [Activision Blizzard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Activision_Blizzard). In the realm of technology, 2023 saw [the continued rise of generative AI models](73291899.md), with increasing applications across various industries. These models, leveraging advancements in [machine learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_learning) and [natural language processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_language_processing), had become capable of creating realistic and coherent text, music, and images.

## Demographics

The [world population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_population) on January 1, 2023, was estimated at 7.943 billion people,[^cite_note-297] and was expected to increase to 8.119 billion on January 1, 2024.[^cite_note-298] An estimated 134.3 million births and 60.8 million deaths were expected to take place in 2023.[^cite_note-299][^cite_note-300] The average global [life expectancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life_expectancy) was 73.16 years,[^cite_note-World_Life_Expectancy_1950-2022_|MacroTrends-301] an increase of 0.18 years from 2022.[^cite_note-World_Life_Expectancy_1950-2022_|MacroTrends-301] The rate of [child mortality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child_mortality) was 36.7 per 1000 live births, a decrease from 38.2 in 2022.[^cite_note-302] Less than 23% of people were living in [extreme poverty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extreme_poverty) (on or below the [international poverty line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_threshold)), a decrease from 2022. In April, [India](14533.md) surpassed [China](5405.md) as the most populated country in the world.[^cite_note-303]
