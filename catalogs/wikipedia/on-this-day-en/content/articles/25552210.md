**Rio Bonito do Iguaçu** is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Brazil) in the [state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_of_Brazil) of [Paraná](229411.md) in the [Southern Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Region,_Brazil) of Brazil.[^cite_note-IBGE_DTB_2008-2][^cite_note-IBGE_Pop_2009-3][^cite_note-PNUD_IDH_2000-4][^cite_note-IBGE_PIB_2005-5]

## History

*Rio Bonito* means *nice river* and *Iguaçu* in [Tupi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tupi_language) means *big water*: *y* (water) and *gûasu* (*big*).[^cite_note-6]

### 2025 tornado

Main article: [2025 Rio Bonito do Iguaçu tornado](81549673.md)

On [November 7, 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Southern_Brazil_tornado_outbreak), an [F4](165206.md) [tornado](37530.md) with winds exceeding 300 km/h impacted Rio Bonito do Iguaçu, leaving a trail of destruction, six confirmed deaths, and dozens injured.[^cite_note-G1-7][^cite_note-8] Previously rated as an [F3](165206.md), however, experts from [PREVOTS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PREVOTS), Simepar, and MetSul Meteorologia, rated the tornado as an F4 a few days later.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]
