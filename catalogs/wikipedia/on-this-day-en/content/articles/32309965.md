The **4th Division** (第4師団) is one of nine active [divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_\(military\)) of the [Japan Ground Self-Defense Force](2978818.md). The division is subordinated to the [Western Army](32283031.md) and is headquartered in [Kasuga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasuga,_Fukuoka), [Fukuoka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fukuoka_Prefecture). Its responsibility is the defense of the [Fukuoka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fukuoka_Prefecture), [Nagasaki](221380.md), [Ōita](221919.md) and [Saga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saga_Prefecture) prefectures.[^cite_note-1]

The division was established on 15 August 1962.

    , in [Ōmura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%8Cmura,_Nagasaki), with [Type 96 armored personnel carriers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_96_armored_personnel_carrier)

  - 40th Infantry Regiment, in [Kitakyūshū](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kitaky%C5%ABsh%C5%AB)

  - 41st Infantry Regiment, in [Beppu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beppu,_%C5%8Cita)

  - [Tsushima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsushima_Island) Security Unit, in [Tsushima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsushima,_Nagasaki), with 1 × headquarters, 1 × infantry, and 1 × logistic support company

  - 4th Reconnaissance Combat Battalion, in Kasuga, with [Type 16 maneuver combat vehicles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_16_maneuver_combat_vehicle), and [Type 87 armored reconnaissance vehicles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_87_ARV)

  - 4th Anti-Aircraft Artillery Battalion, in Kurume, with [Type 81](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_81_surface-to-air_missile) and [Type 93](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_93_surface-to-air_missile) [surface-to-air missile](182664.md) systems

  - 4th Engineer Battalion (Combat), in Ōmura

  - 4th Signal Battalion, in Kasuga

  - 4th Aviation Squadron, in [Yoshinogari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoshinogari,_Saga), flying [UH-1J](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_UH-1_Iroquois) and [OH-6D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hughes_OH-6_Cayuse) helicopters

  - 4th [NBC-defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weapon_of_mass_destruction) Company, in Kasuga

  - 4th Logistic Support Regiment, in Kasuga&#x20;

    - 1st Maintenance Battalion

    - 2nd Maintenance Battalion

    - Supply Company

    - Medical Company

    - Transport Company
