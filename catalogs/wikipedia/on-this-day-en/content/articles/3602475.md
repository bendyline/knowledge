# FIFA World Cup records and statistics

See also: [National team appearances in the FIFA World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_team_appearances_in_the_FIFA_World_Cup)

&#x20;As of the [2026 edition](36581929.md), 84 national teams have competed at the finals of the men's [FIFA World Cup](11370.md).[^cite_note-1] [Brazil](149286.md) is the only team to have appeared in all 23 tournaments to date, with [Germany](250204.md) having participated in 21, [Argentina](454699.md) in 19, and [Italy](362466.md) and [Mexico](808402.md) both in 18.[^cite_note-participations-2] Eight nations have won the tournament. The inaugural winners in 1930 were [Uruguay](679782.md); the current champions are [Spain](609825.md). The most successful nation is Brazil, which has won the cup on five occasions.[^cite_note-3] Five teams have appeared in [FIFA World Cup finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_FIFA_World_Cup_finals) without winning,[^cite_note-4] while twelve more have appeared in the semi-finals.[^cite_note-5]

### Overall team records

The system used in the World Cup up to 1990 was 2 points for a win. In this ranking [3 points are awarded for a win](4960100.md), 1 for a draw, and 0 for a loss. Per statistical convention in football, matches decided in [extra time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extra_time_\(association_football\)) are counted as wins and losses, while matches decided by [penalty shoot-outs](511302.md) are counted as draws. Teams are ranked by total points, then by goal difference, then by goals scored.[^cite_note-all_time-41][^cite_note-42]

[^cite_note-167]: &#x20;The following statistics apply **[Opta's assist criteria](1167721.md#Opta_criteria)** to every FIFA World Cup match from 1930 onward.\
    Under Opta's criteria:<sup>[\[134\]](#cite_note-166)</sup> Assists are **not awarded** when the ball is significantly deflected or rebounded by an opposing player, altering its trajectory before reaching the goalscorer. Assists are **not awarded** for penalties, direct goals from corners or free kicks, or own goals. While [Opta Analyst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opta_Sports)'s published World Cup assist records cover all tournaments from 1966 onward, these shown statistics retrospectively apply the same criteria to earlier editions, from 1930–1962, using available match footage. Where video evidence is unavailable, assists are assessed and verified from contemporary newspaper reports and detailed descriptions of the goals. Consequently, these figures may differ from those published by other sources that do not retrospectively apply Opta's criteria to pre-1966 tournaments and from Opta itself, as they treat all pre-1966 assists as non-existent in their records and statistics.
