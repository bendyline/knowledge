On 7 April, 2026, three gunmen affiliated with the [Islamic State](9087364.md)[^cite_note-2] attacked the [Israeli Consulate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Consulate) in [Istanbul](3391396.md), Turkey. One attacker was killed while two other gunmen and two police officers sustained injuries.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] Both Turkey and [Israel](9282173.md) declared the attack an [act of terrorism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrorism_in_Turkey).[^cite_note-AJ-5]

## Background

The attack took place during the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), causing high tensions all around the Middle East and the European continent.[^cite_note-6] The conflict has strained [Israel–Turkey relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel%E2%80%93Turkey_relations).[^cite_note-\:1-7]

The three gunmen arrived in Istanbul via a rental car from [İzmit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%B0zmit), around 100 kilometres east of Istanbul.[^cite_note-\:0-8]

## The attack

On 7 April, 2026, at 12\:15 pm ([UTC+3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_Turkey)),[^cite_note-\:0-8] three perpetrators opened fire next to the [Israeli Consulate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Consulate) building in Istanbul. At least two dozens shots were heard in less than a minute. The consulate was not staffed at the time of the shooting.[^cite_note-BBC-1] One of the attackers was killed by a [police officer](20141676.md). Two perpetrators were injured and captured, and two police officers were lightly injured.[^cite_note-9] Both officers were transported to hospital in non-life threatening condition.[^cite_note-BBC-1]

## Investigation

Turkey police sealed off the area and the incident was being investigated as an terrorist attack.[^cite_note-10] Turkish interior minister [Mustafa Çiftçi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mustafa_%C3%87ift%C3%A7i) identified the deceased gunman as Yunus E.S., and the injured being Onur Ç and Enes Ç, who are brothers. He claimed Yunus E.S. belonged to a group connected to "an organisation that exploits religion". [Turkish media](2945560.md) speculated that this might be the [Islamic State](9087364.md), although no group had claimed responsibility for it at the time.[^cite_note-BBC-1] Nine people were detained in connection with the attack.[^cite_note-11]

In a nationwide operation Turkey arrested nearly 200 Islamic State suspects.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

## Reactions

The [Israeli Ministry of Foreign Affairs](18133296.md) condemned the attack, calling it an act of [Islamic terrorism](566821.md), and thanked the Turkish security forces' quick action.[^cite_note-BBC-1]

[United States Ambassador to Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ambassadors_of_the_United_States_to_Turkey) [Tom Barrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Barrack) condemned the attack, calling it an "attack on international order" and commending the Turkish security forces for their response.[^cite_note-14]

Turkish president [Recep Tayyip Erdoğan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recep_Tayyip_Erdo%C4%9Fan) called it a "heinous terrorist attack" and said it would not affect or damage Turkey's trust and security.[^cite_note-\:1-7]
