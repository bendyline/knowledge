**Napo** (Spanish pronunciation: [\[ˈnapo\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish)) is a [province](576127.md) of [Ecuador](9334.md). Its capital is [Tena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tena,_Ecuador). The province is named after the [Napo River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Napo_River). The province is not well developed and does not have much industrial presence. The thick [rainforest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainforest) is home to many natives that remain isolated by preference, descendants of those who fled the Spanish invasion in the Andes, and the Incas years before. In 2000, the province was the sole remaining majority-indigenous province of Ecuador, with 56.3% of the province either claiming indigenous identity or speaking an indigenous language.[^cite_note-3]

This province is one of the many located in Ecuador's section of the [Amazon rainforest](48139.md).

In Napo province are also [Antisana Ecological Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antisana_Ecological_Reserve), [Sumaco Napo-Galeras National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sumaco_Napo-Galeras_National_Park), and [Limoncocha National Biological Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limoncocha_National_Biological_Reserve).

## Demographics

Ethnic groups as of the [Ecuadorian census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuadorian_census) of 2010:[^cite_note-4]

- [Indigenous](18429324.md) 56.8%

- [Mestizo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mestizo) 38.1%

- [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Latin_American) 2.7%

- [Afro-Ecuadorian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afro-Ecuadorian) 1.6%

- [Montubio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montubio) 0.6%

- Other 0.2%
