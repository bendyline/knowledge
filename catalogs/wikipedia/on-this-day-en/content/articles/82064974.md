**Ashrafiyah** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الأشرفية) also spelled **Ashrafieh**, is a neighborhood in northeastern [Aleppo](159244.md) in northern [Syria](7515849.md). In 2025, it had an estimated population of 400,000 (including the bordering neighborhood of [Sheikh Maqsoud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikh_Maqsoud)).[^cite_note-reliefweb-1] Bordering Ashrafiyah are Sheikh Maqsoud from the northeast, Bani Zaid from the northwest, Al Zuhour, As Sabil, and As Siryan from the south.

## History

### Fall of the Assad regime

Following the [fall of the Assad Regime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fall_of_the_Assad_Regime) in 2024 and the [Second Battle of Aleppo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Aleppo_\(2024\)), most of Aleppo came under the control of the [Syrian Transitional Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_Transitional_Government). However, the neighbourhoods of Ashrafieh and [Sheikh Maqsoud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikh_Maqsoud) remained under the control of the [Syrian Democratic Forces](48205248.md). Kurdish control over Ashrafieh was later formalized through agreements between the SDF and Syrian transitional government in December 2024 and April 2025. These deals specified that only the SDF-affiliated [Asayish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asayish_\(Democratic_Autonomous_Administration_of_North_and_East_Syria\)) would remain in Ashrafieh.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

### SDF–STG conflict

See also: [SDF–Syrian Transitional Government clashes (2025–present)](81146732.md) and [Aleppo clashes (2025–2026)](82109129.md)

On 8 January 2026, the [Syrian Army](3423927.md) captured the majority of the Ashrafieh neighborhood after three days of fighting.[^cite_note-4] By 10 January the SDF had completely withdrawn from Aleppo, including Ashrafieh.[^cite_note-5]

## Demographics

Ashrafiyah has a diverse demographic makeup, with [Kurds](17068.md) and [Arabs](2185.md) making up the majority and smaller communities of [Syriacs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syriac_people), [Armenians](387816.md), and [Mardellis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mhallami). The religious makeup includes [Muslims](19541.md), [Christians](18337522.md), and [Yazidis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yazidis).[^cite_note-reliefweb-1]
