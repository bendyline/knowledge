A **human chain** is a form of [demonstration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demonstration_\(people\)) in which people link arms or hands as a show of political [solidarity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solidarity).

&#x20;The chains can involve thousands of people, with the world record being claimed in 2020 by [Bihar](442482.md), India, which was estimated to include 51.7 million people across 18,000 kilometres (11,000 mi), to support the government's efforts towards environment conservation and eradication of social evils.[^cite_note-1]
