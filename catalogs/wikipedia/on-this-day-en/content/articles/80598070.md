Legislative elections were held in [Vietnam](202354.md) on 15 March 2026.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] Early elections were held in the [islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_islands_of_Vietnam) of the [South Central Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Central_Coast), [Mekong Delta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mekong_Delta) and [Southeast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southeast_\(Vietnam\)) regions from 26 February 2026.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

This election has elected the [16th National Assembly of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/16th_National_Assembly_of_Vietnam), which would eventually appoint [Trần Thanh Mẫn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tr%E1%BA%A7n_Thanh_M%E1%BA%ABn) as the [National Assembly Chairman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairman_of_the_National_Assembly_of_Vietnam), [Tô Lâm](54878346.md) as [President](466686.md), [Lê Minh Hưng](56144048.md) as the [Prime Minister](439887.md) alongside [his cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_L%C3%AA_Minh_H%C6%B0ng), constituting the Vietnamese 2026–2031 administration.&#x20;

The results were announced on 21 March 2026, paving the way for the 16th National Assembly's inaugural session, which convened on 6 April 2026.[^cite_note-9]

## Electoral system

The members of the [National Assembly](4420212.md) are elected from 184 multi-member constituencies using the two-round system, with a maximum number of 500 candidates to be elected. [Block voting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Block_voting) is used, with each district having two or three seats.[^cite_note-10] Candidates have to receive at least 50% of the vote in the first round to be elected, with a second round held on a plurality basis.[^cite_note-\:0-11]

## Background

All participating candidates and elected members are expected to be nominal members of the [Vietnamese Fatherland Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnamese_Fatherland_Front). Meanwhile, for the ruling [Communist Party of Vietnam](400546.md) (CPV), the incumbent [General Secretary of the Central Committee](7482670.md) was [Tô Lâm](54878346.md). After multiple rumors and speculations, the [14th National Congress of the Communist Party of Vietnam](82002075.md) ultimately confirmed Tô Lâm to stay as the Party General Secretary for the next term, alongside the new [Central Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/14th_Central_Committee_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Vietnam) and [Politburo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/14th_Politburo_of_the_Communist_Party_of_Vietnam), effectively platforming the Vietnamese political orientation for the 2026–2031 term.[^cite_note-12]

## Candidates

A total of 864 candidates from 182 [constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_district) contested for 500 seats in the election. Of these, nearly 93% were members of the CPV, while 7.5% were independents;[^cite_note-13] five candidates were self-nominated.[^cite_note-14]

## Response

On 19 March 2026, independent journalist [Nguyễn Hoàng Vi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_Ho%C3%A0ng_Vi) was arrested, detained and beaten by police officers after posting critical comments about the legitimacy of the election on social media.[^cite_note-\:02-15]
