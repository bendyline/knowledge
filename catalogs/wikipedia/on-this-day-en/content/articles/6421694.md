**General Treviño** is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Mexico) in the Mexican state of [Nuevo León](222247.md).[^cite_note-4]



Butchers in General Treviño, 2020

General Treviño originated from a ranch named “El Puntiagudo” that was given to Don Juan Baptista Chapa on 14 January 1688. Don Francisco Chapa made the foundation in 1705. Its erection in the villa was verified on 9 December 1868, being Governor of the State [Nuevo León](222247.md), General [Jerónimo Treviño](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jer%C3%B3nimo_Trevi%C3%B1o); it belonged to the villas founded during the 19th century. The main reason for its foundation was agriculture.

General Treviño is located northeast of the state of [Nuevo León](222247.md), in the region called “Gran Llanura de Norte America”, in the zone denominated “Fidenor”, located in the coordinates 26º28' of west longitude. The municipality has an elevation of 188 metres (617 ft) above sealevel. General Trevino borders to the North and the West with [Agualeguas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agualeguas), Nuevo León and [Ciudad Mier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ciudad_Mier), [Tamaulipas](222254.md); to the South with [Melchor Ocampo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melchor_Ocampo) and to the East with [Los Aldamas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Aldamas).

The traditional foods of General Trevino include the [Elote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elote) [tamale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamale), the “[carne seca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carne_seca)” and the “[cabrito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabrito)”. The typical candy is made of “fríjol” (bean).

General Treviño contains 1,808 inhabitants as of the 2020 census. There are 49 localities in the municipality, of which the most important are: San Javier, Rancho El Faro, Buena Vista and Los Maldonado.
