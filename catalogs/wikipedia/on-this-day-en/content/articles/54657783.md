**Ginza Six** is a luxury shopping complex located in the [Ginza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ginza) area of Tokyo, jointly developed by [Mori Building Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mori_Building_Company), [J. Front Retailing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._Front_Retailing), [Sumitomo Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sumitomo_Corporation) and [L Catterton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L_Catterton) Real Estate.[^cite_note-Homma-2][^cite_note-LeMonde-3] The name Ginza Six or **G Six** reflects the building address in [Ginza 6-chome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_addressing_system) as well as the desire to provide an exceptional "six-star" shopping experience.[^cite_note-Homma-2]

## History

Ginza Six was built on the location of the former [Matsuzakaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matsuzakaya) department store, which was Ginza's first ever department store.[^cite_note-Homma-2] The complex was inaugurated on 17 April, 2017, in a ceremony attended by Prime Minister of Japan [Shinzo Abe](2634434.md), Governor of Tokyo [Yuriko Koike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuriko_Koike), Chairman of LVMH [Bernard Arnault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernard_Arnault), and President of J. Front Retailing [Ryoichi Yamamoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryoichi_Yamamoto?action=edit\&redlink=1), among others.[^cite_note-LVMH-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-JTimes-6] It is the largest retail space in Ginza.[^cite_note-Homma-2]

### Incidents

On May 25, 2026, tear gas was reportedly used at the lobby of Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation, affecting 25 persons with reports of severe headaches, sore throats, and coughing.[^cite_note-7]

## Architecture

The building has space for up to 241 stores, including flagship facilities for [Fendi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fendi), [Kenzo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenzo_\(brand\)), [Vivienne Westwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vivienne_Westwood), [Alexander McQueen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_McQueen), [Yves Saint Laurent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yves_Saint_Laurent_\(brand\)) and [Van Cleef & Arpels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Van_Cleef_&_Arpels).[^cite_note-LeMonde-3] It also contains six floors of office space (floors 7—12), 24 restaurants and cafes, a banquet hall, a 480-seater [Noh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noh) theater and a 4,000 square-meter [rooftop garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roof_garden).[^cite_note-Homma-2][^cite_note-JTimes-6][^cite_note-Lonely-8] A terminal for tourist buses, a tourist information center, currency exchanges and duty exemption services cater to tourists.[^cite_note-JTimes-6]

Ginza Six has an art program run by [Fumio Nanjo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fumio_Nanjo) of the [Mori Art Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mori_Art_Museum). The complex focuses on contemporary Japanese art, in an attempt to "sp\[eak] to the creativity associated with modern-day Japan rather than the traditional Japanese aesthetic."[^cite_note-9] The Central atrium artworks are a symbol of GINZA SIX and the inaugural exhibits included works by [Yayoi Kusama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yayoi_Kusama) and [Patrick Blanc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Blanc), among others.[^cite_note-G6Art-10]
