**Nenad Vujić** ([Serbian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_language): Ненад Вујић; born 26 October 1966) is a Serbian politician serving as [minister of justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Justice_\(Serbia\)) since 2025.[^cite_note-1] From 2010 to 2025, he served as director of the Judicial Academy.[^cite_note-2]
