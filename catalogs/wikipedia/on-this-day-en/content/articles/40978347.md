**Gir Somnath district** is a [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_India) of [Gujarat](53707.md), India. Gir somnath district is located on the southern corner of the [Kathiawar peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathiawar) with its headquarters at the city of [Somnath](4029615.md).&#x20;

Gir Somnath was split from [Junagadh district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junagadh_district) in August 2013, when seven new districts came into existence in Gujarat.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-DG-01-3]&#x20;

[Veraval](4029615.md), [Talala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talala,_Gujarat), [Sutrapada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sutrapada), [Kodinar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kodinar), [Una](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Una,_Gujarat) and [Gir-Gadhada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gir_Gadhada_Taluka) are the talukas of Gir Somnath.[^cite_note-profile-4]&#x20;

The [Gir Forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gir_Forest) is a home of many wildlife creatures including lions, deer, and monkeys. [Asiatic Lions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asiatic_Lions) can be only be found in the Gir Forest.[^cite_note-profile-4]&#x20;

## Demographics

At the time of the 2011 census, Gir-Somnath district has a population of 1,217,477, of which 333,009 (27.35%) lived in urban areas. Gir-Somnath had a sex ratio of 964 females per 1000 males. [Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scheduled_Castes_and_Scheduled_Tribes) made up 113,822 (9.35%) and 17,761 (1.46%) of the population respectively.[^cite_note-census-5]&#x20;
