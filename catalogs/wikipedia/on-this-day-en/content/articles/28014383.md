**Dilovası** is a municipality and [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Turkey) of [Kocaeli Province](499408.md), [Turkey](11125639.md).[^cite_note-2] Its area is 125 km<sup>2</sup>,[^cite_note-3] and its population is 53,416 (2022).[^cite_note-tuik-1] The district Dilovası was created in 2008 from part of the district of [Gebze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gebze), along with the districts [Darıca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dar%C4%B1ca) and [Çayırova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%87ay%C4%B1rova,_Kocaeli).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] The mayor is Hamza Şayir ([AKP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justice_and_Development_Party_\(Turkey\))).
