Runner-up Alexander Zverev and the winner Jannik Sinner receiving the trophy after the final

Defending champion [Jannik Sinner](60061043.md)[^cite_note-1] defeated [Alexander Zverev](39755766.md) in the final, 6–7<sup>(7–9)</sup>, 7–6<sup>(7–2)</sup>, 6–3, 6–4 to win the gentlemen's singles tennis title at the [2026 Wimbledon Championships](80434323.md).[^cite_note-2] It was his second [Wimbledon](91195.md) title and fifth [major](197638.md#Tournaments) title overall.[^cite_note-3] Zverev was the oldest man in the [Open Era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Era) to reach the finals of all four majors for the first time, at 29 years, 81 days old.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-WimbyFinal26-6]

With his first-round victory, [Novak Djokovic](16100029.md) continued a 21-year-long streak of having never lost in the opening round of Wimbledon, and became the first player in the Open Era to win more than 80 opening-round matches at the majors.[^cite_note-7] During the tournament, he surpassed [Roger Federer](262376.md)'s record of Wimbledon men's singles match wins, reaching 107.[^cite_note-8] The quarterfinal match between Djokovic and [Félix Auger-Aliassime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F%C3%A9lix_Auger-Aliassime) was the longest quarterfinal in tournament history, at 5 hours and 15 minutes long.[^cite_note-9] At 39 years, 48 days old, Djokovic was the second-oldest Wimbledon semifinalist in the Open Era, only behind [Ken Rosewall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Rosewall) in [1974](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1974_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles) (aged 39 years, 243 days).[^cite_note-10]

[Arthur Fery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Fery) was the second [wildcard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_card_\(tennis\)) to reach the Wimbledon semifinals in the Open Era, after [Goran Ivanišević](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goran_Ivani%C5%A1evi%C4%87) in [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles),[^cite_note-WimbyFinal26-6][^cite_note-11] the first unseeded British player at Wimbledon and the first British male wildcard at any major to do so in the Open Era.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] Aged 36 years, 71 days, [Jan-Lennard Struff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan-Lennard_Struff) became the oldest first-time major quarterfinalist in the Open Era.[^cite_note-14]
