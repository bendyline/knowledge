# 1500 metres freestyle

&#x20;The **1500 metres freestyle** is an event in [competitive swimming](22425760.md). World records are ratified by [World Aquatics](317029.md) (formerly FINA). The current long course world record holders are Germany's [Johannes Liebmann](83967922.md) and the US' [Katie Ledecky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Ledecky), while the current short course world record holders are Germany's [Florian Wellbrock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florian_Wellbrock), and Ledecky.

The men's event was first contested at the [Olympic Games](22576.md) in 1908, while the women's event was introduced in 2020; the current Olympic champions are the US' [Bobby Finke](54696773.md) and Ledecky. The men's event has been contested at the [World Aquatics Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Aquatics_Championships) since the inaugural championships in 1973, while the women's event has been contested since 2001; the current world champions (long course) are Tunisia's [Ahmed Jaouadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmed_Jaouadi), and Ledecky. The men's event has been contested at the [World Aquatics Swimming Championships (25m)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Aquatics_Swimming_Championships_\(25m\)) since the inaugural championships in 1993, while the women's event has been contested since 2022; the current world champions (short course) are Jaouadi, and Germany's [Isabel Gose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isabel_Gose).
