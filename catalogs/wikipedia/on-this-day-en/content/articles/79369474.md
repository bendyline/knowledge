The **98th Academy Awards** ceremony, presented by the [Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Motion_Picture_Arts_and_Sciences) (AMPAS), took place on March 15, 2026, at the [Dolby Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolby_Theatre) in [Hollywood, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood,_Los_Angeles). During the gala, the AMPAS presented [Academy Awards](324.md) (commonly referred to as Oscars) in 24 categories honoring [films released in 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_in_film). The ceremony, televised in the United States by [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company) and streamed on [Hulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hulu), was produced by Raj Kapoor and Katy Mullan, and was directed by [Hamish Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamish_Hamilton_\(director\)).[^cite_note-4] Comedian [Conan O'Brien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conan_O'Brien) hosted the show for the second consecutive year.[^cite_note-5]

In related events, the Academy held its 16th [Governors Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governors_Awards) ceremony at the Ray Dolby Ballroom of the [Ovation Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ovation_Hollywood) complex in Hollywood on November 16, 2025.[^cite_note-Governors_Awards-6] The [Academy Scientific and Technical Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Scientific_and_Technical_Award) were presented by host [Sofia Carson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_Carson) on April 28, 2026, in a ceremony at the [Academy Museum of Motion Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Museum_of_Motion_Pictures) in Los Angeles.[^cite_note-7]

*[One Battle After Another](73258137.md)* won the most awards with six, including [Best Picture](61702.md) and the inaugural award for [Best Casting](81860114.md).[^cite_note-Guardian_Oscar_winners_list-8] *[Sinners](75895455.md)* received a record sixteen nominations and won four awards. Other winners included *[Frankenstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankenstein_\(2025_film\))* with three; *[KPop Demon Hunters](67104864.md)* with two; and *[All the Empty Rooms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_the_Empty_Rooms)*, *[Avatar: Fire and Ash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatar:_Fire_and_Ash)*, *[F1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F1_\(film\))*, *[The Girl Who Cried Pearls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Girl_Who_Cried_Pearls)*, *[Hamnet](73465374.md)*, *[Mr Nobody Against Putin](79060547.md)*, *[Sentimental Value](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sentimental_Value)*, *[The Singers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Singers)*, *[Two People Exchanging Saliva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two_People_Exchanging_Saliva)*, and *[Weapons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weapons_\(2025_film\))* with one each.[^cite_note-Guardian_Oscar_winners_list-8] The telecast drew 17.86 million viewers in the United States.

## Winners and nominees

The nominees for the 98th Academy Awards were announced on January 22, 2026, at the [Samuel Goldwyn Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Goldwyn_Theater) in [Beverly Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beverly_Hills), by actress [Danielle Brooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danielle_Brooks) and actor [Lewis Pullman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Pullman).[^cite_note-LA_Times_Oscar_nominees_list_2026-9] *Sinners* led with sixteen nominations, the most in Oscar history; *One Battle After Another* came in second with thirteen.[^cite_note-10] The winners were announced during the awards ceremony on March 15.[^cite_note-11]

[Michael B. Jordan](2310146.md) became the second actor to win an Oscar for a [dual role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_role) after [Lee Marvin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Marvin) for *[Cat Ballou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cat_Ballou)* (1965).[^cite_note-12] At the age of 30, [Timothée Chalamet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timoth%C3%A9e_Chalamet) became the youngest actor to earn three acting nominations since [Marlon Brando](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marlon_Brando), and also became the youngest person to be nominated for both acting and producing in the same year.[^cite_note-13] The 40-year span between [Amy Madigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Madigan)'s first nomination, for her supporting role in *[Twice in a Lifetime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twice_in_a_Lifetime_\(film\))* (1985), and her recent nomination for *Weapons*, set the record for the longest gap between Oscar nominations for an actress.[^cite_note-14] With her fifth [Best Costume Design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Costume_Design) nomination, [Ruth E. Carter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruth_E._Carter) became the most-nominated Black woman in Oscar history across all categories.[^cite_note-15] Best Cinematography winner [Autumn Durald Arkapaw](42818757.md) became the first female and Black winner in the category as well as the first Filipino winner; she was also the first woman of color to be nominated in the category.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] The joint win by *[The Singers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Singers)* and *[Two People Exchanging Saliva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two_People_Exchanging_Saliva)* for Best Live Action Short Film marked the first occurrence of a tie since the [85th ceremony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/85th_Academy_Awards) in 2013, and the seventh tie overall.[^cite_note-18]

### Governors Awards

The Academy held its 16th annual [Governors Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governors_Awards) ceremony on November 16, 2025, during which the following awards were presented:[^cite_note-Governors_Awards-6]

#### Academy Honorary Awards

- [Debbie Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debbie_Allen) – "A trailblazing choreographer and actor, whose work has captivated generations and crossed genres".

  [^cite_note-Honorary-20]

- [Tom Cruise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Cruise) – For his "incredible commitment to \[the] filmmaking community, to the theatrical experience, and to the stunts community".

  [^cite_note-Honorary-20]

- [Wynn Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wynn_Thomas) – "Production designer \[who] has brought some of the most enduring films to life through a visionary eye and mastery of his craft".

  [^cite_note-Honorary-20]

#### Jean Hersholt Humanitarian Award

- [Dolly Parton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolly_Parton) – A "beloved performer \[who] exemplifies the spirit of the [Jean Hersholt Humanitarian Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Hersholt_Humanitarian_Award) through her unwavering dedication to charitable efforts".

  [^cite_note-Honorary-20]

## Ceremony information

Photo of Conan O'Brien in January 2025.

[Conan O'Brien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conan_O'Brien) hosted the 98th Academy Awards.

In March 2025, the Academy hired television producers Raj Kapoor and Katy Mullan, for the third year in a row, to oversee production of the 2026 ceremony, with comedian and former talk show host [Conan O'Brien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conan_O'Brien) as host of the event for the second consecutive year. Jeff Ross and Mike Sweeney, long-time collaborators of O'Brien, also returned as producers for a second time; Sweeney also served as a writer.[^cite_note-29] "We are thrilled to bring back Conan, Raj, Katy, Jeff, and Mike for the 98th Oscars," stated AMPAS CEO [Bill Kramer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Kramer_\(executive\)) and then-AMPAS president [Janet Yang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janet_Yang) in a press release. "\[Last] year, they produced a hugely entertaining and visually stunning show that celebrated our nominees and the global film community in the most beautiful and impactful way. Conan was the perfect host — skillfully guiding us through the evening with humor, warmth and reverence. It is an honor to be working with them again."[^cite_note-30] In response, Kapoor and Mullan stated in a press release, "We are both so honored to be returning in our roles for the 98th Oscars. We can't wait to work with Conan and his entire team as we continue to explore even more special and heartfelt opportunities to celebrate next year's nominees and the impact of film around the world."[^cite_note-31]

Several others participated in the production of the ceremony and related events. Musician Michael Bearden was the musical director for the telecast; production designers Alana Billingsley and Misty Buckley designed a new stage for the show.[^cite_note-Team-32][^cite_note-33] According to Buckley and Billingsley in an article published by *[Vanity Fair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanity_Fair_\(magazine\))*, the stage was designed to resemble a garden courtyard made out of glass, metal, and stone adorned with handmade trees and plants. The stage was also equipped with moving components, including mechanized LED panels that could move around the stage and reveal new spaces.[^cite_note-34] Comedian and journalist [Amelia Dimoldenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amelia_Dimoldenberg) served as the social media and red carpet correspondent for the Oscars' online outlets.[^cite_note-35] Choreographer Mandy Moore oversaw performances of the Best Original Song nominees "[Golden](80331847.md)" from *[KPop Demon Hunters](67104864.md)* and "[I Lied to You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Lied_to_You)" from *Sinners*; the latter featured cameos from [Buddy Guy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddy_Guy), [Shaboozey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaboozey), [Brittany Howard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brittany_Howard), [Christone "Kingfish" Ingram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christone_%22Kingfish%22_Ingram), [Bobby Rush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobby_Rush_\(musician\)), Alice Smith, [Eric Gales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Gales), and [Misty Copeland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Misty_Copeland).[^cite_note-36] [Josh Groban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Groban), [Jane Lynch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane_Lynch), [Sterling K. Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterling_K._Brown), [Ashley Nicole Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashley_Nicole_Black), and [Jim Downey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Downey_\(comedian\)) appeared in scripted [sketches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sketch_comedy), the latter reprising his role from *One Battle After Another*.[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-Fienberg-38]

### Introduction of Best Casting award and other rule changes

Beginning with this ceremony, AMPAS introduced a new competitive award that would honor [casting directors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casting_\(performing_arts\)#Casting_director). The award was announced in a February 2024 press release with Academy CEO Kramer and then-AMPAS president Yang stating, "Casting directors play an essential role in filmmaking, and as the Academy evolves, we are proud to add casting to the disciplines that we recognize and celebrate."[^cite_note-Casting-39] Nominees would be initially voted in a preliminary round to determine a shortlist consisting of ten films. Each of the shortlisted films would have its respective casting director showcase their work in a shortlist presentation utilizing written notes highlighting the process, film reel clips, and a Q\&A with members of the Casting branch. The members in that branch would narrow down the roster to five nominees, and the entire membership would then vote for the winner.[^cite_note-40] A category to award achievement in casting had previously been rejected by the Academy in 1999.[^cite_note-41]

Additional rule changes approved by the AMPAS Board of Governors in April 2025 included:[^cite_note-42][^cite_note-43]

- Voters must now watch each of the nominated films in each category in order to vote for the winners of said category. Academy members could fulfill this requirement by watching the nominees on the Academy's streaming platform or uploading a ticket stub or any form of documentation stating that the member watched the film.

- In accordance with recommendations by the Academy’s Science and Technology Council regarding usage of Generative Artificial Intelligence in the filmmaking process, the group determined that the tools neither helped nor harmed a film's chances for receiving a nomination. The Academy and its branches would judge each achievement "taking into account the degree to which a human was at the heart of the creative authorship when choosing which movie to award."

- In the Best Picture category, films released in the first six months of the year must have shown proof of submission for [Producers Guild of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Producers_Guild_of_America) (PGA) mark certification or awards-only determination by September 10, 2025; films released in the remaining months of the year must submit proof of submission for the PGA by November 13.

- For Best Animated Short Film, all Academy members were invited to participate in the nominations round.

- The Best Cinematography category would first have a preliminary short list featured ten to twenty films which would later be narrowed down to five nominees.

- For International Feature Film, eligibility rules regarding creative control now were updated in consideration of filmmakers with refugee or asylum status. The submitting country must confirm that creative control of the film was largely in the hands of citizens, residents, or individuals with refugee or asylum status in said submitting country.

### Box office performance of Best Picture nominees

When the nominations were announced, eight of the ten films nominated for Best Picture had earned a combined gross of nearly \$661.7 million at the American and Canadian box offices at the time. *Sinners* was the highest-grossing film among the Best Picture nominees with \$280 million in domestic box office receipts.[^cite_note-44] *F1* came in second with \$189.6 million; this was followed by *Marty Supreme* (\$80.6 million), *One Battle After Another* (\$71.6 million), *Bugonia* (\$17.7 million), *Hamnet* (\$15.3 million), *Sentimental Value* (\$4.3 million), and *The Secret Agent* (\$2.6 million). The box office figures for *Frankenstein* and *Train Dreams* were unavailable due to distributor Netflix's policy of refusing to release such figures.[^cite_note-45]

### Critical reviews

Robert Lloyd of the *[Los Angeles Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Times)* wrote, "With Conan O'Brien returning as host in 2026, the ceremony was much in the spirit of his first go in 2025, except this was all in all a livelier, funnier show." He also gave high marks toward the Best Original Song performances and the presentation of the "In Memoriam" segment.[^cite_note-46] [Rob Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Sheffield) of *[Rolling Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone)* complimented the "surprise wins, timely jokes, poignant speeches", host O'Brien's "right amount of bratty irreverence" and the Redford tribute by Streisand, describing it as "the kind of Oscar magic that could only happen on this award show".[^cite_note-47] Ben Travers of *[IndieWire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IndieWire)* opined that the show "felt like the first in a long time that were made with cinephiles top of mind," adding that O'Brien "brought his signature silliness and deft professionalism to a ceremony that thrived by giving film fans what they want".[^cite_note-48]

*[Variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_\(magazine\))* chief film critic [Owen Gleiberman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owen_Gleiberman) called the show "a tasteful and overly safe show sustained by just enough suspense". He described the production design as "pleasingly bland and comfortable and a bit generic, like the show itself" and criticized the lack of a "more explicit salute" to Best Picture winner *One Battle After Another*.[^cite_note-49] Television critic Daniel Fienberg of *The Hollywood Reporter* complimented O'Brien's hosting and "stirring history-making wins," but pointed out the technical issues.[^cite_note-Fienberg-38] Television critic Judy Berman of *[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_\(magazine\))* criticized prioritizing "devastating news headlines or late-night-style topical zingers or impassioned hand-wringing of any variety" over "a celebration of film artistry do what art does better than almost anything else". However, she did reserve praise for O'Brien's hosting performances and several of the winners' acceptance speeches.[^cite_note-50]

### Ratings and reception

The American telecast on ABC drew in an average of 17.86 million people over its length, which was a 9% decrease from the previous year's ceremony.[^cite_note-51] The show also had 8.4% of households watching the ceremony based on [Nielsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nielsen_Media_Research) estimates, which was lower than the previous ceremony.[^cite_note-PDF-3] Additionally, it garnered a lower 18–49 demo rating with a 3.92 rating among viewers in that demographic.[^cite_note-52]

In July 2026, the broadcast was nominated for six awards at the [78th Primetime Emmy Awards](81631496.md).[^cite_note-53]
