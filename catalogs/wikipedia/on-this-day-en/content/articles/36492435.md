**Liberty and Refoundation** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Libertad y Refundación*), also known as **Libre** (lit. 'Free'), is a [left-wing](18499.md)[^cite_note-nation-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] [political party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_party) in [Honduras](13394.md). Libre was founded in 2011 by the [National Popular Resistance Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Popular_Resistance_Front) (FNRP), a leftist coalition of organizations opposed to the [2009 coup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Honduran_coup_d'%C3%A9tat) against [Manuel Zelaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_Zelaya).[^cite_note-nation-8] The party has been led since its foundation by Zelaya and his wife [Xiomara Castro](6016595.md) who served as the president of Honduras from early 2022 to early 2026.

## History

[Xiomara Castro](6016595.md), the wife of former president [Manuel Zelaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_Zelaya), who was deposed in the 2009 coup, was the presidential candidate of the party in the [2013 presidential election](36492620.md); Zelaya was not allowed to run for a second term under the constitution. Castro took second place in the four-way race, receiving approximately 29 percent of the vote behind [Juan Orlando Hernández](25895048.md)'s 34 percent.[^cite_note-11]

At least eighteen Libre pre-candidates, candidates, family members, and campaign leaders were killed between June 2012 and October 2013.[^cite_note-12] Additionally, it is strongly [opposed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-capitalism) to [free market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_market) [capitalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitalism) and the [neo-liberal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neoliberalism#Criticism) economic model, and maintains a long-term goal of "establishing an alternative economic system."[^cite_note-\:0-5][^cite_note-\:1-6]

On 28 November 2021, Castro, presidential candidate of Liberty and Refoundation, won 53% of the votes in the [presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Honduran_general_election) to become the first female president of Honduras.[^cite_note-13] Castro stated during the campaign that she would promote [democratic socialism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_socialism) and ask the [National Congress](1604127.md) to draft a new constitution.[^cite_note-\:0-5] It also won 50 seats in the National Congress.

In the [2025 Honduran general election](77491476.md), the party lost the presidency under candidate [Rixi Moncada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rixi_Moncada), placing third. It also placed third in the National Congress.

## Factions

There are at least seven factions within Liberty and Refoundation.[^cite_note-14]

- **28 June Movement** (*Movimiento 28 de junio*)/ **M28** since 2017

- **People's Resistance Movement** (*Movimiento Resistencia Popular*, MRP) /**Renewal and New Alternatives Movement** (*Movimiento Renovación Nuevas Alternativas, MORENA*) since 2025

- **Organized People in Resistance** (*Pueblo Organizado en Resistencia*, POR)

- **People's Refoundation Force** (*Fuerza de Refundación Popular*, FRP)

- **5 July Movement** (*Movimiento 5 de julio*)

- **We are +** (*Somos+*)

- **New Movemment** (*Nueva Corriente*)
