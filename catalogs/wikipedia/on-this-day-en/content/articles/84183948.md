At 11\:25 [CEST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_European_Summer_Time) on 9 September 2026, a [PKP Intercity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PKP_Intercity) train derailed after a [concrete mixer lorry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete_mixer_truck) had collided into a carriage of the train at a [level crossing](193595.md) on the [Tomaszów Mazowiecki–Radom railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomasz%C3%B3w_Mazowiecki%E2%80%93Radom_railway) in [Sokolniki Suche](19901749.md), [Masovian Voivodeship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masovian_Voivodeship) in Poland.

At least 1 passenger was killed and 20 others sustained injuries.[^cite_note-2] 111 passengers and three crew members were onboard the train.[^cite_note-\:2-3]

## Background

The [Tomaszów Mazowiecki–Radom railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomasz%C3%B3w_Mazowiecki%E2%80%93Radom_railway) is an [electrified](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Railway_electrification) 87.2 km (54.2 mi) long railway line, connecting [Tomaszów Mazowiecki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomasz%C3%B3w_Mazowiecki_railway_station?action=edit\&redlink=1) (in the east) with [Radom Główny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radom_G%C5%82%C3%B3wny_railway_station) (in the west).[^cite_note-4] In this section were the accident took place, which was extensively modernised with new tracks in 2025, the line is [double-track](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double-track_railway); its maximum speed is 120 km/h (75 mph).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] The [level crossing](193595.md) is unguarded but contains working warning lights with [CCTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CCTV). The crossing is situated approximately 50 m (160 ft) north of [national road 12](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_road_12_\(Poland\)?action=edit\&redlink=1), between [Wieniawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wieniawa_railway_station?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Skrzynno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skrzynno_railway_stations?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-\:5-7]

## Accident

At 11\:25 [CEST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_European_Summer_Time) on 9 September 2026, the [PKP Intercity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PKP_Intercity) "Koziołek" train from [Lublin Główny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lublin_G%C5%82%C3%B3wny_railway_station) to [Szczecin Główny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Szczecin_G%C5%82%C3%B3wny_railway_station), [reporting mark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reporting_mark) 2820/1,[^cite_note-\:2-3] [derailed](1093599.md) after a [concrete mixer lorry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete_mixer_truck) had collided into the side[^cite_note-\:3-8] of the train's first carriage.[^cite_note-\:0-9][^cite_note-\:4-10] It occurred at an unguarded [level crossing](193595.md) with warning lights[^cite_note-\:6-11] in [Sokolniki Suche](19901749.md), between [Wieniawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wieniawa_railway_station?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Skrzynno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skrzynno_railway_stations?action=edit\&redlink=1) railway stations, on the [Tomaszów Mazowiecki–Radom railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomasz%C3%B3w_Mazowiecki%E2%80%93Radom_railway).[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-\:1-13][^cite_note-\:5-7] All six carriages hauled by a [PKP class EP09](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PKP_class_EP09)-046 [electric locomotive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_locomotive) derailed, including the locomotive which tipped onto its side to the adjacent track.[^cite_note-\:0-9]

The train was travelling 117 km/h (73 mph) upon derailment; the maximum line speed is 120 km/h (75 mph).[^cite_note-\:4-10] The driver of the lorry became trapped inside his vehicle and had to be freed by [firefighters](146303.md).[^cite_note-14]

## Casualties and responses

At least one female passenger, who was in the first carriage died, and another 20 were injured.[^cite_note-15] Seven of whom were hospitalised,[^cite_note-16] which included the train driver and two crew members, and the lorry driver; all of whom sustained serious injuries.[^cite_note-\:1-13] 30 [fire](146303.md) units, eight [ambulances](146317.md), and two [air ambulances](32993239.md) attended the scene.[^cite_note-\:2-3] 67 passengers[^cite_note-\:3-8] were transported to a [community centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Community_centre) in [Skrzynno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skrzynno,_Masovian_Voivodeship).[^cite_note-17]

Polish prime minister [Donald Tusk](648724.md) said "We will begin work on tightening regulations for those who enter level crossings despite barriers and red lights. This has to stop."[^cite_note-\:7-18]

A few hours after the accident, the [Trade Union of Train Drivers in Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trade_Union_of_Train_Drivers_in_Poland?action=edit\&redlink=1) (ZZMK) called for all [train drivers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Train_driver) in Poland to reduce their speed 20 km/h (10 mph) each time when passing a [level crossing](193595.md), all day on 11 September.[^cite_note-19] The appeal applies to all level crossings, regardless of their category. ZZMK are also advising for train drivers to take their time to exercise extreme caution; "carrying out departure procedures with the utmost caution and without haste". This also comes after the major increase in level crossing accidents in the country, since the start of September of this year.[^cite_note-20] The time of the protest was ultimately reduced to take place until noon of 11 September.[^cite_note-21] [PKP Polskie Linie Kolejowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PKP_Polskie_Linie_Kolejowe) reported that around 30% of trains in Poland took part in the protest,[^cite_note-22] which accumulated to over 23,000 minutes of delays.[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24]

[PKP Intercity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PKP_Intercity) cancelled its 25th anniversary celebrating the founding of PKP Intercity and the 100th anniversary of [Polish State Railways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_State_Railways). It was scheduled for 10 September to be held at [Warszawa Główna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warszawa_G%C5%82%C3%B3wna_railway_station).[^cite_note-25]

## Aftermath

The line suffered extreme damage; both tracks were [deformed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deformation_\(engineering\)), and the [overhead wires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overhead_wires) were destroyed together with their support beams.[^cite_note-\:8-1] Traffic on the [Wolanów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolan%C3%B3w_railway_station?action=edit\&redlink=1)–[Przysucha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Przysucha_railway_station?action=edit\&redlink=1) section of the [Tomaszów Mazowiecki–Radom railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomasz%C3%B3w_Mazowiecki%E2%80%93Radom_railway) was suspended. [Rail replacement bus services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rail_replacement_bus_service) were organised for the passengers and other affected services.[^cite_note-26] Tickets for [PKP Intercity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PKP_Intercity) trains were accepted onboard [Polregio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polregio) and [Łódź Agglomeration Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%81%C3%B3d%C5%BA_Agglomeration_Railway) services.[^cite_note-\:7-18] PKP Intercity trains were rerouted, causing delays. [Masovian Railways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masovian_Railways) route R82 was substituted by a replacement bus between [Radom Główny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radom_G%C5%82%C3%B3wny_railway_station) and Wolanów, in conjunction with another bus service between Wolanów and [Drzewica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drzewica).[^cite_note-\:8-1]

The locomotive involved in the collision, [EP09](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PKP_class_EP09)-046, named *Rodzyn*, along with two of the derailed carriages, will be [scrapped](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scrap).[^cite_note-27] [PKP Polskie Linie Kolejowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PKP_Polskie_Linie_Kolejowe) (PLK) announced its plan to reopen at least the first track by 11 or 12 September,[^cite_note-\:8-1] and estimated the cost of damages at approximately 3 million [PLN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_z%C5%82oty).[^cite_note-28] Work on removing the derailed locomotive and carriages began on 10 September,[^cite_note-29] using two cranes.[^cite_note-30] According to new findings published by [Polsat News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polsat_News) on the same day, the train actually collided into the lorry, rather than it colliding into the side of the first carriage.[^cite_note-31]

The last carriage was rerailed on the evening of 11 September. PLK reopened the line at 23\:25 on the same day, with the line speed (in the section of the accident) being temporarily restricted to 60 km/h (35 mph) and level crossing speed to 20 km/h (10 mph) whilst further repair works are underway.[^cite_note-32]

## Preliminary cause

An investigation team was dispatched to the accident.[^cite_note-33] According to preliminary findings, the [concrete mixer lorry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete_mixer_truck) struck the first carriage of the train. This impact is what is believed to have caused the entire train to derail.[^cite_note-\:0-9] According to [PKP Polskie Linie Kolejowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PKP_Polskie_Linie_Kolejowe), the [level crossing](193595.md) was unguarded with working warning lights and [CCTV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CCTV).[^cite_note-\:9-34][^cite_note-\:5-7]

The Deputy [Minister of Infrastructure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Infrastructure_\(Poland\)), Piotr Malepszak, said the driver of the lorry claims to have been "blinded by the sun",[^cite_note-\:6-11] although this claim is yet to be confirmed. Malepszak also said that "the broader problem lay with driver training".[^cite_note-35] The prosecutor in [Radom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radom) confirmed the collision was an accident, saying "We have no grounds to claim that he intended to cause a disaster. We assume that he failed to exercise due care".[^cite_note-\:9-34]
