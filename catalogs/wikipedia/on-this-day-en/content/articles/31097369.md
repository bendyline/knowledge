A **permanent representative to the United Nations** (sometimes called a "**UN ambassador**")[^cite_note-1] is the head of a country's [diplomatic mission](8970.md) to the [United Nations](31769.md).

Of these, the most high-profile UN [permanent representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permanent_representative) are those assigned to [headquarters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Headquarters) in [New York City](645042.md). However, member states also appoint permanent representatives to the other [UN offices in Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Office_at_Geneva), [Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Office_at_Vienna), and [Nairobi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Office_at_Nairobi).

Many countries call their UN permanent representative "UN ambassadors". Although a permanent representative holds the equivalent [diplomatic rank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplomatic_rank) of an [ambassador](68651.md) (or [chief of mission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_mission) or [high commissioner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_commissioner)), they are accredited to an international organisation, and not to a [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) (as a nation's ambassador would be) or to a [head of government](37570.md) (as a high commissioner would be).

## Representatives to UN councils

Some diplomats are representatives to UN councils, such as the [Economic and Social Council of the United Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_and_Social_Council_of_the_United_Nations).

Main article: [Goodwill ambassador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodwill_ambassador)

[UNESCO](21786641.md) has permanent delegates heading the diplomatic missions to the organisation, rather than permanent representatives. However, there are also [UNESCO Goodwill Ambassadors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNESCO_Goodwill_Ambassador), such as many celebrities who act as UNESCO goodwill ambassadors for a particular thematic area. [UNHCR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNHCR) has similar [UNHCR Goodwill Ambassadors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNHCR_Goodwill_Ambassador).

A UN permanent representative is sometimes called an ambassador to the UN, or rarely, a UN permanent ambassador, to distinguish the position from being a UNESCO goodwill ambassador. However, again, the term "[ambassador](68651.md)" is more commonly used to describe each of nation's government officials who are assigned to handle some affairs with another nation.
