The **Czech Republic women's national tennis team** is the representative national team of the [Czech Republic](5321.md) (also known as Czechia) in [Billie Jean King Cup](1108893.md) competition. The Czech Republic team in its current incarnation began competing in [1993](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_Federation_Cup). It is recognised as the official successor to the Czechoslovak team.

Czechoslovakia won the Billie Jean King Cup five times between 1975 and 1988 (when the competition was known as the Federation Cup), but the victory in 2011 (by which time the competition had been renamed the Fed Cup) was the team's first win as the Czech Republic.[^cite_note-1] They have since won the competition six more times, in 2012, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2018, and 2026.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

[Martina Navratilova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martina_Navratilova), one of the Czechoslovak team's greatest players, helped guide the team to victory in [1975](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1975_Federation_Cup). In 1981 she became a US citizen and in later tournaments, notably the [1986 Federation Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986_Federation_Cup) final, she played for the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Billie_Jean_King_Cup_team) against her former nation.
