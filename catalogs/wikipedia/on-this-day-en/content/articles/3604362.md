**Hodeidah** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): الْحُدَيْدَة, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Al Ḥudaida*) is a [governorate](690277.md) of [Yemen](350939.md). Its capital is [Hodeidah](769308.md). The governorate is also sometimes referred to as the **Western Coast**. With an estimated population of nearly 4 million, it is the third-most populous governorate in Yemen, behind [Ibb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibb_Governorate) and [Taiz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiz_governorate).

Hodeidah Governorate borders the [Red Sea](26191.md) and is part of the narrow [Tihamah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tihamah) region. Its capital, Hodeidah, also serves as an important local port city.
