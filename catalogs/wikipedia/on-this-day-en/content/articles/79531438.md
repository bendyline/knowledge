[Norway](21241.md) competed at the [2026 Winter Olympics](38416091.md) in [Milan](36511.md) and [Cortina d'Ampezzo](568829.md), Italy, from 6 to 22 February 2026.

Two days before the closing ceremony, Norway won its 17th gold medal, thus breaking the record for the most gold medals won at a single Winter Olympics. The previous record was 16, also set by Norway, [at the 2022 Winter Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway_at_the_2022_Winter_Olympics).[^cite_note-1] Meanwhile, [Johannes Høsflot Klæbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_H%C3%B8sflot_Kl%C3%A6bo) and Aurora Grinden Løvås were the country's flagbearer during the [closing ceremony](76159922.md).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]
