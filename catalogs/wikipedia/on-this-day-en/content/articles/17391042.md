**Liu Shyh-fang**[^cite_note-LY9EnBio-1] (or Liu Shih-fang;[^cite_note-LY5EnBio-2] [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language): 劉世芳; [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *Liú Shìfāng*; born 15 August 1959) is a Taiwanese politician who currently serves as the [Minister of the Interior](21019185.md) since 2024.

She graduated from the [Oklahoma State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oklahoma_State_University) and is a member of the [Democratic Progressive Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Progressive_Party). She was deputy secretary-general of the office of President [Chen Shui-bian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chen_Shui-bian).[^cite_note-3]

## Education

Liu graduated from [Tamkang University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamkang_University) with a [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) (B.S.) in [chemical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_engineering). She then earned a [Master of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) (M.S.) in [environmental engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_engineering) from [Oklahoma State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oklahoma_State_University) and an [M.B.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Business_Administration) from [National Taiwan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Taiwan_University).[^cite_note-LY9EnBio-1][^cite_note-4]

## Interior ministry

On 12 April 2024, Liu was appointed to [Cho Jung-tai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cho_Jung-tai)'s incoming cabinet as [Minister of the Interior](21019185.md).[^cite_note-5]

On 8 January 2026, Liu was sanctioned by the [Taiwan Affairs Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan_Affairs_Office) of the People's Republic of China as a "[diehard "Taiwan independence" separatist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diehard_%22Taiwan_independence%22_separatists)" for "blatantly promoting separatist ‘Taiwan independence’ rhetoric", as well as "persecuting [mainland spouses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mainland_spouses) in Taiwan and actively creating obstacles to cross-strait personnel exchanges".[^cite_note-6]
