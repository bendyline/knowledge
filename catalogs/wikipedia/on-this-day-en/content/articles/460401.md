The **Pinar del Río Province** is one of the 15 [provinces of Cuba](239171.md). It is at the western end of the island of [Cuba](5042481.md). The capital and largest city is [Pinar del Río](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinar_del_R%C3%ADo) (191,081 pop. in 2022).[^cite_note-stats2022-2]

## Geography

The Pinar del Río province is Cuba's westernmost province and contains one of Cuba's three main [mountain ranges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_range), the [Cordillera de Guaniguanico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guaniguanico), divided into the easterly [Sierra del Rosario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_del_Rosario) and the westerly [Sierra de los Órganos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_de_los_%C3%93rganos). These form a landscape characterised by steep sided [limestone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limestone) hills (called [mogotes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mogote)) and flat, fertile valleys. One such topographic feature, the [Viñales Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vi%C3%B1ales_Valley), is a [UNESCO](21786641.md) [World Heritage Site](44940.md).

The northern coast opens to the great [Gulf of Mexico](21076367.md), and is lined by the [Colorados Archipelago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorados_Archipelago), a string of [cays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cay) and isles developed on a [reef](240844.md) barrier. The westernmost point of Cuba, [Cabo San Antonio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_San_Antonio,_Cuba), is located on the [Guanahacabibes Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guanahacabibes_Peninsula), which is a [National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Park) and a [Biosphere Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biosphere_Reserve).

## History

The city was founded by the Spanish as *Nueva [Filipinas](23440.md)* (New [Philippines](23440.md)) in response to an influx of Asian laborers coming from the Philippine Islands to work on tobacco plantations[^cite_note-5] and the city was renamed Pinar del Río in 1774. The province was founded in 1879.

In 1851 the [filibustering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filibuster_\(military\)) [Lopez Expedition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lopez_Expedition) landed in the province in an effort to seize control of the island, but were defeated by the [Spanish Army](2142325.md) and their leaders executed.

## Economy

Ever since [tobacco](30942.md) was discovered by European colonists, the region has relied heavily on tobacco farming, with Pinar del Río producing 70% of Cuba's crop, used to make the famed [Cuban cigars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuban_cigar). Tobacco is grown and harvested in the province, and shipped to [Havana](49719.md) where they are rolled into cigars and exported. The best tobacco, used for more expensive cigar brands, is grown in the flat lands of [San Juan y Martínez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Juan_y_Mart%C3%ADnez).[^cite_note-6]

### Tourism



Viñales Valley

Tourism is also an important part of the province's economy. Though the town of Pinar del Río (the provincial capital) has some places of cultural and historical interest (such as the Cathedral of San Rosendo, a 19th-century construction), most attractions are to be found in rural or natural settings. A major destination is the [Viñales Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vi%C3%B1ales_Valley), a [karstic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karstic) depression located in the Sierra de los Organos, north of the town of [Viñales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vi%C3%B1ales). Designated a World Heritage Site by [UNESCO](21786641.md) in 1999 because of its natural beauty and the traditional agricultural techniques which continue to be used in the cultivation of tobacco and other crops there, the valley is a popular hiking and rock climbing location.

Many of the valley's surrounding hills are dotted with caves which may be explored by visitors and spelunking has become another popular activity in the province (a particularly interesting cave is the Cueva de los Portales, beside the Río Caiguanabo, where [Che Guevara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Che_Guevara) set up his staff headquarters and dormitory as commander of the Western Army during the Cuban Missile Crisis).

Accommodations in and near Viñales have also grown in number in response to growing tourism in the region. In addition to *[casas particulares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casas_particulares)* (licensed bed and breakfasts in private residences), there are a number of hotels some kilometers from the town.

Another major tourist destination in the province was [Las Terrazas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Terrazas), a unique model village located 20 kilometer northeast of [Soroa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soroa) and, from 2011, part of the new [Artemisa Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artemisa_Province). Surrounded by mountains, the village was founded in 1971 as part of rural development and reforestation project which spans 5,000 ha (12,355 acres). As in other parts of the province, there are several hiking trails which weave through the surrounding mountains. Some companies offer guided hiking tours on a daily basis (in some areas, in fact, a guide is mandatory).

With around 30 diving sites, Pinar del Río is also considered one of Cuba's premier scuba diving destinations. [Cayo Levisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cayo_Levisa),[^cite_note-7] about two kilometers offshore, is known for its copious black coral and excursions to this cay are afforded by tour agencies based in the province. Another hugely popular location is María la Gorda beach,[^cite_note-8] which boasts many nearby dive sites (as close as 200 m (220 yd) from the beach).

An increasing number of tourists also visit the San Diego hot springs, in search of the health benefits reportedly afforded by the warm sulfur-rich waters there. An additional spectrum of health-related services has also become available at this spa, including massages and mud baths.

## Demographics

In 2010, the province of Pinar del Río had a population of 592,042.[^cite_note-atenas-10] With a total area of 8,884 km<sup>2</sup> (3,430 sq mi),[^cite_note-census2002-11] the province had a population density of 67.0/km<sup>2</sup> (174/sq mi).
