**&#x20;Cualac&#x20;**&#x20;is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Guerrero) in the [Mexican](3966054.md) [state](8196151.md) of [Guerrero](195816.md). The municipal seat lies at [Cualac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cualac,_Guerrero). The municipality covers an area of 196.8 km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 6,816.[^cite_note-1]
