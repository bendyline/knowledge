**Sarah Khalifa** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): سارة خليفة, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Sāra Ḫalīfa*)[^cite_note-age-1] is an Egyptian [influencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Influencer), [entrepreneur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entrepreneur) and former [television presenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television_presenter).[^cite_note-5]

On 5 September 2026, she was [sentenced to death](4542038.md) by a court in [Cairo](6293.md) on charges related to [drug trafficking](218449.md) and received an additional prison sentence for the [sexual assault](218407.md) of a young man.

## Career

Khalifa was born in Cairo on 29 March 1994 and began her career in entertainment journalism working for the [Arab Radio and Television Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_Radio_and_Television_Network) (ART) for several years.[^cite_note-\:0-6][^cite_note-\:1-7] She also took part in programming aired on [Al Sharqiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Sharqiya), an [Iraqi television station](231027.md).[^cite_note-\:0-6] In 2024, Khalifa co-hosted a streaming programme on [YouTube](3524766.md) along with singer Hamo Beka, featuring interviews with celebrities from Egyptian media and society.[^cite_note-\:0-6]

Khalifa rose to prominence when she hosted the crime and security show *Mission Impossible* on [Mehwar TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehwar_TV).[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] The programme included interviews with police officers, security officials and crime suspects.[^cite_note-12]

By 2025, Khalifa relied heavily on her business career and other media-related activities.[^cite_note-\:0-6] She also owns a [cosmetic surgery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmetic_surgery) clinic in Cairo and an event planning company.[^cite_note-\:2-13] Khalifa has more than 2.5 million followers on [Instagram](31591547.md), making her a renowned personality within Egyptian media and being considered a [lifestyle influencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Influencer).[^cite_note-\:0-6][^cite_note-\:3-14][^cite_note-ew-4][^cite_note-15][^cite_note-jpost-3]

## Criminal prosecution

### Drug trafficking case

Khalifa was arrested in Cairo in April 2025, accused of being part of a [drug trafficking](218449.md) network that imported raw materials to produce [synthetic drugs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synthetic_drugs) in the country.[^cite_note-\:4-16][^cite_note-\:5-17] She was also charged with a count related to the [sexual assault](218407.md) of a young man. In the same operative, the [Egyptian National Police](17957135.md) arrested dozens of other individuals who were accused of taking part in these activities.[^cite_note-\:0-6][^cite_note-18] The prosecution presented evidence in the trial that included more than 750 kilograms of narcotics that were seized by police during the investigation.[^cite_note-\:5-17][^cite_note-19] Khalifa wept while giving testimony before the judges, asking for "mercy and humanity."[^cite_note-\:3-14]

During her trial in mid-2026, Khalifa denied the charges and said that she had never seen any drugs until she was photographed by their side after her arrest in April 2025.[^cite_note-20] In August 2026, the court found her and twenty others guilty of drug trafficking, referring a request of opinion to the country's [Grand Mufti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Mufti_of_Egypt) on whether a [death sentence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_sentence) should be handed down.[^cite_note-\:1-7][^cite_note-\:4-16]

On 5 September 2026, upon receiving the Grand Mufti's advice, the court sentenced Khalifa and eleven other defendants to [death by hanging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Execution_by_hanging), while nine others were sentenced to [life in prison](148681.md).[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24] Among those sentenced to death was Khalifa's brother, Mohamed, who was convicted of involvement in the manufacturing of the materials.[^cite_note-\:6-25] Her lawyer stated that they would appeal the verdict to a higher court, and if the appeal is denied, they will file another motion to the [Court of Cassation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court_of_cassation).[^cite_note-\:6-25]

### Sexual assault case

Khalifa was additionally sentenced to five years in prison for the kidnapping and sexual assault of a young man who worked as her driver.[^cite_note-\:2-13][^cite_note-26] When she was arrested for the drug trafficking case, investigators found footage in her cell phone showing the youth being stripped naked, assaulted and filmed inside Khalifa's home.[^cite_note-\:6-25]

## Personal life

Khalifa was married to footballer [Kahraba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kahraba_\(footballer\)) in 2016, divorcing six months later.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28]
