On 24 March 2026, two women were killed in a [school shooting](59628.md) at the Antón Makárenko Educational Institute in [Lázaro Cárdenas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%A1zaro_C%C3%A1rdenas_\(Michoac%C3%A1n\)), Michoacán, Mexico.[^cite_note-1] The perpetrator was identified as a 15-year-old male named Osmer H.[^cite_note-2] It is the deadliest school shooting in the history of Mexico.

## Background

[Michoacán](195805.md) has long been one of Mexico's most violent states, primarily in [extortion](162449.md), [kidnapping](169376.md) and other criminal activities. School shootings are rare in Mexico despite a high level of organized crime.[^cite_note-\:0-3]

## Shooting

At approximately 9\:30 AM, 15-year-old Osmer H. attempted to enter the school armed with a high-powered assault rifle. Two female staff members prevented him from entering the building, at which point he shot at them multiple times, killing them both.[^cite_note-4] He was subsequently apprehended by students,[^cite_note-5] who tied him up and then turned him in when police arrived. He supposedly carried 67 rounds, shooting 24 of them during the attack.[^cite_note-\:1-6] Following reports of gunfire, the [National Guard](61282251.md) and [municipal police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_police) carried out an operation at the school, according to the Security Ministry.[^cite_note-7]

## Victims

The victims were identified as two female staff members, 36-year-old Marìa del Rosario Sagrero Chávez and 37-year-old Tatiana Madrigal Bedolla.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

## Perpetrator

The 15-year-old shooter was identified as Osmer H. He took the weapon, an [AR-15–style rifle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AR-15%E2%80%93style_rifle), along with a singular high-capacity [magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magazine_\(firearms\)),[^cite_note-\:0-3] used in the crime from his stepfather, who was a member of the [Mexican Navy](1225277.md).[^cite_note-\:1-6] Shortly before the shooting, he posted content on [Instagram](31591547.md) relating to the [incel](57379245.md) subculture, suggesting a motive,[^cite_note-10] along with multiple self-recorded photographs and videos of himself holding the rifle.[^cite_note-11] His profile used the name "vodka.om", an alleged reference to "VoDKa", the username used by [Dylan Klebold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dylan_Klebold), one of the two perpetrators of the [Columbine High School massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbine_High_School_massacre).[^cite_note-12]

The state prosecutor stated the intention to charge the perpetrator as an adult, citing the gravity of the crime along with past cases of underage hitmen being prosecuted as such.[^cite_note-\:1-6]

## Sentencing

On 17 July 2026, the perpetrator was sentenced to three years' incarceration – the maximum permissible under Michoacán's specialized code of adolescent justice, to be served at a juvenile facility in the state capital, [Morelia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morelia) – and to the payment of 3,280,769 [pesos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MXN) in restitution to the victims' families.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]
