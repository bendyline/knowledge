**Kirehe** is a district (*akarere*) in [Eastern Province](3624633.md), [Rwanda](25645.md). Its capital is Kirehe town (which is usually known as [Rusumo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rusumo,_Rwanda), being the major settlement of the former Rusumo district).

## Geography

The district comprises areas in the far south-eastern corner of Rwanda, bordering [Tanzania](30118.md) and [Burundi](21490998.md). Its most noteworthy feature is [Rusumo Falls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rusumo_Falls), the waterfall on the [Kagera River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kagera_River), which has been key to Rwandan history.

### Climate

The district is characterized by [savanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savanna), acacia trees and few natural forests, these and the existence of the [Kagera River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kagera_River) contributes to a [temperate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperate) climate in the region.[^cite_note-2]

## Sectors

Kirehe district is divided into 12 [sectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sectors_of_Rwanda) (*imirenge*): [Gahara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gahara), [Gatore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gatore), [Kigarama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kigarama), [Kigina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kigina?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Kirehe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirehe_\(sector\)?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Mahama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahama,_Rwanda?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Mpanga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mpanga,_Rwanda?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Musaza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musaza?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Mushikiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mushikiri?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Nasho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasho,_Rwanda?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Nyamugari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyamugari?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Nyarubuye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyarubuye).
