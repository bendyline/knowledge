**Washington State Prison** is located in [Davisboro](110420.md) in [Washington County, Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_County,_Georgia). It is a facility for a maximum of 1548 adult male inmates at minimum and medium security. It is a part of the [Georgia Department of Corrections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Department_of_Corrections).[^cite_note-1]

The Washington State Prison is not to be confused with the [Washington State Penitentiary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_State_Penitentiary), located in [Walla Walla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walla_Walla,_Washington) in the state of [Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_\(U.S._state\)).

In January 2026, the prison was placed on lockdown when four inmates were killed, 12 inmates hospitalized, and one guard was injured due to inmates apparently fighting among themselves.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]
