**Uasin Gishu County** is one of the 47 [counties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_Kenya) of [Kenya](188171.md), located in the former [Rift Valley Province](72874.md). [Eldoret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eldoret) city has the county's largest population centre as well as its administrative and commercial centre.[^cite_note-2] It is bordered by [Elgeyo-Marakwet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elgeyo-Marakwet) to the East, [Trans Nzoia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans_Nzoia) to the North, [Kakamega](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kakamega) to the west, [Nandi](6910690.md) and [Kericho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kericho) to the [South West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwest) and [Baringo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baringo_County) to the South East. It is a highland plateau with altitudes falling gently from 2,700 metres (8,900 ft) above sea level to about 1,500 metres (4,900 ft) above sea level. The topography is higher to the east and declines gently towards the western border".[^cite_note-3]

Uasin Gishu is located on a plateau and has a cool and temperate climate. The county borders [Trans-Nzoia County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans-Nzoia_County) to the north, [Elgeyo-Marakwet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elgeyo-Marakwet_County) and [Baringo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baringo_County) counties to the east, [Kericho County](3377688.md) to the south, [Nandi County](6910690.md) to the south and south-west and [Kakamega County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kakamega_county) to the west.

## Etymology

The county is named after Illwuasin-kishu, a Maasai clan. The land was the grazing area of the clan. They surrendered the land to the colonial government in the Anglo-Maasai agreement of 1911 and were subsequently pushed towards [Trans Mara District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans_Mara_District). The plateau that they once occupied was then registered in its Anglicised version, Uasin Gishu.[^cite_note-4]

## History

In 1903, it was proposed to use the area as a Jewish homeland; [British Uganda Programme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Uganda_Programme), which was rejected by the Jewish community leaders in the Seventh Zionist Congress (1905).

In 1908, fifty-eight families of [Afrikaans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afrikaans)-speaking [South Africans](17416221.md) settled in the Uasin Gishu plateau. They were followed by sixty more families in 1911 and more later.[^cite_note-5] The town of [Eldoret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eldoret) was established in the midst of the farms they created.

## Economy

[Uasin Gishu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uasin_Gishu) is the 8th Largest Economy in Kenya with a GDP of **\$6.8B** at PPP and **\$2.5B** at Nominal. It is the 11th richest County in Africa with a GDP per Capita of \$2,050.

### Agriculture



Cherangani hills as viewed from farmlands near Karuna, Moiben, Uasin Gishu



Kesses Reservoir

Uasin Gishu along with neighbouring [Trans-Nzoia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans-Nzoia_County), are considered Kenya's breadbasket due to their large-scale maize and wheat farms which produce the bulk of the country's total harvest.[^cite_note-11]

The National Cereals Board has a cereal storage depot located at [Moi's Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moi's_Bridge) town located in the north of the county, which consists of eight large silos with a capacity of approximately 5 million tonnes of grain.[^cite_note-12] It is one of the largest in the country and plays a significant role in Kenya's food security.

The county also produces sizable quantities of milk and horticultural produce, with a wide variety of crops and animals produced in smaller quantities.

### Aquaculture

The county has 1,728 operational fish ponds covering 486,000 m<sup>2</sup> with annual fish production of 593,000 kg worth KShs 285,900,000. The county also has many private and public dams suitable for capture fisheries with an annual production of 33,048 kg worth KShs 9,914,400.[^cite_note-13]

### Industry

The county capital, [Eldoret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eldoret), is home to a textile industry as well as East Africa's only manufacturer of small arms and ammunition. There is also a substantial [agribusiness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agribusiness) sector that makes use of the produce from the county and surrounding regions.[^cite_note-14]

### Services

Eldoret is a major commercial centre in western Kenya. Service industries like wholesale & retail trade, auto repair, entertainment centres and various IT services abound within and outside the town. Almost all Kenyan banks have a presence in the town and these act to service the region.[^cite_note-15]

#### Tourism

Tourism, sports tourism in particular, is a growing sub-sector in the county - the result of long term performances by athletes from the region.[^cite_note-16]

## Transport

Uasin Gishu has 300 kilometres (190 mi) of tarmac roads, 549 kilometres (341 mi) of murram roads and 377 kilometres (234 mi) of earth roads. It also has 17 kilometres (11 mi) of railway line with 8 railway stations in addition to an inland container depot. The [Eldoret International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eldoret_International_Airport) and two airstrips are also located in the county, all combining to make it the regions service hub.[^cite_note-17]

## Education

The county has several universities including [Moi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moi_university) and the [University of Eldoret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Eldoret), which have their main campuses in the county. Several technical and vocational institutes are also located in the county, including Eldoret National Polytechnic, [Rift Valley Technical Training Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rift_Valley_Technical_Training_Institute), Kipkabus Technical and Vocational College, Moiben Technical and Vocational College, Kosyin Technical Training Institute, Chebororwa Agricultural Training Centre, Tumaini and Technical Training Institute, Eldoret Technical training institute [Kenya School of TVET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenya_School_of_TVET) (Moiben Campus), amongst others.
