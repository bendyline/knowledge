**Holborn and St Pancras** ([/ˈhoʊbərn ən sənt ˈpæŋkrəs/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English)) is a [parliamentary constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_United_Kingdom_Parliament_constituencies) in [Greater London](45367389.md) that was created in 1983. From [2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_United_Kingdom_general_election) to 2026 it was represented in the [House of Commons](13828.md) of the [UK Parliament](13964.md) by Sir [Keir Starmer](18681491.md), who served as [Prime Minister](24150.md) from 2024 to 2026 and [Leader of the Labour Party](19043368.md) from 2020 to 2026. He announced his resignation from parliament on 1 September 2026, triggering the [2026 Holborn and St Pancras by-election](84114410.md).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

## Constituency

Holborn and St Pancras as drawn in 2010[^cite_note-5] included all but a small western portion of the [London Borough of Camden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Borough_of_Camden).[^cite_note-6] After the completion of the [2023 review of Westminster constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_review_of_Westminster_constituencies), boundary changes led to 29.7% of the geographical area of the constituency transferring to the [Hampstead and Highgate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hampstead_and_Highgate) constituency for the 2024 general election.[^cite_note-7]

Within the constituency, 18.5% of people are living in fuel poverty (2023), 36.3% of children are living in poverty (2024) and 16.7% of people are considered to have a disability (2024). There is a higher than national average amount of people living in social housing or in private rented accommodation (2024).[^cite_note-8] [King's Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_King's_Cross_railway_station), [St Pancras International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Pancras_International), and [Euston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euston_railway_station) railway termini are within the geographical area.[^cite_note-os-9]

### Political history

[Labour Party](19279158.md) MPs have served this constituency since its creation in 1983. The majorities achieved have been varied, from a relatively marginal 13.9% in [2005](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_United_Kingdom_general_election) (making it within the lowest 150 seats for the party in that year by percentage of majority) to a landslide 51.7% in [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_United_Kingdom_general_election). The 2015 result ranked the seat as the 77th safest of the party's 232 seats (by percentage majority).[^cite_note-10] Political groups active in the constituency include Camden Labour Left.[^cite_note-11]

## Boundaries

### Historic

The seat was created in 1983 as a successor to [Holborn and St Pancras South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holborn_and_St_Pancras_South_\(UK_Parliament_constituency\)).[^cite_note-12] The constituency has contained the following wards of the London Borough of Camden:

1983–1997

Bloomsbury, Brunswick, Camden, Castlehaven, Caversham, Chalk Farm, Gospel Oak, Grafton, Holborn, King's Cross, Regent's Park, St John's, St Pancras, and Somers Town.[^cite_note-13]

1997–2010

As above, less Gospel Oak.[^cite_note-14]

[Map](1344871.md)

Map of boundaries 2010–2024.

2010–2024

[Bloomsbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomsbury_\(ward\)), [Camden Town with Primrose Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camden_Town_with_Primrose_Hill), [Cantelowes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantelowes), [Gospel Oak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gospel_Oak_\(ward\)), [Haverstock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haverstock_\(ward\)), [Highgate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highgate_\(Camden_ward\)), [Holborn and Covent Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holborn_and_Covent_Garden), [Kentish Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentish_Town_\(ward\)), [King's Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_Cross_\(ward\)), [Regent's Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regent's_Park_\(Camden_ward\)), and [St Pancras and Somers Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Pancras_and_Somers_Town).[^cite_note-\:1-15]

The [fifth periodic review of Westminster constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fifth_periodic_review_of_Westminster_constituencies) by the [Boundary Commission for England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boundary_Commission_for_England) was implemented nationally in 2010.[^cite_note-16] Parts of Highgate, Gospel Oak, Haverstock, Camden Town and Primrose Hill wards[^cite_note-\:1-15] were transferred from the former constituency of [Hampstead and Highgate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hampstead_and_Highgate), when it was abolished.[^cite_note-17]

### Current

Further to the 2023 review of Westminster constituencies, which came into effect for the [2024 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_Kingdom_general_election), the constituency is composed of the following London Borough of Camden wards:[^cite_note-18]

- [Bloomsbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomsbury_\(ward\)); [Camden Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camden_Square_\(ward\)); [Camden Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camden_Town_\(ward\)); [Haverstock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haverstock_\(ward\)); [Holborn and Covent Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holborn_and_Covent_Garden); [Kentish Town North](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentish_Town_North_\(ward\)); [Kentish Town South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentish_Town_South_\(ward\)); [King's Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_Cross_\(ward\)); [Primrose Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primrose_Hill_\(ward\)) (part); [Regent's Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regent's_Park_\(Camden_ward\)); [St. Pancras and Somers Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Pancras_and_Somers_Town_\(ward\)).

## Members of Parliament



[Keir Starmer](18681491.md) represented the constituency from [2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_United_Kingdom_general_election) until his resignation in 2026.

The seat was held from [1983](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1983_United_Kingdom_general_election) to [2015](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_United_Kingdom_general_election) by [Frank Dobson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Dobson) of the [Labour Party](19279158.md), who had been elected in [1979](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1979_United_Kingdom_general_election) to the predecessor seat of [Holborn & St Pancras South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holborn_&_St_Pancras_South). Dobson was the longest-serving Labour MP in London until he stood down in 2015. The constituency has been represented by [Keir Starmer](18681491.md) since the 2015 general election. Starmer served as [Leader of the Labour Party](19043368.md) (consequently [Leader of the Opposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leader_of_the_Opposition_\(United_Kingdom\)) until his [2024 election victory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_Kingdom_general_election)) from [April 2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Labour_Party_leadership_election_\(UK\)) to [July 2026](83143566.md), and the [Prime Minister](24150.md) from July 2024 to July 2026.
