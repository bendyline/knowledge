**Nandurbar district** (Marathi pronunciation: [\[nən̪d̪uɾbaːɾ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Marathi)) is an administrative district in the northwest corner of [Maharashtra](20629.md) state in [India](14533.md). On 1 July 1998 [Dhule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhule_district) was bifurcated as two separate districts now known as Dhule and Nandurbar. Nandurbar is a tribal-dominated district with its headquarters located at [Nandurbar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nandurbar) city. The district occupies an area of 5,955 square kilometres (2,299 sq mi) and has a population of 1,648,295 of which 16.71% were urban (as of 2011).[^cite_note-districtcensus-2]

Nandurbar district is bounded to the south and south-east by Dhule district, to the west and north is the state of [Gujarat](53707.md), to the north and north-east is the state of [Madhya Pradesh](47945.md). The northern boundary of the district is defined by the [Narmada River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narmada_River).

## Divisions

The district comprises six talukas. These talukas are [Nandurbar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nandurbar), [Navapur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navapur_Taluka), [Shahada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahada,_Maharashtra), [Taloda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talode), [Akkalkuwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akkalkuwa) and [Akrani Mahal (also called Dhadgaon)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhadgaon).

There is one [Lok Sabha](275456.md) constituency in the district which is [Nandurbar (ST)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nandurbar_\(Lok_Sabha_constituency\)) reserved for Scheduled Tribes. There are four [Maharashtra Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maharashtra_Assembly) seats namely [Akkalkuwa (ST)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akkalkuwa_\(Vidhan_Sabha_constituency\)), [Shahada (ST)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahada_\(Vidhan_Sabha_constituency\)), [Nandurbar (ST)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nandurbar_\(Vidhan_Sabha_constituency\)), [Nawapur (ST)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navapur_\(Vidhan_Sabha_constituency\)).

[Sakri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sakri_Assembly_constituency) and [Shirpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shirpur_Assembly_constituency) assembly seats from [Dhule district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhule_district) are also part of Nandurbar Lok Sabha seat. Nandurbar is primarily a tribal (Adiwasi) district.

## History

Nandurbar is a part of Satpuda Pradesh, meaning Seven Hills Region. The district was part of the district with Dhule and Jalgaon till July 1998. The ancient name of the region was Rasika, when Nandurbar was also called Nandanagri after the name of its king Nandaraja.[^cite_note-4]

On 3 June 1818 the Maratha Peshwa surrendered Khandesh to the British rule.

#### Main languages

At the time of the [2011 Census of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Census_of_India), 45.45% of the population in the district spoke [Bhili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhili_language), 16.06% [Marathi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marathi_language), 10.46% [Khandeshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khandeshi_language), 7.34% [Pawri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rathwi_Bareli_language), 4.40% [Mawchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mawchi_language), 3.79% [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu), 2.61% [Kukna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhodia-Kukna_language), 2.55% [Hindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindi), 1.91% [Gujarati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gujarati_language) and 1.62% speak [Gujari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gujari_language) as their first language mostly by [Gurjars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gurjar).[^cite_note-languages-8]

#### Other languages

Aadivasi (tribal) Languages spoken include Ahirani, a Khandeshi tongue with approximately 780,000 speakers, similar to [Marathi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marathi_language) and [Bhili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhili_language).[^cite_note-ethnoahirani-9] and [Pauri Bareli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pauri_Bareli), a [Bhil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhil_languages) and other tribal language with approximately 175 000 speakers, written in the [Devanagari script](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devanagari_script).[^cite_note-10]

### Literacy rate and sex ratio

The [population growth rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_planning_in_India) over the decade 2001–2011 was 25.5%.[^cite_note-districtcensus-2] Nandurbar has a [sex ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_ratio) of 972 [females](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women_in_India) for every 1000 males,[^cite_note-districtcensus-2] and a [literacy rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literacy_in_India) of 64.38%. 12.25% of the population live in ruban areas. Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes make up 2.91% and 69.28% of the population respectively The Bhils form the major group in the district.[^cite_note-districtcensus-2]

As of 2001 India census,[^cite_note-12] Nandurbar District had a population of 1,309,135, being 50.62% male and 49.38% female. Nandurbar District has an average literacy rate of 46.63%: male literacy is 55.11%, and female literacy is 37.93%.

## Economy

In 2006 the [Ministry of Panchayati Raj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Panchayati_Raj) named Nandurbar one of the country's 250 [most backward districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_in_India) (out of [a total of 640 districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_India)).[^cite_note-brgf-13] It is one of the twelve districts in Maharashtra currently receiving funds from the Backward Regions Grant Fund Programme (BRGF).[^cite_note-brgf-13]

The [climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate) of Nandurbar District is generally Hot and Dry. As the rest of India Nandurbar District has three distinct seasons: summer, [monsoon](57630.md) (rainy) and the winter season.

Summer is from March to mid of June. Summers are usually hot and dry. During the month of May the summer is at its peak. Temperatures can be as high as 45 °C (113 °F) during the peak of Summer. The Monsoon sets in during the mid or end of June. During this season the weather is usually humid and hot. The northern and western regions receive more rainfall than the rest of the region. The average rainfall is 767 mm (30.2 in) through the district.[^cite_note-16] Winter is from the month of November to February. Winters are mildly cold but dry.
