The **Parliament of Cameroon** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Parlement du Cameroun*) is the [legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature) of [Cameroon](5447.md). A [bicameral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicameralism) body, it consists of the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_\(Cameroon\)) and the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_\(Cameroon\)).[^cite_note-1] The parliament is composed of 280 members, 180 MPs and 100 senators.
