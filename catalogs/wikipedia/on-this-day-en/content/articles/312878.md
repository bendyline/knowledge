**Sainte-Anne** (French pronunciation: [\[sɛ̃t an\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French); [Guadeloupean Creole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antillean_Creole): *Sentann'*) is a city in the southern part of [Grande-Terre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grande-Terre,_Guadeloupe), [Guadeloupe](12343.md) in the [French West Indies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_West_Indies). It is one of the most popular tourist destinations of the island, along with [Le Gosier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Gosier) and [Saint-François](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Fran%C3%A7ois,_Guadeloupe).

The [commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commune_in_France) has developed its tourist infrastructure in recent decades, but still remains dependent on agriculture.
