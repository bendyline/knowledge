**Sooke** ([/ˈsuːk/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English)) is a [district municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/District_municipality) on the southern tip of [Vancouver Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver_Island), Canada, 38 kilometres (24 mi) by road from [Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria,_British_Columbia), the capital of [British Columbia](3392.md). Sooke, the westernmost of [Greater Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Victoria)'s [Western Communities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Communities), is to the north and west of the [Sooke Basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sooke_Basin). It is a regional centre for residents in neighbouring communities, including [Otter Point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otter_Point,_Greater_Victoria), [Shirley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shirley_\(British_Columbia\)) and [Jordan River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_River,_British_Columbia).

## Geography

### Climate

&#x20;Sooke has a [Mediterranean climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediterranean_climate), with warm summers and mild winters, defined by the [Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) as *Csb*. Although its precipitation is more like dry summer climates, its temperatures resemble [oceanic climates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceanic_climate) as found in [Ireland](147575.md), for example.[^cite_note-3]

## Demographics

In the [2021 Census of Population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Canadian_census) conducted by [Statistics Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistics_Canada), Sooke had a population of 15,086 living in 6,129 of its 6,431 total private dwellings, an increase of 16% from its 2016 population of 13,001. With a land area of 56.6 km<sup>2</sup> (21.9 sq mi), it had a population density of 266.5/km<sup>2</sup> (690.3/sq mi) in 2021.[^cite_note-SCref21-2]

## Governance

The District of Sooke was incorporated as a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality) on December 7, 1999. It is one of 13 municipalities within the [Capital Regional District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_Regional_District) and is located in the traditional territories of the [T'Sou-ke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T'Sou-ke_Nation) and [Scia'new](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scia'new_First_Nation) First Nations.

### Municipal

Sooke is governed by a mayor and six councillors, who are elected every four years. The current council was elected on October 15, 2022.[^cite_note-14]

### Provincial

Sooke was part of the riding of [Langford-Juan de Fuca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langford-Juan_de_Fuca), represented by the [BC NDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Columbia_New_Democratic_Party)'s [Ravi Parmar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravi_Parmar). He succeeded [John Horgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Horgan), the former Premier of British Columbia (2017–2022) who first won the seat in 2009 and retired from politics for health reasons in 2023.

In April 2023, the BC Electoral Boundaries Commission created the new riding of [Juan de Fuca-Malahat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_de_Fuca-Malahat). It encompasses 44,980 people in Sooke, Port Renfrew, Jordan River, Otter Point, East Sooke, Metchosin, Malahat, Mill Bay and Shawnigan Lake. Its first elected representative, [Dana Lajeunesse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dana_Lajeunesse), won election in a tight race in October, 2024 following a recount.

## Tourism and recreation



Sooke Harbour

Well-known destinations in Sooke include Whiffin Spit Park, the [Sooke Potholes Regional Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sooke_Potholes_Regional_Park) and adjacent [Sooke Potholes Provincial Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sooke_Potholes_Provincial_Park).

Sooke is home to the [Sooke Region Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sooke_Region_Museum).

The area is the base for visiting the wilderness parks of [Vancouver Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver_Island)'s southwest coast — the [West Coast Trail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Coast_Trail) and the [Juan de Fuca Provincial Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_de_Fuca_Provincial_Park), which includes the [Juan de Fuca Marine Trail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_de_Fuca_Marine_Trail). Beaches near Sooke include Sandcut, French Beach, Fishboat Bay, China Beach, and Mystic Beach.

The [Galloping Goose Regional Trail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galloping_Goose_Regional_Trail), part of the [Trans-Canada Trail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans-Canada_Trail), runs through Sooke as far as [Leechtown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leechtown,_British_Columbia), the former site of a gold-rush town circa 1865. Once a rail line, the Goose is now a popular pedestrian and cycling route connecting Sooke to Victoria.

## Arts and culture

The [Sooke Fall Fair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sooke_Fall_Fair) was first held in 1913 and continues on Labour Day weekends annually at the Sooke Community Hall.

## Real estate

In April 2022, an average single-family home in Sooke costs \$887,500 based on the home price index, drawing from data from the Victoria Real Estate Board (VREB).[^cite_note-19] By June 2022, that number (directly from VREB) increased to \$938,000.[^cite_note-20] The average home value as of July 1, 2022 as determined by [BC Assessment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BC_Assessment_Authority) was [\$831,000](https://www.timescolonist.com/local-news/island-property-assessments-rise-up-to-20-colwood-highlands-see-biggest-jump-in-capital-region-6320429).

## Education

Sooke is a part of the [School District 62 Sooke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_District_62_Sooke). There is one high school, [Edward Milne Community School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Milne_Community_School), and one junior high school, [Journey Middle School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journey_Middle_School). The four elementary schools in the area are John Muir (in the town's west end), Sooke Elementary (town centre), the French immersion Ecole Poirier (town centre), and Saseenos Elementary (east end). In 2018, SD62 announced it had purchased land to build the future Sunriver Elementary in the Sunriver neighbourhood. Continuing adult education programs are offered by the [Edward Milne Community School (EMCS) Society](https://www.emcsprograms.ca/), which also operates with day, evening and weekend programs. The closest post-secondary institutions are [Royal Roads University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Roads_University) and [Camosun College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camosun_College#Interurban)'s Interurban campus. The non-profit [Sooke Region Lifelong Learning](https://www.sookeregionchn.org/sooke-region-lifelong-learning) offers a diverse set of educational programs and workshops both virtually and at the Sooke library.
