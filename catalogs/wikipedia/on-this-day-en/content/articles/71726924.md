**Shifa International Hospitals Limited&#x20;**&#x20;(**SIH**) is a Pakistani [hospital network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hospital_network) headquartered in [Islamabad](51153.md).[^cite_note-donorcheating-2][^cite_note-3]

It runs two medical centers in [Islamabad](51153.md) and another hospital in [Faisalabad](401880.md).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-Faisalabad-5]

## History

[Zaheer Ahmad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaheer_Ahmad), a [Pakistani American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_American) medical doctor, is often credited as the founder of Shifa International Hospital. He also served as a chief executive at the hospital.[^cite_note-ET-6]

The Hospital was incorporated on September 20, 1987 as a [private limited company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_limited_company). It was converted to a [public limited company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_limited_company) on October 12, 1989. The construction activity began in 1988. Shifa was officially opened in 1993, with 8 OPD clinics and 36 beds.

As of 2018, about 85% of the [liver transplant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liver_transplant) operations in Pakistan were performed at SIH, of which 700 organ transplants were done successfully. In 2018, the hospital suspended the transplant program due to fraud alleged by a patient's family.[^cite_note-donorcheating-2]

Shifa National Hospital Faisalabad, a subsidiary of Shifa International Hospital Limited, began its construction in 2022.[^cite_note-Faisalabad-5]

## Controversies

In a case involving alleged negligence, President [Arif Alvi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arif_Alvi) directed the [Islamabad Healthcare Regulatory Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamabad_Healthcare_Regulatory_Authority) (IHRA) to ensure that Shifa International Hospital in Islamabad reimbursed Rs. 2.9 million to the family of a patient who died during the course of treatment. Additionally, the President upheld the Rs. 900,000 fine imposed on the hospital.[^cite_note-7] The investigation, prompted by a complaint from the patient's daughter, revealed the hospital's negligence, professional misconduct, and malpractice, leading to these actions being taken.[^cite_note-8]

In a significant case, an Islamabad court issued arrest warrants for a former consultant at Shifa International Hospital, Dr. Aasim Rahman. The patient, Hajira Bibi, claimed the hospital failed to diagnose her real disease and accused them of maltreatment and misdiagnosis. After being diagnosed with [leprosy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leprosy) at a different hospital, she sought a refund and compensation totaling Rs15.5 million from Shifa under Islamabad consumer law.[^cite_note-9]

The [Federal Investigation Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Investigation_Agency) (FIA) transferred a case to the [Capital Development Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_Development_Authority_\(Islamabad\)) (CDA) regarding Shifa International Hospital's alleged encroachment. The hospital was accused of violating building regulations in 2020 by encroaching on land near a nullah.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]
