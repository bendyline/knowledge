**Kagadi** is a town in the [Western Region](25370326.md) of [Uganda](31816.md). It is the commercial and administrative headquarters of [Kagadi District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kagadi_District).[^cite_note-2]

## Location

Kagadi is in Buyaga East County, approximately 95 kilometres (59 mi) south-west of [Hoima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoima), the largest town in the [Bunyoro sub-region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bunyoro_sub-region).[^cite_note-3] This is approximately 245 kilometres (152 mi), by road, west of [Kampala](56689.md), Uganda's capital and largest city.[^cite_note-4] The coordinates of the town are 0°56'28.0"N, 30°48'39.0"E (Latitude\:0.941111; Longitude: 30.810833).[^cite_note-5]

## Population

The 2002 national population census put the population of Kagadi at 13,568. In 2010, the [Uganda Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uganda_Bureau_of_Statistics) (UBOS) estimated the population at 20,600. In 2011, UBOS estimated the mid-year population at 21,600.[^cite_note-6] In 2014, the national census put the population at 22,813.[^cite_note-KGDI-1]
