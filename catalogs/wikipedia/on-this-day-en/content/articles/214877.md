The **governor of Minnesota** is the [head of government](37570.md) of the state of [Minnesota](19590.md), leading the state's [executive branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_\(government\)). Forty people have been governor of Minnesota, and previously three of [Minnesota Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnesota_Territory). [Alexander Ramsey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Ramsey), the first territorial governor, also served as state governor several years later. State governors are elected to office by popular vote; territorial governors were appointed to the office by the [United States president](24113.md). The current governor of Minnesota is [Tim Walz](2216593.md) of the [Democratic-Farmer-Labor Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnesota_Democratic%E2%80%93Farmer%E2%80%93Labor_Party) (DFL), affiliated with the [Democratic Party](5043544.md).

## Powers and qualifications

Similar to the [U.S. president](24113.md), the governor has [veto](74661.md) power over bills passed by the [Minnesota State Legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnesota_State_Legislature). As in most states, but unlike the U.S. president, the governor can also make [line-item vetoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line-item_veto), where specific provisions in bills can be stripped out while allowing the overall bill to be signed into law.

The governor of Minnesota must be 25 years old upon assuming office, and must have been a Minnesota resident for one year before the election.

Since a 1958 amendment[^cite_note-3] to the [Minnesota Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnesota_Constitution), governors are elected to four-year terms, with [no limits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Term_limits_in_the_United_States) on the number of terms they may serve.[^cite_note-4]

## Cabinet

The governor has a [cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_\(government\)) consisting of the leaders of various state departments. The governor appoints these department heads, who, other than the head of the Department of Military Affairs and the chairs of the Metropolitan Council and the Metropolitan Sports Facilities Commission, are called [commissioners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commissioner). ## Residence

Main article: [Minnesota Governor's Residence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnesota_Governor's_Residence)

The Minnesota Governor's Residence is located in [Saint Paul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Paul,_Minnesota), at 1006 [Summit Avenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summit_Avenue_\(St._Paul\)).

## Succession

Main article: [Gubernatorial lines of succession in the United States § Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gubernatorial_lines_of_succession_in_the_United_States#Minnesota)

The line of succession for the Governor is established by Article V, Section 5 of the [Minnesota Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnesota_Constitution)[^cite_note-22] and Minnesota Statute 4.06.[^cite_note-23]

## Traditions

### Minnesota Governor's Fishing Opener

The Minnesota Governor's Fishing Opener is a tradition that dates back to 1948.[^cite_note-24] The event was designed to promote the development of Minnesota's recreation industry.[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26] The Governor goes to a selected lake in Minnesota to fish on the opening weekend of the fishing season.[^cite_note-27]
