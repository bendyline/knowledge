The **Ministry of Mines and Energy** ([Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language): *Ministério de Minas e Energia*, **MME**) is a Brazilian [government ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Brazil) established in 1960. It fosters investments in [mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mining) and [energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy)-related activities, funds research and sets out government policies. Previously, mines and energy were the responsibility of the [Ministry of Agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Agriculture_\(Brazil\)). As of 1 January 2023, the minister of mines and energy is [Alexandre Silveira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandre_Silveira).
