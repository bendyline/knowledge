# Sudan women's national under-17 football team

The **Sudan women's national under-17 football team** is a national [association football](10568.md) youth team of [Sudan](27421.md) and is controlled by the [Sudan Football Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudan_Football_Association).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] The [2026 CECAFA Women's U-17 Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_CECAFA_Women's_U-17_Championship) was the country's first appearance in women's football since the start of the [Sudanese civil war](73556699.md).[^cite_note-3] The team captain is Nura Mohamed.[^cite_note-4]
