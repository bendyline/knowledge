**Baldonnel** ([Irish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_language): *Baile Dhónaill*, meaning 'homestead of the O'Donnels') is a [townland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Townland) in [County Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Dublin), Ireland.[^cite_note-1] It is an agricultural and [industrial area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_suburb) near [Rathcoole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rathcoole,_County_Dublin), [Clondalkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clondalkin), [Tallaght](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tallaght), [Lucan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucan,_Dublin), [Saggart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saggart), [Newcastle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newcastle,_County_Dublin) and [Naas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naas). It is around 15 km west of Dublin city centre.

The name Baldonnel, from the Irish *Baile Dhónaill*, derives from the name of former landowners in the area. The earliest written record of the name comes from a 1717 deed for Baldonnell, which lists alternate spellings of Baldownan, Balldonnan, and Ballydonnell. Two surviving local houses (the oldest to the north of [Baldonnel Airfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldonnel_Airfield), and the newest further east in the same area) claim the name Baldonnell House, and the ruins of the original house are located to the east of the airfield.[^cite_note-2]

The [River Camac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Camac) flows through the townland before flowing by way of [Corkagh Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corkagh_Park) to join the [River Liffey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Liffey).[^cite_note-3]
