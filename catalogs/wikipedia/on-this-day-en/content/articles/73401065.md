# List of protests in Pakistan

|  | This list is [incomplete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia\:WikiProject_Lists#Incomplete_lists); you can help by [adding missing items](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=List_of_protests_in_Pakistan\&action=edit). *(September 2023)* |
| --- | --- |

The history of protests in [Pakistan](23235.md) dates back to its establishment as an independent nation for India's Muslim population in 1947. Since then, Pakistan has seen a multitude of protests and uprisings, serving as a reflection of the various political, social, and economic challenges it has encountered throughout its history. These protests have been a prominent part of Pakistan's socio-political landscape.[^cite_note-1]

## 1947-1950s

- 1947: Kashmir Day protests against the Indian controlled Kashmir.

  [^cite_note-4]

  [^cite_note-5]

- 1951: [Rawalpindi conspiracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rawalpindi_conspiracy) protests against the arrest of military officers by the government.

- 1953: [Lahore riots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahore_riots) protests against the Ahmadiyya Muslim community.

## 1960s

- 1968: [1968–69 Pakistan revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1968%E2%80%9369_Pakistan_revolution) against [Ayub Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayub_Khan) regime protests against the dictatorship of President Ayub Khan.

## 1970s

- 1971: [Bangladesh independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_independence) protests against the [Pakistani Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_Army) during the [Bangladesh Liberation War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Liberation_War).

- 1972: [Karachi labour unrest of 1972](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karachi_labour_unrest_of_1972), Labour movement protests against the government's labour policies.

- 1977: [1977 Pakistan uprising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1977_Pakistan_uprising), movement against [Zulfikar Ali Bhutto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zulfikar_Ali_Bhutto) protests against the dictatorship of Prime Minister Zulfikar Ali Bhutto.

## 1980s

- 1984: [Movement for the Restoration of Democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Movement_for_the_Restoration_of_Democracy), Movement against [Zia-ul-Haq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zia-ul-Haq) regime protests against the dictatorship of President Zia-ul-Haq.

- 1986: Movement against the [Hudood Ordinance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudood_Ordinance) protests against the government's implementation of Islamic laws.

## 1990s

- 1990: [Movement for the Restoration of Democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Movement_for_the_Restoration_of_Democracy) protests against the government's rigging of elections.

  [^cite_note-6]

- 1992: Protests against the [demolition of the Babri Masjid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demolition_of_the_Babri_Masjid) in India.

  [^cite_note-7]

- 1997: [Lawyers' movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawyers'_movement) protests against the government's attempts to remove the Chief Justice of Pakistan.

  [^cite_note-8]

## 2000s

- 2001: [War in Afghanistan protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_in_Afghanistan_protests) against the US-led invasion of Afghanistan.

- 2007: [Lawyers' Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawyers'_Movement) protests against the suspension of Chief Justice of Pakistan Iftikhar Muhammad Chaudhry.

- 2008: Long March protests against the government's attempts to remove the Chief Justice of Pakistan.

  [^cite_note-9]

## 2010s

- 2011: [Raymond Allen Davis incident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raymond_Allen_Davis_incident) protests against the release of [Raymond Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raymond_Allen_Davis), a US contractor who killed two Pakistani citizens.

- 2013: Islamabad sit-in protests against the government's electoral rigging.

  [^cite_note-10]

- [Long March (Pakistan)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_March_\(Pakistan\))

- 2014: [Inqilab March](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inqilab_March)

- November–December 2017: [2017 Fayzabad protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Fayzabad_protests)

- [Sadae Mazlomeen Dharna (Parachinar)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sadae_Mazlomeen_Dharna_\(Parachinar\))

- 2018: Pashtun Tahaffuz Movement protests against the Pakistani state's extrajudicial killing and enforced disappearance of Pashtuns.
