[Elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elections_in_the_United_States) were held in the United States on November 4, 2025. The [off-year election](3114588.md) included [gubernatorial](69180119.md) and [state legislative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_legislature_\(United_States\)) elections in a few states, as well as numerous [mayoral](101146.md) races and a variety of other local offices on the ballot. [Special elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_elections) to the [United States Congress](31756.md) for newly vacant seats also took place.

In what was widely described as a "[blue wave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wave_elections_in_the_United_States)" election, [Democrats](5043544.md) both flipped the [governorship of Virginia](253093.md) and held onto the [governorship of New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_New_Jersey) by landslide margins, [flipped 13 seats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Virginia_House_of_Delegates_election) in the [Virginia House of Delegates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_House_of_Delegates), [flipped 5 seats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_New_Jersey_General_Assembly_election) in the [New Jersey General Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Jersey_General_Assembly) (reaching a [supermajority](638313.md) in the chamber), scored a [decisive redistricting referendum victory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_California_Proposition_50) in [California](5407.md), and won several other down-ballot statewide races in [Virginia](32432.md), [Pennsylvania](23332.md) and [Georgia](48830.md). In [New York City](645042.md), Democrat and self-identified [democratic socialist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_socialism) [Zohran Mamdani](64618347.md) won a three-way race in the [mayoral election](73831797.md).

Democratic success in this election was driven by widespread concern over the [economy](32022.md). Voters felt that President [Donald Trump](4848272.md) was failing to deliver on [his 2024 campaign promises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump_2024_presidential_campaign) of economic renewal from the [2021–2023 inflation surge](69605317.md). Many voters also felt that Trump's [sweeping tariffs](78448911.md) were worsening the state of the economy.[^cite_note-1] Voters in Virginia were impacted heavily by Trump's policies, as [Northern Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Virginia) is home to many federal government workers who suffered either from the [mass layoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_United_States_federal_mass_layoffs) ordered by Trump and [Elon Musk](909036.md) with the [Department of Government Efficiency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Government_Efficiency) throughout the year or the [concurrent federal government shutdown](81225965.md).[^cite_note-2]

[Hispanic voters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_and_Latino_Americans_in_politics) swung hard to the Democratic Party after a string of rightward shifts towards the [Republican Party](32070.md) starting in [2020](48410011.md), calling into question a perceived political [realignment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_realignment) among the constituency. Some Hispanic voters expressed frustration during and after the election with Trump's handling of the economy and [ICE](216537.md)'s [aggressive deportations](78314324.md) under his administration.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

Several elections also took place throughout the year, notably on April 1, 2025, which included an [election for the Wisconsin Supreme Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Wisconsin_Supreme_Court_election) and two special elections for the [United States House of Representatives](19468510.md) in Florida's [1st](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Florida's_1st_congressional_district_special_election) and [6th](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Florida's_6th_congressional_district_special_election) congressional districts.[^cite_note-5]
