**Ipil** ([/ˈiːpiːl/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English) [*EE-peel*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:Pronunciation_respelling_key)), officially the **Municipality of Ipil** ([Cebuano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cebuano_language): *Lungsod sa Ipil*; [Maguindanaon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maguindanaon_language): *Inged nu Ipil*, [Jawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawi_Alphabet): ايڠد نو ايڤل; [Chavacano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chavacano): *Municipalidad de Ipil*; [Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language): *Bayan ng Ipil*), is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality_of_the_Philippines) and capital of the [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Province) of [Zamboanga Sibugay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zamboanga_Sibugay), [Philippines](23440.md). According to the 2024 census, it has a population of 96,363 people.[^cite_note-5] Ipil is the most populous municipality of Zamboanga Sibugay, and the second most populous in Region IX after [Sindangan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sindangan).

The town hosts its only airport in the province, [Ipil Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ipil_Airport) located at Barangay Sanito, along the [Pan Philippine Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan_Philippine_Highway). Its large income and population makes it a major commercial and economic center in Zamboanga Sibugay.

## History

Ipil used to be known as **Sanito**, a place under barrio Bacalan under the Municipality of Kabasalan. It was a swampy area and a docking spot for pioneering [Ilocanos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilocano_people) who settled in the upper areas of Titay. Ipil was a jumping point for their lantsa sailing to [Zamboanga City](25167165.md). The first mayor of Ipil was Gregorio Dar, an Ilocano who came from Titay. The Dar Family were the second batch of Ilocanos who settled upon the invitation of Mariano Families who are among the first batch of Ilocanos from Luzon. When Sanito became a Town in 1949,[^cite_note-6] its name was changed to Ipil, as there were many [Ipil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intsia_bijuga) trees found within the said locale.

The territory was further reduced when, through *[Batas Pambansa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batas_Pambansa) Blg. 183* of 1982, fourteen *[barangays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barangay)* were separated to create the municipality of [Roseller Lim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roseller_Lim,_Zamboanga_Sibugay).[^cite_note-7]

### Early Ilocano routes

The first Ilocanos used Ipil as the nearest jump point in connecting Titay with Zamboanga City. They would walk via Lumbia then to Longilog then Gabo reaching Mayabang their original settlement. It was this route that they do not have to cross the rivers as it is uphill. It was the Ilocanos who first set foot on these areas. Mayabang came from the first Tagalog settlers who intermarried with the first ilocano settlers of the place.

### Ipil massacre

Main article: [1995 Ipil massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1995_Ipil_massacre)

On the morning of April 4, 1995, Ipil was attacked by approximately 200 heavily armed [Abu Sayyaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Sayyaf) militants who fired upon residents, strafed civilian homes, plundered banks, took up to 30 hostages and then [burned](184695.md) the centre of the town to the ground.[^cite_note-oca-8][^cite_note-tu-9]

The militants allegedly arrived in the town by boat and bus, and a number of them had been dressed in military clothing.

The town's [Chief of Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_Police) was reportedly killed in the attack and close to a [billion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1000000000_\(number\)) [pesos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Peso) were looted from eight commercial banks.[^cite_note-10] Army commandos pursued some rebel gunmen in nearby mountains while officials said that the rebels were looting farms and seizing civilians as "human shields" as they fled the town of[^cite_note-nyt1995-11] about 40 rebels, who may have taken hostages, were cornered in a school compound west of Ipil on April 6 when an elite army unit attacked. In the fighting that followed, the television station [GMA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GMA_Network) reported, 11 civilians were killed.[^cite_note-nyt1995-11]

## Geography

Ipil is located three hours from the key cities in the region ([Dipolog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dipolog), [Pagadian](1694645.md) and [Zamboanga City](25167165.md)). The Ipil seaport is 4 kilometres (2 mi) south of the town center.

Ipil is a significant commercial and investment center in the Zamboanga Peninsula region. Due to its central location, Ipil is considered the region's logistics and distribution hub. The town also attracts many domestic tourists within its region. Many businesses, regional and national, have set their foot in the town. This includes Budgetwise Shopping Center and Cecile's Pharmacy, both businesses tracing its roots to nearby [Zamboanga City](25167165.md), and [Gaisano Grand Malls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaisano_Grand_Malls), which has opened its first mall in the region, Gaisano Grand Ipil.
