The **Eastern Conference** is one of two [conferences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athletic_conference) that make up the [National Basketball Association](22093.md) (NBA), the other being the [Western Conference](28141073.md). Both conferences consist of 15 teams organized into three [divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_\(sport\)). The Eastern Conference comprises the [Atlantic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Division_\(NBA\)), [Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Division_\(NBA\)), and [Southeast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southeast_Division_\(NBA\)) Divisions.

The current divisional alignment was adopted at the start of the [2004–05 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004%E2%80%9305_NBA_season), when the now [Charlotte Hornets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte_Hornets) began play as the Bobcats, NBA's 30th franchise. This necessitated the move of the [New Orleans Hornets (now Pelicans)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans_Pelicans) from the Eastern Conference's [Central Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Division_\(NBA\)) to the newly created [Southwest Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwest_Division_\(NBA\)) of the [Western Conference](28141073.md).

The NBA first started awarding an Eastern Conference championship trophy during the [2000–01 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000%E2%80%9301_NBA_season), renaming it after Hall of Famer [Bob Cousy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Cousy) in the [2021–22 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_NBA_season).[^cite_note-1] In 2021–22, the league began awarding the Larry Bird Trophy to the [Eastern Conference Finals Most Valuable Player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_Conference_Finals_Most_Valuable_Player_Award), named after Hall of Famer [Larry Bird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_Bird).[^cite_note-2]
