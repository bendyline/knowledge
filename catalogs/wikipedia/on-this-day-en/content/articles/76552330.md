**Imran Ahmed** (born September 1978) is a British [political strategist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_strategist), author, and activist who has worked for [Labour](19279158.md) candidates[^cite_note-1] and is the CEO of the [Center for Countering Digital Hate](7445939.md).[^cite_note-Tablet-02Oct23-2]

## Career

Ahmed was born in Manchester, England,[^cite_note-Congress-3] and attended [Manchester Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_Grammar_School), where he was the editor of the student newspaper.[^cite_note-Tablet-02Oct23-2] He is ethnically [Pashtun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashtun).[^cite_note-4] His parents are Muslims, and he was brought up as a Muslim, but now identifies as an atheist.[^cite_note-Independent-27Mar17-5] He studied [political science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_science) at the [University of Cambridge](25978572.md) and began a career as a [political strategist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_strategist) for the [Labour Party](19279158.md).

He worked for candidates [Andy Slaughter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Slaughter) and then-[shadow foreign secretary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shadow_Secretary_of_State_for_Foreign,_Commonwealth_and_Development_Affairs) [Hilary Benn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilary_Benn).[^cite_note-auto-6][^cite_note-7]

Ahmed was head of communications for [Angela Eagle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angela_Eagle)'s brief campaign to replace [Jeremy Corbyn](415036.md) in the [2016 Labour Party leadership election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Labour_Party_leadership_election_\(UK\)).[^cite_note-Guardian-22Sep16-8] A few hours after Eagle formally declared that she was challenging Corbyn for leadership of the Labour Party, someone broke a window in the building holding Eagle's constituency party headquarters in [Wallasey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wallasey). Ahmed issued a press release under the headline "Violence and threats in past 24 hours", saying that "A brick was thrown through the window of Angela Eagle's constituency office in Wallasey either overnight or this morning."[^cite_note-Guardian-22Sep16-8] When a journalist from *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)* questioned this version of the incident, Ahmed banned party staff at the constituency office from talking to the journalist.[^cite_note-Guardian-22Sep16-8] An internal investigation by the Labour Party found that "Eagle's office had endured a 'significant amount of abuse', including intimidating phone calls that led staff to unplug the phone, a death threat towards her and what appeared to be coordinated denial of service attacks on her internet."[^cite_note-9]

Ahmed was working as a Labour advisor in [Portcullis House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portcullis_House) when the [22 March 2017 Westminster terrorist attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Westminster_attack) occurred, and later gave an interview to *[The Independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Independent)* about what happened, and how he felt.[^cite_note-Independent-27Mar17-5]

In 2018, he founded the [Center for Countering Digital Hate](7445939.md), a not-for-profit with the stated aim of challenging hate and [misinformation](2203174.md) online.[^cite_note-auto-6] In April 2020, he was appointed to the United Kingdom's Steering Committee of the Pilot Task Force of the [Commission for Countering Extremism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commission_for_Countering_Extremism).[^cite_note-10] [Morgan McSweeney](75790152.md), was formerly a director of this organisation.[^cite_note-Grauniad-24Dec25-11]

In December 2025, the [US State Department](31975.md) imposed a visa sanction on Ahmed for leading what US Secretary of State [Marco Rubio](5502549.md) called "organized efforts to coerce American platforms to censor, demonetize, and suppress American viewpoints they oppose".[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] At the time this was announced, Ahmed was living in Washington, D.C., with his family.[^cite_note-Grauniad-24Dec25-11]

## Personal life

Ahmed has a [green card](300237.md) which gives him permanent residency permit in the US; his wife and daughter are US citizens.[^cite_note-14]
