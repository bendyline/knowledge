**Al Khawkhah District** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): مديرية الخوخة) is a [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Yemen) of the [Al Hudaydah Governorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Hudaydah_Governorate), Yemen. As of 2003, the district had a population of 33,764 inhabitants.[^cite_note-1]
