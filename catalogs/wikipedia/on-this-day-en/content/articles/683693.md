**East China** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 华东; [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *huá dōng*) is a [geographical region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_regions_of_China) in the [People's Republic of China](5405.md), mainly consisting of seven [province-level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province-level_divisions_of_China) [administrative divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_division), namely the [provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_China) (from north to south) [Shandong](105032.md), [Jiangsu](166412.md), [Anhui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anhui), [Zhejiang](96602.md), [Jiangxi](173817.md), [Fujian](88585.md), and the [direct-administered municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct-administered_municipality) [Shanghai](27643.md).

The region was defined in 1945 as the [jurisdiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurisdiction) area of the [Central Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Committee_of_the_Chinese_Communist_Party)'s **East China Bureau** (华东局), which was a merger [politburo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politburo) agency of the Shandong Bureau and the Central China Bureau previously established during the [Second Sino-Japanese War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Sino-Japanese_War). After the [founding of the People's Republic of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Founding_of_the_People's_Republic_of_China), the region included all the aforementioned provinces except [Jiangxi](173817.md), which was previously considered part of [South Central China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Central_China) before being reassigned in 1961. The East China Bureau was abolished in 1966 due to the [Cultural Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_Revolution), but in 1970 the [fourth five-year plan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourth_five-year_plan_\(China\)) redefined the region as the **East China Coordinated Region** (华东协作区), which supported the [logistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_logistics) of the [Jinan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jinan_Military_Region) and [Nanjing Military Regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanjing_Military_Region). This geographical definition was retained after the [economic reform of the 1980s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reform_and_opening_up).

Since the [Chinese Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Government) [claims sovereignty](66555.md) over [Taiwan](25734.md) and the few outlying islands of Fujian ([Kinmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinmen) and [Matsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matsu_Islands)), which have been ruled by the exiled [Government of the Republic of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_the_Republic_of_China) (who [fled to Taiwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retreat_of_the_government_of_the_Republic_of_China_to_Taiwan) after losing the [Chinese Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Civil_War) in 1949) as [its own territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_area_of_the_Republic_of_China), the nominal "[Taiwan Province, People's Republic of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan_Province,_People's_Republic_of_China)" is also classified in this region.
