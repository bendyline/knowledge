The opening ceremony of the [2025 SEA Games](45634454.md) took place on 9 December 2025 across [Bangkok](56615.md), began at 19\:00 [ICT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indochina_Time) (12\:00 [UTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinated_Universal_Time)) and ended at 21\:30 [ICT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indochina_Time) (14\:30 [UTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinated_Universal_Time)).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] The Games was formally opened by the [king of Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monarchy_of_Thailand), [Vajiralongkorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vajiralongkorn). The ceremony marked the decades of the [1975](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1975_SEAP_Games), [1985](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985_SEA_Games) and [1995 Southeast Asian Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1995_SEA_Games).
