The **Maaten al-Sarra Air Base** is an airbase in southernmost [Libya](17633.md) located near the [Ma'tan as-Sarra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ma'tan_as-Sarra) oasis in the [Kufra](3337477.md) [district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Libya). It is one of the 13 military airbases in Libya.[^cite_note-5]

During the final phase of the [Chadian-Libyan conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chadian-Libyan_conflict), Maaten al-Sarra was the main air base in Southern Libya,[^cite_note-6] having three modern [runways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runway) and parking space for over 100 [combat aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_aircraft).[^cite_note-7]

## History

When in 1987 the [Chadian army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_of_Chad) attacked Libyan positions in northern [Chad](5488.md), in the [Toyota War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota_War), after a string of victories the Chadians were defeated in August in the [Battle of Aouzou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Aouzou), mainly due to Libyan airpower. The Chadian command decided that before renewing the offensive against the [Aouzou Strip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aouzou_Strip), it was vital to deal with the threat represented by the [Libyan Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libyan_Air_Force_\(1951-2011\)), and thus planned [a surprise attack on Maaten al-Sarra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Maaten_al-Sarra), 60 miles north of the Chadian–Libyan border. The attack, which took place on September 5, was one of the most significant Chadian victories in the conflict, with 1,700 Libyans killed and 300 taken prisoner.[^cite_note-8] The Chadian victory, because of Libyan demoralization at home and international hostility, brought an agreed [ceasefire](592591.md) on September 11 that put an end to the war.[^cite_note-9]

## Accidents and incidents

On 9 February 2026, a medical helicopter crashed at the base while returning from a medical evacuation mission, killing all five occupants on board.[^cite_note-10]
