**Larache Province** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): إقليم العرائش; ⵜⴰⵙⴳⴰ ⵏ ⵍⵄⵔⴰⵢⵛ) is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Morocco) in the [Tanger-Tetouan-Al Hoceima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanger-Tetouan-Al_Hoceima) region of [Morocco](19291.md). Its capital is [Larache](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larache). According to the 2024 census, it had a population of 510,211.[^cite_note-1]

The major cities are:

- [Larache](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larache)

- [Ksar el Kebir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ksar_el_Kebir)

## History

The territory of present-day Larache Province has a long history linked to its strategic position on the Atlantic coast of northern Morocco. The region experienced various occupations during antiquity and the Islamic period, notably around the archaeological site of [Lixus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lixus_\(ancient_city\)).

## Geography

Larache Province is located in northwestern Morocco, along the Atlantic coast. It is bordered:

- to the north by the [Tangier-Assilah Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangier-Assilah_Prefecture);

- to the east by [Chefchaouen Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chefchaouen_Province) and [Ouezzane Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouezzane_Province);

- to the south by [Sidi Kacem Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidi_Kacem_Province);

- to the west by the [Atlantic Ocean](698.md).

The province includes two main geographical areas:

- a mountainous zone in the north and east (Beni Arous, Aaroura, Tazrout), part of the western [Rif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rif);

- a broad plain in the south, corresponding to the extension of the [Gharb plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gharb_\(Morocco\)) (Ksar el-Kebir, Larache, Zouada, Laouamra), which is favorable to agriculture.

The climate is Mediterranean with oceanic influence, characterized by mild, wet winters and moderate summers.

## Administrative divisions

The province is composed of several urban and rural communes. It notably includes the municipalities of [Larache](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larache) and [Ksar el Kebir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ksar_el_Kebir), along with a number of rural communes distributed across its territory.
