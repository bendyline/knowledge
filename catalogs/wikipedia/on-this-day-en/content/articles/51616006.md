# City court

A **city court** or **municipal court** is a [court of law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court_of_law) with [jurisdiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurisdiction) limited to a [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City) or other [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality). It typically addresses "violations of [city ordinances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_ordinance) and may also have jurisdiction over minor criminal cases...and over certain civil cases."[^cite_note-Gifis-1][^cite_note-Ballentine1916-2] Examples include [Moscow City Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_City_Court) in Russia, [Municipal Court of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_Court_of_Chicago)[^cite_note-Goldfield2006-3][^cite_note-IL-JudicialTimeline-4] and [New York City Civil Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City_Civil_Court) in the United States.
