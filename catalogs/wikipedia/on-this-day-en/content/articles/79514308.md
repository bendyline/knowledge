***Punch*** is a 2024 play written by British playwright [James Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Graham_\(playwright\)). It is based on the non-fiction book *Right from Wrong* by Jacob Dunne. It premiered at [Nottingham Playhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nottingham_Playhouse) in 2024, before transferring to [Young Vic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Vic) in 2025.[^cite_note-1]

It subsequently transferred to the [Apollo Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apollo_Theatre) in the [West End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_End_theatre) from 22 September to 29 November 2025, where it ran concurrently with its [Broadway](725252.md) counterpart.

## Synopsis

Jacob Dunne punches a man with fatal consequences.[^cite_note-\:1-2]

## Background

The play is based on Jacob Dunne's memoir, *Right From Wrong*.[^cite_note-\:0-3] Dunne was jailed for the manslaughter of trainee paramedic James Hodgkinson in 2011.[^cite_note-Stand-4] Hodgkinson had been visiting Nottingham to attend a test match,[^cite_note-5] and was killed as a result of an unprovoked[^cite_note-6] single punch.[^cite_note-7] Through [Restorative justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Restorative_justice), Hodgkinson's mother Joan Scourfield later forgave Dunne.[^cite_note-8] Dunne and Scourfield have subsequently campaigned about the risks of a single punch,[^cite_note-9] and taken part in Question and Answer sessions together following some performances of the play.[^cite_note-10]

After only a handful of performances[^cite_note-Stand-4] Dunne was no longer able to watch the second act of the play.[^cite_note-Stand-4] After the interval Punch explores him meeting Hodgkinson's mum, a meeting that Dunne acknowledged had a profound effect on him.[^cite_note-Stand-4]

*Punch* was written by [James Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Graham_\(playwright\)), with direction by Adam Penford.[^cite_note-\:0-3] Graham is an [Olivier Award](16432264.md)-winner who is often described as a state of the nation playwright.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] His previous work includes *[Best of Enemies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_of_Enemies_\(play\)), [Dear England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dear_England), [This House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/This_House_\(play\)),* and *[Sherwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherwood_\(2022_TV_series\)).* [^cite_note-\:2-13] The original productions featured design by [Anna Fleischle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Fleischle),[^cite_note-\:Text-14] with lighting by Robbie Butler,[^cite_note-\:Text-14] sound design by Alexandra Faye Braithwaite,[^cite_note-\:Text-14] and movement direction by Leanne Pinder.[^cite_note-\:Text-14]

The play was published by [Methuen Drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methuen_Publishing) in March 2025.[^cite_note-15] Jacob Dunne and James Graham appeared as guests on the [Rest is Politics: Leading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leading_\(podcast\)) podcast to discuss the play and its significance.[^cite_note-16]

## Production history

### Nottingham (2024)

The world premiere of *Punch* opened on 4 May 2024, at the [Nottingham Playhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nottingham_Playhouse) for a limited run and closed on 25 May 2024.[^cite_note-17] The cast included Julie Hesmondhalgh, Tony Hirst, [David Shields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Shields_\(actor\)), Alec Boaden, Shalisha James-Davis, and Emma Pallant.[^cite_note-\:2-13] The production received critical acclaim[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20] and was nominated for two [WhatsOnStage Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WhatsOnStage_Awards), including Best New Play.[^cite_note-\:1-2]

### Off-West End (2025)

*Punch* transferred to [Young Vic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Vic) the following year. Previews began 1 March 2025, with an opening night on 6 March.[^cite_note-Young-21] The original cast returned,[^cite_note-\:1-2] with the show running until 26 April.[^cite_note-Young-21]

### West End (2025)

The show was transferred to the [Apollo Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apollo_Theatre) in the [West End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_End_theatre) from 22 September to 29 November 2025,[^cite_note-22] where it ran concurrently with the Broadway production.[^cite_note-23]

### Broadway (2025)

*Punch* premiered on Broadway on 10 September 2025 as part of the [Manhattan Theatre Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhattan_Theatre_Club)'s 2025-2026 season at the [Samuel J. Friedman Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_J._Friedman_Theatre).[^cite_note-24] Initial casting was announced in June 2025, with [Will Harrison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Harrison_\(actor\)) playing Jacob.[^cite_note-25]

### Uk Tours

Following the West End run the play returned to [Nottingham Playhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nottingham_Playhouse) in March 2026, before a short tour at [Leeds Playhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeds_Playhouse) from 7 to 11 April and [Theatre Royal, Plymouth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theatre_Royal,_Plymouth) from 14 to 18 April 2026.[^cite_note-26]
