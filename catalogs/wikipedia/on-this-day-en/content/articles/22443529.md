**Financial compensation** refers to the act of providing a person with [money](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Money) or other things of [economic value](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_value) in exchange for their [goods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goods), [labor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Work_\(human_activity\)), or to provide for the costs of injuries that they have incurred. The aim of financial compensation is the preservation of relationships between those engaged in economic exchange.[^cite_note-miat-1]

Kinds of financial compensation include:

- [Damages](8134.md), legal term for the financial compensation recoverable by reason of another's breach of duty

- [Nationalization compensation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nationalization#Compensation), compensation paid in the event of nationalization of property

- [Payment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Payment)

- [Remuneration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remuneration)&#x20;

  - [Deferred compensation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deferred_compensation)

  - [Executive compensation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_compensation)

  - [Royalties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royalties)

  - [Salary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salary)

  - [Wage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wage)

  - [Employee benefits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Employee_benefits)

- [Workers' compensation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Workers'_compensation), to protect employees who have incurred work-related injuries

Financial compensation is often provided after [service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Service_\(economics\)) delivery failure in order to regain [customer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Customer) trust.[^cite_note-tfor-2] An associated response is to offer an [apology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apology_\(act\)) that communicates the transgressor feels [remorse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remorse).[^cite_note-miat-1]

Financial compensation may be offered as an [incentive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incentive).[^cite_note-dfcf-3] Some offers are too good to refuse. They may become an undue inducement in which they distort people's judgment, contravene their interests and thereby cause [harm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harm).[^cite_note-dfcf-3]

Financial compensation may be imposed by a [judge](44719.md), before a court, to a victim.[^cite_note-trf-4]
