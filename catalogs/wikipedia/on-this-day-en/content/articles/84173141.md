As a result of the [resumption of hostillities](84167596.md) in Yemen in a spillover of the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md) and the [increasing tensions in the Middle East](75895273.md) and in Yemen (after the [2026 Sanaa airport strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Sanaa_airport_strike) by the Saudi allied Yemeni government), the Houthis launched a barrage of strikes against Saudi Arabia who they deem of helping the Yemini [Presidential Leadership Council](70488867.md).[^cite_note-1]

The attacks have led to widespread civilian injuries in Saudi Arabia with 73 or more civilians injured. No fatalities were reported immediately.[^cite_note-2]

The attacks came after the Houthis alleged that Saudis had taken part in the counteroffensive by the Yemeni Government forces and struck a prison facility.[^cite_note-3]

The forces of the [Saudi-led intervention in Yemen](46230181.md) declared the attacks 'sinful' and vowed of direct military retaliation against the Houthis with Major General [Turki al Maliki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turki_al_Maliki?action=edit\&redlink=1) saying that the Saudi Arabian forces will take 'firm action'.[^cite_note-4]
