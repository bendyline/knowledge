**Harvey Weinstein** (born March 19, 1952) is an American former film producer and convicted sex offender. He co-founded the film production companies [Miramax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miramax) and [the Weinstein Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Weinstein_Company). His productions have grossed over \$10.78 billion worldwide, and he has won an [Academy Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award) and eight [Tony Awards](54741.md). Weinstein was one of the most influential producers in Hollywood until his downfall in 2017 following multiple [sexual abuse allegations](55516959.md).

In October 2017, following sexual abuse allegations dating back to the late 1970s, Weinstein was dismissed from his company and expelled from the [Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Motion_Picture_Arts_and_Sciences). More than 80 women made allegations of sexual harassment or rape against him by October 31.[^cite_note-9] The allegations sparked the [#MeToo](55551931.md) [social media](5897742.md) campaign and subsequent sexual abuse allegations against many powerful figures worldwide; this phenomenon is referred to as the "[Weinstein effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weinstein_effect)".

In May 2018, Weinstein was arrested and charged with [rape](7980471.md) in [New York City](645042.md); in February 2020, he was found guilty of two of five felony counts.[^cite_note-10] Weinstein was sentenced to 23 years in prison on March 11, 2020.[^cite_note-11] On July 20, 2021, Weinstein was [extradited](239648.md) to [Los Angeles](18110.md) to face further charges at a subsequent trial, where he was found guilty of three of seven charges on December 19, 2022.[^cite_note-Salahieh-12] Weinstein was sentenced to 16 years in the Los Angeles trial, with his California prison term required to be served separately from his New York sentence.[^cite_note-LA-16-not-concurrent-13]

On April 25, 2024, the [New York Court of Appeals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Court_of_Appeals) overturned the New York rape convictions because of "egregious errors" of procedure, ordering a retrial.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-\:0-15][^cite_note-\:2-16] Weinstein remained in prison because of the California conviction. The retrial began on April 15, 2025.[^cite_note-juryselectionprocess-17][^cite_note-18] On June 11, 2025, Weinstein was convicted in a mixed verdict.[^cite_note-19] On June 12, 2025, an additional rape charge case against Weinstein was given a mistrial.[^cite_note-20] On June 25, 2026, a month after a second mistrial was declared, Manhattan District Attorney [Alvin Bragg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alvin_Bragg) announced that Weinstein would not receive an additional retrial for the alleged rape of Jessica Mann.[^cite_note-21]

On September 23, 2026, Weinstein was sentenced to 15 years in prison, followed by five years of post-release supervision, and was ordered to register as a sex offender.[^cite_note-22]

## Early life

Weinstein was born on March 19, 1952, in the [Flushing](267693.md) neighborhood of [Queens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queens), in New York City,[^cite_note-britannica-1][^cite_note-23] to diamond cutter Max Weinstein (died 1976)[^cite_note-NYT01-24] and his wife, Miriam (*née* Postel; died 2016).[^cite_note-NYT01-24][^cite_note-auto-25] The family is [Jewish](25955086.md),[^cite_note-26] and Weinstein's maternal grandparents [immigrated](4599312.md) to the United States from [Poland](22936.md).[^cite_note-annistonmiramaxnamesakediesat90-27] Harvey grew up with a younger brother, [Bob](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Weinstein), in the [Electchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomonok,_Queens) [housing co-op](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Housing_cooperative) in New York City; he graduated from [John Bowne High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Bowne_High_School) and then attended the [State University of New York at Buffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_at_Buffalo).[^cite_note-biskind-28] With his brother, Bob, and Corky Burger, he formed *Harvey & Corky Productions,* which independently produced [rock concerts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_concert) in [Buffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buffalo,_New_York) through most of the 1970s.[^cite_note-biskind-28][^cite_note-Mason-29] Among the top acts it brought to town were [Frank Sinatra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Sinatra), [the Who](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Who), [Jackson Browne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackson_Browne), and [the Rolling Stones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rolling_Stones). Weinstein's longtime friend Jonathan A. Dandes followed him to Buffalo and has described Harvey as "aggressive" and "consumed" in matters of business.[^cite_note-30][^cite_note-31] Although Weinstein attended the University at Buffalo from 1969 to 1973, he did not ultimately graduate, choosing instead to concentrate on his business interests.[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33]

## Career

### 1970s: Early work and creation of Miramax

See also: [Miramax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miramax)

In the late 1970s, using profits from their concert promotion business, Weinstein and his brother founded the independent film distribution company [Miramax Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miramax), named after their parents Miriam and Max.[^cite_note-auto-25] The company's initial releases were primarily music-oriented concert films such as [Paul McCartney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_McCartney)'s *[Rockshow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockshow)*.[^cite_note-34]

### 1980s: Success with arthouse and independent films

In the early 1980s, Miramax Films acquired the rights to two British films of benefit shows filmed for the [human rights organization](30335636.md) [Amnesty International](18947898.md). Working closely with [Martin Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Lewis_\(humorist\)), the producer of the original films, the Weinstein brothers edited the two films into one movie tailored for the American market. The resulting film was released as *[The Secret Policeman's Other Ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Secret_Policeman's_Ball_mediagraphy#US_film_releases)* in May 1982, and it became Miramax Films' first hit. The movie raised considerable sums of money for Amnesty International and was credited by Amnesty with having helped to raise its profile in the United States.[^cite_note-biskind-28][^cite_note-Mason-29] The Weinsteins slowly built upon this success throughout the 1980s with [arthouse films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_film) which achieved critical attention and modest commercial success.

Weinstein and Miramax Films gained wider attention in 1988 with the release of [Errol Morris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Errol_Morris)' documentary *[The Thin Blue Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Thin_Blue_Line_\(1988_film\))*, which detailed the struggle of [Randall Dale Adams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randall_Dale_Adams), a wrongfully convicted inmate sentenced to [death row](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_row). The publicity that soon surrounded the case resulted in Adams' release and nationwide publicity for Miramax Films. In 1989, their successful launch release of [Steven Soderbergh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Soderbergh)'s *[Sex, Lies, and Videotape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex,_Lies,_and_Videotape)* propelled Miramax Films to become the most successful independent studio in America.[^cite_note-35]

In 1989, Miramax Films also released two [arthouse films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_film), [Peter Greenaway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Greenaway)'s *[The Cook, the Thief, His Wife & Her Lover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Cook,_the_Thief,_His_Wife_&_Her_Lover)* and director [Pedro Almodóvar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Almod%C3%B3var)'s film *[Tie Me Up! Tie Me Down!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tie_Me_Up!_Tie_Me_Down!)*, both of which received an [X-rating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-rating) from the [MPAA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MPAA) [rating board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MPAA_film_rating_system), effectively stopping nationwide release for these films. Weinstein sued the [MPAA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_Picture_Association) over the rating system. His lawsuit was later thrown out, but the MPAA introduced the [NC-17](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NC-17) rating two months later.[^cite_note-36]

### 1990s–2004: Further success, Disney ownership deal



Weinstein at the 2002 [Cannes Film Festival](101460.md)

Miramax Films continued to grow its library of films and directors until, in 1993, after the success of *[The Crying Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Crying_Game)*, [Disney](37398.md) offered the Weinsteins \$80 million for ownership of Miramax Films.[^cite_note-37] The brothers agreed to the deal which in turn cemented their Hollywood clout and also ensured that they would remain at the head of their company. The following year, Miramax Films released its first blockbuster, [Quentin Tarantino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quentin_Tarantino)'s *[Pulp Fiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulp_Fiction)*, and distributed the popular independent film *[Clerks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clerks_\(film\))*.

Miramax Films won its first [Academy Award for Best Picture](61702.md) in 1997 with the victory of *[The English Patient](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_English_Patient_\(film\))*. *Pulp Fiction* was nominated in 1995 but lost to *[Forrest Gump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forrest_Gump)*.[^cite_note-38] This started a string of critical successes that included *[Good Will Hunting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Will_Hunting)* (1997) and *[Shakespeare in Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shakespeare_in_Love)* (1998), with both films receiving several awards, including numerous Academy Awards.[^cite_note-39][^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42]

### 2005–2017: The Weinstein Company

See also: [The Weinstein Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Weinstein_Company)

On September 30, 2005, the Weinstein brothers left Miramax Films to form their own production company, [The Weinstein Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Weinstein_Company) (TWC), with several other media executives, directors [Quentin Tarantino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quentin_Tarantino) and [Robert Rodriguez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Rodriguez), and [Colin Vaines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colin_Vaines), who had successfully run the production department at Miramax Films for 10 years.[^cite_note-43] In February 2011, filmmaker [Michael Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Moore) took legal action against the Weinstein brothers, claiming they owed him \$2.7 million in profits for his documentary *[Fahrenheit 9/11](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fahrenheit_9/11)* (2004), which he said were denied to him by "[Hollywood accounting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_accounting) tricks."[^cite_note-44] In February 2012, Moore dropped the lawsuit for an undisclosed settlement.[^cite_note-45]

Weinstein was thanked or praised in 34 [Academy Award](324.md) acceptance speeches—as many times as God, and second only to [Steven Spielberg](26940.md) (with 43 mentions), as found by an analysis of speeches made between 1966 and 2016.[^cite_note-46]

On October 8, 2017, Harvey Weinstein was fired from TWC after a list of sexual abuse charges was released to the press. After months of unsuccessful attempts to sell the company or its library, TWC filed for bankruptcy, with [Lantern Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lantern_Entertainment) subsequently purchasing all assets in 2018. The company was shut down on July 16, 2018, and its website sometime thereafter.

While lauded for opening up the [independent film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_film) market and making it financially viable, Weinstein has been criticized for the techniques he applied in his business dealings. [Peter Biskind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Biskind)'s book *Down and Dirty Pictures: Miramax, Sundance and the Rise of Independent Film*[^cite_note-biskind-28] denounced Miramax's release history and editing of arthouse films. For example, the book states that *[54](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/54_\(film\))* had been originally made as an [arthouse film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_film) but, after [Ryan Phillippe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Phillippe)'s sudden rise to stardom, Weinstein forced director [Mark Christopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Christopher_\(director\)) to re-edit and re-shoot the film to make it more mainstream.



Weinstein in 2014

Weinstein re-edited several Asian films and dubbed them in English. Weinstein tried to release the English-dubbed versions of *[Shaolin Soccer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaolin_Soccer)* and *[Hero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hero_\(2002_film\))* in the United States theatrically, but they scored badly in test screenings, leading Weinstein to release the films in United States cinemas in their original language.[^cite_note-47][^cite_note-48] Furthermore, Weinstein re-edited 1993 [Cannes](101460.md) [Palme d'Or](612946.md) winner *[Farewell My Concubine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farewell_My_Concubine_\(film\))* for American theatrical release; 1993 [Cannes](101460.md) jury head [Louis Malle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Malle) was furious. "The film we admired so much in Cannes is not the film seen in this country, which is twenty minutes shorter—but it seems longer, because it doesn't make any sense", complained Malle.[^cite_note-49]

When Weinstein was charged with handling the American release of *[Princess Mononoke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_Mononoke)*, director [Hayao Miyazaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayao_Miyazaki) was reported to have sent him a [samurai sword](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samurai_sword) in the mail. Attached to the blade was a stark message: "No cuts." Miyazaki commented on the incident: "Actually, my producer did that. Although I did go to New York to meet this man, this Harvey Weinstein, and I was bombarded with this aggressive attack, all these demands for cuts. I defeated him."[^cite_note-50] Weinstein and his brother Bob have also been criticized for altering the vision of foreign filmmakers hired to create movies for Miramax, such as on the 1997 projects *[Mimic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mimic_\(film\))* (directed by [Guillermo del Toro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guillermo_del_Toro))[^cite_note-51] and *[Nightwatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nightwatch_\(1997_film\))* (directed by [Dane](419786.md) [Ole Bornedal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ole_Bornedal)).[^cite_note-52] Weinstein has always insisted that such changes were done in the interest of creating the most [financially viable film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_finance). "I'm not cutting for fun," he said in an interview. "I'm cutting for the shit to work. All my life I served one master: the film. I love movies."[^cite_note-Mason-29][^cite_note-53]

Another example cited by Biskind was [Phillip Noyce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phillip_Noyce)'s *[The Quiet American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Quiet_American_\(2002_film\))* (2002), the release of which Weinstein delayed following the [September 11 attacks](5058690.md) owing to audience reaction in test screenings to the film's critical tone toward past U.S. foreign policy. After being told the film would go [straight to video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Straight_to_video), Noyce planned to screen the film at the [Toronto International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto_International_Film_Festival) in order to mobilize critics to pressure Miramax to release it theatrically. Weinstein decided to screen the film at the festival only after he was lobbied by star [Michael Caine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Caine), who threatened to boycott publicity for another film he had made for Miramax. *The Quiet American* received mostly positive reviews at the festival, and Miramax eventually released the film theatrically. However, it was alleged that Miramax did not make a major effort to promote the film for Academy Award consideration, though Caine was nominated for an [Academy Award for Best Actor](23245410.md).[^cite_note-biskind-28]

Weinstein acquired a reputation for ruthlessness and fits of [anger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anger).[^cite_note-54] According to Biskind, Weinstein once put a *[New York Observer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Observer)* reporter in a headlock while throwing him out of a party. On another occasion, Weinstein yelled at director [Julie Taymor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julie_Taymor) and her husband during a disagreement over a test screening of her movie *[Frida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frida_\(2002_film\)),* saying to Taymor, "You are the most arrogant person I have ever met!" and to her husband, film composer [Elliot Goldenthal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliot_Goldenthal), "I don't like the look on your face. Why don't you defend your wife, so I can beat the shit out of you?"[^cite_note-Mason-29]

In a 2004 *[New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_\(magazine\))* magazine article, Weinstein appeared somewhat repentant for his often aggressive discussions with directors and producers.[^cite_note-Mnookin-55] However, a *[Newsweek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newsweek)* story on October 13, 2008, criticized Weinstein, who was accused of "hassling [Sydney Pollack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney_Pollack) on his deathbed" about the release of the film *[The Reader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Reader_\(2008_film\))*. After Weinstein offered \$1 million to charity if the accusation could be proven, journalist [Nikki Finke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikki_Finke) published an email sent by [Scott Rudin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Rudin) on August 22 asserting that Weinstein "harassed" [Anthony Minghella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Minghella)'s widow and a bedridden Pollack until Pollack's family asked him to stop.[^cite_note-56][^cite_note-57]

In September 2009, Weinstein publicly voiced opposition to efforts to extradite [Roman Polanski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Polanski) from Switzerland to the U.S. regarding a 1977 charge that he had drugged and raped a 13-year-old girl, to which Polanski pleaded guilty before fleeing the country.[^cite_note-58] Weinstein, whose company distributed *[Roman Polanski: Wanted and Desired](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Polanski:_Wanted_and_Desired)*, a film about the Polanski case, questioned whether Polanski committed any crime,[^cite_note-59] prompting [Los Angeles County](79734.md) District Attorney [Steve Cooley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Cooley) to insist that Polanski's guilty plea indicated that his action was a crime, and that several other serious charges were pending.[^cite_note-60]

## Sexual abuse accusations, convictions and appeals

Main article: [Harvey Weinstein sexual abuse cases](55516959.md)

In October 2017, *[The New York Times](30680.md)* and *[The New Yorker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_Yorker)* reported that more than a dozen women accused Weinstein of [sexually harassing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexually_harassing), [assaulting](218407.md), or [raping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raping) them.[^cite_note-Kantor-61][^cite_note-Farrow-62] Many other women in the film industry subsequently reported similar experiences with Weinstein,[^cite_note-63][^cite_note-WaPo-64][^cite_note-65] who denied "any nonconsensual sex". As a result of these allegations, Weinstein was dismissed from his production company,[^cite_note-66] suspended from the [British Academy of Film and Television Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Academy_of_Film_and_Television_Arts),[^cite_note-67] and expelled from the [Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Motion_Picture_Arts_and_Sciences).[^cite_note-The_Guardian_October_14,_2017-68] Weinstein also resigned from the [Directors Guild of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directors_Guild_of_America)[^cite_note-69] and was denounced by leading figures in politics whom he had supported.[^cite_note-70] The [Los Angeles Police Department](218985.md) opened a criminal investigation for alleged rape,[^cite_note-71] and New York and London police began investigating other sexual assault allegations.[^cite_note-72] On October 10, 2017, Weinstein's wife, [Georgina Chapman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgina_Chapman), announced that she was leaving him;[^cite_note-Saperstein-73] their divorce was finalized in July 2021.[^cite_note-Independent20210714-74]

The sexual abuse allegations precipitated a wave of "national reckoning" against sexual harassment and assault in the United States[^cite_note-Rutenberg:_Reckoning-75] known as the [Weinstein effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weinstein_effect). Compounded by other sexual harassment cases earlier in the year, the Weinstein reports and subsequent [#MeToo hashtag campaign](55551931.md), which encouraged individuals to share their suppressed stories of [sexual misconduct](457212.md), created a cavalcade of allegations across multiple industries that brought about the swift ouster of many men in positions of power both in the United States and, as it spread, around the world.[^cite_note-Cook/Simons-76][^cite_note-USA_Today_2017-10-25-77]

[Ronan Farrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronan_Farrow) reported in *The New Yorker* in November 2017 that Weinstein had hired British-Israeli private intelligence firm [Black Cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Cube) in order to stop the publication of the abuse allegations against him. Using false identities, private investigators from Black Cube reportedly tracked and met journalists and actresses, in particular [Rose McGowan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rose_McGowan), who accused Weinstein of rape. Weinstein reportedly had Black Cube, [Kroll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kroll_Inc.) and other agencies "target, or collect information on, dozens of individuals, and compile psychological profiles that sometimes focused on their personal or sexual histories".[^cite_note-78][^cite_note-79][^cite_note-80] Former Israeli prime minister [Ehud Barak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ehud_Barak) admitted to having introduced Weinstein to Black Cube, but denied knowing how Weinstein would employ their services.[^cite_note-81]

Weinstein reportedly sought help from Farrow's father [Woody Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Allen) to help stop Farrow from reporting on the claims of sexual abuse against Weinstein. Allen declined to help.[^cite_note-82] Weinstein also reportedly used Black Cube to attempt to silence journalists [Megan Twohey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megan_Twohey) and [Jodi Kantor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jodi_Kantor) reporting on the allegations against him. According to Kantor, a Black Cube agent posing as a women's rights advocate attempted to manipulate and dupe her.[^cite_note-83] Weinstein approached [Hillary Clinton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hillary_Clinton) in an attempt to help him stop Farrow from publishing the sexual misconduct allegations against him. Clinton publicist Nick Merrill emailed Farrow and unsuccessfully attempted to convince him to not publish the story.[^cite_note-84][^cite_note-85] According to Rose McGowan, [Jennifer Siebel Newsom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Siebel_Newsom), California Governor [Gavin Newsom](418947.md)'s wife, aided Weinstein lawyer [David Boies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Boies) in attempting to bribe McGowan and keep her silent about her allegations against Weinstein.[^cite_note-86][^cite_note-87]

In 2019, the documentary *[Untouchable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Untouchable_\(2019_film\))* was released with interviews from several of his accusers.[^cite_note-88]

The [New York County District Attorney's Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_County_District_Attorney) charged Weinstein with "rape, criminal sex act, sex abuse and sexual misconduct for incidents involving two separate women" on May 25, 2018. He was arrested the same day after surrendering to the [New York City Police Department](255413.md) (NYPD)[^cite_note-BBC_News_25_May_2018-8] and released after US\$1 million [bail](170652.md) was posted on his behalf. He surrendered his passport and was required to wear an ankle monitor, with travel being restricted to New York and Connecticut. His lawyer, [Benjamin Brafman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Brafman), said Weinstein would plead not guilty.[^cite_note-The_Guardian_25_May_2018-89] A trial date was set for January 6, 2020.[^cite_note-90] On that date, Weinstein was also charged in [Los Angeles](18110.md) with raping one woman and sexually assaulting another in 2013.[^cite_note-91]

After deliberating for five days, a jury convicted Weinstein on February 24, 2020, of two of five criminal charges: one count of criminal sexual assault in the first degree and one count of rape in the third degree.[^cite_note-92][^cite_note-93][^cite_note-94][^cite_note-95] The jury found him not guilty regarding predatory sexual assault, which could have led to a life sentence.[^cite_note-96][^cite_note-97][^cite_note-98][^cite_note-99] Weinstein was remanded to jail at [Rikers Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rikers_Island) in New York City pending his sentencing hearing on March 11, when he was sentenced to 23 years in prison.[^cite_note-100][^cite_note-101][^cite_note-NYT20200225-LAtrial-102] Weinstein was then transferred to [Wende Correctional Facility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wende_Correctional_Facility) in [Erie County, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erie_County,_New_York).[^cite_note-News-103][^cite_note-U.S._News_&_World_Report-104] Through his attorneys, Weinstein stated that he would appeal the verdict.[^cite_note-105] Weinstein was stripped of his honorary [CBE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_British_Empire) (Commander of the Most Excellent Order of the British Empire) on September 18, 2020.[^cite_note-106] Once incarcerated, Weinstein hired [prison consultant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prison_consultant) [Craig Rothfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craig_Rothfeld).[^cite_note-107][^cite_note-108] By June 2021, Weinstein had been transferred to the [Mohawk Correctional Facility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohawk_Correctional_Facility) in [Rome, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome,_New_York).[^cite_note-109]

On June 2, 2022, the [New York State Supreme Court, Appellate Division, First Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_State_Supreme_Court,_Appellate_Division,_First_Department) upheld the verdicts and judgment on appeal. For the court's decision, Judge [Angela Mazzarelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angela_Mazzarelli) wrote, "We perceive no basis for reducing the sentence, and we have considered defendant's remaining arguments and find them unavailing."[^cite_note-110][^cite_note-111] On August 25, 2022, Weinstein was granted a further appeal before the [New York Court of Appeals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Court_of_Appeals).[^cite_note-112]

### California conviction

On July 20, 2021, Weinstein was flown to Los Angeles and taken to the [Twin Towers Correctional Facility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twin_Towers_Correctional_Facility).[^cite_note-113] The trial in Los Angeles commenced in October 2022. Weinstein was charged with 11 counts of rape, forcible oral copulation and sexual battery, stemming from alleged acts between 2004 and 2013.[^cite_note-114] He was found guilty of three of seven charges (four of the initial 11 charges were dropped) on December 19, 2022.[^cite_note-Salahieh-12] Convictions included charges of rape, forced oral copulation and third-degree sexual misconduct.[^cite_note-115] On February 23, 2023, Weinstein was sentenced to 16 years in prison for these convictions.[^cite_note-116][^cite_note-117] His sentence in California prisons must be served separately from (i.e., consecutively to rather than concurrently to) his time served in New York.[^cite_note-LA-16-not-concurrent-13] Weinstein is appealing the Los Angeles conviction.[^cite_note-118] On April 11, 2023, Weinstein was extradited from California and back to upstate New York's Mohawk Correctional Facility.[^cite_note-119] After the New York convictions were overturned in April 2024, and plans were made to retry him in New York City, Weinstein was transferred back to Rikers Island prison.[^cite_note-rikersreturn-2][^cite_note-weinsteinjail-120]

On June 8, 2022, Weinstein was formally charged by the [Metropolitan Police](192450.md) with two counts of indecent assault against a woman in London between July 31 and August 31, 1996.[^cite_note-121]

On July 9, 2024, New York prosecutors announced an investigation was underway for additional claims against Weinstein for sexual assault, including for claims that fall within the state's statute of limitations, though no indictment was immediately sent to the grand jury.[^cite_note-122]

### New York conviction overturned

On April 25, 2024, the [New York Court of Appeals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Court_of_Appeals) overturned the New York rape convictions and said that the trial judge had made "egregious errors" with the attorney stating Weinstein had been "tried on his character, not the evidence."[^cite_note-\:0-15] The Court of Appeals ordered a retrial.[^cite_note-\:2-16]

After the New York appeal ruling, a number of actresses who had made allegations against Weinstein responded:

[Ashley Judd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashley_Judd), who was among the first to disclose her rape, told the *New York Times*: "That is unfair to survivors. We still live in our truth. And we know what happened." Later at a press conference, Judd added: "This is what it's like to be a woman in America, living with male entitlement to our bodies."

[Rosanna Arquette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosanna_Arquette), who disclosed that Weinstein assaulted her, said in a statement to the *Hollywood Reporter*: "Harvey was rightfully convicted. It's unfortunate that the court has overturned his conviction. As a survivor, I am beyond disappointed."

[Rose McGowan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rose_McGowan), who also shared her story of assault from Weinstein, said in a video statement: "No matter what they overturn, they cannot take away who we are and what we know, what we've gone through and what we can achieve in this life. We are not victims. We are people that were injured by evil."[^cite_note-123]

#### Retrial

Despite the successful appeal, Weinstein remains set to face a retrial for the overturned New York convictions in the fall of 2024.[^cite_note-124] During a court hearing on July 19, 2024, Judge Curtis Farber ruled that Weinstein would be retried and tentatively set for the retrial to start on November 12, 2024.[^cite_note-retrial-125] Despite this, Farber also ruled that previous plans to have the retrial start in September 2024 were still an option, and the start date of the retrial would depend on pretrial discoveries.[^cite_note-retrial-125] Weinstein's lawyers have called for the retrial to begin as soon as possible.[^cite_note-retrial-125] On January 29, 2025, Judge Farber set the retrial date to April 15, 2025,[^cite_note-126] The jury selection process for the retrial began on April 15, 2025,[^cite_note-juryselectionprocess-17][^cite_note-127] with opening statements and witness testimony then beginning on April 23, 2025.[^cite_note-128][^cite_note-129][^cite_note-130] Jury deliberations in the retrial would then begin on June 5, 2025.[^cite_note-131] On June 11, 2025, Weinstein was found guilty of one count of criminal sexual act and not guilty of another. No verdict would be reached on an allegation made by a third woman.[^cite_note-132] On May 15, 2026, mistrial was declared in his retrial for the Jessica Mann case after the presiding jury remained deadlocked.[^cite_note-133] A hearing was then set for June 24, 2026 to determine whether or not prosecutors would seek another retrial for Mann's case.[^cite_note-134]

#### Additional charges in New York

On September 12, 2024, a New York grand jury indicted Weinstein on new charges, as announced by prosecutors from the Manhattan District Attorney's office.[^cite_note-135] These specific charges were initially unknown, as the indictment was sealed and only opened upon Weinstein's arraignment.[^cite_note-136] On September 18, 2024, Weinstein was arraigned in New York for this new indictment, entering a plea of not guilty to one count of criminal sexual act in the first degree.[^cite_note-additionalindiction-137][^cite_note-138] The prosecution filed a motion to consolidate the new charges into Weinstein's retrial rather than have two separate New York trials.[^cite_note-additionalindiction-137] The presiding judge granted the prosecution's motion to consolidate on October 23.[^cite_note-consolidation-139] On June 12, 2025, Weinstein received a mistrial on this charge following tensions within the jury.[^cite_note-140] Another mistrial would be declared on May 15, 2026, though the prosecution would still be granted the authority to determine whether or not another retrial would be pursued after a 30-day waiting period.[^cite_note-141]

### Dismissal of Jessica Mann case

On June 25, 2026, Manhattan District Attorney Alvin Bragg declared that Weinstein would not receive a fourth criminal trial for the alleged rape of Jessica Mann. However, Weinstein remains convicted in two separate cases, one in New York and one in California.[^cite_note-142] In the New York case he is scheduled for sentencing in September, 2026.[^cite_note-NYT26-ChargeDropped-143] In the California case, Weinstein's appeal of his 2022 rape conviction was unanimously denied by the three-judge panel of the Appeals Court, but the panel did set aside his 16-year sentence in the case because it was based, in part, on a New York conviction which has since been overturned; he will be re-sentenced.[^cite_note-NYT26-Resentence-6]

## Personal life

Weinstein has been married twice. In 1987, he married his assistant Eve Chilton; the couple divorced in 2004.[^cite_note-Mnookin-55][^cite_note-144] They had three daughters.[^cite_note-145] In 2007, Weinstein married English fashion designer and actress [Georgina Chapman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgina_Chapman).[^cite_note-146] They have a daughter and a son.[^cite_note-147][^cite_note-148] On October 10, 2017, after the sexual harassment accusations became public, Chapman announced that she was leaving Weinstein.[^cite_note-Saperstein-73] They reached a settlement in January 2018, and their divorce was finalized in July 2021.[^cite_note-Independent20210714-74]

### Activism

Weinstein was active on issues such as [gun control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gun_control), [poverty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty), [AIDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HIV/AIDS), [juvenile diabetes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diabetes_mellitus_type_1), and [multiple sclerosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiple_sclerosis) research. Until October 2017,[^cite_note-149] he served on the board of the [Robin Hood Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robin_Hood_Foundation), a [New York City](645042.md)-based non-profit that targets poverty, and co-chaired one of its annual benefits.[^cite_note-AME-150] Weinstein was critical of the lack of [universal healthcare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_health_care) in the United States.[^cite_note-151]

Weinstein has been a longtime supporter of and contributor to the [Democratic Party](5043544.md), including the campaigns of President [Barack Obama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barack_Obama) and presidential candidates [Hillary Clinton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hillary_Clinton) and [John Kerry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Kerry).[^cite_note-152] Weinstein supported Hillary Clinton's [2008 presidential campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hillary_Clinton_presidential_campaign,_2008).[^cite_note-153] In 2012, he hosted an election fundraiser for Obama at his home in [Westport, Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westport,_Connecticut).[^cite_note-ALTIMARI2012-154]

### Fashion

Weinstein was active in the fashion industry. He produced the fashion [reality show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reality_show) *[Project Runway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Runway)*, making stars of designer [Michael Kors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Kors), model [Heidi Klum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidi_Klum) and editor [Nina Garcia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nina_Garcia).[^cite_note-155] Weinstein was instrumental in the revival of [Halston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halston), collaborating with [Tamara Mellon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamara_Mellon), [Sarah Jessica Parker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Jessica_Parker), and stylist [Rachel Zoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Zoe). He licensed the option to revive the [Charles James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_James_\(designer\)) brand. Celebrities were asked to wear [Marchesa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marchesa_\(brand\)) (the label of his then-partner and later then-wife, Georgina Chapman) at least once if they were cast in a Weinstein movie. Weinstein's production companies were frequently involved in fashion-themed movies, including [Madonna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madonna_\(entertainer\))'s *[W.E.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W.E.)*, [Robert Altman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Altman)'s *[Prêt-à-Porter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pr%C3%AAt-%C3%A0-Porter_\(film\))*, and [Tom Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Ford)'s *[A Single Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Single_Man)*. Stars of Weinstein's films appeared on more than a dozen *[Vogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vogue_\(magazine\))* covers.[^cite_note-156]

## Awards and honors

Weinstein has won numerous awards. On September 26, 2000, he was awarded the [honorary degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_degree) of [Doctor of Humane Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Humane_Letters) (DHL) by the [University at Buffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_at_Buffalo).[^cite_note-163] On April 19, 2004, Weinstein was appointed an honorary [commander of the Order of the British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander_of_the_Order_of_the_British_Empire) (CBE) in recognition of his contributions to the British film industry. This award was honorary because Weinstein is not a citizen of a [Commonwealth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_of_Nations) country.[^cite_note-164] On March 2, 2012, Weinstein was made a knight of the French [Legion of Honour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Honour), in recognition of Miramax's efforts to increase the presence and popularity of foreign films in the United States.[^cite_note-165]

The University at Buffalo revoked Weinstein's honorary doctorate, saying his conduct "contradicts the spirit of the honorary degree", while French President [Emmanuel Macron](43671127.md) revoked his Legion of Honour, both in late 2017.[^cite_note-166][^cite_note-honourawardrevoke-167][^cite_note-revokehonourhuff-168] On September 18, 2020, Weinstein was stripped of his honorary CBE, following his conviction for rape and sexual assault earlier that year.[^cite_note-169][^cite_note-170]
