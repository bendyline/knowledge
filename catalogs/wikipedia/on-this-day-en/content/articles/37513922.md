**Piotta** is a village in the [municipality of Quinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quinto,_Ticino) in the Swiss [canton of Ticino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_Ticino).

## Geography



Aerial view (1964)

The village, located next to the neighbouring [Ambrì](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambr%C3%AC), lies in the [Leventina Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leventina_Valley), below the [Lepontine Alps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lepontine_Alps), and is crossed in the middle by [Ticino river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ticino_\(river\)). It is 3 km far from Quinto, 5 from [Airolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airolo) and 52 from [Bellinzona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bellinzona).

## Transport

Piotta is the location of the base station of the [Ritom funicular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ritom_funicular), linking the village to [Ritom Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lago_Ritom). Along with the neighbouring village of [Ambrì](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambr%C3%AC), Piotta is served by the infrequently served [Ambrì-Piotta railway station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambr%C3%AC-Piotta_railway_station) on the [Gotthard railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gotthard_railway), and is located close to [Ambri Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambri_Airport).

## Sport

The village hosts the professional ice hockey team [HC Ambrì-Piotta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HC_Ambr%C3%AC-Piotta), that plays in indoor sporting arena of "[Valascia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valascia)", located in Ambrì.[^cite_note-1]
