The **Prime Minister of the Government of Equatorial Guinea** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Primer Ministro del Gobierno de Guinea Ecuatorial*; [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Premier ministre du gouvernement de la Guinée équatoriale*; [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language): *Primeiro-Ministro do Governo da Guiné Equatorial*) is the [head of government](37570.md) of the [Republic of Equatorial Guinea](9366.md). They are member of the [Council of Ministers](16902309.md), which is chaired by the [President of the Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_presidents_of_Equatorial_Guinea). The appointment of the Prime Minister is made by the President, and since incumbent President [Teodoro Obiang Nguema Mbasogo](263622.md) came to power, all prime ministers have been affiliated with the ruling party, the [Democratic Party of Equatorial Guinea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_of_Equatorial_Guinea) (PDGE). Generally the post is responsible for administrative coordination within the country's government.[^cite_note-1] The incumbent is [Manuel Osa Nsue Nsua](77653363.md), having taken office on 17 August 2024.[^cite_note-2]

## History

The name of the position used to be President of the Council of Autonomous Government during the time of [Spanish Guinea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Guinea).[^cite_note-3] [Bonifacio Ondó Edu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonifacio_Ond%C3%B3_Edu) was the first person to hold the preceding office of prime minister of [Spanish Guinea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Guinea), taking effect on 15 December 1963. After independence, the title was post was defunct until 1982, when it was restored and bestowed upon [Cristino Seriche Bioko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristino_Seriche_Bioko), an independent politician.
