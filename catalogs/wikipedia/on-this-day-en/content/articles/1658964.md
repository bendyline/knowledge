**Minna** ([Pronunciation](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/2/22/Minna_%28gari%29.ogg/Minna_%28gari%29.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>)is a city in the [North Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Belt) of [Nigeria](21383.md). It is the capital city of [Niger State](1723431.md), one of Nigeria's 36 federal [states](226734.md). Its original major ethnic groups are the [Gbagyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gbagyi_people), Nupe and the [Hausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nupe_people) peoples.[^cite_note-4]



Minna, Niger State Government House

## History

Archaeological evidence suggests settlement in the area dates back to about 47,000–37,000 years ago. [Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim) culture filtered into Minna by way of the ancient Saharan trade routes much later, and the city contains many [mosques](19894.md) including Minna Central Mosque and Muslim organizations like the [Islamic Education Trust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Educational_Trust), Minna, Muslim Students' Society of Nigeria - Minna Area Council (MSSN-MNAC), Da'watu-Ilallahi-Wa-Rasulihi Association (DAWRA), etc. Sharia law is practised. Christianity is the second most practised religion in the city. Popular churches include the [Faith Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faith_Church), [Grace Baptist Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grace_Baptist_Church), [Nupe Kalvari Churches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nupe_Kalvari_Church?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Anglican Churches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican_Church), [ECWA churches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evangelical_Church_Winning_All), [Baptist churches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baptist_Church), [Victory Christian Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victory_Christian_Church), the [Apostolic Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apostolic_Christian_Church) and many others.[^cite_note-5]

Minna is the home state of Nigeria's former military President [Gen. Ibrahim B. Babangida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_Babangida), and of former Head of State Gen. [Abdulsalami Abubakar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdulsalami_Abubakar).

## Pollution

Based on research carried out, it was reported that Minna is being affected by [land pollution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land_pollution) caused by improper disposal of solid waste products. This has caused several diseases such as Malaria fever, Yellow fever, Lassa fever, etc.[^cite_note-6]

## Climate

Minna has a typical [Middle Belt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Belt) [tropical savanna climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_savanna_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *Aw*) with two seasons: an arid, dusty, [harmattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmattan)-dominated [dry season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_season) from November to April and a humid, oppressive [wet season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wet_season) dominated by monsoonal air masses from May to October. Temperatures are hot to sweltering year round, except at the height of the wet season when air temperatures are merely very warm but the high humidity makes it equally or more uncomfortable than the hotter dry season.

## Literary Festival

The current trend of literary and book art festivals in Nigeria, in the last 15 years, could be traced to Minna, Niger State, which started its literary and arts festival in May 1995.[^cite_note-9]

## Economy



A rice farm and local farmers returning from the farm at Sabon Daga area of Minna, Niger state.

[Cotton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton), [guinea corn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinea_corn) (sorghum), [Maize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize) and [ginger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ginger) are the main agricultural products of the city. Yam is also extensively cultivated throughout the city. The economy also supports cattle trading, brewing, [shea nut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shea_nut) processing and [gold](12240.md) mining. There are also FMCGs such as PZ Cussons that deal in toilet soaps, baby products, medicaments and so on. [^cite_note-britannica-concise-10] Traditional industries and crafts in Minna include [leather](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leather) work and [metalworking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metal).

## Transport



Transportation in Minna

Minna is connected to neighbouring cities by road. [Abuja](56823.md), the capital of the country, is only 150 km away. Minna is also connected by [railroad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rail_transport_in_Nigeria) to both [Kano](508782.md) in the north and [Ibadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibadan) and [Lagos](85232.md) in the south. The city is served by [Minna Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minna_Airport).

## Education and health

Minna has many primary health clinics with the [General Hospital, Minna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Hospital,_Minna?action=edit\&redlink=1) serving as the general health care.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]
