**Rashid bin Abdullah bin Ahmed Al Khalifa** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): راشد بن عبدالله بن أحمد آل خليفة; born 1954) is a member of the [Bahraini royal family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Khalifa)[^cite_note-mer1july-1] and since 2004 has been the [minister of interior](32625782.md) of [Bahrain](18933277.md).

## Family

Rashid is the only son of Abdullah bin Ahmed Al Khalifa, son of Ahmad bin Muhammad Al Khalifa with Saeda, son of Muhammad bin Isa Al Khalifa Al Haj, son of [Isa ibn Ali Al Khalifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isa_ibn_Ali_Al_Khalifa) ruler of Bahrain.[^cite_note-2]

His son, Abdullah bin Rashid bin Abdullah Al Khalifa, was appointed Bahrain's ambassador to the United States in April 2017.[^cite_note-allgov-3] He replaced another member of the royal family, [Abdullah bin Mohammad bin Rashed Al Khalifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdullah_bin_Mohammad_bin_Rashed_Al_Khalifa),[^cite_note-allgov-3] who in 2013 replaced [Houda Nonoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houda_Nonoo), who had served in the post since 2008.

## Career

Khalifa served as [chief of staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_staff) of the [Bahrain Defence Force](67658.md) from October 2001 to May 2004 with the rank of [major general](36301471.md).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-wt24-5]

Khalifa was appointed by [King Hamad](392300.md) as interior minister in May 2004.[^cite_note-6] Khalifa replaced [Mohammed bin Khalifa bin Hamad Al Khalifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammed_bin_Khalifa_bin_Hamad_Al_Khalifa) in the post,[^cite_note-wt24-5][^cite_note-7] who had been in office since 1974, and was dismissed following the clashes between [Shia](26961.md) protesters and security forces in [Manama](20481.md).[^cite_note-8] Mohammed was appointed deputy head of the ruling family council. Rashid was promoted to the rank of [lieutenant general](201916.md) following his appointment as interior minister.[^cite_note-9]
