**Jin Mingri** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language): 金明日, born 1969), also known as **Ezra Jin**, is the pastor of [Zion Church of Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zion_Church_of_Beijing), an independent Chinese Christian church in Beijing, China. The church started in 2007 and soon grew to over 1,000 worshippers.[^cite_note-1] Journalist [Evan Osnos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evan_Osnos) said Jin has a "lively televangelist flair".[^cite_note-Osnos-2]

Born in [Heilongjiang](173816.md), he is part of the [Korean ethnic minority in China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koreans_in_China).[^cite_note-Kurlberg2020-3] He is a graduate of [Beijing University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing_University) (BA), [Nanjing Union Theological Seminary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanjing_Union_Theological_Seminary) (MDiv) and [Fuller Theological Seminary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuller_Theological_Seminary) (DMin).[^cite_note-4] Following the [1989 Tiananmen Square protests and massacre](44534.md), he started attending a [Three-Self Patriotic Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three-Self_Patriotic_Movement) church. Within a few months, he became a [Christian convert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conversion_to_Christianity).[^cite_note-Kurlberg2020-3]

In 2018, the authorities shut down the church and put the pastor under house arrest.[^cite_note-5] In October 2025, Jin was detained by Chinese authorities along with more than 20 other members of Zion Church.[^cite_note-6] In July 2026, Jin was released and arrived in Los Angeles on July 4.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]
