# Prisons in Hong Kong

Further information on prisons in other countries: [list of prisons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_prisons)

**Prisons in Hong Kong** are [correctional facilities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Correctional_facilities) in [Hong Kong](13404.md), which are managed by the [Correctional Services Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_Correctional_Services). Facilities have different purposes.&#x20;

## Description

Prisons in Hong Kong comprise [correctional facilities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Correctional_facilities) in [Hong Kong](13404.md), a city and special administrative region of [China](5405.md). They are managed by the [Correctional Services Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_Correctional_Services). Facilities have different purposes, including training centres,[^cite_note-1] detention centres, rehabilitation centres, and drug addiction treatment centres.[^cite_note-2]

Hong Kong has one of the highest rates of [imprisonment](213806.md) in the region at around 163 per 100,000. Imprisonment rates have declined from levels above 200 per 100,000 during 1991-1996 due to fluctuations in the numbers of [illegal immigrants](7124412.md).

This is a **list of prisons in Hong Kong** are managed (or previously managed) by the [Correctional Services Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_Correctional_Services).

### Hong Kong Island

- Cape Collinson Correctional Institution, [Cape Collinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Collinson), Hong Kong Island&#x20;

  (Male young offenders undergoing training under the Training Centres Ordinance)

- Chi Lan Rehabilitation Centre, [Shek O](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shek_O), Hong Kong Island&#x20;

  (Female young offenders undergoing Phase I training under the Rehabilitation Centres Ordinance)

- Ma Hang Prison, Ma Hang, Hong Kong Island&#x20;

  (Male adult prisoners and clinically old prisoners of low security risk)

- Pak Sha Wan Correctional Institution, [Stanley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley,_Hong_Kong), Hong Kong Island&#x20;

  (Male adult prisoners)

- [Stanley Prison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_Prison), [Stanley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley,_Hong_Kong), Hong Kong Island&#x20;

  (Male adult convicted prisoners and male adult [remand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remand_\(detention\)) prisoners)

- Tung Tau Correctional Institution, [Stanley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley,_Hong_Kong), Hong Kong Island&#x20;

  (Male adult prisoners of low security risk)

- Queen Mary Hospital Custodial Ward, Hong Kong Island&#x20;

  (Male inmates who are suffering from illness and referred out by the Medical Officers of various penal institutions)

- Green Island Reception Centre, [Green Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Island,_Hong_Kong)&#x20;

  (Currently closed)

- [Tai Tam Gap Correctional Institution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tai_Tam_Gap_Correctional_Institution), [Tai Tam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tai_Tam), Hong Kong Island&#x20;

  (Currently closed)

- [Victoria Prison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_Prison), [Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central,_Hong_Kong), Hong Kong Island&#x20;

  (Closed Permanently in 2006. Now redeveloped into a cultural and shopping destination generally called [Tai Kwun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tai_Kwun) )

### Kowloon

- [Lai Chi Kok Reception Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lai_Chi_Kok_Reception_Centre), [Lai Chi Kok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lai_Chi_Kok), New Kowloon&#x20;

  (Male adult persons who are: (a) prisoner on remand in any category; (b) judgement respited prisoners; (c) detainees under the provisions of the Immigration Ordinance; (d) civil debtors; (e) appellants (except under life sentence); (f) Star and Ordinary Class adult prisoners (except under life sentence); or (g) persons remanded under the Drug Addiction Treatment Centres Ordinance)

- Lai Hang Rehabilitation Centre, [Tai Wo Ping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tai_Wo_Ping), New Kowloon&#x20;

  (Male young offenders who completed Phase I training in Lai Chi Rehabilitation Centre under the Rehabilitation Centres Ordinance)

- Phoenix House, [Tai Wo Ping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tai_Wo_Ping), New Kowloon&#x20;

  (Young male inmates discharged from Detention Centre, Training Centre & Drug Addiction Treatment Centre)

- Pelican House, [Tai Wo Ping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tai_Wo_Ping), New Kowloon&#x20;

  (Male adult inmates released under supervision from Drug Addiction Treatment Centre, and male adult inmates under the Release Under Supervision Scheme, Pre-Release Employment Scheme, Conditional Release Scheme, Post-Release Supervision of Prisoners Scheme)

- Queen Elizabeth Hospital Custodial Ward, Kowloon&#x20;

  (Male and female inmates who are suffering from illness and referred out by the Medical Officers of various penal institutions)
