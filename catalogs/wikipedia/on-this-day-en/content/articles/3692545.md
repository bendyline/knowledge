**Blida** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): ولاية البليدة) is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Algeria) (*[wilaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilaya)*) in [Algeria](358.md). Its capital is [Blida](1304276.md). The [Chréa National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chr%C3%A9a_National_Park) is situated here.

## History

The province was created from parts of [Alger (department)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alger_\(department\)) and El Asnam department in 1974.

In 1984 [Tipaza Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tipaza_Province) was carved out of its territory.

## Natural features

This province has one of the few [habitat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habitat) areas in Algeria that supports a sub-population of the [Barbary macaque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbary_macaque), *Macaca sylvanus*.[^cite_note-2]
