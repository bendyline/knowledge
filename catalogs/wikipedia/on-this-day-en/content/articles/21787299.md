**Palestine Cellular Communications Company** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): شركة الاتصالات الخلوية الفلسطينية), trading as **Jawwal** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): جوال), is a Palestinian communications company providing cellular and wireless communications. Jawwal operates in the [West Bank](33209.md) and [Gaza Strip](12047.md) as part of [Paltel Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paltel_Group).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

## History

Jawwal was established in 1999 and obtained the first mobile license in the Palestinian territories, operating with GSM communications.[^cite_note-3] The service first launched in Gaza on August 15, 1999 and then to the West Bank on October 2 the same year.[^cite_note-4]

## Network

Jawwal has struggled with the limited frequency allocated to Jawwal by the [Israeli authorities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Communications_\(Israel\)). Jawwal started offering [3G](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3G) services commercially on 23 January 2018.[^cite_note-5] [4G LTE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4G_LTE) and [5G](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5G) services are unavailable.[^cite_note-6]
