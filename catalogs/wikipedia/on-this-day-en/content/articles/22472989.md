# List of parties to the Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons



Participation in the Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons&#x20;

| Signed and ratified   Acceded or succeeded | Accepted   Only signed |
| --- | --- |

The **list of parties to the Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons** encompasses the states who have signed and [ratified](429650.md) or acceded to the international agreement prohibiting or restricting the use of certain conventional weapons which may be deemed to be excessively injurious or have indiscriminate effects.

On April 10, 1981, the [Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons](3111840.md) (CCWC) was opened for signature. [Mexico](3966054.md) became the first state to deposit the treaty on February 11, 1982. The treaty [came into force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coming_into_force) on December 2, 1983. Since April 10, 1982, states that did not sign the treaty can now only accede to it. The instrument of ratification, accession, or succession is deposited with the [Secretary-General of the United Nations](162415.md).

As of July 2024, 128 states have ratified or acceded to the treaty, the most recent being Singapore on September 21, 2023.[^cite_note-1] Four states have signed but not ratified the treaty.
