In [business](39206.md), the **trading day** or **regular trading hours (RTH)** is the time span that a [stock exchange](28168.md) is open, as opposed to [electronic or extended trading hours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_trading_hours) (ETH). For example, the [New York Stock Exchange](21560.md) is, as of the year 2026, open from 9\:30 AM [Eastern Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Time) to 4\:00 PM Eastern Time. Trading days are usually Monday through Friday. When a trading day ends, all trading ends and is frozen in time until the next trading day begins. There are several special circumstances which would lead to a shortened trading day, or no trading day at all, such as on holidays or on days when a [state funeral](714370.md) of a [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) is scheduled to take place.

## NYSE and NASDAQ

The NYSE and [NASDAQ](21559.md) average about 251 trading days a year. This is from 365.2425 (days on average per year) \* 5/7 (proportion work days per week) - 10 (holidays) = 250.8875 ≈ 251.

The holidays where the stock exchange is closed are [New Year's Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Year's_Day), [Martin Luther King Jr. Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Luther_King_Jr._Day), [Presidents' Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidents'_Day), [Good Friday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Friday), [Memorial Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memorial_Day), [Juneteenth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juneteenth), [Independence Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_Day_\(United_States\)), [Labor Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labor_Day), [Thanksgiving Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thanksgiving_Day), and [Christmas Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christmas_Day); there are also some holidays where trading is permitted, including [Columbus Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbus_Day), [Veterans Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veterans_Day), and [New Year's Eve](277922.md).[^cite_note-1] If Juneteenth, Independence Day, Christmas Day and New Year's Day fall on a weekend, the [public holiday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_holiday) is observed on the preceding Friday (for a Saturday holiday) or the following Monday (For a Sunday holiday) instead, meaning that every holiday results in one fewer trading day.[^cite_note-2]

Up to three trading days (July 3, [the day after Thanksgiving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Friday_\(shopping\)), and [Christmas Eve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christmas_Eve)) are shortened, *i.e.* the exchanges are open from 9\:30AM–1\:00PM, depending on where they fall in the calendar year (if July 3 or Christmas Eve fall on a weekend, the shortened day is simply skipped).

[Juneteenth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juneteenth) was declared a national holiday on June 17, 2021. It was first observed as a national holiday and a market holiday on June 20, 2022.[^cite_note-3]

## Trading day time zones

Each stock exchange has opening hours that are based on specific time zones. People can trade in these exchanges remotely using [electronic trading platforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_trading_platform). For those trading in different parts of the world, there are unique trading days based on the hours associated with any given time zone. For example, NASDAQ is open 9\:30–16\:00 ET and anyone outside of the Eastern Time Zone will have a different trading day (for example, in Vancouver a trading day would run from 6\:30–13\:00). During the part of the year when North America is on standard time, it would be 17\:30–24\:00 in [Moscow](19004.md), and in [Shanghai](27643.md) it would be 22\:30–5\:00.[^cite_note-4] Remote traders should find their trading hours based on the stock exchange's hours and time zone.
