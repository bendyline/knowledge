**Sabon Birni**[Listen](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/4/40/LL-Q56475_%28hau%29-Eunice_Ameh-Sabon_Birini.wav/LL-Q56475_%28hau%29-Eunice_Ameh-Sabon_Birini.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup> is a town and [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Areas_of_Nigeria) in [Sokoto State](2227660.md), [Nigeria](21383.md).

Sabon Birni shares a border with the [Republic of Niger](21373.md) to the north. It has an area of 2,354 km<sup>2</sup> and a population of 207,599 at the 2006 census.[^cite_note-2]

The [postal code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_code) of the area is 842.[^cite_note-3]

## Climate

With a yearly [temperature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperature) of 34 °C (93 °F), 4.47% warmer than the national average for Nigeria, and 50.53 days with [precipitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precipitation), Sabon Birni, Nigeria, has a [subtropical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subtropics) steppe climate.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-\:0-5]

### Temperature

From March 20 to June 2, the hot season, which has an average daily high temperature exceeding 38 °C (101 °F), lasts for 2.4 months. With an average high temperature of 39 °C (103 °F) and low temperature of 28 °C (82 °F), May is the hottest month of the year in Sabon Birni. The average daily maximum temperature during the 1.7-month mild season, which runs from December 10 to January 31, is below 32 °C (89 °F). With an average low temperature of 16 °C (61 °F) and high temperature of 31 °C (87 °F), January is the coldest month of the year in Sabon Birni.[^cite_note-\:0-5][^cite_note-6]
