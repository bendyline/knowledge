**Salem** is a [town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_England_town) in [Rockingham County, New Hampshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockingham_County,_New_Hampshire), United States. The population was 30,089 at the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census).[^cite_note-3] Salem is a suburb of [Boston](24437894.md) and [Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester,_New_Hampshire)[^cite_note-4] located on [Interstate 93](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_93). As the first town along I-93 northbound in [New Hampshire](21134.md), Salem has grown into a regional commercial hub for the northern section of [Greater Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Boston), anchored by the [Mall at Rockingham Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mall_at_Rockingham_Park) and Tuscan Village. Other major sites include [Canobie Lake Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canobie_Lake_Park), a large amusement park; and [America's Stonehenge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/America's_Stonehenge), a stone structure of disputed origins. It is the former home of [Rockingham Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockingham_Park), a horse racetrack. The Sununu political family hails from Salem, including former New Hampshire governor and White House Chief of Staff [John H. Sununu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_H._Sununu), and his sons [John E. Sununu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_E._Sununu), a former U.S. senator, and [Chris Sununu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Sununu), former New Hampshire governor.

## History



The Square, now known as Salem Depot, in 1908

The area was first settled in 1652. As early as 1736, Salem was the "North Parish" of [Methuen, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methuen,_Massachusetts), or "Methuen District". In 1741, when the boundary line between [Massachusetts](1645518.md) and New Hampshire was [fixed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_boundary_of_Massachusetts), the "North Parish" became part of New Hampshire, and was given the name "Salem", taken from nearby [Salem, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem,_Massachusetts). The town was incorporated in 1750 by [colonial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thirteen_Colonies) governor [Benning Wentworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benning_Wentworth).[^cite_note-5] The meetinghouse of the old north parish, erected in 1738, still stands, eventually becoming the town hall of Salem before it was turned into the Salem Historical Society museum.[^cite_note-6]

In 1902, [Canobie Lake Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canobie_Lake_Park) was established in Salem by the Massachusetts Northeast Street Railway Company to encourage leisure excursions on its [trolleys](30733.md). The plan was successful, and the enterprise quickly became one of the leading resorts of its type in [New England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_England). Crowds arrived from all over, including the nearby [mill towns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mill_town) of [Haverhill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haverhill,_Massachusetts), [Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence,_Massachusetts), [Lowell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lowell,_Massachusetts) and [Methuen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methuen,_Massachusetts) in Massachusetts, and [Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester,_New_Hampshire) and [Nashua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashua,_New_Hampshire) in New Hampshire. Factory workers and others found respite strolling along tree-lined promenades, between flower beds or beside the lake. Rides, [arcades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penny_arcade_\(venue\)), and a [dance hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dance_hall) provided lively entertainments. The rise of the [automobile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automobile), however, brought the decline of the trolley. But Canobie Lake Park, one of the few former [street railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Street_railway) amusement resorts still in existence, continues to be popular.[^cite_note-arcadia-7]

Other features of Salem's [tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourism) history include [America's Stonehenge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/America's_Stonehenge), a curiosity (formerly "Mystery Hill"). Other attractions include the Icenter, a skating arena, as well as Field of Dreams and Hedgehog Pond Park.

Starting in the 1950s, Salem developed rapidly as part of [Greater Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Boston), with suburban-style residential neighborhoods and a long strip of commercial development along [NH Route 28](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Hampshire_Route_28). Commercial construction has continued to focus on Route 28, as well as on the commercial zone off Exit 2 on [Interstate 93](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_93). Starting in 2017, the Tuscan Village complex has been under construction, a multi-million dollar mixed-use commercial property that includes retail, medical offices, condos, and apartments. The complex is being built on the site of the former Rockingham Park race track.[^cite_note-8]

The [Manchester and Lawrence branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_and_Lawrence_Railroad) of the [Boston and Maine Railroad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_and_Maine_Railroad) ran through Salem until 2001. In 2009, the New Hampshire Department of Transportation commissioned a study exploring the reactivation of the branch and instituting [commuter rail](6286.md) service connecting to the [MBTA Haverhill Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haverhill_Line) and onward to Boston. The study's cost/benefit analysis recommended taking no action to reactivate beyond preserving the option for consideration at a future time.[^cite_note-i93study-9]

## Geography

Salem is in southeastern New Hampshire, in the southwest corner of [Rockingham County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockingham_County,_New_Hampshire). The southern and southeastern border of the town follows the [Massachusetts](1645518.md) state line. Via [Interstate 93](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_93), Salem is 21 miles (34 km) southeast of [Manchester, New Hampshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester,_New_Hampshire), and 34 miles (55 km) north-northwest of [Boston, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston,_Massachusetts).

According to the [U.S. Census Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Census_Bureau), the town has a total area of 25.9 square miles (67.1 km<sup>2</sup>), of which 24.8 square miles (64.3 km<sup>2</sup>) are land and 1.0 square mile (2.7 km<sup>2</sup>) are water, comprising 4.07% of the town.[^cite_note-CenPopGazetteer2021-1] Salem is drained by the [Spicket River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spicket_River) and its tributary Policy Brook, part of the [Merrimack River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merrimack_River) watershed. [Canobie Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canobie_Lake) is on the western boundary, [Arlington Mill Reservoir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arlington_Mill_Reservoir) is in the north, and World End Pond is in the southeast. None of the town's residential water supply incorporates sodium fluoride, a water additive that helps ensure strong teeth enamel.[^cite_note-Government-10]

The highest point in Salem is the summit of Gordons Hill, at 366 feet (112 m) above [sea level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_level), along the town's western border.[^cite_note-11]

*Under the Apple Tree* in 1908, Canobie Lake Park

At the 2000 [census](6889.md),[^cite_note-GR2-13] there were 28,112 people, 10,402 households and 7,603 families residing in the town. The population density was 1,138.0 inhabitants per square mile (439.4/km<sup>2</sup>). There were 10,866 housing units at an average density of 439.9 per square mile (169.8/km<sup>2</sup>). The racial makeup of the town was 95.01% [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.55% [African American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_American_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.21% [Native American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_American_\(U.S._Census\)), 2.27% [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.06% [Pacific Islander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Islander_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.83% from [other races](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_\(United_States_Census\)), and 1.07% from two or more races. [Hispanic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_\(U.S._Census\)) or [Latino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latino_\(U.S._Census\)) of any race were 1.96% of the population.

There were 10,402 households, of which 34.3% had children under the age of 18 living with them, 60.6% were [married couples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marriage) living together, 8.7% had a female householder with no husband present, and 26.9% were non-families. Of all households 21.2% were made up of individuals, and 7.5% had someone living alone who was 65 years of age or older. The average household size was 2.69 and the average family size was 3.16.

Age distribution was 25.3% under the age of 18, 6.2% from 18 to 24, 31.7% from 25 to 44, 25.3% from 45 to 64, and 11.5% who were 65 years of age or older. The median age was 38 years. For every 100 females, there were 99.1 males. For every 100 females age 18 and over, there were 96.9 males.

The [median household income](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Median_household_income) was \$58,090, and the median family income was \$67,278. Males had a median income of \$46,330 versus \$31,031 for females. The [per capita income](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Per_capita_income) for the town was \$26,170. About 3.1% of families and 4.1% of the population were below the [poverty line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_line), including 5.1% of those under age 18 and 7.6% of those age 65 or over.

Salem Town Hall

Salem's town government consists of a town council and a town manager. Salem is a part of [New Hampshire House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Hampshire_House_of_Representatives) District 25 and is currently represented by nine Republican representatives: Lori Ball, Tanya Donnelly, [Fred Doucette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fred_Doucette), John Janigian, [Dennis Mannion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dennis_Mannion_\(politician\)), [Valerie McDonnell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valerie_McDonnell), [Joe Sweeney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Sweeney_\(American_politician\)), [John Sytek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Sytek) and Susan Vandecasteele. In the [New Hampshire Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Hampshire_Senate), Salem is in the 22nd District and is currently represented by Republican [Daryl Abbas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daryl_Abbas). On the [New Hampshire Executive Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Hampshire_Executive_Council), Salem is in District 3 and is currently represented by Republican Janet Stevens. In the [U.S. House of Representatives](19468510.md), Salem is in [New Hampshire's 2nd congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Hampshire's_2nd_congressional_district) and is currently represented by Democrat [Maggie Goodlander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maggie_Goodlander).

Salem is a Republican stronghold in presidential elections. No Democratic presidential nominee has carried the town since [Bill Clinton](3356.md) received a plurality of the vote in 1996.

## Infrastructure

### Transportation

Highways include [NH 28](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Hampshire_Route_28), [NH 38](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Hampshire_Route_38), [NH 97](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Hampshire_Route_97), [NH 111](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Hampshire_Route_111), and [Interstate 93](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_93).

The nearest airport is [Manchester–Boston Regional Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester%E2%80%93Boston_Regional_Airport) along the border of Londonderry and Manchester.

The [Manchester Transit Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_Transit_Authority) operates a shopper bus shuttle service three days a week.[^cite_note-16]
