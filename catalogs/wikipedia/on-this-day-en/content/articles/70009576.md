# Foreign fighter

&#x20;A **foreign fighter** is someone who travels abroad to participate in an armed conflict, or fights for a country of which he or she is not a national. However, different definitions have been used by different journalists, policymakers, and researchers, depending on what distinctions they've wished to make.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]
