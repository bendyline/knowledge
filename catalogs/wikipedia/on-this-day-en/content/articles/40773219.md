The **Kuwait Ministry of Defense** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): وزارة الدفاع الكويتية) is one of [the governmental bodies](16688.md) of [Kuwait](7515890.md). Its minister in charge is a member of the [Cabinet of Kuwait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Kuwait). The current Minister of Defense is [Abdullah Ali Al-Abdullah Al-Salem Al-Sabah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdullah_Ali_Al-Abdullah_Al-Salem_Al-Sabah?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-MoD2022-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

## Minister

On 1 July 1961, when the ministry was not established yet during [Operation Vantage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Vantage); the Kuwait Army was the de facto command leadership of the available armed forces since establishment and acted as official minister advising the [Emir of Kuwait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emir_of_Kuwait) on course of action. The [Kuwait Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_of_Kuwait) redesignated in 1953 was founded 13 years before the enacting of the [Kuwait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Kuwait) ministry, mainly by [Field Marshal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_Marshal) Sheikh Abdullah Mubarak Al-Sabah (youngest son of [Mubarak Al-Sabah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mubarak_Al-Sabah)) in 1949.

## General functions

The major function of the ministry is to implement the government's defense policy and to govern all branches of the Kuwait Armed Forces.[^cite_note-epi-14] It is also responsible for the production, transfer, use, storage, and coordination of [mines](18172.md); and for [mine clearance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mine_clearance).[^cite_note-epi-14]

The ministry publishes a monthly [magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magazine) called *Homat Al Watan*.[^cite_note-15]
