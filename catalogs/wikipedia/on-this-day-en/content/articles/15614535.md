**Al-Qarara** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): القرارة) is a Palestinian town in the [Khan Yunis Governorate](13900254.md) of the [Gaza Strip](12047.md). It is located just north of [Khan Yunis](101330.md) and approximately 24 kilometres (15 mi) south of [Gaza City](55387.md). According to the [Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Central_Bureau_of_Statistics), the town had a population of 29,004 people in 2017.[^cite_note-PrelimCensus2017-1]

Two months after the beginning of the [Gaza war](74998519.md) in 2023, [al-Qarara was besieged for four months](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Al-Qarara) by the [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md). During this time, numerous residential buildings in the town were destroyed, and the town became the site of a displacement camp.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

## History

Al-Qarara, which used to be a farming village,[^cite_note-csmonitor20210914-4] lies on trade routes dating back to the [Bronze Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronze_Age).[^cite_note-csmonitor20210914-4] The town was home to the [al-Qarara Cultural Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Qarara_Cultural_Museum),[^cite_note-csmonitor20210914-4] which housed several [Canaanite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canaan) and [Roman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Rome) artifacts. Construction on a pedestrian bridge over [Salah al-Din Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salah_al-Din_Road), funded by [Japan](15573.md), was completed in the town in September 2023.[^cite_note-wafa20230919-5]

## Gaza war

In December 2023, two months into the [Gaza war](74998519.md), [Israel](9282173.md) issued [evacuation orders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_Strip_evacuations) to the inhabitants of al-Qarara, and initiated [a siege of the town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Al-Qarara) shortly thereafter.[^cite_note-6] During and after the siege, much of the town's infrastructure was destroyed.[^cite_note-7]



This photo depicts Al-Qarara's pedestrian bridge over Salah Aldin Street. On the left side of the photo, an UNRWA school. On the street, a donkey cart which became the main mean of transportation in Gaza after start of Gaza war

By 12 December of that year, al-Qarara was completely [encircled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encirclement) by Israeli troops, who also engaged a number of other towns in the area, such as [Absan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Absan?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Al-Nazla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Nazla?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Bani Suheila](12918976.md), and [al-Satr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Satr?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]
