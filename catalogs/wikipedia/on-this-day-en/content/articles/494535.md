# Same-sex sexual practices

**Same-sex sexual practices** are [sexual practices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_practices) involving people of the same [sex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biological_sex), regardless of [sexual orientations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_orientation).

## Sexual practices between women

[Sexual practices between women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_practices_between_women) are [sexual activities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_positions) involving [women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women), regardless of their [sexual orientation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_orientation).[^cite_note-RM-1] These may include, for example, [oral sex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oral_sex), [mutual masturbation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutual_masturbation), [tribadism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tribadism), and [anal sex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anal_sex).[^cite_note-2] In medicine, the term *[women who have sex with women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women_who_have_sex_with_women)* is used to refer to all individuals who engage in sex between women.

## Sexual practices between men

[Sexual practices between men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_practices_between_men) are [sexual activities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_positions) involving [men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men), regardless of their [sexual orientation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_orientation).[^cite_note-Syst.Rev._2023-3] These may include, for example, [oral sex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oral_sex), [mutual masturbation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutual_masturbation), [anal sex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anal_sex), and [frot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frot).[^cite_note-4] In medicine, the term *[men who have sex with men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_who_have_sex_with_men)* is used to refer to all individuals who engage in sex between men.
