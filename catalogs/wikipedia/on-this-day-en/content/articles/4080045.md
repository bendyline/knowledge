**San Cristóbal** is a city in the center-north of the [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Argentina) of [Santa Fe](520560.md), Argentina, 179 km north-northwest from [the provincial capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Fe,_Argentina). It had about 14,000 inhabitants at the 2010 [census](6889.md) \[[INDEC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Statistics_and_Census_of_Argentina)] and it is the head town of the San Cristóbal Department.

The town was founded in 1890 and the attained the status of *comuna* ([commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commune_\(subnational_entity\))) on 1894-01-21. It became a city on 1959-03-01.
