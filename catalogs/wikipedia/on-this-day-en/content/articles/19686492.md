# Ministry of Public Health (Democratic Republic of the Congo)

The **Ministry of Public Health** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Ministère de la Santé Publique*) is the health ministry of the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md). [Samuel Roger Kamba Mulamba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Roger_Kamba_Mulamba) is the current minister since March 2023.[^cite_note-1]

It has a [public health system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_health_system) based loosely on the historical [Belgian colonial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgian_Congo) public health system. The ministry is largely a policy and oversight organization, with operational functions embodied within a number of subordinate ministry organizations, including the [Institute of Tropical Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Tropical_Medicine_\(Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo\)?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Institute of Biomedical Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Biomedical_Research_\(Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo\)?action=edit\&redlink=1) (*Institut National de Recherché Biomédicale*) and the [Kinshasa School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinshasa_School_of_Public_Health). The latter trains physicians in public health, staffs the public health infrastructure composed of [Health Zones](81388530.md), including the Health Zone doctors who provide both public health services and partial staffing of district hospitals.

In November 2012, Dr. [Félix Kabange Numbi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F%C3%A9lix_Kabange_Numbi) was appointed Health Minister of the Democratic Republic of the Congo.[^cite_note-2] In 2018, the position was held by [Oly Ilunga Kalenga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oly_Ilunga_Kalenga).[^cite_note-3]
