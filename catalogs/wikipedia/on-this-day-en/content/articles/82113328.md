The [convicted child sex offender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Litigation_involving_Jeffrey_Epstein) [Jeffrey Epstein](6253522.md) cultivated connections with influential individuals and institutions across the globe.[^cite_note-who-partied-with-Epstein-1] Epstein was personally connected with many figures in [international business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transnational_capitalist_class), academia, politics, royalty, entertainment, and other areas. He was involved with many institutions as a member, donor, or both.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-Epstein-high-society-3] Many of these connections continued to associate with Epstein even after his crimes came to light.[^cite_note-network-of-defenders-4][^cite_note-network-rich-powerful-5]

Epstein had a decades-long association with socialite [Ghislaine Maxwell](32018562.md), who recruited young girls for him, leading to her 2021 conviction for sex trafficking and [child sexual abuse](18721790.md). Epstein was business partners or friends with politicians such as [Ehud Barak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ehud_Barak), [Bill Clinton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relationship_of_Bill_Clinton_and_Jeffrey_Epstein), [Robert F. Kennedy Jr.](522298.md), and [Donald Trump](80511968.md), businessmen such as [Leon Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leon_Black), [Richard Branson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Branson), [Sergey Brin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergey_Brin), [Bill Gates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Gates), [Elon Musk](909036.md), and [Peter Thiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Thiel), media figures like [Steve Bannon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Bannon) and [Noam Chomsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noam_Chomsky), and royals like [former Prince Andrew, Duke of York](81480934.md) and [Mette-Marit, Crown Princess of Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relationship_of_Mette-Marit,_Crown_Princess_of_Norway,_and_Jeffrey_Epstein), along with many others.[^cite_note-6]

While many of Epstein's connections were known prior to the [partial release](81594904.md#Fifth_release_%E2%80%93_January_30,_2026) of the [Epstein files](80415168.md) in 2025 and 2026, the files thus far published uncovered a vast network of previously unknown connections and revealed others were far deeper than previously realized.[^cite_note-7]

## Background

In 2008, Epstein reached a [plea deal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plea_deal) with prosecutors after the parents of a 14-year-old girl told Florida police that Epstein had molested their daughter at his Palm Beach home. As a result of the plea deal, Epstein was convicted of soliciting prostitution from a minor, became a registered [sex offender](2957039.md), and served 13 months in a minimum-security county jail, during which he was allowed to leave the facility up to 12 hours a day.[^cite_note-\:33-8]

The plea deal granted [immunity](5685623.md) from all federal criminal charges to not only Epstein, but also the four named co-conspirators and any unnamed "potential co-conspirators". According to the *Miami Herald*, the non-prosecution agreement "essentially shut down an ongoing FBI probe into whether there were more victims and other powerful people who took part in Epstein's sex crimes." At the time, this halted the investigation and sealed the indictment.[^cite_note-deal-9] The plea deal was subsequently described as a "[sweetheart deal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweetheart_deal)".[^cite_note-10] [Alexander Acosta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Acosta), the U.S. attorney for the Southern District of Florida who had negotiated the plea deal, later allegedly said he offered a lenient deal because he was told that Epstein "belonged to intelligence", was "above his pay grade" and to "leave it alone".[^cite_note-Ward_2019-11][^cite_note-observer-12][^cite_note-13] In an October 2025 article in *[Skeptic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skeptic_\(American_magazine\))*, Mark Hoffer wrote that the frequently cited claim that Acosta had been told that Epstein "belonged to intelligence" originated with an anonymous source in a 2019 *[Daily Beast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Daily_Beast)* report and was subsequently denied under oath by Acosta. Hoffer writes that this quote later serves as a foundational myth of Epstein conspiracy theory, that build a connection between him and intelligence agencies, and cites controversial author [Whitney Webb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_Webb)'s works as representative of this narrative.[^cite_note-fp-14]

Ten years later in 2018, the *[Miami Herald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami_Herald)* published interviews with Epstein's victims.[^cite_note-bbc.com-15][^cite_note-Anderson-16] In 2019, Epstein was arrested by the [FBI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FBI)-[NYPD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NYPD) Crimes Against Children Task Force.[^cite_note-jpost-17][^cite_note-18] [Epstein's Manhattan townhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_N._Straus_House) was searched by the FBI, which turned up evidence of Epstein's child sex trafficking.[^cite_note-Stanglin-2019-19]



Massage room with a [massage table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massage_table) in [Epstein's NYC townhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_N._Straus_House) in 2019

On July 8, Epstein was charged with running a network of underage girls for sex to wealthy clientele. The grand jury indictment alleged that "dozens" of underage girls were brought into Epstein's mansions for sexual encounters.[^cite_note-Shallwani-20][^cite_note-Chaitin-2019-21][^cite_note-dienst1-22]

&#x20;On August 10, 2019, [Epstein died](61499571.md) in his jail cell while awaiting trial. Nineteen days later, the case against Epstein was closed.[^cite_note-epsteinclosed-23][^cite_note-epsteinreutersdismiss-24]



Protestors calling for the [Epstein files](80415168.md) to be released

From 2019 to 2026, there was public pressure to release the [Epstein files](80415168.md), a collection of millions of documents accumulated during investigations into Epstein conducted as a result of his 2008 and 2019 indictments, and Donald Trump ran on releasing the Epstein files as a [campaign promise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campaign_promise) of [his 2024 presidential bid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump_2024_presidential_campaign).[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29]

In November 2025, the [U.S. House of Representatives](19468510.md) passed the [Epstein Files Transparency Act](81594904.md),[^cite_note-NBC-2025-11-18-30] the [U.S. Senate](24909346.md) unanimously approved it,[^cite_note-Carney_&_Fuchs-31] and President [Donald Trump](4848272.md) signed the bill the next day.[^cite_note-Rimmer_2025-32]

In December 2025, the [U.S. Department of Justice](52563.md) released a relatively small portion of the files. On January 30, 2026, an additional 3 million pages were released, including 2,000 videos and 180,000 images.[^cite_note-justicedepartmentjan302026release-33] While the Department of Justice acknowledged that a total of 6 million pages might qualify as files required to be released,[^cite_note-epsteinfiles6millions-34] it stated that the January 30 release would be the final one, and that it had met its legal obligations.[^cite_note-reportedlegalobligationcompliance-35][^cite_note-36]

The released files [mentioned a number of public figures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_people_named_in_the_Epstein_files), many of whom had previously unknown connections to Epstein. Others were shown to have deeper connections to Epstein than previously revealed.[^cite_note-\:19-37][^cite_note-NYT-20260202-38]

## People

### Inner circle

*[Al Jazeera](30204052.md)* reproduced a diagram from the US Justice Department mapping Epstein's inner circle, with the following persons: [Jean-Luc Brunel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Luc_Brunel), [Lesley Groff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Litigation_involving_Jeffrey_Epstein), Darren Indyke, [Richard Kahn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Kahn_\(accountant\)), [Ghislaine Maxwell](32018562.md), and Harry Beller. Names of other employees and aides were redacted.[^cite_note-AJ-visual-guide-39][^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41]

The *[New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Times)* in 2019 reported that [Sarah Kellen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Kellen), [Nadia Marcinkova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nadia_Marcinko), Lesley Groff, Adriana Ross, and Haley Robson were part of the Epstein system, and published that "Recruiters were allegedly told to target young, financially desperate women, and to promise them help furthering their education and careers."[^cite_note-nyt19a-42] Rina Oh was another Epstein recruiter who spoke to the press in 2021.[^cite_note-poli1-43] Kellen, Groff, Ross, and Marcinkova were listed as "unindicted co-conspirators" in the Acosta 2007 plea deal;[^cite_note-poli1-43] when Judge [Alison Nathan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alison_Nathan) sentenced Maxwell in 2022, she described Kellen as "a knowing participant in the criminal conspiracy" and said she was "a criminally responsible participant".[^cite_note-tco22a-44][^cite_note-bi22a-45]

#### Ghislaine Maxwell

Main article: [Ghislaine Maxwell § Relationship with Jeffrey Epstein](32018562.md#Relationship_with_Jeffrey_Epstein)

British [socialite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialite) [Ghislaine Maxwell](32018562.md) was one of Epstein's close affiliates, a partner in crime to his sex trafficking operations as well as a romantic partner. Maxwell and Epstein were associated with each other by as early as February 1993, and may have been introduced to one another by alleged Israeli spy [Robert Maxwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Maxwell), Ghislaine's father, in the late 1980s.[^cite_note-\:31-46][^cite_note-npc1-47] Ghislaine had a romantic relationship with Epstein for several years in the early 1990s and remained closely associated with him for more than 25 years until [his death](61499571.md) in 2019.[^cite_note-\:02-48][^cite_note-TelegMar11-49] In 2021, she was found guilty of child sex trafficking and other offenses in connection with Epstein.[^cite_note-50]

#### Jean-Luc Brunel

Main article: [Jean-Luc Brunel § Relationship with Jeffrey Epstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Luc_Brunel#Relationship_with_Jeffrey_Epstein)

The French modeling agent [Jean-Luc Brunel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Luc_Brunel) was introduced to Epstein by Ghislaine Maxwell, reportedly in the 1980s.[^cite_note-\:18-51] The relationship between Brunel and Epstein strengthened in the 1990s.[^cite_note-six-52] In the 2000s, Brunel founded [MC2 Model Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MC2_Model_Management) with financial support from Epstein.[^cite_note-\:18-51] Brunel was accused of acting as a recruiter for Epstein, luring young girls from disadvantaged backgrounds to the United States with the prospect of modeling jobs.[^cite_note-proc-53]

In 2020, Brunel was taken into custody in Paris and held for 14 months while awaiting trial on charges of rape of minors and sexual harassment.[^cite_note-six-52] Eleven women had "accused Brunel of sexual assault in that case, which was closed after he died by suicide" in the La Santé Prison in Paris, in February 2022.[^cite_note-54]

### Personal connections

Epstein had close personal relationships with many politicians, investors, scientists, royals, and other famous persons. He was a longtime associate of [Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor](125267.md) and [Tom Barrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_J._Barrack_Jr.).[^cite_note-michaelwolff-55] He socialized with numerous public figures, including among others [Crown Princess Mette-Marit of Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relationship_of_Mette-Marit,_Crown_Princess_of_Norway,_and_Jeffrey_Epstein),[^cite_note-nettavisenep-56] [Harvey Weinstein](1624086.md),[^cite_note-57] [Steve Bannon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Bannon), [Reid Hoffman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reid_Hoffman), [Elon Musk](909036.md),[^cite_note-58] [Donald Trump](4848272.md),[^cite_note-59] and [John Casablancas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Casablancas).[^cite_note-\:12-60]

#### Richard Axel

[Molecular biologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molecular_biologist) and [Nobel laurette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nobel_laureates_in_Physiology_or_Medicine) [Richard Axel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Axel) was friends with Epstein. #### Steve Bannon

Main article: [Steve Bannon § Connection to Jeffrey Epstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Bannon#Connection_to_Jeffrey_Epstein)

Bannon was first in contact with Epstein in 2017 and remained in contact with until his 2019 arrest. He reportedly met with him at his home in New York, had dinners with him, and exchanged thousands of emails and texts.[^cite_note-69]

Bannon also worked with [Ehud Barak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ehud_Barak) and Epstein attorney Reid Weingarten to attempt to fix Epstein's damaged public image.[^cite_note-70][^cite_note-71] Bannon reportedly prepped Epstein for an interview with *[60 Minutes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/60_Minutes)* that ultimately never occurred. Bannon did say that he did tape 15 hours of interviews with Epstein but denied that he was coaching him for further interviews and that the interviews were for an unreleased documentary on Epstein.[^cite_note-72][^cite_note-73] In late 2025, the [House Oversight Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_Oversight_Committee) released emails and text messages between Bannon and Epstein. In 2019 they had discussed using a Kovel agreement, a legal maneuver, to extend [attorney-client privilege](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attorney-client_privilege) to Bannon, allowing him to join Epstein's legal team, despite Bannon having no formal legal training. The proposed agreement would allow Bannon to withhold footage of his interviews with Epstein.[^cite_note-74][^cite_note-75]

#### Ex-Prime Minister Ehud Barak

Main article: [Ehud Barak § Relationship with Jeffrey Epstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ehud_Barak#Relationship_with_Jeffrey_Epstein)

Ex-[Israeli prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Prime_Minister) [Ehud Barak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ehud_Barak) maintained an extensive relationship with Epstein, beginning when they first met in 2003. Barak stayed at Epstein's apartments in New York several times over the years.[^cite_note-76]

Leaked emails show that on one occasion when Barak planned to visit [Little Saint James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Saint_James), Epstein's private island, in January 2014, Barak was "arranging that the security guys will not come with us to the island".[^cite_note-77] In 2023, it was revealed that Barak had visited Epstein around 30 times from 2013 to 2017 and had also flown on the [Lolita Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lolita_Express), Epstein's private jet. Barak denied any wrongdoing.[^cite_note-TOI2-78][^cite_note-JTA-79][^cite_note-80] In February 2026, Barak said that he had spent three hours at Epstein's home in the U.S. Virgin Islands together with his wife and security guards, and that the only other people he saw there were Epstein and maintenance staff.[^cite_note-81]

In 2015, Barak invested in [Carbyne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbyne_\(company\)) (formerly Reporty), an Israeli tech startup that developed video streaming and geolocation software; a large portion of the funding invested by Barak was supplied by Epstein.[^cite_note-TOI_19-82]

Leaked emails show that Epstein leveraged his relationship with Barak to approach powerful figures like [Peter Thiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Thiel), a former director of Israeli signals intelligence, and two people in [Vladimir Putin](32817.md)'s circle: former Russian Deputy Minister of Economic Development [Sergey Belyakov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergei_Belyakov) and [Viktor Vekselberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktor_Vekselberg).[^cite_note-Reason-83] Epstein arranged for Barak to meet Thiel in New York on 9 June 2014.[^cite_note-\:15-84][^cite_note-Reason-83] During the same time, Barak also tried to arrange to meet Putin's ally [Viktor Vekselberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktor_Vekselberg) early in June 2014.[^cite_note-Reason-83] An email sent in April 2015 shows that Barak asked Epstein for his opinion on Vekselberg-backed Fifth Dimension, a startup which later shut down after being sanctioned in 2018 by the US for alleged election meddling.[^cite_note-Reason-83] This startup's leadership also included [Benny Gantz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benny_Gantz) (former [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md)' chief-of-staff) and [Ram Ben-Barak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ram_Ben-Barak) (former deputy Mossad director).[^cite_note-85] According to *[The San Francisco Standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_San_Francisco_Standard)*, leaked emails show that, around the same time Barak and Epstein contacted Thiel, other Israeli officials tried to build a relationship with Thiel as well, "jockeying for Thiel's attention", often aiming at a "lucrative" job at [Palantir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palantir), Thiel's intelligence technology company.[^cite_note-86]

For Epstein's 63rd birthday in 2016 a number of letters written for the occasion by high-profile individuals were compiled as a birthday gift;[^cite_note-87] among these were a letter from Barak and his wife.[^cite_note-88]

#### John Brockman

Main article: [John Brockman (literary agent) § Association with Jeffrey Epstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Brockman_\(literary_agent\)#Association_with_Jeffrey_Epstein)

[Jewish-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish-American) literary agent and author [John Brockman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Brockman_\(literary_agent\)) was a long-term associate of Epstein's, both hosting and being hosted by Epstein at various events. Alongside [then-Prince Andrew](125267.md), Brockman once attended a dinner at Epstein's mansion to celebrate Epstein's release from prison for charges of child sex trafficking.[^cite_note-89] Brockman was photographed aboard Epstein's private jet, the [Lolita Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lolita_Express), in 2000, alongside [Steven Pinker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Pinker), [Daniel Dennett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Dennett), Katinka Matson (Brockman's wife and president of Brockman Inc.), and [Richard Dawkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dawkins).[^cite_note-\:16-90][^cite_note-91][^cite_note-92] Brockman's name was also found in Epstein's private jet log.[^cite_note-Epstein-high-society-3]

Brockman was described in *[The New Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_Republic)* as Epstein's “intellectual enabler” for his role in connecting Epstein to eminent scientists.[^cite_note-93] Brockman hosted an annual "Billionaires' Dinner" during the [TED conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TED_conference) in Monterey, California, which Epstein attended from 2000 to as late as 2011, years after his conviction for child sex crimes. Brockman's dinners were, for a number of years after Epstein's conviction, almost entirely funded by Epstein as documented in his annual tax filings.[^cite_note-94] This allowed Epstein to mingle with scientists, startup icons and tech billionaires.[^cite_note-tg19mit-95] References to Epstein's attendance of these dinners on Brockman's website stating that they were one of Epstein's "favorite events", and that Epstein "enjoys hanging with stimulating and provocative thoughtful minds, who have achieved a high degree of success in finance, company, high tech, and scientific research" were later deleted.[^cite_note-\:16-90]

*[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)* alleged that [John Casablancas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Casablancas), founder of [Elite Model Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elite_Model_Management), was connected to Jeffrey Epstein, and, according to a 2019 lawsuit, he had sent a 15-year-old female model in 1990 to meet a photographer later identified as Epstein, who sexually assaulted her.[^cite_note-\:12-60] The newspaper had earlier published an obituary stating, "Casablancas was frank about his personal preference for girls of only just legal age – 'child women'."[^cite_note-guardian2-96]

#### George Church

Main article: [George Church (geneticist) § Support of open consent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Church_\(geneticist\)#Support_of_open_consent)

### Mobile phones

*[Wired](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wired_\(magazine\))* investigators bought location data from Near Intelligence that covered multiple residences on Epstein's private island. 200 mobile devices were tracked from July 2016 to July 6, 2019, when Epstein was arrested. The data cache also linked visitors to an estimated 166 locations in the United States, as well as cities in [Ukraine](31750.md), the [Cayman Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cayman_Islands) and [Australia](4689264.md). Due to the specificity of the device coordinate data, the investigators were able to pinpoint the most frequent visitors coming from [gated communities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gated_communities) in [Michigan](18859.md) and [Florida](18933066.md); homes in [Martha’s Vineyard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martha%E2%80%99s_Vineyard) and [Nantucket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nantucket); a [nightclub](19321330.md) in [Miami](53846.md) and the sidewalk across the street from [Trump Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_Tower) in New York City. Some of the coordinates point to multi-million mansions, while others point to lower-income areas where Epstein's victims attended school and lived.[^cite_note-Wired-investigation-236]

### The Epstein files

The [Epstein files](80415168.md) are a collection of millions of documents accumulated during investigations into Epstein conducted as a result of his 2008 and 2019 indictments. They include his contacts book, flight logs of his planes, court documents, images, videos, and emails.[^cite_note-237][^cite_note-ABC_Musk_Epstein-238][^cite_note-Independent_Epstein-239][^cite_note-240]

After the *[Miami Herald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami_Herald)* published interviews with Epstein's victims in 2018, and Epstein was charged with federal sex trafficking crimes in New York in 2019, there was considerable public interest in the Epstein files, and their reveal was anticipated to occur during the course of Epstein's court case.[^cite_note-bbc.com-15][^cite_note-Anderson-16] However, [Epstein died in jail](61499571.md) in 2019 while awaiting his trial. With Epstein dead, the case was closed and the documents were not released.[^cite_note-epsteinclosed-23][^cite_note-epsteinreutersdismiss-24]

The released files [mentioned a number of public figures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_people_named_in_the_Epstein_files), many of whom had previously unknown connections to Epstein. Others were shown to have deeper connections to Epstein than previously revealed.[^cite_note-\:19-37][^cite_note-NYT-20260202-38]
