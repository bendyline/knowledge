This page provides the list of members – incumbent and nominated – of the [State Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Council_of_South_Korea) of South Korean President [Yoon Suk Yeol](62877952.md). Though Yoon was removed from office on 4 April 2025 due to [his impeachment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impeachment_of_Yoon_Suk_Yeol) by the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_South_Korea), and Prime Minister [Han Duck-soo](4827727.md) and First Deputy Prime Minister [Choi Sang-mok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Choi_Sang-mok) resigned on 1 May 2025, the rest of the cabinet continued to govern until the inauguration of [Lee Jae-myung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Jae-myung) on 4 June.

In December 2023, Yoon carried out a major overhaul, replacing about a third of his cabinet members ahead of the [legislative election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_South_Korean_legislative_election) in April 2024.[^cite_note-4]

### President

[Yoon Suk Yeol](62877952.md) defeated the [Democratic Party](42316753.md) nomineee, [Lee Jae Myung](50305655.md), in the [2022 presidential election](63655548.md). He assumes office on 11 May 2022.[^cite_note-NYT_win-5] Yoon was [suspended from 14 December 2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impeachment_of_Yoon_Suk_Yeol) onwards and was [arrested on 15 January 2025](78791194.md).[^cite_note-6] He was [successfully impeached from office on 4 April 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impeachment_of_Yoon_Suk_Yeol).[^cite_note-7]
