# April 2026 attack on French UNIFIL peacekeepers in Lebanon

On 18 April 2026, an attack on French UNIFIL peacekeeper patrol took place in [Southern Lebanon](4205968.md). One French soldier, [Sergeant](219424.md) Florian Montorioum, was killed. The incident took place during a ceasefire between Lebanon and Israel and while [peace talks](82916672.md) were in progress.

## Background

Since established in 1978, France has been one of the major contributors to the [United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon](787766.md). The force is missioned to monitor and the border between Lebanon and Israel while maintaining security in Southern Lebanon.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-lemonde-3]

## Attack

On 18 April 2026, while trying to reach a United Nation outpost in [Al-Ghandouriyah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Ghandouriyah), a French UNIFIL patrol doing reconnaissance activity, was ambushed and attacked in Southern Lebanon.[^cite_note-lemonde-3][^cite_note-lorientlejour-4][^cite_note-reuters-5][^cite_note-apnews-6] In the attack one French soldier, Sergeant Florian Montorioum was killed and three others were injured, two of them seriously.[^cite_note-lemonde-3][^cite_note-apnews-6][^cite_note-reuters2-7]

## Responsibility

According to French officials, including French President [Emmanuelle Macron](43671127.md), said the evidence shows Hezbollah was responsible for the deadly attack.[^cite_note-lemonde-3][^cite_note-reuters-5][^cite_note-apnews-6][^cite_note-reuters2-7] UNIFIL joined the accusation pointing at Hezbollah.[^cite_note-apnews-6] The organization denies the attack was done by them, calling for an investigation of the incident.[^cite_note-elpais-8]

## Reactions

 [United Nations](31769.md): [Antonio Guterres](471806.md), United Nations Secretary condemned the attack in Lebanon, calling all parties to respect the ceasefire.[^cite_note-reuters2-7]

 [France](5843419.md): French President Macron condemned the attack.[^cite_note-elpais-8]

 [Lebanon](17771.md): Both Lebanese president [Aoun](53467128.md) and PM [Salam](21077090.md) condemned the attack on the French forces.[^cite_note-lemonde-3][^cite_note-lorientlejour-4]

 [Hezbollah](13919.md): Denied any responsibility to the attack, calling for an investigation of the incident.[^cite_note-apnews-6]
