**Vorapak Tanyawong** ([Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language): วรภัค ธันยาวงษ์, [RTGS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Thai_General_System_of_Transcription): *Woraphak Thanyawong*) is a Thai politician who served as Deputy Minister of Finance in 2025.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

## Career

Vorapak previously served as an executive at [Krungthai Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krungthai_Bank), before joining the [Anutin Charnvirakul Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Anutin_cabinet) in September 2025.[^cite_note-\:1-4] According to *Whale Hunting*, Vorapak was previously listed as an adviser to Cambodian bank BIC group, which allegedly has ties to a money laundering network.[^cite_note-\:1-4]

### Cybercrime hub investigation

In late September 2025, the *Whale Hunting* newsletter written by journalist [Tom Wright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Wright_\(journalist\)) published that that Vorapak and his wife, Kanokporn Tanyawong, were connected to a cybercrime hub network in Cambodia.[^cite_note-\:0-5][^cite_note-6] The newsletter made allegations that Kanokporn had been paid \$3 million in cryptocurrency to a Chinese-Cambodian criminal network led by South African fixer [Benjamin Mauerberger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Mauerberger) that Vorapak was previously tasked with investigation as a member of a government committee.[^cite_note-7] The \$3 million was cited from signed corporate filings in Singapore.[^cite_note-\:1-4]

On 21 October 2025, Prime Minister of Thailand Anutin Charnvirakul requested Vorapak provide a written response to allegations, while also denying reports that Vorapak had been named to lead Thailand's new anti-scam committee.[^cite_note-8]

Vorapak resigned on 22 October 2025.[^cite_note-\:0-5] He denied the allegations, and stated that he planned to file a defamation lawsuit.[^cite_note-\:0-5][^cite_note-9] Later on 22 October 2025, Anutin stated he would not fill the vacancy created by Vorapak's resignation.[^cite_note-10]

Following his resignation, opposition lawmakers called for an investigation into Vorapak's activities.[^cite_note-11]

On 25 March 2026, *Whale Hunting* published an article focused on Vorapak's son Kant, who has posted photos of himself on social media traveling on Mauerberger's Gulfstream and vacationing on his superyacht.[^cite_note-12]
