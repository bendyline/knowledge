# Automobile manufacturers and brands of China

&#x20;There are currently about 150 active automobile brands in the China, the country with the largest [automotive industry](163778.md) in the world. Among them are 97 Chinese domestic brands and 43 joint venture (JV) brands.[^cite_note-1] Until 2010, the traditional "Big Four" were the major state-owned car manufacturers: [SAIC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAIC_Motor), [FAW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FAW_Group), [Dongfeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dongfeng_Motor_Corporation) and [Changan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chang'an_Automobile_Group). Other Chinese car manufacturers, both from public and private sectors, like [Geely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geely_Auto), [BAIC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAIC), [BYD](1461760.md), [Chery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chery), [GAC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GAC_Group), [Great Wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Wall_Motor), [JAC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JAC_Group) and [Seres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seres_\(automobiles\)), emerged as the major players with the expansion of the Chinese automotive industry.

### Central state-owned manufacturers

*Central state-owned manufacturer* refers to manufacturers directly owned by the [State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of the State Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State-owned_Assets_Supervision_and_Administration_Commission_of_the_State_Council) (SASAC) of the [People's Republic of China](5405.md). Currently, [FAW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FAW_Group), [Dongfeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dongfeng_Motor_Corporation) and [Changan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Changan_Automobile) are owned and controlled by the Central Government.[^cite_note-2] According to the [civil service ranks of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_service_of_China), the central government controlled state-owned enterprises are ranked as Sub-Ministerial-Level Enterprise.

- **[FAW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FAW_Group)** (**China First Automobile Works Group Corporation**, 中国第一汽车集团有限公司) is a central state-owned automotive manufacturing company headquartered in [Changchun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Changchun) which is directly under the control of the central government of China. Founded on 15 July 1953, it is the oldest car manufacturer of the People's Republic of China. Currently FAW sells products under their brands including [Hongqi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hongqi_\(marque\)), [Jiefang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FAW_Jiefang), [Bestune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bestune) and [Yueyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bestune). FAW also operates joint ventures with [Toyota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota), [Volkswagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volkswagen) and [Audi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audi).

- **[Dongfeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dongfeng_Motor_Corporation)** (**Dongfeng Motor Corporation**, 东风汽车集团有限公司) is a central state-owned automobile manufacturer headquartered in [Wuhan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuhan), [Hubei](83319.md), which is directly under the control of the central government of China. Originally known as **Second Automobile Works** when it was founded in 1969, FAW and SAW were the two major manufacturers before the [reform and opening up](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reform_and_opening_up) of China. It currently owns [Voyah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voyah), [M-Hero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-Hero), [Aeolus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dongfeng_Fengshen), [Forthing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forthing), [Dongfeng Nammi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dongfeng_Nammi) and operates joint ventures including [Cummins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cummins), [Honda](13729.md), [Nissan](21240.md), [Infiniti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infiniti), and [Stellantis](64563392.md) ([PSA Peugeot Citroën](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PSA_Peugeot_Citro%C3%ABn)).

- **[Changan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Changan_Automobile)** (**China Changan Automobile Group**, 中国长安汽车集团有限公司) is a central state-owned automobile manufacturer headquartered in [Chongqing](171752.md). It is the oldest automobile manufacturer in China, and can be traced back to 1862 during the [Qing Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qing_dynasty). Changan designs, develops, manufactures and sells passenger cars sold under the Changan Auto, [Deepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deepal), [Avatr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatr_Technology) and Kaicene brands. Changan operates joint venture companies [Ford](30433662.md) and [Mazda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mazda).

### Local state-owned manufacturers

*Local state-owned manufacturers* refers to manufacturers owned by the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of local governments (instead of the [State Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Council_of_the_People's_Republic_of_China)). Most local government-controlled manufacturers are ranked as Bureau-Director Level Enterprise or even lower level.

- **[SAIC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAIC_Motor)** (**Shanghai Automotive Industry Corporation**, 上海汽车集团股份有限公司) is a Chinese state-owned automotive manufacturing company headquartered in [Shanghai](27643.md). It is controlled by the municipal government of Shanghai. SAIC sells vehicles under a variety of brands including [IM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IM_Motors), [Rising Auto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rising_Auto), [Maxus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxus), [MG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MG_Motor), [Roewe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roewe), [Wuling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuling_Motors), [Baojun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baojun), and [Yuejin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuejin). Joint venture brands include [Buick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buick), [Chevrolet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevrolet), [Iveco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iveco), [Volkswagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volkswagen), and [Audi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audi).

  [^cite_note-9]

- **[GAC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GAC_Group)** (**Guangzhou Automobile Corporation**, 广州汽车集团股份有限公司), is a Chinese state-owned automobile manufacturer headquartered in [Guangzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guangzhou) and controlled by the municipal government of Guangzhou. GAC sells passenger cars under the [Trumpchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trumpchi), [Aion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GAC_Aion), and [Hyptec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyptec) brands and operates foreign joint ventures with [Honda](13729.md) and [Toyota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota).

  [^cite_note-10]

- **[BAIC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAIC)** (**Beijing Automotive Group Co., Ltd.&#x20;**, 北京汽车集团有限公司), is a state-owned enterprise located in [Beijing](18603746.md) and controlled by the municipal government of [Beijing](18603746.md). Its owns the brands of [Arcfox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcfox), [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAIC_Motor), [Beijing Off-road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BAIC_Motor), and [Foton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foton_Motor). It has foreign joint ventures with [Hyundai](42257.md) and [Mercedes-Benz](18564164.md).

  [^cite_note-11]

- **[JAC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JAC_Motors)** (**Anhui Jianghuai Automobile Group Corporation**, 安徽江淮汽车集团股份有限公司) is a state-owned enterprise based in [Hefei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hefei), [Anhui Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anhui_Province), and controlled by the Provincial Government of Anhui. It owns the brands of JAC Motors, JAC Yiwei, JAC Refine, and collaborates with Huawei under the [Maextro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maextro) brand.

### State-private mixed ownership manufacturer

- **[Chery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chery)** (**Chery Automobile**, 奇瑞汽车股份有限公司), previous state-owned automobile manufacturer based in Anhui and controlled by the municipal government of [Wuhu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuhu). It was restructured into a state-private mixed ownership manufacturer in 2025. It sells cars under the Chery, [Exeed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeed), [Jetour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jetour) and [iCar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICar_\(marque\)) brands. It also has [a foreign joint venture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chery_Jaguar_Land_Rover) with [Jaguar Land Rover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaguar_Land_Rover) for the production of [Jaguar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaguar_Cars) and [Land Rover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land_Rover) cars in China.

- **[Seres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seres_Group)** (**Seres Group**, 赛力斯集团股份有限公司), is one of the first [state-private mixed ownership](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed_economy) manufacturer in China. Headquartered in [Chongqing](171752.md), it is currently co-owned by the founder's family, the state-owned company [Dongfeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dongfeng_Motor_Corporation) and municipal government of Chongqing. It used to be famous for producing light commercial vehicles and budget passenger vehicles but is transforming to produce premium electric vehicles starting in 2021, after partnering with Chinese tech giant [Huawei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huawei). It owns the brands of [Seres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seres_\(automobiles\)), [AITO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AITO_\(marque\)), [Fengon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DFSK_Motor), and [DFSK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DFSK_Motor).

### Notable privately owned manufacturers

- **[BYD](1461760.md)** (**BYD Auto**, 比亚迪汽车有限公司), is an automobile manufacturer based in [Shenzhen](83368.md), founded by [BYD Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BYD_Company) which are known for their batteries. It is currently the largest electric vehicle manufacturer of the world. It operates brands such as BYD, [Denza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denza), [Fangchengbao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fangchengbao), [Yangwang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yangwang), and [Linghui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linghui).

  [^cite_note-16]

- **[Geely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geely)** (**Zhejiang Geely Holding Group**, 浙江吉利控股集团有限公司), is one of the biggest privately owned automobile manufacturers and is headquartered in [Taizhou, Zhejiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taizhou,_Zhejiang). Currently one of the fastest growing automotive groups in the world, Geely is known for its ownership of the Swedish luxury car brand [Volvo Cars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volvo_Cars), its performance counterpart [Polestar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polestar), and the British sports car company [Lotus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lotus_Cars). It also owns the brands of [Geely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geely_Auto), [Livan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livan_Automotive), [Lynk & Co](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynk_&_Co), [Zeekr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeekr), [Volvo Cars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volvo_Cars), [LEVC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Electric_Vehicle_Company), [Farizon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farizon_Auto), [Ji Yue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jidu_Auto), [Proton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proton_Holdings) and [Smart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_\(marque\)).

- **[GWM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Wall_Motor)** (**Great Wall Motor**, 长城汽车股份有限公司), is a private company famous for manufacturing [SUVs](47696.md) headquartered in [Baoding, Hebei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baoding,_Hebei). GWM sells vehicles under the brands of [Haval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haval_\(marque\)), [Wey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wey_\(marque\)), [Tank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tank_\(marque\)) and [Ora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ora_\(marque\)). It operates a joint venture called [Spotlight Automotive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spotlight_Automotive) with [BMW Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BMW_Group) to produce [Mini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mini_\(marque\)) brand electric cars.

- **[Leapmotor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leapmotor)** (**Zhejiang Leapmotor Technology**, 浙江零跑科技股份有限公司) is an EV manufacturer founded in 2015, headquartered in [Hangzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangzhou). [Stellantis](64563392.md) acquired a 20% stake in Leapmotor in 2023.

- **[Li Auto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Auto)** (**Beijing Car And Home Information Technology**, 北京车和家信息技术有限公司) is a manufacturer founded in 2015. It specializes in developing and manufacturing premium [range-extender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended-range_electric_vehicle) electric vehicles and has rapidly grown into a major player in the Chinese automobile market.

  [^cite_note-19]

- **[XPeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XPeng)** (**Guangzhou Xiaopeng Motors Technology**, 广州小鹏汽车科技有限公司) is an EV manufacturer founded in 2014 and headquartered in [Guangzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guangzhou). Together with [Nio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nio_Inc.) and [Li Auto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Auto), it is one of the first EV companies in China. [Volkswagen Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volkswagen_Group) acquired a 5% stake in XPeng in 2023.

- **[Xiaomi Auto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiaomi_Auto)** (**Xiaomi Automobile Co., Ltd**, 小米汽车有限公司) is the subsidiary of Chinese consumer electronics giant [Xiaomi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiaomi) that produces EVs. It was founded in 2021 in Beijing and launched its first product, the [Xiaomi SU7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiaomi_SU7), in 2024. Xiaomi is currently the only Chinese tech company that is directly involved in automotive manufacturing.

- **[Nio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nio_Inc.)** (**Shanghai Nio Automobile**, 上海蔚来汽车有限公司) is an EV manufacturer founded in 2014 and headquartered in [Shanghai](27643.md). The company is noted for its [battery-swapping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battery_swapping) stations and premium EV. The company launched an entry-level brand, [Onvo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onvo), and the high-end brand [Firefly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firefly_\(marque\)) in 2024.

### Foreign manufacturers

Following the [reform and opening up](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reform_and_opening_up), from 1994 to 2018, Chinese automotive policy mandated that foreign carmakers had to establish joint ventures with a Chinese counterpart to produce vehicles in the country, with the Chinese partner owning at least 50% of the venture. This measure was implemented to protect local manufacturers and provide it with the chance to bridge the technology gap and develop their brands.[^cite_note-23]

On April 17, 2018, the [National Development and Reform Commission](10465505.md) (NDRC) of China announced that foreign ownership limits on automakers would be phased out over a five-year period.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25] On 28 July 2018, China lifted foreign ownership restrictions on new energy vehicle production, which benefited American electric car manufacturer [Tesla, Inc](5533631.md). The company established a plant in Shanghai, becoming the first foreign automaker to open a wholly owned manufacturing facility in China.[^cite_note-China_Rolls_Closer_to_Relaxed_Ownership_Rules_for_Foreign_Electric-Car_Makers_-_Caixin_Global-26][^cite_note-27] The liberalization was followed by commercial vehicles in 2020 and passenger cars in 2022. The rule prohibiting foreign automakers from setting up more than two joint ventures in China was also lifted in 2022. Therefore, it became legally possible for foreign automakers to buy out local partners from joint ventures. In 2022, BMW and Volkswagen had acquired 75% stake in their joint ventures.[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29]

## Reversed joint ventures and cooperation

See also: [List of foreign brand vehicles made by automobile manufacturers of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_foreign_brand_vehicles_made_by_automobile_manufacturers_of_China)

In the 1990s, Chinese automakers pursued Western technology through joint ventures. However, a reversal occurred in the 2020s, with Western manufacturers now seeking technological assistance from Chinese counterparts and invested in China through joint ventures.[^cite_note-42] Several Chinese electric vehicle startups have leveraged their technological advantages, attracting investments from traditional Western automotive giants such as [Renault](162292.md)-[Nissan](21240.md), [VW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volkswagen), [BMW](3772.md), [Mercedes-Benz](18564164.md), [Toyota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota) and [Stellantis](64563392.md).

- **Renault**&#x20;

  - In 2017, [Renault](162292.md)-[Nissan](21240.md) and Dongfeng set up a joint venture called [eGT New Energy Automotive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EGT_New_Energy_Automotive) to produce [an A-segment EV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renault_City_K-ZE).

    [^cite_note-43]

  - In August 2023, [Geely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geely) and [Renault](162292.md) signed an agreement to set up a joint venture, later named [Horse Powertrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horse_Powertrain), with each entity holding 50% stake, to manufacture [internal combustion engine](41228673.md) (ICE) and hybrid [powertrains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Powertrain) for Renault vehicle with Geely's technology.

    [^cite_note-44]

  - In July 2019, [Renault](162292.md) Group announced a capital injection of 1 billion yuan to acquire a 50% stake in [JMEV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JMEV), an EV subsidiary of [Jiangling Motors Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiangling_Motors).

    [^cite_note-45]

- **BMW**&#x20;

  - In July 2018, [BMW](3772.md) and [Great Wall Motor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Wall_Motor) formed the [Spotlight Automotive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spotlight_Automotive) joint venture to produce and develop [Mini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mini_\(marque\))-branded EVs.

    [^cite_note-46]

- **Mercedes-Benz**&#x20;

  - In 2019, [Mercedes-Benz](18564164.md) announced the establishment of a joint venture partnership with Chinese automaker [Geely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geely).

    [^cite_note-47]

    &#x20;Geely acquired 50% of [Smart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_\(marque\)) brand to produce EVs based on Geely's [SEA platform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sustainable_Experience_Architecture_platform).

    [^cite_note-motor1.com-48]

- **Toyota**&#x20;

  - In 2020, [Toyota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota) announced its joint venture with Chinese manufacturer [BYD](1461760.md). The joint venture was set to assist technical know-how for Toyota's EV development and supply the battery, electric motor and electronic control unit for Toyota's EV.

    [^cite_note-49]

    &#x20;[Toyota bZ3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota_bZ3), the first electric sedan of Toyota, was built under the assistance of BYD with its technology.

- **Volkswagen**&#x20;

  - In July 2023, [Audi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audi) and [SAIC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAIC_Motor) announced their partnership that the EV platform from [IM Motors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IM_Motors), the subsidiary of SAIC, would be introduced into Audi's electric models. In 2024, SAIC and Audi jointly announced the launch of the [AUDI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AUDI_\(sub-brand\)) brand.

    [^cite_note-50]

  - In July 2023, [Volkswagen Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volkswagen_Group) announced its investment of \$700 million in [XPeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XPeng), the EV startup venture from China, for purchasing 4.99% stake of the company. The VW will collaborate with XPeng to develop two VW brand electric models for the mid-size segment in the Chinese market in 2026.

    [^cite_note-51]

    [^cite_note-52]

- **Ford**&#x20;

  - In September 2023, [Ford](30433662.md) and [Changan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Changan_Automobile) announced to establish a new joint venture [Changan Ford NEV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Changan_Ford_NEV?action=edit\&redlink=1), to produce and distribute Ford vehicles based on Changan's technology of electric vehicle. Changan holds 70% stake in the JV while Ford holds 30%.

    [^cite_note-53]

    [^cite_note-54]

- **Stellantis**&#x20;

  - In October 2023, [Stellantis](64563392.md) announced its investment to [Leapmotor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leapmotor) at the price of 1.5 billion euro, acquiring 20% of Leapmotor for the support of technology to produce EVs.

    [^cite_note-55]

  - In May 2026, [Dongfeng Motor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dongfeng_Motor_Corporation) and [Stellantis](64563392.md) signed a strategic cooperation agreement with a total investment exceeding 8 billion RMB, planning to produce new energy vehicles for the [Peugeot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peugeot) and [Jeep](15658.md) brands at the [Dongfeng Peugeot-Citroën](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dongfeng_Peugeot-Citro%C3%ABn) (DPCA) Wuhan plant starting in 2027 with two off-road vehicle models under the Jeep brand will be first introduced targeting at the global market.

    [^cite_note-56]

    [^cite_note-57]

  - In August 2026, Dongfeng and [Stellantis](64563392.md) announced to established an joint venture called Dongfeng Stellantis Automotive Technology. ## Chinese technology industry involvement in the automotive industry

Since the 2020s, Chinese technology corporations such as [Huawei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huawei), [Baidu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baidu), and [DJI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DJI), with their advanced software or hardware technological capabilities, started entering the automotive business through various approaches.

[Huawei's partnership with automobile manufacturers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huawei_Intelligent_Automotive_Solution) has taken the form of three business models, from the standardized parts supply model, the "Huawei Inside" (HI) model, and the [Harmony Intelligent Mobility Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmony_Intelligent_Mobility_Alliance) (HIMA).[^cite_note-th-65][^cite_note-66] Baidu and DJI have provided autonomous driving system and hardware to automotive manufacturers.[^cite_note-vf-67][^cite_note-68] [Qihoo 360](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qihoo_360) invested in the Chinese EV startup company [Hozon Auto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hozon_Auto).[^cite_note-ev-69] [Geely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geely) collaborates with Baidu to set up joint venture brands, and acquired Chinese smartphone company [Meizu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meizu) for its [Polestar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polestar) and [Lynk & Co](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynk_&_Co) brands with its auto [OS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operating_system) and [AR system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augmented_reality). [Oppo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oppo) collaborates with [SAIC Motor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAIC_Motor) on the [infotainment system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infotainment_system) used for [MG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MG_Motor) EVs. [Xiaomi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiaomi) is the first and the only Chinese tech company that is [directly involved in automotive design, development and manufacturing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiaomi_Auto), and operates its factory in Beijing.[^cite_note-xt-70]

### Major autonomous driving solution companies

- **[Yinwang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yinwang) (Shenzhen Yinwang Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd**, 深圳引望智能技术有限公司) is a company of Huawei which specialized in providing parts and hardware and software solutions for automotive manufacturers. The company was formerly known as **Huawei Intelligent Automotive Solution** (HIAS, 华为智能汽车解决方案), the automobile business unit of Huawei.

  [^cite_note-76]

  &#x20;The company is deeply involved in China's automotive industry, providing autonomous driving solutions for most manufacturers and brands.

- **[Momenta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Momenta)** (**Momenta Global Limited**) is a developer of autonomous driving technology based in [Beijing](18603746.md) and [Suzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzhou,_Jiangsu). Founded in 2016, it is backed and invested by multiple manufacturers including [SAIC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAIC_Motor), [GM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Motors), [Toyota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota) and [Mercedes-Benz](18564164.md).

  [^cite_note-77]

- **[Qianli Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qianli_Technology) (Chongqing Qianli Technology Co., Ltd** 重庆千里科技股份有限公司), formerly known as **Lifan Technology (Group) Co., Ltd.** (力帆科技（集团）股份有限公司), is a Chinese technology company specializing in the integration of artificial intelligence (AI) and automotive technologies. Headquartered in Chongqing, the companies is mostly owned by [Geely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geely) and is the major provider of autonomous driving solution for the Geely Group.

- **[Zhuoyu Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhuoyu_Technology)** (**Shenzhen Zhuoyu Technology Co., Ltd,** 卓驭科技), formerly known as **DJI Automotive**, is an autonomous driving solution company invested by [FAW Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FAW_Group) and [DJI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DJI). The company was originally established as DJI's automotive division in 2016 before being spun off as an independent entity in 2023.

- **[Horizon Robotics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horizon_Robotics)** (地平线) is a Chinese technology company headquartered in [Beijing](18603746.md). The company develops [artificial intelligence](1164.md) (AI) chips used in [self-driving cars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-driving_car) and [advanced driver assistance systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advanced_driver-assistance_system) (ADAS). The company is the major autonomous driving solution provider of [Li Auto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Auto), [SAIC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAIC_Motor), [BYD](1461760.md) and [Chery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chery).

- **[DeepRoute.ai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeepRoute.ai)** (元戎启行), is a Chinese autonomous driving company founded in 2019 and headquartered in Shenzhen, China. The company is invested and backed by [GWM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Wall_Motor).
