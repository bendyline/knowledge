**Ülle Madise** ([née](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Name_at_birth) **Anton**; born 11 December 1974) is an Estonian lawyer who is the [president-elect of Estonia](1581712.md), scheduled to take office on 12 October 2026. She has served as [Chancellor of Justice](41302199.md) since 2015.

## Education

Madise graduated in 1993 from [Tartu Descartes School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tartu_Descartes_School?action=edit\&redlink=1) and in 1998, received a *[cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_Honors)* from the [University of Tartu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tartu), Faculty of Law.[^cite_note-etis-1] In 2001, she defended her Master’s thesis titled “The Guarantee of Local Municipal Governments in the 1992 Constitution of the Republic of Estonia,” supervised by Kalle Meruski, at the University of Tartu.[^cite_note-\:3-2]

From 2001 to 2006, she was a doctoral student at the University of Tartu and in 2007, defended her doctoral dissertation, “Elections, Political Parties, and Legislative Performance in Estonia: Institutional Choices from the Return to Independence to the Rise of E-democracy” (advisor [Wolfgang Drechsler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfgang_Drechsler)), at the Institute of Public Administration at [Tallinn University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tallinn_University_of_Technology) and was awarded a Doctor of Philosophy degree in public administration.[^cite_note-\:3-2]

## Biography

Her father is [Tõnu Anton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%B5nu_Anton), a former member of the [Supreme Court of Estonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_Estonia) and signatory of the ratification of the [Estonian restoration of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estonian_restoration_of_Independence). Ülle Madise is also a professor of constitutional law at the [University of Tartu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tartu).[^cite_note-3]

In December 2021 Madise was reappointed by the [Riigikogu](794927.md) for a second term as Chancellor of Justice.[^cite_note-\:0-4][^cite_note-5] In Estonia, the justice chancellor is not a political appointment, but is appointed by the Riigikogu for a seven-year term. The chancellor is "to act as independent supervisor of the basic principles of the Constitution of Estonia and the protector of individual human and social rights, human dignity and equality, and the rule of law".[^cite_note-\:2-6]

From 2010 she served five years on the Board of Governors of the Estonian Academy of Arts. She was a member of the European Commission Against Racism and Intolerance, served on the Estonian Council of the Ethics of Public Officials, the European Region of [International Ombudsman Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Ombudsman_Institute) (IOI) and the IOI World Board.[^cite_note-\:1-7]

As chancellor of justice, Madise has commented on plans for surveillance cameras,[^cite_note-8] the publishing of details from surveillance phone calls of people not accused of a crime,[^cite_note-9] intervened in the issuing of digital identification cards,[^cite_note-10] and called the Estonian car tax law illegal.[^cite_note-11]

Madise was the host of two regular radio science shows on Estonian radio station [Radio Kuku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Kuku) between 2009 and 2018, and spends her leisure time watching nature documentaries.[^cite_note-\:1-7][^cite_note-\:2-6]

On 2 September 2026, the Riigikogu [elected](69822115.md) her to succeed [Alar Karis](67085807.md) as president of Estonia. She will be sworn into office on 12 October.[^cite_note-12]

## Work

- 1997–1998 Specialist, Department of Public Law, [Ministry of Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Justice_\(Estonia\))

- 1998–2002 Head of the Department of Public Law, Ministry of Justice

- 2001–2005 Lecturer at the University of Tartu

- 2002 Parliamentary Adviser to the Minister for Justice

- 2002–2005 Adviser to the Constitutional Committee of the [Riigikogu](794927.md)

- 2005–2009 Teacher at the Ragnar Nurkse Department of Innovation and Governance, [Tallinn University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tallinn_University_of_Technology)

- 2006–2010 Member of the Estonian National Electoral Committee

- 2009–2015 Legal adviser to the [President of Estonia](1581712.md)

- 2009–2011 Professor of Public Law at the Tallinn University of Technology

- 2011-2015, professor of constitutional law at the University of Tartu, Department of Public Law, Department of State and Administrative Law

- 2015-2026, The Chancellor of Justice of Estonia

- From October 2026, The President of Estonia
