The **Ministry of National Defense** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): وزارة الدفاع الوطني *Wizārat al-Difāʾ al-Waṭanī*) is [Lebanon](17771.md)'s service section for the [Lebanese Armed Forces](17779.md). The Ministry is located in [Yarzeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yarze), [Baabda District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baabda_District), [Mount Lebanon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Lebanon).[^cite_note-1] The building which is considered the biggest Ministry building in Lebanon was designed by the [French](5843419.md) architect [André Wogenscky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9_Wogenscky) in 1968.[^cite_note-2] The ministry building also houses the [Lebanese Military Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Military_Museum).
