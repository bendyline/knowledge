A **rival government** is a [political group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_group) that claims to be the legitimate [government](12229.md) ([exclusive mandate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exclusive_mandate)) of a [sovereign state](23604120.md) in opposition to another independent government.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] Such governments tend to be formed in the aftermath of a [civil war](7085.md), [revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolution), or [military coup](25914154.md). They differ from a [government in exile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_in_exile) as they are based in, and have control over territory within the claimed state.
