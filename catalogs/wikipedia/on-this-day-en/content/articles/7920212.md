**Staškov** ([Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_language): *Szaniszlófalva*, until 1899 *Sztaskó*) is a [village](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village) and [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality) in [Čadca District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%8Cadca_District) in the [Žilina Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%BDilina_Region) of northern [Slovakia](26830.md).

## History

In [historical records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History) the [village](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village) was first mentioned in 1640.

## Geography

The [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality) lies at an [altitude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altitude) of 444 metres (1,457 ft)[^cite_note-base_info-2] and covers an [area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area) of 21.85 km<sup>2</sup> (8.44 sq mi) (2025).[^cite_note-M-SK-A-1]
