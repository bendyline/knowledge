# Mexico at the FIFA World Cup

For the women's team, see [Mexico at the FIFA Women's World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico_at_the_FIFA_Women's_World_Cup).



Mexico national team at the [2018 FIFA World Cup](13327177.md) in Russia

This is a record of the results of the [Mexico national football team](808402.md) at the [FIFA World Cup](11370.md). The World Cup is the premier competitive international football tournament, first played in [1930](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1930_FIFA_World_Cup), and then every four years since, except [1942 and 1946](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_FIFA_World_Cup#Cancellation_with_the_onset_of_World_War_II), due to [World War II](32927.md).

Mexico has competed in the tournament since the inaugural 1930 World Cup, and has entered ever since, for a total of 18 tournaments, although they failed to qualify for the finals proper on three occasions: [1934](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1934_FIFA_World_Cup) (Italy), [1974](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1974_FIFA_World_Cup) (West Germany), and [1982](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1982_FIFA_World_Cup) (Spain). In addition, it withdrew from the finals in [1938](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1938_FIFA_World_Cup) (France), and was banned by FIFA from entering the [1990 World Cup](157232.md) (Italy) after fielding over-aged players during the [1988 Olympic Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_at_the_1988_Summer_Olympics) qualifiers, which was a youth tournament.

The tournament consists of two parts, the [qualification phase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_World_Cup_qualification) and the final phase (officially called the *World Cup Finals*). The qualification phase, which currently take place over the three years preceding the Finals, is used to determine which teams qualify for the Finals. The current format of the Finals involves 48 teams competing for the title, at venues within the host nation (or nations) over a period of about a month. The World Cup final is the most widely viewed sporting event in the world, with an estimated 715.1 million people watching the [2006 tournament final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup_final).[^cite_note-2006coverage-1][^cite_note-2]

Since they finished in the quarter-finals in [1986](157231.md), Mexico have failed to advance beyond the round of 16 in subsequent editions.[^cite_note-3]
