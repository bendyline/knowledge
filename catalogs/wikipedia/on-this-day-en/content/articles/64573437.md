**Bucha Raion** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Бучанський район, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Ukrainian): *Buchanskyi raion*) is a [raion](6719452.md) (district) of [Kyiv Oblast](494884.md), [Ukraine](31750.md). It was created in July 2020 as part of the reform of administrative divisions of Ukraine. Its administrative center is the town of [Bucha](3310001.md). One abolished raion, [Borodianka Raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borodianka_Raion), parts of abolished [Kyiv-Sviatoshyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyiv-Sviatoshyn_Raion) and [Makariv Raions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makariv_Raion), as well as [Irpin Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irpin_Municipality) and the city of Bucha, which was previously incorporated as a [city of oblast significance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_regional_significance_\(Ukraine\)), were merged into Bucha Raion.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-2020_reform_maps-4] The population of the raion is 375,536 (2022 estimate).[^cite_note-ua2022estimate-2]
