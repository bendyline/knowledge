**Sertolovo** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Се́ртолово; [Finnish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_language): *Sierattala*) is a [town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) in [Vsevolozhsky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vsevolozhsky_District) of [Leningrad Oblast](480845.md), Russia, located north of [St. Petersburg](24320051.md). Population: 68,241 ([2021 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Russian_census));[^cite_note-2021Census-7] 47,457 ([2010 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Russian_census));[^cite_note-2010Census-3] 38,444 ([2002 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Russian_census));[^cite_note-PopCensus-8] 17,705 ([1989 Soviet census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_Soviet_census)).[^cite_note-Census1989-9]

## History



&#x20;War cemetery in Sertolovo

It was founded in 1936[^cite_note-hist-2] on the place of a former settlement of [Ingrian Finns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingrian_Finns), whose inhabitants were [deported](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_transfer_in_the_Soviet_Union). The settlement was mentioned in earlier sources as **Sirotala**. In 1936, military personnel moved in and in 1939–1940, Sertolovo was used as a base from where troops were sent to the [Winter War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winter_War). From the beginning, Sertolovo was a part of Vsevolozhsky District of Leningrad Oblast. During [World War II](32927.md), it was not occupied and was together with the city of [Leningrad](24320051.md) [surrounded by German and Finnish troops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Leningrad). After the war, there was almost no civil population in Sertolovo, until massive housing construction started in the 1950s. In 1977, Sertolovo was granted urban-type settlement status, and in 1998, town status.[^cite_note-hist-2] It was the town of oblast significance until 2010, when the administrative structure of Leningrad Oblast was harmonized with its municipal structure.[^cite_note-10] At that time, Sertolovo became a [town of district significance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Town_of_district_significance).

## Administrative and municipal status

Within the [framework of administrative divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Administrative_divisions), it is, together with the [settlement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) of [Zapadnaya Litsa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zapadnaya_Litsa_\(rural_locality\)?action=edit\&redlink=1), incorporated within [Vsevolozhsky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vsevolozhsky_District) as **Sertolovskoye [Settlement Municipal Formation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Town_of_district_significance)**.[^cite_note-LeningradO_adm-1] As a [municipal division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivisions_of_Russia#Municipal_divisions), Sertolovskoye Settlement Municipal Formation is incorporated within Vsevolozhsky Municipal District as **Sertolovskoye Urban Settlement**.[^cite_note-LeningradOVsevolozhskyD_mun-4]

## Economy

The economy of Sertolovo is based on military installations. About 70% of the population as of 2009 were the military personnel and their families.[^cite_note-gen-11]

### Industry

Industrial enterprises in Sertolovo produce concrete constructions and plastic containers.[^cite_note-gen-11]

### Transportation

Sertolovo is essentially a suburb of [St. Petersburg](24320051.md) and is included into the suburban road network. In particular, it is located on the highway heading in the direction of [Vyborg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vyborg).

The town is several kilometers northeast of the [Pesochnaya railway platform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pesochnaya_railway_platform?action=edit\&redlink=1) located on the railroad which connects St. Petersburg and Vyborg.
