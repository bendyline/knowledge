# Border barrier

Not to be confused with [Defensive wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defensive_wall).



[Mexico–United States barrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico%E2%80%93United_States_barrier) at the [Algodones Dunes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algodones_Dunes) in California



[Israeli West Bank barrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_West_Bank_barrier)

A **border barrier**, **border fence** or **border wall** is a [separation barrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Separation_barrier) that runs along or near an [international border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border). Such barriers are typically constructed for [border control](247927.md) purposes such as curbing [illegal immigration](7124412.md), [human trafficking](19333662.md), and [smuggling](28645.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] Some such barriers are constructed for defence or security reasons. In cases of a [disputed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_border_conflicts) or [unclear border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_boundary_delimitation), erecting a barrier can serve as a *de facto* unilateral consolidation of a [territorial claim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Territorial_dispute) that can supersede formal [delimitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_boundary_delimitation). A border barrier does not usually indicate the location of the actual border, and is usually constructed unilaterally by a country, without the agreement or cooperation of the other country.

Examples of border walls include the ancient [Great Wall of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Wall_of_China), a series of walls separating China from [nomadic empires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nomadic_empire) to the north. The construction of border barriers increased in the early 2000s; half of all the border barriers built since World War II, which ended in 1945, were built after 2000.[^cite_note-FriedmanWalls-3]

### Antiquity

- [Antonine Wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonine_Wall) (began in AD 142 by the [Roman province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Britain) in [Britain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Britain))

- [Great Wall of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Wall_of_China) (parts were built as early as the 7th century BC by the [Qi dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qi_\(state\)) in [China](5405.md))

- [Great Wall of Gorgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Wall_of_Gorgan) (built in 5th or 6th century AD)

- [Hadrian's Wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadrian's_Wall) (begun in AD 122)

- [Madukkarai Wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madukkarai_Wall) (may have been built as early as the 1st century AD in [India](14533.md))

- [Southern Great Wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Great_Wall), southern counterpart wall to the Great Wall, erected to protect and divide the [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Han_Chinese) from the "southern barbarians" called [Miao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hmong_people) (meaning barbaric and nomadic)

### Middle Ages

- [Cheolli Jangseong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheolli_Jangseong)

- The [fortifications, castles and border walls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_of_Granada) between the [Emirate of Granada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emirate_of_Granada) and the [Crown of Castile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_of_Castile), later [Spain](26667.md) between 1238 and 2 January 1492 although it continued as the internal border of the [Crown of Castile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_of_Castile), then [Spain](26667.md) as [Kingdom of Granada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Granada) from 2 January 1492 to 29 September 1833. Currently the Wall is in good condition.

- [Macau](19068.md): [Ming dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ming_dynasty) (currently [China](5405.md)) built a wall around [Macau](19068.md) in 1570.
