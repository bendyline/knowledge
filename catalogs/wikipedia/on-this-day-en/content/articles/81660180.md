# 2026 FIFA World Cup Group A

&#x20; Group A of the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md) took place from June 11 to 24, 2026.[^cite_note-match_schedule-1] The group consisted of national football teams representing [Mexico](808402.md) (co-host), [South Africa](1018598.md), [South Korea](1018627.md), and the [Czech Republic](729739.md). This also marked the return of the Czech Republic and South Africa to the World Cup, respectively, since [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup) and [2010](168079.md).&#x20;

The top two teams, Mexico and South Africa, secured their place in the [round of 32](81667005.md#Round_of_32). Mexico won the group, winning three consecutive games, while South Africa won one game and drew the other, having lost their opening match to Mexico. South Korea finished third, having won one game, while the Czech Republic finished fourth with a single point.

### Mexico vs South Africa



Inside Estadio Azteca prior to the opening match

The two teams had faced each other four times, most recently in a [1–1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_FIFA_World_Cup_Group_A#South_Africa_vs_Mexico) draw in the [2010 FIFA World Cup](168079.md) [opening match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_FIFA_World_Cup_opening_matches), with South Africa as the host nation.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] This match coincidentally took place exactly 16 years after. Additionally, South Africa manager [Hugo Broos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugo_Broos) and Mexico’s [Javier Aguirre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javier_Aguirre) encountered each other on the same pitch during the [1986 World Cup](157231.md) as players for [Belgium](679716.md) and Mexico, respectively.

Mexico went in front in the 9th minute when [Julián Quiñones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juli%C3%A1n_Qui%C3%B1ones) scored with a low right footed finish through the legs of South African goalkeeper [Ronwen Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronwen_Williams) from just inside the penalty area after [Sphephelo Sithole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sphephelo_Sithole) was caught in possession on the edge of the penalty area by [Erik Lira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erik_Lira). Four minutes into the second half, South Africa's Sphephelo Sithole was sent-off after fouling Mexico's [Brian Gutiérrez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Guti%C3%A9rrez) when he was going through on goal.[^cite_note-14] It was 2–0 in the 67th minute when [Raúl Jiménez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ra%C3%BAl_Jim%C3%A9nez) scored his first goal at a World Cup finals, a header at the back post to the left of the net after a cross from the right by [Roberto Alvarado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_Alvarado).

In the 73rd minute, South African substitute [Themba Zwane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Themba_Zwane) was sent off for a slap in the face of Mexican forward Roberto Alvarado. In the 92nd minute there was a third [red card](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_FIFA_World_Cup_red_cards) in the game with Mexican captain [César Montes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%A9sar_Montes) sent off for denying a goalscoring opportunity by fouling [Khuliso Mudau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khuliso_Mudau) with Mexico going on to win the game 2–0.[^cite_note-15]

This marked the first time Mexico won an opening match as they had lost opening matches in [1930](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1930_World_Cup), [1950](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1950_FIFA_World_Cup) and [1954](60523.md), and drawing in the [1970](157227.md) (as hosts) and [2010](168079.md) editions.[^cite_note-16]

### South Korea vs Czech Republic



Pre-match ceremony between South Korea and Czech Republic

The teams had played each other three times, most recently in a 2016 friendly, a 2–1 victory for the South Koreans.[^cite_note-20] South Korea and [Czechoslovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechoslovakia_national_football_team), the Czech Republic's predecessor state, met previously three times in the 1980s, with a win for the Koreans and two draws.[^cite_note-21] The fixture marked the Czech Republic's return to the World Cup after a 20-year absence, following a group-stage elimination in [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup) during their debut as an independent nation.

The Czech Republic went in front in the 59th minute with a header from captain [Ladislav Krejčí](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ladislav_Krej%C4%8D%C3%AD_\(footballer,_born_1999\)) to the right of the net from six yards out after a long throw-in from [Vladimir Coufal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Coufal) from the right. It was 1–1 in the 67th minute when [Hwang In-beom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hwang_In-beom) received the ball from [Lee Kang-in](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Kang-in) before turning back onto his right foot before scooping the ball over the advancing [Matěj Kovář](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mat%C4%9Bj_Kov%C3%A1%C5%99) and into the right of the net.[^cite_note-22]

In the 78th minute, [Tomáš Souček](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom%C3%A1%C5%A1_Sou%C4%8Dek) headed in a free kick from the left, but the goal was ruled out for offside. In the 80th minute, [Hwang In-beom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hwang_In-beom) crossed low from the right and [Oh Hyeon-gyu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oh_Hyeon-gyu) finished from six yards out to the left of the net to put South Korea 2–1 in front. In the 82nd minute, South Korean goalkeeper [Kim Seung-gyu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Seung-gyu) saved down low to his right to stop [Adam Hložek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Hlo%C5%BEek)'s close-range effort with South Korea going on to win 2–1.[^cite_note-23]

### Czech Republic vs South Africa

The teams had only played each other in the [1997 FIFA Confederations Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997_FIFA_Confederations_Cup), where the teams drew [2–2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997_FIFA_Confederations_Cup_Group_B#South_Africa_v_Czech_Republic).[^cite_note-26] This was the only match in Group A held outside of Mexico.

By officiating this match, referees [Tori Penso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tori_Penso), [Brooke Mayo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooke_Mayo), and [Kathryn Nesbitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathryn_Nesbitt) became the second all-female on-field refereeing trio, and the first from the United States, to take charge of a match at a men's FIFA World Cup.[^cite_note-27]

In the 6th minute, [Michal Sadílek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michal_Sad%C3%ADlek) scored with a finish from the left of the penalty area into the right of the net. In the 83rd minute, South Africa were awarded a penalty when a shot from [Thapelo Maseko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thapelo_Maseko) hit the arm of [Pavel Šulc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavel_%C5%A0ulc) inside the penalty area. [Teboho Mokoena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teboho_Mokoena_\(soccer,_born_1997\)) scored the penalty, sending the goalkeeper the wrong way with a shot to the left to make it 1–1.[^cite_note-28]

### Czech Republic vs Mexico

The teams only played each other once in 2000, a 2–1 win for the Czechs.[^cite_note-36] Czechoslovakia played Mexico at the [1962 FIFA World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1962_FIFA_World_Cup) in a match in which Czechoslovakia scored after just 15 seconds, the second-fastest goal in World Cup history. However, Mexico came back to win [3–1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1962_FIFA_World_Cup_Group_3#Mexico_vs_Czechoslovakia). Their other two meetings saw both teams win once in friendlies.[^cite_note-37]

By defeating the Czech Republic 3–0, Mexico won all three World Cup group stage matches for the first time in its history. This achievement left Mexico as the only co-host to secure a perfect group stage record, following Canada's [2–1 loss](81660182.md#Switzerland_vs_Canada) to [Switzerland](681862.md) and the United States [3–2 defeat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_FIFA_World_Cup_Group_D#Turkey_vs_United_States) against [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey_national_football_team). Additionally, they were one of only three teams in the tournament to achieve a perfect nine-point streak, alongside [France](250197.md) and defending champions [Argentina](454699.md).[^cite_note-38] Czechia were eliminated from the tournament after losing their second group game, and South Africa defeated South Korea to claim second place in the group.

Additionally, [Guillermo Ochoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guillermo_Ochoa) secured his sixth FIFA World Cup squad selection, joining [Lionel Messi](2150841.md) of Argentina and [Cristiano Ronaldo](623737.md) of Portugal in achieving this historic milestone.[^cite_note-39]

### South Africa vs South Korea



Pre-match ceremony between South Africa and South Korea

The two teams had never met before.

In the 63rd minute, [Thapelo Maseko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thapelo_Maseko) received the ball on the right before shooting left-footed low into the right corner of the net from twelve yards out.

With a 1–0 victory, South Africa qualified for the knockout stages of a World Cup for the first time, as runners-up of Group A, while South Korea finished in third and was ultimately eliminated from the group stage as a result of their third-place ranking dropping down after other group results were finalized.[^cite_note-42]

South Korea's exit from the tournament prompted head coach [Hong Myung-bo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Myung-bo) to resign on June 28, amid fierce public backlash that included sharp condemnation from President [Lee Jae-myung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Jae-myung).[^cite_note-43] His lack of tactical flexibility shocked South Korean media and fans, and he was strongly criticized for overseeing a group-stage elimination at the World Cup for the second time, following [his previous failure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_FIFA_World_Cup_Group_H) in [2014](656933.md) after losses against Algeria and Belgium.[^cite_note-44]

## Discipline

The team conduct ("fair play") score would have been used as a tiebreaker if the head-to-head and overall records of teams were tied. It would also be used as a tiebreaker for the [third-place ranking](36581929.md#Ranking_of_third-place_teams) between groups if the overall records of teams were tied. The score was calculated based on yellow and red cards received by players and team officials in all group matches as follows:[^cite_note-regulations-11]

- yellow card: −1 point;

- indirect red card (second yellow card): −3 points;

- direct red card: −4 points;

- yellow card and direct red card: −5 points;

Only one of the above deductions could be applied to a player or team official in a single match.
