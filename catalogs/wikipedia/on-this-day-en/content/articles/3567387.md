**Inhambane** [\[iɲɐ̃ˈbani\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Portuguese) (also known as **Terra de Boa Gente** lit. 'Land of the Good People') is a city located in southern [Mozambique](19301.md), lying on Inhambane Bay, 470 km northeast of [Maputo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maputo). It is the capital of the [Inhambane Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inhambane_Province) and, according to the 2017 census, has a population of 79,724, growing from 54,157 in the 1997 census.[^cite_note-1]

The settlement owes its existence to a deep inlet into which the small [Mutamba River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutamba_River?action=edit\&redlink=1) flows. Two protective sandy headlands protect the harbour and form a sandbank. The sister town of [Maxixe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxixe,_Mozambique) is located across the bay from Inhambane.

## History

Inhambane is one of the oldest settlements on Mozambique's Eastern coast. In their history, they are recorded as [city-state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inhambane_\(city-state\)?action=edit\&redlink=1) that submitted to different empires throughout their time, starting from the [Kilwa Sultanate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilwa_Sultanate), the [Gaza Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_Empire), and finally [Portuguese Mozambique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Mozambique). [Dhows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhows) traded here as early as the 11th century. Muslim and [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_people) traders were the first outsiders to reach the area by sea and traded [pearls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pearls) and [ambergris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambergris). They also traded at [Chibuene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chibuene) in the north. The area became well known for its local [cotton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton) spinning and production by the [Bitonga tribe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsonga_people). Sometime before the [Portuguese](23033.md) reached the area, the [Karanga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karanga_people) had invaded Inhambane and formed a number of local [chiefdoms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiefdom). They dominated the Tonga cotton workers and reaped the rewards of trading with the Muslims.[^cite_note-A_History_of_Mozambique-2]

When [Vasco da Gama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasco_da_Gama) rounded Africa in the late 15th century, he pulled into Inhambane to replenish stocks and to explore. He took an immediate liking to the area and named it Terra de Boa Gente or 'Land of the Good People'. In 1505, a ship sent by [Francisco de Almeida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francisco_de_Almeida) was shipwrecked south of the town, but the Portuguese gained an initial meeting with the Karanga chiefs. Later, their sons landed on [Mozambique Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mozambique_Island) to survey the situation. The Portuguese eventually established a permanent trading post at settlement in 1534.[^cite_note-3] Inhambane was then chosen as the first [Jesuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesuit) mission to Southeast Africa in 1560.[^cite_note-A_History_of_Mozambique-2] The port gradually grew as an [ivory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivory) and [slave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slave) trading centre, particularly in the eighteenth century under mostly [Indian](14533.md) control. It was destroyed in 1834 by [Soshangane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soshangane),[^cite_note-EB1911-4] but grew rapidly in the second half of the century as a town of [Portuguese East Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_East_Africa).[^cite_note-EB1911-4] The old [cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathedral) and old [mosque](19894.md) were built during that period. However, in the 20th century the status of the town declined, and the economic situation worsened as [Maputo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maputo) (called Lourenço Marques before 1975) became the main centre.

The 170-year-old Cathedral of our Lady of Conception is located in the old quarter of the city where a rusted ladder leads to the top of the spire, offering panoramic views of the city and harbour.[^cite_note-5] The city is now home to a [museum](37585.md) and a [market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Market_\(place\)) and is known for its nearby [beaches](40630.md) of Tofo and Barra. The central market located along the main boulevard called simply "Mercado Central" offers numerous foods, ranging from a colourful array of [spices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spices) and [vegetables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vegetables) to [prawns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prawns), [fishes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fishes), and [cashew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cashew) nuts.

Motor and [dhow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhow) taxis sail from the town to [Maxixe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxixe,_Mozambique). The town of Inhambane has one of the largest working fleets of dhows on the [Swahili Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swahili_Coast).

## Recreation



Barra beach (Ponto do Barra)

Notable sites in the surrounding district of Inhambane and Vilanculos include [Bazaruto National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bazaruto_National_Park), the largest sea park in Africa, the Praia do [Tofo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tofo), Praia dos Cocos, [Ponto do Barra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barra_Beach), Ilha de Benquerra, Bazaruto National Sea Park, Guinjata Bay. [Scuba diving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuba_diving) in Inhambane is particularly popular in locations such as Manta Reef and Gallaria. Giant [manta rays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manta_rays), [whale sharks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whale_shark), [turtles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turtle) and other marine life are regularly seen and there are many professional scuba diving operations throughout the province. Many tourists are under the impression that they can dive from Inhambane itself. The closest diving to Inhambane is actually at Praia do [Tofo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tofo) - 22 km from Inhambane City. [Tofo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tofo) is known as the [whale shark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whale_shark) mecca of the world.

## Transport



Village in Inhambane province near the town

The easiest way to reach Inhambane is by road from Maputo on the EN 1. It is also possible to reach Inhambane from [Beira, Mozambique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beira,_Mozambique) or [Zimbabwe](34399.md) on the EN 6 and EN 1 after [Inchope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inchope?action=edit\&redlink=1). Inhambane has an airport [Inhambane Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inhambane_Airport) and there are flights from Maputo on most days and charters flying in from [Johannesburg](58893.md) in [South Africa](17416221.md).[^cite_note-6]
