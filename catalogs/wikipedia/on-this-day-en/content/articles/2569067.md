**Bekkersdal** is a township situated 7 km east of [Westonaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westonaria) and 14 km south of [Randfontein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randfontein) in the [Gauteng](309661.md) province. It was established in [1945](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1945_in_South_Africa),[^cite_note-wlm-2] to house Africans who worked in town and at the surrounding [gold](12240.md) mines. In [1983](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1983_in_South_Africa) the township was granted municipal status. The township was the site of violent protests ahead of the [general elections on 7 May 2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_African_general_election,_2014).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] In September 27, 2026, seventeen people were killed and 15 more were injured in a [mass shooting](76022905.md) carried out by eight gunmen at the [KwaNoxolo tavern](272204.md) with [Police](1228194.md) launching a [manhunt](11592326.md) for the gunmen.
