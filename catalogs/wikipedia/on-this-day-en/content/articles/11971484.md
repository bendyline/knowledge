&#x20; [Niue](21228.md) maintains [diplomatic relations](28165911.md) with various other countries and multilateral organisations. Since 1974, Niue has been a self-governing state in [free association](346414.md) with [New Zealand](4913064.md). The [King in right of New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monarchy_of_New_Zealand) is the head of state of Niue, making Niue part of the [Realm of New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Realm_of_New_Zealand).[^cite_note-MFAT-1]

## History

On 19 October 1974, Niue entered into [free association](346414.md) agreement with New Zealand. Under the [Niue Constitution Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niue_Constitution_Act), the state would remain part of the [Realm of New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Realm_of_New_Zealand) and would recognise the [Monarch of New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monarchy_of_New_Zealand) as its head of state. [^cite_note-Te_Ara-2] Under the arrangement, New Zealand also retains a constitutional link with [Niue](21228.md) in relation to [citizenship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizenship), with people from Niue being citizens of New Zealand.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-Te_Ara-2] Per the Niue Constitution Act, New Zealand provides economic and administrative assistance to Niue as well as assistance in foreign affairs, defence and security at the request of the Niuean Government.[^cite_note-MFAT-1]

The *Repertory of Practice of United Nations Organs* records that in 1988 "New Zealand stated ... that its future participation in international agreements would no longer extend to ... Niue".[^cite_note-UN-4] Niue was granted membership of [UNESCO](21786641.md) in 1993 and the [World Health Organization](33583.md) in 1994. Also in 1994, the [United Nations Secretariat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Secretariat) "recognized the full treaty-making capacity ... of Niue".[^cite_note-UN-4]
