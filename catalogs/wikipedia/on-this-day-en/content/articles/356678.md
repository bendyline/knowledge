The **Progressive Party of Working People** ([Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language): Ανορθωτικό Κόμμα Εργαζόμενου Λαού, ΑΚΕΛ, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Greek): *Anorthotikó Kómma Ergazómenou Laoú*, **AKEL**; [Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_language): *Emekçi Halkın İlerici Partisi*) is a [Marxist-Leninist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marxist-Leninist) party[^cite_note-COM1-2] in [Cyprus](5593.md).

AKEL is one of the two major parties in Cyprus, and it supports a [federal solution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federation) of the internal aspect of the [Cyprus problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyprus_problem) and it places particular emphasis on [rapprochement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapprochement) with the [Turkish Cypriots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_Cypriots). It supported entry into the [European Union](9317.md) with certain reservations. Initially supportive of the [Annan Plan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annan_Plan) in 2004, the AKEL ultimately opposed the plan because the [UN Security Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_Security_Council) did not provide guarantees on post-reunification security.[^cite_note-12]

As a strong supporter of welfare benefits and nationalization, AKEL successfully put into practice several social measures to support the economic welfare of Cypriots during the [Great Recession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Recession), such as increasing low pensions by 30% and strengthening the welfare benefits given to university students to €12 million per year. Overall, €1.2 billion were spent on welfare benefits during the first three years that AKEL was in power, with various improvements made in social welfare provision.[^cite_note-\:0-13][^cite_note-\:1-14] The party has been in opposition since the [2013 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Cypriot_presidential_election). The party's candidate was defeated in the [2018 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Cypriot_presidential_election) against the incumbent president. For the [2023 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Cypriot_presidential_election), the party supported independent candidate [Andreas Mavroyiannis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andreas_Mavroyiannis), who was also defeated.[^cite_note-15]

## History

It was founded in 1926 with the name **[Communist Party of Cyprus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Party_of_Cyprus)** (KKK; [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language): Κομμουνιστικό Κόμμα Κύπρου, [Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_language): *Kıbrıs Komünist Partisi*). The communist party set as its aim not only the struggle against exploitation, but also the independence of Cyprus from British rule. The party became illegal in 1931 when the British colonial government-imposed restrictions on civil rights following the [Cyprus revolt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1931_Cyprus_revolt) riot. In 1941, leading members of the underground communist party and others founded the **Progressive Party of Working People** (AKEL). In the first municipal elections in 1943 (before that mayors were appointed) AKEL candidates became mayors of [Limassol](57577.md) ([Ploutis Servas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ploutis_Servas)) and [Famagusta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Famagusta) (Adam Adamantos). At the 5th KKK congress in November 1944, the KKK and AKEL united into one party, the KKK was merged into AKEL.[^cite_note-16]

Unlike its predecessor, AKEL was not against [Enosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enosis). Instead, AKEL supported a gradual process, starting off with a constitution and self-government, while Cyprus would remain a colony, leading to self-determination and Enosis. After the failure of the consultative assembly in 1949 to grant a constitution acceptable to the Cypriot members, AKEL changed line, supporting immediate Enosis with no intermediate stages.

During the late 1950s, AKEL was opposed to the violent tactics followed by the anti-British resistance movement of [EOKA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EOKA). EOKA accused AKEL of being collaborators with the British, even though AKEL had also been illegal since 1955. Several AKEL members were assassinated by EOKA at the time for being "traitors", including AKEL supporter Savas Menikou, who was [stoned to death](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stoned_to_death). AKEL denounced EOKA's leadership as being anti-communist, as its leader [George Grivas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Grivas) had fought against the communist side during the [Greek Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_Civil_War). Grivas later founded [EOKA B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EOKA_B), which supported the 1974 coup d'état following his death.



Foreign Minister of Greece [Stavros Lambrinidis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stavros_Lambrinidis) and President of Cyprus [Demetris Christofias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demetris_Christofias) during his tenure in [New York City](645042.md) in October 2011

About 1958, the Turkish Cypriot nationalist organization [TMT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_Resistance_Organization) started forcing [Turkish Cypriots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_Cypriots) members of AKEL to leave. Editor of a workers' newspaper [Fazıl Önder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faz%C4%B1l_%C3%96nder) was killed and the head of the Turkish bureau of [PEO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pancyprian_Federation_of_Labour) (AKEL's trade union) Ahmet Sadi moved to the [UK](31717.md) to save his life.

In the first presidential elections for independent Cyprus, AKEL backed Ioannis Kliridis (father of [Glafkos Klerides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glafkos_Klerides)) against [Makarios III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makarios_III). The last [Turkish Cypriot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_Cypriot) to be a member of the central committee of AKEL, [Derviş Ali Kavazoğlu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dervis_Ali_Kavazoglou), was killed by TMT in 1965.

In the mid 1960s the [U.S. State Department](31975.md) estimated the party membership to be approximately 10,000 (3.25% of the working age population).[^cite_note-17]

### Recent history



Greek Foreign Minister [Stavros Dimas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stavros_Dimas) (to the right) and leader of AKEL [Andros Kyprianou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andros_Kyprianou)



AKEL headquarters in [Nicosia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicosia), [Cyprus](5593.md)

At the [legislative elections on 27 May 2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Cypriot_legislative_election), the party won 34.7% of the popular vote and 20 out of 56 seats. After this election, AKEL's General Secretary, Dimitris Christofias, was elected as President of the [House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_of_Cyprus), serving in that post until 2006. His election was supported by AKEL, [Movement for Social Democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Movement_for_Social_Democracy) (EDEK), and the [Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_\(Cyprus\)) (DIKO).

AKEL is a member of the [European United Left - Nordic Green Left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_United_Left_-_Nordic_Green_Left) political group in the [European Parliament](9581.md), and it is considered to be moderately [Eurosceptic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurosceptic). Cyprus joined the EU in 2004, and in the [2004 European parliament election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_European_Parliament_election_in_Cyprus), AKEL elected two members ([Adamos Adamou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adamos_Adamou) and [Kyriacos Triantaphyllides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyriacos_Triantaphyllides)).

AKEL remained the largest political party in the [2006 Cypriot legislative election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Cypriot_legislative_election); however, the party lost two seats, winning 18 seats with 31.31% of the vote.

In the second round presidential election held on 24 February 2008, Dimitris Christofias, General Secretary of AKEL, was elected President of Cyprus. Christofias won 53.36% of the vote against his right-wing opponent [Ioannis Kasoulidis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ioannis_Kasoulidis)' 46.64%.[^cite_note-18]

On 21 January 2009, [Andros Kyprianou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andros_Kyprianou) was elected general secretary of the party with 54.3% in the central committee election.

In the [2009 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_European_Parliament_election_in_Cyprus) to the European Parliament, AKEL received 34.9% of the votes, and again elected two out of Cyprus' six members ([Kyriacos Triantaphyllides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyriacos_Triantaphyllides) and [Takis Hadjigeorgiou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Takis_Hadjigeorgiou)). In the [2014 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_European_Parliament_election_in_Cyprus), they held their two seats with a reduced 27% of the vote.

In the [22 May 2011 legislative election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Cypriot_legislative_election) AKEL received 32.67% of the votes, and elected 19 out of the 56 members of parliament.[^cite_note-19]

In an interview with Athens News Agency, party leader Andros Kyprianou said that AKEL was considering Cyprus' exit from the [eurozone](184391.md), saying, "It is an option on the table", but that it will require "study and planning".[^cite_note-20]

In the [2013 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Cypriot_presidential_election), [Stavros Malas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stavros_Malas), who was supported by AKEL lost by a margin of 42.52% to 57.48%.[^cite_note-21] In the 2018 presidential [election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Cypriot_presidential_election), conservative president [Nicos Anastasiades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicos_Anastasiades) won a second five-year term with 56 percent of the vote. The AKEL-backed independent candidate, Stavros Malas, lost the election with 44 percent.[^cite_note-22]

In 2016 legislative [election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Cypriot_legislative_election) AKEL was the second largest party with 25.7 percent of the vote, 7 percent less than the previous election.[^cite_note-23]

[Niyazi Kızılyürek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niyazi_K%C4%B1z%C4%B1ly%C3%BCrek) was elected to the European Parliament in [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_European_Parliament_election_in_Cyprus) for AKEL, making him the first Turkish-Cypriot to enter the European Parliament and thus breaking what was considered a taboo on the island. AKEL advocates the creation of a federal state in which Greek Cypriots and Turkish Cypriots would live together.[^cite_note-24]
