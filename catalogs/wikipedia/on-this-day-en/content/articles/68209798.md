**Diana Maximovna Shnaider** (Russian: Диа́на Макси́мовна Шна́йдер, pronounced [\[dʲɪˈanə mɐˈksʲiməvnə ˈʂnajdɨr\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Russian); born 2 April 2004) is a Russian professional [tennis](29773.md) player.[^cite_note-3] She has a career-high singles ranking by the [WTA](57768.md) of No. 11, achieved on 5 May 2025, and a best doubles ranking of No. 8, reached on 16 June 2025.&#x20;

Shnaider has won five singles titles and three doubles titles on the [WTA Tour](1398173.md), and was a silver medalist in women's doubles at the [2024 Paris Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_doubles), with [Mirra Andreeva](71915009.md).[^cite_note-4]

## Early life

Shnaider was born in [Zhigulevsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhigulevsk) to father Maxim and mother Yulia. Her father is a lawyer and former boxer of [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volga_Germans) descent, while her mother is an English teacher. Her family later moved to [Tolyatti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tolyatti).[^cite_note-SportExpress-5]

She began playing tennis at the age of four. At the age of eight, she began pursuing the sport seriously, training with coach Samvel Minasyan in [Moscow](19004.md).[^cite_note-SportExpress-5] In 2022, she moved to the United States and enrolled at [North Carolina State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_State_University), where she played college tennis for the [NC State Wolfpack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NC_State_Wolfpack).[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

Shnaider's signature on-court look features a colored bandanna. She began wearing headscarves as a child to prevent sunburn, preferring them over caps and visors.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

## Junior tennis

Shnaider won the girl's doubles titles at the [2021 Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Girls'_doubles), partnering Belarusian [Kristina Dmitruk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristina_Dmitruk),[^cite_note-10] and the [2022 Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Girls'_doubles), partnering with American [Clervie Ngounoue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clervie_Ngounoue).[^cite_note-11]

On the [ITF Junior Circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ITF_Junior_Circuit), Shnaider had a career-high combined ranking of No. 3, achieved on 13 December 2021.

## Professional

### 2022: First WTA 125 title

Shnaider won her first [WTA 125](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTA_125_tournaments) title at the [Montevideo Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Montevideo_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles), defeating [Léolia Jeanjean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%A9olia_Jeanjean) in straight sets in the final.[^cite_note-12]

### 2023: Major debut, WTA Tour final, top 60



Shnaider at the [2023 Wimbledon qualifying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Wimbledon_Championships)

Shnaider made her Grand Slam tournament debut at the [2023 Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Australian_Open) after qualifying.[^cite_note-13] She defeated [Kristína Kučová](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krist%C3%ADna_Ku%C4%8Dov%C3%A1) for her first win at a major,[^cite_note-14] before losing in the second round to sixth seed [Maria Sakkari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Sakkari).[^cite_note-15] As a result, she reached the top 100, at world No. 94, on 30 January 2023.

After the Australian Open, Shnaider played one season of college tennis for [North Carolina State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NC_State_Wolfpack_women's_tennis).[^cite_note-16] She went 20–3 in singles to help the Wolfpack win the [ACC tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACC_women's_tennis_tournament) and reach the [2023 NCAA Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_NCAA_Division_I_Women's_Tennis_Championships) final.[^cite_note-ACC2023-17] She was named the ACC tournament's most valuable player and ACC Freshman of the Year and received first-team All-ACC and All-American honors in singles and doubles.[^cite_note-ACC2023-17]

At the [Budapest Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Budapest_Grand_Prix), she defeated top seed [Bernarda Pera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernarda_Pera),[^cite_note-18] but lost in the second round to lucky loser and eventual champion, [Maria Timofeeva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Timofeeva).[^cite_note-19] Shnaider reached the semifinals at the [Hamburg Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Hamburg_European_Open) defeating third seed Bernarda Pera in the quarterfinals,[^cite_note-20] before losing to home favorite, wildcard entrant [Noma Noha Akugue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noma_Noha_Akugue).[^cite_note-21]

In her debut on the Asian swing, she defeated eighth seed [Claire Liu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claire_Liu) at the [Guangzhou Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Guangzhou_Open).[^cite_note-22] She lost in the second round to [Wang Xiyu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Xiyu)[^cite_note-23] At the next tournament, she reached the semifinals by beating second seed [Petra Kvitová](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petra_Kvitov%C3%A1) at the [Ningbo Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Ningbo_Open).[^cite_note-24] Next, she defeated [Linda Fruhvirtová](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linda_Fruhvirtov%C3%A1) to reach her first WTA Tour final[^cite_note-25] but lost to top seed [Ons Jabeur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ons_Jabeur).[^cite_note-26] Following a semifinal showing at the [Jiangxi Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Jiangxi_Open), she reached the top 60 on 23 October 2023.[^cite_note-27]

### 2024: Four WTA titles, doubles Olympic silver, top 20



Shnaider with [Mirra Andreeva](71915009.md) at the [2024 Olympic doubles final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_doubles)&#x20;

In [Hua Hin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Thailand_Open_\(tennis\)), Thailand, she reached her fourth career quarterfinal, defeating top seed [Magda Linette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magda_Linette)[^cite_note-28] and [Paula Badosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paula_Badosa) by retirement.[^cite_note-29] Next, she defeated qualifier [Dalma Gálfi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalma_G%C3%A1lfi)[^cite_note-30] and third seed [Wang Xinyu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Xinyu)[^cite_note-31] to reach her second career final. Shnaider then defeated second seed [Zhu Lin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhu_Lin_\(tennis\)) in three sets to win her first ever WTA Tour title.[^cite_note-32] At the [Miami Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Miami_Open), she lost in the second round to 17th seed [Madison Keys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madison_Keys).[^cite_note-33]

She won her second career title at the [2024 Bad Homburg Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Bad_Homburg_Open) defeating [Donna Vekić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donna_Veki%C4%87) in the final.[^cite_note-34][^cite_note-WLM_Tennis-35] As a result, she reached the top 30 on 1 July 2024. On her [Wimbledon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Wimbledon_Championships) debut, she advanced to the third round with wins over former finalist [Karolína Plíšková](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karol%C3%ADna_Pl%C3%AD%C5%A1kov%C3%A1)[^cite_note-36] and [Sloane Stephens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloane_Stephens),[^cite_note-DS-37][^cite_note-38] before losing to 19th seed [Emma Navarro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Navarro).[^cite_note-39]

Shnaider won her third title of the year at the [Budapest Grand Prix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Budapest_Grand_Prix), defeating [Aliaksandra Sasnovich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliaksandra_Sasnovich) in straight sets in the final.[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41] Subsequently, she moved up to a career-high singles ranking No. 18 on 19 August 2024.[^cite_note-42]

At the [Paris Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_doubles), Shnaider partnered with Mirra Andreeva to win silver in the women's doubles, losing in the final to [Sara Errani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sara_Errani) and [Jasmine Paolini](46663470.md).[^cite_note-43]

Seeded sixth at the [Pan Pacific Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Toray_Pan_Pacific_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles) in October, she reached the semifinals with a win over [Viktoriya Tomova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktoriya_Tomova)[^cite_note-44] along with a quarterfinal walkover against injured qualifier [Sayaka Ishii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sayaka_Ishii).[^cite_note-45] She lost in the last four to top seed and eventual champion [Zheng Qinwen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zheng_Qinwen).[^cite_note-46]

At the [Hong Kong Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Hong_Kong_Tennis_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles) where she was top seed, Shnaider defeated qualifier [Kyoka Okamura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoka_Okamura),[^cite_note-47] [Priscilla Hon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priscilla_Hon),[^cite_note-48] [Suzan Lamens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzan_Lamens)[^cite_note-49] and defending champion and third seed [Leylah Fernandez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leylah_Fernandez)[^cite_note-50] to reach the final where she overcame second seed [Katie Boulter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Boulter) in straight sets to claim her fourth title of the season.[^cite_note-51][^cite_note-52]

### 2025: First WTA 1000 doubles title, fifth singles title

Partnering Mirra Andreeva, Shnaider won her first WTA Tour doubles title at the [Brisbane International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Brisbane_International_%E2%80%93_Women's_doubles), defeating [Priscilla Hon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priscilla_Hon) and [Anna Kalinskaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Kalinskaya) in the final.[^cite_note-53] The following week, at the [Adelaide International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Adelaide_International_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), she lost in the quarterfinals to [Yulia Putintseva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yulia_Putintseva).[^cite_note-54] Seeded 12th at the [Australian Open](81715708.md), she lost in three sets to Donna Vekić in the third round.[^cite_note-55] In doubles, Shnaider and Mirra Andreeva avenged their Olympics final loss against [Sara Errani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sara_Errani) and [Jasmine Paolini](46663470.md) in the second round[^cite_note-56] before losing in the semifinials to top seeds and eventual champions [Kateřina Siniaková](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate%C5%99ina_Siniakov%C3%A1) and [Taylor Townsend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Townsend).[^cite_note-57]

Again partnering Andreeva, Shnaider won her first WTA 1000 doubles title at the [Miami Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Miami_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_doubles), defeating [Cristina Bucșa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristina_Buc%C8%99a) and [Miyu Kato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miyu_Kato_\(tennis\)) in the final.[^cite_note-58]

Seeded 13th at the [Italian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Italian_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), she received a bye and then [double bagelled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagel_\(tennis\)) [Caroline Dolehide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caroline_Dolehide),[^cite_note-59] before defeating [Jaqueline Cristian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaqueline_Cristian)[^cite_note-60] and 25th seed [Elise Mertens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elise_Mertens) to reach the quarterfinals,[^cite_note-61] at which point she lost to sixth seed and eventual champion [Jasmine Paolini](46663470.md).[^cite_note-62] In the [doubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Italian_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_doubles), she and Andreeva lost to eventual champions Errani and Paolini in the semifinals.[^cite_note-63]

Seeded 11th at [French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), Shnaider lost to [Dayana Yastremska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dayana_Yastremska) in the second round.[^cite_note-64] In the [doubles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_doubles), she and Andreeva again lost to eventual champions Errani and Paolini at the semifinal stage.[^cite_note-65]

In June, Shnaider recorded wins over [Magdalena Fręch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magdalena_Fr%C4%99ch)[^cite_note-66] and Katie Boulter to make it through to the quarterfinals at the [Queen's Club Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Queen's_Club_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles).[^cite_note-67] She lost to second seed Madison Keys in the last eight.[^cite_note-68] In doubles, she and [Anna Danilina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Danilina) lost in the final to [Asia Muhammad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia_Muhammad) and [Demi Schuurs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demi_Schuurs).[^cite_note-69] Seeded 12th at [Wimbledon](80007712.md), Shnaider lost in the second round to qualifier [Diane Parry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diane_Parry).[^cite_note-70]

As third seed at the [Monterrey Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Monterrey_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles), she received a first-round bye and then defeated [Kamilla Rakhimova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamilla_Rakhimova),[^cite_note-71] fifth seed Mertens[^cite_note-72] and [Alycia Parks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alycia_Parks) to reach the final.[^cite_note-73] Shnaider overcame second seed [Ekaterina Alexandrova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekaterina_Alexandrova) in the championship match to claim her fifth WTA Tour singles title.[^cite_note-74][^cite_note-75] Seeded 20th at the [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), she was upset by [Laura Siegemund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Siegemund) in the first round.[^cite_note-76]

In October at the [Ningbo Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Ningbo_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles), wins against Wang Xiyu,[^cite_note-77] [Karolína Muchová](55266410.md)[^cite_note-78] and Zhu Lin saw her make it into the semifinals,[^cite_note-79] where her run was ended by fourth seed Alexandrova.[^cite_note-80]

Shnaider and Mirra Andreeva qualified for the doubles event at the end of season [WTA Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_WTA_Finals_%E2%80%93_Doubles) in [Riyadh](51554.md),[^cite_note-81] but were eliminated in the group stage.[^cite_note-82]

### 2026: Maiden major singles semifinal

Seeded ninth, Shnaider reached the semifinals at the [Adelaide International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Adelaide_International_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), registering wins over Leylah Fernandez,[^cite_note-83] Kateřina Siniaková[^cite_note-84] and sixth seed Emma Navarro,[^cite_note-85] before losing to her former doubles partner, third seed and eventual champion, Mirra Andreeva, in the last four.[^cite_note-86] Seeded 23rd at the [Australian Open](81715708.md), she lost in the third round to 12th seed [Elina Svitolina](27630639.md).[^cite_note-87]

At the [Charleston Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Credit_One_Charleston_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles), she received a bye in the first round due to being seventh seed and then defeated [Katie Volynets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Volynets)[^cite_note-88] and ninth seed Leylah Fernandez to make it through to the quarterfinals,[^cite_note-89] at which point she lost to top seed and defending champion [Jessica Pegula](32944701.md) in three sets.[^cite_note-90]

Reunited with Mirra Andreeva, Shnaider made it into the doubles final at the [Madrid Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Mutua_Madrid_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles).[^cite_note-91] They lost to second seeds Kateřina Siniaková and [Taylor Townsend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Townsend) in straight sets.[^cite_note-92] At the [Italian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Italian_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_doubles), she again teamed up with Mirra Andreeva to win their second WTA 1000 doubles title as a partnership, defeating seventh seeds [Cristina Bucșa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristina_Buc%C8%99a) and [Nicole Melichar-Martinez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Melichar-Martinez) in the final.[^cite_note-93]

Seeded 25th at the [French Open](83033049.md), Shnaider defeated 19th seed Madison Keys in the fourth round to advance to her first major quarterfinal.[^cite_note-94] After trailing 6–3, 4–1, she won 12 of the final 13 games to overcome world No. 1, Aryna Sabalenka, in three sets in the quarterfinals to reach her first major semifinal.[^cite_note-95] However, she was defeated there in straight sets by qualifier and eventual finalist, [Maja Chwalińska](53209114.md).[^cite_note-96] At [Wimbledon](83354904.md) she lost to [Liudmila Samsonova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liudmila_Samsonova) in the second round.[^cite_note-97]

Moving onto the North American hard court swing of the season, Shnaider reached the semifinals at the [Washington Open](83780909.md), losing to third seed Jessica Pegula.[^cite_note-98] The following week at the [Canadian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Canadian_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), she turned the tables on Pegula, defeating her en route to the quarterfinals,[^cite_note-99] at which point her run was ended by seventh seed [Iga Świątek](51838558.md).[^cite_note-100] As 15th seed at the [US Open](84044135.md), Shnaider lost in the third round to Taylor Townsend.[^cite_note-101]
