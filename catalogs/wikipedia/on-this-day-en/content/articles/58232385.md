The **Chief of Defence Forces** (**CDF**) is the professional head of the [Uganda People's Defence Force](56928.md) (UPDF). The CDF is responsible for the administration and the operational control of the Ugandan military. The position was established after the [National Resistance Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Resistance_Army) (NRA) was reconstituted as the UPDF, three years after the [NRA's victory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Kampala) in the [Ugandan Bush War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ugandan_Bush_War) in 1986. The current CDF is General [Muhoozi Kainerugaba](29207362.md).
