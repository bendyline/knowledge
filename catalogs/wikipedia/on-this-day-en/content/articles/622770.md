**Mahbubnagar**, formerly known as **Rukmammapeta** and **Palamooru**[^cite_note-3] is a city[^cite_note-4] in [Mahbubnagar District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahbubnagar_District) of the [Indian state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_state) of [Telangana](990267.md) named after the [6th Nizam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6th_Nizam) of [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad_State), [Mahboob Ali Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahboob_Ali_Khan). It is the headquarters of Mahbubnagar [mandal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehsil) in Mahbubnagar revenue division.[^cite_note-census-5] The city is also the largest in the district with an area of 39.64 km<sup>2</sup> (15.31 sq mi) and [7th most populous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_in_Telangana_by_population) in the state.[^cite_note-info-6]

The region was historically known for milk production. On 4 December 1890, the name was changed to Mahbubnagar in honour of Mir Mahbub Ali Khan, the 6th Nizam of Hyderabad.&#x20;

It is the headquarters of Palamoor mandal in Palamoor revenue division.[^cite_note-census-5] The city is also the largest in the district with an area of 39.64 km<sup>2</sup> (15.31 sq mi) and 7th most populous in the state.[^cite_note-info-6]

## Etymology

Mahbubnagar was formerly known as **Palamoor**, meaning "land of milk" due to its milk production.[^cite_note-7] The name was changed to Mahabubnagar on 4 December 1890, in honour of [Mir Mahbub Ali Khan Asaf Jah VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahboob_Ali_Khan), the [Nizam of Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nizam_of_Hyderabad) (1869–1911 AD).[^cite_note-8]

## History

Mahbubnagar was under *Janapada* rule by 6th century BCE, and later under Maurya territory. The region was at the core of the [Satavahana dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satavahana_dynasty) from 221 B.C.E. to 218 C.E., and also a large part of the [Chalukya dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalukya_dynasty) from the 7th to the 11th century CE, also part of the [Kakatiya dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kakatiya_dynasty) from 11th to the 14th century CE, The region was later part of the Kingdom of Golkonda (c.  – 1512), with its capital city [Golkonda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golkonda) located near Hyderabad.

In 1518, the region came under control of the [Qutb Shahi dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qutb_Shahi_dynasty), which reigned until 1687. The region was then a part of [Hyderabad State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad_State), ruled by the [Asaf Jahi Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nizam_of_Hyderabad), from 1724 to 1948.

## Geography

Mahbubnagar is located at [16°44′N 77°59′E  /  16.73°N 77.98°E  / 16.73; 77.98](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Mahbubnagar\&params=16.73_N_77.98_E_).[^cite_note-9] It has an average elevation of 498 metres (1633 feet). The city of Mahbubnagar is located at a distance of 98 km from [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad,_India), 130 km from [Kurnool](1039379.md) and 151 km from [Gulbarga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulbarga).

## Governance

*Mahbub Nagar municipal corporation* is the civic body of the city, which was constituted in 1942 as a *third grade municipality*. It was upgraded to *second grade* in 1959, to *first grade* in 1983 and finally to *Special grade municipality* in 2004 and got upgraded to Municipal Corporation in 2025.[^cite_note-info-6] It is spread over an area of 98.64 km<sup>2</sup> (38.09 sq mi).[^cite_note-12] Mahbub Nagar urban agglomeration consists of Mahbub Nagar Municipal Corporation, [census towns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Census_towns) of Boyapalle, Yenugonda and the [out growths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Out_growth) of Mahbub Nagar (rural) (full), Yedira (part) village.[^cite_note-census-5]

## Transport



Mahabubnagar railway station

The city is connected to major cities and towns by means of road, railway and airway. [National](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_\(India\)) and [state highways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_highways) that pass through the city are [NH 44](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NH_44), [NH 167N](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_167N_\(India\)?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [NH 167](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Highway_167_\(India\)), State highway 20 and 23. [TSRTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TSRTC) operates buses to various destinations from Mahbub Nagar bus station of the city.[^cite_note-16]

### Air

[Rajiv Gandhi International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajiv_Gandhi_International_Airport) in Hyderabad is only 86  km from the city.

&#x20;Mahbubnagar Airport is a proposed airport, for which multiple locations were surveyed and found unsuitable in 2025,[^cite_note-17] new alternate locations are being reviewed to address the hurdles identified by the [Airports Authority of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airports_Authority_of_India) (AAI).[^cite_note-18] including [Devarakadra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devarakadra) as a potential location.[^cite_note-19]

### Railway

The city has 4 railway stations in serving with the presence of [MahbubNagar Main Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahbubnagar_railway_station), Mahbub Nagar Town, Yenugonda Station and Diviti Pally Stations.
