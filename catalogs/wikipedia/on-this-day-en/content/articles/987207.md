The **province of Bergamo** ([Italian](14708.md): *provincia di Bergamo*; [Bergamasque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergamasque_dialect): *proìnsa de Bèrghem*) is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Italy) in the region of [Lombardy](43807.md) in [northern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Italy) [Italy](14532.md). Its capital is the city of [Bergamo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergamo). The province has a population of 1,119,233 in an area of 2,754.91 square kilometers (1,063.68 sq mi) across its 243 municipalities.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-area-1]

## Geography

Further information: [Laghi Gemelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laghi_Gemelli)



View of [Laghi Gemelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laghi_Gemelli)

The province of Bergamo borders the [province of Sondrio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Sondrio) to the north, the [province of Brescia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Brescia) to the east, the [province of Cremona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Cremona) to the south and the [Metropolitan City of Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_City_of_Milan) and the [provinces of Monza and Brianza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Monza_and_Brianza) and [Lecco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Lecco) to the west. The northern part spans the [Orobian Alps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergamo_Alps) with the highest point being [Mount Coca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Coca) at 3,052 meters (10,013 ft). Its rivers include the [Serio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serio_River), [Dezzo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dezzo), [Cherio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherio_River), [Brembo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brembo_River), and [Adda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adda_River). Its valleys include the [Seriana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Val_Seriana), [Cavallina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Val_Cavallina), and [Brembana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Val_Brembana). Other, smaller but important valleys include the [Valle Imagna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valle_Imagna?action=edit\&redlink=1), the [Val di Scalve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Val_di_Scalve?action=edit\&redlink=1), the [Val Brembilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Val_Brembilla), the [Val Serina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Val_Serina?action=edit\&redlink=1), and the [Val Taleggio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Val_Taleggio). The southern part is mainly made up of flatlands.

In the east, [Lake Iseo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Iseo) forms its boundary which the [Oglio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Oglio) from the [Camonica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camonica) Valley flows through. Minerals are found in the area of [Trescore Balneario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trescore_Balneario) and [San Pellegrino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Pellegrino_Terme) (the source of the mineral water of the same name) and other places.

## Economy



[ABB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABB) plants

The low-lying areas are rich in pastures along with corn, wine, grain, [rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice) and [flax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flax) that are cultivated. Hunting is common. Very common is also the breeding of [poultry](23197.md), pigs with modern techniques, and the traditional [sheep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheep) herding.

The provincial economy is based on [SMEs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_and_medium_enterprises), and varied products are made. The main heavy industries of the province of Bergamo are mineral processing, especially [iron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron), [concrete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete) and [marble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marble). There is a large tractor plant, in [Treviglio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treviglio), a lot of global companies have a plant in Bergamo, like [Tenaris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenaris) and [ABB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABB) in [Dalmine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalmine). In [Curno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curno) is placed the headquarter of [Brembo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brembo) which is also seated in [Stezzano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stezzano) inside a famous technology area named [Kilometro Rosso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilometro_Rosso?action=edit\&redlink=1). Another international brand present is [Alfa Laval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfa_Laval) in [Suisio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suisio).

[Silks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silk), metallurgical products and clothing are also common products. A Heineken Italia brewery makes *Moretti La Rossa* in [Comun Nuovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comun_Nuovo). Bergamo masons and assemblers are very famous for their ability and dedication to work.
