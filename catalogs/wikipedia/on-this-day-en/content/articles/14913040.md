**Chah Ab District** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): **ولسوالی چاه آب**) is an important **strategic** district of [Takhar Province](457289.md), [Afghanistan](737.md), situated along the [Panj River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panj_\(river\)) at the border with [Tajikistan](30108.md). [^cite_note-map-4]

While its economy is traditionally rooted in **agriculture** and livestock, Chah Ab is historically renowned as a major center for **gold** mining and traditional **jewelry** craftsmanship called *zargari*.&#x20;

The **majority** of Chah Ab's residents are ethnic **Tajiks**, locally referred to as *Chahabi* and who speak a conservative Tajik dialect of **Persian** language closely related to that of [Kulyab](439884.md) across the Panj River and [Badakhshan](1282198.md).

The name **Chah Ab** (*Persian*: **چاه‌** **آب**) is of [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language) origin, derived from *chah* (“well”) and *ab* (“water”). The toponym literally means *“water well”* and refers to a settlement established around a natural water source or well.

## Geography

The geography of Chah Ab is characterized by a combination of mountains, hills, plains, and river valleys. Much of the district consists of rugged uplands and rolling hill terrain connected to the Badakhshan mountain system. Several mountain ridges and smaller hills surround the region, particularly to the southeast and northeast.

The climate is relatively cold compared to other parts of [Takhar](457289.md), with significant snowfall during winter. Harsh terrain and seasonal weather historically limited transportation and communication between villages.

Chah Ab borders the [Amu Darya River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amu_Darya) to the north, while its eastern and western boundaries extend toward pasturelands, valleys, and historic settlements connected with Badakhshan and neighboring districts.

Chah Ab has an area of 764 kilometers, comparatively equivalent to [Dominica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominica).[^cite_note-islands-5] There are 63 villages within this district.[^cite_note-devplan-1]

### Location

Chah Ab is located in the northeast corner of Takhar province, and borders [Shahri Buzurg](8382350.md) to the east, [Rustaq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rustaq_District,_Afghanistan) to the south, [Yangi Qala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yangi_Qala_District) to the west, and [Khatlon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khatlon_Region) to the north. Shahri Buzurg is located in [Badakhshan Province](1282198.md).[^cite_note-natlmap-6][^cite_note-unfpa-2] Khatlon is a province, located in [Tajikistan](30108.md). The border between Chah Ab and Khatlon is the [Panj river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panj_river).

## Demographics

87860 people live in the district as of 2015. The district has 99 males for every 100 females, and the median age is 16.2. 40.3% of the population was employed in 2015, however 5% of those people were seeking work. The average household size is 6.5 people.[^cite_note-unfpa-2]

## Economy

Farming and livestock are the main economic activities of the area. The main agricultural crops grown are wheat, barley, flax, chickpea and sesame. Agricultural output is low because of a lack of new farming technologies and skilled workers.

Chah Ab is rich with mines and natural resources, and one material mined is gold.[^cite_note-7] However, they are largely unregulated. In addition, the forests are being cut down for light and fuel due to a lack of electricity.[^cite_note-devplan-1]

## Education and health

Only about a third of the population ages 15–24 could read in 2015,[^cite_note-unfpa-2] and under 10% in 2006. Education has poor quality, with a shortage of money, staff, supplies, and building space. Some people object to sending girls to school due to traditional sentiments.

There is only one health clinic in the district as of 2006, and it cannot serve everyone that needs health care. This causes a high mortality rate.[^cite_note-devplan-1]
