**Rob Arnoldus Adrianus Jetten** (Dutch: [\[ˈrɔp ˈjɛtə(n)\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Dutch); born 25 March 1987) is a Dutch politician who has served as [prime minister of the Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister_of_the_Netherlands) since February 2026. He has also served as [leader](172352.md#Leadership) of the [Democrats 66](172352.md) (D66) since August 2023. Previously, he served in the [fourth Rutte cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourth_Rutte_cabinet) as [Minister for Climate and Energy Policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ministers_of_climate_policy_of_the_Netherlands) from 2022 to 2024 and as [First Deputy Prime Minister of the Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Prime_Minister_of_the_Netherlands) in 2024.

Jetten began his political career as a policy advisor for D66 and as chair of the [Young Democrats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Democrats_\(Netherlands\)), and served in the [municipal council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_council_\(Netherlands\)) of [Nijmegen](21713.md) from 2010 to 2017. He was also elected to the [House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_\(Netherlands\)) in the [2017 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Dutch_general_election) and was chosen as D66's youngest-ever parliamentary leader in 2018. Following the resignation of [Sigrid Kaag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigrid_Kaag), Jetten became D66's leader ahead of the [2023 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Dutch_general_election), in which D66 lost several seats, forcing them into [opposition](3379016.md).

Jetten replaced Kaag as the outgoing First Deputy Prime Minister in 2024. In the [2025 general election](74282122.md), Jetten led D66 to its best-ever general election result as D66 became the joint-largest party in parliament. A four-month [cabinet formation](81512169.md) led to the minority [Jetten cabinet](82284736.md) in February 2026, which made Jetten the youngest person, and the first politician from D66 to serve as Prime Minister of the Netherlands.

## Early life and education

Rob Arnoldus Adrianus Jetten was born on 25 March 1987 in [Veghel, North Brabant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veghel), Netherlands, and grew up in [Uden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uden). He received his secondary education at [Udens College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Udens_College) between 1999 and 2005. He then studied at [Radboud University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radboud_University_Nijmegen) between 2005 and 2011, where he obtained a [BA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) and an [MA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) in [public administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_administration).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] After a period as a management trainee at the Dutch railway authority [ProRail](168969.md), he continued working there as a consultant and as a regional supply manager for the northeastern Netherlands.[^cite_note-3]

## Political career

### Early career

Jetten began his political career as a policy advisor for the [Democrats 66](172352.md) (D66) parliamentary group in the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_\(Netherlands\)) of the [States General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_General_of_the_Netherlands) and as the chairman of the [Young Democrats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Democrats_\(Netherlands\)). In addition, between 2010 and 2017, he was a member of the [municipal council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_council_\(Netherlands\)) of [Nijmegen](21713.md). In the [2017 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Dutch_general_election), he was elected as a member of the [House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_\(Netherlands\)). Jetten then became his party's spokesperson for climate, energy and gas, railways, democratic renewal and economic affairs.[^cite_note-4]

On 9 October 2018, Jetten was chosen as the new parliamentary leader of D66 in the House of Representatives, succeeding [Alexander Pechtold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Pechtold).[^cite_note-october-5] This did not automatically make him the new [party leader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_leader) as the new leader eventually was to be chosen in the 2020 Democrats 66 leadership election. Aged 31, Jetten became the youngest-ever parliamentary leader of D66. After his election, Jetten faced criticism in Dutch media because of his relatively young age.[^cite_note-6] He was nicknamed *Robot Jetten* due to his rehearsed answers.[^cite_note-lightning_career-7]

In June 2020, Jetten announced that he would not submit a candidacy for the position of party leader, naming [Sigrid Kaag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigrid_Kaag) as a better candidate.[^cite_note-8] He was instead put in second place on D66's candidate list.[^cite_note-9] When the [fourth Rutte cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourth_Rutte_cabinet) was sworn in on 10 January 2022, Jetten became Minister for Climate and Energy Policy. Under his leadership, the Dutch government announced plans in 2022 to invest 750 million euros (\$789 million) through to 2031 to have the country's gas network operator [Gasunie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gasunie) develop a national hydrogen transportation network.[^cite_note-10]

### Party leader



Jetten with [European Parliament President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_European_Parliament) [Roberta Metsola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberta_Metsola) in 2023

On 14 July 2023, Jetten announced his candidacy to become the next [leader of the D66](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leader_of_the_Democrats_66).[^cite_note-11] He succeeded Sigrid Kaag after she announced that she would not lead D66 into the [2023 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Dutch_general_election), due to the impact of the "hate, intimidation and threats" she received against her family.[^cite_note-12] Jetten returned to the House of Representatives as parliamentary leader after the election, in which D66 obtained 9 seats, a loss of 24.[^cite_note-13] Following Kaag's resignation effective 8 January 2024 to fill a role at the [United Nations](31769.md), Jetten took over her position as Deputy Prime Minister, and he served as acting Minister of Finance for four days.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] However, his tasks for the latter were in practice delegated to State Secretary for Finance [Marnix van Rij](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marnix_van_Rij).[^cite_note-17]

Jetten's term as minister ended on 2 July 2024, when the [Schoof cabinet](77133656.md) was sworn in.[^cite_note-18] When that cabinet presented its [2025 budget](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Netherlands_budget) which included €2 billion in education cuts, Jetten formed the self-named "unholy alliance" with centrist and conservative opposition parties. Coalition parties needed additional support in the [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_\(Netherlands\)), but D66 pulled out of negotiations in early December 2024, a week before an agreement was reached to reverse €750 million of the cuts.[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20] During the [Russian invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine), he advocated for Dutch defense spending to rise from 2% to 3% of [gross domestic product](12594.md), believing that Europe could no longer rely on the United States for its security following the second inauguration of US president [Donald Trump](4848272.md) in 2025.[^cite_note-21]

In the [2025 general election](74282122.md), Jetten's Democrats 66 (D66) and the [right-wing populist](8354382.md) [Party for Freedom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_for_Freedom) (PVV) ended up jointly being the largest parties, each winning 26 seats in the House. D66 experienced their best-ever result in general elections.[^cite_note-22] Jetten was credited for his optimism during the campaign.[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24] In the following [cabinet formation](81512169.md), D66 agreed to form a [minority cabinet](271434.md) with the [People's Party for Freedom and Democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Party_for_Freedom_and_Democracy) (VVD) and the [Christian Democratic Appeal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Democratic_Appeal) (CDA).[^cite_note-25]

### Appointment

Jetten was appointed as [formateur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formateur) on 3 February 2026. Debate in the legislature before his appointment as formateur focused on the proposed plans of his proposed parliamentary coalition to increase the age for the [Algemene Ouderdomswet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algemene_Ouderdomswet).[^cite_note-26] The minority [Jetten cabinet](82284736.md) was inaugurated on 23 February 2026, which made Jetten the youngest person, the [first openly gay man and the first openly LGBT person](54215417.md), and the first politician from the [Democrats 66](172352.md) party to serve as [Prime Minister of the Netherlands](273726.md).[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-auto-28][^cite_note-29]

### Coalition

The coalition agreement, entitled "*Getting to Work!*", promises "a better climate for [investment](63121.md)" and tax measures favourable to [businesses](39206.md).[^cite_note-\:0-30] Citizens, for their part, will be required to pay a "freedom contribution", a new tax intended to finance the increase in [military spending](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_spending). The government of Jetten also announced cuts to [social spending](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_spending), particularly in the healthcare budget, the reduction of the duration of unemployment benefits from 24 to 12 months, and lower benefits paid to people who are unable to work. The conditions for qualifying for a full pension will be tightened, and the [retirement age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retirement_age) could be raised to 70. The prime minister also promised the construction of new cities to address the [housing crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Housing_crisis), as well as the building of nuclear power plants.[^cite_note-31][^cite_note-32][^cite_note-\:0-30]

Following [US–Israeli strikes on Iran](82533582.md) in 2026, Jetten maintained a critical stance toward Tehran, calling for an end to Iranian military activities and expressing 'deep concern' over the regime's internal repression and its role in regional escalation.[^cite_note-33]

## Personal life

Jetten has been in a relationship with Argentine field hockey player [Nicolás Keenan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicol%C3%A1s_Keenan) since 2022.[^cite_note-34] They announced their engagement in November 2024.[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36]
