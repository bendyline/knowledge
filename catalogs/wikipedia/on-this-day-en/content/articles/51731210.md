**Deir Qanoun an-Naher** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): دير قانون النهر, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Dayr Qānūn an-Nahir*) is a municipality in [Southern Lebanon](4205968.md), located in [Tyre District](11166788.md), [Governorate of South Lebanon](1283570.md).

## Etymology

According to [E. H. Palmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Henry_Palmer) in 1881, Deir Kânûn meant: the convent of the rule (canon).[^cite_note-1]

## History

In the early 1860s, [Ernest Renan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernest_Renan) noted here a decorated [Sarcophagus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarcophagus).[^cite_note-2]

In 1875, [Victor Guérin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Gu%C3%A9rin) found the village to be inhabited by 400 [Metualis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metuali).[^cite_note-3] He further noted: "Here I saw an ancient rock-cut basin, many cut-stones built up in private houses or forming the enclosure of gardens and [cisterns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cistern), and, on the surface of a block lying on the ground, figures carved, to the number of five, each in a different frame. Unfortunately they are much mutilated by time and rough usage. The best preserved has the head surmounted by the high Egyptian coiffure known under the name of [pschent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pschent), and holds in one hand a sort of curved stick."[^cite_note-4]

In 1881, the [PEF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Exploration_Fund)'s [*Survey of Western Palestine* (SWP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PEF_Survey_of_Palestine) described it: "A village, built of stone, situated on the top of a hill, surrounded by gardens, fig-trees, olives, and arable land, containing about 250 [Metawileh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metawileh); water supply from springs, birket, and cisterns."[^cite_note-5]

## Demographics

In 2014 [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 99.71% of registered voters in Deir Qanoun an-Naher. 99.27% of the voters were [Shiite Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims).[^cite_note-6]
