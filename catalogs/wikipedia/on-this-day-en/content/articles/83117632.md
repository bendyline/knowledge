On 6 May 2026, an [Antar Lintas Sumatera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antar_Lintas_Sumatera) bus and an [oil tanker truck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oil_truck) collided on the [Trans-Sumatra Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans-Sumatra_Highway) in [Karang Jaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karang_Jaya?action=edit\&redlink=1), [North Musi Rawas Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Musi_Rawas_Regency), [South Sumatra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Sumatra). The accident resulted in the deaths of 19 people.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

## Chronology



Oil tanker truck involved in the collision

On 6 May 2026, an [Antar Lintas Sumatera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antar_Lintas_Sumatera) (ALS) bus was traveling from [Lubuklinggau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lubuklinggau) toward [Pekanbaru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pekanbaru) and [Medan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medan).

While passing through Karang Jaya District, sparks reportedly emerged from the bus. The driver attempted to steer the vehicle toward the right side of the road to minimize risk. At 12\:39 [WIB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Indonesian_Time), an oil tanker truck traveling at high speed from the opposite direction appeared simultaneously, resulting in an unavoidable collision.[^cite_note-3]

## Casualties

A total of 16 people were killed in the incident, including 14 bus passengers and 2 crew members of the tanker truck.[^cite_note-4]

## Response and evacuation

Following the incident, personnel from the Regional Disaster Management Agency (BPBD) and the [Indonesian National Police](18251498.md) were deployed to the scene.[^cite_note-5]

## Investigation

The exact cause of the accident remains under investigation by the [National Transportation Safety Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Transportation_Safety_Committee), police, and relevant authorities.[^cite_note-6]
