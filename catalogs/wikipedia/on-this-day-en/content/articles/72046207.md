***Semafor*** is an American news website founded in 2022 by [Ben Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Smith_\(journalist\)), a former editor-in-chief of *[BuzzFeed News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BuzzFeed_News)* and media columnist at *[The New York Times](30680.md),* and [Justin B. Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_B._Smith), the former CEO of [Bloomberg Media Group](3057255.md).[^cite_note-Scire-1][^cite_note-Allsop-2022-2] *Semafor* focuses on news related to business, technology, politics, and other subjects, as well as covering global issues. As of 2026, *Semafor* is valued at \$330 million after recording its first profitable year in 2025.[^cite_note-Simonetti-3]

*Semafor*'s advisory board includes [Microsoft](19001.md) CEO [Satya Nadella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satya_Nadella), [Nvidia](39120.md) CEO [Jensen Huang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang), [Anthropic](6206236.md) President [Daniela Amodei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniela_Amodei), and [LinkedIn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn) co-founder [Reid Hoffman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reid_Hoffman).[^cite_note-sfstandard.com-4]

## Description and etymology

*[Vox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vox_\(website\))* has described Semafor as "a collection of newsletters, plus a website, aimed at an upscale audience that understands topics like Washington politics and Silicon Valley tech but wants more". The website is free to access and supported by advertisements, though *Semafor*'s co-founders have described plans to convert to a paid subscription model.[^cite_note-Vox-5] The organization is based in New York City.[^cite_note-CJR2-6]

The name "Semafor" is derived from the word "[semaphore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semaphore)", which "appears in similar form in many languages". According to *The New York Times*, "semaphore" is "often used in a nautical context" and can be described as "a visual signaling apparatus often involving flags, lights and arm gestures".[^cite_note-Grynbaum-2022-7] The co-founders appreciated that the word "sounds about the same in thirty-five languages".[^cite_note-CJR2-6]

## History

### Founding

*Semafor* was launched by journalist [Ben Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Smith_\(journalist\)), who was previously a media columnist for *The New York Times*,[^cite_note-Grynbaum-2022-7] and [Justin B. Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_B._Smith) (no relation), the [chief executive officer](52234.md) of Bloomberg Media Group, in October 2022.[^cite_note-Robertson-2022-8][^cite_note-Vox-5] The duo had met in Manhattan in 2008 and kept in touch; at a conference in [Davos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davos) in 2018, they began a series of discussions that led to the development of *Semafor* over four years.[^cite_note-CJR2-6] Ahead of the launch, both men resigned from their previous positions in January 2022.[^cite_note-VF-9][^cite_note-10] A federal trademark registration for *Semafor* was filed on January 16, and the Smiths announced the organization's name in March 2022.[^cite_note-Grynbaum-2022-7] In a memo Justin Smith sent to "close confidants", he described a new company that would "reimagine quality global journalism" aimed at what he said was an "English-speaking, college-educated, professional class" that had "lost trust in all sources of news and information".[^cite_note-11] *Semafor* launched on October 18, 2022, with [Gina Chua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gina_Chua) as executive editor.[^cite_note-Scire-1][^cite_note-Allsop-2022-2]

### Expansion

Leading up to the launch, *Semafor* advertised heavily on [Twitter](9988187.md). *Semafor* and Twitter also launched a video distribution partnership.[^cite_note-Deadline2-12] *Semafor* partnered with [Gallup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gallup,_Inc.) for data, and uses office space in the [analytics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analytics) company's headquarters in Washington, D.C.[^cite_note-Robertson-2022-8] In addition to offices in New York City and Washington, D.C., *Semafor* has a presence in London and Africa. There are plans to expand to Asia, other parts of Europe, and Latin America.[^cite_note-13] *Semafor* has an equity program that shares profits with employees.[^cite_note-Scire-1]

In December 2023, [Ozy Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ozy_Media) sued *Semafor*, Buzzfeed, and Ben Smith for allegedly stealing [trade secrets](29960.md).[^cite_note-14]

In July 2024, *Semafor* launched a Middle East vertical, adding another world region to its existing coverage of the United States and sub-Saharan Africa.[^cite_note-15] In December 2024, Semafor launched *Semafor Gulf*, with Mohammed Sergie (previously with Bloomberg News and the Council on Foreign Relations) as editor.[^cite_note-16]

### Funding

Operations were initially supported by \$25 million in investment funding, as well as revenue generated from advertising and in-person events. Investors have included [Sam Bankman-Fried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Bankman-Fried), [David G. Bradley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_G._Bradley), [Jorge Paulo Lemann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jorge_Paulo_Lemann),[^cite_note-CJR2-6] and [Jessica Lessin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Lessin).[^cite_note-Robertson-2022-8] [Genesis Motor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genesis_Motor)/[Hyundai Motor Company](42257.md), [Mastercard](378791.md), [Pfizer](62304.md), [Qualcomm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qualcomm), the Indian multinational conglomerate [Tata Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Group), and [Verizon](18619278.md) were among ten founding launch partners.[^cite_note-CJR2-6][^cite_note-Deadline2-12] Other advertisers and sponsors included [Cisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cisco) and [Alibaba Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alibaba_Group).[^cite_note-Stenberg-17][^cite_note-\:1-18] In December 2022, *Semafor* Climate Editor Bill Spindle left the company after unsuccessfully trying to remove adverts from [Chevron](284749.md) next to articles about [climate change](5042951.md), criticizing *Semafor's* "Over-Dependence on Chevron Advertising". Semafor said the advertising did not influence its coverage.[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21] Dylan Byers of [Puck News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puck_\(media_company\)) wrote in 2025 that Semafor has "morphed into an events business oriented almost entirely around sponsorship deals."[^cite_note-22]

In 2023, *Semafor* announced plans to repurchase Bankman-Fried's investment following the [bankruptcy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bankruptcy_of_FTX) of [FTX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FTX).[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24] *Semafor's* co-founder Justin Smith stated that Bankman-Fried had received "no actual shares" in *Semafor* due to the company's dual-class share structure and that neither he nor any other investors influence [editorial](6958049.md) coverage or operations.[^cite_note-25] Although Bankman-Fried was *Semafor's* largest external investor, he would only have received a small minority stake if he had converted his investment to [equity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equity_\(finance\)).[^cite_note-26] In May 2023, *Semafor* raised \$19 million in additional funding from investors to replace the money received from Bankman-Fried.[^cite_note-27]

*Semafor* has received criticism for its relationship with individuals and companies tied to the [Chinese Communist Party](7175.md).[^cite_note-Fischer-2023-28] In January 2023, [Voice of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voice_of_America) reported that *Semafor* received sponsorship funding from [Chinese](5405.md) [e-commerce](9611.md) giant Alibaba Group.[^cite_note-\:1-18]

In January 2026, *Semafor* raised \$30 million in a new funding round, with plans to expand its events business and hire additional journalists.[^cite_note-Simonetti-3] The financing valued the company at \$330 million after its first profitable year in 2025.[^cite_note-Simonetti-3]

### Staff

Co-founders Justin B. Smith and Ben Smith are the chief executive officer and [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief), respectively.[^cite_note-Coster-29] Gina Chua was announced as the executive editor in March 2022.[^cite_note-30] Rachel Oppenheim was hired as the [chief revenue officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_revenue_officer), Kellen Henry as the [head of product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_product),[^cite_note-Robertson-2022-8] and Al Lucca as the [head of design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_design_officer).

*Semafor* launched with approximately 60 employees—at least half of whom were reporters.[^cite_note-CJR2-6][^cite_note-CNBC-31] Initial hires included [Reed Albergotti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reed_Albergotti) of *[The Washington Post](102226.md)*, Liz Hoffman of *[The Wall Street Journal](173070.md)*,[^cite_note-Robertson-2022-8][^cite_note-32] and Max Tani of *[Politico](9172097.md)*, who would focus on technology, business and finance, and media, respectively.[^cite_note-Allsop-2022-2] In his role as editor-at-large,[^cite_note-Deadline2-12] [Steven Clemons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Clemons), also formerly of *The Wall Street Journal*, was hired to oversee live journalism operations, moderate on-stage interviews, and write a newsletter about American politics and policy.[^cite_note-Robertson-2022-8] [David Weigel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Weigel) writes a newsletter called *Americana*.[^cite_note-Deadline2-12]

Benjy Sarlin is chief of the Washington, D.C., bureau.[^cite_note-Deadline2-12] [Nigerian](21383.md) editor Yinka Adegoke leads *Semafor Africa*, the organization's first international edition.[^cite_note-CJR2-6] Alexis Akwagyiram joined as managing editor from the *[Financial Times](136566.md)*.[^cite_note-Scire-1]

## Content

*Semafor's* articles and newsletters are structured into short sections, each with a separate subtitle. The first section generally consists of a short summary of the relevant facts, followed by "The Reporter's View", a section containing analysis or an editorial by the writer of the article.[^cite_note-Allsop-2022-2] Other common sections include "The View From", which describes perspectives from countries or parties involved in the story, and "Room for Disagreement", which explores why the writer's analysis may be wrong.[^cite_note-Allsop-2022-2][^cite_note-CNBC-31] The structure, which *Semafor* calls "Semaform", is intended to clearly separate fact from opinion.[^cite_note-Robertson-2022-8][^cite_note-33]

*Semafor* publishes a daily newsletter entitled *Flagship* that covers [world news](29071145.md),[^cite_note-Deadline2-12] as well as distinct newsletters focusing on Africa, U.S. politics and policy, business and finance, climate, international security, media, and technology. Each newsletter contains a regular feature called "One Good Text" in which journalists conduct one-question interviews with politicians, business executives, and other notable individuals via [text message](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Text_messaging).[^cite_note-34]

## Events

Events are a significant part of *Semafor's* operations: 30% of its revenue in 2022, its first year of operation, was generated by events.[^cite_note-HR-35] In 2025, events accounted for more than half of its revenue.[^cite_note-36] Co-founder Justin B. Smith noted that part of the motivation for dedicating a large portion of operations to events was that traditional sources of revenue, such as subscriptions and advertising, take a long time to cultivate, while events provide an immediate source of income for a news [startup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Startup_company).[^cite_note-Stenberg-17] Even before launching, *Semafor* had already held 12 events,[^cite_note-Bauder-37] including one in July 2022 with [Tucker Carlson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tucker_Carlson) and [Taylor Lorenz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Lorenz) that focused on [polarization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Media_bias_in_the_United_States) and trust within the news industry,[^cite_note-Deadline2-12] and another in November 2022 called "Media, Government, and a Healthy Democracy" with [Karine Jean-Pierre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karine_Jean-Pierre) and [Anthony Scaramucci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Scaramucci).[^cite_note-CJR2-6]

*Semafor* also hosted several live journalism event series. In December 2022, it hosted an event focusing on Africa to coincide with that year's [U.S.–Africa Leaders Summit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States%E2%80%93Africa_Leaders_Summit_2022) in Washington, D.C. The event featured interviews with [Rwandan](25645.md) president [Paul Kagame](393593.md),[^cite_note-38] U.S. trade representative [Katherine Tai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katherine_Tai),[^cite_note-39] and [Qin Gang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qin_Gang), [China's ambassador to the U.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ambassadors_of_China_to_the_United_States)[^cite_note-Stenberg-17][^cite_note-HR-35] Subsequent events included a "media summit" in April 2023 in New York City with [Barry Diller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_Diller),[^cite_note-40] [Chris Licht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Licht), [Jen Psaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jen_Psaki),[^cite_note-41] [Stephen A. Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_A._Smith), and [Kara Swisher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kara_Swisher),[^cite_note-HR-35][^cite_note-42] and a "World Economy Summit" in Washington, D.C., in April 2023 with [National Economic Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Economic_Council_\(United_States\)) director [Lael Brainard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lael_Brainard)[^cite_note-43][^cite_note-44] and [Microsoft](19001.md) president [Brad Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Smith_\(American_lawyer\)).[^cite_note-45]

## China

In March 2023, *Semafor* launched its "China and Global Business Initiative", a collaboration with the [Center for China and Globalization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_for_China_and_Globalization) (CCG), a [think tank](37101.md) registered as a [non-governmental organization](46539.md) (though its independence from the Chinese Communist Party has been disputed).[^cite_note-Fischer-2023-28] The initiative consists of regular events in [New York City](645042.md) and [Beijing](18603746.md), and would foster dialogue between business leaders amid increasing [China–U.S. tensions](277880.md).[^cite_note-46]

That same year, *Semafor's* partnership with the CCG came under scrutiny by *[Axios](52436164.md)* and other news outlets.[^cite_note-47] *Semafor* CEO Justin Smith said *Semafor* would go into the collaboration with its "eyes wide open" and that it was under "\[no] illusion that Chinese business leaders or other local groups operate independently of the Chinese Communist Party." Smith also said *Semafor* would retain [editorial independence](6958049.md) and full ownership of the project.[^cite_note-Fischer-2023-28]

The project's advisory board contains both U.S. and Chinese business leaders and academics,[^cite_note-\:0-48][^cite_note-sfstandard.com-4] such as [Robin Zeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robin_Zeng)—the chairman of [Contemporary Amperex Technology Co., Limited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CATL) and a member of the [Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_People's_Political_Consultative_Conference)—and [Wang Huiyao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Huiyao), founder and President of CCG. Other advisors include [Chen Deming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chen_Deming), a former [Minister of Commerce](638506.md); [Cui Tiankai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cui_Tiankai), a former Chinese ambassador to the U.S.; and [Zhou Xiaochuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhou_Xiaochuan), a former governor of [People's Bank of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Bank_of_China). U.S. board members include [Jessica Chen Weiss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Chen_Weiss), a professor at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) and former senior advisor to the [United States Department of State](31975.md)'s [Policy Planning Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Policy_Planning_Staff_\(United_States\)); [Jerry Yang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerry_Yang), co-founder of [Yahoo!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahoo!); [Susan Thornton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susan_Thornton), former acting [Assistant Secretary of State for East Asian and Pacific Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_Secretary_of_State_for_East_Asian_and_Pacific_Affairs); [Rana Mitter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rana_Mitter), S.T. Lee Chair in U.S.–Asia Relations at the [Harvard Kennedy School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Kennedy_School); [John L. Thornton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_L._Thornton), former co-president of [Goldman Sachs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goldman_Sachs); and [David Rubenstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Rubenstein), a philanthropist, investor, and former [Deputy Assistant to the President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Assistant_to_the_President) for domestic policy.[^cite_note-\:0-48]
