The **Pahlavi dynasty** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): خاندان پهلوی, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Persian): *Xândân-e Pahlavi*) is an [Iranian royal dynasty](13877.md) that was the [last to rule Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pahlavi_Iran) before the country's monarchy was overthrown in the [Iranian Revolution](347268.md) in 1979. It was founded in 1925 by [Reza Shah Pahlavi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reza_Shah), born Reza Khan, a non-aristocratic Iranian soldier of [Mazanderani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mazanderani_people) origin,[^cite_note-3] who took on the name of the [Pahlavi scripts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pahlavi_scripts) of the [Middle Persian language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Persian) from the [Sasanian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sasanian_Empire) of [pre-Islamic Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim_conquest_of_Persia). The dynasty largely espoused this form of [Iranian nationalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_nationalism) rooted in the pre-Islamic era (notably based on the [Achaemenid Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achaemenid_Empire)) during its time in power, especially under its last Shah [Mohammad Reza Pahlavi](20065598.md).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

The dynasty replaced the [Qajar dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qajar_family) in 1925 after the [1921 coup d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1921_Persian_coup_d'%C3%A9tat), beginning on 14 January 1921 when 42-year-old soldier Reza Khan was promoted by British General [Edmund Ironside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmund_Ironside,_1st_Baron_Ironside) to lead the British-run [Persian Cossack Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_Cossack_Brigade).[^cite_note-GhaniGhanī2001-8] About a month later, under British direction, Reza Khan's 3,000–4,000 strong detachment of the Cossack Brigade reached [Tehran](57654.md).[^cite_note-Zirinsky-9][^cite_note-10] The rest of the country was taken by 1923, and by October 1925 the [Majlis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majlis) agreed to depose and formally exile [Ahmad Shah Qajar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmad_Shah_Qajar).[^cite_note-11] The Majlis declared Reza Pahlavi as the [Shah of Iran](13877.md) on 12 December 1925, pursuant to the [Persian Constitution of 1906](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_Constitution_of_1906).[^cite_note-12] Initially, Pahlavi had planned to declare the country a republic, as his contemporary [Mustafa Kemal Atatürk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mustafa_Kemal_Atat%C3%BCrk) had done in [Turkey](11125639.md), but he abandoned the idea in the face of British and clerical opposition.[^cite_note-13]

The dynasty ruled Iran as an [autocratic monarchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Absolute_monarchy), with a pluralistic period from 1941 to 1953, when [Mohammed Mossadegh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammed_Mossadegh) was [overthrown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1953_Iranian_coup_d'%C3%A9tat), returning to authoritarianism, with a brief [one party state period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rastakhiz_Party) until the dynasty was [removed from power](347268.md) in 1979.

## Family background

See also: [Pahlavi family tree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pahlavi_family_tree)



[Reza Shah Pahlavi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reza_Shah) Founder of the Pahlavi Dynasty in 1940.

In 1878, Reza Khan was born at the village of [Alasht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alasht) in [Savadkuh County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savadkuh_County), Mazandaran Province. His parents were Abbas Ali Khan and Noushafarin Ayromlou.[^cite_note-Afkhami2008-14][^cite_note-Zirinsky-9] His mother was a Muslim immigrant from [Georgia](48768.md) (then part of the [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire))[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16] whose family had emigrated to mainland [Qajar Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qajar_Iran) after Iran was forced to cede all of its territories in the [Caucasus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caucasus) following the [Russo-Persian Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russo-Persian_Wars) several decades prior to Reza's birth.[^cite_note-17] His father was a [Mazandarani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mazanderani_people), commissioned in the 7th [Savadkuh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savadkuh_County) Regiment, and served in the [Anglo-Persian War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Persian_War) in 1856.

## Heirs



[Prince Ali-Reza Pahlavi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_Reza_Pahlavi_\(born_1922\)), the heir presumptive until his death in 1954

The  [1906 constitution of Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_Constitution_of_1906) specifically provided that only a male who was not descended from the [Qajar dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qajar_family) could become the [heir apparent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heir_apparent).[^cite_note-Dareini-24] This made all half-brothers of [Mohammad Reza](20065598.md) ineligible to become heirs to the throne.[^cite_note-Dareini-24] Until his death in 1954, the Shah's only full brother [Ali Reza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Ali_Reza_Pahlavi_\(born_1922\)) was his [heir presumptive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heir_presumptive).[^cite_note-Dareini-24] The constitution also required the Shah to be of [Iranian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_peoples) descent, meaning that his father and mother are Iranian.[^cite_note-25]

- [Shâh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shah): Emperor, followed by Shâhanshâh of Iran, with style *His Imperial Majesty*

- [Shahbânu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahbanu): Shahbânu or Empress, followed by first name, followed by "of Iran", with style *Her Imperial Majesty*

- Valiahd: Crown Prince of Iran, with style *His Imperial Highness*

- Younger sons: Prince (Shâhpūr, or Shah's Son), followed by first name and surname (Pahlavi), and style *His Imperial Highness*.

- Daughters: Princess (Shâhdokht, or Shah's Daughter), followed by first name and surname (Pahlavi), and style *Her Imperial Highness*.

- Children of the monarch's daughter/s use another version of Prince (Vâlâ Gohar, "of superior essence") or Princess (Vâlâ Gohari), which indicate descent in the second generation through the female line, and use the styles *His Highness* or *Her Highness*. This is then followed by first name and father's surname, whether he was royal or a commoner. However, the children by the last Shah's sister [Fatemeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatemeh_Pahlavi), who married an American businessman as her first husband, are surnamed Pahlavi Hillyer and do not use any titles.

"For reasons still obscure to medical science, Queen Fawzia bore only one child; thus unfortunately no male heir issued from our marriage. Under the Persian Constitution the crown must pass by direct line of descent to a male heir. This rules out not only my daughter but also my three sisters. The Constitution further stipulates that no one descended from the previous Qajar dynasty is eligible to become king. Since two of my father's wives were of Qajar blood, my half-brothers who are their sons are ineligible. In fact I had only one brother not related to the Qajar line, and to my sorrow he was to die in an aeroplane crash in 1954. With these limitations it is no wonder that my advisors felt it important for my wife to bear a son. It is true that the Constitution might have been amended, but the dimate of opinion seemed opposed to tampering with the provisions relating to the royal succession. Besides, I was young and, quite apart from the constitutional factor, I wanted more children. When Queen Fawzia went to Egypt on an extended stay, we decided on a divorce."
