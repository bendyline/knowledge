**Taco Bell Corporation**,[^cite_note-8] [doing business as](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doing_business_as) **Taco Bell**, is an American multinational chain of [fast food restaurants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_food_restaurant) founded in 1962 by [Glen Bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glen_Bell) (1923–2010) in [Downey, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downey,_California).[^cite_note-9] Taco Bell is a [subsidiary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subsidiary) of [Yum! Brands, Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yum!_Brands,_Inc.), who are also the owners of sister brand [KFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KFC). The restaurants serve a variety of [Mexican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_cuisine)-inspired foods, including [tacos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taco), [burritos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burrito), [quesadillas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quesadilla), [nachos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nachos), novelty, and specialty items, and a variety of "[value menu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Value_menu)" items. As of 2023, Taco Bell serves over two billion customers each year, at 8,212 restaurants, more than 94 percent of which are owned and operated by independent [franchisees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franchisees) and licensees.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

[PepsiCo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PepsiCo) purchased Taco Bell in 1978.[^cite_note-12] PepsiCo [spun off](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporate_spin-off) its restaurants division in 1997 as Tricon Global Restaurants; 2002, it changed its name to [Yum! Brands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yum!_Brands).

## History



[Glen Bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glen_Bell), the founder of Taco Bell

Taco Bell was founded by [Glen Bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glen_Bell), an entrepreneur who first opened a [hot dog stand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_dog_stand) called Bell's Drive-In, in [San Bernardino, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Bernardino,_California), in 1948. Bell watched long lines of customers at a Mexican restaurant called the Mitla Cafe, located across the street, which became famous among residents for its [hard-shelled tacos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hard-shelled_taco). Bell attempted to [reverse-engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reverse-engineer) the recipe, and eventually the owners, along with head chef Gloria Hoyle, allowed him to see how the tacos were made. Hoyle (Mitla Cafe still has a special named after her today)[^cite_note-13] taught Glen Bell her version of the Dorado taco style which Mitla Cafe was known for.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15] Bell took what he had learned and opened a [taco stand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taco_stand) in 1951. The name underwent several changes, from Taco-Tia through El Taco, before settling on Taco Bell.[^cite_note-16]

Glen Bell opened the first Taco Bell on July 6, 1962 at 7112 Firestone Boulevard in [Downey, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downey,_California).[^cite_note-company-17][^cite_note-18] The original location was a 400-square-foot (37 m<sup>2</sup>) building about the size of a two-car garage, and was built with [mission-style](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mission-style) arches that covered a walk-up window that served the original menu items: [tacos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taco), [burritos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burrito), [tostadas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tostada_\(tortilla\)), Chiliburgers and [frijoles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frijoles_negros), all for 19 cents each (\$2 in 2025 dollars[^cite_note-inflation-US-19]). The first restaurant closed in 1986,[^cite_note-savenumerouno-20] while the building was saved from demolition November 19, 2015, and was moved 45 miles (72 km) to the Taco Bell Corporate Office at 1 Glen Bell Way in [Irvine, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irvine,_California) and is currently stored intact on the corporate parking lot premises and known as "Taco Bell Numero Uno".[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22] Currently, there is a Taco Bell location across the street at 7127 Firestone Blvd, in Downey, which opened in 1996. The oldest operating Taco Bell location is store #2 (now operating as store #40400) at 1822 Santa Fe, in Long Beach, CA, which opened on August 7, 1962.[^cite_note-23] Initially designed exactly like "Numero Uno", the building has since been remodeled extensively. Two former "Numero Uno" buildings still operate as other businesses, including another one in Long Beach, California (opened in early 1963),[^cite_note-24] and one in Hawaiian Gardens, California (opened as store #3 in September 1962).[^cite_note-25]



Exterior of a modern Taco Bell in [Murphy, North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murphy,_North_Carolina)

In 1964, the first [franchise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franchising) was purchased by former police officer Kermit Bekke. This location, located at 1654 West Carson Street in [Torrance, California](107690.md),[^cite_note-Newspapers.com-26] had its grand opening on May 28, 1965. Bekke sold his franchise a year and a half later.[^cite_note-southbayconnection-27] This location closed in 1975 when it moved across the street to store #1130 at 1619 West Carson Street,[^cite_note-TacoBellfirstfranchiserelocation-28] which itself would close in the late 1990s and subsequently be repurposed. On January 20, 1967, the 100th Taco Bell grand opening took place in [Anaheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anaheim), located at 400 South Brookhurst (store #63[^cite_note-29]); this location would later be replaced by a new location at 324 South Brookhurst in 1993.[^cite_note-records.anaheim.net-30] The first Taco Bell restaurant east of the [Mississippi River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippi_River) (the 270th to open,[^cite_note-TacoBellNo258-31] store #258[^cite_note-32]) opened at 2050 East Main Street in [Springfield, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springfield,_Ohio), on September 20, 1968.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-company-17] Original Taco Bells featured only walk-up window service without indoor seating or drive-thru service. Inside seating was added sporadically in 1968 and drive-thru service was not generally available until 1980. As of 2025, seven original mission-style operating Taco Bell restaurants still remain, located in California and Colorado. A mission-style location (opened in April 1976[^cite_note-34]) that operated in Honolulu, Hawaii (store #1281[^cite_note-35]) remodeled in 2024,[^cite_note-36] and another Mission-style location in Scottsdale, Arizona (store #31,[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38] opened circa July 3, 1966[^cite_note-39]) closed on April 12, 2025.[^cite_note-40]

The corporate office has had a few locations, and previously maintained an office at 2516 Via Tejon in [Palos Verdes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palos_Verdes), then at 2424 Moreton Street in Torrance before settling into Irvine (at 17901 Von Karman Avenue) in 1976.[^cite_note-southbayconnection-27]

### PepsiCo subsidiary



Former headquarters at 17901 Von Karman Avenue in Irvine, California

In 1978, [PepsiCo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PepsiCo) purchased Taco Bell from Glen Bell.[^cite_note-company-17] In the 1980s, Taco Bell entered rapid expansion when it acquired several other fast food joints and turned those locations into their own. This included Taco Charley, a [San Mateo, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Mateo,_California)-based chain,[^cite_note-41] in 1982,[^cite_note-42] [Pup 'N' Taco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pup_'N'_Taco), a [Long Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Beach), California-based chain in 1984,[^cite_note-43] and [Zantigo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zantigo), a [Minneapolis](6097240.md), [Minnesota](19590.md)-based Mexican chain, in 1986.[^cite_note-44] These acquisitions (and later conversions) resulted in many early Taco Bell stores closing and moving to these newer facilities. Taco Bell also dropped its existing Mexican-based branding, replacing it with a more mainstream design in 1984.[^cite_note-45] In 1990, the [Hot 'n Now](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_'n_Now) chain was acquired.[^cite_note-lat-46] Taco Bell sold Hot 'n Now to a Connecticut company in 1997.[^cite_note-47]

In 1991, Taco Bell opened the first Taco Bell Express in [San Francisco](49728.md).[^cite_note-48] Taco Bell Express locations operate primarily inside convenience stores, truck stops, shopping malls, and airports; this concept had been experimented with as early as 1984, with several test mall locations opening.[^cite_note-49] Taco Bell began [co-branding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Co-branding) with [KFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KFC) in 1995 when the first such co-brand opened in [Clayton, North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clayton,_North_Carolina).[^cite_note-50] The chain has since co-branded with [Pizza Hut](37561.md)[^cite_note-51] and [Long John Silver's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_John_Silver's) as well.[^cite_note-52]

In 1997, PepsiCo experimented with a new "fresh grill" concept, opening at least one Border Bell restaurant in [Mountain View, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_View,_California), on El Camino Real ([SR 82](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_Route_82)). Close to the time that PepsiCo spun off its restaurant business in 1997,[^cite_note-TRICON-GLOBAL-RESTAURANTS-Aug-1997-10-12B/A-53] the Border Bell in Mountain View was closed and converted to a Taco Bell restaurant which was still open in 2018.[^cite_note-54]

In September 2000, up to \$50 million worth of Taco Bell-branded shells were [recalled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/StarLink_corn_recall) from supermarkets.[^cite_note-TRICON-GLOBAL-RESTAURANTS-Oct-2000-10-Q-55] The shells contained a variety of [genetically modified corn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genetically_modified_corn) called StarLink that was not approved for human consumption.[^cite_note-TRICON-GLOBAL-RESTAURANTS-Feb-2001-8-K-56] StarLink was approved only for use in animal feed because of questions about whether it can cause allergic reactions in people.[^cite_note-57] It was the first-ever recall of [genetically modified food](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genetically_modified_food) (GMO). Corn was not segregated at grain elevators and the miller in Texas did not order that type.[^cite_note-58] In 2001, Tricon Global announced a \$60 million settlement with the suppliers. They stated that it would go to Taco Bell franchisees and TGR would not take any of it.[^cite_note-59]

### Yum! Brands subsidiary

PepsiCo spun out Taco Bell and its other restaurant chains in late 1997 in [Tricon Global Restaurants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tricon_Global_Restaurants).[^cite_note-TRICON-GLOBAL-RESTAURANTS-Jul-1997-10-12B/A-60][^cite_note-61] With the purchase of Yorkshire Global Restaurants, the owners of [A\&W](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A\&W_Restaurants) and [Long John Silver's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_John_Silver's) chains, Tricon changed its name to Yum! Brands on May 16, 2002.[^cite_note-YUM!-Brands-May-2002-8-K-62][^cite_note-63]

In March 2005, the [Coalition of Immokalee Workers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coalition_of_Immokalee_Workers) (CIW) won a landmark victory in its national boycott of Taco Bell for human rights. Taco Bell agreed to meet all the coalition's demands to improve wage and working conditions for [Florida](18933066.md) tomato pickers in its supply chain.[^cite_note-64] After four years of boycott, Taco Bell and Yum! Brands agreed to make an agreement called the *CIW-Yum agreement* with representatives of CIW at Yum! Brands headquarters.[^cite_note-65]

The downstairs interior of the Taco Bell Cantina flagship store in Las Vegas, Nevada.

The downstairs interior of the Taco Bell Cantina flagship store in [Las Vegas, Nevada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Vegas,_Nevada)

Taco Bell began experimenting with fast-casual and urban concepts when it created U.S. Taco Co. and Urban Taproom in 2014 reflecting a market shift due to the popularity of [Chipotle Mexican Grill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chipotle_Mexican_Grill). The menu consisted of tacos with American fillings, and did not sell the food sold in Taco Bell restaurants, such as burritos. It was launched in [Huntington Beach, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huntington_Beach,_California), in August 2014.[^cite_note-luna-66]

U.S. Taco Co. closed on September 15, 2015, so the company could focus on its new similar Taco Bell Cantina concept, which featured special menu items and served alcohol. It opened its first location a few days later in Chicago's [Wicker Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wicker_Park,_Chicago) neighborhood, followed by a location in [San Francisco](49728.md) about a month later, located less than a block away from [AT\&T Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AT\&T_Park).[^cite_note-67] In 2016, Taco Bell launched the Taco Bell Cantina flagship store located on the [Las Vegas Strip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Vegas_Strip).[^cite_note-68] The 24-hour restaurant serves alcohol, unique menu items, and features a [DJ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DJ). It was announced in August 2017 that the store would begin hosting weddings.[^cite_note-69] Taco Bell Cantina currently has locations in San Francisco, [Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California), [Chicago](6886.md) (2 locations), [Las Vegas](47737.md), [Austin](1998.md), [Fayetteville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fayetteville,_Arkansas), [Cincinnati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cincinnati), [Cleveland](5951.md), [Atlanta](3138.md), [Newport Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newport_Beach), [San Diego](28504.md), [San Jose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Jose,_California), [Sacramento](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacramento), [Nashville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville), with them once having plans to open soon in [Somerville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somerville,_Massachusetts), Massachusetts, but as of 2025, there are instead Taco Bell Cantinas in [Brookline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brookline,_Massachusetts) and [Allston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allston), [Massachusetts](1645518.md).[^cite_note-70][^cite_note-71] In March 2020, Taco Bell announced that it would be converting 3 of its suburban stores into Cantinas that year as part of a test run.[^cite_note-72]

In March 2016, Taco Bell introduced private beta testing of an artificial intelligence bot on the messaging platform [Slack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slack_\(software\)) designed to take orders of select menu items from local Taco Bell locations and have the orders delivered. Taco Bell planned to have a wider roll-out of this functionality in the coming months.[^cite_note-73]

Previously, Taco Bell's hot sauces were only available in packets at the chain itself. In February 2014, Taco Bell made its hot sauces available at grocery stores, sold in bottles.[^cite_note-74] These would be followed by further grocery store products including chips in May 2018[^cite_note-75] and shredded cheese in 2019.[^cite_note-76] In September 2016, Taco Bell opened a pop-up in New York City in the [SoHo, Manhattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SoHo,_Manhattan) area called the Taco Bell VR Arcade. Taco Bell and VR fans could demo [PlayStation VR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_VR), games, and food.[^cite_note-77]

In 2016, Taco Bell debuted a modular restaurant constructed from five reclaimed [shipping containers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intermodal_container) at the [South by Southwest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_by_Southwest) (SXSW) festival in [Austin, Texas](1998.md). Following its successful reception, the structure was permanently relocated to [South Gate, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Gate,_California), where it commenced operations as a standard franchise location in 2017. The restaurant features Taco Bell's full menu, with outdoor seating, a walk-up window, and a drive-thru, but no indoor seating unlike regular Taco Bell locations.[^cite_note-78] Taco Bell announced plans in November 2017 to open 300 more urban and Cantina-style locations by 2022, with 50 to be located about New York City's five boroughs.[^cite_note-79] In 2019, Taco Bell opened a pop-up hotel called "The Bell: A Taco Bell Hotel and Resort" for one weekend in August. Upon the announcement, the hotel was booked up in two minutes.[^cite_note-80]

Taco Bell announced plans to keep its current corporate headquarters in Irvine until 2030.[^cite_note-81]

## Menu and advertising

The first Taco Bell commercials premiered in 1968, featuring advertisements with [Mel Blanc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mel_Blanc), [Pat Harrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pat_Harrington_Jr.), and the then-host of *[Truth or Consequences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truth_or_Consequences)*, [Bob Barker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Barker).[^cite_note-82][^cite_note-83]

In 1992, [Johnny Cash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Cash) starred in a television commercial for Taco Bell's [value menu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Value_menu).[^cite_note-Lazarus_1992-84]

In 1993, Taco Bell was used as [product placement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Product_placement) and [plot device](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plot_device) in the movie [*Demolition Man*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demolition_Man_\(film\)) updating their currently used logo.[^cite_note-starring-tacobell-85][^cite_note-86]

From August 1997 to July 2000, [Gidget the Chihuahua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taco_Bell_chihuahua) was the brand's advertising mascot, a campaign developed by [TBWA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TBWA_Worldwide). Voiced by [Carlos Alazraqui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Alazraqui), slogans included “¡Yo quiero Taco Bell!” (I want Taco Bell!), “Drop the Chalupa,” and “¡Viva Gorditas!” All became catchphrases in popular culture.[^cite_note-87]

In 2000, *Taco Bell: Tasty Temple Challenge* was released for [DOS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disk_operating_system) by Taco Bell as the publisher and developed by BrandGames[^cite_note-88] as a kid-friendly [first-person 3D action shooter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First-person_shooter) with gameplay similar to that of *[Doom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doom_\(1993_video_game\))* and *[Wolfenstein 3D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfenstein_3D)* in terms of combat and exploration, serving as an [advertisement for the brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advergame). The player controls Baja Bill, who explores a temple of a lost jungle civilization, battling snakes and scorpions with [Taco Bell's hot and wild sauce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_hot_sauces). Food from the brand also appears in game and is used to replenish the protagonist's health.[^cite_note-89][^cite_note-90]



Several Taco Bell menu items. Clockwise from lower right: chalupa supreme, combo burrito, double decker taco

In March 2001, Taco Bell announced a promotion to coincide with the re-entry of the [Mir space station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mir_space_station). They towed a large target out into the Pacific Ocean, announcing that if the target was hit by a falling piece of Mir, every person in the United States would be entitled to a free Taco Bell taco. The company bought a sizable insurance policy for this gamble.[^cite_note-91] No piece of the station struck the target.

In 2004, a local Taco Bell franchisee bought the [naming rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naming_rights) to the [Boise State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise_State) Pavilion in [Boise, Idaho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise,_Idaho), and renamed the stadium [Taco Bell Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taco_Bell_Arena).[^cite_note-92] Also, in 2004, [Mountain Dew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_Dew) offered Taco Bell stores the exclusive right to carry Mountain Dew [Baja Blast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baja_Blast), a tropical lime flavor of the popular soft drink.[^cite_note-Baja_Blast-93]

In 2005, Taco Bell released the menu item called the [Crunchwrap Supreme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crunchwrap_Supreme) as a limited-time offer. Due to its success, the Crunchwrap Supreme was made a permanent menu offering in 2006.[^cite_note-94]

In 2007, Taco Bell first offered the "Steal a Base, Steal a Taco" promotion—if any player from either team [stole a base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stolen_base) in the [World Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007_World_Series), the company would give away free tacos to everyone in the United States in a campaign similar to the Mir promotion, albeit with a much higher likelihood of being realized.[^cite_note-95] After [Jacoby Ellsbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacoby_Ellsbury) of the [Boston Red Sox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_Red_Sox) stole a base in Game 2, the company made good on the promotion on October 30, 2007. The promotion has subsequently been offered in multiple World Series.

Main article: [Steal a Base, Steal a Taco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Base,_Steal_a_Taco)

Taco Bell sponsors a promotion at home games for both the [Portland Trail Blazers](72897.md) and the [Cleveland Cavaliers](72868.md) in which everyone in attendance receives a coupon for a free Chalupa if the home team scores 100 points or more.[^cite_note-96][^cite_note-97]

In 2009, Taco Bell introduced a [music video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_video) style commercial entitled "It's all about the Roosevelts"; the video was composed and produced by Danny de Matos at his studio for Amber Music on behalf of DraftFCB Agency. Featuring, Varsity Fanclub's [Bobby Edner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobby_Edner), the [rap music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rap_music) style commercial shows a group of friends gathering change as they drive toward Taco Bell. The commercial represents Taco Bell's first foray into movie theater advertising, featuring the ad during the opening previews of *[Transformers: Revenge of the Fallen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformers:_Revenge_of_the_Fallen)* and *[Public Enemies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Enemies_\(2009_film\))* as well as screens in some movie theater lobbies.[^cite_note-98]

On July 1, 2009, Taco Bell replaced 20-year sponsor [McDonald's](2480627.md) as the fast-food partner of the [NBA](22093.md). Taco Bell and the NBA agreed on a four-year deal allowing them to advertise on [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company), [TNT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TNT_\(U.S._TV_network\)), and [ESPN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESPN), and NBA-themed promotions.[^cite_note-99] On July 21, 2009, [Gidget](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taco_Bell_chihuahua), the [Chihuahua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chihuahua_\(dog_breed\)) featured in Taco Bell ads in the late 1990s, was [euthanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euthanized) after suffering a stroke.[^cite_note-100] She was 15 years old. 2009 commercials for the "Frutista Freeze" frozen drink feature [Snowball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snowball_\(cockatoo\)), an [Eleonora cockatoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eleonora_cockatoo) noted for his ability to dance to human music.[^cite_note-nwi-101] In an effort to promote its \$2 Meal Deals, Taco Bell started a Facebook group in June 2010 to collect signatures on a petition that appeals to the [Federal Reserve](10819.md) to produce more [two-dollar bills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_two-dollar_bill).[^cite_note-102]

A large advertising push by Taco Bell had begun in late February 2011 in response to a consumer protection lawsuit filed against the company by an Alabama law firm. The promotion sought to counter allegations that the company falsely advertised the ratio of ingredients in its beef filling for its tacos. The spots featured employees and franchisees stating that the filling has always been a mixture of 88% beef and various spices and binders and nothing else. The ad followed several full-page print ads in the *New York Times* and other newspapers that featured the headline "Thank you for suing us."[^cite_note-Independent-Advert-103] Additionally, the chain added a new social campaign using [Twitter](9988187.md) and [Facebook](7529378.md). The company invested heavily in the campaign, spending more than \$3 million ([USD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USD)) putting out its message—about 20 percent more than the company usually spends on an advertising program. The various campaigns came shortly before the company began its official response to the suit in the [United States District Court for the Central District of California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_District_Court_for_the_Central_District_of_California) and were designed to bring public opinion into their camp. Various analysts stated that the company would have been better off using a grass-root campaign that involved in store advertising and other non-broadcast media.[^cite_note-DB-AP-104] The suit was eventually withdrawn,[^cite_note-AP-Schreiner-105] and the company continued its advertising response by publicly requesting an apology from the suing firm of Beasley Allen. Analyst Laura Ries, of marketing strategy firm Ries & Ries, stated she believed Taco Bell's latest response was a mistake. She commented that reviving memories of a suit that the majority of the public had forgotten after the initial burst of publicity was the wrong strategy from Taco Bell.[^cite_note-106]

### Discontinued menu items

One of Taco Bell's original 1962 menu items was the Chiliburger, renamed the Bell Burger in 1969, then the Bell Beefer in 1979; this was a [loose meat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loose_meat) sandwich originally filled with taco-seasoned ground beef in mild red sauce with shredded lettuce and diced onion served on a steamed hamburger bun. Later, shredded cheese and diced tomato were added to the sandwich. Taco Bell discontinued the Bell Beefer around 1986 to maintain a more typical [Tex-Mex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tex-Mex)-inspired menu.[^cite_note-156]

Other discontinued Taco Bell menu items include the [Enchirito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enchirito) (name revived for a different menu item); Taco Lite; Taco Grande; Chilito (Chili Cheese Burrito); Beefy Crunch Burrito; Beefy Melt Burrito; Seafood Salad; Chicken Fiesta Burrito; Potatorito; Volcano Taco; BLT Taco; Cheesarito; Cinnamon Crispas; Nacho Crunch Grilled Stuft Burrito; Chicken Caesar Grilled Stuft Burrito; Grilled Stuft Nacho; Fully Loaded Nachos; Crunchwrap Sliders;[^cite_note-157] Blackjack Taco; Bean Burrito Especial; Border Ices; and the Meximelt.[^cite_note-158]

In September 2019, Taco Bell made new changes to its menu. Items discontinued include the Beefy Mini Quesadilla; Chips and Salsa; Chipotle Chicken Loaded Griller; Double Decker Taco; Cool Ranch and Fiery Doritos Locos Tacos; Double Tostada; Power Menu Burrito, and the XXL Grilled Stuft Burrito.[^cite_note-What's_New_with_Taco_Bell's_Menu-134]

As of August 13, 2020, the menu underwent another update as part of their efforts to streamline processes in their restaurants in response to the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md), discontinuing the following items: Grilled Steak Soft Taco; 7-Layer Burrito; Nachos Supreme; Beefy Fritos Burrito; Spicy Tostada; Triple Layer Nachos; Cheesy Fiesta Potatoes; Loaded Grillers, both Cheesy Potato and Beefy Nacho; Chips & Dips; and Mini Skillet Bowl.[^cite_note-159]

Access is restricted to relevant service personnel.[^cite_note-USbases-231]

In the late 2000s, Yum! Brands announced that it was reopening Taco Bell locations in the United Kingdom as part of a large, planned expansion into Europe. Yum! took advantage of the recent [great recession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_recession) which led to increasing sales at other fast food outlets; it also said that there was now a greater awareness of Mexican food in the UK and that it can be successful with improved menu offerings and marketing.[^cite_note-online.wsj.com-225][^cite_note-propertyweek.com-226] The first new store opened at the [Lakeside Shopping Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakeside_Shopping_Centre) in Essex on June 28, 2010.[^cite_note-232] Another store opened in [Basildon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basildon), Essex, on November 29, 2010, and a third in the [Manchester Arndale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_Arndale) Food Court on November 7, 2011.

On March 1, 2013, it was announced that trace amounts of [horse meat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horse_meat) from a European supplier [had been found in various food products](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_horse_meat_scandal), including Taco Bell's beef in the UK.[^cite_note-233] By August 2017, there were 17 Taco Bell branches in the United Kingdom, all of which were in England and outside of London.[^cite_note-234] The first Scottish branch was opened in Glasgow in December 2017.[^cite_note-235] As of June 2019 there are 39 branches in Scotland and England, including three in London.[^cite_note-236] Beginning in October 2019, Taco Bell locations in the UK began serving Doritos Locos Tacos.[^cite_note-237]

On March 2, 2020, the first [Welsh](69894.md) Taco Bell branch was opened in [Cardiff](5882.md).[^cite_note-238]

More recently, in November 2021, Taco Bell opened its 77th UK store, and a return to the British Midlands with a store in [The Royal Town of Sutton Coldfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Royal_Town_of_Sutton_Coldfield), [Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham).

In August 2022 a Taco Bell was opened in [Torquay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torquay), Devon, the second in the county after [Plymouth](23508.md). As of 2024 there are now more than 130 locations in the UK.[^cite_note-239]

### Middle East

A Taco Bell opened in the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md) in November 2008 in [Dubai](211583.md) at the [Dubai Mall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubai_Mall).[^cite_note-240] A fourth UAE location was also planned for [Bawadi Mall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bawadi_Mall,_Al-Ain) in the city of [Al Ain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Ain). The locations at Dubai Mall, Deira City Centre, and Mirdif City Centre have all closed and Taco Bell has completely pulled out of the Emirati market.[^cite_note-241] Taco Bell is still open in [Kuwait](7515890.md) and has not been pulled out ever since from the Kuwaiti market, as the chain is franchised in this region by [Kout Food Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kout_Food_Group).[^cite_note-242][^cite_note-243][^cite_note-244]

### North America



Taco Bell in Canada

#### Canada

Taco Bell has been present in [Canada](5042916.md) since 1979, with the first store opening in [Windsor, Ontario](33596.md).[^cite_note-245] There are currently Taco Bell locations in 8 of the 10 Canadian provinces: [British Columbia](3392.md), [Alberta](717.md), [Saskatchewan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saskatchewan), [Manitoba](18926.md), [Ontario](22218.md), [New Brunswick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Brunswick) and [Nova Scotia](21184.md), [Newfoundland and Labrador](21980.md).[^cite_note-246] Taco Bell had operated in the province of [Quebec](7954867.md) for close to 15 years, however announced its withdrawal on January 6, 2022.[^cite_note-247] For some time it was possible to order draft beer with one's order. Taco Bell offers free soda refills in its stores.

On March 31, 2011, [Priszm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priszm), who was the largest franchisee for Taco Bell locations in Canada, went into bankruptcy protection in Ontario and British Columbia.[^cite_note-248] On May 6, 2011, Priszm Income Fund was delisted from the [Toronto Stock Exchange](205584.md) for failure to meet the continued listing requirements.[^cite_note-249] Since then, some Taco Bell restaurants have been closed down including those in Guelph, Hamilton, and Cambridge, Ontario, among others.

### Oceania

#### Australia

Taco Bell first opened in [Australia](4689264.md) in September 1981, but Taco Bell was ordered to change its name after the owner of a local restaurant successfully sued Taco Bell for misleading conduct.[^cite_note-250] The local restaurant was called "Taco Bell's Casa" and had been operating in Australia since the 1970s. The owner successfully argued that Sydneysiders would confuse the takeaway chain with his restaurant, and this would damage his reputation. Taco Bell later opened in 1997 in Australia with a store in the cinema district on [George Street, Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Street,_Sydney) and a year later in 1998 within a few [KFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KFC) stores in the state of New South Wales, but by 2005, the Taco Bell brand was pulled out of the country.[^cite_note-251]

On September 13, 2017, [Collins Foods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collins_Foods) announced that Taco Bell would return to the Australian market, with their first store being situated in the [Brisbane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brisbane) suburb of [Annerley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annerley) in [Queensland](59051.md) using a refurbished [Sizzler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sizzler) restaurant which had closed earlier that year.[^cite_note-252] The Annerley store opened on November 4, 2017.[^cite_note-253] In 2018, Collins Foods signed a development deal with Yum! Brands to open over 50 Taco Bell restaurants across Australia between January 2019 and December 2021.[^cite_note-254] A legal dispute with [Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_\(Australia\))-based restaurant chain [Taco Bill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taco_Bill) was resolved in February 2020.[^cite_note-255]

As of February 2024, there are 39 Taco Bell restaurants across Australia; 12 in [New South Wales](21654.md), 13 in [Queensland](59051.md), nine in [Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_\(Australia\)), and four in [Western Australia](33613.md).[^cite_note-256] There are also plans to open restaurants in other locations, such as [Canberra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canberra),[^cite_note-257][^cite_note-258] the [Central Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Coast_\(New_South_Wales\)),[^cite_note-259][^cite_note-260] [Port Macquarie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_Macquarie)[^cite_note-261][^cite_note-262] and [Toowoomba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toowoomba).[^cite_note-263] While the chain has grown nationwide, both of its two outlets in [North Queensland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Queensland) closed in November 2022, which were previously locations in [Cairns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cairns)[^cite_note-264] and [Townsville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Townsville).[^cite_note-265] In April 2025, Taco Bell owner Collins Foods announced to exit the Australian operations, with the prospect of finding a buyer in 12 months or else winding up the business.[^cite_note-266]

In March 2026, just weeks from the deadline, Collins Foods successfully transferred the operations to [Restaurant Brands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Restaurant_Brands), another partner in the area. 6 stores will close in Queensland and 1 in Victoria, leaving 20 in the deal but saving the company from demise and a more exorbitant closure.[^cite_note-267][^cite_note-268]

#### New Zealand

Taco Bell officially opened its first [New Zealand](4913064.md) location on November 12, 2019, in the suburb of [New Lynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Lynn), [Auckland](18660332.md).[^cite_note-269]

As of January 2023, there are 14 Taco Bell restaurants across New Zealand; 10 on the [North Island](62046.md) and four on the [South Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Island).[^cite_note-270] By city, there are seven in [Auckland](18660332.md), three in [Christchurch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christchurch) and one each in [Dunedin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunedin), [Rotorua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotorua), [Taupiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taupiri) and [Wellington](33804.md). There are two outlets at airports in New Zealand, one at [Auckland Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auckland_Airport) and the other at [Christchurch Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christchurch_Airport).

#### Territories of the United States

Taco Bell operates several stores in [Guam](11974.md).[^cite_note-271]

Taco Bell operates one restaurant in the [Northern Mariana Islands](21402.md), located on the island of [Saipan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saipan).[^cite_note-272]

In 2005 Taco Bell started the permitting process to set up a restaurant in [American Samoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Samoa) in [Pago Pago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pago_Pago).[^cite_note-273]

Taco Bell also operates stores in [Puerto Rico](23041.md).

### Former operations

#### Iceland

Taco Bell in [Iceland](14531.md) was operated as a part of the KFC establishment in [Hafnarfjörður](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hafnarfj%C3%B6r%C3%B0ur), suburb of [Reykjavík](25798.md). It was established in late 2006, after the departure of the U.S. Navy from [Naval Air Station Keflavik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Air_Station_Keflavik). A second location opened in the Ártúnshöfði part of Reykjavík in November 2008.[^cite_note-287] Taco Bell closed its Hafnarfjörður location in late 2023.

#### Mexico

Taco Bell has attempted to enter the Mexican market twice. After a highly publicised launch in Mexico City in 1992, all the restaurants were closed two years later. In September 2007, Taco Bell returned to [Monterrey](40455.md), projecting an American image with an Americanized menu that included french fries, but it closed in January 2010 due to low patronage.[^cite_note-288][^cite_note-289][^cite_note-290]

#### Poland

The first Polish Taco Bell store was opened in 1993. Following an aggressive campaign of expansion, Taco Bell's efforts soon withered, and the chain withdrew from Poland shortly thereafter.[^cite_note-291] In 2026, [AmRest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AmRest) announced, that Taco Bell will return to the Polish market. The first new Taco Bell restaurant is expected to open in the fourth quarter of 2026, with additional locations to follow over time.

#### Russia

In the early 1990s, PepsiCo opened several Taco Bell locations inside the [Moscow Metro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Metro) on, for example, [Park Kultury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Park_Kultury_\(Koltsevaya_line\)) and [Komsomolskaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Komsomolskaya_\(Koltsevaya_line\)) stations.[^cite_note-292] This experiment lasted only a few years.[^cite_note-293] These locations live on under different ownership and a different name.

## Animal welfare

Taco Bell is subject to the [animal welfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_welfare) standards of its parent company, Yum! Brands.

In 2016, Taco Bell stopped sourcing eggs from egg-laying hens raised in [battery cages](6693736.md) in their US restaurants.[^cite_note-302]

In 2024, Taco Bell announced that it would publish benchmarks on moving away from [gestation crates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gestation_crate) in pork production by the end of 2024. However, no such benchmarks were published that year.[^cite_note-303]

## Clothing line



Man wearing a white Taco Bell hooded sweatshirt, part of the 2017 Forever 21 Taco Bell collection

In 2017, [Forever 21](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forever_21) produced a clothing line in partnership with Taco Bell, featuring branded [T-shirts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T-shirt) and sweatshirts, as well as [bodysuits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bodysuit) made to look like Border Sauce packets.[^cite_note-abc_forever21-304][^cite_note-huffpo_forever21-305]

In November 2019, Taco Bell announced *Taco Bell's Taco Shop*, an online store with Taco Bell branded merchandise including hoodies and tees, holiday ornaments, inflatable sauce packets, and wedding-related items.[^cite_note-306]



Hollister × Taco Bell clothing line displayed for sale at a Hollister Co. store location in [Orlando, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando,_Florida).

In late 2025, [Hollister Co.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollister_Co.) partnered with Taco Bell for a limited-edition, Y2K-inspired capsule collection.[^cite_note-307] The drop, the first Hollister × Taco Bell collaboration, includes 11 pieces: vintage-style graphic tees, baggy denim, soft “Feel Good Fleece” hoodies and sweatpants, cozy socks, and a hot-sauce packet keychain accessory. The collection officially launched in Hollister stores and online on December 1, 2025, with prices ranging from US \$14.95 to US \$79.95, and sizes offered from XS to XL.[^cite_note-308]
