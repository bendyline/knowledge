**Jolfa** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): جلفا)[^cite_note-5] is a city in the [Central District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_District_\(Jolfa_County\)) of [Jolfa County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jolfa_County), [East Azerbaijan](530348.md) province, [Iran](14653.md), serving as capital of both the county and the district.[^cite_note-Jolfa_and_Marand_Counties-6] Jolfa is separated by the [Aras River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aras_River) from its namesake, the town of [Julfa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julfa,_Azerbaijan_\(city\)) on the Azerbaijan side of [the border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran-Azerbaijan_border). The two towns are linked by a road bridge and a railway bridge.&#x20;

## Demographics

### Population

At the 2006 National Census, the city's population was 4,983 in 1,365 households.[^cite_note-2006_East_Azerbaijan_Province-7] The following census in 2011 counted 5,628 people in 1,448 households.[^cite_note-2011_East_Azerbaijan_Province-8] At the 2016 census, the population was 8,810 people in 2,543 households.[^cite_note-2016_East_Azerbaijan_Province-3]

## Climate

Jolfa has a [cold desert climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_desert_climate) (*BWk*) according to the [Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification).
