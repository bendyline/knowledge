**Eilish Sheerin** (born 5 October 1992) is an [Australian rules footballer](2403.md) with [North Melbourne](22106.md#AFL_Women's_team) in the [AFL Women's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFL_Women's) (AFLW). Sheerin was drafted by [Richmond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond_Football_Club#AFL_Women's_team) with their second selection and fifty-eighth overall in the [2022 AFL Women's draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_AFL_Women's_draft).[^cite_note-1] She made her debut against [Geelong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geelong_Football_Club#AFL_Women's_team) at [GMHBA Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kardinia_Park) in the first round of [2022 season 7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_AFL_Women's_season_7).[^cite_note-2]

Sheerin was traded to [North Melbourne](22106.md) in exchange for [draft pick 18](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_AFL_Women's_draft) in the 2024 trade period.[^cite_note-3]
