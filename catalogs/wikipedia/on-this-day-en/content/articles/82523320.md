The **2026 Women's Twenty20 Asia Cup** also branded as **DP World Women's Asia Cup 2026** was the sixth edition of the [Women's Twenty20 Asia Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_Twenty20_Asia_Cup) and overall the tenth [Women's Asia Cup](11395320.md). It was hosted by the [Emirates Cricket Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emirates_Cricket_Board) from 28 August to 13 September 2026 with 8 teams competing in 15 matches at the [Dubai International Cricket Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubai_International_Cricket_Stadium). [Sri Lanka](7061015.md) were the defending champions and were defeated in the [final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Women's_Twenty20_Asia_Cup_final) by [India](2281524.md) who went onto win their record-extending fourth title in the T20 format and overall the eighth title in the Asia Cup.

## Background

The [Women's Twenty20 Asia Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_Twenty20_Asia_Cup) is a biennial [international cricket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_cricket) competition in [Women's Twenty20 International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_Twenty20_International) (WT20I) format, organised by the [Asian Cricket Council](584073.md) (ACC). It was inaugurated in [2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Women's_Asia_Cup) as [Women's Asia Cup](11395320.md), an annual [Women's One Day International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_One_Day_International) (WODI) competition and was held as such until [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Women's_Asia_Cup). It was converted to a biennial tournament from then on and since [2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Women's_Twenty20_Asia_Cup), has been held in the WT20I format.[^cite_note-Hist-1][^cite_note-etk-2] [Sri Lanka](7061015.md) were the defending champions having defeated [India](2281524.md) in the [previous edition's final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Women's_Twenty20_Asia_Cup#Final).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

In the [2025–2029 ICC Women's Future Tours Programme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%932029_ICC_Women's_Future_Tours_Programme), the tournament was scheduled to be held in September–October 2026.[^cite_note-5] However on 6 August 2026, the ACC announced that the tournament would instead be held from 28 August to 13 September 2026 in [Dubai](211583.md).[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] Eight participating teams were divided into two groups of four teams each. In the group stage, each team played every other team in its group once in a [round-robin](939742.md) format. The top two teams from each group advanced to the knockout stage, which consisted of two semi-finals, with the winners facing off in the final.[^cite_note-etk-2][^cite_note-format-8]

## Qualification

The four [full members](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_International_Cricket_Council_members#Full_members) of the ACC with a women's team: [Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_women's_national_cricket_team), India, [Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_women's_national_cricket_team) and Sri Lanka were given direct qualification for the tournament, while eighteen of the [associate teams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_International_Cricket_Council_members#Associate_members) of the ACC competed in the [2026 ACC Women's Premier Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_ACC_Women's_Premier_Cup) to determine four entrants: [Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_women's_national_cricket_team), [Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesia_women's_national_cricket_team), [Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thailand_women's_national_cricket_team) and the [United Arab Emirates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Arab_Emirates_women's_national_cricket_team).[^cite_note-ACQ-9]

[Nilakshika Silva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nilakshika_Silva?action=edit\&redlink=1) scored 22 runs before being dismissed while the other remaining batters were all dismissed for single-digit scores, [bundling out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_out_\(cricket\)) Sri Lanka for 111 runs as India secured a 72-run victory.[^cite_note-sc1-56][^cite_note-sc2-57][^cite_note-sc3-58] This was India's fourth T20 Asia Cup victory after 2012, [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Women's_Twenty20_Asia_Cup) and [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Women's_Twenty20_Asia_Cup); and overall eighth Asia Cup victory including the four WODI titles in 2004, [2005](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_Women's_Asia_Cup), 2006 and [2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Women's_Asia_Cup).[^cite_note-4-8-59][^cite_note-Hist-1] Shafali received the player of the match award for her [batting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batting_\(cricket\)) performance as well as her [bowling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowling_\(cricket\)).[^cite_note-60] After the match, the ACC's chairman [Mohsin Naqvi](72818268.md), who is also the charmain of the [Pakistan Cricket Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Cricket_Board) and the interior minister of Pakistan presented medals to the runners-up Sri Lanka.
