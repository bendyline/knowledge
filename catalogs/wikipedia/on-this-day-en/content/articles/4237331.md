**San Marcos** is a town in the [Mexican state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_state) of [Guerrero](195816.md). It serves as the municipal seat of the [surrounding municipality of San Marcos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Marcos_Municipality,_Guerrero).

The town currently has about 12 000 inhabitants.

Culturally and ethnically, the town and the municipality belong to what is known in Mexico as the [Costa Chica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Chica_of_Guerrero) (small coast) of [Guerrero](195816.md) and [Oaxaca](156896.md). This is a 125 km long region along the Pacific coast; it begins south of [Acapulco](1446.md) and ends near the town of [Puerto Ángel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_%C3%81ngel) in the neighbouring state of Oaxaca.

## Geography

The town is located approximately 59 km south of Acapulco. It is located 18 km inland and along [Federal Highway 200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Federal_Highway_200), known locally as the Acapulco–Pinotepa Nacional highway. It is only about one hour's drive from Acapulco and about three hours (157 km) from [Pinotepa Nacional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinotepa_Nacional).

## History

Officially, the town and municipality were established on 28 September 1885 and were named in honour of [Saint Mark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Mark), a Catholic saint considered the patron saint of the region. However, the town already existed, firstly under the name of Xolutla and later La Estancia.
