# List of first overall CFL draft picks

&#x20;This is a **list of first overall Canadian Football League draft picks**. The [CFL draft](6237354.md) is an annual [sports draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_draft) in which the teams of the [Canadian Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Football_League) (CFL) select eligible Canadian/non-import players, typically from the ranks of [U Sports football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U_Sports_football) or [NCAA](60706.md) [college football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_football). The [first official draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1953_IRFU_college_draft) took place in 1953, with [Doug McNichol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doug_McNichol) becoming the first player to be drafted and was therefore drafted first overall.[^cite_note-1] This list does not include the [1952 IRFU college draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1952_IRFU_college_draft) due to its unofficial status.[^cite_note-2] While only the [eastern teams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interprovincial_Rugby_Football_Union) participated for the first three years, all nine teams began participating in the [1956 draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1956_Canadian_college_draft). From 1960 to 1962, however, due to a dispute over talent from western universities and a reluctance of eastern draftees to move west, [BC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BC_Lions), [Edmonton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmonton_Eskimos), [Saskatchewan](254166.md), and [Winnipeg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winnipeg_Blue_Bombers) were awarded territorial rights to players from their city's university. Because [Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calgary_Stampeders) had no university program in their territory, they continued to participate in the draft during these three years.[^cite_note-Draft_History-3] From 1973 to 1984, teams were granted territorial rights players before the actual draft took place, hence, players listed in those years were actually drafted later.[^cite_note-Draft_History-3] Since 2016, in non-[pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_Canada) years, the draft has consisted of eight rounds, with teams selecting in reverse order of their placement in the previous season.

## Statistics

- Since 1956, [Simon Fraser University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Fraser_Clan) and the [University of Western Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Ontario_Mustangs) hold the record for most first overall picks with five apiece.

  [^cite_note-5]

  &#x20;Among NCAA schools, the only ones with more than one first overall pick are [Washington State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_State_Cougars_football) and [Weber State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weber_State_Wildcats_football), each with two. (Although Simon Fraser is now an NCAA member, it did not hava a top overall pick since joining that body, and has since disbanded [its football team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Fraser_Red_Leafs_football).)

- Since 1956, the [Toronto Argonauts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto_Argonauts) have had the most first overall picks with 11.

- The Montreal Alouettes have gone the longest without a first overall draft pick, after selecting first in 1972 and then waiting until 2022 before picking first again. The Alouettes have also had the fewest first overall selections with three.

- Only two first overall draft selections have been awarded the [CFL's Most Outstanding Rookie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CFL's_Most_Outstanding_Rookie_Award) since the award was created in 1972; [Orville Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orville_Lee) in 1988 and [Jordan Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Williams_\(linebacker,_born_1994\)) in 2021.

- No team has ever made the first overall draft pick for more than three straight years.
