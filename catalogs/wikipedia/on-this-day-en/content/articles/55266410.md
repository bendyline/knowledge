**Karolína Muchová** (Czech pronunciation: [\[ˈkaroliːna ˈmuxovaː\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Czech); born 21 August 1996) is a Czech professional [tennis](29773.md) player. She has a career-high singles [ranking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTA_rankings) of world No. 6 by the [WTA](57768.md), achieved in July 2026, and a best doubles ranking of No. 156, reached in January 2026.

Muchová has won three [WTA Tour](1398173.md) singles titles, including a [WTA 1000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTA_1000) event at the [2026 Qatar Ladies Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Qatar_TotalEnergies_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles). She has contested two [major](197638.md#Tournaments) singles finals, at the [2023 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles) and [2026 Wimbledon Championships](83354904.md). She has won a mixed doubles title at the [2026 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Mixed_doubles), with compatriot [Jakub Menšík](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakub_Men%C5%A1%C3%ADk).

Muchová turned professional in 2013.[^cite_note-\:3-2] She made her Grand Slam tournament main-draw debut at the [2018 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles) as a qualifier, reaching the third round by defeating No. 12, [Garbiñe Muguruza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garbi%C3%B1e_Muguruza). In 2019, she reached her maiden major quarterfinal at [Wimbledon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), upsetting No. 3, [Karolína Plíšková](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karol%C3%ADna_Pl%C3%AD%C5%A1kov%C3%A1), captured her first WTA Tour title at the [Korea Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Korea_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles), and reached the semifinals at the [WTA Elite Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_WTA_Elite_Trophy_%E2%80%93_Singles). In 2021, she reached the semifinals at the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), defeating world No. 1, [Ashleigh Barty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashleigh_Barty), and made quarterfinals at the [Madrid Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Mutua_Madrid_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), defeating world No. 2, [Naomi Osaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naomi_Osaka). In 2023, she reached her first major final at the French Open and her first WTA 1000 final at the [Cincinnati Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Western_&_Southern_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles). In 2026, she won her first WTA 1000 title at the Qatar Ladies Open and reached her second major final at the Wimbledon Championships.

## Early life

Karolína Muchová was born in August 1996 in [Olomouc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olomouc), [Czech Republic](5321.md).[^cite_note-\:0-3] Her father is former Czech footballer [Josef Mucha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josef_Mucha).[^cite_note-4] He introduced her to tennis at the age of seven. She also has a brother, with whom she did many sports when they were kids. Since there were tennis courts near her home, she decided to pick up a tennis racquet, and then when she was about 12, she chose tennis over handball.[^cite_note-\:1-5] In 2019, she moved to [Prague](23844.md) to train at the [I. ČLTK Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I._%C4%8CLTK_Prague).[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-\:1-5] She stated that her tennis idol growing up was [Roger Federer](262376.md).[^cite_note-\:3-2] She struggled with a lot of injuries during her junior years.[^cite_note-\:1-5]

## Career

### 2013–2018: Breakthrough



Muchová at the 2018 US Open

Muchová began playing on the [ITF Women's Circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ITF_Women's_Circuit) in October 2013 at the age of 17. Her first tournament was a \$10k event in [Dubrovnik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubrovnik), where she reached the second round.[^cite_note-\:2-6] In July the following year, she won her first ITF title in [Michalovce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michalovce), Slovakia. She then started to produce low-performances until March 2016, when she won her second singles title at Sharm El Sheikh, and two weeks later another event at the same venue.[^cite_note-7] In July 2017, Muchová reached the final of the \$75k [ITS Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_ITS_Cup_%E2%80%93_Singles) in Olomouc, losing there to her countrymate [Markéta Vondroušová](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark%C3%A9ta_Vondrou%C5%A1ov%C3%A1). Muchová then made her [WTA Tour](1398173.md) main-draw debut at the [2017 Korea Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Korea_Open) after defeating two low-ranked players, and then lost in the first round of the main draw to [Priscilla Hon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priscilla_Hon).[^cite_note-\:2-6] Ranked No. 202, Muchová made her main-draw debut at a [major](197638.md) event at the [2018 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_US_Open_\(tennis\)) winning three qualifying matches. After winning her opening-match against [Dayana Yastremska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dayana_Yastremska),[^cite_note-8] Muchová upset two-time Grand Slam champion and 12th seed [Garbiñe Muguruza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garbi%C3%B1e_Muguruza) in the second round to score her first top-20 victory, advancing to the third round of the tournament.[^cite_note-9] In the third round, she lost to [Ashleigh Barty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashleigh_Barty).[^cite_note-10]

### 2019: Wimbledon quarterfinal, WTA 250 title

Muchová made her main-draw debut at the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles) after qualifying. She lost in the first round to No. 8 [Karolína Plíšková](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karol%C3%ADna_Pl%C3%AD%C5%A1kov%C3%A1).[^cite_note-11] Still after qualifying, Muchová made her main-draw debut at the [Premier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTA_Premier_tournaments) events at the [Qatar Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Qatar_Total_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles). She defeated lucky loser [Samantha Stosur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samantha_Stosur) and [Hsieh Su-wei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hsieh_Su-wei) to reach her first Premier-level quarterfinal. She lost in the quarterfinals to No. 7 [Elina Svitolina](27630639.md) in straight sets.[^cite_note-12] Still after qualifying, she debuted at the [Premier Mandatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTA_Premier_tournaments) events at the [Miami Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Miami_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles). She defeated qualifier [Nao Hibino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nao_Hibino) before losing to No. 4 [Angelique Kerber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angelique_Kerber).[^cite_note-13]

Muchová received a wildcard into the [Prague Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_J\&T_Banka_Prague_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles) where she defeated qualifier [Iga Świątek](51838558.md), [Jennifer Brady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Brady), [Natalia Vikhlyantseva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natalia_Vikhlyantseva), and [Bernarda Pera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernarda_Pera) to reach her first WTA Tour final.[^cite_note-14] In the final, she lost to qualifier [Jil Teichmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jil_Teichmann) in three sets.[^cite_note-15] Nevertheless, her ranking rose to No. 74.[^cite_note-16] At the [French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), she defeated No. 17 [Anett Kontaveit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anett_Kontaveit)[^cite_note-17] before losing to [Irina-Camelia Begu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irina-Camelia_Begu).[^cite_note-18]



Muchová at Wimbledon, 2019

At the [Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), Muchová defeated [Aleksandra Krunić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandra_Kruni%C4%87), [Madison Brengle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madison_Brengle), and No. 20 Anett Kontaveit[^cite_note-19] to reach the fourth round. She defeated No. 3 Karolína Plíšková in a match that went to a third-set tiebreak and lasted three hours to claim her first top 10 victory and reach her first major quarterfinal.[^cite_note-20] She lost in the quarterfinals to No. 8 Elina Svitolina in straight sets.[^cite_note-21] Reaching the quarterfinals, she reached a career-high ranking of No. 43.[^cite_note-22]

At the [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), Muchová defeated qualifier [Elena Rybakina](55541443.md) and No. 28 Hsieh Su-Wei[^cite_note-23] before losing to No. 8 [Serena Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serena_Williams).[^cite_note-24]

At the [Korea Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Korea_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles), Muchová defeated [Alison Van Uytvanck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alison_Van_Uytvanck), qualifier [Timea Babos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timea_Babos),[^cite_note-25] qualifier Priscilla Hon,[^cite_note-26] and [Wang Yafan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Yafan)[^cite_note-27] to reach her second WTA tour final. In the final, she defeated [Magda Linette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magda_Linette) in straight sets to claim her first WTA Tour title.[^cite_note-28] At the [Kremlin Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Kremlin_Cup_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), Muchová defeated [Svetlana Kuznetsova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Svetlana_Kuznetsova), No. 21 [Donna Vekić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donna_Veki%C4%87), and [Ekaterina Alexandrova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekaterina_Alexandrova) to reach the semifinals. In the semifinals, she lost to [Anastasia Pavlyuchenkova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anastasia_Pavlyuchenkova) in three sets.[^cite_note-29] At the end of the season, Muchová qualified for the [WTA Elite Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_WTA_Elite_Trophy_%E2%80%93_Singles) for the first time. She defeated [Sofia Kenin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_Kenin) and [Alison Riske](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alison_Riske) to make the semifinals.[^cite_note-30] In the semifinals, she lost to [Aryna Sabalenka](50222577.md) in straight sets.[^cite_note-31] She finished the year as world No. 21.[^cite_note-32]

### 2020: US Open fourth round

In 2020, Muchová produced rather mixed results. At the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), she defeated [Kirsten Flipkens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirsten_Flipkens) before losing to [CiCi Bellis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CiCi_Bellis).[^cite_note-33] At the [Dubai Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Dubai_Tennis_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), she lost in the first round to qualifier [Kateřina Siniaková](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate%C5%99ina_Siniakov%C3%A1). At the [Qatar Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Qatar_Total_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles), she defeated Magda Linette[^cite_note-\:2-6] before losing to No. 6 [Kiki Bertens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiki_Bertens).[^cite_note-34] After the six month suspension of the WTA Tour due to [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md), Muchová first played at the [Cincinnati Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Western_&_Southern_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles). She beat qualifier [Ann Li](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ann_Li_\(tennis\))[^cite_note-\:2-6] before losing to No. 10 [Naomi Osaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naomi_Osaka).[^cite_note-35] Her best performance of the season came at the [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles). She beat [Venus Williams](167378.md),[^cite_note-36] [Anna Kalinskaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Kalinskaya), and [Sorana Cîrstea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorana_C%C3%AErstea)[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38] to reach the fourth round, where she lost to No. 27 [Victoria Azarenka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_Azarenka) in three sets.[^cite_note-39] At the [French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), she lost in the first round to [Christina McHale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christina_McHale).[^cite_note-40] At the [Ostrava Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_J\&T_Banka_Ostrava_Open_%E2%80%93_Singles), she defeated [Zhang Shuai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhang_Shuai) before losing to No. 21 [Elise Mertens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elise_Mertens). However, she spent the whole year inside the top 30.[^cite_note-\:4-41]

### 2021: Australian Open semifinal, top 20

At the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), Muchová defeated [Jeļena Ostapenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Je%C4%BCena_Ostapenko), [Mona Barthel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mona_Barthel),[^cite_note-42] No. 6 Karolína Plíšková,[^cite_note-43] and No. 16 Elise Mertens,[^cite_note-44] without dropping a set, to reach her second major quarterfinal and her first at the Australian Open. She came back from a set down to upset world No. 1 Ashleigh Barty and reach her first major semifinal.[^cite_note-45] In the semifinals, she lost to No. 24 [Jennifer Brady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Brady) in three sets.[^cite_note-46]

At the [Madrid Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Mutua_Madrid_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), Muchová defeated [Wang Qiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Qiang_\(tennis\)) in the first round. In the second round, she upset world No. 2 Naomi Osaka in three sets.[^cite_note-47] She defeated No. 16 [Maria Sakkari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Sakkari) to reach her first WTA 1000 quarterfinal.[^cite_note-48] In the quarterfinals, she lost to Anastasia Pavlyuchenkova in straight sets. Nevertheless, she reached a new career-high ranking of No. 19.[^cite_note-profile-49] At the [French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), she defeated [Andrea Petkovic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Petkovic) and qualifier [Varvara Lepchenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varvara_Lepchenko)[^cite_note-50] before losing to [Sloane Stephens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloane_Stephens).[^cite_note-51]

At the [Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), Muchová defeated Zhang Shuai,[^cite_note-52] [Camila Giorgi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camila_Giorgi),[^cite_note-53] No. 19 Anastasia Pavlyuchenkova,[^cite_note-54] and [Paula Badosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paula_Badosa)[^cite_note-55] to reach her second consecutive Wimbledon quarterfinal. In the quarterfinals, she lost to No. 28 [Angelique Kerber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angelique_Kerber) in straight sets.[^cite_note-56]

At the [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_US_Open_\(tennis\)), Muchová was ousted in the first round by [Sara Sorribes Tormo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sara_Sorribes_Tormo).[^cite_note-57]

### 2022: Multiple injuries, out of top 200

Muchová didn't play at the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles) because of an injury.[^cite_note-58] She came back at the [Miami Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Miami_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles). She defeated [Tereza Martincová](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tereza_Martincov%C3%A1) and No. 22 [Leylah Fernandez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leylah_Fernandez),[^cite_note-59] but withdrew from her match against Naomi Osaka due to a left abdominal injury.[^cite_note-60]

Using protected ranking at the [Madrid Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Mutua_Madrid_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), Muchová defeated wildcard entrant [Zheng Qinwen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zheng_Qinwen) before losing to No. 13 [Belinda Bencic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belinda_Bencic).[^cite_note-61] In [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Italian_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), she lost in the first round to qualifier [Petra Martić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petra_Marti%C4%87). At the [French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), she defeated wildcard entrant [Carole Monnet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carole_Monnet) and No. 3 Maria Sakkari to reach the third round. She retired during her match against No. 28 [Amanda Anisimova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Anisimova) due to thigh and ankle injuries.[^cite_note-62]

At [Wimbledon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), Muchová lost in the first round to No. 18 [Simona Halep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simona_Halep).[^cite_note-63] At the [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), she lost in the first round to [Ajla Tomljanović](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ajla_Tomljanovi%C4%87).[^cite_note-64] Her ranking fell outside the top 200.[^cite_note-65]

### 2023: French Open final, top 10

Muchová started the season in [Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_ASB_Classic_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles). She defeated [Wang Xiyu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Xiyu) and qualifier [Elena-Gabriela Ruse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elena-Gabriela_Ruse) to reach the quarterfinals, losing there to qualifier [Rebeka Masarova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebeka_Masarova) in straight sets. Using protected ranking at the [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), she defeated qualifier [Lesia Tsurenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lesia_Tsurenko)[^cite_note-66] before losing to No. 13 [Danielle Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danielle_Collins).[^cite_note-67]

At the [Dubai Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Dubai_Tennis_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), Muchová defeated Bernarda Pera,[^cite_note-68] Sorana Cîrstea,[^cite_note-69] and No. 8 Belinda Bencic,[^cite_note-70] without dropping a set, to reach her second WTA 1000 quarterfinal. Although she withdrew from her quarterfinal match, her ranking rose to No. 76.[^cite_note-ranking-71]

At the [Indian Wells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_BNP_Paribas_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), Muchová defeated [Yulia Putintseva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yulia_Putintseva),[^cite_note-72] No. 14 Victoria Azarenka,[^cite_note-73] No. 26 Martina Trevisan,[^cite_note-74] and Markéta Vondroušová[^cite_note-75] to reach her third WTA 1000 quarterfinal. Although losing to No. 10 and eventual champion Elena Rybakina in three sets,[^cite_note-76] her ranking rose to No. 55.[^cite_note-ranking-71] Entering the [Miami Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Miami_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles) after qualifying, she defeated Jil Teichmann and [Zhu Lin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhu_Lin_\(tennis\))[^cite_note-77] before losing to Sorana Cîrstea.[^cite_note-78]

Ranked No. 43 at the [French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), Muchová defeated No. 8 Maria Sakkari,[^cite_note-79] [Nadia Podoroska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nadia_Podoroska),[^cite_note-80] No. 27 Irina-Camelia Begu[^cite_note-81] and lucky loser [Elina Avanesyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elina_Avanesyan)[^cite_note-82] to reach her fourth major quarterfinal and her first at Roland Garros. She defeated Anastasia Pavlyuchenkova to reach her second major semifinal.[^cite_note-83] She upset world No. 2, Aryna Sabalenka, in a three-set match lasting over three hours, saving a match point and recovering from 2–5 down in the final set to reach her first major final, becoming the fifth Czech player to reach the final at Roland Garros in the [Open Era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Era).[^cite_note-84] She was the fourth-lowest-ranked women's finalist in the French Open history.[^cite_note-85] In the final, she lost to world No. 1, [Iga Świątek](51838558.md), in three sets.[^cite_note-86] Nevertheless, she reached a new career-high ranking of No. 16.[^cite_note-87]

At [Wimbledon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), Muchová lost in the first round to [Jule Niemeier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jule_Niemeier).[^cite_note-88]

In [Montreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_National_Bank_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), Muchová defeated No. 27 [Anastasia Potapova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anastasia_Potapova),[^cite_note-89] and Sorana Cîrstea[^cite_note-90] before losing again to Iga Świątek, in three sets.[^cite_note-91] In [Cincinnati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Western_&_Southern_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles), Muchová defeated No. 19 [Beatriz Haddad Maia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beatriz_Haddad_Maia), Petra Martić[^cite_note-92] and No. 8 Maria Sakkari[^cite_note-93] to reach her fourth WTA 1000 quarterfinal. She advanced to her first WTA 1000 semifinal as [Marie Bouzková](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie_Bouzkov%C3%A1) retired due to injury.[^cite_note-94] In a rematch of their French Open semifinal, she upset world No. 2, Aryna Sabalenka, again in three sets to advance to her first WTA 1000 final.[^cite_note-95] In the final, she lost to No. 7 [Coco Gauff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coco_Gauff), in straight sets.[^cite_note-cincinnati-96] Nevertheless, her ranking rose to the top 10.[^cite_note-cincinnati-96]

At the [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_US_Open_-_Women's_singles), Muchová defeated wildcard entrant [Storm Hunter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storm_Hunter),[^cite_note-97] [Magdalena Fręch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magdalena_Fr%C4%99ch),[^cite_note-98] [Taylor Townsend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Townsend),[^cite_note-99] and [Wang Xinyu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Xinyu)[^cite_note-100] to reach her fifth major quarterfinal and her first at US Open. She then quickly dispatched Sorana Cîrstea, only losing three games, to reach her second major semifinal of the season.[^cite_note-101] In the semifinals, she lost again to No. 6 Coco Gauff in straight sets.[^cite_note-102] Despite the loss, she reached a career-high ranking of No. 8.[^cite_note-103]

Muchová qualified for the end-of-season [WTA Finals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_WTA_Finals_%E2%80%93_Singles) for the first time,[^cite_note-104] but was forced to withdraw from the tournament less than a week before it began due to a right wrist injury she suffered at the US Open.[^cite_note-105]

## Playing style and coaching



Muchová hitting a forehand

Muchová plays an aggressive all-court game, characterized by a variety of shots. She hits groundstrokes with sufficient depth and power from both the forehand and backhand sides to generate winners from any position on the court. She has been praised for her ability to incorporate softer shots, such as drop shots, lobs, and [sliced](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backspin) backhands, into her game, constantly breaking up the pace of baseline rallies, and being able to hit winners with these typically defensive shots. Her first serve averages 103 mph (166 km/h) and peaks at 110 mph (180 km/h), allowing her to serve aces frequently; she also possesses a second serve that, despite averaging 83 mph (134 km/h), possesses a high amount of topspin, meaning that it can be deployed effectively without being attacked by aggressive players. Muchová is also proficient at defending her second serve. Her footwork, speed, and anticipation allow her to be one of the strongest return players on the WTA Tour. Physically, her conditioning allows her to sustain long rallies, and is an effective counterpuncher, waiting for openings hit winners. Additionally, her experience in doubles contributes to her net play making her a frequent and effective volleyer when moving forward to finish points.

Muchová has had several coaches in her career: Emil Miške (2017–2019, Apr 2023–), [David Kotyza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Kotyza) (2020 – Aug 2022), Jan Blecha (Sep 2022 – Apr 2023), [Kirsten Flipkens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirsten_Flipkens) (2023–2025) and [Sven Groeneveld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sven_Groeneveld) since January 2026.[^cite_note-coach-187]
