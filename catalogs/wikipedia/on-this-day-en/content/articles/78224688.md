**Canada–Syria relations** were established on 20 May 1965, but were significantly affected by the [Syrian Civil War](30741795.md). Post-war relations have begun to improve in alignment with other western nations.

## History

### Ba'athist Syria

Canada and [Ba'athist Syria](57134800.md) opened embassies in each other's countries following the establishment of diplomatic relations in 1965.[^cite_note-4] Relations became strained due to [Ba'athist Syria's human rights issues](78548501.md) and [involvement in regional conflicts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_wars_involving_Syria), and Canada's diplomatic stance shifted further with the outbreak of the [Syrian civil war](30741795.md).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-France_24-7]

Canada closed its embassy in [Damascus](8914.md) on 5 March 2012 and suspended formal diplomatic relations,[^cite_note-8] while Syria's embassy in [Ottawa](22219.md) was closed on 29 May 2012.[^cite_note-9] For political reasons, the Syrian consulate in [Montreal](7954681.md) was closed in 2016.[^cite_note-SyrCon-10]

As of November 2024, Syria still has an honorary consulate in [Vancouver](32706.md).[^cite_note-11]

### Post-Assad

After the collapse of the Ba'athist rule in Syria in December 2024, Canada welcomed the [end of the Assad regime](78548066.md), and Syrian exiles across Canada celebrated its fall.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

In March 2025, Canada has officially suspended economic sanctions against Syria and has appointed the ambassador to Lebanon, Stefanie McCollum, to be its non-resident ambassador to Syria.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15]

In December 2025, Canada removed Syria from its list of foreign state supporters of terrorism and revoked the terrorist designation of [Hay'at Tahrir al-Sham](53015755.md), while maintaining targeted sanctions on former regime officials.[^cite_note-16]

## Humanitarian aid

Canada has been an active humanitarian donor during the Syrian crisis, contributing funds for [Syrian refugees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refugees_of_the_Syrian_civil_war) and internally displaced persons.[^cite_note-17] Canada is one of the largest resettlement countries for Syrian refugees, admitting tens of thousands since the beginning of the war.[^cite_note-18]

## Political positions



Syrian solidarity demonstration in Montreal, March 2011.

Canada imposed [sanctions](67328508.md) on [Assad regime](57134800.md) officials and institutions in response to human rights abuses.[^cite_note-19] It has also supported international efforts to seek accountability for [war crimes in the Syrian Civil War](36355222.md).[^cite_note-France_24-7]
