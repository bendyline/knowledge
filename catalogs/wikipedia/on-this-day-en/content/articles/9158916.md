Map of Yemen

Yemen, officially the Republic of Yemen, is a country located in [West Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Asia), occupying the southwestern to southern end of the [Arabian Peninsula](47858.md). It is bordered by [Saudi Arabia](349303.md) to the north, the [Red Sea](26191.md) to the west, the [Gulf of Aden](58716.md) and [Arabian Sea](2747.md) to the south, and [Oman](22316.md) to the east. The capital and largest city of Yemen is [Sana'a](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sana'a). Yemen is divided into twenty [governorates](690277.md) (*[muhafazah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhafazah)*) and the Capital district. The governorates are subdivided into 333 [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_districts_of_Yemen) (*muderiah*), which are subdivided into 2,210 sub-districts, and then into 38,284 villages (as of 2001).

On 28 March 2015, a [no-fly zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No-fly_zone) was imposed over the country by the [Royal Saudi Air Force](570367.md) as part of the [Saudi-led intervention in the Yemeni civil war](46230181.md).[^cite_note-1] The blockade was lifted in November 2015 and reinstated in February 2016.[^cite_note-2] The blockade was lifted in November 2017.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] Flights were cancelled once again, for four days (28-31 January 2018), but resumed on 1 February 2018.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]
