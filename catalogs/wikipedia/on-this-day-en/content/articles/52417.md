**2025** ([MMXXV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_numerals)) was a [common year starting on Wednesday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_year_starting_on_Wednesday) of the [Gregorian calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregorian_calendar), the 2025th year of the [Common Era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Era) (CE) and *[Anno Domini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anno_Domini)* (AD) designations, the 25th year of the [3rd millennium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3rd_millennium) and the [21st century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21st_century), and the 6th year of the [2020s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020s) decade.&#x20;

The year saw an escalation of major ongoing armed conflicts,[^cite_note-1] including the [Gaza war](74998519.md), marked by [famine](75828824.md) and [a humanitarian crisis](75277791.md) and the [Russian invasion of Ukraine](70149799.md), which prompted peace negotiations involving [Vladimir Putin](32817.md) and [Donald Trump](4848272.md); civil wars in [Myanmar](69807451.md), [Sudan](73556699.md) and [Yemen](46215853.md) also continued throughout the year.

Internal crises in [Armenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_political_crisis_\(2020%E2%80%93present\)), [Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_post-resignation_violence_\(2024%E2%80%932026\)), [Bulgaria](72847983.md), [the Democratic Republic of the Congo](71001703.md), [Ecuador](72171044.md), [France](77741666.md), [Georgia](78750834.md), [Germany](78189355.md), [Haiti](59942434.md), [Libya](44791215.md), [Pakistan](73265939.md), [Peru](60938753.md), [Somalia](76521890.md), and [South Korea](78508419.md) persisted into the year, with the latter culminating in the [arrest](78791194.md) and [removal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impeachment_of_Yoon_Suk_Yeol) of President [Yoon Suk Yeol](62877952.md) from office. Furthermore, a [diplomatic crisis between China and Japan](81642186.md) broke out in November.

The year also witnessed a [wave of protests](77442342.md) predominantly led by [Generation Z](4015968.md), with some movements—such as [those in Nepal](81013780.md) and [Madagascar](81184334.md)—resulting in the overthrow of targeted governments. Several brief conflicts arising from longstanding tensions emerged mid-year, including clashes between [India and Pakistan](79896622.md) in May, [Iran and Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve-Day_War) in June, and [Cambodia and Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Cambodia%E2%80%93Thailand_border_conflict) in July. In the latter case, a [leaked phone call](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thailand%E2%80%93Cambodia_phone_call_leak) involving Thai prime minister [Paetongtarn Shinawatra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paetongtarn_Shinawatra) and Cambodian senate president [Hun Sen](393779.md) [resulted in the removal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Thai_political_crisis) of the former from office.

In economics and business, the [return of Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_inauguration_of_Donald_Trump) to the [U.S. presidency](24113.md) ushered in [a series of tariffs imposed by the United States on the rest of the world](78448911.md), significantly disrupting global trade and reinvigorating the [China–United States trade war](57055272.md). The technology sector was also affected by the release of the [DeepSeek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeepSeek_\(chatbot\)) chatbot, a Chinese [large language model](73248112.md) that competed with [ChatGPT](72417803.md).

Aviation and aerospace experienced several accidents during the year, including [a collision between two aircraft half a mile south of](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Potomac_River_mid-air_collision) [Washington National Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronald_Reagan_Washington_National_Airport) in January, and the crash of [Air India Flight 171](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_India_Flight_171), a [Boeing 787](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_787_Dreamliner) bound for [Gatwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gatwick_Airport), in [Ahmedabad](206337.md), India, in June. The year also saw notable advances in space exploration, including the [first crewed polar-orbit spaceflight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fram2) and the [first fully successful lunar landing by a private company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Ghost_Mission_1).
