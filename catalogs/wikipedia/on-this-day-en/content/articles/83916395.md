The **2026 Plovdiv murder** occurred on 4 August 2026 on [Dzhendem tepe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dzhendem_tepe) ([Bulgarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_language): Младежки хълм, Youth Hill) in [Plovdiv](182553.md), [Bulgaria](3415.md).[^cite_note-bta_custody-1] Georgi Kuzev, a 37-year-old resident of [Krichim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krichim), was lured to a park area by a group of teenagers using a [social media](5897742.md) [dating application](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dating_application).[^cite_note-fakti_details-2][^cite_note-163_crime-3] Kuzev was ambushed, subjected to an extended physical assault and torture lasting over an hour, and robbed.[^cite_note-fakti_details-2][^cite_note-balkan_insight-4] He succumbed to his injuries in a hospital later that evening.[^cite_note-fakti_overview-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

Ten teenagers aged between 14 and 17 were initially detained in connection with the attack.[^cite_note-bnt_arrests-9][^cite_note-fakti_custody-10] On 7 August 2026, the Plovdiv District Court ordered five minors—three boys and two girls—to remain in permanent pre-trial custody on charges of premeditated murder committed with exceptional cruelty and hooligan motives.[^cite_note-bta_custody-1][^cite_note-163_crime-3][^cite_note-11]

## Background

### Victim

The victim, Georgi Kuzev, was a 37-year-old worker from Krichim.[^cite_note-fakti_overview-5] Relatives and municipal officials described Kuzev as a quiet, trusting individual who lived alone following the deaths of his parents.[^cite_note-fakti_vigil-12] Relatives stated that Kuzev was using online social networks to seek a romantic relationship.[^cite_note-fakti_vigil-12]

### Luring scheme

According to the Plovdiv District Prosecutor's Office, the perpetrators created fake social media accounts to arrange meetings under false pretenses.[^cite_note-163_crime-3] A 17-year-old female member of the group posed online as a 15-year-old girl ([catfishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catfishing)) to set up a meeting with Kuzev at Youth Hill (Dzhendem tepe).[^cite_note-163_crime-3][^cite_note-bta_custody-1] Prosecutors noted that Kuzev did not conceal his identity or age during their exchanges.[^cite_note-163_crime-3] Supervising prosecutor Anelia Trifonova stated that the group claimed to emulate online "pedophile hunter" vigilantism groups, though law enforcement emphasized that the youths' true objective was to launch a physical assault rather than contact law enforcement.[^cite_note-163_crime-3][^cite_note-fakti_custody-10][^cite_note-13]

## Incident

On 4 August 2026, Kuzev arrived at Youth Hill in central Plovdiv to meet the individual with whom he had communicated.[^cite_note-163_crime-3][^cite_note-bta_custody-1] Upon his arrival, a group of up to ten teenagers surrounded him and forced him into a wooded area of the park.[^cite_note-fakti_details-2][^cite_note-163_crime-3]

For over an hour, the group subjected Kuzev to continuous physical abuse.[^cite_note-fakti_details-2][^cite_note-bta_custody-1] According to court statements by Judge Petko Minev and prosecutors, the attackers repeatedly beat, kicked, and strangled Kuzev.[^cite_note-163_crime-3] During the assault, members of the group shaved his eyebrows, extinguished cigarette butts on his skin, drew [swastikas](45943.md) on his body, and spat on him.[^cite_note-fakti_details-2][^cite_note-163_crime-3] Several accomplices filmed the assault using mobile phones and broadcast the footage live on social media platforms.[^cite_note-fakti_details-2][^cite_note-fakti_custody-10]

Following the beating, the perpetrators stole approximately 30 euros from Kuzev.[^cite_note-fakti_details-2][^cite_note-163_crime-3] The teenagers subsequently used the stolen money to purchase [doner kebabs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doner_kebab).[^cite_note-fakti_details-2][^cite_note-163_crime-3]

Kuzev managed to move to the foot of the hill after the attackers fled.[^cite_note-bta_custody-1][^cite_note-163_crime-3] At approximately 5\:20 p.m. EEST, passersby discovered him lying helpless with visible signs of severe trauma and alerted emergency services via 112.[^cite_note-fakti_overview-5][^cite_note-163_crime-3] Medical personnel transported him to a local hospital, where doctors diagnosed him with severe [traumatic brain injury](1057414.md), multiple fractures, and internal organ lacerations, including ruptures to his lungs, liver, and kidneys.[^cite_note-bta_custody-1][^cite_note-fakti_details-2] Kuzev died from his injuries later that evening.[^cite_note-fakti_overview-5][^cite_note-163_crime-3]

## Legal proceedings

Following an investigation by the Second Regional Department of the [Ministry of Internal Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Interior_\(Bulgaria\)) in Plovdiv, police detained ten minors aged 14 to 17.[^cite_note-fakti_details-2][^cite_note-bta_custody-1]

The Plovdiv District Prosecutor's Office filed formal murder charges against five of the detained minors—three boys and two girls.[^cite_note-bta_custody-1][^cite_note-fakti_custody-10] The charges specified premeditated homicide committed with exceptional cruelty and hooligan motives.[^cite_note-bta_custody-1][^cite_note-163_crime-3]

On 7 August 2026, the Plovdiv District Court held a closed-door detention hearing.[^cite_note-bta_custody-1][^cite_note-fakti_hristov-14] The court ordered all five primary defendants remanded in permanent pre-trial custody, ruling that the evidence indicated deliberate intent and extreme brutality without provocation from the victim.[^cite_note-bta_custody-1][^cite_note-163_crime-3] Under the Bulgarian Penal Code, juvenile offenders face statutory sentence reductions, carrying potential prison terms of 3 to 10 years for minors under 16, and 5 to 12 years for those aged 16 to 17.[^cite_note-fakti_custody-10]

## Reaction

The mayor of [Krichim Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krichim_Municipality), Atanas Kalchev, issued an official statement extending condolences to Kuzev's family and demanding full legal accountability for the perpetrators.[^cite_note-fakti_overview-5][^cite_note-15] On 6 August 2026, residents of Krichim gathered for a vigil outside Kuzev's home, rejecting the alleged vigilantism claims and demanding maximum legal penalties.[^cite_note-bta_custody-1][^cite_note-fakti_vigil-12]

[Deputy Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Prime_Minister_of_Bulgaria) [Ivo Hristov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivo_Hristov) publicly commented on the case, criticizing attempts to minimize juvenile responsibility in violent offenses.[^cite_note-fakti_hristov-14] Human rights organizations, including the [Bulgarian Helsinki Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_Helsinki_Committee), issued statements urging thorough judicial review of bias motives and youth violence prevention measures.[^cite_note-bhc_bta-16]
