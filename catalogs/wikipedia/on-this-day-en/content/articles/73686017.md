# Floods in South Sudan

&#x20;**Floods in [South Sudan](32350676.md)** are a frequent occurrence, with the country's location in the [Nile River Basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nile_Basin) and its low-lying topography making it vulnerable to floods. They have been recorded in the country since the 1960s, and their impacts have become increasingly severe in recent years due to [climate change](5042951.md) and poor drainage infrastructure.[^cite_note-1]

## Impacts

Floods have caused displacement and the loss of gomes personal belongings. Many schools have also been closed due to the floods.[^cite_note-2] The floods have also destroyed crops, leading to food shortages and increased food prices. Livestock has also been impacted, with many animals being swept away by floodwaters.[^cite_note-3]

## Ongoing 2024 floods

The [2024 South Sudan floods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_South_Sudan_floods) started in August 2024 and have been so catastrophic that some parts of the country have been under water for several years.[^cite_note-4]
