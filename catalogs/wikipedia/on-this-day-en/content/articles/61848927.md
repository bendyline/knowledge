**Sami Abu Shehadeh** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): سامي ابو شحادة, [Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): סָאמִי אַבּוּ שְׁחַאדַה) is a [Palestinian Israeli](492331.md) politician. He is the leader of [Balad](44494596.md),[^cite_note-1] and served as a member of the [Knesset](74656.md) for the [Joint List](45229828.md) from 2019 to 2022.[^cite_note-2]

## Biography

Abu Shehadeh was born in [Lod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lod) to a Muslim family and raised in [Jaffa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaffa). He attended Terra Santa High School, a Catholic school located in [Old Jaffa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Jaffa).

Abu Shehadeh studied for a master's degree in Middle Eastern history at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University), and wrote a doctorate on [Jaffa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaffa) as a cultural center during the [British Mandate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_Palestine).[^cite_note-3]

## Political career

Abu Shehadeh was a member of the [Tel Aviv-Yafo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv-Yafo_Municipality) City Council[^cite_note-4] for a number of years until 2013, on behalf of the Jaffa faction. He served as the Director of the Yaffa Youth Movement.[^cite_note-\:1-5]

In the elections for the 22nd Knesset, he was placed third on the [Balad](44494596.md) list following the retirement of Mazen Ghnaim, and after uniting with the other Arab parties, he was placed 13th on the Joint List.[^cite_note-6] On 23 January 2021, he was elected as the party chairman of the Balad party in its primaries.[^cite_note-\:1-5] In the [2022 Israeli legislative election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Israeli_legislative_election), he was placed first on the [Balad](44494596.md) list, however, he lost his seat due to the failure of the party to pass the 3.25% election threshold.[^cite_note-7]

Amid the bombing campaign against Gaza started in retaliation for the Hamas-led incursion into southern Israel from Gaza in the [Gaza war](74998519.md), Shehadeh accused US President Biden and other international leaders of giving "a green light for ethnic cleaning" to Israel.[^cite_note-UNHRC-1210-8] Writing in *[The New Arab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_Arab)*, he added that: "Israel is not killing the Hamas leadership; they are not getting revenge out of Hamas. There is [collective punishment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collective_punishment) for 2.2 million people."[^cite_note-UNHRC-1210-8]

[Otzma Yehudit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otzma_Yehudit) petitioned the [Central Elections Committee](4456077.md) (CEC) to disqualify Abu Shehadeh over an article he wrote on 8 October which described the [7 October attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7_October_attacks) as an "important historical event". The party said it displayed "support for armed struggle against Israel", one of the reasons for disqualification under the [Basic Laws of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_Laws_of_Israel). [Attorney General of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attorney_General_of_Israel) [Gali Baharav-Miara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gali_Baharav-Miara) did not support his disqualification at the time.[^cite_note-9]

The CEC barred Abu Shehadeh on 23 September 2026 from running in the [2026 Israeli legislative election](71244652.md).[^cite_note-\:2-10] A 30-4 vote by the committee disqualified Abu Shehadeh for denying Israel’s existence as a [Jewish state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_state) and a democratic state.[^cite_note-tjp29sept-11] Committee chair [Noam Sohlberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noam_Sohlberg), who normally does not vote, broke decades of precedent to vote in favor,[^cite_note-\:2-10] while voting in favor of disqualifying him on the basis of support for armed struggle.[^cite_note-tjp29sept-11]

Baharav-Miara filed a paper with the [Supreme Court of Israel](1666510.md) on 29 September calling for his disqualification, solely on the basis of his support for armed struggle.[^cite_note-tjp29sept-11] The court indicated on 1 October that a majority was prepared to disqualify Abu Shehadeh, with [President of the Supreme Court of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Supreme_Court_of_Israel) [Yitzhak Amit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yitzhak_Amit) recommending that Abu Shehadeh withdraw his candidacy.[^cite_note-12] Balad withdrew his candidacy the following day by submitting his resignation to the Central Elections Committee.[^cite_note-13]

Abu Shehada lives in Jaffa.[^cite_note-14]
