The **president of the Republic of Malawi** ([Chichewa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chichewa_language): *Mtsogoleri wa Dziko la Malawi*; [Tumbuka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tumbuka_language): *Mlongozgi wa Charu cha Malaŵi*) is the [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) and [head of government](37570.md) of [Malawi](19088.md). The president leads the [executive branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_\(government\)) of the [Government of Malawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Malawi) and is the [commander-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief) of the [Malawian Defence Force](35413708.md). The current president is [Peter Mutharika](29471682.md), who has served since being re-elected in September 2025.&#x20;

## Executive branch

Under the 1995 [constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution), the [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_\(government_title\)), who is both [head of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) and [head of government](37570.md), is chosen through universal direct [suffrage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suffrage) every five years. Malawi has a [vice-president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice-President_of_Malawi) who is elected with the president. The president has the option of appointing a second vice-president, who must be from a different party. It also includes a presidentially appointed [cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_\(government\)). The members of the [cabinet of Malawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_Malawi) can be drawn from either within or outside of the [legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature). The President is also concurrently the Minister of Defence.&#x20;

[Peter Mutharika](29471682.md) was sworn in as president of Malawi on 4 October 2025 in Blantyre.[^cite_note-1]&#x20;

## Origins of the presidency

On 6 July 1964, [Nyasaland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyasaland) became independent from British rule and renamed itself Malawi, with [Elizabeth II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_II) as [Queen of Malawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_of_Malawi). Under a new constitution in 1966, Malawi became a republic with prime minister [Hastings Banda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hastings_Banda) becoming its first president. Under the country's 1966, 1994 and 1995 constitutions, the president is executive head of state and government, and serves a five-year term. The first president, Banda, was elected by the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_\(Malawi\)), and was named [president for life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_for_life) in 1971. Subsequent presidents have been popularly elected.&#x20;

After the restoration of multiparty democracy in 1993, the presidency was hedged about with a number of checks and balances to prevent its total dominance over the political system.&#x20;

## Election to the presidency

The president is elected by a majority vote (50% + 1) of the electorate through direct, universal and equal suffrage. All [Malawian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malawian) citizens that have attained the age of 18 are eligible to vote in all elections. Every presidential candidate shall declare who shall be the vice-president if elected at the time of the candidate's nomination. The vice-president shall be elected concurrently with the president, with both names on the ballot.&#x20;

## Term limits

As of 2021, the [Constitution of Malawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Malawi) limits a president to two consecutive five-year terms but there is no limit on the total number of terms. There was an attempt to modify the term limits in 2003 for [Bakili Muluzi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakili_Muluzi), but it did not materialize.[^cite_note-termlimits-2]&#x20;
