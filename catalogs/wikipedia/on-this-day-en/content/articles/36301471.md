**Major general** is a [military rank](150237.md) used in many countries. It is derived from the older rank of [sergeant major general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergeant_major_general).[^cite_note-1]

In [English-speaking countries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_English-speaking_population), when appointed to a field command, a major general is typically in command of a [division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_\(military\)) consisting of around 6,000 to 25,000 troops (several [regiments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regiment) or [brigades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigade)). It is a rank that is subordinate to the rank of [lieutenant general](201916.md) and senior to the rank of [brigadier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigadier) or [brigadier general](206220.md). In the Commonwealth, major general is equivalent to the navy rank of [rear admiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rear_admiral). In air forces with a separate rank structure (Commonwealth), major general is equivalent to [air vice-marshal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_vice-marshal).&#x20;

In some countries, including much of [Eastern Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Europe), that do not use the brigadier general rank, major general is the lowest of the [general officer](148537.md) ranks.

See also: [Military ranks of the Royal Brunei Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_ranks_of_the_Royal_Brunei_Armed_Forces)

In the sultanate of [Brunei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brunei), the rank of Major general ([Malay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_language): *Mejar jeneral*) is used by the [Royal Brunei Land Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Brunei_Land_Force) and the [Royal Brunei Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Brunei_Air_Force). The rank is held by the [Commander of the Royal Brunei Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander_of_the_Royal_Brunei_Armed_Forces).[^cite_note-2]

#### Canada

See also: [Canadian Armed Forces ranks and insignia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Armed_Forces_ranks_and_insignia)

In the [Canadian Armed Forces](182792.md), the rank of major-general (MGen) ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *major-général*) is both a [Canadian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Army) and [Royal Canadian Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Canadian_Air_Force) rank equivalent to the [Royal Canadian Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Canadian_Navy)'s rank of [rear-admiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rear-admiral). A major-general is a [general officer](148537.md), the equivalent of a naval [flag officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_officer). The major-general rank is senior to the ranks of [brigadier general](206220.md) and [commodore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commodore_\(rank\)), and junior to [lieutenant-general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant-general_\(Canada\)) and [vice admiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_admiral). Prior to 1968, the [Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Canadian_Air_Force) used the rank of [air vice-marshal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_vice-marshal), instead.

The rank insignia for a major-general in the Royal Canadian Air Force is a wide braid under a single narrow braid on the cuff, as well as two silver maple leaves beneath a crossed sword and baton, all surmounted by St. Edward's Crown. In the Canadian Army, the rank insignia is a wide braid on the cuff, as well as two gold maple leaves beneath a crossed sword and baton, all surmounted by St. Edward's Crown. It is worn on the shoulder straps of the service dress tunic, and on slip-ons on other uniforms. On the visor of the service cap are two rows of gold oak leaves.

Major-generals are initially addressed as 'general' and name, as are all general officers; thereafter by subordinates as 'sir' or 'ma'am' as applicable in English ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *mon général*). Major-generals are normally entitled to [staff cars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staff_car).

#### Myanmar

In Myanmar, a Major General rank is usually held by someone that is a Regional Military Command General Officer Commanding (Regional Commander or တိုင်းမှူး) or a Director such as Director of Defence Service Intelligence ([Khin Nyunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khin_Nyunt) for example)

#### New Zealand

In the [New Zealand Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Army), major-general is the rank held by the chief of army (formerly the chief of general staff). The more senior rank of lieutenant-general is reserved for when an army officer holds the position of chief of defence force, who commands all of New Zealand's armed forces. This position is subject to rotation between the heads of the [New Zealand Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Air_Force), New Zealand Army, and [New Zealand Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Navy).

#### Pakistan

Major general in the [Pakistan Army](1965925.md) is equivalent to rear admiral in the [Pakistan Navy](1183789.md) and air vice marshal in the [Pakistan Air Force](377433.md). It is the lowest of the general officer ranks, ranking between brigadier and lieutenant general.

#### Portugal

The rank of major-general was reintroduced in the [Portuguese Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Army), [Portuguese Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Air_Force), and [Portuguese National Republican Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_National_Republican_Guard) in 1999, replacing the former rank of [brigadier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigadier) in the role of brigade commander. As a rank, it had previously been used in the Army only for a brief period (from 1862 to 1864). It is equivalent to the rank of *contra-almirante* (rear-admiral) in the [Portuguese Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_Navy). In 2015, the rank of major-general was moved up one level, with the role of brigade commander being assumed by the below rank of brigadier-general.

In most of the 19th and first half of the 20th century, major-general was not used as a rank in the Portuguese military, but as an appointment title conferred to the general officer that acted as the military head of a [service branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Service_branch). The roles of Major-General of the Navy (*Major-General da Armada*) and Major-General of the Army (*Major-General do Exército*) became extinct in 1950, with their roles being unified in the then created [Chief of the General Staff of the Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Staff_of_the_Armed_Forces_\(Portugal\)).

#### Russia

In the [Russian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Army), the rank 'major general' is known as [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): генера́л-майо́р, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Russian): *generál-mayór*. It is equivalent to a British [brigadier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigadier_\(United_Kingdom\)) or an American [brigadier general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigadier_general_\(United_States\)).[^cite_note-3]

#### Turkey

Main article: [tr\:Tümgeneral](https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%BCmgeneral)

The Turkish Army and Air Force refer to the rank as *tümgeneral*. The Turkish Navy equivalent is *tümamiral*. The name is derived from *[tümen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tumen_\(unit\))*, the Turkish word for a military division (*tümen* itself is an older Turkish word meaning 10,000). Thus, linguistically, it is similar to the French equivalent for a major general, [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *général de division*.

### *Generalmajor*

Main articles: [Generalmajor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalmajor) and [Generalmajor (Sweden)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalmajor_\(Sweden\))

***Generalmajor*** is the [Germanic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germanic_languages) variant of major general, used in a number of [Central](5188.md) and [Northern European](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Europe) countries, including [Austria](26964606.md), [Belgium](3343.md), [Denmark](76972.md), [Finland](10577.md), [Germany](11867.md), [Norway](21241.md), and [Sweden](5058739.md).
