# List of pastoral trips made by Pope Leo XIV



Pastoral visits of Pope Leo XIV

Since [his election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_conclave) on 8 May 2025, [Pope Leo XIV](54520372.md) has made six international trips outside Italy, during which he has visited ten countries. His visit to Turkey included an [ecumenical](146656.md) commemoration of the 1,700th anniversary of the [First Council of Nicaea](11118.md). His visit to Algeria was the first ever [papal visit](26660117.md) to the country.[^cite_note-1]

## International visits

### 2025

Main article: [Visit by Pope Leo XIV to Turkey and Lebanon](81282363.md)

- ** [Turkey](11125639.md) and  [Lebanon](17771.md) (27 November – 2 December)**

Pope Leo XIV arrived in Turkey on 27 November 2025. His visit included a prayer meeting in the Catholic Cathedral in Istanbul. He celebrated the 1,700th anniversary of the [First Council of Nicaea](11118.md) with the Ecumenical Patriarch of Constantinople [Bartholomew I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartholomew_I).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] The Pope then arrived in Lebanon on 30 November 2025, where he celebrated a public Mass in [Beirut](37428.md). He met with the President [Joseph Aoun](53467128.md) and other civil authorities, as well as leaders of the Maronite and other Eastern Catholic Churches. The Pope also held an interreligious meeting with Muslim representatives and addressed young people and members of civil society.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

### 2026

- ** [Monaco](19261.md) (28 March)**

  [^cite_note-\:0-6]

[Prince Albert II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_II,_Prince_of_Monaco) invited the Holy Father to visit the Principality during a private audience at the [Vatican](32408.md) on 17 January 2026. The visit was part of the ongoing, centuries-old ties that bind the [Grimaldi dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grimaldi_dynasty), which has reigned over Monaco since 1297, to the [Successors of Peter](23056.md). It also aimed to strengthen the “long-standing and trusting” diplomatic relations, according to the press office of the [Prince’s Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince's_Palace_of_Monaco), between the two smallest states in the world.[^cite_note-7]

Main article: [Visit by Pope Leo XIV to Algeria, Cameroon, Angola, and Equatorial Guinea](82960214.md)

- ** [Algeria](358.md),  [Cameroon](5447.md),  [Angola](701.md) and  [Equatorial Guinea](9366.md) (13–23 April)**

Pope Leo XIV was the first Pope to travel to Algeria. He visited [Annaba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annaba), the site of the ancient city of [Hippo Regius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hippo_Regius), where [Saint Augustine](2030.md) did his work and died. Augustine is the spiritual inspiration of the [Augustinian Order](144980.md), of which Pope Leo is a member.[^cite_note-\:0-6] The Pope focused on promoting [interfaith dialogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interfaith_dialogue) between [Christianity and Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christianity_and_Islam); he visited the [Great Mosque of Algiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djamaa_el_Djaza%C3%AFr) and held silent prayer. He also met with Algerian [president](380664.md) [Abdelmadjid Tebboune](54133968.md) and paid tribute to the [19 martyrs of Algeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/19_martyrs_of_Algeria) while visiting the [Augustinian Missionary Sisters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustinian_nuns) in [Bab El Oued](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bab_El_Oued).[^cite_note-8] He gave a homily at the [Basilica of Our Lady of Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basilica_of_Our_Lady_of_Africa) and offered prayers at a monument for [migrants who died in the Mediterranean Sea](63864017.md).[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

- ** [Spain](26667.md) (6–12 June)**

Visit to the capital, [Madrid](41188263.md) (June 6–9), a reception at the [Royal Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Palace_of_Madrid) by the [King of Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_of_Spain) [Felipe VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felipe_VI), an address to the Cortes (Parliament), and large gatherings, including a Mass attended by over a million of the faithful. In [Barcelona](4443.md) (June 9–10), a visit marked by a vigil at the Montjuïc Olympic Stadium and the inauguration of the Tower of Jesus Christ at the [Sagrada Família](59545.md), marking the centenary of [Antoni Gaudí](31566384.md)'s death. Finally, in the [Canary Islands](5717.md) (June 11–12): A stop dedicated to the issue of migration (particularly in [Gran Canaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gran_Canaria) and [Tenerife](269223.md)), including a meeting with migrants in [La Laguna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Crist%C3%B3bal_de_La_Laguna) and a closing Mass in [Santa Cruz de Tenerife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Cruz_de_Tenerife).**[^cite_note-\:0-6]**

- ** [San Marino](27248.md) (22 August)**

The visit coincided with the centenary of diplomatic relations between the Holy See and the small republic, home to about 34,000 inhabitants. It was also the third visit of a Pontiff to San Marino after those made by Saint John Paul II in 1982 and Benedict XVI in 2011.[^cite_note-11]

Pope Leo's journey started at the base of [Mount Titano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Titano), and arrived in San Marino in the early morning. He first met with civil authorities, including the [Captains Regent](569685.md) ([Alice Mina](82654433.md) and [Vladimiro Selva](82654543.md) during this visit), and local leaders at the [Palazzo Pubblico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palazzo_Pubblico_\(San_Marino\)), delivering an address praising San Marino's commitment to freedom, human dignity, and international peace efforts.[^cite_note-VNSanMarino1-12] He then met with the local clergy, youth, and relgiious community, giving an address that emphasized the Christian roots established by [Saints Marinus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Marinus) (San Marino's namesake) and [Leo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Leo_I) (the Pope's namesake). He also urged them to meet modern challenges through the Gospel.[^cite_note-VNSanMarino2-13] Pope Leo XIV later visited the [Basilica of San Marino](29447471.md) to complete his journey that day, and departed to the nearby Italian city of Rimini to continue his pastoral journey.[^cite_note-VNSanMarino1-12]

- ** [France](5843419.md)** **(25–28 September)**

He arrived in Paris on Friday, September 25, 2026, and proceeded to the [Élysée Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89lys%C3%A9e_Palace) for a courtesy visit with the President [Emmanuel Macron](43671127.md). He later gave a message to the authorities, civil society, and diplomatic corps; his message highlighted the responsibility to protect life.[^cite_note-14] According to reports, Macron initially offered a ceremonial welcome for Leo, which consisted a conferral of the [Legion of Honour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Honour), a state dinner, and a private tour of the Notre-Dame Cathedral, but Leo reportedly declined the offer.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17]

In the afternoon of September 25, he visited the [UNESCO Headquarters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNESCO_Headquarters), 46 years after the last visit made by [Pope John Paul II](23805.md); in his address to the UNESCO Member States, National Commissions, and members of the UNESCO family from across the world, he highlighted the resounding message of peace and human dignity, while also touching on the responsible use of [artificial intelligence](1164.md).[^cite_note-18] He presided over [Vespers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vespers) at [Notre-Dame de Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notre-Dame_de_Paris), the first visit by a Pope since the Cathedral [caught fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notre-Dame_fire) and its reopening in 2024[^cite_note-19], followed a prayer vigil with young people at [Stade de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stade_de_France). His visit to Paris concluded with Holy Mass at the [Place de la Concorde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Place_de_la_Concorde) on Saturday, September 26, with an estimated 800,000 people participating, according to the Vatican.[^cite_note-20]

The second part of the Pope's visit to Frances centered on [Lourdes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lourdes), where he prayed at the [Sanctuary of Our Lady of Lourdes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanctuary_of_Our_Lady_of_Lourdes) and participated in a candlelight procession. He also celebrated Mass at the *Praire* of the Shrine on Sunday, September 27[^cite_note-21], and met with the staff and sick community of *Accueil Notre Dame*. He also met with the [Bishops' Conference of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishops'_Conference_of_France) at Lourdes, where he encouraged the bishops to support the spiritual life of family and uphold the voice of the Church.[^cite_note-22] The final part of the apostolic journey took place on Monday, September 28 at [Metz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metz), where he held an interreligious meeting and a farewell Mass at [Metz Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metz_Cathedral), before departing back to Rome.[^cite_note-23]

## Visits within Italy

Pope Leo XIV has regularly visited churches within the city of [Rome](25458.md) and resumed the practice of retreat at [Castel Gandolfo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castel_Gandolfo).[^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30][^cite_note-31]

### 2025

- **[Assisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assisi)**: 20 November

  [^cite_note-32]

Pope Leo's first visit outside of Rome was to Santa Maria degli Angeli for a meeting with the Italian Bishops Conference.

### 2026

- **[Pompeii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pompeii) and [Naples](55880.md)**: 8 May

  [^cite_note-2026Italy-33]

Pope Leo visited the [Shrine of the Virgin of the Rosary of Pompei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrine_of_the_Virgin_of_the_Rosary_of_Pompei), the [Naples Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naples_Cathedral), and then met the faithful in [Piazza del Plebiscito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piazza_del_Plebiscito).

- **[Acerra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acerra)**: 23 May

  [^cite_note-2026Italy-33]

Pope Leo visited the [Acerra Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acerra_Cathedral) and met with local clergy and families of victims of the [Naples waste management crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naples_waste_management_crisis) in the [Triangle of death](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangle_of_death_\(Italy\)). He also held an event at the Piazza Nicola Calipari with civic leaders and the broader public.

[^cite_note-34]

- **[Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavia) and [Sant'Angelo Lodigiano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sant'Angelo_Lodigiano)**: 20 June

  [^cite_note-2026Italy-33]

- **[Lampedusa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lampedusa)**: 4 July

  [^cite_note-2026Italy-33]

- **[Assisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assisi)**: 6 August

  [^cite_note-2026Italy-33]

Pope Leo XIV visited with young people for transitus of Saint Francis.

- **[Rimini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rimini)**: 22 August

  [^cite_note-2026Italy-33]

Pope Leo attended the 47th Meeting for Friendship among Peoples at the Rimini Expo Centre, followed by tours of key cultural and educational exhibitions at the event. He later visited the *Villaggio Ragazzi* to meet local youth, families, and young volunteers.

[^cite_note-VNRimini1-35]

&#x20;In the Great Auditorium, Leo delivered an address on peace, fraternity, and overcoming division, emphasizing the importance of people over [borders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border).

[^cite_note-VNRimini1-35]

&#x20;Afterwards, he was transported to the [Rimini Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rimini_Cathedral) and met with approximately 650 people assisted by [Caritas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caritas_Italy), including disabled, sick, and individuals [living in poverty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_in_Italy).

[^cite_note-36]

&#x20;In the evening, he celebrated Mass at the Port of Rimini

[^cite_note-37]

&#x20;and returned to the Vatican.
