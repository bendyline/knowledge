# South Africa at the FIFA World Cup

For the women's team, see [South Africa at the FIFA Women's World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa_at_the_FIFA_Women's_World_Cup).



South Africa facing Uruguay at home soil in 2010

The South Africa men's national football has played at the [FIFA World Cup](11370.md) on four occasions, in [1998](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_FIFA_World_Cup), [2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_FIFA_World_Cup), as hosts in [2010](168079.md), and in [2026](36581929.md) in North America.

South Africa did not make it past the first round until 2026. The team's first attempt to qualify was for the [1994 FIFA World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_FIFA_World_Cup), where they finished second in their group behind [Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigeria_national_football_team). Their first appearance was in [1998](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_FIFA_World_Cup), six years after they had been readmitted to the global football family. The team finished third in the group stage in both [1998](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_FIFA_World_Cup) and [2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_FIFA_World_Cup) World Cups. The team failed to qualify for the [2006 FIFA World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup) after finishing third in their qualifying group. During the [2010 World Cup](168079.md) on [their home soil](17416221.md), they beat [France](250197.md) 2–1 and drew 1–1 to [Mexico](808402.md), but lost 0–3 to [Uruguay](679782.md). They lost out on progression to the round of 16, on goal difference, becoming the first World Cup host nation to fail to advance past the group stage,[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] a feat repeated by Qatar in 2022.

### South Africa vs Mexico

South Africa vs Mexico was the opening match of the World Cup, held on 11 June 2010.[^cite_note-Mirror-6] It was described as an "enthralling" and "pulsating" match. South Africa opened the scoring in the 55th minute after [Siphiwe Tshabalala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siphiwe_Tshabalala) scored off a pass through Mexico's defence by [Teko Modise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teko_Modise). Mexico's captain [Rafael Márquez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_M%C3%A1rquez) equalised following a [corner kick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corner_kick) in the 79th minute.[^cite_note-7] In the final minutes of the match, [Katlego Mphela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katlego_Mphela) almost scored a winning goal for South Africa, but his shot bounced off the post.[^cite_note-Mirror-6]

Tshabalala was named as the [man of the match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Man_of_the_match). South Africa's coach, [Carlos Alberto Parreira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Alberto_Parreira) called the result "fair", while Mexico's coach [Javier Aguirre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javier_Aguirre) stated "we could have won, we could have lost".[^cite_note-8]

### France vs South Africa

Two teams have met three times but recently in the [1998 FIFA World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_FIFA_World_Cup) won by [France](250197.md) 3–0.

[Bongani Khumalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bongani_Khumalo) scored the first goal to make it 1–0 in 25th minute [Yoann Gourcuff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoann_Gourcuff) was shown a red card after a serious foul, [Katlego Mphela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katlego_Mphela) made it 2–0 in half-time, South Africa made several chances in second half, [Hugo Lloris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugo_Lloris) saved Katlego Mphela's shot to make a third goal, [Bacary Sagna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bacary_Sagna) made a back pass to the French player [Florent Malouda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florent_Malouda) scored a goal to make it 2–1 from an assist by [Franck Ribéry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franck_Rib%C3%A9ry), South Africa made even more chances in second half, 2-1 was the final score of the match. France were eliminated from the World Cup with a single point in the Group stage.

Although South Africa became the first host nation to exit the World Cup group stage in history.
