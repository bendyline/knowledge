**Marigot** (French pronunciation: [\[maʁiɡo\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/4/4e/LL-Q150\_%28fra%29-Lepticed7-marigot.wav/LL-Q150\_%28fra%29-Lepticed7-marigot.wav.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/4/4e/LL-Q150_%28fra%29-Lepticed7-marigot.wav/LL-Q150_%28fra%29-Lepticed7-marigot.wav.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>; [Haitian Creole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haitian_Creole_language): *Marigo*) is a [commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_communes_of_Haiti) in the [Jacmel Arrondissement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacmel_Arrondissement), in the [Sud-Est](492832.md) [department](10937444.md) of [Haiti](13373.md). It has 50,739 inhabitants.

In early 1793, during the [Haitian Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haitian_Revolution), Marigot's black inhabitants rebelled and built a fort in the city.[^cite_note-2] Abbé Aubert, a white priest and leader of the rebellion in the area, also commandeered Marigot's cannons and contributed them to [Romaine-la-Prophétesse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romaine-la-Proph%C3%A9tesse)'s slave uprising around and siege of [Jacmel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacmel).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTERey2017129–130-3]

The Haitian writer [Émile Célestin-Mégie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89mile_C%C3%A9lestin-M%C3%A9gie?action=edit\&redlink=1) was born in Marigot.[^cite_note-4]
