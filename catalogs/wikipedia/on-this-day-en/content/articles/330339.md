**Ateneo de Manila University** ([Filipino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filipino_language): *Pamantasang Ateneo de Manila*; [Spanish](26825.md): *Universidad Ateneo de Manila*), commonly referred to as the **Ateneo de Manila** or the **Ateneo**, is a [private](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_university) [Catholic](606848.md) research university in [Quezon City](284223.md), [Philippines](23440.md). Established in 1859 by the [Society of Jesus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Jesus) (Jesuits), it is one of the oldest Jesuit-administered institutions of higher education in the [Asia-Pacific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia-Pacific) region.

The university offers [primary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primary_education) and [secondary education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_education), as well as [undergraduate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undergraduate_education) and [graduate programs](153981.md) in the [humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humanities), [social sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_science), [natural sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_science), [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering), and [business](39206.md). It offers professional degrees through the Graduate School of Business, the School of Government, the School of Medicine and Public Health, and the Ateneo Law School. The university follows the Jesuit tradition of liberal arts education, with an emphasis on the humanities across all levels of education.

## History

Main article: [History of Ateneo de Manila University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Ateneo_de_Manila_University)

In 1581, the first Spanish [Jesuits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesuits) arrived in the Philippines. They established Colegio de San José (College of St. Joseph), a [seminary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seminary), in 1605. In 1768, King [Charles III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_III_of_Spain) decreed the [expulsion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suppression_of_the_Society_of_Jesus) of the Jesuit order from [Spain and its colonies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Empire). Following their banishment, control of the college was transferred to the [Dominicans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominican_Order).

The Jesuits returned to the Philippines in the 19th century after their suppression, and were tasked with managing a small public school in [Manila](184334.md) called the Escuela Municipal in 1859, renaming it Ateneo Municipal de Manila in 1865 to reflect its elevation to a secondary education institution.[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]

Initially situated in [Intramuros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intramuros), the school, which forcibly became privatized in 1902 at the start of the American colonization, was renamed into Ateneo de Manila to reflect the transition. Ateneo moved to [Padre Faura Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padre_Faura_Street), [Ermita, Manila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ermita,_Manila), after the original campus was destroyed by fire in 1932. However, the new campus was destroyed during the [Battle of Manila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Manila_\(1945\)) by [Japanese forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Japanese_Army) in 1945. After [World War II](32927.md), in 1952, the university relocated to its present campus in [Loyola Heights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loyola_Heights), [Quezon City](284223.md). The law and graduate business schools remained at the Padre Faura campus until 1977, when both moved to [Makati City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makati_City), and the Ermita site was sold (now [Robinsons Manila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robinsons_Manila)).[^cite_note-9]

Fr. Pacífico Ortíz, [SJ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesuits) became the first Filipino president of the university in 1959 and again for his second term on September 25, 1969.[^cite_note-guidon1-10] During his tenure, the Graduate School was divided into the Graduate School of Arts and Sciences and what would later become the Graduate School of Business. In July 1964, the Educational TV Center of Ateneo was organized and began operations, becoming one of the first educational television channels in the country.[^cite_note-11] In 1968, the Ateneo co-founded the [Asian Institute of Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Institute_of_Management).[^cite_note-AIM-12] Women were first admitted to the college in 1973.[^cite_note-13] By 1976, the Graduate School of Arts and Sciences had moved to Loyola Heights, and the Padre Faura campus was finally closed in 1977.[^cite_note-14]

In the 1970s, Ateneo students became active in protests against the [Marcos dictatorship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martial_law_under_Ferdinand_Marcos) and its imposition of [martial law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proclamation_No._1081),[^cite_note-downfromthehill-15] and many later participated in the 1986 [People Power Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_Power_Revolution).[^cite_note-16]

In 1991, the Ateneo Law School became the first in the country to offer the [Juris Doctor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juris_Doctor) degree.[^cite_note-17] The School of Government was established in 1996, and a new campus for the Graduate School of Business, Law School, and School of Government opened in [Rockwell Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockwell_Center) in 1998.[^cite_note-18] Significant developments in the 2000s include the establishment of the [Loyola Schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loyola_Schools) and completion of various educational and recreational facilities.[^cite_note-19]

The university continued to expand and innovate, launching the Pathways to Higher Education initiative in 2002,[^cite_note-20] and consecrating the [Church of the Gesù](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_the_Ges%C3%B9,_Quezon_City) at the Loyola Heights campus in July 2002.[^cite_note-21]

In 2003, the Ateneo formalized its partnership with [Gawad Kalinga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gawad_Kalinga) for university-wide social action.[^cite_note-22] The Manuel V. Pangilinan Center for Student Leadership opened in 2006, and the [Ateneo School of Medicine and Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ateneo_School_of_Medicine_and_Public_Health) began operations in 2007.[^cite_note-23]

In recent years, significant events include [a shooting incident in July 2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Ateneo_de_Manila_University_shooting) that led to the cancellation of the law school's graduation ceremonies,[^cite_note-24] and the consolidation of the university's higher education units in October 2022.[^cite_note-higher_ed-25]

## Campuses

The Ateneo de Manila's main campus is located in [Loyola Heights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loyola_Heights), [Quezon City](284223.md), covering 205 acres (83 ha).[^cite_note-campus_size-26] It houses the university's primary, secondary, and tertiary education units, along with various research centers and institutes.[^cite_note-27]

### Loyola Heights



Aerial view of the Loyola Heights campus, looking northeast.

The Loyola Heights campus, adjacent to [Miriam College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miriam_College), includes the grade school, high school, and college. It features facilities such as the Henry Lee Irwin Theater, the Science Education Complex, and the John Gokongwei School of Management Complex.[^cite_note-2007b-28] In 2018, the university inaugurated the Areté, a creative hub that includes the [Ateneo Art Gallery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ateneo_Art_Gallery), theaters, and collaborative spaces.[^cite_note-29]

The Rizal Library and several research centers are located here, including the Institute of Philippine Culture and the [Manila Observatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manila_Observatory). Athletic facilities include the [Blue Eagle Gym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Eagle_Gym) and the Moro Lorenzo Sports Center.

The campus also hosts the [Church of the Gesù](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_the_Ges%C3%B9,_Quezon_City) and multiple chapels for various departments.&#x20;

Within the university is home to the largest Jesuit community in the Philippines. It hosts facilities such as the Arrupe International Residence, which houses international Jesuits and guests pursuing studies in the Philippines, San Jose Major Seminary, the Loyola School of Theology, and the Loyola House of Studies.[^cite_note-2007b-28]

### Satellite campuses

The Rockwell Center in [Makati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makati) houses the School of Law and the Graduate School of Business, among other Professional School units.[^cite_note-2007b-28] The Salcedo Village campus contains various facilities, including the Ateneo Center for Continuing Education. The [Ateneo School of Medicine and Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ateneo_School_of_Medicine_and_Public_Health) is located at the Don Eugenio López Sr. Medical Complex in [Ortigas Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ortigas_Center), [Pasig](359702.md), alongside its partner hospital, [The Medical City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Medical_City).[^cite_note-2007b-28]

### Ateneo Blue Cloud

During the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_the_Philippines), the Ateneo launched Ateneo Blue Cloud, a [virtual campus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtual_campus) providing [online](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E-learning), [blended](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blended_learning), and face-to-face learning materials.[^cite_note-30]

## Administration and organization

See also: [List of Ateneo de Manila University faculty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Ateneo_de_Manila_University_faculty)

The university is overseen by a [board of trustees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_directors), chaired by Bernadine T. Siy.[^cite_note-31] The current university president, priest and economist [Roberto Yap SJ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_Yap), leads the central administration.[^cite_note-awebadmin-32] The university's present sectoral structure includes vice-presidents for social development, university and global relations, finance, and administration and human resources.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36]

The Ateneo de Manila has been granted autonomous status by the [Philippine Commission on Higher Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commission_on_Higher_Education_\(Philippines\)) (CHED) since 2001, granting it flexibility in introducing new programs. It is one of a select few universities in the country to receive this status.[^cite_note-37]

The university is organized into twelve schools, divided between the higher education and [basic education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_education) units. Each unit is managed by a vice-president.[^cite_note-38]

### Higher education

The Ateneo's higher education unit comprises nine schools: the School of Humanities, the [John Gokongwei School of Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Gokongwei_School_of_Management), the School of Science and Engineering, the School of Social Sciences, the Gokongwei Brothers School of Education and Learning Design,[^cite_note-39] the Graduate School of Business, the School of Law, the School of Medicine and Public Health, and the School of Government.[^cite_note-higher_ed-25]

### Basic education



The Chapel of the Holy Guardian Angels and a statue of [St. Ignatius of Loyola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ignatius_of_Loyola) at the Ateneo de Manila Grade School campus



The Chapel of [St. Stanislaus Kostka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanislaus_Kostka), and classrooms lining the Promenade of Our Lady at the Ateneo de Manila Junior High School campus

The Ateneo de Manila Grade School (AGS) offers [elementary education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_education) and was the university's first unit. The school transitioned to [co-education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Co-education) in the 2024–25 academic year.[^cite_note-40] The AGS was one of the first to adopt the [Singapore math](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_math) curriculum in the Philippines.[^cite_note-41]

The Ateneo de Manila Junior High School (AJHS) provides [middle school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_school) education and was founded in 2013 to comply with the [Department of Education](5464548.md) [K-12 system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%E2%80%9312).[^cite_note-\:0-42]

In August 2024, the Ateneo began its transition for the Grade School and the Junior High School to become fully co-educational, and welcomed female students of [grade 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_grade) and [grade 7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seventh_grade) for the school year 2024–2025. It announced that by the school year 2029–2030, basic education from [kindergarten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kindergarten) to [grade 12](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelfth_grade) will be fully co-educational.[^cite_note-43]

The Ateneo de Manila Senior High School (ASHS) has offered the [eleventh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eleventh_grade) and [twelfth grades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelfth_grade) since 2013 and became co-educational in 2016.[^cite_note-44]

### Loyola School of Theology

The Loyola School of Theology, affiliated with the university, offers baccalaureate, licentiate, and doctoral degrees in [theology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theology) and pastoral ministry.[^cite_note-45]

The Ateneo de Manila University provides education at the primary, secondary, undergraduate, and graduate levels. The university offers 48 undergraduate, 93 graduate, and 11 professional degree programs. [Dual and joint degrees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_degree) are also available, including partnerships with international universities.[^cite_note-46][^cite_note-47]

The primary language of instruction is English, with some classes offered in [Filipino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filipino_language).[^cite_note-48]

The Ateneo de Manila University emphasizes a [liberal arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_arts_education) undergraduate core curriculum that includes philosophy, literature, theology, history, and the social sciences. This curriculum is designed to foster [student-centred learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Student-centred_learning) and community engagement.[^cite_note-bullinfo-49][^cite_note-sclprimer-50]

The Ateneo College Entrance Test (ACET) is required for an admission offer to the Ateneo College (Loyola Schools), considered anecdotally as the most difficult entrance examination in the Philippines aside from the University of the Philippines' College Admissions Test (UPCAT). Furthermore, admission to the university requires strong extracurriculars, recommendations, an essay, and a consistent academic performance from middle school to high school.[^cite_note-\:1-54]

Acceptance through the Scholastic Aptitude Test (SAT) is also welcomed with a minimum SAT score of 1200, taken within the last 2 years before the application.[^cite_note-\:1-54]

The Ateneo de Manila University is ranked as a top institution in the Philippines by the *[Times Higher Education World University Rankings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Times_Higher_Education_World_University_Rankings)* and second nationally, and first-ranked private university nationally in the *[QS World University Rankings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QS_World_University_Rankings)*. It was first among private universities and second overall in the Philippines in both QS's World and Asia rankings.[^cite_note-qs_rank-53][^cite_note-the_rank-52]

As of 2026, the Ateneo is ranked third in the Philippines by the *[Webometrics Ranking of World Universities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Webometrics_Ranking_of_World_Universities)* and third by *EduRank*.[^cite_note-55][^cite_note-56]

The university has 11 Centers of Excellence (CoE) and 6 Centers of Development (CoD) as recognized by CHED.[^cite_note-57]

The Ateneo's academic programs are accredited at Level IV by CHED and the [Philippine Accrediting Association of Schools, Colleges and Universities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Accrediting_Association_of_Schools,_Colleges_and_Universities) (PAASCU).[^cite_note-58]

Ateneo's John Gokongwei School of Management and the Ateneo Graduate School of Business are both members of the [Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_to_Advance_Collegiate_Schools_of_Business), a step towards accreditation. The university is also a frequent publisher in [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus) database, a member of the [ASEAN University Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ASEAN_University_Network), [Association of Christian Universities and Colleges in Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_Christian_Universities_and_Colleges_in_Asia), [International Association of Jesuit Universities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Association_of_Jesuit_Universities), [Association of Jesuit Colleges and Universities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_Jesuit_Colleges_and_Universities), Jesuit Basic Education Commission, and a participant of the [Erasmus Programme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erasmus_Programme) of the [European Union](9317.md).&#x20;

In addition, the university has linkages in multiple and elite universities in Asia, Europe, Oceania, North and South Americas. Ateneo is anecdotally also a feeder for top Filipino students for [Ivy League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivy_League) postgraduate programs, [M7 business schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M7_business_schools), and other highly ranked foreign universities.&#x20;

#### Graduate school rankings

The [Legal Education Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legal_education_in_the_Philippines#Legal_Education_Board) has ranked the Ateneo de Manila School of Law as the top law school in the past decade, producing a 7.18% share or 1,794 out of total new lawyers.[^cite_note-59] The school continues to consistently have the highest passing rate for first-time examinees, topping the 2020/2021 and 2022 [Philippine Bar Examinations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Bar_Examinations).[^cite_note-60] Graduates of the Law School have an average grade of 89.2 in the Bar Examinations and the school has produced 23 top notchers in history, the second highest number of bar top notchers.[^cite_note-61]

The [Professional Regulation Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_Regulation_Commission) has ranked the School of Medicine and Public Health as the best-performing [medical school in the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_education_in_the_Philippines) in 2019, as the school had a 100% passing rate in the Philippine Physician Licensure Examination out of 133 examinees.[^cite_note-62]

The School of Government has been ranked as one of the top [public policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_policy) schools and is the only Philippine institution listed in the first-ever ranking of public-policy schools in the [Asia-Pacific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia%E2%80%93Pacific) region. The school was ranked 25th by citation impact factor, with a total of 7 citations scoring 1.17 impact factor.[^cite_note-63]

## Social initiatives



The Ateneo campus in 2022

The Ateneo de Manila University integrates social engagement into its curriculum at all levels. Key initiatives include the Ateneo-[Mangyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangyan) Project for Understanding and Development, the *Bigay Puso* program, and housebuilding activities with [Gawad Kalinga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gawad_Kalinga). These projects aim to promote social responsibility and leadership among students.[^cite_note-pr2005-69][^cite_note-aweboutreach-70]

The university's main social action program is its partnership with Gawad Kalinga, focusing on community-building and rehabilitation efforts in various regions.[^cite_note-pr2005-69][^cite_note-aweboutreach-70]

The Ugat Foundation, which supports grassroots families, is also hosted by Ateneo de Manila University.[^cite_note-71]

### International collaboration

The Ateneo de Manila University collaborates with various global institutions, facilitating exchange programs for students and faculty. It partners with [Sun Yat-sen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_Yat-sen_University) in running the [Confucius Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confucius_Institute), which offers [Mandarin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandarin_Chinese_in_the_Philippines) language courses and a master's program in Teaching Chinese as a Foreign Language.[^cite_note-72]

The Ateneo also has joint masters programs with [Queen Mary University of London](422461.md) and partnerships with [Le Cordon Bleu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Cordon_Bleu), [École supérieure des sciences commerciales d'Angers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89cole_sup%C3%A9rieure_des_sciences_commerciales_d'Angers), [Kyushu University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyushu_University), [Regis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regis_University), and the [University of Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sydney).[^cite_note-73][^cite_note-74][^cite_note-75][^cite_note-76][^cite_note-77][^cite_note-78]

The university's Asian Peacebuilders Scholarship, in collaboration with the [Nippon Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nippon_Foundation) and the [University for Peace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_for_Peace), trains students in [peacebuilding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peacebuilding), awarding degrees from both Ateneo and UPEACE.[^cite_note-79][^cite_note-80]

## Libraries and museum



The New Rizal Library of Ateneo de Manila University, opened in 2009



The [American Historical Collection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Historical_Collection) at the Old Rizal Library

The Ateneo de Manila University's largest library is the Rizal Library, which is split into the Old and New Rizal Libraries. The New Rizal Library, inaugurated in November 2009, contains the circulation section, technical services, and modern study areas.[^cite_note-newlibopen-81]

### Ateneo Art Gallery

Main article: [Ateneo Art Gallery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ateneo_Art_Gallery)

The Ateneo Art Gallery, established in 1960, is the first museum of [modern art](163543.md) in the Philippines. It is housed in the Arts Wing of the Areté and features a collection of modern and contemporary [Filipino art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arts_in_the_Philippines).[^cite_note-82]

### University Archives

The University Archives, located in Faber Hall, preserve the historical records and documents of Ateneo de Manila University. They support research on the university's history and administrative functions.[^cite_note-83]

## Student life

### Housing

The Ateneo de Manila University offers on-campus housing through several residence halls: Cervini Hall, Eliazo Hall, the University Dormitory–North Tower, the University Dormitory–South Tower, and the International Residence Hall. These facilities provide accommodations for both undergraduate and graduate students.

### Student organizations

The Ateneo Debate Society ranks among the top debate teams in Asia, having secured a top 15 global ranking and winning the 2023 [World Universities Debating Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Universities_Debating_Championship).[^cite_note-84][^cite_note-85]

The [Ateneo College Glee Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ateneo_College_Glee_Club) (ACGC) is the oldest university chorale in the Philippines and has achieved international recognition for its performances.[^cite_note-86]

*Dulaang Sibol*, founded in 1955, is noted as the oldest high school theater group in the Philippines.

*[Tanghalang Ateneo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanghalang_Ateneo)* is the oldest undergraduate student theater group.

## In popular culture

The Ateneo de Manila has been associated in Philippine popular culture with perceptions of elitism due to its high tuition costs, historical reputation, and prominent alumni.[^cite_note-qs_rank-53][^cite_note-the_rank-52][^cite_note-87][^cite_note-88] The university has also been referenced in jokes and [internet memes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_meme) relating to traffic congestion along [Katipunan Avenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katipunan_Avenue).[^cite_note-89] Alongside [Lasallians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_La_Salle_University), Ateneans are commonly associated with using "[conyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conyo)", a form of code-switching between English and Filipino linked to upper-class urban youth culture in the Philippines.[^cite_note-90] Despite these perceptions, the university provides academic and financial aid scholarships for students from various socioeconomic backgrounds.[^cite_note-91]

## Notable alumni

Main article: [List of Ateneo de Manila University alumni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Ateneo_de_Manila_University_alumni)

[José Rizal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Rizal), who is widely regarded as the [National Hero of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Hero_of_the_Philippines), completed his [Bachelor of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) as a *sobresaliente* (among the top students) in 1877.[^cite_note-92]

The Ateneo de Manila University produced the second-highest number of Philippine presidents, only behind to the [University of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Philippines). Presidents [Benigno Aquino III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benigno_Aquino_III)—a graduate from A.B. Economics, Ateneo Grade School, and Ateneo High School; [Corazon Aquino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corazon_Aquino)—L.H.D. *honoris causa*; [Joseph Estrada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Estrada)—grade school and high school; [Gloria Macapagal Arroyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Macapagal_Arroyo)—M.A. Economics; and [Fidel V. Ramos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fidel_V._Ramos)—MBA, LL.D. *honoris causa* are among its alumni.&#x20;

The university has also produced several [National Artists of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Artist_of_the_Philippines), including painter [Federico Aguilar Alcuaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federico_Aguilar_Alcuaz)—LL.B.; director [Lamberto V. Avellana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamberto_V._Avellana)—A.B. *magna cum laude*; stage designer [Salvador Bernal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvador_Bernal_\(artist\))—A.B. Philosophy *cum laude*; author and former education secretary [Alejandro Roces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alejandro_Roces)—L.H.D. *honoris causa*; and architect [Ildefonso P. Santos Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ildefonso_P._Santos_Jr.)—high school.

Other alumni include [chief justices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Justice_of_the_Philippines) and [associate justices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_Justice_of_the_Supreme_Court_of_the_Philippines) of the [Supreme Court of the Philippines](1335534.md), [senators](299521.md), members of the [House of Representatives](293605.md), actors, artists, journalists, scientists, academics, lawyers, physicians, business executives, and local government officials.
