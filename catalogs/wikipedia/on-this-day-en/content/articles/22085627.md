**Babanusa** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): بابنوسة) is a town in [West Kordofan](767647.md), Sudan.

## History

In 1965, 72 [Dinka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinka) civilians were [massacred](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1965_Babanusa_massacre) in the town by an Arab mob during the [First Sudanese Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Sudanese_Civil_War).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

In January 2024, the [Siege of Babanusa](75934201.md) began. The intense battle lasted until February where the siege was lifted by the Sudanese army.[^cite_note-4] On 11 November 2025, the [RSF](43053874.md) launched a second attack on the city and claimed full control on 1 December.[^cite_note-5]

## Transport

It is a railway junction on the national railway network where the line to [Waw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wau,_Sudan) in [South Sudan](32350676.md) branches off to the south from the line westwards to [Nyala](508734.md).
