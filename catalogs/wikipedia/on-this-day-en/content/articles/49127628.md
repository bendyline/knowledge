The **Minister of Education**, formerly the **Minister of Human Resources Development** (1985–2020),[^cite_note-News18-1] is the head of the [Ministry of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education_\(India\)) and a member of the Union Cabinet and one of the portfolios of the [union cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_cabinet) in the [Government of India](553883.md).
