**I Am Maximus** (foaled 27 March 2016) is a French-bred and Irish-trained [Thoroughbred](18842022.md) [racehorse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racehorse) which competes in [National Hunt racing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Hunt_racing) and won the [Irish Grand National](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Grand_National) in 2023, and twice won the [Grand National](74730.md) in [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Grand_National) and [2026](82918023.md).

## Racing career

I Am Maximus began his career being trained by [Nicky Henderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicky_Henderson),[^cite_note-chelt-2][^cite_note-newbury-3] in the yard at [Seven Barrows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_Barrows), [Berkshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkshire).[^cite_note-7barrows-4]

He made his debut ridden by [Nico de Boinville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_de_Boinville) in a bumper at [Cheltenham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheltenham_Racecourse) on 24 October, 2020, winning from a field of 14 over 2 miles.[^cite_note-chelt-2] He won over hurdles for the first time on December 15, 2021 at [Newbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newbury_Racecourse), from a field of 13.[^cite_note-newbury-3]

In November 2022, I Am Maximus was moved to [Willie Mullins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willie_Mullins)’ stables based at Closutton, [Bagenalstown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagenalstown), [County Carlow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Carlow), in the [Ireland](14560.md).[^cite_note-gnat-5]

I am Maximus was entered for the 2023 [Brown Advisory Novices' Chase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broadway_Novices'_Chase) at [Cheltenham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheltenham_Racecourse) finishing 4th.[^cite_note-gnat-5] Following this effort, I Am Maximus was sold by owner Claudio Michael Grech on the 22nd March 2023, for an undisclosed fee to Irish businessman [J. P. McManus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._P._McManus).[^cite_note-gnat-5] Following this purchase, he won the [Irish Grand National](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Grand_National) at odds of 8/1.[^cite_note-6]

I Am Maximus entered the [2024 Grand National](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Grand_National) and was assigned a weight of 11st 6lb,[^cite_note-7] Starting as the 7/1 joint favourite he went on to win the race by 7 and a half lengths.[^cite_note-max-8]

For the [2025 Grand National](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Grand_National) I Am Maximus was assigned the heaviest weight of 11st 12lb,.[^cite_note-auto-9] and finished in second place. His starting price was 7/1 again making him slight second favourite to Iroko. However, 33/1 outsider [Nick Rocket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Rockett) won the race that year.

I Am Maximus won the [2026 Grand National](82918023.md) carrying the top weight of 11st 12lb, this time starting a heavy 9/2 favourite after being backed heavily before the race.[^cite_note-win-10]

After his second Grand National win on 11 April 2026, his record was 7 wins, 5 seconds, 2 thirds, from 22 races.[^cite_note-form-1] His career earnings totalled £1,602,703 [^cite_note-form-1]
