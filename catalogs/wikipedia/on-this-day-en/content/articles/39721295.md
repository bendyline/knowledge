**Nasry Juan Asfura&#x20;**(Latin American Spanish pronunciation: [\[ˈnasɾi ˈxwan asˈfuɾa saˈbla\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish); born 8 June 1958) is a Honduran politician and construction businessman serving as the [president of Honduras](23420892.md) since 2026. A member of the [National Party of Honduras](1641714.md) (PNH), he previously served as the mayor of [Tegucigalpa](25994100.md) from 2014 to 2022.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

In October 2020, an anti-corruption unit of the [Public Prosecutor's Office](50802294.md) requested criminal prosecution against Asfura, alleging that he was responsible for the embezzlement of 17.4 million [lempiras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lempiras) for his own benefit between 2017 and 2018, as well as for other crimes. In June and July of the following year, the request was dismissed by the Criminal Chamber of the Supreme Court of Justice.[^cite_note-3] In October 2021, his name appeared in the [Pandora Papers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pandora_Papers).[^cite_note-4]

A construction industry magnate, he previously served as a deputy in the [National Congress of Honduras](1604127.md), representing the [Francisco Morazán department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francisco_Moraz%C3%A1n_department) from 2010 to 2014. Asfura ran in the [2021 presidential election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Honduran_general_election), losing to [Xiomara Castro](6016595.md) of the [Liberty and Refoundation](36492435.md) party. After becoming the PNH's party president, he ran and won the [2025 presidential election](77491476.md), narrowly defeating [Salvador Nasralla](41103646.md) of the [Liberal Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_Party_of_Honduras).[^cite_note-5] He was sworn in on 27 January 2026.

## Early life and career

Nasry Juan Asfura Zablah was born on 8 June 1958 in [Tegucigalpa](25994100.md), Honduras, the son of Nasry Juan Asfura and Gloria Zablah de Asfura.[^cite_note-reuters1-6][^cite_note-doce-7] His mother Gloria died in 2019, while his father Nasry died before 2015.[^cite_note-doce-7][^cite_note-8] He is the grandson of [Christian Palestinian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Palestinian) immigrants from [Bethlehem](4312.md), with his paternal grandparents arriving in Honduras in the late 1890s and his maternal grandparents in 1918.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] His family nicknamed him affectionately "Tito" from "Nasryto".[^cite_note-nicolle-12]

Asfura graduated from high school at the San Francisco Institute and studied [civil engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_engineering) at the [National Autonomous University of Honduras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Autonomous_University_of_Honduras), but quit his course to instead pursue a career in the construction industry.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-paishn-14]

## Political career (1990s–2026)



Asfura with Taiwanese president [Tsai Ing-wen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsai_Ing-wen), in January 2017

Asfura first became involved in politics and entered into public life in the 1990s. Between 1990 and 1994, served as a municipal tax assistant in the capital, and as an assistant to Mayor [Nora Gúnera de Melgar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nora_G%C3%BAnera_de_Melgar).[^cite_note-15] He was also a member of the administration of the mayors [César Castellanos Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%A9sar_Castellanos_Madrid) and [Vilma Reyes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilma_Reyes_Escalante).[^cite_note-doce-7][^cite_note-nicolle-12]

In 2005 Asfura participated in the PNH's internal elections as a pre-candidate for mayor of the Central District, but was defeated by [Ricardo Álvarez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricardo_%C3%81lvarez_Arias).[^cite_note-16] During those elections, he ended an impromptu radio announcement with the phrase that slipped out of his mouth, "Papi a la orden!" (Daddy at your service!), which became his nickname.[^cite_note-doce-7][^cite_note-nicolle-12][^cite_note-17] Álvarez became mayor of the capital in the [general elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_Honduran_general_election) that year, and Asfura became a councilman in his administration.[^cite_note-doce-7]

He ran in the [2009 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Honduran_general_election) and was elected as a member of the country's National Congress, representing the department of [Francisco Morazán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francisco_Moraz%C3%A1n_Department). He served in that position from 2010 to 2014, when he left to serve as mayor of Honduras' capital [Tegucigalpa](25994100.md). During his tenure as mayor, Asfura became known for his road infrastructure projects.[^cite_note-18] He would continue in the position until 2022. In May 2025, Asfura became the National Party's president, succeeding David Chávez.

### 2021 presidential campaign

Main article: [2021 Honduran general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Honduran_general_election)

Asfura was chosen as the 2021 presidential candidate for the incumbent National Party.[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20] He defeated [Mauricio Oliva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mauricio_Oliva) in the party's primary election. At the beginning of the campaign, polls indicated a tight race between Asfura and his left-wing opponent, [Xiomara Castro](6016595.md), the leader of [LIBRE](36492435.md) and the wife of the former president [Manuel Zelaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_Zelaya), but she went on to win the election by a comfortable margin.[^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22]

Asfura prioritized upgrades to infrastructure and job creation. In addition, Asfura pledged support towards the business, health, education, and manufacturing sectors.[^cite_note-23]

### 2025 presidential campaign

Main article: [2025 Honduran general election](77491476.md)

Asfura was once again nominated by the National Party to be its candidate in the [2025 presidential election](77491476.md). He defeated [Ana García Carías](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana_Garc%C3%ADa_Car%C3%ADas) in the party's primary election. He was endorsed by [United States](3434750.md) [President Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_Donald_Trump) and [Argentine](18951905.md) [President Javier Milei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_Javier_Milei),[^cite_note-\:0-24][^cite_note-25] with Trump's administration pledging to reduce its foreign aid to Honduras if Asfura was not elected the country's president.[^cite_note-26] During the campaign, Asfura spent time in [Washington, D.C.](108956.md) to demonstrate his alignment with the Trump administration's foreign policy.[^cite_note-\:0-24] He also pledged to bring "development and opportunities for everyone", to "facilitate foreign and domestic investment into the country", and "generate employment for all".[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28] He pledged to restore Honduras's ties with Taiwan, who were broken in 2023 in order to establish ties with [mainland China](5405.md).[^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30]

Asfura won the election, obtaining 40.3% of the vote. The election results were marred by delays, with opposition candidates [Salvador Nasralla](41103646.md) and [Rixi Moncada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rixi_Moncada) criticizing the vote counting process.[^cite_note-31] He became the most voted candidate in the history of the National Party.[^cite_note-nicolle-12] Under his leadership, the PNH won a plurality in the country's National Congress, obtaining 49 seats and 34.51% of the vote.

[María Antonieta Mejía](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mar%C3%ADa_Antonieta_Mej%C3%ADa), one of Asfura's elected vice presidents, reported on 26 December, just a month before his inauguration, that Asfura will assume the presidency in a ceremony in the National Congress, unlike the traditional inauguration at the [National Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chelato_Ucl%C3%A9s_National_Stadium), stating that he wants a "symbolic and unostentatious" event, to refrain from a ceremony "that will have a large budget".[^cite_note-32]

On 21 January 2026, Asfura and his three vice presidents received the credentials for their elected positions from the [National Electoral Council](41170763.md).[^cite_note-33]

He was sworn in on 27 January 2026 alongside [Minister of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_branch_of_the_government_of_Honduras) in the National Congress.[^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36] Asfura is the oldest president to take office.[^cite_note-nicolle-12] No foreign dignitaries were invited to the ceremony, and outgoing President Xiomara Castro did not attend.[^cite_note-37]

In June 2026, Asfura's approval rating is 31%.[^cite_note-38]

### Foreign policy

See also: [List of international presidential trips made by Nasry Asfura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_international_presidential_trips_made_by_Nasry_Asfura)

#### Taiwan and People's Republic of China

During the 2025 presidential campaign, Asfura pledged to restore diplomatic relations between Honduras and [Taiwan](25734.md), which had been severed in 2023 by the government of his predecessor [Xiomara Castro](6016595.md) in favor of establishing relations with the [People's Republic of China](5405.md). Asfura said that Honduras had been "100 times better off" when it maintained diplomatic relations with Taiwan and proposed strengthening relations with Taiwan and the [United States](3434750.md).[^cite_note-39]

Following his inauguration in January 2026, Asfura ordered a review of the agreements signed between Honduras and China under the Castro administration. His government indicated that it was evaluating the country's relationship with Beijing and the possibility of restoring diplomatic relations with Taiwan.[^cite_note-40] In July 2026, Taiwan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs stated that Honduras' relations with Taiwan were an internal matter for Honduras, after China criticized exchanges between Honduras and Taiwan. At that time, Honduras had not formally restored diplomatic relations with Taiwan.[^cite_note-41]

Asfura has also sought to maintain economic relations with China while reconsidering diplomatic recognition. During the campaign, he stated that Honduras could maintain commercial exchanges with China while restoring diplomatic relations with Taiwan.[^cite_note-42]

#### Ukraine

Relations between Honduras and [Ukraine](31750.md) strengthened under Asfura. On 20 April 2026, Ukrainian President [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](59210523.md) held the first leader-level conversation in the history of bilateral relations with Asfura. Zelenskyy said that Ukraine valued Honduras's support for Ukrainian sovereignty and territorial integrity. The two presidents discussed cooperation in agriculture, drone technology, energy decentralization, and digitalization, and agreed that their governments would work on agreements in these areas.[^cite_note-43]

On 19 June, Asfura made the first visit to Ukraine by a Honduran president since the establishment of diplomatic relations between the two countries. During his meeting with Zelenskyy in [Kyiv](585629.md), Asfura expressed support for Ukraine and its defense of democracy and freedom. The two governments discussed economic cooperation, agriculture, digitalization, and defense technologies, including drones. Honduras also expressed interest in Ukrainian agricultural and technological expertise.[^cite_note-44]

#### Israel

Asfura has sought to strengthen relations between Honduras and [Israel](9282173.md) that was previously broken down under Xiomara Castro. On 18 January 2026, shortly before his inauguration, Asfura met Israeli President [Isaac Herzog](4645588.md) in [Jerusalem](16043.md). Herzog thanked Asfura for his commitment to deepening relations between Israel and Honduras. The two discussed strengthening trade and cooperation in agriculture, technology, and tourism.[^cite_note-45]

Asfura has also identified Israel, alongside the [United States](3434750.md) and [Taiwan](25734.md), as a country with which Honduras should maintain close relations. During his presidential campaign, he proposed strengthening cooperation with the three countries as part of his foreign-policy orientation.[^cite_note-46]

#### United States



Asfura, Trump, Salvadoran President [Bukele](46452145.md), Chilean President [Kast](55052190.md), and other leaders at the [Shield of the Americas](82587150.md) summit in Florida, March 2026

Relations with the [United States](3434750.md) have been a major component of Asfura's foreign policy. During the 2025 presidential election, U.S. President [Donald Trump](4848272.md) endorsed Asfura, and Asfura campaigned on strengthening relations with the United States. After winning the election, Asfura traveled to [Washington, D.C.](108956.md) in January 2026 and met with senior U.S. officials, including Secretary of State [Marco Rubio](5502549.md), as well as officials from the [Inter-American Development Bank](382592.md).[^cite_note-47]

On 8 February 2026, Trump met with Asfura at [Mar-a-Lago](1452754.md) in [Palm Beach, Florida](109617.md). Trump highlighted the security partnership between the United States and Honduras, including cooperation against drug trafficking and criminal organizations. The two leaders also discussed migration and the return of Honduran nationals from the United States.[^cite_note-48]

## Controversies

In 2020, Asfura was indicted by the Honduran authorities on charges of embezzling public funds, [money laundering](19390.md), fraud, and abuse of authority. He and co-defendant, Nilvia Ethel Castillo Cruz, were accused of misappropriating more than 28 million [lempiras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lempiras) for their personal benefit.[^cite_note-49][^cite_note-50][^cite_note-51] In 2021, the judiciary seized nine real estate properties and three businesses belonging to Asfura, Cruz, and associates.[^cite_note-52] Six of these properties (valued at a total 28.5 million lempiras) were ordered to be returned to their owners following a ruling against the government department Unidad Fiscal Especializada Contra Redes de Corrupción (UFERCO) on 25 September 2023, by the Juzgado de Letras de Privación de Dominio de Bienes de Origen Ilícitoin Asfura. UFERCO appealed the charges, but was unsuccessful, and the assets were returned.[^cite_note-53] The case continued until 15 December 2025, when the [Supreme Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_Honduras) fully annulled all charges against Asfura and Cruz.[^cite_note-54]

In early October 2021, while the aforementioned legal case was ongoing, Asfura was named in the [Pandora Papers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pandora_Papers).[^cite_note-Pandora_Papers-55]

## Personal life

Asfura married [Lissette del Cid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lissette_del_Cid) in 1985 and has three daughters.[^cite_note-56][^cite_note-57]
