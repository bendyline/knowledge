# List of international prime ministerial trips made by Andy Burnham



Prime Minister [Andy Burnham](414818.md) arrives in [Ukraine](31750.md), August 2026

[Andy Burnham](414818.md) has served as [Prime Minister](24150.md) of the [United Kingdom](31717.md) since 20 July 2026. As of 22 September 2026, Burnham has made two international trips to two countries as prime minister.

## 2026

| # | Country/Territory | Location | Date | Details | Image |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | [Ukraine](31750.md) | [Kyiv](585629.md) | 24 August | Burnham visited Ukraine for his inaugural trip as the prime minister of the United Kingdom.[^cite_note-1] Burnham co-chaired his first meeting of the [coalition of the willing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coalition_of_the_willing_\(Russo-Ukrainian_war\)) alongside German Chancellor [Friedrich Merz](1967626.md) and, virtually, French President [Emmanuel Macron](43671127.md).[^cite_note-2] During the trip, Burnham announced his decision to allow defence company [MBDA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MBDA) to declassify information on UK components for the long-range Franco–British [Storm Shadow](306908.md) missile.[^cite_note-3] Burnham and Ukrainian President [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](59210523.md) signed an AI partnership agreement, making the United Kingdom the first country to be granted access to Ukrainian battlefield data used to train AI models to identify and strike Russian targets.[^cite_note-4] |  |
| **2** | [United States](3434750.md) | [New York City](645042.md) | 22 September | Burnham attended the [81st United Nations General Assembly](83962647.md).[^cite_note-5] Burnham met with Spanish Prime Minister [Pedro Sanchez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Sanchez) at [Central Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Park) for a walk and a private meeting; Burnham later held his first in-person meeting with US President [Donald Trump](4848272.md).[^cite_note-6] |  |
