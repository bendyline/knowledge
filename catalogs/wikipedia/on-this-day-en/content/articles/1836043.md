The **Saint Lucia Labour Party** (**SLP**) is a [social democratic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_democratic) [political party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_party) in [Saint Lucia](27208.md). It currently holds 14 out of 17 seats in the [House of Assembly](4388564.md).

## History

The party was established in 1949, backed by the Saint Lucia Workers Cooperative Union.[^cite_note-N1-4] In the [first elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1951_Saint_Lucian_general_election) held under universal suffrage in 1951, and led by party founder [George Charles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Charles), the party won five of the eight seats. It retained all five seats in the [1954 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1954_Saint_Lucian_general_election), and increased their majority to seven of the eight seats in [1957](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1957_Saint_Lucian_general_election) and nine of the ten seats in [1961](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1961_Saint_Lucian_general_election).

In [1964](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1964_Saint_Lucian_general_election), the party lost an election for the first time, with the [United Workers Party](1836046.md), born out of a schism from within the Labour Party led by [John Compton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Compton) and eventual merger of the breakaway faction with the [People's Progressive Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Progressive_Party_\(Saint_Lucia\)), winning six of the ten seats, with the Labour Party reduced to two. It gained a seat in the [1969 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1969_Saint_Lucian_general_election), and increased their representation to seven seats in [1974](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1974_Saint_Lucian_general_election), although the UWP remained in power as the total number of seats rose to 17.

The Labour Party returned to power after winning the [1979 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1979_Saint_Lucian_general_election) (12/17), led by Allan Louisy, replaced as prime minister during the term by [Winston Cenac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winston_Cenac), himself replaced by [Michael Pilgrim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Pilgrim). The 1979 elections were the first elections held following independence from the [United Kingdom](31717.md), declared on 22 February 1979.

It lost the [1982 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1982_Saint_Lucian_general_election) to Compton's UWP when they were reduced to just two seats, challenged on their left by a breakaway faction, [George Odlum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Odlum)'s [Progressive Labour Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progressive_Labour_Party_\(Saint_Lucia\)) taking 1 seat. It remained in opposition following the [two elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6_April_1987_Saint_Lucian_general_election) of [April 1987](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/30_April_1987_Saint_Lucian_general_election), increasing its presence to 8 seats in both contests, and in [1992](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_Saint_Lucian_general_election) (6/17).

Led by Dr. [Kenny Anthony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenny_Anthony), former cabinet minister in the 1979–1982 government, it won the [1997 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997_Saint_Lucian_general_election), taking 16 of the 17 seats. It remained in power after the [2001 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Saint_Lucian_general_election) (14/17).

It lost the [2006 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Saint_Lucian_general_election) to the UWP, who had called back [John Compton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Compton)[^cite_note-5] as leader a year before - he had retired in 1996. Kenny Anthony remained leader of the party throughout its time as [loyal opposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loyal_opposition). The Labour Party won the [2011 Saint Lucian general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Saint_Lucian_general_election) (28 November 2011), winning in 11 out of a 17-seats contest and defeating UWP leader [Stephenson King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephenson_King) who had lost to John Compton as Prime Minister (d. 2007, in office).

The Labour Party lost the [2016 elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Saint_Lucian_general_election) to the UWP by 11 seats to 6, and Kenny Anthony resigned as party leader. Former Deputy PM [Philip J. Pierre](4388649.md) was confirmed as party leader on 18 June 2016.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] The party then formed government in the [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Saint_Lucian_general_election) and [2025 general elections](68577799.md), under Pierre.
