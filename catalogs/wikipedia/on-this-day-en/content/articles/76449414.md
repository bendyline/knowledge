**Spaceport Kii** ([Japanese](15606.md): スペースポート紀伊; sometimes stylized as **Space Port Kii**) is a [commercial](752732.md) [spaceport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spaceport) located in [Kushimoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kushimoto,_Wakayama), [Wakayama Prefecture](179766.md) in [Japan](15573.md). Japan's first private spaceport, it is operated by [Space One](76117343.md), who are using it to launch their solid-fuel rocket [*KAIROS*](76117481.md). Three launch attempts have been made on the site, on 13 March and 17 December 2025 and on 4 March 2026, which all ended up being a failure.[^cite_note-NBC-1][^cite_note-SpaceNews-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-\:0-4]

## Location and construction

Spaceport Kii is located near the southernmost tip of [Honshu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honshu) and is only a two-minute (1.9 kilometer) drive from [Kii-Uragami Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kii-Uragami_Station) in [Nachikatsuura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nachikatsuura).

The launch site was funded by several Japanese corporations, including [Canon Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canon_Inc.), [Shimizu Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shimizu_Corporation) (who also constructed the site),[^cite_note-JPSO-5] and [IHI Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IHI_Corporation).[^cite_note-6] Ground broke on 19 November 2019.[^cite_note-JPSO-5]

## Launches

The first launch attempt of the *KAIROS* rocket was scheduled on 8 March 2024,[^cite_note-7] however it was delayed to the 13th due to a ship in too close of proximity.[^cite_note-8] It launched on the 13th, but disintegrated just seconds later.[^cite_note-NBC-1]

The second launch of KAIROS was initially scheduled for 14 December 2024, but was ultimately scrubbed twice to 18 Dec due to bad weather. The 2nd launch had a successful liftoff, but ultimately suffered a loss of control nearing the end of the first-stage burn and was terminated. Its third launch while carrying a small government test satellite, on 4 March 2026, also ended in failure. The flight was terminated two minutes after launch.[^cite_note-\:0-4]

Space One has stated that it plans to have around 20 launches of *KAIROS* a year.[^cite_note-JPSO-5][^cite_note-SpaceNews-2]
