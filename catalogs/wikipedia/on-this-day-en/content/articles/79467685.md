The **2025–26 Ekstraklasa** (also known as **[PKO Bank Polski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PKO_Bank_Polski) Ekstraklasa** due to [sponsorship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sponsor_\(commercial\)) reasons)[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] was the 100th season of [the Polish Football Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Polish_football_champions), the 92nd season of [the highest tier domestic division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_league) in the [Polish football league system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_football_league_system) since its establishment in [1927](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1927_Ekstraklasa) and the 18th season of the [Ekstraklasa](30871677.md) under its current title. The league was operated by the Ekstraklasa [S.A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S.A._\(corporation\)).

[Lech Poznań](677622.md), the [defending champions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Ekstraklasa),[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] won their second consecutive title after a 3–1 win over [Radomiak Radom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radomiak_Radom) on 16 May 2026. The season's runner-ups were [Górnik Zabrze](677580.md), with [Jagiellonia Białystok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonia_Bia%C5%82ystok) finishing third.

## Season overview

The season started on 18 July 2025 with a match between [Jagiellonia Białystok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonia_Bia%C5%82ystok) and [Bruk-Bet Termalica Nieciecza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruk-Bet_Termalica_Nieciecza) and concluded on 23 May 2026.

The regular season was played as a [round-robin tournament](939742.md). A total of 18 teams participated, 15 of which competed in the league campaign during the [previous season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Ekstraklasa), while the remaining three were promoted from the [I liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_I_liga). Each team played a total of 34 matches, half at home and half away. It was the ninth Ekstraklasa season to use [VAR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_assistant_referee).

## Teams

A total of 18 teams participated in the 2025–26 edition of the Ekstraklasa.

The first team to win promotion from [2024–25 I liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_I_liga) was [Arka Gdynia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arka_Gdynia) after a win over [Bruk-Bet Termalica Nieciecza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruk-Bet_Termalica_Nieciecza) on 4 May 2025, returning to the top flight after five years of absence. The second promoted team was Bruk-Bet Termalica, following [Wisła Płock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wis%C5%82a_P%C5%82ock)'s draw with [Górnik Łęczna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GKS_G%C3%B3rnik_%C5%81%C4%99czna) on 17 May 2025. Bruk-Bet Termalica returned to the top flight after a three-year absence. Wisła Płock became the third and final promoted team after the victory in play-off against [Miedź Legnica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mied%C5%BA_Legnica) on 1 June 2025, returning to the top flight after two years of absence.

On 11 May 2025, the first two relegated teams to [2025–26 I liga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_I_liga), at the same time, were [Śląsk Wrocław](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%9Al%C4%85sk_Wroc%C5%82aw) and [Stal Mielec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stal_Mielec). Following [Lechia Gdańsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lechia_Gda%C5%84sk)'s win over [Korona Kielce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korona_Kielce), both Śląsk and Stal's relegations were confirmed, after respective spells of seventeen and five years in the top flight. The last team to be relegated, after losing to [Stal Mielec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stal_Mielec) on 12 May 2025, was [Puszcza Niepołomice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puszcza_Niepo%C5%82omice), ending their top flight run after two seasons.
