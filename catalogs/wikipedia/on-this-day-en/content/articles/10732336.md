**Cucunubá** is a municipality and town of [Colombia](5222.md) in the [Ubaté Province](11964310.md), part of the [department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Departments_of_Colombia) of [Cundinamarca](189405.md). It borders with the municipalities of [Ubaté](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ubat%C3%A9), [Lenguazaque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenguazaque), [Suesca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suesca), [Chocontá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chocont%C3%A1), [Tausa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tausa) and [Sutatausa](10733837.md).[^cite_note-websiteCucunuba-1]

## Geography

The urban centre of the municipality is located in the [Ubaté Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ubat%C3%A9_River) at an altitude of 2,590 metres (8,500 ft), while other parts stretch over the mountainous sectors of the [Eastern Ranges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cordillera_Oriental_\(Colombia\)) of the Colombian [Andes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andes). The urban centre is at a distance of 90 kilometres (56 mi) from the capital [Bogotá](211271.md).

## Etymology

The name Cucunubá comes from [Chibcha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chibcha_language) and means "Similarity to a face".[^cite_note-websiteCucunuba-1][^cite_note-TiempoCundin-2]

## History

Cucunubá in the times before the [Spanish conquest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_conquest_of_the_Muisca) was part of the [Muisca Confederation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muisca_Confederation), a loose confederation of [rulers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muisca_rulers) of the [Muisca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muisca_people).

Modern Cucunubá was founded on August 2, 1600, by Luis Enríquez.[^cite_note-websiteCucunuba-1]

## Economy

Main economical activity of Cucunubá is [carbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbon) [mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mining). More than half of the municipality's area is covered with farmfields and [livestock](25160767.md) farming producers. Main agricultural products are [potatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potato), [peas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pea) and [wheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheat).[^cite_note-websiteCucunuba-1]
