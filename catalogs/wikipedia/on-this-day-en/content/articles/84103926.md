**Kim Song-gi** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 김성기) is a North Korean politician.[^cite_note-1]

## Biography

On February 2026, following the [9th WPK Congress](81523935.md), he was elected as a member of the [9th Convocation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9th_Central_Committee_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea) of the [Central Committee of the Workers' Party of Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Committee_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea)[^cite_note-\:0-2] [9th Convocation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9th_Central_Military_Commission_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea) of the [Central Military Commission of the Workers' Party of Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Military_Commission_of_the_Workers'_Party_of_Korea).[^cite_note-3]

On August 30, 2026, the [Korean Central News Agency](632399.md) reported that [No Kwang-chol](50532350.md) had been dismissed as the [defense minister](35636876.md), with Song-gi having replacing him.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]
