**Oddar Meanchey** ([Khmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_language): ឧត្តរមានជ័យ, *Ŏttâr Méanchoăy* [\[ʔoɗɑː miəncɨj\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Khmer); lit. 'victorious North') is a [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Cambodia) of [Cambodia](334751.md) in the remote northwest. It borders the provinces of [Banteay Meanchey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banteay_Meanchey_province) to the west, [Siem Reap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siem_Reap_province) to the south and [Preah Vihear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preah_Vihear_province) to the east. Its long northern boundary demarcates part of Cambodia's [international border](65369821.md) with [Thailand](30128.md). The capital is [Samraong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samraong_\(town\)). The final stronghold of [Anlong Veng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anlong_Veng) became the jungle headquarters of [Saloth Sâr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pol_Pot) (*Pol Pot*) and the [Khmer Rouge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_Rouge) of [Democratic Kampuchea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Kampuchea) once they rebuild their former bases in the mountainous tropical dense jungle on 18 February 1994.

The province was created on 27 April 1999, after being carved from the northern half of Siem Reap province and part of Banteay Meanchey. It existed as a province already from 1962 to 1970 under [Norodom Sihanouk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norodom_Sihanouk)'s [Sangkum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangkum) regime, but later became an administrative no-man's-land, with its status alternating between a province and a district under successive regimes.[^cite_note-2]

## Etymology

*Oddar Meanchey* means 'victorious North' and the name represents to victory over siam when the province was returned to Cambodia in 1907. The province's name is of [Sanskrit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit) origin through [Pali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pali) and is derived from the words *uttarā* (उत्तर), meaning 'north', and *jaya* (जय) which means 'victory'. Khmer *Mean* (មាន) means 'to have'.

## Ecology

This province is in the [Dângrêk Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C3%A2ngr%C3%AAk_Mountains). There is a serious problem of [deforestation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deforestation) in the area.[^cite_note-3] However, the first community-based mosaic [REDD+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reducing_emissions_from_deforestation_and_forest_degradation) project in Asia was awarded [Verified Carbon Standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verified_Carbon_Standard) and Climate, Community and Biodiversity Standard validation for its forest conservation work in the province.[^cite_note-4]

[Forest fires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest_fires) are common in the dry forest and there is much [illegal logging](465780.md) as a result of economic land concessions and migrant forest encroachment.[^cite_note-5]

## History



Grave of [Khmer Rouge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_Rouge) dictator [Pol Pot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pol_Pot) who died in [Anlong Veng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anlong_Veng_District) in 1998.

The mountainous and forested area that now makes up Oddar Meanchey province was part of Siamese province of Monthon Isan from 1795 to 1907 under the supervision of Sangkha. After the neighboring Thai province of [Inner Cambodia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inner_Cambodia) and Oddar Meanchey were returned to Cambodia in 1907, King Sisowath split the province into Battambang and Siem Reap (Oddar Meanchey was included with Siem Reap). Following the [Franco-Thai War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franco-Thai_War) from 1941 to 1946 the province was under Siamese administration as [Phibunsongkhram province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phibunsongkhram_province).[^cite_note-6] The province was returned to Cambodian rule in 1946 following French diplomatic efforts at the end of [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South-East_Asian_theatre_of_World_War_II). The province was officially organized in 1966. During the [Cambodian Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambodian_Civil_War), the remote area was used as a base by the Khmer Rouge when they fought against the [Khmer Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_Republic) led by general [Lon Nol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lon_Nol).

The [Khmer Rouge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_Rouge) of [Democratic Kampuchea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Kampuchea) led by [Saloth Sâr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pol_Pot) (*Pol Pot*, [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language) for *Politique Potentielle*) and [Khieu Samphan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khieu_Samphan) rebuilt their former jungle bases in the Dangrek mountain range area, along the border with [Thailand](30128.md) and made [Anlong Veng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anlong_Veng_District) their base from 1989 until 1997. Oddar Meanchey province is one of the most [landmine](18172.md)-ridden areas in Cambodia.[^cite_note-7]
