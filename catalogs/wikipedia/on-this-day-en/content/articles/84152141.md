**F. Dale Sanders** (born June 14, 1935), also known by his trail name **Grey Beard**, is an American hiker known for breaking world records.

## Early life

Sanders was born in [Lickskillet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lickskillet,_Logan_County,_Kentucky), [Logan County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logan_County,_Kentucky), [Kentucky](16846.md) on June 14, 1935.[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-\:1-2] His parents had a tobacco farm.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

He was bullied in [middle school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_school) because of his small size.[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-\:1-2]

## Career

During his high school years, he was a lifeguard at Camp Curry (a boys' camp on [Kentucky Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentucky_Lake)) for one summer.[^cite_note-\:0-1] He became a certified swimming instructor and a specialist in handicap swimming.[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-\:1-2]

In the 1950s, he joined the [Navy](20518076.md) and rehabilitated wounded [Korean War](16772.md) veterans with his handicap swimming skills.[^cite_note-\:0-1] He became a regional [MWR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morale,_Welfare_and_Recreation) director.[^cite_note-\:0-1] He left the Navy after four years but remained an MWR inspector in the [Civil Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_service_\(United_States\)) for over thirty years until his retirement in 2002.[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-\:1-2]

During his long career, he earned an [associate's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_degree) and [bachelor's](188874.md) degree in parks and recreation as well as a [master's](170346.md) degree in education.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

He was also a US athlete and was on the national championship team in [spearfishing](306254.md) with which he won a gold medal in 1965.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

## Personal life

He met and married Meriam in 1980. They have three children, two sons and one daughter.[^cite_note-\:0-1]
