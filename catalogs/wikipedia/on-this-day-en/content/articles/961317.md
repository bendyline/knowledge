The **French Alps**[^cite_note-1] are the portions of the [Alps](981.md) [mountain range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_range) that stand within [France](5843419.md), located in the [Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes](45093325.md) and [Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provence-Alpes-C%C3%B4te_d'Azur) regions. While some of the ranges of the French Alps are entirely in France, others, such as the [Mont Blanc massif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mont_Blanc_massif), are shared with [Switzerland](26748.md) and [Italy](14532.md).

At 4,808 metres (15,774 ft), [Mont Blanc](44768.md), on the [France–Italy border](47329029.md), is the highest mountain in the Alps, and the [highest Western European mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest_points_of_European_countries).[^cite_note-2]

Notable towns in the French Alps include [Grenoble](50972.md), [Chamonix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamonix), [Annecy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annecy), [Chambéry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamb%C3%A9ry), [Évian-les-Bains](550686.md) and [Albertville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albertville).

The French Alps will host the [2030 Winter Olympics](57658858.md), marking the fourth occasion when the [Winter Olympics](34069.md) were held in the region. The games were previously held in Albertville in [1992](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_Winter_Olympics), Grenoble in [1968](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1968_Winter_Olympics), and the very first in Chamonix in [1924](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1924_Winter_Olympics).

## Activities

A range of winter and summer activities are available in the French Alps. In the winter, these include skiing and snowboarding; alternative activities include snowshoeing and sledging. There is a range of other activities that happen such as gliding, which is more popular during the summer months.[^cite_note-3] Summer activities include hiking, mountaineering, biking and rock climbing.[^cite_note-4]
