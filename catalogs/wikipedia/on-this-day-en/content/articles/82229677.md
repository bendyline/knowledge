***Devon Bay*** was a Singaporean [bulk carrier](1324895.md) that was built in 2013 and serviced Asia. On January 23, 2026, the vessel [capsized](1199716.md) and sank in the [South China Sea](74209.md) near the disputed [atoll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atoll) of [Scarborough Shoal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarborough_Shoal) after the cargo it was carrying shifted to one side of the vessel during bad weather. Six crew members were killed and 15 were rescued.

## Description

*Devon Bay* was a [Singaporean](27318.md)-flagged [bulk carrier](1324895.md) owned by [Japanese](15573.md) shipping company [K Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K_Line). It was launched on 15 March 2013 and was completed by [Mitsui E\&S](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitsui_E\&S) on 1 July 2013. It was 189.99 m (623 ft 4 in) long and 32.25 m (105 ft 10 in) wide, with a capacity of 28 crew members.[^cite_note-1] The max speed of the vessel was 16.5 [kn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knot_\(unit\)) (30.6 km/h; 19.0 mph), while the normal speed was 14.5 [kn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knot_\(unit\)) (26.9 km/h; 16.7 mph).[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

## Sinking



Search and rescue operations by the [Philippine Coast Guard](1954144.md)

The vessel was loaded with 55,000 tons of nickel ore en route from [Zamboanga del Sur](256096.md) to [Yangjiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yangjiang), [China](5405.md), when contact was lost approximately 141 nautical miles off [Pangasinan](1691800.md), Philippines, on 22 January at around 8\:30 p.m. local time. An [SOS](145605.md) signal was received by the [Chinese Coast Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Coast_Guard) (CCG) at 1\:34 a.m. on 23 January that the vessel had capsized in the [South China Sea](74209.md) approximately 55 nautical miles off [Scarborough Shoal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarborough_Shoal).[^cite_note-4] Two CCG vessels were dispatched, including one that rescued 10 crew members. The PCG also dispatched two vessels and two aircraft. Out of the 21 crew members on board at the time of the sinking, two died, four were reported missing, including the captain, and 15 were rescued, including one receiving emergency medical treatment.[^cite_note-ST-5] The 14 other crew members were reported to be in stable condition.[^cite_note-6] The survivors and deceased victims were recovered by the [BRP *Teresa Magbanua*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BRP_Teresa_Magbanua).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] Search and rescue (SAR) operations concluded on February 1, and the four missing crew members were presumed dead.[^cite_note-K_Line-9]

The [Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maritime_and_Port_Authority_of_Singapore) confirmed it was investigating.[^cite_note-ST-5] According to the PCG, cargo [liquefaction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liquefaction) may have caused shifting in the weight of the load, forcing the vessel to tilt to one side and capsize.[^cite_note-10]

[K Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K_Line) said they are deeply grateful to the Philippines and China Coast Guards, the co-ordinating rescue centers and all the vessels and crew that assisted in SAR operations. They also extended condolences to the families of the victims.[^cite_note-K_Line-9]
