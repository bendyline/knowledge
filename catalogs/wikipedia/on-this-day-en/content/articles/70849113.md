# Pizzeria



A pizzeria with its staff along Via Depretis in [Naples](55880.md) c. 1910



A pizza chef baking pizza in a [pizza oven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pizza_oven)

A **pizzeria** (Italian: [\[ˌpitːseˈriːa\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Italian)) is a commercial establishment specializing in the preparation and sale of [pizza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pizza).[^cite_note-1] It may offer take-away, where the customer orders their food either in advance or at the [restaurant](26437.md) and then takes the prepared food with them in a [pizza box](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pizza_box). A pizzeria may [deliver food](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pizza_delivery) to the customer's home.

## History

Main article: [History of pizza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_pizza)

One of the world's first pizzerias was founded in [Naples](55880.md) by [Raffaele Esposito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raffaele_Esposito) in 1830.[^cite_note-2] The restaurant, which continues to operate to this day, is nowadays named Pizzeria Brandi.[^cite_note-3]

In the [United States](3434750.md), sales of pizza started in 1905, when [Gennaro Lombardi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gennaro_Lombardi) installed a [pizza oven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pizza_oven) in his shop-café and started selling [pizza by the slice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pizza_by_the_slice).[^cite_note-history-4]

## Types

Pizza sales are not exclusive to pizzerias, as the food product is also sold in *[rosticcerie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosticcerie?action=edit\&redlink=1)*, [bakeries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakeries), [delicatessens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delicatessen), and [hypermarkets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypermarket).[^cite_note-5]

### Pizzeria-restaurant

This is a particular type of [restaurant](26437.md) in which [calzones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calzone) and various [fried foods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fried_food) are also available alongside other dishes. Customers choose their orders from the [menu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Menu) and are served at the table by a [waiter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waiter).[^cite_note-6] The [chef](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chef) is assisted by a *[pizzaiolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pizzaiolo)*, who is responsible for using the [oven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oven) and preparing the pizza.[^cite_note-7] It is usually served *al piatto*, although in some cases its length can be chosen (*pizza al metro*).[^cite_note-8] Sometimes, *al piatto* pizza is also sold for takeaway ([take-out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Take-out)).[^cite_note-9]

### Takeaway pizzeria

Smaller than a pizzeria-restaurant, it is a venue where pizza is purchased for consumption at home. Pizza may also be eaten on-site, at counters or tables provided inside the establishment.[^cite_note-10] Takeaway pizzerias often have a refrigerator from which customers can also purchase [beverages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beverage).[^cite_note-11]

### Fast food



*[Pizza al taglio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pizza_al_taglio)* for sale in [Rome](25458.md), Italy

In the late 20th century, the concept of [fast food](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_food) caused changes in pizza sales. Food stands offering *[pizza al taglio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pizza_al_taglio)* (lit. '[pizza by the slice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pizza_by_the_slice)') started appearing in [Italy](14532.md), baking large rectangular pizzas sold in small slices. These slices are seldom topped with more than two ingredients, not counting tomato and cheese.[^cite_note-12]

#### Fast food chains

There are also [fast food](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_food) chains that combine speed of service with the widespread consumption of pizza.[^cite_note-13]

### Take and bake pizzeria

Take-and-bake pizzas are typically made to order out of fresh ingredients (though the pizzeria may also keep a number of commonly ordered or special sale price pizzas on hand for convenience). Often because the pizzas are made with fresh and unbaked ingredients and not heated in-store, the pizzas can be paid for in some states with [food assistance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_Stamp_Program) [EBT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_benefit_transfer) cards.[^cite_note-14]

According to *[Nation's Restaurant News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nation's_Restaurant_News)*, take-and-bake pizzerias typically have lower costs because they require less restaurant space and equipment. As a result, they are often able to undercut the national pizza giants.[^cite_note-15]

## Home delivery

See also: [Pizza delivery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pizza_delivery)

Home delivery is equally widespread and is sometimes used as a form of advertising; discount vouchers may accompany deliveries as promotional offers.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] In many countries, a delivery charge and tip is added to the bill.[^cite_note-18] In Italy, it’s usually around one [Euro](9472.md).[^cite_note-19] Delivery generally takes place using small vehicles such as [scooters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scooter_\(motorcycle\)), equipped with [special storage boxes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pizza_box).[^cite_note-20]

Pizza delivery by [motorcycle](19876.md) inspired the video game *[Radikal Bikers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radikal_Bikers)*.[^cite_note-21]
