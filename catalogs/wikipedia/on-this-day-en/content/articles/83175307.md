**Aleksandr Mikhailovich Shuvaev** (Russian: Александр Михайлович Шуваев; born on 7 May 1981) is a [Russian](59510.md) [politician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politician) and former army officer who is acting as the [governor](68851709.md) of [Belgorod Oblast](478857.md) since 13 May 2026.[^cite_note-1]

[Hero of the Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hero_of_the_Russian_Federation) (2025). [Major General](36301471.md) (2024).

## Biography

Shuvaev was born on May 7, 1981, in [Novy Oskol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novy_Oskol) [Belgorod Oblast](478857.md).

After graduating from high school, he received higher education at the Ryazan Institute of Airborne Troops. He graduated there in 2002.

In 2015, he graduated from the M.V.Frunze Combined Arms Academy of the Armed Forces.

In 2015–2025, he served in the [Armed Forces of the Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Forces_of_the_Russian_Federation).

On January 19, 2026, he was appointed Deputy Governor of the [Irkutsk Oblast](79477.md).

On April 10, 2026, Shuvaev as the main candidate for the position of Governor of the Belgorod Region.

Since May 13, 2026, he has been the acting governor of the Belgorod Oblast.

## Awards

- Hero of the Russian Federation

- Order of Merit for the Fatherland, IV degree (with swords)

- Order of Alexander Nevsky

- Orders of Courage

- Medal Suvorov Medal.
