[Ziraat Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ziraat_Bank) is a state-owned bank in Turkey founded in 1863.



[TCDD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_State_Railways) is a state owned rail transportation monopoly. HT80004 waits to depart the high-speed section of the Ankara railway station for Konya.

In Turkey, **state-owned enterprises** are called "Kamu İktisadi Teşebbüsü" or abbreviation "KİT".[^cite_note-airbus-1]
