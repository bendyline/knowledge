**Aeroport District&#x20;**&#x20;([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): райо́н Аэропо́рт, *rayon Aeroport*, "Airport District") is an administrative district ([raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raion)) of the [Northern Administrative Okrug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Administrative_Okrug) and one of the 125 raions of [Moscow](19004.md), [Russia](25391.md). The area of the district is 4.58 square kilometers (1.77 sq mi).&#x20;

The district is named for the [Khodynka Aerodrome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khodynka_Aerodrome), which closed in 2003. The [Aeroport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aeroport_\(Moscow_Metro\)) Metro Station opened in the district in 1938.

## Economy

The aviation companies [Irkut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irkut_Corporation),[^cite_note-2] [Ilyushin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilyushin),[^cite_note-3] and [Yakovlev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yakovlev) have their head offices in the district.[^cite_note-4]

## Social Sphere

### Education

[Moscow Automobile and Road Construction State Technical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Automobile_and_Road_Construction_State_Technical_University) is located in the district.

The [New Humanitarian School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Humanitarian_School), a private school, is in the district.[^cite_note-5]

### Culture

[Petrovsky Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petrovsky_Palace) is located here.

### Sports

[Central Dynamo Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Dynamo_Stadium) was located in the district, until its destruction and replacement with [VTB Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VTB_Arena).
