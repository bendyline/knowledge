**Labrador**, officially the **Municipality of Labrador** ([Pangasinan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangasinan_language): *Baley na Labrador*; [Ilocano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilocano_language): *Ili ti Labrador*; [Tagalog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagalog_language): *Bayan ng Labrador*), is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipality_of_the_Philippines) in the [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Province) of [Pangasinan](1691800.md), [Philippines](23440.md). According to the 2024 census, it has a population of 26,995 people.[^cite_note-5]

## History

On October 8, 1903, by virtue of Act No. 931, the municipality of San Isidro Labrador was merged with the municipality of Sual, with the seat of the municipal government at Sual.[^cite_note-6]

On January 27, 1908, by virtue of Executive Order No. 6, issued by Governor-General James F. Smith, San Isidro Labrador was separated from Sual and reestablished as a municipality, effective January 1, 1909.

On February 28, 1914, by virtue of Act No. 2390, the names of the two municipalities of Pangasinan, San Isidro Labrador and San Isidro de Potot, were changed to Labrador and Burgos, respectively.

## Geography

Labrador is situated 12.69 kilometres (7.89 mi) from the provincial capital, [Lingayen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lingayen), and 221.62 kilometres (137.71 mi) from the country's capital city of [Manila](184334.md). It is between [Sual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sual) and [Bugallon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bugallon) on the [Olongapo–Bugallon Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olongapo%E2%80%93Bugallon_Road).

## Government

### Local government

Main article: [Sangguniang Bayan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangguniang_Bayan)

Labrador is part of the [second congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_districts_of_Pangasinan) of the province of [Pangasinan](1691800.md). It is governed by a mayor, designated as its local chief executive, and by a municipal council as its legislative body in accordance with the Local Government Code. The mayor, vice mayor, and the councilors are elected directly by the people through an election which is being held every three years.
