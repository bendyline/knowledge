**Omar Abdulkadir Artan** ([Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language): *Cumar Cabdulqaadir Cartan*; [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): عمر عبد القادر عرتن; born 6 June 1992) is a Somali [football](10568.md) [referee](501260.md). Artan was the first referee from Somalia to take charge of a continental final, overseeing Egyptian side [Pyramids FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyramids_FC)'s [2024–25 CAF Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_CAF_Champions_League) triumph over South Africa's [Mamelodi Sundowns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mamelodi_Sundowns_F.C.) in [Cairo](6293.md).[^cite_note-2025CAFCLF-1] He has also officiated at the [Africa Cup of Nations](250680.md) (AFCON). He was the sole representative from [Sub-Saharan Africa](27067.md) to officiate at the [2025 FIFA U-20 World Cup](73421401.md).[^cite_note-\:2-2][^cite_note-\:3-3][^cite_note-\:1-4] Somali sporting authorities and media have described Artan's achievements as a source of pride and an inspiration for the country's sporting development.[^cite_note-\:4-5] He was the first Somali referee selected for a [FIFA World Cup](11370.md) tournament, among three centre referees chosen from [Africa](715265.md). However, Artan was not allowed to enter the United States and was removed from the tournament, generating controversy.[^cite_note-6] Shortly after, he was selected as the referee for the [2026 UEFA Super Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_UEFA_Super_Cup), which took place in August, weeks after the 2026 World Cup [came to a close](75845879.md).

## Early life

Omar Abdulkadir Artan was born in Mogadishu, Somalia on 6 June 1992.[^cite_note-\:0-7][^cite_note-8]

## Refereeing career

Artan began his refereeing career in local Somali leagues and became an official referee in the [Somali First Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_First_Division). He has been a member of the [FIFA-listed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_International_Referees_List) referees since 2018.[^cite_note-9] Artan made history in January 2024 as the first Somali to officiate at the Africa Cup of Nations, overseeing the Group E match between Tunisia and Namibia. He has also handled high-profile fixtures in the [CAF Champions League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CAF_Champions_League) and [2026 FIFA World Cup qualifiers](60000294.md).[^cite_note-\:0-7] Artan was a part of a nine-member contingent from Africa that includes three referees and six assistant referees selected.[^cite_note-\:1-4][^cite_note-\:4-5]

Somalia's footballing landscape faced numerous challenges due to instability and conflict that plagued the country for decades. However, recent efforts to restore peace and security have created opportunities for football revival. [FIFA](11049.md) played a pivotal role in this revival by offering training to Somali referees and young footballers. Training had been halted for many years due to the unstable situation in the country. However, with the security improvement, FIFA resumed its training programs.[^cite_note-10]

Upon being appointed for the FIFA World Cup in April 2026, president [Hassan Sheikh Mohamud](36973989.md) applauded Artan for his "effort, skills, and integrity."[^cite_note-landing-11]

### 2026 FIFA World Cup

See also: [List of 2026 FIFA World Cup controversies](83181783.md)

In April 2026, Artan was selected to referee the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md).[^cite_note-usatoday-12] On 6 June, he flew from [Istanbul](3391396.md), Turkey, to [Miami](53846.md) over the weekend to attend a mandatory FIFA match officials seminar ahead of the World Cup.[^cite_note-13] He had secured multiple three-month US entry visas and received a [diplomatic passport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplomatic_passport) facilitated by the Somali Embassy in [Nairobi](21482.md),[^cite_note-peter-14] but upon arrival at [Miami International Airport](128968.md), [US Customs and Border Protection](757040.md) (CBP) denied him entry,[^cite_note-15] immediately placing him on a return flight to Istanbul.[^cite_note-16] CBP stated that Artan was found inadmissible following inspection due to unspecified "vetting concerns" and was therefore denied admission to the United States.[^cite_note-usatoday-12]

Some sources blamed the stringent vetting processes and tight immigration restrictions implemented by [Trump administration](78298108.md).[^cite_note-17] Artan later said that he had been subjected to an 11-hour immigration interview before being refused entry. He stated that US officials did not provide a clear explanation for the decision, despite his possessing the required travel documents, and suggested that his Somali nationality may have been a factor. Describing the World Cup as "the biggest dream of my life", he expressed disappointment at being unable to participate in the tournament.[^cite_note-18] After his return to Somalia, Artan said that among the questions made by the US officials were topics related to [Al-Qaeda](1921.md) and [Al-Shabaab](8726463.md).[^cite_note-landing-11] An unnamed US official subsequently stated that Artan's alleged "association with suspected members of terror organizations" was the reason for refusing entry.[^cite_note-19][^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21]

The entry denial was condemned by human rights advocates, critics, and Canadian political figures including [Toronto](64646.md) Mayor [Olivia Chow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Chow), whose city was [co-hosting tournament matches](36581929.md). Chow criticized the decision, stating that "denying entry to Omar Artan, who has earned his place on the world stage through hard work and perseverance, is not right," while affirming that he would be welcome to officiate in Toronto.[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-23]

FIFA confirmed that he would be unable to participate as a match official at the tournament, noting that decisions regarding entry and visas ultimately rested with the host government.[^cite_note-peter-14] Although the tournament was jointly hosted by the United States, Canada, and Mexico, referees were required to attend centralized preparations in the United States. FIFA referees committee chairman [Pierluigi Collina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierluigi_Collina) had established a training hub in Miami for the tournament's 52 referees and 88 assistant referees, making participation in the United States an essential part of tournament officiating preparations.[^cite_note-24]

On 10 June 2026, Artan landed back in Somalia, receiving a warm welcome by authorities and civilians as well as hailing him as a hero.[^cite_note-25] In a brief statement to the press, Artan expressed disappointment with the United States and called on Somalis to defend the name of the country, further stating that he hopes to be at the [next FIFA World Cup](39367087.md).[^cite_note-landing-11] Afterwards, FIFA confirmed that Artan would receive his full fee of \$100,000 for the 2026 World Cup despite being unable to officiate at the tournament.[^cite_note-26]

### 2026 UEFA Super Cup

Main article: [2026 UEFA Super Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_UEFA_Super_Cup)

On 11 June 2026, days after his US entry denial, Artan was selected by the [Union of European Football Associations](32332.md) (UEFA) to referee of the [2026 UEFA Super Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_UEFA_Super_Cup) match between [Paris Saint-Germain](357488.md) and [Aston Villa](42173.md), which took place at the [Red Bull Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Bull_Arena_\(Salzburg\)) in [Salzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salzburg), Austria on 12 August 2026.[^cite_note-2026USC-27][^cite_note-28] His selection followed negotiations between UEFA and the [Confederation of African Football](715265.md) (CAF).[^cite_note-29][^cite_note-30] He became the first ever African to be a referee in a UEFA competition match.[^cite_note-31]

Artan successfully managed the high-profile fixture alongside his assistant referees, Liban Abdoulrazack Ahmed of Djibouti and Stephen Yiembe of Kenya.[^cite_note-32] Following the final whistle, Artan signed the official match ball with the poignant message, *"This was my World Cup,"* expressing deep gratitude to UEFA for giving him a defining moment on the global stage.[^cite_note-33] His performance also earned him a follow-up invitation from UEFA to attend an elite referee summer course in [Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva), Switzerland, from 31 August to 2 September, alongside indications from the European governing body that he may officiate future European club competition matches.[^cite_note-34]

### 2025–26 Kuwaiti Premier League

Main article: [2025–26 Kuwaiti Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Kuwaiti_Premier_League)

On 21 June 2026, while the World Cup was underway, the [Kuwait Football Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuwait_Football_Association) (KFA) invited Artan to officiate the final match of the [2025–26 Kuwaiti Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Kuwaiti_Premier_League) season between [Kuwait SC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuwait_SC) and [Qadsia SC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qadsia_SC). The [Somali Football Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_Football_Federation) welcomed the invitation. Prior to the 29 June fixture, Artan received an award of excellence from the KFA before refereeing the match.[^cite_note-35]
