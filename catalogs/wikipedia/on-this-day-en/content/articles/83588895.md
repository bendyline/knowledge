The **2026 ESPY Awards**, the 34th annual ceremony of the [ESPY Awards](615059.md), was held on July 15, 2026, at the [David H. Koch Theater](1587430.md) in [New York City](645042.md). It was broadcast by [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company).

## Ceremony information

Comedian [Marcello Hernández](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcello_Hern%C3%A1ndez) hosted the ceremony at the [David H. Koch Theater](1587430.md) in [New York City](645042.md).[^cite_note-1]

## In Memoriam

No in memoriam segment was held during the ceremony. Recently deceased athletes such as [Kyle Busch](1467805.md) were not mentioned during the broadcast. Despite this, [Jason Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Collins) was posthumously awarded with the [Arthur Ashe Courage Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Ashe_Courage_Award).
