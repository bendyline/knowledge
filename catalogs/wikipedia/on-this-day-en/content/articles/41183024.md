Visitors to **[Nicaragua](21362.md)** must obtain an [e-Visa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_visa) unless they are citizens of one of the visa-exempt countries.

All visitors must hold a passport valid for at least 6 months from the date of arrival.

Citizens of other countries are eligible to obtain an e-Visa.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14]

The country launched the electronic visa portal in March 2026.[^cite_note-15]

## Transit

Transit without a visa is generally allowed for travelers who normally require a visa but are transiting within 24 hours and hold onward tickets.\


Citizens of [Afghanistan](737.md), [Albania](738.md), [Armenia](10918072.md), [Bangladesh](3454.md), [Bosnia and Herzegovina](3463.md), [Botswana](3464.md), [Cameroon](5447.md), [China](5405.md), [Republic of the Congo](19599929.md), [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md), [Cuba](5042481.md), [Eritrea](17238590.md), [Haiti](13373.md), [India](14533.md), [Iraq](7515928.md), [Kenya](188171.md), [Laos](17752.md), [Lebanon](17771.md), [Liberia](17791.md), [Mali](19127.md), [Mongolia](19271.md), [Nepal](171166.md), [Nigeria](21383.md), [Pakistan](23235.md), [Sierra Leone](27308.md), [Somalia](27358.md), [Sri Lanka](26750.md), [Sudan](27421.md), [Syria](7515849.md), [Timor-Leste](19159399.md), [Vietnam](202354.md) and [Yemen](350939.md) are required to obtain a transit visa.&#x20;

## Tourist card

Citizens from all visa-exempt countries are required to obtain a tourist card (US\$10) on arrival. Exempt are the citizens of [El Salvador](9356.md), [Guatemala](17238567.md), and [Honduras](13394.md), as well as holders of diplomatic, official, service or special passports issued to any country.

## Central America-4 Border Control Agreement

The [Central America-4 Border Control Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_America-4_Border_Control_Agreement) is a treaty between [Guatemala](17238567.md), [El Salvador](9356.md), [Honduras](13394.md) and Nicaragua. A visa issued by one of the four countries is honored by all four of the countries.\
&#x20;The time period for the visa, however, applies to the total time spent in any of the four countries without leaving the CA-4 area.[^cite_note-16]
