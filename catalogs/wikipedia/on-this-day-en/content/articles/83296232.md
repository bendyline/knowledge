The **2026–27 Malaysia Super League**(also known as the 2026–27 **Unifi Liga Super** for sponsorship reasons) ([Malay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_language): *Liga Super Malaysia 2026–27*) will be the 23rd season of the [Malaysia Super League](42924474.md), the top-tier professional [football](10568.md) league in [Malaysia](3607937.md) since its establishment in 2004, and the 45th season of the top-flight [Malaysian football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaysian_football_league_system) overall.

[Johor Darul Ta'zim](39138667.md) are the defending champions, having won their record 12th Super League title in the previous season.[^cite_note-1]

## Teams

### Changes from the previous season

A total of twelve teams are confirmed to contest the league, including eleven from the [2025–26 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Malaysia_Super_League), one promoted from the [A1 Semi-Pro League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_Malaysia_A1_Semi-Pro_League). Kelantan Red Warrior will play in the Super League for the first time in their history.[^cite_note-2] Kelantan The Real Warriors and PDRM were ejected from Super League due to failure to obtain a national license. Immigration FC rebranded to [Star City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_City_F.C.) for this season.[^cite_note-3]

## Foreign players

For the 2026–27 Malaysia Super League season, clubs can register a maximum of 15 foreign players, while the matchday rules allow up to 6 foreign players on the field at any one time. A maximum of 6 foreign players are permitted on the field simultaneously. Distributed as 4 open slots, 1 Asian (AFC) slot, and 1 ASEAN slot. Up to 3 additional foreign players are allowed on the substitutes' bench
