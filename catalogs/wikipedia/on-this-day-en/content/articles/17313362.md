The **General Staff of the Armed Forces of Ukraine** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Генеральний штаб Збройних сил України, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Ukrainian): *Heneralnyi shtab Zbroinykh syl Ukrainy*) is the [military staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staff_\(military\)) of the [Armed Forces of Ukraine](42799055.md). It is the central organ of the Armed Forces Administration and oversees operational management of the armed forces under the [Ministry of Defence of Ukraine](14284651.md).

The [chief of the General Staff of the Armed Forces of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_the_General_Staff_\(Ukraine\)) is appointed by the [president of Ukraine](20688479.md), who is the [supreme commander-in-chief of the Armed Forces of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Commander-in-Chief_of_the_Armed_Forces_of_Ukraine). On 28 March 2020 the position of [commander-in-chief of the Armed Forces of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander-in-chief_of_the_Armed_Forces_of_Ukraine) was separated from that of the chief of the General Staff. The current chief of the General Staff is [Ihor Skybiuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ihor_Skybiuk), and the Commander-in-Chief is [Mykhailo Drapatyi](78479295.md).

The General Staff was created in 1991–1992 on the basis of the [Kyiv Military District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyiv_Military_District) headquarters of the former [Soviet Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Armed_Forces).
