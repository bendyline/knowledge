**Qidong County** ([simplified Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 祁东县; [traditional Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 祁東縣; [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *Qídōng Xiàn*; lit. 'the east of [Qiyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qiyang_County)') is a [county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_China) and the [8th most populous county-level division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_County-level_divisions_of_Hunan_by_population) in the [Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_China) of [Hunan](166409.md), China; it is under the administration of [Hengyang prefecture-level city](1260021.md). The county was formed from the eastern part of Qiyang County in April 1952 and was named after the *east of [Qiyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qiyang_County)*. Qidong County covers an area of 1,872 km<sup>2</sup> (723 sq mi), and as of 2015, it had a registered population of 1,067,000.[^cite_note-population2015-1] The county has four [subdistricts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdistricts_of_China), 17 [towns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Towns_of_China) and three [townships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Townships_of_China) under its jurisdiction, and the [county seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_seat) is [Hongqiao Subdistrict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hongqiao_Subdistrict,_Qidong) (洪桥街道).[^cite_note-divison2014-2]

## Geography

Located in the central south of Hunan Province, the county is bordered to the north by [Hengyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hengyang_County) and [Shaodong Counties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaodong_County), to the west by [Shaoyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaoyang_County) and [Dong'an Counties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaoyang_County), to the south by [Lengshuitan District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lengshuitan_District) of [Yongzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yongzhou), [Qiyang County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qiyang_County), and to the east by [Changning City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Changning_City,_Hunan) and [Hengnan County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hengnan_County).

Qidong means the *east of Qiyang County*, the county was named after its location. It lies in the southwest of Hengyang and the northern bank of the middle reaches of the [Xiang River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiang_River). It is long and narrow from east to west, with higher altitudes in the north and low in the south. The terrain is tilted northwest to southeast, with the *Siming Mountains* encroaching on the west, and the *Qishan Mountains* stretching on the southwest, the generalized *Dayun Mountains* runs through the northeastern portion with the [county seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_seat) at its feet.[^cite_note-3] The [Xiang-Gui railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunan%E2%80%93Guangxi_railway), Xiang-Gui High-speed Railway, [G322 National Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_National_Highway_322), [G72 Quannan Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G72_Quanzhou%E2%80%93Nanning_Expressway), Hunan [provincial route](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provincial_route) S210 and S317 have passed through its territory. The county is known as the *Gorgeous Throat of Xiang-Gui*.[^cite_note-4]

## Economy

According to preliminary accounting of the statistical authority, the [gross domestic product](12594.md) of Qidong County in 2017 was 29,636 million [yuan](66958.md) (4,389 million US dollars), up by 8.9 percent over the previous year. Of this total, the value added of the primary industry was 6,840 million yuan (1,013 million [US dollars](18717338.md)), up by 3.2 percent, that of the secondary industry was 9,908 million yuan (1,467 million US dollars), up by 7.5 percent and that of the tertiary industry was 12,889 million yuan (1,909 million US dollars), up by 13.5 percent. The [value added](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Value_added) of the primary industry accounted for 23.08 percent of the GDP; that of the secondary industry accounted for 33.43 percent; and that of the tertiary industry accounted for 43.49 percent. The per capita GDP in 2017 was 29,827 yuan (4,418 US dollars).[^cite_note-8]
