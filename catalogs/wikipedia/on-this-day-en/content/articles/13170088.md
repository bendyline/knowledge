**Panticosa** (in [Aragonese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aragonese_language): *Pandicosa*[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]) is a municipality located in the [province of Huesca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huesca_\(province\)), [Aragon](39443.md), Spain.

During the late 19th century and early 20th century, it was a successful [spa town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spa_town), famous for its pure mountain waters which supposedly cured everything from [liver disease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liver_disease) to [herpes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herpes).&#x20;

Its [Belle Époque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belle_%C3%89poque) spa buildings are well-kept and still in use.

The first-ever [FAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F%C3%A9d%C3%A9ration_A%C3%A9ronautique_Internationale) World [Paragliding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paragliding) Hike and Fly [Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Paragliding_Championships) will take place in Panticosa from June 6 to June 13, 2027.[^cite_note-4]
