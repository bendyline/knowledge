The **speaker of the Council of Representatives of Iraq** is the presiding officer of that legislature. The position was preceded by the Speaker of the National Assembly of Iraq, up to the [Iraq War](5043324.md).

The role of the speaker is reserved for a [Sunni Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Islam_in_Iraq) as per ethno-sectarian quota to ensure diversity within Iraq's government.[^cite_note-Arab_Gulf_States_Institute-1] The role of first and second deputy speakers are respectively prearranged for a [Shia](22350732.md) and [Kurdish](35423251.md) politician as well.[^cite_note-BBC_Arabic-2]

[Haibat al-Halbousi](81981369.md) is the current incumbent speaker[^cite_note-3], being elected to the position on 29 December 2025.&#x20;
