**Cubarral** is a town and municipality in the [Meta Department](549697.md), [Colombia](5222.md). The municipality of [Guamal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guamal) borders Cubarral to the north, to the west is bordered by the department of [Cundinamarca](189405.md), to the east by the municipality of [San Martín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Mart%C3%ADn,_Meta) and to the south by the municipality of [El Dorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Dorado,_Meta). Furthermore, According to [DANE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Administrative_Department_of_Statistics_\(Colombia\)) projections from the 2005 census, Cubarral would have a population of approximately 5946 people by 2015.&#x20;

## Hydrography

There are a lot of rivers that cross the municipality of Cubarral, and according to National Environmental Licensing Authority (ANLA) in Spanish, some of the main watercourses are:

- Ariari river

- Humadea river

- Tonoa river

## Climate

Cubarral has a [tropical rainforest climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_rainforest_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *Af*) with heavy to very heavy rainfall year-round.
