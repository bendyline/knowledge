**Fakhar Zaman**[^cite_note-3] (pronounced [\[fəxər zəmaːɳ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Hindi_and_Urdu); born 10 April 1990) is a Pakistani international [cricketer](25675557.md) who plays for the [Pakistan national cricket team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_national_cricket_team).[^cite_note-ICC-4] A left-handed opening batter, he is noted for his attacking style of play.

He represents the [Lahore Qalandars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahore_Qalandars) in the [Pakistan Super League](38199013.md).[^cite_note-Bio-5] Zaman gained prominence after scoring a century in the [2017 ICC Champions Trophy Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_ICC_Champions_Trophy_Final), where Pakistan defeated India by 180 runs and he was named player of the match for his innings of 114 off 106 balls.[^cite_note-6]

On 20 July 2018, he became the first Pakistani batsman to score a [double century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_One_Day_International_cricket_double_centuries) in a [One Day International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Day_International) (ODI).[^cite_note-7] Two days later, he became the fastest batsman to reach 1,000 ODI runs.[^cite_note-Fastest1k-8] In August 2018, he was among 33 players awarded central contracts by the [Pakistan Cricket Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Cricket_Board) for the 2018–19 season.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

On 4 April 2021, while chasing against South Africa in the second ODI, Zaman scored 193 before being [run out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Run_out) by [Quinton de Kock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quinton_de_Kock). His 193 became the highest individual score in an ODI chase, surpassing [Shane Watson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_Watson)'s 185 against [Bangladesh](3454.md).[^cite_note-11] In November 2023, he hit 11 sixes against New Zealand during the [2023 Cricket World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Cricket_World_Cup), breaking [Imran Nazir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imran_Nazir_\(cricketer\))'s record for most sixes by a Pakistani in a World Cup innings.[^cite_note-12]

## Early and personal life

Fakhar was born on 10 April 1990 in [Katlang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katlang) in the [Mardan District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mardan_District) of [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md) into a [Pashtun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashtuns) family. The youngest among five brothers and two sisters, his father is a farmer while an elder brother is a teacher at Government High School Katlang.[^cite_note-13]

He moved to [Karachi](17123.md) at the age of 16 following [matriculation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matriculation).

In 2007, Zaman joined the [Pakistan Navy](1183789.md) as a sailor after receiving his schooling and training at sea from the [Pakistan Navy School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Navy_School), Bahadur.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15][^cite_note-Cricinfo-16] His choice of career was persuaded by his father, who was dissuaded by Fakhar's childhood addiction to cricket and wanted his son to focus on his academics.[^cite_note-Sundaresan-17] The name *Fakhar* literally translates to "pride".[^cite_note-Sundaresan-17] Amongst his teammates, he is known by the nickname *fauji* (soldier).[^cite_note-Cricinfo-16][^cite_note-Geo-18][^cite_note-19]

In 2012, he represented the navy in the [International Defence Cricket Challenge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Defence_Cricket_Challenge) Cup in Australia and was named best player of the tournament. Later in 2020, the navy awarded him the honorary rank of [Lieutenant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant), in recognition of his contribution to [cricket](25675557.md).[^cite_note-Siddique_2020-20][^cite_note-21]

## Domestic and franchise career

In Karachi, he continued to play cricket occasionally in inter-departmental matches, including representations for the Pakistan Navy cricket team. He was first identified by his navy coach, Azam Khan, who encouraged him to pursue professional cricket. In 2013, he left his naval career after a "tough decision" in pursuit of this dream and began appearing for regional outfits like [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khyber_Pakhtunkhwa_cricket_team), [Abbottabad Falcons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbottabad_Falcons), [Balochistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balochistan_cricket_team) and several Karachi teams. He became the second-highest run scorer in the [2016 Pakistan Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Pakistan_Cup) and also impressed selectors in the 2016–17 [Quaid-e-Azam Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quaid-e-Azam_Trophy). He was soon noticed by Pakistan coach [Mickey Arthur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mickey_Arthur) during a training camp in [Lahore](125315.md), following which he earned a call-up for the national side.[^cite_note-Geo-18]

In August 2017, he was named in [Durban Qalandars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durban_Qalandars)' squad for the first season of the [T20 Global League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T20_Global_League).[^cite_note-T20GL-22] However, in October 2017, [Cricket South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cricket_South_Africa) initially postponed the tournament until November 2018, with it being cancelled soon after.[^cite_note-PP-23]

In July 2019, he was selected to play for the Rotterdam Rhinos in the [inaugural edition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Euro_T20_Slam) of the [Euro T20 Slam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euro_T20_Slam) cricket tournament.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25] However, the following month the tournament was cancelled.[^cite_note-26]

In January 2021, he was named in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's squad for the [2020–21 Pakistan Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_Pakistan_Cup).[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28]

In December 2022, he was signed by the [Khulna Tigers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khulna_Tigers) for the [2022–23 Bangladesh Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%9323_Bangladesh_Premier_League).[^cite_note-29]

Zaman was picked by the [Lahore Qalandars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahore_Qalandars) side for the [2017 Pakistan Super League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Pakistan_Super_League), where he was closely mentored by New Zealand player [Brendon McCullum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendon_McCullum).[^cite_note-31] In his first three seasons, he experienced consistent setbacks as Lahore ended at the bottom of the league table each time, failing to qualify for the knockouts. Individually, he delivered decent performances, mount up to 739 runs, including a match winning performance of 94 runs in [2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Pakistan_Super_League).[^cite_note-32] He saw more success in the [2020 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Pakistan_Super_League) of the PSL, ending the tournament as the second highest run scorer.[^cite_note-33] His performance played a crucial role in Qalandar's journey to the finals, although they ultimately lost to [Karachi Kings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karachi_Kings).[^cite_note-34]

Zaman was released by Qalandars before the [2022 PSL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Pakistan_Super_League), although he was picked up again in the drafts.[^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36]

He led Qalandars to their first PSL title in 2022. He concluded the season as the highest run scorer, surpassing [Babar Azam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babar_Azam)'s record for scoring the most runs and achieving the most 50 plus scores in a single season.[^cite_note-37] In the same season, he also scored his first PSL century against Karachi Kings.[^cite_note-38]

In the [following season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Pakistan_Super_League), he scored his second PSL century against [Islamabad United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamabad_United) on 9 March 2023.[^cite_note-39] He also became the second batsman to reach 2000 runs in PSL.[^cite_note-40] His contributions led Qalandars to winning the second PSL title in a row.

## International career

### International debut

In March 2017, he was named in Pakistan's [limited overs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limited_overs_cricket) squad for their matches [against the West Indies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_cricket_team_in_the_West_Indies_in_2016%E2%80%9317).[^cite_note-Pak-LO-41] He made his [Twenty20 International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twenty20_International) (T20I) debut for Pakistan against the West Indies on 30 March 2017.[^cite_note-T20I-42]

In April 2017, he was named in Pakistan's [One Day International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Day_International) (ODI) squad for the [2017 ICC Champions Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_ICC_Champions_Trophy).[^cite_note-Champ-43] He made his ODI debut for Pakistan against [South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa_national_cricket_team) in the [Champions Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_Champions_Trophy) on 7 June 2017, scoring 31 runs.[^cite_note-44][^cite_note-45]

In the final against India, Zaman edged to the [wicket-keeper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wicket-keeper), when he was on 3, but survived as it was a no-ball, bowled by [Jasprit Bumrah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jasprit_Bumrah) and that was the biggest turning point in that match.[^cite_note-46] After that, Zaman went on to score his maiden ODI century and helped Pakistan post a total of 338. With that, Zaman became the first batsman for Pakistan to score a century in an ICC event final.[^cite_note-47] India batting second collapsed and Pakistan won the match by 180 runs to become the winners of [Champions Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_Champions_Trophy) for the first time. Zaman was adjudged [man of the match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Man_of_the_match) for his batting performance.[^cite_note-48] Zaman and [Azhar Ali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azhar_Ali)'s partnership in the match was 128 runs, making it the highest opening partnership in the Champions Trophy history for Pakistan.[^cite_note-49]

### 2018: England, Zimbabwe and Australia

In April 2018, he was named in Pakistan's [Test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Test_cricket) squad for their tours to [Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_cricket_team_in_Ireland_in_2018) and [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_cricket_team_in_England_in_2018) in May 2018, but he did not play.[^cite_note-IreEng-50]

On 8 July 2018, Fakhar scored 91 runs against [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia_national_cricket_team) in the [tri-series final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Zimbabwe_Tri-Nation_Series#Final) in [Harare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harare_Sports_Club) to help Pakistan secure the title. He was named man of the final and man of the series.[^cite_note-51][^cite_note-52] During his innings he also became the first Pakistani batsman to score 500 or more runs in T20Is in a calendar year.[^cite_note-53]

On 20 July 2018, in the fourth ODI [against Zimbabwe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_cricket_team_in_Zimbabwe_in_2018), he became the first batsman for Pakistan to [score a double century in ODIs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_One_Day_International_cricket_double_centuries), finishing 210 [not out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Not_out).[^cite_note-FZ200-54][^cite_note-55] In the same match, he and [Imam-ul-Haq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imam-ul-Haq) made the then [highest opening partnership in ODIs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_One_Day_International_cricket_records#Highest_partnerships_by_wicket), scoring 304 runs.[^cite_note-open304-56] His innings propelled Pakistan's total to 399, which was their [highest score in ODIs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Pakistan_One_Day_International_cricket_records#Highest_runs_in_an_inning).[^cite_note-Pak399-57]

Two days later, in the fifth match of the series, Zaman became the fastest player to score 1,000 runs in ODIs.[^cite_note-Fastest1k-8] He reached the milestone in 18 innings, beating the previous record of 21 innings, held by five other batsmen.[^cite_note-Fastest1kICC-58] Zaman went on to score 85 runs in the match, bringing his total to 515 runs in the series, the most by a Pakistan batsman in a bilateral ODI series.[^cite_note-515runs-59] Zaman and Imam had scored 705 runs together across the series, the most by a pair in a bilateral ODI series.[^cite_note-515runs-59] Zaman also recorded the most runs scored by a batsman between two dismissals in ODIs, with 455.[^cite_note-455runs-60]

In September 2018, he was named in Pakistan's [Test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Test_cricket) squad for their series [against Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_cricket_team_in_the_United_Arab_Emirates_in_2018%E2%80%9319).[^cite_note-PakTest-61] He made his Test debut for Pakistan against [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia_national_cricket_team) on 16 October 2018.[^cite_note-62] He made 94 and 66 on Test debut.[^cite_note-63]

### 2019: Cricket World Cup year

In April 2019, he was named in [Pakistan's squad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Cricket_World_Cup_squads#Pakistan) for the [2019 Cricket World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Cricket_World_Cup).[^cite_note-64][^cite_note-65] Ahead of the Cricket World Cup, in the ODI series [against England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_cricket_team_in_England_in_2019), Zaman scored 138 runs in the second ODI match. This was the highest individual total for a Pakistan batsman against England in a One Day International.[^cite_note-66] However, three days later, [Imam-ul-Haq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imam-ul-Haq) set a new record with 151 runs, in the third ODI of the series.[^cite_note-67]

### 2020–2022

In June 2020, he was named in a 29-man squad for Pakistan's [tour to England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_cricket_team_in_England_in_2020) during the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md).[^cite_note-68][^cite_note-69] However, on 23 June 2020, Zaman was one of seven players from Pakistan's squad to test positive for COVID-19.[^cite_note-70]

On 4 April 2021, in the second ODI [against South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_cricket_team_in_South_Africa_in_2020%E2%80%9321), Zaman scored 193 runs.[^cite_note-71] It was the highest individual score while chasing in an ODI match, surpassing 185 scored by [Shane Watson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_Watson) against Bangladesh.[^cite_note-72] Pakistan lost the ODI by 17 runs, with Fakhar's 193 being the second-highest score in an ODI defeat, behind [Charles Coventry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Coventry_\(Zimbabwean_cricketer\))'s 194 [not out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Not_out).[^cite_note-73] On 5 May 2021, Zaman was nominated for the [ICC's Player of the Month](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_Player_of_the_Month) award for April.[^cite_note-74] In September 2021, he was named as one of three travelling reserve players in Pakistan's squad for the [2021 ICC Men's T20 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_ICC_Men's_T20_World_Cup).[^cite_note-75]

In the annual [ICC Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_Awards) in January 2022, Fakhar Zaman was named in the [ICC Men's ODI Team of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_Men's_ODI_Team_of_the_Year) for the year 2021.[^cite_note-76]

In the [ICC Men's T20 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_Men's_T20_World_Cup) 2022, Zaman was ruled out of the event after he suffered a knee injury when playing against the Netherlands.[^cite_note-77]

### 2023 Cricket World Cup

Fakhar Zaman was selected in Pakistan's squad for the [2023 Cricket World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Cricket_World_Cup).[^cite_note-78] He failed to perform in the first game and was dropped from the team immediately. He returned to play against Bangladesh on 31 October, scoring match-winning 81 runs off 74 balls.[^cite_note-79] He was awarded Man of the Match for his spectacular performance. On 4 November 2023, Fakhar Zaman scored 126 runs from 81 balls against New Zealand scoring the 5th fastest century in the 2023 ICC Cricket World Cup and the 10th fastest overall in ODI World Cup history. He was also awarded Player of the Match for his performance, scoring 11 sixes and 8 fours.[^cite_note-80]

He was named as [ICC Men's Player of the Month](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_Awards#Men's_Player_of_the_Month) award for April 2023.

### T20 Series 2024

Before the arrival of world cup 2024, Pakistan played 3 series against New Zealand (home), Ireland (away) and England[^cite_note-81] (away). Fakhar selected for these series and led him to make his strong position in T2O World cup squad. He played two innings against New Zealand and scored 61 & 43 runs respectively, Where as against Ireland he played a brilliant innings and mark unbeatable 78 runs off 40 balls which led the team to win the first match of series. He also got a chance to play against England but unfortunately, failed to achieve a victory.

### 2024 Cricket World Cup

In May 2024, he was named in Pakistan's squad for the [2024 ICC Men's T20 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_ICC_Men's_T20_World_Cup) tournament.[^cite_note-82]

## Playing style

Fakhar Zaman is a left-handed opening batter known for his attacking style. He uses a high backlift and a strong bottom hand, which help him play powerful shots, especially through the off side.[^cite_note-83] He is considered an aggressive player in limited-overs cricket and often plays a leading role when Pakistan bats first.[^cite_note-84] His performances in the Pakistan Super League helped him become a regular opener for the national team.[^cite_note-85]

## Beyond cricket

### Cricket academy in Australia

In January 2024, Zaman established a cricket academy in [Melbourne](17306237.md), Australia. The inauguration event was attended by a large number of children who interacted with Zaman, participated in various activities, and took photographs with him.[^cite_note-86]

### Men's grooming brand launch

On 27 February 2024, Zaman launched his men's grooming brand, FZ39 BEAUTY, at the [Pearl Continental Hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pearl-Continental_Hotels_&_Resorts) in Lahore. The brand features organic and natural products for beard care, along with a signature fragrance named GORJ39 Perfume. The launch event was attended by Zaman's family members, teammates from Lahore Qalandars, and friends.[^cite_note-87]
