**Chivhu**, originally known as **Enkeldoorn**, is a small town in [Zimbabwe](34399.md), with an estimated population of 10,000 in 2007. It is located 146 km (91 mi) south of [Harare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harare) on the main road south to [Masvingo](447930.md) and South Africa.

## Name

Chivhu's original name, Enkeldoorn, is a [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_language) and [Afrikaans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afrikaans) word meaning "lone thorn".[^cite_note-1] It refers to the tree *[acacia robusta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acacia_robusta)* and implies that a single specimen once grew there. The name was adopted in 1891 but was changed to Chivhu in 1982, on the second anniversary of Zimbabwe's independence. The current name comes from the [Shona language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shona_language) and means "anthill".[^cite_note-2]

## History

Enkeldoorn was founded by Afrikaans-speaking Boer farmers and settlers in around 1850, and was the first [white](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_people) settlement in [Zimbabwe](34399.md). It became an [Afrikaner](18947965.md) stronghold in a predominantly English-speaking white [Rhodesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhodesia), giving it the nickname of 'the Republic of Enkeldoorn'.[^cite_note-3]

Chivhu has an agricultural economy, based in poultry farming and [dairy cattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dairy_cattle). Beef, pork, maize and [millet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millet) are also important produce.
