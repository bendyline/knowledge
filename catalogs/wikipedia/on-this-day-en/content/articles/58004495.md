The **Kampala–Gulu Highway**, also **Kampala–Gulu Road**, is a road connecting [Uganda](31816.md)'s capital city of [Kampala](56689.md), in the [Central Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Region,_Uganda), with the city of [Gulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulu), the largest urban centre in the country's [Northern Region](6067246.md).[^cite_note-2R-2]

## Location

The road starts in Kampala and continues north, through eight [Ugandan districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Uganda), and ends in Gulu, a distance of approximately 330 kilometres (205 mi).[^cite_note-1R-1]

The road passes through the districts of [ Kampala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kampala_District), [Wakiso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wakiso_District), [ Luweero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luweero_District), [Nakasongola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakasongola_District), [Kiryandongo](30339077.md), [Oyam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oyam_District), [Omoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omoro_District) and [Gulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulu_District).

## Overview

The road from Kampala to Gulu is old (first constructed in the 1940s), and narrow.[^cite_note-4R-3] Instead of the regulatory 7 metres (23 ft) of roadway, with shoulders and drainage channels, this road is only 6 metres (20 ft) in some sections, leaving little room for vehicles to overtake one another.[^cite_note-5R-4] The Kampala–Gulu Road, is one of the most accident-prone in the country, along with [Kampala–Jinja Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kampala%E2%80%93Jinja_Road), [Kampala–Masaka Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kampala%E2%80%93Masaka_Road) and the [Kampala Northern Bypass Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kampala_Northern_Bypass_Highway).[^cite_note-6R-5][^cite_note-7R-6][^cite_note-8R-7][^cite_note-9R-8][^cite_note-10R-9]

In 2016, the [Uganda National Roads Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uganda_National_Roads_Authority) (UNRA), committed to widen the narrow sections of this road.[^cite_note-5R-4]

## Intersections

Along its journey, the Kampala–Gulu Highway intersects with several major roadways, including the following: (a) at [Bwaise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bwaise), it cuts across the Kampala Northern Bypass Highway.[^cite_note-11R-10] (b) the 42 kilometres (26 mi) [Matugga–Kapeeka Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matugga%E2%80%93Kapeeka_Road) peels off the highway at [Matugga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matugga).[^cite_note-12R-11] (c) in [Wobulenzi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wobulenzi), the [Ziroobwe–Wobulenzi Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ziroobwe%E2%80%93Wobulenzi_Road) connects with this highway (d) the [Luweero–Butalangu Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luweero%E2%80%93Butalangu_Road) intersects with the Gulu highway in [Luweero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luweero). (e) the Kafu–Masindi Road, measuring 41 kilometres (25 mi),[^cite_note-13R-12] branches off the Gulu highway at Kafu, Uganda, in Kiryandongo District.[^cite_note-14R-13]

(f) At [Rwekunye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rwekunye), the [Rwekunye–Apac–Aduku–Lira–Kitgum–Musingo Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rwekunye%E2%80%93Apac%E2%80%93Aduku%E2%80%93Lira%E2%80%93Kitgum%E2%80%93Musingo_Road) meets the Kampala–Gulu Highway. (g) The 52 kilometres (32 mi)[^cite_note-15R-14] Masindi–Kigumba Road meets with the Gulu Highway. (h) in [Karuma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karuma), north of the Karuma Bridge, the [Karuma–Olwiyo–Pakwach–Nebbi–Arua Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karuma%E2%80%93Olwiyo%E2%80%93Pakwach%E2%80%93Nebbi%E2%80%93Arua_Road) makes a sharp left turn, off the Kampala-Gulu Highway.[^cite_note-16R-15] (i) Approximately 11 kilometres (7 mi)[^cite_note-17R-16] further north, at [Kamdini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamdini), the [Kamdini–Lira Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lira%E2%80%93Kamdini%E2%80%93Karuma_Road), makes a sharp right turn to continue on to [Lira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lira,_Uganda), 68 kilometres (42 mi) to the east.[^cite_note-18R-17]

In Gulu, this road interacts with the [Gulu–Nimule Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulu%E2%80%93Nimule_Road) and with the Gulu-Kitgum Road.[^cite_note-19R-18]

## Other considerations

When the [Kampala–Bombo Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kampala%E2%80%93Bombo_Expressway) is built, as expected, it will constitute the first 33 kilometres (21 mi) of the Kampala–Gulu Highway.[^cite_note-20R-19] From Kamdini, the Kampala–Gulu Highway continues north, for another 62 kilometres (39 mi), as the [Kamdini–Gulu Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamdini%E2%80%93Gulu_Road).[^cite_note-21R-20][^cite_note-22R-21]
