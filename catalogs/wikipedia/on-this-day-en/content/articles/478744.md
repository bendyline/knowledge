**Rostov Oblast**[^cite_note-9] is a [federal subject](701438.md) of [Russia](25391.md) (an [oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oblast)), located in the [Southern Federal District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Federal_District). The [oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oblast) has an area of 100,967 square kilometers (38,984 sq mi) and a population of 4,200,729 ([2021 Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Census_\(2021\))),[^cite_note-2021Census-6] making it the sixth most populous federal subject in Russia. Its [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center) is the [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) of [Rostov-on-Don](317562.md), which also became the administrative center of the Southern Federal District in 2002.

Rostov Oblast borders [Ukraine](31750.md) ([Donetsk](494889.md) and [Luhansk Oblasts](494904.md)) and also [Volgograd](478748.md) and [Voronezh Oblasts](473401.md) in the north, [Krasnodar](474125.md) and [Stavropol Krais](474074.md) in the south, and the [Republic of Kalmykia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Kalmykia) in the east. The Rostov oblast is located in the [Pontic-Caspian steppe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontic%E2%80%93Caspian_steppe). It is directly north over the [North Caucasus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Caucasus) and west of the [Yergeni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yergeni) hills.[^cite_note-GE-10]

It is within the Russian [Southern Federal District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Federal_District).

### Rivers and lakes

The [Don River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_River_\(Russia\)), one of [Europe](9239.md)'s longest rivers, flows through the oblast for part of its course. Lakes cover only 0.4% of the oblast's area.

## Administrative divisions

Main article: [Administrative divisions of Rostov Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Rostov_Oblast)

According to the Law "On the Administrative-territorial structure of the Rostov Region", the subject of the Russian Federation includes:

- 55 administrative-territorial entities:

- 12 urban districts

- 43 municipal districts and 408 administrative-territorial units:&#x20;

  - 18 urban settlements

  - 390 rural settlements.

Within the framework of the administrative-territorial structure, they include settlements and districts in the city.

The administrative center of the Rostov Oblast is the city of Rostov-on-Don.

## Public authority

The system of public authorities of the Rostov region, formed in accordance with the Charter, consists of:

- Legislative Assembly of the Rostov Oblast (the highest and only body of legislative (representative) power)

* Governor of the Rostov Oblast (the highest official of the Rostov region)

- The Government of the Rostov Oblast (the highest executive authority)

## History



[Tanais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanais), former ancient Greek city and medieval Italian trading city

Historically, at various times, the territory was ruled either entirely or partly by [Scythia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scythia), [ancient Greeks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Greeks), [Old Great Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Great_Bulgaria), [Khazars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khazars), [Kipchaks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kipchaks), Italians ([Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Pisa),[^cite_note-11] [Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Venice), [Genoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Genoa))[^cite_note-EB1911-12] the [Mongol Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongol_Empire), the [Crimean Khanate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crimean_Khanate), the [Ottoman Empire](22278.md),[^cite_note-EB1911-12] [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire) and [Soviet Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Ukraine).[^cite_note-day-13] The ancient Greek city of [Tanais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanais) is located in the province. [Taganrog](1288065.md) served as the capital of the [Ukrainian Soviet Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_Soviet_Republic) in March–April 1918,[^cite_note-day-13] and was the place of establishment of the [Communist Party of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Party_of_Ukraine_\(Soviet_Union\)).

The Rostov Oblast was formed in 1937 out of the [Azov-Black Sea Krai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azov-Black_Sea_Krai). During [World War II](32927.md), it was occupied by [Nazi Germany](21212.md) in 1941–1943.

### Ethnic groups

Residents identified themselves as belonging to 157 different ethnic groups, including 27 of more than 2,000 persons each. The largest ethnicities are[^cite_note-2010Census-14] the 3,795,607 [Russians](59510.md) (90.3%); the 110,727 [Armenians](387816.md) (2.6%) and the 77,802 [Ukrainians](145144.md) (1.9%). Other important groups are the 35,902 [Turks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_people) (0.9%); 16,493 [Belarusians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarusians) (0.4%); 13,948 [Tatars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatars) (0.3%); 17,961 [Azerbaijanis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijanis) (0.4%); 11,449 [Chechens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chechens) (0.3%); 16,657 [Romani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romani_people) (0.4%); 11,597 [Koreans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koreans) (0.3%); 8,296 [Georgians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgians) (0.2%), and 2,040 [Assyrians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assyrian_people) (.05%). There were also 76,498 people (1.8%) belonging to other ethno-cultural groupings. 76,735 people were registered from administrative databases, and could not declare an ethnicity. It is estimated that the proportion of ethnicities in this group is the same as that of the declared group.[^cite_note-20]

[Church of the Intercession, Rostov-on-Don](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_the_Intercession,_Rostov-on-Don)

The [Ascension Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novocherkassk_Cathedral) is the largest [Russian Orthodox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Orthodox) church in [Novocherkassk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novocherkassk), Rostov Oblast, [Russia](25391.md). It used to be one of the largest churches of the [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire) and the main church of the [Don Host Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Host_Oblast).

The five-domed building, which stands 75 meters tall, is a notable example of Russian [Neo-Byzantine architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neo-Byzantine_architecture). It was erected between 1891 and 1904 on the site of an earlier church. The first church on the site was built to [Luigi Rusca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luigi_Rusca)'s designs. It collapsed in 1846. A replacement church collapsed 17 years later.[^cite_note-23]

Church of the Intercession of the Holy Virgin ― one of the oldest churches in Rostov-on-Don. For a considerable period of time, Intercession Church served as the principal church not only for the fortress of St. Dimitry of Rostov but also for the people of local settlements. Since the end of the 18th century, the Church of Intercession had been considered to be a [cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathedral). The status changed in 1822, when [Church of the Nativity of the Blessed Virgin Mary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathedral_of_the_Nativity_of_the_Blessed_Virgin_Mary,_Rostov-on-Don) on the decree of the [Holy Synod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Synod) was declared cathedral.

[St. Nicholas' Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Nicholas'_Church_\(Bogdanov\)) in Bogdanov is one of only four churches in the Kamensky district of Rostov Oblast which have survived since the [October Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/October_Revolution). There is also [Church of St. Nicholas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_St._Nicholas_\(Elanskaya\)) in Elanskaya village.

[St. George's Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._George's_Church_\(Sandata\)) in Sandata village was built in the early 20th century.[^cite_note-24]

[St. Catherine Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Catherine_Church,_Krasnodonetskaya) in Krasnodonetskaya (1879) and [The Church of the Transfiguration of the Savior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_the_Transfiguration_\(Zaymo-Obryv\)) (1910) are officially declared as objects of [cultural heritage of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_heritage_of_Russia).

[Church of the Transfiguration (Olkhovchik)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_the_Transfiguration_\(Olkhovchik\)) was built in 1873.&#x20;

### Theatres



[Taganrog Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taganrog_Theatre)

- Rostov academic drama theatre, named after Maxim Gorky.

- [Rostov State Musical Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rostov_State_Musical_Theater). The theater opened in September 1999, and is the successor to the 1919 Rostov Musical Comedy Theater, one of the best operetta theaters in the Soviet Union.

The theater has two stages as well as a music and entertainment center, and hosts about 300 performances and concerts annually, as well as various forums and festivals. Its repertoire encompasses both musical traditions, as well as experiments in the field of contemporary art.

- Rostov state puppet theatre.

- Rostov regional academic youth theatre (former Rostov theatre for young spectators).

- [Taganrog Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taganrog_Theatre). The Taganrog Theater was established in 1827 by governor Alexander Dunaev. The theater was subsidized by the Taganrog's City Council since 1828, and its first director was Alexander Gor. The first group of Russian drama artists was directed by Perovsky and toured around the region, giving performances in Rostov on Don, Novocherkassk, Bahmut. The repertoire consisted mainly of dramas, melodramas and vaudevilles. In 1874, the Taganrog Municipality acquired the theater building by the purchase of its stocks.

- Don theatre of drama and Comedy V. F. Komissarzhevskaya (the Cossack drama theatre) Novocherkassk.

- Shakhty drama theatre, Shakhty.

- Novoshakhtinskiy drama theatre, Novoshakhtinsk.

## Economy

Major industries of Rostov Oblast are [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture), agricultural industry, food processing, heavy industry, [coal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coal) [mining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mining) and [automobile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automobile) manufacture.[^cite_note-26]

The largest companies in the region include [Novoshakhtinsk Petrochemical Plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novoshakhtinsk_Petrochemical_Plant?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Gloria Jeans Corp.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Jeans), [Rostvertol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rostvertol), [Oil-extracting factory "Yug Rusi"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oil-extracting_factory_%22Yug_Rusi%22?action=edit\&redlink=1), [North Caucasian Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Caucasian_Railway).[^cite_note-27]

In the Rostov Region in 1989, the first commercial bank in Southern Russia, [Rostovsoсbank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rostovsocbank), was created on the basis of the regional division of the [Zhilsotsbank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhilsotsbank?action=edit\&redlink=1) USSR.[^cite_note-28] The bank existed from 1989 to 1998,[^cite_note-29] with four branches operating in Rostov-on-Don. In addition, the bank had branches in the cities of Taganrog, Shakhty, Gukovo and Donetsk. In 1998 Rostovsotsbank lost solvency, but was able to ensure full repayment of deposits to all depositors before the establishment of the Deposit Insurance System, even before the revocation of the banking license.

From 1999 to 2010, one of the largest banks in Southern Russia was [Donskoy Narodny Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donskoy_Narodny_Bank) (*Don People's Bank*), located in Rostov-on-Don.

As of 2022, there are 7 regional credit organizations and 21 bank branches operating in Rostov Oblast. The leading positions are taken by [Sberbank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sberbank), [VTB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VTB_Bank), [Alfa-Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfa-Bank), [Rosbank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosbank), [Center-Invest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center-Invest?action=edit\&redlink=1) and [Fora-Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fora-Bank?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-30]
