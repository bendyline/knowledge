**Leyte's 1st congressional district** is one of the five [congressional districts of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congressional_districts_of_the_Philippines) in the province of [Leyte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leyte_\(province\)). It has been represented in the [House of Representatives of the Philippines](293605.md) since 1916 and earlier in the [Philippine Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippine_Assembly) from 1907 to 1916.[^cite_note-congleg-2] The district consists of the provincial capital, [Tacloban](746499.md), and adjacent municipalities of [Alangalang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alangalang,_Leyte), [Babatngon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babatngon), [Palo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palo,_Leyte), [San Miguel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Miguel,_Leyte), [Santa Fe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Fe,_Leyte), [Tanauan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanauan,_Leyte) and [Tolosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tolosa,_Leyte) since 1987. It is currently represented in the [20th Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/20th_Congress_of_the_Philippines) by [Martin Romualdez](45505193.md) of the [Lakas–CMD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakas%E2%80%93CMD) (Lakas), who served as the [House Speaker](7835723.md) from July 2022 to September 2025.[^cite_note-congmem-3]

Until 1931, it consisted of the sub-province of [Biliran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biliran) and the northwestern municipalities of [Albuera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albuera,_Leyte), [Baybay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baybay), [Calubian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calubian), [Leyte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leyte,_Leyte), [Merida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merida,_Leyte), [Ormoc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ormoc), [Palompon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palompon), [San Isidro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Isidro,_Leyte), and [Villaba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villaba). Following the creation of the [fifth district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leyte's_5th_congressional_district), Albuera, Baybay, and Ormoc were redistricted to the [second district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leyte's_2nd_congressional_district), while other municipalities were retained;[^cite_note-4] [Isabel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isabel,_Leyte), [Matag-ob](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matag-ob), and [Tabango](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tabango) were later established as new municipalities after the district was re-created in 1945. Following the creation of [Southern Leyte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Leyte) in 1959, these areas under this district were reapportioned to the [third district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leyte's_3rd_congressional_district), and the district was redefined to consist of the city of Tacloban and the eastern municipalities of [Abuyog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abuyog), Babatngon, [Dulag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dulag,_Leyte), [Javier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javier,_Leyte), [MacArthur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacArthur,_Leyte), [Mahaplag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahaplag), [Mayorga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayorga,_Leyte), Palo, San Miguel, Santa Fe, Tanauan, and Tolosa, all previously from the [fourth district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leyte's_4th_congressional_district), from 1961 until its second dissolution in 1972.[^cite_note-RA2227-5]

Together with [Negros Occidental's 3rd district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negros_Occidental's_3rd_congressional_district), Leyte's 1st district has seen two House Speakers, tied for the most by any district: [Daniel Z. Romualdez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Z._Romualdez) and his nephew, Martin Romualdez.
