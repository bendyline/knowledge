**Askar** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): مخيم عسكر) is a [Palestinian refugee camp](400347.md). It is located on the outskirts of the [West Bank](33209.md) city of [Nablus](233369.md) and was established in 1950 on 119 [dunums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunam) of land. Residents of the camp refer to this as “New Askar”.[^cite_note-2]

## History

### Ancient period

Askar is identified with Ein Sukkar, an ancient settlement featured in the [Mishnah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mishnah) and [Talmud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talmud), as well as in the [New Testament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Testament). Thanks to its fertility, the Ein Sukkar Valley is mentioned in [Rabbinic literature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabbinic_literature) as a place from which the grain was brought as a [wave offering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wave_offering) to the [Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_in_Jerusalem) when no barley was found in a place closer to [Jerusalem](16043.md).[^cite_note-3] In later years, a [Samaritan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samaritans) settlement was established on the site; according to ancient Samaritan writings, the town was inhabited by [Samaritan High Priests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samaritan_High_Priest).[^cite_note-4] A Samaritan [mausoleum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mausoleum), still in use during the fourth century CE, was found at the site.[^cite_note-\:0-5]

The name Askar preserves the ancient name of Ein Sukkar.[^cite_note-\:0-5]

Schenke believed that Askar was first settled during the early [Iron Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_ancient_Israel_and_Judah).[^cite_note-6] However, Campbell dated the settlement to the [Hellenistic period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hellenistic_period).[^cite_note-7]

### Modern history

During the [Second Intifada](71717.md) and 2002 [Operation Defensive Shield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Defensive_Shield), camps such as Askar were a source of considerable activity from Palestinian militants. IDF incursions are still common in Askar refugee camp and are generally conducted for the purposes of interrogating individuals or arresting suspected militants who Israeli authorities consider to be affiliated with listed [terrorist organisations](2646043.md).

The [UNRWA](32270.md) has several installations in Askar refugee camp including schools and health clinics. In addition to these, the camp has several of its own community centers including the Center of Peace and Development located in New Askar. International volunteer work camps are run at the center annually which are hosted by the [An-Najah National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/An-Najah_National_University).

According to the PCBC, the population of the camp is 11,304.[^cite_note-PrelimCensus2017-1]
