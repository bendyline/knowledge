The **women's singles** [badminton](3956.md) tournament at the [2026 Asian Games](51790406.md) will be held from 25 to 29 September 2026 at the Ichinomiya City Municipal Gymnasium in [Ichinomiya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ichinomiya,_Aichi), Japan.[^cite_note-1] [An Se-young](60664879.md) of [South Korea](27019.md) is the defending champion.

## Schedule

All times are Japanese Standard Time ([UTC+09\:00](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC+09\:00))

| Date | Day | Time | Event |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 15\:00 | Round of 32 |  |  |
| 26 September | Saturday | 09\:30 | Round of 32 |
| 15\:00 | Round of 16 |  |  |
| 27 September | Sunday | 09\:30 | Quarter-finals |
| 28 September | Monday | 09\:30 | Semifinals |
| 29 September | Tuesday | 11\:00 | Gold medal match |

## Seeds

The top eight Asian players were seeded based on the [BWF World Rankings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BWF_World_Rankings) published on 25 August 2026.

1. ** [An Se-young](60664879.md) ([KOR](84129937.md)) (champion)**

2.  [Wang Zhiyi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Zhiyi) ([CHN](83783500.md)) *(semi-finals)*

3.  [Akane Yamaguchi](37094638.md) ([JPN](81296528.md)) *(final)*

4.  [Chen Yufei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chen_Yufei) ([CHN](83783500.md)) *(semi-finals)*

5.  [Ratchanok Intanon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ratchanok_Intanon) ([THA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thailand_at_the_2026_Asian_Games)) *(quarter-finals)*

6.  [Putri Kusuma Wardani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Putri_Kusuma_Wardani) ([INA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesia_at_the_2026_Asian_Games)) *(third round)*

7.  [Tomoka Miyazaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomoka_Miyazaki) ([JPN](81296528.md)) *(third round)*

8.  [Pornpawee Chochuwong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pornpawee_Chochuwong) ([THA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thailand_at_the_2026_Asian_Games)) *(second round)*
