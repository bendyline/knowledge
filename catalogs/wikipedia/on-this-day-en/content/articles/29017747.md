The **National Assembly** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Asamblea Nacional*) is the [unicameral](204304.md) [legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_branch) of [Ecuador](9334.md). It replaced the [National Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Congress_\(Ecuador\)) in 2009 following reforms under the [2008 Constitution](18864296.md).[^cite_note-5] Within [Ecuador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics_of_ecuador), the National Assembly has the power to pass [laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislation), while appointment of judges to the [National Court of Justice](9338.md#Judicial_branch) is done by a separate Judicial Council.[^cite_note-6]

## Eligibility

According to Article 119 of the 2008 Constitution of Ecuador, candidates to the National Assembly must meet the following requirements:[^cite_note-7]

- Be an Ecuadorian national.

- Be at least 18 years of age at the time of registering for one's candidacy

- Be in possession of political rights
