The **2025 ICC Women's Cricket World Cup** was the 13th edition of the [Women's Cricket World Cup](3977980.md).[^cite_note-2] [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_Control_for_Cricket_in_India) hosted the World Cup for the fourth time, after the [1978](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1978_Women's_Cricket_World_Cup), [1997](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997_Women's_Cricket_World_Cup) and [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Women's_Cricket_World_Cup) editions, with the tournament held from 30 September to 2 November 2025.[^cite_note-Host-1] This was the last time the tournament had eight teams.[^cite_note-3] [India](2281524.md) became champions after defeating [South Africa](2281510.md) in the final, securing their maiden World Cup title.[^cite_note-4] [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia_women's_national_cricket_team) were the defending champions, but were knocked out in the semi-final by eventual champions India.

## Background

### Neutral venue arrangements

On 19 December 2024, following an agreement between the [BCCI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_Control_for_Cricket_in_India) and [PCB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Cricket_Board), the [ICC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Cricket_Council) confirmed that matches between India and Pakistan at ICC events in [2024–2027](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICC_Future_Tours_Programme#Tournaments_summary) will be played at neutral venues.[^cite_note-5] Similarly, in accordance with this agreement all of Pakistan's matches along with a few other matches were shifted to Sri Lanka.[^cite_note-6]

### Marketing

Before the commencement of the tournament, the ICC hosted a Trophy Tour, during which the trophy was taken to various locations across the four Indian host cities and in later to [Colombo](56636.md).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] The tour began on 11 August in Mumbai. ICC Chairman [Jay Shah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Shah), along with former cricketers [Mithali Raj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mithali_Raj) and [Yuvraj Singh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuvraj_Singh), and Indian cricketers [Harmanpreet Kaur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmanpreet_Kaur), [Smriti Mandhana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smriti_Mandhana), and [Jemimah Rodrigues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jemimah_Rodrigues), launched the Trophy Tour at a 50-day countdown event held in Mumbai.[^cite_note-9]

### Opening ceremony

The opening ceremony was held in [Assam Cricket Association Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assam_Cricket_Association_Stadium,_Guwahati), [Guwahati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guwahati) ahead of the tournament opener between India and Sri Lanka on 30 September.[^cite_note-10]

Indian singer [Shreya Ghoshal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shreya_Ghoshal) performed the official World Cup anthem, "Bring it Home".[^cite_note-11]

## Venues

It was originally planned that matches would be played at five different Indian venues,[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] with the [BCCI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_Control_for_Cricket_in_India) prioritising cities likely to enjoy favourable weather conditions and which were efficient in terms of transport and logistics.[^cite_note-15]

After Pakistan's qualification, it was agreed that their matches would be played outside India at a neutral venue.[^cite_note-16] In June 2025, the [ICC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Cricket_Council) announced the final venues in India and Sri Lanka.[^cite_note-venues-17] The cities of [Guwahati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guwahati), [Indore](365670.md), [Visakhapatnam](238760.md), and [Colombo](56636.md) (Sri Lanka) were initially announced as venues.[^cite_note-SL-18] The following month, the ICC replaced Bengaluru with the [DY Patil Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DY_Patil_Stadium) in [Navi Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navi_Mumbai).[^cite_note-revised-19]

One semi-final was played in Guwahati, while the other semi-final and the final were played in Navi Mumbai.

The [International Cricket Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Cricket_Council) (ICC) announced a prize fee of a total of \$13.88 million.[^cite_note-33] This marks a 297% increase in the total prize pool compared to the US\$3.5 million announced for the 2022 edition in New Zealand, and even surpasses the total prize money of the 2023 Men's Cricket World Cup. The winner's purse of US\$6.58 million also exceeds the US\$4 million awarded in the men's 2023 edition, highlighting a landmark commitment to gender equity in cricket.[^cite_note-34]
