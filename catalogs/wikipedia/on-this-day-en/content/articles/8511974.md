**Khinjan District** ([Dari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dari_language): ولسوالی خنجان; [Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto_language): خنجان ولسوالۍ) is one of the 16 [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Afghanistan) of [Baghlan Province](457216.md) in northeastern [Afghanistan](737.md). It has an estimated population of about 37,502 people,[^cite_note-population-1] with ethnic [Tajiks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tajiks) being the majority followed by [Pashtuns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashtuns), [Hazaras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazaras), [Uzbeks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzbeks), and others.[^cite_note-2]

Khinjan District is located in the southern part of the province in the [Hindu Kush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_Kush) mountains. The town of [Khenjan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khenjan) serves as the district's [administrative center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_center). The main [Kabul-Kunduz Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afghanistan_Ring_Road) passes through the district from south to west.&#x20;
