The **Khatam al-Anbiya Central Headquarters** (**KCHQ**; [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): قرارگاه مرکزی خاتم‌ الانبیا (ص)) is the operational headquarters of the [Islamic Republic of Iran Armed Forces](14305018.md). It coordinates operations for the [General Staff](50548383.md). It is tasked with planning and coordinating joint military operations within the Iranian forces.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] Its official spokesperson is [Ebrahim Zolfaghari](82829140.md).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

## History

In 2016, the KCHQ was separated from the General Staff as a standing independent command responsible for operational command and control (C2); previously, the KCHQ would only be stood up in wartime.[^cite_note-6]

In April 2024, both the KCHQ and by then-commander Major General [Gholam Ali Rashid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gholam_Ali_Rashid) were sanctioned by the [United Kingdom](31717.md)[^cite_note-7] and [Canada](5042916.md)[^cite_note-8] in response to [Operation True Promise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_2024_Iranian_strikes_on_Israel), the Iranian missile attack against Israel on 13 April, which Iran said was in retaliation to the [Israeli airstrike on the Iranian consulate in Damascus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_airstrike_on_the_Iranian_consulate_in_Damascus) on 1 April.

On 13 June 2025, during the [Twelve-Day War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve-Day_War), Rashid was killed in an [Israeli airstrike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve-Day_War#13_June). He was succeeded by Brigadier General [Ali Shadmani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_Shadmani). Four days later, Shadmani was also killed in an [Israeli airstrike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve-Day_War#17_June).[^cite_note-9] On 7 March 2026, during the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), its chief of staff, [Abolghasem Babaeian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abolghasem_Babaeian), who also was the military secretary of [Iran's supreme leader](36814344.md) was assassinated by Israel.[^cite_note-10]

In August 2026, according to a decree issued by the office of Supreme Leader [Mojtaba Khamenei](23324096.md) and reported by state media on 10 August 2026, Abdollahi was appointed chief of staff of the [Islamic Republic of Iran Armed Forces](14305018.md), with [Kioumars Heydari](52711558.md) named as his deputy; the same decree instructed him to complete the merger of the General Staff with the Khatam al-Anbiya Central Headquarters.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12]
