**Huize County** ([simplified Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 会泽县; [traditional Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 會澤縣; [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *Huìzé Xiàn*, old name: *Dongchuan 東川*) is a [county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_\(China\)), under the [jurisdiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurisdiction) of [Qujing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qujing) City, [Yunnan](166410.md) province, People's Republic of China.

## Demography

The city has grown considerably over the past 20 years. It has 871,200 inhabitants.

## Geography



Map including Huize (labeled as HUI-TSE (HWEITSEH) (TUNGCHWAN) 會澤) ([AMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Army_Map_Service), 1954)

Huize is in northeastern Yunnan and borders [Xuanwei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xuanwei) and [Weining County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weining_County), [Guizhou](173815.md) to the east, [Zhanyi District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhanyi_District) and [Xundian County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xundian_County) to the south, [Dongchuan District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dongchuan_District) and [Qiaojia County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qiaojia_County) to the west and [Ludian County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludian_County) to the north.

It is 120 km (75 mi) from the center of Qujing.

Huize is located between mountains with rice fields at one side and a large earth dam to the south. Some of the mountain areas are blasted for construction materials.

## Climate

Tempered by the low latitude and moderate elevation, Huize has a mild subtropical highland climate ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *Cwb*), with short, mild, dry winters, and warm, rainy summers. A great majority of the year's rainfall occurs from June to September.

## Tourist attractions

On 23 July 2023 a glass segment of touristic glass slide had completely dislodged, leaving a void at a height of 10 metres. It caused collisions between rafts but nobody was injured. A video of the accident was in the worldwide media.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]
