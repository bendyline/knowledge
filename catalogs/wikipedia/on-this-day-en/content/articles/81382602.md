**The Game Awards 2025** was an award show to honor the best [video games of 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_games_of_2025). It was the twelfth show hosted by [Geoff Keighley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoff_Keighley), creator and producer of [the Game Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards), and held with a live audience at the [Peacock Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peacock_Theater) in Los Angeles on December 11, 2025, and [live streamed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_stream) across online platforms globally. It featured presentations from celebrity guests including [David Harbour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Harbour), [Dan Houser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Houser), [Rahul Kohli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rahul_Kohli), and [Lenny Kravitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenny_Kravitz), and a musical performance from [Evanescence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evanescence).

*[Clair Obscur: Expedition 33](77750771.md)* led the ceremony with thirteen nominations and nine wins, the most in the show's history, including [Game of the Year](62662783.md), the first debut game to win this accolade. The Game Changer award honored [Girls Make Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girls_Make_Games), an organization supporting young girls to pursue careers in the [video game industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_industry). Several new games were announced, including *[Control Resonant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Control_Resonant)*, *[Divinity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divinity_\(upcoming_video_game\))*, *[Mega Man: Dual Override](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mega_Man:_Dual_Override)*, *[Tomb Raider: Catalyst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomb_Raider:_Catalyst)*, and *[Star Wars: Fate of the Old Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars:_Fate_of_the_Old_Republic)*. The show was viewed by over 171 million streams, the most in its history, though responses were mixed.

## Background

As with previous iterations of [the Game Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards), the 2025 show was hosted and produced by Canadian games journalist [Geoff Keighley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoff_Keighley).[^cite_note-Variety_Amazon-1] He returned as an executive producer alongside Kimmie Kim, while Richard Preuss returned as director, LeRoy Bennett as creative director, and Michael E. Peter as co-executive producer.[^cite_note-Variety_Nominees-2] Sydnee Goodman returned as host of the 30-minute preshow, titled Opening Act.[^cite_note-GamesRadar_Live-3] The presentation took place at the [Peacock Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peacock_Theater) in Los Angeles on December 11, 2025.[^cite_note-Engadget_Date-4] Public tickets became available for purchase on November 1.[^cite_note-TGA_Tickets-5] Keighley dedicated the show to [his father](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Keighley), who died in August; an empty seat was reserved in his honor, beside Keighley's mother.[^cite_note-GamesRadar_Live-3]

The event was [live streamed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_stream) across online platforms including [TikTok](56822861.md), [Twitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitch_\(service\)), [Twitter](9988187.md), and [YouTube](3524766.md), as well as [Amazon Prime Video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Prime_Video) for the first time.[^cite_note-Variety_Amazon-1] It was broadcast on a record number of platforms in China, including [Bilibili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilibili), [Huya Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huya_Live), and [Weibo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weibo).[^cite_note-Variety_Viewers-6] The Game Awards partnered with [Amazon](90451.md) for the show, creating a dedicated store for merchandise, deals, and new reveals. The Twitch broadcast was streamed in [1440p](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1440p) for the first time.[^cite_note-Variety_Amazon-1] Like the 2024 show, the Future Class initiative—an annual list of 50 individuals from the industry who best represent its future, introduced in 2020—did not return in 2025, and the list of previous inductees was removed from the show's website.[^cite_note-Game_Developer_Future_Class-7][^cite_note-Guardian_Future_Class-8]

### Announcements

Keighley sought to balance time dedicated to announcements and awards, wanting to satisfy both sides of the audience; he felt the reveals attracted new viewers and boosted attention to the winners.[^cite_note-Game_Business-9]<sup>: 5\:35 </sup> His team worked directly with developers to prepare trailers and schedule them at specific points in the show to create an emotional arc for viewers.[^cite_note-Game_Business-9]<sup>: 6\:55 </sup> Most of the three-hour show was focused on announcements, with approximately 17 minutes dedicated to awards.[^cite_note-TheGamer_Runtime-10] According to *[Kotaku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kotaku)*, publishers paid up to US\$450,000 for a 60-second trailer and over \$1 million for a three-minute slot.[^cite_note-Kotaku_Cost-11] *[Forbes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes)*'s Paul Tassi reported that *[Highguard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highguard)*'s developer did not pay for its reveal; the show's producers scheduled it as the final trailer.[^cite_note-Game_Rant_Highguard-12][^cite_note-TheGamer_Highguard-13]

## Winners and nominees

Nominees were announced on November 17, 2025;[^cite_note-VGC_Nominees-26][^cite_note-GameSpot_Nominees-27] the [Game of the Year](62662783.md) nominees leaked a few hours before the official announcement.[^cite_note-Kotaku_Nominees-28] Any game released for public consumption on or before November 21 was eligible for consideration,[^cite_note-GameSpot_FAQ-29] including [downloadable content](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downloadable_content) (DLC), expansions, [remakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_remake), [remasters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_remaster), and seasonal content.[^cite_note-TGA_FAQ-30] The nominees were compiled by a jury panel composed of members from 154 media outlets globally—a 14% increase from [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2024)'s 134, particularly from Europe and Latin America.[^cite_note-Polygon_Jury-31] Specialized juries decided nominees for categories like accessibility, adaptation, and [esports](564204.md).[^cite_note-GameSpot_FAQ-29] Winners were determined between the jury (90 percent) and public voting (10 percent);[^cite_note-Engadget_Nominees-32] the latter was held via the official website and [Discord](49271581.md) server[^cite_note-33] until December 10.[^cite_note-Deadline_Nominees-34][^cite_note-Gematsu_Nominees-35] The exception was the publicly voted Players' Voice, for which voting opened on December 1.[^cite_note-Engadget_Nominees-32] Millions of votes were cast within hours, causing website delays, which Keighley called "unprecedented";[^cite_note-USA_Today_Voting-36] 123 million votes were cast overall, a 10% increase from 2024.[^cite_note-Variety_Viewers-6] As with preceding years, a hub world was developed in *[Fortnite](57813623.md)* by Nighttimes and Evil Eye Pictures, allowing players and jury members to vote from November 21 for their favorite user-created islands among ten nominees; the winner was announced during the ceremony.[^cite_note-TGA_Fortnite-37]

2025 marked the first year in which multiple Game of the Year nominees were [independent games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_games),[^cite_note-Polygon_GOTY_Noms-38] representing a 50/50 split.[^cite_note-Game_Rant_GOTY-39] The six nominees sold a combined 22.3 million copies[^cite_note-Sales1-40] and each saw sales increases[^cite_note-42] after their nominations.[^cite_note-Eurogamer_Sales-41][^cite_note-Alinea_Sales_1-43] Three games—*[Clair Obscur: Expedition 33](77750771.md)*, *[Death Stranding 2: On the Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_Stranding_2:_On_the_Beach)*, and *[Ghost of Yōtei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghost_of_Y%C5%8Dtei)*—were nominated for all five major craft awards (Best Game Direction, Narrative, Art Direction, Score and Music, and Audio Design),[^cite_note-Polygon_Jury-31] and *Expedition 33* was the second game to receive three nominations in a single category (Best Performance) after [2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2016)'s *[Uncharted 4: A Thief's End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uncharted_4:_A_Thief's_End)*.[^cite_note-Game_Rant_E33-44] *[Megabonk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megabonk)*'s Best Debut Indie Game nomination was removed on November 18 at the request of its developer, who felt he did not qualify as it was not his debut game;[^cite_note-GameSpot_Megabonk-45][^cite_note-Kotaku_Megabonk-46][^cite_note-PC_Gamer_Megabonk-47] it was the first time a developer had willingly withdrawn a nomination, and the second rescission overall following the removal of two [fangame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fangame) nominations in 2016.[^cite_note-TheGamer_Megabonk-48] It was subsequently nominated in the 30-game first round of Players' Voice.[^cite_note-IGN_Megabonk-49]

The winners were announced during the awards ceremony on December 11, 2025.[^cite_note-IGN_Winners-50][^cite_note-NYT_Winners-51] *Clair Obscur: Expedition 33* won all but one of its jury-nominated awards, including Game of the Year and four of the major craft awards; it lost Best Audio Design to *[Battlefield 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battlefield_6)* and the fan-voted Players' Voice to *[Wuthering Waves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuthering_Waves)*.[^cite_note-IGN_GOTY-52][^cite_note-Polygon_GOTY-53][^cite_note-BBC_Winners-54] It was the first debut and first French-developed Game of the Year winner,[^cite_note-Le_Monde_Winners-55][^cite_note-Game_Rant_Macron-56] and the first to also be nominated in (and win) the indie game categories.[^cite_note-Polygon_A24-57] Most of the 30-person development team, Sandfall Interactive, flew from France and bought tickets to attend the show,[^cite_note-Kotaku_Cost-11][^cite_note-Australian_GOTY-58] each dressed in traditional French [marinière](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marini%C3%A8re) with red [berets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beret).[^cite_note-Le_Monde_Winners-55][^cite_note-GameSpot_GOTY-59] The game's engagement tripled after the show, with its most successful sales day since May and most cumulative Steam players (57,000) since June; more than 300,000 copies were sold in the following five days.[^cite_note-Alinea_Sales_2-24][^cite_note-IGN_Sales-60] The game saw a 160% weekly increase in players on December 13, of whom 17% were new. Of the other winners, *[South of Midnight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_of_Midnight)* and *[No Man's Sky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Man's_Sky)* saw a 32% and 14% increase in [daily active users](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daily_active_users) on the following weekend.[^cite_note-Game_Business_Stats-25]

*[Baldur's Gate 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldur's_Gate_3)*'s Best Community Support win was its eighth award overall (briefly breaking the show's overall record before *Expedition 33*'s ninth win) and its third consecutive year at the show (following its six wins in [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2023) and one in 2024).[^cite_note-TheGamer_Winners-61][^cite_note-PC_Gamer_GOTY-62][^cite_note-TheGamer_GOTY-63] [Nintendo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nintendo) won two awards, totaling 24 at the Game Awards overall, while *[Final Fantasy Tactics: The Ivalice Chronicles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Final_Fantasy_Tactics:_The_Ivalice_Chronicles)*'s win marked [Square Enix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_Enix)'s twelfth and *[Final Fantasy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Final_Fantasy)*'s tenth overall. [id Software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Id_Software) and [Supergiant Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supergiant_Games) received their third awards with *[Doom: The Dark Ages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doom:_The_Dark_Ages)* and *[Hades II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hades_II)*, having won two each with *[Doom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doom_\(2016_video_game\))* and *[Hades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hades_\(video_game\))* in 2016 and 2020, respectively. Several studios received their first award, including [Compulsion Games](52354550.md) for *South of Midnight* (its first nomination), Team Cherry for *[Hollow Knight: Silksong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollow_Knight:_Silksong)*, KOF Studio and [SNK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SNK) for *[Fatal Fury: City of the Wolves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatal_Fury:_City_of_the_Wolves)*, and [Kuro Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuro_Games) for *Wuthering Waves*. *No Man's Sky* won Best Ongoing Game for the second time, having done so in [2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2020).[^cite_note-TheGamer_Winners-61] *[Grand Theft Auto VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_VI)* is the second game to win Most Anticipated Game twice consecutively, after *[Elden Ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elden_Ring)* in 2020 and [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2021),[^cite_note-Game_Rant_GTA-64] and *[The Last of Us](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_of_Us_\(TV_series\))* is the first two-time Best Adaptation winner, having won for [its first season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_of_Us_season_1) in 2023.[^cite_note-TheGamer_Winners-61]

## Reception

### Nominees

Some viewers and journalists criticized the decision to discontinue the Future Class initiative.[^cite_note-Engadget_Nominees-32][^cite_note-BBC_Nominees-88][^cite_note-TheGamer_Future_Class-89][^cite_note-AV_Club_Future_Class-90] Several alumni felt they had been abandoned after not receiving responses from Keighley or Future Class organizer Emily Weir. Some suspected the initiative's discontinuation was in response to an [open letter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_letter) they wrote to Keighley [in 2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2023#Ceremony).[^cite_note-Game_Developer_Future_Class-7][^cite_note-Guardian_Future_Class-8] *[Polygon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polygon_\(website\))* writers criticized the show's categories: Austin Manchester felt the term "indie" was misapplied, considering *Clair Obscur: Expedition 33* and *[Dispatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dispatch_\(video_game\))* to be [double-A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double-A_\(video_game_industry\)) and [triple-I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triple-I_\(video_game_industry\)), respectively;[^cite_note-Polygon_Indie-91] Paulo Kawanishi considered the Best Role Playing Game category too broad due to the diversity of its nominees;[^cite_note-Polygon_RPG-92] and Giovanni Colantonio suggested the show implement smaller, more knowledgeable juries (like the [Academy Awards](324.md)) for categories like Best Mobile Game to better represent genres and mediums.[^cite_note-Polygon_Mobile-93] Some *Clair Obscur: Expedition 33* cast members called for a new category for [motion capture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_capture) actors,[^cite_note-VGC_E33_Mocap-94] and [Charlie Cox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Cox) credited his nomination to his character's motion-capture performer, Maxence Cazorla.[^cite_note-VGC_Cox-95]

Several journalists highlighted the lack of Game of the Year nominations for *[Blue Prince](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Prince)*, *Ghost of Yōtei*, *[Indiana Jones and the Great Circle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_Jones_and_the_Great_Circle)*, *[Silent Hill f](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silent_Hill_f)*, and *[Split Fiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Split_Fiction)*, which they attributed to the quality of the competition,[^cite_note-Polygon_Snubs-96][^cite_note-CBR_Snubs-97][^cite_note-GameSpot_Snubs-98] and felt *[ARC Raiders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARC_Raiders)*, *Dispatch*, *Donkey Kong Bananza*, and *South of Midnight* deserved additional nominations.[^cite_note-Polygon_Snubs-96][^cite_note-GameSpot_Snubs-98][^cite_note-Gamepressure_Snubs-99][^cite_note-Escapist_Snubs-100] Some thought *[The Hundred Line: Last Defense Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hundred_Line:_Last_Defense_Academy)* deserved recognition for its narrative[^cite_note-Polygon_Snubs-96][^cite_note-GameSpot_Snubs-98][^cite_note-Kotaku_Snub-101] and [Alex Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Jordan) for his performance in *[The Alters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Alters)*.[^cite_note-Polygon_Snubs-96][^cite_note-TheGamer_Snubs-102] The Best Adaptation category was criticized for its nominees' mixed reception; some felt *[Sonic the Hedgehog 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonic_the_Hedgehog_3_\(film\))* was snubbed, theorizing its December 2024 release date weakened its chances.[^cite_note-Kotaku_Snub-101][^cite_note-TheGamer_Snubs-102][^cite_note-ScreenHub_Nominees-103][^cite_note-TheGamer_Snub-104] Streamer [Shroud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shroud_\(gamer\)), who won Content Creator of the Year in [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2019) and was nominated for Trending Gamer in [2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2017), called the awards "rigged" after *ARC Raiders* was not nominated for Game of the Year,[^cite_note-Game_Rant_Shroud-105][^cite_note-IGN_Shroud-106][^cite_note-GamesRadar_Shroud-107] though journalists called these claims unfounded and noted the game had tough competition.[^cite_note-Polygon_Shroud-108][^cite_note-Kotaku_Shroud-109][^cite_note-Gamereactor_Shroud-110]

Keighley's post-show Twitter poll received mixed responses, with almost 39% of over 404,000 votes grading the show "D or below".[^cite_note-TheGamer_Grade-112][^cite_note-Destructoid_Grade-113] *Kotaku*'s Alyssa Mercante criticized the ticket prices, noting that nominees received only two tickets and were required to buy more—priced between \$58 for the upper [mezzanine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mezzanine) and \$1,000 for the rear orchestra—for additional attendees, who were often seated apart. She highlighted the complications in mixing developer and public seating, referencing [the 2022 stage interruption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2022#Stage_interruption).[^cite_note-Kotaku_Cost-11] Dozens of members of the [union](17626.md) United Videogame Workers protested outside the Peacock Theater to raise awareness of [recent industry layoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%932025_video_game_industry_layoffs), with some directing their message at [Phil Spencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Spencer_\(business_executive\)) and [Strauss Zelnick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strauss_Zelnick), the [chief executive officers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_executive_officers) of Microsoft Gaming and [Take-Two Interactive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Take-Two_Interactive), respectively.[^cite_note-Guardian_Protest-114][^cite_note-RPS_Verdict-115]

Several critics considered the show a celebration of smaller, independent games, citing *Clair Obscur: Expedition 33*'s awards;[^cite_note-Guardian_Review-19][^cite_note-Polygon_A24-57][^cite_note-NPR_Review-80] *Polygon*'s Oli Welsh compared its success to films like *[Everything Everywhere All at Once](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everything_Everywhere_All_at_Once)* and *[Oppenheimer](66850554.md)* leading the Academy Awards in [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/95th_Academy_Awards) and [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/96th_Academy_Awards), respectively.[^cite_note-Polygon_A24-57] *[GamesIndustry.biz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GamesIndustry.biz)*'s Rob Fahey suspected its success would lead to attempted emulations,[^cite_note-GamesIndustry_Review-116] though *Kotaku*'s Ethan Gach considered its record-breaking status "overblown" and thought it should have been ineligible for the indie categories[^cite_note-Kotaku_Review-117] and *[Bloomberg News](3057255.md)*'s [Jason Schreier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Schreier) felt its sweeping wins removed tension from the awards.[^cite_note-Bloomberg_Review-18] The [French president](24899.md), [Emmanuel Macron](43671127.md), congratulated the developers, calling it "a great pride" for [Montpellier](73369.md) and France.[^cite_note-Game_Rant_Macron-56][^cite_note-Eurogamer_Macron-118][^cite_note-PC_Gamer_Macron-119] *[Gamepressure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamepressure)*'s Matt Buckley found the in-person excitement "contagious", lauding the show's atmosphere.[^cite_note-Gamepressure_Review-120]

*[The Washington Post](102226.md)*'s Gene Park considered the ceremony more entertaining than previous years and praised the live staging but recommended different categories to celebrate fields like character and [level design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Level_design).[^cite_note-WP_Review-121] *Polygon*'s Claire Lewis criticized important awards being relegated to the preshow or announced in quick succession but applauded the lack of time restrictions for speeches and the quality of the announcements, writing, "Yes, it was a three-and-a-half-hour commercial. But it was a *good* three-and-a-half-hour commercial".[^cite_note-Polygon_Real-21] Some critics named independent game announcements, like *Bradley the Badger* and *Coven of the Chicken Foot*, among the show's highlights, as well as the *Resident Evil Requiem* and two *[Tomb Raider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomb_Raider)* trailers.[^cite_note-Guardian_Review-19][^cite_note-WP_Review-121][^cite_note-People_Biggest-122] *Kotaku*'s Gach found most announcements unmemorable.[^cite_note-Kotaku_Review-117] Several journalists considered the show's final reveal—*Highguard*, a [free-to-play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free-to-play) [first-person shooter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First-person_shooter) from a new studio—questionable or lackluster, with some viewers expecting an announcement from an established series or different genre,[^cite_note-Polygon_Highguard-123][^cite_note-Screen_Rant_Highguard-124][^cite_note-PC_Gamer_Highguard-125] though others found the trailer intriguing regardless.[^cite_note-Pure_Xbox_Highguard-126][^cite_note-Kotaku_Highguard-127][^cite_note-Game_Informer_Highguard-128]

### Viewership

An estimated 171 million viewers watched the ceremony, the most in the show's history and an 11% increase from the previous year; the figure does not include Prime Video viewers. According to *Streams Charts*, the ceremony peaked at 4.4 million concurrent viewers—the most in its history and a 9% increase from 2024—including 1.4 million viewers on the official YouTube broadcast (an 8% increase) and 1.8 million on Twitch. On YouTube, the ceremony peaked at 2.4 million total concurrent viewers (a 9% increase), including a record 8,600 co-streams.[^cite_note-Variety_Viewers-6] More than 16,500 creators co-streamed on Twitch—a record for the show, representing a 50% yearly increase—with total unique viewers and hours watched each increasing 5% from 2024.[^cite_note-Variety_Viewers-6][^cite_note-Eurogamer_Viewers-129] On Twitter, posts about the show increased by 12%, with more than 1.79 million posts from December 10–12, while the broadcast and related videos received over 60 million views.[^cite_note-Variety_Viewers-6] *[Eurogamer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurogamer)*'s Connor Makar noted the figures "overshadowed" the [97th Academy Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/97th_Academy_Awards), which received 19.7 million viewers in the United States.[^cite_note-Eurogamer_Viewers-129]
