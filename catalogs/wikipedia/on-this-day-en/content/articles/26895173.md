Russia has competed at the [Paralympic Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paralympic_Games) as different teams in its history. The nation competed as part of the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) at the [1988 Summer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988_Summer_Paralympics) and [Winter Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988_Winter_Paralympics), while after the [dissolution of the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissolution_of_the_Soviet_Union) in 1991, Russia competed as part of the [Unified Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unified_Team_at_the_Olympics) in 1992. The nation competed for the first time as *Russia* at the [1994 Winter Paralympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_Winter_Paralympics).

## Doping bans

On 7 August 2016, the Russian Paralympic Committee was banned from participating in the [2016 Summer Paralympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Summer_Paralympics) and the [2018 Winter Paralympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Winter_Paralympics) due to [the doping scandal in Russia](55980489.md), although in 2018 they were allowed to compete as the [Neutral Paralympic Athletes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutral_Paralympic_Athletes_at_the_2018_Winter_Paralympics). The decision to ban the entire Russian team contrasts the treatment of the [Russian Olympic team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia_at_the_2016_Summer_Olympics) during the [2016 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Summer_Olympics), whose members could compete if they were allowed by their sport's respective governing body.[^cite_note-bbc-appeallost-1]

On 9 December 2019, the [World Anti-Doping Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Anti-Doping_Agency) (WADA) banned Russia from all international sport for four years, after it was found that data provided by the [Russian Anti-Doping Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Anti-Doping_Agency) had been manipulated by Russian authorities with a goal of protecting athletes involved in its [state-sponsored doping scheme](55980489.md). Russian athletes would be allowed to participate in the Paralympic under a neutral flag and with a neutral designation.

Russia later appealed against the WADA decision in the [CAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court_of_Arbitration_for_Sport). On 17 December 2020, the CAS announced its decision, reducing the suspension to two years and allowing Russian athletes to participate under the flag of the Russian Paralympic Committee, rather than under a neutral flag, and use the Russian national colours.[^cite_note-2] For all victory ceremonies, [Pyotr Tchaikovsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyotr_Tchaikovsky)'s [Piano Concerto No. 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piano_Concerto_No._1_\(Tchaikovsky\)) will be used in lieu of the Russian national anthem.[^cite_note-3]

## Impact of the Russo-Ukrainian War

After the [2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine](79400268.md), the [International Olympic Committee](15147.md) (IOC) condemned Russia's "breach of the [Olympic Truce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_Truce) adopted by the UN General Assembly".[^cite_note-4] Following this, the [International Paralympic Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Paralympic_Committee) initially announced that the RPC team designation would be banned and that Russian athletes could only compete at the [2022 Winter Paralympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Winter_Paralympics) under a fully neutral designation as in 2018.[^cite_note-5] After boycott threats from other nations, the IPC on 3 March 2022 banned Russian athletes from competing entirely.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] On 16 November 2022, the IPC again suspended the RPC at an extraordinary meeting of the IPC General Assembly.[^cite_note-8]

On 29 September 2023, the IPC decided to continue to partially suspend both the [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Paralympic_Committee) and [Belarusian NPCs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarus_at_the_Paralympics) for two years. Their athletes and their support personnel may participate at the Paralympic Games under individual and neutral capacities.[^cite_note-9] In September 2025, the IPC voted to lift the bans on the Russian and Belarusian [National Paralympic Committees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Paralympic_Committee), with the countries returning to the Games in [2026](41045653.md).[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]
