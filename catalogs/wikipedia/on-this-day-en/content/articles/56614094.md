# Lulua River



The Lulua River in the Kasai catchment (center right)

**Lulua River** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Rivière Lulua*; [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_language): *Lulua Rivier*) is a river in the [Congo Basin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Congo_Basin) in Africa situated in the [Democratic Republic of Congo](76762.md). It is a right tributary of the [Kasai River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasai_River).[^cite_note-1]
