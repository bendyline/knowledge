The **Sevastopol Naval Base** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Севастопольская военно-морская база; [Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Севастопольська військово-морська база) is a [naval base](291782.md) located in [Sevastopol](51587.md), in the [disputed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_status_of_Crimea) [Crimean Peninsula](163045.md). The base is used by the [Russian Navy](383258.md), and it is the main base of the [Black Sea Fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Sea_Fleet). Internationally, the base is recognised as part of Ukrainian territory under Russian occupation.

## Geography

The Sevastopol Naval Base is almost completely located within the [administrative territory of Sevastopol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_and_municipal_divisions_of_Sevastopol). It has several berths located in several bays of Sevastopol – [Severnaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Severnaya_Bay?action=edit\&redlink=1) ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Северная бухта; [Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Північна бухта), [Yuzhnaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuzhnaya_Bay) ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Южная бухта; [Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Південна бухта), [Karantinnaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karantinnaya_Bay?action=edit\&redlink=1) ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Карантинная бухта; [Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Карантинна бухта) and others.

## History

### Tsarist Russia

The port was renovated in 1772, while the [Russo-Turkish War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russo-Turkish_War_\(1768%E2%80%931774\)) was still ongoing, and was finished in 1783, following the [annexation of Crimea by the Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annexation_of_Crimea_by_the_Russian_Empire). On 13 May 1783, the first eleven ships of the [Imperial Russian Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Russian_Navy) reached the [Sevastopol Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sevastopol_Bay).[^cite_note-poezdka-1]

During the [Crimean War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crimean_War) (1853–1856), all large ships were [scuttled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuttling) in the entrance to the bay in 1854 to prevent the entry of enemy ships into the bay. The city [defended itself for 349 days](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Sevastopol_\(1854%E2%80%931855\)) against the allied armies of [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_French_Empire), [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom_of_Great_Britain_and_Ireland), [Ottoman Empire](22278.md) and [Piedmont-Sardinia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Sardinia). Eventually, the Russians had to abandon Sevastopol on 9 September 1855.[^cite_note-poezdka-1]

On 29 October 1914, during a surprise attack as part of the [Black Sea raid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Sea_raid), the [Ottoman Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman_Navy) battlecruiser *Yavuz* (formerly *[SMS Goeben](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SMS_Goeben)* of the [Imperial German Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_German_Navy)) shelled the Sevastopol base and attacked various Russian ships in the vicinity.[^cite_note-2] Within days of this seemingly relatively militarily minor encounter, the [Ottoman Empire](22278.md) [formally joined](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman_entry_into_World_War_I) in [World War I](4764461.md), fighting against the [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire) and its [Entente allies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allies_of_World_War_I).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

### Soviet Russia

During [World War I](4764461.md), the [Imperial German Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_German_Army) [occupied](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Faustschlag) Sevastopol on 1 May 1918 despite the ongoing negotiations to reach the [Treaty of Brest-Litovsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Brest-Litovsk). After further negotiations, the most important ships of the [Black Sea Fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Sea_Fleet) in [Tsemes Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsemes_Bay) in front of [Novorossiysk](1190336.md) were sunk by their crews.[^cite_note-poezdka-1]

During the [Crimean campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crimean_campaign) (1941–1942) of [World War II](32927.md), the Black Sea Fleet of the [Soviet Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Navy) was able to fend off the first air attack by the [Nazi German](21212.md) [Luftwaffe](17885.md). However, after the city [defended itself for 250 days](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Sevastopol_\(1941%E2%80%931942\)), Sevastopol fell to the Germans on 4 July 1942.[^cite_note-poezdka-1]

### Ukrainian control

After the [dissolution of the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissolution_of_the_Soviet_Union) at the end of 1991, the former Soviet Black Sea Fleet initially came under jurisdiction of [United Armed Forces of the Commonwealth of Independent States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Armed_Forces_of_the_Commonwealth_of_Independent_States). The ownership over Black Sea Fleet as well as its home base in Sevastopol became a subject of [dispute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Sea_Fleet_dispute) between sovereign Russia and Ukraine.

In 1997, the Russian Federation and Ukraine signed the [Partition Treaty on the Status and Conditions of the Black Sea Fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partition_Treaty_on_the_Status_and_Conditions_of_the_Black_Sea_Fleet) which ended the dispute. Ukraine which allowed the Russian-allocated ships to remain on Ukrainian territory until 2017, sharing the Sevastopol Bay along with ships of the [Ukrainian Navy](1590554.md).

The lease arrangements were altered by the [2010 Kharkiv Pact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Kharkiv_Pact), which tied a 30% reduction in the price of [natural gas](22131.md) [charged by Russia since 2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Russia%E2%80%93Ukraine_gas_dispute) to the continued occupation of the naval base until 2042.[^cite_note-osw-5]

From then on, [Russia](25391.md) paid an annual lease to [Ukraine](31750.md) for the use of the base [until the annexation-eviction occurred in 2014](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annexation_of_Crimea_by_the_Russian_Federation).[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-osw-5]

### Russian control (2014–present)

Since the [annexation of Crimea by the Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annexation_of_Crimea_by_the_Russian_Federation) in 2014, the naval base is again under Russian control.[^cite_note-8]

#### Post-invasion of 2022

Main article: [Crimea attacks (2022–present)](71954377.md)

On 13 September 2023, [Storm Shadow](306908.md) missiles were used in a strike against the [port of Sevastopol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Sevastopol),[^cite_note-9] seriously damaging the [Kilo-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilo-class_submarine) submarine *[Rostov na Donu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_submarine_B-237)* and seriously damaging (according to some sources, beyond repair[^cite_note-10]) the [Ropucha-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ropucha-class_landing_ship) landing ship *[Minsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_landing_ship_Minsk)*.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

On 22 September 2023, at least three Storm Shadow missiles [hit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_missile_strike_on_the_Sevastopol_Naval_Base) Black Sea Fleet headquarters in Sevastopol, reportedly killing Admiral [Viktor Nikolayevich Sokolov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktor_Nikolayevich_Sokolov), commander of the Black Sea Fleet. Russia later released videos purporting to show him alive,[^cite_note-14] while Ukraine later said it was re-evaluating his claimed death.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]

On 25 September 2023, Russian authorities have announced that they would demolish the existing headquarters of the Black Sea Fleet.[^cite_note-17]
