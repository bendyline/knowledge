# List of largest comebacks in NBA games

&#x20; This is a list of the largest deficits overcome to win [NBA](22093.md) games, from the regular season or postseason. Wins after trailing by 25 points or more have been rare in NBA history, with only a few dozen out of tens of thousands of regular season games, and thousands of playoff games. A team may have a second-half comeback after having fallen well behind in the first half, or a fourth-quarter comeback after having fallen well behind with only one quarter of play remaining.

The greatest comeback in [National Basketball Association](22093.md) play occurred on November 27, 1996, when the [Utah Jazz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_Jazz), down by 36 points to the [Denver Nuggets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denver_Nuggets) late in the second quarter (it was 70–36 at the half and 70–34 just before), overcame this deficit to win 107–103.[^cite_note-1]

The largest comeback in a playoff game occurred in [2019](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_NBA_playoffs) when the Los Angeles Clippers overcame the Golden State Warriors 135–131 after trailing by 31 points. The largest comeback in the [NBA Finals](890793.md) was Game 4 of the [2026 Finals](82427430.md), when the [New York Knicks](72855.md) came back from a 29-point deficit to defeat the [San Antonio Spurs](72889.md) by a score of 107-106.

The following comebacks are sorted based on a points deficit basis:
