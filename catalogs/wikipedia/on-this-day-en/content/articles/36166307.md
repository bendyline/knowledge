The **secretary of public works and highways** ([Filipino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filipino_language): *Kalihim ng mga Pagawain at Lansangang Bayan*) is the head of the [Department of Public Works and Highways](4701882.md) in the national government of the [Philippines](23440.md), and is a member of the [president's Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_of_the_Philippines).[^cite_note-1]

The current secretary is [Vince Dizon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vince_Dizon) who assumed the post on September 1, 2025, on an *ad interim* basis as he has yet to be confirmed by the [Commission on Appointments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commission_on_Appointments).[^cite_note-2]
