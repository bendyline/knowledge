# Tunisian Forum for Economic and Social Rights

The **Tunisian Forum for Economic and Social Rights** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): المنتدى التونسي للحقوق الإقتصادية والإجتماعية; [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Forum tunisien pour les droits économiques et sociaux*, or **FTDES**) is a [non-governmental organization](46539.md) in [Tunisia](30188.md). FTDES studies and makes recommendations about such socioeconomic issues as protest movements, economic policy, unemployment, state violence, transitional justice, anti-corruption, marginalization of Tunisia's interior (west-central) regions, and the Mediterranean migration crisis.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-4]

## Origin

The organization cites the January 2008 [Gafsa Basin protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Tunisian_protests) as well as the [2011 revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunisian_Revolution) as inspiration for its work.[^cite_note-\:0-3]

## Leadership and structure

As of 2021, the FTDES executive director is [Alaa Talbi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alaa_Talbi).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

## Actions

Among the core activities of the FTDES is researching and publishing monitoring reports on current issues of social and economic concern in Tunisia.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10]

In 2015, the FTDES worked with families of missing youth who had migrated to Italy to pressure the government into forming a commission of inquiry about their fates.[^cite_note-11]

## Collaborations

FTDES is a member organization of the [EuroMed Rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroMed_Rights) network alongside other Tunisian organizations including the [Committee for the Respect of Freedom and Human Rights in Tunisia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_for_the_Respect_of_Freedom_and_Human_Rights_in_Tunisia?action=edit\&redlink=1) (CRLDHT), the [Tunisian Association of Democratic Women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunisian_Association_of_Democratic_Women) (ATFD), the [Federation of Tunisians for a Citizenship of the Two Shores](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federation_of_Tunisians_for_a_Citizenship_of_the_Two_Shores?action=edit\&redlink=1) (FTCR), and the [Tunisian Human Rights League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunisian_Human_Rights_League) (LTDH).[^cite_note-12] It also part of the [La Roujou3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Roujou3?action=edit\&redlink=1) ("No Return" or "Never Again") national awareness campaign for transitional justice with [Lawyers Without Borders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawyers_Without_Borders) and [Al Bawsala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Bawsala?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15] The Forum is also a member of the Legal Empowerment Network.[^cite_note-\:0-3]
