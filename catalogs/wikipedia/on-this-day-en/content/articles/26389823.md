The **ambassador extraordinary and plenipotentiary of the Russian Federation to the Republic of Armenia** is the official representative of the [president](239646.md) and the [government of the Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_the_Russian_Federation) to the [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Armenia) and the [government of Armenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Armenia).[^cite_note-KBS-1][^cite_note-2]

The ambassador and his staff work at large in the [Embassy of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassy_of_Russia_in_Yerevan) in [Yerevan](34352.md).[^cite_note-3] There is a [consulate general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consulate_general) in [Gyumri](1529357.md).[^cite_note-4] The post of Russian ambassador to Armenia is currently held by [Sergey Kopyrkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergey_Kopyrkin?action=edit\&redlink=1), incumbent since 6 April 2018.[^cite_note-5]

## History of diplomatic relations

Main article: [Armenia–Russia relations](13875647.md)

Diplomatic relations were briefly established between the [Russian Soviet Federative Socialist Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Soviet_Federative_Socialist_Republic) and the [Democratic Republic of Armenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Republic_of_Armenia) in 1920.[^cite_note-KBS-1] [Boris Legran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Legran) was the Russian representative between 28 July and 29 November 1920, prior to the Armenian republic's conquest by Soviet forces and its 1922 amalgamation into the [Transcaucasian Socialist Federative Soviet Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transcaucasian_Socialist_Federative_Soviet_Republic).[^cite_note-KBS-1] With the [dissolution of the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissolution_of_the_Soviet_Union) in 1991, the Republic of Armenia was established as an independent country, with diplomatic relations established with the Russian Federation on 3 April 1992.[^cite_note-6]
