**Leader of the Opposition** is a constitutionally sanctioned office in Guyana. The [Leader of the Opposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leader_of_the_Opposition) is elected among non-governmental members of the [National Assembly of Guyana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_Guyana).[^cite_note-hist-1][^cite_note-2] Usually the person comes from the largest opposition group in the [National Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_Guyana).&#x20;

The position was first established in 1966 by the [Constitution of Guyana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Guyana). Historically the leader was appointed by [Governor-General of Guyana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor-General_of_Guyana).
