**Tayr Debba** ([Levantine Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levantine_Arabic_language): طير دبّا, romanized: *Ṭayr Debbā*) is a municipality in the [Tyre District](11166788.md) in the [South Governorate](1283570.md) of [Lebanon](17771.md), located 7 kilometres east of [Tyre](30796.md) and 88 kilometers south of [Beirut](37428.md).[^cite_note-localiban-1] Its total land area consists of 578 [hectares](24919513.md) and its average elevation is 200 meters above sea level.[^cite_note-localiban-1] There are two schools, one public and the other private, in Tayr Debba which collectively enrolled a total of 607 students in 2006.[^cite_note-localiban-1]

## Etymology

According to [E. H. Palmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Henry_Palmer), *Teir Dubbeh* means "the fortress of the bear".[^cite_note-2]

## History

In 1881, the [PEF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_Exploration_Fund)'s [*Survey of Western Palestine* (SWP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PEF_Survey_of_Palestine) described it: "A village built of stone, containing 250 [Metawileh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metawileh), situated on a ridge surrounded by olives and fig-trees and arable land. There are three [cisterns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cistern) in the village."[^cite_note-3]

Tayr Debba is the hometown of the [Hezbollah](13919.md) commander [Imad Mughniyeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imad_Mughniyeh), who was assassinated in 2008.[^cite_note-4]

## Demographics

In 2014 [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Lebanon) made up 99.61% of registered voters in Tayr Debba. 99.57% of the voters were [Shiite Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanese_Shia_Muslims).[^cite_note-5]
