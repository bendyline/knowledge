# Heavyweight

For other uses, see [Heavyweight (disambiguation)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heavyweight_\(disambiguation\)).



Heavyweight boxers [Andrew Golota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Golota) (on the left) vs. [Kevin McBride](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_McBride) at [Madison Square Garden](75129.md)

**Heavyweight** is a [weight class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weight_class) in [combat sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_sport) and [professional wrestling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_wrestling). In [professional boxing](4283645.md), it is the maximum [weight category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weight_class), including all male boxers over 91 kg and female boxers over 81 kg.

## Boxing

### Professional

Male boxers who weigh over 200 [pounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pound_\(mass\)) (91 kg; 14 st 4 lb) are considered heavyweights by 2 of the 4 major professional boxing organizations: the [International Boxing Federation](241038.md)[^cite_note-1] and the [World Boxing Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Boxing_Organization).[^cite_note-2] In 2020, the [World Boxing Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Boxing_Council)[^cite_note-3] increased their heavyweight classification to 224 pounds (102 kg; 16 st) to allow for their creation of the [bridgerweight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridgerweight) division. The [World Boxing Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Boxing_Association) (WBA) did the same in 2023.[^cite_note-WBA_2023_ESPN-4] Female boxers who weigh over 175 [pounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pound_\(mass\)) (79 kg; 12 st 7 lb) are considered heavyweights by all four of the boxing federations.

#### Historical development

Because this division has no upper weight limit, it has historically been vaguely defined. In the 19th century, for example, many [heavyweight champions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_heavyweight_boxing_champions) weighed 170 pounds (77 kg; 12 st 2 lb) or less (although others weighed 200 pounds).

In 1920, the light heavyweight division was formed, with a maximum weight of 175 pounds (79 kg; 12 st 7 lb). Any fighter weighing more than 175 pounds was a heavyweight. The cruiserweight division (first for boxers in the 175–190 pound range) was established in 1979 and recognized by the various boxing organizations in the 1980s with a maximum weight of either 190 pounds (86 kg; 13 st 8 lb) or 195 pounds (88 kg; 13 st 13 lb). Later these organizations increased the cruiserweight limit to 200 pounds.

Since 1975, the [Amateur Athletic Union of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amateur_Athletic_Union_of_the_United_States) and the Soviet Sports Committee established a new concept in international boxing, called "Heavy Duals," an all-heavyweight team contest between the United States and the Soviet Union.[^cite_note-5]

As of 2025, [Wladimir Klitschko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wladimir_Klitschko) holds records of beating the most boxers for the world heavyweight title, with 23, and the [longest cumulative heavyweight title reign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_heavyweight_boxing_championship_records_and_statistics#Longest_title_reigns) of all time, with 4,382 days as world heavyweight champion.[^cite_note-12] [Joe Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Louis) has won the most world heavyweight title bouts, with 27,[^cite_note-boxing-records-8] and had the most consecutive title defenses, with 26.[^cite_note-13] This is also the record for most consecutive title defenses in boxing history.[^cite_note-14]

Four boxers have regained the heavyweight title in an immediate rematch: [Floyd Patterson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floyd_Patterson) in 1960, [Muhammad Ali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_Ali) in 1978, [Lennox Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lennox_Lewis) in 2001, and [Anthony Joshua](33343240.md) in 2019. [George Foreman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Foreman) holds the record for being the oldest heavyweight to ever achieve championship status, becoming champion at the age of 45, while [Mike Tyson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Tyson) possesses the record for youngest heavyweight champion at 20. Tyson also became the first heavyweight to own all three major belts – WBA, WBC, and IBF as well as The Ring and lineal heavyweight titles at the same time.

### Amateur

The lower limit for heavyweight was established in 1948 at 81 kg (178.6 lb). A weight class named "super heavyweight" was established in 1984, and with it a maximum 91 kg (200.6 lb) for the heavyweight division.

## Kickboxing

- In [kickboxing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kickboxing), a heavyweight fighter generally weighs between 88 and 100 kg (194 and 220 lb; 13 st 12 lb and 15 st 10 lb). The fighters over 100 kg (220 lb; 15 st 10 lb) are considered [super heavyweights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_heavyweight).

- [International Kickboxing Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Kickboxing_Federation) (IKF) Heavyweight (Pro & Amateur) 215.1–235 lb or 97.6–106.6 kg.

- In [Glory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glory_\(kickboxing\)) promotion, a heavyweight division is over 95 kg (209 lb) and no upper weight limit.

- In [ONE Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ONE_Championship), the heavyweight division has an upper limit of 120.2 kg (265 lb).

## Mixed martial arts

Main article: [Heavyweight (MMA)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heavyweight_\(MMA\))

The heavyweight division in [MMA](228344.md) generally groups fighters between 206 and 265 lb (93 and 120 kg).

Heavyweight is also the title of a documentary film that documented the fight camp of [Fabrício Werdum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fabr%C3%ADcio_Werdum) when he became the UFC Heavyweight Champion.[^cite_note-19]

## Wrestling

Main articles: [Wrestling weight classes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrestling_weight_classes) and [Professional wrestling weight classes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_wrestling_weight_classes)

The term "[world heavyweight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_wrestling_championship)" in modern wrestling generally refers to a champion wrestler who is seen as a prominent competitor, rather than an adherent to a particular weight class. The World Heavyweight Championship in wrestling is usually considered the main title in a given promotion. Prior to the wrestling industry publicly acknowledging the predetermined nature of the sport, a Heavyweight title was generally competed for by larger wrestlers while smaller wrestlers competed as (among other names and classifications) "[Junior Heavyweights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NWA_World_Junior_Heavyweight_Championship)", "Cruiserweights" and "Light-Heavyweights". The lucha libre promotion [Consejo Mundial de Lucha Libre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consejo_Mundial_de_Lucha_Libre) notably still has weight division for its champions. While most other promotions do not.

## Analogous uses

The word "heavyweight" is sometimes used in other fields (*e.g.* politics) to denote a person who is especially powerful or influential. Other boxing analogies include "punching above his \[their] weight" to denote a person or entity (*e.g.* a country) whose influence is arguably greater than his/its basic attributes would suggest.
