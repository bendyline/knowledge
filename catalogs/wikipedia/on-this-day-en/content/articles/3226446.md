The **Ministry of Defense** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): משרד הביטחון, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Hebrew): *Misrad HaBitahon*, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'Ministry of Security', acronym: [Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): משהב״ט) of the government of [Israel](9282173.md), is the governmental department responsible for defending the State of Israel from internal and external military threats. Its political head is the defense minister of Israel, and its offices are located in [HaKirya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HaKirya), Tel Aviv.

The Ministry of Defense oversees most of the [Israeli security forces](315904.md), including the [Israel Defense Forces](39237.md) (IDF), [Israel Military Industries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Military_Industries) (IMI), and [Israel Aerospace Industries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Aerospace_Industries) (IAI).[^cite_note-1]

The ministry was established when the [British Mandate of Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandate_for_Palestine) ended, and the [British Army](4887.md) departed [Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine_\(region\)) and the State of Israel was formed. This ended the rag-tag militia units during British rule and gave way to the formal defense of the Jewish state.

## Minister of Defense



Minister of Defense flag

The defense minister of Israel ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): שר הביטחון, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Hebrew): *Sar HaBitahon*, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'Minister of Security')[^cite_note-4] heads the ministry. The post is considered to be the second most important position in the [Israeli cabinet](5006115.md), and usually has a deputy minister. The defense minister is also a permanent member of the [Security Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security_Cabinet_\(Israel\)).

Due to the great importance of the defense portfolio, [prime ministers](24898.md) have often held the position in addition to their prime ministerial duties; eight of the twenty defense ministers to date were also serving prime ministers. Six of them ([Moshe Dayan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moshe_Dayan), [Yitzhak Rabin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yitzhak_Rabin), [Ehud Barak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ehud_Barak), [Shaul Mofaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaul_Mofaz), [Moshe Ya'alon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moshe_Ya'alon) and [Benny Gantz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benny_Gantz)) are also former [Chiefs of Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_of_General_Staff_\(Israel\)) of the Israel Defense Forces.

Amongst the duties of the post, defense ministers can request [administrative detention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_detention). Because of the intensive work and the tension between the political echelon to the military echelon, frequently disagreements and difference of opinion are created between the defense minister and the chief of staff.
