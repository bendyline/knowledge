**Rukuba** (also *Ungwan Rukuba*) is a district in [Jos North Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos_North) of [Plateau State](1723458.md) in the [Middle Belt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Belt) region of [Nigeria](21383.md).[^cite_note-Min-2] The postal code for the area is 930.[^cite_note-3]

## People and language

The indigenous people of the area are the Bache people who speak [Kuche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rukuba_language), a Ninzic language.
