# Sankuru River



Sankuru River (upper right) entering Kasai River, seen from space



The Sankuru River, in red

The **Sankuru River** ([Swahili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swahili_language): *Mto Sankuru*) is a major river in the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md). Its approximate length of 1,200 km[^cite_note-Brit-1] makes it the longest tributary of the [Kasai River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasai_River).

Above the confluence with its tributary [Mbuji-Mayi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mbuji-Mayi_River) it is also known as **Lubilash** or **Lubilanji**.[^cite_note-Brit-1] It flows northwards and then westwards crossing through a few towns, most notably [Lusambo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lusambo). Then it enters the Kasai River near [Bena-Bendi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bena-Bendi?action=edit\&redlink=1), at [4°17′S 20°25′E  /  4.283°S 20.417°E  / -4.283; 20.417](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Sankuru_River\&params=4_17_S_20_25_E_).
