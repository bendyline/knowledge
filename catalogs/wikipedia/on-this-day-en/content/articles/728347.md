**Imbabura** (Spanish pronunciation: [\[imbaˈβuɾa\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish)) is a [province](576127.md) located in the [Andes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andes) of northern [Ecuador](9334.md). The capital is [Ibarra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibarra,_Ecuador). The people of the province speak Spanish, and a large portion of the population also speak the Imbaburan [Kichwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kichwa) variety of the [Quechua language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quechua_language).

The summit of [Cotacachi Volcano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotacachi_Volcano) at an elevation of 4,944 metres (16,220 ft) is 15 kilometres (9.3 mi) north-east of the town of Cotacachi. The volcano is located in the large [Cotacachi Cayapas Ecological Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotacachi_Cayapas_Ecological_Reserve).

[Imbabura Volcano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imbabura_Volcano) is also located in the province. Best reached from the town of [La Esperanza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Esperanza,_Ecuador), the 4,609 metres (15,121 ft) high mountain can be climbed in a single day.

## Today

The governor of Imbabura is [Paolina Vercoutere Quinche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paolina_Vercoutere). She was appointed on 17 June 2022.[^cite_note-4]
