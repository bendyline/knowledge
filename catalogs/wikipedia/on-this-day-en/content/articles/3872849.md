**Churu district** is a district of the northern Indian [state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_and_territories_of_India) of [Rajasthan](26291.md). Churu lies in the [Sekhawati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sekhawati) region of northern [Rajasthan](26291.md) and shares boundaries with the [Hanumangarh District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanumangarh_District) to the north, the [Haryana](14189.md) state to the east, the [Jhunjhunu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jhunjhunu_District) and [Sikar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sikar_District) districts to the southeast, the [Nagaur District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagaur_District) to the south, and the [Bikaner District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bikaner_District) to the west.

The district has an area of approximately 16,830 km<sup>2</sup>, with a road length of 1901 km. As of 2011, the population of the district was approximately 2,039,547. The gender ratio is 938 females per 1,000 males; literacy among residents is 67.46%. There are 8 [tehsils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehsil) in the district: [Churu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Churu_\(tehsil\)), [Sidhmukh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidhmukh), [Ratangarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ratangarh,_Churu), [Taranagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taranagar), [Rajgarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajgarh,_Churu), [Sardarshahar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sardarshahar), [Sujangarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sujangarh), [Bidasar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bidasar,_Churu). The major crops include [bajra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pearl_millet) and [guar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guar).

## Attractions

[Sujangarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sujangarh) to the south, [Ratangarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ratangarh,_Churu) to the southwest, [Sardarshahar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sardarshahar) to the north, [Taranagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taranagar) to the northeast, [Rajgarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajgarh,_Kota) to the east, [Ratannagar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ratannagar), [Chapper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapper?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Bidasar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bidasar,_Churu) and [Rajaldesar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajaldesar) are some of the major towns in the district. The [Tal Chhapar Sanctuary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tal_Chhapar_Sanctuary), a [blackbuck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackbuck) sanctuary, has more than 1,680 black bucks and migratory birds. It is a major attraction for wildlife enthusiasts. Other attractions include the grand palatial [Havelies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haveli) in the towns of Ratannagar, Ratangarh and Sardarshahar, the Hanuman temple at [Salasar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salasar_Balaji) and the Venkateshwar temple at Sujangarh, the birthplace of local deity, [Gogaji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gogaji), in Dadrewa. The Bhadrakali Temple at Rajaldesar is popular among Shakta Tantra followers from all over India. The Baba Phool Nath Temple at Nawa, Rajgarh is popular and related to the Nath community.

According to the [2011 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_census_of_India), Churu district has a [population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_of_India) of approximately 2,039,547,[^cite_note-districtcensus-7] roughly equal to the nation of [Botswana](3464.md)[^cite_note-cia-8] or the US state of [New Mexico](21649.md).[^cite_note-9] This gives it a ranking of 224th in India (out of a total of [640 districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_India)).[^cite_note-districtcensus-7] The district has a population density of 148 inhabitants per square kilometre (380/sq mi).[^cite_note-districtcensus-7] Its [population growth rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_planning_in_India) over the decade 2001-11 was approximately 6.1%. Churu has a [sex ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_ratio) of 938 [females](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women_in_India) for every 1000 males, and a [literacy rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literacy_in_India) of about 67.46%.[^cite_note-districtcensus-7] 28.25% of the population lives in urban areas. Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes make up 22.15% and 0.55% of the population respectively.[^cite_note-districtcensus-7]

## Infrastructure

A majority of the district's power supply is provided by the Bhakra Hydel Complex through 132 kV lines coming via Hissar in Haryana. 902 of the district's 926 villages have access to electricity. Water is available at the depth of 30 to 48 metres, with the exception of places such as Sujangarh and Taranagar Tehsils.

The district has good connections within and outside the state. Mega Highway (Bathinda-Ajmer) National Highway Nos. 11 (Agra-Bikaner), 65 (Ambala-Pali), and 709 Ext. (Rohtak-Rajgarh) pass through the district. The total length of roads in the district is 3,010 km and is 240 km from Delhi via Hisar and 210 km. via Behal. The Churu district is served by broad gauge lines of the Northern-Western Railway. The total length of rail line is 363 km. The broad gauge is operational in the district. The nearest airport is at Jaipur (approximately 245 km outside the district).
