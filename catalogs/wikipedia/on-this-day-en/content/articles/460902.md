The **South Aegean Region** ([Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language): Περιφέρεια Νοτίου Αιγαίου, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Greek): *Periféreia Notíou Aigaíou* [\[periˈferia noˈtiu eˈʝeu\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Greek)) is one of the thirteen [administrative regions of Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_regions_of_Greece). It consists of the [Cyclades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclades) and [Dodecanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dodecanese) island groups in the central and southeastern [Aegean Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aegean_Sea).

## Administration

The South Aegean region was established in the 1987 administrative reform. With the 2010 [Kallikratis plan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kallikratis_plan), its powers and authority were redefined and extended. Along with the [North Aegean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Aegean) region, it is supervised by the Decentralized Administration of the Aegean based at [Piraeus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piraeus). The capital of the region is situated in [Ermoupoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ermoupoli) on the island of [Syros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syros). The administrative region includes 50 inhabited islands, including the popular tourism destinations of Mykonos, Santorini and Rhodes.[^cite_note-4]

Until the Kallikratis reform, the region consisted of the two [prefectures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefectures_of_Greece) of the [Cyclades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclades) (capital: [Ermoupoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ermoupoli)) and the [Dodecanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dodecanese) (capital: [Rhodes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhodes_\(city\))). Since 1 January 2011 it is divided into 13 [regional units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_units_of_Greece), formed around major islands:[^cite_note-Kallikratis-5]

## Demographics

The region was one in only two in Greece to grow in population between 2011 and 2021 along with Crete, adding a total of 15,527 people (increase of 5%).[^cite_note-2021census-1] It overtook [Epirus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epirus_\(region\)) and is presently the 9th largest region by population in Greece.

## Economy

The [Gross domestic product](12594.md) (GDP) of the province was 6.4 billion € in 2018, accounting for 3.5% of Greek economic output. GDP per capita adjusted for purchasing power was 22,400 € or 74% of the EU27 average in the same year. The GDP per employee was 79% of the EU average. South Aegean is the region in Greece with the second highest GDP per capita.[^cite_note-6]
