**Matanzas** (Latin American Spanish pronunciation: [\[maˈtansas\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Spanish)) is one of the [provinces of Cuba](239171.md). Major towns in the province include [Cárdenas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%A1rdenas,_Matanzas,_Cuba), [Colón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Col%C3%B3n,_Cuba), [Jovellanos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jovellanos,_Cuba) and the capital of the same name, [Matanzas](1386915.md). The resort town of [Varadero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varadero) is also located in this province.

Among Cuban provinces, Matanzas is one of the most [industrialized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrialise), with [petroleum](23195.md) wells, refineries, [supertanker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supertanker) facilities, and 21 [sugar](27712.md) mills to process the [harvests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvest) of the fields of [sugarcane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugarcane) in the province.

## Geography

The second largest province in [Cuba](5042481.md), Matanzas is largely flat, with its highest point (Pan de Matanzas) at only 380m above [sea level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_level).

The northwestern coast is largely rocky, with a few beaches, while the northeastern coast has numerous small [cays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cay) of its coast (part of [Sabana-Camaguey Archipelago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabana-Camaguey_Archipelago)), and [scrubland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scrubland) and [mangroves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangrove) near the shoreline. Cuba's northernmost point is located on the [Hicacos Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hicacos_Peninsula).

The southern coast has one of Cuba's most distinctive features: an enormous [marsh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marsh), [Ciénaga de Zapata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ci%C3%A9naga_de_Zapata), that covers both the southern part of the province and the [Zapata Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zapata_Peninsula). East of the peninsula lies the [Bay of Pigs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay_of_Pigs), the site of the failed U.S.-backed [invasion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay_of_Pigs_Invasion).

## Municipalities

From 1976 to 2010, Matanzas was subdivided into 14 municipalities.[^cite_note-3] Starting from 2011, the municipality of [Varadero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varadero) was abolished and merged to [Cárdenas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%A1rdenas,_Cuba). Thus, Matanzas now has 13 municipalities.

## Demographics

In 2004, the province of Matanzas had a population of 675,980.[^cite_note-atenas-4] With a total area of 11,802.72 km<sup>2</sup> (4,557.06 sq mi),[^cite_note-census2002-6] the province had a population density of 57.3/km<sup>2</sup> (148/sq mi).
