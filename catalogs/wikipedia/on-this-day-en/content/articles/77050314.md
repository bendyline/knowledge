**Expedition 74** was the 74th [long-duration expedition](9835533.md) to the [International Space Station](15043.md) (ISS). The expedition began with the departure of [Soyuz MS-27](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soyuz_MS-27) on 9 December 2025 with [NASA](18426568.md) astronaut [Michael Fincke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Fincke) taking over the ISS command and concluded with the undocking of [Soyuz MS-28](76567874.md) on 26 July 2026. It continued the extensive scientific research conducted aboard the ISS, focusing on various fields, including biology, human physiology, physics, and materials science. The crew members also maintained and upgraded the space station systems.

## Background, Crew, and Events

The expedition commenced with a crew consisting of [NASA](18426568.md) astronauts [Zena Cardman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zena_Cardman), [Michael Fincke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Fincke), and [Christopher Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Williams_\(astronaut\)), [JAXA](716184.md) astronaut [Kimiya Yui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kimiya_Yui), and [Roscosmos](406573.md) cosmonauts [Oleg Platonov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oleg_Platonov_\(cosmonaut\)), [Sergey Kud-Sverchkov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergey_Kud-Sverchkov), and [Sergey Mikayev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergei_Mikayev).[^cite_note-1]

On January 7, 2026, NASA announced that [SpaceX Crew-11](78935408.md) would return to Earth about a month early due to an undisclosed "medical situation" affecting one crew member.[^cite_note-Clark_2026-2][^cite_note-Medical_Issues_NYT-3][^cite_note-NASA_2026-02-25-4] The incident led to the cancellation of two planned [spacewalks](9792.md) on January 8 and 15, including preparations for installation of [ISS Roll-Out Solar Arrays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roll_Out_Solar_Array) (iROSAs) and other maintenance tasks on the [Harmony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmony_\(ISS_module\)) module and the station’s S6 and S4 truss.[^cite_note-5] NASA administrator [Jared Isaacman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jared_Isaacman) confirmed the early return on January 8.[^cite_note-ARS_2026-01-08_Decision-6]

On January 12, Fincke handed over command of the space station to Kud-Sverchkov. Crew-11 departed with Cardman, Fincke, Yui, and Platonov on January 14 approximately one month ahead of schedule.[^cite_note-Garcia_2026-7][^cite_note-NSF_Crew_11_Return_Early-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] Fincke later stated that he experienced the medical event, was stabilized by crewmates under the guidance of NASA [flight surgeons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_surgeon), and returned early to access advanced [medical imaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_imaging) unavailable on the station. After splashdown, he was treated at [Scripps Memorial Hospital La Jolla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scripps_Health) and later reported he was "doing very well".[^cite_note-NASA_2026-02-25-4]

[SpaceX Crew-12](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SpaceX_Crew-12) arrived at the ISS on February 14, transporting NASA astronauts [Jessica Meir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Meir) and [Jack Hathaway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Hathaway), [ESA](10363.md) astronaut [Sophie Adenot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophie_Adenot), and Roscosmos cosmonaut [Andrey Fedyaev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrey_Fedyaev).[^cite_note-NASA-11]

The expedition saw three spacewalks. On March 18, Meir and Williams conducted a 7-hour, 2-minute spacewalk to prepare the 2A power channel for future installation of another [iROSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roll_Out_Solar_Array) solar array.[^cite_note-Taveau_2026-12][^cite_note-Garcia_2025-03-18-13] On May 27, Kud-Sverchkov and Mikayev conducted a 6-hour, 5-minute spacewalk to install a solar experiment on the [Zvezda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zvezda_\(ISS_module\)) service module, remove science hardware from the [Poisk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poisk_\(ISS_module\)) and [Nauka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nauka_\(ISS_module\)) modules, and photograph the [Kurs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurs_\(docking_navigation_system\)) rendezvous antenna on the [Progress MS-33](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progress_MS-33) cargo spacecraft that had failed to deploy after its March launch.[^cite_note-\:1-14] On June 30, Williams and Meir conducted a 7-hour, 20-minute spacewalk to repair [Canadarm2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_Servicing_System), removing a spare wrist joint from [ESP2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/External_stowage_platform), swapping it for the broken one, and bringing the broken joint inside the station for return to Earth for inspection and potential refurbishment.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-\:2-16]

[Soyuz MS-29](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soyuz_MS-29) arrived at the station on July 14, transporting Roscosmos cosmonauts [Pyotr Dubrov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyotr_Dubrov) and [Anna Kikina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Kikina), along with NASA astronaut [Anil Menon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anil_Menon). After a two-week handover period, Kud-Sverchkov transferred command of the ISS to Meir on July 25. The expedition came to a close on July 26 with the departure of Kud-Sverchkov, Mikayev, and Williams on Soyuz MS-28.
