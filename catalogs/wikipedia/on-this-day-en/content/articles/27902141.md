The **Grand Council of Aargau** ([German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language): *Grosser Rat*) is the [legislature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature) of the [canton](180752.md) of [Aargau](2467.md), in [Switzerland](26748.md). Aargau has a [unicameral](204304.md) legislature. The Grand Council has 140 seats, with members elected every four years. The most recent election was in 2024.
