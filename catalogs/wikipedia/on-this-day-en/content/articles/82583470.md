**IRIS *Bushehr*** is the second ship of the [*Bandar Abbas* class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandar_Abbas-class_replenishment_ship) that are used as [fleet supply ships](1580755.md) and are operated by the [Islamic Republic of Iran Navy](2160969.md). It is named after the port city of *[Bushehr](205669.md),* home to an important naval base of [Iran Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran_Navy). The ship is operated as a combined tanker and store ship, which carries victuals, armaments, and general stores. The vessel does not have facilities for [replenishment at sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Replenishment_at_sea) (RAS).[^cite_note-1]

During the [2026 Iran war](82533582.md), the ship and her crew of 208 sailors was interned by the [Sri Lankan Navy](6186841.md) at [Trincomalee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trincomalee) and the crew were transferred to Welisara Naval Base.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]
