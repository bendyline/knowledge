**Wau Airport** is a civilian [airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airport) that serves the city of [Wau](3888859.md), South Sudan and surrounding communities. [Feeder Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feeder_Airlines) has three return flights a week between [Khartoum](17320.md)-[Wau](3888859.md)-[Juba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juba,_South_Sudan).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] [Southern Star Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Star_Airlines) used to provide service on Wednesdays and Sundays to [Juba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juba,_South_Sudan); however, this service has been suspended in 2011 when the airline ceased flying.[^cite_note-4]

## Location

Wau Airport ([IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IATA_airport_code): **WUU**, [ICAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICAO_airport_code): **HSWW**) is located in *Wau County*, [Western Bahr el Ghazal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Bahr_el_Ghazal), in the city of [Wau](3888859.md). The airport is located to the northeast of the [central business district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_business_district) of the city.

This location lies approximately 511 kilometres (318 mi), by air, northwest of [Juba International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juba_Airport), the largest airport in [South Sudan](32350676.md).[^cite_note-5] The geographic coordinates of Wau Airport are: 7° 72' 58.12"N, 27° 97' 31.00"E (Latitude: 7.7250; Longitude: 27.9800). This airport sits at an elevation of 433 metres (1,421 ft) above sea level. In December 2012, a new paved runway measuring 2,500 metres (8,200 ft) in length was opened.

## Accidents and incidents

- [2017 South Supreme Airlines Antonov An-26 crash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_South_Supreme_Airlines_Antonov_An-26_crash): occurred on 20 March 2017

- 2 September 1991 – [Lockheed L-100-20 Hercules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_L-100_Hercules) N521SJ of [Southern Air Transport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Air_Transport) was taxiing at [Wau](21081541.md) and hit a landmine. The crew of five survived but the aircraft was a writeoff.

  [^cite_note-6]

- 2 December 2025 – Samaritan's Purse Cessna 208 hijacking: a gunman demanding to be flown to Chad hijacked a [Cessna Grand Caravan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cessna_Grand_Caravan) aircraft owned by [Samaritan's Purse](294814.md) that was carrying out a humanitarian relief mission from [Juba](1514879.md) to [Maiwut County](34546573.md). He was arrested after the pilot made a refueling stop at Wau Airport.
