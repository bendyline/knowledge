**Touggourt** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): ولاية تقرت) is an Algerian [province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Algeria) *([wilaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilayah))* created in 2019, previously, a delegated wilaya created in 2015. It is in the Algerian Sahara.

## Geography

Touggourt Province is located in the [Algerian Sahara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algerian_Desert) and has an area of 17,428 km². Its main settlement and capital, [Touggourt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Touggourt), is located on an oasis.

It is delimited by:

- to the north by the [El M'Ghair Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_M'Ghair_Province), [ouled Djellal Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouled_Djellal_Province);

- to the east by the [El Oued Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Oued_Province);

- to the west by the [Djelfa Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djelfa_Province), [Ghardaia Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghardaia_Province);

- and to the south by the [Ouargla Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouargla_Province), [Illizi Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illizi_Province) .

## History

The wilaya of Touggourt was created on November 26, 2019.[^cite_note-2]

Previously, it was a [delegated wilaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delegated_wilaya?action=edit\&redlink=1), created according to the law n° 15–140 of May 27, 2015, creating administrative districts in certain wilayas and fixing the specific rules related to them, as well as the list of municipalities that are attached to it. Before 2019, it was attached to the [Ouargla Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouargla_Province).

## Organization of the wilaya

During the administrative breakdown of 2019, the delegated wilaya of Touggourt is made up of 5 [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Algeria) and 13 [communes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_Algeria).
