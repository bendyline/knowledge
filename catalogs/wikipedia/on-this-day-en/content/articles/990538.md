**Serian Division** is a administrative division in [Sarawak](28258.md) which separated on 11 April 2015 from [Samarahan Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samarahan_Division). It is about 64 kilometres (40 mi) from [Kuching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuching). Sub-district headquarters towns are [Balai Ringin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balai_Ringin) and [Tebedu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tebedu).

Serian Division is known for its [durians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durian). This has prompted the Serian District Council to erect a giant monument to this "king of fruits" in the middle of the market square. The town is connected to the [hinterland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinterland) by road and by water. Most produce is brought in from Kalimantan, Indonesia as the prices are cheaper there.

## Geography

Limestone formations and [paddy fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paddy_field) can be seen along the way to [Tebedu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tebedu). Other attractions of Serian include *Gua Tiab Bilanting* which is situated at Kampung Pichin and also *Bukit Parang* which is located on the Sarawak-[Kalimantan](165470.md) border. The southernmost point in Malaysia, Bukit Niat, is also located on the border between Serian Division and Kalimantan, southeast of Tebedu, at a latitude of 0°51'10" N.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

There are many waterfalls found in Serian District. *Ranchan Waterfall* is located at about 5 km (3.1 mi) from Serian town and is easily accessible by road. *Simuja Waterfall* is another waterfall in Serian but was damaged badly by logging activities. It is about 7 km (4.3 mi) away from town. There is no proper road to the waterfall and visitors have to hike 1–2 km (0.62–1.24 mi) from the Serian-Sri Aman trunk road to get there.

Tebedu (formerly known as Bandar Mutiara), located about 50 km (31 mi) away from Serian town on the [Indonesia-Malaysia border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesia-Malaysia_border), is one of only three official land [border crossings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_crossing) between Indonesia and Malaysia.

[Tebakang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tebakang), a small town approximately 12 km (7.5 mi) away from Serian, is home to Mayang Tea plantation, Sarawak's sole tea producer.

## Climate

Serian has a [tropical rainforest climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_rainforest_climate) (Af) with heavy to very heavy rainfall year-round.
