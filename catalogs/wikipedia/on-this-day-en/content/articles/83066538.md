The following is a **list of the highest-grossing biographical films** of all time, ranked by worldwide box office revenue. [Biographical films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biographical_films), commonly known as biopics, depict the lives of real people. The rankings are based on each film's lifetime worldwide theatrical gross as reported by reliable box office sources and are not adjusted for inflation.
