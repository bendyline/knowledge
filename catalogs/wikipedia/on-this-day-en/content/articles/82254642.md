On 28 January 2026, **SATENA Flight 8849**, a scheduled [domestic flight](30208174.md) in Colombia from [Camilo Daza International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camilo_Daza_International_Airport) in [Cúcuta](623811.md) to [Aguas Claras Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aguas_Claras_Airport) in [Ocaña](2511569.md), crashed in Curasica, [Playa de Belén](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Playa_de_Bel%C3%A9n), [Norte de Santander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norte_de_Santander). The [Beechcraft 1900](2192224.md) operating the flight was carrying 13 passengers and two crew members. All 15 occupants were killed.[^cite_note-1]

## Background

### Aircraft

The aircraft involved in the accident was HK-4709, a [Beechcraft 1900D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beechcraft_1900D) manufactured in 1994 and operated by SEARCA for [SATENA](2094254.md). The aircraft had 32,000 flight hours at the time of accident.[^cite_note-2]

### Passengers and crew

15 people were on board at the time of the accident, consisting of two crew members and 13 passengers. Among the passengers were [Diógenes Quintero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Di%C3%B3genes_Quintero), a member of the [Chamber of Representatives](645347.md), and Carlos Salcedo, a candidate running in the [March 2026 congressional election](79604693.md).[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] The pilot had more than 10,000 hours of flying experience.[^cite_note-5]

## Accident

The plane took off from [Camilo Daza International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camilo_Daza_International_Airport) in [Cúcuta](623811.md) at 16\:42 UTC.[^cite_note-6] The last contact was made at 16\:54 UTC near the [Venezuelan border](47414233.md).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] The wreckage was discovered in a rugged, densely covered area of the [Cordillera Oriental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cordillera_Oriental_\(Colombia\))[^cite_note-10] known as Curasica in Playa de Belén.[^cite_note-11] The plane was completely destroyed upon impact and no survivors were reported. The tail section and a chunk of fuselage remained intact.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

## Investigation

The accident is being investigated by the [Colombian Civil Aviation Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombian_Civil_Aviation_Authority). A preliminary report was released on 10 February.[^cite_note-DIAAC_prelimiary_report-14]
