**Kirovske** (in US intelligence, *Kirovskoye*)[^cite_note-1] ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Кировское; [Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Кіровське) is a [Russian Aerospace Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Aerospace_Forces) base located in [Kirovske Raion](4814619.md), near the town of [Kirovske](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirovske,_Crimea), in [Russian occupied Crimea](71944314.md), Ukraine.

## History

The base is home to a detachment of the [929th State Flight Test Centre named for V. P. Chkalov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/929th_State_Flight_Test_Centre_named_for_V._P._Chkalov).[^cite_note-2]

Kirovske was the primary [anti-submarine warfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-submarine_warfare) (ASW) test and development center for [Soviet Naval Aviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Naval_Aviation), and it worked closely with the flight test center at [Akhtubinsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akhtubinsk_\(air_base\)).[^cite_note-akh82-3]

An interceptor regiment, the 136 IAP (136th Fighter Aviation Regiment) at Kirovske operated the [Sukhoi Su-9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukhoi_Su-9) (ASCC: Fishpot) in the 1960s and 1970s.[^cite_note-phaseout81-4] These were last seen at Kirovske in August 1979 before the runway was closed for expansion in the early 1980s.[^cite_note-phaseout81-4] Other aircraft such as the [Mikoyan-Gurevich MiG-15](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikoyan-Gurevich_MiG-15) (ASCC: Fresco) and [Sukhoi Su-7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukhoi_Su-7) (ASCC: Fitter) were known to be present at Kirovske in the 1970s.[^cite_note-5]

The base was used by the 326th Fighter Aviation Regiment between 1950 and 1979.[^cite_note-6]

In April 2024, the UK's [Ministry of Defence](203793.md) (MoD) reported that Kirovske had [Su-30](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Su-30) fighter [decoys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_dummy) painted on its [hardstands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hardstand) and that at least a dozen more Russian air bases had painted similar decoys. The MoD believed the decoys were a reaction to Ukrainian attacks following the [Russian invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8]
