**Hangu District** ([Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto_language): هنګو ولسوالۍ, [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): ضلع ہنگو) is a [district](639402.md) within the [Kohat Division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kohat_Division) of the [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md) province, Pakistan. It is located close to the [Pak-Afghan border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pak_Afghan_border) with the [Khost Province](457249.md), [Afghanistan](737.md). The district takes its name from the town of [Hangu](3620886.md), which is its administrative centre. The name Hangu may also sometimes be applied to the [Miranzai Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miranzai_Valley) which is partly within the district, bordering the [Samana Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samana_Range).

## History

From 1540 to 1893, Hangu was ruled by two prominent figures: the Malak Khails and the [Khans of Hangu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khan_of_Hangu). The Malak Khails were centrally located in the [Darsamand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darsamand) and Mammu regions. Historically, nomads from Afghanistan would travel southwards during the winters. However, this free movement was curtailed after the 1970s due to heightened border controls.

On 30 June 1996, Hangu District was carved out from a section of the [Kohat District](4466450.md). The demarcation of its territory commences from the village of Khawaja Khizer (Jawzara), a point line between the Kohat and Hangu Districts. The 13th and the final Khan of Hangu was Muzaffar Khan Bangash. Today, his descendants live in various districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa including Hangu.

As of the [2023 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Pakistani_census), Hangu district has 61,148 households and a population of 528,902. The district has a sex ratio of 96.91 males to 100 females and a literacy rate of 43.15%: 66.04% for males and 22.02% for females. 152,504 (29.01% of the surveyed population) are under 10 years of age. 85,727 (16.21%) live in urban areas.[^cite_note-2023_census-1] [Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto) was the predominant language, spoken by 99.59% of the population.[^cite_note-4]

## Constituencies

The district comprises two constituencies for the [Provincial Assembly of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provincial_Assembly_of_Khyber_Pakhtunkhwa).[^cite_note-mpa-11] The [Pakistan Muslim League (N)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Muslim_League_\(N\)) (PML-N) has an overwhelming majority here. NA-9 and now NA-16 have been the stronghold of the party. Maulvi Naimatullah, [Syed Ifthikhar Hussain Gilani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syed_Iftikhar_Hussain_Gillani) and [Javed Ibrahim Piracha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javed_Ibrahim_Paracha) have won consecutively since 1985. In the 2002 elections, PML-N did not run a candidate, but in 2008, Dr. Farooq Bangash, the PML-N NA-16 Candidate, who technically could not contest elections being British Citizen, surrendered to [ANP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awami_National_Party) Syed Haider Ali Shah, who won marginally from [Muttahida Majlis-e-Amal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muttahida_Majlis-e-Amal) (MMA). Basically it was Bangash's strategy to ensure a Pir Haider win. Now the majority and whole control for PTI, In Local body election PTI also gain the main power, and there is still strong opposition of JUI.[^cite_note-12]

## Education

Various public and private schools and colleges exist in district of Hangu. Hangu population is near by 0.8 million people and they demand for university in 2013. Agriculture University campus was to be built in Hangu City but still the project is pending while funds transfer to Charsada University during ANP government. Recently the government degree college Hangu started a BS Hons 4-year program under [Kohat University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kohat_University_of_Science_and_Technology).
