**Floods in Afghanistan** start in March and continue until May. Out of 34 provinces in [Afghanistan](737.md), 21 are vulnerable to [floods](50482.md). The western region and central belt are at the highest risk. However, the southwest and a few northern provinces ([Hirat](312559.md), [Ghor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghor_Province), [Urozgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urozgan_Province), [Jozjan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jozjan_Province), [Balkh](457218.md), and [Faryab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faryab_Province)) can be severely affected by both [flooding](50482.md) and [drought](37335.md).

Many Afghan provinces are affected by multiple hazards, and lie under the Multi Hazard High Risk Zone. [Earthquakes](10106.md) and [landslides](37208.md) are of concern in the northern regions. Being mountainous, these areas have poor accessibility due to [geographical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topography) conditions and [harsh weather](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inclement_weather) that further increase the vulnerability of populations living in these areas. Also, the remote central region of Afghanistan experiences recurrent floods and droughts. The southern region is primarily drought-prone.

Although as of 2016, no precise records existed in Afghanistan, it has been assumed that [drowning](219367.md), fatal [injuries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Injury), [venomous snakebites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venomous_snake), [electrocutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrocution), and [contamination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contamination) by [toxic materials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toxin) occur at much greater than reported rates.[^cite_note-1]

Sudden massive flooding caused by [rivers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River) overflowing, [glacial lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glacial_lake) outbursts, or the collapse of [dams](51518.md) can cause many deaths due to drowning and related traumatic injuries.[^cite_note-2] Massive flooding affects the community as to education, [water](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drinkable_water) and [sanitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanitation), agriculture, general [health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health), and social life, which in general will lead to a decline of the community. There is a need to focus on early warning, early recovery and community empowerment.[^cite_note-3]
