The **2026 Chevron Championship** was the 55th [Chevron Championship](1527628.md), an [LPGA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LPGA) golf tournament held April 23–26 at [Memorial Park Golf Course](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memorial_Park_Golf_Course) in [Houston, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houston,_Texas). The tournament was in its fifth year with [Chevron Corporation](284749.md) as the title sponsor and its 44th year as a [major championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_major_golf_championships).

[Nelly Korda](54748975.md) led wire-to-wire to win her second Chevron Championship and third major. The win also propelled her to the number one [world ranking](4197326.md).[^cite_note-1]

## Round summaries

### First round

*Thursday, April 23, 2026*

[Nelly Korda](54748975.md) took a two shot lead, opening with a bogey-free round of 65 (−7). [Lee So-mi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_So-mi) and [Patty Tavatanakit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patty_Tavatanakit) both shot 67 to tied for second place. Defending champion [Mao Saigo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mao_Saigo) shot a 73 (tied for 59th) and world number one [Jeeno Thitikul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeeno_Thitikul) shot a 74 (tied for 80th). Two amateurs were in the top-10, [Farah O'Keefe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farah_O'Keefe), the 2025 [Women's Amateur Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_Amateur_Championship) runner-up and [Yang Yun-seo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yang_Yun-seo?action=edit\&redlink=1), the 2025 [Women's Amateur Asia-Pacific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_Amateur_Asia-Pacific) champion.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-leaderboard-5]
