The **2029 World Athletics Championships** ([Swahili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swahili_language): *Mashindano ya Dunia ya Riadha ya 2029*) will be the twenty-second edition of the [World Athletics Championships](218926.md), and will be held in 2029 in [Nairobi](21482.md), [Kenya](188171.md). This will be the first time the event will be held in Africa.[^cite_note-1]

## Host selection and venue

Four cities were in the running to host.

- [Germany](11867.md) [Munich](19058.md), [Germany](11867.md) ([Olympiastadion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympiastadion_\(Munich\)))

  [^cite_note-2]

- [Kenya](188171.md) [Nairobi](21482.md), [Kenya](188171.md) ([Kasarani Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moi_International_Sports_Centre))

  [^cite_note-3]

- [Italy](14532.md) [Rome](25458.md), [Italy](14532.md) ([Stadio Olimpico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stadio_Olimpico))

  [^cite_note-4]

- [United Kingdom](31717.md) [London](17867.md), [United Kingdom](31717.md) ([London Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Stadium))

  [^cite_note-5]

On 15 September 2026, Nairobi was selected to host the 2029 World Athletics Championships.[^cite_note-6] Nairobi previously bid for [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_World_Athletics_Championships) but lost out the Tokyo.
