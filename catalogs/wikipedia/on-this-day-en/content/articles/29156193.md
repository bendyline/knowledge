**Al Minhad Air Base** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): قاعدة المنهاد الجوية, [IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IATA_airport_code): **NHD**, [ICAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICAO_airport_code): **OMDM**, also just **Minhad Air Base**[^cite_note-DoD-FLIP-ENAME-1]) is a military installation in the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md). The base is located approximately 15 miles (24 km) south of [Dubai](211583.md) and is operated by the [United Arab Emirates Air Force](10815770.md).&#x20;

It is the headquarters of the [Australian Defence Force](67538.md)'s Joint Task Force 633 and supports Australian operations in the Middle East. A 2010 US diplomatic cable said that the base was "a critical hub for Coalition/[ISAF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Security_Assistance_Force) partners in Afghanistan, including the Australians, Dutch, Canadians, Brits and Kiwis."[^cite_note-2]

As of March 2024 the airbase is host to the permanent British Support Operations facility *Donnelly Lines*.[^cite_note-3]

## Facilities

The airfield resides at an [elevation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elevation) of 172 ft (52 m) above [mean sea level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean_sea_level). It has one [runway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runway), 09/27 which has an [asphalt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asphalt_concrete) surface measuring 12,901 ft × 150 ft (3,932 m × 46 m),[^cite_note-DoD-FLIP-ENAME-1] and a parallel taxiway with a width of 38 m (125 ft).[^cite_note-4]

## British Armed Forces

In January 2013, the UK's [No. 906 Expeditionary Air Wing RAF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No._906_Expeditionary_Air_Wing_\(United_Kingdom\)) was stood up at the base.[^cite_note-5] 906 Wing was an expansion of the [RAF](25679.md)'s presence. Prior to Christmas 2012, [No. 6 Squadron RAF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No._6_Squadron_RAF) had exercised their [Eurofighter Typhoons](167667.md) from the UAE.&#x20;

The base was being used extensively by the RAF during the [Operation Pitting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Pitting), the UK's evacuation of [Kabul](16826.md) in 2021, with an [Airbus KC2 Voyager](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbus_Voyager) used for long-haul back to [RAF Brize Norton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAF_Brize_Norton) and the [Boeing C-17A Globemaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C-17A_Globemaster_III#Royal_Air_Force) and [Lockheed C-130J Hercules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Martin_C-130J_Super_Hercules) operating between Kabul and Minhad Air Base.

In March 2024, [HRH The Princess Royal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne,_Princess_Royal) opened an "expansive" new permanent facility called **Donnelly Lines** to support British operations in the region. The facility contains a headquarters and accommodation.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7]

## Coalition use

Further information: [Military intervention against ISIL order of battle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_intervention_against_ISIL_order_of_battle)

Several foreign countries allied to the United Arab Emirates are believed to have made use of Al Minhad Air Base since the early 2000s to support the logistics supply chain for their military operations in [Afghanistan](737.md). Use of the Al Minhad Air Base is a sensitive matter for the Government of the United Arab Emirates, which imposes a diplomatic agreement stating that the militaries of foreign governments not advertise the host nation nor location of their operations in the United Arab Emirates due to "local sensitivities" about allowing a foreign military presence within its borders.

-  [Australia](4689264.md) - The [Australian Defence Force](67538.md) (ADF) has used Al Minhad as their main transport and logistics hub in the Middle East since the withdrawal of Australian combat forces from Iraq in 2008. The RAAF had operated AP-3C Orions from the base from 2003 to support operations in the region. In 2008, an agreement was signed allowing Australia to command its regional headquarters there.

  [^cite_note-8]

  &#x20;At Al Minhad, the ADF maintains a facility named Camp Baird.

  [^cite_note-Nautilus_Al_Minhad_Air_Base-9]

  &#x20;The base supported the [military intervention against the Islamic State of Iraq and the Levant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_military_intervention_against_the_Islamic_State_of_Iraq_and_the_Levant), deploying [Boeing F/A-18F Super Hornets](845012.md), [Boeing E-7A Wedgetails](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_737_AEW\&C) and [Airbus KC-30As](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airbus_A330_MRTT).

  [^cite_note-AFMNOV14-10]

  &#x20;In early 2015, Boeing F/A-18F Super Hornets replaced the [McDonnell Douglas F/A-18A Hornets](45998.md).

  [^cite_note-AFMAUG15-11]

*  [Canada](5042916.md) - The [Canadian Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Forces) operated a forward logistics support facility in the Middle East, called [Camp Mirage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_Mirage). It is widely believed that Camp Mirage was located at Al Minhad Air Base from its founding in autumn 2001 until its closure in autumn 2010, due to an unrelated disagreement over securing additional landing rights for UAE's civilian airlines at Canadian airports. The United States military relied on the UAE bases for assistance during the Gulf War and the [counter ISIS conflict in Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_American_intervention_in_Iraq).

-  [Netherlands](21148.md) - The [Armed forces of the Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_forces_of_the_Netherlands) had deployed assets here.

*  [New Zealand](4913064.md) - The [New Zealand Defence Force](21359.md) had deployed assets here.
