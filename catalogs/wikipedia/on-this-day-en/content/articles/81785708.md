# Jasveen Sangha

&#x20;**Jasveen Sangha** (born July 22, 1983) is a British-American convicted felon and drug dealer known as the **Ketamine Queen**. She gained international attention following her indictment and subsequent guilty plea in connection with the overdose death of actor [Matthew Perry](233338.md). Prosecutors alleged that Sangha operated a drug distribution network from her [North Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Hollywood,_Los_Angeles) home for several years. On April 8, 2026, Sangha was sentenced to 15 years in prison for her role in supplying the ketamine that would cause Perry's death.[^cite_note-NBCSentence-1][^cite_note-detainedsince2024-2] She has remained in prison since August 2024.[^cite_note-detainedsince2024-2]

## Early life and education

Sangha was born in [London](17867.md). She is of [Punjabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjabis) origin and is the daughter of entrepreneur Nilem Singh and Baljeet Singh Chhokar.[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-\:2-4] Her grandparents amassed a fortune in the fashion retail industry in [east London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_London).[^cite_note-\:2-4] Following her mother's remarriage, the family relocated to [Calabasas, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calabasas,_California), where Sangha spent her youth.[^cite_note-\:2-4]

In 2001, Sangha completed her high school education in Calabasas.[^cite_note-\:1-5] Her senior yearbook entry featured the quote: "It isn't what they say about you, it's what they whisper."[^cite_note-\:1-5] She attended the [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine), graduating with a bachelor's degree in [social sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_science).[^cite_note-\:1-5] In 2010, she earned an M.B.A. from [Hult International Business School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hult_International_Business_School) in London.[^cite_note-\:1-5][^cite_note-\:2-4]

Sangha was involved with a business in [Studio City, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Studio_City,_Los_Angeles), known as the Stiletto Nail Bar.[^cite_note-\:1-5][^cite_note-\:2-4] Prosecutors noted in court filings that she did not appear to hold legitimate employment from 2019 onward.[^cite_note-\:1-5]

## Drug trafficking activities

According to the indictment, Sangha used her apartment located in a midrise complex in [North Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Hollywood,_Los_Angeles) to produce, stockpile, and sell illicit drugs over a five-year period.[^cite_note-\:1-5] As part of her plea agreement, she acknowledged that her residence had functioned as a distribution hub starting no later than 2019.[^cite_note-\:0-3] She was referred to by associates and customers as the Ketamine Queen.[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-\:1-5]

In August 2019, Sangha sold [ketamine](16948.md) to a customer named Cody McLaury, who died from an [overdose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug_overdose) shortly after the transaction.[^cite_note-\:1-5] Prosecutors alleged that Sangha was aware her product had caused McLaury's death citing a text message she received from the victim's sister but continued her trafficking operations regardless.[^cite_note-\:1-5][^cite_note-\:2-4] During a search of her home in March 2024, law enforcement agents recovered a variety of substances, including cocaine, counterfeit [Xanax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xanax), [methamphetamine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methamphetamine_in_the_United_States) pills, and nearly 80 vials of liquid ketamine.[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-\:1-5]

After Dr. Salvador Plasencia reduced his distribution of ketamine, Sangha became the main supplier of ketamine to actor [Matthew Perry](233338.md).[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]In October 2023, Sangha collaborated with [Erik Fleming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erik_Fleming_\(director\)) to provide ketamine to Perry.[^cite_note-\:0-3] She first supplied a sample of the drug in an unbranded glass vial capped with a blue lid.[^cite_note-\:1-5] Following this initial exchange, she sold approximately 50 vials to Perry's assistant, Kenneth Iwamasa, through Fleming.[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-\:1-5] During a subsequent transaction involving 25 vials, she included ketamine-infused lollipops as a bonus.[^cite_note-\:1-5]

Prosecutors stated that the specific dose responsible for Perry's death came from one of the vials Sangha sold.[^cite_note-\:1-5] When news of the actor's death broke, Sangha reportedly messaged Fleming on an encrypted app, instructing him to delete their chat history.[^cite_note-\:1-5]

### Legal proceedings

Authorities arrested Sangha on drug charges in March 2024, after which she was released on a \$100,000 bond.[^cite_note-\:1-5] She was indicted again in August 2024, along with four co-defendants, for her specific role in Perry's death. Following her August 2024 arrest, Sangha's bail was subsequently revoked, and she has since remained detained at a federal prison.[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-sentencedandjailedsince2024-11][^cite_note-NBCSentence-1][^cite_note-detainedsince2024-2][^cite_note-\:1-5]

On September 3, 2025, Sangha entered a guilty plea for five federal charges: one count of maintaining a drug-involved premises, one count of distribution resulting in death, and three counts of ketamine distribution.[^cite_note-\:3-12] The crimes carried a maximum sentence of up to 65 years in prison.[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-\:3-12] Sentencing was initially set for December 10, 2025.[^cite_note-\:3-12] However, her sentencing was later scheduled to take place on February 25, 2026, and then once again rescheduled to take place on April 8, 2026.[^cite_note-13] Prosecutors sought a prison sentence of 15 years plus three years of [supervised release](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_probation_and_supervised_release_in_the_United_States), while Sangha's lawyers requested she be sentenced to [time served](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_served).[^cite_note-NBCSentence-1] On April 8, 2026, Judge [Sherilyn Peace Garnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherilyn_Peace_Garnett) sentenced Sangha to 15 years in federal prison.[^cite_note-14]

## Personal life

On her social media profiles, Sangha cultivated an image as a curator of art and a frequent traveler between London and Los Angeles.[^cite_note-\:1-5] In the months following Perry's death, she documented travels to Japan and Mexico, posting photos of herself enjoying luxury amenities such as caviar and afternoon tea.[^cite_note-\:1-5] She also celebrated her 40th birthday at a Los Angeles Koreatown lounge called *Kiss Kiss Bang Bang*.[^cite_note-\:1-5]

In July 2024, weeks before being indicted for Perry's death, she posted an image of a bracelet adorned with mushroom charms, captioned "Pulling out old raver candy #ravetothegrave."[^cite_note-\:1-5]
