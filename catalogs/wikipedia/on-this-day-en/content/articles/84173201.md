On 8 September 2026, four artworks by [Pierre-Auguste Renoir](24546.md) were stolen from the [Musée Renoir](84175718.md) in [Cagnes-sur-Mer](3546923.md) in France, though two of the paintings were later abandoned by the thieves and found in the museum's garden. The works had an estimated value of €9 million (£7.7m; \$10.5m) each.[^cite_note-bbc_amos-hadfield-1][^cite_note-telegraph_emily-2][^cite_note-sky-3][^cite_note-4]

## Theft

On the morning of 8 September, two thieves broke into the [Musée Renoir](84175718.md) in [Cagnes-sur-Mer](3546923.md), and stole four paintings by Renoir before dropping two during their escape. According to mayor [Bryan Masson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryan_Masson), the museum's alarm was triggered at 5\:48 a.m. [CEST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_European_Summer_Time), with police arriving five minutes later. The thieves, wearing helmets, gloves, and camouflage fatigues, cut through the museum's wire fencing with an [electric knife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_knife) and [hacksaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hacksaw) to access the museum grounds, timing the theft to coincide with a shift change in security personnel. They cut the clips securing the paintings and hastily escaped the scene due to the [alarm system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alarm_device) activating. They abandoned two paintings while fleeing, which were later found in the museum's garden.[^cite_note-bbc_amos-hadfield-1][^cite_note-sky-3][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

## Paintings stolen

French regional newspaper *[Nice-Matin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nice-Matin)* reported that the paintings taken were:

- *[Jeune Femme au puits](84181484.md)* (1886)

- *[Portrait of Madame Colonna Romano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portrait_of_Madame_Colonna_Romano)* (c. 1913)

- *Portrait of Madame Stephen Pichon* (1895)

- *Coco lisant* (1905)

Each of the paintings had an estimated value of approximately €9 million (£7.7m; \$10.5m).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-bbc_amos-hadfield-1] The two paintings reportedly dropped by the thieves were the *Portrait of Madame Stephen Pichon* and *Coco lisant* while the two still missing were *Madame Colonna Romano* and *Jeune Femme au puits*.[^cite_note-bbc_amos-hadfield-1][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-telegraph_emily-2]
