The **administrative divisions of Tanzania** are controlled by Part I, Article 2.2 of the [Constitution of Tanzania](29547627.md).[^cite_note-1] Tanzania is divided into thirty-one [regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Tanzania) (*mkoa* in [Swahili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swahili_language)). Each region is subdivided into [districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Tanzania) (*[wilaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilayah)* in Swahili). The districts are sub-divided into [divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_\(country_subdivision\)) (*tarafa* in Swahili) and further into [local wards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_\(electoral_subdivision\)) (*kata* in Swahili). Wards are further subdivided for management purposes: for [urban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_area) wards into [streets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Street) (*mtaa* in Swahili) and for [rural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rural_area) wards into [villages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village) (*kijiji* in Swahili). The villages may be further subdivided into [hamlets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamlet_\(place\)) (*kitongoji* in Swahili).

## Regions

Main article: [Regions of Tanzania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Tanzania)

In 1922 under the [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Empire) Tanganyika was divided into twenty-two regions, known as "divisions": Arusha, Bagamoyo, Bukoba, Dar es Salaam, Dodoma, Iringa, Kilwa, Kondoa-Irangi, Lindi, Mahenge, Morogoro, Moshi, Mwanza, Pangani, Rufiji, Rungwe, Songea, Tabora, Tanga, Ufipa, Ujiji, and Usambara.

## Districts

Main article: [Districts of Tanzania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Tanzania)

According to the 2012 Tanzania National Census, Tanzania was divided into 169 districts. There is one type of rural district: a *District Council*. And there are three types of urban districts: *Town Council*, *Municipal Council* and *City Council*.[^cite_note-Census_2012-2]

## Divisions

A division is an administrative organization for several wards.

## Wards

A village is the lowest government administrative structure at the community level. In an urban area, a cluster *(mtaa)* can include a number of streets. A ward *(kata)* is an administrative structure for one single town or portion of a bigger town (Urban Wards). Rural wards are composed of several villages
