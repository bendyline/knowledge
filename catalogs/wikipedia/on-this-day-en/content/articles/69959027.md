**Alagaan Natin Ating Kalusugan** (lit. 'let's take care of our health'), also known as the **Anakalusugan Party List** (stylized as **AnaKalusugan**, a portmanteau of Tagalog words for "offspring" and "health") is a health advocacy group with representation in the [House of Representatives of the Philippines](293605.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] It aims to represent the [Philippines' health sector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_care_in_the_Philippines).[^cite_note-3]

## History

### 18th Congress

During the [18th Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/18th_Congress_of_the_Philippines), Anakalusugan had [Mike Defensor](9457128.md) as its representative. Defensor proposed the passage of Free Annual Medical Checkup Act that would mandate the government to provide free annual medical examinations to Filipinos.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] Anakalusugan also played a part in the House of Representatives' probe against Philhealth regarding a corruption scandal involving funds worth billions of pesos in 2020.[^cite_note-6]

Amid the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md), Defensor along with Sagip Party-list representative [Rodante Marcoleta](68240498.md) initiated an event called "ivermectin pan-three" that distributes the anti-parasitic drug [ivermectin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivermectin), despite warnings from the World Health Organization on the lack of evidence to support ivermectin's efficacy against COVID-19.[^cite_note-7]
