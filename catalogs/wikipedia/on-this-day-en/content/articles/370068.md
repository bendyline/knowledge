**Ankara Province** ([Turkish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_language): *Ankara ili*, pronounced [\[ˈaŋkaɾa iˈli\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Turkish)) is a [province](428896.md) and [metropolitan municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_municipalities_in_Turkey) of [Turkey](11125639.md) with the capital city [Ankara](802.md). Its area is 25,632 km<sup>2</sup>,[^cite_note-harita-2] and its population is 5,782,285 (2022).[^cite_note-tuik-1]

## History

Main article: [History of Ankara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Ankara)

The site of the modern city has been home to settlements by many historic Anatolian civilizations in antiquity and classical times, including [Phrygians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phrygians), [Lydians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lydians), [Persians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achaemenid_Empire) and [Alexander the Great](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_the_Great), [Romans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Empire), and [Galatians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galatians_\(People\)). The city of [Ankara](802.md) became a fortified stronghold of the [Byzantines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byzantine_Empire); it fell to the [Seljuk Turks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seljuk_Turks) and later the [Ottoman Empire](22278.md). It was finally chosen by [Mustafa Kemal Atatürk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mustafa_Kemal_Atat%C3%BCrk) and the [Turkish National Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_National_Movement) as the site of the [provisional government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_the_Grand_National_Assembly) and the [Turkish parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_National_Assembly_of_Turkey) in 1920, and in 1923 as the capital city of the newly established [Republic of Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Turkey).

Ankara is mostly in the [Central Anatolia region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Anatolia_region), and partly in the [Black Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Sea_Region) region. Ankara has mountain forests to its north, and the dry plain of [Konya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konya) to its south. The province is irrigated by the [Kızılırmak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C4%B1z%C4%B1l%C4%B1rmak_River) and [Sakarya River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sakarya_River) systems, the Sarıyar reservoir and many natural lakes and pools. 50% of the land is used for agriculture, 28% is forest and another 10% is meadow and grazing land. [Lake Tuz](1905943.md), the second largest lake in Turkey, partly lies in the Şereflikoçhisar district of the province. The highest point of the province is the 2,015 meters tall Işık Mountain in the Kızılcahamam district.

### Climate

The climate is hot and dry in summer, rainy in spring and autumn, cold and snowy in winter, wetter in the north of the province than the drier plains to the south. The annual precipitation in the city of Ankara located the center of the province is 415 mm (16.3 in),[^cite_note-14] the annual precipitation of [Kızılcahamam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C4%B1z%C4%B1lcahamam) located in the north of the province is 592 mm (23.3 in), and the annual precipitation of [Şereflikoçhisar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%9Eerefliko%C3%A7hisar) located in the southeast of the province is 357 mm (14.1 in).[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]
