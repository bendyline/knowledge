# Krokstadelva



Mjøndalsbrua crosses over Drammenselva connecting Krokstadelva with Mjøndalen

**Krokstadelva** is a [town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_towns_in_Norway) in [Drammen](178144.md) [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Norway), part of the [Buskerud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buskerud) [county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_Norway) in [Norway](21241.md). It is situated north of the [Drammenselva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drammenselva) river and opposite [Mjøndalen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mj%C3%B8ndalen).

## History

The name Krokstadelva comes from the farm name Krokstad ("a Krokstadum"), where the first part is possibly the rare male name Krok ("Krókr").[^cite_note-\:0-1]

On the mountainside a fortification is present, supposed to defend the area against a possible Swedish invasion in connection with the dissolution of the union in 1905.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

From 17 July 2026, [a large fire ran through the hilly neighbourhood](83729628.md). More than 100 homes were destroyed, and at least 400 people were severely affected.[^cite_note-2]

## Industry and commerce

Historically Krokstadelva was known for its industry; including paper & pulp production, [forestry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forestry), technical-chemical manufacturing and apparel production. Today much of this industry has been phased out, and the local commerce has been given a boost in a large shopping mall located close to the town center; Alti Buskerud, formerly Buskerud Storsenter and Spareland. The shopping mall is owned by CityCon, and has around 70 stores and 1200 parking spots.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

## Education

The town has four public schools, two primary schools (1st–7th grade) and two secondary schools (8th–10th grade). The largest primary school is *Krokstad skole* with about 540 students[^cite_note-5] and the largest secondary school is *Eknes skole* with about 330 students.[^cite_note-6] The Nedre Eiker Municipal Music school is also located in Krokstadelva, where about 105 students are engaged in extracurricular music education.[^cite_note-7] There are two public preschools in Krokstadelva, with a total capacity of 165 children. The town also has seven private preschools.[^cite_note-8]

## Culture and sports activities

Krokstadelva features a combined sports hall and public indoor swimming pool, situated next to Eknes skole. The local sports association is [IF Birkebeineren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IF_Birkebeineren) (IBK) which primarily focuses on football, crosscountry skiing, cycling and tennis. The town also has a judo club, and there is ample opportunity for hiking, crosscountry running and crosscountry skiing in the forest areas adjacent to the town. There was previously a gymnasticsclub in IF Birkebeineren
