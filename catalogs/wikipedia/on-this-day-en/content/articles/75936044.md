The **World Chess Championship 2026** is an upcoming [chess](5134.md) match to determine the [World Chess Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Chess_Champion). It will be played between the defending champion, [Gukesh Dommaraju](59333658.md), and the challenger, [Javokhir Sindarov](58816590.md), the winner of the [Candidates Tournament 2026](75933820.md). The match is set to take place between 24 November and 12 December 2026 in [Cologny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cologny), Geneva, Switzerland.[^cite_note-fidelocationannouncement-1][^cite_note-ESPNWCClocation-2][^cite_note-GuardianWCClocation-3]

## Defending champion

After defeating [Ding Liren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ding_Liren) in the [2024 championship match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Chess_Championship_2024), Gukesh has been noted for his poor form in both classical and faster time controls. After achieving a peak rating of 2794, he went on to lose 43 rating points altogether in 2025 due to placing 41st at the [2025 FIDE Grand Swiss Tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIDE_Grand_Swiss_Tournament_2025) and by exiting in the 3rd round of the [2025 Chess World Cup](78998807.md) as the first seed of the latter event.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-Narayan2025-6] His form has garnered criticism from former world champions [Magnus Carlsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnus_Carlsen) and [Garry Kasparov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garry_Kasparov), as the latter highlighted the differences in strength between the reign of Gukesh compared to other world champions, including himself.[^cite_note-Narayan2025-6] Carlsen further expanded on his criticism in an interview with [chess.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chess.com) in September 2026, stating that due to Gukesh's form, Sindarov was "a pretty significant favorite" and that "\[Gukesh's] evaluations are way too optimistic. That makes him take way more chances than he should. I think especially now his confidence hasn’t been too high, he just tries to do a little bit too much".[^cite_note-BardenSept2026-7] Though his opponent Sindarov fended off external opinions on Gukesh's form, stating in an interview with [Reuters](18998750.md) that "he is very strong. I played with him a few times and I really feel he is a very strong player".[^cite_note-SindarovReuters-8] He also referenced Ding's resurging performance in the 2024 championship match, highlighting that "recent form offered little indication of how the championship would unfold".[^cite_note-SindarovReuters-8]

After a rough stretch of results in 2026 at the [Tata Steel Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Steel_Chess_Tournament_2026) and that year's [Prague Chess Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague_Chess_Festival), where he finished in joint-last place with 3½/9 points, Gukesh announced his withdrawal from the classical events of the [2026 Grand Chess Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Chess_Tour_2026).[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] In June 2026, Gukesh reflected on his form in an interview with *[Hindustan Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindustan_Times)* where he described it as an "elephant in the room" and that the situation overall was "not looking great". He further expanded on these statements by stating that he is now "trying to find a more internal source of motivation, one less dependent on results".[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] He then finished last at both [Norway Chess 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway_Chess_2026#Open_Section) and [Chennai Grand Masters 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai_Grand_Masters#2026), to end on a rating of 2703.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] Despite the setbacks, both [Viswanathan Anand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viswanathan_Anand) and [Srinath Narayanan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Srinath_Narayanan) highlighted before the conclusion of the latter tournament that the [upcoming Chess Olympiad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/46th_Chess_Olympiad) will be an important factor towards regaining his form.[^cite_note-15]

Playing on board 4 for India and looking to defend the contingent's open event Olympiad title from the [2024 edition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/45th_Chess_Olympiad), Gukesh's form improved as he notably scored a win in the decider match against Germany's [Alexander Donchenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Donchenko) in round 9 of the event and subsequently maintained India's contention for the gold medal by defeating Sindarov's compatriot [Ortik Nigmatov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ortik_Nigmatov) in the penultimate round.[^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] Heading into the final round of the event with an undefeated record, Gukesh went on to lose his game against Hungary's [Gleb Dudin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gleb_Dudin), though the Indian team secured a 2nd place finish at the open event over Germany due to tiebreaks.[^cite_note-19]

## Championship match

### Organisation

Bids for the hosting of the World Championship were to be presented to [FIDE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIDE) no later than 31 May 2026,[^cite_note-29] but the deadline was later extended to 21 June.[^cite_note-30] Acting president of FIDE [Viswanathan Anand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viswanathan_Anand) revealed that several countries, including India, the United States, and Cyprus expressed interest in hosting the event.[^cite_note-fidelocationannouncement-1] It was then announced on 28 July that [Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva), Switzerland was originally chosen to host the match from 24 November to 12 December 2026, which marked the first time since [2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_World_Chess_Championship_2004) that a world chess championship match will take place in Switzerland.[^cite_note-ESPNWCClocation-2][^cite_note-GuardianWCClocation-3] It was later revealed, however, that the [Fondation Martin Bodmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bodmer_Library), located in [Cologny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cologny) was chosen as the venue to host the match.[^cite_note-fidecolognypost-31]

Software company [Salesforce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salesforce) was announced as the main sponsor of the match, with [Freedom Holding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedom_Holding) owned by incumbent president of FIDE [Timur Turlov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timur_Turlov) serving as the general partner.[^cite_note-fidecolognypost-31][^cite_note-32]

### Prize fund

The prize fund allocated for the event is US\$2.5 million, unchanged from 2024.[^cite_note-33] Each player would receive US\$200,000 for each game won (including forfeits), and the remainder of the money will be distributed equally except if the score is 7.5–6.5 after 13 decisive games or 8–6 after 14 rounds, in which case the winner would receive US\$1.3 million and the runner-up would receive US\$1.2 million, which is the same amount of money the winner and runner-up would receive if the tournament went to tie-breaks.

### Format

Each of the 14 classical games shall be played with 120 minutes for each player since the first move, with 30 minutes added on each player's clock after black's 40th move, and 30 seconds added on each player's clock per move from 41st move. Drawing of colors shall take place at the opening ceremony or before as decided by FIDE. The colors drawn for the first game apply to all odd-ordered games and are reversed for all even ordered games.[^cite_note-\:0-34]

Players are not allowed to agree upon a draw before black's 40th move. Any draw occurred before black's 40th move is permitted only if it is due to threefold repetition or stalemate and if approved by the Chief Arbiter or Deputy Arbiter.

#### Tiebreaks

If the score after 14 classical games remains equal (7 each), a set of 4 rapid games shall be played with the time control of 15 minutes each player + 10 seconds of increment each player per move. If the score remains equal, a set of 2 rapid games shall be played with 10 minutes each player + 5 seconds of increment each player per move. If draw still remains, a set of 2 blitz games shall be played with the time control of 3 minutes each player + 2 seconds of increment each player per move. If draw still remains, a series of single game elimination matches shall be played in the same blitz format with the first game's colour drawn and reversed after each game until a decisive result occurs.[^cite_note-\:0-34]
