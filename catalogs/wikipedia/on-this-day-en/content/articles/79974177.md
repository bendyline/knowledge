**Trump Tower Belgrade**, also known as **Belgrade Centrum**,[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEBartenstein2024-1] was[^cite_note-2] a proposed mixed-use [luxury hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxury_hotel) and [condominium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Condominium) apartment building on the site of the [General Staff Building](5722707.md) in [Savski Venac](1073737.md), [Belgrade](55904.md), Serbia.

The real-estate developer [Donald Trump](4848272.md), who later served as the 45th and 47th [president of the United States](24113.md), devised several plans to construct a property in Serbia. The Yugoslav Ministry of Defence Building, which was destroyed in the [NATO bombing of Yugoslavia](322473.md), received renewed attention from [Richard Grenell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Grenell), the special envoy to the [Balkans](4829.md) in [Trump's first presidency](52231341.md). According to [Aleksandar Jovanović Ćuta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandar_Jovanovi%C4%87_%C4%86uta), a Serbian environmental activist, a [memorandum of understanding](1018279.md) was signed in December 2022, offering the site to Kushner Realty and Atlantic Incubation Partners.

## Site

Trump Tower Belgrade was set to be constructed at the site of the [General Staff Building](5722707.md), which was destroyed in the [NATO bombing of Yugoslavia](322473.md) in 1999.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEBartenstein2024-1]

## Architecture

Trump Tower Belgrade was designed as a 175-room[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELipton2025a-3] [luxury hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxury_hotel) with commercial space and 1,500 residences.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEBartenstein2024-1] The tower is designed to have thirty floors and be 820,000 ft<sup>2</sup> (76,000 m<sup>2</sup>).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEMayes-Osterman2025-4] It will include a museum and memorial in honor of the victims of the bombing of Yugoslavia, which will be managed by the [government of Serbia](608371.md) and designed by Serbian architects.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELipton2024b-5] The complex will include a Trump International Hotel, the first in [Europe](9239.md).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELipton2025b-6] Renders of Trump Tower Belgrade were created by Studio Genesis.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEBartenstein2024-1]

## History



The [General Staff Building](5722707.md) in 2022

Several attempts at constructing a Serbian property branded with [Donald Trump](4848272.md)'s name had been established prior to Trump Tower Belgrade,[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELiptonHabermanSwan2024-7] and multiple governments have attempted to restore the Yugoslav Ministry of Defence Building, according to Goran Vasić, the minister of construction, transport and infrastructure.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTE"Bušatlija:_Zgradu_Generalštaba_ne_poklanjati"._Danas-8] In 2013, Trump told then-prime minister [Ivica Dačić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivica_Da%C4%8Di%C4%87) that he intended to construct a luxury hotel at the site of the Yugoslav Ministry of Defence Building and several [Trump Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_Organization) associates had visited the site, though it did not yield in any discussions before Trump was elected president of the United States [in 2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_United_States_presidential_election). During [Trump's first presidency](52231341.md), [Richard Grenell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Grenell), the special envoy to the [Balkans](4829.md), advocated for having [Serbia](29265.md) and the [United States](3434750.md) rebuild the Yugoslav Ministry of Defence Building, including after Serbian president [Aleksandar Vučić](13349267.md) met Trump at the [White House](33057.md) in 2020.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELiptonHabermanSwan2024-7] *[The New York Times](30680.md)* wrote in April 2024 that Trump Tower Belgrade "benefited from relationships built in part during the Trump administration".[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELiptonSwanHaberman2024b-9]

In March 2024, [Aleksandar Jovanović Ćuta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandar_Jovanovi%C4%87_%C4%86uta), a Serbian environmental activist, alleged that a [memorandum of understanding](1018279.md) had been signed in December 2022, offering the site of the Yugoslav Ministry of Defense Building to Kushner Realty and Atlantic Incubation Partners.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTE"Kakva_je_sudbina_oštećenog_Generalštaba_u_Kneza_Miloša"._Politika-10] [Bloomberg News](3057255.md) and *The New York Times* later disclosed that [Jared Kushner](6191053.md) was pursuing a real estate development in [Belgrade](55904.md); Kushner later confirmed the reports. The project would be funded by [Affinity Partners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affinity_Partners), an investment company that had secured US\$2 billion from the [Public Investment Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Investment_Fund) and hundreds of millions from the [Emirates Investment Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emirates_Investment_Authority) and the [Qatar Investment Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatar_Investment_Authority), though the money was largely uninvested by then.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELiptonSwanHaberman2024a-11] According to Kushner, Grenell encouraged him to seek the Belgrade deal.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELiptonHabermanSwan2024-7] The outline of the agreement would give Affinity Partners a ninety-nine year lease at no cost.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELipton2024a-12] Kushner later told the *Times* that the parties of the deal had tentatively agreed to give the [government of Serbia](608371.md) twenty-two percent of the profits from Trump Tower Belgrade. The agreement also includes the option of transferring the property to Affinity Partners after its construction. Trump Tower Belgrade was estimated to cost US\$500 million.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELiptonHabermanSwan2024-7]

The government of Serbia approved the contract with Kushner in May. The terms of the agreement gave Affinity Partners a set period of time to complete the project. By that month, Kushner had agreed to work with Asher Abehsera, who was designated to oversee development;[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELipton2024b-5] he had also enlisted Grenell on the project.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELiptonKaraj2024-13] The site of Trump Tower Belgrade was leased from the government of Serbia. In January 2025, the Trump Organization announced that it would partner with Kushner on the complex. Kushner had also formed a partnership with Emirati property developer [Mohamed Alabbar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Alabbar)'s company, [Eagle Hills Properties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eagle_Hills_Properties).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELipton2025a-3] Construction of the tower faced a setback in May after Vasić, the acting director of the Republican Institute for the Protection of Cultural Monuments, was arrested for allegedly fabricating a document justifying removing the Yugoslav Ministry of Defence Building's cultural status.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELipton2025b-6] Vučić later stated that the project would continue, comparing criticism to the arguments raised against [Belgrade Waterfront](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgrade_Waterfront).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEKraja2025-14]

In December 2025, Kushner was reported to have withdrawn "hours after Serbian prosecutors charged four senior government officials there with corruption in connection with the half-billion-dollar project".[^cite_note-15]

## Reception

Trump Tower Belgrade has garnered controversy over the involvement of [Jared Kushner](6191053.md), the son-in-law of U.S. president [Donald Trump](4848272.md). Robert Weissman, the president of [Public Citizen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Citizen), criticized the planned development and additional deals in [Albania](738.md), stating that the properties appear to "involve the worst of every corrupt tendency of the [Trump administration](52231341.md) and [Trump family](52170275.md)." Kathleen Clark, a government ethics professor at the [Washington University School of Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_University_School_of_Law), postulated that the project would be a conflict of interest if Trump were to be elected president. Serbian representative [Danijela Nestorović](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danijela_Nestorovi%C4%87) and other members of [Together](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Together_\(Serbia\)) condemned Trump Tower Belgrade as a "mockery to the citizens of Serbia".[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELiptonHabermanSwan2024-7] Serbian president [Aleksandar Vučić](13349267.md) dismissed concerns at a rally, stating that he "died laughing" at the suggestion he was influencing Trump.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELipton2024a-12] Kushner and [Richard Grenell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Grenell) have rejected claims of impropriety, stating that they are acting as private citizens.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTERussellDunai2024-16]

The project also elicited ethics concerns from [Democrats](5043544.md) in the [United States House of Representatives](19468510.md), who called for an investigation from [Republicans](32070.md) on the [House Foreign Affairs Subcommittee on Oversight and Accountability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_House_Foreign_Affairs_Subcommittee_on_Oversight_and_Accountability).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELipton2024b-5] Senator [Ron Wyden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_Wyden) and general [Wesley Clark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wesley_Clark), who commanded [NATO](21133.md) during the [Kosovo War](16760.md), have criticized the concept of a memorial to victims of the [bombing of Yugoslavia](322473.md) constructed by an American company.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEBrownSalamaAcosta2024-17] Wyden and representative [Jamie Raskin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamie_Raskin) urged attorney general [Merrick Garland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merrick_Garland) to investigate the property in a letter sent in October 2024.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEBartensteinPerlberg2025-18]

Protests have been held in Serbia against Trump Tower Belgrade.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELipton2025b-6] In March 2025, thousands of students protested at the site of the Yugoslav Ministry of Defence Building as part of broader [anti-corruption protests](78597389.md).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEKuzmanovic2025-19]
