**Smolensk Oblast**[^cite_note-9], informally also called **Smolenshchina**,[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] is a [federal subject](701438.md) of [Russia](25391.md) (an [oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oblast)). Its [administrative centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_centre) is the [city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) of [Smolensk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smolensk). As of the [2021 Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Russian_census), its population was 888,421.[^cite_note-2021Census-12]

## Geography



Roslavlsky old tract, the village of [Boreshino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boreshino?action=edit\&redlink=1)

The oblast was founded on 27 September 1937.[^cite_note-ATS1981-1] It borders [Pskov Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pskov_Oblast) in the north, [Tver Oblast](463952.md) in the northeast, [Moscow Oblast](372315.md) in the east, [Kaluga Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaluga_Oblast) in south, [Bryansk Oblast](476473.md) in the southwest, and [Mogilev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mogilev_Region) and [Vitebsk Oblasts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitebsk_Region) of [Belarus](3457.md), in the west and northwest, as part of the [Belarus–Russia border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarus%E2%80%93Russia_border).

### Hydrography

The main river of the region is the [Dnieper](380138.md) river and its tributaries, [Desna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desna_\(river\)), [Vop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vop_\(river\)), [Vyazma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vyazma). By the rivers of the Volga basin and its tributary [Vazuza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vazuza) [Gzhat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gzhat) and tributary of the [Oka river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oka_river), [Ugra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ugra_River_\(Oka\)). To the north-west flows a short section of the [Western Dvina River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Dvina_River) and its tributary river [Kasplya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasplya) .

Among the major reservoirs supplying water are Moscow Vazuzssky Yauza and reservoirs in the north-east, as well as cooling power plants, [Smolensk reservoir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smolensk_reservoir?action=edit\&redlink=1) in the north near the village of Lake and [Desnogorsk Reservoir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desnogorsk_Reservoir?action=edit\&redlink=1) in the south area of the city near the Desnogorsk.

### Climate

The climate of Smolensk Oblast is humid continental, similar to the climate of [Moscow Oblast](372315.md), but slightly warmer in winter due to being located further west. Summers are short, warm and rainy, while winters are long, cold and snowy. Average temperatures range from −9 °C (16 °F) in January to 17 °C (63 °F) in July (although in the south it ranges from −8 °C (18 °F) in January to 18 °C (64 °F) in July). Annual precipitation varies from 630 to 730 mm (25 to 29 in) per year.

## Politics



Building of the Oblast Administration

During the [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) period, the high authority in the oblast was shared between three persons: The first secretary of the Smolensk CPSU Committee (who in reality had the biggest authority), the chairman of the oblast Soviet (legislative power), and the Chairman of the oblast Executive Committee (executive power). Since 1991, CPSU lost all the power, and the head of the Oblast administration, and eventually the governor was appointed/elected alongside elected [regional parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_parliaments_of_Russia).

The Charter of Smolensk Oblast is the fundamental law of the region. The Legislative Assembly of Smolensk is the province's standing legislative (representative) body. The Legislative Assembly exercises its authority by passing laws, resolutions, and other legal acts and by supervising the implementation and observance of the laws and other legal acts passed by it. The highest executive body is the Oblast Government, which includes territorial executive bodies such as district administrations, committees, and commissions that facilitate development and run the day to day matters of the province. The Oblast administration supports the activities of the Governor who is the highest official and acts as guarantor of the observance of the oblast Charter in accordance with the [Constitution of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Russia). From July 1941-September 1943 part of region fell to Germany.

Smolensk Oblast is divided into 25 districts (*[raions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raion)*) and two [cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_federal_subject_significance), which are further divided into 15 [towns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia), 12 [urban-type settlements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban-type_settlement), and 302 [rural okrugs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selsoviet).

## Economy

Smolensk Oblast has a diverse economy, and exports large amounts of [ammonium nitrate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ammonium_nitrate), fertilizers, wheat, and wood. The top exports in 2021 were fertilizers (19.2%, \$284M), wood products (13.2%, \$194M), wheat (9.8%, \$144M), and machinery (7.6%, \$112M).&#x20;
