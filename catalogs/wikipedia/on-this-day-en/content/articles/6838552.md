# List of diplomatic missions of Israel



Diplomatic missions of Israel

This is a **list of diplomatic missions of Israel**, excluding [honorary consulates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_consulate). As of November 2021, there are 82 resident embassies, including a Taiwan office, and 22 [consulate-generals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consulate-general) and two representative missions in the [165 states that recognise](32359767.md) [Israel](9282173.md).[^cite_note-1]

Israel also maintains five missions to multilateral organisations, of which four missions are to the [United Nations](31769.md) and one mission to the [European Union](9317.md). Israel also maintains an economic and cultural office in [Taiwan](25734.md) and a representative office to the [International Renewable Energy Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Renewable_Energy_Agency) in the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md).[^cite_note-2]

Israel's biggest diplomatic coup in the international community came with peace treaties and recognition from Arab countries such as [Egypt](8087628.md) in the late 1970s, and [Jordan](7515964.md) in the early 1990s, leading to embassies being opened in [Cairo](6293.md) and [Amman](45007.md). During the late 1980s, several Israeli embassies were opened/reopened in former [Eastern Bloc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Bloc) states as the [Cold War](325329.md) ended. At the beginning of the 1990s, Israel established official relations with the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union), [India](14533.md) and [China](5405.md). The prospects of a [Middle East peace agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_East_peace_process) in the mid-1990s led to Israeli government offices appearing as trade representative offices being opened in a handful of Arab states such as [Bahrain](18933277.md), [Qatar](8238258.md), [Oman](22316.md) and [Morocco](19291.md). By 2000s, all have since closed the Israeli offices.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] Israel closed its embassies in [Mauritania](18778516.md) and [Venezuela](32374.md) after the [2008 Gaza War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_War_\(2008%E2%80%9309\)), following a request to do so by their national governments. Following the signing of the [Abraham Accords](64883396.md), Israel opened embassies in Abu Dhabi[^cite_note-7] and Manama in 2021,[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] a consulate-general in Dubai,[^cite_note-10] and a liaison office in Rabat.[^cite_note-11]
