**K2 Airways** was a Pakistani [cargo airline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cargo_airline) based in [Karachi](17123.md), Pakistan. The airline was established in 2018 and operated scheduled and charter cargo services on domestic and international routes. It received its [air operator's certificate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_operator's_certificate) from the [Pakistan Civil Aviation Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Civil_Aviation_Authority) in December 2024. The airline ceased operations after its sole aircraft, a [Boeing 737-400](15103676.md), crashed into the [Arabian Sea](2747.md) on 7 July 2026 while operating as [K2 Airways Flight 1732](83639320.md).[^cite_note-2]

## History

The airline was first founded in 2018, later opening its social media campaign in 2019. [^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]

In early 2023, almost 5 years after the airline was first founded, it stated that it intended to begin passenger flight operations with [E190](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embraer_E-Jet_family) aircraft, which would be the first airline in [Pakistan](23235.md) to operate [Embraer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embraer) aircraft.[^cite_note-5] The plan was however mulled in late 2023 as the aircraft lessor was not able to provide the airline with a second aircraft.[^cite_note-CH_Aviation_K2_Airways-1]

In July 2024, K2 Airways received its first aircraft, a cargo converted [B737-4M0(BDSF)](15103676.md), which was registered in Pakistan as AP-BOI.[^cite_note-CH_Aviation_K2_Airways-1]

K2 Airways received its [air operator's certificate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_operator's_certificate) on December 20, 2024, and subsequently began flight operations a week later on December 27, launching its inaugural flight from Karachi to [Lahore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allama_Iqbal_International_Airport).[^cite_note-6]

On 16 August 2026, it was reported that the airline had allowed its insurance to expire several weeks prior to the crash of [Flight 1732](83639320.md). This finding, along with the loss of the company's sole aircraft in the crash, resulted in K2 Airways operations being suspended indefinitely.[^cite_note-y495-7][^cite_note-n866-8]

## Accidents and incidents

- On 7 July 2026, at around 9\:18 PM PST, K2 Airways’ only aircraft, a 27 year old [Boeing 737-4M0(BDSF)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_737-400F) (registered as AP-BOI) aircraft, was operating [K2 Airways Flight 1732](83639320.md) from [Sharjah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharjah_International_Airport) to [Karachi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jinnah_International_Airport). It crashed into the [Arabian Sea](2747.md) following a navigation systems issue.
