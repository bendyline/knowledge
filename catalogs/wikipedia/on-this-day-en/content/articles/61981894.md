**Vtorovo** ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Второво) is a [rural locality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) (a [selo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Village#Russia)) and the administrative center of Vtorovskoye Rural Settlement, [Kameshkovsky District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kameshkovsky_District), [Vladimir Oblast](463919.md), Russia. The population was 990 as of 2010.[^cite_note-2] There are 8 streets.

## Geography

Vtorovo is located 20 km southwest of [Kameshkovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kameshkovo,_Vladimir_Oblast) (the district's administrative centre) by road. Mirny is the nearest rural locality.[^cite_note-3]
