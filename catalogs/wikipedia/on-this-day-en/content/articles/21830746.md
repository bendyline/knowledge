**Frank Dennis Saylor IV** (born 1955) is a [senior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_status) [United States district judge](834368.md) of the [United States District Court for the District of Massachusetts](915466.md) and was formerly a Judge on the [United States Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Foreign_Intelligence_Surveillance_Court).

## Early life and education

Saylor was born in [Royal Oak, Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Oak,_Michigan). He received a [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) degree from [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) in 1977, and a [Juris Doctor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juris_Doctor) from [Harvard Law School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Law_School) in 1981.

## Career

Saylor was in private practice at [Goodwin Procter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodwin_Procter) in [Boston](24437894.md), from 1981 to 1987, and from 1993 to 2004. He was an [assistant United States attorney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_United_States_attorney) in the U.S. Attorney's Office for the District of Massachusetts from 1987 to 1990. He later was a special counsel and chief of staff to [Robert Mueller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Mueller), then assistant attorney general of the Criminal Division at the [United States Department of Justice](52563.md), from 1990 to 1993.

While at [Goodwin Procter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodwin_Procter), Saylor represented Circor International, Inc., KF Industries, Inc., and senior company officials as a criminal defense attorney while those companies were under investigation for smuggling Chinese-manufactured valves and selling the valves as a product of the U.S. between 2001 and 2004.[^cite_note-1] The U.S. Attorney for the Southern District of Texas, Michael T. Shelby, dismissed the investigation days after Saylor was confirmed by the Senate.

### Federal judicial service

On July 30, 2003, Saylor was nominated by President [George W. Bush](3414021.md) to a seat on the [United States District Court for the District of Massachusetts](915466.md) vacated by [Robert Keeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Keeton). Saylor was confirmed by the [United States Senate](24909346.md) on June 1, 2004, and received his commission on June 2, 2004. He also served a term on the [United States Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Foreign_Intelligence_Surveillance_Court) from 2011 to 2018.[^cite_note-2] Saylor became the chief judge on January 1, 2020, serving it until July 1, 2025.[^cite_note-3] Saylor assumed [senior status](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_status) on July 31, 2025.[^cite_note-4]
