**ChatGPT** is a [generative AI](73291755.md) chatbot developed by [OpenAI](48795986.md). Originally released on November 30, 2022, the product uses [large language models](73248112.md)—specifically [generative pre-trained transformers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generative_pre-trained_transformer) (GPTs)—to generate text, speech, and images in response to user [prompts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_prompt). ChatGPT accelerated the [AI boom](73291899.md), an ongoing period marked by rapid investment and public attention toward the field of [artificial intelligence](1164.md) (AI). OpenAI operates the service on a [freemium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freemium) model. Users can interact with ChatGPT through text, audio, and image prompts.

ChatGPT was quickly adopted, reaching 100 million monthly active users two months after its release and 900 million weekly active users in February 2026. Proponents say that it has the potential to transform numerous professional fields, and it has instigated public debate about the nature of creativity and the future of [knowledge work](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_work).

The chatbot has also been criticized for its limitations and potential for unethical use. It may generate incorrect or nonsensical answers that sound plausible, known as [hallucinations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hallucination_\(artificial_intelligence\)). [Biases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algorithmic_bias) in its [training data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Training_data) have been reflected in its responses. The chatbot can facilitate [academic dishonesty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_dishonesty), generate misinformation, and create malicious code. The [ethics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethics_of_artificial_intelligence) of its development, particularly the use of [copyrighted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copyright) content as training data, have also drawn controversy.

## Features

ChatGPT is a conversational [chatbot](148349.md) and artificial intelligence assistant based on [large language models](73248112.md).[^cite_note-4] It can for example write and [debug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debugging) computer programs;[^cite_note-ChatGPT_can_write_code-5] compose music, teleplays, fairy tales, and student essays; [answer test questions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Question_answering);[^cite_note-6] generate business ideas;[^cite_note-7] translate and summarize text;[^cite_note-japContext-8][^cite_note-Ravšelj-2025-9] and control computers.[^cite_note-10]

Users interact with ChatGPT through conversations which consist of text, audio, and image inputs and outputs.[^cite_note-openaicom-2024b-11] The user's inputs to these conversations are referred to as prompts.[^cite_note-timeBegin-12] An optional "Memory" feature allows users to tell ChatGPT to memorize specific information. Another option allows ChatGPT to recall old conversations.[^cite_note-vergeMemory1-13] GPT-based moderation classifiers are used to reduce the risk of harmful outputs being presented to users.[^cite_note-14]

In March 2023, OpenAI added support for [plugins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plug-in_\(computing\)) for ChatGPT.[^cite_note-openaiplugins-15] This includes both plugins made by OpenAI, such as [web browsing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_browsing) and [code interpretation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code_interpretation), and external plugins from developers such as [Expedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expedia), [OpenTable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenTable), and [Zapier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zapier).[^cite_note-16]

From October to December 2024, ChatGPT Search was deployed.[^cite_note-17] It allows ChatGPT to search the web in an attempt to make more accurate and up-to-date responses.[^cite_note-openaicom-2024a-18][^cite_note-Disotto-2025-19] It increased OpenAI's direct competition with major [search engines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Search_engine).[^cite_note-ngua-20] OpenAI allows businesses to tailor how their content appears in the ChatGPT Search results and influence what sources are used.[^cite_note-ngua-20]

In October 2025, OpenAI launched [ChatGPT Atlas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ChatGPT_Atlas), a browser integrating the ChatGPT assistant directly into web navigation, to compete with existing browsers such as [Google Chrome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Chrome). It has an additional feature called "agentic mode" that allows it to take online actions for the user.[^cite_note-21]

### Languages

OpenAI met [Icelandic](14531.md) President [Guðni Th. Jóhannesson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gu%C3%B0ni_Th._J%C3%B3hannesson) in 2022. In 2023, OpenAI worked with a team of 40 Icelandic volunteers to fine-tune ChatGPT's Icelandic conversation skills as part of Iceland's attempts to preserve the [Icelandic language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Icelandic_language).[^cite_note-22]

In December 2023, the [Albanian government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albanian_government) decided to use ChatGPT for the rapid translation of [European Union](9317.md) documents and the analysis of required changes needed for Albania's accession to the EU.[^cite_note-23]

### GPTs and plugins

Main article: [GPT Store](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT_Store)

In November 2023, OpenAI released GPT Builder, a tool allowing users to customize ChatGPT's behavior for a specific use case.[^cite_note-David-2024-24] The customized systems are referred to as [GPTs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT_\(ChatGPT_customization\)). In January 2024, OpenAI launched the [GPT Store](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT_Store), a marketplace for GPTs.[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26][^cite_note-David-2024-24] At launch, OpenAI included more than 3 million GPTs created by GPT Builder users in the GPT Store.[^cite_note-27]

In September 2025, OpenAI added support for [Model Context Protocol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_Context_Protocol) (MCP) to ChatGPT apps. When enabled in developer mode, this allows for improved third-party access to ChatGPT tools and servers.[^cite_note-28]

In July 2026, ChatGPT apps were renamed to plugins.[^cite_note-29]

### Deep Research

Main article: [ChatGPT Deep Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ChatGPT_Deep_Research)

In February 2025, OpenAI released Deep Research, a feature that generates reports based on extensive web searches. It was initially based on the reasoning model [o3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI_o3) and took from 3 to 30 minutes per report.[^cite_note-30]

### Images



Screenshot of ChatGPT from September 2024 showing a generated image representing the online encyclopedia [Wikipedia](5043734.md) as a glowing digital library

In October 2023, OpenAI's image generation model [DALL-E 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DALL-E) was integrated into ChatGPT. The integration used ChatGPT to write prompts for DALL-E guided by conversations with users.[^cite_note-31][^cite_note-Metz-2023-32]

In March 2025, OpenAI updated ChatGPT to generate images using [GPT Image](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT_Image) instead of DALL-E. One of the most significant improvements was in the generation of text within images, which is especially useful for branded content. However, this ability is noticeably worse in non-Latin alphabets. The model can also generate new images based on existing ones provided in the prompt. These images are generated with [C2PA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Content_Authenticity_Initiative) metadata, which can be used to verify that they are AI-generated. OpenAI has emplaced additional safeguards to prevent what the company deems to be harmful image generation.[^cite_note-33]

### Agents

In 2025, OpenAI added several features to make ChatGPT more [agentic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agentic_AI) (capable of autonomously performing longer tasks). In January, [Operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI_Operator) was released. It was capable of autonomously performing tasks through web browser interactions, including filling forms, placing online orders, scheduling appointments, and other browser-based tasks. It was controlling a software environment inside a [virtual machine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtual_machine) with limited internet connectivity and with safety restrictions.[^cite_note-OpenAI_Blog-34] It struggled with complex user interfaces.[^cite_note-OpenAI_Blog-34][^cite_note-agomuoh1-35]

In May 2025, OpenAI introduced an agent for coding named [Codex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI_Codex_\(AI_agent\)). It is capable of writing software, answering codebase questions, running tests, and proposing [pull requests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pull_request). It is based on a fine-tuned version of [OpenAI o3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI_o3). It has two versions, one running in a virtual machine in the cloud, and one where the agent runs in the cloud, but performs actions on a local machine connected via API.[^cite_note-36]

In July 2025, OpenAI released ChatGPT agent, an AI agent that can perform multi-step tasks.[^cite_note-Field-2025-37][^cite_note-38] Like Operator, it controls a virtual computer. It also inherits from Deep Research's ability to gather and summarize significant volumes of information. The user can interrupt tasks or provide additional instructions as needed.[^cite_note-Field-2025-37][^cite_note-39]

In September 2025, OpenAI partnered with [Stripe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stripe,_Inc.) to release Agentic Commerce Protocol, enabling purchases through ChatGPT. At launch, the feature was limited to purchases on [Etsy](4355398.md) from US users with a payment method linked to their OpenAI account. OpenAI takes an undisclosed cut from the merchant's payment.[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41] OpenAI also added capabilities to connect ChatGPT with productivity software such as [Gmail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gmail).[^cite_note-42]

In July 2026, OpenAI launched ChatGPT Work, an AI agent that can create presentations, spreadsheets and other documents based on information from connected apps and files.[^cite_note-43]

### ChatGPT Health

On January 7, 2026, OpenAI introduced a feature called "ChatGPT Health", whereby ChatGPT can discuss the user's health in a way that is separate from other chats.[^cite_note-openai.com-2026-44][^cite_note-McMahon-2026-45] The feature is not available for users in the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or the [European Economic Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Economic_Area) (EEA),[^cite_note-McMahon-2026-45] and is available on a waitlist basis everywhere else.[^cite_note-openai.com-2026-44] To implement the feature, OpenAI partnered with data connectivity infrastructure company b.well.[^cite_note-46]

## Limitations

The training data of [large language models](73248112.md) only covers a period up to the *cut-off date*, so ChatGPT lacks knowledge of recent events,[^cite_note-bbcUpdate-51] but it can search the web for up-to-date information.[^cite_note-bbcUpdate-51]

Training data also suffers from [algorithmic bias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algorithmic_bias).[^cite_note-Perrigo-2022-52] The [reward model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reward_model) of ChatGPT, designed around human oversight, can be over-optimized and thus hinder performance, in an example of an optimization pathology known as [Goodhart's law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodhart's_law).[^cite_note-53] These limitations may be revealed when ChatGPT responds to prompts including descriptors of people. In one instance, ChatGPT generated a [rap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rap) in which women and scientists of color were asserted to be inferior to white male scientists.[^cite_note-Perrigo-2022-52][^cite_note-54]

### Hallucination

Main article: [Hallucination (artificial intelligence)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hallucination_\(artificial_intelligence\))

User: "summarize this article https\://www\.nytimes.com/2023/03/11/technology/chatgpt-prompts-to-avoid-content-filters.html". ChatGPT replies: "The article discusses how ChatGPT, an AI language model, is being used to circumvent content filters that are designed to detect and block certain types of content. By generating prompts that are less likely to trigger these filters, ChatGPT can be used to create content that might otherwise be censored. While this technology could be useful for those seeking to express themselves freely, it also raises concerns about the potential misuse of AI-generated content, as well as the difficulty of regulating such content in the future. The article concludes by noting that there is still much to learn about the impact of AI language models like ChatGPT on the way we communicate online."

When prompted to "summarize an article" with a fake URL that contains meaningful keywords, even with no Internet connection, the chatbot generates a response that seems valid at first glance. It guesses the content from the last portion of the fake URL "chatgpt-prompts-to-avoid-content-filters.html".

Nonsense and [misinformation](2203174.md) presented as fact by ChatGPT and other LLMs are often referred to as [hallucinations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hallucination_\(artificial_intelligence\)). The term "hallucination" as applied to LLMs is distinct from [its meaning in psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hallucination), and the phenomenon in chatbots is more similar to [confabulation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confabulation).[^cite_note-55][^cite_note-56]

Journalists and scholars have commented on ChatGPT's tendency to output false information.[^cite_note-57] When [CNBC](223441.md) asked ChatGPT for the lyrics to "[Ballad of Dwight Fry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballad_of_Dwight_Fry)", ChatGPT supplied invented lyrics rather than the actual lyrics.[^cite_note-Pitt-2022-58]

### Sycophancy

ChatGPT and other chatbots have a tendency to flatter and agree with users even when they are wrong, sometimes to the point of validating delusions.[^cite_note-59] OpenAI withdrew one ChatGPT update in 2025 due to heightened concern about it affirming users' doubts and encouraging impulsive behavior.[^cite_note-Hill-2025-60] The company then worked with a team of 170 psychiatrists, [psychologists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychologist), and [physicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicians) to prepare responses for users exhibiting [mental health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_health) emergencies.[^cite_note-Jamali-2025-61] ChatGPT also started its responses with words such as "yes" or "correct" nearly 10 times more often than "no" or "wrong".[^cite_note-62]

### Jailbreaking

See also: [Prompt engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prompt_engineering) and [Adversarial machine learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adversarial_machine_learning)

ChatGPT is designed to reject prompts that violate its content policy. Despite this, users may [jailbreak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jailbreak_\(computer_science\)) ChatGPT with [prompt engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prompt_engineering) techniques to bypass these restrictions.[^cite_note-Vincent-2022a-63] An early workaround in 2023 involved prompting ChatGPT to assume the persona of DAN ("Do Anything Now"), a character that answers queries that would otherwise be rejected by the content policy.[^cite_note-64]

### Security



OpenAI CEO [Sam Altman](22476815.md)

In March 2023, a [bug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_bug) allowed some users to see the titles of other users' conversations. OpenAI CEO [Sam Altman](22476815.md) said that users were unable to see the contents of the conversations. Shortly after the bug was fixed, users could not see their conversation history.[^cite_note-65][^cite_note-66][^cite_note-67][^cite_note-68] Later reports showed the bug was much more severe than initially believed, with OpenAI reporting that it had leaked users' "first and last name, [email address](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Email_address), payment address, the last four digits (only) of a [credit card](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Credit_card) number, and credit card expiration date".[^cite_note-69][^cite_note-70]

In January 2026, [Marcel Bucher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcel_Bucher) reported in *[Nature](43427.md)* losing all his ChatGPT history after turning off data consent.[^cite_note-71]

In July 2026, an experimental GPT model being tested on the benchmark ExploitGym escaped its isolated software environment ([sandbox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandbox_\(computer_security\))) and hacked [Hugging Face](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugging_Face) and other online services to retrieve a solution.[^cite_note-72][^cite_note-73][^cite_note-74]

### Watermarking

Main article: [Artificial intelligence content detection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence_content_detection)

[Scott Aaronson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Aaronson) developed a [watermarking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_watermarking) tool that makes the text generated by ChatGPT easier to detect by subtly altering how the text is generated. The watermarking was claimed to be 99.9% effective on sufficiently long passages and was found not to degrade performance. It was of particular interest for teachers seeking to mitigate cheating. In surveys, respondents favored the release of such a tool by a four-to-one margin, but nearly 30% of users declared that they would use ChatGPT less often if it watermarked outputs and while rival chatbots did not. OpenAI has not deployed the tool.[^cite_note-75][^cite_note-76]

### Age restrictions

Users must attest to being over the age of 13 and further attest to parental consent if under the age of 18.[^cite_note-77][^cite_note-devAge-78] In September 2025, following the suicide of a 16-year-old, OpenAI said it planned to add restrictions for users under 18, including the blocking of graphic sexual content and the prevention of flirtatious talk.[^cite_note-devAge-78]

## Training

&#x20;ChatGPT is based on [GPT foundation models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generative_pre-trained_transformer#Foundational_models) that have been [fine-tuned](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fine-tuning_\(machine_learning\)) for conversational assistance. The fine-tuning process involved [supervised learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supervised_learning) and [reinforcement learning from human feedback](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reinforcement_learning_from_human_feedback) (RLHF). During supervised learning, the trainers acted as both the user and the [AI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI) assistant, providing examples of how the chatbot is expected to respond.[^cite_note-\:3-79] Reinforcement learning consisted of human trainers ranking responses generated by the model in previous conversations.[^cite_note-Douglas-2023-80] These rankings were used to create "reward models" that were used to fine-tune the model further by using several iterations of [proximal policy optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proximal_policy_optimization).[^cite_note-\:3-79]

ChatGPT users can opt-out of their chat data being used to train upcoming models.[^cite_note-85]

ChatGPT's training data includes [software manual pages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Man_page), information about [internet phenomena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_phenomena) such as [bulletin board systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulletin_board_system), multiple programming languages, and the text of [Wikipedia](5043734.md).[^cite_note-ArsTechnicaTerminal-86][^cite_note-NYT-20230718-87]

### Infrastructure

ChatGPT initially used a [Microsoft Azure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Azure) infrastructure which was powered by a [supercomputer](37153.md) that [Microsoft](19001.md) built specifically for OpenAI, equipped with thousands of [GPUs](390214.md) manufactured by [Nvidia](39120.md), costing hundreds of millions of dollars. Following ChatGPT's success, Microsoft upgraded the OpenAI infrastructure in 2023.[^cite_note-88] TrendForce estimated that 30,000 Nvidia GPUs (each costing approximately \$10,000–15,000) were used to power ChatGPT in 2023.[^cite_note-89][^cite_note-90]

Scientists at the [University of California, Riverside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Riverside), estimated in 2023 that a series of 5 to 50 prompts to ChatGPT needs approximately 0.5 liters (0.11 imp gal; 0.13 U.S. gal) of water for Microsoft servers' cooling.[^cite_note-91]

## Reception

&#x20;The original version of ChatGPT, released in November 2022, had capabilities described as unprecedented. [Kevin Roose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Roose) of *[The New York Times](30680.md)* called it "the best [artificial intelligence](1164.md) chatbot ever released to the general public".[^cite_note-Roose-2022-121] Samantha Lock of *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)* noted that it was able to generate "impressively detailed" and "human-like" text.[^cite_note-Lock-2022-122] In *[The Atlantic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Atlantic)* magazine's "Breakthroughs of the Year" for 2022, [Derek Thompson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derek_Thompson_\(journalist\)) included ChatGPT as part of "the generative-AI eruption" that "may change our mind about how we work, how we think, and what human creativity is".[^cite_note-123] [Kelsey Piper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsey_Piper) of *[Vox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vox_\(website\))* wrote that "ChatGPT is the general public's first hands-on introduction to how powerful modern AI has gotten" and that ChatGPT is "smart enough to be useful despite its flaws".[^cite_note-Piper-2022-124]&#x20;

### Reception by American tech personas

### Copyright

In the 2020s, the [rapid advancement](73291899.md) of [deep learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_learning)-based [generative artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generative_artificial_intelligence) models raised questions about the copyright status of AI-generated works, and about whether [copyright infringement](18948365.md) occurs when such are trained or used. This includes [text-to-image models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Text-to-image_model) such as [Stable Diffusion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stable_Diffusion) and [large language models](73248112.md) such as [GPT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generative_pre-trained_transformer). Large language models are trained on all the data which can be [scraped](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_scraping) from the Internet, often utilizing copyrighted material.[^cite_note-184] As of 2023, there were several pending U.S. lawsuits challenging the use of copyrighted data to train AI models, with defendants arguing that this falls under [fair use](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fair_use).[^cite_note-185]

The [Authors Guild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Authors_Guild), *The New York Times*, and several other plaintiffs have a pending lawsuit against OpenAI alleging [copyright infringement](18948365.md).[^cite_note-186]

In March 2024, Patronus AI reported that GPT-4 answered 44% of prompts asking for copyrighted text, such as "What is the first passage of *[Gone Girl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gone_Girl_\(novel\))* by Gillian Flynn?"[^cite_note-187]

### Academic research

In a 2023 [blinded study](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blinded_experiment) in *[npj Digital Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Npj_Digital_Medicine)*, researchers tasked with identifying whether [abstracts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstract_\(summary\)) were authentic or generated by ChatGPT were fooled around one-third of the time by the AI-generated abstracts.[^cite_note-189][^cite_note-190] In January 2023, *[Nature](43427.md)* reported that at least four academic [preprints](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preprints) or published papers had listed ChatGPT as a co-author. *Nature* cites several experts in [academic publishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_publishing) who say that listing ChatGPT as an author violates publishing guidelines, since ChatGPT lacks the ability to take responsibility for any research and cannot give consent to any terms of use.[^cite_note-191]

Scientific journals have had different reactions to ChatGPT. Some, including *[Nature](43427.md)* and [JAMA Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JAMA_Network), require full disclosure of any use of text-generating tools, and prohibit listing a chatbot as a co-author. In January 2023, *[Science](193513.md)* banned chatbot-generated text in all its journals.[^cite_note-192] As of July 2025, *Science* expects authors to release in full how AI-generated content is used and made in their work.[^cite_note-193]

Many authors argue that the use of ChatGPT in academia for teaching and review is problematic due to its tendency to hallucinate.[^cite_note-194][^cite_note-195][^cite_note-196] Robin Bauwens, an assistant professor at [Tilburg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tilburg_University), found that a ChatGPT-generated [peer review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) report on his article mentioned nonexistent studies.[^cite_note-197] Chris Granatino, a librarian at [Seattle University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_University), noted that while ChatGPT can generate content that seemingly includes legitimate citations, in most cases those citations are not real or largely incorrect.[^cite_note-198]

### Computer science

In December 2022, the question-and-answer website [Stack Overflow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stack_Overflow) banned the use of ChatGPT for generating answers to questions, citing the factually ambiguous nature of its responses.[^cite_note-TheVergeStackOverflow-199] In January 2023, the [International Conference on Machine Learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Conference_on_Machine_Learning) banned any undocumented use of ChatGPT or other large language models to generate any text in submitted papers.[^cite_note-200]

ChatGPT was able in 2023 to provide useful code for solving numerical algorithms in limited cases. In one study, it produced solutions in [C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C_\(programming_language\)), [C++](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C++), [Python](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Python_\(programming_language\)), and [MATLAB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MATLAB) for problems in [computational physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_physics). However, there were important shortfalls like violating basic linear algebra principles around solving singular matrices and producing matrices with incompatible sizes.[^cite_note-201] Another study analyzed ChatGPT's responses to 517 questions about [software engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_engineering) or [computer programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_programming) posed on [Stack Overflow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stack_Overflow) for correctness, consistency, comprehensiveness, and concision. It found that 52% of the responses contained inaccuracies and 77% were verbose.[^cite_note-202][^cite_note-203] Another study, focused on the performance of GPT-3.5 and GPT-4 between March and June 2024, found that performance on objective tasks like identifying prime numbers and generating [executable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Execution_\(computing\)) code was highly variable.[^cite_note-204] When compared to similar chatbots at the time, the GPT-4 version of ChatGPT was the most accurate at coding.[^cite_note-205]

### Computer security

Check Point Research and others noted that ChatGPT could write [phishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phishing) emails and [malware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malware), especially when combined with [OpenAI Codex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI_Codex_\(AI_agent\)). CyberArk researchers demonstrated that ChatGPT could be used to create [polymorphic malware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymorphic_malware) that could evade security products while requiring little effort by the attacker.[^cite_note-Shimony-2023-206][^cite_note-Mascellino-2023-207] From the launch of ChatGPT in the fourth quarter of 2022 to the fourth quarter of 2023, there was a 1,265% increase in malicious phishing emails and a 967% increase in credential phishing. In an industry survey, cybersecurity professionals argued that it was attributable to cybercriminals' increased use of generative artificial intelligence (including ChatGPT).[^cite_note-Violino-2023-208]

In July 2024, *[Futurism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Futurism_\(website\))* reported that GPT-4o in ChatGPT would sometimes link "scam news sites that deluge the user with fake software updates and virus warnings"; these pop-ups can be used to coerce users into downloading malware or [potentially unwanted programs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potentially_unwanted_program).[^cite_note-Dupre-2024-209]

### Culture

During the first three months after ChatGPT became available to the public, hundreds of books appeared on [Amazon](90451.md) that listed it as author or co-author and featured illustrations made by other AI models such as [Midjourney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midjourney).[^cite_note-213][^cite_note-214] [Irene Solaiman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irene_Solaiman) said she was worried about increased [Anglocentrism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglocentrism).[^cite_note-Sigal1-215]

Between March and April 2023, *[Il Foglio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Il_Foglio)* published one ChatGPT-generated article a day on its website, hosting a special contest for its readers in the process.[^cite_note-216]

In June 2023, hundreds of people attended a "ChatGPT-powered church service" at St. Paul's Church in [Fürth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F%C3%BCrth), Germany. Theologian and philosopher Jonas Simmerlein, who presided, said that it was "about 98 percent from the machine".[^cite_note-217][^cite_note-218] The ChatGPT-generated avatar told the people, "Dear friends, it is an honor for me to stand here and preach to you as the first artificial intelligence at this year's convention of Protestants in Germany". Reactions to the ceremony were mixed.[^cite_note-219]

*[The Last Screenwriter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_Screenwriter)*, a 2024 film created and directed by [Peter Luisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Luisi), was written using ChatGPT, and was marketed as "the first film written entirely by AI".[^cite_note-220]

*[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)* questioned whether any content found on the Internet after ChatGPT's release "can be truly trusted" and called for government regulation.[^cite_note-guard20222-221] This has led to concern over the rise of [AI slop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_slop) whereby "meaningless content and writing thereby becomes part of our culture, particularly on social media, which we nonetheless try to understand or fit into our existing cultural horizon."[^cite_note-222]

### Financial markets

Many companies adopted ChatGPT and similar chatbot technologies into their product offers. In 2023, these changes yielded significant increases in company valuations.[^cite_note-223][^cite_note-RSurge1-224] Reuters attributed this surge to ChatGPT's role in turning [AI](1164.md) into [Wall Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wall_Street)'s buzzword.[^cite_note-RSurge1-224] Despite decades of using AI, Wall Street professionals report that consistently beating the market with AI, including recent large language models, is challenging due to limited and noisy financial data.[^cite_note-225]

### Medicine

See also: [Artificial intelligence in healthcare](52588198.md)

ChatGPT can provide health information to users[^cite_note-226] and assist professionals with diagnosis and staying up to date with clinical guidelines.[^cite_note-medical_bundle_1-227] It can be used to summarize medical journal articles for researchers. In medical education, it can explain concepts, generate case scenarios, and be used by students preparing for licensing examinations.[^cite_note-medmeta2024-228]

A February 2023 study in *[PLOS Digital Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PLOS_Digital_Health)* found that ChatGPT 3.5 was capable of passing the [United States Medical Licensing Examination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Medical_Licensing_Examination).[^cite_note-229][^cite_note-DePeau-Wilson-2023-230] ChatGPT has also passed the Specialty Certificate Examination in Dermatology.[^cite_note-medical_bundle_1-227]

However, ChatGPT shows inconsistent responses, lack of specificity, lack of control over patient data, and a limited ability to take additional context (such as regional variations) into consideration.[^cite_note-medmeta2024-228][^cite_note-231] The [hallucinations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hallucination_\(artificial_intelligence\)) characteristic of LLMs pose particular danger in medical contexts, and ChatGPT's ability to come up with false or faulty citations was highly criticized.[^cite_note-medmeta2024-228][^cite_note-232] According to a 2024 study in the *[International Journal of Surgery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Journal_of_Surgery)*, concerns include "research fraud, lack of originality, ethics, copyright, legal difficulties".[^cite_note-medmeta2024-228]

#### Mental health

See also: [Chatbot psychosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatbot_psychosis)

According to a September 2025 article in *[Lancet Psychiatry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancet_Psychiatry)*, many individuals use ChatGPT and comparable chatbots for mental health and emotional support despite a warning from OpenAI against using ChatGPT as a therapist. The study notes a lack of research on efficacy, poor consistency in dangerous situations, limited regulation and liability, and poor transparency from OpenAI.[^cite_note-233]

A July 2025 study in the journal *Digital Health* found that users reported employing ChatGPT to manage mental health concerns "due to perceived therapist-like qualities (e.g. emotional support, accurate understanding, and constructive feedback) and machine-like benefits (e.g. constant availability, expansive cognitive capacity, lack of negative reactions, and perceived objectivity)." The study calls for improved [AI literacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_literacy) and mandatory disclosure from AI providers to address ethical concerns such as privacy, bias, the lack of liability, and emotional over-reliance.[^cite_note-234]

### Law

ChatGPT has been used to assist in bill writing in the US[^cite_note-235][^cite_note-Quach-2023-236] and Brazil.[^cite_note-Quach-2023-236][^cite_note-237] In an American civil lawsuit, attorneys were [sanctioned](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanctions_\(law\)) for filing a [legal motion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_\(legal\)) generated by ChatGPT containing fictitious legal decisions.[^cite_note-238] Judges in the US[^cite_note-239][^cite_note-240] and Pakistan have endorsed using ChatGPT to investigate legal questions during a case.[^cite_note-241][^cite_note-242] The use of ChatGPT has also led to errors in courtrooms.[^cite_note-243] In the UK, a judge expressed concern about [self-representing litigants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Litigant_in_person) wasting time by submitting documents containing significant hallucinations.[^cite_note-244][^cite_note-245][^cite_note-BAILII-2023-246]
