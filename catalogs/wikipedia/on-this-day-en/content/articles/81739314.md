The **ambassador of Russia to Mali** is the official representative of the [president](239646.md) and the [government of the Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_the_Russian_Federation) to the [president](430390.md) and the [government of Mali](19131.md).

The ambassador and his staff work at large in the [Russian embassy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassy_of_Russia_in_Bamako) in [Bamako](56606.md).[^cite_note-1] The current Russian ambassador to Mali is [Igor Gromyko](83061253.md), incumbent since 17 June 2019.[^cite_note-2]

## History of diplomatic relations

Main article: [Mali–Russia relations](18055161.md)

Formal diplomatic relations between Mali and the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) were established on 14 October 1960.[^cite_note-3] [Aleksandr Loshchakov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandr_Loshchakov?action=edit\&redlink=1) was appointed as the first ambassador on 17 December 1960.[^cite_note-kbs-4] With the [dissolution of the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissolution_of_the_Soviet_Union) in 1991, Mali recognised the Russian Federation as its [successor state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Successor_state) on 16 January 1992. The incumbent Soviet ambassador, [Aleksandr Trofimov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandr_Trofimov_\(diplomat\)), continued as ambassador from Russia until 1992.[^cite_note-kbs-4]

Diplomatic relations between Russia and Niger were established in 1972, and [Gennady Sokolov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gennady_Sokolov?action=edit\&redlink=1) was appointed the first ambassador on 13 June 1972. The embassy in [Niamey](57061.md) was closed in 1992 as part of cost-saving measures, and the ambassador to Mali was thereafter given [dual accreditation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_accreditation) as the non-resident ambassador to Niger. This practice came to an end with the reopening of the embassy in Niamey, and the appointment of [Viktor Voropayev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viktor_Voropayev?action=edit\&redlink=1) as the [ambassador to Niger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ambassadors_of_Russia_to_Niger) in 2025.[^cite_note-kbs2-5]
