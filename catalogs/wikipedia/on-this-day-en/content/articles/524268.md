# Street artist

This article is about all forms of art in public places. For artworks in public locations, see [Street art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Street_art).



Street artist, [Place du Tertre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Place_du_Tertre), [Paris](22989.md)

A **street artist** is a person who makes art in public places.[^cite_note-1] Street artists include [portrait artists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portrait_artists), [caricaturists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caricature), [graffiti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graffiti) artists, [muralists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mural) and people making [crafts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craft). Street artists can also refer to [street performers](236424.md) such as [musicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musician), [acrobats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acrobatics), [jugglers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juggler), [living statues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Living_statue), and [street theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Street_theatre) performers. Street artists can be seen throughout the world.

## Legality

Some countries and sub-national jurisdictions require a license or permit in order to do business as a street artist. Without legal authorization, artists run the risk of being fined or arrested by the police if municipal ordinances prohibit their activities. For graffiti artists and muralists, part of the experience may be finding a suitable wall, permitted or not. In some municipalities, artists may apply for a license that allows them to legally occupy public space or a permit to paint a building. These licenses and permits place limitations on where the artist can perform or create their art, and may also regulate what artists are allowed to sell or depict. [San Francisco](49728.md), [Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California), and [Seattle](11388236.md) are American cities that regulate the display and trade of artists selling their wares on the street.[^cite_note-2] Additionally, San Francisco has a public approval process for murals painted on public buildings or using public money on private property.[^cite_note-3]

Graffiti art is often seen as a controversial art form and can at times be referred to as vandalism.[^cite_note-4] However some graffiti artists have become famous for their art, notably [Banksy](396241.md) and [Shepard Fairey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shepard_Fairey) also known as Obey.
