The **2026 Indian Premier League**, also known as **IPL 19** and branded as **TATA IPL 2026**, was the 19th edition of the [Indian Premier League](13260340.md), a professional [Twenty20](770690.md) [cricket](25675557.md) league. The tournament featured 10 teams competing in 74 matches from 28 March to 31 May across 13 venues.

Defending champions [Royal Challengers Bengaluru](15861688.md) defeated [Gujarat Titans](70008752.md) by 5 wickets in [the final](80074642.md) to secure their second consecutive IPL title. They became the first franchise to win the IPL and the [Women's Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Women's_Premier_League_\(cricket\)) in the same year. [Vaibhav Sooryavanshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaibhav_Sooryavanshi) of [Rajasthan Royals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajasthan_Royals) scored the most runs (776) while [Kagiso Rabada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kagiso_Rabada) of Gujarat took the most wickets (29). Sooryavanshi was also named the most valuable player of the tournament.

## Background

The [Indian Premier League](13260340.md) (IPL) is a professional [Twenty20](770690.md) (T20) [cricket](25675557.md) league, organised by the [Board of Control for Cricket in India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_Control_for_Cricket_in_India) (BCCI). It has been held annually since the [first edition in 2008](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Indian_Premier_League).[^cite_note-1] [Royal Challengers Bengaluru](15861688.md) were the defending champions, having won their maiden title in the [previous season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Indian_Premier_League) after defeating [Punjab Kings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjab_Kings) in the [final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Indian_Premier_League_final).[^cite_note-2]

### Format

Each team played once against the teams in their group and twice against the teams in the other group. After the [group stage](#League_stage), the top four teams, based on aggregate points, advanced to the [playoffs](#Playoffs). In this stage, the top two teams competed with each other (in a match titled "[Qualifier 1](#Qualifier_1)"), as did the remaining two teams (in a match titled "[Eliminator](#Eliminator)"). While the winner of the Qualifier 1 directly qualified for the final match, the losing team had another chance to qualify for the final match by competing against the winning team of the Eliminator (in a match titled "[Qualifier 2](#Qualifier_2)"). The winner of this subsequent Qualifier 2 advanced to the final match. Teams were seeded into groups based on the number of titles they had won.[^cite_note-sch2-3][^cite_note-faq-4][^cite_note-5]

The [IPL Governing Council](13260340.md#Organization) had initially announced that the IPL would expand to 84 matches for 2026 and 2027,[^cite_note-6] with it expected to expand to 94 matches from 2028 onwards with the return of the complete [double round-robin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_round-robin) format that was used until 2021.[^cite_note-7] However, the number of matches was kept as 74 for one more edition as it was in the previous four seasons.[^cite_note-8]

### Schedule

According to [ESPNcricinfo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cricinfo), the dates of the tournament window for the three seasons between 2025 and 2027 were sent to franchises ahead of the [2025 auction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_2025_Indian_Premier_League_personnel_changes).[^cite_note-9] In November 2024, the tentative dates for the three seasons were announced, with the 2026 edition set to take place from 15 March to 31 May.[^cite_note-10] In December 2025, it was announced that the 2026 season would commence on 26 March to allow for a three-week gap after the [2026 Men's T20 World Cup final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Men's_T20_World_Cup_final) scheduled to take place on 8 March.[^cite_note-11] In February 2026, it was announced that the 2026 season would commence 2 days later on 28 March.[^cite_note-sch0-12] This led to a clash with the [2026 Pakistan Super League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Pakistan_Super_League) for the second consecutive year taking place from 26 March to 3 May 2026, which was again moved from its typical February–March window for the same reason.[^cite_note-13]

In March 2026, it was announced that the fixtures for that season would be announced in two parts due to the [state assembly elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_elections_in_India#State_Assembly_elections) in [Assam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Assam_Legislative_Assembly_election), [Tamil Nadu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Tamil_Nadu_Legislative_Assembly_election), and [West Bengal](77457015.md);[^cite_note-sch0-12][^cite_note-14] for which the dates were not announced till 15 March.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17] The first part of the schedule comprising the first twenty matches was announced on 11 March, with the inaugural match being hosted at [M. Chinnaswamy Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M._Chinnaswamy_Stadium) between the defending champions Royal Challengers Bengaluru and [Sunrisers Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunrisers_Hyderabad) on 28 March.[^cite_note-sch1-18] The complete fixtures for the league stage were announced on 26 March.[^cite_note-sch2-3] On 6 May the playoff fixtures were confirmed[^cite_note-pos1-19] with the Qualifier 1 at [HPCA Cricket Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Himachal_Pradesh_Cricket_Association_Stadium) on 26 May, the Eliminator and Qualifier 2 at [Maharaja Yadavindra Singh Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maharaja_Yadavindra_Singh_International_Cricket_Stadium) on 27 and 29 May,[^cite_note-pos3-20] and the final at [Narendra Modi Stadium](80201506.md) on 31 May, with the venue hosting its fourth final after [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Indian_Premier_League_final), [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Indian_Premier_League_final) and 2025.[^cite_note-pos2-21][^cite_note-22]

### Exclusion of Mustafizur Rahman

See also: [2026 Men's T20 World Cup § Withdrawal of Bangladesh](69284234.md#Withdrawal_of_Bangladesh)

Bangladeshi fast bowler [Mustafizur Rahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mustafizur_Rahman) was bought by [Kolkata Knight Riders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolkata_Knight_Riders) for [₹](490783.md)9.2 [crore](121420.md) (US\$960,000) in the auction.[^cite_note-23] Following [anti-Hindu violence in Bangladesh during 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Bangladesh_anti-Hindu_violence) and the [lynching of Dipu Chandra Das](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynching_of_Dipu_Chandra_Das) in [December 2025](81879081.md), there were calls for the exclusion of Bangladeshi players from the IPL.[^cite_note-24] In January 2026, the BCCI requested that the franchise release Rahman from the squad and permitted them to select a replacement player.[^cite_note-25] This move was criticized by former Indian cricketer [Madan Lal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madan_Lal),[^cite_note-26] [Congress](149333.md) politician [Shashi Tharoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shashi_Tharoor),[^cite_note-27] and former Bangladeshi captains [Khaled Mahmud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khaled_Mahmud) and [Mohammad Ashraful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Ashraful)[^cite_note-28] while former Indian cricketer [Aakash Chopra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aakash_Chopra) and[^cite_note-29] [BJP](149330.md) politician [Sangeet Singh Som](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangeet_Singh_Som) defended the BCCI's move.[^cite_note-30]

This led the BCCI to put the [Indian tour of Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_cricket_team_in_Bangladesh_in_2026) on hold due to tensions.[^cite_note-31] Furthermore, the [Bangladesh Cricket Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Cricket_Board) requested the [International Cricket Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Cricket_Council) (ICC) to shift their matches at the [2026 Men's T20 World Cup](69284234.md) which was to be co-hosted by India and Sri Lanka, out of India, similar to that of Pakistan's.[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33] But the request was rejected by the ICC, and Bangladesh ultimately withdrew from the T20 World Cup.[^cite_note-34][^cite_note-Scot-35] The Bangladeshi government also banned the broadcast of IPL in their country.[^cite_note-36] Following the [2026 Bangladeshi general election](72614737.md), the newly elected government clarified that the broadcast of the IPL is not banned in their country anymore.[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38]

### Marketing

The [Tata Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Group) renewed their contract as the title sponsors of the IPL for a tenure of 5 years (2024–2028) for ₹2,500 crore (US\$260 million).[^cite_note-39] The advertising campaign for the 2026 IPL consisted of 27 sponsors including – [Google AI Mode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_AI_Mode), Campa Energy, [Havells & Lloyd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Havells), [Birla Opus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aditya_Birla_Group), [Hero MotoCorp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hero_MotoCorp) and [Amazon](90451.md).[^cite_note-40]

### Broadcasting

[JioStar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JioStar)'s [Star Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Sports_\(India\)) and [JioHotstar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JioHotstar), held the television and digital rights respectively, for the remainder of the 2023–2027 cycle.[^cite_note-41] In the opening weekend of the season which included two matches, linear television reported 7.84 million viewership, with an 18.8% drop in the average television viewership rating compared to last season, while JioHotstar reported 515 million viewership, with a 26% increase in total watch time to 32.6 billion minutes.[^cite_note-42][^cite_note-43]

### Personnel changes

Main article: [List of 2026 Indian Premier League personnel changes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_2026_Indian_Premier_League_personnel_changes)

The franchises were required to submit their retention lists before 15 November 2025,[^cite_note-TOI-61] and a total of 173 players were retained ahead of the auction.[^cite_note-62] The auction was held on 16 December 2025 in [Abu Dhabi](18950756.md), United Arab Emirates[^cite_note-63] with the auction purse for each franchise set at ₹125 crore (US\$13 million).[^cite_note-retain1-64] A total of 1,355 players registered for the auction, of which 369 players were shortlisted and 156 were featured in the auction and 77 were sold in the auction.[^cite_note-sold1-65][^cite_note-sold2-66][^cite_note-unsold-67] [Cameron Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Green) became the most expensive overseas player and the third most expensive player in the history of the IPL when he was bought by Kolkata for ₹25.20 crore (US\$2.6 million).[^cite_note-68] [Prashant Veer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prashant_Veer) and [Kartik Sharma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kartik_Sharma) became the joint-most expensive uncapped players in the history of the IPL when they were both bought by [Chennai Super Kings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chennai_Super_Kings) for ₹14.20 crore (US\$1.5 million) each.[^cite_note-69] The 79 unsold players included players such as [Jake Fraser-McGurk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jake_Fraser-McGurk), [Jonny Bairstow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonny_Bairstow), [Daryl Mitchell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daryl_Mitchell_\(New_Zealand_cricketer\)), [Devon Conway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devon_Conway), [Alzarri Joseph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alzarri_Joseph), [Gus Atkinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gus_Atkinson), [Deepak Hooda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deepak_Hooda) and [Karn Sharma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karn_Sharma).[^cite_note-unsold-67]

## Venues

The league stage was played at 13 stadiums across India. 12 venues from the previous season returned as respective home grounds with HPCA Cricket Stadium and [ACA Cricket Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assam_Cricket_Association_Stadium) as secondary home venues for three Punjab Kings matches and three [Rajasthan Royals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajasthan_Royals) matches respectively.[^cite_note-sch1-18][^cite_note-sch2-3] [ACA–VDCA Cricket Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACA%E2%80%93VDCA_Cricket_Stadium) was dropped as [Delhi Capitals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi_Capitals) secondary home venue[^cite_note-70] while [Shaheed Veer Narayan Singh Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaheed_Veer_Narayan_Singh_International_Cricket_Stadium) which had previously served as secondary home venue for [Delhi Daredevils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi_Daredevils) until 2016, was added as secondary home venue for two Royal Challengers Bengaluru matches.[^cite_note-faq-4][^cite_note-sch2-3][^cite_note-71] The opening match was played at M. Chinnaswamy Stadium.[^cite_note-sch1-18] The playoffs were played at HPCA Cricket Stadium and Maharaja Yadavindra Singh Stadium with the final at Narendra Modi Stadium.[^cite_note-pos3-20]
