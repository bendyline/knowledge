On 16 March 2026, [Israel](9282173.md) began a [ground invasion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invasion) in [Lebanon](17771.md), as part of the [2026 Lebanon war](82549840.md), [Hezbollah–Israel conflict](75005425.md), and broader [Middle Eastern crisis](75895273.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-\:0-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-The_Times_of_Israel-20260316-4] While Israel had already been occupying some pieces of Lebanese territory,[^cite_note-10] the [Israeli Defense Forces](39237.md) expanded its [military occupation](816214.md) within Lebanon to a total of 570–600 square kilometers (220–232 sq mi) by the [2026 Israel–Lebanon temporary ceasefire](82970933.md).[^cite_note-\:0-2][^cite_note-\:3-11]

## Background

On 28 February 2026, Israel and the [United States](3434750.md) launched a [war against Iran](82533582.md).[^cite_note-Bernama-2026-12][^cite_note-france24.com-13] With Israel having violated the [November 2024 Israel–Lebanon ceasefire agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Israel%E2%80%93Lebanon_ceasefire_agreement) on a near-daily basis[^cite_note-14] and [Iran](14653.md) being [Hezbollah](13919.md)'s primary backer,[^cite_note-15] the [assassination of Iranian Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei](82537558.md) moved the militant group to resume rocket strikes on Israel on 2 March.[^cite_note-16] The [Israeli Air Force](177619.md) quickly responded with airstrikes across much of Lebanon, including the capital [Beirut](37428.md), thus initiating the Lebanon war.

The 2026 occupation marks the seventh occupation of Lebanon by Israel. Other such occupations include during the [1948–1949 Arab–Israeli War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948%E2%80%931949_Arab%E2%80%93Israeli_war),[^cite_note-17] the annexation of Shebaa Farms since the [Six-Day War](29328.md) in 1967,[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] the [1978 South Lebanon conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1978_South_Lebanon_conflict), the [1982–2000 occupation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_occupation_of_Southern_Lebanon_\(1982%E2%80%932000\)), the [2006 Lebanon War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Lebanon_War), and the [2024 Lebanon war](78006796.md).[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21][^cite_note-22]

## Occupation

By 16 March, the [Israeli Ground Forces](64121347.md) expanded the war with a ground invasion, while air units continued to destroy civilian infrastructure linking the south from the rest of Lebanon.[^cite_note-The_Times_of_Israel-202603162-23] On 24 March, the [Israeli Minister of Defense](3226446.md) [Israel Katz](19041378.md) declared that Israel would demolish Lebanese border villages and permanently occupy Lebanese territory up to the [Litani River](1920925.md).[^cite_note-France24_occupation-24]

The Israeli occupation of Lebanon has resulted in the [forced expulsion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forced_expulsion) of more than 1.2 million Lebanese civilians (>20% of the country's population), as Israel prohibits them from [returning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_of_return) "until [security](240468.md) is guaranteed for the residents of the [north \[of Israel\]](324281.md)."[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-Los_Angeles_Times-20260314-26][^cite_note-\:1-27] Israel has issued evacuation orders over about 2,000 square kilometers (772 sq mi) of Lebanese territory south of the [Zahrani River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zahrani_River),[^cite_note-\:1-27] with further orders in southern Beirut.[^cite_note-\:3-11]

On 15 June 2026, Israeli Prime Minister [Benjamin Netanyahu](56472.md) announced that Israeli forces will continue to occupy southern Lebanon, stating that, "We will stay in the Lebanon security buffer zone for as long as necessary."[^cite_note-28]

On 24 June 2026, Israeli defence minister Israel Katz says Israeli forces will not withdraw from southern Lebanon even if "the [United States](3434750.md) demands it", adding that "200,000 residents will not return" to their homes.[^cite_note-29]

On 5 July 2026, Netanyahu says some [Christian](4796145.md)-majority villages in [southern](4205968.md) [Lebanon](17771.md) asked to be [annexed by Israel](2781576.md) for protection from [Hezbollah](13919.md), while villages in the [Marjayoun District](8968039.md) deny seeking annexation. On the same day, Netanyahu said Israel will maintain the occupation.[^cite_note-30]

## Life under occupation

Despite Israeli requests to evacuate most of the villages, towns and cities occupied by the Israeli Defense Forces, some citizens in areas which were non-Shia-majority were allowed to stay in their residences,[^cite_note-\:2-31] including [Christian](18337522.md), [Druze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druze) and [Sunni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Islam) inhabited areas. Life under occupation has been characterized by [arrests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrest), [searches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Search_and_seizure), strict [curfews](297681.md) and restricted [freedom of movement](1270497.md).[^cite_note-\:2-31][^cite_note-32] Even in authorized villages and towns the population suffered from food shortages, electricity shortage and limited healthcare accessibility.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34]

Despite harsh economic, social and material conditions in permitted areas, people continued to attend religious infrastructures, flock their sheep and attend [school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School).[^cite_note-35]

According to the [New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Times), Israel contacted local Christian and Druze in private and pressured them to expel Shiite citizens from their towns.[^cite_note-36]
