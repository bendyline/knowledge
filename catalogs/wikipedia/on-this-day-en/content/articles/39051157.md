**Khalifa Economic Zones Abu Dhabi - KEZAD Group** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): مناطق خليفة الاقتصادية أبوظبي – مجموعة كيزاد, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Madīnat Khalīfah Al-Iqtesā'diyah Abu Dhabi - Majmoua Kezad*) operates economic zones, industrial real estate, [logistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logistics) [infrastructure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infrastructure), and staff accommodation in the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md). It is managed by [AD Ports Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AD_Ports_Group) under its Economic Cities & Free Zones cluster.[^cite_note-1]

## Background

KEZAD Group was launched in September 2022 through the consolidation of Khalifa Industrial Zone Abu Dhabi (KIZAD) and ZonesCorp.[^cite_note-2] It was established to expand [Abu Dhabi](18950756.md)’s economic cities and free zones, offering and streamlining infrastructure development, regulatory frameworks, and investment facilitation.[^cite_note-3]

## Operations

As of 2025, KEZAD Group’s infrastructure and services span 12 economic zones across Abu Dhabi, Al Ain and Al Dhafra Region, as well as an industrial and logistics zone in East Port Said, Egypt, covering a total area of 570 sq km. [^cite_note-4]

KEZAD Group’s platform supports over 2,300 businesses across 17 economic sectors, including logistics, automotive, food and beverages, pharmaceuticals, metals, chemicals, packaging, and clean energy industries.[^cite_note-5]

## Location & Connectivity



Industrial zone and wetlands, coast of [Persian Gulf](24761.md)

KEZAD Areas A and B, which make up approximately 410 sq km of the 550 sq km total area, are located between [Abu Dhabi](18950756.md) and [Dubai](211583.md), adjacent to [Khalifa Port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khalifa_Port), a major deep-water container port in the UAE.&#x20;

It lies along the E11 Highway with future road links connecting to Al Ain, and is near three major airports: [Zayed International Airport](487794.md) (35 km); [Al Maktoum International Airport](3260694.md) (52 km); and [Dubai International Airport](154742.md) (85 km). Companies based in KEZAD have access to multimodal transport by sea, air and road, with seamless integration [Etihad Rail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etihad_Rail) network that will connect KEZAD directly to industrial and consumer hubs across the UAE and [GCC](220950.md).

## Infrastructure and facilities

KEZAD provides pre-built and build-to-suit infrastructure and real estate solutions, including:

- 587,000 m² of built-up industrial and logistics space, with 93,000 m² dedicated to cold storage.

- 250,000 m² of warehousing capacity under development with AED 621 million investment.

- Serviced plots, cold storage units, light industrial units, and startup-ready facilities.

## Sustainability & Digital transformation

KEZAD Group incorporates [environmental, social, and governance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental,_social,_and_governance) (ESG) standards and digital initiatives into its zone operations and infrastructure. Programmes include carbon capture and [circular economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circular_economy) measures, [plastic recycling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plastic_recycling), water reuse and [waste management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waste_management).[^cite_note-12] The group has developed 24 km of landscaped areas and green corridors.[^cite_note-13]

KEZAD Group and BEEAH have formed a joint venture to deliver integrated, eco-friendly waste management services to customers across the group’s economic cities and free zones.[^cite_note-14] It also partnered with [Masdar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masdar) on a [green hydrogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_hydrogen) hub project aligned with the UAE’s National Hydrogen Strategy[^cite_note-15] and has collaborated with [Siemens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siemens) to support [Industry 4.0](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourth_Industrial_Revolution) adoption and deploy smart manufacturing systems.[^cite_note-16]
