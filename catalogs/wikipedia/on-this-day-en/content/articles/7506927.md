**Siak** ([Jawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawi_script): سياق) is a [regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regency_\(Indonesia\)) (*kabupaten*) of [Riau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riau) Province, on the island of [Sumatra](28679.md), [Indonesia](14579.md). It has an area of 7,803.57 km<sup>2</sup> and had a population of 376,742 at the 2010 Census[^cite_note-Biro_Pusat_Statistik_2011-4] and 457,940 at the 2020 Census;[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2021-5] the official estimate as at mid 2025 was 507,729 (comprising 260,571 males and 247,158 females).[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2026-3] The administrative centre of the regency is located at [Siak Sri Indrapura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siak_Sri_Indrapura) in Mempura District . The northern part of this regency contains a large part of the [Bukit Batu Biosphere Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giam_Siak_Kecil-Bukit_Batu_Bioreserve).

Previously the area was part of the [Sultanate of Siak Sri Indrapura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultanate_of_Siak_Sri_Indrapura). At the beginning of the independence of Indonesia, Sultan Syarif Kasim II, the Sultanate of Siak was the last state to join the Republic of Indonesia. Later this region was included in an area under the Siak Kewedanan Bengkalis. On 4 October 1999 under Law No. 53 of 1999, a Siak Regency was established from what had previously been the southern districts of [Bengkalis Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengkalis_Regency), and Siak Sri Indrapura was declared the administrative capital of Siak Regency.

## Transportation

### Water

From [Pekanbaru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pekanbaru), Siak can be reached by river from Pekanbaru's Port, There is usually a boat that operates every 4-5 times a week with a travel time of around 2–4 hours. In addition to Pekanbaru itself, this port also serves other destinations including [Bengkalis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengkalis_Regency), Selat Panjang, and [Batam](716783.md) (via Buton Bengkalis).

### Land



The Siak Sri Indrapura Bridge or Tengku Agung Sultanah Latifah Bridge was opened in 2007 and was the Grandest Bridge until 2009.

Other than by river, Siak can also be reached by road, From [Pekanbaru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pekanbaru) which is about a 3 to 4 hour journey. In addition Siak has a bridge that connects Siak with its capital which is named&#x20;*&#x20;Tengku Agung Sultana Latifah&#x20;*&#x20;which is the name of a previous Sultan of Siak. This bridge was inaugurated on 11 August 2007 and was known as the longest bridge in Indonesia until 2010, until the construction of the [Suramadu Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suramadu_Bridge) in Surabaya was finished.

## Tourism



As-Sayyid Al-Sharif Hasyim Abdul Jalil Syaifuddin, Sultan of Siak (r.1889-1908), circa 1900

Tours of Siak include the [Siak Sri Indrapura Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siak_Sri_Indrapura_Palace), which is a historic palace of the Sultanate of Siak. The palace is very famous, many tourists visit to see the grandeur of the palace and the objects located within, such as the golden throne which belonged to the Sultan, the royal dining chairs and a Komet Music Box of which only one other remains in the world. This palace is open from 10 am to 4 pm.
