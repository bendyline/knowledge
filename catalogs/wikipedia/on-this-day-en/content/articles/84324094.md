Main article: [Swimming at the 2026 Asian Games](84060195.md)

The **men's 400 metre freestyle** event at the [2026 Asian Games](51790406.md) was held on 25 September 2026 at the [Tokyo Aquatics Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Aquatics_Centre).[^cite_note-1] China's Zhang Zhanshuo won the gold medal in a Games-record time of 3\:41.28, finishing 4.27 seconds ahead of Japan's silver medallist Kazushi Imafuku.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]
