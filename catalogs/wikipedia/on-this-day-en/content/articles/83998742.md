The **Our Lady of Mercy monument** ([Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_language): *Monument Matki Bożej Miłosiernej*) in [Konotopie](18205919.md), [Poland](22936.md), is a monument dedicated to the [Virgin Mary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virgin_Mary) under the [Marian title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titles_of_Mary,_mother_of_Jesus) Our Lady of Mercy. It is the tallest statue of Mary in Europe and tallest statue of any kind in the [European Union](9317.md).

## History

Businessman [Roman Karkosik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Karkosik?action=edit\&redlink=1) and his wife Grażyna commissioned the creation of a monument dedicated to [Mary, mother of Jesus](73513.md) in the town of [Konotopie](18205919.md), [Poland](22936.md).[^cite_note-euronews-1] The Konotopie local government has been aware of plans for the statue as early as 2023.[^cite_note-construction-euronews-2] The ceremonial laying of the foundation was held on 12 March 2025 within the vicinity of the Sanctuary of Our Lady of Sorrows.[^cite_note-euronews-1][^cite_note-construction-euronews-2]

The monument dedicated to Mary, under the title "Our Lady of Mercy" was consecrated on 15 August 2026.[^cite_note-euronews-1] It was blessed by Bishop Krzysztof Wętkowski.[^cite_note-vatican-3] At the time of its completion, it is noted for being the tallest Marian statue in Europe.[^cite_note-vatican-3][^cite_note-euronews-1][^cite_note-ap-4]

## Design

Adam Jakub Matejkowski was the sculptor of the Our Lady of Mercy monument.[^cite_note-euronews-1] He asked his students Krzysztof Skóra and Marek Purs to collaborate on him on the project.[^cite_note-tvn-5] Matejkowski said he based the facial features of the statue on his wife and the expression on his 11-year-old daughter.[^cite_note-dw1-6] It depicts the [Virgin Mary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virgin_Mary) standing with her gaze directed downward and her arms slightly outstretched in an inviting gesture.[^cite_note-tvn-5]

It is the tallest statue of Mary in Europe at 55.6 meters (182 ft).[^cite_note-7] The height includes a crown-shaped 15-meter (49 ft) pedestal.[^cite_note-ap-4][^cite_note-dw1-6] The [Mother of All Asia – Tower of Peace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mother_of_All_Asia_%E2%80%93_Tower_of_Peace) in the [Philippines](23440.md) is still the tallest Marian statue in the world at 98 meters (322 ft).[^cite_note-euronews-1]
