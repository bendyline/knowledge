[Mirra Andreeva](71915009.md) defeated [Maja Chwalińska](53209114.md) in the final, 6–3, 6–2 to win the women's singles tennis title at the [2026 French Open](81641720.md).[^cite_note-1] It was her first [major](197638.md#Tournaments) title.[^cite_note-2] Andreeva was the youngest woman (19 years and 1 month) to win the tournament since [Monica Seles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monica_Seles) in [1992](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles).[^cite_note-3] Chwalińska was the second qualifier in the [Open Era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Era) to reach a major final, after [Emma Raducanu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Raducanu) at the [2021 US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles).[^cite_note-4] She was the third woman to reach the French Open final on her tournament debut, after [Evonne Goolagong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evonne_Goolagong) in [1971](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1971_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles) and [Chris Evert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Evert) in [1973](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1973_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles).[^cite_note-5] She was also the third Polish major finalist in the Open Era, following [Agnieszka Radwańska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agnieszka_Radwa%C5%84ska) and [Iga Świątek](51838558.md). [^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] Ranked No. 114, Chwalińska became the lowest-ranked finalist in French Open history.[^cite_note-8]

[Coco Gauff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coco_Gauff) was the defending champion,[^cite_note-9] but lost in the third round to [Anastasia Potapova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anastasia_Potapova).[^cite_note-10] This was the first time since 2021 that Gauff was ousted from the French Open by someone other than [Iga Świątek](51838558.md).

[Marta Kostyuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marta_Kostyuk) became the first Ukrainian woman to reach the semifinals at the French Open, defeating compatriot [Elina Svitolina](27630639.md) in the first all-Ukrainian major quarterfinal in the Open Era. She was the third Ukrainian major semifinalist in the Open Era, after Svitolina and [Dayana Yastremska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dayana_Yastremska).[^cite_note-11] With her third-round win, [Sorana Cîrstea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorana_C%C3%AErstea) became the oldest woman, at 36 years, 52 days old, in the Open Era to score a [double bagel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagel_\(tennis\)#Double_bagel) in a singles major main-draw match, overtaking [Victoria Azarenka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_Azarenka) from [the previous year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles).[^cite_note-12] By reaching the quarterfinals for the first time since [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles),[^cite_note-13] Cîrstea set the longest gap between a player's first two quarterfinals at a women's singles major in the Open Era.[^cite_note-14]

[Aryna Sabalenka](50222577.md) retained the [world No. 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_WTA_number_1_ranked_singles_tennis_players) singles ranking after [Elena Rybakina](55541443.md) lost in the second round.[^cite_note-15] Her loss to [Diana Shnaider](68209798.md) in the quarterfinals guaranteed a first-time [ major champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Grand_Slam_women's_singles_champions).[^cite_note-16] This was the first major since the [1977 French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1977_French_Open) to have no former major champions reach the semifinals of either the women's or the [men's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Men's_singles) singles events.[^cite_note-17]
