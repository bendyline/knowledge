# Colombia–Venezuela border



The border between Colombia and Venezuela



Colombia and Venezuela in 1898



The border between Colombia and Venezuela

The **Colombia–Venezuela border** is an international border of 2219 [kilometers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilometer) (1378 mi) between [Colombia](5222.md) and [Venezuela](32374.md), with a total of 603 milestones that demarcate the line.[^cite_note-Límites-1] It is the longest border of both Colombia and Venezuela.

The border, at least in its terrestrial part, was essentially demarcated by two treaties: the Spanish Arbitration Award of Queen Maria Cristina of 1891 and the Treaty of Limits and River Navigation of 1941.[^cite_note-Monografía-2] But the definition of the border in the [Gulf of Venezuela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_of_Venezuela) is still disputed, causing diplomatic clashes between the two countries.

In August 2015, two Venezuelan soldiers were injured during a fight with alleged Colombian smugglers,[^cite_note-3] prompting Venezuelan Dictator [Nicolás Maduro](7161476.md) to close a large part of the border, except in [Táchira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%A1chira).[^cite_note-4]
