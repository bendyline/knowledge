The **Pakistan Super League** (**PSL**), also known as **HBL PSL** for sponsorship reasons, is a professional [Twenty20](770690.md) (T20) [cricket](25675557.md) league in [Pakistan](23235.md), organised by the [Pakistan Cricket Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Cricket_Board) (PCB).

The league was founded by the PCB in 2015, and as of 2026 features eight city-based franchise teams.[^cite_note-expansion26-3] Instead of operating as an association of independently owned teams, the league operates as a single entity in which each franchise is owned and controlled by investors.[^cite_note-4]

Each team plays group-stage matches in a [double round-robin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_round-robin) format, with the top four teams qualifying for the playoffs, culminating in the final.

The [2026 Pakistan Super League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Pakistan_Super_League) was the eleventh season of the league. [Islamabad United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamabad_United) and [Lahore Qalandars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahore_Qalandars) are the most successful teams, with three titles each, followed by [Peshawar Zalmi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peshawar_Zalmi) with two titles.[^cite_note-5]

## History

Main article: [History of the Pakistan Super League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Pakistan_Super_League)

### Establishment

In September 2015, the [Pakistan Cricket Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Cricket_Board) officially announced the launch of the PSL. Former [Pakistani national team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_national_cricket_team) captains [Wasim Akram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wasim_Akram) and [Rameez Raja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rameez_Raja) signed up to promote the PSL and become [brand ambassadors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brand_ambassadors) of the league for three years.[^cite_note-6] After several years of planning and two previous failed attempts,[^cite_note-gt21sep15-7][^cite_note-aj4feb16-8] the league officially began on 4 February 2016 in the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md). The [UAE](69328.md) [sports minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_sports), [Nahyan bin Mubarak Al Nahyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahyan_bin_Mubarak_Al_Nahyan), inaugurated the opening ceremony.[^cite_note-PSL-9] The first two seasons included five teams based on the capital cities of Pakistan's provinces and the Federal capital.[^cite_note-wsj23feb16-10][^cite_note-11] In its first season the PSL had a higher percentage of international players. The league uses a draft system for player recruitment similar to that used in many North American professional sports leagues and as opposed to the auction system used in some other T20 leagues.[^cite_note-tribune15dec15-12]

The PSL's official logo was launched on 20 September 2015 in a ceremony in [Lahore](125315.md), and was revealed by 3Di.The ceremony was attended by current and retired cricketers, as well as Pakistani celebrities.

The commercial rights to the initial franchises were sold for US\$93 million for a span of 10 years in December 2015. The market value of PSL in 2017 was up to US\$300 million according to [Arif Habib](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arif_Habib),[^cite_note-13] and has grown significantly in the years since.[^cite_note-14]

### Expansion

#### Five to Six teams (2018)

The league expanded to six franchises with the inclusion of [Multan Sultans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multan_Sultans) for the [2018 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Pakistan_Super_League). The PCB had shortlisted [Faisalabad](401880.md), [FATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federally_Administered_Tribal_Areas), [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad,_Sindh), [Dera Murad Jamali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dera_Murad_Jamali) and [Multan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multan) as candidate cities,[^cite_note-15] with several political and regional stakeholders expressing interest in securing representation for their areas.[^cite_note-16] [Multan Sultans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multan_Sultans) was announced as the new franchise on 1 June 2017, purchased by [Schön Properties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sch%C3%B6n_Properties) for US\$5.2 million per year.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18] On 10 November 2018, the PCB terminated the franchise agreement with Schön Properties, after which new owners took control and retained the Multan Sultans name.[^cite_note-Multerm-19]

#### Six to Eight teams (2026)

Following the expiration of the franchise's operating agreement, the Pakistan Cricket Board auctioned the [Multan Sultans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multan_Sultans) franchise, which was purchased by Walee Technologies on 9 February 2026 for a bid of PKR 2.45 billion, making it the most expensive franchise sale in PSL history.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21] After they acquisition the owners decided to relocate the franchise to [Rawalpindi](343524.md) and rebranded it as [Rawalpindiz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rawalpindiz).[^cite_note-espn1-22]

PCB held an auction to sale rights of two more teams and added [Sialkot Stallionz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sialkot_Stallionz) and [Hyderabad Kingsmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad_Kingsmen) as seventh and eighth team.[^cite_note-expansion26-3][^cite_note-23] Later the owners of [Sialkot Stallionz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sialkot_Stallionz) sold the franchise to CD Ventures before the start of [2026 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Pakistan_Super_League) who decided to bring back [Multan Sultans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multan_Sultans) as team identity and relocated the franchise from [Sialkot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sialkot) to [Multan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multan).[^cite_note-24]

### Trophy

From its inception, the Pakistan Super League has featured new trophy designs for most editions. The [inaugural 2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Pakistan_Super_League) "Shooting Star" trophy was designed by [Swarovski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swarovski) and unveiled in [Dubai](211583.md) ahead of the first season, followed by the "Spirit Trophy" in 2017 which continued Swarovski's involvement.[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26] Similar Swarovski-made trophies were used in the 2018 and 2019 seasons before production shifted to Pakistan.[^cite_note-27][^cite_note-28]

On 19 February 2020, the first domestically made trophy was unveiled by squash legend [Jahangir Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jahangir_Khan) and PCB chairman [Ehsan Mani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ehsan_Mani) at the [National Stadium, Karachi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Stadium,_Karachi). The 65-centimetre-tall, eight-kilogram design featuring a crescent and star was used from 2020 to 2022, with each champion's name engraved on its base.[^cite_note-29] In 2023, a new silver-plated "Supernova" trophy, crafted by Lahore-based Mahfooz Jewellers, was introduced at the [Shalimar Gardens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shalimar_Gardens,_Lahore) in [Lahore](125315.md), the first to be fully produced in Pakistan.[^cite_note-30][^cite_note-31]

The 2025 trophy was called "Luminara", which was revealed on 14 March 2025 through a video on the PSL's official YouTube channel. It incorporated luminous crystal and reflective metalwork to mark a decade of PSL cricket.[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33]

The 2026 edition of the Pakistan Super League will feature a new trophy design.[^cite_note-34] The trophy is named "Infinity" and was revealed in ceremony held at [National Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Stadium), [Karachi](17123.md). It is handcrafted by 18 artisans who completed it in 2,600 hours.[^cite_note-35] It features a crescent and a star at the top, The crescent is embedded with 1600 small crystals, There are eight emerald-cut gemstones at the base of the trophy representing the eight PSL franchises.[^cite_note-36]

### Profits

In May 2016, the [PCB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Cricket_Board) announced that the inaugural season of the PSL had yielded profits of US\$2.6 million.[^cite_note-37] The [2023 edition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Pakistan_Super_League) recorded revenue of Over Rs. 5 Billion. The revenue was accumulated through various sources, including broadcasting rights, title sponsorship, [gate money](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gate_receipts), and other related rights.[^cite_note-38][^cite_note-39]

## Format



PSL playoffs system

The PSL is played in a double [round-robin](939742.md) format. Every team plays each other twice and the top four advance to the playoffs. The playoffs use the [Page playoff system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Page_playoff_system), like the Indian Premier League.

The league follows the rules and regulations dictated by the [International Cricket Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Cricket_Council). However, it introduced DRS system in T20s which was later emulated by [International Cricket Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Cricket_Council) as well. In the group stage, two points are awarded for a win, one for a no-result and none for a loss. In the event of tied scores after both teams have faced their quota of overs, a [super over](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_over) is used to determine the match winner. In the group stage teams are ranked on the following criteria:

1. Higher number of points

2. If equal, better run rate

3. If equal, highest number of wins

4. If equal, fewest losses

5. If still equal, the results of head-to-head meeting

In any play-off match in which there is no result, a super over is used to determine the winner. If the super over is not possible or the result of the over is a tie, the team which finished in the highest league position at the end of the regular season is deemed the winner of the match.

## Schedule

The PSL traditionally been held between February and March each year, with the only major disruption occurring during the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_in_Pakistan). The league previously took place before the start of the [Indian Premier League](13260340.md). The [2025 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Pakistan_Super_League) was the last to be held in the traditional February–March slot, during which it overlapped with the IPL for the first time.[^cite_note-40] However, scheduling changes announced by the [Pakistan Cricket Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Cricket_Board) in 2025 confirmed that from the [2026 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Pakistan_Super_League) onward, the PSL would move to an April–May window to improve overseas player availability and avoid weather disruptions.[^cite_note-41] [2026 Pakistan Super League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Pakistan_Super_League) will start on 26 March and will end on 3 May

According to a PCB press release, around 20 parties showed an interest in buying franchises for the league before the first season.[^cite_note-NDTVSPORTS-42] On 18 October 2015 the [Pakistan Cricket Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Cricket_Board) began accepting tenders for franchises with a deadline for bids of 15 November.[^cite_note-43] Bid winners were granted the rights to a franchise for ten years.[^cite_note-44]

All five franchises for the first season of the league were sold on 3 December 2015, for a total price of US\$93 million, after seven bidders presented formal proposals.[^cite_note-45][^cite_note-46]

In April 2017, the PCB invited bids for a sixth team. As many as 40 national and international parties expressed an interest in buying the sixth franchise[^cite_note-47] and on 1 June the rights to the Multan franchise was bought by [Schön Properties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sch%C3%B6n_Properties) for a price of US\$5.2 million a year, the most expensive franchise rights in the league.[^cite_note-48] In November 2018, however, the rights were terminated by the PCB due to issues with payments. The rights were resold to a consortium led by Ali Tareen for US\$6.35 million.

In November 2025, following the conclusion of the original ten-year agreements, the PCB conducted a re-evaluation of franchise values through [Ernst & Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernst_&_Young). The financial model was shifted from US dollars to [Pakistani Rupees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistani_Rupee) for the next ten-year cycle (2026–2035). Five of the original six franchises renewed their rights under the new valuation terms, with annual fees set at Rs. 670 million for [Lahore Qalandars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahore_Qalandars), Rs. 640 million for [Karachi Kings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karachi_Kings), Rs. 490 million for [Peshawar Zalmi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peshawar_Zalmi), Rs. 480 million for [Islamabad United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamabad_United), and Rs. 340 million for [Quetta Gladiators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quetta_Gladiators).[^cite_note-49][^cite_note-50] In late November 2025, [Multan Sultans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multan_Sultans) didn't finalized their renewal agreement with the [Pakistan Cricket Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Cricket_Board), resulting in the franchise being placed under PCB administration and scheduled for auction on 9 February 2026 ahead of the [2026 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Pakistan_Super_League).[^cite_note-51][^cite_note-52]

The PCB confirmed that the PSL would expand from six to eight teams beginning with the [2026 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Pakistan_Super_League), following the expiry of the original franchise agreements after the [2025 edition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Pakistan_Super_League).[^cite_note-53][^cite_note-54] In November 2025 the PCB published a shortlist of six candidate cities for the two new franchises: [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad,_Sindh), [Rawalpindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rawalpindi_\(PSL_team\)), [Faisalabad](401880.md), [Gilgit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilgit), [Muzaffarabad](1119495.md) and [Sialkot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sialkot).[^cite_note-55] On 8 January, the new teams were announced to be representing the cities Hyderabad and Sialkot, with FSK and OZ Developers winning the bid with Rs. 1.75 billion and Rs. 1.85 bilion respectively.[^cite_note-expansion26-3]

Following the 9 February 2026 auction, the [Multan Sultans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multan_Sultans) franchise was sold to Walee Technologies for a record PKR 2.45 billion, which subsequently relocated the team to [Rawalpindi](343524.md) and rebranded it as [Rawalpindiz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rawalpindiz).[^cite_note-56][^cite_note-57] However, the Sultans brand returned to the league shortly after when the newly established expansion team, the [Sialkot Stallionz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sialkot_Stallionz), faced immediate financial instability. Following the reported bankruptcy of original owners OZ Developers, [Syed Mohammad Gohar Hussain Shah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gohar_Shah) of CD Ventures acquired a 99% stake in the Sialkot franchise in February 2026 to stabilize the team ahead of its debut.[^cite_note-Wisden26-58]

On 3 March 2026, the [Pakistan Cricket Board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Cricket_Board) officially approved Shah's request to rebrand the Sialkot entity as the [Multan Sultans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multan_Sultans), citing his desire to restore [South Punjab](24751.md)'s representation in the league.[^cite_note-GeoSuper-59][^cite_note-60] The transition involved increasing the annual franchise fee from PKR 1.85 billion to PKR 2 billion, alongside a one-time US\$1 million name-change fee.[^cite_note-Wisden26-58] Under the new management, Shah introduced a Total Cricket philosophy and appointed [Ashton Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashton_Turner) as captain to lead the rebranded team for the [2026 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Pakistan_Super_League).[^cite_note-61]

## Results

Main article: [List of Pakistan Super League records and statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Pakistan_Super_League_records_and_statistics)

The [first season of the PSL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Pakistan_Super_League) was played entirely in the [United Arab Emirates](69328.md) due to security reasons.[^cite_note-64][^cite_note-65] The inaugural champions were [Islamabad United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamabad_United), who defeated [Quetta Gladiators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quetta_Gladiators) in the final. [Peshawar Zalmi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peshawar_Zalmi) were the 2017 PSL champions, beating Quetta Gladiators in [Lahore](125315.md) on 5 March 2017. Islamabad United were the 2018 PSL champions, beating defending champions [Peshawar Zalmi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peshawar_Zalmi) on 25 March 2018. Quetta Gladiators defeated Peshawar Zalmi to win their first title on 17 March 2019 in [Karachi](17123.md), in 2020 [Karachi Kings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karachi_Kings) took the trophy home.[^cite_note-The_News-66] [Multan Sultans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multan_Sultans), who won their first title defeating [Peshawar Zalmi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peshawar_Zalmi) on 24 June 2021 in Abu Dhabi. [Lahore Qalandars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahore_Qalandars) won their first title on 27 February 2022, against reigning champions [Multan Sultan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multan_Sultans_in_2022). Lahore Qalandars again defeated Multan Sultans with a minor margin of 1 run to win their consecutive 2nd title of the tournament in its 8th edition on 18 March 2023. [Islamabad United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamabad_United) became first team to win three title on 18 March 2024 when they defeated Multan Sultans by 2 wickets. It was Multan's fourth consecutive final and third consecutive runner up finish. Lahore Qalandars also won their third title by defeating Quetta Gladiators by 6 wickets on 25 May 2025. [Shaheen Afridi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaheen_Afridi) is most successful captain of the League.

### Individual awards

The Pakistan Super League honors outstanding individual excellence through three dedicated awards named after iconic figures of Pakistan cricket. The **Hanif Mohammad Award** (Green Cap) is presented to the tournament's leading run-scorer, while the **Fazal Mahmood Award** (Maroon Cap) recognizes the leading wicket-taker. The best wicket-keeper is honored with the **Imtiaz Ahmed Award**. Although these named trophies were formally introduced in the 2017 season, the league officially recognizes the statistical leaders from the 2016 inaugural edition as the original recipients of these honors.[^cite_note-67]

## Sponsorship

The PSL's initial title sponsorship was awarded to [HBL Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBL_Pakistan) for three years in December 2015.[^cite_note-berecorder-79] At that time sponsorship deals for the league, including the title sponsorship, were estimated to be worth more than US\$6 million.[^cite_note-80] HBL renewed its title sponsorship in 2018 and again in 2021, extending its arrangement through the 2025 season.[^cite_note-hbl2021-81]

## Broadcasters

### Historic partners

The league signed a broadcast production deal with UK–based company [Sunset and Vine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tinopolis) for the first three seasons of the competition.[^cite_note-85] Domestically TV broadcast rights were sold to [PTV Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PTV_Sports) and [Ten Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ten_Sports) for the same period,[^cite_note-86] with digital broadcast rights sold to [YouTube](3524766.md) in Pakistan for the first season.[^cite_note-87] The digital rights were subsequently sold to cricketgateway.[^cite_note-88][^cite_note-89]

For the period from 2019 to 2021, broadcast rights were sold to Blitz Advertising.[^cite_note-90][^cite_note-91][^cite_note-92] Domestic TV rights for the same period were acquired by [PTV Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PTV_Sports) and [Geo Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geo_Network) with cricketgateway retaining the digital rights.

From 2021 to 2025 broadcast rights were sold to TransGroup International[^cite_note-93] with PTV Sports, [A Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Sports) and Ten Sports having the domestic broadcast rights until 2023.[^cite_note-94][^cite_note-95] A Sports and Walee Technologies acquired these rights for the 2024–2025 seasons along with the digital streaming rights which had been sold to [Daraz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daraz?action=edit\&redlink=1) for the 2022 and 2023 seasons.[^cite_note-96][^cite_note-97]

### Current partners

Global media rights (minus India) for 2026 season were bought by Walee Technologies.[^cite_note-98] It was a record-breaking deal at 149% increase compared to the previous cycle.[^cite_note-99] Walee Technologies also bought local broadcasting rights for PKR 26.11 billion for the 2026 – 2029 period.[^cite_note-100]

## Other media

### Hamaray Heroes

Main article: [HBL PSL Hamaray Heroes Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBL_PSL_Hamaray_Heroes_Award)



HBL PSL Hamaray Heroes Award

The HBL PSL Hamaray Heroes campaign was introduced in 2020 season. The initiative rewarded prominent Pakistanis like [squash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squash_\(sport\)) player [Farhan Mehboob](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farhan_Mehboob), founder of ACF Animal Rescue [Ayesha Chundrigar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayesha_Chundrigar), [mountaineer](20341.md) [Ali Sadpara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_Sadpara), and professional [eSports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ESports) player [Arslan Ash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arslan_Ash). A total of 32 [HBL PSL Hamaray Heroes Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBL_PSL_Hamaray_Heroes_Award) were handed out during the 2020 season.[^cite_note-124]

### Cancer awareness

Since 2019, the league has reserved a day for [breast cancer awareness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breast_cancer_awareness), with a [pink ribbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pink_ribbon) theme. A day has also been set aside to raise awareness of [childhood cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Childhood_cancer) with a gold ribbon.[^cite_note-125][^cite_note-126]
