**Nāʻālehu** is a community in [Hawaiʻi County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawaii_County,_Hawaii), [Hawaii](13270.md), United States. *Nāʻālehu* is [Hawaiian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawaiian_Language) for "the volcanic ashes".[^cite_note-2] It is one of the southernmost communities with a post office in the 50 states of the United States. (*See [List of extreme points of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_extreme_points_of_the_United_States).*) For statistical purposes, the [United States Census Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Census_Bureau) has defined Nāʻālehu as a [census-designated place](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Census-designated_place) (CDP). The census definition of the area may not precisely correspond to local understanding of the area with the same name. The population was 866 at the [2010 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_United_States_Census),[^cite_note-Census_2010-3] down from 919 at the [2000 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_United_States_census).

## History

Initially created as one of several plantation camps by the Hutchinson Sugar Plantation Company, Nāʻālehu would later be developed as a proper town by plantation Manager J. Beaty in an effort to make plantation life more comfortable for workers and "citify" the scattered plantation camps. These camps were initially consolidated into four main villages: Nāʻālehu, [Honuapo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honuapo), Hīlea and Kaʻalaiki; with those last three villages consolidated into the town of Nāʻālehu by 1928.

At one time there were two mills, an 8-roller mill at Nāʻālehu and a 9-roller mill at [Honuapo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honuapo). Hilea also had its own mill, but it was dismantled in 1907. Around 1911, the Nāʻālehu mill wore out and a new central mill was built at Honuapo to grind the cane. The landing was located at Honuapo and it had the reputation of being able to load and ship sugar cheaper than any other port in the islands.[^cite_note-4]

## Geography

Nāʻālehu is located near the southern tip of the island of [Hawaiʻi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawaii_\(island\)) at [19°3′57″N 155°35′15″W  /  19.06583°N 155.58750°W  / 19.06583; -155.58750](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=N%C4%81%CA%BB%C4%81lehu,_Hawaii\&params=19_3_57_N_155_35_15_W_type\:city) (19.065925, -155.587528)[^cite_note-GR1-5] in the [Kaʻū District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kau,_Hawaii). It is bordered to the west by [Waiohinu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waiohinu,_Hawaii), and [Discovery Harbour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discovery_Harbour,_Hawaii) is to the southwest. (The two communities are [census-designated places](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Census-designated_place) but do not have post offices.)

According to the [United States Census Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Census_Bureau), the Nāʻālehu CDP has a total area of 2.2 square miles (5.6 km<sup>2</sup>), all of it land.[^cite_note-Census_2010-3]

As of the [census](6889.md)[^cite_note-GR2-7] of 2000, there were 919 people, 290 households, and 209 families residing in the CDP. The population density was 426.1 inhabitants per square mile (164.5/km<sup>2</sup>). There were 332 housing units at an average density of 153.9 per square mile (59.4/km<sup>2</sup>). The racial makeup of the CDP was 8.38% [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.33% [African American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_American_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.11% [Native American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_American_\(U.S._Census\)), 45.59% [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_\(U.S._Census\)), 13.82% [Pacific Islander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Islander_\(U.S._Census\)), 0.33% from [other races](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_\(United_States_Census\)), and 31.45% from two or more races. [Hispanic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_\(U.S._Census\)) or [Latino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latino_\(U.S._Census\)) of any race were 5.44% of the population.

There were 290 [households](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Household), out of which 31.7% had children under the age of 18 living with them, 54.1% were [married couples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marriage) living together, 11.0% had a female householder with no husband present, and 27.9% were non-families. 25.9% of all households were made up of individuals, and 12.1% had someone living alone who was 65 years of age or older. The average household size was 3.17 and the average family size was 3.77.

In the CDP the population was spread out, with 30.6% under the age of 18, 7.7% from 18 to 24, 23.4% from 25 to 44, 21.8% from 45 to 64, and 16.5% who were 65 years of age or older. The median age was 36 years. For every 100 females, there were 96.8 males. For every 100 females age 18 and over, there were 96.3 males.

The median income for a household in the CDP was \$31,750, and the median income for a family was \$36,964. Males had a median income of \$23,625 versus \$20,125 for females. The [per capita income](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Per_capita_income) for the CDP was \$11,755. 20.4% of the population and 16.8% of families were below the [poverty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty) line. Out of the total population, 22.0% of those under the age of 18 and 6.6% of those 65 and older were living below the poverty line.

## Points of interest

Nāʻālehu is home to one of several ground communication stations operated by [Bigelow Aerospace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bigelow_Aerospace)[^cite_note-8] that is used to communicate with its space modules in orbit. The southernmost church in the United States is Lighthouse Baptist Church.

## Education

The statewide school district is the [Hawaii State Department of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawaii_State_Department_of_Education), and it covers Hawaii County.[^cite_note-9] Nāʻālehu Elementary School is in the community.[^cite_note-10]

The [Hawaii State Public Library System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawaii_State_Public_Library_System) operates Naalehu Public Library. The library presently used opened on February 24, 1994, and it reopened after an expansion on November 15, 2021. It originally had 775 square feet (72.0 m<sup>2</sup>) of area, while the expansion increased its size by, 901 square feet (83.7 m<sup>2</sup>).[^cite_note-11]
