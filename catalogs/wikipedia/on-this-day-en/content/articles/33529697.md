# Canada at the FIFA World Cup

For the women's team, see [Canada at the FIFA Women's World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_at_the_FIFA_Women's_World_Cup).



Canada lineup before the [2026 World Cup](36581929.md) match against [Qatar](1111350.md) at [BC Place](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BC_Place) in [Vancouver](32706.md)

The [Canada men's national soccer team](723688.md) have played at the [FIFA World Cup](11370.md) on three occasions: in [1986](157231.md), [2022](17742072.md), and [2026](36581929.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-spor_Onea-3][^cite_note-nyti_SOCC-4] For most other editions of the World Cup, Canada has not succeeded in gaining one of the places reserved for the North American [CONCACAF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONCACAF) teams.[^cite_note-5]

Canada made their World Cup debut in [1986](157231.md), but were eliminated in the group stage after failing to win a single match. In [2022](17742072.md), Canada qualified for the World Cup for only the second time in history, ending a 36-year drought.[^cite_note-6] Four years later, Canada automatically qualified for the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md) as co-hosts (together with the [United States](582488.md) and [Mexico](808402.md)). At this World Cup, Canada earned their first-ever group stage point in a 1–1 draw against Bosnia and Herzegovina and their first-ever World Cup match win six days later in a decisive 6–0 win over Qatar, the widest win margin for a CONCACAF team in World Cup history,[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8] with [Jonathan David](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_David) scoring the first World Cup [hat-trick](411541.md) for a host nation since [Geoff Hurst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoff_Hurst) in [1966](61629.md).[^cite_note-9]
