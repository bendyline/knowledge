The **2026 [London Athletics Meet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Athletics_Meet)** was the 73rd edition of the annual outdoor track and field meeting held in [London](17867.md). Held on 18 July at [London Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Stadium), it was the eleventh leg of the [2026 Diamond League](81417246.md) – the highest level international track and field circuit.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]
