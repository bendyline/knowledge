**BMW Brilliance** (officially **BMW Brilliance Automotive Ltd.**) is an automobile manufacturing company headquartered in [Shenyang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shenyang), China, and a [joint venture](538782.md) between [BMW](3772.md) (as majority holder) and [Brilliance Auto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brilliance_Auto). Its principal activity is the production, distribution, and sale of BMW passenger cars in mainland China.

The BMW Brilliance Zinoro [all-electric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battery_electric_vehicle) [crossover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossover_\(automobile\)), based on the [BMW X1 (E84)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BMW_X1_\(E84\)), is the first product of BMW Brilliance's brand and the first [new energy vehicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_energy_vehicle) (NEV) from a Chinese premium manufacturer. Since early 2014, the Zinoro 1E is available for leasing only in [Beijing](18603746.md) and [Shanghai](27643.md).

## History

On August 23, 2020, the steel structure of BMW's largest paint shop in the world was completed, and the new [BMW](3772.md) factory in Tiexi was completed.

On 27 March 2003 BMW and Brilliance Auto agreed to form a joint venture to produce BMW cars for the Chinese market, with BMW holding a 50 per cent stake, Brilliance Auto 40.5 per cent and the Shenyang municipal government 9.5 per cent.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-bbc27303-3] BMW and Brilliance agreed to invest an initial €450 million (US\$483 million) in the venture.[^cite_note-bbc27303-3] The first Chinese-made BMW, a BMW 325, was sold in October 2003.[^cite_note-4]

In April 2009 BMW Brilliance announced that it would construct a second automobile assembly plant in China.[^cite_note-5] Construction of the plant began in Shenyang in June 2010, with a planned cost of US\$73.53 million and a production capacity of 100,000 units.[^cite_note-6] Production at the plant began in May 2012.[^cite_note-7]

In January 2011 BMW Brilliance announced that it would begin Chinese production of a BMW 5 series electric hybrid car later that year.[^cite_note-8]

In 2013, BMW Brilliance opened a research and development centre. It was expanded in 2020, making it BMW's largest R\&D centre outside of Germany.[^cite_note-\:0-9]

In October 2018, BMW announced that it would increase its stake from 50% to 75%, making BMW the first foreign carmaker to take majority control of its joint venture in China.[^cite_note-10]

In February 2022, BMW acquired a majority 75% stake in BMW Brilliance with Brilliance indirectly owning the remaining 25% of the company.[^cite_note-11]
