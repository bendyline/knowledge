The **Ministry of Health of the Russian Federation**[^cite_note-1] ([Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Министерство здравоохранения Российской Федерации, in short [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language): Минздрав России, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Russian): *Minzdrav Rossii*) is a [ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_\(government_department\)) of the [Government of Russia](9787373.md) responsible for [health care](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_care) and [public health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_health).

The Ministry of Health oversees the legal regulation and state policies of health in Russia, including [pharmaceuticals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharmaceuticals), the [public health care](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Publicly_funded_health_care) system, [health insurance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_insurance), medical rehabilitation, [sanitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanitation), [disease prevention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disease_prevention), and the circulation of medicines. It is headquartered in [Tverskoy District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tverskoy_District), [Moscow](19004.md).[^cite_note-2]

The Ministry of Health was formed in 2012 from the Ministry of Health and Social Development which was split in two under Prime Minister [Dmitry Medvedev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitry_Medvedev's_First_Cabinet), with the [social security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_security) departments forming the [Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Labour_and_Social_Affairs_\(Russia\)).

[Mikhail Murashko](62888702.md) has been Minister of Health since 21 January 2020.

## History

In [1992](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992), following the [collapse of the USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collapse_of_the_USSR), the [Ministry of Health of the USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Health_\(Soviet_Union\)) was liquidated, and the Ministry of Health of the RSFSR was transformed into the Ministry of Health of the Russian Federation.

In March 2004, by [Decree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decree_of_the_President_of_Russia) No. 314 of March 9 2004, the ministry was abolished and the Ministry of Health and Social Development of the Russian Federation (Ministry of Health and Social Development of Russia) was formed.

On May 22, 2012, the Ministry of Health and Social Development of Russia was divided into two ministries — the Ministry of Health of Russia and the [Ministry of Labor of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Labour_and_Social_Protection).

## Duties

Its main official responsibilities include:

- developing and implementing state policy in healthcare;

- developing and implementing federal health programs, including initiatives on diabetes, tuberculosis, health promotion, health education, disease prevention etc.;

* developing draft legislation and presenting it to the [State Duma](1010187.md);

* governance of federal medical facilities;

* medical education and manpower development;

* epidemiological and environmental health monitoring and health statistics;

* control of infectious diseases;

* development of health regulations;

* development of federal standards and recommendations for quality assurance;

* development and implementation of federal health programs;

* control and licensing of drugs.
