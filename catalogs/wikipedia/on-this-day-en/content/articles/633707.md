# List of intelligence agencies of France

This is a **list of current and former French [intelligence agencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_agencies)**.

## Currently active



[Organization chart given on a website of French intelligence community](http://www.academie-renseignement.gouv.fr/files/plaquette-presentation-comrens.pdf)

- DGSE: [Directorate-General for External Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directorate-General_for_External_Security) – *[Direction générale de la sécurité extérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direction_g%C3%A9n%C3%A9rale_de_la_s%C3%A9curit%C3%A9_ext%C3%A9rieure)*. It is the military foreign intelligence agency, which succeeded the *[Service de Documentation Extérieure et de Contre-Espionnage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Service_de_Documentation_Ext%C3%A9rieure_et_de_Contre-Espionnage)* (SDECE) in 1982 (itself preceded by the *[Direction Générale des Études et Recherches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direction_G%C3%A9n%C3%A9rale_des_%C3%89tudes_et_Recherches)* (DGER), dependent on the BCRA.

- DGSI: General Directorate for Internal Security – *[Direction générale de la sécurité intérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direction_g%C3%A9n%C3%A9rale_de_la_s%C3%A9curit%C3%A9_int%C3%A9rieure)*. Founded in 2008 by the merger of the [RG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direction_centrale_des_renseignement_g%C3%A9n%C3%A9raux) and the [DST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direction_de_la_surveillance_du_territoire), it is tasked with [counter-espionage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counter-espionage), [counter-terrorism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counter-terrorism) and the surveillance of potential threats on French territory.

- DRM: Directorate of Military Intelligence – *[Direction du renseignement militaire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direction_du_renseignement_militaire)*. It was created by Interior Minister [Pierre Joxe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Joxe) in 1992, after the [Gulf War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_War), to centralize military intelligence information.

- TRACFIN : Intelligence Processing and Action Against Clandestine Financial Circuits - *[Tracfin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tracfin)*. Founded in 1990, it is tasked with tackling [money laundering](19390.md) and the financing of [terrorism](30636.md).

  [^cite_note-1]

- [DRSD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DPSD): Directorate of Intelligence and Security of Defense – *[Direction du renseignement et de la sécurité de la Défense](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direction%20du%20renseignement%20et%20de%20la%20s%C3%A9curit%C3%A9%20de%20la%20D%C3%A9fense)*. It is a military intelligence agency, charged with conducting [counter-espionage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counter-espionage).

- BRGE: Intelligence and Electronic Warfare Brigade – *[Brigade de renseignement](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigade%20de%20renseignement)*.

- DCPJ: Judicial Police – *[Direction centrale de la police judiciaire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direction_centrale_de_la_police_judiciaire)*.

- CNCTR: National Commission for the Control of Security Interceptions – *[Commission nationale de contrôle des techniques de renseignement](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commission%20nationale%20de%20contr%C3%B4le%20des%20techniques%20de%20renseignement)*.

- SCRT: Central Service of Territorial Surveillance - *[Service central de surveillance du territoire](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Service%20central%20du%20renseignement%20territorial)*

- SNRP: *[Service national du renseignement pénitentiaire](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Service%20national%20du%20renseignement%20p%C3%A9nitentiaire)*, a French intelligence agency for carceral security (riot prevention, surveillance of radicalized prisoners for avoid others radicalization, etc.

## Former agencies

- *[Bureau Central de Renseignements et d'Action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bureau_Central_de_Renseignements_et_d'Action)* (BCRA) was an intelligence agency created during [World War II](32927.md) in 1940.

- *[Cabinet noir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabinet_noir)* More formally, the cabinet du secret des postes. c. 1750.

- [Deuxième Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deuxi%C3%A8me_Bureau): France's external military intelligence agency from 1871 to 1940. It was dissolved together with the Third Republic upon the armistice with Germany.

- DST: Directorate of Territorial Security – *[Direction de la surveillance du territoire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direction_de_la_surveillance_du_territoire)*. It was an intelligence agency of the Minister of Interior, charged with conducting counter-espionage (in particular in economic matters), and, since the end of the Cold War, also with counterterrorism issues. It was abolished in 2008 and merged into the [DCRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direction_centrale_du_renseignement_int%C3%A9rieur).

- RG: General Intelligence Directorate – *[Direction centrale des renseignements généraux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direction_centrale_des_renseignements_g%C3%A9n%C3%A9raux)*. It was the intelligence agency of the [French police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_police), directed by the [Minister of the Interior](871703.md). It was charged with monitoring gambling activities, criminal activities, and political radicals (far right and far left). It also used to have as a mission to monitor French political parties, but officially is not charged with this anymore. It was merged into the *[Direction centrale du renseignement intérieur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direction_centrale_du_renseignement_int%C3%A9rieur)* in 2008.

- *[Service de documentation extérieure et de contre-espionnage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Service_de_documentation_ext%C3%A9rieure_et_de_contre-espionnage)* (SDECE), successor to BCRA.
