**Huanjiang Maonan Autonomous County** ([Zhuang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_Zhuang): *Vanzgyangh Mauznamzcuz Swci Yen*; [simplified Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 环江毛南族自治县; [traditional Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 環江毛南族自治縣; [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *Huánjiāng máonánzú Zìzhìxiàn*) is an [ethnic Maonan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maonan_people) autonomous county in the north of [Guangxi](47836950.md), China, bordering [Guizhou](173815.md) province to the north and northwest. It is under the administration of [Hechi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hechi) city. It is the only [Maonan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maonan_people) autonomous county in China.

## Administrative divisions

Huangjiang County is divided into 6 towns, 5 townships and 1 ethnic township:[^cite_note-1]

Towns

- Si'en Town 思恩镇

- Shuiyuan Town 水源镇

- Luoyang Town 洛阳镇

- Chuanshan Town 川山镇

- Minglun Town 明伦镇

- Dongxing Town 东兴镇

Townships

- Dacai Township 大才乡

- Xianan Township 下南乡

- Da'an Township 大安乡

- Changmei Township 长美乡

- Longyan Township 龙岩乡

Ethnic township

- Xunle Miao Ethnic Township 驯乐苗族乡

## City



Scene of a Wet Market in Huanjiang

The city of Huanjiang is the [county seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_seat) and a [county-level city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County-level_city). It's located 30km away from [Hechi City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hechi). In 2020, [Xi Jinping](2017814.md) gave instruction to the [Maonan people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maonan_people) of Huanjiang to work hard and alleviate poverty. [^cite_note-2] This in turn brought more investment into the city for development and a High Speed Rail was constructed and inaugurated in 2022 which connects the 2 cities to [Nanning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanning) and [Guizhou](173815.md). Other modes of inter-city transportation are via [coach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coach_\(bus\)), [Passenger trains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Passenger_rail_transport_in_China) or private taxi.

## Culture

Although designated as an ethnic city, the [Maonan people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maonan_people) still constitute the minority of the city as the other dominant groups are [Han](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Han_people) and [Zhuang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhuang_people). In terms of cuisine, a speciality of the city is [Maonan-style beef jerky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakkwa) (毛南香牛肉干) and other dishes found in restaurants are Pickled pork (酸肉) and a variety of other pickled vegetables.

## Tourism



Huanjiang High-Speed Railway Station

One of the economical outputs of the county is its numerous scenic spots like waterfalls, caves and [mountaineering](20341.md) through its vast range of [karst mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karst).
