**Shostka** ([Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainian_language): Шостка, IPA: [\[ˈʃɔstkɐ\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Ukrainian) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/1/1d/Uk-%D0%A8%D0%BE%D1%81%D1%82%D0%BA%D0%B0.ogg/Uk-%D0%A8%D0%BE%D1%81%D1%82%D0%BA%D0%B0.ogg.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/1/1d/Uk-%D0%A8%D0%BE%D1%81%D1%82%D0%BA%D0%B0.ogg/Uk-%D0%A8%D0%BE%D1%81%D1%82%D0%BA%D0%B0.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>) is a city in [Sumy Oblast](1567618.md), northeastern [Ukraine](31750.md). Shostka serves as the administrative center of [Shostka Raion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shostka_Raion). Population: 71,966 (2022 estimate).[^cite_note-ua2022estimate-1]&#x20;

The city lies on the [Shostka River](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Shostka_\(Desna_tributary\)\&action=edit\&redlink=1), a tributary of the [Desna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desna_\(river\)), from which it gets its name. Shostka is an important centre of industry: in chemicals (see [Svema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Svema)) and in dairy, the Shostka City Milk Plant, acquired by the [Bel Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bel_Group).&#x20;

## History

In 1739, a [gunpowder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gunpowder) factory (now [Zirka factory](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Zirka_\(gunpowder_factory\)\&action=edit\&redlink=1)) was built there. Since that time, Shostka was one of the most important gunpowder suppliers in the [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire). In 1893, a branch of a nearby railroad line was built. Shostka was granted [municipal rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_status) in 1920. In 1931, a film factory was built in Shostka which was one of the main suppliers of cinema and photo film in the [USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR).&#x20;

During the [2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine](79400268.md), Shostka was besieged by Russian troops on February 24.[^cite_note-2] and may have been partially occupied.[^cite_note-3] During the withdrawal from the [Chernihiv Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chernihiv_Oblast) and Sumy Oblast, Russian troops left Shostka.[^cite_note-4]&#x20;

## Sports

Shostka has the [football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_\(soccer\)) team [FC Shostka](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=FC_Shostka\&action=edit\&redlink=1).&#x20;

## In popular culture

Shostka is the hometown of the fictional Mousekewitz mice family in the 1986 animated film *[An American Tail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/An_American_Tail)*, the opening of which depicts [Cossack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cossack) and Cossacks' cats raiding the town.&#x20;
