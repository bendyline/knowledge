**Alejandro Gertz Manero**[^cite_note-2] (born 31 October 1939) is a Mexican lawyer and politician. He served as [secretary of public security](59219267.md) from 2000 to 2004 during the first part of [Vicente Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vicente_Fox)'s government. In the [2009 mid-term election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Mexican_legislative_election), he was elected to a [plurinominal seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_regions_of_Mexico) in the [Chamber of Deputies](411807.md).[^cite_note-3] He later served as [attorney general](2586359.md) under [Andrés Manuel López Obrador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9s_Manuel_L%C3%B3pez_Obrador) and [Claudia Sheinbaum](57813311.md) from 2019 until his resignation in November 2025.[^cite_note-4]

On 7 January 2026, President Sheinbaum announced her intention for Gertz Manero to be appointed [ambassador to the United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassy_of_Mexico,_London).[^cite_note-5] He presented his [letters of credence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letters_of_credence) to King [Charles III](125248.md) on 6 May 2026.[^cite_note-6]

## Controversies

In 2021 he was admitted to the [National Researchers System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sistema_Nacional_de_Investigadores) by a special committee, after having tried unsuccessfully for 11 years due to insufficient scientific output.[^cite_note-7] Writer [Guillermo Sheridan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guillermo_Sheridan) showed evidence of plagiarism in a biography of [Guillermo Prieto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guillermo_Prieto) published by Gertz Manero, which was also reported by a group of 77 researchers.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9]

In 2022 he was accused of abusing his position as federal attorney general for a personal vendetta, illegally persecuting and jailing Alejandra Cuevas and Laura Morán for the death of his brother Federico Gertz in 2015.[^cite_note-10] He was accused of influence trafficking and criminal association by the former [Counsel to the President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legal_Counsel_of_the_Federal_Executive), [Julio Scherer Ibarra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julio_Scherer_Ibarra?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-11] He owns several real estate properties in Madrid, Ibiza, Paris, New York, and Los Angeles, and bought 122 luxury vehicles.[^cite_note-12]

When he was secretary of public security in the government of president [Vicente Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vicente_Fox), Gertz Manero was detained by [DEA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DEA) and [ATF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATF) when he landed in New York in an official government plane, carrying a false passport and US\$50,000 in cash. In 2022 the US government opened another investigation on him.[^cite_note-13]



Gertz Manero meets with [U.S. Homeland Security Secretary](212926.md) [Alejandro Mayorkas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alejandro_Mayorkas) at FGR Headquarters in [Mexico City](18987.md) in June 2021.
