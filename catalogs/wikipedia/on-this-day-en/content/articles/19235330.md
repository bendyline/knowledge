The **government of Latvia** is the [central government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_government) of the [Republic of Latvia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Latvia). The [Constitution of Latvia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Latvia) ([Latvian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvian_language): *Satversme*) defines Latvia as a [parliamentary republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_republic) represented by a [unicameral](204304.md) [parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament) ([Saeima](499765.md)) and the Cabinet of Ministers of the Republic of Latvia ([Latvian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvian_language): *Latvijas Republikas Ministru kabinets*), which form the executive branch of Latvia.

Since the early 2000s cabinet meetings in Latvia have been open to the public. In June 2013, the Latvian government became one of the first in Europe to offer live internet broadcasts of cabinet meetings.
