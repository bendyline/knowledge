# NATO summit



[2026 Ankara summit](80796458.md) of [NATO](21133.md), formal meeting of the heads of state and heads of government of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization.

A **NATO summit** is a [summit meeting](3271361.md) that is regarded as a periodic opportunity for [heads of state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_state) and [heads of government](37570.md) of [NATO](21133.md) member countries to evaluate and provide strategic direction for Alliance activities.[^cite_note-1]

NATO summits are not regular meetings like the more frequent NATO ministerial meetings, but rather are important junctures in the alliance's decision-making process on the highest level. Summits are often used to introduce new policy, invite new members into the alliance, launch major new initiatives, and build partnerships with non-NATO countries.
