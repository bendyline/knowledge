The **Italian Tennis and Padel Federation** ([Italian](14708.md): *Federazione Italiana Tennis e Padel*, FITP since 2023; formerly [Italian](14708.md): *Federazione Italiana Tennis*, FIT) is the national governing body for the [tennis](29773.md) sport in Italy.

## History

The FITP, originally *Federazione Italiana Lawn Tennis* (FILT), was born in [Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence) in 1910 with just 26 affiliated clubs.[^cite_note-PRESIDENT-2]

In 2022, the FIT decided to extend its activities to [padel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padel), [beach tennis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beach_tennis), and [pickleball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pickleball), as of January 2023.

## Presidents

Here are the 110 years of presidents of the *Italian Tennis Federation*.[^cite_note-PRESIDENT-2]

- 1910: Marquess Piero Antinori

- 1913 - 1927: Beppe Croce

- 1928: [Augusto Turati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augusto_Turati)

- 1929 - 1938: [Alessandro Lessona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alessandro_Lessona)

- 1939: Attilio Fontan

- 1940 - 1941: Erberto Vasellia

- 1943: Giorgio de' Stefani (Regent Presidential Committee)

- 1944: Riccardo Sabbadini (Regent [CONI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CONI))

- 1949 - 1956: Aldo Tolusso

- 1958 - 1969: Giorgio de' Stefani

- 1969 - 1973: Luigi Orsini

- 1973 - 1976: Giorgio Neri

- 1976 - 1988: Paolo Galgani

- 1988 - 1989: [Mario Pescante](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mario_Pescante) (Commissary)

- 1989 - 1998: Paolo Galgani

- 1998 - 2000: Francesco Ricci Bitti

- 2000: Gianguido Sacchi Morsiani (Commissary)

- 2000: Luigi Tronchetti Provera (Commissary)

- from 2001: [Angelo Binaghi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angelo_Binaghi?action=edit\&redlink=1)
