**Thandwe** ("Thandway" in Arakanese; [Burmese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_language): သံတွဲမြို့, [MLCTS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MLC_Transcription_System): *samtwai: mrui.* [\[θàɰ̃dwɛ́ mjo̰\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Burmese); formerly **Sandoway**), with classical name being called **Dvaravatī**, is a town and major [seaport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port) in [Rakhine State](493373.md), the westernmost part of [Myanmar](19457.md).

## Recent history

During the ongoing [Myanmar civil war](69807451.md), the town was the site of clashes between the [Arakan Army](38106209.md) and Myanmar's military, with the Arakan Army taking control of [Ngapali Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ngapali_Beach) and several military outposts surrounding the town.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] According to reports, the Arakan Army seized the town's prison on 15 July 2024, with the town's remaining military battalions falling the next day and giving the Arakan Army full control of the town.[^cite_note-4]

## Geography

Thandwe is very ancient, and is said to have been at one time the [capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_\(political\)) of Rakhine State, then called Arakan. The district has an area of 3,784 square miles (9,800 km<sup>2</sup>). The area is mountainous, and spurs of the Arakan Mountains reach the coast. Some of the peaks in the north are over 4,000 feet (1,200 m) high. The streams are only mountain torrents to within a few miles of the coast; the mouth of the Khwa forms a good anchorage for small boats. The rocks in the [Arakan Range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arakan_Yoma) and its spurs are [metamorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metamorphic_rock), and include [clay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clay), [slates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slate), [ironstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ironstone) and indurated [sandstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandstone); towards the south, ironstone, trap and rocks of [basaltic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basalt) character are common; veins of [steatite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steatite) and white fibrous [quartz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quartz) are also found.[^cite_note-EB1911-5] Nearby Ngapali Beach is popular among tourists.

### Climate

Thandwe has a [tropical monsoon climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_monsoon_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *Am*). Temperatures are very warm throughout the year. There is a winter dry season (November–April) and a summer wet season (May–October). Torrential rain falls from June to September, with over 1,200 millimetres (47 in) falling in each of the months of June, July and August.[^cite_note-WMOCLINO-6] Between 1991 and 2020, the mean annual rainfall was 5,384 millimetres (212.0 in). The [rainfall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainfall) in 1905 was 230.49 inches (5,854 mm).[^cite_note-EB1911-5]
