**West Darfur State** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): ولاية غرب دارفور Wilāyat Ḡarb Dārfūr), historically **Dar Masalit** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): دار مساليت),[^cite_note-5] is one of the [states of Sudan](579458.md), and one of five comprising the [Darfur](605837.md) region. Prior to the creation of two new states in January 2012, it had an area of 79,460 km² and an estimated population of approximately 1,775,945 in 2018. It borders [North](767666.md) and [Central Darfur](34420004.md) to the east. The [Chadian](5488.md) [prefectures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefectures_of_Chad) of [Biltine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biltine_Prefecture) and [Ouaddaï](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouadda%C3%AF_Prefecture) lie to the west, while to the north is the prefecture of [Bourkou-Ennedi-Tibesti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borkou-Ennedi-Tibesti_Prefecture). [Al-Junaynah](1501534.md) is the capital of the state. West Darfur has been the site of much of the [Darfur conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darfur_conflict).[^cite_note-6]

During the [2023 Sudan conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Sudan_conflict), ethnic violence has been rampant across the region.[^cite_note-7] This has included several [Masalit massacres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masalit_massacres_\(2023%E2%80%93present\)).

## Geography

### Topography

West Darfur covers an area of 79,460 km<sup>2</sup>, approximately the size of [Czechia](5321.md). The region is mostly [savannah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savanna), though the northern regions are sandy, while the rest of the state is mountainous.[^cite_note-8] The highest peak is Sawani mountain, with a height of 1154m and a [prominence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prominence) of 210m.[^cite_note-9] There are several valleys across the state such as Wādī Kaja, and there are dense forests in the mountains.

### Climate

As the state varies from dry desert in the northern regions to rich savannah in the south, the climate is largely hot and dry,[^cite_note-10] with June and July being the hottest months, at times reaching over 45 °C. As is typical of deserts, nights reach much cooler temperatures, around 15-20 °C. Though rainfall is very low, it is generally higher in the southern parts of the state. ## Demographics

The population of West Darfur was estimated in 2023 at 1.9 million people, with 90% living in urban areas.

### Ethnic groups

The major ethnic groups in West Darfur are the [Erenga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erenga_people), Gimir, [Masalit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masalit_people), Misseriya Jebel, Sinngar, and [Zaghawa](1467480.md).[^cite_note-13]

## History

The Darfur region is divided into five [federal states](579458.md): [Central Darfur](34420004.md), [East Darfur](34420916.md), [North Darfur](767666.md), [South Darfur](767650.md) and West Darfur.

According to [Human Rights Watch](46530.md), hostilities broke out in West Darfur in 1998. The 1998 clashes, were relatively minor, but more than 5,000 [Masalit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masalit_people) were displaced. Clashes resumed in 1999 when nomadic herdsmen again moved south earlier than usual.

The 1999 clashes were deadlier, with hundreds killed, including a number of Arab tribal chiefs. The government brought in military forces in an attempt to quell the violence and took direct control of security. A reconciliation conference held in 1999 agreed on compensation. Many Masalit intellectuals and notables were arrested, imprisoned, and tortured in the towns as government-supported Arab militias began to attack Masalit villages; a number of Arab chiefs and civilians were also killed in these clashes.[^cite_note-14]

In 2000, a clandestine group consisting mostly of Darfuris published the *[Black Book](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Black_Book:_Imbalance_of_Power_and_Wealth_in_the_Sudan)*, a dissident manuscript detailing the domination of the north and the impoverishment of the other regions. It was widely discussed, despite attempts to censor it, and many of the writers went on to help found the rebel [Justice and Equality Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justice_and_Equality_Movement).

### Sudanese Civil War

From April to November 2023, the [Rapid Support Forces](43053874.md) (RSF) carried out attacks on the capital city of Geneina, killing thousands.[^cite_note-15]
