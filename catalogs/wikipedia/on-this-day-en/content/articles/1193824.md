&#x20;The **Bob Hope Humanitarian Award** was established in 2002 by the [Board of Governors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Television_Arts_&_Sciences#Board_of_Governors) of the [Academy of Television Arts & Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Television_Arts_&_Sciences) in recognition of [Bob Hope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Hope)'s trailblazing career. The award, one of the highest honors presented by the Board, recognizes the contributions accomplished by Hope, for more than half a century, to the growth and development of [broadcasting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broadcasting) in [radio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio) and [television](29831.md) as a family medium, and as a platform for [political](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics) and [social commentary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_commentary).[^cite_note-1]

## Recipients

- [2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/54th_Primetime_Emmy_Awards) – [Oprah Winfrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oprah_Winfrey)

- [2003](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/55th_Primetime_Emmy_Awards) – [Bill Cosby](158894.md)

- [2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/56th_Primetime_Emmy_Awards) – [Danny Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Thomas) ([posthumous](21021837.md) accepted by his daughter, [Marlo Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marlo_Thomas), on his behalf)

- [2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/62nd_Primetime_Emmy_Awards) – [George Clooney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Clooney)

- [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/74th_Primetime_Emmy_Awards) – [Sean Penn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Penn)

- [2025](79427113.md) – [Ted Danson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Danson) and [Mary Steenburgen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Steenburgen)

- [2026](81631496.md) – [Michael J. Fox](68904.md)
