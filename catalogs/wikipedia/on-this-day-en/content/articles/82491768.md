The **2026–27 Premier League** is the 35th season of the [Premier League](11250.md) and the 128th season of top-flight [English football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_football) overall. The season began on 21 August 2026 and is scheduled to conclude on 30 May 2027. The full list of fixtures was released at 10\:00 BST on 19 June 2026.&#x20;

The 2026–27 season consists of 33 weekend and five midweek rounds of matches.[^cite_note-\:0-1] The summer transfer window opened on 15 June 2026 and closed at 23\:00 BST on 1 September 2026. The winter transfer window will open on 1 January 2027 and close at 23\:00 GMT on 1 February 2027.[^cite_note-2]

[Arsenal](2174.md) are the defending champions.[^cite_note-3]

## Summary

### Developments

This season saw the usual opening fixtures and final matchweek postponed by one week due to the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md) taking place during the off-season. The fixtures were published at 10\:00 BST on 19 June 2026.[^cite_note-\:0-1][^cite_note-4]

The previous calendar of five international breaks during the season will be reduced to four, with a three week international break between late September and early October occurring for the first time.[^cite_note-5] During the late September-early October international break, it was confirmed by the Premier League that Manchester City were found guilty of 114 charges related to breaching the Premier League's financial regulations between 2009 and 2018.[^cite_note-6]

The Premier League implemented a ban on [gambling sponsors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gambling_advertising) appearing on the front of match day shirts from the 2026–27 season.[^cite_note-7] This initiative aims to reduce the visibility of betting brands, following pressure regarding gambling normalisation, though betting companies can still sponsor sleeves, training kits, LED signs, and other club partnerships.[^cite_note-8]

## Teams

Twenty teams will compete in the league – the top seventeen teams from the previous season and the three teams promoted from the [Championship](79604105.md). The promoted teams are [Coventry City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coventry_City_F.C.), [Ipswich Town](352843.md) and [Hull City](451358.md), returning to the top flight after absences of twenty-five, one and nine years respectively.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11] They replaced [West Ham United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Ham_United_F.C.), [Burnley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burnley_F.C.) and [Wolverhampton Wanderers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolverhampton_Wanderers_F.C.), who were relegated to the [Championship](82934740.md) after fourteen, one and eight years in the top flight respectively.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] Following [West Ham United](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Ham_United_F.C.) relegations, It will be their first English top-flight season (since the inception of the Football League during the [1888-89](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1888%E2%80%9389_Football_League) season), that they will be without a team name begins with the letter "W".
