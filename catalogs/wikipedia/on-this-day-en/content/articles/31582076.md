The **Ministry of Sport and Youth** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): وزارت ورزش و جوانان جمهوری اسلامی ایران, *Vezârat-è Varzeš-o javânân-è Jomhuri-è Eslâmi-ye Irân*) is the [government ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_ministry) of youth affairs and sports in the [Islamic Republic of Iran](14653.md).[^cite_note-2] It was founded in 2010 after the [Physical Education Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_Education_Organization) was dissolved.

## Owning Persepolis and Esteghlal

The Ministry of Sport and Youth owns two [major clubs of Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_in_Iran), [Persepolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persepolis_F.C.) and [Esteghlal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esteghlal_F.C.). The supporters of these clubs have been asking for privatization from many years ago. The Ministry of Sport and Youth also chooses the Persepolis and Esteghlal managers.&#x20;

On 18 June 2019, Persepolis supporters protests against [Iraj Arab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraj_Arab), managing board and the Ministry of Sport and Youth,[^cite_note-3] because of the possibility of ending the cooperation with their coach. Protests by supporters of Persepolis were responded by [riot police](1148542.md).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] Also, Supporters of [Esteghlal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esteghlal_F.C.) had protested in the past.[^cite_note-7] Esteghlal fans among Iranian football teams have always been exposed to injustices such as budget cuts. Also, Esteghlal Club was known as the richest team in Iran and Asia before changing its name.
