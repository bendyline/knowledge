**IRIS *Shahid Soleimani*** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): شهید سلیمانی) was the lead ship of an Iranian class of [missile corvettes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corvette) with the [same name](77543208.md). It was built by the Shahid Mahallati Shipyard and is operated by the [Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps Navy](4676345.md) of Iran.

## History

After the end of construction at the Shahid Mahallati Shipyard[^cite_note-\:0-3] on 14 June 2022, it underwent [sea trials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_trial). At that time, it was equipped with four 12.7 mm [machine guns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_gun) which were later replaced with four 20 mm gatling guns.

On 5 September 2022, *Shahid Soleimani* was commissioned into [IRGC's navy](4676345.md) branch in a [commissioning ceremony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_commissioning).[^cite_note-a-1]

On 11 March 2026, US CENTCOM confirmed that all [Shahid Soleimani-class corvettes](77543208.md) were sunk or destroyed. [^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5]

## Design

### Hull

The ship has a [catamaran](195952.md) (twin hull) design with sharp angles to give it a more [stealthy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stealth_technology) design. Its design has been compared to the Chinese [Type 22 missile boat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_22_missile_boat) and the Taiwanese [*Tuo Chiang* class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuo_Chiang-class_corvette). According to Iranian officials the ship's hull is made of aluminium instead of steel which decreases the weight and is a new innovation in the Iranian shipbuilding industry.[^cite_note-a-1]

### Armament

This warship is equipped with six [anti-ship cruise missiles](140725.md) including four long-range ones (likely [Noor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noor_\(missile\)) or [Ghadir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghadir_\(missile\))) and two short-range anti-ship missiles (likely [Nasir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasir_\(missile\))) in an order of two long-range and one short-range box launchers on each side behind the [superstructure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superstructure). It is also the first Iranian warship to be equipped with a [vertical launching system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertical_launching_system) (VLS). Its VLS includes 6 bigger cells for [surface-to-surface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface-to-surface) [cruise missiles](6590.md) and 16 smaller ones for [surface-to-air missiles](182664.md) all of which are located behind the [command bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridge_\(nautical\)). The warship *Shahid Soleimani* is also equipped with four 23 mm [gatling guns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gatling_gun) (likely [Asef](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asif)), one 30 mm [auto-canon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autocannon) and two [chaff dispensers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaff_\(countermeasure\)).[^cite_note-a-1][^cite_note-\:0-3] According to Commodore [Alireza Tangsiri](58274150.md), the *Shahid Soleimani*-class vessels have been equipped with different missiles with ranges of 200, 300 and 750 kilometers, as well as with a new 2,000-kilometer-range cruise missile named Ghadr-474.[^cite_note-tangsiri_tasnim-6] Tangsiri later said that *Sayyad Shirazi* and *Hassan Baqeri* are equipped with 16 Navvab vertically-launched missiles and 6 Sayyad-3 missiles.[^cite_note-7]

### Radars and electronics

Intelligence and reconnaissance monitoring, surface and subsurface and air traffic monitoring are among the duties of this vessel and it is equipped with Iran's most advanced [electronic warfare](82272.md) systems.[^cite_note-a-1]

### Propulsion

*Shahid Soleimani* is powered by four indigenous [diesel engines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diesel_engine) which gives it a top speed of 32 [knots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knot_\(unit\)) (59 km/h; 37 mph) and a range of 5,000 [nautical miles](21289.md) (9,300 km; 5,800 mi).[^cite_note-a-1]

### Other equipment

The warship also has a helipad and a crane under it which is used to launch and retrieve [fast attack boats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_attack_craft). The ship can carry three fast attack boats and has the ability to retrieve and rearm them.[^cite_note-a-1]
