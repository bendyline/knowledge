**Biddeford** ([/ˈbɪdɪfərd/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/English) [*BID-if-ərd*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:Pronunciation_respelling_key)) is a city in [York County, Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/York_County,_Maine), United States. It is the principal commercial center of York County. Its population was 22,552 at the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census).[^cite_note-3] The twin cities of [Saco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saco,_Maine) and Biddeford include the resort communities of [Biddeford Pool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biddeford_Pool) and [Fortunes Rocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortunes_Rocks,_Maine). The town is the site of the [University of New England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_England_\(United_States\)) and the annual [La Kermesse Franco-Americaine Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Kermesse_Franco-Americaine_Festival). First visited by Europeans in 1616, it is the site of one of the earliest European settlements in the United States. It is home to [Saint Joseph's Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Joseph's_Church_\(Biddeford,_Maine\)), the tallest building in Maine.[^cite_note-4]

Biddeford is a principal population center of the [Portland-South Portland-Biddeford metropolitan statistical area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland-South_Portland-Biddeford_metropolitan_area).

## History



Town hall c. 1885

The first European to settle at Biddeford was physician Richard Vines in the winter of 1616–17 at Winter Harbor, as he called [Biddeford Pool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biddeford_Pool). This 1616 landing by a European antedates the *Mayflower* landing in [Plymouth, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plymouth,_Massachusetts) (100 miles to the south), by about four years, a fact much of New England lore overlooks.[^cite_note-5] In 1630, the [Plymouth Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plymouth_Company) granted the land south of the [River Swanckadocke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saco_River) to Vines and John Oldham. In 1653, the town included both sides of the river, and was incorporated by the [Massachusetts General Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_General_Court) as Saco.[^cite_note-Coolidge-6]

Biddeford was first incorporated as the Town of Saco in 1653.[^cite_note-7] Roger Spencer was granted the right in 1653 to build the first [sawmill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sawmill). Lumber and fish became the community's chief exports. In 1659, Major William Phillips of [Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston,_Massachusetts) became a proprietor, and constructed a [garrison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garrison) and [watermill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watermill) at the falls. Settlers withdrew to Winter Harbor for safety, and their homes and mills upriver at the falls were burned. In 1693, a stone fort was built a short distance below the falls, but it was allegedly captured by Native Americans in 1703, when 11 colonists were killed and 24 taken captive to [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_France). In 1688, [Fort Mary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Mary_\(Maine\)) was built near the entrance to Biddeford Pool.[^cite_note-8] The town was reorganized in 1718 as Biddeford, after [Bideford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bideford), a town in [Devon, England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devon,_England), from which some settlers had emigrated. After the [Fall of Quebec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fall_of_Quebec) in 1759, hostilities with the indigenous people ceased.[^cite_note-Coolidge-6] During [King Philip's War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Philip's_War) in 1675, the town, once again, was allegedly attacked by Native Americans.

In 1762, the land northeast of the river was set off as Pepperellborough, which in 1805 was renamed Saco. The first bridge to Saco was built in 1767. The river divides into two falls that drop 40 feet (12 m), providing [water power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_power) for mills. Factories were established to make boots and shoes. The developing [mill town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mill_town) also had [granite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granite) [quarries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quarry) and [brickyards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brickmaking), in addition to lumber and [grain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gristmill) mills. Major [textile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textile) manufacturing facilities were constructed along the riverbanks, including the Laconia Company in 1845, and the Pepperell Company in 1850. Biddeford was incorporated as a city in 1855.[^cite_note-9]

The mills attracted waves of immigrants, including [Irish](775859.md), [Albanians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Albanians_in_Maine), and [French-Canadians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French-Canadian) from [Quebec](7954867.md). At one time, the textile mills employed as many as 12,000 people, but as happened elsewhere, the industry entered a long period of decline. As of 2009, the last remaining textile company in the city, WestPoint Home, closed. The property occupying the mill has been sold and is being redeveloped into housing and new businesses. The last [log drive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Log_driving) down the Saco River was in 1943, with the last log sawn in 1948. Biddeford's name is engraved near the top level of the [Pilgrim Monument](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pilgrim_Monument), in [Provincetown, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provincetown,_Massachusetts), along with the names of some of the oldest cities and towns in New England.[^cite_note-10]

During [World War II](32927.md), the [Biddeford Pool Military Reservation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biddeford_Pool_Military_Reservation) was established from 1942 to 1945, on what is now the Abenakee Golf Club. It had four circular concrete platforms called "[Panama mounts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panama_mount)" for [155-mm guns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/155_mm_gun_M1918), three of which remain today.[^cite_note-11]

On July 13, 2026, a [United States Immigration and Customs Enforcement](216537.md) agent [fatally shot Joan Sebastián Durán Guerrero](83686932.md), a 26-year old Colombian national, in Biddeford.[^cite_note-12]

## Geography



Tugboat *Hersey* tied up at Bragdon's Wharf, Biddeford, 1912

According to the [United States Census Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Census_Bureau), the city has an area of 59.08 square miles (153.02 km<sup>2</sup>), of which 28.99 square miles (75.08 km<sup>2</sup>) are covered by water.[^cite_note-Gazetteer_files-13] Situated beside [Saco Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saco_Bay_\(Maine\)) on the Gulf of Maine, Biddeford is drained by the [Little River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_River_\(Goosefare_Bay\)) and the [Saco River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saco_River). The city proper has very diverse geography, from inland rolling hillside, to urban settlement, to coastal sprawl.

The city is crossed by [Interstate 95](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_95_in_Maine), [U. S. Route 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_1_in_Maine), and state routes [5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maine_State_Route_5), [9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maine_State_Route_9), [111](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maine_State_Route_111), and [208](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maine_State_Route_208). It is bordered by the city of [Saco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saco,_Maine) to the north, the [Atlantic Ocean](698.md) to the east, the towns of [Dayton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dayton,_Maine) and [Lyman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyman,_Maine) to the west, and the towns of [Kennebunkport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kennebunkport) and [Arundel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arundel,_Maine) to the south. The Little River forms a portion of the border between Biddeford and the [Goose Rocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goose_Rocks) neighborhood of Kennebunkport, in Biddeford's most southerly region (Granite Point). East Point, located on the peninsula of Biddeford Pool, is the easternmost point in York County.

Timber Island, the most southerly point in the City of Biddeford, lies in Goosefare Bay at the mouth of the Little River, and is accessible at low tide from Goose Rocks Beach in Kennebunkport. The island and most of adjacent Timber Point became part of the Rachel Carson National Wildlife Refuge in December 2011.

The city has almost 15 miles (24 km) of frontage along the Saco River, and an Atlantic coastline on which the seaside neighborhoods of [Hills Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hills_Beach), Biddeford Pool, Fortunes Rocks and [Granite Point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granite_Point?action=edit\&redlink=1) are located. Biddeford includes [Wood Island Light](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wood_Island_Light), a [lighthouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lighthouse) located about a mile offshore from Biddeford Pool.

### Climate

This [climatic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate) region is typified by large seasonal temperature differences, with warm to hot (and often humid) summers and cold (sometimes severely cold) winters. According to the [Köppen climate classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification), Biddeford has a [humid continental climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_continental_climate), *Dfb* on climate maps.[^cite_note-14]

### 2020 census

As of the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census), Biddeford had a population of 22,552. The median age was 38.2 years. 15.9% of residents were under the age of 18 and 18.3% of residents were 65 years of age or older. For every 100 females there were 90.9 males, and for every 100 females age 18 and over there were 88.2 males age 18 and over.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-16][^cite_note-Census2020PL-17]

86.3% of residents lived in urban areas, while 13.7% lived in rural areas.[^cite_note-Census2020DHC-18]

There were 9,507 households in Biddeford, of which 21.4% had children under the age of 18 living in them. Of all households, 36.6% were married-couple households, 22.6% were households with a male householder and no spouse or partner present, and 28.8% were households with a female householder and no spouse or partner present. About 34.1% of all households were made up of individuals and 12.3% had someone living alone who was 65 years of age or older.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-16]

There were 10,686 housing units, of which 11.0% were vacant. The homeowner vacancy rate was 1.1% and the rental vacancy rate was 4.7%.[^cite_note-Census2020DP-16]

There were 8,598 households, of which 27.3% had children under the age of 18 living with them, 40.4% were [married couples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marriage) living together, 12.3% had a female householder with no husband present, 5.1% had a male householder with no wife present, and 42.2% were non-families. 30.0% of all households were made up of individuals, and 11.1% had someone living alone who was 65 years of age or older. ## Economy



The old mills of Biddeford

Biddeford is one of Maine's fastest-growing commercial centers, due to its close proximity to the [Seacoast Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seacoast_Region_\(New_Hampshire\)) of New Hampshire and to northern Massachusetts. In recent years, strip malls have developed along the [State Route 111](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maine_State_Route_111) corridor. In late 2006, a 500,000-square-foot (46,000 m<sup>2</sup>) shopping center known as The Shops at Biddeford Crossing opened, with 20 stores and five restaurants.

Recent interest in revitalizing the downtown area has brought new life to the old mills. The North Dam Mill is one example of this movement offering retail stores, art studios, cultural events, and upscale housing.

Biddeford is home to large institutions including MaineHealth Maine Medical Center Biddeford and the [University of New England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_England,_Maine), a fast-growing school located along the coast which includes Maine's only medical school, the [University of New England College of Osteopathic Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_England_College_of_Osteopathic_Medicine).[^cite_note-21] Telecommunications company [GWI.net](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GWI.net) is headquartered in the city. The city also possesses a wide array of community facilities including public beaches, an ice arena, a full-service [YMCA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YMCA), and one school which has been recently recognized as a [National School of Excellence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Ribbon_Schools_Program).

### Tourism

Anchoring Biddeford's historic downtown are [McArthur Public Library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McArthur_Public_Library) and [Biddeford's City Theater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biddeford's_City_Theater). Biddeford has a number of properties and two Historic Districts entered into the [National Register of Historic Places](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Register_of_Historic_Places).[^cite_note-22] The newest addition is the Main Street Historic District, entered into the National Register on December 24, 2009. Other downtown National Register properties include the Biddeford-Saco Mills Historic District, Biddeford City Hall, Dudley Block and the U.S. Post Office. National Register properties outside of downtown and in the Biddeford Pool area include the John Tarr House, [First Parish Meetinghouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Parish_Meetinghouse), Fletcher's Neck Lifesaving Station and the James Montgomery Flagg House.[^cite_note-23] There are also many festivals and concerts held around the city.[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25]

## Infrastructure

### Transportation

Biddeford was the eastern terminus of the now-defunct New England Interstate [Route 11](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Route_11_\(New_England\)), which ended in [Manchester, Vermont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester,_Vermont). State Route 111, which travels through Biddeford's main commercial corridor, is now numbered in Old Route 11's place. [Biddeford Municipal Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biddeford_Municipal_Airport) is located two miles south of the [central business district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_business_district). The [Saco Transportation Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saco_Transportation_Center) Amtrak stop serves downtown Biddeford.

Local bus service in Biddeford is provided by [Biddeford-Saco-Old Orchard Beach Transit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biddeford-Saco-Old_Orchard_Beach_Transit), connecting the city to destinations in [Saco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saco,_Maine) and [Portland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland,_Maine).

It is in the [Biddeford School District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biddeford_School_District?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-26] Schools include the [Biddeford High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biddeford_High_School) and the [Biddeford Regional Center of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biddeford_Regional_Center_of_Technology).
