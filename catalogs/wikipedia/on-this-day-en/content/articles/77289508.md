**Zubir Ahmed** (born 1980 or 1981)[^cite_note-1] is a [Scottish Labour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Labour) politician and surgeon serving as [Member of Parliament](3776360.md) (MP) for [Glasgow South West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow_South_West) since 2024. He served as Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Health Innovation and Safety from 2025 to 2026.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

## Early life and medical career

Ahmed was born to Pakistani parents who emigrated to Scotland in the 1960s, and was the eldest of five children.[^cite_note-record_oct_2023-4] His father was a taxi driver and he grew up in [Govanhill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Govanhill), [Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow).

Ahmed was educated at [Hutchesons' Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hutchesons'_Grammar_School), initially fee-paying and latterly on scholarship,[^cite_note-auto-5] where he was a contemporary of [Anas Sarwar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anas_Sarwar) and [Humza Yousaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humza_Yousaf).[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-record_oct_2023-4]

Ahmed received a [Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Medicine,_Bachelor_of_Surgery) degree from the [University of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow) in 2005, where he studied alongside Sarwar once again.[^cite_note-Herald_Feb23-7] He was awarded a [Master of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) degree in healthcare from [University College London](52029.md) in 2012, a research fellowship in transplantation at [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London) in 2019, and a [Master of Business Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Business_Administration) degree from the [University of Strathclyde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strathclyde) in 2020.

Ahmed was a transplant and vascular surgeon in Glasgow and volunteered in [Pakistan](23235.md).[^cite_note-labourlist2024-8][^cite_note-record_oct_2023-4] He has been a fellow of the European Board of Surgery and the [Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_College_of_Physicians_and_Surgeons_of_Glasgow).[^cite_note-9] Following his election to Parliament, he continued to practise to maintain his medical licence.[^cite_note-\:0-10]

## Political career

Ahmed joined the [Labour Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Labour) as a teenager, and decided to stand for office during the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md).[^cite_note-11] He was the Labour candidate for [Glasgow Pollok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow_Pollok_\(Scottish_Parliament_constituency\)) in the [2021 Scottish Parliament election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Scottish_Parliament_election), coming second to the [Scottish National Party](28887.md)'s (SNP) [Humza Yousaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humza_Yousaf).[^cite_note-Herald_Feb23-7]

Ahmed was elected as [Member of Parliament](3776360.md) for [Glasgow South West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow_South_West) at the [2024 general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_Kingdom_general_election), defeating SNP incumbent [Chris Stephens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Stephens).[^cite_note-BBC_results-12] He was appointed as a [Parliamentary Private Secretary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_Private_Secretary) to the [Health Secretary](356531.md), [Wes Streeting](17169295.md), several weeks after his election.[^cite_note-\:0-10]

Ahmed received a £10,000 donation from the think tank [Labour Together](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labour_Together).[^cite_note-13]

On 12 May 2026, he resigned as junior health minister, citing the Labour Party's poor performance in the [2026 Scottish Parliament election](65934215.md) and calling for Keir Starmer to resign.[^cite_note-14]

## Personal life

Ahmed met his wife at university. They have two sons and live in Glasgow South West.[^cite_note-15][^cite_note-auto-5][^cite_note-16]
