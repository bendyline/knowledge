**Qalandia Camp** is a [Palestinian refugee camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_refugee_camp) established in 1949 by the [Red Cross](15487.md)[^cite_note-2] on land leased from [Jordan](7515964.md). It covers 353 dunums (0.353 km<sup>2</sup>; 35.3 ha) as of 2006[^cite_note-ochaoptPDF-3] and had a population of 8,336 in 2017.[^cite_note-PrelimCensus2017-1] Israeli authorities consider it part of [Greater Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Jerusalem), and it remains under their control.[^cite_note-unrwa-4]
