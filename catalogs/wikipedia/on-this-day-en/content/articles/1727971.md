**San** ([Bambara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bambara_language): ߛߊߣ tr. San) is an [urban commune](16112417.md), town and capital of the [Cercle of San](21408559.md) in the [Ségou Region](1382091.md) of [Mali](19127.md). The town lies 10 kilometres or 6 miles south of the [Bani River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bani_River). In the 2009 census, the commune had a population of 68,078.

## History

The area of San was for centuries a fishing camp for [Bozo people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bozo_people). Around 1400 CE, a trade city dependent on the kingdom of [Daa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dah,_Mali) began to develop at a crucial ford over the Bani linking the Inner Niger Delta to the goldfields to the south, although oral traditions conflict on whether [Marka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marka_people) from [Dia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dia,_Mali) or [Dyula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dyula_people) from [Djenne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djenne) were responsible. The town was attacked by [Askia Ishaq I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Askia_Ishaq_I) of the [Songhai Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songhai_Empire) in 1542. At the time it may have been known under the name 'Sama',[^cite_note-Massing-3] although the name could also refer to [Sama Foulala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sama_Foulala).[^cite_note-4]

In 1690 San, a prosperous market town, was conquered by [Biton Coulibaly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biton_Coulibaly) and integrated into the growing [Bamana Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bamana_Empire). In 1739, [Famaghan Ouattara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Famaghan_Ouattara?action=edit\&redlink=1) of the [Kong Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kong_Empire) intervened in a Bamana civil war and destroyed the town. It was later rebuilt somewhat further north. San was incorporated into the [Massina Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massina_Empire) by [Cheikhou Amadou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheikhou_Amadou) in the 1830s, and became a theocratic state. For this reason, when [El-Hajj Omar Tall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El-Hajj_Omar_Tall) destroyed Massina in the 1860s he spared the city.[^cite_note-Massing-3]

The Almamy of San was forced to sign a protectorate treaty with France in 1891. The city's independence was soon curtailed by the colonial administration, however. In 1915, the area witnessed a series of rebellions against forced recruitment into the French army and forced labor during [World War I](4764461.md), and the town's population plummeted.[^cite_note-Massing-3]

San was elevated to the status of commune under the French colonial regime in November 1955.[^cite_note-sake-5]

## Economy and Culture

San is the center for production of [bògòlanfini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%B2g%C3%B2lanfini), a traditional Malian fabric. It also is a center of [horse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horse) breeding. The main engine of the economy, however, is trade on the main highways that pass through town and the [rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice) fields along the Bani river.[^cite_note-Massing-3]

Attractions in the town include the San Museum and the [Sudanic-style](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudano-Sahelian_architecture) [mosque](19894.md). The yearly Sanke Mo fishing festival takes place every June, and has been officially classified as part of the national cultural patrimony of Mali.[^cite_note-6]

## Climate

San has a [hot semi-arid climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_semi-arid_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *BSh*) with a wet season from late May to early October. Almost no rain falls during the long dry season from early October to late May, while afternoon temperatures are sweltering except in December and January, when they are merely hot in the afternoon and pleasant to warm in the mornings.
