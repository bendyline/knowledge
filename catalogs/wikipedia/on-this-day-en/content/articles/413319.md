The **Serb Democratic Party** ([Serbian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_language): Српска демократска странка, **СДС**, *Srpska demokratska stranka, **SDS***) is a [Serb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbs_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina) [conservative](6675.md) [political party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_political_parties_in_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina) in [Bosnia and Herzegovina](3463.md). It was founded by [Radovan Karadžić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radovan_Karad%C5%BEi%C4%87) in 1990, ahead of the first [multi-party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-party_system) [Bosnian general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_Bosnian_general_election).

In the parliamentary elections of October 2006, the SDS lost its status as the leading party in [Republika Srpska](347422.md) and the main Serb party in Bosnia and Herzegovina to the [Alliance of Independent Social Democrats](4358875.md) (SNSD), led by the [president](15903684.md) of Republika Srpska [Milorad Dodik](4218129.md).[^cite_note-4] Despite making minor gains in the 2010 and 2014 elections, by 2018 the party had fallen to below 20% of the parliament, the lowest seat standing in its history.

The party is under sanctions from the [United States](3434750.md) for "failing to arrest and turn over war crimes suspects to an international tribunal." The sanctions prohibit any transfer of funds and material from the United States to the SDS and vice versa.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] The party is on the list of *[Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specially_Designated_Nationals_and_Blocked_Persons_List)* by the [Office of Foreign Assets Control](231045.md) U.S. agency.[^cite_note-7] To avoid the sanctions, the SDS officials established a separate legal entity called **SDS – The Will of the People** ([Serbian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_language): СДС – Воља народа) in 2020.

## History

### Establishment

[Radovan Karadžić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radovan_Karad%C5%BEi%C4%87) founded the Serb Democratic Party in July 1990. The party aimed at unifying the [Bosnian Serb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbs_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina) community, as [Jovan Rašković](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jovan_Ra%C5%A1kovi%C4%87)'s [Serb Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serb_Democratic_Party_\(Croatia\)) did with the Serbs in Croatia, and staying part of Yugoslavia (as the "Third Yugoslavia" with [Serbia and Montenegro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbia_and_Montenegro)) in the event of secession by those two republics from the federation.[^cite_note-8]

### 1991

Throughout September 1991, the SDS began to establish various "[Serb Autonomous Regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serb_Autonomous_Regions)" throughout Bosnia and Herzegovina. After the Bosnian parliament voted on sovereignty on 15 October 1991, a separate [Serb Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_\(Republika_Srpska\)) was founded on 24 October 1991 in [Banja Luka](385109.md), in order to exclusively represent the Serbs in Bosnia and Herzegovina. The following month, Bosnian Serbs held a [referendum](51582.md) which resulted in an overwhelming vote in favor of staying in a federal state with Serbia and Montenegro, as part of Yugoslavia. In December 1991, a top-secret document entitled ‘[For the organization and activity of organs of the Serbs people in Bosnia-Herzegovina in extraordinary circumstances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variant_A/B_Instructions)’ was drawn up by the SDS leadership. This was a centralized program for the takeover of each municipality in the country, through the creation of shadow governments and para-governmental structures through various "crisis headquarters", and by preparing loyal Serbs for the takeover in co-ordination with the [Yugoslav People's Army](536643.md) (JNA).[^cite_note-9]

## Ideology

Historically, the party had a strong [ultranationalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultranationalism),[^cite_note-10][^cite_note-\:0-11] [separatist](286527.md)[^cite_note-\:0-11][^cite_note-Nordsieck-12] and [Islamophobic](162173.md) ideology.
