***Liberation*** is a 2025 American [memory play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memory_play) by [Bess Wohl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bess_Wohl). Developed by the [Roundabout Theatre Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roundabout_Theatre_Company), it explores [second-wave feminism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second-wave_feminism), memory, and the complexities of social change across generations. The play premiered [off-Broadway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Off-Broadway) at the [Laura Pels Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Pels_Theatre) in February 2025, directed by Whitney White, and transferred to [Broadway](725252.md) in October 2025 at the [James Earl Jones Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Earl_Jones_Theatre). The production won the 2026 [Tony Award for Best Play](250941.md). On May 4, 2026, it was announced that Bess Wohl won the [2026](83103447.md#Books,_drama,_and_music) [Pulitzer Prize for Drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulitzer_Prize_for_Drama) for *Liberation.*[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2]

## Premise

Set in [Ohio](22199.md) in 1970 the play opens in a rec-centre basement where Lizzie convenes a consciousness-raising group of women. Fifty years later Lizzie's daughter looks back to try to understand her mother's radical past.[^cite_note-3] Structured as a memory play, the narrative shifts fluidly between past and present, with the narrator directly addressing the audience.[^cite_note-Holdren-4]

## Productions

### Off-Broadway (2025)

*Liberation* premiered off-Broadway at Roundabout's Laura Pels Theatre. Previews began on January 31, 2025, with an opening night on February 20. It ran until April 6, having extended a week from its originally planned closing date of March 30.[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] The production was directed by Whitney White. The creative team included David Zinn (scenic design), Qween Jean (costume design), Cha See (lighting), and Palmer Hefferan (sound).[^cite_note-7]

### Broadway (2025)

In August 2025 it was announced that a Broadway production had been scheduled at the [James Earl Jones Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Earl_Jones_Theatre) for a 14-week engagement.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] Previews started October 8,[^cite_note-10] with an opening on October 28, 2025. The original off-Broadway cast reprised their roles.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] The engagement was later extended by three weeks to February 1, 2026.[^cite_note-13]

### London (2027)

In May 2026 a London production was announced for 2027. [Daryl Roth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daryl_Roth), Eva Price, Rachel Sussman, [Eleanor Lloyd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eleanor_Lloyd) Productions, and Eilene Davidson Productions are involved in the transfer.[^cite_note-14]

## Reception

The play received widespread acclaim from critics. [Sara Holdren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sara_Holdren) of [*Vulture*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vulture_\(website\)) praised it as "the best play I've seen this season", lauding how it "balances the intensely personal and the broadly civic, the ethical and the theatrical, with extraordinary rigor and grace".[^cite_note-Holdren-4]

*[The Week](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Week)* remarked that the play "takes an old form and shakes it like a freshly laundered sheet in the breeze", singling out the cast's performances in a "growing wave of charisma, camaraderie, tenderness, and tension".[^cite_note-16]

*[The Wrap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wrap)* declared that "2025 has already delivered its first great new play", commending Wohl's deft mixture of sobering, funny, provocative, and "totally engaging" elements and noting her narrator is "even more direct and efficient ... than the Stage Manager in *Our Town*".[^cite_note-17]

Some reviewers offered more reserved praise. Rachel Graham of *TheaterMania* observed that while the play does create "multidimensional characters and deftly balances a personal story with the history of the women's movement", ultimately "the landing doesn't quite stick", though "there's enough to keep the thoughtful Liberation in the air throughout its two-and-a-half hours".[^cite_note-18]
