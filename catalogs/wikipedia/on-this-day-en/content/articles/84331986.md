# 2026 Bangkok floods

| Thunderclouds developing over Thailand |  |
| --- | --- |
| Date | 24–30 September 2026 (6 days) |
| Location | [Bangkok](56615.md) and adjacent provinces |
| Cause | Heavy rainfall triggered by an active low-pressure system/tropical depression and strong seasonal monsoon conditions |
| Deaths | 40 (including floods across the country)[^cite_note-CNA-1] |
| Property damage | [฿](89761.md)11 billion+  ([US\$](18717338.md)327 million+)[^cite_note-Reuters28-2] |

On 26 September 2026 at 10\:16 local time ([UTC+7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTC+7)), a flood disaster emergency was declared in [Bangkok](56615.md) following 48 hours of continuous heavy rain, which submerged main roads[^cite_note-3] and triggered mass evacuations.[^cite_note-4] Over 300 mm (12 in) of rain was recorded in certain parts of Bangkok, with floodwaters blocking traffic on main roads in 37 locations.[^cite_note-5] The [Bangkok Metropolitan Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangkok_Metropolitan_Administration) (BMA) reported that it would take two to three days to drain the floodwaters after the rain stopped.[^cite_note-6] Authorities declared [all 50 districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_districts_of_Bangkok) of the city a disaster zone.[^cite_note-7] It was the worst flooding to affect Bangkok in [15 years](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_Thailand_floods).[^cite_note-8]

On the morning of 24 September, the [Japan Meteorological Agency](457886.md) (JMA) reported that [a tropical depression](80781992.md#September_Thailand_Tropical_Depression) had formed over [Cambodia](334751.md) near [Krong Siem Reap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siem_Reap).[^cite_note-JapanMeteorologicalAgency-9] The system exited the basin the following day,[^cite_note-JapanMeteorologicalAgency-9] and the [IMD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India_Meteorological_Department) began monitoring a related upper-level cyclonic circulation over southern Thailand and Myanmar on 26 September.[^cite_note-10] The next day, the [Joint Typhoon Warning Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Typhoon_Warning_Center) (JTWC) issued a TCFA for the system, citing a high chance of development within 24 hours.[^cite_note-JTWC2026-11] On 28 September, the IMD upgraded the well-marked low-pressure area to a [depression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_North_Indian_Ocean_cyclone_season#Deep_Depression_BOB_07).[^cite_note-12] Later that day, the IMD further upgraded the system to a deep depression.[^cite_note-13] On 29 September at 00\:30 local time, the JTWC canceled the TCFA as the system moved inland.[^cite_note-JTWC2026-11] Later that day, the IMD reported that the system had weakened to a depression[^cite_note-14] as the floodwaters began to ease.[^cite_note-15] By 30 September, the depression weakened into a low pressure area over [Myanmar](19457.md).[^cite_note-16]

## Impact



Flooding near [Lak Si Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lak_Si_station) on 27 September

A 58-year-old woman died from electrocution in the car park of a housing complex in [Khlong Chan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khlong_Chan).[^cite_note-17] Two fatalities were confirmed in [Bueng Kum district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bueng_Kum_district): a 73-year-old man who drowned after slipping and falling while moving belongings from his house, and a 78-year-old bedridden man who drowned after being unable to evacuate.[^cite_note-18]

The city closed over 400 municipal schools on 28 September as flooding persisted in the outer districts, even though the weather was improving.[^cite_note-19]

The Cabinet of Thailand approved 28 and 29 September as special holidays in Bangkok, [Pathum Thani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pathum_Thani_province), [Samut Prakan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samut_Prakan_province), and [Nonthaburi](344757.md) for people to "take care of themselves and their families."[^cite_note-20] On 28 September, a man was arrested for riding a [jet ski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jet_ski) through the floodwaters in [Huai Khwang district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huai_Khwang_district).[^cite_note-21]



Backlogged cargo overflowed the cargo terminal at Suvarnabhumi Airport as a result of staff shortages due to the flooding.

At [Suvarnabhumi Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suvarnabhumi_Airport), flooding disrupted [Thai Airways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_Airways) operations by limiting the availability of ground-handling staff.[^cite_note-22] This led to numerous flight cancellations to and from Bangkok on 28 and 29 September, leaving 5,600 bags stranded inside the airport. Sixty-nine [Royal Thai Air Force](1778758.md) personnel were called in to clear the backlog and aid in ground operations.[^cite_note-23][^cite_note-24] Thai Airways CEO Chai Eamsiri apologized for the luggage crisis and set a 72-hour target to clear the remaining bags from the airport.[^cite_note-25] On 2 October, the board of directors of Thai Airways decided to remove Chai from his post.[^cite_note-26]

On 30 September, [Toyota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota) shut down four plants since continued flooding prevented parts deliveries, as the death toll rises to forty people.[^cite_note-CNA-1]

### Wider area

The heavy rains that caused the Bangkok flooding were widespread across Thailand. Over half a million people were affected across 25 provinces. Samut Prakan, just south of Bangkok, was the most severely hit.[^cite_note-27] Pattaya also experienced flooding, although it was less severe than in Bangkok.[^cite_note-28]

By 29 September, the death toll from the floods had risen to 23 people, with the disaster causing at least [\$320 million](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thailand_baht) in damages.[^cite_note-Reuters28-2][^cite_note-Reuters29-29] Eight people died in Samut Prakan, six in [Sa Kaeo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sa_Kaeo_province), and four in Bangkok.[^cite_note-30]
