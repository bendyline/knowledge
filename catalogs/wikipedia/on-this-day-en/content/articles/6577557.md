# Saegheh

For the shoulder-launched anti-tank rocket, see [Saegheh (rocket)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saegheh_\(rocket\)). For the Iranian fighter aircraft, see [HESA Saeqeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HESA_Saeqeh). For the stealth UCAV, see [Saegheh (UAV)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saegheh_\(UAV\)).

The **Saegheh** ("Lightning" or "Thunder" in [Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language); also spelled Saegre, Saeghe, Saeqeh, etc.) is any of at least eight completely separate Iranian weapons systems: a [rocket-propelled grenade](26286.md) (RPG) warhead, an anti-tank guided missile family, a surface-to-surface rocket, a target drone family, an air-to-air missile, a claimed stealth [unmanned aerial vehicle](58900.md), a fighter jet, and an anti-ship cruise missile.

## Saegheh ATGM



The Saeghe 1 ATGM (cutaway, front) and a Saeghe 2 ATGM (rear)

The first system, the *Saeghe 1*, is an Iranian reverse-engineered clone of the American [M47 Dragon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M47_Dragon) [wire-guided](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wire-guided_missile) [SACLOS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SACLOS) [ATGM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATGM), introduced in 2001.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] It seems to have entered production in 2002.[^cite_note-3] Iran later introduced the *Saeghe 2*, a more advanced variant with a [tandem-warhead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tandem-warhead) to defeat [explosive reactive armor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Explosive_reactive_armor),[^cite_note-mashreghnews.ir-4] and the *Saeghe-4*, with a [thermobaric warhead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermobaric_weapon).[^cite_note-5] The *Saeghe-1* weights 6.1 kilograms (13 lb) and can penetrate armor up to 500 millimetres (20 in). The 7.4-kilogram (16 lb) *Saeghe 2* missile has a [tandem warhead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tandem_warhead) and can penetrate up to 760 millimetres (30 in) of armor.[^cite_note-mashreghnews.ir-4] The *Saeghe 1 & 4 65 to 1,000 metres (210 to 3,280 ft)* and *Saeghe 2* ATGMs have a range of 50 to 1,500 metres (160 to 4,920 ft).[^cite_note-mashreghnews.ir-4]

Despite being essentially obsolete, *Saegheh* anti-tank guided missiles have been exported to Syria, Hezbollah,[^cite_note-SyrHez1-6] and Shia militias in Iraq. The *Saeghe 1* and *Saeghe 2* were in production and service as of 2011, though they were not widely deployed.[^cite_note-7] The *Saeghe* ATGM is easy to aim thank to SACLOS IR wire-guided and in Iranian service it appears to be limited to IRGC and rapid response forces.[^cite_note-8]

## Other Saegheh name designations

In 2006, Iran tested a completely unrelated short-range [surface-to-surface missile](1483499.md)[^cite_note-chinaview-9] that is also named Saegheh. It has a range of 80 to 250 kilometres (50 to 155 mi).[^cite_note-chinaview-9]

Under the Saegheh name, Iran has also built a [variant warhead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saegheh_\(rocket\)) for RPG-7 style rocket launchers. Iran also uses the Saeghe name for a [target drone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghods_Saeghe)[^cite_note-10] (which comes in two variants: Saeghe 1 and Saeghe 2)[^cite_note-11] and an air-to-air missile.[^cite_note-12]

The semi-official [Tasnim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tasnim) news agency of Iran reported in September 2016 that an [unmanned aerial vehicle](58900.md) (UAV, drone) named [Saegheh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahed_Saegheh) similar to the US [RQ-170 Sentinel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RQ-170_Sentinel) spy drone had been built. It was said to be able to carry four [precision-guided bombs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precision-guided_bomb); range was not stated. An RQ-170 had been [captured by Iran in 2011](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran%E2%80%93U.S._RQ-170_incident).[^cite_note-13]

The Saegheh name is further reused for the [HESA Saeqeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HESA_Saeqeh), a low production jet fighter, and for the Saeghe anti-ship cruise missile.[^cite_note-14]
