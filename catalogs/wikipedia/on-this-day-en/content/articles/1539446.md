**Jefferson Avenue** is a 64-mile-long (103 km)[^cite_note-\:0-1] [scenic road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scenic_route) along the eastern part of the [Metro Detroit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metro_Detroit) area in the [southeast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southeast_Michigan) of the U.S. state of [Michigan](18859.md). It travels alongside [Lake Erie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Erie), the [Detroit River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit_River), and [Lake St. Clair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_St._Clair), and is a major link between [Downtown Detroit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downtown_Detroit), the neighborhoods of the [Lower East Side](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_neighborhoods_in_Detroit#Lower) and [Southwest Detroit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwest_Detroit), and the suburban regions of [Downriver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downriver) and the [Grosse Pointes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grosse_Pointe). Jefferson is one of [Detroit](8687.md)'s five principal avenues along with [Woodward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodward_Avenue), [Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_Avenue_\(Michigan\)), [Grand River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_River_Avenue), and [Gratiot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gratiot_Avenue).&#x20;

## History

Jefferson is one of five major Detroit avenues planned by Judge [Augustus B. Woodward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustus_B._Woodward) in 1805 that extend radially from downtown Detroit in differing directions. First surveyed in 1807, it was named Main Street.[^cite_note-2] It was later renamed Jefferson Avenue after U.S. President [Thomas Jefferson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Jefferson), who was a friend of Woodward.[^cite_note-3] Jefferson Avenue was planned to extend due east of downtown, parallel to the Detroit River, but later additions to the road have extended it west of downtown as well, where it travels near the waterfront through the [Downriver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downriver) area.[^cite_note-4]

## Route description



The [Detroit People Mover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit_People_Mover)'s [Renaissance Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renaissance_Center) station is on Jefferson Avenue.



[Chauncey Hurlbut Memorial Gate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurlbut_Memorial_Gate), Waterworks Park

Jefferson Avenue radiates east and west from Downtown Detroit, originating at an intersection with Woodward Avenue next to Hart Plaza. Eastward, Jefferson Avenue runs along the [Renaissance Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renaissance_Center), and intersects with the beginning of [I-375](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_375_\(Michigan\)) on the eastern edge of Downtown.[^cite_note-5] There, it narrows from six lanes to five, and begins to include [bike lanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bike_lane) which run to the city limits.[^cite_note-6] Continuing east, Jefferson intersects with the [MacArthur Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacArthur_Bridge_\(Detroit\)), the entrance to [Belle Isle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belle_Isle_Park). Jefferson Avenue passes through the Eastside neighborhoods of [West Village](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Village,_Detroit), [Indian Village](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Village,_Detroit), and [Jefferson-Chalmers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jefferson%E2%80%93Chalmers_Historic_Business_District).[^cite_note-7] Jefferson Avenue continues through the suburb of [Grosse Pointe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grosse_Pointe), and its name changes to Lake Shore Drive alongside [Lake St. Clair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_St._Clair). Northward along the shore, Jefferson Avenue passes through [St. Clair Shores](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Clair_Shores,_Michigan)[^cite_note-8] and [Chesterfield Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chesterfield_Township,_Michigan).[^cite_note-9] Jefferson Avenue has a gap in its route at [Selfridge Air National Guard Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selfridge_Air_National_Guard_Base). Jefferson Avenue's east end is at 23 Mile Road, near the boundary between Chesterfield Township and [New Baltimore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Baltimore,_Michigan).

Westward from Downtown, Jefferson Avenue passes under the [Huntington Place](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huntington_Place) convention center, and is routed along the southern end of [M-10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-10_\(Michigan_highway\)). Jefferson Avenue travels south along the Detroit River, connecting parts of the [Detroit Riverwalk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit_International_Riverfront).[^cite_note-10] Parks located on the river side of Jefferson include the Ralph C. Wilson, Jr. Centennial Park[^cite_note-11] and Riverside Park.[^cite_note-12] Jefferson Avenue passes under the [Ambassador Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambassador_Bridge)[^cite_note-13] and the [Gordie Howe International Bridge](26342191.md)[^cite_note-14], before crossing over the [River Rouge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Rouge_\(Michigan\)) to enter the city of [River Rouge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Rouge,_Michigan). Jefferson passes though the Downriver cities of [Ecorse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecorse,_Michigan), [Wyandotte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wyandotte,_Michigan) (where it is known as Biddle Avenue), and [Trenton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trenton,_Michigan).[^cite_note-\:0-1] The western end of Jefferson Avenue is in Berlin Township at an intersection with [Dixie Highway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dixie_Highway).

In downtown Detroit, the southern end of [M-10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-10_\(Michigan_highway\)) is routed along Jefferson to the corner of Randolph Street/[M-3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-3_\(Michigan_highway\)). From the corner of Randolph to the southern end of [I-375](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_375_\(Michigan\)) between Beaubien and St. Antoine streets, Jefferson is the extremely short, 0.015-mile-long (0.024 km), unsigned [Business Spur I-375](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_375_Business_\(Detroit,_Michigan\)).
