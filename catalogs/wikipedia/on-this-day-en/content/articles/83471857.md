**Tropical Storm Arthur** was a short-lived and disorganized [tropical cyclone](8282374.md) that brought significant [flash flooding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flash_flooding) and tornadoes to portions of the [Southern United States](179553.md). The first [named storm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_cyclone_naming#North_Atlantic_Ocean) of the [2026 Atlantic hurricane season](65440738.md), the system’s origins can be tracked back to when a [tropical wave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_wave) combined with the remnants of [Tropical Storm Cristina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_Storm_Cristina_\(2026\)) just east of the [Yucatán Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yucat%C3%A1n_Peninsula).[^cite_note-Wave_development-1][^cite_note-Gulf_entry-2] The disturbance was tracked as it made its way into the [Gulf of Mexico](21076367.md) and eventually into [Mexico](3966054.md) where it interacted with a frontal boundary.[^cite_note-Frontal_interaction-3] Upon reaching the coast of the [US state](18618239.md) of [Texas](29810.md), it was labeled as a [potential tropical cyclone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potential_tropical_cyclone) and eventually a [tropical storm](8282374.md) becoming named *Arthur* on June 17.[^cite_note-Potential_cyclone_label-4][^cite_note-Storm_naming-5] It did not last long due to an ill-defined center and being affected by strong wind shear, degenerating into a [post-tropical cyclone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-tropical_cyclone) and opening into a trough early the following morning after making landfall near [Galveston](53840.md).

As of June 18, 2026, at least four people were confirmed to have been killed as a result of Arthur. As of July 2026, [Arthur J. Gallagher & Co.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_J._Gallagher_&_Co.) estimated losses to be greater than US\$1 billion.[^cite_note-\:20-6] Rains as strong as those from Arthur are estimated to have a 1-in-1,000-year [return period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Return_period).[^cite_note-\:20-6]

A tropical wave combined with the remnant moisture of Pacific [Tropical Storm Cristina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_Storm_Cristina_\(2026\)) entered the [Bay of Campeche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay_of_Campeche) on June 12.[^cite_note-Wave_development-1] The disturbance was monitored by the National Hurricane Center as it traveled generally westward across the Gulf of Mexico and moved ashore in northeastern [Mexico](3966054.md) on June 14 and began interacting with a frontal boundary inland.[^cite_note-Frontal_interaction-3] The disturbance was designated as a [potential tropical cyclone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potential_tropical_cyclone) while located over southern [Texas](29810.md) at 15\:00 UTC on June 16, producing widespread convection but lacking a well-defined center.[^cite_note-Potential_cyclone_label-4] Development of the storm was enabled with [sea surface temperatures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_surface_temperature) above 83.5 °F (28.6 °C) off the [Texas coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Gulf_Coast).[^cite_note-7] The following day on June 17 at 15\:00 UTC, the system [became](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_cyclogenesis) Tropical Storm Arthur after [convective organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convection) proved sufficient for classification.[^cite_note-Storm_naming-5]



Tropical Storm Arthur undergoing a center relocation on June 17, as the old center (*left*) dissipates over Matagorda, a new center (*right*) reforms near Galveston

Arthur was poorly organized, under a hostile environment of strong westerly wind shear, most of Arthur’s deep convection remained displaced from its [low-level circulation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low-level_circulation_center) (LLC), managing to peak with sustained winds of 45 miles per hour (72 km/h) and a minimum pressure of 999 mb (29.5 inHg) in accordance with reconnaissance data and surface observations.[^cite_note-8] Later that same day, the NHC had analyzed that Arthur had weakened some as it began to undergo a center relocation as the old circulation went on to make landfall near [Matagorda County, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matagorda_County,_Texas), with a new LLC reforming near [Galveston, Texas](53840.md), driven by an offshore convective burst.[^cite_note-9] Despite the reformation of a new center, no organized convection developed around the new center as it elongated as a consequence. As a result, Arthur was deemed a [post-tropical cyclone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-tropical_cyclone) as it was located northeast of Galveston inland on June 18 at 03\:00 UTC.[^cite_note-10] The NHC continued to track the remnants of Arthur for possible redevelopment off the [US East Coast](89126.md) as the storm trekked northeastward.[^cite_note-11] The next day, strong upper-level wind shear and Arthur’s merging with a nearby [frontal system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_front) inhibited the chances for possible redevelopment as it was located off the southeastern coast.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13]

## Preparations

The precursor disturbance to Arthur bringing heavy rainfall to [Texas](29810.md).

[Tropical storm watches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_cyclone_warnings_and_watches) were issued from [Sargent, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sargent,_Texas), to [Morgan City, Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morgan_City,_Louisiana), on June 16 upon designation as a potential tropical cyclone.[^cite_note-14] This was later upgraded to a tropical storm warning between [Sabine Pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabine_Pass) and Morgan City.[^cite_note-15] The following day, the tropical storm warning was extended to Sargent.[^cite_note-16] [Flood watches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flood_watch) were issued at least 16 million people from [South Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Texas) to Central [Mississippi](16949861.md).[^cite_note-\:8-17][^cite_note-18] After Arthur developed, Flood Watches were extended further east to [South Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Georgia_\(region\)).[^cite_note-19] On June 18, a rare high risk of excessive rainfall was issued across the central Gulf Coast.[^cite_note-20]

[Greg Abbott](1492735.md), [governor of Texas](214883.md), issued a [state of emergency](298608.md) for 101[ counties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_Texas) and ordered the [Texas Division of Emergency Management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Division_of_Emergency_Management) to increase 24-hour operations and activate resources.[^cite_note-21] Sandbag distributions were organized across Texas.[^cite_note-\:5-22][^cite_note-23] In Houston, festivals for the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md) were cancelled for June 15 and shortened on June 16.[^cite_note-\:5-22] Despite the storm, a World Cup match between [Portugal](23033.md) and the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md) scheduled for June 17 was not cancelled because [the stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NRG_Stadium) had a covered roof.[^cite_note-24] This was the first World Cup game to be played in the vicinity of a tropical cyclone.[^cite_note-Times-25] Several school districts across Texas halted programs and activities during the passage of the storm.[^cite_note-\:8-17][^cite_note-\:6-26] [Texas A\&M University–Corpus Christi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A\&M_University%E2%80%93Corpus_Christi) moved work and classes online while the [Kingsville campus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A\&M_University%E2%80%93Kingsville) delayed opening till 10\:00[ CDT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Time_Zone), both on June 16.[^cite_note-\:6-26]

Several [parishes in Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parishes_in_Louisiana) distributed sandbags in anticipation of the storm.[^cite_note-27] Evacuations were required in parts of Southeast Louisiana as several creeks were expected to overflow their banks.[^cite_note-28] Residents in part of the [New Orleans metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans_metropolitan_area) was asked to stay clear of bodies of water. Pumping stations and drainage systems were cleared to prepare for heavy rainfall, while additional pumping equipment was brought to assist stations that were offline.[^cite_note-29]

A senior center in [Wiggins, Mississippi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiggins,_Stone_County,_Mississippi) opened as a shelter.[^cite_note-30] Those around the [Jourdan River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jourdan_River) were urged to evacuate before the water levels crested.[^cite_note-\:14-31] Double red flags were flown on beaches in [Baldwin County, Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldwin_County,_Alabama).[^cite_note-32] [Mobile County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_County,_Alabama) closed county offices.[^cite_note-\:11-33] [Bishop State Community College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishop_State_Community_College) closed all campuses, partially for [Juneteenth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juneteenth).[^cite_note-\:11-33] Officials in [Santa Rosa County, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Rosa_County,_Florida), offered free sandbag filling at four stations.[^cite_note-34]

## Impact

### Mexico

[Plan DN-III-E](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plan_DN-III-E) was activated.[^cite_note-\:3-35]

In [Veracruz](63125.md), damage was reported in 13 [municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Veracruz), including damage to 84 homes.[^cite_note-\:0-36] Dozens of landslides occurred across the state.[^cite_note-\:0-36] [Coxquihui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxquihui) had the most reported damaged homes as a result of flooding in the [Arroyo Cocinero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arroyo_Cocinero?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-\:0-36] Over 100 mm (3.9 in) of rain fell in [Xalapa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xalapa).[^cite_note-\:0-36] Damage occurred on a bridge in [Espinal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Espinal,_Veracruz).[^cite_note-\:2-37] Teams were deployed in Xalapa and [Veracruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veracruz_\(city\)) to aid with clean up efforts.[^cite_note-\:2-37]

In [Coahuila](160592.md), flooding caused road closures, including [Fed. 29](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Highway_29_\(Mexico\)) in [Morelos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morelos_Municipality,_Coahuila) and [Zaragoza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaragoza_Municipality,_Coahuila).[^cite_note-\:1-38] In some parts of the state, over five inches (130 mm) of rain fell in two hours.[^cite_note-\:1-38] There were river floods affecting [Piedras Negras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piedras_Negras,_Coahuila) and eight inches (200 mm) of rain fell.[^cite_note-\:3-35] Pumping equipment was placed in Piedras Negras to drain out floodwater.[^cite_note-\:4-39] Two meal centers and a shelter were opened for those affected by the rains.[^cite_note-\:3-35][^cite_note-\:4-39] One person died in Piedras Negras.[^cite_note-\:3-35] The [Rio Grande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_Grande) (Río Bravo) in Coahuila exceeded 3.5 m (11 ft) in some locations.[^cite_note-40]

In [Tamaulipas](222254.md), between 70% and 80% of roads in [Ciudad Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ciudad_Victoria) were damaged by heavy rains according to the mayor of the city.[^cite_note-\:10-41] Support teams were formed to clean the city after the storm.[^cite_note-\:10-41] Heavy rainfall worsened a sewage leakage in [Tampico](224093.md).[^cite_note-42]

### United States



Rainfall map with Arthur in the USA.

#### Texas



Flooding in [Brazoria County, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazoria_County,_Texas), due to Arthur.

[Flash flooding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flash_flooding) occurred across Texas due to Arthur and its precursor.[^cite_note-43] In [Central Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Texas), dozens of roads were closed to flooding.[^cite_note-\:9-44] The city of [Waco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waco,_Texas) recorded 2.72 inches (69 mm) of rain on June 14. Flooding stalled several vehicles on [I-35](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_35_in_Texas) near [Robinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robinson,_Texas) and on roadways in [Hewitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hewitt,_Texas). An RV Park had to be evacuated due to flooding while emergency responders rescued an individual who had climbed a tree to escape floodwaters near [Midway High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midway_High_School).[^cite_note-45] At least 20 people were rescued from floodwaters in Waco.[^cite_note-46] A county road in [Milam County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milam_County,_Texas) was washed out.[^cite_note-\:9-44] The [Barton Springs Pool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barton_Springs_Pool) was closed due to flooding in [Austin](1998.md).[^cite_note-47] Over 100 low-water crossings were closed across the Austin area. A water rescue occurred in South Austin as a driver attempted to navigate a blocked crossing and was swept away by floodwaters. [Camp Mabry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_Mabry) set a daily rainfall record for June 15, measuring 3 inches (76 mm) of rain within a few hours, surpassing the previous record set in 1964. Other rain gauges along the [Colorado River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_River_\(Texas\)) reported rainfall amounts up to 4.5 in (110 mm) and a floodgate at [Tom Miller Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Miller_Dam) was released. [Waymo](29156200.md) temporarily suspended operations in Austin amidst flooding.[^cite_note-48] Rainfall at [San Antonio International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Antonio_International_Airport) on June 15 reached 2.6 in (66 mm), beating the daily record of 1.68 in (43 mm) set on June 15, 1894.[^cite_note-49] Rainfall up to 6 in (150 mm) caused widespread flooding across [Kendall County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kendall_County,_Texas). Several roads were flooded across the area, and some water crossings had to be closed for several days for damage inspections. Flooding also occurred in [Kerr County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerr_County,_Texas), though less significant than [flooding that occurred along the Guadalupe River the previous year](80354641.md).[^cite_note-50]

On June 15, [Weslaco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weslaco,_Texas) received 6 in (150 mm) of rain causing floodwaters to enter homes.[^cite_note-51] Due to roads being flooded, [Sinton Independent School District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinton_Independent_School_District) halted summer school, testing, and food distribution.[^cite_note-\:6-26] At one point, over 9,000 customers reported power outages in South Texas.[^cite_note-52] Additional power outages affecting roughly 2,000 customers each occurred in [Galveston](53840.md) and in the [Texas City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_City,_Texas)—[La Marque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Marque,_Texas) areas.[^cite_note-53] Heavy rainfall affected [Brazoria County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazoria_County,_Texas), accumulating to over 8 in (200 mm) in parts of [Freeport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freeport,_Texas) and [Clute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clute,_Texas). However, the worst of the flooding was avoided due to water levels in drainage systems being lowered before the storm.[^cite_note-54] Two occupants were rescued from a vehicle that became stranded in rising floodwater in Freeport.[^cite_note-55] As a result of heavy rainfall from Arthur and previous storms, the San Jacinto River Authority released water from [Lake Conroe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Conroe) to prevent flooding.[^cite_note-56] The storm's impact bypassed much of [Houston](13774.md) but the city received several inches of rain, and portions of [I-10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_10_in_Texas) near the city closed.[^cite_note-Times-25][^cite_note-57]

One person was killed due to floodwaters in [Bandera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandera,_Texas).[^cite_note-\:7-58] Another drowned in a flooded retention pond near Houston.[^cite_note-59]

#### Louisiana



Damage to a manufactured home from an EF1 tornado in [Avondale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avondale,_Louisiana).



A rare high risk of excessive rainfall as a result of widespread significant flash flooding associated with Arthur.

Rainfall totals in [Louisiana](18130.md) peaked at 29.06 inches (738 mm) in [Cottonport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cottonport,_Louisiana), making it the most rainfall recorded in the state during a 24-hour period, surpassing the record set by a [tropical depression in 1962](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1962_Atlantic_hurricane_season#Other_systems).[^cite_note-60] The [Tchefuncte River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tchefuncte_River) overflowed, impacting surrounding buildings in [St. Tammany Parish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Tammany_Parish,_Louisiana) and leading to evacuations and water rescues.[^cite_note-61] [Plaucheville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plaucheville,_Louisiana) was left stranded due to floodwaters and water infiltrated homes.[^cite_note-62] Several water pumps and drainage pumps went offline in New Orleans during the storm from a [power dip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voltage_sag) that the [frequency converters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frequency_converters) were too sensitive to handle.[^cite_note-\:16-63][^cite_note-64] Two people were injured in the western part of [Avondale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avondale,_Louisiana) when an EF1 tornado rolled an unanchored mobile home into another mobile home. Another EF1 tornado struck the northern part of [New Orleans](53842.md), damaging trees and buildings.[^cite_note-\:16-63] Across the state, there were 14 confirmed tornadoes, including one that tracked into [Mississippi](16949861.md); seven of them were rated EF1, while the other seven were rated EF0.[^cite_note-\:17-65][^cite_note-\:19-66] Around 39,520 residents were left without power across the state.[^cite_note-67] A 12-car train derailment was reported.[^cite_note-68] Downed trees and power lines on the tracks also forced [Amtrak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amtrak) to run a bus bridge for its *[Mardi Gras Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mardi_Gras_Service)* between [New Orleans](53842.md) and [Bay St. Louis, Mississippi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay_St._Louis,_Mississippi) on June 18.[^cite_note-69][^cite_note-70] The same day, Amtrak's *[Crescent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crescent_\(train\))* was also forced to use bus transportation between New Orleans and [Birmingham, Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham,_Alabama) and the northbound *[City of New Orleans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_New_Orleans_\(train\))* was also delayed departing departing New Orleans due to downed trees and power lines.[^cite_note-71][^cite_note-72][^cite_note-73] A new power complex for drainage in New Orleans fell offline after a [voltage drop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voltage_drop).[^cite_note-74] In [Kenner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenner,_Louisiana), 50 to 70 stranded vehicles were reported. In [Slidell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slidell,_Louisiana), an [eighteen wheeler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eighteen_wheeler) flipped over.[^cite_note-75] At least 200 homes in [Avoyelles Parish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avoyelles_Parish,_Louisiana) were flooded.[^cite_note-\:14-31] Many farms were damaged as well in the parish.[^cite_note-\:22-76]

After reviewing the damage, the [governor of Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_Louisiana), [Jeff Landry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Landry), declared a state of emergency for several parishes in the state.[^cite_note-77] He also requested for a [presidential disaster declaration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stafford_Disaster_Relief_and_Emergency_Assistance_Act) for aid towards six parishes.[^cite_note-\:19-66] After [Donald Trump](4848272.md) approved the declaration on July 2, the [Small Business Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_Business_Administration) offered loans to affected regions.[^cite_note-78][^cite_note-79] Those receiving aid from the [Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supplemental_Nutrition_Assistance_Program) were temporarily permitted to use aid for hot and prepared food.[^cite_note-80] The head of [Pointe Coupee Parish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pointe_Coupee_Parish,_Louisiana)'s homeland security claimed that the extended time to approve the declaration was difficult for its citizens.[^cite_note-81] The Sewage and Water Board of New Orleans modernized one of their power stations after Arthur.[^cite_note-82] The [Department of Agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Agriculture) made available funds for farms impacted by Arthur.[^cite_note-\:22-76] The [Food and Drug Administration](11632.md) issued warnings to avoid eating foods exposed to flood waters spurred by the storm.[^cite_note-83] Many evacuees from the storm were put into several hotels for months, moving between hotels until they could return home.[^cite_note-84]

#### Elsewhere



[SPC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storm_Prediction_Center) Storm Reports (2026-06-18 1200 UTC – 2026-06-19 1159 UTC) showing damage by wind (blue), large hail (green) and tornado (red).&#x20;

On [I-59 in Mississippi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_59_in_Mississippi), rain from the predecessor to Arthur left the highway submerged in over one foot (0.30 m) of water.[^cite_note-85] Many other major roads were closed in [Pearl River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pearl_River_County,_Mississippi) and [Hancock Counties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hancock_County,_Mississippi).[^cite_note-\:13-86] A road worker died working on clean up efforts after Arthur in [Franklin County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_County,_Mississippi).[^cite_note-\:13-86] A dam on [Anchor Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anchor_Lake_\(Mississippi\)) presented a threat of failing, prompting the evacuation of 30 homes.[^cite_note-87] Officials drained the lake to reduce strain on the dam's systems.[^cite_note-88] The [Jourdan River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jourdan_River) overflowed its banks near [Kiln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiln,_Mississippi), went into homes, and blocked [MS 603](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippi_Highway_603).[^cite_note-89] The [Biloxi River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biloxi_River) rose 30 feet (9.1 m) above typical levels.[^cite_note-90] Approximately 38 people were rescued from floodwaters.[^cite_note-\:14-31] The [governor of the state](233842.md), [Tate Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tate_Reeves), declared a state of emergency for impacted areas.[^cite_note-91] Later, Reeves would request a presidential disaster declaration for eight counties in his state.[^cite_note-\:21-92] Four EF1 tornadoes were confirmed across the state; an additional EF1 tornado tracked into the state out of [Louisiana](18130.md).[^cite_note-\:17-65] More than 500 homes reported damage in Mississippi, the majority in [Harrison County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harrison_County,_Mississippi).[^cite_note-\:21-92][^cite_note-93] Further, seventeen businesses and five farms also reported damage.[^cite_note-\:21-92]

[SR 198](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alabama_State_Route_198) partially closed to flash flooding.[^cite_note-94] Over 20,000 customers lost power in [Alabama](303.md), primarily in Mobile County.[^cite_note-\:12-95] Five weak tornadoes were confirmed in the state.[^cite_note-0618MOBsum-96][^cite_note-97]

Around 100 customers in [Escambia County, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escambia_County,_Florida), lost power.[^cite_note-\:12-95] Based on radar measurements, 4–8 inches (100–200 mm) fell across the [Florida Panhandle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_panhandle).[^cite_note-\:18-98] This rainfall partially alleviated drought conditions in the region.[^cite_note-\:18-98]

Over 4,800 people lost power due to the storms in [Georgia](48830.md), and several fan festivals for the [2026 FIFA World Cup](36581929.md) were also cancelled.[^cite_note-\:15-99] A ground stop was also issued for [Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartsfield-Jackson_Atlanta_International_Airport).[^cite_note-\:15-99] Ten weak tornadoes were confirmed in [Central Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Georgia).[^cite_note-100]

Over 2,000 people lost power in [Brunswick County, North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brunswick_County,_North_Carolina).[^cite_note-101]
