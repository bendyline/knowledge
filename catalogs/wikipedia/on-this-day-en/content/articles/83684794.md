The **2026 Washington Open** (branded as the **Mubadala DC Open** for sponsorship reasons) was a [tennis](29773.md) tournament played on outdoor [hard courts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hard_court). It was the 57th edition of the [Washington Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_Open_\(tennis\)) for the men, and the 14th edition for the women. The event was an [ATP 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_500_tournaments) tournament on the [2026 ATP Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_ATP_Tour) and a [WTA 500](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTA_500_tournaments) tournament on the [2026 WTA Tour](81301779.md). The tournament took place at the [William H.G. FitzGerald Tennis Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_H.G._FitzGerald_Tennis_Center) in Washington, D.C. from July 27 to August 3, 2026.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] The men's draw was reduced from 48 players to 32 players this year.
