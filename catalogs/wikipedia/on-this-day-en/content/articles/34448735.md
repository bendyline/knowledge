**Uror County** is a [county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_South_Sudan) of [Jonglei state](767655.md) in the [Greater Upper Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Upper_Nile) region of [South Sudan](32350676.md). It has nine [payams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Payam_\(administrative_division\)): [Pathai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pathai), Pieri, [Pulchuol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulchuol), Palouny, Motdit, [Motot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motot), Karam, Pajut, Weykol and Padiek.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4]
