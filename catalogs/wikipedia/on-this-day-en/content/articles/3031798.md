A boulder-strewn slope at Cholpon-Ata has numerous examples of [petroglyphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petroglyph), some showing people hunting animals which are now extinct or scarce in the area

**Cholpon-Ata**[^cite_note-1] is a [resort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resort) town on the northern shore of [Lake Issyk-Kul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Issyk-Kul) in [Kyrgyzstan](170131.md) with a population of 14,237 (2021).[^cite_note-pop21-2] It is the administrative center of the [Issyk-Kul District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Issyk-Kul_District) of [Issyk-Kul Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Issyk-Kul_Region); this district occupies most of the lake's north shore. To the west along [highway](48519.md) A363 is [Tamchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamchy) and to the east is [Bosteri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosteri).

The town contains numerous large and small [sanatoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanatorium), hotels and guesthouses to accommodate the many visitors who descend upon the [lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake) in summer. During the [Soviet era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) it was much frequented by vacationers brought here in organized mass tours from other parts of the [USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union). Holidaymakers now usually visit on their own or in small groups and originate mainly from [Kyrgyzstan](170131.md), [Kazakhstan](16642.md), [Uzbekistan](31853.md) and [Russia](25391.md) with some tourists from other countries. Attracting more demanding foreign tourists will require upgrading of the existing facilities, but the location is certainly attractive. The view of the imposing alpine ranges of the [Tian Shan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tian_Shan) across the [lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake) is impressive. There is a local [museum](37585.md) and an open-air site with about 2000 [petroglyphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petroglyphs) dating from 800 BC to 1200AD.[^cite_note-3]

The first three [World Nomad Games](50350427.md) were held in Cholpon-Ata, in 2014, 2016, and 2018.[^cite_note-4]
