The **Spanish Socialist Workers' Party of Extremadura** (Spanish: *Partido Socialista Obrero Español de Extremadura*, PSOE–E) is the regional branch in [Extremadura](68204.md) of the [Spanish Socialist Workers' Party](272596.md) (PSOE), main centre-left party in Spain since the 1970s.
