# Bosnia and Herzegovina at the FIFA World Cup

The [Bosnia and Herzegovina national football team](1008974.md) has qualified for the [FIFA World Cup](11370.md) on two occasions, their first in [2014](656933.md) and second in [2026](36581929.md).[^cite_note-Bosnians_make_history-1][^cite_note-Ibišević_sparks_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina_joy-2]

## Bosnian players for Yugoslavia



[Bosnian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Republic_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina) [Josip Katalinski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josip_Katalinski) celebrates a [Yugoslav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yugoslavia_national_football_team) goal vs [Zaire](1030243.md) (present day DR Congo) at the [1974 FIFA World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1974_FIFA_World_Cup).

[Yugoslavia men's national football team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yugoslavia_men's_national_football_team) participated in the World Cup eight times between 1930 and 1990. While in the early editions they were mainly represented by Serbian players, the squads became all-inclusive after the World War II, from 1945 until break-up.

Several Bosnian players were part of the [1974](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1974_FIFA_World_Cup) first team line-up that advanced from the group stage unbeaten and ahead of [Brazil](149286.md). Striker [Dušan Bajević](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Du%C5%A1an_Bajevi%C4%87) scored three goals in one of the highest World Cup victories of all time: Yugoslavia's 9–0 against [Zaire](1030243.md). Defender [Josip Katalinski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josip_Katalinski) also scored during this game. Other Bosnians from that years' team include goalkeeper [Enver Marić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enver_Mari%C4%87) and defender [Enver Hadžiabdić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enver_Had%C5%BEiabdi%C4%87).

Playmaker [Safet Sušić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safet_Su%C5%A1i%C4%87), widely regarded finest player of his generation, and considered to be the best Bosnian player of all time, represented Yugoslavia at the [World Cup in 1982](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Cup_1982), together with other Bosnian players such as [Pašić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Predrag_Pa%C5%A1i%C4%87), [Šljivo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edhem_%C5%A0ljivo), [Halilhodžić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vahid_Halilhod%C5%BEi%C4%87) and [Vujović](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zlatko_Vujovi%C4%87) brothers. Safet Sušić played crucial playmaking role again in Italy in [1990 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_World_Cup), when the team was managed by Bosnian coach [Ivica Osim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivica_Osim), and often fielded many Bosnian players, such as [Jozić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davor_Jozi%C4%87), [Hadžibegić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faruk_Had%C5%BEibegi%C4%87), [Baždarević](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehmed_Ba%C5%BEdarevi%C4%87), Vujović, [Baljić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirsad_Balji%C4%87), [Šabanadžović](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refik_%C5%A0abanad%C5%BEovi%C4%87), [Omerović](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fahrudin_Omerovi%C4%87). This Yugoslav team reached the quarter-finals in 1990 and was captained by [Zlatko Vujović](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zlatko_Vujovi%C4%87), while Sušić put some of the best performances of the tournament and was one of the highest rated Yugoslav players, along with his compatriot Davor Jozić, Serbian [Dragan Stojković](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragan_Stojkovi%C4%87), and Croatian goalkeeper [Tomislav Ivković](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomislav_Ivkovi%C4%87).

## Overall record



Bosnia and Herzegovina [2014 FIFA World Cup](656933.md) match at [Estádio do Maracanã](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Est%C3%A1dio_do_Maracan%C3%A3) against [Argentina](454699.md).

After Bosnia and Herzegovina [gained independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_Day_\(Bosnia_and_Herzegovina\)) from [Yugoslavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Federal_Republic_of_Yugoslavia) on 1 March 1992, the national football team was soon formed but could not enter qualifying for the [1994 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994_FIFA_World_Cup) as the national association was not yet a member of [FIFA](11049.md). During qualifiers for the [1998 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_FIFA_World_Cup), Bosnia's first home match against Croatia was played in [Bologna](21069333.md).[^cite_note-3] The match was held at the neutral venue due to the renovation of the Asim Ferhatović Hase Stadium in [Sarajevo](26786.md).

Bosnia finished in third place during the [2006 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification_%E2%80%93_UEFA_Group_7) qualifiers, recording two draws with Spain along the way. During qualifiers for the [2010 World Cup](168079.md) Bosnia reached its first ever [playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification_%E2%80%93_UEFA_second_round) for a major tournament, where they eventually lost 2–0 on aggregate to Portugal.

The [2014 World Cup](656933.md) was the first time Bosnia appeared at a major tournament as an independent nation, having qualified by topping [UEFA Group G](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014_FIFA_World_Cup_qualification_%E2%80%93_UEFA_Group_G).

## Historical performances

The Bosnia-Herzegovina team records during its participation in the FIFA World Cup.

- **2014**: On 15 June 2014, [Vedad Ibišević](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vedad_Ibi%C5%A1evi%C4%87) made history by scoring Bosnia and Herzegovina's first-ever FIFA World Cup goal, coming on as substitute in their opening match defeat vs [Argentina](454699.md) in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maracan%C3%A3_Stadium).

- **2014**: Kolašinac's own goal (vs [Argentina](454699.md)) after two minutes and nine seconds broke the [record for the fastest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_FIFA_World_Cup_own_goals) [own goal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Own_goal) in the history of the FIFA World Cup, surpassing [Carlos Gamarra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Gamarra)'s own goal (two minutes and 46 seconds) in [Paraguay's](804025.md) [first group stage match](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup_Group_B#England_vs_Paraguay) against [England](9904.md) at the [2006 FIFA World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_FIFA_World_Cup).

  [^cite_note-12]

* **2014**: [Edin Džeko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edin_D%C5%BEeko) goal vs [Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigeria_national_football_team) was ruled out for offside, which replays later showed to be an incorrect decision.

  [^cite_note-13]

  &#x20;Referee Peter O'Leary later admitted Džeko's goal was disallowed in error.

  [^cite_note-14]

- **2014**: On 25 June 2014, a historic first victory in FIFA World Cup was recorded vs [Iran](272865.md).

* **2026**: Second European team to play 2 host nations ([Canada](723688.md) and the [US](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USA_men's_national_soccer_team)) at single edition since [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey_men's_national_football_team) in [2002 FIFA World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_FIFA_World_Cup) and third overall, the other being [South Africa](1018598.md) also in the 2026 edition.

- **2026**: [Edin Džeko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edin_D%C5%BEeko) is a first player **to start** a knock-outs match over age 40 (followed by [ Luka Modrić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luka_Modri%C4%87) and [Cristiano Ronaldo](623737.md) the following day; [Vozinha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vozinha) two days later).

* **2026**: [Kerim Alajbegović](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerim_Alajbegovi%C4%87) at 18 years and 276 days old is 8th youngest goalscorer in World Cup tournament history, right behind legends like [Pelé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pel%C3%A9). Kerim's strike outside the box broke the previous record held by [Kylian Mbappe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylian_Mbappe) (19 years and 207 days).

- **2026**: On June 24 2026, Bosnia and Herzegovina victory over [Qatar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatar_men's_national_football_team) ensured a historic knockout berth as one of the [eight best third-place teams](36581929.md#Ranking_of_third-place_teams).

* **2026**: On July 1, 2026 in a [round of 32](81667041.md) game between Bosnia and the US the match referee [Raphael Claus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raphael_Claus) issued a controversial red card following a VAR review to Balogun, which many including [Tarik Muharemović](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarik_Muharemovi%C4%87) (the player he had been involved in the initial incident with) considered excessive or illegitimate.

  [^cite_note-16]

  [^cite_note-17]

  [^cite_note-18]

  &#x20;Several experts compared the incident to that of [Lionel Messi](2150841.md) in the group stage game between Argentina vs Algeria, where he did not get a red card for the same offense on [Aïssa Mandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A%C3%AFssa_Mandi), with some speculating that his status played a role in the decision.

  [^cite_note-19]

  [^cite_note-20]

  [^cite_note-21]

  &#x20;The automatic 1 game suspension for receiving a red card was overturned by FIFA following a call from United States President [Donald Trump](4848272.md) to FIFA President Gianni Infantino, prompting accusations of political manipulation from [UEFA](32332.md)

  [^cite_note-22]

  &#x20;and others in the football world, including former FIFA President [Sepp Blatter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sepp_Blatter).

  [^cite_note-23]

  &#x20;As a result, Balogun would play in the United States vs Belgium match on 6 July 2026 in Seattle. The US team lost the match vs Belgium 1-4.
