# Kyrgyz

Wiktionary logo

Look up ***[Kyrgyz](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Kyrgyz)*** in Wiktionary, the free dictionary.

**Kyrgyz**, **Kirghiz**, or **Kirgiz** may refer to:

- Someone or something related to [Kyrgyzstan](170131.md) or Kyrgyz people

- [Kyrgyz people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyrgyz_people), Turkic ethnic group in Central Asia

- [Kyrgyz language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyrgyz_language), Turkic language of the Kipchak branch

- The [Fuyu Kyrgyz language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuyu_Kyrgyz_language) in [Northeastern China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeastern_China)

- [Yenisei Kyrgyz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yenisei_Kyrgyz), Ancient Turkic people

- An obsolete term used in Russian Empire to refer to some other Central Asian peoples: [Kazakhs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazakhs) ("Kirgiz-Kaysaks"), [Karakalpaks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karakalpaks) ("Kirgiz-Karakalpaks")
