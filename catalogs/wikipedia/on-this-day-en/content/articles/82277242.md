The men's [giant slalom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_slalom) competition of the [2026 Winter Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpine_skiing_at_the_2026_Winter_Olympics) was held on Saturday, 14 February, at [Stelvio Ski Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stelvio_\(ski_course\)) in [Bormio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bormio).[^cite_note-schedule-1] [Lucas Pinheiro Braathen](60184111.md) of Brazil won the event. This was the first medal for Brazil at the Winter Olympics, and also the first medal for a [tropical nation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_nations_at_the_Winter_Olympics) and [South America](26769.md). [Marco Odermatt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marco_Odermatt) of Switzerland, the defending champion, won the silver medal and teammate [Loïc Meillard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lo%C3%AFc_Meillard) took bronze.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3]

## Background

The [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpine_skiing_at_the_2022_Winter_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men's_giant_slalom) champion, [Marco Odermatt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marco_Odermatt), qualified for the event, as did the silver medalist, [Žan Kranjec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%BDan_Kranjec). Bronze medalist [Mathieu Faivre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathieu_Faivre) had retired from competitions.[^cite_note-4] Prior to the Olympics on the [World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%9326_FIS_Alpine_Ski_World_Cup) circuit, Odermatt led in the [overall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Alpine_Skiing_World_Cup_%E2%80%93_Men's_overall) and [giant slalom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Alpine_Skiing_World_Cup_%E2%80%93_Men's_giant_slalom) standings, having won three of seven events. [Raphael Haaser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raphael_Haaser) was the reigning [world champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIS_Alpine_World_Ski_Championships_2025_%E2%80%93_Men's_giant_slalom).

## Results

The race was started at 10\:00 (first run) and 13\:30 (second run) [local time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_European_Time) ([UTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinated_Universal_Time)+1), both under overcast skies. The first run's snow condition was icy; the air temperature was 0.4 °C (33 °F) at the starting gate and 0.7 °C (33 °F) at the finish area. The second run's snow condition was wet; the air temperature was 1.5 °C (35 °F) at the starting gate and 4.5 °C (40 °F) at the finish area.
