**Bandar Mahshahr County** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): شهرستان بندر ماهشهر) is in [Khuzestan](448079.md) province, Iran. Its capital is the city of [Bandar-e Mahshahr](1851356.md).[^cite_note-Khuzestan_Province_Structure-3]

## Demographics

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the county's population was 247,804 in 53,347 households.[^cite_note-2006_Khuzestan_Province-4] The following census in 2011 counted 278,037 people in 69,488 households.[^cite_note-2011_Khuzestan_Province-5] The 2016 census measured the population of the county as 296,271 in 81,211 households.[^cite_note-2016_Khuzestan_Province-2]
