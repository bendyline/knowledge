The **National Assembly** ([Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language): *Assembleia Nacional*) is the [unicameral](204304.md) legislative body of the [Democratic Republic of São Tomé and Príncipe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Republic_of_S%C3%A3o_Tom%C3%A9_and_Pr%C3%ADncipe).

The Assembly is elected for a four-year term. The Assembly can be dissolved by the President of the Republic in case of a *serious institutional crisis*,[^cite_note-1] only with the approval of the Council of State, a consultative assembly composed of 12 members.[^cite_note-2]

The National Assembly was founded in 1975, as a result of the adoption of the [Constitution of São Tomé and Príncipe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_S%C3%A3o_Tom%C3%A9_and_Pr%C3%ADncipe). From 1975 to 1990, the Assembly served as a [highest organ of state power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_state_organ_of_power) led by the [Movement for the Liberation of São Tomé and Príncipe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Movement_for_the_Liberation_of_S%C3%A3o_Tom%C3%A9_and_Pr%C3%ADncipe) where, per the principle of [unified power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unified_power), all state organs had been subordinated to it. With the [constitutional reforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_S%C3%A3o_Tom%C3%A9an_constitutional_referendum) of 1990, the country became a multi-party democracy and the first competitive elections were organised in [1991](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_S%C3%A3o_Tom%C3%A9an_legislative_election).
