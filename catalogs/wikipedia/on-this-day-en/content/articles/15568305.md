The **Dominican Republic women's national volleyball team** had its biggest recent win in 2023, when the team beat out the USA in 5 sets at the [2023 NORCECA Women’s Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_NORCECA_Volleyball_Championship) in Quebec. The team is ranked 11th in the FIVB world ranking as of 8 January 2025.
