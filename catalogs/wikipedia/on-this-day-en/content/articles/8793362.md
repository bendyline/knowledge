[Cambridge Botanic Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Botanic_Garden) Weather Station where a temperature of 38.7 °C (101.7 °F) was recorded in the [2019 European heat wave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_European_heat_waves)

The **United Kingdom weather records** show the most [extreme weather](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extreme_weather) ever recorded in the [United Kingdom](31717.md), such as temperature, wind speed, and rainfall records. Reliable temperature records for the whole of the United Kingdom go back to about 1880.[^cite_note-\:0-1]

## Records

Daily records unless otherwise specified are from 0900-2100 UTC. As of 13 August 2026, the records, as recorded by the [Met Office](319671.md), are:[^cite_note-met-2]

| High level (above 500 m) | 173 | 278 | 150 | [Cairn Gorm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cairn_Gorm), [Scottish Highlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Highlands) | 20 March 1986 |

[Shetland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shetland) holds the unofficial British record for wind speed. A gust of 197 mph (317 km/h) was reported on [1 January 1992](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992_New_Year's_Day_Storm). An earlier gust in [1962](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Sheffield_Gale) was recorded at 177 mph (285 km/h), both at [RAF Saxa Vord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAF_Saxa_Vord).[^cite_note-18] However, it is expected that higher gusts than those reported would have been achieved as during both storms the measuring equipment was destroyed by the extreme weather.[^cite_note-19]

A wind gust of 194 mph (312 km/h) was recorded at [Cairn Gorm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cairn_Gorm) on 19 December 2008 but was discovered too late to be verified by the [Met Office](319671.md).[^cite_note-20]

The highest wind speed in England was a wind gust of 125.4 mph (201.8 km/h) recorded at [The Needles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Needles), [Isle of Wight](15102.md), on 18 February 2022 during [Storm Eunice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storm_Eunice).[^cite_note-21]
