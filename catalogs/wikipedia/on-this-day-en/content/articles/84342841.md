The **2026 Berlin Marathon** was the 52nd edition of the annual [marathon race in Berlin](1663911.md), held on Sunday, September 27, 2026. A [Platinum Label](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Athletics_Label_Road_Races) marathon, it was one of the six [World Marathon Majors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Marathon_Majors) events scheduled for 2026.

Ethiopian runner [Tigst Assefa](51512379.md) won the women's race running the 3rd all-time best marathon time and a course record of 2\:11\:04 despite acquiring an injury which forced her to adopt a visible limp in the final two kilometers.[^cite_note-1] In the men's race, the 2021 Berlin marathon champion [Guye Adola](44166759.md) ran a personal best to capture his second title in the event.[^cite_note-2] Swiss wheelchair athletes [Marcel Hug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcel_Hug) and [Catherine Debrunner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_Debrunner) won their respective races.

## Competitors

Defending champion [Sabastian Sawe](73093597.md) was forced to withdraw from the event due to injury a month out from the race.[^cite_note-3] A few days prior, former champion [Kenenisa Bekele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenenisa_Bekele) was also forced to withdraw due to an Achilles injury.[^cite_note-4] In the women's race, [Esther Pfeiffer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esther_Pfeiffer?action=edit\&redlink=1) was aiming to break the German women's national record set by [Irina Mikitenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irina_Mikitenko) in 2008 but was forced to withdraw mid-race due to physical conditions.[^cite_note-5]
