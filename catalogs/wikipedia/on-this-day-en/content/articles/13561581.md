The **Mali women's national basketball team** ([French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language): *Équipe nationale féminine de basketball du Mali*) is the nationally controlled [basketball](3921.md) team representing [Mali](19127.md) at world basketball competitions for women. The Malian squad had one [continental championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA_Africa_Championship_for_Women), which came in 2007 with a win over host [Senegal](13537015.md).

## History

The Malian squad won a medal at only two [continental tournaments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA_Africa_Championship_for_Women) with a bronze medal at the [1968 games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA_Africa_Championship_for_Women_1968?action=edit\&redlink=1) and a gold at the [2007 games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA_Africa_Championship_for_Women_2007). The squad qualified for its first Olympic appearance with a win at the 2007 African championship. At the [2010 FIBA World Championship for Women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_FIBA_World_Championship_for_Women) they finished in 15th place.

### FIBA Africa Championship for Women 2007

Mali traveled to Senegal for the [FIBA Africa Championship for Women 2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIBA_Africa_Championship_for_Women_2007) qualifying tournament for the [2008 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_at_the_2008_Summer_Olympics) in Beijing. The squad won the first round with a 4–1 record, losing only to host Senegal. Mali also had the highest positive point differential of any squad in the tournament. In the ensuing rounds, Mali beat [Cameroon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameroon_women's_national_basketball_team), followed by [Angola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angola_women's_national_basketball_team). In the championship match, Mali beat Senegal 63–56, qualifying automatically for the 2008 Olympics for the first time. Captain [Hamchétou Maïga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamch%C3%A9tou_Ma%C3%AFga) was voted the MVP of the tournament, while teammate [Diéné Diawara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Di%C3%A9n%C3%A9_Diawara) grabbed the most rebounds.
