The **Municipal Chamber of Rio de Janeiro** ([Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language): *Câmara Municipal do Rio de Janeiro*) is the [unicameral](204304.md) legislative body of the city of [Rio de Janeiro](25936.md). It was founded in 1565 by the Portuguese colonists.
