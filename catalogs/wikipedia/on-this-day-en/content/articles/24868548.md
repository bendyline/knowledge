**Arewa Dandi** is a town and [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Areas_of_Nigeria) in [Kebbi State](2227643.md), [Nigeria](21383.md), sharing a boundary with the [Republic of Niger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Niger).

It has an area of 3,901 km<sup>2</sup> and a population of 184,030 at the 2006 census.

The [postal code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_code) of the area is 861.[^cite_note-1]

## Geography, Climate, Temperature

Arewa Dadin Local Government Area (LGA) encompasses a total expanse of 3,901 [square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square) [kilometers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilometre) or 1,506 [square miles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_miles) and experiences two well-defined seasons: the [rainy](19009110.md) season and the dry season. The dry season, in particular, is marked by scorching temperatures, with an average temperature of approximately 34 [degrees Celsius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celsius) or 93 [degrees Fahrenheit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Degrees_Fahrenheit). The area maintains an average [humidity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humidity) level of around 24 percent. Its terrain is predominantly composed of dry and arid [plains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plain), accentuated by scattered hills and other elevated features.[^cite_note-\:0-2]

Arewa Dandi has a semi-arid Sudan–Sahel climate with hot temperatures and low annual rainfall. The rainy season occurs from June to September, while the rest of the year is dominated by dry Harmattan winds, which reduce humidity and visibility.[^cite_note-3]

## Economy

Agriculture stands as the predominant economic activity in Arewa Dadin Local Government Area, with a reputation for [cultivating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tillage) various crops, including [grains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grain) and [onions](51258.md). [Cattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cattle) rearing also holds a prominent place in the local economy, particularly among the Fulani community residing in the area.[^cite_note-\:0-2]
