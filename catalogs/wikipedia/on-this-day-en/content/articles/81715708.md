[Elena Rybakina](55541443.md) defeated [Aryna Sabalenka](50222577.md) in the final, 6–4, 4–6, 6–4 to win the women's singles tennis title at the [2026 Australian Open](81711108.md). It was her second [major](197638.md#Tournaments) title.[^cite_note-1] Rybakina was the fourth woman (after [Jennifer Capriati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Capriati), [Serena Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serena_Williams), and [Madison Keys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madison_Keys)) since the establishment of the [WTA rankings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTA_rankings) to defeat both the world No. 1 and No. 2 players en route to the [Australian Open](97851.md) title.[^cite_note-2] The final was a rematch of the [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles) final, won by Sabalenka.[^cite_note-3]

Keys was the defending champion,[^cite_note-4] but lost in the fourth round to [Jessica Pegula](32944701.md).[^cite_note-5]

Sabalenka was the first woman to reach four consecutive Australian Open women's singles finals since [Martina Hingis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martina_Hingis) in [2000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles). Sabalenka and Rybakina both reached the final without losing a set en route, the first time both finalists at a major did so since Serena and [Venus Williams](167378.md) at the [2008 Wimbledon Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles).

Aged 45 years and 7 months old, Venus Williams became the oldest woman to compete in the singles main draw at the Australian Open, surpassing the previous record set by [Kimiko Date](495296.md) in [2015](44445186.md).[^cite_note-6] The first round match between [Karolína Plíšková](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karol%C3%ADna_Pl%C3%AD%C5%A1kov%C3%A1) and [Sloane Stephens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloane_Stephens) was the first major main draw match contested between two players ranked outside the top 1,000 since the current [WTA Tour](1398173.md) format was established in 1990.[^cite_note-7]

[Zeynep Sönmez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeynep_S%C3%B6nmez) became the first Turkish woman to record a win at the event[^cite_note-8] and the first Turkish player to advance to the third round.[^cite_note-9] This was the first Australian Open to have four Americans in the quarterfinals since [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Australian_Open_%E2%80%93_Women's_singles).[^cite_note-10] For the first time in the Open Era, the top six seeds reached the quarterfinals in both the women's and [men's](81715568.md) singles competitions.[^cite_note-11]
