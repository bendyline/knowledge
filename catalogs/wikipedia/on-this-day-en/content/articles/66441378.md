**Sonexay Siphandone** (born 26 January 1966) is a Laotian politician who has served as the [prime minister of Laos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_minister_of_Laos) since 30 December 2022. A member of the [Lao People's Revolutionary Party](203651.md) (LPRP), he previously served as the [deputy prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Prime_Minister_of_Laos) from 2016 to 2022. He is the son of former LPRP Chairman and former president [Khamtai Siphandone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khamtai_Siphandone).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTECreakSayalath2017182-1]

## Biography

Sonexay Siphandone was born on 26 January 1966 in [Huaphanh Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houaphanh_province). He is the son of [Khamtai Siphandone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khamtai_Siphandone), former [President of Laos](2135738.md) between 1998 and 2006.[^cite_note-\:0-2] Sonexay Siphandone holds a B.A. degree at a military academy in Laos and studied political strategy. He served in the armoured forces of the [Lao People's Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lao_People's_Army) and became governor of [Champasack province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champasack_province) in 1987.[^cite_note-\:0-2][^cite_note-\:1-3]

Sonexay Siphandone has held several senior posts in the Lao government and party. He previously served as head of the Prime Minister's Office, minister of planning and investment, and later as deputy prime minister while concurrently holding the planning and investment portfolio. In 2022, he was appointed deputy prime minister responsible for economic affairs.[^cite_note-\:0-2][^cite_note-\:1-3]

On 30 December 2022, the Laotian parliament approved Sonexay Siphandone as [Prime Minister](3971499.md) (149 of 151 votes).[^cite_note-4]
