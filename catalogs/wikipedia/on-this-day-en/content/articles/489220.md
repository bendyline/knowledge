**Artibonite** (French pronunciation: [\[aʁtibɔnit\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/French); [Haitian Creole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haitian_Creole_language): *Latibonit*) is one of the ten [departments of Haiti](10937444.md) located in central Haiti. With an area of 4,887 km<sup>2</sup> it is Haiti's largest department. As of 2015, its estimated population was 1,727,524.[^cite_note-\:0-3] The region is the country's main rice-growing area. The main cities are [Gonaïves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gona%C3%AFves) (the capital) and [Saint-Marc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Marc). In February 2004 an [insurgency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Revolutionary_Front_for_the_Liberation_of_Haiti) tried unsuccessfully to declare Artibonite's independence.

## Etymology

The name L'Artibonite is derived from the [Artibonite River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artibonite_River), the longest river on [Hispaniola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispaniola). L'Artibonite is derived from the Taino *ja'tibonicu* meaning "Great High Place of the [Sacred Waters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacred_waters)." Under the administration of [Toussaint Louverture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toussaint_Louverture), the department was known as Toussaint's Department.

## History

### Taino period

During the pre-colonial period, the area of the present-day department was divided among the Taíno [cacicazgos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cacicazgo) of Marien, Maguana, and [Jaragua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaragua). The border between these chiefdoms is generally believed to have followed the [Artibonite River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artibonite_River). Although the department's capital, Gonayibo, is thought to have belonged to the Xaragua chiefdom, it was located near the border between the three territories.&#x20;

Haitian oral tradition recounts that the Taíno queen traveled annually from her principal city of [Yaguana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yaguana) to Gonayibo, accompanied by her nitainos (nobles), a journey that is said to have inspired the origins of Haitian Rara traditions.

Some of the best-known Taíno settlements and archaeological sites in the department are [Gonaibo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gona%C3%AFves), [Amani-y](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Marc), the caves of [Dondon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dondon) and archeological sites in the Cote-des-Arcadins area.

### French period

Under the French administration the towns of St-Marc, [L'Éstère](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L'Est%C3%A8re). [Mirebalais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirebalais) and [Petite-Rivières-de-l'Artibonite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petite_Rivi%C3%A8re_de_l'Artibonite) were important French settlements with multiple plantations in the [Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artibonite_Valley). During that period the town of St-Marc was under the control of the colonialists and was the seat of [Assemblée Colonial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Domingue) or the Colonial Assembly.

### Haitian Revolution

At the beginning of the [Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haitian_Revolution), St-Marc was burned by [Dessalines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Jacques_Dessalines) and Gonaives by Vernet under the order of [Toussaint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toussaint_Louverture) in response to [Napoléon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Napoleon)'s tyrannic behaviors. Later on, Toussaint defeated the French troops at the Bataille-de-la-Ravine-à-Couleuvre near the town of Gonaives. In Gonaives the [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Domingue_expedition) captured Toussaint and he was later sent to France and died.

In April 1802 during the siege of [Crête-à-Pierrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Cr%C3%AAte-%C3%A0-Pierrot) that lasted almost one month, the indigenous troops offer the French generals the greatest resistance. During that battle, Dessalines threatened to set fire to the gunpowder reserve in case the French would enter the fort. The Haitian hero [Marie-Jeanne Lamartinière](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie-Jeanne_Lamartini%C3%A8re) surprised the French troops with her courageous behavior.

### Haitian period

Gabart commander of St-Marc, Vernet commander of Gonaives, and Dessalines commander of the Indigenous Army are signatories of the [Haitian Declaration of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haitian_Declaration_of_Independence). Gonayiv is the first city to be gained from the French troops by the Indigenous Army, and Gabart freed St-Marc on September 4, 1803.

On January 1, 1804, the city of Gonaives hold the first Haitian political conference and witness the [Declaration of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haitian_Declaration_of_Independence) under the eyes of Dessalines, Henry, Pétion, Geffrard, Boirond Tonnerre, some British troops and many more. The town of [Dessalines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dessalines) became the world's first black capital and the seat of the [Haitian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Empire_of_Haiti). Many times, the department has dissociated itself from [Port-au-Prince](57391.md) and the West many times notably under the leadership of [Henry Christophe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri_Christophe), [Nissage Saget](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Nicolas_Nissage_Saget), receiving [Fabre Geffrard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fabre_Geffrard) against [Faustin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faustin_Soulouque). In the contemporary period, Gonaives rose furiously in 1986 and 2004 overthrowing both [Duvalier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duvalier_dynasty) and [Aristide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Bertrand_Aristide).

## Geography

The department is bordered to the north by [North](492813.md) and [North-West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nord-Ouest_\(department\)), on the west the [Gulf of Gonave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_of_Gon%C3%A2ve), the south [West](492791.md) and the east and [Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_\(department\)). A core geographic feature of this department is the [Artibonite River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artibonite_River), which runs through the southern half of the department, allowing for rice cultivation in the [Artibonite Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artibonite_Valley). The department has two [mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography_of_Haiti) ranges running from NW to SE. On the North shore of the Valley is Black Mountain and on the south shore is Mattheux Mountain. The Bay of Gonaïves is one of the best-protected bays in the country. The Bay of St-Marc is the biggest U-shaped bay in the country. The department doesn't have any major fault line through it.

## Environment

One of Haiti's biggest [mangrove](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gQwZUxYvTiw) forests, harboring flamingos and other rare species, is located south of Gonaïves. There are also sand dunes in that area. There are many lagoons and rivers that run through the Valley. The largest lake in Artibonite is Bois-Neuf Lake south of St-Marc.

## Economy

The department has been in a state of economic decline since insurgencies and coups have reduced the nation's stability. Since the election of President [René Garcia Préval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_Garcia_Pr%C3%A9val), the production of rice and other crops has been increasing.

### Tourism

From St-Marc to [Montrouis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montrouis) the coastal area is known for its breathtaking beaches and potential for resort tourism. Some of the most notable beaches are Amani-y, Grosse-Roche, Decameron, Moulin-sur-Mer and more.

Cultural tourism focusing on Haitian history, forts, and [Vodoun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haitian_Vodou).

### Agriculture and Fishing

The Valley is known as Haiti's food basket. Until the 1980s Haiti was able to be self-sufficient. Many families have farm animals such as pigs, goats, beef, chicken, and more.

### Mining

Gonaïves is a point of interest for mining notably in the disaffected mines of [Sedren](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zqm8tFl8MrA).

### Salt Production

Grande-Saline and Anse-Rouge are known for their Haitian Sea Salt production.

### Energy

Haiti's biggest [electrical dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P%C3%A9ligre_Dam) is also used to control the flow of the Artibonite.

## Transport

The main road through the department is the RN1 connecting [Okap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cap-Ha%C3%AFtien) to [Pòtoprens](57391.md), passing through [Gonayiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gona%C3%AFves) and [Sen-Mak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Marc). This road often causes congestion in Sen-Mak since it goes through the city. The RN5 connects Gonayiv to [Gwomòn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gros-Morne,_Artibonite). The RD103 and RD304 connect Gonayiv to [Ench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinche) and the Haitian-Dominican border. The RD101 and RD301 connect Sen-Mak to [Mibalè](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirebalais) and the Haitian-Dominican border.

Both St-Marc and Gonaïves are port towns harboring ships.

The Artibonite river is navigable by small rafts called *kannòt* and even jet skies

The department has no international airport although Gonaïves has a small airway.

## Health

Population health in Haiti has suffered from political instability and natural disasters.[^cite_note-4] The [Hôpital Albert Schweitzer Haiti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C3%B4pital_Albert_Schweitzer_Haiti) is located in Deschapelles, Haiti. Established in 1956, it has grown to provide health care in the hospital and clinics to the Artibonite region with a budget of \$6 million in 2015. In addition to founder Dr. Larry Mellon, notable figures in its history include Dr Harold May, Founder and President of FAMILY, Inc. (Fathers and Mothers, Infants, eLders, and Youth).[^cite_note-5] In the Verrettes commune, FAMILY seeks to promote sustainable development.[^cite_note-6] In addition, U. S. [Tuskegee University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuskegee_University) has partnered with FAMILY to establish the FAMILY Tuskegee Institute of Haiti to develop academic and vocational training in the areas of forestry, agriculture and health care.

Trow other major hospitals are St-Nicolas in St-Marc and The Providence in Gonaïves.

## Historic places and landmarks

Several noteworthy events of the [Haitian Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haitian_Revolution) took place in the Artibonite Department.

- **[Gonaïves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gona%C3%AFves)**: the City of Independence

- **Ravine-à-Couleuvre**: site of the [famous battle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Ravine-%C3%A0-Couleuvres) between [Major General Toussaint Louverture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toussaint_Louverture) and French troops commanded by [Rochambeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donatien-Marie-Joseph_de_Vimeur,_vicomte_de_Rochambeau).

- **[Petite Rivière de l'Artibonite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petite_Rivi%C3%A8re_de_l'Artibonite)**: where [Henry Christophe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri_Christophe) built his palace with 365 doors.

- **Fort de La Crête-à-Pierrot**: site of the [Battle of Crête-à-Pierrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Cr%C3%AAte-%C3%A0-Pierrot) between Haitian rebels and French troops.

- **Habitation Georges**: site of Major-General Toussaint Louverture's arrest.

- **[Dessalines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dessalines_Arrondissement)** (formerly **Marchand**): Capital of the [First Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Empire_of_Haiti).

- **Vieux Bac**: near [Grande-Saline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grande-Saline,_Artibonite) where Toussaint Louverture defeated Colonel Brisbane in December 1794.

- **Deschapelles**, in the [Artibonite Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artibonite_Valley), is the location of the [Hôpital Albert Schweitzer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C3%B4pital_Albert_Schweitzer_Haiti).

- **Saint Marc**, in the [Artibonite Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artibonite_Valley), is the [Origin of soldiers who were sent to Savannah, Georgia USA in 1779](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chasseurs-Volontaires_de_Saint-Domingue) to [fight for the Port of Savannah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Savannah).

- Fort Blockhaus (*Blòkòs*) and Diamond (*Djanman*) in Saint-Marc

- Henry's castle ruin in Saint-Marc

Other facts:

- Dessalines and [Maire-Claire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie-Claire_Heureuse_F%C3%A9licit%C3%A9) got married in St-Marc

- After the execution of a St-Marc officer in [Sansousi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milot,_Haiti) Palace, the city rebelled against the crown leading to the King's suicide in October 1820.
