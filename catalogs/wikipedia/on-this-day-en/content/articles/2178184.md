**Hongtong County** ([simplified Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 洪洞县; [traditional Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 洪洞縣; [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *Hóngtóng Xiàn*) is a county in the southwest of [Shanxi Province](81262.md), China.[^cite_note-\:0-3] It is under the administration of the [prefecture-level city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefecture-level_city) of [Linfen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linfen).[^cite_note-\:0-3] The county spans an area of 1,494 square kilometers, and has a population of approximately 766,579 as of 2019.[^cite_note-\:2-1][^cite_note-\:1-2]

## History

During the reigns of the [Hongwu Emperor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hongwu_Emperor) through the [Yongle Emperor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yongle_Emperor), there was a concerted effort to resettle Chinese peasants from the Southern China to the [North China Plains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_China_Plain), which had been afflicted by conflict and natural disasters shortly before their reigns.[^cite_note-\:3-4][^cite_note-\:4-5] While en route to their new homes in the North China Plains, many migrants gathered at the [Great Pagoda Tree of Hongtong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locust_tree_of_Hongdong),[^cite_note-\:3-4][^cite_note-\:4-5] and many even chose to settle in and around [Pingyang Fu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pingyang_Fu?action=edit\&redlink=1),[^cite_note-\:3-4] which contained present-day Hongtong County.

## Administrative divisions

Hongtong County is divided into nine [towns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Towns_of_China) and seven [townships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Townships_of_China).[^cite_note-\:0-3] The county's seat of government is located in the town of [Dahuaishu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dahuaishu?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-\:0-3]

The county's nine towns are Dahuaishu, [Ganting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ganting,_Hongtong_County?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Quting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quting?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Subao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subao?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Guangshengsi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guangshengsi?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Mingjiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mingjiang,_Hongtong_County?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Zhaocheng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhaocheng,_Hongtong_County?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Wan'an](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wan'an,_Hongtong_County), and [Liujiayuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liujiayuan,_Hongtong_County?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-\:0-3]

The county's seven townships are [Yandi Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yandi_Township?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Xingtangsi Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xingtangsi_Township?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Dicun Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dicun_Township,_Hongtong_County?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Xincun Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xincun_Township,_Hongtong_County?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Longma Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longma_Township,_Hongtong_County?action=edit\&redlink=1), [Shantou Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shantou_Township?action=edit\&redlink=1), and [Zuomu Township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zuomu_Township?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-\:0-3]

## Geography

Lying in the northern end of the Linfen Basin, Hongtong County has an average altitude of 430 meters, with its highest peak, Huoshan, reaching 2,347 meters in elevation.[^cite_note-\:2-1] The [Fen River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fen_River) runs north to south through the county.[^cite_note-\:2-1]

### Climate

On an annual basis, Hongtong County experiences an average temperature of 12.7 °C, 441.5 millimeters of precipitation, and 2079.1 hours of sunshine.[^cite_note-\:2-1]

## Demographics

The county's population was 766,579 as of 2019, of which 45.7% of the population lived in urbanized areas, whereas the remaining 54.3% lived in rural areas.[^cite_note-\:1-2]

## Economy

As of 2019, Hongtong County has a [GDP](12594.md) of 15.15 billion [Renminbi](66958.md).[^cite_note-\:1-2] Of this, the [primary sector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primary_sector_of_the_economy) accounted for 6.6% of the economy, the [secondary sector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secondary_sector_of_the_economy) accounted for 45.8% of the economy, and the [tertiary sector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tertiary_sector_of_the_economy) accounted for 47.6% of the economy.[^cite_note-\:1-2] Retail sales in 2019 totaled 7.03 billion Renminbi.[^cite_note-\:1-2] Residents of Hongtong County had a [disposable income](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disposable_and_discretionary_income) of 19,545 yuan per capita, which stood at 30,848 yuan for urban residents, and 13,025 yuan for rural residents, respectively.[^cite_note-\:1-2]

Major agricultural products in the county include [corn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize), [wheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheat), [millet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millet), [beans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bean), [yams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yam_\(vegetable\)), [apples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple), [red dates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jujube), and edible [nuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nut_\(food\)).[^cite_note-\:1-2]

Major industrial products in the county include [coal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coal), [coal coke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coke_\(fuel\)), [benzene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benzene), refined [methanol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methanol), [cast iron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cast_iron), [natural gas](22131.md), [cement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cement), chemical [fertilizers](37401.md), and [plastic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plastic) products.[^cite_note-\:1-2]

## Education

Hongtong County has 352 schools, which includes 145 [kindergartens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kindergarten), 162 [elementary schools](25058.md), 33 [junior high schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junior_High_Schools), 9 ordinary [high schools](554992.md), and 3 secondary [vocational schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vocational_school).[^cite_note-\:1-2]

## Culture

The [Locust Tree of Hongtong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locust_tree_of_Hongtong) retains a high degree of cultural significance, which is visited by about 200,000 tourists each year.[^cite_note-\:3-4] The Great Pagoda Tree has become a notable site in [Chinese ancestor veneration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancestor_veneration_in_China), with tourists leaving messages at the site for their ancestors, and paying homage to them.[^cite_note-\:3-4][^cite_note-\:5-8] The site also hosts large events for the annual [Qingming Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qingming_Festival).[^cite_note-\:5-8]

## Transportation

By the end of 2019, there were 417 registered taxis, as well as 16 bus lines operational in the county serviced by 223 buses.[^cite_note-\:1-2]

Major expressways which run through Hongtong County include [National Highway 108](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_National_Highway_108), [National Highway 309](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_National_Highway_309), the [G5 Beijing–Kunming Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G5_Beijing%E2%80%93Kunming_Expressway), and the [G22 Qingdao–Lanzhou Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G22_Qingdao%E2%80%93Lanzhou_Expressway).[^cite_note-\:2-1]

The [Datong–Xi'an passenger railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Datong%E2%80%93Xi'an_passenger_railway) and [Datong–Puzhou railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Datong%E2%80%93Puzhou_railway) both run through Hongtong County.[^cite_note-\:2-1]
