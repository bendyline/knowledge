**Change for Chile** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Cambio por Chile*, **CpCh**) is a Chilean electoral coalition between the far-right [Republican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republican_Party_\(Chile,_2019\)), [Christian Social](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Social_Party_\(Chile\)) and [National Libertarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Libertarian_Party) parties, created ahead of the [2025 parliamentary election](74379670.md).[^cite_note-\:0-1] Before its official registration in August 2025, the coalition was known by the names **New Right** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Nueva Derecha*) and **United Right** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Derecha Unida*).

### Establishment

On 15 April 2025, three far-right[^cite_note-bloomberg-2][^cite_note-infobae-3] parties ([PRCh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republican_Party_\(Chile,_2019\)), [PSC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Social_Party_\(Chile\)), [PNL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Libertarian_Party)) had announced a preliminary agreement to compete together in the [2025 parliamentary election](74379670.md) in a coalition called "New Right".[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5] With this decision, the parties closed the door to the option of creating a single right-of-centre list together with [Chile Vamos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chile_Vamos), which sought a rapprochement with all the forces opposed to the government of [Gabriel Boric](47329497.md).[^cite_note-6] In July 2025, the parties ratified their intention to form a parliamentary alliance by concluding a pact, albeit renaming it the "United Right".[^cite_note-7] In parallel, the [Republican Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republican_Party_\(Chile,_2019\)) opened up to a negotiation with Chile Vamos to reach an omission pact in the senatorial election in the binomial regions (where two senators are elected).[^cite_note-8]

The alliance was not replicated for the [2025 presidential election](74379670.md), as the parties that formed it support different presidential candidates: the [Republican Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republican_Party_\(Chile,_2019\)) and the [Christian Social Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Social_Party_\(Chile\)) supported [José Antonio Kast](55052190.md);[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] while the [National Libertarian Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Libertarian_Party) supported the candidacy of [Johannes Kaiser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Kaiser_\(Chilean_politician\)), all parties in the coalition endorsed Kast in the second round, which he won.[^cite_note-11]

### Registration

The Republican, Social Christian, and National Libertarian parties formalized their pact before the [Electoral Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_Service) (Servel) on 8 August 2025, under the name "Change for Chile".[^cite_note-\:0-1] The list announced the presentation of 183 candidates for the [Chamber of Deputies](716259.md).[^cite_note-12]
