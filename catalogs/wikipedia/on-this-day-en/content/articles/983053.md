**Jodhpur** (Hindi pronunciation: [\[ˈd͡ʒoːd̪ʱ.pʊr\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Hindi_and_Urdu) [//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/5/5e/Jodhpur.ogg/Jodhpur.ogg.mp3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/transcoded/5/5e/Jodhpur.ogg/Jodhpur.ogg.mp3)<sup>ⓘ</sup>) is the [second-largest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cities_and_towns_in_Rajasthan) city of the north-western [Indian state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_state) of [Rajasthan](26291.md), after its capital [Jaipur](166537.md). As of 2025, the city has a population of 1.6 million.[^cite_note-11] It serves as the administrative headquarters of the [Jodhpur district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jodhpur_district) and [Jodhpur division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jodhpur_division). It is the historic capital of the [Kingdom of Marwar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Marwar), founded in 1459 by [Rao Jodha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jodha_of_Mandore), a [Rajput](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Rajput_dynasties_and_states) chief of the [Rathore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rathore_dynasty) clan.[^cite_note-12] On 11 August 1947, 4 days prior to the [Indian independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_independence_movement), [Maharaja Hanwant Singh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanwant_Singh) the last ruler of Jodhpur state signed the Instrument of Accession and merged his state in [Union of India](14533.md).[^cite_note-13] On 30 March 1949, it became part of the newly formed state of [Rajasthan](26291.md), which was created after merging the states of the erstwhile [Rajputana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajputana_Agency).[^cite_note-14]

Jodhpur is a famous tourist spot with a palace, fort, and temples, set in the stark landscape of the [Thar Desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thar_Desert). It is also known as the 'Blue City' due to the dominant color scheme of its buildings in the old town.[^cite_note-bluecity-1] The old city circles the [Mehrangarh Fort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehrangarh) and is bounded by a wall with several gates.[^cite_note-Madan-15] Jodhpur lies near the geographic centre of the Rajasthan state, which makes it a convenient base for travel in a region much frequented by tourists.[^cite_note-bluecity-1]

## Etymology

The name "Jodhpur" is derived from its founder, [Rao Jodha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jodha_of_Mandore), who established the city in 1459.[^cite_note-16] "Jodh" represents Rao Jodha, and "pur" means city or town in [Sanskrit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit), making it the "City of Jodha."[^cite_note-17]

## Geography and climate

Jodhpur has a [hot desert climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_desert_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification) *BWh*), due to its very high [potential evapotranspiration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potential_evapotranspiration). Although the average rainfall is around 362 mm (14.3 in), which falls mostly from June to September, it fluctuates greatly. In the [famine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Famine_in_India) year of 1899, Jodhpur received only 24 mm (0.94 in), but in the flood year of 1917, it received as much as 1,178 mm (46.4 in). [Jojari river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jojari_River), a tributary of [Luni River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luni_River), flows from [Banad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banar) to Salawas in Jodhpur Urban Area. A riverfront development project for the Jojari River, covering a 35 km stretch within the Jodhpur urban Area, was approved in January 2021 under the [Namami Gange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Namami_Gange_Programme) programme of the [Ministry of Jal Shakti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Jal_Shakti). Previously, the project was overseen by the Jodhpur Development Authority. Pin Code of Jodhpur is 342001 which comes under Jodhpur postal division (Jodhpur Region).[^cite_note-18]

Temperatures are extreme from March to October, except when the [monsoonal rain](8282374.md) produces thick [clouds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloud) to lower it slightly. In April, May, and June, high temperatures routinely exceed 40 °C. During the monsoon season, average temperatures decrease slightly, but the city's generally low humidity rises, which adds to the perception of the heat. The highest temperature recorded in Jodhpur was on 20 May 2016, when it rose to 48.8 °C (119.8 °F).

## History

### Mythology

The Rajasthan District Gazetteer's of Jodhpur gives examples from the Hindu epic [Ramayana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramayana) (composed up to the 4th century AD), which mention that the [Abhiras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abhira_people) inhabited the region of what later became Marwar. The [Aryans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aryan) are also mentioned as a race that migrated to this region.[^cite_note-27]

### Early history

The Ghatiyala pillar inscription (AD 996) which was erected by the Pratihara ruler Kakkuka, gives important information about the region.[^cite_note-28] Marwar formed a part of the [Gurjara-Pratihara Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pratihara_dynasty) and until 1100 AD was ruled by a powerful Pratihara King. The Pratihars later formed a marital alliance with the Rathores and [Mandore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandore) was given in [dowry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dowry) by the Pratiharas to a [Rathore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rathore_\(Rajput_clan\)) chieftain called [Chunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chunda_of_Mandore). Chunda thus established his rule in [Mandore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandore) and took advantage of the declining [Tughlaq dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tughlaq_dynasty). He defended Marwar against Zafar Khan and conquered Khatu, Didwana, Sambhar, Nagaur and Ajmer from the Tughlaqs.[^cite_note-KA-29]

Jodhpur was founded in 1459 by [Rao Jodha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rao_Jodha), a [Rajput](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajput) chief of the [Rathore clan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rathore_\(Rajput_clan\)). Jodha succeeded in conquering the surrounding territory from the [Delhi Sultanate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi_Sultanate) and thus founded a kingdom that came to be known as [Marwar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Marwar).[^cite_note-30] As Jodha hailed from the nearby town of [Mandore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandore), that town initially served as the [capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_\(political\)) of this state; however, Jodhpur soon took over that role, even during the lifetime of Jodha. The city was located on the strategic road linking [Delhi](37756.md) to [Gujarat](53707.md). This enabled it to profit from a flourishing trade in [opium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opium), [copper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copper), [silk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silk), [sandalwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandalwood), [dates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Date_\(fruit\)), and other tradeable goods.[^cite_note-history1-31]

### Early modern period

Main articles: [Rajput War (1679–1707)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajput_War_\(1679%E2%80%931707\)) and [Rajput Rebellion (1708–1710)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajput_Rebellion_\(1708%E2%80%931710\))

After the death of Rao [Chandrasen Rathore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chandrasen_Rathore) in 1581, the kingdom was annexed by the Mughal Emperor [Akbar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akbar), Marwar thus became a Mughal vassal, owing [fealty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fealty) to them while enjoying internal [autonomy](191145.md). Jodhpur and its people benefited from this exposure to the wider world as new styles of [art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_art) and [architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Architecture) made their appearance and opportunities opened up for local tradesmen to make their mark across northern India.[^cite_note-history1-31]



View of the Rajasthan High Court, Sardar Museum in Umaid Park and upper right is Jodhpur fort in 1960.

[Aurangzeb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aurangzeb) briefly sequestrated the state (*circa* 1679) after the death of [Maharaja Jaswant Singh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maharaja_Jaswant_Singh), but his son [Maharaja Ajit Singh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ajit_Singh_of_Marwar) was restored to the throne by [Durgadas Rathore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durgadas_Rathore) at the death of Aurangzeb in 1707 ending the 30 year long Rathore rebellion. The [Mughal Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mughal_Empire) declined gradually after 1707, but the Jodhpur court was beset by intrigue; rather than benefiting from circumstances, Marwar descended into strife and invited the intervention of the [Marathas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maratha), who soon supplanted the Mughals as overlords of the region. In 1755 [Jai Appa Scindia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jayappaji_Rao_Scindia) attacked [Nagaur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagaur) after looting several places of Rajasthan. Jai Appa halted his army near samas pond of Tausar which was 3.5 km from Nagaur fort. He surrounded Nagaur fort and cut off food and water supply. Maharaja Vijay Singh called Darbar and asked for volunteers to kill Scindia. Gaji Khan Khokhar (Chawata Kallan) and Kan Singh (Dotalai) volunteered and took responsibility for killing Jaiappa Scindia. Both changed their outfit as traders and opened shop near Jaiappa's army. They observed their activities for two month. On 25 July 1755 on Friday at 11 am, when found opportunity attacked Jaiappa with daggers and killed him (Painting situated in Mandore museum). While fighting the loyal soldiers of Jodhpur both were killed. From then on a common proverb still people say "Khokhar bada khuraki kha gaya appa jaisa daaki" (Khokhar are great gluttons, eaten demon like appa). Even after the killing of Jai Appa Sindhia, the Maratha army continued fighting for a few months near Nagaur but they lost hope after Jai Appa's death. Dissipated the wealth of the state, which sought the help of the British and entered into a [subsidiary alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subsidiary_alliance) with them.[^cite_note-history1-31][^cite_note-ReferenceA-32]

### British colonial period

A major revolt occurred in [1857](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Rebellion_of_1857) by some Rathore nobles of [Pali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pali,_Rajasthan) led by Thakur [Kushal Singh of Auwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kushal_Singh_of_Auwa), but the rebels were defeated by the British Army under Colonel Holmes and peace was restored.[^cite_note-history1-31][^cite_note-ReferenceA-32]



Street Scene of Jodhpur in 1906

During the [British Raj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Raj), the state of Jodhpur had the largest land area in the [Rajputana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajputana). The land area of the state was 93,424 km<sup>2</sup> (36,071 sq mi) its population in 1901 was 44,73,759. It enjoyed an estimated revenue of [£](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C2%A3)3,529,000. Its merchants, the [Marwaris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marwaris), flourished and came to occupy a position of dominance in trade across India.

### After independence

In 1947, when [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_of_India) became independent, the state merged into the union, and Jodhpur became the second-largest city after the Jaipur city in Rajasthan.[^cite_note-history1-31][^cite_note-33] At the time of division, the ruler of Jodhpur, [Hanwant Singh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanwant_Singh), did not want to join India, but finally, due to the effective persuasion of [Vallabhbhai Patel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vallabhbhai_Patel) at the time, the state of Jodhpur was included in the Indian Republic. Later after the [State Reorganisation Act, 1956](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_Reorganisation_Act,_1956) came into effect, it was included within the state of Rajasthan.[^cite_note-history1-31]

As of the 2011 census official report, Jodhpur Municipal Corporation had 196,436 households and a population of 1,056,191. 134,761 (12.76%) were under the age of 7. Jodhpur had a sex ratio of 902 females per 1000 males and a literacy rate of 80.33% for those 7 years and above. Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes made up 133,395 (12.63%) and 25,253 (2.39%) of the population respectively.[^cite_note-Jodhpur-Census-2011-4]

The Jodhpur Urban/Metropolitan area includes Jodhpur, Kuri Bhagtasani, [Mandore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandore) Industrial Area, Nandri, Pal Village and [Sangariya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangaria,_India). Its urban/metropolitan population is 1,137,815 of which 599,332 are males and 538,483 are females. With the inclusion of 395 villages in Jodhpur city in the month of February 2021 by JoDA, the new population count for the city is 1,663,000 and is expected to grow by 33.04% over the next decade. In the year 2031 population of Jodhpur city is expected to be more than 3.1 million. The population of Jodhpur city after expansion of city borders is 1,663,000.[^cite_note-censusjodhpur-34][^cite_note-37]

## Economy

Jodhpur contributes \$4 billion (approx) to Rajasthan's economy through different Industries. It is also considered the center of India's \$200 million handicraft industry.[^cite_note-39] The city is also a major tourist destination, claiming attractive heritage hotels and sights from its long history.[^cite_note-40][^cite_note-41]

In addition to these directly elected members, the corporation has four *ex-officio* members (one member of parliament, three members of legislative assembly, namely Sardarpura, Soorsagar, City), and three nominated members. Currently, the city has two civic bodies – Jodhpur North and Jodhpur South each headed by a mayor. Each municipal corporation has 80 wards, making a total of 160 wards in the city.[^cite_note-45] The Jodhpur Development Authority (JDA) executes and supervises plans and schemes for the development of the urban region.[^cite_note-46]

## Strategic location

Jodhpur is a significant city of western Rajasthan and lies about 250 km from the border with [Pakistan](23235.md). This location makes it a key base for the [Indian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Army), [Indian Air Force](403355.md) (IAF), and [Border Security Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border_Security_Force). Jodhpur's [South Western Air Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Western_Air_Command) is one of Asia's largest and one of the most critical and strategically located [airbases of the IAF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Indian_Air_Force_bases) (The [Jodhpur Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jodhpur_Airport) played the crucial role during the Indo-Pakistani wars of [1965](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Pakistani_War_of_1965) and [1971](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Liberation_War)) deployed [fighter jets](3793034.md) and [advanced light helicopters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HAL_Dhruv). There are 5 squadrons of the Indian Air force that are known as the 32 wing.

## Culture

Jodhpur has culturally been known by the name of *Jodhana* by the locals.[^cite_note-47] The city is famous for its food and its popularity can be judged by the fact that one can find sweet shops named "Jodhpur Sweets" in many cities throughout [India](14533.md). Being at the onshore of [Thar Desert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thar_Desert), life has been influenced by ways of select nomadic tribes (so-called "gypsy" groups – *[Banjara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banjara)* in Hindi – have settled in some parts of the city).[^cite_note-48] Jodhpur has distinct cultural identity through its food and is famous for its [Mirchi Bada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirchi_Bada), Rabdi Ghewar and Mawa Kachori.[^cite_note-49]

### Research

Major research institutes and organizations have been established in the city for promoting research:

- [Arid Forest Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arid_Forest_Research_Institute) is one of the institutes of the [Indian Council of Forestry Research and Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Council_of_Forestry_Research_and_Education) working under the Ministry of Environment and Forests, for carrying out scientific research in forestry to provide technologies to increase the vegetative cover and to conserve the biodiversity in the hot arid and semiarid region of Rajasthan, Gujarat, and Dadara union, and Nagar Haveli union territory. The campus covers 66 hectares on the New Pali Road.
