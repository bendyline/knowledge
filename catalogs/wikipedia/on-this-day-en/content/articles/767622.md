**White Nile State** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): النيل الأبيض, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *An Nīl al Ābyaḍ*) is one of the 18 [wilayat](579458.md) or states of [Sudan](27421.md). It has an area of 39,701 [km](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilometre)<sup>2</sup> and an estimated population of approximately 2,493,880 people (2018 est). The state borders [Khartoum](767612.md) to the north, [Gezira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gezira_State) and [Sennar](767672.md) to the east, [South Sudan](32350676.md) to the south, and [Kordofan](1127161.md) to the west. Since 1994, [Rabak](14063078.md) has been the capital of the state; other important cities include [Kosti](6671707.md) and [Ed Dueim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Dueim). On 21 May 2025, the [Sudanese Armed Forces](27429.md) said it had cleared White Nile State of the [Rapid Support Forces](43053874.md) during the [civil war](73556699.md).[^cite_note-4]
