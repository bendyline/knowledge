The **minister of foreign affairs** ([Nepali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepali_language): परराष्ट्र मन्त्री, romanized: *Pararāṣṭra Mantrī*, or simply, the **foreign minister**) is the head of the [Ministry of Foreign Affairs](35831443.md) of the [Government of Nepal](19233409.md). One of the senior-most offices in the [federal cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers_of_Nepal), the chief responsibility of the foreign minister is to represent Nepal and its government in the international community. The Foreign Minister also plays an important role in determining and implementing [Nepalese foreign policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_relations_of_Nepal).[^cite_note-1]

The current foreign minister is [Shishir Khanal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shishir_Khanal),[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3] who assumed office on 27 March 2026 under Prime Mninister [Balendra Shah](70833999.md)[^cite_note-4]
