Canadian embassy in [Copenhagen](5166.md), Denmark.

[Canada](5042916.md) and [Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Denmark) have longstanding [bilateral relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilateral_relations). Canada has an embassy in [Copenhagen](5166.md). Denmark has an embassy in [Ottawa](22219.md) and a consulate-general in [Toronto](64646.md).[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2] Both countries are full members of [NATO](21133.md)[^cite_note-3] and the [Arctic Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arctic_Council).[^cite_note-4] They share a short land border on the island of [Hans Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Island), which is split between the [Canadian territory](75763.md) of [Nunavut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nunavut) and the Danish autonomous territory of [Greenland](12118.md).&#x20;

## History

In 1928, the bilateral relations between Canada and Denmark were strengthened, when members of the [Canadian National Railways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_National_Railways) met with [Thomas Madsen-Mygdal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Madsen-Mygdal).[^cite_note-5]

The first treaty between Canada and Denmark was a visa requirements agreement, signed on 22 September and 14 October 1949.[^cite_note-6] Both countries signed an agreement concerning taxes in 1956.[^cite_note-7] Canada and Denmark agreed to cooperate with defence science in 1969.[^cite_note-8] In 1983, a [marine environmental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marine_biology), social and economic agreement was signed in Copenhagen.[^cite_note-9]

In 2010, the Denmark–USA/Canada Program was launched. The programme aims the internationalization of Danish education programmes.[^cite_note-10]

## Hans Island dispute

Main article: [Whisky War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whisky_War)

[Hans Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Island) is a small, uninhabited barren knoll measuring 1.3 km<sup>2</sup> (0.5 sq mi), located in the centre of the [Kennedy Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kennedy_Channel) of [Nares Strait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nares_Strait). Until 2022, the island was claimed by both Canada and [Greenland](12118.md) with the Kingdom of Denmark.[^cite_note-11] In 1973 Canada and the Kingdom of Denmark ratified a treaty defining the border in the area. The treaty did not define the border at Hans Island as no agreement was made on this. In 1984, [Tom Høyem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_H%C3%B8yem), the Danish [Minister for Greenland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_for_Greenland), raised the Danish flag on the island.[^cite_note-bbc-12] On 25 July 2005, Canadian [Defence Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_National_Defence_\(Canada\)) [Bill Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Graham_\(Canadian_politician\)) visited the island, sparking anger in Denmark. The Government of Denmark sent a [letter of protest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Letter_of_protest) to Canada.[^cite_note-bbc-12] Canada also sent two warships in 2005 to Hans Island, [HMCS *Shawinigan*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMCS_Shawinigan_\(MM_704\)) and [HMCS *Glace Bay*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMCS_Glace_Bay_\(MM_701\)).[^cite_note-13]

On 19 September 2009, both governments put in place a process to end the dispute.&#x20;

> As friendly countries, of course, it is our shared objective that we resolve this issue – that we put this issue behind us… "We now have a process – a process in which the officials will be working together, gathering all of the relative information and trying to find a way forward to do this…

— [Pierre Pettigrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Pettigrew)[^cite_note-14]

In January 2011, both countries were close to a resolution over the island.[^cite_note-strained-15] However, the border agreement signed in November 2012 did not contain a solution to the dispute.

On 10 June 2022, Canadian newspaper *[The Globe and Mail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Globe_and_Mail)* reported that the Canadian and Danish governments had settled on a border across the island, dividing it between the Canadian territory of [Nunavut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nunavut) and the Danish constituent country of [Greenland](12118.md), to be formally unveiled on 14 June 2022.[^cite_note-16]

## High level visits

Princess [Margrethe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margrethe_II_of_Denmark) (later Queen of Denmark) and her husband Prince [Henrik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henrik,_Prince_Consort_of_Denmark) visited Canada in September 1967.[^cite_note-17] Queen Margrethe also visited Canada in 1991.[^cite_note-18] Crown Prince [Frederik](339155.md) and Crown Princess [Mary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary,_Crown_Princess_of_Denmark) visited [Ottawa](22219.md) and [Toronto](64646.md) in 2014.[^cite_note-19] When [Prince Joachim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Joachim_of_Denmark) married [Marie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_Marie_of_Denmark) they spent their honeymoon in Canada.[^cite_note-20]

## Diaspora

About 200,000 people in Canada are [of Danish origin or birth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish-Canadian). They mostly live in [Ontario](22218.md), [Alberta](717.md) and [British Columbia](3392.md).

[New Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Denmark,_New_Brunswick) is a Canadian rural community in [Victoria County, New Brunswick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_County,_New_Brunswick). The community derives its name from several Danish settlers who inhabited the area in 1872, eventually forming the largest and what would become the oldest Danish community in Canada; the Danish influence has diminished somewhat in recent decades due to out-migration.[^cite_note-21]

The Embassy of Denmark in [Ottawa](22219.md) ([Danish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish_language): *Danmarks Ambassade, Ottawa*) is [Denmark](76972.md)'s [embassy](8970.md) in [Canada](5042916.md). It is located at suite 450, 47 Clarence Street in [Ottawa](22219.md), the Canadian capital.

Denmark operates secondary Canadian consulate offices in [Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calgary), [Edmonton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmonton), [Halifax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halifax,_Nova_Scotia), [Iqaluit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iqaluit), [Montreal](7954681.md), [St. John's](70581.md), [Toronto](64646.md), [Vancouver](32706.md) and [Winnipeg](100730.md).

The Embassy of Canada in [Copenhagen](5166.md) is [Canada](5042916.md)'s [embassy](8970.md) in [Denmark](76972.md). It is located at Kristen Bernikowsgade 1, 1105 Copenhagen K., Denmark. In 2026, Canada opened a consulate in [Nuuk](53303.md), [Greenland](12118.md).[^cite_note-22]
