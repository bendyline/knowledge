The **Government of Peace and Unity** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): حكومة السلام والوحدة, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Ḥukūmat as-Salām wal-Waḥda*) is a [rival government](80144294.md) and [quasi-state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-state)[^cite_note-auto-6][^cite_note-auto4-2] established in April 2025 to administer areas of [Sudan](27421.md) controlled by the [Rapid Support Forces](43053874.md) (RSF) and allied groups during the [Sudanese Civil War](73556699.md), in opposition to the internationally recognised [government](79211943.md) appointed by the [Transitional Sovereignty Council](61549039.md).[^cite_note-7] The Government of Peace and Unity is headed by the leader of RSF [Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Hamdan_Dagalo), known by the [*nom de guerre*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nom_de_guerre) "Hemedti".[^cite_note-auto-6]

## Background

Sudanese president [Omar al-Bashir](393625.md) was overthrown in a [coup d'état](60469131.md) in April 2019 following [large-scale protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudanese_revolution). A 39-month [transition to democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudanese_transition_to_democracy_\(2019%E2%80%932021\)) was planned, with the role of head of state being performed by a [Transitional Sovereignty Council](61549039.md), and a [transitional government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdalla_Hamdok_government) led by [Abdalla Hamdok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdalla_Hamdok) was formed to govern the country until [elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Next_Sudanese_general_election) planned for July 2023. The transition period ended abruptly when the transitional government was dissolved following a further [coup d'état](69110231.md), led by [Abdel Fattah al-Burhan](60480545.md), in October 2021. A [civil war](73556699.md) between the al-Burhan led [Sudanese Armed Forces](27429.md) (SAF) and the paramilitary [Rapid Support Forces](43053874.md) (RSF) led by "Hemedti" broke out in April 2023.

Initially, the RSF gained control of most of the capital, [Khartoum](17320.md) and parts of Southern and Western Sudan. However, by early 2025, the SAF had [regained ground](73563331.md) around Khartoum and by mid-February 2025, had control over about two-thirds of the Khartoum metropolitan area.[^cite_note-8]

## Establishment

See also: [Sudan Founding Alliance](79348683.md)

The Chairman of the Transitional Sovereignty Council, Abdel Fattah al-Burhan, announced plans to form a civilian-led [transitional government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_government_of_Sudan_\(2025%E2%80%93present\)) on 10 February 2025.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] In response, the RSF convened a conference in [Nairobi](21482.md), [Kenya](188171.md), on 18 February 2025 to draft a Sudan Founding Charter leading to the formation of a [Sudan Founding Alliance](79348683.md).[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] On 4 March 2025, the Sudan Founding Alliance announced that it had adopted a transitional constitutional framework to pave the way for forming a rival Government of Peace and Unity to administer territory under the control of the RSF and allied groups.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15][^cite_note-\:0-16][^cite_note-\:1-17]

Consultations on the make-up of the proposed government began on 23 February 2025 in Nairobi, Kenya.[^cite_note-18] However, on 24 February 2025, disagreements emerged between members of the Sudan Founding Alliance over allocating positions within the proposed government.[^cite_note-19] On 8 March 2025, the RSF announced that arrangements to form the Government of Peace and Unity were complete and plans to issue passports, official documents, and currency were being prepared.[^cite_note-20][^cite_note-21]

On 25 March 2025, it was reported that the Rapid Support Forces would gain the chairmanship of the presidential council and at least 40% of positions on the transitional cabinet, the Sudan People's Liberation Movement–North (al Hilu) would gain the vice-chairmanship of the presidential council and 30% of positions on the transitional cabinet with the remainder shared between the other members of the Sudan Founding Alliance.[^cite_note-22] On 28 March 2025, it was announced that the commander of the Rapid Support Forces, Hemedti, was nominated to be the chairman of the Presidential Council in the future government. Along with this announcement, it was announced that negotiations are nearing their close and the government would likely be formed shortly after [Eid al-Fitr](154944.md).[^cite_note-auto3-23] On 14 April 2025, Sudan's foreign minister, Ali Youssef al-Sharif, said he expected the GPU to be established that week.[^cite_note-24]

Rapid Support Forces leader Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo officially announced the establishment of the Government of Peace and Unity on 15 April 2025.[^cite_note-25][^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27] The following day, Dagalo stated that the GPU would begin issuing its identity cards and currency. [^cite_note-28] On 25 July 2025, it was reported that the headquarters of the GPU would be based in [Nyala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyala,_South_Darfur), with some institutions also operating from [Geneina](1501534.md) and [Ed Daein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Daein).[^cite_note-29]

## Territorial control

See also: [Territory of the Rapid Support Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Territory_of_the_Rapid_Support_Forces) and [New Sudan (territory)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Sudan_\(territory\))

In November 2025, the Government of Peace and Unity controlled the [states](579458.md) of [Central Darfur](34420004.md), [East Darfur](34420916.md), [South Darfur](767650.md) and [West Darfur](767645.md) in their entirety. It also controlled most of [North Darfur](767666.md), and [West Kordofan](767647.md), and parts of [North Kordofan](767669.md), and [South Kordofan](767653.md).[^cite_note-30]

## Monetary policy

In the areas controlled by the Government of Peace and Unity a parallel monetary system have emerged with old currency retaining its value. When the Sudanese government started printing new 500 and 1,000-pound notes in November 2024, the rival government reacted by banning the usage of new banknotes in May 2026. While in Khartoum one dollar was selling for more than 9,000 Sudanese pounds, in Nyala it sold for only 4,600 pounds. Due to severe cash shortage people in Western Sudan have been forced to use the Bankak app from the Sudanese government, leading to heavy transfer fees imposed by the Darfur traders, with the commission reaching 40% in East Darfur and 50% in West Darfur.[^cite_note-31] Additionally the Central Bank of Sudan have often closed Bankak accounts linked to Rapid Support Forces increasing risks of trading in the areas.

For most of the war all banks stopped functioning in RSF-held areas, with their premises looted and destroyed. In January 2026 they launched a new bank, "El Mustaqbal Bank" (*[Future Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Future_Bank_\(Sudan\))*), in Nyala to restore financial services. As of September 2026 it has expanded to West Darfur.[^cite_note-32]

On 21 May 2026 the Government of Peace and Unity appointed Yahya Hussein Jangoul as a governor of a parallel central bank in Nyala. The group started printing new banknotes in late May bearing the signature of Jangoul and using them to pay salaries.[^cite_note-33][^cite_note-34]

The transitional constitutional framework adopted by the Sudan Founding Alliance on 4 March 2025 prescribed the Government of Peace and Unity structure. It contains provisions for dividing Sudan into eight administrative regions and defines the powers of national, regional and local authorities.[^cite_note-\:1-17][^cite_note-\:2-35][^cite_note-\:0-16]

### Presidential Council

The Presidential Council has 15 members, seven of whom are nominated by the Sudan Founding Alliance and eight regional governors who serve as [*ex officio*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ex_officio) members.[^cite_note-\:2-35][^cite_note-\:1-17] The Rapid Support Forces leader, [Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Hamdan_Dagalo), also known as "Hemedti", was named chairman of the presidential council on 28 March 2025.[^cite_note-auto3-23] The president and vice-president constitute the presidium of the presidential council. The full membership of the presidential council was announced on 26 July 2025.[^cite_note-36][^cite_note-auto2-37][^cite_note-38]

### Transitional Cabinet

A Transitional Cabinet consists of a prime minister, nominated by the Sudan Founding Alliance, and up to 16 other ministers nominated by the prime minister in consultation with the signatories to the Sudan Founding Charter.[^cite_note-\:2-35][^cite_note-\:1-17] Mohammed Hassan al-Ta'ishi was named prime minister on 26 July 2025.[^cite_note-39][^cite_note-40]

Members

- [Mohammed Hassan al-Ta'ishi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammed_Hassan_al-Ta'ishi) – Transitional Prime Minister of the Transitional Cabinet of the Transitional Government of Peace and Unity

- Lieutenant General [Abdul Rahim Dagalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Rahim_Dagalo) – Defense

- Suleiman Sandal – Interior

- [Ibrahim al-Mirghani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_al-Mirghani) – Information and cabinet affairs

### Constituent Legislative Body

A [bicameral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicameral) Constituent Legislative Body comprises a 24-member Council of Regions and a 177-member Council of Deputies.[^cite_note-\:2-35][^cite_note-\:1-17] The Speaker of the Legislative Council is currently [Fadlallah Burma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fadlallah_Burma?action=edit\&redlink=1).[^cite_note-41]

### Judiciary

On 1 April 2026, Hemedti issued a decree to establish the Interim Council of Justice to prepare for the selection of the Chief Justice and the Attorney General, as well as the formation of the Constitutional Court.[^cite_note-42]

### Regions

The transitional constitutional framework contains provisions to divide Sudan into eight Regions: Khartoum Region, Eastern Region, Northern Region, Darfur Region, Central Region, Kordofan Region, South Kordofan/Nuba Mountains Region, and New Funj Region.[^cite_note-\:2-35][^cite_note-\:1-17] Eight regional governors were named on 26 July 2025, including for areas not controlled by the RSF and allied groups.[^cite_note-auto2-37][^cite_note-43]
