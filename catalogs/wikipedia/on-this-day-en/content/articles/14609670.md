**Harnai** ([Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_language): ضلع ہرنائی) is a [district](639402.md) in the northeastern part of the [Balochistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balochistan_\(Pakistan\)) province of [Pakistan](23235.md). [Harnai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harnai) is the principal town of this district and serves as its administrative headquarters.

Until 2007, Harnai had been a tehsil of [Sibi District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sibi_District). In August that year, the Balochistan Government announced the Harnai district would be created by splitting the Sibi district and forming the new district from [Harnai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harnai_Tehsil) and Shahrag tehsils and the sub-tehsil of Khost.[^cite_note-2]

## History

The name Harnai refers to an influential Hindu personality, Harnam Das, supposed founder of [Harnai town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harnai), the capital of Harnai District. The town is quite close to [Loralai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loralai), [Ziarat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ziarat), [Sibi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sibi), and [Quetta](93764.md). Harnai is surrounded by imposing hills on all sides. The encircling hill ranges have the resounding names of 'Khalifat' and 'Zarghun'.&#x20;

Harnai proper has a population of about 200,000. The majority of the population of Harnai are [Tarins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarins?action=edit\&redlink=1) and they mostly speak a unique dialect, or language, [Tarino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarino) ([Wanetsi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wanetsi)), which is quite different from the Pashto spoken in other parts of Balochistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa provinces, and is probably a mixture of [Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto), [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu) and other languages developed as a 'lingua franca' sometime during the 18th and 19th centuries, when a variety of people of different ethnic origins lived here. There seems to be no earlier historical record or trace of it.&#x20;

## Geography and climate

Harnai is surrounded by mountainous ranges. The minimum and maximum winter temperature of the area is −2˚C to 20˚C. The summer is extreme in the area and the minimum and maximum temperatures range from 20˚C to 48˚C. Harnai has a fertile rainy season during monsoon time. In general, Harnai has pleasant weather in the winter season.

### Population

As of the [2023 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Pakistani_census), Harnai district has 16,393 households and a population of 127,571. The district has a sex ratio of 111.89 males to 100 females and a literacy rate of 39.83%: 48.91% for males and 29.71% for females.[^cite_note-2023table1-6][^cite_note-2023table12-7] 54,999 (43.11% of the surveyed population) are under 10 years of age.[^cite_note-2023table5-8] 33,433 (26.21%) live in urban areas.[^cite_note-2023table1-6]

### Language

Languages of Harnai district (2023)

1. [Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto) (89.2%)

2. [Balochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balochi_language) (8.20%)

3. [Sindhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sindhi_language) (0.70%)

4. Others (1.86%)

At the time of the 2023 census, 89.24% of the population spoke [Pashto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashto), 8.20% [Balochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balochi_language) and 0.7% [Sindhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sindhi_language) as their first language.
