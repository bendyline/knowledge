**Elk Mountain, Utah** refers to several things. Most recently, a ski resort near [Beaver, Utah](136864.md).[^cite_note-1]

Elk Meadows Ski Area initially closed but reopened in December 2010 under new ownership and is now called **Eagle Point Ski Resort**. Eagle Point has added many new amenities, including restaurants, hot tubs, etc.[^cite_note-2]
