**USS *Tripoli* (LHA-7)** is the second [*America*-class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/America-class_amphibious_assault_ship) [amphibious assault ship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphibious_assault_ship) built for the [United States Navy](20518076.md). In May 2012, [United States Secretary of the Navy](44001.md) [Ray Mabus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Mabus) announced the ship's name as *Tripoli*, in honor of the [US Marine Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Marine_Corps) victory against [Tripoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman_Tripolitania) at the [Battle of Derna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Derna_\(1805\)) during the [First Barbary War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Barbary_War).[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9] This is the third [US Naval ship to carry the name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Tripoli), the first being [USS *Tripoli* (CVE-64)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Tripoli_\(CVE-64\)), an [escort carrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escort_carrier) from [World War II](32927.md) and the second being [USS *Tripoli* (LPH-10)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Tripoli_\(LPH-10\)), an [amphibious assault ship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphibious_assault_ship) that served during the [Cold War](325329.md), [Operation Desert Storm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Desert_Storm), and [Operation Restore Hope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Restore_Hope).

## Design and construction

The design of *Tripoli* is based on [USS *Makin Island*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Makin_Island_\(LHD-8\)), which is an improved version of the [*Wasp*-class amphibious assault ship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wasp-class_amphibious_assault_ship). Approximately 45% of the Flight 0 design is based on USS *Makin Island*, with the [well deck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Well_deck) removed to allow more room for aircraft and aviation fuel.[^cite_note-10] The removal of the well deck for [landing craft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landing_craft) allows for an extended hangar deck with two significantly wider high bay areas, each fitted with an overhead crane for aircraft maintenance.

Other enhancements include a reconfigurable command and control complex, an on-board hospital, and numerous aviation support spaces.[^cite_note-11] The design of *Tripoli* features an enlarged hangar deck, realignment and expansion of the aviation maintenance facilities, and a significant increase in her available stowage for parts and support equipment. She was intended to be the first LHA replacement ship to deliver fully ready to integrate the entire future air combat element of the [U.S. Marine Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Marine_Corps) to include the [F-35B Lightning II](11812.md), but construction delays pushed final F-35 capability installs until delivery.[^cite_note-NNS140621-05-2][^cite_note-NNS170914-17-4]

*Tripoli* was built by [Ingalls Shipbuilding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingalls_Shipbuilding) (Huntington Ingalls Industries) at the company's shipyard in [Pascagoula, Mississippi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pascagoula,_Mississippi). Fabrication of ship components began in July 2013. The ship's [keel was laid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keel_laying) in a ceremony on 20 June 2014 in Pascagoula.[^cite_note-NNS140621-05-2] *Tripoli* was [launched](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Launched_\(ship\)) on 1 May and [christened](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceremonial_ship_launching) on 16 September 2017, with Lynne Mabus, wife of former Navy Secretary Ray Mabus, as her sponsor.[^cite_note-NNS170914-17-4] *Tripoli* was delivered to the Navy, later than scheduled, on 28 February 2020.[^cite_note-delivery-5]

## Service history

In September 2020, *Tripoli* completed a homeport shift from Pascagoula, Mississippi to [San Diego](28504.md), [California](5407.md).[^cite_note-12]

On 2 May 2022, *Tripoli* departed [Naval Station San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Station_San_Diego) for the [Western Pacific Ocean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Pacific_Ocean) on her maiden deployment, taking on 20 F-35Bs at one point, in a test of the "lightning carrier" concept.[^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14] On 25 July 2022, she transitioned to an amphibious ready role by embarking the [31st Marine Expeditionary Unit](5885654.md) at [Naval Base Okinawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Base_Okinawa), Japan, before transiting the South China Sea to make a port call at Singapore's [Changi Naval Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Changi_Naval_Base) on 31 August 2022.[^cite_note-15] She returned to San Diego on 29 November 2022.[^cite_note-16]

In February 2025, *Tripoli* was confirmed to be deploying to Sasebo, Japan as the forward-deployed amphibious warship, replacing *America*.[^cite_note-17]

### COVID-19 pandemic

During the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_on_naval_ships), on 17 April 2020, Navy officials stated that at least nine sailors assigned to the ship had tested positive for [SARS-CoV-2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Severe_acute_respiratory_syndrome_coronavirus_2)[^cite_note-tripoli.wsj-18] while the ship was docked in Pascagoula, Mississippi.[^cite_note-delivery-5][^cite_note-20] About 630 sailors were moved off the ship as a preventative measure, and the outbreak spread to only "around a couple dozen sailors".[^cite_note-tripoli.tribune-19] Due to the pandemic the ship's public [commissioning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_commissioning) ceremony, originally planned for [NAS Pensacola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NAS_Pensacola) in June, was cancelled, and *Tripoli* was commissioned on 15 July 2020 in Pascagoula, Mississippi, where she had been built.[^cite_note-21]

### 2026 Iran war

On 28 March 2026 *Tripoli* arrived in the [US Central Command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Central_Command) (CENTCOM) 'area of responsibility'—which spans north-east Africa, the Middle East and central and south Asia—to provide support for [Operation Epic Fury](82533582.md).[^cite_note-22] *Tripoli* is part of an [amphibious ready group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphibious_ready_group) (ARG), consisting of [USS *San Diego*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_San_Diego_\(LPD-22\)) and [USS *New Orleans*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_New_Orleans_\(LPD-18\)) according to [USNI News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USNI_News). The ARG is reportedly transporting around 2,200 [Marine Corps](17349325.md) personnel from the [Okinawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okinawa_Island)-based [31st Marine Expeditionary Unit](5885654.md) (MEU) and will join the [USS *Abraham Lincoln*](199106.md)-led [Carrier Strike Group 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrier_Strike_Group_3) which is already deployed in the region. The embarked air wing of *Tripoli* includes around 20[^cite_note-23] [F-35B Lightning II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F-35B_Lightning_II) fighters of the [VMFA-121](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VMFA-121) “Green Knights”, [MV-22B Osprey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MV-22B_Osprey) of the [VMM-265](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VMM-265) "Dragons" and [MH-60S Seahawk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MH-60S_Seahawk) from [HSC-25](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HSC-25) "Island Knights".[^cite_note-\:0-24][^cite_note-\:1-25]

The ship had departed Okinawa on 11 March as per [MarineTraffic](48412872.md) and reached near [Singapore](27318.md), approaching the northern exit of the [Strait of Malacca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strait_of_Malacca), on 17 March 2026 as per the [AIS](1825702.md) tracking data accessed by the [CNN](62028.md). *San Diego* and *New Orleans* crossed the strait one day prior to *Tripoli*.[^cite_note-\:0-24][^cite_note-\:1-25] As of 20 March, the ship is located to the south of [Sri Lanka](26750.md) and is expected to enter the [Arabian Sea](2747.md) the following week. The ship will reach the war theatre around 22–23 March.[^cite_note-26][^cite_note-27]

On 19 April 2026, as the U.S. Navy was maintaining the [naval blockade of Iran](82937256.md), *Tripoli* along with the [*Arleigh Burke*-class](197341.md) [destroyer](8779.md) [USS *Spruance*](11831645.md) disabled the Iranian-flagged [*Touska*](82992016.md). *Tripoli* had deployed US Marines to board the ship and take control of the vessel.[^cite_note-28][^cite_note-29]

## Etymology

*Tripoli* is the third U.S. Navy ship named for the [Battle of Derne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Derne) in 1805. It was the decisive victory of a mercenary army led by a detachment of [United States Marines](17349325.md) and [soldiers](32087.md) against the forces of [Tripoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman_Tripolitania) during the [First Barbary War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Barbary_War). It was the first recorded land battle of the United States fought overseas.[^cite_note-32] *Fallujah*, after the [Second Battle of Fallujah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Battle_of_Fallujah), was suggested as a name but was ultimately not chosen.[^cite_note-33] This name was given to another *America*-class ship, [LHA-9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Fallujah).[^cite_note-LHA-9_Named-34]
