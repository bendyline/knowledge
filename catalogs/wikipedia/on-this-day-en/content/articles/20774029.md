**&#x20;San Dimas&#x20;**&#x20;is a [municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Durango) in the [Mexican](3966054.md) [state](8196151.md) of [Durango](222234.md). The municipal seat lies at [Tayoltita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tayoltita). The municipality covers an area of 5,620.5 km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 19,691,[^cite_note-SEDESOL-1] up from 19,303 as of 2005.[^cite_note-2]

The municipality has 590 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) are: [Tayoltita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tayoltita) (5,124), classified as urban, and [San Miguel de Cruces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Miguel_de_Cruces,_Durango) (1,816), classified as rural.[^cite_note-SEDESOL-1]
