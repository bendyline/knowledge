**Bagudo** is a town and [Local Government Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_Government_Areas_of_Nigeria) in [Kebbi State](2227643.md), [Nigeria](21383.md), sharing a boundary with the [Republic of Niger](21373.md) and [Republic of Benin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Benin).

It has an area of 7,782 km<sup>2</sup> and a population of 865,817 at the 2006 census.

The [postal code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_code) of the area is 871.[^cite_note-1]

Bagudo as how the name implies was founded by a Fulani Cattle Rearer called Wabudo, the town has a good settled population which are mainly Fula, Hausas and Zabarmawa with a little Yoruba population and the mainly spoken languages are Hausa and Fula. [Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_in_Nigeria) arrived the city in the 16 century or earlier primarily through the [trans-Saharan trade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans-Saharan_trade) and as a result became wealthy and the commercial nerve centre of the region and is still associated as the "[centre of Farming, Herdsmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Nigerian_states'_nicknames) and Many more.

## Geography, Temperature, Climate

The geography of Bagudo LGA encompasses a total expanse of 7,782 [square kilometres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_kilometre) (3,005 square miles) and encompasses two distinct seasons; the dry and rainy seasons. Notably, the [Niger River](21437.md) traverses through this region, contributing to a [humidity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humidity) level of approximately 12%, while the average temperature is 32 [°C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celsius) or 89.6 [°F](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C2%B0F).[^cite_note-\:0-2]

The temperature in the wet season is hot, oppressive, and overcast, while in the dry season it's sweltering and partly cloudy.[^cite_note-3]

Climate change is raising temperatures in Bagudo, affecting air quality, melting glaciers, rising sea levels, desertification, and the frequency of extreme weather events.[^cite_note-4]

## Economy

[Fishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fishing) emerges as a vital component, [coexisting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coexistence) with the predominant practice of [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture), where various [crops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crop) are cultivated in substantial quantities. Additionally, trade thrives within Bagudo Local Government Area, featuring several markets, including the bustling Lolo central market, offering a diverse array of commodities for prospective buyers.[^cite_note-\:0-2]
