**Hanwha Group** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 한화그룹) is a South Korean business conglomerate (*[chaebol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaebol)*).[^cite_note-\:3-2] Founded in 1952 as **Korea Explosives Co.** (한국화약주식회사; 韓國火藥株式會社), the group has grown into a large multi-profile business conglomerate, with diversified holdings stretching from explosives—their original business—to energy, materials, aerospace, mechatronics, finance, retail, and lifestyle services. In 1992, the company adopted its abbreviation as its new name: "Hanwha".

## History

### 1952–1999

[Kim Chong-hee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Chong-hee) founded Korea Explosives Co. in October 1952. Prior to founding the company, Kim worked as a [gunpowder engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Explosives_engineering) for the "Chosun Explosives Factory", a [Japanese](15573.md) company. Later, he won the bid for the company and its [Incheon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incheon) factory and started the company there.[^cite_note-\:0-3][^cite_note-\:1-4]

From 1952 to 1963, the Korea Explosives Co. produced industrial explosives domestically, which was needed for construction and engineering of infrastructure. In the same time period, the Korea Explosives Co. started producing [nitroglycerin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nitroglycerin), which gave it a monopoly in the field of explosives and [gunpowder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gunpowder).[^cite_note-\:0-3] In 1959, the Hanwha group started producing domestic [dynamite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamite).[^cite_note-\:1-4]

From 1964 to 1980 the Hanwha group started to make investments in various fields, starting the foundation of it becoming a chaebol. Later in the mid-1960s, Korea Hwasung industrial Co. was founded (now [Hanwha Solutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanwha_Solutions)), and entered the petrochemical market. Hanwha increased its competitiveness in the machinery market by acquiring Shinhan Bearing Industrial.[^cite_note-\:0-3] Hanwha founded Kyung-in Energy in 1969, and the Hankook Precision Tools (now [Hanwha Corporation/Momentum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanwha_Corporation?action=edit\&redlink=1)) followed suit, being founded in 1971.[^cite_note-\:0-3]

From 1981 to 1995, [Kim Seung-youn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Seung-yeon) became the second chairman of the company, and more investments in diverse markets were initiated. It expanded further into the chemical industry by acquiring both Hanyang Chemicals (now also [Hanwha Solutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanwha_Solutions)) and Dow Chemicals Korea in 1982, expanded into the resorts industry by acquiring the Junga group (now [Hanwha Hotels & Resorts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanwha_Hotels_&_Resorts?action=edit\&redlink=1)) in 1985, and expanded into the leisure and distribution industry by acquiring Hanyang Stores (now [Hanwha Galleria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanwha_Galleria?action=edit\&redlink=1)) in 1986.[^cite_note-\:0-3] In the 1990s, the Hanwha Group founded Hanwha BASF Urethane, Hanwha NSK Precision, Hanwha GKN, Hanwha Machinery Hub Eye Bearings, SKF Hanwha Auto Parts, and Hanwha Motors. In 1992, the Korea Explosives Co. changed its name to Hanwha, and [Binggrae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binggrae) was separated and made independent from the company.[^cite_note-\:0-3]

### 2000 to present



[Hanwha 63 City building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/63_Building) in [Yeouido](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeouido), [Seoul](19159283.md)

In 2002, Hanwha expanded into the life insurance industry by acquiring Korea Life Insurance. From 2007–present, Hanwha is undergoing global expansion. Hanwha acquired Azdel, an [American](3434750.md) company in 2007 and established a PVC plant in [Ningbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ningbo), [Zhejiang](96602.md), [China](5405.md) in 2011.[^cite_note-\:0-3] [Hanwha Q CELLS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanwha_Q_Cells) was launched in 2012. In 2014, Hanwha acquired Samsung Techwin, Samsung Thales, and Samsung Total.[^cite_note-\:0-3] Since 2019, Hanwha is operating the largest solar module plant (1.7 GW) in the United States; It is located in [Dalton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalton,_Georgia), [Georgia](48830.md).[^cite_note-\:2-5] As of 2019, Hanwha has a total of 466 affiliates, 84 being domestic and 382 overseas.[^cite_note-\:0-3] Hanwha completed its take over of Daewoo Shipbuilding & Marine Engineering (DSME), renaming it [Hanwha Ocean](16742825.md) in 2023.[^cite_note-6]

In April 2026, Hanwha Group rose to fifth place among [Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korea)’s business conglomerates.[^cite_note-7]

#### Controversy

In 2011, Kim Seung-yeon, the current chairman of the Hanwha Group, was fined 5.1 billion KRW and was jailed for 4 years on charges of embezzlement and breach of trust.[^cite_note-8][^cite_note-\:3-2]

## Business areas

### Energy & Materials

#### Solar Energy

In a meeting held on May 21, 2022, Kim Dong-kwan, the president of Hanwha Solutions, laid down a plan to spend 36.7 trillion KRW on its energy and aerospace sectors.[^cite_note-9] He said he wanted to commit the company to solar energy to reduce its carbon footprint and supply high quality energy. Additionally, he announced plans to build another modular factory in the United States.[^cite_note-\:2-5] In order to tackle the problem of stagnating bee populations, Hanwha has created a solar beehive, that helps to protect and maintain a stable bee population.[^cite_note-10] [Hanwha QCELLS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanwha_Q_CELLS) has launched a new brand that ventures into the electric vehicle charging market called Hanwha Motiev in 2022.[^cite_note-11]

#### Wind Energy

Hanwha operates in the onshore and offshore wind power generation sectors, with a focus on Europe through its subsidiary Q Energy, as well as the domestic market.[^cite_note-12]

#### Hydrogen

Hanwha develops a hydrogen value chain, which includes production, utilization, and storage services, utilizing renewable energy sources. Hanwha acquired Thomassen Energy/PSM in July 2021, which enables retrofitting of gas turbines for hydrogen use.[^cite_note-13]

#### Materials

[Hanwha Solutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanwha_Solutions) produces basic petrochemical products, such as [PVC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyvinyl_chloride) (Polyvinyl Chloride), LLDPE (Linear Low-Density Polyethylene), [CA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caustic_soda) (Caustic Soda), ASR (Alkali Water Soluble Resin), and [TDI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toluene_diisocyanate) (Toluene Diisocyanate), as well as working on eco-friendly technologies including low-impact [plasticizers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plasticizers).[^cite_note-14]

### Aerospace

#### Space

Hanwha is also extensively investing in the space market, and jointly established "Space Research Center" with [KAIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KAIST). Hanwha Aerospace contributes to South Korea's space industry, including satellite observation services and the development of the [Nuri](31909917.md) space launch vehicle. Hanwha took an 8.8% stake in British company [OneWeb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OneWeb), a satellite communication service provider as of 2022.[^cite_note-15]

#### Aircraft Engine

[Hanwha Aerospace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanwha_Aerospace) is South Korea's sole aircraft engine producer, partnering with global aviation engine companies for component manufacturing.[^cite_note-16]

#### Vision Solutions

[Hanwha Vision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanwha_Vision) (formerly Samsung Techwin, before getting acquired by Hanwha) delivers video surveillance services with a focus on optics design, image processing, and AI-based platforms to improve service offerings.[^cite_note-17]

### Finance

Hanwha operates in the financial industry, providing insurance, securities, and asset-management services, and investing in technology-based financial services.

### Retail & Services

Hanwha engages in retail & services such as department stores, hotels, and resorts, as well as developing large-scale complexes.[^cite_note-18]
