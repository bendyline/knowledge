**Suliman Arcua Minnawi** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): سليمان أركو مناوي; born 12 December 1968), also known as "**Minni Minnawi**" (مني مناوي), is a Sudanese politician and the leader of a faction of the [Sudanese Liberation Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudanese_Liberation_Army). A former educator, Minnawi served as secretary to Sudan Liberation Army leader [Abdul Wahid al-Nur](20343335.md) before the organization split in 2004. Minnawi has occupied every political identity in modern day Sudan. He was a prominent rebel commander of the Darfuri rebel group [Sudan Liberation Movement](2031543.md) (SLM) during the 2000s conflict, later signed a peace agreement to join the government, after which he went back into opposition, and was finally appointed Governor of Darfur under a transitional framework after the sigining of the Juba peace accord.[^cite_note-4]

Minnawi signed a treaty, known as the [Darfur Peace Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darfur_Peace_Agreement), with the [Khartoum](17320.md) government in May 2006. Nevertheless, in July 2006, fighting broke out around the [North Darfur](767666.md) town of Korma, resulting in the deaths of at least 80 people.[^cite_note-5] In the same year, Minnawi was appointed the top Sudanese official in the Darfur region, as chairman of the [Transitional Darfur Regional Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitional_Darfur_Regional_Authority), and was technically the fourth ranking member of the Presidency, as [Senior Assistant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_President_of_Sudan) to the [President of the Republic](450116.md).

On 14 September 2006, in defiance of the central government's opposition, Minnawi supported the [new UN peacekeeping force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darfur_conflict#New_peacekeeping_force) detailed in [UNSC Resolution 1706](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Security_Council_Resolution_1706), which was designed to protect the Sudanese people.[^cite_note-6] Minnawi opposed the Sudan government's genocidal agenda, which was exercised by [Omar al-Bashir](393625.md) against the [Zaghawa people](1467480.md) and other civilians, alleged crimes for which Bashir was later indicted by the International Criminal Court (ICC).[^cite_note-7]

In December 2010, after repeated complaints of non-compliance by the [National Congress Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Congress_\(Sudan\)) with the peace accords, the Sudan Liberation Movement under Minnawi withdrew from the [Darfur Peace Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darfur_Peace_Agreement).[^cite_note-8] Minnawi resigned as Senior Assistant to the President of Sudan and as Chairperson of the Transitional Darfur Regional Authority,[^cite_note-9] to ally his faction, SLM/MM, with the other resistance defense forces in Darfur, including Abdul Wahid's faction the SLM/AW, in resisting attacks by the Sudanese Armed Forces, and its militias, on citizens in rebel-held areas. In 2011, SLM/MM joined the [Sudan Revolutionary Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudan_Revolutionary_Front) (SRF) opposed to the Khartoum Government.[^cite_note-10]

In May 2021, Minni Minnawi was appointed governor of the [Darfur Regional Government](14497212.md) after signing the [Juba Peace Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juba_Peace_Agreement) the previous year. He was inaugurated on 10 August.[^cite_note-11]

During the [Sudanese Civil War](73556699.md), the rival [Government of Peace and Unity](79260861.md) (GPU) appointed El Hadi Idris Yahya as governor of their alternative Darfur regional administration in June 2024 during. The GPU-affiliated [Rapid Support Forces](43053874.md) militia took control of [El-Fashir](3956787.md), the capital of [Darfur](605837.md) in October 2025. Minni Minnawi continues to lead the *de jure* Darfur Regional Government from [Port Sudan](213572.md).
