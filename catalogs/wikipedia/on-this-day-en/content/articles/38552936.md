# National Hero of Indonesia

&#x20;  **National Hero of Indonesia** ([Indonesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_language): *Pahlawan Nasional Indonesia*) is the highest-level [title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orders,_decorations,_and_medals_of_Indonesia) awarded in [Indonesia](14579.md).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELaw_of_the_Republic_of_Indonesia_No._20_of_2009-1] It is posthumously given by the [Government of Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Indonesia) for actions which are deemed to be heroic, defined as "actual deeds which can be remembered and exemplified for all time by other citizens"[^cite_note-2] or "extraordinary service furthering the interests of the state and people".[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-FOOTNOTEIndonesian_State_Secretariat,_Prosedur-4] The [Ministry of Social Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Social_Affairs_\(Indonesia\)) gives seven criteria which an individual must fulfill, as follows:[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEIndonesian_State_Secretariat,_Prosedur-4]

1. Have been an Indonesian citizen

   [^cite_note-5]

   &#x20;who is deceased and, during their lifetime, led an armed struggle or produced a concept or product useful to the state;

2. Have continued the struggle throughout their life and performed above and beyond the call of duty;

3. Have had a wide-reaching impact through their actions;

4. Have shown a high degree of [nationalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nationalism);

5. Have been of good moral standing and respectable character;

6. Never surrendered to their enemies; and

7. Never committed an act which taints their legacy.

   [^cite_note-6]

Nominations undergo a four-step process and must be approved at each level. A proposal is made by the general populace in a [city or regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_regencies_and_cities_of_Indonesia) to the mayor or regent, who must then make a request to the [province's governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_current_provincial_governors_in_Indonesia). The governor then makes a recommendation to the Ministry of Social Affairs, which forwards it to the [president](10011631.md), represented by the Board of Titles (*Dewan Gelar*);[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEIndonesian_State_Secretariat,_Prosedur-4] this board consists of two academics, two persons of a military background, and three persons who have previously received an award or title.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTELaw_of_the_Republic_of_Indonesia_No._20_of_2009-1] Those selected by the president, as represented by the Board, are awarded the title at a ceremony in the Indonesian capital of [Jakarta](16275.md).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEIndonesian_State_Secretariat,_Prosedur-4] Since 2000, the ceremony has occurred in early November, coinciding with [Indonesia's Heroes' Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroes_Day_\(Indonesia\)) (*Hari Pahlawan*).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEIndonesian_State_Secretariat,_Daftar_Nama_Pahlawan_(2)-7]

The legal framework for the title, initially styled National Independence Hero (*Pahlawan Kemerdekaan Nasional*), was established with the release of Presidential Decree No. 241 of 1958. The title was first awarded on 30 August 1959 to the politician turned writer [Abdul Muis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Muis), who had died the previous month.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEIndonesian_State_Secretariat,_Daftar_Nama_Pahlawan_(1)-8][^cite_note-FOOTNOTEJCG,_Abdul_Muis-9][^cite_note-FOOTNOTEArtaria2002539-10] This title was used for the rest of [Sukarno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukarno)'s rule. When [Suharto](217513.md) rose to power in the mid-1960s, the title was given its current name. Special titles at the level of National Hero have also been awarded. Hero of the Revolution (*Pahlawan Revolusi*) was given in 1965 to ten victims of the [30 September Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/30_September_Movement) that resulted in end of Sukarno reign, while Sukarno and former vice-president [Mohammad Hatta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Hatta) were given the title Proclamator Heroes (*Pahlawan Proklamator*) in 1986 for their role in reading the [Proclamation of Indonesian Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proclamation_of_Indonesian_Independence).[^cite_note-FOOTNOTEIndonesian_State_Secretariat,_Daftar_Nama_Pahlawan_(2)-7][^cite_note-FOOTNOTEIndonesian_State_Secretariat,_Daftar_Nama_Pahlawan_(1)-8][^cite_note-FOOTNOTEArtaria2002539-10]

A total of 198 men and 18 women have been deemed national heroes.[^cite_note-hero2023-11] These heroes have come from all parts of the [Indonesian archipelago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_archipelago), from [Aceh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aceh) in the west to [Papua](15199.md) in the east. They represent numerous [ethnicities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_groups_in_Indonesia), including [native Indonesians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Indonesians), [ethnic Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Indonesians), [Arabs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_Indonesians) and [Eurasians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo_people). They include [prime ministers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Prime_Ministers_of_Indonesia), [guerrillas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guerrilla), government ministers, soldiers, royalty, journalists, and a bishop.

The following list is initially presented in alphabetical order; owing to differing cultural naming conventions, not all entries are sorted by last name. The list is further sortable by year of birth, death, and recognition. Names are standardised using the [Indonesian Spelling System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enhanced_Indonesian_Spelling_System) and thus may not reflect the original spelling.[^cite_note-12]
