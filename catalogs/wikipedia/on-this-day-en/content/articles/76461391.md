**Andris Sprūds** (born 13 July 1971) is a Latvian foreign policy researcher, lecturer, and politician. He is a professor at [Rīga Stradiņš University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riga_Stradi%C5%86%C5%A1_University),[^cite_note-1] a member of the [14th Saeima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourteenth_Saeima_of_Latvia),[^cite_note-2] and former [Minister of Defence of Latvia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ministers_of_defence_of_Latvia). He represents the [Progressives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progressives_\(Latvia\)) party.

## Personal life

In 1997, he obtained a master's degree in Central European history from [Central European University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_European_University) in Budapest, and in 1998, a master's degree in political science in international relations from [University of Latvia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Latvia), and in 2005, he defended his doctoral degree in political science at [Jagiellonian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University) in Krakow, Poland.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-\:1-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6] He has also studied and researched at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University), [University of Oxford](31797.md), [Uppsala University](31801.md), the Norwegian Institute of International Affairs, and the Japan Institute of Energy Economics on various topics related to energy security and policy in the Baltic Sea region, [post-Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-Soviet_states) domestic and foreign policy, as well as transatlantic relations.[^cite_note-\:0-7]

He has been a member of the advisory board of the Latvian Institute of International Affairs and its director.[^cite_note-\:0-7]

## Political career

On 1 October 2022, Sprūds was [elected to the 14th Saeima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Latvian_parliamentary_election) from the list of the [Progressives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progressives_\(Latvia\)) party, not being its member.[^cite_note-8] He works in the Foreign Affairs and European Affairs Committees of the Saeima. On 24 November 2022, he was elected chairman of the European Affairs Committee of the Saeima.[^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10] In 2023, he joined the Progressives party.[^cite_note-11]

On 15 September 2023, he was appointed [Minister of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ministers_of_defence_of_Latvia) in the [cabinet](74828536.md) of [Evika Siliņa](74610811.md).[^cite_note-\:1-4][^cite_note-12] Sprūds resigned on 10 May 2026 after a [drone incursion incident](83132995.md#Latvia), with Colonel [Raivis Melnis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raivis_Melnis) taking his place,[^cite_note-13] this may change after [Progressives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progressives_\(Latvia\)) talk to Prime Minister.[^cite_note-14] It is also possible that the Saeima will not elect Raivis Melnis a new Minister of Defense.[^cite_note-15]
