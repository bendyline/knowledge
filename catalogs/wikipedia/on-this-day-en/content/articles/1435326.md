***Andøya*** ([Norwegian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_language)), ***Ánda*** ([Northern Sami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Sami_language)), or ***Ánddasuolu*** ([Northern Sami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Sami_language))[^cite_note-1] is the northernmost island in the [Vesterålen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vester%C3%A5len) [archipelago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archipelago), situated about 300 kilometres (190 mi) inside the [Arctic Circle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arctic_Circle). Andøya is located in [Andøy Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/And%C3%B8y_Municipality) in [Nordland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nordland) county, [Norway](21241.md). The main population centres on the island include the villages of [Andenes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andenes), [Bleik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bleik), and [Risøyhamn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ris%C3%B8yhamn).

The island has an area of 489 square kilometres (189 sq mi), making it the [tenth largest island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_islands_of_Norway_by_area) in Norway. The island is connected to the neighboring island of [Hinnøya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hinn%C3%B8ya) via the [Andøy Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/And%C3%B8y_Bridge). The [Andfjorden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andfjorden) lies to the east of the island, the [Risøysundet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ris%C3%B8ysundet) strait lies to the south and east side of the island, and the [Gavlfjorden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gavlfjorden) lies to the southwest side. The [Norwegian Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Sea) lies to the west and north.[^cite_note-snl-2]

The numerous [bog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bog) areas on Andøya are used for the extensive production of [peat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peat). Andøya is also well known for its [cloudberries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloudberries). Besides the flat and largely continuous bog areas, Andøya also consists of steep mountain ranges reaching up to 700 metres (2,300 ft) high. The sharp peak of [Kvasstinden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kvasstinden) is the highest point on the island at 705 metres (2,313 ft). [Andøya Space](30864935.md), which was founded in 1962, is also located on the island near the [Andøya Air Station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/And%C3%B8ya_Air_Station).[^cite_note-3]

## Important Bird Area

A 10,000-hectare (25,000-acre) area comprising low-lying boggy ground in central Andøya, along with a stretch of the eastern coast and the lake of [Skogvollvatnet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skogvollvatnet) on the west coast, has been designated an [Important Bird Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Important_Bird_Area) (IBA) by [BirdLife International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BirdLife_International) (BLI) because it supports several thousand [barnacle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnacle_goose) and [pinkfooted geese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pink-footed_goose) on [passage migration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bird_migration), as well as about ten breeding pairs of [Eurasian curlews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurasian_curlew). Protected areas within or overlapping the site include the Skogvoll, Risøysundet, and Åholmen [nature reserves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nature_reserve) and the Skogvoll and Risøysundet [Ramsar sites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramsar_site).[^cite_note-bli-4]
