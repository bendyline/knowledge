**Lower Juba** ([Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_language): *Jubbada Hoose*, [Maay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maay_language): *Jubithy Hoosy*, [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): جوبا السفلى, [Italian](14708.md): *Basso Giuba*) is an administrative region (*[gobol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Somalia)*) in southern [Somalia](27358.md).[^cite_note-Cwfsom-4] With its capital at [Kismayo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kismayo), it lies in the [autonomous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_and_regions_of_Somalia) [Jubaland](38364812.md) region.&#x20;

Lower Juba is bordered by [Kenya](188171.md), the Somali regions of [Gedo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gedo) and [Middle Juba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Juba) (Jubbada Dhexe), and the Somali Sea. The province is named after the [Jubba River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jubba_River) that passes through it and empties into the Somali Sea at [Goobweyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goobweyn). The [Lag Badana National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lag_Badana_National_Park) is situated in Lower Juba.

## Districts

Lower Juba Region consists of 5 districts:[^cite_note-Dsts-5]

- [ Kismaayo District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kismaayo_District)

- [Afmadow District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afmadow_District)

- [Badhaadhe District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badhaadhe_District)

- [Jamaame District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamaame_District)

- [ Xagar District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xagar_District)

The [Bajuni Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bajuni_Islands) are also within the region.
