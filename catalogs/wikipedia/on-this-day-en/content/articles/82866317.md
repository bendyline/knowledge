On April 3, 2026, a [M<sub>w</sub>](6207163.md#Mw) 5.8 [earthquake](10106.md) struck the [Hindu Kush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_Kush) region of [Afghanistan](737.md). The epicenter was located 35 kilometres (22 mi) S of [Jurm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurm,_Afghanistan) at a depth of 188.0 km (117 mi). Twelve people were killed and six others were injured.[^cite_note-usnews-1][^cite_note-Express_News-2]

## Tectonic setting

The [Himalayas](37966.md), partially formed by the collision of [tectonic plates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tectonic_plate), are prone to devastating earthquakes. Afghanistan is situated near the southern extent of the [Eurasian plate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurasian_plate).[^cite_note-3] Most of these earthquakes are associated with [reverse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reverse_fault), [thrust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thrust_fault), or [strike-slip faulting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strike-slip_faulting). Large earthquakes with magnitudes of up to 7.5 have occurred in the region with an average recurrence interval of 15 years. These earthquakes correspond to reverse faulting at a depth of 170 to 280 km (110 to 170 mi).[^cite_note-Zarifi-4] These earthquakes, rather than occurring at a plate boundary, are sourced from within the Indian plate as it dives beneath the [Hindu Kush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_Kush). As the tectonic slab of the Indian plate descends at a near-vertical angle into the [mantle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mantle_\(geology\)), it stretches and begins to "tear", eventually leading to a [slab detachment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slab_detachment).[^cite_note-Kakar-5] This action results in stress accommodation along [faults](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fault_\(geology\)) that produces earthquakes when [ruptured](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earthquake_rupture). Smaller shallow focus earthquakes are also observed in the region, particularly associated with north–south trending zones of right lateral strike-slip, such as the [Chaman Fault](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaman_Fault), with an increasing degree of shortening to the north, together accommodating the highly oblique convergence between the [Indian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_plate) and the [Eurasian plates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurasian_plate).[^cite_note-Ambraseys_&_Bilham_2003-6]

## Earthquake

The earthquake struck about 35 km (22 mi) south of [Jurm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurm,_Afghanistan) in [Badakhshan Province](1282198.md). An estimated 16.54 million people in Afghanistan, [Pakistan](23235.md) and [Tajikistan](30108.md) felt MMI II–III (*Weak*) shaking.[^cite_note-USGS-7] Tremors were also felt across [Delhi](37756.md) and several parts of north [India](14533.md), including [Jammu and Kashmir](61449812.md) and [Punjab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjab,_India).[^cite_note-8] It was also felt in [Islamabad](51153.md) and [Gilgit-Baltistan](18807336.md), Pakistan.[^cite_note-9]

## Impact

Twelve people were killed and four others were injured in Afghanistan.[^cite_note-10] Five homes collapsed and another 33 were damaged in six [provinces of Afghanistan](412343.md), affecting 40 families.[^cite_note-usnews-1] The casualties included eight people killed and a three-year-old boy injured when a house collapsed near [Kabul](16826.md), all members of the same family.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12] Two people were injured in [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](21950.md), Pakistan.[^cite_note-Express_News-2] The [Kapoor Haveli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kapoor_Haveli) mansion in [Peshawar](66230.md) partially collapsed due to the shaking and heavy rainfall.[^cite_note-13] Authorities said that a solid assessment of damage could potentially be delayed because of the remoteness of the epicenter of the earthquake.[^cite_note-14] Due to the epicenter being remote, it can often take several hours before local authorities can relay information back to Kabul.[^cite_note-15] Provincial health authorities in the country were put on alert.[^cite_note-16]

## Responses

India announced on 5 April that they sent an aid package containing kitchen utensils, hygiene kits, blankets and other essential items to victims of the earthquake and recent floods in the country.[^cite_note-17]
