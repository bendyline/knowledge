**Eswatini–Taiwan relations** refer to the international relations between the [Eswatini](27451.md) (officially the Kingdom of Eswatini) and [Taiwan](25734.md) (officially the Republic of China). Eswatini maintains [an embassy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassy_of_Eswatini_in_Taipei) in [Taipei](57648.md), and Taiwan maintains [an embassy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embassy_of_Taiwan,_Mbabane) in [Mbabane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mbabane).

## History



Embassy of Taiwan in Mbabane

Eswatini recognized the Republic of China (ROC) over the [People's Republic of China](5405.md) (PRC) on 16 September 1968, and have since maintained formal diplomatic relations with the ROC.[^cite_note-ROC_Foreign_Relation_Yearbook_2016-1]

Eswatini is one of 12 nations that [officially recognizes Taiwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_recognition_of_Taiwan). In May 2018, [Burkina Faso](3470.md) switched to recognize the People's Republic of China,[^cite_note-2] thus ending diplomatic ties with Taiwan, making Eswatini the last African country to recognize the ROC instead of the PRC.[^cite_note-3]

On the 2018 summit of [Forum on China–Africa Cooperation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forum_on_China%E2%80%93Africa_Cooperation) in Beijing, China declared “we hope that by the time the China-Africa cooperation forum Beijing summit happens, we can have a happy picture of the whole family”, i.e. it hopes to establish diplomatic relations with Swaziland before the summit begins.[^cite_note-4] The [government of Eswatini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Eswatini) has rejected these overtures from the [People's Republic of China](5405.md).[^cite_note-5][^cite_note-2018-10-05-quartz-6] In response, China has turned up the visa restrictions on Swazis to force Eswatini to establish diplomatic links with itself. Declaring "No diplomatic relations, no business benefits." In response to the Chinese pressure on Eswatini, Ou Jiang'an, the director of the Foreign Ministry Information Department of Taiwan said "The ugly nature of the Chinese regime and its despicable means of suppression are contemptible, and it should be deterred by international public opinion."[^cite_note-7]

*[The Economist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Economist)* claimed in 2021 that Eswatini's relations with Taiwan are second in importance only to [those with South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eswatini%E2%80%93South_Africa_relations).[^cite_note-8]

## Bilateral visits



President [Tsai Ing-wen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsai_Ing-wen) meeting Prime Minister [Russell Dlamini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Dlamini)



President [Lai Ching-te](22609914.md) and King [Mswati III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mswati_III) in May 2026

In June 2018, King of Eswatini, [Mswati III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mswati_III), made his 17th[^cite_note-\:0-9] visit to Taiwan, after [Taiwan President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan_President) [Tsai Ing-wen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsai_Ing-wen) visited Eswatini in April.[^cite_note-10]

In April 2026, [Taiwan President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan_President) [Lai Ching-te](22609914.md) suspended his planned trip to Eswatini after China pressured [Seychelles](27288.md), [Mauritius](19201.md), and [Madagascar](18964.md) to revoke overflight permissions for his chartered plane.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13] Additionally, the Czech and German governments denied requests for Taiwan to let President Lai to fly through Czech and German airspace en route to Eswatini.[^cite_note-14] Despite China's pressures, [Taiwan's Minister of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Foreign_Affairs_\(Taiwan\)) [Lin Chia-lung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lin_Chia-lung) arrived in Eswatini 5 days later, saying Taiwan would not be held back by "authoritarian forces".[^cite_note-15] Following this incident, Eswatini Deputy Prime Minister [Thuli Dladla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thuli_Dladla) swiftly visited Taiwan on 30 April. President Lai was quoted by the [Presidential Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_the_President_\(Taiwan\)) saying "the friendship between Taiwan and Eswatini cannot be shaken by Chinese pressure, as the two nations do not bow to hegemons".[^cite_note-16]

On 2 May 2026, President Lai Ching-te travelled to Eswatini.[^cite_note-17] This act angered China, who described the visit as a "stowaway-style escape farce" and condemned Lai as a "rat".[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19]

During the state visit, Lai was received by King Mswati III at Mandvulo Grand Hall and welcomed by Prime Minister [Russell Dlamini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Dlamini).[^cite_note-s24-receive-20] Eswatini's Minister of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation, Senator [Pholile Shakantu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pholile_Shakantu), and Taiwan's Foreign Minister [Lin Chia-lung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lin_Chia-lung) signed a Joint Communiqué to strengthen trade and cooperation between the two countries, together with a customs agreement.[^cite_note-s24-receive-20] Around the time of the visit, China extended tariff-free access to its market to African countries with which it holds diplomatic ties while continuing to exclude Eswatini, which remained the only African state without such access owing to its recognition of Taipei.[^cite_note-s24-receive-20][^cite_note-npr-eswatini-21] Lai concluded the visit on 4 May and returned to Taiwan the following day, his aircraft escorted on the final leg of the journey by four [Republic of China Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_China_Air_Force) [F-16](11642.md) jets.[^cite_note-s24-f16-22]

## Economic relations

In 2024, Eswatini exported US\$171,000 to Taiwan and Taiwan exported US\$7.88 million to Eswatini. The main products that Eswatini exports to Taiwan are frozen beef, raw sugar and processed fruits and nuts; the main products that Taiwan exports to Eswatini are rice, [paper machines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paper_machine) and textile footwear.

From 2019 to 2024, exports from Eswatini to Taiwan decreased at an annualized rate of 25.3%, from US\$735,000 in 2019 to US\$171,000 in 2024. Exports from Taiwan to Eswatini decreased at an annualized rate of 14%, from US\$16.8 million in 2019 to US\$7.88 million in 2024.[^cite_note-23]

Taiwan has been constructing a large [industrial park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_park) and strategic oil reserve in Eswatini, named the Taiwan Innovation Park.[^cite_note-24] Outside of the Taiwan Innovation Park, Taiwanese firms in 2025 provided 13,000 jobs for Eswatini.[^cite_note-25]

## Taiwanese aid

Taiwan has provided economic aid to Eswatini, funding a [rural electrification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rural_electrification) scheme and university scholarships.[^cite_note-26][^cite_note-\:0-9] Over 25 years it is estimated that aid is about two billion dollars.[^cite_note-27]

In September 2021, Taiwan announced it would be providing US\$22.9 million in aid to repair schools, hospitals and other infrastructure that were damaged during the [civil unrest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Eswatini_protests).[^cite_note-28]
