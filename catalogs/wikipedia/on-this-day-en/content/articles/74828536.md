The **Evika Siliņa cabinet** ([Latvian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvian_language): *Siliņas ministru kabinets*) was the 42nd [government](19235330.md) of [Latvia](17514.md) between 15 September 2023 and 28 May 2026, led by [Evika Siliņa](74610811.md) from [New Unity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Unity) as [Prime Minister](481129.md).[^cite_note-4]

The government began as a coalition between [New Unity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Unity), [Union of Greens and Farmers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_of_Greens_and_Farmers), and [The Progressives](57853722.md).[^cite_note-5]

After the explosion on 7 May in [Rēzekne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C4%93zekne) caused by Ukrainian strayed drones targeting Russia, losing public trust at defense, [Andris Sprūds](76461391.md) from the Progressive Party resigned on 11 May. Losing the ruling majority, Siliņa stepped down after the Progressive Party withdrew their support for her government.[^cite_note-6]
