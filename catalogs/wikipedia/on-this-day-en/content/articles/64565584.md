The **AFC Women's Champions League** is the top-tier [women's football](191466.md) club competition in Asia. It involves the top clubs from countries affiliated with the [Asian Football Confederation](715249.md) (AFC). The tournament was established in 2024.[^cite_note-afc24-1]

## History

The concept of an Asian women's club competition was first recommended in 2018.[^cite_note-2] The following year, the [inaugural edition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_AFC_Women's_Club_Championship) of the [AFC Women's Club Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFC_Women's_Club_Championship), a pilot women's club championship for Asian teams, was held as a [round-robin tournament](939742.md) among four teams.[^cite_note-3]

In 2023, the format for the new club competition called AFC Women's Champions League was unveiled. Starting with the [2024–25](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_AFC_Women's_Champions_League) edition, the competition includes a preliminary stage, a twelve-team group stage and a knockout stage. Each association gets one team to enter.[^cite_note-afc24-1][^cite_note-4] In the first four seasons, there will be one entry per participating member association,[^cite_note-5] with allocation based on the AFC Women's Club Competitions Ranking, which will combine their clubs' performance in the AFC Women's Champions League and the latest [FIFA Women's World Ranking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_Women's_World_Ranking) as follow:[^cite_note-6]

- up to 2027–28: 70% club points + 30% national team points;

- from 2028–29 to 2029–30: 90% club points + 10% national team points;

- from 2030–31 onwards: 100% club points

## Prize money

Starting with the 2024–25 season, the distribution of the prize money is as follows.[^cite_note-11]

- Group stage: \$100,000

- Quarter-finals: \$80,000

- Semi-finals: \$120,000

- Runners-up: \$500,000

- Champions: \$1,000,000
