The **Belgium women's national basketball team** (nicknamed **Belgian Cats**) represents [Belgium](3343.md) in international [basketball](667977.md), and are controlled by [Basketball Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_Belgium). Belgium's first appearance in a major international tournament came at [EuroBasket 1950](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_1950_Women). The team has participated in the [European Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_Women) 15 times overall. Their best results at the event are winning two titles ([2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_Women_2023),[^cite_note-2] [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_Women_2025)[^cite_note-3]) and two third place finishes ([2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_Women_2017),[^cite_note-4] and [2021](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_Women_2021)[^cite_note-5]). Belgium has also competed on the global stage, where they have made three appearances at the [World Cup](5779563.md) ([2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_FIBA_Women's_Basketball_World_Cup),[^cite_note-6] [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_FIBA_Women's_Basketball_World_Cup),[^cite_note-7] [2026](73471455.md)[^cite_note-8]), and two at the [Olympic Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_at_the_Summer_Olympics) ([2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_at_the_2020_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_tournament),[^cite_note-9] [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_tournament)[^cite_note-10]). Belgium will be the co-host for the FIBA Women's EuroBasket in [2027](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroBasket_Women_2027) along with Finland, Sweden and Lithuania.
