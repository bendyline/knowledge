**Garden Grove** is a city in northern [Orange County, California](45330.md), United States. The population was 171,949 at the [2020 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census). [State Route 22](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_Route_22), also known as the [Garden Grove Freeway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garden_Grove_Freeway), passes through the city in an east–west direction. The western portion of the city is known as [West Garden Grove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Garden_Grove).

## History



Garden Grove, c. 1950s

### Nineteenth century

[Rancho Las Bolsas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rancho_Las_Bolsas) was a land grant under Mexican governance that primarily dealt in cattle ranching in the central Orange County floodplains. Native inhabitants were displaced under the Spanish colonization and Mexican land grants.[^cite_note-\:1-7]

Garden Grove was founded by [Alonzo Cook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alonzo_Cook) in 1874. A school district and [Methodist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methodist) church were organized that year. It remained a small rural crossroads and farming community until the arrival of the Pacific Electric Railroad in 1905. The rail connection helped the town prosper with the influx of tourists, visitors and eventually settlers,[^cite_note-8] and it was noted for its crops of [oranges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_\(fruit\)), [walnuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walnut), [chili peppers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chili_pepper) and later [strawberries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strawberry).[^cite_note-9]

### Twentieth century

In 1933, much of the town's central business district was destroyed by the [Long Beach earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Beach_earthquake), and one person was killed at the high school. The post-[World War II](32927.md) boom led to rapid development, and Garden Grove was incorporated as a city in 1956 with about 44,000 residents.[^cite_note-10]

In 1956, [Orange County Plaza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_County_Plaza) (now The Promenade) was opened at Chapman and Brookhurst, and upon its expansion in 1959, it had 60 stores, including a [J. C. Penney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._C._Penney), 2 variety stores and 2 supermarkets, and billed itself as both the largest and the first regional shopping center in Orange County.[^cite_note-11][^cite_note-Independent_Press-Telegram_1959-02-23-12][^cite_note-13]

### Twenty-first century

During the 1970s, Korean Americans began to move into Garden Grove, in several waves of migration.[^cite_note-\:1-7] It eventually turned the area into "Little Seoul" (also referred to as [Korea Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koreatown,_Garden_Grove)), and grew to be the second largest Korean business district on the west coast. Since its initial establishment, the Korean American population has expanded to other nearby cities like Fullerton and Irvine, but it claims to have the oldest "Little Seoul" in Orange County.

Main article: [Garden Grove chemical leak](83247730.md)

In May 2026, a leak of [methyl methacrylate](1985376.md) at the [GKN Aerospace](424537.md) plant forced the evacuation of 40,000 residents in the immediate area due to the risk of the 34,000 gallon tank either spilling over or exploding.[^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16]

## Geography

Garden Grove has a rugged set of boundaries with many [panhandles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salient_\(geography\)). The [West Garden Grove](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Garden_Grove) neighborhood is west of [Beach Boulevard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_Route_39) and is largely separated from the rest of Garden Grove by the city of [Stanton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanton,_California), with a small bridge of jurisdiction linking the two along Garden Grove Boulevard. A panhandle in the southern part of the town's borders situated between [Westminster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westminster,_California)'s Ward Street to the west and [Santa Ana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Ana,_California)'s Euclid Street to the east creates a small border with the city of [Fountain Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fountain_Valley,_California). Other neighboring cities include [Cypress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cypress,_California) and [Anaheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anaheim) to the north, [Orange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange,_California) to the east, and the cities of [Seal Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seal_Beach) and [Los Alamitos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Alamitos,_California) to the west.[^cite_note-17]

According to the [United States Census Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Census_Bureau), the city has a total area of 46.5 km<sup>2</sup> (18.0 mi<sup>2</sup>), 0.10% of which is water.

### 2020 census

The [2020 United States census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_United_States_census) reported that Garden Grove had a population of 171,949. The population density was 9,576.1 inhabitants per square mile (3,697.4/km<sup>2</sup>). The racial makeup of Garden Grove was 21.9% [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Americans), 1.0% [African American](2154.md), 1.2% [Native American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Americans_in_the_United_States), 42.4% [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Americans), 0.5% [Pacific Islander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Islander_Americans), 20.9% from [other races](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_\(United_States_Census\)), and 12.1% from two or more races. [Hispanic or Latino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_and_Latino_Americans) of any race were 37.3% of the population.[^cite_note-DP1-39]

The census reported that 98.8% of the population lived in households, 0.8% lived in non-institutionalized group quarters, and 0.3% were institutionalized.[^cite_note-DP1-39]

There were 47,362 households, out of which 39.3% included children under the age of 18, 53.3% were married-couple households, 5.7% were [cohabiting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohabitation) couple households, 24.8% had a female householder with no partner present, and 16.2% had a male householder with no partner present. 14.2% of households were one person, and 7.3% were one person aged 65 or older. The average household size was 3.59.[^cite_note-DP1-39] There were 38,030 [families](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_\(U.S._Census\)) (80.3% of all households).[^cite_note-40]

The age distribution was 20.9% under the age of 18, 10.0% aged 18 to 24, 26.2% aged 25 to 44, 28.2% aged 45 to 64, and 14.7% who were 65 years of age or older. The median age was 39.1 years. For every 100 females, there were 97.4 males.[^cite_note-DP1-39]

There were 48,562 housing units at an average density of 2,704.5 units per square mile (1,044.2 units/km<sup>2</sup>), of which 47,362 (97.5%) were occupied. Of these, 54.0% were owner-occupied, and 46.0% were occupied by renters.[^cite_note-DP1-39]

In 2023, the US Census Bureau estimated that 45.3% of the population were foreign-born. Of all people aged 5 or older, 32.1% spoke only English at home, 29.0% spoke [Spanish](26825.md), 1.1% spoke other [Indo-European languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-European_languages), 36.8% spoke Asian or Pacific Islander languages, and 0.9% spoke other languages. Of those aged 25 or older, 75.4% were high school graduates and 24.9% had a bachelor's degree.[^cite_note-41]

The median household income was \$90,166, and the [per capita income](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Per_capita_income) was \$32,387. About 9.4% of families and 12.2% of the population were below the poverty line.[^cite_note-42]

### 2010

The [2010 United States census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_United_States_census)[^cite_note-43] reported that Garden Grove had a population of 170,883. The population density was 9,515.3 inhabitants per square mile (3,673.9/km<sup>2</sup>). The racial makeup of Garden Grove was 68,149 (39.9%) [White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_American), 2,155 (1.3%) [Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_American), 983 (0.6%) [Native American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_Americans_in_the_United_States), 63,451 (37.1%) [Asian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_American), 1,110 (0.6%) [Pacific Islander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Islander_American), 28,916 (16.9%) from [other races](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Race_\(United_States_Census\)), and 6,119 (3.6%) from two or more races. [Hispanic or Latino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_and_Latino_Americans) of any race were 63,079 persons (36.9%). [Non-Hispanic whites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-Hispanic_whites) were 22.6% of the population,[^cite_note-44] down from 90.6% in 1970.[^cite_note-45] [Vietnamese Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnamese_Americans) numbered [47,331](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_U.S._cities_with_large_Vietnamese-American_populations) of the population. At 27.7% this was the highest concentration of any city in the United States except for adjacent [Westminster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westminster,_California).

The census reported that 168,942 people (98.9% of the population) lived in households, 1,234 (0.7%) lived in non-institutionalized group quarters, and 707 (0.4%) were institutionalized.

There were 46,037 households, out of which 21,361 (46.4%) had children under the age of 18 living in them, 26,659 (57.9%) were [opposite-sex married couples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marriage) living together, 6,866 (14.9%) had a female householder with no husband present, 3,588 (7.8%) had a male householder with no wife present. There were 2,025 (4.4%) [unmarried opposite-sex partnerships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/POSSLQ), and 269 (0.6%) [same-sex married couples or partnerships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Same-sex_partnerships). 6,491 households (14.1%) were made up of individuals, and 2,842 (6.2%) had someone living alone who was 65 years of age or older. The average household size was 3.67. There were 37,113 [families](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_\(U.S._Census\)) (80.6% of all households); the average family size was 3.94.

The population was spread out, with 43,763 people (25.6%) under the age of 18, 17,383 people (10.2%) aged 18 to 24, 49,105 people (28.7%) aged 25 to 44, 42,106 people (24.6%) aged 45 to 64, and 18,526 people (10.8%) who were 65 years of age or older. The median age was 35.6 years. For every 100 females, there were 99.6 males. For every 100 females age 18 and over, there were 7.7 males.

There were 47,755 housing units at an average density of 2,659.1 per square mile (1,026.7/km<sup>2</sup>), of which 26,240 (57.0%) were owner-occupied, and 19,797 (43.0%) were occupied by renters. The homeowner vacancy rate was 1.2%; the rental vacancy rate was 4.6%. 96,308 people (56.4% of the population) lived in owner-occupied housing units and 72,634 people (42.5%) lived in rental housing units.

According to the 2010 United States census, Garden Grove had a median household income of \$59,988, with 15.5% of the population living below the federal poverty line.[^cite_note-46]

## Arts and culture



Main Street Archway

Garden Grove is home to two stage theaters, the Gem Theater and the Festival Amphitheater. The Festival Amphitheater hosts Shakespeare Orange County, which presents an annual Shakespeare Festival each summer. Both venues are owned by the City of Garden Grove, but operated by outside entities.

The [Garden Grove Playhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garden_Grove_Playhouse) used to be an active theatre, now closed down. It was operated by a non-profit group of the same name.

### Memorial Day

An annual event held over [Memorial Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memorial_Day) weekend, the Garden Grove Strawberry Festival is one of the largest community festivals in the western United States, attracting an estimated 250,000 visitors.[^cite_note-48][^cite_note-49] It began in 1958 and celebrates the city's agricultural past, which includes cultivating chili peppers, oranges, walnuts and strawberries. Part of the festivities include the cutting of the world's largest [strawberry shortcake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strawberry_shortcake_\(dessert\)), carnival rides, food vendors, live music, and a celebrity-filled parade.[^cite_note-50][^cite_note-51] Numerous Garden Grove organizations, including the Miss Garden Grove Scholarship Program, are part of the Memorial Day weekend festivities every year. In commemoration of Garden Grove's 50th anniversary, the city painted some of its [fire hydrants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_hydrant) with a design that featured a strawberry, recognizing the festival as a big part of Garden Grove's history.[^cite_note-52][^cite_note-53]

## Government

See also: [List of mayors of Garden Grove, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_mayors_of_Garden_Grove,_California)

The mayor is Stephanie Klopfenstein.[^cite_note-54]

Garden Grove uses a [council-manager](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council-manager) form of government. In July 2015, the city was sued by a resident who claimed that the longstanding at-large elections had affected the Latino vote and was in violation of the [California Voting Rights Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Voting_Rights_Act). On January 26, 2016, the city council voted to settle the lawsuit, and therefore adopted that council members would be voted by district (six districts total) and no longer at-large; the mayor, however, will continue to be elected at-large.[^cite_note-55] The [city council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_council) consists of [mayor](101146.md) Stephanie Klopfenstein, George S. Brietigam III, Phillip Nguyen, Cindy Ngoc Tran, Joe DoVinh, Yesenia Muneton, and Ariana Arestegui.[^cite_note-56] According to the city's most recent Comprehensive Annual Financial Report, the city's various funds had \$206.0 million in revenues, \$193.0 million in expenditures, \$1,098.9 million in total assets, \$251.5 million in total liabilities, and \$196.3 million in cash and investments.[^cite_note-City_of_Garden_Grove_CAFR-57]

### Politics

According to the [Orange County Registrar of Voters](https://ocvote.gov/), as of May 15, 2025, Garden Grove has 94,969 registered voters.[^cite_note-59] Of those, 26,604 (36.56%) are registered Democrats, 21,449 (29.48%) are registered Republicans, and 21,941 (30.15%) have declined to state a political party/are independents.[^cite_note-60] Although Democrat [Kamala Harris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamala_Harris) easily won California and also won Orange County in the [2016 United States Senate election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_United_States_Senate_election_in_California), Democrat [Loretta Sanchez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loretta_Sanchez) easily won Garden Grove by a 67%–33% margin, her widest margin of victory for any city in Orange County.[^cite_note-Loretta_Sanchez_Garden_Grove-61]

### Crime

The Uniform Crime Report (UCR), collected annually by the FBI, compiles police statistics from local and state law enforcement agencies across the nation. The UCR records Part I and Part II crimes. Part I crimes become known to law enforcement and are considered the most serious crimes including homicide, rape, robbery, aggravated assault, burglary, larceny, motor vehicle theft, and arson. Part II crimes only include arrest data.[^cite_note-62] The 2023 UCR Data for Garden Grove is listed below:

## Education

The [Garden Grove Unified School District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garden_Grove_Unified_School_District) (GGUSD) serves most of the city. Other school districts with portions in Garden Grove include: [Westminster School District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westminster_School_District) (elementary only) [Huntington Beach Union High School District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huntington_Beach_Union_High_School_District) (includes the Westminster SD area), [Anaheim Elementary School District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anaheim_Elementary_School_District), [Anaheim Union High School District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anaheim_Union_High_School_District), and the [Orange Unified School District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_Unified_School_District)[^cite_note-64]

GGUSD operates the following high schools in Garden Grove:

- [Bolsa Grande High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolsa_Grande_High_School)

- [Garden Grove High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garden_Grove_High_School)

## Infrastructure

### Transportation

The [Orange County Transportation Authority (OCTA)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_County_Transportation_Authority) operates 15 bus routes servicing 269 stops in Garden Grove.[^cite_note-69]

The [OC Streetcar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OC_Streetcar) is planned to terminate at Harbor Transit Center at the intersection of Harbor Boulevard and Westminster Avenue. A future stop at the Willowick golf course has also been proposed.[^cite_note-70]

### Emergency services

The Garden Grove Police Department provides law enforcement, with mutual aid assistance offered at times by the [Anaheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anaheim) Police Department's helicopter and the [Orange County Sheriff's Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_County_Sheriff's_Department_\(California\)) Air Unit.

In August 2019, the city of Garden Grove entered into a 10-year contract with the [Orange County Fire Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_County_Fire_Authority) (OCFA) for fire and rescue services, and merged all existing Garden Grove Fire Department personnel and equipment into the OCFA.[^cite_note-71]

### Health care

Garden Grove is serviced by two health care facilities:

- [Garden Grove Hospital and Medical Center](https://gardengrovehospital.com/) (acute-care hospital)

- [Nhan Hoa Comprehensive Healthcare Clinic](https://nhanhoa.org/)

### Water services

Water in Garden Grove is supplied by the City of Garden Grove Water Services Division, which sources its water from the [Metropolitan Water District of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_Water_District_of_Southern_California), importing water from the State Water Project in Northern California and the Colorado River Aqueduct. Additionally, groundwater is supplied by 12 wells tapped into an underground reservoir managed by the Orange County Water District.[^cite_note-72]
