**Samarkandek** ([Kyrgyz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyrgyz_language): Самаркандек)[^cite_note-2] is a village in [Batken Region](1649599.md) of [Kyrgyzstan](170131.md). It is the seat of the Samarkandek rural community (айыл аймаг&#x44B;*, ayyl aymagy*) within the [Batken District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batken_District).[^cite_note-3] Its population was 7,566 in 2022.[^cite_note-pop22-4]

Until 2013, the strategic road Batken-Isfana passed through Samarkandek. In 2013, ethnic conflict between enclave of [Tajikistan](30108.md), [Vorukh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vorukh) and Samarkandyk led to mutual closing of borders. As Batken-Isfana road passed through Voruh also, government officials started to build a detouring road which would lay entirely in Kyrgyzstan borders. This latter event left Samarkandyk off the road.
