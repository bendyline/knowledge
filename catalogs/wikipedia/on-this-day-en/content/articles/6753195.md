**Colli sul Velino** is a *[comune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comune)* (municipality) in the [Province of Rieti](1448595.md) in the [Italian](14532.md) region of [Latium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lazio), located about 70 kilometres (43 mi) northeast of [Rome](25458.md) and about 13 kilometres (8 mi) northwest of [Rieti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rieti).

Colli sul Velino borders the following municipalities: [Contigliano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contigliano), [Labro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labro,_Lazio), [Morro Reatino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morro_Reatino), [Rieti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rieti), [Rivodutri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rivodutri), [Terni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terni).
