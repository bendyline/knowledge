**SATENA** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Servicio Aéreo a Territorios Nacionales*, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'Air Service to the National Territories') is a [state-owned airline](204768.md) of [Colombia](5222.md) established to guarantee access to the most remote and least connected parts of the country.[^cite_note-1][^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] It is wholy-owned by the [Government of Colombia](76741.md) and its operational mandate is fulfilled by the [Colombian Aerospace Force](6719810.md).

The airline primarily operates out of its main hub at [El Dorado International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Dorado_International_Airport) in [Bogotá](211271.md), its secondary hub at the [Olaya Herrera Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olaya_Herrera_Airport) in [Medellín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medell%C3%ADn), and another secondary hub at [Alfonso Bonilla Aragón International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfonso_Bonilla_Arag%C3%B3n_International_Airport) in [Cali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cali). The airline also has sizeable operations in [Barranquilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barranquilla) and [Villavicencio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villavicencio) and, as of July 2026, operates in 27 of the 32 [Departments of Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Departments_of_Colombia).

## History



SATENA [Douglas C-54 Skymaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_C-54_Skymaster) at [La Vanguardia Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Vanguardia_Airport) in 1980.

SATENA was first established on April 12, 1962, by then [President of the Republic](251302.md), [Alberto Lleras Camargo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberto_Lleras_Camargo), via [decree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_decree) to:

*"to form an aerial transport service for the benefit of the underdeveloped regions of the country, with the object of promoting the welfare of educational campaigns, agricultural and pastoral development, and the economic and social progress of such territories."*

To achieve this under government auspices, the airline was then entrusted to the control of the [Colombian Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombian_Air_Force), initiating its first operations with the routes [Bogotá](211271.md) - [Leticia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leticia,_Colombia) and [Leticia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leticia,_Colombia) – [Tarapacá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarapac%C3%A1,_Amazonas) – [El Encanto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Encanto) – [Puerto Leguízamo](10704804.md). The Government equipped the company with one [C-54](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C-54), two [C-47](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C-47_Skytrain), two Beaver L-20, and later added two [PBY Catalina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PBY_Catalina) amphibious, the property of the Rotary Fund of the [Colombian Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombian_Air_Force). Later, in 1964, it added three additional [C-47](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C-47_Skytrain) and two more [C-54](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C-54) aircraft, all of them donated by the [United States](3434750.md) International Development Agency.

In 1965, SATENA, in coordination with the Bank of Bogotá, began a new service named *AeroBanco*, which was created to facilitate air travel to other areas largely or completely inaccessible by road. Due to great success in the areas served by the airline, President [Carlos Lleras Restrepo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Lleras_Restrepo) put into effect Law 80 of December 12, 1968, where it was decreed that SATENA would be treated as a public establishment, with all legal functions controlled by the [Ministry of National Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_National_Defence_\(Colombia\)).

In 1972, SATENA acquired newer British-Built [AVRO HS-748](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AVRO_HS-748) [turboprops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turboprop) capable of carrying 48 passengers. Between 1984 and 1985, the airline incorporated two [Fokker F28](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fokker_F28) jet aircraft with the capacity to transport 65 passengers.

Since its creation, the airline has performed many public services on behalf of the [Colombian people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombian_people), having provided service during crises in the country's history, as was the case of the [Huila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huila_Department) and [Cauca](549662.md) earthquake of June 6, 1994, when SATENA performed search and rescue operations, airlifted residents, as well as transporting needed medicines, foods and aid in general to the site of the tragedy.

In 1996, the largest modernization of the airline began, with the arrival of six [Dornier 328s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dornier_328) capable of carrying 32 passengers. These aircraft represent the new generation of equipment for regional routes.

In 2002, SATENA incorporated into its fleet one [Embraer ERJ 145](273890.md) with a capacity for 50 passengers, which arrived to replace the departing [Fokker F28](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fokker_F28), which was reaching its maximum airframe hours. In 2004, SATENA became the airline with the newest and most modern aerial fleet in [Colombia](5222.md), when it incorporated two new [Embraer ERJ 145](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embraer_ERJ_145) aircraft, allowing it to perform faster and more efficient services to its most isolated destinations.

At the end of 2010, SATENA suffered a financial crisis, consisting of bank debts amounting to 120 billion pesos and losses totaling 25 billion pesos. Due to this, the National Government proposed a law that would allow the commercial airline to get out of said crisis and compete in the air transport market in the country. This law consists of capitalizing this company at 98 billion pesos, where 49% of the company's shares will be put up for sale in a democratic process and within two years, while the remaining 51% would continue in the hands of the state, something that was never done in the end.

On April 29, 2018, the airline moved all its routes to Terminal Puente Aéreo belonging to [El Dorado International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Dorado_International_Airport), to facilitate the service to passengers on regional flights.[^cite_note-5]

## Accidents and incidents

- On September 8, 1969, a [Douglas C-47](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_C-47_Skytrain) FAC-685 crashed near [Apiay Air Force Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apiay_Air_Force_Base); all 32 people on board died. The aircraft was operating a domestic scheduled passenger flight from [Monterrey Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monterrey_Airport) to Apiay.

  [^cite_note-ASN080969-14]

- On January 21, 1972, a [Douglas DC-3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_DC-3) FAC-661 crashed at San Nicolas whilst operating a domestic non-scheduled passenger flight from [Enrique Olaya Herrera Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enrique_Olaya_Herrera_Airport), [Medellín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medell%C3%ADn) to [Gerardo Tobar López Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerardo_Tobar_L%C3%B3pez_Airport), [Buenaventura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buenaventura,_Colombia).

  [^cite_note-ASN210172-15]

- On January 9, 1974, a [HS 748](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HS_748) (FAC 1103) crashed into the Gabinete Peak 50 km north of [Gustavo Artunduaga Paredes Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustavo_Artunduaga_Paredes_Airport) Florencia whilst on a flight from Florencia to Bogota. All 31 people on board died.

  [^cite_note-SATENA-16]

- On January 8, 1975, a [Douglas DC-3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_DC-3) (FAC-688) crashed shortly after take-off from [Benito Salas Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benito_Salas_Airport), [Neiva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neiva,_Colombia) on a flight to [Gustavo Artunduaga Paredes Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustavo_Artunduaga_Paredes_Airport), [Florencia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florencia,_Caquet%C3%A1). All 30 people on board died.

  [^cite_note-ASN080175-17]

- On May 3, 1975, a [Douglas DC-3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_DC-3) (FAC-663) crashed at [Sardinata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sardinata). The aircraft was on a domestic scheduled passenger flight from [Aguas Claras Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aguas_Claras_Airport), [Ocaña](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oca%C3%B1a,_Norte_de_Santander) to [Camilo Daza Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camilo_Daza_Airport), [Cúcuta](623811.md). Three of the six occupants died.

  [^cite_note-ASN030575-18]

- On April 2, 1976, a [Douglas DC-3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_DC-3) (FAC-676) crashed on approach to [Tres de Mayo Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tres_de_Mayo_Airport), [Puerto Asís](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_As%C3%ADs). The aircraft was on a flight from [Gustavo Artunduaga Paredes Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustavo_Artunduaga_Paredes_Airport), [Florencia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florencia,_Caquet%C3%A1). Five of the 16 people on board died.

  [^cite_note-ASN020476-19]

- On February 17, 1977, a [Douglas C-47B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_C-47B) (FAC-1125) was damaged beyond economic repair in a take-off accident at [Fabio Alberto León Bentley Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fabio_Alberto_Le%C3%B3n_Bentley_Airport), [Mitú](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mit%C3%BA). All 28 people on board survived.

  [^cite_note-ASN170277-20]

- On November 20, 1977, a [Douglas C-47B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_C-47B) (FAC-1127) crashed in Colombia.

  [^cite_note-ASN201177a-21]

- On November 20, 1977, a [Douglas C-47A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_C-47A) (FAC-1120) crashed at Llanos del Yori, Colombia.

  [^cite_note-ASN201177b-22]

- On February 21, 1978, a [Douglas DC-3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_DC-3) (FAC-668) crashed at Serranía los Cobardes in Colombia.

  [^cite_note-ASN210278-23]

- On June 25, 1981, a [Douglas C-47](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_C-47) (FAC-1129) was damaged beyond repair in an accident. The aircraft was subsequently withdrawn from use and stored at [La Vanguardia Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Vanguardia_Airport), [Villavicencio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villavicencio).

  [^cite_note-ASN250681-24]

- On May 3, 1983, a [Douglas C-47B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_C-47B) (FAC-1126) was damaged beyond repair in an accident at [Palmaseca Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palmaseca_Airport), [Cali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cali).

  [^cite_note-ASN030583-25]

- On May 5, 2010, an [Embraer ERJ-145](273890.md) overran the runway at [Fabio Alberto León Bentley Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fabio_Alberto_Le%C3%B3n_Bentley_Airport), [Mitú](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mit%C3%BA), [Colombia](5222.md). The aircraft crashed through the airport fence and then came to rest in a field, suffering substantial damage. None of the 37 passengers and 4 crew were injured.

  [^cite_note-26]

  &#x20;The runway at the time of the accident was wet as it had recently rained in the area.

- On January 28, 2026, a [Beechcraft 1900D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beechcraft_1900D) operating as [Flight 8849](82254642.md) went missing shortly after takeoff from [Cucuta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cucuta), [Colombia](5222.md). The flight was later found and no survivors were found out of the 15 passengers and crew.
