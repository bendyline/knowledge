**Deli Serdang Regency** ([Indonesian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_language): *Kabupaten Deli Serdang*; [Jawi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawi_script): **دلي سردڠ**) is a [regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regency_\(Indonesia\)) in the [Indonesian](14579.md) province of [North Sumatra](453595.md). It surrounds the city of [Medan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medan), and also borders to the west the city of [Binjai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binjai), which is effectively a bedroom community for [Medan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medan). The entire regency lies within the [Medan metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medan_metropolitan_area). It occupies an area of 2,579.98 km<sup>2</sup> (906.14 sq miles). Its administrative center is [Lubuk Pakam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lubuk_Pakam), which is located approximately 30 km east of Medan. The national census of 2000 recorded 1,573,987 people, but by 2010 the regency's population increased by 13.76% to 1,790,431,[^cite_note-Biro_Pusat_Statistik_2011-2] and at the 2020 Census the total was 1,931,441.[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2021-3] The official estimate as of mid-2025 was 2,078,046 (comprising 1,044,002 males and 1,034,044 females), of whom 65.1% lived in the western 53.6% of the area, surrounding Medan city, while 34.9% lived in the eastern 46.4% of the area.[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2026-1] Medan's [Kualanamu International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kualanamu_International_Airport) is located in this regency, 23 kilometres (14 mi) east of downtown Medan.

Deli Serdang is the most populous regency in Indonesia outside the island of Java, with 2,078,046 inhabitants in mid 2025.[^cite_note-Badan_Pusat_Statistik_2026-1] Its population is comparable to the U.S state of [Nebraska](21647.md).

## Neighbouring areas

The external [boundaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border) of the regency are with:

- To the **north**: the [Langkat Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langkat_Regency) and the [Strait of Malacca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strait_of_Malacca).

- To the **south**: the [Karo Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karo_Regency) and [Simalungun Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simalungun_Regency).

- To the **east**: the [Serdang Bedagai Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serdang_Bedagai_Regency) and the Strait of Malacca.

- To the **west**: the [Karo Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karo_Regency), [Langkat Regency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langkat_Regency) and the city of [Binjai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binjai).

The major city of Medan is a semi-enclave within the regency (except in the north, where the city reaches the [Malacca Strait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malacca_Strait) at [Belawan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belawan)).

### Plantations

Plantation commodities in Deli Serdang in 2024 demonstrated diverse yields, with oil palm being the leading crop. Oil palm plantations were spread across all sub-districts, covering an area of 18,162.23 hectares, and production reached 51,666.29 tons. The districts of Sinembah Tanjung Muda Hilir, Sinembah Tanjung Muda Hulu, and Hamparan Perak (all in Deli Serdang) were the main palm oil producing areas, with planted areas of 3,736.11 hectares, 1,984.00 hectares, and 2,114.96 hectares, respectively. Rubber contributed 4,564 tons of production from a planted area of 5,164 hectares. Coconut is another important commodity with 3,247 tons of yield from a planted area of 3,841 hectares, dominated by the Districts of Hamparan Perak and Percut Sei Tuan. Cocoa plants recorded a production of 2,072.43 tons with an area of 2,666.33 hectares, mostly produced from Kutalimbaru and Sinembah Tanjung Muda Hilir.[^cite_note-26]
