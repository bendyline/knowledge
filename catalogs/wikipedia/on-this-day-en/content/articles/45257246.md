The **Spanish Socialist Workers' Party of Andalusia** (Spanish: *Partido Socialista Obrero Español de Andalucía*, PSOE–A) is the [Andalusian](2736.md) branch of the [Spanish Socialist Workers' Party](272596.md), as well as the largest and most important federation within the PSOE.

Formerly known as the **Andalusian Socialist Federation** (FSA), it was founded in 1977. The party was the ruling party of Andalusia uninterruptedly from 1978 to 2019, having won all regional elections in that time except for the [2012 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012_Andalusian_parliamentary_election), after which it nonetheless was able to maintain the regional government through a [coalition](6036.md) with [United Left](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Left_\(Spain\)). It lost power for the first time after the [2018 election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Andalusian_regional_election), when a [PP](48424869.md)-[Cs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizens_\(Spanish_political_party\)) [coalition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moreno_Government) was formed. In the [2026 election](69228186.md), it worsened its already worst result ever.
