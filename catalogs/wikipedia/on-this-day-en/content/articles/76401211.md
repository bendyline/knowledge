**Hung Cao** (born 1971) is an American politician and former military officer who has served as the [Under Secretary of the Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Under_Secretary_of_the_Navy) since 2025.[^cite_note-phelan_welcomes_cao-1] Following the resignation of Secretary of the Navy [John C. Phelan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_C._Phelan) in April 2026, he became acting secretary; he has been nominated by President [Donald Trump](4848272.md) to take the role permanently.[^cite_note-cao_permanent_job-2] Cao has been the [Republican Party](32070.md)'s nominee in two Virginia elections: for the [10th congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia's_10th_congressional_district) in [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Virginia#District_10),[^cite_note-3] and for the Senate in [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_Senate_election_in_Virginia).[^cite_note-4]

## Early life and career



Cao as commanding officer of the Naval Diving and Salvage Training Center in 2015

Hung Cao ([Vietnamese](32511.md): *Cao Hùng*)[^cite_note-5] was born in 1971 in [Saigon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saigon), South Vietnam.[^cite_note-6] His father, Quan Cao (Vietnamese: *Cao Quản*),[^cite_note-7] was a native of [Quảng Trị province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qu%E1%BA%A3ng_Tr%E1%BB%8B_province) who studied in the [Philippines](23440.md) on a scholarship from the [Rockefeller Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockefeller_Foundation), earned a Ph.D. at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University),[^cite_note-8] and worked in the Ministry of Agriculture of [South Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Vietnam), later serving as an assistant to the deputy prime minister.[^cite_note-9] In 1975, shortly before the [fall of Saigon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fall_of_Saigon),[^cite_note-cao_salvaged_plane-10] the Cao family fled to the [United States](3434750.md).[^cite_note-The_Washington_Post-11] They spent some time in [Niger](21373.md) because Cao's father, by now an agricultural specialist at [USAID](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USAID), was posted there.[^cite_note-erosion_of_excellence-12]

Cao graduated from [Thomas Jefferson High School for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Jefferson_High_School_for_Science_and_Technology) in 1989, being part of the first class to do so,[^cite_note-erosion_of_excellence-12] and joined the [U.S. Navy](20518076.md). During his years of active duty, he was deployed to Iraq, Afghanistan, and Somalia.[^cite_note-yaf-13][^cite_note-14] A specialist in ordnance disposal and salvage diving, he obtained the Navy Diving Officer and Explosive Ordnance Disposal Warfare Officers Badges,[^cite_note-15] led the recovery of the bodies of [John F. Kennedy Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_F._Kennedy_Jr.), [Carolyn Bessette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carolyn_Bessette), and [Lauren Bessette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauren_Bessette) following [their plane crash off Martha's Vineyard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1999_Martha's_Vineyard_plane_crash),[^cite_note-cao_salvaged_plane-10] and was commanding officer of the Naval Diving and Salvage Training Center, near [Naval Support Activity Panama City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Support_Activity_Panama_City), from September 2013 to June 2016.[^cite_note-16] He obtained a bachelor's degree in ocean engineering from the [United States Naval Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Naval_Academy) in 1996, and a Master's of Science in [Applied Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_Physics) from the [Naval Postgraduate School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Postgraduate_School) in 2008.[^cite_note-17]

Cao retired as a [captain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Captain_\(United_States_O-6\)) in 2021, and was hired as a vice president of [CACI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CACI), a U.S. government contractor that provides services to defense, intelligence, and homeland security agencies.[^cite_note-18]

In June 2024, Cao and his wife April published the book *Call Me an American: Refugee to Patriot: Lessons Learned for a Strong America*.[^cite_note-19] It recounts his childhood and military service and shares his views on patriotism and citizenship.[^cite_note-20]

## Congressional elections

### 2022 House bid

Main article: [2022 United States House of Representatives elections in Virginia § District 10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Virginia#District_10)

Cao was one of eleven candidates in the Republican primary of the 2022 election for [Virginia's 10th congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia's_10th_congressional_district).[^cite_note-prime-21] He placed first by about 2,800 votes, advancing to the general election against Democratic incumbent [Jennifer Wexton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Wexton).[^cite_note-22][^cite_note-prime-21]

Cao was considered a strong candidate in a district home to many [Asian Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Americans) and military families.[^cite_note-23] He expressed support for the [deregulation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deregulation) of government across nearly all facets of life,[^cite_note-24] and his [anti-abortion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-abortion) and [pro-gun rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_to_keep_and_bear_arms) stances were targeted by Wexton as being too "extreme" for the district.[^cite_note-general-25] He lost [the election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections_in_Virginia) 53% to 47%.[^cite_note-29]

### 2024 Senate bid

Main article: [2024 United States Senate election in Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_Senate_election_in_Virginia)

In July 2023, Cao entered the Republican primary for the following year's Senate election [in Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_Senate_election_in_Virginia).[^cite_note-30] Receiving the endorsement of former President [Donald Trump](4848272.md),[^cite_note-31] he comfortably won, receiving 61.8% of the vote in a five-candidate race.[^cite_note-32][^cite_note-33] In the general election, he was defeated by Democratic incumbent [Tim Kaine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Kaine) by a margin of 54% to 45%.[^cite_note-34][^cite_note-35][^cite_note-36]

In an interview during the campaign for the primary, Cao feared that "witchcraft" could intrude into Virginia, as it allegedly had in [Monterey, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monterey,_California),[^cite_note-37][^cite_note-38] and called himself an [African American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_American) because of his time in Niger.[^cite_note-39] In other media appearances, he claimed, as he did during his 2022 campaign, that he has lasting injuries from enemy fire. An investigation by *[USA Today](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USA_Today)* found that he was awarded neither the [Purple Heart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purple_Heart) nor the [Combat Action Ribbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combat_Action_Ribbon), both normally given to soldiers in these circumstances.[^cite_note-40]

## Department of the Navy

### Under Secretary of the Navy (2025–present)

On February 28, 2025, President [Donald Trump](4848272.md) nominated Cao for the position of [Under Secretary of the Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Under_Secretary_of_the_Navy).[^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42][^cite_note-43] Cao was confirmed by the Senate on October 1 by a margin of 52-45, with Republican [Lisa Murkowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_Murkowski) joining every Democrat present in rejecting the nomination,[^cite_note-44][^cite_note-45] and was sworn in by Secretary of Defense [Pete Hegseth](34076202.md) on October 3.[^cite_note-phelan_welcomes_cao-1] He was rarely involved in decisions made by the Department of the Navy, and was forbidden from attending meetings on behalf of Secretary of the Navy [John C. Phelan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_C._Phelan).[^cite_note-46]

### Acting Secretary of the Navy (2026–present)



Cao visiting Vietnam in 2026

On April 22, 2026, Phelan resigned; as Under Secretary, Cao replaced him on an interim basis.[^cite_note-auto-47] Cao took charge during the [Iran War](82533582.md).

On July 21, 2026, Cao awarded the [Navy Unit Commendation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navy_Unit_Commendation) to the crew of the [ USS *Charlotte*](1121727.md),[^cite_note-48] credited with [sinking](82572206.md) Iranian warship [IRIS *Dena*](74329424.md).[^cite_note-49]

The [USS *Abraham Lincoln*](199106.md), which had left its home port of [Naval Air Station North Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Air_Station_North_Island) on November 21, 2025,[^cite_note-fiery_meeting-50] also became involved in the war, extending its deployment for many months. Politicians and families of the ship's crew became concerned about reports of [deteriorating mental health](199106.md#Mental_health_on_the_Lincoln_during_Iran_War), including suicide attempts by jumping overboard.[^cite_note-51][^cite_note-navytimes_20260811-52] On August 6, 2026, Cao attended two town halls with family members, revealing that the *Abraham Lincoln* would be relieved by the [USS *Theodore Roosevelt*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Theodore_Roosevelt_\(CVN-71\)).[^cite_note-fiery_meeting-50] On August 14, he released a statement which acknowledged the stresses being faced by the crew, but called media coverage "dishonest".[^cite_note-53][^cite_note-54]

On September 1, 2026, Trump nominated Cao to serve permanently as Navy Secretary.[^cite_note-cao_permanent_job-2] A 2021 U.S. law requires that nominees for military secretaries must have been retired from the military for at least seven years. Captain Cao retired in October 2021 and will not be eligible without a congressional waiver until 2028.[^cite_note-55]

On September 9, 2026, Cao admitted that Naval Support Activity Bahrain had been heavily damaged by Iran in February 2026,[^cite_note-\:1-56] and that this limited the Navy's ability to support the *USS Abraham Lincoln* during it's deployment. He also stated that the Navy would not be returning to the base "anytime soon." The Pentagon had previously denied any impact on the base and its facilities.[^cite_note-\:1-56]

## Personal life

Cao and his wife April Lakata Cao have two sons and three daughters—one adopted from Thailand, and a pair of twins.[^cite_note-Cline-57] All of their children have been [homeschooled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeschooled) by April.[^cite_note-58] The Caos live in [Purcellville, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purcellville,_Virginia),[^cite_note-Cline-57] and attend Cornerstone Chapel in [Leesburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leesburg,_Virginia).[^cite_note-church-59]
