The [volleyball](32558.md) tournaments at the [2028 Summer Olympics](12695048.md) in [Los Angeles](18110.md) will be held from 15 to 30 July 2028. 24 volleyball teams and 48 beach volleyball teams will participate in the tournament. Indoor volleyball competitions will occur at [Honda Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_Center) with the beach volleyball tournament staged at [Alamitos Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alamitos_Beach,_Long_Beach,_California).[^cite_note-1]

## Qualification summary

### Indoor volleyball

On 11 December 2025, [Fédération Internationale de Volleyball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F%C3%A9d%C3%A9ration_Internationale_de_Volleyball) announced the qualification system for the volleyball tournaments at the 2028 Summer Olympics.[^cite_note-fivb-qual-system-2] The host nation, the United States, automatically qualified for both the men's and women's tournaments. The champions of the five continental championships in each gender, scheduled for 2026, will also qualify directly. An additional three places per gender will be awarded to the highest-placed teams not yet qualified at the 2027 FIVB Volleyball World Championships, which will be held under the rebranded FIVB Volleyball World Cup name.[^cite_note-3] The remaining three places in each tournament will be allocated according to the FIVB World Ranking following the conclusion of the preliminary round of the 2028 FIVB Volleyball Nations League.

### Beach volleyball

On 11 December 2025, the Fédération Internationale de Volleyball (FIVB) announced the qualification system for the beach volleyball tournaments at the 2028 Summer Olympics. A total of 24 teams per gender will compete. The host nation, the United States, is guaranteed one berth in each tournament. The champions of the five continental championships in 2026 will qualify directly, while the winners of the 2027 FIVB Beach Volleyball World Championships will also secure Olympic berths. The remaining places will be allocated through the Olympic Ranking and the Olympic Qualifier Series.[^cite_note-fivb-qual-system-2]
