## Wrestling

Main article: [Wrestling at the 2026 Asian Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrestling_at_the_2026_Asian_Games)

South Korea entered twelve male and six female wrestlers.[^cite_note-9]

**Key**:

- VT (ranking points: 5–0 or 0–5) – [Victory by fall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pin_\(amateur_wrestling\)).

- VB (ranking points: 5–0 or 0–5) – Victory by injury (VF for forfeit, VA for withdrawal or disqualification)

- PP (ranking points: 3–1 or 1–3) – Decision by points – the loser with technical points.

- PO (ranking points: 3–0 or 0–3) – Decision by points – the loser without technical points.

- ST (ranking points: 4–0 or 0–4) – Great superiority – the loser without technical points and a margin of victory of at least 8 (Greco-Roman) or 10 (freestyle) points.

- SP (ranking points: 4–1 or 1–4) – Technical superiority – the loser with technical points and a margin of victory of at least 8 (Greco-Roman) or 10 (freestyle) points.
