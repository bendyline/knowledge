**Mona Hamoudi** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): مونا حمودی;[^cite_note-FD-1] born 11 November 1993) is an Iranian [footballer](191466.md) who plays as a [midfielder](548981.md) for [Kowsar Women Football League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kowsar_Women_Football_League) club [Bam Khatoon FC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bam_Khatoon_F.C) and the [Iran women's national team](9896170.md).[^cite_note-AFC-3]

In March 2026, Hamoudi, along with several teammates from the Iranian women's national football team, [left the team's training camp](82634971.md) in [Australia](4689264.md) and sought refuge due to fears of retaliation from Iranian authorities. Reports indicate that political figures, including [Reza Pahlavi](613947.md), called for protection for the players.[^cite_note-4] US president [Donald Trump](4848272.md) publicly urged the Australian government to grant asylum to the players, stating they could face persecution if they returned to Iran.[^cite_note-5] The Australian government subsequently granted humanitarian visas to the players.[^cite_note-6] On 14 March, Manoudi, together with fellow Iranian players Soltan Meshkehkar and [Zahra Sarbali](64381444.md), reversed their earlier decision to seek asylum and chose to return to Iran. The *[Tasnim News Agency](42800472.md)* added that they had rejected "psychological warfare, extensive propaganda and seductive offers".[^cite_note-7]
