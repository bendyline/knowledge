**Lingwala** is a municipality (*[commune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_Kinshasa)*) in the [Lukunga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lukunga) district of [Kinshasa](45773.md), the [capital city](181337.md) of the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](76762.md).[^cite_note-2]

It is situated in northern Kinshasa, south of [Gombe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gombe,_Kinshasa) and *[Boulevard du 30 Juin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boulevard_du_30_Juin)*.
