The **Patriotic Movement for Safeguard and Restoration** (**MPSR**)[^cite_note-1] has been the ruling [military junta](216815.md) of [Burkina Faso](3470.md) since the [January 2022 Burkina Faso coup d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_2022_Burkina_Faso_coup_d'%C3%A9tat).[^cite_note-2] Originally it was led by [Paul-Henri Sandaogo Damiba](69868896.md), but he was overthrown by dissatisfied junta members during the [September 2022 Burkina Faso coup d'état](71882074.md). In his place, [Ibrahim Traoré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_Traor%C3%A9) was installed as the leading figure.[^cite_note-3][^cite_note-4] Aside from Traoré and Sorgho (the group's official spokesperson), other MPSR members are not known to the public.[^cite_note-5]
