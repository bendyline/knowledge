The **2026 LPGA Tour** is the 77th edition of the [LPGA Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LPGA_Tour), a series of professional golf tournaments for elite female golfers from around the world. The season began at the [Hilton Grand Vacations Tournament of Champions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilton_Grand_Vacations_Tournament_of_Champions), in [Orlando, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando,_Florida) on January 29, and will end on November 22, at the [Tiburón Golf Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tibur%C3%B3n_Golf_Club) in the [CME Group Tour Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CME_Group_Tour_Championship) at [Naples, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naples,_Florida).[^cite_note-2026schedule-1] The tournaments were sanctioned by the United States-based [Ladies Professional Golf Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LPGA) (LPGA).

## Schedule and results

The number in parentheses after each winners' name is the player's total number of wins in official money individual events on the LPGA Tour, including that event. Tournament and winner names **in bold** indicate [LPGA majors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LPGA_majors). The schedule and purse amount for each tournament is listed on the LPGA website.[^cite_note-2] The LPGA has a standard formula for payout percentages and distribution of its purse and prize money for every event. The winner typically gets 15% of the total, second place gets 9.3%, third place 6.75%, etc.[^cite_note-3]

The total purse for 2026 is \$132 million.[^cite_note-2026schedule-1]
