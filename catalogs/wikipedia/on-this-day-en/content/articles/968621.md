**Gyeongsan** ([Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language): 경산; Korean pronunciation: [\[kjʌŋ.san\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help\:IPA/Korean)) is a satellite city in [North Gyeongsang Province](314848.md), South Korea. Its western border abuts the metropolitan city of [Daegu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daegu), and much of Gyeongsan lies within the Daegu [metropolitan area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_area).

Numerous [universities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University) are located in Gyeongsan, such as [Yeungnam University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeungnam_University), [Daegu University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daegu_University), [Daekyeung University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daekyeung_University), [Catholic University of Daegu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_University_of_Daegu), [Daegu Haany University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daegu_Haany_University) and [Gyeongil University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyeongil_University?action=edit\&redlink=1).

Gyeongsan was the host for the 38th [International Chemistry Olympiad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Chemistry_Olympiad) (2 July 2006 – 11 July 2006).

## City symbols

The city flower is the [magnolia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnolia), which grows well in poor soil conditions. The city bird is the [magpie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magpie). The city tree is the [ginkgo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ginkgo_biloba). [^cite_note-2]

## Climate

The climate is mild because of its southern location. However, being landlocked makes its climate continental.

The amount of rainfall is less than the average yearly rainfall of Korea, due to its geographical characteristics.[^cite_note-5]
