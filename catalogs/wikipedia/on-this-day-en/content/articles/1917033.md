**Kruonis Pumped Storage Plant** (the KPSP) is a [pumped storage hydroelectric power plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pumped-storage_hydroelectricity) located near [Kruonis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kruonis), [Lithuania](17675.md), 34 km (21 mi) east of [Kaunas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaunas). Its main purpose is to provide [grid energy storage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grid_energy_storage). It operates in conjunction with the [Kaunas Hydroelectric Power Plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaunas_Hydroelectric_Power_Plant). During periods of low demand, usually at night, Kruonis PSHP raises water from the lower [Kaunas reservoir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaunas_reservoir) to the upper one using cheap surplus energy. The station is designed to have an installed capacity of 1,600 MW but only four 225 MW generators are currently operational. With a fully filled upper reservoir the plant can generate 900 MW for about 12 hours.

The KPSP uses [hydro-resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydroelectric) of artificial water pools existing at different geographical levels.

The electricity from this power plant is supplied to a 330 kV electricity line to [Elektrėnai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elektr%C4%97nai), where the [largest fossil fuel plant in Lithuania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elektr%C4%97nai_Power_Plant) is operating, and Kaunas. At times of surplus [electricity generation](9540.md), the KPSP uses the surplus electricity to pump water from the lower pool to the upper pool.

At times of electricity output deficit, the Kruonis PSP operates as a regular hydro power plant, letting water flow from the upper pool to the lower pool to generate additional electricity. Kruonis Plant is the only [pumped-storage station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pumped-storage_hydroelectricity) in the [Baltic states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltic_states).[^cite_note-1]

The Kruonis PSP Industrial Park and Kruonis Technology Park were created as the location, infrastructure and low electricity price are attractive for data centers.[^cite_note-2]

A neighbourhood for workers of the Kruonis pumped storage plant was built in the 1980s and early 1990s in [Elektrėnai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elektr%C4%97nai), doubling the size of the town.
