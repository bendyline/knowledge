The **Italy women's national tennis team** represents [Italy](14532.md) in international [women's tennis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_tennis) and is directed by the [Federazione Italiana Tennis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federazione_Italiana_Tennis). The team played in the first ever tournament in [1963](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1963_Federation_Cup_\(tennis\)), and is one of four teams that has taken part in every single edition since.[^cite_note-feditamain-2].

The Italian national team was initially unsuccessful in world tennis, only winning twenty ties in the first two decades of competition, but in recent times they have become one of the most successful teams in the world. They have been a regular member of the [ITF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Tennis_Federation) rankings World Top Ten since the initiation of the rankings in [2002](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_Fed_Cup), and became one of five teams to reach the World No. 1 position in [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Fed_Cup), after they managed to reach all but one final from [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Fed_Cup) to [2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Fed_Cup). They have also reached every semifinal since [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Fed_Cup), and since [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_Fed_Cup) have accumulated a 22–9 win–loss ratio.[^cite_note-feditamain-2] [Francesca Schiavone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesca_Schiavone) holds the Italian Fed Cup record for most singles wins and total wins, while [Roberta Vinci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberta_Vinci) holds the record for most doubles wins and years of participation. [Sandra Cecchini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandra_Cecchini), however, holds the record for most appearances, having played in two more ties than Schiavone and Vinci.[^cite_note-feditamain-2] Former ATP World No. 7[^cite_note-3] and Davis Cup captain[^cite_note-4] [Corrado Barazzutti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corrado_Barazzutti) was the captain of the Italian Fed Cup team, having held that position from 2002 until 2016.[^cite_note-5] In October 2016, Tathiana Garbin was appointed as the new captain.[^cite_note-6]

On 20 November 2024 the Italian national team won their fifth [Billie Jean King Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Billie_Jean_King_Cup). On 21 September 2025, Italy [defended its title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Billie_Jean_King_Cup) by beating the U.S. team in the final. <https://www.bbc.com/sport/tennis/articles/c04qqvkpddlo>

The team is currently the World No. 1 in the ITF rankings.[^cite_note-RankingGuide-7]

## History

Italy competed in its first Fed Cup in [1963](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1963_Federation_Cup_\(tennis\)), the team consisting of [Lea Pericoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lea_Pericoli) and [Silvana Lazzarino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silvana_Lazzarino). They are one of four nations to have competed in every edition of the tournament. Italy won the Cup in [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Fed_Cup), [2009](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Fed_Cup), [2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010_Fed_Cup), [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Fed_Cup), [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Billie_Jean_King_Cup) and [2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Billie_Jean_King_Cup). Their six titles since the year 2000 are the most by any country, tied with the [Czech Republic](10829679.md).
