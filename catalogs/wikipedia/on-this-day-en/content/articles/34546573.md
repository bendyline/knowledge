**Maiwut County** is an administrative area of [Upper Nile State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Nile_\(state\)) in the [Greater Upper Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Upper_Nile) region of [South Sudan](32350676.md).[^cite_note-2] It borders [Nasir County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasir_County) to its west, [Longechuk County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longechuk_County) to its north and [Ethiopia](187749.md) to its east and south.[^cite_note-csrf-3]

In the [South Sudanese independence referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Sudanese_independence_referendum), Maiwut County made a historic milestone with all of its registered population voting 100% for Separation of the southern Sudan.[^cite_note-4]

The *[payam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Payam_\(administrative_division\))* administrator of Maiwut County is Thing Stephan Reat.[^cite_note-UNMISS-5]

## Society

The people there are faced with the issues of lack of education, lack of youth job opportunities, petty crime, gang violence, bad access to roads, the rising cost of commodities, needing to heal from trauma and the lack of health facilities for women and children.[^cite_note-UNMISS-5]

The primary mode of communication are though mobile phones.

## Climate Change

The Greater Region of the [Sobat River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sobat_River) has experienced unprecedented levels of flooding displacing the county's population.[^cite_note-6] Extreme measures have been put in place throughout the entire [South Sudan](32350676.md), [Greater Upper Nile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Upper_Nile) Region and [Upper Nile State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Nile_State) to combat extreme flooding.&#x20;
