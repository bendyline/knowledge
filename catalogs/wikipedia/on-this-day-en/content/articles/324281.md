The **Northern District** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): מָחוֹז הַצָּפוֹן, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Hebrew): *Mekhoz HaTzafon*; [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): منطقة الشمال, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Minṭaqat ash-Shamāl*) is one of [Israel](9282173.md)'s [six administrative districts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Israel). The Northern District has a land area of 4,473 km<sup>2</sup>, making it the second largest district in Israel.

The [Golan Heights](7515865.md) has been run as a sub-district of the North District of Israel since the 1981 [Golan Heights Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golan_Heights_Law) was passed, although the claim is only recognized by [the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_recognition_of_the_Golan_Heights_as_part_of_Israel)[^cite_note-4] and Colombia[^cite_note-jp2-5], while the [United Nations Security Council](31956.md) condemned the [annexation](29328.md) in its [Resolution 497](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Security_Council_Resolution_497) without enforcing it. The Golan Heights covers a land area of 1,154 km<sup>2</sup> and the remainder of the Northern District covers 3,324 km<sup>2</sup> (3,484 km<sup>2</sup> including water).

## Geography

### Climate

The Northern District has a [Mediterranean climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediterranean_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification): *Csa*), with hot, dry summers and cool, rainy occasionally snowy winters. It is home to the wettest locations in Israel, including [Harashim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harashim).

## Demographics

According to the [Israeli Central Bureau of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Central_Bureau_of_Statistics) data for 2022:[^cite_note-6]

- Total population: 1,527,800 (2022)

- Ethnic:&#x20;

  - [Arabs](2185.md): 816,800 (53.5%)

  - [Jews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Jews): 647,500 (42.4%)

  - Others: 63,500 (4.2%)

In the Israeli census, no distinction is made between [Arab citizens of Israel](492331.md) and [Syrian inhabitants](8862873.md) of the [Golan Heights](7515865.md), many of whom are not citizens of Israel, but of [Syria](7515849.md).

- Religions:&#x20;

  - [Jews](25955086.md): 647,500 (42.4%)

  - [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Muslims): 597,300 (39.1%)

  - [Druze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druze): 120,300 (7.9%)

  - [Christians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Christians): 97,800 (6.4%)

  - Not classified: 55,600 (3.6%)

- [Density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_density): 335/km

The Northern District is the only [district of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Israel) where the majority of inhabitants are [Arabs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_Arabs).
