# List of storms named Fay

The name **Fay** or **Faye** has been used for 24 [tropical cyclones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_cyclones) worldwide: 6 in the Atlantic Ocean; 1 in the Australian region; 1 in the South Pacific Ocean; and 16 in the Western Pacific Ocean.&#x20;

In the Atlantic:

- [Hurricane Faye (1975)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Faye_\(1975\)), a Category 2 hurricane that had minor effects in Bermuda

- [Tropical Storm Fay (2002)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_Storm_Fay_\(2002\)), a tropical storm that caused minor damage in Texas and northern Mexico

- [Tropical Storm Fay (2008)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_Storm_Fay_\(2008\)), a near-hurricane strength tropical storm that made landfall in Florida four times, the first known storm in history to do so

- [Hurricane Fay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Fay) (2014), a Category 1 hurricane that affected Bermuda

- [Tropical Storm Fay (2020)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_Storm_Fay_\(2020\)), a moderate tropical storm that affected New Jersey, earliest sixth named storm in the Atlantic basin

- [Tropical Storm Fay (2026)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_Storm_Fay_\(2026\)), a strong and long-lived tropical storm that took an erratic path and did not affect land

In the Australian region:

- [Cyclone Fay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclone_Fay) (2004), a Category 5 storm (Category 4 on the Saffir-Simpson scale) that made landfall in Western Australia; name retired afterward

In the South Pacific:

- [Cyclone Fay (1978)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclone_Fay_\(1978\)), affected Fiji; no fatalities reported

In the Western Pacific:

- [Typhoon Faye (1949)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon_Faye_\(1949\))

- [Tropical Storm Faye (1952)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropical_Storm_Faye_\(1952\))

- [Typhoon Faye (1957)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon_Faye_\(1957\))

- [Typhoon Faye (1960)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon_Faye_\(1960\))

- [Typhoon Faye (1963)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon_Faye_\(1963\)), a Category 3 typhoon that made landfall in Hong Kong; 3 fatalities were reported

- [Typhoon Faye (1965)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon_Faye_\(1965\)), a Category 4 super typhoon that stayed at sea

- [Typhoon Faye (1968)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typhoon_Faye_\(1968\))
