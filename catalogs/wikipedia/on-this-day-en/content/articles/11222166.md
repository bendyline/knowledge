# Law enforcement in Georgia (country)

"Georgian Police" redirects here. For the police force in the U.S. state of Georgia, see [Georgia State Patrol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_State_Patrol).



Georgian police patrol car [Škoda Octavia IV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%A0koda_Octavia#Fourth_generation_\(NX;_2019\)).

**Law enforcement in [Georgia](48768.md)** is conducted by the [Ministry of Internal Affairs of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Internal_Affairs_of_Georgia). Currently, there are more than 42,000 registered police officers.&#x20;

## History

The Georgian police introduced an 022 [emergency dispatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergency_telephone_number) service in 2004.[^cite_note-1] As of 2017, you can contact Georgian police with a 112 [Emergency Dispatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergency_telephone_number).[^cite_note-2]&#x20;

## Restructuring

In the mid-2000s, the Patrol Police Department of the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Georgia underwent a radical transformation. In 2005, Georgian President [Mikheil Saakashvili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikheil_Saakashvili) fired "the entire traffic police force" of the Georgian National Police due to [corruption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corruption_in_Georgia_\(country\)),[^cite_note-mcclatchydc.com-3] numbering around 30,000 police officers.[^cite_note-4]&#x20;

A new force was built around recruits.[^cite_note-mcclatchydc.com-3] The United States State Department's [Bureau of International Narcotics and Law Enforcement Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bureau_of_International_Narcotics_and_Law_Enforcement_Affairs) has assisted in the training efforts.[^cite_note-5] Patruli was first introduced in the [summer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summer) of 2005, replacing the traffic police, which were accused of corruption.[^cite_note-6] Throughout the reformation, policemen were presented with new [Volkswagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volkswagen) cars and navy blue uniforms, with "Police" written on the back. They were armed with [Israeli Jericho-941SFL pistols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IWI_Jericho_941) instead of [PMs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makarov_pistol).&#x20;

The Georgian Immigration Enforcement Training Video Unit (GIETVU) works to improve training methods for immigration enforcement operatives.[^cite_note-7] In 2009, the U.S. State Department launched *the U.S. State Department’s International Narcotics and Law Enforcement Program "The Georgia-to-Georgia Exchange Program"*, providing Georgian policemen with education courses in the [State of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_Georgia). In June, the United States provided \$20 million for these courses.&#x20;
