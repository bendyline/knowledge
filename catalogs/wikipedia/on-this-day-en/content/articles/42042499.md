The **Iranian Traffic Police** ([Persian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language): پلیس راهنمایی و رانندگی فراجا, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Persian): *Polis-e Râhnamâii vâ Rânandegi-ye Faraja*, [lit.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literal_translation) 'FARAJA Traffic Police'), abbreviated as **RAHVAR**[^cite_note-1] (راهور), is a unit of the [Police Command of the Islamic Republic of Iran](5187924.md) responsible for [traffic guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traffic_guard) and [highway patrol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highway_patrol).
