The **United List** ([Latvian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvian_language): *Apvienotais saraksts*, **AS**) is a [regionalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regionalism_\(politics\)) and [green conservative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_conservatism) political alliance in [Latvia](17514.md) established ahead of the [2022 parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Latvian_parliamentary_election). The list was formed by the [Latvian Green Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvian_Green_Party), the [Latvian Association of Regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvian_Association_of_Regions), and the [Liepāja Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liep%C4%81ja_Party), together with the "United List of Latvia" ([Latvian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvian_language): *Apvienotais Latvijas saraksts*) association led by [Liepāja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liep%C4%81ja) construction contractor [Uldis Pīlēns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uldis_P%C4%ABl%C4%93ns).

## History

The idea for the electoral alliance, developed from May 2022 onwards, initially used the name "United List of Latvia" (*Latvijas apvienotais saraksts*). Initiated by [Uldis Pīlēns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uldis_P%C4%ABl%C4%93ns), it sought to bring together political parties and non-partisan professionals in a common electoral list based on the earlier experience of the United List of Liepāja (*Liepājas apvienotais saraksts*). The non-governmental association created to support the project was scheduled to hold its founding event on 1 July and was to be named United List of Latvia (*Apvienotais Latvijas saraksts*).[^cite_note-11]

One of the catalysts in the origins of the alliance was the refusal of the Green and Liepāja parties to work with their former [Union of Greens and Farmers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_of_Greens_and_Farmers) partners who had continued to promote [Aivars Lembergs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aivars_Lembergs) as its prime ministerial candidate despite his criminal conviction.[^cite_note-12]

The alliance's joint candidate list for the [2022 Latvian parliamentary election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Latvian_parliamentary_election) was registered on 26 July 2022, with [Didzis Šmits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Didzis_%C5%A0mits?action=edit\&redlink=1) as the lead candidate, followed by [Edvards Smiltēns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edvards_Smilt%C4%93ns), [Edgars Tavars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edgars_Tavars), [Māris Kučinskis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C4%81ris_Ku%C4%8Dinskis), and [Juris Viļums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juris_Vi%C4%BCums). Pīlēns was announced as the alliance's candidate for Prime Minister, although he did not run as a parliamentary candidate.[^cite_note-13] The United List received 100,631 votes (11.01%) and won 15 seats in the [Saeima](499765.md), becoming the third-largest parliamentary group.[^cite_note-14]

On 6 December 2022, the United List announced its ministerial candidates as part of the coalition government formation process with [New Unity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Unity) and the [National Alliance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Alliance_\(Latvia\)). Its nominees included the non-affiliated [Līga Meņģelsone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C4%ABga_Me%C5%86%C4%A3elsone) for Health Minister, Didzis Šmits for Agriculture Minister, [Māris Kučinskis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C4%81ris_Ku%C4%8Dinskis) for Interior Minister, and [Māris Sprindžuks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C4%81ris_Sprind%C5%BEuks?action=edit\&redlink=1) for Minister for Environmental Protection and Regional Development.[^cite_note-15]

## Ideology

The alliance places [national security](240468.md), effective state administration, and the development of a [green economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_economy) among its main priorities. Its programme highlights the importance of the [defence industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defence_industry) and a stronger [NATO](21133.md) presence in the region. It also supports greater use of local [renewable energy](25784.md) resources and [sustainable economic development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sustainable_economic_development). In addition, the alliance advocates making [referendums](51582.md) easier to initiate.[^cite_note-16]

The alliance also emphasizes cooperation with [European Union](9317.md) partners. Its economic priorities include reducing regional inequalities and promoting more balanced development across the country, particularly by supporting [rural development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rural_development) and increasing political accountability to municipalities.[^cite_note-17]
