The **Las Mujeres Ya No Lloran World Tour** is the seventh [concert tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concert_tour) by the Colombian singer [Shakira](6479315.md). The stadium tour commenced on 11 February 2025 in [Rio de Janeiro](25936.md), Brazil, and is scheduled to conclude on 28 November 2026 in [El Cairo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Cairo), Egypt.[^cite_note-Bishkek-3] It was first announced in 2021 but was delayed due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md) and was re-announced in 2024, in support of her twelfth studio album, *[Las Mujeres Ya No Lloran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Las_Mujeres_Ya_No_Lloran)* (2024).

The tour has broken numerous ticket sales and venue records while also leaving a significant [cultural impact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_of_the_Las_Mujeres_Ya_No_Lloran_World_Tour). As of January 2026, it has earned over [US\$](18717338.md)421.6 million within its first 86 concerts, breaking the [Guinness World Record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinness_World_Record) for the [highest-grossing Latin tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_concert_tours_by_Latin_artists) of all time, while also becoming the fifth-[highest-grossing tour by a female artist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_concert_tours_by_women).

## Background and development

Following her [halftime performance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LIV_halftime_show) at [Super Bowl LIV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LIV), [Live Nation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_Nation_\(events_promoter\)) announced [Shakira](6479315.md) would tour in 2021.[^cite_note-4] Further news was delayed, due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](62750956.md). In September 2023, Shakira told *[Billboard](18309966.md)* she planned to release new music and embark on a "global tour" in the following year. She told the publication: "Putting a tour together is fun, but it's a great effort and you have to put everything on the balance and decide what the fans really want to hear, what songs you want to hear and how much production you want. In the end, the more production you have, the higher the ticket price. I want the tickets to be affordable. But to me, the most important thing is the repertoire. That's why I think \[my next tour] will be the tour of a lifetime, because I have so many songs."[^cite_note-5]

Las Mujeres Ya No Lloran Tour was announced by Shakira during her appearance as a special guest during [Bizarrap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bizarrap)'s set at [Coachella](494630.md) on 13 April 2024.[^cite_note-6][^cite_note-7] Three days later, Live Nation announced concerts in North America starting that November,[^cite_note-Live_Nation_PR_1-8] with additional concerts added due to demand in [Palm Springs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palm_Springs), [Miami](53846.md) and [New York City](645042.md), respectively.[^cite_note-Daily_Jang_1-9] In October 2024, Shakira's official website revealed concerts in the Latin America region;[^cite_note-Official_Website_1-10][^cite_note-CBN_1-11] additional concerts were subsequently added in several cities eight days later.[^cite_note-G1-12]

Shakira announced plans to postpone the North American concerts, due to the "overwhelming demand", as well as the "need for larger venues".[^cite_note-Rolling_Stone_1-13] Following her announcement, the upgraded concerts and venues were announced for May and June 2025 by Live Nation.[^cite_note-Live_Nation_PR_2-14] Additional concerts in [Atlanta](3138.md), [Houston](13774.md) and [Phoenix](49121.md) were announced on 10 December 2024.[^cite_note-Billboard_2-15] On 5 February 2025, it was announced Shakira would headline the [Sueños Music Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sue%C3%B1os_\(music_festival\)).[^cite_note-Billboard_4-16] Later that month, it was announced Brazilian DJ Dennis would serve as a supporting act for the tour's opening night.[^cite_note-CNN_Brasil_1-17] *Billboard* reported that "an exclusive and unique performance" from the 20 February concert will be featured as part of the [Lo Nuestro Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lo_Nuestro_Awards) on [Univision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Univision).[^cite_note-Billboard_3-18] On 14 February, a concert in [Santo Domingo](57638.md), Dominican Republic, was announced;[^cite_note-CDN_37_1-19] the concert was postponed due to operational issues,[^cite_note-La_Voz_del_Interior_1-20] and later rescheduled.[^cite_note-La_Patria_1-21] On 16 February, Shakira cancelled her concert in [Lima](85423.md), Peru, for that same day, due to hospitalization;[^cite_note-Univision_1-22][^cite_note-NTN24_1-23] a rescheduled date was announced the following day, with a third concert added.[^cite_note-Radio_Programas_del_Perú_1-24]

## Production



A giant wolf emerges in the final act and remains on stage throughout Shakira's performance of "[She Wolf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/She_Wolf_\(Shakira_song\))" and "[BZRP Music Sessions, Vol. 53](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shakira:_Bzrp_Music_Sessions,_Vol._53)"

Rehearsals for the tour started around mid-August 2024, with Shakira announcing them with a series of pictures through her social networks.[^cite_note-78] On another teaser from Shakira through [Instagram](31591547.md), aimed at Latin America, the singer performs at Electric Air Studios a new arrangement for her 1997 single "[Antología](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antolog%C3%ADa_\(song\))," accompanied by her longtime collaborator, [Luis Fernando Ochoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Fernando_Ochoa).[^cite_note-79] In late October, Shakira posted, also through her Instagram account, another teaser video, this time aimed at North America, singing at the same aforementioned studio her 1996 hit single "[Pies Descalzos, Sueños Blancos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pies_Descalzos,_Sue%C3%B1os_Blancos)."[^cite_note-80] During an interview for Brazilian website *[GShow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grupo_Globo)*, Shakira revealed the latter song, plus other signature hits of hers, such as "[Estoy Aquí](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estoy_Aqu%C3%AD)" and "[Waka Waka (This Time for Africa)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waka_Waka_\(This_Time_for_Africa\))", would be included to the set list, with the tour being described by herself as "the biggest show of \[her] career, the most comprehensible one," whose rehearsals include a daily 12-14 hour full schedule of preparations along with her crew, to perform "these most, most relevant concerts of \[her] musical lifetime."[^cite_note-81] Later on, she also revealed the members for her band, most of them already longtime collaborators of her, such as musical director and guitarist [Tim Mitchell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Mitchell), drummer Brendan Buckley and piano and keyboard player Albert Menéndez, with also the addition of bass player Donald Alford II.[^cite_note-82]

In an interview for *[GQ España](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GQ_Espa%C3%B1a)*, Shakira has described the current process of her tour: "It's the most ambitious tour of my entire career, the biggest production I've had so far. Not because the public asks for it, but because I deserve it after so many years working in this world. I deserve the tour of my life. I'm throwing the house out the window. I am happy because I will be able to take a tour of the different stages of my artistic life, until today. It will be the longest show I’ve ever done with the biggest screen and everything you can imagine. More is more and better!"[^cite_note-83]

On 21 January 2025, Shakira moved from the USA to Mexico to finish her last rehearsals before tour properly kicks off in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.[^cite_note-nataliacano01-84] Shakira was actively involved in the production, ensuring that every element reflected her "vision".[^cite_note-ellemexico-85] The on-screen visuals were created using [artificial intelligence](1164.md) and [CGI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer-generated_imagery) technology.[^cite_note-86][^cite_note-cnn_spanish-87] 45 people worked on them, including companies Blink[^cite_note-ellemexico-85] and Los Angeles-based creative studio Actual Objects. The visuals included a realistic projection of the singer. Artist Isabella Mebarak claimed that a likeness analysis of the singer was conducted to resemble her features.[^cite_note-linkedin-88] The "first of its kind" AI/CGI animated version of Shakira is shown during the interludes.[^cite_note-NME_opening_night-89][^cite_note-BillboardOpeningNight-90] The tour features an "elaborate production",[^cite_note-NME_opening_night-89] including 160’ screens and a diverse set of props, such as a [pole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pole_dance) attached to a giant "S" used during the "[Soltera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soltera_\(Shakira_song\))" performance. This set element was constructed by specialty fabricators ShowFab.[^cite_note-91] According to Chilean media outlet *[Culto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Tercera)*, the cost of producing each concert is estimated to be more than US\$3 million.[^cite_note-culto-92] Preparations lasted about 10 months.[^cite_note-ellemexico-85] Attire and accessories were mostly custom-made by fashion houses and designers, such as Indian [Anamika Khanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anamika_Khanna) and [Atelier Versace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Versace).[^cite_note-93] Shakira worked with company Yellow Studio to design "a stage that evolves along with the show".[^cite_note-ellemexico-85] The stage includes [hydraulic platforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolving_stage) and a 25-meter ramp.[^cite_note-ellemexico-85] The creative direction for the stage was handled by the Squared Division.[^cite_note-ellemexico-85]

## Critical reception

Olivia Petty of *[The San Diego Union-Tribune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_San_Diego_Union-Tribune)* praised Shakira's performance and versatility, but criticized the on-screen visuals, stating that the "AI-generated visuals that filled the transitions between some songs left much to be desired, feeling out of place and generally unappealing to the eye."[^cite_note-94]

## Ticket sales

In North America, during the pre-sale period of Shakira's North American arena leg, the tickets of most of the shows were sold out. The tickets vary widely in prices, between circa \$200 and \$2000 (excluding VIP or other add-on tickets), depending on the cities.[^cite_note-95] Due to overwhelming demand, Shakira added extra shows in Palm Springs, Miami and New York City.[^cite_note-96] Meanwhile, her shows in Phoenix, Inglewood, San Antonio, Dallas, Charlotte, Washington DC, and Chicago were mostly sold out. In Miami, she was supposed to extend the record of being the female artist to perform most shows in [Kaseya Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaseya_Center), 11 in total. However, the whole leg was moved to May 2025 due to Live Nation's decision to upgrade the several North American shows to stadiums.[^cite_note-Rolling_Stone_1-13]

In Argentina, Peru and Chile, she broke the record by selling all the tickets in 40 minutes. With multiple shows completely sold out,[^cite_note-recordtour-97] new dates were promptly added to several cities.[^cite_note-G1-12] In Colombia, the second stadium date in Bogotá was sold out in 1.5 hours.[^cite_note-98][^cite_note-maspaiseslatam-99] In Barranquilla, Shakira moved the first show date from February 21 to 20 because it clashed with [La Guacherna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barranquilla_Carnival).[^cite_note-guacherna01-100] However, the first date was quickly sold out due to high demand, which prompted the city's mayor, [Alex Char](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alejandro_Char_Chaljub), to announce that the carnival celebration would be moved to February 22, so a second date could be scheduled.[^cite_note-guacherna02-101] In total, two million fans registered for the ticket notification.[^cite_note-102] The singer promised she will bring the tour to most of the Latin American countries due to the overwhelming demand.[^cite_note-maspaiseslatam-99]

In [Mexico City](18987.md), she is the first female artist to perform seven sold-out shows at [Estadio GNP Seguros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estadio_GNP_Seguros), breaking the record previously held by [Taylor Swift](5422144.md) with four. She is also the first artist to ever perform seven shows of the same tour at the venue, breaking the record previously held by [RBD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RBD) with six.[^cite_note-103] The stadium is named as "Shakira stadium" on Google Maps, honoring her record-breaking achievement.[^cite_note-104] In an article, *Billboard* claimed the seven shows in Mexico City would gather crowds of "about 455,000 people in total".[^cite_note-nataliacano01-84] Ticketmaster Mexico reported that 2.5 million people visited its website to search for tickets to Shakira’s concerts since the tour was announced in the country last October. [^cite_note-nataliacano02-105]

## Commercial performance

### Box office

Live Nation reported that in less than two hours of sale in Latin America, 950,000 tickets were sold across 18 stadiums,[^cite_note-Box_office_1-106][^cite_note-Box_office_2-107] which broke the all-time record for ticket sales among female artists.[^cite_note-Box_office_3-108] With more than 30 shows ahead, Live Nation revealed on 17 March 2025 that more than two million tickets have already been sold.[^cite_note-Box_office_4-109] In March 2025, *[Billboard](18309966.md)* reported that \$32.9 million were made from the first six shows, which had sold over 282,000 tickets. The outlet also projected the tour to finish with over \$200 million in total grosses with shows announced as of 27 March 2025.[^cite_note-Box_office_5-110] In May 2025, *Billboard* published their midyear report, covering concerts, including her first seven nights at Estadio GNP Seguros that March.[^cite_note-Box_office_6-111]

Additional reports in May were made,[^cite_note-Box_office_7-112] as well as in the months of June, July, and September 2025.[^cite_note-Box_office_8-113][^cite_note-Box_office_9-114][^cite_note-Box_office_10-115][^cite_note-Box_office_11-116] In October 2025, *Billboard* reported the tour had earned [US\$](18717338.md)327.4 million and an attendance of 2.5 million within its first 64 concerts. making it the highest-grossing Latin tour ever by a woman ever, as well as the second highest-grossing Latin tour ever.[^cite_note-Box_office_12-117][^cite_note-Box_office_13-118] In their year-end report for 2025, *Billboard* recognized the tour as the fifth-best tour of the year.[^cite_note-Box_office_14-119] Similarly, *Pollstar* named the tour as the fifth-most lucrative tour of 2025, with an average gross of US\$5 million based on 64 reported shows.[^cite_note-Box_office_15-120]

In January 2026, *Billboard* reported that \$35.7 million was generated from ten concerts, with an attendance of 267,000 also noted. Individual reports regarding the December 2025 concerts in Buenos Aires and Montevideo were also provided.[^cite_note-Box_office_16-121] They further reported the tour had generated US\$421.6 million and attracted an audience of 3.3 million over its first 86 concerts, setting a new [Guinness World Record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinness_World_Record) for the highest-grossing Latin tour in history.[^cite_note-Box_office_17-1]

## Impact

Main article: [Impact of the Las Mujeres Ya No Lloran World Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_of_the_Las_Mujeres_Ya_No_Lloran_World_Tour)

### Economy, commerce and local business

In January 2025, [LATAM Airlines Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LATAM_Airlines_Group)'s [Brazilian branding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LATAM_Airlines_Brasil) announced an additional flight on 12 February of the same year, from Confins Airport to Guarulhos Airport ahead of Shakira's concert the following day, to accommodate the increase in passenger demand.[^cite_note-Estado_de_Minas_1-135] That same month, their [Colombian branding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LATAM_Airlines_Colombia) added eight additional flights to [Barranquilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barranquilla), from [Bogotá](211271.md) and [Medellín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medell%C3%ADn), to meet the demand for Shakira’s concert and the carnival in her hometown.[^cite_note-136] In a press release, Brazilian travel agency Decolar reported a 67% increase in demand for flights to [Rio de Janeiro](25936.md) and a 24% increase in demand for flights to [São Paulo](390875.md) during the period of the singer's shows in those cities in relation to the same periods in 2024.[^cite_note-137][^cite_note-Diário_da_Guanabara_1-138]

In Mexico, the tour also set a historic precedent by boosting tourism and generating millions in economic revenue across the three major cities of the country. According to data from [Ticketmaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ticketmaster), between 30% and 40% of attendees at Shakira’s concerts in Mexico travel from another state in the country to one of the three cities hosting the shows, as well as international travelers from the United States, [El Salvador](9356.md), Colombia, and Peru. In Monterrey, the hotel demand was increased by 66%. In Guadalajara, the two shows led to double hotel occupancy, with approximately 40,000 rooms booked per night. This generated around 80 million pesos (about \$4 million) in direct hotel revenue and contributed an estimated 900 million pesos (around \$44.4 million) in total tourism revenue. The seven sold-out shows at Estadio GNP Seguros are projected to have an economic impact of 5.5 billion pesos (around \$275 million), according to the CDMX Secretariat of Tourism, as reported by Billboard Español.[^cite_note-nataliacano02-105]

Barranquilla's local government estimated that Shakira's shows generated [Col\$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombian_peso)66.8 billion in "economic movement", of which [Col\$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombian_peso)18 billion were "irrigated" by a chain of transactions of local businesses in the city. The Oxford Economics study took into account the subsequent purchases of products and services derived from tourism activities such as hotels, gastronomy, transportation, commerce and services.[^cite_note-139][^cite_note-140] According to *[Forbes Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes)*, the singer's shows in the country generated an economic impact of approximately [Col\$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombian_peso)206 billion (\$US49,6 million).[^cite_note-141][^cite_note-142]

According to *Billboard*, the first seven sold out shows in Mexico City generated around 20,000 jobs in logistics, security, transportation, and production, benefiting workers across various sectors such as hospitality, restaurants, and airlines, as well as street vendors and small businesses near the venues.[^cite_note-nataliacano02-105] In July 2025, Shakira pledged to donate a portion of the proceeds from her 5 July concert in [San Antonio](53848.md) to Hill Country disaster relief fund following the [floods in Central Texas](80354641.md).[^cite_note-Austin_American-Statesman_1-143]

In May 2026, following her performance at Todo Mundo no Rio in Copacabana, Rio de Janeiro, *Rolling Stone* reported that an estimated [US\$](18717338.md)150 million in tourism revenue had been generated.[^cite_note-Rolling_Stone-Kreps-144]

## Controversy

### Cancellations and postponements

Several media outlets noted the high number of cancelled and postponed shows during the North American and Latin American legs of the tour, citing "production and logistic issues".[^cite_note-La_Voz_del_Interior_1-20][^cite_note-FD-146][^cite_note-147] The last-minute suspensions of shows in Medellín and Santiago,[^cite_note-FD-146][^cite_note-culto-92] as well as cancellations in Boston and Washington, D.C., stirred controversy and speculation.[^cite_note-NBC10_Boston_1-38][^cite_note-The_Washington_Post_1-40][^cite_note-WCVB-TV_1-148][^cite_note-Newsweek_1-149] In response to the cancellation in Washington, D.C., which was to be part of WorldPride, Mayor [Muriel Bowser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muriel_Bowser) shared a public message to the singer, telling her to "get yourself here".[^cite_note-WTOP-FM_1-150] Following the postponement of the 13 June concert in San Antonio, fans cited their frustration with the lack of communication regarding the cancellation, which happened less than one-hour prior to show time.[^cite_note-KENS_1-41]

Following the postponement of the 20 June concert in Inglewood, California, due to the [June 2025 Los Angeles protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_2025_Los_Angeles_protests),[^cite_note-De_Último_Minuto_Media_1-44] fans spoke out against the continued lack of communication from the singer.[^cite_note-Hola_1-151] Preceding the cancellation of the 26 September show in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, which was originally rescheduled from 2 April, fans expressed concern about the lack of information as advertising for the event had been withdrawn from the media and public spaces in the prior weeks.[^cite_note-Diario_Libre_1-152]

### Animal abuse by Barranquilla City Council

Several Colombian animal rights advocates denounced that more than 100 stray cats that used to live in the surroundings of [Estadio Metropolitano Roberto Meléndez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estadio_Metropolitano_Roberto_Mel%C3%A9ndez) were allegedly killed by the [Alejandro Char](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alejandro_Char_Chaljub) administration days prior Shakira's concerts at the venue, in an effort to "embellish" the city for tourists.[^cite_note-153][^cite_note-154][^cite_note-155]
