***Pterocetus*** is an extinct genus of [beaked whale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beaked_whale) (family [Ziphiidae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ziphiidae)) known from the [Early Pliocene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Early_Pliocene). The genus contains two species: the [type species](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_species), ***Pterocetus benguelae***, named in 2007, and a second species, ***Pterocetus diamantinae***, named in 2026. Both are known from partial skulls collected from the seafloor. Fossils of *P. benguelae* were [trawled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trawling) from the Atlantic Ocean near [Saldanha Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saldanha_Bay) on the west coast of South Africa.[^cite_note-Bianucci2007-1] Fossils of *P. diamantinae* were collected from the a depth of 7 kilometres (4.3 mi) in the [Diamantina fracture zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamantina_fracture_zone) of the Indian Ocean, west of Cape Lewin, Australia.[^cite_note-Peng2026-2]
