The **Ministry of the Interior** is responsible for law enforcement and public safety in [Bahrain](18933277.md). The headquarters of the ministry is the Diwan Fort (also known as Manama Fort) in [Manama](20481.md), colloquially referred to as "al-gal'aa".

The current Interior Minister is Lieutenant General [Rashid bin Abdullah Al Khalifa](39097118.md), who has been in office since 2004.[^cite_note-1] He is a member of the [Al Khalifa royal family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Khalifa) and a cousin of [King Hamad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Hamad). Apart from the [Bahrain Defence Force](67658.md), which reports to the [Minister of Defence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defence_\(Bahrain\)?action=edit\&redlink=1), the public security forces and the Coast Guard report to the Minister of Interior.
