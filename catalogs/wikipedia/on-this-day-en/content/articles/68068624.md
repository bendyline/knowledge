[KP Sharma Oli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KP_Sharma_Oli) was appointed Prime Minister of [Nepal](171166.md) for a third time on 13 May 2021 by President Bidya Devi Bhandari, as a minority prime minister, as no opposition party formed a majority government or claimed it time.[^cite_note-1] Citing [Article 76 (3)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Nepal) of the constitution, Oli, leading the largest party in the House of Representatives, was re-appointed prime minister, requiring him to again prove a majority in the house within 30 days. On 22 May 2021, the [House of Representatives](2707214.md) was dissolved for the second time in 6 months by a cabinet decision followed by the approval of the President with elections on [12 and 19 November](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Nepalese_general_election) in two phases.[^cite_note-2]

## After dissolving parliament

On 4 June 2021, the cabinet reshuffle took place when the Mahantha-Mahato faction of [PSP-N](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Socialist_Party,_Nepal_\(2020\)) joined the government with 8 cabinet ministers, 2 state ministers, and 5 others from [UML](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Party_of_Nepal_\(Unified_Marxist%E2%80%93Leninist\)). [Bishnu Prasad Paudel](16975783.md), [Raghubir Mahasheth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raghubir_Mahaseth) and [Rajendra Mahato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajendra_Mahato) were made [Deputy Prime-minister](58719229.md).[^cite_note-3] While the previous expansion was yet to be clarified by the Supreme Court, Oli made another reshuffle and included 7 ministers from [UML](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Party_of_Nepal_\(Unified_Marxist%E2%80%93Leninist\)) and 1 from [PSP-N](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Socialist_Party,_Nepal_\(2020\)).[^cite_note-4] On 22 June, the Supreme Court removed Oli's recently appointed ministers,[^cite_note-5] stating article 77(3) of [Constitution of Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Nepal) prohibits a caretaker prime-minister from such action.[^cite_note-6]

## Dissolution of cabinet

The Supreme Court ordered the President to appoint Sher Bahadur Deuba as the next [Prime Minister Of Nepal](2888818.md) within 28 hours, citing article 76(5) of the [Constitution of Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Nepal).[^cite_note-7] Similarly, the dissolution of the House of Representatives was disapproved.[^cite_note-8]
