# Bell Huey family

"Super Huey" redirects here. For the helicopter flight simulation game known simply as "Super Huey" on some versions, see [Super Huey UH-IX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Huey_UH-IX).

| Bell Huey family |
| --- |
| UH-1A |

The **Bell Huey family** of [helicopters](8286923.md) includes a wide range of civil and military aircraft produced since 1956 by [Bell Helicopter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Helicopter). This H-1 family of aircraft includes the utility [UH-1 Iroquois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UH-1_Iroquois) and the derivative [AH-1 Cobra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AH-1_Cobra) attack helicopter series and ranges from the [XH-40](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UH-1_Iroquois_variants) prototype, first flown in October 1956, to the 21st-century [UH-1Y Venom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UH-1Y_Venom) and [AH-1Z Viper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AH-1Z_Viper). Although not flown in military service in the USA, the Bell 412 served in Canada and Japan and, like the UH-1Y, is a twin engine four blade rotor design based on the Bell 212.
