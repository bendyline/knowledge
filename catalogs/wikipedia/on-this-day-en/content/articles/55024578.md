# Western Iran

Not to be confused with administrative [Regions of Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Iran).



The provinces of Western Iran

**Western Iran** consists of [Armenian Highlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_Highlands), northern [Zagros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagros), and the rich agricultural area of the [Khuzestan Plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khuzestan_Plain) in the south.

It includes the three majority [Kurdish-inhabited](17068.md) provinces of [Kurdistan](448112.md) ([Kurdistan](448112.md)), [Kermanshah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kermanshah_Province) and [Ilam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilam_Province), the majority [Luri-inhabited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lurs) province of [Lorestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorestan), and the multi-ethnic province of [Hamadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamadan_Province). Some references also count [West Azerbaijan Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Azerbaijan_Province) and [Khuzestan Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khuzestan_Province) to this region.

The major cities are [Sanandaj](29953972.md), [Kermanshah](1489244.md), [Ilam](482372.md), [Khorramabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khorramabad) and [Hamadan](43610555.md).

If the provinces of [Khuzestan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khuzestan) and [West Azerbaijan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Azerbaijan) are also included in this classification, the cities of [Ahvaz](599079.md), [Urmia](615663.md), [Piranshahr](30865712.md), [Dezful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dezful), [Bukan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bukan), [Mahabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahabad), [Salmas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salmas), and [Abadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abadan) can also be considered as important cities of the western region of Iran.

## Climate

- [Humid continental climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_continental_climate) in the north.

- [Hot-summer Mediterranean climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot-summer_Mediterranean_climate) in the north.

- [cold semi-arid climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BSk) in the Zagros mountains.

- [Warm summer continental climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_continental_climate) all over the central mountain ranges.

- [Hot desert climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BWh) in Khuzestan.
