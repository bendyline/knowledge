**Mbam-et-Inoubou** is a [department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Departments_of_Cameroon) of [Centre Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_Province) in [Cameroon](5447.md). The department covers an area of 7,125 km<sup>2</sup> and as of 2001 had a total population of 153,020.[^cite_note-1] The capital of the department lies at [Bafia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bafia).

## Subdivisions

The department is divided administratively into nine [communes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communes_of_Cameroon) and in turn into villages.
