The **2025–26 [Scottish Cup](883238.md)** (known as the **[Scottish Gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Gas) Men's Scottish Cup** for sponsorship reasons)[^cite_note-1] was the 141st season of Scotland's most prestigious football knockout competition.

[Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aberdeen_F.C.) were the defending champions after defeating [Celtic](69708.md) in the [2025 final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Scottish_Cup_final). They were defeated in the quarter-finals by [Dunfermline Athletic](451181.md).

## Preliminary round one

The draw for preliminary round one was made on 23 July 2025.[^cite_note-pr-3] The seven teams without an SFA club licence were required to be included in the first preliminary round draw – namely [Culter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culter_F.C.) ([North Region Juniors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_North_Region_Junior_Football_League#Premier_League) champions), [Dundee North End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dundee_North_End_F.C.) ([Midlands League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Midlands_Football_League) champions), [Invergordon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invergordon_F.C.) ([North Caledonian League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_North_Caledonian_Football_League) champions), [Johnstone Burgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnstone_Burgh_F.C.) ([Junior Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_Scottish_Junior_Cup) winners), [Lochar Thistle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lochar_Thistle_F.C.) ([South of Scotland League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_South_of_Scotland_Football_League) champions), [Steins Thistle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steins_Thistle_F.C.?action=edit\&redlink=1) ([Amateur Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Amateur_Cup) winners) and [Whitburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitburn_F.C.) (Cup Winners' Shield winners). They were drawn into four ties alongside one of the 49 licensed clubs. The remaining 48 licensed clubs received a bye to the second preliminary round.[^cite_note-format-4]

Johnstone Burgh, Lochar Thistle, Steins Thistle and Whitburn are all taking part in the competition for the first time.
