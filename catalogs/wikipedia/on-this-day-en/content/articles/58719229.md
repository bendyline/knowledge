The **deputy prime minister of Nepal** ([Nepali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepali_language): नेपालका उप-प्रधानमन्त्री, romanized: *Nēpālakā upa-pradhānamantrī*) is the deputy [head of government](37570.md) of [Nepal](171166.md). The deputy prime minister is second in seniority in the [Council of Ministers of Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Ministers_of_Nepal). The deputy prime minister is the senior-most member of cabinet after prime minister in the [Government of Nepal](19233409.md). The deputy prime minister presides and chairs the cabinet in the absence of prime minister.

## Constitutional provisions

### Remuneration

The remuneration of Deputy Prime Minister of Nepal as per Section 78 of 2015 [Constitution of Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Nepal) shall be as provided for by Federal Act. Until such an Act is formulated, it shall be as determined by the Government of Nepal.[^cite_note-constituteproject-2]

### Oath of office

The Deputy Prime Minister shall take the oath of office and secrecy before the President as per Section 80 of 2015 [Constitution of Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Nepal).[^cite_note-constituteproject-2]
