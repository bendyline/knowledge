**Shanyin County** ([simplified Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 山阴县; [traditional Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 山陰縣; [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *Shānyīn Xiàn*) is a county in the northwest of [Shanxi](81262.md) province, China. It is under the administration of [Shuozhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shuozhou) City.

The [Guangwu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guangwu_\(Great_Wall\)) section of the Great Wall runs along the county. Near the section of the Great Wall are the historic villages of [New Guangwu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Guangwu) and [Old Guangwu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Guangwu?action=edit\&redlink=1).
