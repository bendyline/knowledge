This article lists **political parties in Kosovo**. [Kosovo](17391.md) has a [multi-party system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-party_system) with numerous [political parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_party), in which no one party often has a chance of gaining power alone, and parties must work with each other to form [coalition governments](6036.md).
