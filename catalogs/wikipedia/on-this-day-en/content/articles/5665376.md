**West Sweden** ([Swedish](26689.md): *Västsverige* or *Västra Sverige*) is a [national area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Areas_of_Sweden) (*riksområde*) of [Sweden](5058739.md). The national areas are a part of the [NUTS statistical regions of Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NUTS_statistical_regions_of_Sweden).

## Geography

It is located in the south-west of the country, centered on [Västra Götaland County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%A4stra_G%C3%B6taland_County) and the city of [Gothenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothenburg). After [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm_County) it is the second most populated region. It borders with [Norway](21241.md) and the *riksområden* of [North Middle Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Middle_Sweden), [East Middle Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Middle_Sweden), [Småland and the islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sm%C3%A5land_and_the_islands) and [South Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Sweden).

The most populous cities are [Gothenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothenburg), [Borås](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bor%C3%A5s), [Halmstad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halmstad), [Vänersborg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%A4nersborg), [Uddevalla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uddevalla), [Trollhättan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trollh%C3%A4ttan), [Mölndal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%B6lndal), [Skövde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sk%C3%B6vde), [Varberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varberg), [Kungsbacka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kungsbacka) and [Lidköping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lidk%C3%B6ping).

## Subdivision

West Sweden includes 2 [counties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_of_Sweden):&#x20;

-  **[Halland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halland_County)** (seat: [Halmstad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halmstad))

-  **[Västra Götaland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%A4stra_G%C3%B6taland_County)** (seat: [Gothenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothenburg))

## Economy

The Gross domestic product (GDP) of the region was 101 billion € in 2021, accounting for almost fifth of Swedish economic output.[^cite_note-3] GDP per capita adjusted for purchasing power was 36,000 € or 119% of the EU27 average in the same year. The GDP per employee was 109% of the EU average.[^cite_note-4]
