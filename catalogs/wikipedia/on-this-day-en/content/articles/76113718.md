**Zhang Zhanshuo** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 张展硕, Pinyin: Zhāng Zhǎnshuò, born 14 May 2007) is a Chinese swimmer. He became the fastest swimmer aged 16 or under in history in the 400 m individual medley at the [2023 World Aquatics Junior Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_World_Aquatics_Junior_Swimming_Championships). He won the gold medal in the [4 × 200 m freestyle relay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_at_the_2024_World_Aquatics_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_4_%C3%97_200_metre_freestyle_relay) at the [2024 World Aquatics Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_World_Aquatics_Championships). In 2026, he swam the fastest 200 m freestyle relay split in history, recording 1\:42.92 as the anchor leg of China's gold-medal-winning team at the [2026 Asian Games](51790406.md). He also set a record for the most swimming gold medals won by an athlete at a single edition of the Asian Games, winning seven.

## Career

### 2023 season

In September 2023, at the age of 16, Zhang competed in the [World Junior Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_FINA_World_Junior_Swimming_Championships). In the 800 m freestyle, he won bronze with a time of 7\:50.03.[^cite_note-2023worldjunior-1] In the 400 m individual medley, he won silver with a time of 4\:12.44,[^cite_note-2] becoming the fastest age 16 and under swimmer in history in the 400-meter individual medley.[^cite_note-2023worldjunior-1] In the 1500 m freestyle, he won bronze with a time of 15\:11.94.[^cite_note-3]

### 2024 season

In February 2024, Zhang competed in the [World Aquatics Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_World_Aquatics_Championships). In the [4 × 200 m freestyle relay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_at_the_2024_World_Aquatics_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_4_%C3%97_200_metre_freestyle_relay), he anchored in 1\:45.80 to help China win the gold medal.[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

### 2025 season

In July 2025, Zhang competed in the [World Aquatics Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_World_Aquatics_Championships). In the [4 × 200 m freestyle relay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_at_the_2025_World_Aquatics_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men's_4_%C3%97_200_metre_freestyle_relay), he anchored in 1\:44.20 to help China claim the silver medal.[^cite_note-2025worldsrelay-7] Their clocking of 7\:00.91 also marked a new [Asian Record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Asian_records_in_swimming).[^cite_note-2025worldsrelay-7]

In September, Zhang participated in the Chinese National Championships. In the 400 m freestyle, he ripped a new lifetime best of 3\:42.99 to win gold, establishing a new [World Junior Record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_world_junior_records_in_swimming).[^cite_note-2025sep400free-8]

In November, at the [National Games of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_National_Games_of_China), Zhang swept the individual freestyle races spanning the 200 m, 400 m, 800 m, 1500 m. In the 400 m freestyle, he fired off a new World Junior Record of 3\:42.82 en route to gold.[^cite_note-2025chinesenationalgames400free-9] In the 200 m freestyle, he ripped a new lifetime best of 1\:44.86.[^cite_note-10] In the 800 m freestyle, he touched in a time of 7\:46.69.[^cite_note-11] In the 1500 m freestyle, he touched in 14\:52.73.[^cite_note-12]

### 2026 season

In March 2026, at the China Open Swimming Championships, Zhang again swept the individual freestyle races. In the 200 m freestyle, he crushed a new lifetime best of 1\:44.53.[^cite_note-13] In the 1500 m freestyle, he put up a new personal best of 14\:51.93.[^cite_note-14] In the 400 m freestyle, he busted out a career-swiftest performance of 3\:41.55.[^cite_note-15] In the 800 m freestyle, he notched a fourth personal best time of 7\:44.45.[^cite_note-16]

In September, Zhang competed in the [Asian Games](51790406.md). In the [4 × 200 m freestyle relay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_at_the_2026_Asian_Games_%E2%80%93_Men's_4_%C3%97_200_metre_freestyle_relay), he anchored China's gold-medal-winning team, swimming a split of 1\:42.92, the fastest 200-metre freestyle relay split with a flying start in history, to help China overcome a 1.89-second deficit to Japan and finish with a time of 7\:00.17, breaking the Asian record.[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] In the [1500 m freestyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_at_the_2026_Asian_Games_%E2%80%93_Men's_1500_metre_freestyle), he touched in 14\:42.42 for a new lifetime best.[^cite_note-20] In the [200 m freestyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_at_the_2026_Asian_Games_%E2%80%93_Men's_200_metre_freestyle), he scorched a lifetime best of 1\:43.49, breaking [Hwang Sun-woo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hwang_Sun-woo)’s Asian record of 1\:43.92.[^cite_note-21] In the [800 m freestyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_at_the_2026_Asian_Games_%E2%80%93_Men's_800_metre_freestyle), he clocked 7\:42.49 for a new Asian Games record, overtaking the 7\:46.03 [Kim Woo-min](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Woo-min) notched in [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_at_the_2022_Asian_Games_%E2%80%93_Men's_800_metre_freestyle).[^cite_note-2026asiangames800free-22] Just minutes after his 800 m freestyle victory, Zhang headed to the [4 × 100 m freestyle relay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_at_the_2026_Asian_Games_%E2%80%93_Men's_4_%C3%97_100_metre_freestyle_relay), splitting 47.65 on the third leg to help China win the gold and set a new Asian record.[^cite_note-2026asiangames800free-22] On the final day of swimming, Zhang took the [400 m freestyle](84324094.md) with a time of 3\:41.28, overtaking the Asian Games record of 3\:41.53 [Park Tae-hwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Park_Tae-hwan) set in [2010](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_at_the_2010_Asian_Games_%E2%80%93_Men's_400_metre_freestyle) and completing his sweep of seven gold medals.[^cite_note-23] He became the first swimmer in history to win seven golds at a single Asian Games. After his final race, he said, "Today feels like a perfect conclusion".[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25]
