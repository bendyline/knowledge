**Yilan City** ([Mandarin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwanese_Mandarin) [pinyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinyin): *Yílán Shì*; [Hokkien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hokkien) [POJ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pe%CC%8Dh-%C5%8De-j%C4%AB): *Gî-lân-chhī*) is a [county-administered city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County-administered_city) and the [county seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_seat) of [Yilan County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yilan_County,_Taiwan), [Taiwan](25734.md). The city lies on the north side of the [Lanyang River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanyang_River).

## History



Map of Yilan (labeled as GIRAN) and surrounding area (1944).



Map of the region including Yilan (labeled as I-lan (Giran) 宜蘭) (1954).



Map of Yilan City in Yilan County

The [Yilan Plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yilan_Plain) in which the city is located has historically been referred to as **Kapalan** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language): 蛤仔難; [Pe̍h-ōe-jī](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pe%CC%8Dh-%C5%8De-j%C4%AB): *Kap-á-lān*),[^cite_note-2] **Kapsulan** (蛤仔蘭; *Kap-chú-lân*; also 甲子蘭), **Komalan** (噶瑪蘭; *Kat-má-lán*), etc. These names, as well as that of Yilan itself, were given to the sites by the [Kavalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kavalan_people) tribe of [Taiwanese aborigines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwanese_aborigine). Later arrivals included [Han Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Han_Chinese) settlers during the Qing dynasty in China (1802) and settlers from [Okinawa](22477.md) during Taiwan's [Japanese era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan_under_Japanese_rule) (1895–1945).

### Qing dynasty

In 1810 under [Qing dynasty rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan_under_Qing_rule), a formal administration office was established at **Wuwei** (五圍) and "Komalan Subprefecture" (噶瑪蘭廳; *Kat-má-lán Thiaⁿ*) was at the present day location of Yilan City. Construction of the city wall was completed a year later. After a few years once the basic infrastructure was ready, the city assumed the political, economical, cultural and educational center for the [Lanyang Plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanyang_Plain). In 1878, Komalan was a large rice production area commonly called *Kapsulan*, and became a district called *Gilan Hsien*.[^cite_note-3] It was one of the three new districts that constituted the new [Taipeh Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taipeh_Prefecture).

### Empire of Japan

According to the 1904 census, the population of *Giran* town was about 15,000.[^cite_note-FOOTNOTETakekoshi1907200-4]

### Republic of China

After the [handover of Taiwan from Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Taiwan_since_1945) to the [Republic of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_China_\(1912%E2%80%931949\)) on 25 October 1945, **Yilan City** was created in January 1946 as a [county-administered city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County-administered_city) the [county seat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_seat) of the newly created [Yilan County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yilan_County,_Taiwan) and continued to become the political, economical and cultural centers of the region.[^cite_note-5]

### Climate

Yilan City experiences a [humid subtropical climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humid_subtropical_climate) ([Köppen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%B6ppen_climate_classification): *Cfa*) with mild winters and hot, humid summers.
