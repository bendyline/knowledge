***UFC Freedom 250*** (also known as ***UFC White House*** and ***UFC at the White House***)[^cite_note-2] was a [mixed martial arts](228344.md) event produced by the [Ultimate Fighting Championship](169660.md) that took place on June 14, 2026, on the [South Lawn](15728461.md) of the [White House](33057.md) in [Washington, D.C.](108956.md), United States.[^cite_note-intro-3][^cite_note-anotherjune-4] The event's name is a reference to the [250th anniversary of the United States Declaration of Independence](57473130.md) on the following July 4, 20 days later.[^cite_note-5]

There was seating for roughly 4,300 people.[^cite_note-Ramaswamy_2026-6][^cite_note-7] No public seating was made available, with seats reserved for financial sponsors, political allies, and active-duty service members.[^cite_note-BBCPartner-8][^cite_note-att-1] The event marked the [only UFC event to end with all fights ending by knockout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_UFC_records#Highest_percentage_of_knockouts_-_In_a_single_event),[^cite_note-Stern_2026-9] and also tied events [UFC Fight Night: Rockhold vs. Bisping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Fight_Night:_Rockhold_vs._Bisping) and [UFC on ESPN: Santos vs. Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_on_ESPN:_Santos_vs._Hill) for having [all fights end by finish at a modern-era UFC event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_UFC_records#Highest_percentage_of_finishes_-_In_a_single_event), which had 11 and 10 fights respectively.[^cite_note-Hiergesell_2026-10]

The event was the most-watched exclusive live event on Paramount+ as of June 2026. It had 34 million viewers worldwide and was one of the most watched MMA events of all time.[^cite_note-Viewership_1-11][^cite_note-Viewership_2-12] There was controversy surrounding the event's use of the White House to stage cage fighting, with most Americans disapproving of the idea according to a poll by [Reuters](18998750.md)/[Ipsos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ipsos).[^cite_note-\:0-13]

## Background



The [South Lawn](15728461.md) of the [White House](33057.md) held the first professional sporting event staged at the presidential residence.

[^cite_note-14]

To commemorate the [250th anniversary of the United States](57473130.md), the UFC planned to hold a mixed martial arts event on the South Lawn of the White House in June 2026.[^cite_note-intro-3]

The event was first announced by President [Donald Trump](4848272.md) on July 3, 2025, during a rally at the [Iowa State Fairgrounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_Fairgrounds).[^cite_note-trumpiowa-15] On August 29, 2025, UFC CEO [Dana White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dana_White) confirmed that plans for the event had been finalized, stating: "We had the meeting at the White House ... The White House fight is on."[^cite_note-16] The announcement followed Trump's remarks at the rally, where he referenced his long-standing friendship with White. Trump had frequently attended UFC events in recent years, including [UFC 316](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_316) in New Jersey on June 7, 2025, where several fighters greeted him cageside.[^cite_note-trumpiowa-15]

While initial reports suggested a [4th of July](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4th_of_July) date, the event was officially rescheduled for June due to logistical reasons.[^cite_note-Junedate-17] During a speech at [Naval Station Norfolk](896196.md) on October 6, 2025, Trump announced that it would take place on June 14, 2026, coinciding with his 80th birthday and the [Flag Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_Day_\(United_States\)) holiday.[^cite_note-18] The rescheduled date drew criticism over its occurrence on Trump's birthday three weeks before July 4, with several news outlets describing it as celebrating Trump's birthday more than America's.[^cite_note-Gelston_2026-19][^cite_note-McCreesh_20260615-20][^cite_note-Ramaswamy_2026-6][^cite_note-Gedeon_2026-21][^cite_note-Caitlin_2026-22]

The event represented the UFC's third visit to Washington, D.C., and first since [UFC on ESPN: Overeem vs. Rozenstruik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_on_ESPN:_Overeem_vs._Rozenstruik) in 2019.[^cite_note-23] The weigh-ins took place at the [Lincoln Memorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Memorial).[^cite_note-anotherjune-4] The card aired on [Paramount+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount+).[^cite_note-24][^cite_note-25] The event name, UFC Freedom 250, was announced during the [UFC 326](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_326) broadcast on March 7, 2026.[^cite_note-26]

## Logistics and planning



The event's venue under construction at the [White House South Lawn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_House_South_Lawn)

In a September 2025 interview, White said the seating capacity would be under 5,000 due to security concerns, a significant reduction from Trump's earlier estimate of 20,000 to 25,000 attendees. In a February 2026 interview, [TKO Group Holdings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TKO_Group_Holdings) CEO [Ari Emanuel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ari_Emanuel) stated that there would be 3,000 to 4,000 people in attendance.[^cite_note-tkointerview-27] White revealed in May that the capacity was expected to be 4,300.[^cite_note-28] He also mentioned plans to install large screens at [The Ellipse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ellipse) capable of hosting up to 85,000 spectators for a public viewing experience.[^cite_note-29] White later announced that the 85,000 tickets for the viewing area would be available for free.[^cite_note-30] The venue also held a fan fest the day before, headlined by the [Zac Brown Band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zac_Brown_Band), and featured the ceremonial weigh-ins, meet and greets, on-stage entertainment, interactive experiences, and appearances from UFC athletes and other celebrities.[^cite_note-31]

To prepare for the event, [Stageco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stageco) constructed a 92-foot tall arch called "the Claw" around an eight-sided fighting pit known as the "Octagon".[^cite_note-Ramaswamy_2026-6][^cite_note-32] Further reporting described the South Lawn audience as an invite-only crowd. *[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_\(magazine\))* reported that at least 1,200 of the approximately 4,300 seats would be allocated to active military members, while the remaining seats would be divided among the White House, TKO Group Holdings and the UFC. Tickets to the Ellipse events remained free but required registration, while South Lawn attendees were expected to present identification.[^cite_note-TimeAccess-33] In a separate interview with [TMZ Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TMZ_Sports), White said Trump had 1,000 tickets, White had 200, Emanuel had 200, and that the remaining seats would go to the military.[^cite_note-TMZTickets-34]

On May 29, *[The Washington Post](102226.md)* reported that the [Department of Defense](7279897.md) had solicited military personnel to attend the event, with internal messages directing commands to identify junior enlisted personnel and junior officers. According to the report, personnel selected for tickets were required to pay their own travel costs, wear short-sleeve dress uniforms, and meet current waist-to-height ratio and physical fitness standards. One message reviewed by the newspaper also said tickets should be distributed to "genuine UFC fans" rather than high-ranking distinguished visitors.[^cite_note-WaPoMilitary-35]

[Department of Homeland Security](58236.md) officials told [TMZ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TMZ) that the event would feature Level 1 [Special Event Assessment Rating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_Event_Assessment_Rating) (SEAR) security measures for the card happening on the South Lawn. The fan fest event, scheduled for June 13, would also feature SEAR 1 level security. Typically events that feature that level of security have multiple agencies monitoring and assistance could include explosive detection canine teams, cyber risk assessments, venue screening, field intelligence teams, air security and tactical operations support, including a no-drone zone, ground security as well as screening and surveillance along with security checkpoints.[^cite_note-36]

During a fight week media day on June 10, UFC [Chief Content Officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Content_Officer) Craig Borsari spoke to reporters about possible issues with the weather and other logistical complications. Two obvious concerns raised by media, fighters, and fans alike were how the UFC planned to deal with the summer sun and heat, and the possibility of rain—or worse, a thunderstorm. He stated that "part of the reason why we picked an 8 p.m. start time was so that we could get some of the intense direct sunlight off of the canvas" and revealed they have a custom cover for the canvas, as well as a 100-foot (30 m) canopy or tarp “that also provides a lot of shade and reduces the heat quite a bit.” He also stated that light to moderate rain with no lightning will not stop the event and in the worst-case weather scenario, they have a contingency planning.[^cite_note-37]

### Budget and production



A custom belt designed exclusively for this event was revealed on May 6 and was awarded to both title fight winners.

[^cite_note-38]

The promotion is expected to cover the full cost of the event, including roughly \$700,000 to restore the South Lawn after use, and no public tickets will be made available.[^cite_note-39][^cite_note-40] In January 2026, it was reported that no taxpayer funding is being sought, and the overall expense of the event is projected to exceed the \$21 million budget required to stage [UFC 306](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_306)'s [Sphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sphere_\(venue\)) show in September 2024.[^cite_note-41][^cite_note-42] In February 2026, it was estimated the cost rose to \$60 million.[^cite_note-43] TKO president [Mark Shapiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Shapiro_\(media_executive\)) stated that the event is not expected to turn a profit, with the goal being to recoup approximately half of the total cost through sponsors and new partnerships.[^cite_note-44] In a June 2026 interview, White contended the cost would be worth it to propel MMA further into the mainstream, stating: "This is a huge brand play."[^cite_note-45]

Although no public tickets were made available, combat sports journalist [Ariel Helwani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariel_Helwani) reported in May 2026 that the UFC had circulated a "partner investment" deck offering special access at a reported \$1.5 million price point. The reported package included access to a partner welcome reception, reserved press-conference seating, ceremonial weigh-ins, the [Zac Brown Band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zac_Brown_Band) concert, floor seats for [UFC 329](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_329), and [WWE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE) event integration and ring signage. [BBC Sport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_Sport) reported that UFC sources confirmed the existence of special-guest packages but did not confirm the price.[^cite_note-BBCPartner-8] [NBC News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBC_News) also reported that sponsorship packages including ringside seats had been selling for \$1 million or more, while the tickets themselves were free.[^cite_note-46]

### Regulation

[Marc Ratner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Ratner), the UFC Vice President of Regulatory Affairs, revealed that because the White House grounds are federal property, the District of Columbia Combat Sports Commission would not regulate the event. The UFC was then expected to self‑regulate, as the [District of Columbia Department of Parks and Recreation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/District_of_Columbia_Department_of_Parks_and_Recreation) is responsible for oversight of such events within the district.[^cite_note-47]

On March 16, the District of Columbia Combat Sports Commission announced that outcomes of the fights in this event could not be recognized on the athletes’ official records as the promotion was not going to pay a \$100 permit to hold the event.[^cite_note-48] In turn, the UFC announced that the [Association of Boxing Commissions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_Boxing_Commissions) (ABC), which oversees boxing and MMA in the U.S., will be on hand to "serve as an independent third party to advise on the regulatory operations" of the event. "As the event is being held on federal property, there is no requirement for the UFC to select a state athletic commission to oversee the event," ABC president Timothy Shipman said in a release. "The UFC expresses its commitment to ensuring that this event is among the most thoroughly regulated in the history of the sport and has requested that the ABC serve as an independent third party to assist in assembling the most qualified group of judges, referees and inspectors in the world."[^cite_note-49]

A [UFC Lightweight Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Lightweight_Championship) title unification bout between current champion and former [UFC Featherweight Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Featherweight_Championship) [Ilia Topuria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilia_Topuria) and current two-time interim champion (also a former [WSOF Lightweight Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WSOF_Lightweight_Championship)) [Justin Gaethje](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Gaethje) headlined the event.[^cite_note-bouts-53] [Arman Tsarukyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arman_Tsarukyan) was originally expected to serve as backup and potential replacement for this fight.[^cite_note-54] However, he opted against weighing in and [Diego Lopes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diego_Lopes_\(fighter\)), who was scheduled to fight earlier in the event at featherweight (see below), also weighed in as the new potential replacement.[^cite_note-55]

An interim [UFC Heavyweight Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Heavyweight_Championship) bout between former two-time [UFC Light Heavyweight Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Light_Heavyweight_Championship) [Alex Pereira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Pereira) (who also previously held the [UFC Middleweight Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Middleweight_Championship) and the [Glory Middleweight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glory_\(kickboxing\)#Glory_Middleweight_Championship) and [Light Heavyweight Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glory_\(kickboxing\)#Glory_Light_Heavyweight_Championship) titles in [kickboxing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kickboxing)) and former interim [Heavyweight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heavyweight_\(MMA\)) champion [Ciryl Gane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ciryl_Gane) served as the co-main event. Pereira vacated his title in April in order to move up and challenge for the interim heavyweight title.[^cite_note-PereiraGane-50] Current champion [Tom Aspinall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Aspinall) was unable to compete following an eye injury suffered during his title defense against Gane in October 2025 at [UFC 321](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_321).[^cite_note-56]

#### Contract discussions and fighter availability

Dana White stated during the post-fight press conference for [UFC 326](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_326) in March 2026 that, although there had been discussions between the promotion and [Jon Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Jones), a former two-time [UFC Light Heavyweight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Light_Heavyweight_Championship) and one-time [Heavyweight Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Heavyweight_Championship), he was never considered as a potential headliner for the event.[^cite_note-61] Days later, Jones disputed White's comments, stating that he had been in negotiations with the UFC in the days leading up to the announcement.[^cite_note-62] He also publicly requested his release from the promotion.[^cite_note-63]



President [Donald Trump](4848272.md) (center) with the four fighters involved in the headliners; from left to right: [Alex Pereira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Pereira), [Ilia Topuria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilia_Topuria), [Justin Gaethje](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Gaethje) and [Ciryl Gane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ciryl_Gane)

Ariel Helwani also reported that Jones was in negotiations to compete on the card, contradicting White's claim that Jones had not been considered, but that the sides did not agree on compensation. He further stated that [Conor McGregor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conor_McGregor) was not in serious talks to appear at the event and was instead expected to return during International Fight Week in July.[^cite_note-helwani-plans-64]

In a June 2026 interview, White stated that had current heavyweight champion Aspinall not been injured, he would have been defending his title against Pereira.[^cite_note-65]

Separately, Topuria and his manager stated that he had been offered current [UFC Welterweight Champion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Welterweight_Championship) (also former lightweight champion) [Islam Makhachev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islam_Makhachev) as a potential opponent before the bout with Gaethje was finalized.[^cite_note-66] According to Topuria, he selected Makhachev as his preferred opponent and expressed interest in moving up to challenge for the welterweight title in an attempt to become the first three-division champion in UFC history, but Makhachev informed UFC officials that an injury prevented him from competing.[^cite_note-67] In contrast to Topuria's claim that the bout fell through due to an injury on Makhachev's side, Makhachev stated that the negotiations broke down because Topuria and his team declined the UFC's offer after requesting \$20 million. According to Makhachev, that demand ultimately prevented the fight from being finalized.[^cite_note-68]

According to Helwani, the UFC's original planning for the event included a potential [UFC Women's Bantamweight Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Women's_Bantamweight_Championship) bout between reigning champion [Kayla Harrison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kayla_Harrison), who is a [2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judo_at_the_2012_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_78_kg) and [2016 Olympic gold medalist in judo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judo_at_the_2016_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women's_78_kg) and a two-time [PFL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_Fighters_League) women's lightweight tournament champion, and former two-time champion [Amanda Nunes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Nunes), who also held the [UFC Women's Featherweight Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Women's_Featherweight_Championship). However, Harrison was not ready to compete. He also reported that Pereira vs. Gane had initially been planned as the main event before the promotion asked Topuria whether he preferred to face Makhachev at welterweight or Gaethje at lightweight. Topuria reportedly chose Makhachev, but the bout was not made after Makhachev was unavailable due to a hand injury, leading to Gaethje receiving the title fight.[^cite_note-helwani-plans-64]

### Entertainment

Country singer [Zac Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zac_Brown) performed the [U.S. national anthem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Star-Spangled_Banner) at the event.[^cite_note-69] This marked the first time since [UFC 33](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_33) in September 2001 that the promotion planned for the national anthem to be performed during a fight card.[^cite_note-70] The fighters entered the Octagon from various locations inside the White House, including the [Oval Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oval_Office) for both headliners, with their walkout music performed live by the [United States Marine Band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Marine_Band).[^cite_note-71]

Unlike typical UFC events that show previews for upcoming fights and promotional videos between fights, this event played vignettes celebrating the 250th anniversary of the United States, much like the [UFC 306](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_306) event that took place inside [Sphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sphere_\(venue\)) in [Las Vegas](47737.md).[^cite_note-72]

## Legal challenges

On June 6, 2026, an emergency lawsuit was filed by the Public Integrity Project, an anti-corruption [watchdog group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watchdog_group), on behalf of two plaintiffs, which aimed to block the event from occurring, describing it as a "deeply corrupt ... private, for-profit sports event".[^cite_note-73] The lawsuit challenged the transformation of the White House grounds without congressional approval and the financial benefits of the event for the UFC, Dana White, Donald Trump, and their allies. A Trump administration official responded to the suit, calling it "obstructionist" and stating that the fight card was no different than other events hosted at the White House.[^cite_note-74][^cite_note-75] The lawsuit also highlighted that Trump purchased between \$15,001 and \$50,000 worth of stock in TKO Group Holdings, the UFC's parent company, in March 2026. A [Citizens for Responsibility and Ethics in Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizens_for_Responsibility_and_Ethics_in_Washington) spokesperson called it "one of the worst conflicts of interest you could imagine".[^cite_note-76] White later said that they were already expecting a lawsuit, but they "thought it would be sooner".[^cite_note-77] On June 10, attorneys representing the [National Park Service](161535.md) and [U.S. Department of the Interior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Department_of_the_Interior) filed a memorandum in opposition to the plaintiffs’ emergency application for a temporary restraining order.[^cite_note-78] On June 12, Judge [Amit Mehta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amit_Mehta) of the [U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._District_Court_for_the_District_of_Columbia) refused to issue a preliminary injunction, allowing the event to proceed as planned.[^cite_note-Gardner_and_Peter_2026-79]

## Bonus awards

In April 2026, the UFC announced that [Crypto.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crypto.com) would fund a US\$1 million [cryptocurrency](36662188.md) bonus for the event, intended to reward the top performances on the card.[^cite_note-83] The special \$1 million cryptocurrency prize pool created for the event was split evenly between Justin Gaethje and Ilia Topuria.[^cite_note-84]

On June 12, 2026, Dana White announced that the fight night awards for this event would receive a major increase as [World Liberty Financial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Liberty_Financial) (a venture of the [Trump family](52170275.md) and the family of [Steve Witkoff](40901053.md), Trump’s friend and special envoy to the Middle East) joined as a new sponsor and added \$250,000 to the bonus pool. In addition to the standard UFC amounts, "Performance of the Night" recipients earned \$100,000 from the UFC, \$125,000 in [USD1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Liberty_Financial#USD1) sponsored by World Liberty Financial, and \$200,000 in CRO sponsored by Crypto.com, for a total of \$425,000 each. "Fight of the Night" winners received \$100,000 from the UFC along with \$300,000 in CRO from Crypto.com, totaling \$400,000 per athlete. The additional \$25,000 UFC finish bonuses for knockouts or submissions not selected among the main post‑fight awards also remained in effect.[^cite_note-85][^cite_note-86][^cite_note-87]

## Aftermath

This event marked the [only UFC event to end with all fights ending by knockout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_UFC_records#Highest_percentage_of_knockouts_-_In_a_single_event).[^cite_note-Stern_2026-9] It also tied events [UFC Fight Night: Rockhold vs. Bisping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_Fight_Night:_Rockhold_vs._Bisping) and [UFC on ESPN: Santos vs. Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFC_on_ESPN:_Santos_vs._Hill) for having [all fights end by finish at a modern-era UFC event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_UFC_records#Highest_percentage_of_finishes_-_In_a_single_event), which had 11 and 10 fights respectively.[^cite_note-Hiergesell_2026-10]

In a post-fight interview with [Joe Rogan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Rogan), fighter [Josh Hokit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Hokit) repeated previously stated false claims that former [First Lady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Lady) [Michelle Obama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Obama) is [actually a man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transvestigation). His comments drew outrage throughout social media. Dana White told *Time* he is "completely against saying nasty and false things about people's families." However, no sanction was announced.[^cite_note-88]

### Attempted terrorist attack

After the event, the [FBI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FBI) claimed they disrupted an attempted terrorist attack targeting attendees of the event. According to investigators, the plan involved using [explosive-laden drones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-way_attack_drone) to strike buildings near the event, triggering an evacuation. Attendees would then be directed toward an area where snipers were positioned, followed by a planned attack on the White House gates. The FBI reportedly discovered the threat on June 10, with suspects allegedly conducting final preparations in [Fredericksburg, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredericksburg,_Virginia). Numerous suspects were later taken into custody.[^cite_note-Lebowitz_2026-89][^cite_note-90]

On July 9, 2026, the [U.S. Department of Justice](52563.md) announced that a federal grand jury has indicted eight men over an alleged plot to attack the event.[^cite_note-91]

## Reception

*[The New York Times](30680.md)* described it as a "mass-media spectacle unlike any in the history of the presidency", highlighting its use of the White House for "violent cage fighting" and eruptions of "casual cruelty" as "astonishing".[^cite_note-McCreesh_20260615-20] The *[Associated Press](18935732.md)* described the event as "[bread and circuses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bread_and_circuses)" and interviewed historians who compared it to gladiatorial games held in [Imperial Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Rome).[^cite_note-Weissert_2026-92] Conversely, [Michael Caputo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Caputo) praised the event in *[The American Conservative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_American_Conservative)*, dismissing critics as elitist "[snobs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snob)".[^cite_note-93] Polling conducted by [Reuters](18998750.md) and [Ipsos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ipsos) found that only 16% of Americans found the event appropriate to hold on the White House lawn, with only a third of Republicans approving of the plan.[^cite_note-\:0-13]

### Viewership

According to Dana White, the card brought in high numbers of viewers with reportedly 200,000 fans attending the two-day event on the Ellipse, and that viewership numbers on Paramount+ were "monstrous".[^cite_note-94][^cite_note-95] It was broadcast exclusively on [Paramount+](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount+) in the U.S. and Latin America, with a reported average of 8.2 million viewers and at least 17 million unique viewers making it the most-watched exclusive live event on Paramount+ to-date.[^cite_note-Viewership_1-11]

The UFC stated that updated international metrics increased total viewership for the event to an estimated 34 million, setting the domestic record for the most‑watched event in the promotion's history.[^cite_note-Viewership_2-12]
