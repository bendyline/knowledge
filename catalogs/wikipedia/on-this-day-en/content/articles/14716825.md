The **Chaldean Catholic Patriarchate of Baghdad**, or simply the **Chaldean Patriarchate** ([Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_language): *Patriarchatus Chaldæorum*), is the official title held by the primate of the [Chaldean Catholic Church](698821.md). The Patriarchate is based in the [Cathedral of Our Lady of Sorrows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathedral_of_Our_Lady_of_Sorrows), [Baghdad](4492.md), [Iraq](7515928.md). The patriarchal see is currently held by [Mar Paul III Nona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_III_Nona). He is assisted by the [archbishop of Erbil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archbishop_of_Erbil) [Shlemon Warduni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shlemon_Warduni) and the [Auxiliary Bishop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auxiliary_Bishop) of Baghdad, [Basel Yaldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basel_Yaldo).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

[Chaldean Catholics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaldean_Catholics) are the majority of [Assyrians in Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assyrians_in_Iraq), an indigenous people of [Upper Mesopotamia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_Mesopotamia).[^cite_note-7][^cite_note-8][^cite_note-9][^cite_note-10][^cite_note-11]

## Etymology

In 1552, there was a [schism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schism_of_1552) within the [Church of the East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_the_East). This was caused by discontent among the bishops (metropolitans) over actions of the patriarch [Shemʿon VII Ishoʿyahb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shemon_VII_Ishoyahb) in following the tradition of previous patriarch Shemʿon IV Basidi who made the patriarchal succession hereditary, normally from uncle to nephew. [Joseph I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_I_\(Chaldean_Patriarch\)) (1681–1696), who served as the [Metropolitan of Amid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaldean_Diocese_of_Amid) (modern-day [Diyarbakır](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diyarbak%C4%B1r), Turkey) led an off-shoot of the Church of the East and joined the [Catholic Church](606848.md). His successor, [Joseph II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_II_\(Chaldean_Patriarch\)) (1696–1713), was officially bestowed with the symbolic title *Patriarch of Babylon*. Although this patriarchate was established in the city of Diyarbakır, it was eventually moved to the city of [Mosul](200475.md) and finally to [Baghdad](4492.md) where it remains to this day. The title *Patriarch of Babylon* or *Patriarch of Babylon of the Chaldeans* remained in popular usage until the name [Babylon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babylon) was officially abandoned in August, 2021.[^cite_note-12][^cite_note-13][^cite_note-14][^cite_note-15][^cite_note-16][^cite_note-17]
