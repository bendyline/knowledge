The **Legislative Assembly** ([Spanish](26825.md): *Asamblea Legislativa*) is the [legislative branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature) of the government of [El Salvador](9356.md).

## History

The organization was founded in 1824 as the Central American Congress ([Spanish](26825.md): *Congreso Federal Centroamericano*).[^cite_note-historia-1] The 1824 constitution first established a bicameral legislature,[^cite_note-incep-2] including Senate. The 1880 constitution retained a bicameral legislature. The 1886 constitution introduced a unicameral legislature.[^cite_note-incep-2]

## Structure



Blue Room of the Legislative Assembly of El Salvador.

The Salvadoran legislature is a [unicameral](204304.md) body. Until 2024, it was made up of 84 [deputies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamber_of_Deputies), all of whom are elected by direct popular vote according to open-list proportional representation to serve three-year terms and are eligible for immediate re-election. Of these, 64 were elected in 14 multi-seat [constituencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constituency), corresponding to the country's 14 [departments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Departments_of_El_Salvador), which return between 3 and 16 deputies each. The remaining 20 deputies were selected on the basis of a single national [constituency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constituency).

To be eligible for election to the assembly, candidates must be (Art. 126, Constitution):

- over 25;

- Salvadoran citizens by birth, born of at least one parent to be a Salvadoran citizen;

- of recognised honesty and education, and

- have not had the privilege of one's rights as a citizen cancelled in the previous five years.

On 1 June 2023, Salvadoran president [Nayib Bukele](46452145.md) issued a proposal to the Legislative Assembly to reduce the number of its seats from 84 to 60.[^cite_note-3] The proposal was passed by the Legislative Assembly on 7 June 2023 and went into effect on 1 May 2024.[^cite_note-4]

## Other parliamentary bodies

El Salvador also returns 20 deputies to the supranational [Central American Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_American_Parliament), also elected according to [open-list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_list) proportional representation from a single national constituency.
