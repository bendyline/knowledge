**Balboa** is a town and municipality in the [Cauca Department](549662.md), [Colombia](5222.md).

Founded by Order No. 02 on 3 November 1967, the municipality covers an area of 360 km<sup>2</sup> (139 sq mi) and has a population of 27,030.[^cite_note-caucanet-2] The population is primarily engaged in agriculture and [ranching](30865437.md).[^cite_note-caucanet-2]
