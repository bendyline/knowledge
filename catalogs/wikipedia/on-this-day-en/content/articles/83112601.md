On 5 May 2026, a 13-year-old boy opened fire at the Instituto São José school in [Rio Branco, Acre](818818.md), Brazil. The shooter killed two school workers and injured two other people, including an 11-year-old student, before being apprehended by police.[^cite_note-2][^cite_note-3][^cite_note-\:2-4]

## Shooting

At 13\:30, the 13-year-old student entered the school with a gun and opened fire.[^cite_note-5] Since the school was undergoing renovations, other students thought the shooting sounds were coming from the construction activity.[^cite_note-6] A worker of a hotel next to the school said that some students tried to climb a six-meter wall next to the hotel; only one was successful, and they hid in the hotel.[^cite_note-7] The shooter later surrendered to the police.

## Victims

The deceased were identified as 53-year-old Alzenir Pereira da Silva and 37-year-old Raquel Sales Feitosa.[^cite_note-\:0-8][^cite_note-\:1-9][^cite_note-\:3-10] A third employee and an 11-year-old student were also shot.[^cite_note-\:0-8][^cite_note-\:1-9] Both were discharged from the hospital.[^cite_note-\:0-8][^cite_note-\:3-10]

## Perpetrator

The perpetrator was identified as a 13-year-old student who used his stepfather’s [.38 TPC pistol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/.38_TPC). His stepfather is a lawyer.[^cite_note-11] Authorities are investigating the motives behind the attack, including the possibility of school [bullying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bullying).[^cite_note-12] According to investigators, some students knew of the plan in advance.[^cite_note-13]

## Aftermath

Shortly after the attack ended, students formed a [prayer circle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prayer_circle) in the school's [pitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitch_\(sports_field\)).[^cite_note-14]

After the attack, the municipal governments of Rio Branco and Acre suspended classes at all schools and high schools in the region for three days (8 May 2026).[^cite_note-\:4-15] The classes will be returned in the Monday (11 May 2026).[^cite_note-\:5-16] The government also announced measures such as follow-up care for the victims of the attack and psychological support for students and teachers.[^cite_note-\:5-16]

The shooter’s stepfather responsible for the gun was fired from his state government job and also detained, later released after being charged with neglecting to keep the gun away from a child's reach, a [misdemeanor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Misdemeanor) under [Brazilian gun laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gun_control_in_Brazil).[^cite_note-17][^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19]
