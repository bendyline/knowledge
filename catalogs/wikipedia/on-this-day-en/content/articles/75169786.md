**Fernando Gabriel Mendoza V** (born October 1, 2003) is an American professional [football](18951490.md) [quarterback](25277.md) for the [Las Vegas Raiders](22312.md) of the [National Football League](21211.md) (NFL). Mendoza played [college football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_football) for the [California Golden Bears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Golden_Bears_football) for three seasons before transferring to the [Indiana Hoosiers](6135487.md) in 2025, where he won the [Heisman Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heisman_Trophy) en route to a [national championship](75819266.md). He was selected by the Raiders [first overall](588809.md) in the [2026 NFL draft](73265133.md).

## Early life

Fernando Gabriel Mendoza V was born on October 1, 2003, in [Boston](24437894.md), Massachusetts, to Elsa Espino and Fernando G. Mendoza IV, who was completing his [medical residency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Residency_\(medicine\)) at the time.[^cite_note-EisenbergDraft-1] He has two younger brothers, [Alberto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberto_Mendoza) and Maximo.[^cite_note-2] His family is of [Cuban](1288758.md) descent; all four of his grandparents were born and raised in Cuba before [emigrating to Miami, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuban_migration_to_Miami), in 1960 following the [Cuban Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuban_Revolution).[^cite_note-3] Through his family lineage, Mendoza is a descendant of Don Antonio Ysidoro González de Mendoza Bonilla (1828–1906), a prominent lawyer of [Spanish](963152.md) descent who served as the first president of the [Supreme Court of Cuba](5880536.md).[^cite_note-4][^cite_note-5][^cite_note-6]

Mendoza was raised in Miami and attended [Christopher Columbus High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Columbus_High_School_\(Miami-Dade_County,_Florida\)) in the [Miami-Dade County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami-Dade_County,_Florida) community of [Westchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westchester,_Florida).[^cite_note-MiamiRoots-7][^cite_note-herald-8][^cite_note-9] Growing up, he considered [Tom Brady](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Brady) to be his football idol.[^cite_note-10] As his high school team's starting [quarterback](25277.md) across two seasons, Mendoza completed 133 of 203 [pass attempts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pass_attempt) for 1,396 yards and 16 [touchdowns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Touchdowns) with four [interceptions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interception), while also rushing for 137 yards and a touchdown on the ground.[^cite_note-\:0-11] During his senior year in 2021, he led Columbus to an 11–3 record and a berth in the state semifinals against [Venice High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice_High_School_\(Florida\)) in the [FHSAA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FHSAA) Class 8A football playoffs.[^cite_note-\:0-11][^cite_note-12]

## College career

### California

#### 2022 season

See also: [2022 California Golden Bears football team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_California_Golden_Bears_football_team)

Mendoza initially committed to play [college football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_football) for the [Yale Bulldogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_Bulldogs_football) in 2021. In February 2022, however, he [signed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Letter_of_Intent) with the [California Golden Bears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Golden_Bears_football) of the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Golden_Bears_football).[^cite_note-EisenbergDraft-1][^cite_note-13] Mendoza [redshirted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redshirted) during the [2022 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_California_Golden_Bears_football_team).[^cite_note-14]

#### 2023 season

See also: [2023 California Golden Bears football team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_California_Golden_Bears_football_team)

Mendoza earned his first career start for California in week six of the [2023 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_California_Golden_Bears_football_team) against [Oregon State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Oregon_State_Beavers_football_team).[^cite_note-15] He completed 21 of 32 pass attempts for 207 yards and two touchdowns with an interception in a loss to the Beavers.[^cite_note-16] A week later, he was 10 of 17 passing for 149 yards, two touchdowns and an interception in a loss to [Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Utah_Utes_football_team).[^cite_note-17] Mendoza was named the team's starting quarterback heading forward.[^cite_note-18][^cite_note-19] In eight starts as a [redshirt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redshirt_\(college_sports\)) freshman, he completed 63 percent of his passes for 1,708 yards and 14 touchdowns, leading the Golden Bears to a 6–7 record and an [Independence Bowl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Independence_Bowl) appearance against [Texas Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Texas_Tech_Red_Raiders_football_team).[^cite_note-20]

#### 2024 season

See also: [2024 California Golden Bears football team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_California_Golden_Bears_football_team)

Entering the [2024 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_California_Golden_Bears_football_team), Mendoza won the starting quarterback job, beating graduate transfer [Chandler Rogers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chandler_Rogers).[^cite_note-21] In week two against [Auburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Auburn_Tigers_football_team), Mendoza completed 25 of 36 passes for 233 yards and two touchdowns, leading the Bears to an upset win over the Tigers.[^cite_note-22] Mendoza was named ACC quarterback of the week in back-to-back games against [Oregon State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Oregon_State_Beavers_football_team) and [Wake Forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Wake_Forest_Demon_Deacons_football_team), setting career highs in passing yards and completions. His 56 passing attempts against Wake Forest were the most by a California quarterback since 2016. On December 11, 2024, Mendoza announced he would be entering the [NCAA transfer portal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCAA_transfer_portal).[^cite_note-23] In 2025, he graduated with a [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in [Business Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_administration) from Berkeley's [Haas School of Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haas_School_of_Business).[^cite_note-24]

### Indiana



Mendoza playing in the [2026 Rose Bowl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Rose_Bowl) with the [Indiana Hoosiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Indiana_Hoosiers_football_team)



Mendoza following the [2026 CFP championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_CFP_championship)

On December 23, 2024, Mendoza announced that he would transfer to [Indiana University Bloomington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University_Bloomington) to play for the [Hoosiers](6135487.md).[^cite_note-25] At Indiana, he pursued a [Master of Business Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Business_Administration) (MBA) degree at the [Kelley School of Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelley_School_of_Business).[^cite_note-26]

#### 2025 season

See also: [2025 Indiana Hoosiers football team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Indiana_Hoosiers_football_team)

In the [2025 season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Indiana_Hoosiers_football_team) opener against the [Old Dominion Monarchs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Old_Dominion_Monarchs_football_team), Mendoza completed 18 of 31 passes for 193 yards and scored a touchdown on a five-yard rush as Indiana won 27–14.[^cite_note-27] He completed 18 of 25 passes for 245 yards and four touchdowns against the [Kennesaw State Owls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Kennesaw_State_Owls_football_team), leading the Hoosiers to a 56–9 win.[^cite_note-28] Against the [Indiana State Sycamores](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Indiana_State_Sycamores_football_team), Mendoza completed 19 of 20 passes for 270 yards and five touchdowns, and ran for an additional touchdown in a 73–0 win; he did not return to the game after halftime.[^cite_note-29]

Mendoza threw for a career-high five passing touchdowns against the No. 9 [Illinois Fighting Illini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Illinois_Fighting_Illini_football_team) on 21 of 23 passing with 267 yards.[^cite_note-30] In a 20–15 win against the [Iowa Hawkeyes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Iowa_Hawkeyes_football_team), Mendoza completed 13 of 23 passes for 233 yards, two touchdowns, and one interception.[^cite_note-31] He completed 20 of 31 passes for 215 yards, one touchdown, and one interception in a 30–20 upset win on the road against the No. 3 [Oregon Ducks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Oregon_Ducks_football_team).[^cite_note-32] Mendoza threw for a season-high 332 yards on 24 of 28 passing while defeating the [Michigan State Spartans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Michigan_State_Spartans_football_team) 38–13.[^cite_note-33] He led the Hoosiers to a 56–6 win against the [UCLA Bruins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_UCLA_Bruins_football_team), completing 15 of 22 passes for 168 yards, three touchdowns, and one interception, adding a rushing touchdown; he left the game in the third quarter.[^cite_note-34] Mendoza completed 14 of 21 passes for 201 yards, one touchdown, and one interception, with one rushing touchdown, as the Hoosiers defeated the [Maryland Terrapins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Maryland_Terrapins_football_team), 55–10.[^cite_note-35]

In a 27–24 road win against the [Penn State Nittany Lions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Penn_State_Nittany_Lions_football_team), Mendoza completed 19 of 30 passes for 218 yards, one touchdown, and one interception, adding a rushing touchdown; the passing touchdown, to wide receiver [Omar Cooper Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omar_Cooper_Jr.), came in the final moments of the game to take the lead and was widely described as "a [Heisman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heisman) moment".[^cite_note-36][^cite_note-37] Against the [Wisconsin Badgers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Wisconsin_Badgers_football_team), he threw his 30th touchdown pass of the season, eclipsing the Indiana single-season program record set by [Kurtis Rourke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurtis_Rourke) the previous season; Mendoza finished the game 22 of 24 passing for 299 yards and four touchdowns in a 31–7 win.[^cite_note-38] In the final game of the regular season, the [Old Oaken Bucket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Oaken_Bucket) rivalry game against the [Purdue Boilermakers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Purdue_Boilermakers_football_team), Mendoza completed 8 of 15 passes for 177 yards and two touchdowns, with one rushing touchdown, before leaving the game in the third quarter; Indiana won 56–3 to seal the program's first perfect regular season in the coldest game ever played at [Ross-Ade Stadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross-Ade_Stadium).[^cite_note-39]

In the [2025 Big Ten Championship Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Big_Ten_Football_Championship_Game) against the No. 1 [Ohio State Buckeyes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Ohio_State_Buckeyes_football_team), Mendoza threw for 222 yards and a touchdown, leading Indiana to a 13–10 victory and their first Big Ten title since 1967.[^cite_note-40] He was named the game's [MVP](235000.md) for his performance.[^cite_note-41] Mendoza led Indiana to [its first bowl game win since 1991](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_Indiana_Hoosiers_football_team), defeating the No. 9 [Alabama Crimson Tide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Alabama_Crimson_Tide_football_team) 38–3 in the [2026 Rose Bowl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Rose_Bowl);[^cite_note-42] he completed 14 of 16 passes for 192 yards and three touchdowns.[^cite_note-43] Mendoza completed 17 of 20 passes for 177 yards and five touchdowns while defeating Oregon 56–22 in the [2026 Peach Bowl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Peach_Bowl).[^cite_note-44] In the [2026 College Football Playoff National Championship](75819266.md) against the [Miami Hurricanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Miami_Hurricanes_football_team), he completed 16 passes for 186 yards while also rushing for a 12-yard touchdown, leading the Hoosiers to a 27–21 victory and the school's first-ever national title.[^cite_note-45] For his performance, Mendoza received the Offensive Player of the Game award.[^cite_note-46]

For the 2025 season, Mendoza was named the [AP College Football Player of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AP_College_Football_Player_of_the_Year),[^cite_note-47] the winner of the [Chicago Tribune Silver Football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Tribune_Silver_Football),[^cite_note-48] and the [Walter Camp Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Camp_Award).[^cite_note-49] He was also the winner of both the [Maxwell Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxwell_Award) and the [Davey O'Brien Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davey_O'Brien_Award). He was also named [Big Ten Offensive Player of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Ten_Offensive_Player_of_the_Year), [Big Ten Quarterback of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Ten_Quarterback_of_the_Year), and [first-team All-Big Ten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_All-Big_Ten_Conference_football_team).[^cite_note-50] He was announced to be one of the four [Heisman Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heisman_Trophy) finalists, along with Ohio State quarterback [Julian Sayin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julian_Sayin), Vanderbilt quarterback [Diego Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diego_Pavia), and Notre Dame running back [Jeremiyah Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremiyah_Love), ultimately being named the recipient of the award.[^cite_note-51] Mendoza became the first player in Indiana school history to win the Heisman Trophy.[^cite_note-52] After the season, he announced he would forego his final year of [NCAA eligibility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCAA_eligibility) and declare for the [2026 NFL draft](73265133.md).[^cite_note-53]

## Personal life

Mendoza is a devout [Roman Catholic](606848.md).[^cite_note-62] He has spoken publicly about his faith and incorporates daily [Mass](5684685.md) into his game-day routine. While at Indiana, he also helped organize team [Bible studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bible_study_\(Christianity\)) on campus and has credited his faith with fostering camaraderie among his teammates.[^cite_note-63][^cite_note-64][^cite_note-65]

Mendoza's younger brother, [Alberto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberto_Mendoza), was the backup quarterback for the [Indiana Hoosiers](6135487.md) during Mendoza's tenure; he transferred to the [Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Tech_Yellow_Jackets_football) in January 2026.[^cite_note-66][^cite_note-67][^cite_note-68]

### Cultural identity



Fernando Mendoza greets [Dominican priests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominican_Order) he invited from the campus ministry at Indiana University to the 2026 CFP National Championship.

Mendoza cites his Cuban heritage as being a major factor for his and his brother's passion as football players. In a 2025 interview with [Indiana University Bloomington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University_Bloomington) news website *Peegs.com*, he said, "Alberto and I play football not for ourselves, not for fulfillment and satisfaction of ourselves—we have a lot of whys why we do it for. One of the whys is our mom. Another why is our entire family. Our entire family comes from a Cuban background. All of our grandparents were born and raised in Cuba, and that's something we always take deeply to heart".[^cite_note-69][^cite_note-PatForde-70]

He says that his [Spanish](26825.md) language skills are more conversational than fluent, yet he delivered portions of his 2025 [Heisman Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heisman_Trophy) acceptance speech in Spanish to honor his family.[^cite_note-71] Mendoza has emphasized the role of the "prideful and supportive" nature of Hispanic family culture as a cornerstone of his professional stability.[^cite_note-PatForde-70]

On December 18, 2025, Mendoza was named the inaugural recipient of the [Hispanic Football Hall of Fame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_Football_Hall_of_Fame)'s [Hispanic College Football Player of the Year Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_Football_Hall_of_Fame#Hispanic_Football_Player_of_the_Year).[^cite_note-72]

### Business ventures

Mendoza is an advocate for the [National Multiple Sclerosis Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Multiple_Sclerosis_Society), and his mother, Elsa, lives with [multiple sclerosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiple_sclerosis). Mendoza, alongside his brother, has created multiple menu items at restaurants in the towns of the colleges in which they have played; the money raised has gone to multiple sclerosis research. The items include the "Mendoza Burrito" at La Burrita in [Berkeley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California), as well as the "Mendoza Bros. Burger" at BuffaLouie's and the "Mendoza Bros. Cubano" at Gable's Bagels in [Bloomington, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomington,_Indiana).[^cite_note-73][^cite_note-74]

In March 2026, Mendoza participated in an online promotional campaign for [Epic Games](573199.md)' [Fortnite](57813623.md) alongside [Olympic figure skater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figure_skating_at_the_Olympic_Games) [Alysa Liu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alysa_Liu).[^cite_note-75][^cite_note-76] In April 2026, he appeared in a commercial for the professional networking plaform [LinkedIn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LinkedIn) as part of his NFL draft campaign; the collaboration stemmed from his notable use of the platform to share career milestones.[^cite_note-77] Mendoza has also signed [name, image, and likeness (NIL)](68930532.md) partnerships with brands including [Adidas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adidas), [Dr Pepper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr_Pepper), and [T-Mobile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T-Mobile_US).[^cite_note-78]
