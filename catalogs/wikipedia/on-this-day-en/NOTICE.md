This news catalog selects the last 365 UTC dates from Wikipedia's daily
current-events portal, including the current (potentially incomplete) day, and
the Wikipedia articles linked directly from those daily news sections. It does
not import anniversary lists. Referent articles are current revision-pinned
snapshots, rather than reconstructions of what they said on each news date.
Repeated mentions and redirect aliases share one article identified by its
canonical Wikipedia page ID. `LICENSES/selection.json` records the date window
and each day's article IDs. Missing or non-article referents are recorded there.

Wikipedia text is adapted to Markdown, navigation and images are omitted, and
links may be rewritten. Per-document revision and history URLs credit Wikipedia
contributors. Rendered HTML hashes capture template-dependent input differences.
The automatic standard-open license assessment records Wikipedia's site-wide
rights declaration and the recognized full license text in `LICENSES/`.
External news links remain citations; external news stories and separately
licensed media are not downloaded as part of this catalog.
